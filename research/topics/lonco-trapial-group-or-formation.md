## Is Lonco Trapial a group or a formation, and what rank is Cerro Carnerero?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Lonco Trapial is a formation, and Cerro Carnerero is one of its members. Governs `rank` on [Lonco Trapial](../../stratigraphy/l/lonco-trapial.yml) and [Cerro Carnerero](../../stratigraphy/c/cerro-carnerero.yml), `parent: Lonco Trapial` on Cerro Carnerero, and `location.formation: Lonco Trapial` with `location.member: Cerro Carnerero` on [*Amygdalodon patagonicus*](../../genera/A/Amygdalodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nullo1975a, p. 142: Lesta and Ferello "definen el Grupo Lonco Trapial con las Formaciones Cajón de Ginebra, Cañadón Puelman y Cerro Carnerero" ("define the Lonco Trapial Group with the formations Cajón de Ginebra, Cañadón Puelman and Cerro Carnerero", translated).
- nullo1975a, p. 142: the group "debe ser considerada como Formación" ("should be considered a Formation", translated), and "las Formaciones que integraban el Grupo Lonco Trapial deben ser consideradas como miembros" ("the formations that made up the Lonco Trapial Group should be considered members", translated).
- nullo1975a, p. 142: "Formación Lonco Trapial: Miembro Pampa de Agnia. Miembro Cerro Carnerero. Miembro Cajón de Ginebra."
- nullo1975a, p. 133, Resumen: "este último se redefine como Formación Lonco Trapial" ("the latter is redefined as the Lonco Trapial Formation", translated).
- cúneo2013a, p. 1269 (in-press version, p. 3), Regional geology and stratigraphy: "The overlying Lonco Trapial Formation".
- pol2020a, p. 3, Radioisotopic geochronology: "volcanic and pyroclastic rocks of the Lonco Trapial Formation".
- zaffarana2020a, p. 2, Introduction: "known as the Lonco Trapial Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lonco Trapial Group.* Rauhut (2003) calls the Cerro Carnerero "the basal formation of the Lonco Trapial Group" (rauhut2003c, p. 174), using the 1972 scheme that Nullo and Proserpio (1975) replaced. Cúneo and colleagues (2013) speak of "the so-called Lonco Trapial Group (e.g., Nullo and Proserpio, 1975…)" (cúneo2013a, p. 1269), which cites for the group the paper that abolished it; they use formation rank in the next paragraph.
- *Cerro Carnerero Formation.* Rauhut (2003, p. 174) and Pol and colleagues (2022: "found in the Cerro Carnerero Formation", pol2022a, p. 135) keep formation rank. Both follow the pre-1975 scheme; neither argues for it.
- *Cerro Carnerero as the value of `location.formation`.* A member name in the formation field misstates the rank; the member goes in `location.member` under formation Lonco Trapial.

**Open.** Cúneo and colleagues (2013, p. 1269) also place the Taquetrén Formation in their "Lonco Trapial Group", while Nullo and Proserpio (1975, p. 133) treat the Taquetrén as a separate, younger unit. Not examined further.
