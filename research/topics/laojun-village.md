# Laojun Village

## How is the Laojun Village locality in Yunyang written?

*2026-10-10*

**Conclusion.** "Laojun Village, Pu'an Township, Yunyang County", Dai and colleagues' (2022) printed form, on every record from the site. The five describing papers spell the same place several ways, and one site takes one spelling. Governs `location.locality` on [*Bashanosaurus primitivus*](../../genera/B/Bashanosaurus.yml), [*Sanxiasaurus modaoxiensis*](../../genera/S/Sanxiasaurus.yml), [*Yunyangosaurus puanensis*](../../genera/Y/Yunyangosaurus.yml), [*Omeisaurus puxiani*](../../genera/O/Omeisaurus.yml) and [*Yuzhoulong qurenensis*](../../genera/Y/Yuzhoulong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dai2022b: "Laojun Village, Pu'an Township, Yunyang County, Chongqing Municipality".
- li2021a: "Laojun Village, Puan Township, Yunyang County, Chongqing Municipality".
- dai2020a: "Laojun Village, Puan Township, Yunyang City, Chongqing".
- tan2021a: "Laojun Village, Pu'an Town, Yunyang County, Chongqing Municipality".
- dai2022a: "Laojun Village, Pu'an Town, Yunyang Country, Chongqing Municipality".

**Ruled out.**
- *Each record on its own paper's spelling.* The differences are spelling, not different sites; "Yunyang Country" is a misprint.

**Open.** Nothing.
