# Zhucheng sample numbers

## Are 090414 and 090413 locality numbers for the Zhucheng bonebeds?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** No. They are the prefixes of An and colleagues' (2015) detrital-zircon sample numbers: the 090414 samples come from the Kugou–Longgujian section and 090413-4-D from Zangjiazhuang. Neither is a site designation, and the records carry the bare site names. Governs `location.locality: Longgujian` on [*Shantungosaurus giganteus*](../../genera/S/Shantungosaurus.yml) and `location.locality: Zangjiazhuang` on [*Sinankylosaurus*](../../genera/S/Sinankylosaurus.yml), [*Sinoceratops*](../../genera/S/Sinoceratops.yml), [*Zhuchengtitan*](../../genera/Z/Zhuchengtitan.yml) and [*Zhuchengtyrannus*](../../genera/Z/Zhuchengtyrannus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- an2015a, p. 76: "Four samples, 090414-2-D, 090414-5-D, 090414-24-D, and 090414-25-D, were collected from a section in Kugou–Longgujian … sample 090413-4-D was collected from Zangjiazhuang … and sample 090415-D was collected outside Kugou".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Loc. 090414" for Longgujian and "loc. 090413" for Zangjiazhuang.* The digit strings match the prefixes of An and colleagues' sample numbers, one per sampled area, and no paper read uses them as locality numbers.
- *An and colleagues' section coordinates as the specimens' positions.* The coordinates beside each sample (an2015a, p. 76) locate the sampled sections, not any holotype.

**Open.** Nothing.
