# Laochangqing

## How are the Laochangqing localities written?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** The village is "Laochangqing", the form of the *Chuanjiesaurus* describing paper's locality line, on all three records. Only Wu and colleagues (2009) number a quarry, for the *Shidaisaurus* site, so "Quarry 1" goes on that record alone; the quarry shared by *Chuanjiesaurus* and *Analong* is unnumbered, and nothing ties it to Wu's quarry 1. Governs `location.locality` on [*Chuanjiesaurus anaensis*](../../genera/C/Chuanjiesaurus.yml), [*Analong chuanjieensis*](../../genera/A/Analong.yml) and [*Shidaisaurus jinae*](../../genera/S/Shidaisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fang2000a: "the village of Madishan which lies 1.8 km SW of the village of Laochangqing in Chuanjie Township".
- ren2020a, Discussion: "Though preserved in the same quarry, comparative morphology suggests that *Chuanjiesaurus anaensis* … is different from *Analong*".
- wu2009a: "a new dinosaur site near Laochangjing, A'na Village, Chuanjie Township"; "In quarry 1 of the site, eight dinosaur skeletons have been exposed".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Laochangjing".* A second romanization of the same village.
- *"Quarry 1" on* Chuanjiesaurus *and* Analong. The number is Wu's, for the *Shidaisaurus* site.

**Open.** Whether the *Shidaisaurus* site is the *Chuanjiesaurus* quarry.
