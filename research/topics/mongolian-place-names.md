# Mongolian place names

## Which spellings do Mongolian locality names take, and on what authority?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Locality names follow Benton's (2000) gazetteer, which gives a canonical form for each site and cross-references the older transcriptions to it: Altan Uul, Bayan Zag, Hermiin Tsav, Baishin Tsav, Üüden Sair, Tsagaan Khushuu, and Javkhlant as the element of Khugenetjavkhlant. A unit keeps the name it was erected under, so the Bayn Dzak member is unaffected (see [Bayn Dzak](../stratigraphy/b/bayn-dzak.md) and [methods](../methods.md)); Bügiin Tsav has its own note ([Bügiin Tsav](bügiin-tsav.md)). Governs `location.locality` on the records below.

**Evidence.**
- benton2000c, p. xxii: "'Bayn Dzak' becomes 'Bayan Zag'"; p. xxiii: "Double vowels, 'aa', 'ee', 'ii', 'oo', 'öö', 'uu', and 'üü', indicate long vowels"; p. xxiii, older transcriptions in parentheses: "Tsav (tsab), gorge"; "Uul (ula), mountain".
- **Altan Uul**: benton2000c, p. xxiii: "Altan Uul [locality]". Gradziński and colleagues' glossary pairs "Altan Ula" with Алтан Уул (gradziński1969a, p. 81). Records: [*Borogovia*](../../genera/B/Borogovia.yml), [*Deinocheirus*](../../genera/D/Deinocheirus.yml), [*Gobiraptor*](../../genera/G/Gobiraptor.yml), [*Nemegtonykus*](../../genera/N/Nemegtonykus.yml), [*Opisthocoelicaudia*](../../genera/O/Opisthocoelicaudia.yml), [*Rinchenia*](../../genera/R/Rinchenia.yml).
- **Bayan Zag**: benton2000c, p. xxiv: "Bayan Zag [locality]", "Bayn Dzak (= Bayan Zag)", "Flaming Cliffs (= Bayan Zag)"; p. xxvi: "Shabarakh Usu (= Bayan Zag)". Records: [*Archaeornithoides*](../../genera/A/Archaeornithoides.yml), [*Saurornithoides*](../../genera/S/Saurornithoides.yml), [*Pinacosaurus*](../../genera/P/Pinacosaurus.yml), [*Protoceratops*](../../genera/P/Protoceratops.yml), [*Bainoceratops*](../../genera/B/Bainoceratops.yml), [*Oviraptor*](../../genera/O/Oviraptor.yml), [*Velociraptor*](../../genera/V/Velociraptor.yml).
- **Hermiin Tsav**: benton2000c, p. xxv: "Hermiin Tsav [locality]", "Khermeen (= Hermiin)", "Khermin (= Hermiin)". Records: [*Bagaceratops*](../../genera/B/Bagaceratops.yml), [*Ceratonykus*](../../genera/C/Ceratonykus.yml), [*Conchoraptor*](../../genera/C/Conchoraptor.yml), [*Harenadraco*](../../genera/H/Harenadraco.yml), [*Heyuannia*](../../genera/H/Heyuannia.yml), [*Manipulonyx*](../../genera/M/Manipulonyx.yml), [*Natovenator*](../../genera/N/Natovenator.yml), [*Tarchia*](../../genera/T/Tarchia.yml), [*Zaraapelta*](../../genera/Z/Zaraapelta.yml).
- **Baishin Tsav**: benton2000c, p. xxiii: "Baishin Tsav [locality]", "Baisheen (= Baishin)"; p. xxiv: "Baynshin (= Baishin)". Records: [*Erlikosaurus*](../../genera/E/Erlikosaurus.yml), [*Garudimimus*](../../genera/G/Garudimimus.yml), [*Gobihadros*](../../genera/G/Gobihadros.yml), [*Khankhuuluu*](../../genera/K/Khankhuuluu.yml).
- **Üüden Sair**: benton2000c, p. xxvii: "Üüden Sair [locality]", "Udan Sayr (= Üüden Sair)", "Ulaan Sair (= Üüden Sair)". Records: [*Avimimus*](../../genera/A/Avimimus.yml), [*Udanoceratops*](../../genera/U/Udanoceratops.yml).
- **Tsagaan Khushuu**: benton2000c, p. xxvii: "Tsagaan Khushuu [locality]", "Tsagan Ula (= Tsagaan Khushuu)"; Gradziński and colleagues' glossary gives Цааган Хушуу (gradziński1969a, p. 82). Records: [*Gallimimus*](../../genera/G/Gallimimus.yml), [*Alioramus*](../../genera/A/Alioramus.yml).
- **Khugenetjavkhlant**: son2022a, p. 3: "Khugenetjavkhlant is equivalent to the misspelled 'Khugenslavkhant' (Eberth et al., 2009; Tanaka et al., 2019) and 'Khugenetslavkant' (Makovicky & Norell, 2006; …"; benton2000c, p. xxv: "Javkhlant Uul [locality]", "Jibhalanta (= Javkhlant)", and p. xxiv: "Dzhibkhalan (= Javkhlant)", "Dzhibkhalantu (= Javkhlant)". Records: [*Yamaceratops*](../../genera/Y/Yamaceratops.yml), [*Albinykus*](../../genera/A/Albinykus.yml), [*Haya*](../../genera/H/Haya.yml).

**Ruled out.**
- *Khugenetslavkant.* Makovicky and Norell print it in the paper naming *Yamaceratops* ("sandstones at the Khugenetslavkant locality", makovicky2006a, p. 2; also "Kughenetslavkant", p. 18), and eight papers read for this note repeat it; Eberth and colleagues print "Khugenslavkhant", attributing it to Makovicky and Norell (eberth2009a, p. 295). Son and colleagues identify both as misspellings. How often a form is printed shows that it spread, not that it is right.
- *Altan Ula, Khermeen Tsav, Udan Sayr, Tsagaan Khushu, Shabarakh Usu, Flaming Cliffs.* Older transcriptions or other names that Benton cross-refers to the canonical forms above. That "Bayshin Tsav" is a rival of Baishin Tsav is our reading; Benton prints only "Baisheen" and "Baynshin".

**Open.** Whether Ulaan Sair and Üüden Sair are one site: Benton equates them, but Gradziński and colleagues gloss "Ulan Sayr" as Улаан Сайр (gradziński1969a, p. 82), a different word ("red") from *üüden*.

## Which form does the *Gobivenator* locality take?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** Zamyn Khond, Benton's (2000) form. The describing paper prints "Dzamin Khond", which Benton's list does not contain; it gives "Dzamyn Khond" as a cross-reference to Zamyn Khond. Governs `location.locality: Zamyn Khond` on [*Gobivenator mongoliensis*](../../genera/G/Gobivenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- benton2000c, p. xxiv: "Dzamyn Khond (= Zamyn Khond)"; p. xxviii: "Zamyn Khond [locality]".
- tsuihiji2014a, Type locality and age: "Dzamin Khond, Ömnögovi Aimag, Mongolia"; "This locality previously produced the specimen MPC-D 100/42, referred to Oviraptor".

**Ruled out.**
- *Dzamin Khond.* The describing paper's transcription. That it is a variant of Benton's Dzamyn Khond, differing by one vowel, is our reading; Benton does not list it.

**Open.** Nothing.
