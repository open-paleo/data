# Khulsan site numbers

## Which part of Khulsan are the *Tarchia kielanae* and *Tylocephale gilmorei* holotypes from?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** The Central Cliffs. Both describing papers place their holotype by a number on Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz's (1972) map of Khulsan: site 6 for *Tarchia kielanae* and site 10 for *Tylocephale gilmorei*. The map's caption says what was found at each number but names no part of the locality. On the map itself, sites 7, 6, 10 and 8 form one cluster beside the "Central Cliffs" label, with 9 just below. Governs `location.locality: Khulsan, Central Cliffs, site 6` on [*Tarchia kielanae*](../../genera/T/Tarchia.yml) and `location.locality: Khulsan, Central Cliffs, site 10` on [*Tylocephale gilmorei*](../../genera/T/Tylocephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maryańska1977a, p. 94, *Tarchia kielanae*: "incomplete skull with skull roof, occipital part and brain case. Khulsan, Mongolia, Upper Cretaceous, Barun Goyot Formation … GRADZINSKI & JERZYKIEWICZ, 1972, text-fig. 4, no. 6."
- maryańska1974a, p. 51: "Type locality: Khulsan, Nemegt Basin, Gobi Desert, Mongolian People's Republic (see GRADZINSKI & JERZYKIEWICZ, 1972, Text-fig. 4, No. 10)."
- gradziński1972a, p. 24, Fig. 4: "6 — partial skull of an ankylosaurid dinosaur"; "10 — skull of a pachycephalosaurid dinosaur". The place names Northern Cliffs, Central Cliffs, Camp 1971, Eldorado and Main Sayr are printed on the map, not in the caption.

**Ruled out.**
- *Site 6 beside the 1971 camp.* At low resolution a circled number just above the camp can be read as 6. It is a 4; the group around the camp is 2, 3, 4, 5 and 12, and 11 lies apart, below the Northern Cliffs label.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which part of Khulsan is the *Saichania chulsanensis* holotype from?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** The Northern Cliffs. Maryańska (1977) places GI SPS 100/151 by number on the same map of Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz (1972) as the two holotypes below: site 11, whose caption entry is "a skeleton with skull of an ankylosaurid dinosaur". On the map, 11 stands alone just below the "Northern Cliffs" label, away from the Central Cliffs group (7, 6, 10, 8 and 9) and the group around the 1971 camp. Governs `location.locality` on [*Saichania chulsanensis*](../../genera/S/Saichania.yml), which would read `Khulsan, Northern Cliffs, site 11` on the pattern of the entry below.

**Evidence.**
- maryańska1977a, p. 94, *Saichania chulsanensis*: "GI SPS 100/151 – skull and anterior part of postcranial skeleton … Khulsan, Mongolia, Upper Cretaceous, Barun Goyot Formation … (GRADZINSKI & JERZYKIEWICZ 1972, text-fig. 4, no 11)."
- gradziński1972a, p. 24, Fig. 4: "11 — a skeleton with skull of an ankylosaurid dinosaur". The "Northern Cliffs" label is printed on the map, not in the caption.

**Ruled out.**
- *Khulsan with no finer site.* Maryańska's own type line gives the number. The "Khulsan (5)" in her table of Mongolian ankylosaur occurrences (maryańska1977a, p. 88) is the locality's number on a map of the whole region, not a site within Khulsan.

**Open.** Nothing.
