# NHMUK catalogue prefixes

## How should Natural History Museum, London specimen numbers be written?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** The palaeontological vertebrate collection is NHMUK PV, and BMNH is the same institution's older code; NHMUK VP is a misprint for PV. Within PV, the R, RU and OR prefixes are part of the number. Governs `type_specimen.institution: NHMUK` and the prefix in `specimen_id` on records such as [*Pantydraco*](../../genera/P/Pantydraco.yml), [*Abrictosaurus*](../../genera/A/Abrictosaurus.yml) and [*Archaeopteryx*](../../genera/A/Archaeopteryx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sabaj2020a, p. 656: "Current as: NHMUK … NHMUK PV (paleovertebrate collections). Also as: BMNH."
- butler2008b, p. 8: "(BMNH RUB54, holotype; formerly UCL B54)"; sereno2012a, p. 17: "now catalogued as NHMUK RU B54". RU is prefixed to the original University College London number, so the Pant-y-ffynnon specimens are RU P24, RU P77/1 and so on (barrett2005b, p. 808: "BMNH RU P24"; chapelle2018a, Table 1: "NHMUK RU P24").
- hendrickx2022a, p. 963: "London specimen of Archaeopteryx (NHMUK OR 37001)"; about 20 papers read for this note give "BMNH 37001".

**Ruled out.**
- *NHMUK VP.* Isasmendi and colleagues (2024) write "NHMUK VP R9951" 40 times and "NHMUK PV 31806" four times in one paper (isasmendi2024a, pp. 3, 21–22); Mateus and Estraviz-López (2022) and Ibrahim and colleagues (2020) also use VP. The registry and the museum use PV.
- *RUP as one token.* "RU B54" and "RU P24" show RU followed by the UCL series letter.

**Open.** What RU and OR stand for; no source read for this note expands them.
