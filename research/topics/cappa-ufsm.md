# CAPPA/UFSM and UFSM

## Are CAPPA/UFSM and UFSM the same collection?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** No. CAPPA/UFSM is the Centro de Apoio à Pesquisa Paleontológica da Quarta Colônia, São João do Polêsine, with four-digit numbers (some with a/b/c); bare UFSM is the university's collection in Santa Maria, with five-digit numbers. Governs `type_specimen.institution` and the prefix on [*Macrocollum*](../../genera/M/Macrocollum.yml), [*Erythrovenator*](../../genera/E/Erythrovenator.yml), [*Gnathovorax*](../../genera/G/Gnathovorax.yml) and [*Unaysaurus*](../../genera/U/Unaysaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- müller2018a, p. 2: "Holotype CAPPA/UFSM (Centro de Apoio à Pesquisa Paleontológica da Quarta Colônia) 0001a".
- pacheco2019a, p. 4: "Holotype: CAPPA/UFSM 0009" (*Gnathovorax*).
- leal2004a, p. 3: "Holotype. UFSM11069 housed at the Universidade Federal de Santa Maria" (*Unaysaurus*).

**Ruled out.**
- *UFSM 0009 for Gnathovorax.* The describing paper prints the CAPPA/UFSM prefix.

**Open.** Sabaj (2020) has no CAPPA entry.
