# Pant-y-ffynnon fissure fills

## Do the Pant-y-ffynnon types come from a named formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** No. Both types come from a karst fissure fill at Pant-y-ffynnon Quarry, an unnamed deposit cut into Carboniferous limestone, and the records carry the quarry as the locality and no `location.formation`. Governs the absence of `location.formation` on [*Pendraig milnerae*](../../genera/P/Pendraig.yml) and [*Pantydraco caducus*](../../genera/P/Pantydraco.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton2007b, p. 123: "Type horizon and locality: A Rhaetian (Upper Triassic) or Lower Jurassic fissure fill at the old Pant-y-ffynnon Quarry (UK National Grid Reference ST 047741) near Bonvilston".
- spiekman2021a, p. 4, Locality and horizon: "Fissure fills of Pant-y-ffynnon Quarry, southern Wales".
- spiekman2021a, p. 2, Introduction: the fissure fill deposits of southwestern England and southern Wales "were formed as Mississippian Carboniferous Limestone that were exposed at the land surface and which underwent karstic weathering processes and faulting".

**Ruled out.**
- *The host rock's unit.* Whiteside and colleagues (2016) place the Pant-y-ffynnon fissures among those "developed in the limestones, particularly of the Black Rock Limestone Subgroup and/or Gully Oolite Formation". Those are Carboniferous units into which the Triassic fill was later washed, not the beds the bones were deposited in.
- *A description of the deposit in `formation`.* "Fissure fills of Pant-y-ffynnon Quarry, southern Wales" names a kind of deposit at a site. It is not a lithostratigraphic unit, and the quarry is already the locality.
- *A unit from the reference works.* Jones (2026) prints "Wales, United Kingdom" for *Pendraig* (jones2026c, Coelophysoidea systematics table) and "Uncertain, United Kingdom" for *Pantydraco* (jones2026b, Sauropodomorpha systematics table), where the column otherwise gives a formation. Neither names a unit.

**Open.** Whether the two types came from the same fill. Neither describing paper says which fissure at the quarry yielded its specimen.
