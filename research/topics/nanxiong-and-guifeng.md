# Nanxiong Formation and the Ganzhou Basin

## Is "Nanxiong Formation" the right unit for Ganzhou Basin dinosaurs?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The Nanxiong Group (or Formation) belongs to the Nanxiong Basin, in Guangdong and westernmost Jiangxi. The Ganzhou Basin's red beds were once called by the Nanxiong name and have since been given Jiangxi names: the Ganzhou Group below and the Guifeng Group (Hekou, Tangbian and Lianhe formations) above. "Nanxiong Formation" for Ganzhou material is a borrowed name, and a province code cannot separate the basins. Governs `location.formation` on Ganzhou taxa such as [*Qianzhousaurus sinensis*](../../genera/Q/Qianzhousaurus.yml), [*Ganzhousaurus nankangensis*](../../genera/G/Ganzhousaurus.yml), [*Tongtianlong limosus*](../../genera/T/Tongtianlong.yml) and [*Jiangxisaurus ganzhouensis*](../../genera/J/Jiangxisaurus.yml), and the scope of [Nanxiong](../../stratigraphy/n/nanxiong.yml) and [Guifeng](../../stratigraphy/g/guifeng.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhao1991a, English summary: the Nanxiong Basin "embodies Nanxiong and Shixing counties and the western part of Xinfeng county of Jiangxi Province"; xing2016a, p. 303: the basin "extends into Jiangxi Province".
- xing2024a, pp. 114–115: "The Cretaceous red beds of Jiangxi Province used to be considered contiguous with the Nanxiong Group … This traditional scheme has been since revised"; "the Guifeng Group consists of the Hekou, Tangbian, and Lianhe formations".
- yao2026a, pp. 2–3: "the upper Guifeng Group consisting of the Hekou, Tangbian and Lianhe formations", above the Ganzhou Group.
- jin2022a, p. 2: "Lianhe Formation of the Guifeng Group (= Nanxiong Formation), respectively (Liu 1997)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Rank as a dated change.* Both "Nanxiong Formation" and "Nanxiong Group" are in use, including in 2026 papers (yao2026a uses Formation for comparison; others use Group); basin papers tend to use Group and taxon descriptions Formation. It is a rank dispute, not a revision with a date.
- *"Yuanpu" for Ganzhou taxa.* Paul (2024) gives "Northern China; Yuanpu" for *Qianzhousaurus* (paul2024a, p. 123), a Ganzhou taxon assigned to a Nanxiong Basin unit and to the wrong region. Weishampel and colleagues' "Yuanpu Formation (= Nanxiong Formation …)" (weishampel2004b, p. 599) is the older broad sense; Zhang and colleagues (2013) later "divided the original Yuanpu Formation into the Jiangtou, Yuanpu, Dafeng, and Zhutian formations" (as reported in ke2023a, p. 4).
- *A Paleocene Nanxiong Group.* Zhao (1991)'s printed chart (zhao1991a, p. 4, Fig. 2) puts the Nanxiong Group in the Upper Cretaceous and the Luofuzhai Group in the Paleocene.

**Open.** Which Jiangxi formation each Ganzhou taxon belongs to. Jin and colleagues (2022, pp. 3, 7) place the railway-station and Ganxian sites in the Hekou Formation of the Guifeng Group only "if the result of the geological survey is correct"; Yao and colleagues (2026, pp. 2–3) place an oviraptorid (*Jiangxisaurus*, after Wei and colleagues 2013) and a sauropod in the Zhoutian Formation of the Ganzhou Group, which is Cenomanian–Turonian in the registry. The Guifeng names are typified in northeastern Jiangxi (zhang2009b), so the Ganzhou usage itself borrows from another basin.
