# Pigeon Hill

## Which formation holds the Pigeon Hill fossil beds?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** The Longjiang Formation, which both describing papers give. Wang and colleagues (2023) report that the unit is still contested: the same Moqi fossil beds have also been placed in the Guanghua Formation and called the informal "Moqi fossil bed". They side with the Longjiang, and treat the Guanghua as the volcanic sequence overlying it. Both holotypes come from the one site and carry the one printed position. Governs `location.formation: Longjiang`, `location.locality: Pigeon Hill` and `location.coordinates: [48.661322, 123.878097]` on [*Daurlong wangi*](../../genera/D/Daurlong.yml) and [*Migmanychion laiyang*](../../genera/M/Migmanychion.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wang2022a, p. 1, Locality and Horizon: "Pigeon Hill, Morin Dawa Daur Autonomous Banner, Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region (N 48°39′40.76″/E 123°52′ 41.15″); Longjiang Formation, Lower Cretaceous."
- wang2023a, p. 8, Locality and horizon: the same locality and position, "Longjiang Formation (Wang et al., 2019), Lower Cretaceous".
- wang2023a, p. 2: "the stratigraphic unit is still controversial. The fossiliferous layers in Moqi have been referred in the Guanghua Formation (Heilongjiang Bureau of Geology and Mineral Resources, 1993; Jia and Gao, 2016; Zhang et al., 2016, 2017; Gao and Chen, 2017), Longjiang Formation (Li et al., 2013; Ding et al., 2013, 2014; Tan et al., 2018), and 'Moqi fossil bed' (Yu et al., 2021; Dong et al., 2022). This paper agrees that the Guanghua Formation is the volcanic clastic rock section overlying the Longjiang Formation."
- wang2023a, p. 2: "The material was collected at the Pigeon Hill locality, near Baoshan town."

**Ruled out.**
- *Guanghua Formation.* The assignment of the Heilongjiang regional survey and later papers, as Wang and colleagues (2023) report them; the two describing papers place the beds below the Guanghua.
- *"Moqi fossil bed".* An informal name for the fossil beds, not a lithostratigraphic unit.

**Open.** The formation, as a live argument. None of the papers on the Guanghua side has been read, nor Wang and colleagues (2019), whom the 2023 paper cites for the Longjiang.
