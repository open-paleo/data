# Iharkút

## Do the Iharkút type localities take the open pit's printed position? (revisited)

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** No. The pair is printed for the mine as a whole, an open pit with several bone-bearing sites, and no describing paper says which site yielded its holotype; a position for an area larger than the site is not the specimen's. This is the ruling of the 2026-09-02 [*Pneumatoraptor*](../genera/P/Pneumatoraptor.md) entry, which the earlier entry today re-proposed against without new evidence. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Mochlodon vorosi*](../../genera/M/Mochlodon.yml), [*Ajkaceratops kozmai*](../../genera/A/Ajkaceratops.yml), [*Hungarosaurus tormai*](../../genera/H/Hungarosaurus.yml) and [*Pneumatoraptor fodori*](../../genera/P/Pneumatoraptor.yml). Supersedes the entry below.

**Evidence.**
- csiki-sava2015a: the pair belongs to "an abandoned and recultivated bauxite open-pit mine"; "The first fossils were found in the Sz-1 site, at the northen part of the open-pit, and additional bone-yielding horizons were later discovered in the Csehbánya Formation, among which the Sz-6 site at the southeastern end of the quarry is the most extensive (ca. 5000 m2) and productive."
- ősi2005a, Taphonomy: the *Hungarosaurus* site is one part of the mine, "approximately 55 meters square as calculated from its bone map".

**Ruled out.**
- *47°13′52″ N, 17°39′01″ E for any Iharkút holotype.* Printed for the mine, not for a specimen's site.

**Open.** Which site within the pit yielded each holotype, and whether a position is printed for it.

## Do the Iharkút type localities take the open pit's printed position?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Each describing paper gives the type locality as Iharkút, the abandoned bauxite open pit, without naming a bone site within it, and Csiki-Sava and colleagues (2015) print a position for that locality. The pair is printed for the holotype's site at the precision the type locality is given, not for a landmark or a wider area. Governs `location.coordinates: [47.231111, 17.650278]` on [*Mochlodon vorosi*](../../genera/M/Mochlodon.yml), [*Ajkaceratops kozmai*](../../genera/A/Ajkaceratops.yml) and [*Hungarosaurus tormai*](../../genera/H/Hungarosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- csiki-sava2015a: "The locality is an abandoned and recultivated bauxite open-pit mine close to the villages of Németbánya and Bakonyjákó, in the heart of the Bakony Mountains, western Hungary (47°13'52"N, 17°39'01"E)"; "The first fossils were found in the Sz-1 site, at the northen part of the open-pit, and additional bone-yielding horizons were later discovered in the Csehbánya Formation, among which the Sz-6 site".
- ősi2012a, *Mochlodon vorosi*: "Type locality. Iharkút, Veszprém County, Bakony Mountains, Transdanubian Range, western Hungary."
- ősi2005a, *Hungarosaurus tormai*: "Type Locality—Iharkút, Veszprém County, Bakony Mountains, Transdanubian Range, western Hungary."
- ősi2010a, *Ajkaceratops kozmai*: "Horizon and locality. Csehbánya Formation (Upper Cretaceous, Santonian), Iharkút, Veszprém County, Bakony Mountains".

**Ruled out.**
- *No coordinate, reading the pit position as an area.* The pit is the type locality the describing papers name; the rule's exclusion of a basin centroid concerns a position for a larger area than the site.

**Open.** Which bone site within the pit yielded each holotype. A paper naming the site (Sz-6 or another) would allow a finer position, if one is printed.
