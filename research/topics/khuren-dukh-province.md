# Khuren Dukh province

## Which province is the Khuren Dukh locality in?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** Dundgovi (`MN-059`). Kobayashi and Barsbold (2005) give "Dundgov" for the *Harpymimus* type locality, and Chinzorig and colleagues (2025), who worked at Khuren Dukh in 2022 and 2023, thank the governor offices of Dundgovi Province for permission. Among the sources read, only Norman (1998) gives Dornogovi. Khuren Dukh is one locality, so the four records take one code. Governs `location.region: MN-059` on [*Harpymimus okladnikovi*](../../genera/H/Harpymimus.yml), [*Altirhinus kurzanovi*](../../genera/A/Altirhinus.yml), [*Choyrodon barsboldi*](../../genera/C/Choyrodon.yml) and [*Zavacephale rinpoche*](../../genera/Z/Zavacephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kobayashi2005b, p. 98: "Type Locality and Horizon. Dundgov (Eastern Gobi Province)"; the introduction on the same page places the find in "Dundgov (southeastern Mongolia)".
- chinzorig2025a, Acknowledgements: "local governor offices of the Dundgovi Province" are thanked "for fieldwork assistance and permissions"; Locality and horizon: "Khuren Dukh is situated in the Eastern Gobi Basin of Mongolia", with "in-field surveys of this area in 2022 and 2023".
- hicks1999a, p. 835: the locality "lies 60 km south-southwest of the town of Choyr (or Choir)"; p. 838, Table 1, GPS readings "45°49′50.34″N, 108°26′41.48″E" (Khuren Dukh Section) and "45°50′21.43″N, 108°26′48.29″E" (Khuren Dukh Hawaii).
- nichols2006a, p. 243: Khuren Dukh at 45°50.6′ N, 108°26.8′ E, "about 60 km south-southeast of the town of Choyr".

**Ruled out.**
- *Dornogovi (MN-063).* Norman (1998) gives "Khuren Dukh (Хурэн Дух), northwestern Dornogov' (East Gobi Province), Mongolia (approximately 30 km South of the town of Choir [Choyr])" (norman1998a, p. 296), with no coordinate. Hicks and colleagues and Nichols and colleagues both measure about 60 km from Choyr, not 30.
- *Kobayashi and Barsbold's gloss as evidence for Dornogovi.* Their "Dundgov" names Dundgovi, while "Eastern Gobi Province" is the English for Dornogovi, so the line names two provinces (our reading of the names). Chinzorig and colleagues call the setting the Eastern Gobi Basin, a basin name, which may be where the gloss came from; that too is our reading.

**Open.** No source read places the GPS readings relative to the Dundgovi–Dornogovi boundary, so the conclusion rests on Kobayashi and Barsbold's "Dundgov" and on Chinzorig and colleagues' permits, not on the coordinates. Hicks and colleagues put Khuren Dukh south-southwest of Choyr and Nichols and colleagues south-southeast, for nearly the same point.
