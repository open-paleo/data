# Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry

## Do the different minute values in the describing papers mark different sites in the Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry?

*2026-08-23*

**Conclusion.** No. Every paper names the same quarry, the Kitadani (Dinosaur) Quarry in Katsuyama, and the records take the one position printed to the second, 36°7′17.9″ N, 136°32′41.4″ E (Azuma and colleagues, 2016; Hattori and colleagues, 2021). The describing papers give that position to the minute. Azuma and Currie (2000) and Kobayashi and Azuma (2003) print 136°33′ E, and Azuma and Shibata (2010) print 136°32′ E. 32′41.4″ is 32.69′, so 33′ is the rounded figure and 32′ the truncated one; the latitude, 7′17.9″, is 7′ either way. That the two forms are rounding and truncation is our arithmetic; that all of them name one quarry is in the papers. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Fukuiraptor kitadaniensis*](../../genera/F/Fukuiraptor.yml), [*Fukuisaurus tetoriensis*](../../genera/F/Fukuisaurus.yml), [*Fukuititan nipponensis*](../../genera/F/Fukuititan.yml), [*Fukuivenator paradoxus*](../../genera/F/Fukuivenator.yml) and [*Tyrannomimus fukuiensis*](../../genera/T/Tyrannomimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- azuma2000a, p. 1737: "The Kitadani locality is on the Sugiyama River in the northern part of the city of Katsuyama, Fukui Prefecture (Latitude 36°7′N, Longitude 136°33′E)".
- kobayashi2003a, p. 168: "Kitadani Quarry (Late Hauterivian to Barremian), Katsuyama City, Fukui Prefecture, Japan (36° 7' N, 136° 33' E)".
- azuma2010a, p. 455, Type locality and horizon: "The Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry, Katsuyama City, Fukui Prefecture, Japan (N 36°7′, E 136°32′)".
- azuma2016a, p. 2, Type locality and horizon: the quarry "is on the Sugiyama River in the northern part of the city of Katsuyama, Fukui, Japan (36° 7′ 17.9″ N, 136° 32′ 41.4″ E)".
- hattori2021a, p. 1: "The Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry is located in the bank of the Sugiyama River in the northern part of Katsuyama, Fukui (36 ° 7' 17.9" N, 136 ° 32' 41.4" E)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Each record at its own describing paper's minute value.* That would put *Fukuititan* at 136°32′ and *Fukuiraptor* and *Fukuisaurus* at 136°33′, about 1.5 km apart, for one quarry. The arcsecond pair locates the quarry the minute values approximate.
- *The quarry's coordinate as a bonebed's position.* The pair is printed for the quarry as a whole. The holotypes come from different levels, Bonebed I for *Fukuiraptor* and *Fukuisaurus* and Bonebed II for *Fukuititan* (shibata2015b, p. 421), and higher in the section for *Fukuivenator* (see [*Fukuivenator*](../genera/F/Fukuivenator.md)). No paper gives a position for any bonebed.

**Open.** Nothing.
