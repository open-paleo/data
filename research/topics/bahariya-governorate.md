# Bahariya governorate

## Which governorate do the Bahariya Formation holotypes take?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** Giza (EG-GZ). The four Egyptian Bahariya Formation holotypes in the data come from sites in the Bahariya Oasis, and the oasis lies in Giza Governorate on the current subdivision. No source read for this note names the governorate, so the code is derived from the localities, as for any region a paper does not print. Governs `location.region: EG-GZ` on [*Aegyptosaurus baharijensis*](../../genera/A/Aegyptosaurus.yml), [*Bahariasaurus ingens*](../../genera/B/Bahariasaurus.yml), [*Paralititan stromeri*](../../genera/P/Paralititan.yml) and [*Tameryraptor markgrafi*](../../genera/T/Tameryraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- stromer1934a, p. 24: the *Bahariasaurus* holotype, 1922 X 47, was excavated "an der Südostseite des Gebel Ghoräbi, also ganz im Norden des Baharije-Kessels" ("on the southeast side of Gebel Ghoräbi, thus quite in the north of the Bahariya basin", translated).
- weishampel2004b, p. 605: the Baharija Formation is listed under the heading "69.2. MARSA MATRUH, EGYPT", with no locality.
- holtz2004a, p. 77, Table 4.1: *B. ingens* from the "Baharija Formation (Marsa Matruh), Egypt".

**Ruled out.**
- *Matruh (EG-MT).* *The Dinosauria* files the whole formation under Marsa Matruh, in the distribution chapter and in the taxon tables, without naming a site, and other compilations repeat the label. Marsa Matruh is a Mediterranean port and the seat of Matruh Governorate, and the oasis lies outside that governorate; that placement is our reading of the current subdivision, which no source read here states. That the label applies to the formation as a whole rather than to any one site is our reading of the chapter's layout, which lists no locality under the heading.

**Open.** Nothing.
