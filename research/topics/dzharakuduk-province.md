# Dzharakuduk province

## Is Dzharakuduk in Navoiy or in Bukhara Province?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** Navoiy. Sues and Averianov (2016) place the Dzharakuduk escarpment, where the Bissekty Formation crops out, "32 km SW of Mynbulak in the Navoi district". The *Dinosauria* tables give "Bissekty Formation (Bukhoro), Uzbekistan" for every Bissekty taxon, a compilation's label for the formation rather than a statement about any one site. Governs `location.region: UZ-NW` on the Bissekty records whose localities are Dzharakuduk or one of its numbered sites: [*Asiamericana asiatica*](../../genera/A/Asiamericana.yml), [*Bissektipelta archibaldi*](../../genera/B/Bissektipelta.yml), [*Caenagnathasia martinsoni*](../../genera/C/Caenagnathasia.yml), [*Dzharacursor bissektensis*](../../genera/D/Dzharacursor.yml), [*Dzharaonyx eski*](../../genera/D/Dzharaonyx.yml), [*Dzharatitanis kingi*](../../genera/D/Dzharatitanis.yml), [*Itemirus medullaris*](../../genera/I/Itemirus.yml), [*Kuszholia mengi*](../../genera/K/Kuszholia.yml), [*Levnesovia transoxiana*](../../genera/L/Levnesovia.yml), [*Timurlengia euotica*](../../genera/T/Timurlengia.yml), [*Turanoceratops tardabilis*](../../genera/T/Turanoceratops.yml) and [*Ulughbegsaurus uzbekistanensis*](../../genera/U/Ulughbegsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sues2016a, p. 156: the Bissekty Formation crops out "along an approximately 8 km long escarpment near the small settlement of Dzharakuduk ... in the central Kyzylkum Desert, 32 km SW of Mynbulak in the Navoi district, Uzbekistan".
- weishampel2004a, p. 212, Table 11.1: *Kuszholia mengi*, "Bissekty Formation (Bukhoro), Uzbekistan"; the same label recurs for the other Bissekty taxa in the volume's tables.

**Ruled out.**
- *Bukhara (Bukhoro).* The tables attach the province to the formation, in the same form for every taxon, and give no site; Sues and Averianov name the district and a distance from a town in it.

**Open.** Sues and Averianov cite Nessov (1995, 1997) for the sentence, but neither work, as translated, names a province: Nessov (1995) gives Dzharakuduk II only as "Central Kyzylkum, Uzbekistan" (nessov1995a, p. 95, translated). So "Navoi district" is Sues and Averianov's own statement, with the region code following from the named town.
