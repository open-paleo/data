# HGM 41HIII-0002

## Which taxon's holotype is HGM 41HIII-0002?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Both describing papers print the same number: Zhongyuansaurus (2007) and Ruyangosaurus (2009). The earlier use belongs to [*Zhongyuansaurus luoyangensis*](../../genera/Z/Zhongyuansaurus.yml). For [*Ruyangosaurus giganteus*](../../genera/R/Ruyangosaurus.yml) the printed number is a duplicate, and the record's 41HIII-0003 has no published source. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on both.

**Evidence.**
- xu2007a, p. 434: "标本保存于河南省地质博物馆(41HIII-0002)" ("the specimen is housed in the Henan Geological Museum (41HIII-0002)", translated).
- lü2009a, p. 2: "The specimen (41HIII-0002) is housed in the Henan Geological Museum."

**Ruled out.**
- *41HIII-0003 from the literature.* No source read for this note prints 41HIII-0003 for *Ruyangosaurus*; the record attributes it to the number inscribed on the specimen, without a source.

**Open.** The *Ruyangosaurus* number. A photograph or museum statement of the inscription would settle it; the published 41HIII series also lacks 0004, 0005, 0007 and 0009, so a missing 0003 is not distinctive. Printed variants include "41HIII-002" (arbour2015a) and "41 HIII-0002" (jones2026a).
