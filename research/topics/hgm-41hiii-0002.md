# HGM 41HIII-0002

## What is the *Ruyangosaurus* holotype's number?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** HGM 41HIII-0003, the number inscribed on the material, shown in a photograph Nair (2026) posted. This settles the Open line of the 2026-10-04 entry: the inscription is the museum's own number on the specimen, a source that renumbers it rather than one more paper repeating a number, so the record does not fall back on "sensu" qualifiers. [*Zhongyuansaurus luoyangensis*](../../genera/Z/Zhongyuansaurus.yml) keeps 41HIII-0002. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Ruyangosaurus giganteus*](../../genera/R/Ruyangosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nair2026a: "I believe the correct ID for Ruyangosaurus probably is HGM 41HIII-0003, not "0002" (see attached image)"; "The repository inscription appears to be marked on the material after publication of Lü et al. 2009, because it's not in their image of Fig. 3b"; and museums "usually allocate repository IDs chronologically, and Ruyangosaurus was published after Zhongyuansaurus".

**Ruled out.**
- *HGM 41HIII-0002 sensu Lü 2009 and sensu Xu 2007.* That form is for a number the literature applies to two specimens with nothing to choose between them; here the specimen itself carries a different number.

**Open.** The source of the photograph, which the post attaches as a screenshot without saying where it came from, and a statement from the Henan Geological Museum. Nair hedges the number with "probably".

## Which taxon's holotype is HGM 41HIII-0002?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Both describing papers print the same number: Zhongyuansaurus (2007) and Ruyangosaurus (2009). The earlier use belongs to [*Zhongyuansaurus luoyangensis*](../../genera/Z/Zhongyuansaurus.yml). For [*Ruyangosaurus giganteus*](../../genera/R/Ruyangosaurus.yml) the printed number is a duplicate, and the record's 41HIII-0003 has no published source. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on both.

**Evidence.**
- xu2007a, p. 434: "标本保存于河南省地质博物馆(41HIII-0002)" ("the specimen is housed in the Henan Geological Museum (41HIII-0002)", translated).
- lü2009a, p. 2: "The specimen (41HIII-0002) is housed in the Henan Geological Museum."

**Ruled out.**
- *41HIII-0003 from the literature.* No source read for this note prints 41HIII-0003 for *Ruyangosaurus*; the record attributes it to the number inscribed on the specimen, without a source.

**Open.** The *Ruyangosaurus* number. A photograph or museum statement of the inscription would settle it; the published 41HIII series also lacks 0004, 0005, 0007 and 0009, so a missing 0003 is not distinctive. Printed variants include "41HIII-002" (arbour2015a) and "41 HIII-0002" (jones2026a).
