# MCCM and the Las Hoyas collection

## What does the MCCM-LH prefix mean, and how should Cuenca specimens be written?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** MCCM is the Museo de las Ciencias de Castilla-La Mancha, Cuenca (now MUPA), and LH its Las Hoyas collection. The Lo Hueco specimens in the same museum are written HUE-EC-nn, with no MCCM prefix. Governs `type_specimen.institution: MCCM` and the institution's name and city on [*Pelecanimimus*](../../genera/P/Pelecanimimus.yml), [*Concavenator*](../../genera/C/Concavenator.yml), [*Lohuecotitan*](../../genera/L/Lohuecotitan.yml) and [*Qunkasaura*](../../genera/Q/Qunkasaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sabaj2020a, p. 650: "MCCM: Museo de las Ciencias de Castilla-La Mancha, Cuenca. Includes: Las Hoyas collection. Current as: MCCM (fossils), HUE (fossils, Lo Hueco collection)".
- ortega2010a, p. 203: "Holotype. Specimen MCCM-LH 6666 at Museo de las Ciencias de Castilla-La Mancha, Cuenca".
- pérez-moreno1994a, p. 364: "Holotype. LH 7777 (Museo de Cuenca, Cuenca, Spain; provisionally housed in the Unidad de Paleontología of the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid"; the MCCM-LH form for *Pelecanimimus* is later (serrano-brañas2025a).
- díezdíaz2016a, accepted manuscript, p. 7: "HUE: Lo Hueco collection, housed at the Museo de Paleontología de Castilla-La Mancha", holotype "HUE-EC-01".
- chokchaloemwong2019a, p. 5: "MCCM, Museo de Ciencias de Castilla-La Mancha, Cuenca, Spain, now MUPA".

**Ruled out.**
- *Morella.* The name "Coleccions de la Conselleria de Cultura de Morella" for MCCM matches neither Sabaj nor any describing paper.
- *Bare LH as Las Hoyas without context.* Serrano-Brañas and colleagues list both "LH, Long Hao Institute of Geology and Paleontology, Hohhot, China" and "MCCM-LH, Las Hoyas Collection, Museo de Cuenca" (serrano-brañas2025a, p. 2).

**Open.** Whether the museum's current name (MUPA) replaces MCCM in the codes; papers after the renaming still write MCCM-LH.
