# Toundoute

## Which formation are the Toundoute (Tazouda) dinosaurs from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** None has been established. The describing papers use informal units, and the two published correlations disagree: a lateral equivalence with the Toarcian Azilal and Wazzant formations, and a mapping of the beds as the Upper Jurassic–Lower Cretaceous Iouridane Formation. Governs the empty `location.formation` on [*Tazoudasaurus naimi*](../../genera/T/Tazoudasaurus.yml) and [*Berberosaurus liassicus*](../../genera/B/Berberosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- montenat2005a, p. 262: the succession is "divisé en huit termes lithostratigraphiques informels, désignés de A à H" ("divided into eight informal lithostratigraphic units, labelled A to H", translated); term G is the "gisement supérieur … C'est là qu'ont été trouvés les ossements du sauropode Tazoudasaurus" ("upper bone bed … this is where the bones of the sauropod Tazoudasaurus were found", translated; p. 263); term F is the lower bone bed. Montenat and colleagues give the age as "Lias moyen-supérieur probable" ("probably Middle–Upper Lias", translated; p. 263).
- allain2008a, p. 351: the *Tazoudasaurus* holotype elements are from the upper bone bed (To1), with referred material from the lower (To2); allain2007a, p. 611: *Berberosaurus* is from the "Upper bone-bed ('Fossil locality B' of Allain et al., 2004)".
- elbchari2001a, p. 168: the Wazzant Formation "attribué à l'intervalle Domérien terminal - Aalénien (JOSSEN,1988)" ("assigned to the terminal Domerian–Aalenian interval", translated), Jossen's age as reported by El Bchari and colleagues, not their own determination. It bears on the age of the proposed Wazzant equivalence.

**Ruled out.**
- *Azilal or Wazzant Formation as a published assignment by Montenat and colleagues.* Allain and Aquesbi (2008) write that the series "is a lateral equivalent of the Toarcian Azilal and Wazzant formations (Montenat et al. 2005)" (allain2008a, p. 349), but Montenat and colleagues name neither formation. Later papers repeat the correlation: "from the Azilal/Wazzant Formation" (holwerda2018a, p. 292); "a bone bed in the Lower Jurassic Azilal Formation" (rincón2022a, p. 14).

**Open.** Which formation, if any. Benvenuti and colleagues (2024) map the beds as a sub-unit "tentatively equated to the Upper Jurassic-Lower Cretaceous Iuoridane Formation" (benvenuti2024a, p. 5), on lithology and on the structural argument that pre-Toarcian units are "thrusted onto the GI unit" (pp. 8–9), placing the sauropod bed at basal GLc; they also attribute the Wazzant equivalence to Montenat and colleagues. The Central High Atlas has different unit schemes by area: Milhi and colleagues' abstract places the Azilal in the "Aalénien terminal" (milhi2002a, p. 163), against the Toarcian–early Aalenian Allain and Aquesbi relay from Jenny (1980, 1985); and the Zaouiat Ahançal Group "comprises the Amezraï, Tafraout, and Aguerd-n'Tazoult formations" (martín-martín2016a, accepted manuscript, p. 14).
