# Morrison Tithonian labels

## Are Morrison taxa labelled "Tithonian" actually Tithonian on the current chart?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** Often not. Many "lower" or "early Tithonian" labels come from charts that placed the base of the Tithonian at 150.8 Ma (GTS 2004) or about 152 Ma (GTS 2012); the current chart places it at 149.2 Ma. Where a number underlies the label, re-place the number. Governs `stage` on [*Fruitadens haagarorum*](../../genera/F/Fruitadens.yml) (Kimmeridgian), [*Amphicoelias altus*](../../genera/A/Amphicoelias.yml) (Kimmeridgian–Tithonian), and the Carnegie Quarry taxa [*Athenar bermani*](../../genera/A/Athenar.yml) and [*Uteodon aphanoecetes*](../../genera/U/Uteodon.yml), currently Tithonian; and the [Morrison](../../stratigraphy/m/morrison.yml) range.

**Evidence.**
- mannion2021a, p. 5: "C5 systems tract, 150.44–149.21 Ma, lower Tithonian", relayed; on the current chart this is Kimmeridgian, ending at the boundary. barrett2025a, p. 31: the same interval "can be dated to 150.8–149.2 Ma (uppermost Kimmeridgian)".
- sombathy2025a, p. 775: the Fruita Paleontological Area "ca. 152–150 Ma; Maidment & Muxworthy, 2019", Kimmeridgian on the current chart.
- whitlock2025a, pp. 2, 9: the Carnegie Quarry is "(ca. 150-151 Ma; Maidment et al., 2017 …)", "dated to the Tithonian ca. 151-150 Ma", relayed; on the current chart this is Kimmeridgian.
- kirkland2016a, p. 118: near the top of the formation "150.00 ± 0.52 Ma from near the top of the Brushy Basin Member" (Kimmeridgian on the current chart) and "148.96 +2.54 to -2.21 Ma … about 9 m below the base of the Naturita Formation" (Tithonian by about 0.24 Myr at its central value).

**Ruled out.**
- *A Tithonian stage taken from a label alone.* Butler and colleagues give *Fruitadens* "Late Jurassic: early Tithonian" (butler2009a, p. 376) with no number; Xu and colleagues give *Fujianvenator* "(Tithonian stage; 149.9–150.2 Ma …)" (xu2023a, p. 337), dates that are Kimmeridgian at their central values.
- *A Berriasian Morrison.* Cifelli and colleagues' "may also include the late Oxfordian and early Berriasian" (cifelli2014a, accepted manuscript, p. 3) is a hedge; the youngest dates stop about 6 Myr short of the Berriasian.

**Open.** The Carnegie Quarry. A second date, "148.97+ 0.12 Ma by Kowallis et al. (1998)" (carpenter2008a, p. 230), would be Tithonian, but its level relative to the quarry is not stated. On Whitlock and colleagues' date the quarry taxa would be Kimmeridgian, or Kimmeridgian–Tithonian with the older date as a dispute.
