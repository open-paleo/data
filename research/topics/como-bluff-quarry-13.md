# Como Bluff Quarry 13

## What stages do the Quarry 13 holotypes take?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** Oxfordian and Kimmeridgian. Carpenter and Wilson (2008) date Reed's Quarry 13 latest Oxfordian to earliest Kimmeridgian from regional correlations with dated horizons; it is a dating of the quarry, so it applies to each holotype from it. The Salt Wash Member, in which McDonald (2011) places the quarry, takes the Oxfordian with it. Governs `stage: [Oxfordian, Kimmeridgian]` on [*Camptosaurus dispar*](../../genera/C/Camptosaurus.yml) and [*Camarasaurus lentus*](../../genera/C/Camarasaurus.yml), and the Oxfordian of [Salt Wash](../../stratigraphy/s/salt-wash.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carpenter2008a, Fig. 2 caption: "Based on regional correlations from dated horizons, *C. dispar* from Quarry 13 is latest Oxfordian-earliest Kimmeridgian".
- carpenter2008a: "One of the oldest occurrences may be Reed's Quarry 13, which occurs about 35 m below the 156.3 + 2 Ma age for strata at the level of Reed's Quarry 9".
- mcintosh1975a: Quarry 13 yielded "YPM 1910 [type of "*Morosaurus*" *lentus*]".

**Ruled out.**
- *Kimmeridgian alone* for *C. dispar*, and *the Morrison's Kimmeridgian–Tithonian* for *C. lentus*. The quarry is dated.

**Open.** The correlation is regional; a date from the quarry itself would test it.
