# Alamo Wash and Willow Wash

## Which San Juan Basin horizons do "Alamo Wash" and "Willow Wash" name?

*2026-08-19*

**Conclusion.** The names cross between two units. The Willow Wash local fauna is the fauna of the De-na-zin Member of the Kirtland Formation, while the Alamo Wash local fauna is that of the Naashoibito Member of the Ojo Alamo Formation, which lies disconformably above it. The drainage called Alamo Wash, however, holds a De-na-zin locality: the type locality of *Dinevenator*. Neither name settles a horizon, and each record takes its member from its own describing paper. Governs `location.locality: SMP 410b` and `location.member: Naashoibito` on [*Dineobellator notohesperus*](../../genera/D/Dineobellator.yml), and `location.locality: Alamo Wash (north fork)` and `location.member: De-na-zin` on [*Dinevenator robustus*](../../genera/D/Dinevenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jasinski2020a, p. 2, Type locality and horizon: "The type locality, SMP 410b, Bisti/De-na-zin Wilderness, New Mexico. Precise locality information is on file at the State Museum of Pennsylvania"; "collected within a few meters above the base of the Naashoibito Member (Ojo Alamo Formation)".
- jasinski2020a, p. 8, References: ref. 15, "Taxonomic composition of the Alamo Wash local fauna from the Upper Cretaceous Ojo Alamo Formation (Naashoibito Member)", cited for the first mention of *Dineobellator*.
- sullivan2006b, p. 253, Horizon and Type Locality: "De-na–zin Member of the Kirtland Formation. The type locality is SMP loc. no. 388 (Alamo Wash [north fork])".
- dalman2025a, p. 105: "The younger, Willow Wash local fauna (WWlf), from the stratigraphically higher De-na-zin Member of the Kirtland Formation".
- jasinski2026a, p. 13: *Dinevenator* "further supports its unique vertebrate assemblage (Willow Wash local fauna)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Willow Wash as the* Dineobellator *locality.* It names the De-na-zin fauna, older than the Naashoibito Member that yielded SMP VP-2430, and Jasinski and colleagues (2020) give the site only as SMP 410b.
- *The Naashoibito for* Dinevenator *on the strength of "Alamo Wash".* Sullivan (2006) places SMP loc. 388 in the De-na-zin Member; the Alamo Wash local fauna is a different horizon from the drainage of the same name.
- *The pair formerly stored for* Dineobellator*, [36.343899, −108.066399].* Jasinski and colleagues withhold the position, and the same pair was stored for the *Ojoceratops fowleri* holotype, a different specimen.

**Open.** Nothing.
