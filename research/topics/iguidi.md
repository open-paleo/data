# Iguidi

## Do the Iguidi records move from the Echkar to the Farak Formation?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** Not yet. Sereno and colleagues (2026) revisit the type sections of both formations and assign the Iguidi (In Abangharit) beds to the Farak. That reassigns the beds between two existing units rather than renaming one, and it rests on a single paper, so the records keep the describing papers' Echkar, with the reassignment in their location notes. Governs `location.formation: Echkar` and the location notes on [*Carcharodontosaurus iguidensis*](../../genera/C/Carcharodontosaurus.yml) and [*Rugops primus*](../../genera/R/Rugops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno2026a: "We revisited the name-bearing sections for both the Echkar and Farak Formations and exposures of the overlying Cenomanian-Turonian limestone in terrain near Tanout (Fig. 1A). We conclude that both the Jenguebi and Iguidi (In Abangharit) fossil areas represent exposures of the Farak Formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *The Farak now.* It would adopt a single 2026 paper's reassignment before the literature has taken it up.

**Open.** Whether later work follows the reassignment. The [*Inosaurus*](../genera/I/Inosaurus.md) note treats the Echkar as the unit at In Abangharit and would follow any change.
