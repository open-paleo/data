# Lower Cañadón Asfalto taxa

## What stages do the lower Cañadón Asfalto dinosaurs take?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** *Patagosaurus* and *Volkheimeria* are Toarcian, bracketed by dates. *Piatnitzkysaurus* and *Condorraptor* have no bracket of their own; the dating paper gives their interval as Aalenian to probably Bajocian, not latest Toarcian excluded. Governs `stage` on [*Patagosaurus fariasi*](../../genera/P/Patagosaurus.yml) and [*Volkheimeria chubutensis*](../../genera/V/Volkheimeria.yml) (Toarcian), and on [*Piatnitzkysaurus floresi*](../../genera/P/Piatnitzkysaurus.yml) and [*Condorraptor currumili*](../../genera/C/Condorraptor.yml) (currently Toarcian–Aalenian), in the [Cañadón Asfalto Formation](../../stratigraphy/c/cañadón-asfalto.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pol2022a, p. 138: "The locality where Volkheimeria and Patagosaurus were found is stratigraphically bracketed between the horizons of these two dates" (179.17 and 178.07 Ma, relayed from Pol and colleagues 2020); p. 153: "into the middle Toarcian (ca 179–178 Ma)".
- cúneo2013a, p. 1273 (in-press version, p. 7): the interval holding the theropods is "essentially early Middle Jurassic in age (Aalenian to probably Bajocian)", with a latest Toarcian not precluded.

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Middle Jurassic for Patagosaurus and Volkheimeria.* Cúneo and colleagues' "An early Middle Jurassic age for eusauropods such as Patagosaurus and Volkheimeria" (p. 1273) is superseded by Pol and colleagues' bracket.
- *Callovian for Condorraptor.* Rauhut (2005) described it "from the Middle Jurassic (Callovian) Cañadón Asfalto Formation" (rauhut2005b, p. 87), the formation's traditional age.
- *Cúneo and colleagues' "Toarcian–Aalenian boundary interval" as the theropods' age.* The phrase, "which is now constrained essentially to the Toarcian–Aalenian boundary interval" (p. 1273), refers to the timing of a theropod diversification, not to these taxa's horizon.

**Open.** Whether the theropods' stages should be Toarcian–Aalenian or Aalenian–Bajocian: the records' current Toarcian–Aalenian rests on the misread sentence above, while the paper's own interval age includes the Bajocian.
