# Gamma and Cape Lamb

## Do the Gamma and Cape Lamb members belong to the Snow Hill Island Formation, or to the Santa Marta and López de Bertodano?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** Both are members of the Snow Hill Island Formation. The other assignments predate a revision that took the outcrops at Cape Lamb, the Naze and Santa Marta Cove out of the López de Bertodano and the Santa Marta and placed them in the Snow Hill Island Formation. Rozadilla and colleagues (2016) credit that revision to Pirrie and colleagues (1997), which has not been read here. Governs `parent: Snow Hill Island` on [Gamma](../../stratigraphy/g/gamma.yml) and [Cape Lamb](../../stratigraphy/c/cape-lamb.yml), and `location.formation: Snow Hill Island` on [*Antarctopelta oliveroi*](../../genera/A/Antarctopelta.yml), [*Trinisaura santamartaensis*](../../genera/T/Trinisaura.yml), [*Imperobator antarcticus*](../../genera/I/Imperobator.yml) and [*Morrosaurus antarcticus*](../../genera/M/Morrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rozadilla2016a, p. 312: "the Cretaceous outcrops of Cape Lamb, False Island Point, Humps Island, El Morro (The Naze) península, and the vicinity of St. Martha Cove were reassigned by Pirie et al. (1997) to the Snow Hill Island Formation"; earlier workers had followed an assignment of the Naze outcrops "to the Lopez de Bertodano Formation by Del Valle et al. (1982)".
- soto-acuña2024a, p. 80: the Snow Hill Island units are "equivalent to the Gamma Formation (late Campanian) previously referred to Santa Marta Formation"; p. 82, for *Antarctopelta*: "Gamma Member of Snow Hill Island Formation (upper Campanian)".
- ely2019a, p. 14: "The Herbert Sound or Gamma Member, underlies the Cape Lamb Member … within the Snow Hill Island Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Gamma in the Santa Marta Formation.* Salgado and Gasparini give "Lower part of the Gamma Member of the Santa Marta Formation (Marambio Group) (upper Campanian)" (salgado2006a, p. 122), after Olivero and colleagues (1991), and Coria and colleagues (2011) repeat it for the same holotype. It is the assignment before the revision, and Soto-Acuña and colleagues call it previous.
- *The Cape Lamb in the López de Bertodano Formation.* Rozadilla and colleagues' horizon line reads "Cape Lamb Member of the Lopez de Bertodano Formation (Maastrichtian)" (rozadilla2016a, p. 312), while the stratigraphic setting on the same page puts the specimen in the "Cape Lamb Member, Snow Hill Formation" and reports the reassignment. The paper contradicts itself on one page, and its considered statement is the setting.

**Open.** Whether the Cape Lamb straddles the two formations. The chart in Soto-Acuña and colleagues' Fig. 1c (p. 81), drawn after Milanese and colleagues (2020), divides the member into lower and upper parts and draws the López de Bertodano–Snow Hill Island contact through it. That is read from a figure, not from text. Pirrie and colleagues (1997) and Olivero (2012) would settle where the contact runs.
