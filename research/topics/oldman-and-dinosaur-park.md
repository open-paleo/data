# Oldman and Dinosaur Park

## When an older paper puts a Dinosaur Provincial Park specimen in the "Oldman Formation", which formation does the record take?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** The record takes whichever formation a source places that specimen in. A Park locality alone does not settle it, because the Park exposes both formations. Before 1993 the beds now in the Dinosaur Park Formation were included in the Oldman, so an older "Oldman Formation" is the name of the time, not a rival reading; the specimen still needs a source placing it in one formation or the other. Governs `location.formation` on [*Stegoceras*](../../genera/S/Stegoceras.yml), [*Stenonychosaurus*](../../genera/S/Stenonychosaurus.yml), [*Struthiomimus*](../../genera/S/Struthiomimus.yml) and [*Spinops*](../../genera/S/Spinops.yml), and on other Park records.

**Evidence.**
- russell1972a, p. 383: the *Struthiomimus altus* type is from the "(Oldman Formation, probably near Steveville ferry crossing, Dinosaur Provincial Park)".
- eberth1993b, p. 174: "The upper unit is assigned to a new formation, the Dinosaur Park Formation"; eberth2005a, p. 55, describes that paper as having "redefined and limited the stratigraphic extent of the Oldman Formation".
- eberth2005a, p. 54: the Park exposes "the upper 20 m of the Oldman Formation"; ryan2005a, p. 1371: the *Coronosaurus* bone bed in the Park is in the "Oldman Formation, 14.6 m below the contact with the Dinosaur Park Formation".
- schott2011a, p. 7: *Stegoceras* CMN 515, "Dinosaur Park Formation (Campanian)"; vanderreest2017a, p. 920: *Stenonychosaurus* CMN 8539 "about 53 m below the top of the Dinosaur Park Formation".
- farke2011a, p. 691: for *Spinops*, "the quarry lies in either the upper part of the Oldman Formation or the lower part of the Dinosaur Park Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Every Park locality in the Dinosaur Park Formation.* The upper Oldman is exposed in the Park.
- *McFeeters and colleagues (2016) as the source for Struthiomimus.* They place one specimen, ROM 1790, now the *Rativates* holotype, "in the Dinosaur Park Formation … 4.163 m above the contact with the Oldman Formation" (mcfeeters2016a, p. 1); their statement that *Struthiomimus* occurs in the formation is relayed from earlier work and does not place the holotype.

**Open.** No source read for this note places the *Struthiomimus altus* holotype, CMN 930, in either formation; Russell gives only "probably near Steveville ferry crossing".
