# Kugou

## Which unit holds the Kugou bonebed?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** The Wangshi Group, with no formation, on all three records from the bonebed. Every describing paper stops at the group; Xing and colleagues (2014) put the bonebed at the Xingezhuang–Hongtuya transition, probably just below the boundary, while An and colleagues (2015) put it in the Hongtuya. With the formation in dispute, the records keep the describing papers' unit. The stage stays Campanian, from An and colleagues' dating of the bone beds. Governs `location.group: Wangshi`, the empty `location.formation` and `location.part`, and the location notes on [*Anomalipes zhaoi*](../../genera/A/Anomalipes.yml), [*Ischioceratops zhuchengensis*](../../genera/I/Ischioceratops.yml) and [*Zhuchengceratops inexpectus*](../../genera/Z/Zhuchengceratops.yml). Supersedes the formation ruling of the 2026-08-31 [*Ischioceratops*](../genera/I/Ischioceratops.md) entry, which ruled out the Xingezhuang on a compilation without weighing Xing and colleagues.

**Evidence.**
- xu2010c: "Type locality and horizon. Kugou, Zhucheng County, Shandong Province, China. Upper Cretaceous Wangshi Group".
- he2015a: the holotype "was excavated from Kugou, Zhucheng, Shandong Province, China; Upper Cretaceous Wangshi Group".
- xing2014b: "The stratigraphic position of the Kugou bonebed appears to be restricted to the transition from the Xingezhuang Formation to the overlying Hongtuya Formation, and is possibly situated just below the boundary between the two formations".
- an2015a: samples "from the dinosaur bone beds in the Hongtuya Formation", from "a section in Kugou–Longgujian"; "the youngest single grain age … was 77.3 Ma, representing the maximum depositional age of the dinosaur fossil beds".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Hongtuya, lower part.* It takes one side of a live disagreement.

**Open.** Whether the bonebed lies in the top Xingezhuang or the basal Hongtuya.
