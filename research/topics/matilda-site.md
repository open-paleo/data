# Matilda Site

## What part and stages do the Matilda Site holotypes take?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** The upper part of the Winton Formation, Albian to Turonian, for both holotypes from AODL 85. Three readings date the site: latest Albian in the describing paper, Cenomanian after a detrital-zircon maximum age for the overlying sandstone, and Cenomanian to lower Turonian; the stage joins them. The part follows the [*Diamantinasaurus*](../genera/D/Diamantinasaurus.md) ruling on the site's place in the formation, which *Australovenator*'s `part: lower` contradicted. Governs `stage: [Albian, Cenomanian, Turonian]` on [*Australovenator wintonensis*](../../genera/A/Australovenator.yml) and [*Diamantinasaurus matildae*](../../genera/D/Diamantinasaurus.yml), and `location.part: upper` on *Australovenator*.

**Evidence.**
- hocknull2009a, Abstract: "three new dinosaurs from the late Early Cretaceous (latest Albian) Winton Formation".
- poropat2014a: AODL 85 sediments "were dated as Cenomanian by Bryan et al. (2012). These authors provided detrital zircon dates for the sandstone unit directly overlying the *Diamantinasaurus* type specimen, indicating a maximum age for this layer of 95 Ma".
- poropat2021a: "two individuals from the Cenomanian–lower Turonian 'upper' Winton Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian alone* for *Australovenator*. It drops the describing paper's latest Albian.
- *`part: lower`* for *Australovenator*. The site is one horizon, which the *Diamantinasaurus* note places in the upper Winton.

**Open.** A maximum age for the overlying sandstone does not bound the bonebed; a date from the bonebed would narrow the range.
