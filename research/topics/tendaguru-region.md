# Tendaguru region

## Which Tanzanian region are the Tendaguru sites in?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** Lindi (`TZ-12`). Bussert and colleagues (2009) put Tendaguru Hill in the Lindi district, and Mannion and colleagues (2019) give Lindi District for type localities from 600 m to 15 km from the hill. Several compilations instead list Tendaguru occurrences under "Mtwara". Governs `location.region: TZ-12` on [*Giraffatitan brancai*](../../genera/G/Giraffatitan.yml), [*Australodocus bohetii*](../../genera/A/Australodocus.yml), [*Dicraeosaurus hansemanni* and *D. sattleri*](../../genera/D/Dicraeosaurus.yml), [*Dysalotosaurus lettowvorbecki*](../../genera/D/Dysalotosaurus.yml), [*Elaphrosaurus bambergi*](../../genera/E/Elaphrosaurus.yml), [*Janenschia robusta*](../../genera/J/Janenschia.yml), [*Kentrosaurus aethiopicus*](../../genera/K/Kentrosaurus.yml), [*Ostafrikasaurus crassiserratus*](../../genera/O/Ostafrikasaurus.yml), [*Tendaguria tanzaniensis*](../../genera/T/Tendaguria.yml), [*Tornieria africana*](../../genera/T/Tornieria.yml), [*Wamweracaudia keranjei*](../../genera/W/Wamweracaudia.yml) and [*Veterupristisaurus milneri*](../../genera/V/Veterupristisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bussert2009a, p. 142: "Tendaguru Hill located approximately 10 km south of Mtapaia in the Lindi district, southeast Tanzania".
- mannion2019a, p. 788: *Janenschia* from "Quarry B, approximately 900 m southeast of Tendaguru Hill, Lindi District, southeastern Tanzania"; the same paper gives Lindi District for Quarry G, "600 m south of Tendaguru Hill" (p. 817), and for Nambango, "about 15 km southeast of Tendaguru Hill" (p. 812).

**Ruled out.**
- *Mtwara (`TZ-17`).* McIntosh (1990) lists *Brachiosaurus brancai* from "Tendaguru Beds, Mtwara, Tanzania" (mcintosh1990a, Table 16.1); Upchurch and colleagues (2004) give "Tendaguru Formation (Mtwara), Tanzania" (upchurch2004a, Table 13.1); Carrano and colleagues (2012) give "Kindope, north of Tendaguru, Mtwara, Tanzania" for *Elaphrosaurus* (carrano2012a, p. 231). These are compilation rows and name no site evidence; the papers that describe the sites give Lindi.

**Open.** Kindope. No source read gives its district, so its `TZ-12` rests on its lying north of Tendaguru Hill (our inference).
