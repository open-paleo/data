# MLL-Pv-005

## Which taxon's holotype is MLL-Pv-005?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Both: the number is printed as the holotype of the sauropod *Pilmatueia faundezi* (2019) and of the theropod *Lajasvenator ascheriae* (2020), from the same locality, and no correction has been published. The records keep both with a qualifier naming the paper. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Pilmatueia*](../../genera/P/Pilmatueia.yml) and [*Lajasvenator*](../../genera/L/Lajasvenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- coria2019a, accepted manuscript, §4.2: "Holotype: MLL-Pv-005, almost complete posterior dorsal vertebrae"; its Fig. 9 caption calls the same number a "paratype".
- coria2020a, pre-proof, Systematic Paleontology: "Holotype: MLL-PV-005, an incomplete but partially articulated skeleton"; the abstract prints "MLL-PV-Pv-005" and the figures "MLL-Pv-005".
- windholz2019a, p. 370: "the holotype specimen of Pilmatueia faundezi (Coria et al. 2019) consisting of an almost complete posterior dorsal vertebra (MLL‐Pv‐005)"; windholz2022a, p. 996, likewise.

**Ruled out.**
- *A slip in one paper corrected later.* No erratum or update is registered for either paper (Crossref).

**Open.** Which number is correct for each specimen. Both localities are "Pilmatué, 9 km northeast of Las Lajas"; only the museum (Museo Municipal de Las Lajas) could settle it.
