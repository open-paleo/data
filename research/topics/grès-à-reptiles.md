# Grès à Reptiles

## Are the "Grès à Reptiles" of Provence, the Var and the Hérault one unit?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. Three names are in use: the Argiles et Grès à Reptiles of the Aix-en-Provence (Arc) Basin, split into lower and upper units by the Calcaire de Rognac; Le Lœuff's Grès à Reptiles, used for the Var (Fox-Amphoux) succession and for Roques-Hautes; and the Hérault beds near Saint-Chinian, for which no source shows a correlation with either. Assign by basin. Governs `location.formation` on [*Matheronodon provincialis*](../../genera/M/Matheronodon.yml) and [*Arcovenator escotae*](../../genera/A/Arcovenator.yml) (Aix Basin), [*Variraptor mechinorum*](../../genera/V/Variraptor.yml) (Var), and the empty formation on [*Obelignathus*](../../genera/O/Obelignathus.yml) (Hérault), and the scope of [Argiles et Grès à Reptiles](../../stratigraphy/a/argiles-et-grès-à-reptiles.yml), [Grès à Reptiles](../../stratigraphy/g/grès-à-reptiles.yml) and [Lower Argiles Rutilantes](../../stratigraphy/l/lower-argiles-rutilantes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- catzigras1969a, p. 6: the Aix sheet maps "C8a. Argiles et grès inférieurs à Reptiles (250 à 300 m)", "C8b. Calcaires de Rognac (30 m)" and "C8c. Argiles et grès supérieurs à Reptiles (100 m)" under "C8. Rognacien (= Danien)"; in "la partie orientale du bassin de l'Arc à partir de Trets … la limite Bégudien - Rognacien devient imprécise" ("in the eastern part of the Arc basin from Trets … the Bégudien–Rognacian boundary becomes imprecise", translated; pp. 6–7).
- tabuce2013a, p. 655: the unit "historically named Lower 'Argiles et Grès à Reptiles' Formation or Lower 'Argiles Rutilantes' Formation" is "dated from the Lower Rognacian".
- leloeuff1998a, p. 105: *Variraptor* comes from the "Grès à Reptiles Formation" at La Bastide Neuve, "in the Fox-Amphoux syncline, 35 km west of Draguignan" (Var); p. 106 also applies the name to Roques-Hautes (Bouches-du-Rhône).
- tortosa2014a, p. 66: *Arcovenator* from the "Lower Argiles Rutilantes Formation, upper part of the Lower Rognacian, Late Campanian (Cojan and Moreau, 2006)", at Jas Neuf Sud "near the village of Pourrières (Var)", a locality in the Aix-en-Provence Basin (p. 63). The basin therefore extends into the Var (our inference from the two statements).
- czepiński2025a, p. 7: *Obelignathus* from "Montouliers, Saint-Chinian commune, Hérault", "Horizon 'Grès à Reptiles' Formation (upper Campanian-lower Maastrichtian", with the authors' quotation marks.

**Ruled out.**
- *The Hérault beds as the Provence Grès à Reptiles.* Czepiński and colleagues put the name in quotation marks, Csiki-Sava and colleagues label Hérault material "Grès à Reptiles Formation" without arguing a correlation (csiki-sava2015a, p. 41, Fig. 7), and Weishampel and colleagues list the Hérault beds as "Grès de Saint-Chinian" and, in the same section, an "Unnamed unit" (weishampel2004b, p. 589). No source read for this note shows the Hérault rock is the Provence unit.
- *A single Velaux–Fox-Amphoux locality.* Two sites are called La Bastide Neuve: Velaux-La Bastide Neuve in the Aix Basin (*Matheronodon*; godefroit2017a, p. 2) and La Bastide Neuve at Fox-Amphoux in the Var (*Variraptor*), about 70 km apart.

**Open.** Whether the Aix Basin's Lower Argiles et Grès à Reptiles and Lower Argiles Rutilantes are one unit. Tabuce and colleagues treat them as two names for one; Tortosa and colleagues cite Cojan and Moreau (2006) for "Lower Argiles Rutilantes", a name Cojan and Moreau's text does not print (it names the Calcaire de Rognac and the "Upper Argiles Rutilantes", cojan2006a, p. 590). Also open: the *Matheronodon* site is described as "'Begudian' (local stage) sandstones, late Campanian" (godefroit2017a, p. 2) but placed in the Argiles et Grès à Reptiles by Díez Díaz and colleagues (díezdíaz2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 2); Velaux lies off the Aix sheet, so the map does not decide it.
