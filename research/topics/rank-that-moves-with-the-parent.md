# Rank that moves with the parent

## When one source calls a unit a formation and another a member, which rank does the registry record?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** The rank and the parent are recorded together, as one scheme. Each pair below is one disagreement about the parent: the child is a member where the parent is read as a formation, and a formation where the parent is read as a group, so neither source is wrong about the rock. The registry takes the scheme of the recent specialist literature on the parent: the Woodbine as a group containing the Lewisville Formation, the Santana as a group whose concretion-bearing unit is the Romualdo Formation, and the Serra da Galga as a formation of the Bauru Group beside the Marília. Governs `rank: group` on [Woodbine](../../stratigraphy/w/woodbine.yml); `rank: formation` with `parent: Woodbine` on [Lewisville](../../stratigraphy/l/lewisville.yml); `rank: formation` on [Romualdo](../../stratigraphy/r/romualdo.yml); and `rank: formation` with `parent: Bauru` on [Serra da Galga](../../stratigraphy/s/serra-da-galga.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tykoski2023a, p. e2257238-1: "the Lewisville Formation of the Woodbine Group (middle Cenomanian) of north Texas".
- sayão2020a, p. 2: "there is a consensus to consider the former Santana Formation … as the Santana Group, that is further divided from base to top, into the Barbalha, Crato, Ipubi and Romualdo formations".
- cavalcanti2026a, p. 3: the Serra da Galga "was traditionally a member of the Marilia Formation and proposed as a formation a few years ago; Soares et al. 2020". The evidence for the Serra da Galga split is in [Marília and Serra da Galga](marília-and-serra-da-galga.md).

**Ruled out.**
- *The Lewisville as a member of a Woodbine Formation.* McDonald and colleagues write "the Lewisville Member of the Woodbine Formation" (mcdonald2017a, p. 5), and Head writes "the Arlington Member of the Woodbine Formation in North Texas" (head1998a, p. 719). Both use the Woodbine at formation rank; a Lewisville Member inside a Woodbine Group would mix the two schemes.
- *The Romualdo as a member of the Santana Formation.* Naish and colleagues give "Romualdo Member of the Santana Formation, Araripe Group" (naish2004a, p. 60), the scheme Sayão and colleagues call "the former Santana Formation".
- *The Serra da Galga as a member of the Marília.* Santucci and Filippi (2022) write "Serra da Galga Member, Marília Formation, Bauru Group, Maastrichtian" (santucci2022a, p. 357), the scheme before Soares and colleagues (2020).

**Open.** The Arlington. The registry places it as a member directly in the Woodbine, following Head (1998), whose Woodbine is a formation. In Tykoski and colleagues' group scheme the Arlington is a member of the Lewisville: the spillway site is "in either the Arlington Member or Tarrant Member of the Lewisville Formation" (tykoski2023a, p. e2257238-2). Head instead puts the Tarrant Member in the Eagleford Group (head1998a, p. 718), so the two schemes disagree on more than rank. Also unsettled: the Romualdo has no parent, because the registry has no Santana entry, and whether a dispute block is the place for a disagreement that is only about which scheme to use.
