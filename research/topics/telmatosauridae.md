# Telmatosauridae

## Is Telmatosauridae a clade to recognize for Telmatosaurus, Tethyshadros and Kryptohadros?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** Not yet. Analyses on the Xing, Xu, Takasaki–Kobayashi and McDonald matrices often recover *Telmatosaurus* and *Tethyshadros* as sister taxa. The Prieto-Márquez matrices do not. Magyar and colleagues (2026) named the clade, and the only work cited here to use the name since is a faunal list, which credits it to a paper that does not use it. The three genera stay at Hadrosauromorpha with the grouping in their dispute blocks. Governs the absence of a Telmatosauridae node under [Hadrosauromorpha](../../clades/Hadrosauromorpha.yml), and the dispute blocks on [*Telmatosaurus*](../../genera/T/Telmatosaurus.yml), [*Tethyshadros*](../../genera/T/Tethyshadros.yml) and [*Kryptohadros*](../../genera/K/Kryptohadros.yml).

**Evidence.**
- magyar2026a, p. 11, Systematic palaeontology: "Telmatosauridae novel clade name"; "The largest clade containing Telmatosaurus transsylvanicus (Nopcsa, 1900), but not Gobihadros mongoliensis Tsogtbaatar et al., 2019, Hadrosaurus foulkii Leidy, 1858 and Claosaurus agilis Marsh, 1872b"; "No other taxon names are currently in use for the same or approximate clade". The comment opens "Despite the poor to moderate … support found by us for the newly introduced clade".
- xing2014a, p. 17, Phylogenetic Analysis: "Telmatosaurus transsylvanicus is found to be the sister taxon of Tethyshadros insularis. These two taxa constitute the single European clade of basal hadrosauroids".
- mcdonald2017a, p. 25, Discussion: "Telmatosaurus and Tethyshadros comprise a clade of dwarf hadrosauromorphs restricted to the Campanian–Maastrichtian of the European Archipelago".
- lockwood2021a, p. 879, Fig. 23A: *Telmatosaurus* + *Tethyshadros*, bootstrap 86, Bremer support 2, sister to Hadrosauridae.
- kobayashi2021a, p. 9, Fig. 7a: *Tethyshadros* + *Telmatosaurus*, bootstrap 70, Bremer support 2, outside Hadrosauridae.
- dai2024a, p. 11, section 3.3: a position "just below the clade of Telmatosaurus transsylvanicus + Tethyshadros insularius".
- prieto-márquez2013a, Fig. 25: *Bactrosaurus*, *Telmatosaurus*, *Tethyshadros* and *Lophorhothon* as successive outgroups to Hadrosauridae.
- prieto-márquez2021a, p. 431: *Fylax* "forms a clade with the hadrosauroid Tethyshadros insularis".
- longrich2026a, Fig. 14: "Telmatosauridae" in a list of Ibero-Armorican faunal groups, cited to Prieto-Márquez and Carrera Farias (2021).

**Ruled out.**
- *A Telmatosauridae node holding the three genera.* One paper names and tests the clade, and calls its support poor to moderate. *Kryptohadros* is a terminal in that paper's analyses only. If the node were adopted, its `erected_in` would be Magyar and colleagues (2026). They give the definition and the diagnosis in the same entry, so `described_in` would be left out. No earlier family-group name based on *Telmatosaurus* appears in the works cited here.
- *Telmatosauridae as a name already in use before 2026.* Longrich and colleagues (2026) cite it to Prieto-Márquez and Carrera Farias (2021), who write of a "Fylax–Tethyshadros clade" and do not use the name.

**Open.** Whether later analyses take up the name and recover *Kryptohadros* with the other two. Jones (2026) does not use the name and classifies the genera only to Hadrosauromorpha.
