# JVP abstract supplements

## How is the issue of a *Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology* abstract volume recorded?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** As `issue: "3, Supplement"`, with the journal as plain *Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology* and the volume number. The Society of Vertebrate Paleontology's meeting abstracts appeared as a supplement to number 3 of each volume, paged with an A suffix. Governs `journal`, `volume` and `issue` on [milana1995a](../../references/m/milana1995a.yml), [alcober1998a](../../references/a/alcober1998a.yml), [breithaupt1999a](../../references/b/breithaupt1999a.yml), [mateus2001b](../../references/m/mateus2001b.yml), [jinnah2007a](../../references/j/jinnah2007a.yml) and [lü2008c](../../references/l/lü2008c.yml).

**Evidence.**
- milana1995a, p. 45A, running head: "JVP 15(3) September 1995 - ABSTRACTS". alcober1998a, p. 23A, and breithaupt1999a, p. 33A, carry the same head for 18(3) and 19(3).
- mateus2001b, p. 78A; jinnah2007a, p. 96A; lü2008c, p. 108A: even pages, which carry only the footer "© [year] by the Society of Vertebrate Paleontology" and no running head.
- Reference lists across the held corpus cite every one of these six volumes as a supplement to number 3, most often as "(3, Supplement)"; a bare "(3)" is rare.

**Ruled out.**
- *issue: "3".* The running heads print only the number, but the A-paged abstracts are not part of the regular issue's pagination, and a bare 3 would read 44A–45A as pages of that issue.
- *"Supplement" alone, or "3 (supplement)".* Earlier forms in the store; the first drops the number the running heads print, and both differ from the form the literature mostly uses.
- *A "Program and Abstracts" journal name for the 2008 volume.* Its pages are A-paged within volume 28, like the others.

**Open.** From 2009 the abstracts appeared as a separate *Program and Abstracts* book with ordinary page numbers; [foster2016a](../../references/f/foster2016a.yml) and [hohman2021a](../../references/h/hohman2021a.yml) are of that kind and carry no volume or issue. Nothing about them was changed here.
