# Species authorities

## Who replaced Huene's preoccupied *Campylodon* with *Campylodoniscus*?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** Kuhn (1961), in *Die Familien der rezenten und fossilen Amphibien und Reptilien*. Huene's *Campylodon* (1929) was preoccupied by *Campylodon* Cuvier and Valenciennes, 1832, and the technical literature credits the replacement name to Kuhn alone, in 1961; the only Kuhn 1961 work in those bibliographies is *Die Familien*. The species keeps Huene (1929). Governs the species `erected_in: huene1929a`, `erected_as: Campylodon ameghinoi` and the genus-level `erected_in: kuhn1961a` on [*Campylodoniscus ameghinoi*](../../genera/C/Campylodoniscus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- huene1929a, p. 82: "Campylodon Ameghinoi n. gen. n. sp.".
- upchurch2004a, p. 270, Table 13.1: "type of Campylodoniscus Kuhn, 1961".
- olshevsky1991a, p. 144: "Genus: Campylodoniscus Kuhn, 1961".
- powell2003a, p. 57: "Campylodon Huene 1929a, pp. 82-3, non; Cuvier and Valenciennes, 1832", the misplaced semicolon as printed; the replacement is credited to "Kuhn 1961 in Steel 1970", and the section heading gives "Kuhn 1970", the year of the work Powell read it in.

**Ruled out.**
- *Haubold and Kuhn (1961), Lebensbilder und Evolution fossiler Saurier (haubold1961a), as the source of the name.* The record credited it, as two popular books do without a page; no work giving a page cites it for *Campylodoniscus*, and the bibliographies citing "Kuhn, 1961" list only *Die Familien*.

**Open.** *Die Familien* has not been read; it, or Steel (1970, p. 76), would confirm the page and the wording.

## Which paper is the authority for *Hanssuesia*, and for its type species?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** The species is Brown and Schlaikjer's (1943) *Troodon sternbergi*; the genus is Sullivan's JVP 23(1) revision, which erected it as "Hanssuessia", not the later note correcting the spelling. The correction of an incorrect original spelling is a justified emendation, and the corrected name "retains the authorship and date of the original spelling" (ICZN Art. 33.2.2). Governs the species `erected_in: brown1943a`, `erected_as: Troodon sternbergi` and the genus-level `erected_in: sullivan2003b` on [*Hanssuesia sternbergi*](../../genera/H/Hanssuesia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown1943a, p. 131: "Troodon sternbergi, new species".
- sullivan2003b, p. 193: "HANSSUESSIA, gen. nov.".
- sullivan2003a, title: "Hanssuesia, the correct generic name for 'Hanssuessia' Sullivan, 2003".

**Ruled out.**
- *sullivan2003a as the genus authority.* The record credited the correcting note, as CONTRIBUTING's "A corrected misspelling credits the correction" would; Art. 33.2.2 keeps the authority with the paper that published the original spelling. Both papers are Sullivan (2003), so the cited authority is the same either way.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Who named *Rinchenia mongoliensis*, and who erected *Rinchenia*?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** Barsbold (1986) named the species, as *Oviraptor mongoliensis*; the genus is credited to Barsbold (1997). The naming paper has not been read, but the chapter Barsbold co-wrote and Funston and colleagues' (2018) revision give the same attribution. Governs the species `erected_in: barsbold1986a`, `erected_as: Oviraptor mongoliensis` and the genus-level `erected_in: barsbold1997a` on [*Rinchenia mongoliensis*](../../genera/R/Rinchenia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- osmólska2004a, p. 166, Table 8.1: "Rinchenia Barsbold, 1997"; "R. mongoliensis (Barsbold, 1986) (= Oviraptor mongoliensis Barsbold, 1986)".
- funston2018a, p. 114: "The genus Rinchenia, despite being rendered valid when equated by (Osmólska et al., 2004) to 'Oviraptor' mongoliensis, lacked proper description and diagnosis".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barsbold (1997) as the species authority.* The record gave it; the species is Barsbold's 1986 *Oviraptor mongoliensis*, moved to *Rinchenia* later.

**Open.** Whether *Rinchenia* is available from Barsbold (1997). Barsbold (1997) gives no diagnosis, and Funston and colleagues (2018) say the name was "rendered valid" by Osmólska and colleagues (2004), which may make 2004 the genus's date under ICZN Art. 13. Barsbold (1997) would settle it.

## Does `erected_as` give an emended epithet as printed or as corrected?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** As printed. `erected_as` records the binomial as first published, so a later justified emendation of the epithet does not change it; the emendation keeps the original author and date (ICZN Art. 33.2.2), so `erected_in` stays the naming paper. Governs `erected_as: Seismosaurus halli` on [*Diplodocus hallorum*](../../genera/D/Diplodocus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gillette1991a, p. 418: "SEISMOSAURUS HALLI, sp. nov.", with an etymology honoring "the late Reverend James W. Hall ... and his wife Ruth", which shows the singular *-i* to be an inadvertent error correctable under Art. 32.5.1.
- ICZN Art. 33.2.2: a justified emendation "retains the authorship and date of the original spelling".

**Ruled out.**
- *`erected_as: Seismosaurus hallorum`.* The corrected spelling was never printed in that combination by the naming paper; `erected_as` is a record of what the paper printed.
- *The emending work as the species authority.* Art. 33.2.2 keeps the authority with Gillette (1991).

**Open.** Nothing.
