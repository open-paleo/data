# Maortu and Dashuigou: Ulansuhai or Miaogou

## Are the Maortu and Dashuigou dinosaurs from the Ulansuhai Formation or the Miaogou Formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The Miaogou Formation, Early Cretaceous. The Ulansuhai is a separate Upper Cretaceous unit. Governs `location.formation: Miaogou` and Early Cretaceous stages on [*Shaochilong maortuensis*](../../genera/S/Shaochilong.yml), [*Gobisaurus domoculus*](../../genera/G/Gobisaurus.yml), [*Probactrosaurus gobiensis*](../../genera/P/Probactrosaurus.yml), [*Yuanyanglong*](../../genera/Y/Yuanyanglong.yml) and [*Chilantaisaurus tashuikouensis*](../../genera/C/Chilantaisaurus.yml), and keeps [Ulansuhai](../../stratigraphy/u/ulansuhai.yml) and [Miaogou](../../stratigraphy/m/miaogou.yml) apart.

**Evidence.**
- hao2024a, p. 2: the Maortu locality "actually belongs to the Miaogou Formation"; p. 3: "Miaogou Formation (Aptian-Albian)". The Maortu fauna includes "the tetanura Shaochilong maortuensis …, the hadrosauroid Probactrosaurus gobiensis …, and the ankylosaurid Gobisaurus domoculus" (p. 2).
- hao2024a, p. 2: the Miaogou "incorporated the formerly abandoned Dashuigou Formation and Wulanhushao Formation".
- dong2002a, p. 282: "阿拉善左旗吉兰泰北之庆格勒(大水沟)毛尔图" ("Maortu, Qinggele (Dashuigou), north of Jilantai, Alxa Zuo Banner", translated), which places Maortu at Dashuigou.
- carrano2012a, p. 226: *Chilantaisaurus* from "Tashuikou (Dashuigou), 60 km north of Chilantai".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Ulansuhai Formation, Aptian–?Albian."* Weishampel and colleagues (2004) list *Chilantaisaurus tashuikouensis* and *Gobisaurus domoculus* under "Ulansuhai Formation … Age: Aptian-?Albian (Dong, pers. comm., contra Dong 1980)" (weishampel2004b, p. 568). Brusatte and colleagues (2009), Carrano and colleagues (2012) and Arbour and Currie (2015) also assign these taxa to the Ulansuhai but give it "approximately 92 Ma (Turonian" or "Turonian or younger (< 92 Ma)" (carrano2012a, p. 226; arbour2015a, p. 18; Brusatte and colleagues report the Aptian–Albian only to reject it). The shared error is the Ulansuhai assignment.
- *"?Turonian / < 92 Ma" for Chilantaisaurus.* Carrano and colleagues cite Kobayashi and Lü (2003) for it (carrano2012a, p. 226), whose figure is from "Basalts from the Suhongtu Formation" (kobayashi2003b, p. 236), a maximum age for the Ulansuhai near Suhongtu, about 200 km from Dashuigou (our calculation from published coordinates). It does not date the Miaogou.

**Open.** A narrower age for the Dashuigou beds. Van Itterbeeck and colleagues (2004) consider "the sediments at Dashuiguo … Barremian in age" from palynology tied to the radiometrically dated Tebch basalt (vanitterbeeck2004a, p. 406), "more accurate, and older, than the Aptian–Albian age based on dinosaur evidence". No source says whether their Dashuiguo section is the *Chilantaisaurus* quarry or the Maortu beds; Maortu lies about 13 km from the section by published coordinates (our calculation). Until that link is sourced, the Barremian is an inference for these taxa.
