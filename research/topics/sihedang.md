# Sihedang

## Which units and stages do the Sihedang records carry?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Each record keeps its describing paper's unit: the Yixian Formation for *Zhenyuanlong* and *Zhongjianosaurus*, the Jehol Group for *Lingyuanosaurus*, whose paper declines to choose between the Yixian and the Jiufotang. None of the three papers dates the horizon on its own evidence, so each takes its unit's range: Barremian for the two Yixian records, Barremian and Aptian for *Lingyuanosaurus*. Governs `stage` on [*Zhenyuanlong suni*](../../genera/Z/Zhenyuanlong.yml), [*Zhongjianosaurus yangi*](../../genera/Z/Zhongjianosaurus.yml) and [*Lingyuanosaurus sihedangensis*](../../genera/L/Lingyuanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2015a, p. 2: "Sihedang of Jianchang County, Liaoning Province; Yixian Formation".
- xu2017a, p. 130, Locality and horizon: "Sihedang, Lingyuan County, Liaoning Province, China. Possibly Yixian Formation, Aptian, Cretaceous (Swisher et al., 2001)."
- yao2019a, p. 2, Horizon and Locality: "Jehol Group, Lower Cretaceous … The fossil-bearing strata at Sihedang have been assigned to the Yixian Formation in some studies but to the Jiufotang Formation in others. Resolving this stratigraphic issue is beyond the scope of the present paper."

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian alone for* Lingyuanosaurus. It follows the Jiufotang reading the record declines; the Jehol Group spans Barremian and Aptian.
- *Aptian for* Zhongjianosaurus. Xu and Qin's "Aptian" dates the Yixian as a whole, after Swisher and colleagues (2001), not the Sihedang horizon; the [Yixian](../../stratigraphy/y/yixian.yml) entry holds the formation's current dating.

**Open.** Which formation the Sihedang beds belong to; the 2026-08-29 *Lingyuanosaurus* entry names what would settle it.

## Which county is Sihedang in?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** Lingyuan. Governs `location.locality: Sihedang, Lingyuan` on [*Zhenyuanlong suni*](../../genera/Z/Zhenyuanlong.yml), [*Zhongjianosaurus yangi*](../../genera/Z/Zhongjianosaurus.yml) and [*Lingyuanosaurus sihedangensis*](../../genera/L/Lingyuanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yao2019a, p. 2: "the type locality is situated in the town of Sihedang within the county-level city of Lingyuan".
- xu2017a, p. 130, Locality and horizon: "Sihedang, Lingyuan County, Liaoning Province, China".

**Ruled out.**
- *Jianchang County.* Lü and Brusatte (2015) give the *Zhenyuanlong* type locality as "Sihedang of Jianchang County, Liaoning Province" (lü2015a, p. 2), with no further description of the site. The two other describing papers for Sihedang taxa name Lingyuan, and Yao and colleagues (2019) state that the town lies within Lingyuan.

**Open.** That the Sihedang of Lü and Brusatte is the town in Lingyuan, and not another place of the same name, is an inference: their paper gives no map or coordinate. A map or coordinate for the *Zhenyuanlong* site would settle it.
