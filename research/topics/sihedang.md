# Sihedang

## Which county is Sihedang in?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** Lingyuan. Governs `location.locality: Sihedang, Lingyuan` on [*Zhenyuanlong suni*](../../genera/Z/Zhenyuanlong.yml), [*Zhongjianosaurus yangi*](../../genera/Z/Zhongjianosaurus.yml) and [*Lingyuanosaurus sihedangensis*](../../genera/L/Lingyuanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yao2019a, p. 2: "the type locality is situated in the town of Sihedang within the county-level city of Lingyuan".
- xu2017a, p. 130, Locality and horizon: "Sihedang, Lingyuan County, Liaoning Province, China".

**Ruled out.**
- *Jianchang County.* Lü and Brusatte (2015) give the *Zhenyuanlong* type locality as "Sihedang of Jianchang County, Liaoning Province" (lü2015a, p. 2), with no further description of the site. The two other describing papers for Sihedang taxa name Lingyuan, and Yao and colleagues (2019) state that the town lies within Lingyuan.

**Open.** That the Sihedang of Lü and Brusatte is the town in Lingyuan, and not another place of the same name, is an inference: their paper gives no map or coordinate. A map or coordinate for the *Zhenyuanlong* site would settle it.
