# XHPM and the Dalian museum codes

## Which institution code covers the Dalian specimens?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** XHPM, the Xinghai Paleontological Museum of Dalian, of which DNHM (the code in the describing papers) is an obsolete form. Governs `type_specimen.institution: XHPM` on [*Daliansaurus*](../../genera/D/Daliansaurus.yml), [*Dongbeititan*](../../genera/D/Dongbeititan.yml), [*Liaoningvenator*](../../genera/L/Liaoningvenator.yml), [*Wulong*](../../genera/W/Wulong.yml) and [*Jianianhualong*](../../genera/J/Jianianhualong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sabaj2020a, p. 615: "XHPM: Xinghai Paleontological Museum of Dalian … Also known as: Dalian Natural History Museum. Obsolete as: D, DLNM, DMNH, DNHM, DNM".
- shen2017a, p. 360: "housed in the collections of the Dalian Natural History Museum".

**Ruled out.**
- *DMNH as only Dalian.* DMNH is also Denver's code; Sabaj lists it as an obsolete Dalian form, but a bare DMNH number cannot be assigned to Dalian without the paper's abbreviation list.

**Open.** Whether DLXH ("Dalian Xinghai Museum", xu2017b) is the same museum; Foth and colleagues list DLXH and XHPM as two codes (foth2017a, p. 15), and Sabaj does not list DLXH.
