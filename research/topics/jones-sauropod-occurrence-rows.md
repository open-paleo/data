# Jones sauropod occurrence rows

## Do Jones's (2026) occurrences for *Abrosaurus*, *Australodocus* and *Austrosaurus* belong to those taxa?

*2026-08-02*

**Conclusion.** No. In two sauropod tables of Jones (2026) the occurrence column gives each of these three taxa a unit and country that the describing papers contradict. Each record keeps the describing paper's occurrence. Governs `location.country` and `location.formation` on [*Abrosaurus dongpoi*](../../genera/A/Abrosaurus.yml) (CN, Shaximiao), [*Australodocus bohetii*](../../genera/A/Australodocus.yml) (TZ, Tendaguru) and [*Austrosaurus mckillopi*](../../genera/A/Austrosaurus.yml) (AU).

**Evidence.**
- jones2026b, p. 379, Macronaria table: *Abrosaurus dongpoi* "Tendaguru Fm., Tanzania Late Jurassic, Tithonian".
- jones2026b, p. 380, Somphospondyli table: *Australodocus bohetii* "Udurchurkan Fm., Russia Late Cretaceous, Maastrichtian", the occurrence also given to *Arkharavia heterocoelica* two rows above; *Austrosaurus mckillopi* "Tendaguru Fm., Tanzania Late Jurassic, Tithonian". The row between them, *Astrophocaudia slaughteri*, reads "Paluxy Fm., United States".
- ouyang1989a (read in English translation, without the original folios), Locality and stratigraphic position: "Lower Jurassic Xiaximiao Fm., Dashanpu, Zigong Co., Sichuan Province" (see [*Abrosaurus*](../genera/A/Abrosaurus.md) on what this line gives).
- remes2007a, Systematic palaeontology, Text-fig. 1: "Map of the type locality of *Australodocus bohetii*, Tendaguru site G, Upper Saurian Beds (Tithonian)".
- longman1933a, Locality: "'Clutha' is north of the Flinders River, and the homestead is 34 miles from Maxwelton", in north-west Queensland.

**Ruled out.**
- *Tendaguru for* Abrosaurus, *the Udurchukan for* Australodocus, *Tendaguru for* Austrosaurus. These are the Jones rows. Each contradicts its taxon's describing paper. *Austrosaurus* carries the Tendaguru that belongs to *Australodocus*, and *Australodocus* carries *Arkharavia*'s Russian unit, which looks like rows shifted within the table. That reading of how the error arose is an inference.

**Open.** Nothing.
