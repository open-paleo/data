# Bügiin Tsav

## Which aimag is Bügiin Tsav in?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Probably Bayankhongor (MN-069), on the coordinates the *Nomingia* describing paper gives; most other sources say Ömnögovi, so the assignment is disputed. Governs `location.region` on the Bügiin Tsav taxa [*Nomingia gobiensis*](../../genera/N/Nomingia.yml), [*Zanabazar junior*](../../genera/Z/Zanabazar.yml), [*Adasaurus mongoliensis*](../../genera/A/Adasaurus.yml), [*Anserimimus planinychus*](../../genera/A/Anserimimus.yml) and [*Mononykus olecranus*](../../genera/M/Mononykus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barsbold2000a, p. 98: "Type locality: Bugin Tsav, Bayankhongor Province, Gobi Desert, Mongolia (GPS coordinates of the site: latitude: 43-52-0101 N; longitude: 100-00-4247 E", that is about 43.867° N, 100.012° E.
- benton2000c, p. xxiv: "Bügiin Tsav [locality]", "Bugin Tsav (= Bügiin Tsav)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing; Ömnögovi remains the rival reading (see Open).

**Open.** Which aimag. Three sources read for this note say Ömnögovi: "Nemegt Formation at Bugiin Tsav, Omnogov Aimag" (norell2009a, p. 26); "northwest of Altan Uul in the South Gobi Aimak, Mongolia (Suzuki et al., 2000; Eberth, 2017)" (lee2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 5); and "Bugin Tsav, Omnogov" in the appendix tables of Pittman and Xu (2020, pittman2020a, pp. 151, 155, 157). Lee and colleagues' coordinates, "43°51.801´N, 99°58.327´E" (p. 4), lie about 3.5 km from Barsbold and colleagues'. That these coordinates fall in Bayankhongor depends on where the aimag boundary runs, which was not checked against a source; no source read for this note names a soum.
