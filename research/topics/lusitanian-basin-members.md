# Lusitanian Basin members

## How should the Lourinhã and Farta Pão members be recorded, and where does "Sobral" belong?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Follow Mateus and colleagues (2017): the coastal Lourinhã Formation has the Praia da Amoreira–Porto Novo, Praia Azul and other members, and the Farta Pão Formation to the south has its own members, including the Sobral. The coastal unit that Manuppella called "Sobral" is the Praia Azul Member. Governs `location.member` on [*Lourinhanosaurus antunesi*](../../genera/L/Lourinhanosaurus.yml) and [*Lusotitan atalaiensis*](../../genera/L/Lusotitan.yml) (Praia Azul), [*Dinheirosaurus lourinhanensis*](../../genera/D/Dinheirosaurus.yml) and [*Zby atlanticus*](../../genera/Z/Zby.yml) (Praia da Amoreira–Porto Novo), and [*Lourinhasaurus alenquerensis*](../../genera/L/Lourinhasaurus.yml) (Sobral, Farta Pão), and the scope of [Sobral](../../stratigraphy/s/sobral.yml) and [Praia Azul](../../stratigraphy/p/praia-azul.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mateus2017a, p. 79: the Farta Pão members, after Schneider and colleagues (2009); p. 80: the Lourinhã members, after Hill; p. 83: the coastal "Sobral" of Manuppella is the Praia Azul Member; pp. 76, 80: the units are informal. p. 85: "Lourinhanosaurus antunesi (holotype ML370), Lusotitan atalaiensis (lectotype, MG)" in the Praia Azul fauna.
- mateus2014a, p. 619: *Zby* from the "top of the Amoreira–Porto Novo Member"; mannion2011a, p. 523, for *Dinheirosaurus*.
- mocho2014a, p. 877: *Lourinhasaurus* from the "Arruda sub-basin … Farta Pão Formation"; p. 880: "Sobral Formation (or Sobral Member of the Farta Pão Formation sensu Schneider et al., 2009)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sobral as a coastal unit.* The coastal "Sobral" is the Praia Azul Member (mateus2017a, p. 83; also mateus2009b, p. 247; hendrickx2014a, p. 20). The inland Sobral of the Arruda sub-basin is a member of the Farta Pão.
- *Lourinhasaurus in the Farta Pão by Mateus and colleagues.* Mateus and colleagues list it in the Amoreira–Porto Novo fauna (p. 83) and do not mention Moinho do Carmo; the Farta Pão and Sobral placement is Mocho and colleagues' (2014). Mocho and colleagues locate the site both "south of Alcobaça" (p. 876) and in "Alenquer municipality" (p. 880).

**Open.** Rank. Several papers use formation rank for Sobral (mocho2016a, p. 3; carrano2012a; mateus2009b), so "Sobral is never a formation" holds only within Mateus and colleagues' scheme. Whether the Bombarral and Consolação sub-basins share units is an inference; Mateus and colleagues list them separately (p. 76).
