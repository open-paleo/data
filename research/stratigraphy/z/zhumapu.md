# Zhumapu

## What stages does the Zhumapu take?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** Cenomanian to Campanian, the union of the readings of the formation at Zuoyun. Yao and colleagues (1989) date its lowest bed to the Cenomanian and the whole only to the Late Cretaceous; Chen and colleagues (2007) name the Zhumapu among the units holding *Pseudohyria* bivalves and date them Coniacian–Campanian. The two disagree on whether *Pseudohyria* reaches the Cenomanian, and the union takes both. Supersedes the Open line of the 2026-10-09 entry. Governs `stages` on the [Zhumapu Formation](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhumapu.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yao1989a, p. 77: Liu Junying's charophyte flora is transitional, "因而将该植物归入晚白垩世早期 Cenomanian" [so the flora is assigned to the early Late Cretaceous, Cenomanian], for the lowest bed of the formation.
- chen2007a, p. 304: units including the 助马堡组 [Zhumapu Formation] with *Pseudohyria*: "这些地层单位都应归晚白垩世中-晚期(Coniacian-Campanian 期)" [these stratigraphic units should all be placed in the middle to late Late Cretaceous (Coniacian–Campanian)].
- wang2017c: the hadrosauroid quarry in the lower part has an "estimated age" of Cenomanian, after the 1984 Shanxi survey.

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian alone.* It dates the lowest 32 m of a 455 m formation.
- *Late Campanian to early Maastrichtian.* It is the Huiquanpu's reading (see the entry below on whether the two are one unit).

**Open.** Whether the hadrosauroids' "lower part" is the formation as Yao and colleagues redefined it. Wang and colleagues (2013) give the formation as 722 m, about Yao's 455 m plus the 265 m Yao moved into the Zuoyun, so their "lower part" may follow the older base and lie in beds now assigned to the Zuoyun (our inference from the thicknesses, which no paper states).

## Are the Zhumapu and the Huiquanpu one unit?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** Two units, as the registry has them. Two papers equate the names in passing, giving no basis: Xi and colleagues (2021) call the Zhumapu "once called the Huiquanpu Formation" in Hebei, and Pang and colleagues (2024) write the Huiquanpu "equivalent to the Zhumapu Formation of Shanxi". The paper that defines the Zhumapu at Zuoyun, Yao and colleagues (1989), never mentions the Huiquanpu, describes a different succession, and correlates the Tujingzi Formation at Ximalin with the Lower Cretaceous Zuoyun, where Pang and colleagues equate it with the Huiquanpu. Governs the separate [Zhumapu](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhumapu.yml) and [Huiquanpu](../../../stratigraphy/h/huiquanpu.yml) entries, neither listing the other as a variant.

**Evidence.**
- xi2021a: "the Zuoyun and Zhumapu formations (the latter was once called the Huiquanpu Formation in Yuanyang, Hebei)"; the place is Yangyuan.
- pang2024a, p. 52: "灰泉堡组（相当山西的助马堡组）" [the Huiquanpu Formation (equivalent to the Zhumapu Formation of Shanxi)], which "按其岩性可明显地分为两段" [divides clearly into two members by lithology], the lower one "与下伏太古宇集宁群或中元古代长城系高于庄组呈不整合接触" [unconformable on the Archean Jining Group or the Mesoproterozoic Gaoyuzhuang Formation of the Changcheng System].
- yao1989a, p. 74: the Zhumapu is "厚度约455m" [about 455 m thick], named at Zhumapu, Datong; p. 77: it "整合覆于下白垩统左云组之上" [lies conformably on the Lower Cretaceous Zuoyun Formation].
- yao1989a, p. 77: "下白垩统左云组一段、二段可与下白垩统土井子组一段、二段对比……因此很有可能相当上白垩统助马堡组的地层在该地区缺失或未见顶" [Members 1 and 2 of the Lower Cretaceous Zuoyun Formation correlate with Members 1 and 2 of the Lower Cretaceous Tujingzi Formation … so strata equivalent to the Upper Cretaceous Zhumapu Formation are very probably absent in that area, or their top is not seen]; against pang2024a, p. 53: "土井子组（相当灰泉堡组）" [the Tujingzi Formation (equivalent to the Huiquanpu Formation)].

**Ruled out.**
- *One formation.* The equivalence is asserted, not argued, and conflicts with the correlation of the formation's own describer.
- *The Huiquanpu's readings for the Zhumapu.* Xi and colleagues' late Campanian to early Maastrichtian comes from the Tianzhen Huiquanpu, as the entry below records.

**Open.** The official nomenclature, which the Shanxi provincial lithostratigraphy is likely to state.

## What stage do the lower Zhumapu hadrosauroids take?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** The Cenomanian, the only stage stated for their quarry. Governs `stage: [Cenomanian]` on [*Zuoyunlong huangi*](../../../genera/Z/Zuoyunlong.yml) and [*Yunganglong datongensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yunganglong.yml), in the [Zhumapu Formation](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhumapu.yml). The formation's own stages are not settled here.

**Evidence.**
- wang2017c, Type locality and horizon: "Zhumapu Formation, lower Upper Cretaceous. The fossil quarry occurs in the lower part of the formation, and its estimated age is Cenomanian (Shanxi Regional Geological Survey Team 1984)".
- wang2017c, Discussion: *Yunganglong* and *Zuoyunlong* are "both from the same locality and horizon", and the comparison table gives *Yunganglong datongensis* as "Cenomanian, Late Cretaceous".
- wang2013a, Abstract: *Yunganglong* comes "from the early Late Cretaceous Zhumapu Formation", with no stage named.

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian to Santonian.* No source read gives this range for the quarry or the formation.
- *Late Campanian to early Maastrichtian.* Xi and colleagues (2021) give it for the formation as a whole, citing a work on the dinosaur fauna of Tianzhen, not Zuoyun; it is not a statement about this horizon.

**Open.** The formation's stages, which the registry does not record.
