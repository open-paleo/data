# Zuunbayan

## What is the age of the Dzun Bayn (Zuunbayan) unit?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Hauterivian–Albian, as the sum of the two formations it was later split into. Governs `stages: [Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian, Albian]` on Zuunbayan.

**Evidence.**
- khand2000a, p. 62: the Shinekhudag Formation is "Hauterivian-Barremian. According to the Vasilev et al. [19], it was the lower Zuunbayan Formation"; p. 63: the "Khukhteeg Formation is considered to be Aptian-Albian in age. According to Vasilev et al. [19], it was the upper Zuunbayan Formation."
- vanitterbeeck2004a, p. 403: "the Dzun Bayn suite, Mongolia, which according to Khand et al. (2000) ranges in age from Hauterivian to Albian".
- benton2000c, p. xxiv: "Dzun Bayan (= Züünbayan)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Ostracod ranges as the unit's age.* Van Itterbeeck and colleagues print "Stratigraphic distribution. Hauterivian–Albian" (p. 398) for individual ostracod species, not for the unit.

**Open.** Narrower readings recorded on the registry entry (Aptian–Albian only; Barremian) were not re-examined for this note.
