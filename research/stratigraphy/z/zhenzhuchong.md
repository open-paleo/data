# Zhenzhuchong

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Zhenzhuchong, and on what evidence?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Early Jurassic. Dong and colleagues (1983) date the unit by the Lufeng fauna it carries, prosauropods from Dafang County, Guizhou, and Weiyuan County, Sichuan, with an Early Jurassic pollen zone and fern flora, and by its position between the Late Triassic Shunjiahe Formation and the Ziliujing members above, whose Dongyuemiao Member yields the Liassic *Bishanopliosaurus*. The 1978 Sichuan symposium they report also gave the unit "an age of Early Jurassic". Their "Rhaeto-Liassic" glosses the Early Jurassic and adds no Triassic stage. The stage, Sinemurian, rests on Han and colleagues (2024), whose "possibly in Sinemurian" is hedged; this entry does not settle it. Governs `period` on [Zhenzhuchong](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhenzhuchong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 2, The Zhenzhuchong Formation: the unit "was formerly a member of the Ziliujing 'Group'"; Tan and Li (1933) "subdivided them into the Zhenzhuchong clays, Dongyuemiao limestones, Fenbao clays, Guojiaao sandstones, Maanshan clays, Daanzhai limestones and Lianggaoshan sandstones".
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 4: the 1978 Symposium on the Mesozoic Stratigraphy of Sichuan reached "agreement that this unit should be provided formational status with an age of Early Jurassic".
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 4: the Dafang County collection held "the prosauropod *Gyposaurus sinensis*" and "the poposaur *Sinosaurus* sp."; at Hulin, Weiyuan County, "an ungual phalanx was recovered with a size and morphology attributable to *Lufengosaurus*. This data confirms that the Zhenzhucheng Fm. produces an Early Jurassic (Rhaeto-Liassic) Lufeng Fauna." The translation spells the name "Zhenzhuchong" everywhere else.
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 4: "Pollen studies undertaken by Yuanhong Bai identify a *Cyathidites undulatisportes* zone which he identified as at Early Jurassic. Fossil macro plants are dominated by the fern *Coniopteris* which is also regarded Early Jurassic."
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 4: "Combined with the macroplants and pollen data in addition to the fact that the Liassic taxon *Bishanopliosaurus* is derived from the overlying Dongyuemiao Mem. of the Ziliujing Fm., the Zhenzhuchong fauna is confidently recognized as Early Jurassic, or Rhaeto-Liassic."
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 16: the ungual is from the "Early Jurassic (Rhaeto-Liassic) Zhenzhuchong Fm. at Hulin, Huangshiban Commune, Weiyuan Co."
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 133: sedimentation "initiates with the Late Triassic Shunjiahe Fm. and continues with the Early Jurassic Zhenzhuchong Fm, the Early to Middle Jurassic Ziliujing Fm.".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Triassic (Rhaetian), from "Rhaeto-Liassic".* Dong and colleagues (1983) use the term as another name for the Early Jurassic ("Early Jurassic, or Rhaeto-Liassic") and place the unit above the Late Triassic Shunjiahe Formation.

**Open.** Whether Han and colleagues' (2024) "possibly in Sinemurian" can carry the stage: it is a hedge, and the works they cite for it have not been read.

## Is the Zhenzhuchong a member of the Ziliujing Formation, or a formation below it?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** A member of the Ziliujing Formation. The provincial lithostratigraphy of Sichuan (Gu and Liu 1997) adopts it as the Zhenzhuchong Member, the second of five members of the Ziliujing above the Qijiang Member, and does not adopt the "Zhenzhuchong Formation" proposed in 1980–1982; the national lexicon lists it only as a member, and later syntheses follow the provincial scheme. Formation rank is the 1978–1982 usage. Governs `rank: member` and `parent: Ziliujing` on [Zhenzhuchong](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhenzhuchong.yml), unchanged, and `member: Zhenzhuchong` with `formation: Ziliujing` on [*Qianlong shouhu*](../../../genera/Q/Qianlong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gu1997a, p. 390, Appendix II (units adopted in Sichuan), entry 323: "Zhenzhuchong Mem 珍珠冲段", code 51-4118, credited to Tan and Li, 1933; entry 330: "Ziliujing Fm 自流井组", A. Heim, 1930. The name is absent from Appendix III, the units not adopted (pp. 391–417).
- gu1997a, pp. 187–188: on fossil evidence, a 1980–1982 revision "named the former 'Zhenzhuchong Formation', divided into a lower member (Qijiang Member) and an upper member (Zhenzhuchong Member)" and restricted the Ziliujing to the Dongyuemiao, Ma'anshan and Da'anzhai members (translated); the volume's current definition instead divides the Ziliujing into "five lithological members, from bottom to top", the Qijiang, Zhenzhuchong (51-4118), Dongyuemiao, Ma'anshan and Da'anzhai (translated).
- zhang2009b: the lexicon's only entry for the unit is the "Chenchuchong Member", type locality "Zhenzhuchong (Chenchuchong) located 0.5 km north of Ziliujing, Zigong City, Sichuan Province".
- huang2018a: "According to the suggestion of 'Lithostratigraphy of Sichuan Province,' the present paper adopts the scheme of incorporating the Zhenzhuchong Formation into the Ziliujing Formation"; huang2021a: "the Ziliujing Formation (which includes the Zhenzhuchong Formation)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Formation rank.* Dong and colleagues (1983) record the 1978 Sichuan symposium's agreement "that this unit should be provided formational status" (dong1983a), and later papers list a Zhenzhuchong Formation below the Ziliujing (yao2025a, p. e1; wang2019c; xing2015a; lucas2001b), but that is the scheme the 1997 provincial revision set aside.

**Open.** Guizhou usage. *Qianlong* comes from Pingba, Guizhou, where Han and colleagues (2024) and Wang and colleagues (2025) use the Sichuan member name; the Guizhou provincial lithostratigraphy has not been read.
