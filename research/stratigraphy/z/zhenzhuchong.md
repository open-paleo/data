# Zhenzhuchong

## Is the Zhenzhuchong a member of the Ziliujing Formation, or a formation below it?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** A member of the Ziliujing Formation. The provincial lithostratigraphy of Sichuan (Gu and Liu 1997) adopts it as the Zhenzhuchong Member, the second of five members of the Ziliujing above the Qijiang Member, and does not adopt the "Zhenzhuchong Formation" proposed in 1980–1982; the national lexicon lists it only as a member, and later syntheses follow the provincial scheme. Formation rank is the 1978–1982 usage. Governs `rank: member` and `parent: Ziliujing` on [Zhenzhuchong](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhenzhuchong.yml), unchanged, and `member: Zhenzhuchong` with `formation: Ziliujing` on [*Qianlong shouhu*](../../../genera/Q/Qianlong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gu1997a, p. 390, Appendix II (units adopted in Sichuan), entry 323: "Zhenzhuchong Mem 珍珠冲段", code 51-4118, credited to Tan and Li, 1933; entry 330: "Ziliujing Fm 自流井组", A. Heim, 1930. The name is absent from Appendix III, the units not adopted (pp. 391–417).
- gu1997a, pp. 187–188: on fossil evidence, a 1980–1982 revision "named the former 'Zhenzhuchong Formation', divided into a lower member (Qijiang Member) and an upper member (Zhenzhuchong Member)" and restricted the Ziliujing to the Dongyuemiao, Ma'anshan and Da'anzhai members (translated); the volume's current definition instead divides the Ziliujing into "five lithological members, from bottom to top", the Qijiang, Zhenzhuchong (51-4118), Dongyuemiao, Ma'anshan and Da'anzhai (translated).
- zhang2009b: the lexicon's only entry for the unit is the "Chenchuchong Member", type locality "Zhenzhuchong (Chenchuchong) located 0.5 km north of Ziliujing, Zigong City, Sichuan Province".
- huang2018a: "According to the suggestion of 'Lithostratigraphy of Sichuan Province,' the present paper adopts the scheme of incorporating the Zhenzhuchong Formation into the Ziliujing Formation"; huang2021a: "the Ziliujing Formation (which includes the Zhenzhuchong Formation)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Formation rank.* Dong and colleagues (1983) record the 1978 Sichuan symposium's agreement "that this unit should be provided formational status" (dong1983a), and later papers list a Zhenzhuchong Formation below the Ziliujing (yao2025a, p. e1; wang2019c; xing2015a; lucas2001b), but that is the scheme the 1997 provincial revision set aside.

**Open.** Guizhou usage. *Qianlong* comes from Pingba, Guizhou, where Han and colleagues (2024) and Wang and colleagues (2025) use the Sichuan member name; the Guizhou provincial lithostratigraphy has not been read.
