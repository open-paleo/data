# Black Creek

## Does the Black Creek's Campanian rest on its own reading?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Gallagher (1993) gives the formation a late Campanian age because the zone fossil *Exogyra ponderosa* occurs in it; the sentence names three units, but it is a zone age that holds for each, not a span across several. Baird and Horner's (1979) "Tayloran, i.e. Campanian" converts Brett and Wheeler's (1961) relayed "Taylor time" and agrees. Governs the Campanian in `stages` on [Black Creek](../../../stratigraphy/b/black-creek.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- gallagher1993a, p. 86: "E. ponderosa is a zone fossil for the Marshalltown Formation in New Jersey, the Black Creek Formation of North Carolina, and the Taylor Formation of Texas; these units are late Campanian in age."

**Ruled out.**
- *A joint age for three units.* A zone fossil dates each unit it occurs in.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does any published source place the Black Creek in the Maastrichtian?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** No; the Maastrichtian is dropped. It rests only on Brett-Surman (1989), a doctoral thesis, and a thesis never sole-sources an age. Every published work read dates the formation Campanian: Stephenson (1914) places its marine beds in the *Exogyra ponderosa* zone and the *Exogyra costata* zone in the overlying Peedee sand, Gallagher (1993) gives the *E. ponderosa* units as late Campanian, and Baird and Horner (1979) give the formation and its Phoebus Landing assemblage as Campanian. Governs `stages: [Campanian]` on [Black Creek](../../../stratigraphy/b/black-creek.yml); [*Hypsibema crassicauda*](../../../genera/H/Hypsibema.yml), Campanian, is unaffected.

**Evidence.**
- gallagher1993a, p. 86: "*E. ponderosa* is a zone fossil for the Marshalltown Formation in New Jersey, the Black Creek Formation of North Carolina, and the Taylor Formation of Texas; these units are late Campanian in age."
- stephenson1914a, p. 31: "In the Carolinas the vertical ranges of typical specimens of *Exogyra ponderosa* and *Exogyra costata* overlap little if any".
- baird1979a, p. 10: "the Phoebus Landing assemblage is Campanian in age".
- brett-surman1989a, p. 144: "Black Creek Fm, Campanian-Maastrichtian", in a doctoral thesis.

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian, from Brett-Surman (1989).* A thesis, and no published source repeats it.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Black Creek Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian for the dinosaur-bearing beds of Sampson and Bladen counties. Baird and Horner (1979) take Brett and Wheeler's (1961) reading that, upstream and updip from Elizabethtown, the formation was deposited in Taylor time, the zone of *Exogyra ponderosa*, and equate Tayloran with the Campanian. Governs the Campanian in `stages` on [Black Creek](../../../stratigraphy/b/black-creek.yml).

**Evidence.**
- baird1979a, p. 1: "Late Cretaceous dinosaurs from the Black Creek Formation (Campanian) of Sampson and Bladen counties".
- baird1979a, p. 6, quoting Brett and Wheeler (1961, p. 102): "Upstream and updip from Elizabethtown the Black Creek Formation was, without doubt, deposited during Taylor time represented by the Exogyra ponderosa zone (or older near the base)".
- baird1979a, p. 6: "Thus the dinosaurs discussed in this report are Tayloran, i.e. Campanian, late Cretaceous, in age."

**Ruled out.**
- *A stage older than the Campanian at the base of the formation.* Brett and Wheeler's "or older near the base" names no older zone or stage, and Baird and Horner draw only the Campanian from the passage.

**Open.** Whether the Maastrichtian stands. Among the works cited, only Brett-Surman (1989), a doctoral thesis, gives the formation as "Campanian-Maastrichtian". A source that dates the top of the formation would settle it.
