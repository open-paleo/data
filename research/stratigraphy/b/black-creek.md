# Black Creek

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Black Creek Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian for the dinosaur-bearing beds of Sampson and Bladen counties. Baird and Horner (1979) take Brett and Wheeler's (1961) reading that, upstream and updip from Elizabethtown, the formation was deposited in Taylor time, the zone of *Exogyra ponderosa*, and equate Tayloran with the Campanian. Governs the Campanian in `stages` on [Black Creek](../../../stratigraphy/b/black-creek.yml).

**Evidence.**
- baird1979a, p. 1: "Late Cretaceous dinosaurs from the Black Creek Formation (Campanian) of Sampson and Bladen counties".
- baird1979a, p. 6, quoting Brett and Wheeler (1961, p. 102): "Upstream and updip from Elizabethtown the Black Creek Formation was, without doubt, deposited during Taylor time represented by the Exogyra ponderosa zone (or older near the base)".
- baird1979a, p. 6: "Thus the dinosaurs discussed in this report are Tayloran, i.e. Campanian, late Cretaceous, in age."

**Ruled out.**
- *A stage older than the Campanian at the base of the formation.* Brett and Wheeler's "or older near the base" names no older zone or stage, and Baird and Horner draw only the Campanian from the passage.

**Open.** Whether the Maastrichtian stands. Among the works cited, only Brett-Surman (1989), a doctoral thesis, gives the formation as "Campanian-Maastrichtian". A source that dates the top of the formation would settle it.
