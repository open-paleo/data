# Bayin-Gobi

## How is the formation's name spelled, and is it Aptian or Albian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The name appears as Bayan Gobi, Bayin Gobi, Bayin-Gobi, Bayingobi and Bayingebi; all name one unit, whose type area is near Elesitai, Alxa, Inner Mongolia. The age is open between Aptian and Albian. Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` and the `variants` on Bayin-Gobi.

**Evidence.**
- russell1993b, p. 2108: the type area is "(approximately 106°5′E, 41°10′N) … centred 1 km west of the abandoned village of Elesitai".
- Spellings: "Bayan Gobi" in godefroit2005a (p. 697) and sereno2010a (p. 26); "Bayin Gobi" in zanno2010a (p. 516); "Bayingobi" in xu2018c (p. 2855); "Bayingebi" in xu2018a (in-press version).
- Albian, primary: Russell and Dong (1993) give "lacustrine strata of Albian age" (russell1993b, p. 2107), and Godefroit and colleagues (2005) give "Bayan Gobi Formation (Albian, Lower Cretaceous)" (godefroit2005a, p. 697).
- Aptian, primary: "Lower Cretaceous (Aptian) Bayingobi Formation" (xu2018c, p. 2855); "Early Cretaceous (early Aptian)" (xu2018a, in-press version); "Bayan Gobi Formation (Aptian)" (sereno2010a, p. 26); "Aptian, ca. 115 Ma" (sereno2010b, p. 199).

**Ruled out.**
- *A count of compilations as a vote.* Most later Albian assignments relay Godefroit and colleagues (2005): Norman (2015) "dated as Albian (Godefroit, Li & Shang, 2005)", and Wu and Godefroit (2012, wu2012a, p. 293). Zanno (2010) gives "Albian (Jerzykiewicz & Russell 1991)" (zanno2010a, p. 516) and "(Aptian/Albian)" on p. 535 of the same paper. Fowler and colleagues' Aptian is "(Bayingobi Formation; Aptian; Xu et al., 2018)" (fowler2020b, accepted manuscript, p. 4). The question is between the primaries above.
- *"Bayangobi."* No source read for this note uses it.

**Open.** Aptian or Albian. Neither side presents dating of its own beyond biostratigraphic comparison. A third reading, "早白垩世瓦兰今期—欧特里夫期" ("Early Cretaceous Valanginian–Hauterivian", translated; xi2021a, p. 391), rests on zircon dates from volcanics reported by earlier work, and was not examined.
