# Bissekty

## Is the "Taikarshin member" a member of the Bissekty Formation?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** No. It is the earlier informal name of the Bissekty Formation itself. Nessov and colleagues (1989) place the *Turanoceratops* holotype in the "lower part of Taikarshin member" at locality TsBI-27. Nessov (1995) gives the same specimen as from the "lower (basal) part of the Bissekty Formation, site CBI-27", and says the formal name Bissekty replaced the informal "Taikarshi bed". The "lower part" carries over as a position in the formation. The member name does not. Governs the absence of `location.member`, and `location.part: lower`, on [*Turanoceratops tardabilis*](../../../genera/T/Turanoceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nessov1989a, p. 149 (translated): "Holotype. No. 251/12457, left maxilla ... Upper Cretaceous, upper Turonian, lower part of Taikarshin member, locality TsBI-27."
- nessov1995a, p. 55: "CCMGE No. 251/12457, left maxilla ... Upper Cretaceous, upper Turonian, lower (basal) part of the Bissekty Formation, site CBI-27."
- nessov1995a, p. 95: Dzharakuduk II, "Base of the Bissekty Formation (the given formal name replaced the former informal designation Taikarshi bed; Nessov, 1990)".
- nessov1995a, p. 149, Plate VII: the holotype maxilla from "site CBI-27 (level 1 m above the base of the Bissekty Formation)".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Taikarshin Member within the Bissekty.* The name is not a subdivision. Nessov (1995) uses Taikarshi bed and Bissekty Formation for the same body of rock.

**Open.** Nothing.
