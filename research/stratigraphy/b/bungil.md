# Bungil

## Where does the Bungil's base lie?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** In the Hauterivian; the formation is Hauterivian to Aptian. Supersedes the 2026-11-25 entry. Exon (1976) dates the formation "Neocomian to early Aptian" on fossil evidence and has the sea "first" enter the basin "in late Neocomian time, when the paralic sediments of the Bungil Formation were laid down"; the Neocomian is the Berriasian, Valanginian and Hauterivian (Gale and colleagues 2020), and its late part is read here as the Hauterivian. Day's appendix in the same bulletin dates the Nullawurt Member, by his own fossils, only "possible Neocomian". No work read gives the Bungil a Valanginian. Governs `stages: [Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian]` and the note on exon1976a on [Bungil](../../../stratigraphy/b/bungil.yml); [*Minmi paravertebra*](../../../genera/M/Minmi.yml), Aptian, is unaffected.

**Evidence.**
- exon1976a, p. 115: "The age of the formation is thus, on fossil evidence, Neocomian to early Aptian."
- exon1976a, p. 43: "The Cretaceous sea first entered the basin in late Neocomian time, when the paralic sediments of the Bungil Formation were laid down."
- exon1976a, p. 140: "A MARINE FAUNA OF POSSIBLE NEOCOMIAN AGE FROM THE NULLAWURT SANDSTONE MEMBER".

**Ruled out.**
- *Valanginian.* No reading gives it.

**Open.** Burger's (1974) spores from the Kingull Member, not held, would date the base directly.

## Does Scheibner and Basden (1998) put the Bungil's base in the Hauterivian?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** In its table, not in its chart; the Valanginian stays. Answers the 2026-11-23 entry's Open line. Scheibner and Basden (1998) give the formation "Hauterivian-Aptian" in their table, but their correlation chart (Table 22.1), adapted from earlier work, draws the base within the Valanginian. With Leahey and colleagues' (2015) "Valanginian–Barremian" and Exon's (1976) Neocomian, which takes in the Valanginian, the base keeps the Valanginian under the union rule. Governs `stages: [Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian]` and the note on scheibner1998a on [Bungil](../../../stratigraphy/b/bungil.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- scheibner1998a, Table A1.12, pp. 546–547: Bungil Formation, "Spores; marine macrofauna in Queensland", "Hauterivian-Aptian".
- scheibner1998a, Table 22.1, pp. 296–297: the base of the Bungil box falls within the Valanginian row.
- leahey2015a, p. 3: "Minmi Member, Bungil Formation (Valanginian–Barremian)".
- exon1976a, p. 115: "The age of the formation is thus, on fossil evidence, Neocomian to early Aptian."

**Ruled out.**
- *A Hauterivian base, from Scheibner and Basden's table.* Their own chart draws the base lower, and the other readings stand.

**Open.** A source that dates the base of the formation directly, such as the Kingull Member's spores, remains unread.

## Does the Bungil Formation reach the Berriasian?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** No; the formation runs from the Valanginian to the Aptian. Exon (1976) dates it "Neocomian to early Aptian" on fossil evidence, and the Neocomian is the Berriasian, Valanginian and Hauterivian (Gale and colleagues, 2020). Exon places the formation conformably on the Mooga Sandstone and Southlands Formation, and has the sea first enter the basin in late Neocomian time, when the Bungil was laid down. None of the works cited places the formation as low as the Berriasian; the lowest stage any of them gives it is the Valanginian, in the Valanginian–Barremian of Leahey and colleagues (2015) and Agnolín and colleagues (2010). Governs `stages: [Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian]` on [Bungil](../../../stratigraphy/b/bungil.yml).

**Evidence.**
- exon1976a, p. 43: "The Cretaceous sea first entered the basin in late Neocomian time, when the paralic sediments of the Bungil Formation were laid down."
- exon1976a, p. 113: "The Bungil Formation rests conformably on the Mooga Sandstone and Southlands Formation."
- exon1976a, p. 115: "The age of the formation is thus, on fossil evidence, Neocomian to early Aptian."
- gale2020a, p. 1024: the Neocomian "has subsequently been subdivided into three stages—the Berriasian, Valanginian and Hauterivian".
- leahey2015a, p. 3, Fig. 1: "Minmi Member, Bungil Formation (Valanginian–Barremian)".
- agnolín2010a, p. 263: "Bungil Formation (Barremian-Valanginian: Early Cretaceous; Scheibner & Basden 1998)".
- scheibner1998a, Table A1.12, pp. 546–547: the Bungil Formation is "Hauterivian-Aptian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Berriasian, from the whole Neocomian.* Converting Exon's "Neocomian" stage by stage would add the Berriasian, but Exon puts the Bungil's marine onset in the late Neocomian, and no work cited places the formation in the Berriasian.

**Open.** Whether the Valanginian stands. Leahey and colleagues (2015) give no source for their range, and Agnolín and colleagues (2010) attribute theirs to Scheibner and Basden (1998), whose Jurassic–Cretaceous table gives the formation as Hauterivian–Aptian. Exon's "late Neocomian" would fit a Hauterivian base as well. A source that dates the base of the formation, such as the Kingull Member's spores (Burger, 1974), would settle it.
