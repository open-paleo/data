# Brushy Basin

## Does Gillette (1991) date the Brushy Basin Member Early Cretaceous?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No. The member is Kimmeridgian–Tithonian. Governs `period: [Late Jurassic]` and `stages: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` on [Brushy Basin](../../../stratigraphy/b/brushy-basin.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gillette1991a, p. 418, Age and Horizon: "Upper part of the Brushy Basin Member of the Morrison Formation", followed by "radiometric dates for the upper part of the Morrison Formation elsewhere … and stratigraphic evidence (Bowman, 1986) indicate that at least the upper part of the formation may be wholly or partially Early Cretaceous in age. Della Valle (1980) established a minimum sedimentation age for the Brushy Basin Member of 124 ± 28 m.y."
- kirkland2016a, p. 118: "150.00 ± 0.52 Ma from near the top of the Brushy Basin Member, near little Cedar Mesa, Emery County, Utah, based on a recalibrated 40Ar/39Ar age (Trujillo and Kowallis, 2015)". The date is reported, not measured by the paper; it falls in the Kimmeridgian on the current chart (Tithonian base 149.2 Ma).

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Cretaceous (Gillette 1991).* The sentence concerns "the upper part of the Morrison Formation elsewhere", rests on dates the paper relays, and states a possibility ("may be wholly or partially"). The one figure for the member itself is a minimum relayed from Della Valle (1980), and a minimum of 124 ± 28 m.y. says the beds are at least that old, which a Jurassic age satisfies. The title's "(Upper Jurassic/Lower Cretaceous)" labels the formation, not the member.

**Open.** Nothing.
