# Balde de Leyes

## How do Colombi and colleagues date the Balde de Leyes Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Early Jurassic, with no stage, by correlation through its one vertebrate. Colombi and colleagues (2015) split the formation from the top of the Quebrada del Barro Formation and date the two apart on the position of their vertebrates: the Quebrada del Barro is Late Triassic and the new unit Early Jurassic. The Early Jurassic comes from correlating the Balde de Leyes with the Cañón del Colorado Formation of the Mogna depocenter and the Upper Elliot Formation of the Karoo Basin, because its basal sauropodomorph, *Leyesaurus*, is close to *Adeopapposaurus* and *Massospondylus* from those units. The correlation gives a series only, and Martínez and Apaldetti (2017) also give only "Lower Jurassic". Governs `period: [Early Jurassic]` and the absence of `stages` on [Balde de Leyes](../../../stratigraphy/b/balde-de-leyes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- colombi2015a, p. 445, Resumen: "The new stratigraphic unit is defined as the Balde de Leyes Formation, a name assigned due to its proximity to this locality" (translated).
- colombi2015a, p. 447: "in this new stratigraphic scheme the age of the Quebrada del Barro Formation was defined as Late Triassic and that of the new unit as Early Jurassic, based on the precise stratigraphic position of the vertebrates found" (translated).
- colombi2015a, p. 450: "The age of this unit is assigned to the Early Jurassic due to the presence of basal sauropodomorphs close to the genera *Adeopapposaurus* (Cañón del Colorado Formation, Mogna depocenter …) and *Massopondylus* (Upper Elliot Formation, Karoo basin, South Africa …)" (translated).
- colombi2015a, p. 453: "the Balde de Leyes Formation is assigned to the Early Jurassic by its correlation with the Cañón del Colorado Formation of the Mogna depocenter and the Upper Elliot Formation of the Karoo basin, South Africa" (translated).

**Ruled out.** Nothing beyond the Rhaetian reading for the *Leyesaurus* beds that the 2026-10-03 entry in [*Leyesaurus*](../../genera/L/Leyesaurus.md) rules out.

**Open.** The Early Jurassic rests on the affinities of *Leyesaurus*, the only vertebrate the formation has yielded, as the 2026-10-03 entry in [*Leyesaurus*](../../genera/L/Leyesaurus.md) notes. A stage would need an independent age, such as a radiometric date or a second fauna.
