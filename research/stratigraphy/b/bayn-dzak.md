# Bayn Dzak

## Is the Bayn Dzak Member a named unit, and is all Flaming Cliffs material in it?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes to both, for the Flaming Cliffs escarpment itself. Dashzeveg and colleagues (2005) divide the [Djadokhta Formation](../../../stratigraphy/d/djadokhta.yml) into a lower Bayn Dzak Member, typified at the Flaming Cliffs, and an upper Tugrugyin Member, and the Flaming Cliffs section is Bayn Dzak Member only. Ukhaa Tolgod is also referred to the member. Governs `location.member: Bayn Dzak` on Flaming Cliffs (Bayan Zag, Shabarakh Usu) and Ukhaa Tolgod species, such as [*Velociraptor mongoliensis*](../../../genera/V/Velociraptor.yml), [*Protoceratops andrewsi*](../../../genera/P/Protoceratops.yml) and [*Citipati osmolskae*](../../../genera/C/Citipati.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dashzeveg2005a, p. 1: "a lower Bayn Dzak Member … and an upper Tugrugyin Member"; p. 3: "The type section was recorded at the Flaming Cliffs (Gradziński et al., 1969: fig. 30)"; p. 6: "The upper part of the Bayn Dzak Member at the Flaming Cliffs has been eroded. Quaternary pediment sits atop the described section."
- dingus2008a, p. 2: "the exposures at Ukhaa Tolgod are referred to the Bayn Dzak Member of the Djadokhta Formation"; cau2017a, p. 395: "Bayn Dzak Member, Djadokhta Formation (Campanian, ~75–71 Mya), Ukhaa Tolgod".
- benton2000c, p. xxiv: "Flaming Cliffs (= Bayan Zag)", "Bayn Dzak (= Bayan Zag)"; p. xxvi: "Shabarakh Usu (= Bayan Zag)". The locality names are synonyms; the stratigraphic names are listed separately.
- Later users of the member: turner2021a, p. 40; arbour2015a, p. 26 (online-first).

**Ruled out.**
- *Tugrugyin Member for Flaming Cliffs material.* The Tugrugyin Member is not exposed in the Flaming Cliffs section, whose top is eroded. It crops out nearby, at Tsonzh, "about 10 km to the southeast of the Flaming Cliffs", where "Both the Bayn Dzak and Tugrugyin Members are exposed" (dashzeveg2005a, p. 18), so material labeled only "Bayan Zag" from the wider area needs its exact site.

**Open.** Specimen-level assignments. Barsbold and Osmólska (1999) list the *Velociraptor* holotype from "Djadokhta Formation (?early Campanian), Bayn Dzak" and several referred specimens (GIN 100/25, GIN 100/24, GIN 100/2000, PIN 3143/8) from "Tugrikin-Shire" (barsbold1999a, p. 191); placing the latter in the Tugrugyin Member is an inference from Dashzeveg and colleagues' description of Tugrugyin Shireh (p. 22). ZPAL MgD-I/97 is from the Barun Goyot Formation (barsbold1999a, p. 191).
