# Bulldog Shale

## What do the cited works say about the definition, palynozones and opal beds of the Bulldog Shale?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Aptian and Albian, unchanged, in the Marree Subgroup. Alexander and colleagues (2007) give Freytag's (1966) definition, the dark grey shale between the Cadna-owie Formation and the Coorikiana Sandstone, with its type section 8 km south of Bulldog Creek, a conformable lower contact in South Australia, and the Wallumbilla Formation as its lateral equivalent, the name in use depending on whether the Toolebuc Formation is present. They date it "Early Aptian – mid Albian" on the *Cyclosporites hughesii* to *Coptospora paradoxa* palynozones; the zone codes printed beside that label, "PK2.1–PK3.1", repeat the codes they give the Cadna-owie Formation and disagree with their own chart, which puts *C. hughesii* in PK3 and *C. paradoxa* in PK5, as they give the Wallumbilla (PK3–PK5). The codes are a slip; the stage label and zone names stand. Burger (1980) dates zones, not the formation: the *C. hughesii* Subzone, whose reference section is in the Bulldog Shale and whose middle and upper parts equal a zone "considered to represent most of the Aptian"; the *Crybelosporites striatus* Zone, whose reference interval "lies within the Bulldog Shale", early Albian; and the *C. paradoxa* Zone, "(middle?) Albian", in the upper Bulldog Shale and basal Oodnadatta Formation. Those ages come from the zones' association with dated members in the Surat and Eromanga basins. Barrett and colleagues (2010) place the Coober Pedy and Andamooka opal beds in the Bulldog Shale, within the Marree Subgroup, formerly the Marree Formation, where earlier lists had also offered the Cadna-owie or Marree Formation; they relay an Aptian–lower Albian microfossil age for the formation and an Aptian molluscan age for the opal beds. Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` and `parent: Marree` on [Bulldog Shale](../../../stratigraphy/b/bulldog-shale.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- alexander2007a, p. 21: "Bulldog Shale was defined by Freytag (1966) as the dark grey shale between Cadna-owie Formation and Coorikiana Sandstone"; "The type section is outcrop 8 km south of Bulldog Creek (Freytag, 1966)"; the lower boundary "appears conformable in South Australia"; "The upper contact with the Coorikiana Sandstone is conformable. Wallumbilla Formation is a lateral equivalent."
- alexander2007a, p. 22: "The Bulldog Shale is assigned to the Early Aptian – mid Albian PK2.1–PK3.1 and *Cyclosporites hughesii – Coptospora paradoxa* palynozones".
- alexander2007a, p. 20: the Cadna-owie Formation corresponds "to the PK2.1– PK3.1 and *Foraminisporis wonthaggiensis – Cyclosporites hughesii* palynozones"; p. 24: the Wallumbilla Formation corresponds "to the PK3–PK5 and *Cyclosporites hughesii – Coptospora paradoxa* palynozones", and its "Distribution ... is by definition controlled by Toolebuc Formation — where absent, Bulldog Shale is used"; Fig. 5.24 sets *Cyclosporites hughesii* against PK3, *Crybelosporites striatus* against PK4 and *Coptospora paradoxa* against PK5.
- burger1980a, p. 11: the *Cyclosporites hughesii* Subzone's reference section is "Santos Oodnadatta No. 1 Well, Bulldog Shale, 728-964 feet", "of which the middle and upper intervals are equivalent to the *Osmundacidites dubius* Zone"; "The Minmi Member, dated as lower Aptian, is restricted to the Surat Basin. The Doncaster Member ... contains the well-known late Aptian 'Roma' faunas"; "the *Osmundacidites dubius* Zone is considered to represent most of the Aptian"; the *Crybelosporites striatus* Zone's reference interval, 642–700 feet in the same well, "lies within the Bulldog Shale".
- burger1980a, p. 14: "the available evidence points to an early Albian age for the *Crybelosporites striatus* Zone".
- burger1980a, p. 15: the *Coptospora paradoxa* Zone's reference interval "includes the upper part of the Bulldog Shale and the basal part of the Oodnadatta Formation"; "The association of the *Coptospora paradoxa* Zone with the upper Coreena Member in the western Surat Basin confirms the foraminiferal evidence and dates the zone as of (middle?) Albian age".
- barrett2010b, p. 293, title: "Opalized archosaur remains from the Bulldog Shale (Aptian: Lower Cretaceous) of South Australia".
- barrett2010b, p. 294: earlier work listed "the Cadna-owie Formation, the Bulldog Shale and the Marree Formation" as "potential opal-bearing units", and most later workers followed a Marree Formation designation for *Kakuru*; "Detailed lithostratigraphic evaluation has now firmly placed the Coober Pedy and Andamooka opaliferous beds within the Bulldog Shale (a constituent of the Marree Subgroup, which was formerly called the Marree Formation"; "Microfossils suggest an Aptian–lower Albian age for the Bulldog Shale ... however, an Aptian age is usually proposed for the opal-bearing strata on the basis of molluscan remains".

**Ruled out.**
- *PK2.1–PK3.1 as the formation's palynozones.* The codes are the Cadna-owie Formation's, repeated; the same chapter's chart and its entry for the Wallumbilla put the named zones in PK3–PK5. A slip that the document itself contradicts is not a reading, and no record value depends on the codes.
- *The Cadna-owie Formation or the Marree Formation as the source of the Coober Pedy and Andamooka opal beds.* Offered in earlier lists and followed for *Kakuru*; Barrett and colleagues (2010) report that lithostratigraphic work places the beds in the Bulldog Shale, and the former Marree Formation is now the subgroup that contains it.

**Open.** Nothing new; the 2026-10-22 entry names what would settle the base of the formation.

## Does Burger's "Barremian to Aptian" *Odontochitina operculata* Zone put the Bulldog Shale in the Barremian?

*2026-10-22*

**Conclusion.** No. Burger (1980) gives the formation no age of its own; the Barremian belongs to the age of a dinoflagellate zone whose standard reference lies in the Bulldog Shale, and Burger dates that zone from the Minmi and Doncaster members of the Surat and Eromanga basins, not from the Bulldog Shale. Alexander and colleagues (2007) list the same zone among the formation's and date the formation "Early Aptian – mid Albian". Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` on [Bulldog Shale](../../../stratigraphy/b/bulldog-shale.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- burger1980a, p. 23: the zone's standard reference is "Santos Oodnadatta 1 well, South Australia, 286.5-232.3 m interval, as designated by Morgan (1977). This interval lies within the Bulldog Shale"; "On the basis of the age established for the Minmi Member and Doncaster Member, and the evidence for individual samples discussed on page 11, the *Odontochitina operculata* Zone is regarded as of Barremian to Aptian age."
- alexander2007a, p. 22: "The Bulldog Shale is assigned to the Early Aptian – mid Albian PK2.1–PK3.1 and *Cyclosporites hughesii – Coptospora paradoxa* palynozones", with the *Odontochitina operculata* dinoflagellate zone among those it names.

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian for the Bulldog Shale.* The range is the zone's, set by correlation with units in other basins, and the zone's reference interval in the formation is placed in the Aptian–Albian by the later regional synthesis. A zone's full range applied to the part of it that a formation holds is the kind of borrowed range CONTRIBUTING does not let date a unit.

**Open.** Whether the lowest Bulldog Shale reaches below the Aptian anywhere in the basin. A dated palynological sample from the formation's base would settle it.
