# Bulldog Shale

## Does Burger's "Barremian to Aptian" *Odontochitina operculata* Zone put the Bulldog Shale in the Barremian?

*2026-10-22*

**Conclusion.** No. Burger (1980) gives the formation no age of its own; the Barremian belongs to the age of a dinoflagellate zone whose standard reference lies in the Bulldog Shale, and Burger dates that zone from the Minmi and Doncaster members of the Surat and Eromanga basins, not from the Bulldog Shale. Alexander and colleagues (2007) list the same zone among the formation's and date the formation "Early Aptian – mid Albian". Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` on [Bulldog Shale](../../../stratigraphy/b/bulldog-shale.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- burger1980a, p. 23: the zone's standard reference is "Santos Oodnadatta 1 well, South Australia, 286.5-232.3 m interval, as designated by Morgan (1977). This interval lies within the Bulldog Shale"; "On the basis of the age established for the Minmi Member and Doncaster Member, and the evidence for individual samples discussed on page 11, the *Odontochitina operculata* Zone is regarded as of Barremian to Aptian age."
- alexander2007a, p. 22: "The Bulldog Shale is assigned to the Early Aptian – mid Albian PK2.1–PK3.1 and *Cyclosporites hughesii – Coptospora paradoxa* palynozones", with the *Odontochitina operculata* dinoflagellate zone among those it names.

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian for the Bulldog Shale.* The range is the zone's, set by correlation with units in other basins, and the zone's reference interval in the formation is placed in the Aptian–Albian by the later regional synthesis. A zone's full range applied to the part of it that a formation holds is the kind of borrowed range CONTRIBUTING does not let date a unit.

**Open.** Whether the lowest Bulldog Shale reaches below the Aptian anywhere in the basin. A dated palynological sample from the formation's base would settle it.
