# Bauru

## What do the cited works say about the makeup and age of the Bauru Group?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** The group excludes the Caiuá, and takes no Early Cretaceous stage. Fernandes (1992) and Fernandes and Coimbra (1994) separate the Caiuá as a group of its own, and Fernandes and Coimbra (2000), Soares and colleagues (2020), Iori and colleagues (2021) and Arai and Dias-Brito (2023) keep it apart. Brum and colleagues (2021) follow Soares and colleagues (1980) as updated by Pinheiro and colleagues (2018) and put an Aptian aeolian interval inside the group; in the two-group scheme those sandstones are the Caiuá's, and the Aptian is recorded there. The Coniacian–Maastrichtian that Soares and colleagues (2020) and Iori and colleagues (2021) give goes back to Fernandes and Coimbra (2000), who state it for the filling of the basin, Caiuá included, so it counts once and does not bound the group. The group's Turonian–Maastrichtian is the union of its formations' ranges, the Turonian coming from the [Adamantina](../../../stratigraphy/a/adamantina.yml). Governs `period: [Late Cretaceous]` and the absence of the Aptian from `stages` on [Bauru](../../../stratigraphy/b/bauru.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- soares2020a, p. 952: "Fernandes (1992) and Fernandes and Coimbra (1994) reorganized the previous stratigraphic model of the Bauru Basin and further separated the basin succession into two groups: the Caiuá Group and the Bauru Group"; "The Bauru Group is formed by the vertically superposed Araçatuba, Uberaba, Adamantina, and Marília formations"; "The basin evolved from the Coniacian to the Maastrichtian, based on biostratigraphic information (Dias-Brito et al., 2001) and absolute dating of igneous intrusions close to the margins of the basin".
- iori2021a, p. 2, 2. Geological setting: "The Bauru Basin is composed by Caiuá and Bauru groups"; "The deposition of the Bauru Group occurred under semi-arid to arid climatic conditions, between the Coniacian and Maastrichtian ages (Fernandes and Coimbra, 2000)"; "The exact age of the formations included within the Bauru Group is not consensual, however in relation to Marília Formation there seems to be an agreement".
- iori2021a, p. 10, 6. Discussion: "The hierarchical organization of the units of the Bauru Group is not yet consensual".
- fernandes2000a, p. 717: "The filling of the Bauru Basin occurred in a semi-arid to arid climate, between the Coniacian and the Maastrichtian (Ks)" (translated).
- brum2021a, p. 2076, Geological setting: "The Bauru Group consists of two distinct intervals (Pinheiro et al. 2018)"; "The first interval is formed by aeolian to fluvial–aeolian sandstones of the Aptian, and the second by alluvial and fluvial conglomerates, sandstones and mudstones dating from the Santonian to the Maastrichtian"; "here we follow the main lithostratigraphic proposal of Soares et al. (1980) with updates by Pinheiro et al. (2018), with eight recognized formations".
- brum2021a, p. 2094, Distribution of the unenlagiines: "The Bauru Group was surrounded by the Caiua desert, which is primarily Aptian (sensu Pinheiro et al. 2018)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian in the group's stages, from Brum and colleagues (2021).* Their Aptian interval is the aeolian sandstone that the two-group scheme places in the Caiuá Group, which carries the Aptian. The paper itself uses both circumscriptions: the Aptian sandstones sit inside the group in its Geological setting and in a desert surrounding the group in its discussion of unenlagiine distribution.
- *Coniacian–Maastrichtian as the group's own age.* Soares and colleagues (2020) state it for the basin, and Iori and colleagues (2021) relay it for the group from Fernandes and Coimbra (2000), who state it for the basin's filling; the basin also holds the Caiuá.

**Open.** Nothing.
