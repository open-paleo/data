# Bayan Mandahu

## Is "Bayan Mandahu Formation" a defined unit, and is it the Ulansuhai?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** It is an informal name, used since at least 1999 for the red beds at Bayan Mandahu without a published definition. Whether those beds belong to the Ulansuhai (Wulansuhai) Formation, typified about 300 km to the west-southwest in Alxa, is not established. The registry keeps the two as separate units. Governs `informal: true` on [Bayan Mandahu](../../../stratigraphy/b/bayan-mandahu.yml), the notes on it and on [Ulansuhai](../../../stratigraphy/u/ulansuhai.yml), and `location.formation: Bayan Mandahu` on the records from Bayan Mandahu, such as [*Machairasaurus leptonychus*](../../../genera/M/Machairasaurus.yml) and [*Linheraptor exquisitus*](../../../genera/L/Linheraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- godefroit1999a, p. 20: "Stratum typicum: Bayan Mandahu Formation, correlative to the Djadokhta Formation, Campanian", the earliest use among the works read, with no type section, boundaries or thickness.
- czepiński2019a: the Bayan Mandahu strata "were considered equivalents of the Djadokhta Formation …, although sometimes are assigned to the distinct unit, Bayan Mandahu Formation".
- zhang2009b, entry "Wulansuhai Formation": type locality "Wulansuhai, north of Wuliji, Alxa Zuo Banner"; the lexicon has no entry for Bayan Mandahu.
- xu2013a, p. 97: "the Bayan Mandahu beds (the Wulansuhai Formation)". Xu and colleagues use this name for the beds from 2010 on and cite Jerzykiewicz and colleagues (1993) for the correlation and age, not for the name.

**Ruled out.**
- *The same rock as the Ulansuhai, stated as fact.* Earlier notes called Bayan Mandahu "the eastern outcrop of the unit entered as Ulansuhai". Only Hao and colleagues (2024) state it, citing a master's thesis (hao2024a, p. 2, after Tan 2016), and Evans and colleagues (2021) offer it as a hypothesis "based on proximity and lithology" (evans2021a). The compilations treat the two as distinct: Pittman and colleagues (2020) give the Ulansuhai at Suhongtu as Turonian and the Wulansuhai or Bayan Mandahu as Campanian (pittman2020a, pp. 156–158).
- *A formally defined formation.* No work read defines it.

**Open.** Whether the Bayan Mandahu beds belong to the Ulansuhai Formation. Jerzykiewicz and colleagues (1993) and Eberth (1993), on the Bayan Mandahu section, and Tan (2016) have not been read.
