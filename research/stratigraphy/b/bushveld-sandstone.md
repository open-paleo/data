# Bushveld Sandstone

## Which correlations bear on the age of the Bushveld Sandstone?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** Two published correlations, to the Elliot and to the Clarens, which lie one above the other, so both cannot hold. Neither states a stage, so the unit has none. Governs the `dispute` and the absent `stages` on [Bushveld Sandstone](../../../stratigraphy/b/bushveld-sandstone.yml).

**Evidence.**
- vanheerden1979a, p. 68: "the Bushveld Sandstone is more or less the same age as the Elliot Formation farther south".
- smith1993a, p. 160: "Biostratigraphic and lithofacies equivalents to the Clarens sandstone are present in … northern Transvaal (Bushveld sandstone) …, and a general Late Triassic to Early Jurassic age is inferred for these deposits as a whole."
- haughton1924a, p. 450: "The Stormberg Series in the Transvaal is preserved as a number of outliers, forming the Bushveld Series, of which the most important occur on the Springbok Flats".

**Ruled out.**
- *Bordy and Eriksson (2015) as a third correlation.* The paper does not name the Bushveld Sandstone. It reassigns "the upper part of the Irrigasie Formation (Worthing Member – Springbok Flats Basin)" to the Elliot Formation (bordy2015a, p. 315). Reaching the Bushveld from there needs Haughton's Springbok Flats outliers and an assumption that they are the Irrigasie's upper part, which neither paper states.
- *The ages in Galton and Upchurch's Table 12.1.* The Bushveld Sandstone appears in the rows for *Euskelosaurus browni*, "late Carnian or early Norian" across four units, and *Massospondylus carinatus*, "Hettangian-Pliensbachian" across the units of three countries (galton2004a, Table 12.1). Each is the taxon's range, not a dating of this unit ([methods](../../methods.md), on counting an edited book and its chapters).
- *The Elliot's or the Clarens's stages.* The age of a correlative does not date a unit ([CONTRIBUTING](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#age-period-and-stages)).
- *Late Triassic from White (1973).* A catalogue entry, "Upper Triassic, Stormberg Series, Bushveld Sandstone", under *Gigantoscelus*; the methods rule on reference works uses this unit as its case.

**Open.** Which correlation holds, and whether either can be put at stage resolution. Smith and colleagues' Late Triassic to Early Jurassic is stated for the Clarens equivalents "as a whole", so it is a range for several units rather than a dating of this one.
