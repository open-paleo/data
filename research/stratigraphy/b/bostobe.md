# Bostobe

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Bostobe Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Santonian to Campanian, unchanged. Nessov gives the formation Santonian–early Campanian throughout his locality catalogs (Nessov 1995, 1997) and prefers it to the earlier Turonian, late Turonian–early Santonian and late Coniacian–early Campanian datings of particular beds; Shilin and Suslov (1982) define the unit as Santonian–Campanian. Resolved by horizon, Nessov (1997) puts the lower part in the Santonian and a rich vertebrate complex from the upper half in the late Santonian–early Campanian. Norman and Kurzanov's (1997) "87-74 Ma" accompanies Shilin and Suslov's Santonian–Campanian and is not a measured age, so the stated stages are the reading; on the ICS chart v2024/12, 87 Ma falls in the Coniacian, which ends at 85.7 Ma. Governs `stages: [Santonian, Campanian]` on [Bostobe](../../../stratigraphy/b/bostobe.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nessov1997a, p. ?, catalog of localities, Kazakhstan, locality 39 (Shakh-Shakh): "Finds predominantly from the lower half of the age interval. Several other datings of the formation are known (Nessov, 1995a)" (translated).
- nessov1997a, p. ?, catalog of localities, Kazakhstan, locality 36 (Kzyl-Dzhar): "Lens of gray clays in the Bostobinskian Formation, Turonian (Zherikhin, 1978, p. 78) or, most likely, Santonian-Lower Campanian"; locality 37 (Taldysay): "Lens of clays in the Bostobinskian Formation, Upper Coniacian-Lower Campanian (Zherikhin, 1978, p. 80) or Santonian-Lower Campanian" (translated).
- nessov1997a, p. ?, catalog of localities, Kazakhstan, locality 45 (Akkurgan and Akkurgan-Boltyk): "Bostobinsky Suite, Santonian–lower Campanian" (translated).
- nessov1997a, p. ?, Subdivision of the Late Turonian–Early Campanian Interval, Correlation with other regions: "Near the Baibishe well in the upper half of the formation, a rich vertebrate complex was found, named the Baibishinskian (Late Santonian–Early Campanian)" (translated).
- nessov1997a, Table 15: remains "from the lower part of the Bostobinskaya Suite (13; Santonian) of the Baibulat locality" (translated).
- nessov1995a, p. 47: "the Bostobin Formation (Santonian - early Campanian) of the North-Eastern Aral region" (translated).
- nessov1995a, p. 105, locality 126 (Shakh-Shakh): "Bostobin Formation, but not Beleutin Formation"; "Upper Cretaceous, upper Turonian - lower Santonian (Rozhdestvensky, 1964b, 1971; Rozhdestvensky and Khozatsky, 1967), but more probably this is Santonian - lower Campanian rather than Cenomanian - Turonian (Rozhdestvensky, 1964b) or Turonian (Rozhdestvensky, 1976)" (translated).
- norman1997a, p. 192: "Shilin & Suslov (1982) allocate this locality and its beds to the Bostobinskaya Svita … on the basis of a general similarity of lithofacies with other outcrops of the Bostobinskaya Svita in this region. A Late Cretaceous age was proposed (Santonian-Campanian-87-74 Ma) on the basis of comparative palaeobotanical observations."

**Ruled out.**
- *Turonian, Cenomanian–Turonian or late Turonian–early Santonian for the Shakh-Shakh beds.* Rozhdestvensky's datings (1964b, 1971, 1976; with Khozatsky, 1967), which Nessov (1995) lists and judges less probable than Santonian–early Campanian; Nessov (1997) keeps the Santonian–early Campanian.
- *Turonian for the Kzyl-Dzhar clay lens, and late Coniacian–early Campanian for the Taldysay lens.* Zherikhin's (1978) datings as Nessov (1997) reports them; Nessov sets each against a Santonian–early Campanian he calls the more likely.
- *A Coniacian floor from "87-74 Ma".* The figure is given beside the stated Santonian–Campanian, and no measurement is reported for it. Placed on the ICS chart v2024/12, 87 Ma would reach the Coniacian, which the stated stages exclude.

**Open.** Nothing.
