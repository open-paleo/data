# Walloon Coal Measures

## What do Todd and colleagues (2019) say about the age of the Walloon Coal Measures?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Callovian to Tithonian for the unit, from U-Pb dates on its volcanic tuffs that Todd and colleagues (2019) relay, set against an earlier palynological late Bathonian to middle Callovian. They correlate the *Rhoetosaurus* horizon, whose matrix gives a maximum depositional age of 162.6 ± 1.1 Ma, with the base of the unit, just above the Eurombah Formation in the western Surat Basin. How that maximum age bears on the *Rhoetosaurus* stage is set out in the 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-06 entries in [*Rhoetosaurus*](../../genera/R/Rhoetosaurus.md). Governs the Callovian to Tithonian in `stages` on [Walloon Coal Measures](../../../stratigraphy/w/walloon-coal-measures.yml).

**Evidence.**
- todd2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 3: "presently the age of the unit is considered Middle to Upper Jurassic (Callovian to Tithonian) based on extensive new high-precision ID-TIMS U-Pb geochronology of volcanic tuffs from throughout the succession (Wainman et al., 2018a, b)".
- todd2019a, accepted manuscript, pp. 5–6: "Palynological work in the Walloon Coal Measures suggested a late Bathonian to middle Callovian age (~167-164 Ma)"; the tuffs "range between early Callovian and early Tithonian (~165-151 Ma)".
- todd2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 9: "the weighted mean maximum depositional age of 162.6 ± 1.1 Ma correlates with the base of the Walloon Coal Measures, just above the Eurombah Formation, in the western Surat Basin".

**Ruled out.** Nothing beyond the Early Jurassic, which the 2026-10-02 entry rules out.

**Open.** Two points, both resting on dates Todd and colleagues relay rather than measure. First, the youngest tuff, about 151 Ma, is Kimmeridgian on the ICS chart v2024/12 (Tithonian base 149.2 Ma), so their "early Tithonian" may be a label from an older chart; Wainman and colleagues (2018a, 2018b), which give the dates, would settle whether any part of the unit is Tithonian. Second, the dated tuffs begin in the early Callovian, while the Bajocian and Bathonian in `stages` come from palynology and from Hill and colleagues (1966) as Leahey and colleagues (2015) relay them; the same papers would show whether the base of the unit is older than its oldest tuff.

## Is any of the Walloon Coal Measures Early Jurassic?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** No. The Early Jurassic is an early floral reading that later dating supersedes; the unit is Middle to Late Jurassic. Governs the absence of the Early Jurassic from Walloon Coal Measures, and the stage of [*Rhoetosaurus brownei*](../../../genera/R/Rhoetosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- todd2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 3: "presently the age of the unit is considered Middle to Upper Jurassic (Callovian to Tithonian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Jurassic.* Longman (1926) gives "Walloon Series, Jurassic" and quotes Walkom (1919): "the age of this flora is Lower Jurassic" (longman1926a, p. 183). White (1973) repeats "Lower Jurassic, Walloon Series" (white1973a, p. 148) and Dong and colleagues list *Rhoetosaurus* among "Early Jurassic dinosaurs" (dong1983a, English translation, p. 133). All trace to the early floral reading.

**Open.** Nothing.
