# Walloon Coal Measures

## Is any of the Walloon Coal Measures Early Jurassic?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** No. The Early Jurassic is an early floral reading that later dating supersedes; the unit is Middle to Late Jurassic. Governs the absence of the Early Jurassic from Walloon Coal Measures, and the stage of [*Rhoetosaurus brownei*](../../../genera/R/Rhoetosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- todd2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 3: "presently the age of the unit is considered Middle to Upper Jurassic (Callovian to Tithonian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Jurassic.* Longman (1926) gives "Walloon Series, Jurassic" and quotes Walkom (1919): "the age of this flora is Lower Jurassic" (longman1926a, p. 183). White (1973) repeats "Lower Jurassic, Walloon Series" (white1973a, p. 148) and Dong and colleagues list *Rhoetosaurus* among "Early Jurassic dinosaurs" (dong1983a, English translation, p. 133). All trace to the early floral reading.

**Open.** Nothing.
