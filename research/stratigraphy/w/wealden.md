# Wealden

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Wealden?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Berriasian to Aptian. Raven and colleagues (2020) give that span for the whole unit in their title and abstract, and divide it among the units within it. In the Weald Sub-basin the Hastings Beds Group is Berriasian–Valanginian and the Weald Clay Group Hauterivian–Barremian, the latter after Batten and Austen (2011). In the Wessex Sub-basin, where the Wealden ranks as a group, the Wessex Formation is Valanginian–Barremian and the Vectis Formation Aptian, with only the Barremian–Aptian part exposed at the surface. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian]` on [Wealden](../../../stratigraphy/w/wealden.yml).

**Evidence.**
- raven2020a, p. 1: the title, "British Wealden Supergroup (Berriasian–Aptian) Ankylosaurs"; the abstract, "The Lower Cretaceous Wealden Supergroup (Berriasian–Aptian) of Britain".
- raven2020a, p. 23, The Upper and Lower Wealden Dinosaur Faunas: "the Hastings Beds Group (Berriasian–Valanginian)"; "the Weald Clay Group (Hauterivian–Barremian; Batten and Austen, 2011)"; "the Wessex (Valanginian–Barremian) and Vectis (Aptian) formations, although only Barremian–Aptian strata are exposed at the surface".
