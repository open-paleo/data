# Weald Clay

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Weald Clay?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Hauterivian to Aptian, the union of two readings. Raven and colleagues (2020) date the group "Hauterivian–Barremian", citing Batten and Austen (2011) for the range, and repeat it for the group in which they place *Horshamosaurus rudgwickensis* in West Sussex. Norman (2013) heads his account of the group "Barremian-Lower Aptian". Governs `stages: [Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian]` on [Weald Clay](../../../stratigraphy/w/weald-clay.yml).

**Evidence.**
- raven2020a, p. 23: the Weald Sub-basin contains "the Weald Clay Group (Hauterivian–Barremian; Batten and Austen, 2011), which contains the Upper and Lower Weald Clay formations".
- raven2020a, p. 2: "*Horshamosaurus rudgwickensis* from the Weald Clay Group (Hauterivian–Barremian) of West Sussex".
- norman2013a, p. 390: "Weald Clay Group, Barremian-Lower Aptian Stages: distribution".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** The Hauterivian rests on Batten and Austen (2011) as Raven and colleagues relay it; that chapter has not been read, and would show whether the Lower Weald Clay is dated as Hauterivian there.
