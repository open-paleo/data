# Wangjiashan

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Wangjiashan Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** The Bathonian comes from Li and colleagues (2024), who date the lower of the formation's two members, the Straw-yellow Sandstone Member, late Bathonian, on that member's megafossil plants and palynoflora and on the conchostracans, charophytes and bivalves of the overlying Oil Shale Member. Of the works they cite for that date, those checked give only Middle Jurassic or late Middle Jurassic; that reasoning is set out in [Straw-yellow Sandstone](../s/straw-yellow-sandstone.md). Li and colleagues (1982) and Zhang's (2009) lexicon give the formation as a whole only Middle Jurassic, and none of the works cited gives the Oil Shale Member a stage. Li and colleagues (2024) make the Wangjiashan and the Longfengshan Formation the two Middle Jurassic units of the area, resting on a disconformity with Upper Triassic rocks and overlain conformably by Upper Jurassic sediments. Governs `stages: [Bathonian]` and `period: [Middle Jurassic]` on [Wangjiashan](../../../stratigraphy/w/wangjiashan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- li2024a, p. 1, Abstract: "from the Middle Jurassic Wangjiashan Formation of the Pingchuan District, Baiyin City, Gansu Province, China".
- li2024a, p. 2, Geological setting: "The Middle Jurassic strata of the area is composed of two lithostratigraphic units, the Longfengshan Formation and the Wangjiashan Formation. Underlying these Middle Jurassic strata is a disconformity with Upper Triassic rocks, while Upper Jurassic sediments conformably overlie them".
- li2024a, p. 2, Geological setting: "Based on its megafossil plants and palynoflora, as well as the lacustrine invertebrate assemblage of the overlying Oil Shale Member, including conchostracans, charophytes, and bivalves, the Straw-yellow Sandstone Member of the Wangjiashan Formation is considered to be late Bathonian in age".
- li2024a, p. 3, Locality and Horizon: "The materials are from the upper part of the Straw-yellow Sandstone Member of the Wangjiashan Formation, whose age is considered to be Middle Jurassic, late Bathonian".

**Ruled out.** Nothing among the works cited here.

**Open.** The Bathonian dates only the lower member, and only Li and colleagues (2019, 2024) state it; whether the formation reaches the Bajocian or Callovian is not addressed by any work cited. Zhang's (2009) paper on the Baojishan–Honghui Basin, cited by Li and colleagues (2024) for the date, has not been read.
