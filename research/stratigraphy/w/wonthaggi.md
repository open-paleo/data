# Wonthaggi

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Wonthaggi?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Wagstaff and McEwen Mason (1989) support Valanginian to Aptian and not the Albian. They never name the formation; they date vertebrate localities by group, and give the localities of the Strzelecki Group, to which the Wonthaggi belongs, a middle Valanginian to Aptian age, and only the Otway Group localities a late Aptian to early Albian one. Their dates depend, as they say, on spore-pollen zones being the same age across eastern Australia. The base of the zone is a middle Valanginian in their text and the base of the Valanginian in Helby and colleagues (1987), the same stage either way, and a fission-track age at Kilcunda that they relay from Lindsay (1982) agrees with the palynology. Herne and colleagues' (2019) late Barremian for the Flat Rocks locality rests on a personal communication and adds no stage. The record's Albian rests on Carrano and colleagues (2012), which this entry does not settle. Governs `stages` on [Wonthaggi](../../../stratigraphy/w/wonthaggi.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wagstaff1989a, p. 54, Abstract: "the localities in Otway Group sediments are of late Aptian–early Albian age … and the localities in the Strzelecki Group of Valanginian–Aptian age … This age determination is dependent on the isochronicity of spore–pollen zones across eastern Australia."
- wagstaff1989a, p. 59, Age of the *Dictyosporites speciosus* Zone: "The first appearance of *F. wonthaggiensis*, which characterizes the base of the zone, has been dated as middle Valanginian, using associated marine faunas and microplankton in northeastern Australia (D. Burger, personal communication). However, Helby et al. (1987) correlate this first appearance with the base of the Valanginian stage." The same page gives the upper unit of the *Cy. hughesii* Subzone "a ?Barremian–Aptian age (Dettmann 1986)".
- wagstaff1989a, p. 60, Age of the Strzelecki Group Localities: "Fission-track dating of Kilcunda (Lindsay 1982) gave an age of 128 ± 8 m.y. This is within general agreement with the spore–pollen age"; the localities east of Kilcunda (Eagles Nest, Harmers Haven, Twin Reefs, Shack Bay) "fall within the lower or middle *Cy. hughesii* Subzone".
- wagstaff1989a, p. 61, Summary: "The Strzelecki Group localities fall within the *Cy. hughesii* Subzone of the *D. speciosus* Zone and are middle Valanginian–Aptian in age."
- herne2019a, p. 543, Abstract: "The Flat Rocks locality in the Wonthaggi Formation (Strzelecki Group) of the Gippsland Basin, southeastern Australia, hosts fossils of a late Barremian vertebrate fauna".
- herne2019a, p. 546, Geological setting: "Palynological work previously suggested that the region of the Wonthaggi Formation encompassing the Flat Rocks locality was middle Valanginian–middle Barremian in age (following Wagstaff and McEwen Mason, 1989). However, renewed palynological investigations (personal communication, B. Wagstaff, 2018) suggest that the Flat Rocks locality falls within the upper part of the *Foraminisporis wonthaggiensis* … spore-pollen zone of Helby et al. (1987), indicating a late Barremian age (∼125–127.2 Ma …)."

**Ruled out.**
- *The Albian from Wagstaff and McEwen Mason (1989).* Their Albian belongs to the Otway Group localities; the Wonthaggi is a Strzelecki Group unit.
- *Wagstaff and McEwen Mason (1989) as naming the formation.* "Wonthaggi" appears in the paper only in the spore epithet *Foraminisporis wonthaggiensis*, in the zone definitions and a range chart.
- *Herne and colleagues' (2019) late Barremian as a published determination.* It comes from "renewed palynological investigations (personal communication, B. Wagstaff, 2018)", and a personal communication is not a published determination under the rules in methods.md.
- *A middle Barremian end from Wagstaff and McEwen Mason (1989).* Herne and colleagues attribute "middle Valanginian–middle Barremian" to them, but the 1989 paper prints no middle Barremian. It places the localities east of Kilcunda in the lower or middle *Cy. hughesii* Subzone, below an upper unit given as "?Barremian–Aptian"; reading that as ending in the middle Barremian is Herne and colleagues' inference, not the paper's statement.

**Open.** Whether the Albian should stand on Carrano and colleagues' (2012) "Valanginian–Albian" alone. The renewed palynology behind Herne and colleagues' late Barremian has not been published in any work cited.
