# Yongkang

## Where does the Yongkang Group's Aptian come from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** From group-level isotopic ages, not from a child formation. Governs `stages` (Aptian–Turonian) on Yongkang, and keeps the [Chaochuan](../../../stratigraphy/c/chaochuan.yml)'s range separate.

**Evidence.**
- luo2004a, p. 396: "永康群同位素年龄在 113~90 Ma 间。即相当于 Aptian 期到 Turonian 期" ("the isotopic ages of the Yongkang Group lie between 113 and 90 Ma, corresponding to the Aptian through the Turonian", translated); the Guantou Formation is "110 Ma, 正好属于 Aptian 期" ("110 Ma, exactly in the Aptian", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *The Chaochuan Formation as the source.* Pond and colleagues write "both from the Albian–Aptian Chaochuan Formation" (pond2023a, p. 38), giving the range without evidence for either stage.

**Open.** The Chaochuan's own age, which the registry records as disputed.
