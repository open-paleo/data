# Yalovach

## Does the Yalovach reach below the Santonian?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No. Nessov (1995) dates the Kansai level Santonian, after Rozhdestvensky (1977), and rejects the older Cenomanian–Turonian outright. Governs `stages: [Santonian]` on [Yalovach](../../../stratigraphy/y/yalovach.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nessov1995a, p. 99: Kansai, "Yalovach Formation, possibly only its upper part. The age is not Cenomanian - Turonian (Rozhdestvensky, 1964), but Upper Cretaceous, Santonian (Rozhdestvensky, 1977)" (translated).
- averianov2007a, p. 93: "This important lower Santonian vertebrate locality in the Yalovach Formation of the northern Fergana Depression".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian–Turonian.* Nessov reports Rozhdestvensky's (1964) dating only to reject it (nessov1995a, p. 99).
- *Turonian to early Santonian.* *The Dinosauria* distribution chapter gives "Yalovach Svita … Age: Turonian-early Santonian (Rozhdestvensky 1977)" for Kazakhstan and Tajikistan (weishampel2004b, pp. 594–595). A reference work alone does not carry a stage (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-reference-work-corroborates-but-almost-never-carries-a-value-alone)). Nessov gives the same 1977 work as Santonian, and elsewhere says a site so dated in the first edition's list "does not belong to the Yalovach Formation and its age is not Late Cretaceous (Turonian - early Santonian)" (nessov1995a, p. 112, translated).

**Open.** Nothing.
