# Yimen Formation

## What stages does *Tonganosaurus* take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** None; the record takes `period: [Early Jurassic]` alone, as the Yimen takes no `stages`. The describing paper places the holotype in the middle to upper part of the Lower Jurassic Yimen Formation, and no source read dates the horizon or the formation at stage level from evidence. Governs the absent `stage` on [*Tonganosaurus hei*](../../../genera/T/Tonganosaurus.yml) and the absent `stages` on the [Yimen Formation](../../../stratigraphy/y/yimen.yml).

**Evidence.**
- li2010a, p. 185: "化石产地的地层层位应为下侏罗统益门组中上部" [the horizon of the fossil site should be the middle to upper part of the Lower Jurassic Yimen Formation].
- mcphee2016a: gives the Yimen as "?Lower–Middle Jurassic" and recommends "caution in accepting the current age estimates for these units", naming *Tonganosaurus*.

**Ruled out.**
- *Hettangian.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give it without a source (molina-pérez2020a).
- *Pliensbachian.* No source read gives it.
- *Hettangian to Toarcian.* The whole epoch written as stages reads as a stage-level dating no source gives.

**Open.** Whether the horizon is Early Jurassic at all, which McPhee and colleagues (2016) doubt.
