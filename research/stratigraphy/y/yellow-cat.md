# Yellow Cat

## Does the Yellow Cat Member fix a stage?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. The member is diachronous: at Utahraptor Ridge it is Berriasian–Valanginian, while dated sites elsewhere are Barremian. A Yellow Cat taxon takes the stage of its own dated site. Governs `stages` on Yellow Cat and the stages of its taxa, such as [*Falcarius utahensis*](../../../genera/F/Falcarius.yml), [*Martharaptor greenriverensis*](../../../genera/M/Martharaptor.yml) and [*Moabosaurus utahensis*](../../../genera/M/Moabosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- joeckel2023a, p. 1: "a new cryptotephra zircon eruption age of 135.10 ± 0.30/0.31/0.34 Ma" from "36.35 m above the contact" (p. 12); "we interpret the C-isotope record from 32.5 to 42.5 m as Valanginian s and the C-isotope record from 0 to 19.25 m as Berriasian" (p. 12), a correlation the abstract calls tentative. joeckel2019a (accepted manuscript, p. 7): a maximum depositional age of "136.5±1.4 Ma" in the same area.
- mori2009a: "Maximal depositional age calculated from five next to youngest zircons is 121.8± 2.2 Ma" for the *Falcarius* site at Crystal Geyser; kirkland2016a, p. 118: "121.8 ± 2.2 Ma for a sandy unit just above the Crystal Geyser bonebed in the lower Yellow Cat", and "a maximum age of 122.6 ± 0.7 Ma just above the capping sandstone above the Suarez site and below the *Martharaptor* site".
- gale2020a, p. 1031: base of the Aptian 121.4 Ma, so these maximum ages are Barremian (our arithmetic).

**Ruled out.**
- *A single stage for the member.* The Utahraptor Ridge section and the Crystal Geyser and Suarez sites differ by about 13 Myr.
- *Dates from Eberth and colleagues (2006) for Dalton Wells.* Britt and colleagues cite Eberth and colleagues for "a maximum depositional age of 125 Ma" (britt2017a, p. 190, calling it "early Albian" in the text and "early Aptian" in the abstract), and Kirkland and colleagues for "146.6 +4.1 to -3.9 Ma" (kirkland2016a, p. 118); Eberth and colleagues report no zircon dates, only palynology, "latest Neocomian (Hauterivian) to Aptian, and probably Barremian (G. Waanders, pers. com., 2003)" (eberth2006a, p. 222).

**Open.** The Dalton Wells (*Moabosaurus*) maximum age is given as 124.6 ± 2.6 Ma by Mori (2009), relaying Greenhalgh, and 124.2 ± 2.6 Ma elsewhere (mori2009a; kirkland2016a, p. 118); Mori's own Suarez-site value is 122.5 ± 2.4 Ma, not the 122.6 ± 0.7 Ma Kirkland and colleagues report. Kirkland and colleagues read the Dalton Wells fauna as "consistent with that of the upper part of the Yellow Cat Member" (p. 118), against base-of-member placements. Whether Utahraptor Ridge represents a wider Grand County belt is an inference.
