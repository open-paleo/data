## Which of Mori's samples gives 119.2 Ma?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Sample sgz-2 at the Suarez sister's site, and as a probability peak, not a maximum depositional age; it adds no Aptian. Answers the 2026-11-23 entry's Open line. Mori (2009) prints "Peak at 119.2 ± 1.3 Ma" for sgz-2 in a figure that assigns it to the Yellow Cat and Poison Strip Members together, and never cites the value in the text; Joeckel and colleagues (2019) count it among seven "MDAs". Mori gives the package an Early Aptian age of his own, but his work is a master's thesis, and a thesis never carries a value alone. Governs `stages` on [Yellow Cat](../../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.yml), Berriasian to Barremian, unchanged, and the note on mori2009a.

**Evidence.**
- mori2009a, title page: "A thesis submitted to the faculty of Brigham Young University in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Science".
- mori2009a, p. 14, Fig. 4: "sgz-2 Peak at 119.2 ± 1.3 Ma".
- mori2009a, p. 30: "the Yellow Cat/Poison Strip Members range from 125 to 121 Ma (Early Aptian)".
- joeckel2019a, p. ?: "Mori (2009) reported seven detrital zircon U-Pb MDAs ranging between 130±2.4 Ma to 119.2±1.3 Ma from strata of the Yellow Cat Member".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian from Mori (2009), directly or through Joeckel and colleagues (2019).* A thesis, and the 119.2 Ma figure is a peak, not an age.

**Open.** The printed pages of Joeckel and colleagues (2019) have not been read.

## What do the cited works say about the age and extent of the Yellow Cat?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Berriasian to Barremian, unchanged, with the member diachronous as the 2026-09-30 entry concludes. Joeckel and colleagues (2019) date the sections they sampled to the Berriasian–Hauterivian from youngest zircon grains and a palynoflora, and state that Barremian–Aptian strata exist elsewhere in the member. Sprinkel and colleagues (2012) relay the tentative Barremian first given from dinosaur biostratigraphy, a downward extension of the member below its calcretes, and a partial correlation with the Buckhorn Conglomerate. Kirkland and Madsen (2007) relay a fauna-based 125–120 Ma, a 126 ± 2.5 Ma maximum depositional age from Greenhalgh and colleagues (2006), and a lowered base that takes in a fauna that may be older than the one higher in the member. Mori (2009) treats the Yellow Cat and Poison Strip Members as one package of about 124–122 Ma, and puts the Crystal Geyser quarry's maximum depositional age at about 122 Ma, against Kirkland and Madsen's view that the site is substantially older than other Yellow Cat quarries. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on [Yellow Cat](../../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- joeckel2019a, p. ?, Abstract: zircons "from ten stratigraphic horizons in the Yellow Cat Member yield youngest single grain ages ranging from 142.5±2.7 Ma to 133.7 ± 2.7 Ma (Berriasian–late Valanginian)".
- joeckel2019a, p. ?, Summary and conclusions: "A new palynoflora from the Yellow Cat Member is provisionally assigned to the middle Berriasian–early Hauterivian stages".
- joeckel2019a, p. ?, Discussion: "Although earlier studies documented the presence of Barremian–Aptian strata in other parts of the Yellow Cat Member, the stratigraphic sections examined for this zircon U-Pb geochronologic study range through the Berriasian, Valanginian, and Hauterivian stages. This result suggests that the stratigraphic architecture of the Yellow Cat Member is probably more complex than earlier conceptions based on lithostratigraphic perspectives"; Mori (2009) "reported seven detrital zircon U-Pb MDAs ranging between 130±2.4 Ma to 119.2±1.3 Ma from strata of the Yellow Cat Member, suggesting that parts of the unit are no older than Barremian to Aptian in age".
- sprinkel2012a, p. 2: the lower three members "were initially assigned tentative ages based on dinosaur biostratigraphy: Barremian for the Yellow Cat Member in the northern Paradox Basin"; "the Yellow Cat Member extends below the interval of extensive calcretes development" to include "a strongly mottled iron-stained interval that also contains common floating chert pebbles and preserves an Early Cretaceous dinosaur fauna"; the member "is shown to be correlative, at least in part, with the Buckhorn Conglomerate".
- kirkland2007a, p. 7: the member "was originally defined as being underlain by a massive carbonate paleosol", but "Early Cretaceous fossils have now been found below the base of this carbonate interval, thus requiring a redefinition of the lower boundary at the first occurrence of abundant chert pebbles above typical Morrison swelling clays".
- kirkland2007a, p. 9: the 125–120 Ma estimate is credited to Kirkland and colleagues (1997, 1999), Kirkland, Lucas and Estep (1998) and Eberth and colleagues (2006); "A detrital zircon U-Pb maximum depositional age of 126 ± 2.5 Ma is consistent with the biostratigraphic data (Greenhalgh and others, 2006)".
- kirkland2007a, p. 10: the dinosaurs "from in and below the carbonate interval" may "represent a dinosaur fauna distinctly older than that identified from higher in the Yellow Cat Member".
- mori2009a, p. 27: "Kirkland and Madsen (2007) speculated that the site is substantially older than other Yellow Cat quarries. However, from the samples of Crystal Geyser, it can be concluded that the maximum depositional age of the quarry is around 122 Ma".
- mori2009a, p. 30: "the Yellow Cat/Poison Strip Members range from 125 to 121 Ma (Early Aptian)"; p. 70: a quarry "is either in the Yellow Cat/Poison Strip complex or that the Ruby Ranch is contemporaneous with that complex".

**Ruled out.**
- *Berriasian–Hauterivian as the age of the whole member.* Joeckel and colleagues limit it to the sections they sampled and accept Barremian–Aptian strata elsewhere in the member.
- *A stage from Kirkland and Madsen's 126 ± 2.5 Ma.* It is a maximum depositional age, a limit rather than an age, and the paper offers it only as consistent with the faunal estimate.
- *Crystal Geyser as substantially older than other Yellow Cat sites.* Kirkland and Madsen's speculation rests on the quarry's position at the base of the formation; Mori's maximum depositional age of about 122 Ma for the quarry is no older than the other dated sites.

**Open.** Joeckel and colleagues relay Mori's maximum depositional ages for the member as reaching 119.2 ± 1.3 Ma, which on the current chart (Aptian base 121.4 Ma) would make a bed no older than Aptian. Mori's text gives the member 124 to 122 Ma and 121.8 ± 2.2 Ma at Crystal Geyser; which sample, if any, gives 119.2 ± 1.3 Ma, and from which member, would settle whether the 2026-10-07 entry's conclusion holds. The printed pages of Joeckel and colleagues (2019) have not been read; the copy held is the accepted manuscript.

## Do Kirkland and Madsen's 125–120 Ma and Mori's "Early Aptian" put the member in the Aptian?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** No. Both repeat the pattern the 2026-10-07 entry rejected. Kirkland and Madsen's (2007) figures are estimates fitted to the fauna, and Mori's (2009) stage is an older chart's label for maximum depositional ages. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on [Yellow Cat](../../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- kirkland2007a, p. 9: "The dinosaurs, together with pollen and charophytes (green algae), suggest that the Yellow Cat Member rocks are approximately 125-120 million years old"; the sentence names no stage. p. 6, Fig. 4 (modified after Kirkland and colleagues, 1999): the member is plotted in the Barremian, with a query above it in the lower Aptian.
- mori2009a, p. 27: "the Yellow Cat and Poison Strip Members are 124 to 122 M old"; p. 30: "125 to 121 Ma (Early Aptian)", offered as preliminary age brackets from detrital zircon maximum ages such as 121.8 ± 2.2 Ma at Crystal Geyser (pp. 13–14).

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian from the 120 Ma end of Kirkland and Madsen's range.* On the ICS chart v2024/12 (Aptian base 121.4 Ma) 120 Ma is Aptian, but the number is attached to a faunal, pollen and charophyte correlation rather than measured. CONTRIBUTING places a bare number on the current chart only when the number is the measurement. The paper's own chart queries the Aptian, and a query adds no stage.
- *Aptian from Mori's "Early Aptian".* The ages are maxima, which are limits rather than ages, and they are Barremian on the current chart except at the very top of the bracket. Mori's work is also a thesis, which never carries a value alone (research/methods.md).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does the Yellow Cat Member reach the Aptian?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** No. Every Aptian given for the member is an older-chart stage for a detrital-zircon maximum age of about 122–124 Ma, which is Barremian on the current chart (base of the Aptian 121.4 Ma), or a hedge. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on [Yellow Cat](../../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.yml), replacing the Aptian.

**Evidence.**
- joeckel2019a, Discussion: a 124 Ma age near Dalton Wells is cited as "demonstrating that the unit ranges upward into the early Aptian"; 124 Ma is Barremian on the current chart.
- kirkland2016a, p. 118: the "most widely cited age of 124.2 ± 2.6 Ma", and "somewhat ambiguous chemostratigraphic profiles also suggest Barremian to Aptian age".
- joeckel2023a, p. 1: a CA-ID-TIMS age of 135.10 ± 0.30 Ma at Utahraptor Ridge, confirming "the Berriasian–Valanginian ages of part of the Yellow Cat Member".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian.* The dated ages are maxima centered in the Barremian, so they make the beds no older than the Barremian rather than Aptian; CONTRIBUTING's rule on reading ages on the chart takes the number over an older chart's stage. Kirkland and colleagues' chemostratigraphy is hedged as "somewhat ambiguous". Senter and colleagues' "Barremian? - Aptian" (senter2012b) gives no basis.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does the Yellow Cat Member fix a stage?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. The member is diachronous: at Utahraptor Ridge it is Berriasian–Valanginian, while dated sites elsewhere are Barremian. A Yellow Cat taxon takes the stage of its own dated site. Governs `stages` on Yellow Cat and the stages of its taxa, such as [*Falcarius utahensis*](../../../genera/F/Falcarius.yml), [*Martharaptor greenriverensis*](../../../genera/M/Martharaptor.yml) and [*Moabosaurus utahensis*](../../../genera/M/Moabosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- joeckel2023a, p. 1: "a new cryptotephra zircon eruption age of 135.10 ± 0.30/0.31/0.34 Ma" from "36.35 m above the contact" (p. 12); "we interpret the C-isotope record from 32.5 to 42.5 m as Valanginian s and the C-isotope record from 0 to 19.25 m as Berriasian" (p. 12), a correlation the abstract calls tentative. joeckel2019a (accepted manuscript, p. 7): a maximum depositional age of "136.5±1.4 Ma" in the same area.
- mori2009a: "Maximal depositional age calculated from five next to youngest zircons is 121.8± 2.2 Ma" for the *Falcarius* site at Crystal Geyser; kirkland2016a, p. 118: "121.8 ± 2.2 Ma for a sandy unit just above the Crystal Geyser bonebed in the lower Yellow Cat", and "a maximum age of 122.6 ± 0.7 Ma just above the capping sandstone above the Suarez site and below the *Martharaptor* site".
- gale2020a, p. 1031: base of the Aptian 121.4 Ma, so these maximum ages are Barremian (our arithmetic).

**Ruled out.**
- *A single stage for the member.* The Utahraptor Ridge section and the Crystal Geyser and Suarez sites differ by about 13 Myr.
- *Dates from Eberth and colleagues (2006) for Dalton Wells.* Britt and colleagues cite Eberth and colleagues for "a maximum depositional age of 125 Ma" (britt2017a, p. 190, calling it "early Albian" in the text and "early Aptian" in the abstract), and Kirkland and colleagues for "146.6 +4.1 to -3.9 Ma" (kirkland2016a, p. 118); Eberth and colleagues report no zircon dates, only palynology, "latest Neocomian (Hauterivian) to Aptian, and probably Barremian (G. Waanders, pers. com., 2003)" (eberth2006a, p. 222).

**Open.** The Dalton Wells (*Moabosaurus*) maximum age is given as 124.6 ± 2.6 Ma by Mori (2009), relaying Greenhalgh, and 124.2 ± 2.6 Ma elsewhere (mori2009a; kirkland2016a, p. 118); Mori's own Suarez-site value is 122.5 ± 2.4 Ma, not the 122.6 ± 0.7 Ma Kirkland and colleagues report. Kirkland and colleagues read the Dalton Wells fauna as "consistent with that of the upper part of the Yellow Cat Member" (p. 118), against base-of-member placements. Whether Utahraptor Ridge represents a wider Grand County belt is an inference.
