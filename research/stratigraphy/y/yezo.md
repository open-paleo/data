# Yezo

## Does the Yezo Group take the Danian?

*2026-10-03*

Supersedes the 2026-09-30 entry's closing clause: the stage list does include the Danian, so its absence here rests on the evidence below, not on the list.

**Conclusion.** No. The group's top formation reaches the Paleocene, but no source places the Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary within it, so the period includes the Paleocene and the stages stop at the Maastrichtian. Governs the absence of `Danian` from the stages of Yezo and of the [Hakobuchi Formation](../../../stratigraphy/h/hakobuchi.yml).

**Evidence.**
- takashima2004a, p. 384: "The Cretaceous/Palaeogene boundary sequence has not been detected in the Hakobuchi Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Danian from a stated span.* A span such as "Lower Campanian–Paleocene" (takashima2004a, p. 365) gives the period, not a stage; a unit takes the Danian only when a source places the boundary within it.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does the Yezo Group reach the Paleocene?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes, under Takashima and colleagues' (2004) scheme, which runs from the Lower Aptian Soashibetsugawa Formation to the Lower Campanian–Paleocene [Hakobuchi Formation](../../../stratigraphy/h/hakobuchi.yml). Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous, Late Cretaceous, Paleocene]` and `stages` (Aptian–Maastrichtian) on Yezo; the Paleocene part has no stage in the registry's stage list.

**Evidence.**
- takashima2004a, p. 365, abstract: "the Hakobuchi Formation (Lower Campanian–Paleocene shallow-marine sandstone-conglomerate unit)" at the top and "the Soashibetsugawa Formation (Lower Aptian mudstone unit)" at the base.

**Ruled out.**
- *An upper Albian base.* Earlier schemes started the group in the upper Albian (as compared in hayakawa2005a); Takashima and colleagues' base is Lower Aptian.

**Open.** Nothing.
