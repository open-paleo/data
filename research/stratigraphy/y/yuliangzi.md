# Yuliangzi

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Yuliangzi Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Maastrichtian. The three works agree on the stage and differ on the substage. Godefroit and colleagues (2000), writing the name as Yuliangze, date the formation to the Late Maastrichtian because it falls in the *Wodehouseia spinata*–*Aquilapollenites subtilis* palynozone, and *W. spinata* marks the Late Maastrichtian ("Lancian") formations of North America. Xing and colleagues (2022) treat the Yuliangzi as the same age and lithology as the Udurchukan Formation across the Amur River, limit both to the early–middle Maastrichtian, and place the upper Yuliangzi, which yields the Wulaga bonebed, in the middle Maastrichtian. Yao and colleagues (2026) give "?early to middle Maastrichtian". Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]` on [Yuliangzi](../../../stratigraphy/y/yuliangzi.yml).

**Evidence.**
- godefroit2000a, p. 875, Abstract: "Large bonebeds have been excavated in the Late Maastrich[t]ian Yuliangze Formation near Jiayin (Heilongjang Province, northeastern China)".
- godefroit2000a, p. 877: the formation "belongs to the *Wodehouseia spinata*–*Aquilapollenites subtilis* palynozone"; "The abundance of *Wodehouseia spinata* speaks for a Late Maastrichtian age for the Yuliangze Formation".
- xing2022a, p. e2085111-1, Abstract: "the upper Yuliangzi Formation (middle Maastrichtian)".
- xing2022a, p. e2085111-1, Introduction: "the Yuliangzi Formation is temporally and lithologically equivalent to the Udurchukan Formation, and the age of the two formations is limited to the early–middle Maastrichtian".
- xing2022a, p. e2085111-6: the Wulaga locality lies in the "Upper parts of the Udurchukan and Yuliangzi formations that are contemporaneous deposits both in the southern Zeya-Bureya Basin, middle Maastrichtian, Late Cretaceous".
- yao2026a, p. 2, Introduction: "the Yuliangzi Formation (?early to middle Maastrichtian) in Jiayin and Xunke, Heilongjiang Province".

**Ruled out.** Nothing among the works cited here. The Turonian that one compilation gives is ruled out in the 2026-08-05 entry in [*Charonosaurus*](../../genera/C/Charonosaurus.md).

**Open.** The substage. The Late Maastrichtian rests on a palynozone and the early–middle Maastrichtian on correlation with the Udurchukan; neither work cited dates the formation radiometrically.
