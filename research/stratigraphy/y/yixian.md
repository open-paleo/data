# Yixian

## Is the Yixian Formation Barremian, or does it reach the Aptian?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Barremian, on U-Pb dates from the Jin-Yang basin; the Aptian readings are older-chart labels for Barremian-age dates, and the Valanginian–Hauterivian readings rest on earlier Ar/Ar ages. Governs `stages: [Barremian]` on Yixian, its members (for example [Dawangzhangzi](../../../stratigraphy/d/dawangzhangzi.yml) and [Lujiatun](../../../stratigraphy/l/lujiatun.yml)) and its species, such as [*Jeholornis curvipes*](../../../genera/J/Jeholornis.yml) and [*Zhongjianosaurus yangi*](../../../genera/Z/Zhongjianosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhong2021a, p. 1: the "onset at 125.755 ± 0.061 Ma (CY17–9) and termination at 124.122 ± 0.048 Ma (CY18–20) of the Yixian Formation", from samples "in the Jin-Yang basin". The paper names no stage; Barremian is our placement of these dates on the current chart.

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian.* Lefèvre and colleagues give *J. curvipes* as "lower Aptian" on a date "of 124.6 ± 0.3 Mya" (lefèvre2014a, p. 791, relaying Yang and colleagues 2007); Xu and Qin give *Zhongjianosaurus* "Possibly Yixian Formation, Aptian, Cretaceous (Swisher et al., 2001)" (xu2017a, p. 130); Sereno (2010) places the beds "no older than 123–125 Ma, straddling the Barremian-Aptian boundary (Gradstein et al. 2004)" (sereno2010a, p. 22). These dates fall in the Barremian on the current chart; that the other papers used the older Aptian base is an inference, since only Sereno names his timescale.
- *Valanginian–Hauterivian.* Jin and colleagues give "older than 128 million years and younger than 139 million years (Lower Valanginian–Lower Barremian; Swisher et al., 2002)" (jin2012a), from earlier Ar/Ar work; Zhong and colleagues note that earlier ages are inconsistent "between different dating methods (i.e. 40Ar/39Ar and U-Pb …)".
- *A radiometric "Zhou et al. 2007".* Sereno cites "(He at al. 2006; Zhou et al. 2007)" (p. 22), but his only Zhou 2007 is a psittacosaur CT paper; his reference list has an uncited Zhu and colleagues (2007) Ar/Ar dating paper, probably the one meant (our inference).

**Open.** The papers that still give "Barremian–Aptian" (for example Pittman and Xu 2020) are not separately examined here. *Yutyrannus*'s formation rests on a dealer's account given only in its supplement: "acquired … from a fossil dealer", "This strongly suggests that the specimens were collected from the Yixian Formation" (xu2012a, supplementary information, p. 2, §1).
