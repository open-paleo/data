# Khuren Dukh

## Does the Khuren Dukh Formation reach the Barremian?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** No. The only Barremian for the formation is Makovicky and Norell's attribution to Shuvalov (2000), whose paper dates Hüren Dukh Aptian–Albian. Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` on [Khuren Dukh](../../../stratigraphy/k/khuren-dukh.yml), replacing `[Barremian, Aptian, Albian]`.

**Evidence.**
- makovicky2006a, p. 2: the beds "have been interpreted as Aptian-early Albian in age … but have also been dated as Barremian (~128 MYA) by Shuvalov (2000)".
- shuvalov2000a, p. 264: "The Hüren Dukh succession is dated by molluscs and dinosaurs … The molluscs are characteristic of the Aptian-Albian … The spore-pollen assemblage … is also Aptian-Albian"; the Barremian in the paper belongs to the Shinekhudag.
- hicks1999a, p. 843: palynology gives "an Albian age—probably middle to late Albian" for the beds at Khuren Dukh.

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian.* Makovicky and Norell's citation does not contain it. Hicks and colleagues (1999, p. 836) report a Hauterivian–Barremian ostracod age from Matsukawa and colleagues (1997) and do not adopt it; Matsukawa and colleagues have not been read.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which part of the Khuren Dukh Formation is *Choyrodon* from?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** The lower part, as the describing paper gives it: the Lower Member of Ito and colleagues' (2006) scheme. Governs `location.part: lower` on [*Choyrodon barsboldi*](../../../genera/C/Choyrodon.yml). Settles the **Open** line of the entry on *Zavacephale*.

**Evidence.**
- gates2018a, p. 4: the holotype quarry "is located within a thick brown–black organic rich siltstone at the Khuren Dukh locality of the Lower Member of the Khuren Dukh Formation (sensu Ito et al., 2006)".

**Ruled out.**
- *The middle unit, from Chinzorig and colleagues' Fig. 1a.* The figure sets a *Choyrodon* silhouette, credited to PhyloPic, beside the column at about the level of its "Middle Khuren Dukh" band, but the paper gives no text, data or source for *Choyrodon*'s level, and the same figure is inconsistent for its own taxon: its star sits in the "Lower Khuren Dukh" band, its caption says "upper part of the middle KDF", and its text "upper portion of the lower KDF" (chinzorig2025a, p. 1139). The two papers also use different schemes, two members against three units, which no source read relates.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which part of the Khuren Dukh Formation is Zavacephale from, and how is the name spelled?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The upper portion of the lower Khuren Dukh Formation. "Khuren Dukh" is the majority spelling and the registry's headword; Hüren Dukh is the gazetteer's form. Governs `location.part: lower` on [*Zavacephale rinpoche*](../../../genera/Z/Zavacephale.yml) and the variants on Khuren Dukh.

**Evidence.**
- chinzorig2025a, p. 1139, Locality and horizon: "MPC-D 100/1209 was recovered from the upper portion of the lower KDF". In Fig. 1a the specimen's star sits in the bed colored "Lower Khuren Dukh" (our reading of the figure).
- chinzorig2025a, p. 1139: the Khuren Dukh faunas "differ from those of the potentially penecontemporaneous Khukhteeg Formation and warrant distinction".
- benton2000c, p. xxv: "Hüren Dukh [locality]", "Hürendukh Formation/ Svita", "Khooren Dookh (= Hüren Dukh)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Middle Khuren Dukh Formation.* The Fig. 1 caption says "the upper part of the middle KDF" (p. 1139); the paper's text and the figure itself say lower.

**Open.** The position of *Choyrodon*. Gates and colleagues (2018) place it "at the Khuren Dukh locality of the Lower Member of the Khuren Dukh Formation (Ito et al., 2006)", in the beds that yielded *Altirhinus*, "middle to late Albian (Nichols, Matsukawa & Ito, 2006)" (gates2018a, pp. 2, 4); Chinzorig and colleagues' Fig. 1a draws *Choyrodon* in the middle unit. Spellings in the literature also include Khuren-Dukh, Khoren Dukh and Khooren Dukh.
