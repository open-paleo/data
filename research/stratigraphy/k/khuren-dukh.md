# Khuren Dukh

## Which part of the Khuren Dukh Formation is Zavacephale from, and how is the name spelled?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The upper portion of the lower Khuren Dukh Formation. "Khuren Dukh" is the majority spelling and the registry's headword; Hüren Dukh is the gazetteer's form. Governs `location.part: lower` on [*Zavacephale rinpoche*](../../../genera/Z/Zavacephale.yml) and the variants on Khuren Dukh.

**Evidence.**
- chinzorig2025a, p. 1139, Locality and horizon: "MPC-D 100/1209 was recovered from the upper portion of the lower KDF". In Fig. 1a the specimen's star sits in the bed colored "Lower Khuren Dukh" (our reading of the figure).
- chinzorig2025a, p. 1139: the Khuren Dukh faunas "differ from those of the potentially penecontemporaneous Khukhteeg Formation and warrant distinction".
- benton2000c, p. xxv: "Hüren Dukh [locality]", "Hürendukh Formation/ Svita", "Khooren Dookh (= Hüren Dukh)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Middle Khuren Dukh Formation.* The Fig. 1 caption says "the upper part of the middle KDF" (p. 1139); the paper's text and the figure itself say lower.

**Open.** The position of *Choyrodon*. Gates and colleagues (2018) place it "at the Khuren Dukh locality of the Lower Member of the Khuren Dukh Formation (Ito et al., 2006)", in the beds that yielded *Altirhinus*, "middle to late Albian (Nichols, Matsukawa & Ito, 2006)" (gates2018a, pp. 2, 4); Chinzorig and colleagues' Fig. 1a draws *Choyrodon* in the middle unit. Spellings in the literature also include Khuren-Dukh, Khoren Dukh and Khooren Dukh.
