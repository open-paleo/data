## Is the Kayenta Formation Late Triassic or Middle Jurassic in part?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** Neither. The Kayenta is Early Jurassic; the Late Triassic and Middle Jurassic readings are superseded. Governs `period: [Early Jurassic]` on Kayenta.

**Evidence.**
- The record's Early Jurassic sources (not re-examined for this note) stand against the two readings below.

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Triassic.* Lewis and colleagues (1961) write that the fauna "strongly suggests … Late Triassic age" (lewis1961a, pp. 1437–1438), an early faunal reading.
- *Lower or Middle Jurassic.* Kermack (1982) concludes, in his own voice, that the beds "must be Lower or Middle Jurassic age" (kermack1982a, pp. 15–16); Olsen and Galton relay it (olsen1984a, p. 93). It is superseded, not merely relayed.

**Open.** Nothing.
