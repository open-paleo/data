## Does Satyanarayana and colleagues' Table 1 add the Bathonian?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** No. Answers the 2026-11-23 entry's Open line. Table 1 of Satyanarayana and colleagues (1999), after Biswas (1980), sets a single "Aalenian – Bathonian" label beside both the Jhurio and the Kaladongar formations, in one cell with "Callovian – Oxfordian"; their text gives the Kaladongar "? Aalenian-Early Bajocian". A joint age across several units dates none of them. Fürsich and colleagues (2023) date the top of the formation to the Late Bajocian Subfurcatum Zone, and in their sections the Bathonian ammonites come from the Goradongar Formation above. ## What do the cited works say about the age of the Kaladongar?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Pliensbachian to Bajocian. Natarajan and colleagues (2026), on the lithostratigraphy of Fürsich and colleagues (2023), date the formation at the Khadir Uplift from a queried upper Lower Jurassic, "possibly as early as Pliensbachian", to the upper Bajocian, and Fürsich and colleagues give "?Early Jurassic" to Late Bajocian. The ?Aalenian–Early Bajocian of Satyanarayana and colleagues (1999), from Patcham Island, falls inside that span. The bed that yielded the Khadir sauropod, at the transition from the Mouwana Variegated Member to the Lower Flagstone Member, is not dated more closely than the formation. Governs `stages: [Pliensbachian, Toarcian, Aalenian, Bajocian]` and `period: [Early Jurassic, Middle Jurassic]` on [Kaladongar](../../../stratigraphy/k/kaladongar.yml).

**Evidence.**
- natarajan2026a, p. 1, Abstract: a partial sauropod skeleton "from the ?upper Lower Jurassic (Pliensbachian) to Bajocian strata of the Kaladongar Formation (Khadir Uplift), Kachchh Basin, India".
- natarajan2026a, p. 2, Geological Setting: on the restructured lithostratigraphy of Fürsich and colleagues, the formation "is divided into four members, viz. Cheriya Bet Conglomerate, Mouwana Variegated, Lower Flagstone, and Middle Sandstone at the Khadir Uplift, and describes an age duration from the ? upper Lower Jurassic (poorly constrained; possibly as early as Pliensbachian) to upper Bajocian".
- natarajan2026a, p. 2, Lithology of the Amrapar Succession: the section north of Amrapar village "is considered here as a subtle lithostratigraphic transition from the upper Mouwana Variegated Member to the Lower Flagstone Member", and "the age of this section is poorly constrained and may range from somewhere between ?upper Lower Jurassic to Bajocian".
- fürsich2023a, p. 100, Kaladongar Formation, Age and biostratigraphy: "?Early Jurassic" to "Late Bajocian (Subfurcatum Zone".
- satyanarayana1999a, p. 639: "A thick sequence of conglomerate, sandstone and shale exposed in Kaladongar (black hills) range of Patcham Island constitutes the oldest stratigraphic unit of Kutch – the Kaladongar Formation, and is referable to? Aalenian-Early Bajocian age (earliest Middle Jurassic)"; it "has been subdivided into three informal members: (i) lowest – Dingi Hill Member, (ii) the middle – Kaladongar Sandstone Member, and (iii) upper – Babia Cliff Sandstone Member".
- satyanarayana1999a, p. 639: the rocks of Kuar Bet "are lithologically similar to Dingi Hill Member, although exact correlation is not possible for want of marker beds"; the dinosaur remains and "petrified tree trunks and branches" come "from the lowest sandstone beds".
- satyanarayana1999a, p. 639, Table 1: "Kaladongar Formation" for Patcham Island in the row "Aalenian – Bathonian", beside the Jhurio Formation of the Kutch Mainland.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Table 1 of Satyanarayana and colleagues (1999) sets the Kaladongar and the Jhurio in one "Aalenian – Bathonian" row, against the "?Aalenian-Early Bajocian" of their own text, and Weishampel and colleagues (2004) tabulate the unit as Aalenian to Bathonian on that paper. Whether the table row dates the Kaladongar, and so adds the Bathonian, is undecided; the dated sections in Fürsich and colleagues (2023) would settle where the formation's top lies.
