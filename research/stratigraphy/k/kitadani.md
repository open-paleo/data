# Kitadani

## Does Hattori and colleagues' middle to late Aptian narrow the Kitadani?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** No. It adds a reading to the formation without replacing the older ones, so the Kitadani stays Hauterivian–Albian; the bonebed taxa are Aptian on their horizon. This settles the Open question of the 2026-10-02 entry. Governs `stages` on the [Kitadani](../../../stratigraphy/k/kitadani.yml), `stage: [Aptian]` and `bed: Bonebed I` on [*Fukuiraptor kitadaniensis*](../../../genera/F/Fukuiraptor.yml), and `bed: Bonebed I` on [*Fukuisaurus tetoriensis*](../../../genera/F/Fukuisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hattori2021a, p. 2: "the tuff beds zircons U-Pb age of 121.2±1.1 Ma from the directly underlying Akaiwa Formation (Sakai et al., 2019) narrows the inferred age for the Kitadani Formation into middle to late Aptian".
- shibata2015b, p. 421: Bonebed I "has yielded Fukuiraptor, Fukuisaurus, other dinosaur bones and teeth".
- CONTRIBUTING, Age: "a newer, narrower dating adds to the older readings rather than replacing them".

**Ruled out.**
- *Narrowing the formation to the Aptian.* The union rule keeps the Hauterivian to early Aptian of Azuma and Shibata (2010) and the Aptian–Albian of You and colleagues (2009).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Are the Kitadani bonebeds Aptian, and is the formation's Hauterivian–Albian range stale?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** The Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry bonebeds are Aptian. The formation itself is probably middle to late Aptian, which would make its recorded Hauterivian–Albian range too wide; that point has not been ruled. Governs `stages: [Aptian]` on Bonebed I and Bonebed IV and the Aptian of their taxa, such as [*Fukuiraptor kitadaniensis*](../../../genera/F/Fukuiraptor.yml) and [*Koshisaurus katsuyama*](../../../genera/K/Koshisaurus.yml), and bears on the Kitadani's own stages.

**Evidence.**
- sano2017a, accepted manuscript, p. 1: localities including the "Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry (Kitadani Formation) are assigned to the Barremian and the Aptian, respectively".
- hattori2021a, p. 2: "121.2±1.1 Ma from the directly underlying Akaiwa Formation (Sakai et al., 2019) narrows the inferred age for the Kitadani Formation into middle to late Aptian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hauterivian–Barremian for the bonebed taxa.* *Fukuiraptor*'s record carries Hauterivian–Aptian although its holotype is from Bonebed I; the bed is Aptian.

**Open.** The formation's range. The registry's Hauterivian–Albian rests on Azuma and Shibata (2010) and You and colleagues (2009); Hattori and colleagues' (2021) narrowing has not been weighed against them.
