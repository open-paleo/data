# Khodzhakul

## What does Danilov and colleagues (2018) give the Khodzhakul?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** An early Cenomanian age, with no basis. Danilov and colleagues refer to "the lower Cenomanian Khodzhakul Formation" while reporting Nessov's (1978) description of *Tienfucheloides*, and Tong and colleagues (2021) cite them for the Cenomanian of that turtle. The printed paper is in the Turtle Evolution Symposium volume, pp. 34–37, not the proceedings and pages Tong and colleagues give. Governs the note on danilov2018a on [Khodzhakul](../../../stratigraphy/k/khodzhakul.yml) and the book, publisher and pages of the danilov2018a store entry; `stages` unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- danilov2018a, p. 34: specimens "from the lower Cenomanian Khodzhakul Formation of Uzbekistan".
- tong2021a, p. 10: "Tienfucheloides (Khodzhakul Formation (Cenomaian), Uzbekistan [70])".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.

## How does Nessov (1997) date the parts of the Khodzhakul Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Albian to Cenomanian. Nessov (1997) recognizes two vertebrate assemblages in the suite: a Khodzhakul assemblage in its lower and middle parts, which shark teeth date to the end of the Albian (the Vraconian), and a Sheikhdzheili assemblage in its upper part, of early Cenomanian age. His locality catalog follows the division. The lower or middle part is upper Albian at level SH-20 of Khodzhakul I and, with a query, at Parlykala; the upper part is lower Cenomanian at Sheikhdzheili II, Khodzhakul'sai and Ayazkala, and Cenomanian without a substage at Chelpyk and Kyrkkyz. Governs `stages: [Albian, Cenomanian]` on [Khodzhakul](../../../stratigraphy/k/khodzhakul.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nessov1997a, p. ?, Chapter 1, Correlation of Cretaceous deposits with vertebrate remains in the Kyzylkum, Fergana and northeastern Aral region: "In the Khodzhakul'skian suite of Southwestern Kyzylkums, two faunistic complexes are established: the Khodzhakul' complex (lower and middle parts of the suite) of the Late Albian, and the Shejkhdjejli complex (upper part of the suite) of the Early Cenomanian"; the Vraconian age of the lower assemblage "is determined by finds of shark teeth Anacoracidae and *Paraisurus*" (translated).
- nessov1997a, p. ?, Chapter 8.8, Uzbekistan, locality 6 (Khodzhakul I): "Lower part of the Khodzhakul Suite, lower–middle Albian"; locality 7 (level SH-20 at Khodzhakul I): "Lower or middle part of the Khodzhakul Suite, upper (terminal) Albian (vraconian)", and "Until the discovery here of shark remains, the entire Khodzhakul Suite was dated to the Cenomanian (Shultz, 1972)" (translated).
- nessov1997a, p. ?, Chapter 8.8, Uzbekistan, locality 12 (Parlykala): "Lower or middle part of the Khodzhakul Suite, upper Albian(?)" (translated).
- nessov1997a, p. ?, Chapter 8.8, Uzbekistan, localities 16 (Sheikhdzheili II), 17 (Khodzhakul'sai) and 18 (Ayazkala): each "Upper part of the Khodzhakul Formation, lower Cenomanian"; locality 21 (Kyrkkyz): "Khodzhakul Formation (upper part), Cenomanian" (translated).
- nessov1997a, p. ?, Chapter 8.8, Uzbekistan, locality 19 (Chelpyk): "Upper part of the Khodzhakul Formation (but not the lower part of the Beshtybinsk Formation, Nessov, 1977a), Cenomanian (possibly a slightly lower level than for Sheikhdjeli II and Khodzhakul'sai)" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *The whole suite is Cenomanian.* Nessov (1997) reports this as the dating, after Shultz (1972), that stood until shark teeth were found at Khodzhakul I; those teeth place the lower and middle parts in the latest Albian.
- *The fossil-bearing beds at Khodzhakul I (level SH-20) and Chelpyk belong to the lower Beshtyube Suite, overlying the Khodzhakul.* Nessov (1977a, 1977b) assigned the Khodzhakul I level to the lower Turonian lower Beshtyube Suite shown on the geological map of Shultz (1972), and Nessov (1977a) put the Chelpyk beds in the same unit; Nessov (1997) calls the first assignment erroneous and places both in the Khodzhakul Formation.

**Open.** Nessov (1997) is not consistent about the lower part of the suite at Khodzhakul I: his locality catalog dates it lower–middle Albian, while Chapter 1 assigns the lower and middle parts together to the Late Albian. Both readings are Albian, so the stages do not depend on it.
