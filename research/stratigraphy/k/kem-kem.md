# Kem Kem

## Do Sereno and colleagues (1996) or Evers and colleagues (2015) take the Kem Kem outside the Albian–Cenomanian?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** No. Both date the beds Cenomanian. Sereno and colleagues (1996) name the Kem Kem beds informally, in place of the lower red sandstone and upper marly units described by Choubert (1952), and date them Cenomanian on nine elasmobranch species, seven of them shared with the Bahariya Formation. The beds rest with an erosional contact on Paleozoic strata and pass conformably up into a carbonate platform of Late Cenomanian through Turonian age. Evers and colleagues (2015), after reviewing the schemes of Choubert (1948), Sereno and colleagues (1996), Dubar (1949) and Cavin and colleagues (2010), hold that the assemblage is Cenomanian, most likely Early Cenomanian; its traditional "Infracénomanien" age sits at the base of the Cenomanian, Cavin and colleagues found no evidence for pre-Cenomanian fossils at the base of the series, and the shark and dinosaur assemblages match the Early Cenomanian Bahariya Formation. Neither paper changes the Albian–Cenomanian range that the 2026-09-21 entry records. Governs `stages` on [Kem Kem](../../../stratigraphy/k/kem-kem.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno1996a, p. 987: "We informally recognize this distinct, nonmarine deltaic facies as the Kem Kem beds, which can be divided equally into two units that together attain a maximum thickness of approximately 200 m".
- sereno1996a, p. 987: the beds beneath the platform "have been divided informally into a lower red sandstone unit (grés rouges infracénomaniens) and an upper marly unit (marne versicolores à gypse)", citing Choubert (1952) in note 20 (p. 990).
- sereno1996a, p. 987: "Nine elasmobranch species strongly support a Cenomanian age for the deposit [circa (ca.) 93 million years ago (Ma)]".
- sereno1996a, p. 990, note 24: "Seven of the nine elasmobranchs in the Kem Kem beds also occur in the Cenomanian Bahariya Formation".
- sereno1996a, p. 987: the horizons are exposed below "a carbonate platform of Late Cenomanian through Turonian age"; Fig. 1C shows "the erosional contact below with Ordovician and Devonian strata and a conformable contact above with the Cenomanian-Turonian limestone platform".
- evers2015a, p. 5, Geological and Palaeontological Context: Sereno and colleagues "unite the bottom two units of Choubert's systemization to the informal 'Kem Kem beds'", and Dubar (1949) "formally erected three formations", the Ifezouane, Aoufous and Akrabou.
- evers2015a, p. 6, Geological and Palaeontological Context: "The Kem Kem compound assemblage has traditionally been considered "Infracénomanien" in age (i.e., at the base of the Cenomanian)"; Cavin and colleagues (2010) "noted that there is indeed no evidence for pre-Cenomanian fossils at the base of the Cretaceous series"; the shark assemblage resembles that of the Bahariya Formation, "which is dated safely to be Early Cenomanian".
- evers2015a, p. 53, Synonymy of Spinosaurus maroccanus and Sigilmassasaurus brevicollis: the referred specimens "came from the locality of Gara Samani in Algeria, some 550 km to the east of the Kem Kem area. The age of the locality was given as Albian".
- evers2015a, p. 75, Taxonomic and formal considerations: the *Carcharodontosaurus saharicus* syntypes come "from the (?)Albian of the area around Timimoun, western Algeria".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Turonian top from Sereno and colleagues' "ca. 93" Ma.* The figure would fall in the Turonian on the ICS chart v2024/12, whose Turonian begins at 93.9 Ma, but the paper's reading is the elasmobranch Cenomanian, and the beds lie below a platform whose base is Late Cenomanian. A stated stage is carried over rather than re-placed from its number (see [methods](../../methods.md)).
- *An Albian from Evers and colleagues (2015).* The Albian ages in the paper belong to Algerian localities, Gara Samani and Timimoun, not to the Kem Kem, which the paper dates Cenomanian. The group's Albian rests on Smyth and colleagues (2020), as the 2026-09-21 entry records.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the Albian-or-Cenomanian question the dispute block records.

## Does the Kem Kem Group reach the Aptian?

*2026-09-21*

**Conclusion.** No. The group is Albian–Cenomanian. Governs `stages: [Albian, Cenomanian]` on [Kem Kem](../../../stratigraphy/k/kem-kem.yml).

**Evidence.**
- smyth2020a, p. 2: the age "remains controversial, with an Albian or Early Cenomanian age being considered likely by most recent workers".
- evers2015a, p. 6: "the Kem Kem assemblage is Cenomanian, and most likely Early Cenomanian in age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian–Cenomanian (Cau and Paterna 2025).* The phrase "the Kem Kem Assemblage from the Aptian-Cenomanian of Morocco" (cau2025a, p. 166) appears in a list of four units compared in a similarity analysis, with no evidence for the age. The paper defines "Kem Kem Assemblage" as a term for the vertebrate material from the Kem Kem beds that "does not make any explicit reference to a particular stratigraphic framework" (p. 163), so the label dates a faunal assemblage, not the group.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the Albian-or-Cenomanian question the entry's dispute block records.
