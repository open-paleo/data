# Kem Kem

## Does the Kem Kem Group reach the Aptian?

*2026-09-21*

**Conclusion.** No. The group is Albian–Cenomanian. Governs `stages: [Albian, Cenomanian]` on [Kem Kem](../../../stratigraphy/k/kem-kem.yml).

**Evidence.**
- smyth2020a, p. 2: the age "remains controversial, with an Albian or Early Cenomanian age being considered likely by most recent workers".
- evers2015a, p. 6: "the Kem Kem assemblage is Cenomanian, and most likely Early Cenomanian in age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian–Cenomanian (Cau and Paterna 2025).* The phrase "the Kem Kem Assemblage from the Aptian-Cenomanian of Morocco" (cau2025a, p. 166) appears in a list of four units compared in a similarity analysis, with no evidence for the age. The paper defines "Kem Kem Assemblage" as a term for the vertebrate material from the Kem Kem beds that "does not make any explicit reference to a particular stratigraphic framework" (p. 163), so the label dates a faunal assemblage, not the group.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the Albian-or-Cenomanian question the entry's dispute block records.
