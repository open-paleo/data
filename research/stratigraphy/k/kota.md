# Kota

## Do the Hettangian, the Late Jurassic or the Early Cretaceous belong on the Kota?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No. The formation is Sinemurian–Aalenian: Sinemurian–Pliensbachian for the lower Kota, and Toarcian, possibly Aalenian, for the upper. Governs `period: [Early Jurassic, Middle Jurassic]` and `stages: [Sinemurian, Pliensbachian, Toarcian, Aalenian]` on [Kota](../../../stratigraphy/k/kota.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bandyopadhyay2010a, p. 533, Abstract: "the Early Jurassic Kota Formation (Sinemurian to Pliensbachian)"; p. 538: "the age of the upper Kota is Toarcian and may even be extended to Middle Jurassic (?Aalenian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hettangian.* Weishampel and colleagues' distribution chapter gives "Age: Hettangian-Pliensbachian" for the Kota Formation, with no source for the age (weishampel2004b, p. 534). No primary source read for this unit gives the Hettangian, and a reference work does not carry a value alone ([methods](../../methods.md)).
- *Early Cretaceous, on palynology.* Buffetaut and colleagues write that the Kota "may in fact be as recent as Early Cretaceous on the basis of palynology; G. V. R. Prasad, personal communication" (buffetaut2000a, p. 72). A personal communication is not a published determination; this is the case behind that rule in [methods](../../methods.md).
- *Late Jurassic and Early Cretaceous, from Rincón and colleagues (2022).* Their "age estimate has varied from Early Jurassic to Early Cretaceous depending upon the biostratigraphic indicator (see summary in Chinnappa et al., 2019)" reports other authors' endpoints. The paper's own statement is that "An Early–Middle Jurassic age is currently accepted for the upper part of the Kota Formation" (rincón2022a, p. 13, India).

**Open.** Whether the palynological Early Cretaceous was ever published. Chinnappa and colleagues (2019), the summary Rincón and colleagues cite, has not been read.
