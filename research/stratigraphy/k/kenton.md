# Kenton

## Does the Kenton keep the Kimmeridgian?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Held until the member is defined. Answers the 2026-11-26 entry's Open line in part. Schudack and colleagues (1998) date a sample from the upper Morrison of the Kenton section, 40 m above the Bell Ranch Formation, Kimmeridgian on their own charophyte and ostracod biozone 4, with the Kimmeridgian in French usage, succeeded by the Tithonian; the Saurophaganax quarry lies 12.04 m below the top of the Morrison (Chure 1995), so both are high in the formation near Kenton. Neither paper names the Kenton Member, which Danison and colleagues (2024) credit to Richmond and colleagues (2020), not held; placing the sample in the member would be our own correlation. Chure's (2000) "AGE: Kimmeridgian" for the quarry is a thesis and gives no basis. Governs `stages` on [Kenton](../../../stratigraphy/k/kenton.yml) and on [*Saurophaganax maximus*](../../../genera/S/Saurophaganax.yml), unchanged for now.

**Evidence.**
- schudack1998a, p. 407: "Type level. Upper part of Morrison Formation, Kimmeridgian, biozone 4 (this paper), 40 m above Bell Ranch Formation (Kenton section)."
- schudack1998a, p. 392: "Kimmeridgian in this report follows French usage (sensu gallico) in which the Kimmeridgian Stage is succeeded by the Tithonian Stage."
- chure1995a, p. 106: "Stratigraphic position is 12.04m (39. 5feet) below top of the Morrison Fm."
- chure2000a, p. 447: "AGE: Kimmeridgian."
- danison2024a, p. 81: the Kenton Member of the Morrison Formation in western Oklahoma "(Chure 1995; Richmond et al. 2020)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Kimmeridgian from Chure (2000).* A thesis never carries a value alone.

**Open.** Whether the Kenton Member, as Richmond and colleagues (2020) define it, includes the beds of Schudack and colleagues' biozone 4 sample.

## Does the Kenton keep the Tithonian?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Yes, on Carrano and colleagues (2012), not on the 148.96 Ma age. Carrano and colleagues give Stovall Quarry 1 directly as "?Tithonian, Late Jurassic"; the query marks their doubt about a reading they make, and a queried stage counts, as for the [Gruhalde](../g/gruhalde.md). The 148.96 Ma age dates Morrison beds in Utah, is relayed from Kowallis and colleagues (2007), and reaches the Kenton only through correlation, so it adds nothing. Governs the Tithonian in `stages` on the [Kenton Member](../../../stratigraphy/k/kenton.yml), unchanged; [*Saurophaganax maximus*](../../../genera/S/Saurophaganax.yml) is unaffected.

**Evidence.**
- carrano2012a, p. 241: "Stovall Quarry 1, east of Kenton, Cimarron County, Oklahoma, USA; upper Brushy Basin Member equivalent, Morrison Formation; ?Tithonian, Late Jurassic (Stovall 1938; Chure 1995)".
- kirkland2016a, p. 118: "148.96 +2.54 to -2.21 Ma based on U-Pb ages determined by laser ablation of zircons extracted from a volcanic ash bed about 9 m below the base of the Naturita Formation", west of Hanksville, Utah.
- danison2024a, p. 83: "The Kenton Member correlates with the Brushy Basin Member of the Morrison Formation, which is 153–145 million years old".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tithonian from the 148.96 Ma age.* A relayed date from Utah, reaching the Kenton by correlation.

**Open.** The Kimmeridgian. Its only support is the same kind of correlation: Kirkland and colleagues' 150.00 Ma from the top of the Brushy Basin Member in Utah, and Danison and colleagues' relayed 153–145 Ma for the Brushy Basin. No work read dates the Kenton beds themselves Kimmeridgian.

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Kenton Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian–Tithonian, by correlation. Carrano and colleagues (2012) place Stovall Quarry 1 in the "upper Brushy Basin Member equivalent" of the Morrison Formation; no work cited dates rock in Oklahoma. Kirkland and colleagues (2016) report the two youngest Morrison ages from Utah: 150.00 ± 0.52 Ma from near the top of the Brushy Basin Member, Kimmeridgian on the ICS chart v2024/12, and 148.96 +2.54/−2.21 Ma from an ash about 9 m below the base of the overlying Naturita Formation, Tithonian at its central value only, with an error range that reaches the Kimmeridgian. The first is Trujillo and Kowallis's (2015) recalibrated age. Both reach the Kenton only through Carrano and colleagues' correlation. Governs `stages` on the [Kenton Member](../../../stratigraphy/k/kenton.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carrano2012a, p. 241: "Stovall Quarry 1, east of Kenton, Cimarron County, Oklahoma, USA; upper Brushy Basin Member equivalent, Morrison Formation; ?Tithonian, Late Jurassic".
- kirkland2016a, p. 118: "148.96 +2.54 to -2.21 Ma based on U-Pb ages determined by laser ablation of zircons extracted from a volcanic ash bed about 9 m below the base of the Naturita Formation"; "150.00 ± 0.52 Ma from near the top of the Brushy Basin Member, near little Cedar Mesa, Emery County, Utah, based on a recalibrated 40Ar/39Ar age".
- trujillo2015a, p. 4, Table 1: sample LCM-39, recalibrated age 150.00 ± 0.52 Ma, "104.5 m above base of Brushy Basin Mbr.", Little Cedar Mountain, Emery County, Utah.

**Ruled out.**
- *Tithonian from Carrano and colleagues' "?Tithonian".* The label is queried and carries no number; it is an older chart's label, of the kind the [Morrison Tithonian labels](../../topics/morrison-tithonian-labels.md) note does not let add a stage.

**Open.** The 148.96 Ma age is relayed from Kowallis and colleagues (2007), which has not been read, and the Tithonian rests on that age's central value. A date from the Kenton Member itself, or the original determination, would settle whether the Tithonian belongs.
