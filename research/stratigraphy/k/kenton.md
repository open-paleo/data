# Kenton

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Kenton Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian–Tithonian, by correlation. Carrano and colleagues (2012) place Stovall Quarry 1 in the "upper Brushy Basin Member equivalent" of the Morrison Formation; no work cited dates rock in Oklahoma. Kirkland and colleagues (2016) report the two youngest Morrison ages from Utah: 150.00 ± 0.52 Ma from near the top of the Brushy Basin Member, Kimmeridgian on the ICS chart v2024/12, and 148.96 +2.54/−2.21 Ma from an ash about 9 m below the base of the overlying Naturita Formation, Tithonian at its central value only, with an error range that reaches the Kimmeridgian. The first is Trujillo and Kowallis's (2015) recalibrated age. Both reach the Kenton only through Carrano and colleagues' correlation. Governs `stages` on the [Kenton Member](../../../stratigraphy/k/kenton.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carrano2012a, p. 241: "Stovall Quarry 1, east of Kenton, Cimarron County, Oklahoma, USA; upper Brushy Basin Member equivalent, Morrison Formation; ?Tithonian, Late Jurassic".
- kirkland2016a, p. 118: "148.96 +2.54 to -2.21 Ma based on U-Pb ages determined by laser ablation of zircons extracted from a volcanic ash bed about 9 m below the base of the Naturita Formation"; "150.00 ± 0.52 Ma from near the top of the Brushy Basin Member, near little Cedar Mesa, Emery County, Utah, based on a recalibrated 40Ar/39Ar age".
- trujillo2015a, p. 4, Table 1: sample LCM-39, recalibrated age 150.00 ± 0.52 Ma, "104.5 m above base of Brushy Basin Mbr.", Little Cedar Mountain, Emery County, Utah.

**Ruled out.**
- *Tithonian from Carrano and colleagues' "?Tithonian".* The label is queried and carries no number; it is an older chart's label, of the kind the [Morrison Tithonian labels](../../topics/morrison-tithonian-labels.md) note does not let add a stage.

**Open.** The 148.96 Ma age is relayed from Kowallis and colleagues (2007), which has not been read, and the Tithonian rests on that age's central value. A date from the Kenton Member itself, or the original determination, would settle whether the Tithonian belongs.
