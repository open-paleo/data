# Nam Phong

## Is the Nam Phong one formation, and which part is Isanosaurus from?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Racey and Goodall (2009) split it, from subsurface data, into a Rhaetian Lower and a Late Jurassic(?) Upper Nam Phong separated by an unconformity that has not been found at outcrop; the [*Isanosaurus attavipachi*](../../../genera/I/Isanosaurus.yml) site cannot be assigned to either part. Governs keeping Nam Phong as one entry with the union of both parts' ages, and the same span on *Isanosaurus*.

**Evidence.**
- racey2009b, p. 69: "the Nam Phong Formation comprises two distinct units separated by an unconformity"; "The age of the Lower Nam Phong Formation based on palynology is Late Triassic (Rhaetian)"; p. 81: the Upper Nam Phong is "considered to be no older than Pliensbachian"; p. 82: "a Late Jurassic age is tentatively proposed".
- racey2009b, pp. 80–81: "This unconformity has yet to be identified at outcrop"; p. 78: "The Nam Phong Formation at outcrop has, to date, yielded only Late Triassic (late Norian to Rhaetian) palynomorphs".

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper Nam Phong, Early Jurassic, by Racey and Goodall.* Rauhut and colleagues (2020) write that "the Upper Nam Phong Formation that has yielded *Isanosaurus* has recently been dated as Early Jurassic (Racey and Goodall 2009)" (rauhut2020a, p. 2). Racey and Goodall never mention *Isanosaurus* and give the upper part as no older than Pliensbachian, tentatively Late Jurassic.
- *Early Middle Jurassic.* Hanta and colleagues' title gives the site an early Middle Jurassic age (hanta2023a, p. 222), but the paper places the beds only "likely" in the Upper Nam Phong by rock type (p. 238, partly after Booth and Sattayarak 2011) and cites a lower bound "no earlier than Pliensbachian" from an ostracod (p. 224, after Chitnarin and colleagues 2022); nothing supports a Middle Jurassic age.

**Open.** Which part holds the *Isanosaurus* site. Jia and colleagues (2022) report a detrital-zircon maximum of about 208 Ma for the Nam Phong in another basin (jia2022a, p. 3481), which was not weighed here.
