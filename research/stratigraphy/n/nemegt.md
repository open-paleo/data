# Nemegt

## Does Khand and colleagues' Nogoontsav Formation need an entry?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No. It is the 20 m of beds above the Nemegt at Nogoon Tsav and Bugiin Tsav, and the one record from Nogoon Tsav, [*Alioramus remotus*](../../../genera/A/Alioramus.yml), comes from the lower Nemegt. Governs nothing on [Nemegt](../../../stratigraphy/n/nemegt.yml).

**Evidence.** None.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is Karczewska and Ziembińska-Tworzydło's Lower Campanian a reading?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No; it is a bound, and the stages stand. Confirms the 2026-11-23 entry. Karczewska and Ziembińska-Tworzydło (1983) find from the formation's charophytes that it "is not younger than the Lower Campanian", a limit rather than an age, and Van Itterbeeck and colleagues (2005) reject the estimate, since the Nemegt overlies the Baruungoyot, which radiometric dates make Campanian. Their note now says both. Governs the notes on karczewska1983a and vanitterbeeck2005b on [Nemegt](../../../stratigraphy/n/nemegt.yml); `stages` unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- karczewska1983a, p. 137: "Comparisons with the Chinese, European and American assemblages indicate that the Nemegt Formation is not younger than the Lower Campanian, thus is older than it was supposed before."
- vanitterbeeck2005b, p. 714: "Since the Nemegt Formation overlies the Barun Goyot (Gradzinski et al., 1977, fig. 12), it must be younger; accordingly, we regard the age estimation for the Nemegt Formation as proposed by Karczewska".

**Ruled out.**
- *A pre-Campanian Nemegt.* A bound, rejected by later work.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Nemegt?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian and Maastrichtian, as the union of what the sources publish. Khand and colleagues (2000) give the formation as Maastrichtian in the eastern Gobi and Campanian–Maastrichtian at Bugiin Tsav, and report no uppermost Maastrichtian strata anywhere in Mongolia. They also take out of the formation the 20 m of beds above it at Nogoon Tsav and Bugiin Tsav, which Shuvalov (1974) had included in the Nemegt and Khand (1987) called the Upper Nemegt, as the Nogoontsav Formation; an age read in Shuvalov's sense of the name can include those beds. Karczewska and Ziembinska-Tworzydlo (1983) place the formation, on its charophytes, no younger than the Lower Campanian, and set that against the uppermost Campanian to Maastrichtian of Fox (1978) and the ?Upper Campanian and/or ?Lower Maastrichtian of Gradziński, Kielan-Jaworowska and Maryańska (1977), both of which they relay. Their reading adds the Campanian, already carried, and removes nothing: Van Itterbeeck and colleagues (2005) reject it because the Nemegt overlies a Barun Goyot dated radiometrically as Campanian. Governs `stages: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on [Nemegt](../../../stratigraphy/n/nemegt.yml).

**Evidence.**
- khand2000a, p. 63, 2.3.3. Basins of Dornogobi (Eastern Gobi): "The Nemegt Formation (up to 70 m) overlies the Javkhlant Formation", with a fauna and ostracods "that appears to be of the Maastrichtian age".
- khand2000a, p. 68, 3.3. Plants: *Nyssoidea mongolica* "was also found from the Nemegt Formation (Campanian-Maastrichtian) at the locality Bugiin Tsav".
- khand2000a, p. 59, 2.3.1. Basins of Transaltai Gobi: "The Nogoontsav Formation (= Nemegt Formation by Shuvalov [9]; Upper Nemegt Formation by Khand [10]) (20 m thick) is distributed in the Nogoon Tsav and Bugiin Tsav depressions and conformably overlies the Nemegt Formation".
- khand2000a, p. 64, 2.4. Conclusion: "The uppermost Maastrichtian strata have not been documented in Mongolia."
- karczewska1983a, p. 137, abstract: the assemblage "is intermediate between two Upper Cretaceous assemblages from the "Red Beds" from China", and "the Nemegt Formation is not younger than the Lower Campanian, thus is older than it was supposed before".
- karczewska1983a, p. 143: "the assemblage of the Charophyta from the Nemegt Formation can not be considered younger than the Lower Campanian"; relaying Gradziński, Kielan-Jaworowska and Maryańska (1977), "Nemegt Formation — ?Upper Campanian and/or ?Lower Maastrichtian".
- karczewska1983a, p. 144: "According to Fox, the Nemegt Formation is of the uppermost Campanian to Maastrichtian age. This interpretation disagrees with our suggestion based on Charophyta from the Nemegt Formation. The occurrence of the old taxa known from Lower/Upper Cretaceous transition indicates that the Nemegt Formation is not younger than Lower Campanian"; Table 2 sets the Nemegt in the Lower Campanian.
- vanitterbeeck2005b, p. 714: "Karczewska and Ziembinska-Tworzydlo (1983) proposed a Santonian age for the Barun Goyot Formation; however, radiometric calibrations (Shuvalov, 2000) have provided a Campanian age for this formation. Since the Nemegt Formation overlies the Barun Goyot ..., it must be younger; accordingly, we regard the age estimation for the Nemegt Formation as proposed by Karczewska and Ziembinska-Tworzydlo (1983) as incorrect".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Nemegt no younger than the Lower Campanian.* Karczewska and Ziembinska-Tworzydlo (1983) reach it by comparing the charophyte assemblage with those of China, Europe and the Americas, and set the underlying Barun Goyot and Djadokhta older still. Van Itterbeeck and colleagues (2005) report that radiometric calibration has since given the Barun Goyot a Campanian age; the Nemegt lies above it, so the ceiling fails, and they call the estimate "incorrect" on that ground. The Lower Campanian it gives stays within the Campanian the union already carries.

**Open.** Fox (1978) and Gradziński and colleagues (1977) are read here only as Karczewska and Ziembinska-Tworzydlo relay them.
