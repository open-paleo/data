## Which fossils and sites does Jin and colleagues' (2022) Nanxiong–Guifeng equation cover?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Jin and colleagues (2022) apply "Nanxiong Formation (= Guifeng Group)" to the red beds around the Gannan Basin, the vertebrate-bearing part of the Ganzhou Basin, and list under it the oviraptorids *Banji long*, *Ganzhousaurus nankangensis*, *Jiangxisaurus ganzhouensis*, *Nankangia jiangxiensis*, *Huanansaurus ganzhouensis* and *Tongtianlong limosus*, the tyrannosaurids *Qianzhousaurus sinensis* and *Gannansaurus*, and other vertebrates. Their own multituberculate, from the Ganzhou railway station and the Third High School of Ganxian County, they place in the Hekou Formation of the Guifeng Group, Maastrichtian, and on the same page as the equation they also call it the "Hekou Formation of Nanxiong Group", so the paper uses both rank words. The equation is the one the [Nanxiong Formation and the Ganzhou Basin](../../topics/nanxiong-and-guifeng.md) topic note quotes from p. 2, and that note governs which unit Ganzhou taxa take. Governs the account of Jin and colleagues' usage in `dispute.summary` on [Nanxiong](../../../stratigraphy/n/nanxiong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jin2022a, p. 2, Geological setting: "Vertebrate fossils were also reported from the Nanxiong Formation (= Guifeng Group) that is exposed in the vicinity of the Gannan Basin; these fossils include the oviraptorids *Banji long* …, *Ganzhousaurus nankangensis* …, *Jiangxisaurus ganzhouensis* …, *Nankangia jiangxiensis*".
- jin2022a, p. 2, Geological setting: the specimens "were collected from the Upper Cretaceous Hekou Formation of Nanxiong Group, Ganzhou City".
- jin2022a, p. 7, *Erythrobaatar ganensis*, Locality and Horizon: "Ganzhou railway station (GM30516) and Third High School of Ganxian County (GM30496); Hekou Formation (Upper Cretaceous, Maastrichtian), Guifeng Group".

**Ruled out.** Nothing among the works cited here.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the topic note's 2026-09-30 **Open** line, which covers the Hekou placement of these sites.
