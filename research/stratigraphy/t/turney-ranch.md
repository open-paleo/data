# Turney Ranch

## Does the Turney Ranch Formation keep its Albian?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Unlike units whose "Albian" was only a pre-2012 label for dates near 97–99 Ma, the Turney Ranch is bracketed by dates reaching back to about 110 Ma. Governs `stages: [Albian, Cenomanian]` on Turney Ranch and on [*Sonorasaurus thompsoni*](../../../genera/S/Sonorasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- d'emic2016a, p. 112: "radiometric dates bracket the age of Sonorasaurus between ca. 98 and ca. 110 Ma"; "the Turney Ranch Formation is likely younger than ~110 Ma"; "late Albian-early Cenomanian age estimates for the Cintura Formation based on fossil bivalves", a possibly coeval unit.

**Ruled out.**
- *An Albian resting on "(Albian) +. 97 Ma".* Ratkevich gives "Age: Middle Cretaceous (Albian) +. 97 Ma" (ratkevich1998a, p. 72); on the current chart 97 Ma is Cenomanian, so this label alone would not support the Albian. The same paper says the formation "does indeed span the Albian-Cenomanian boundary" (p. 80).

**Open.** Nothing.
