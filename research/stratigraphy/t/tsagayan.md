# Tsagayan

## Does the Udurchukan take a Tsagayan Group as parent, and how old is it?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Yes; the group is Maastrichtian to Danian. Van Itterbeeck and colleagues (2005) give the Udurchukan and Bureya formations as the lower and middle parts of the Tsagayan Group, the scheme behind Alifanov and Bolotsky's (2010) "previously referred to as the Tsagayan Formation or Lower Tsagayan Formation". Zherikhin (1978), treating the unit as a formation, judges it safer to date it within the Maastrichtian–Danian interval. The Bureya needs no entry while no record names it. Governs the new entry [Tsagayan](../../../stratigraphy/t/tsagayan.yml) and `parent` on [Udurchukan](../../../stratigraphy/u/udurchukan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zherikhin1978a, p. 81: "возраст тех, и других отложений осторожнее определять в интервале маастрихт - даний" ["the age of both deposits is more safely determined within the Maastrichtian–Danian interval", translated].

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
