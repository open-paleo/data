# Talarn

## How is the Talarn Formation defined, and how close to the Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary does it reach?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Cuevas (1992) erects the "Formación Lutitas, Areniscas y Conglomerados de Talarn" in the Tremp Group, with a type section of about 140 m in the Barranco de La Mata, southeast of Gurp: red and variegated mudstones, sandstones and conglomerates in a fining-upward megasequence, with no age given. Prieto-Márquez and Sellés (2022) place their Masia de Ramón site in the upper part of the formation, "of the Tremp Group", in the middle of chron C29r, 25 m below the local K–Pg boundary and so within the last 100,000 years of the Maastrichtian; they do not place the boundary inside the formation. Their Fig. 1 draws a hiatus between the Conques and the Talarn, which Fondevilla and colleagues (2019) give as the missing chrons C31n to C30n. The Talarn's Danian, its rank within the Tremp Group, and its absence from the western Tremp Syncline are settled in the [Tremp](tremp.md) entries of 2026-09-30, 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-21; this entry adds the passages from Cuevas and from Prieto-Márquez and Sellés behind them. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]`, `parent: Tremp` and `lithology` on [Talarn](../../../stratigraphy/t/talarn.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cuevas1992a, p. 97: among the units into which the material "designated as «Garumniense» or «Formación Tremp»" is subdivided (translated), "3) Formación Lutitas, Areniscas y Conglomerados de Talarn" ("Talarn Mudstones, Sandstones and Conglomerates Formation", translated); the Conques entry beside it lists two members, the Talarn none.
- cuevas1992a, p. 101: "Formada por lutitas rojas y versicolores, areniscas y conglomerados" ("composed of red and variegated mudstones, sandstones and conglomerates", translated); "El espesor es de unos 140 m en la serie tipo (barranco de la Mata, al SE de Gurp)" ("The thickness is about 140 m in the type section (Barranco de la Mata, SE of Gurp)", translated); the formation "se presenta como una megasecuencia granodecreciente" ("appears as a fining-upward megasequence", translated).
- prieto-márquez2022a, p. 4, Locality, Horizon, and Age: "Masia de Ramón falls within the upper part of the fluvial Talarn Formation of the Tremp Group and isotopic, magnetostratigraphic, and biochronological calibrations indicate that the locality correlates with the middle part of the C29r magnetochron (latest Maastrichtian)"; it "occurs 25 m below the local K-Pg boundary, thus placing it within the last 100,000 years of the Maastrichtian".
- prieto-márquez2022a, p. 2, Fig. 1: the stratigraphic column shows a "Hiatus" between the Conques Fm, in chron C31r, and the Talarn Fm, in chron C29r; the figure prints no duration for it.
- fondevilla2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 11: "Most of the late Maastrichtian (chrons C31n to C30n) is not recorded in the sector".

**Ruled out.** Nothing beyond the readings the 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-21 entries in [Tremp](tremp.md) rule out.

**Open.** How long the hiatus below the Talarn lasts. Neither Prieto-Márquez and Sellés nor Fondevilla and colleagues print a duration; a figure would need the chron ages on a stated time scale.
