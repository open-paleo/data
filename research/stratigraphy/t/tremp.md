# Tremp

## Is Tremp a group or a formation, and when can a formation be named within it?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** A group. Its formations (La Posa, Conques, Talarn) are defined in the eastern Tremp Syncline; in the western syncline only the group is named. An older "Tremp Formation" attribution is coarser, not conflicting. Governs `rank: group` on Tremp, `parent: Tremp` on [Talarn](../../../stratigraphy/t/talarn.yml) and [Conques](../../../stratigraphy/c/conques.yml), and `group: Tremp` with no formation on western taxa such as [*Arenysaurus ardevoli*](../../../genera/A/Arenysaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fondevilla2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 6: the unit was "later defined as Tremp Formation (Fm) by Mey et al. (1968)"; p. 7: "Cuevas (1992) and Pujalte and Schmitz (2006) redefined the Tremp Fm as Tremp Group"; p. 8: "the La Posa Fm represents the 'Grey Garumnian'; the Conques and Talarn formations are equivalent to the 'Lower Red Garumnian'"; "the terms 'Garumnian', 'Garumnian facies' and Tremp Fm were widely used by researchers until recently".
- serrano2021a, pre-proof, p. 9: the Talarn units "correspond to the upper Maastrichtian C29r chron (Fondevilla et al., 2019)".
- prieto-márquez2013a, p. 2: "the Paleogene portion of the Talarn Formation"; dallavecchia2014a, p. 300: "the Cretaceous portion of the Talarn Formation of Cuevas, 1992".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tremp Formation as a rival rank.* It is the 1968 rank that Cuevas (1992) replaced; a paper that writes "Tremp Formation" is using the older, coarser scheme.

**Open.** A chron-to-formation rule for placing taxa: in the Isona–Orcau sector, chron C31r falls in the La Posa and Conques formations, "the complete La Posa and Conques formations" being early Maastrichtian (fondevilla2019a, pp. 6, 10–11), and C29r in the Talarn. Applying this outside that sector is a generalization. Fondevilla and colleagues name no formation for the western sections (p. 12); that none can be named there is an inference. The group and the Talarn reach into the Danian on Prieto-Márquez and colleagues' reading, which the registry's stage lists do not show.
