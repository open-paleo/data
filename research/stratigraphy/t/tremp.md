# Tremp

## What do Cuevas (1992) and Pereda-Suberbiola and colleagues (2009) say about the Tremp Group's limits and its western sites?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Cuevas (1992) defines the Tremp Group in the Conca de Tremp by grouping six formations, from the La Posa to the Claret, between the marine Arén Sandstone below, which he gives as Maastrichtian and into which the lower part of the group passes laterally, and the marine Ilerdian beds above. The only stages the paper prints belong to those bounding units and to earlier authors' units that it reviews; it gives none for its own formations, so the group's stages rest on the dating papers weighed in the 2026-10-07 entry. Pereda-Suberbiola and colleagues (2009) use the older Tremp Formation: at Arén, Blasi 1 lies on top of the Arén Sandstone and the other Blasi sites in "the lower part of the overlying Tremp Formation", which is the *Arenysaurus* type horizon. That is the coarser 1968 unit, not a rival to the group, as the 2026-09-30 entry concludes. This entry adds the passages behind those two points. Governs `rank: group` and `stages` on [Tremp](../../../stratigraphy/t/tremp.yml), and `group: Tremp` with no formation on [*Arenysaurus ardevoli*](../../../genera/A/Arenysaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cuevas1992a, p. 95: the materials studied lie between "los materiales infrayacentes marinos de edad Maastrichtiense de la formación Areniscas de Arén (Mey et al., 1968), a la que pasan lateralmente en la parte inferior" ("the underlying marine materials of Maastrichtian age of the Arén Sandstone formation, into which they pass laterally in the lower part", translated) and "los materiales suprayacentes marinos de edad Ilerdiense" ("the overlying marine materials of Ilerdian age", translated).
- cuevas1992a, p. 97: "las seis formaciones se agrupan en una unidad de rango superior denominada «Grupo de Tremp»" ("the six formations are grouped into a unit of higher rank called the «Grupo de Tremp»", translated).
- cuevas1992a, p. 95, abstract: "The Tremp Group has been defined as a new unit which comprises five formations and is equivalent to the Tremp Formation."
- pereda-suberbiola2009a, p. 560, 1. Introduction: *Pararhabdodon isonensis* "from the Maastrichtian of the Tremp Formation, in Lleida".
- pereda-suberbiola2009a, p. 560, 3. Geological and chronological frame: "Blasi 1 is located on the top of the Aren Sandstone (Fig. 2) whereas the other sites are located in the lower part of the overlying Tremp Formation."
- pereda-suberbiola2009a, pp. 562, 564, 4.6. Type locality and horizon: "Blasi 3 site in Aren (Huesca, Spain); lower part of the Tremp Formation; Late Cretaceous, Late Maastrichtian (upper part of chron 30n)".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Tremp Formation at Arén as a competing unit or rank.* Pereda-Suberbiola and colleagues (2009) write the 1968 Tremp Formation that Cuevas (1992) made a group, so their attribution is the same rock at a coarser resolution, not a conflicting claim (the 2026-09-30 entry). Reading it as a formation of the group would misplace the Blasi sites in the Talarn or another formation the paper does not name; the 2026-09-30 entry records that no formation can be named in the western syncline as an inference.
- *Five formations.* Cuevas's English abstract says the group "comprises five formations", but the paper lists six and groups "las seis formaciones" (p. 97). The abstract's count is a slip that the paper's own text corrects, under the rule on slips in [methods](../../methods.md).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does Pujalte and Schmitz's "upper Maastrichtian–Danian" put the Talarn in the Danian?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** No. Pujalte and Schmitz (2005) give the Talarn that age after redefining its top by a lithohorizon alone, which in the Esplugafreda ravine moves beds previously assigned to the Esplugafreda Formation into the Talarn. Their Danian belongs to the formation as they redraw it, not to the Talarn of Cuevas (1992) that the registry follows, so the 2026-10-07 conclusion stands; this entry adds the paper, which that entry did not weigh. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]` on [Talarn](../../../stratigraphy/t/talarn.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- pujalte2005a, p. 82: "la Fm Talarn corresponde al Maastrichtiense superior-Daniense" ("the Talarn Fm corresponds to the upper Maastrichtian-Danian", translated), the age resting on the Tremp-Colmenar horizon, which "separa carbonatos Danienses y Thanetienses en el corte de Campo (Eichenseer, 1988)" ("separates Danian and Thanetian carbonates in the Campo section", translated).
- pujalte2005a, p. 81: "nuestra propuesta ... es fijar los límites inferiores de las Fms Esplugafreda y Claret exclusivamente mediante el criterio de litohorizontes" ("our proposal ... is to fix the lower boundaries of the Esplugafreda and Claret Fms exclusively by means of the lithohorizon criterion", translated).
- cuevas1992a, p. 101: the Talarn type section in the Barranco de La Mata is about 140 m, its top the Esplugafreda Formation west of the Noguera Pallaresa and the Suterranya and Sant Salvador limestones east of it.

**Ruled out.**
- *The Danian for the Talarn of Cuevas (1992).* The age comes from a boundary drawn higher than his, and the Danian of the Tremp Group is already recorded on the group, where the beds in question fall under either scheme.

**Open.** Whether the registry should follow Pujalte and Schmitz's boundary instead of Cuevas's. That is a choice between schemes, and it would move the Talarn's top and add the Danian together.

## Does the Tremp Group reach the Danian, and does the Talarn?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** The group does; the Talarn does not. The Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary lies inside the Tremp Group, at or just above the top of its lower red unit (the Conques and Talarn formations), at a hiatus below the Danian Suterranya and Vallcebre limestones, and the group continues into Selandian–Thanetian red beds. The Talarn below the boundary is Maastrichtian. Governs `period: [Late Cretaceous, Paleocene]` and the Danian, Selandian and Thanetian in `stages` on [Tremp](../../../stratigraphy/t/tremp.yml), and `stages: [Maastrichtian]` on [Talarn](../../../stratigraphy/t/talarn.yml). Supersedes the 2026-09-30 entry's **Open** line that the Talarn reaches into the Danian.

**Evidence.**
- cuevas1992a: the six formations, from the Posa to the Claret, "se agrupan en una unidad de rango superior denominada «Grupo de Tremp»" ("are grouped into a unit of higher rank called the Tremp Group", translated), with the Calizas de Suterranya y Sant Salvador de Toló above the Talarn.
- díez-canseco2014a, p. 43: "the Lower Red Unit in the studied section may therefore be exclusively Maastrichtian"; the Suterranya Limestone is upper Danian, and "at least the first 4 Ma of the Danian are absent in the Tremp Formation"; the boundary "might be located at the top of the Lower Red Unit or not be there at all".
- rosell2001a, p. 52: the boundary is placed "en torno al techo de las arcillas del Garumniense rojo inferior muy cerca del contacto con las calizas" ("around the top of the clays of the lower red Garumnian, very close to the contact with the limestones", translated), the Danian Vallcebre limestones.
- fondevilla2019a, p. 170: the upper red Garumnian, within the group, is "dated as Selandian to Thanetian".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Talarn straddling the boundary.* Prieto-Márquez and colleagues (2013) relay "the Paleogene portion of the Talarn Formation" from Cuevas (1992), whose paper gives the Talarn no age and does not divide it; Prieto-Márquez and colleagues (2022) place their latest Talarn site "25 m below the local K-Pg boundary" (prieto-márquez2022a).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is Tremp a group or a formation, and when can a formation be named within it?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** A group. Its formations (La Posa, Conques, Talarn) are defined in the eastern Tremp Syncline; in the western syncline only the group is named. An older "Tremp Formation" attribution is coarser, not conflicting. Governs `rank: group` on Tremp, `parent: Tremp` on [Talarn](../../../stratigraphy/t/talarn.yml) and [Conques](../../../stratigraphy/c/conques.yml), and `group: Tremp` with no formation on western taxa such as [*Arenysaurus ardevoli*](../../../genera/A/Arenysaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fondevilla2019a, p. 170: the unit was "later defined as Tremp Formation (Fm) by Mey et al. (1968)"; "Cuevas (1992) and Pujalte and Schmitz (2005) redefined the Tremp Fm as Tremp Group"; "the La Posa Fm represents the 'Grey Garumnian'; the Conques and Talarn formations are equivalent to the 'Lower Red Garumnian'"; "the terms 'Garumnian', 'Garumnian facies' and Tremp Fm were widely used by researchers until recently".
- serrano2021a, pre-proof, p. 9: the Talarn units "correspond to the upper Maastrichtian C29r chron (Fondevilla et al., 2019)".
- prieto-márquez2013a, p. 2: "the Paleogene portion of the Talarn Formation"; dallavecchia2014a, p. 300: "the Cretaceous portion of the Talarn Formation of Cuevas, 1992".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tremp Formation as a rival rank.* It is the 1968 rank that Cuevas (1992) replaced; a paper that writes "Tremp Formation" is using the older, coarser scheme.

**Open.** A chron-to-formation rule for placing taxa: in the Isona–Orcau sector, chron C31r falls in the La Posa and Conques formations, "the complete La Posa and Conques formations" being early Maastrichtian (fondevilla2019a, pp. 170, 172), and C29r in the Talarn. Applying this outside that sector is a generalization. Fondevilla and colleagues name no formation for the western sections (p. 173); that none can be named there is an inference. The group and the Talarn reach into the Danian on Prieto-Márquez and colleagues' reading, which the registry's stage lists do not show.
