# Tremp

## Does the Tremp Group reach the Danian, and does the Talarn?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** The group does; the Talarn does not. The Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary lies inside the Tremp Group, at or just above the top of its lower red unit (the Conques and Talarn formations), at a hiatus below the Danian Suterranya and Vallcebre limestones, and the group continues into Selandian–Thanetian red beds. The Talarn below the boundary is Maastrichtian. Governs `period: [Late Cretaceous, Paleocene]` and the Danian, Selandian and Thanetian in `stages` on [Tremp](../../../stratigraphy/t/tremp.yml), and `stages: [Maastrichtian]` on [Talarn](../../../stratigraphy/t/talarn.yml). Supersedes the 2026-09-30 entry's **Open** line that the Talarn reaches into the Danian.

**Evidence.**
- cuevas1992a: the six formations, from the Posa to the Claret, "se agrupan en una unidad de rango superior denominada «Grupo de Tremp»" ("are grouped into a unit of higher rank called the Tremp Group", translated), with the Calizas de Suterranya y Sant Salvador de Toló above the Talarn.
- díez-canseco2014a, p. 43: "the Lower Red Unit in the studied section may therefore be exclusively Maastrichtian"; the Suterranya Limestone is upper Danian, and "at least the first 4 Ma of the Danian are absent in the Tremp Formation"; the boundary "might be located at the top of the Lower Red Unit or not be there at all".
- rosell2001a, p. 52: the boundary is placed "en torno al techo de las arcillas del Garumniense rojo inferior muy cerca del contacto con las calizas" ("around the top of the clays of the lower red Garumnian, very close to the contact with the limestones", translated), the Danian Vallcebre limestones.
- fondevilla2019a: the upper red Garumnian, within the group, is "dated as Selandian to Thanetian".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Talarn straddling the boundary.* Prieto-Márquez and colleagues (2013) relay "the Paleogene portion of the Talarn Formation" from Cuevas (1992), whose paper gives the Talarn no age and does not divide it; Prieto-Márquez and colleagues (2022) place their latest Talarn site "25 m below the local K-Pg boundary" (prieto-márquez2022a).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is Tremp a group or a formation, and when can a formation be named within it?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** A group. Its formations (La Posa, Conques, Talarn) are defined in the eastern Tremp Syncline; in the western syncline only the group is named. An older "Tremp Formation" attribution is coarser, not conflicting. Governs `rank: group` on Tremp, `parent: Tremp` on [Talarn](../../../stratigraphy/t/talarn.yml) and [Conques](../../../stratigraphy/c/conques.yml), and `group: Tremp` with no formation on western taxa such as [*Arenysaurus ardevoli*](../../../genera/A/Arenysaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fondevilla2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 6: the unit was "later defined as Tremp Formation (Fm) by Mey et al. (1968)"; p. 7: "Cuevas (1992) and Pujalte and Schmitz (2006) redefined the Tremp Fm as Tremp Group"; p. 8: "the La Posa Fm represents the 'Grey Garumnian'; the Conques and Talarn formations are equivalent to the 'Lower Red Garumnian'"; "the terms 'Garumnian', 'Garumnian facies' and Tremp Fm were widely used by researchers until recently".
- serrano2021a, pre-proof, p. 9: the Talarn units "correspond to the upper Maastrichtian C29r chron (Fondevilla et al., 2019)".
- prieto-márquez2013a, p. 2: "the Paleogene portion of the Talarn Formation"; dallavecchia2014a, p. 300: "the Cretaceous portion of the Talarn Formation of Cuevas, 1992".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tremp Formation as a rival rank.* It is the 1968 rank that Cuevas (1992) replaced; a paper that writes "Tremp Formation" is using the older, coarser scheme.

**Open.** A chron-to-formation rule for placing taxa: in the Isona–Orcau sector, chron C31r falls in the La Posa and Conques formations, "the complete La Posa and Conques formations" being early Maastrichtian (fondevilla2019a, pp. 6, 10–11), and C29r in the Talarn. Applying this outside that sector is a generalization. Fondevilla and colleagues name no formation for the western sections (p. 12); that none can be named there is an inference. The group and the Talarn reach into the Danian on Prieto-Márquez and colleagues' reading, which the registry's stage lists do not show.
