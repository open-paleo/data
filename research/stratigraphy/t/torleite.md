## How do Niebuhr and Pürner (2014) define and date the Torleite Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Niebuhr and Pürner (2014) define the formation in the Weißjura Group, naming it after the Torleite, a hill southwest of Dollnstein, and credit its original description to their own work, based on Zeiss (1964, 1977). Ammonites date it to the middle and upper Upper Kimmeridgian, upper *pseudomutabilis* Zone to upper *beckeri* Zone; its top is diachronous, in the middle *beckeri* Zone at the type locality on the western Southern Frankenalb and in the highest *beckeri* Zone near Schamhaupten and Painten in the east. It rests on the Treuchtlingen or the Lower Frankenalb Formation, is overlain by the Geisental and Painten formations in the Southern Frankenalb, and is 20 to over 70 m thick. Its subdivisions are the Netzstall, Arnstorf, Ebenwies, Fürnried, Engelhardsberg and Wattendorf subformations, with the Red Marl Layer at the top at the type locality; the Netzstall below and the Arnstorf above make up the formation on the eastern Southern Frankenalb. The *Juravenator* holotype comes from the Arnstorf's siliceous platy facies at Schamhaupten, and the *Sciurumimus* holotype from the same facies in the Rygol quarry near Painten, the second on a written communication. Governs `stages: [Kimmeridgian]` and the lithology on [Torleite](../../../stratigraphy/t/torleite.yml).

**Evidence.**
- niebuhr2014a, p. 31: "Named after the Torleite, a hill southwest of the market town of Dollnstein"; "Superordinate Units: Weißjura Group"; "Original Description: Niebuhr (this work), based on Zeiss (1964a, 1977)"; "Chronostratigraphy: Upper Kimmeridgian" (translated).
- niebuhr2014a, pp. 31–32, Subordinate Units: the Netzstall Subformation forms "the entire Torleite Formation on the western Southern Frankenalb", and on the eastern Southern and Central Frankenalb its "lowermost part ... underlying the Arnstorf Subformation"; the Red Marl Layer, Ebenwies, Fürnried, Engelhardsberg and Wattendorf subformations follow (translated).
- niebuhr2014a, p. 32: "Lower Boundary: Micritic bedded limestones, platy limestones, and platy chert of the Torleite Formation rest ... on the up to 1.50 m thick sponge-microbialite and intraclast limestones of the Treuchtlingen Formation, and on the other hand, on massive dolomites and limestones of the Lower Frankenalb Formation"; "Upper Boundary: Geisental and Painten Formations of the Southern Frankenalb, on the Central and Northern Frankenalb eroded away"; "Thickness: 20 to over 70 m" (translated).
- niebuhr2014a, p. 33, Age Assignment: "Classification using ammonites yields the middle and upper Upper Kimmeridgian (upper pseudomutabilis zone to upper beckeri zone)" (translated).
- niebuhr2014a, p. 34: "the upper boundary of the Torleite Formation is diachronous (middle beckeri zone on the western Southern Franconian Alb at its type locality – highest beckeri zone on the eastern Southern Franconian Alb near Schamhaupten and Painten)" (translated).
- niebuhr2014a, p. 34, Fossils: "The siliceous platy facies of the Arnstorf Subformation is extraordinarily fossil-rich. Exemplary, the famous fossil locality Schamhaupten yielded over 200 different taxa ..., including the theropod Juravenator starki"; *Sciurumimus albersdoerferi* "was also found in the Rygol quarry near Painten in the siliceous platy facies (R. Albersdörfer, written communication 07/2012)" (translated).

**Ruled out.** Nothing.
