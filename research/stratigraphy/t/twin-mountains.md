# Twin Mountains

## Is the Twin Mountains Upper Cretaceous?

*2026-09-21*

**Conclusion.** No. Early Cretaceous, Aptian–Albian. Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous]` on [Twin Mountains](../../../stratigraphy/t/twin-mountains.yml).

**Evidence.**
- andrzejewski2019a, p. 1: "the Twin Mountains Formation (Aptian) of north-central Texas".
- d'emic2012c, Systematic Palaeontology, *Paluxysaurus jonesi*: "Early Cretaceous (Aptian–Albian) Twin Mountains Formation of north-central Texas".

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper Cretaceous.* Myers and Storrs (2007) write "The Jones Ranch site, located in the Upper Cretaceous Twin Mountains Formation in Texas" (p. 663), and their Table 2, on the same page, lists that Texas site as "Early Cretaceous". Both cite Winkler and colleagues (2000), who give "the Lower Cretaceous Twin Mountains Formation of Texas" (p. 100) and "the Early Cretaceous Twin Mountains Formation", and never Upper or Late Cretaceous. A slip that its own table contradicts (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-slip-that-its-own-document-contradicts-is-not-a-reading)).

**Open.** Nothing.
