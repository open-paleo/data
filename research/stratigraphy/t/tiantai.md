# Tiantai

## What is the age of the Tiantai Group?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Albian–Turonian, from dates on its formations. Governs `stages: [Albian, Cenomanian, Turonian]` on Tiantai and its [Liangtoutang (Laijia) Formation](../../../stratigraphy/l/liangtoutang.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xi2018a, p. 273: "zircon SIMS U-Pb ages of 99–96 and 94–91 Ma in the Laijia and Chichengshan Formations of the Tiantai Group", credited to He and colleagues (2012; the paper meant is He and colleagues 2013, J. Asian Earth Sci. 62).
- yu2010a, p. 98: the Tangshang volcanics "(109.2±1.9)Ma" (biotite Ar–Ar, translated); p. 99: a zircon U-Pb age "near the top of the Chichengshan Formation" of 94 Ma (translated), from an unpublished 2004 survey report; conclusions: "早白垩世晚期albian期至晚白垩世早期" ("late Early Cretaceous, Albian, to early Late Cretaceous", translated).
- xi2021a, p. 385: the Liangtoutang of the provincial bureau is the Laijia Formation (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- Nothing considered was rejected on the text.

**Open.** The top. Xi and colleagues' correlation charts draw the Chichengshan up to about 87 Ma, in the Coniacian (xi2018a, Fig. 9; xi2021a, Fig. 4, with a queried box to about 83 Ma), against their text and Yu and colleagues' dates.
