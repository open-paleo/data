# Tiantai

## Where do the dates on the Tiantai Group's formations fall on the current chart?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Albian at the base, Cenomanian and Turonian above. On the ICS chart v2024/12, the 109.2 Ma age from the lower Tangshang Formation is Albian, the 99–96 Ma ages from the Laijia Formation are Cenomanian, and the 94–91 Ma ages from the Chichengshan Formation run from the latest Cenomanian into the Turonian. Yu and colleagues (2010) also tie the lower Tangshang to the Guantou Formation by its fossils, and give the Chichengshan's 94 Ma twice, once for its volcanics and once for those near its top, both from the 1:250,000 regional geological survey report of the Shengxian sheet (Nanjing Institute of Geology and Mineral Resources, 2004). Xi and colleagues (2018) treat the Qujiang, Yongkang and Tiantai Groups as contemporaneous facies of the late Early to early Late Cretaceous, which agrees. Governs `stages: [Albian, Cenomanian, Turonian]` on [Tiantai](../../../stratigraphy/t/tiantai.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yu2010a, p. 98: "天台群塘上组下部沉积岩中化石是馆头组中常见的分子，火山岩同位素年龄为(109.2±1.9)Ma" ("the fossils in the sedimentary rocks of the lower Tangshang Formation of the Tiantai Group are elements common in the Guantou Formation, and the isotopic age of its volcanic rocks is (109.2±1.9) Ma", translated).
- yu2010a, p. 98: "天台群赤城山组中火山岩锆石U-Pb年龄为94 Ma" ("the zircon U-Pb age of the volcanic rocks of the Chichengshan Formation of the Tiantai Group is 94 Ma", translated), with a footnote to the 2004 Shengxian sheet survey report of the Nanjing Institute of Geology and Mineral Resources.
- xi2018a, p. 273: "He et al. (2012) published zircon SIMS U-Pb ages of 99–96 and 94–91 Ma in the Laijia and Chichengshan Formations of the Tiantai Group, respectively".
- xi2018a, p. 273: "the Qujiang, Yongkang and Tiantai Groups are contemporaneous facies from the late early Cretaceous to early late Cretaceous".
- xi2018a, p. 268: the work its reference list dates 2012 is cited for "magnetostratigraphic and SIMS U-Pb zircon geochronologic data of SK1(s)", on the age of "the termination of the Cretaceous Normal Superchron".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Laijia and Chichengshan ages come from the work Xi and colleagues' reference list dates 2012.* That entry is He and colleagues' (2012) paper on the end of the Cretaceous Normal Superchron in the Songliao Basin's SK-1 core, which the same text cites for that core. The Tiantai Basin ages are in the SIMS zircon dating paper by He and colleagues in Journal of Asian Earth Sciences 62, which the reference list dates 2013; it was released online in November 2012 and printed in January 2013, so it is cited under both years.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the top of the Chichengshan, already open in the 2026-10-01 entry.

## What is the age of the Tiantai Group?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Albian–Turonian, from dates on its formations. Governs `stages: [Albian, Cenomanian, Turonian]` on Tiantai and its [Liangtoutang (Laijia) Formation](../../../stratigraphy/l/liangtoutang.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xi2018a, p. 273: "zircon SIMS U-Pb ages of 99–96 and 94–91 Ma in the Laijia and Chichengshan Formations of the Tiantai Group", credited to He and colleagues (2012; the paper meant is He and colleagues 2013, J. Asian Earth Sci. 62).
- yu2010a, p. 98: the Tangshang volcanics "(109.2±1.9)Ma" (biotite Ar–Ar, translated); p. 99: a zircon U-Pb age "near the top of the Chichengshan Formation" of 94 Ma (translated), from an unpublished 2004 survey report; conclusions: "早白垩世晚期albian期至晚白垩世早期" ("late Early Cretaceous, Albian, to early Late Cretaceous", translated).
- xi2021a, p. 385: the Liangtoutang of the provincial bureau is the Laijia Formation (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- Nothing considered was rejected on the text.

**Open.** The top. Xi and colleagues' correlation charts draw the Chichengshan up to about 87 Ma, in the Coniacian (xi2018a, Fig. 9; xi2021a, Fig. 4, with a queried box to about 83 Ma), against their text and Yu and colleagues' dates.
