# Tatlatui

## What do Arbour and colleagues (2020) say about the lithology and age of the Tatlatui member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Arbour and colleagues (2020) describe the member, one of Eisbacher's (1974) two informal divisions of the Tango Creek Formation, as dark gray mudstones and chert-pebble-bearing arenites with local thin seams of lignite. They date the Turtle Site east of Birdflat Creek, the probable source of the *Ferrisaurus* holotype, by the marker pollen *Pseudoaquilapollenites bertillonites*, whose biozone they give as 68.2–67.2 Ma, and call that age late Maastrichtian; the range is Maastrichtian on the ICS chart v2024/12 as well (base 72.2 Ma). They assign the site to this member on its lithology, so the member's Maastrichtian rests on that preferred reading. The site's mapping as Brothers Peak Formation and the rejected reading that puts it there are in the 2026-11-23 entry in [Tango Creek](tango-creek.md), and the site's link to the holotype in [*Ferrisaurus*](../../genera/F/Ferrisaurus.md). Governs `lithology` and the `Maastrichtian` in `stages` on [Tatlatui](../../../stratigraphy/t/tatlatui.yml); the Campanian rests on Ryan and colleagues (2023).
