# Tiourarén

## What is the age of the Tiourarén Formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Middle to earliest Late Jurassic (about Bathonian–Oxfordian), but only by faunal comparison; there is no radiometric date. Governs `stages: [Bathonian, Callovian, Oxfordian]` on Tiourarén and the stages of its taxa, such as [*Jobaria tiguidensis*](../../../genera/J/Jobaria.yml) and [*Afrovenator abakensis*](../../../genera/A/Afrovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2009a, p. 259: "pre-Kimmeridgian, probably late Middle Jurassic", argued from the phylogenetic position of its taxa (p. 263); p. 260: "no radiometric datings"; the fish are "of very limited utility" (p. 262) and "consistent with a Middle Jurassic age" (p. 263).
- rauhut2016a, p. 43: "probably Middle to earliest Late Jurassic … widely accepted since"; p. 45, Table 3: "?Bathonian-?Oxfordian". Same first author as the 2009 paper.

**Ruled out.**
- *Hauterivian–Barremian, or Neocomian.* Sereno and colleagues (1994) gave Hauterivian–Barremian; Sereno's later papers give "Neocomian" (130–140 Ma, about 135 Ma), as reported by Rauhut and López-Arbarello (rauhut2009a, p. 260). Sánchez-Hernández and Benton's "(?Hauterivian)" (sánchez-hernández2014a, p. 582) follows the older reading.

**Open.** A date. All readings rest on faunal comparison. Mannion and colleagues' "Bathonian–Callovian" (mannion2025a, p. 35) cites Rauhut and López-Arbarello together with Sereno and colleagues (1999); de Vries and colleagues say only "Jurassic horizons" (devries2022a, p. 3). Lasseron and colleagues (2019) have not been read.
