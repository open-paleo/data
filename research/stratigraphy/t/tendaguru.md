# Tendaguru

## What is the age of the Tendaguru Formation, and of its dinosaurs?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The formation runs from the middle Oxfordian into the Early Cretaceous (Valanginian–Hauterivian, possibly Aptian), the Cretaceous belonging at least to the top member; its dinosaurs are Kimmeridgian–Tithonian, with the Upper Dinosaur Member possibly reaching the Berriasian. Governs `stages` (Oxfordian–Hauterivian) on Tendaguru, the stages of the [Upper Dinosaur](../../../stratigraphy/u/upper-dinosaur.yml) and [Middle Dinosaur](../../../stratigraphy/m/middle-dinosaur.yml) members, and the Late Jurassic of taxa such as [*Giraffatitan brancai*](../../../genera/G/Giraffatitan.yml) and [*Kentrosaurus aethiopicus*](../../../genera/K/Kentrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bussert2009a, p. 141: "we raise the Tendaguru Beds to formational rank and recognise six members", the top being the "Rutitrigonia bornhardti-schwarzi Member"; "The age of the whole formation apparently ranges at least from the middle Oxfordian to the Valanginian through Hauterivian or possibly Aptian." p. 149: the top member is Dietrich's "Early Cretaceous 'Schwarzistufe'".
- schrank2005a, p. 49: assemblages from "the Middle Saurian Bed (late Kimmeridgian)", "the Trigonia smeei Bed (Tithonian)" and "probably from the Trigonia schwarzi Bed … (late Valanginian to Hauterivian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *An Early Cretaceous age for the dinosaur-bearing members as established.* No source read for this note puts a dinosaur horizon in the Cretaceous; the records keep all thirteen species Late Jurassic.

**Open.** Where the Jurassic–Cretaceous boundary falls. "The placement of the Jurassic/Cretaceous boundary within the Tendaguru Formation is as yet uncertain" (bussert2009a, p. 170), and "More work is needed to decide unambiguously whether the Upper Dinosaur Member is wholly Late Jurassic or, in part, Early Cretaceous" (p. 165). Schrank (2005, p. 78) does not exclude an Early Cretaceous age, "probably not younger than Berriasian", for the upper Trigonia smeei Bed.
