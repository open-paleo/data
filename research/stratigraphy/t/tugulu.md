# Tugulu

## Do Xu and colleagues (2018) date the upper Tugulu Group to the Late Cretaceous?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No. Their horizon line pairs "Upper Cretaceous" with Barremian and Aptian, both Early Cretaceous stages, and their title says Early Cretaceous. Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous]` on [Tugulu](../../../stratigraphy/t/tugulu.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2018c, p. 2853, Locality and Horizon: "Upper Cretaceous (Barremian-Aptian?) upper part of the Tugulu Group"; title: "Two Early Cretaceous Fossils Document Transitional Stages in Alvarezsaurian Dinosaur Evolution".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Cretaceous.* A slip that the same line and the title contradict (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-slip-that-its-own-document-contradicts-is-not-a-reading)).

**Open.** Nothing.
