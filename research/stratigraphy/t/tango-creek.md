# Tango Creek

## Is a paper's preferred reading of one site enough for the Maastrichtian?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Yes; the Maastrichtian stays. Arbour and colleagues (2020) weigh the published map, which shows the Turtle Site in the Brothers Peak Formation, against the site's lithology, and prefer the reading that the Tango Creek extends into the Maastrichtian there; their own pollen dates the site late Maastrichtian. A preferred reading in the authors' own voice counts. Governs the Maastrichtian in `stages` on [Tango Creek](../../../stratigraphy/t/tango-creek.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- arbour2020a, accepted manuscript, p. 15: "We prefer the second hypothesis, because the lithology at this site more closely matches that of the Tatlatui Member of the Tango Creek Formation".
- arbour2020a, accepted manuscript, p. 15: "The late Maastrichtian age of this site, as suggested by palynomorphs".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Unchanged.

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Tango Creek Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Barremian to Maastrichtian. A. R. Sweet's palynology reports, printed by Evenchick and colleagues (2001), give the oldest samples "Barremian to Early or Middle Albian", with the Barremian as a lower limit set by the angiosperm pollen *Clavatipollenites minutus*, and the youngest "Late Campanian, possibly Early Maastrichtian"; the reports between run from the Albian through the Campanian. Arbour and colleagues (2020) carry the top into the Maastrichtian near the Sustut River, on late Maastrichtian pollen from a site whose lithology they match to the Tatlatui member. Governs `stages` (Barremian to Maastrichtian) on [Tango Creek](../../../stratigraphy/t/tango-creek.yml).

**Evidence.**
- evenchick2001a, p. 61: sample C-112033, NTS sheet 104H: "A lower limit of Barremian is imposed by the presence of monocolpate angiosperm pollen (Clavatipollenites minutus)"; "AGE: Barremian to Early or Middle Albian". Three more samples on sheet 104H carry the same age, one from the same site and two from a basal section in the Eaglenest Range that Sweet calls "Roughly same section as C112032,33".
- evenchick2001a, p. 66: sample C-210643, sheet 94D: "AGE: Late Campanian, possibly Early Maastrichtian".
- evenchick2003a, p. 9: "Northwest of the study area the Tango Creek Formation is early Albian to late Campanian and Brothers Peak Formation late Campanian to early Maastrichtian (A. Sweet in Evenchick et al. (2001))".
- arbour2020a, p. ? (accepted manuscript, p. 5), Geographical and geological setting: the formation "consists of a series of alternating, fining-upwards sandstones and mudstones"; "Eisbacher (1974) further subdivided the Tango Creek Formation into the informal Niven and Tatlatui members".
- arbour2020a, p. ? (accepted manuscript, p. 6): Evenchick et al. (2003) "suggested that the Tango Creek Formation is Barremian or early Albian to late Campanian in age"; "the top of the formation was never younger than late Campanian in all sampled sites".
- arbour2020a, p. ? (accepted manuscript, p. 15), Discussion: the Turtle Site "is in an area currently mapped as Brothers Peak Formation", and its late Maastrichtian age "is younger than that reported for the Tango Creek Formation anywhere in the basin"; of the two readings, the site in the Brothers Peak or "the Tango Creek Formation extends into the Maastrichtian in this part of the basin", "We prefer the second hypothesis, because the lithology at this site more closely matches that of the Tatlatui Member".

**Ruled out.**
- *An early Albian base, with the Barremian credited to Evenchick and colleagues (2003).* Arbour and colleagues (2020) attribute "Barremian or early Albian" to the 2003 paper, but that paper gives "early Albian to late Campanian", citing Sweet's reports. The Barremian is in the reports themselves (evenchick2001a, p. 61), as a lower limit the 2003 summary leaves out. The summary relays the reports and does not overrule them, so the Barremian stands on the reports.
- *The Turtle Site in the Brothers Peak Formation.* The first of Arbour and colleagues' two readings, which follows the map. They reject it on the site's lithology. Under it the formation's top would stay late Campanian, with only Sweet's hedged "possibly Early Maastrichtian" beyond it, so the Maastrichtian rests on their preferred reading.

**Open.** The Barremian comes from one basal section on sheet 104H, and Arbour and colleagues' Fig. 2 caption calls the base of the formation "currently poorly constrained". A dated sample from the formation's base elsewhere in the basin would settle the lower bound.
