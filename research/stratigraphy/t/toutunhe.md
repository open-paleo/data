# Toutunhe

## Is the Toutunhe Formation Middle Jurassic or Oxfordian?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** Disputed between a Middle Jurassic (Bajocian–Callovian) age from fossils and palynology and a younger age implied by detrital zircons. Governs `stages: [Bajocian, Bathonian, Callovian]` and the dispute on Toutunhe.

**Evidence.**
- averianov2005a, p. 151: "dated on the basis of palynoflora as Bathonian", relayed from Wang (2001).
- eberth2001a, pp. 1630, 1639: "a Bajocian or Bathonian age is equally likely for the lower redbeds", on fossils and sequence correlation.
- fang2015a, p. 55, Table 1: the youngest zircon population in the Toutunhe runs about 153–161 Ma.

**Ruled out.**
- *A zircon floor at 161.2 Ma.* Fang and colleagues write "the sedimentation age of the Toutunhe Formation (younger than 161.2 Ma) (Fig. 2b), and thus we term them syndepositional zircons" (fang2015a, p. 59). The 161.2 Ma is the chart age of the formation's Upper Jurassic assignment in their Fig. 2b, "ages … from ICS (2013)" (p. 49), an assignment taken from unpublished regional mapping (p. 50); it is the premise of their reasoning, not a date.

**Open.** Whether the zircons, which reach about 153 Ma, are syndepositional. If they are, the Toutunhe would be Late Jurassic; Fang and colleagues assume so from the chart age, which makes the argument circular.
