# Villalba de la Sierra

## What does G1 name at Lo Hueco?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** A bed, and also an excavation area. Díez Díaz and colleagues (2016) identify "the bed in which the described specimen was collected" as G1, a grey mudstone level of the Lo Hueco section that their Fig. 1b labels among the site's lithosomes, with the specimen marked against it. Their Fig. 1 caption also calls G1 "one of the excavation areas" at Lo Hueco, the area mapped in Fig. 1e. That the excavation area takes its name from the level dug in it is an inference; the paper does not say so. The registry entry is the bed. Governs the G1 entry, with `informal: true`, in `beds` on [Villalba de la Sierra](../../../stratigraphy/v/villalba-de-la-sierra.yml).

**Evidence.**
- díezdíaz2016a, p. ? (accepted manuscript, p. 6, 1. Introduction): "The bed in which the described specimen was collected was identified as G1 (Fig. 1c), corresponding to the proximal part of a flooded muddy plain, close to distributary channels".
- díezdíaz2016a, p. ? (accepted manuscript, p. 6, 1. Introduction): "The Lo Hueco fossil site is placed in sedimentary deposits belonging to the upper part of the Margas, Arcillas y Yesos de Villalba de la Sierra Formation".
- díezdíaz2016a, p. ? (accepted manuscript, p. 37, Fig. 1 caption): "lateral view of the eastern talus of the outcrop during the excavation phase, with the identified litosomes (c)"; "general cartography of one of the excavation areas (G1) at Lo Hueco (e)".
- díezdíaz2016a, Fig. 1b: the "Lo Hueco" column labels its levels S1, V, G1, C, R1, G2, S2, R2 and M, with G1 a grey mudstone about 2–4.5 m above the base and the specimen marked beside it.

**Ruled out.**
- *G1 as an excavation area only.* The text names G1 as the bed, and Fig. 1b draws it as a level of the section alongside G2, R1 and the others, so it is a bed whatever else the caption uses the label for.

**Open.** Nothing.

## How do Vilas and colleagues (1982) define and date the Villalba de la Sierra Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Vilas and colleagues (1982) erect the "Formación Margas, arcillas y yesos de Villalba de la Sierra" for the continental Senonian of the Serranía de Cuenca, earlier described under informal names. The type section is the Barranco de la Haceauilla, 2.5 km north of Villalba de la Sierra, 157 m thick; across its area the formation ranges from 0 to 500 m. It rests on the Brechas Dolomíticas de Cuenca, passes laterally southeastward into the Calizas y Margas de Sierra Perenchiza, and its top is transitional into Lower Tertiary beds at El Santerón and sharp and unconformable elsewhere. They date it Campanian–Maastrichtian on its charophytes. Governs `stages: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` and `variants` on [Villalba de la Sierra](../../../stratigraphy/v/villalba-de-la-sierra.yml).

**Evidence.**
- vilas1982a, p. 495: the heading "Formación Margas, arcillas y yesos de Villalba de la Sierra".
- vilas1982a, p. 496: "This unit has been described informally under the denomination of «Niveau inférieur, moyen et supérieur…»", designating "the continental Senonian materials of the Serranía de Cuenca"; "It has also received the names «Crétacé supérieur continental» … «Facies Garumniense» and «Facies Garumn»"; "We designate as type section the Barranco de la Haceauilla section, 2.5 km north of Villalba de la Sierra"; "The thickness measured at the type section is 157 m"; "The thickness is very variable, from 0 to 500 m"; "passes laterally, toward the SE, to the Formación Calizas y Margas de Sierra Perenchiza"; "The lower boundary is formed by the Brechas Dolomíticas de Cuenca unit. The upper boundary is transitional toward the Lower Tertiary materials in the El Santerón zone and sharp and unconformable in the rest of the area" (translated).
- vilas1982a, p. 497: "The age of the Formation is Campanian-Maastrichtian" (translated).
- vilas1982a, p. 502: the "Arcillas y Yesos de Villalba de la Sierra" represent the same Campanian–Maastrichtian age as the Sierra Perenchiza, "also on the basis of their charophyte content" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *"Facies Garumniense", "Crétacé supérieur continental" or the informal lower, middle and upper levels as variants.* Vilas and colleagues give them as earlier informal designations of the continental Senonian of the Serranía de Cuenca, replaced by the formal name, not as names of this formation; "Garumniense" is a facies term whose use in the Iberian Range they report Gutiérrez and Robles (1979) criticized. A variant is another name papers print for the unit.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does bed G1 at Lo Hueco have stages of its own?

*2026-10-06*

Supersedes the Campanian–Maastrichtian bed G1 carried until 2026-10-06.

**Conclusion.** No. Díez Díaz and colleagues (2016) date the Lo Hueco site's sediments, after Ortega and colleagues (2015), and name G1 only as the bed the specimen came from, so G1 takes the formation's epoch and no stages. *Lohuecotitan pandafilandi* keeps the Campanian–Maastrichtian, which the paper gives for its type locality. Governs `period: [Late Cretaceous]` and the absence of `stages` on G1 of [Villalba de la Sierra](../../../stratigraphy/v/villalba-de-la-sierra.yml).

**Evidence.**
- díezdíaz2016a, 1. Introduction: "Its stratigraphic position and palaeontological content indicate that its sediments were deposited in the late Campanian-early Maastrichtian interval"; later in the paragraph, "The bed in which the described specimen was collected was identified as G1".
- díezdíaz2016a, 3. Systematic palaeontology: "Type locality and horizon. The Lo Hueco fossil site …, Margas, Arcillas y Yesos de Villalba de la Sierra Formation, upper Campanian-lower Maastrichtian."

**Ruled out.**
- *G1, Campanian–Maastrichtian.* The interval is given for the site's sediments as a whole, not for the bed.

**Open.** Nothing.
