# Villar del Arzobispo Formation

## Should the formation be restricted as Aurell and colleagues (2016) propose?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** Not yet. The unit stays in the broad sense, Kimmeridgian to Berriasian, with the restriction given in the dispute. Aurell and colleagues (2016) erect the Aguilar del Alfambra and Galve formations from the upper part of the unit in the Galve sub-basin. Campos-Soto and colleagues (2019) reject the new units, and of the later papers read for this note, only Mannion and colleagues (2019) adopt them from outside Aurell's group, and none of them weighs the two schemes. The most recent, Sánchez-Fenollosa and colleagues (2026), use the unit "sensu Campos-Soto et al., 2019". That is not the broad acceptance a change of definition needs. Of the records in the unit, only *Aragosaurus* would move: Aurell and colleagues place the *Galvesaurus* level in their restricted unit too. Governs the `stages` and `dispute` on [Villar del Arzobispo](../../../stratigraphy/v/villar-del-arzobispo.yml), and `formation` on [*Aragosaurus ischiaticus*](../../../genera/A/Aragosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- aurell2016a: "Two new lithostratigraphic units are defined and characterized, the Aguilar del Alfambra and the Galve formations", replacing a framework of "only two lithostratigraphic units (the Villar del Arzobispo and El Castellar formations) bounded by a single regional unconformity, and this resulted in significant misinterpretations"; *Galvesaurus* was "found in the lower half of the unit", the restricted Villar del Arzobispo.
- campos-soto2019a: "the new units defined by Aurell et al. (2016) cannot be accepted because of several reasons", namely their nomenclature and limits under the International Stratigraphic Guide, ages that disagree with later dating, and boundaries drawn at what "are, in fact, minor and local unconformities". For the Las Zabacheras section itself, though, "we could not obtain foraminifera with chronostratigraphic value".
- santos2018a: the palynology of the *Aragosaurus* level "indicates a Berriasian age (most likely early Berriasian)", in the Villar del Arzobispo Formation.
- mannion2019a: *Aragosaurus* "has most recently been placed in the lower section of the Galve Formation and assigned a probable late Berriasian–early Valanginian age (Aurell et al., 2016)".
- pérez-pueyo2019a: "The 'Cuesta Lonsal-1' site is included within the Villar del Arzobispo Fm. (Aurell et al., 2016)".
- sánchez-fenollosa2026a: "The Villar del Arzobispo Formation (sensu Campos-Soto et al., 2019, eastern Spain)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Adopting the restriction now.* It would add the Aguilar del Alfambra and Galve formations, cut this unit to the middle–upper Tithonian, and move *Aragosaurus* to the Galve, late Berriasian to Hauterivian. Revisit if later work outside the Zaragoza group takes up the new units.

**Open.** Whether the restricted scheme gains wider use.
