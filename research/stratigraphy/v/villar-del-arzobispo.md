# Villar del Arzobispo Formation

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Villar del Arzobispo Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian to Berriasian, in the broad sense that the 2026-10-14 entry keeps. Campos-Soto and colleagues (2019) correlate the dinosaur-bearing record of eastern Spain with the unit. They date it by larger benthic foraminifera as Kimmeridgian–Tithonian, and locally, in the Las Zabacheras–Galve area, as Kimmeridgian to a queried early Berriasian. The Kimmeridgian had been dropped in the 1990s for a Tithonian–Berriasian age and has since been shown at Benagéber and in the western Peñagolosa sub-basin. Campos-Soto and colleagues propose redefining the unit on its parastratotype at Cedrillas. At Galve, Canudo and colleagues (2012) give "middle Tithonian–upper part of the early Berriasian", with the Jurassic–Cretaceous boundary assumed near the top on correlation with the more open eastern parts of the basin. They also record a charophyte near the top at Las Zabacheras of late early or middle Berriasian age. Aurell and colleagues (2016) restrict the unit in the Galve sub-basin to the middle–upper Tithonian, 140 to 170 m thick, below an erosive unconformity "around the late Tithonian" that they tie to a relative fall in sea level at the end of the Jurassic. They record *Anchispirocyclina lusitanica*, which they give a middle Tithonian to earliest Berriasian range, at several levels in it. In the framework they replace, the unit together with the beds of their Aguilar del Alfambra Formation made up a middle Tithonian–middle Berriasian sequence. On their proposal Las Zabacheras lies in the lower Galve Formation, "poorly constrained from late Berriasian to Hauterivian", and the Las Cerradicas tracksite in the upper Villar del Arzobispo. Governs `stages: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian, Berriasian]` and `period: [Late Jurassic, Early Cretaceous]` on [Villar del Arzobispo](../../../stratigraphy/v/villar-del-arzobispo.yml).

**Evidence.**
- campos-soto2019a, p. 471, Abstract: the record "includes deposits previously assigned, depending on the studied area, from the Kimmeridgian to the Barremian (locally even to the Aptian–Albian)", while larger benthic foraminifera "demonstrate a Kimmeridgian–Tithonian age (locally Kimmeridgian-Early Berriasian?)".
- campos-soto2019a, p. 472: "there is not agreement on the age or on the nomenclature of the unit with Aragosaurus ischiaticus".
- campos-soto2019a, p. 475: "since the 90s, the Kimmeridgian age was discarded and they were assigned to the Tithonian–Berriasian"; "More recently, deposits of the Villar del Arzobispo Fm have been dated as Kimmeridgian at the Benagéber area in the South-Iberian Basin ..., and as Kimmeridgian–Tithonian in the western Peñagolosa sub-basin".
- campos-soto2019a, p. 503: the record "has a Kimmeridgian–Tithonian age and, locally, in the Las Zabacheras-Galve area, a Kimmeridgian–Early Berriasian? age", against previous authors who "commonly have considered these deposits as ranging from the Kimmeridgian to the Early Cretaceous (Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian and even, locally, Aptian–Albian"; pp. 503–504: the parastratotype in the Cedrillas area "would be an excellent candidate for redefining the unit".
- canudo2012a, p. 252, Abstract: the fieldwork pinpoints "the position of the low-angle unconformity between the Villar del Arzobispo Formation and the El Castellar Formation".
- canudo2012a, p. 254: "the Jurassic–Cretaceous boundary is assumed to be located near the top of the unit in the light of the correlation with the more open, eastern domains of the basin".
- canudo2012a, p. 255: "the most probable age traditionally considered for the Villar del Arzobispo Formation in Galve is middle Tithonian–upper part of the early Berriasian"; a charophyte from "a carbonate level situated close to the top of the formation near the site of Las Zabacheras" "characterizes a charophyte biochronozone that is from the upper part of the early Berriasian or middle Berriasian in age"; "the locality of Las Zabacheras is located in yellow-grey shales of the lower El Castellar Formation".
- aurell2016a, p. 138, Abstract: "The age of the Galve Formation is poorly constrained from late Berriasian to Hauterivian".
- aurell2016a, p. 140, section 3.1: "the site of Las Zabacheras is located in the lower part of the Galve Formation and the Las Cerradicas tracksite in the upper levels of the Villar del Arzobispo Formation".
- aurell2016a, pp. 154–155, section 5.1: "The Villar del Arzobispo and the Aguilar del Alfambra formations together form a middle Tithonian–middle Berriasian long-term sequence"; the unconformity between them "represents a relative fall in sea level at the end of the Jurassic in the studied Galve sub-basin" and "is located around the late Tithonian"; the unit "contains Anchispirocyclina lusitanica at different stratigraphic intervals", a foraminifer "which ranges from the middle Tithonian to the earliest Berriasian".
- aurell2016a, p. 160, section 6: "the use of the middle–upper Tithonian Villar del Arzobispo Formation should be restricted to an interval with a remarkably homogeneous thickness (140–170 m)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Valanginian to Aptian–Albian ages.* Campos-Soto and colleagues list these as earlier authors' assignments of the dinosaur-bearing record, several made under other formation names, and replace them with their own foraminiferal dates. A reported spread adds no stage (see [methods](../../methods.md)).
- *Tithonian–Berriasian only.* The reading of the 1990s dropped the Kimmeridgian, which Campos-Soto and colleagues report has since been shown at Benagéber and in the western Peñagolosa sub-basin.
- *Middle–upper Tithonian only.* This is Aurell and colleagues' (2016) restricted unit, which the 2026-10-14 entry does not adopt.
- *The top of the unit below Las Zabacheras.* Canudo and colleagues (2012) put the *Aragosaurus* site above a low-angle unconformity at the top of the unit, in the El Castellar Formation, and Aurell and colleagues (2016) put it in their Galve Formation. Campos-Soto and colleagues (2019), who logged Las Zabacheras afterwards, keep its beds in the unit in the broad sense, which the 2026-10-14 entry follows.

**Open.** The Berriasian rests on the Las Zabacheras–Galve area: Campos-Soto and colleagues' queried "Early Berriasian?" there, and the charophyte that Canudo and colleagues report near the top of the unit at Las Zabacheras. Elsewhere the cited works date the unit Kimmeridgian–Tithonian.

## Should the formation be restricted as Aurell and colleagues (2016) propose?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** Not yet. The unit stays in the broad sense, Kimmeridgian to Berriasian, with the restriction given in the dispute. Aurell and colleagues (2016) erect the Aguilar del Alfambra and Galve formations from the upper part of the unit in the Galve sub-basin. Campos-Soto and colleagues (2019) reject the new units, and of the later papers read for this note, only Mannion and colleagues (2019) adopt them from outside Aurell's group, and none of them weighs the two schemes. The most recent, Sánchez-Fenollosa and colleagues (2026), use the unit "sensu Campos-Soto et al., 2019". That is not the broad acceptance a change of definition needs. Of the records in the unit, only *Aragosaurus* would move: Aurell and colleagues place the *Galvesaurus* level in their restricted unit too. Governs the `stages` and `dispute` on [Villar del Arzobispo](../../../stratigraphy/v/villar-del-arzobispo.yml), and `formation` on [*Aragosaurus ischiaticus*](../../../genera/A/Aragosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- aurell2016a, pp. 138, 159: "Two new lithostratigraphic units are defined and characterized, the Aguilar del Alfambra and the Galve formations", replacing a framework of "only two lithostratigraphic units (the Villar del Arzobispo and El Castellar formations) bounded by a single regional unconformity, and this resulted in significant misinterpretations"; *Galvesaurus* was "found in the lower half of the unit", the restricted Villar del Arzobispo.
- campos-soto2019a, pp. 494, 496: "the new units defined by Aurell et al. (2016) cannot be accepted because of several reasons", namely their nomenclature and limits under the International Stratigraphic Guide, ages that disagree with later dating, and boundaries drawn at what "are, in fact, minor and local unconformities". For the Las Zabacheras section itself, though, "we could not obtain foraminifera with chronostratigraphic value".
- santos2018a: the palynology of the *Aragosaurus* level "indicates a Berriasian age (most likely early Berriasian)", in the Villar del Arzobispo Formation.
- mannion2019a: *Aragosaurus* "has most recently been placed in the lower section of the Galve Formation and assigned a probable late Berriasian–early Valanginian age (Aurell et al., 2016)".
- pérez-pueyo2019a: "The 'Cuesta Lonsal-1' site is included within the Villar del Arzobispo Fm. (Aurell et al., 2016)".
- sánchez-fenollosa2026a: "The Villar del Arzobispo Formation (sensu Campos-Soto et al., 2019, eastern Spain)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Adopting the restriction now.* It would add the Aguilar del Alfambra and Galve formations, cut this unit to the middle–upper Tithonian, and move *Aragosaurus* to the Galve, late Berriasian to Hauterivian. Revisit if later work outside the Zaragoza group takes up the new units.

**Open.** Whether the restricted scheme gains wider use.
