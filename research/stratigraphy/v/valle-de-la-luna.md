# Valle de la Luna

## Does Shipman's thesis give the 217.0 ± 1.7 Ma date credited to it for the top of the member?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** No. Shipman (2004) prints a 223 Ma date at 611 m in its section 1, credited to Currie and colleagues (2001), and its own 218.5 ± 1.7 Ma at 352 m in its section 3. Ezcurra's (2010) "217.0 ± 1.7 Ma" on plagioclase at about 610 m pairs the first date's height with the second's uncertainty and a value that appears in neither. Sciscio and colleagues (2017) repeat the figure. The member's Norian does not depend on it: the 225.9 ± 0.9 Ma date of Martínez and colleagues (2012), 630 m above the base, is Norian on the ICS chart v2024/12 (Norian base 227.3 Ma). Governs the `shipman2004a` and `ezcurra2010a` notes on [Valle de la Luna](../../../stratigraphy/v/valle-de-la-luna.yml) and the `ezcurra2010a` note on [Ischigualasto](../../../stratigraphy/i/ischigualasto.yml); `stages: [Carnian, Norian]` is unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- shipman2004a, p. 103, Fig. B-8: "611m, 223 Ma (Currie et al., 2001)" beside section 1; "352m, 218.5± 1.7 Ma" beside section 3; "90m, 228.06± 0.78 Ma (Rogers et al., 1993)" beside section 2. Fig. B-9, p. 104, repeats the three labels.
- shipman2004a, p. 77: "The date we did obtain was from section 3 at 352 m and produced a date of 218 ± 1.7Ma"; p. 126: "Two ashes near the top of the Ischigualasto Formation have been dated at 223 and 218.5±1.5 Ma (this study)".
- ezcurra2010a, p. 372: "Another age dating has been performed by Shipman (2004) on plagioclase crystals from a bentonite ash, approximately 610 m from the base of the formation … This dating yielded an 40Ar/39Ar date of 217.0 ± 1.7 Ma (Shipman 2004)."
- sciscio2017a, Fig. 8 caption: "40Ar/39Ar date of the Valle de la Luna Member (feldspars): 217.0±1.7 Ma by Shipman (2004)".
- martínez2012b, p. 17: bentonite ISCH-6-611, "630 m above the base and 70 m below the top of the formation", at 225.9 ± 0.9 Ma.

**Ruled out.**
- *217.0 ± 1.7 Ma as Shipman's date for the top of the member.* The thesis text and its measured-section figures give no such value. The value at the 611 m height is 223 Ma and is credited to Currie and colleagues (2001), not to the thesis.
- *218.5 ± 1.7 Ma as a date for the top of the Valle de la Luna Member.* It comes from 352 m in Shipman's section 3, one of the two ashes the thesis places "near the top of the Ischigualasto Formation" (p. 126). The thesis names no members, so placing that ash in Currie and colleagues' (2009) scheme would be our inference.

**Open.** Currie and colleagues (2001) is a meeting abstract that has not been read, so the method and uncertainty of the 223 Ma date are unknown. The thesis text says only one of its own samples gave a reliable age (p. 77), but elsewhere calls both near-top dates "this study" (p. 126).

## Does "Valle de la Luna" in the Eoraptor literature mean the member?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No. Sereno and colleagues (2013) use it for the valley in Ischigualasto Provincial Park where the lower members crop out. *Eoraptor* comes from the Cancha de Bochas Member. Governs `member: Cancha de Bochas` on [*Eoraptor*](../../../genera/E/Eoraptor.yml), and which sources date `stages: [Carnian, Norian]` on [Valle de la Luna](../../../stratigraphy/v/valle-de-la-luna.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno2013a, p. 83: "the lower portion of the Ischigualasto Formation in the Valle de la Luna ('Valley of the Moon') of Ischigualasto Provincial Park"; p. 86: the lower two members "are well exposed along the southern edge of the basin in a region called the Valle de la Luna. All of the remains of *Eoraptor lunensis* come from the Cancha de Bochas Member in the Valle de la Luna".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sereno and colleagues' "mid-Carnian" as the member's age.* It dates the Cancha de Bochas Member (sereno2013a, p. 86).
- *Peyre de Fabrègues and colleagues (2015) as naming the member.* Their Table 2 gives *Eoraptor* as "Ischigualasto Formation | Valle de la Luna, Provincia de San Juan", which is the valley in the locality column (peyredefabrègues2015a, Table 2).

**Open.** Nothing.
