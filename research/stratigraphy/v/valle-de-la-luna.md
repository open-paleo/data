# Valle de la Luna

## Does "Valle de la Luna" in the Eoraptor literature mean the member?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No. Sereno and colleagues (2013) use it for the valley in Ischigualasto Provincial Park where the lower members crop out. *Eoraptor* comes from the Cancha de Bochas Member. Governs `member: Cancha de Bochas` on [*Eoraptor*](../../../genera/E/Eoraptor.yml), and which sources date `stages: [Carnian, Norian]` on [Valle de la Luna](../../../stratigraphy/v/valle-de-la-luna.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno2013a, p. 83: "the lower portion of the Ischigualasto Formation in the Valle de la Luna ('Valley of the Moon') of Ischigualasto Provincial Park"; p. 86: the lower two members "are well exposed along the southern edge of the basin in a region called the Valle de la Luna. All of the remains of *Eoraptor lunensis* come from the Cancha de Bochas Member in the Valle de la Luna".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sereno and colleagues' "mid-Carnian" as the member's age.* It dates the Cancha de Bochas Member (sereno2013a, p. 86).
- *Peyre de Fabrègues and colleagues (2015) as naming the member.* Their Table 2 gives *Eoraptor* as "Ischigualasto Formation | Valle de la Luna, Provincia de San Juan", which is the valley in the locality column (peyredefabrègues2015a, Table 2).

**Open.** Nothing.
