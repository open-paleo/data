# Chaochuan

## What do Chen and colleagues (2013) and Ma and colleagues (2016) say about the age of the Chaochuan?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Chen and colleagues (2013) give Albian to Cenomanian from radiometric dates of 105 to 96 Ma that they relay from Luo and Yu (2004) and two other works, and say fossil comparison places deposition in the early Late Cretaceous. Ma and colleagues (2016) date a Chaochuan tuff in the Lishui Basin at 118 ± 1 Ma and give no stage names; on GTS2020, whose Aptian runs from 121.4 to 113.2 Ma, as does the ICS chart v2024/12, that tuff is Aptian. Together they give the Aptian to Cenomanian span, and the two readings stay in dispute. Governs `stages` and `dispute` on [Chaochuan](../../../stratigraphy/c/chaochuan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- chen2013a, p. 659: the Yongkang Basin "contains three mid-Cretaceous formations of the Yongkang Group, namely Guantou, Chaochuan, and Fangyan formations, in ascending sequence"; "The Chaochuan Formation is a lacustrine deposit consisting of purplish sandstone with interbedded igneous rock".
- chen2013a, p. 659: "Radiometric dates for the Chaochuan Formation range from 105 to 96 Ma (Albian-Cenomanian) (Luo and Yu, 2004; …)"; "Fossil comparison and stratigraphic correlation indicate that the Chaochuan Formation was deposited during the early Late Cretaceous".
- chen2013a, p. 660: "Stratigraphic horizon: Late Early to early Late Cretaceous (Albian - Cenomanian), Chaochuan Formation".
- ma2016a, p. 61: one of the five tuff samples "was collected from Chaochuan Formation volcanic rocks in the Laozhu graben" (translated).
- ma2016a, p. 65: sample ZJ66 gives "a 206Pb/238U weighted mean age of (118±1) Ma (MSWD=1.8), which represents the age of the volcanic eruption" (translated).
- ma2016a, p. 66: the lower assemblage "includes the former Guantou and Chaochuan formations", with "volcanic eruption ages of 124–112 Ma"; the upper assemblage, "the Fangyan Formation", has "volcanic eruption ages of 104–91 Ma" (translated).
- ma2016a, p. 56, Chinese abstract: "the lower volcanic-sedimentary assemblage includes the Guantou and Chaochuan formations, formed at 124–112 Ma; the upper volcanic-sedimentary assemblage is represented by the Fangyan Formation, at 104–91 Ma" (translated); the same division recurs in the conclusions, p. 69.
- ma2016a, p. 57, English abstract: "the lower rock suite, consisting of Guantou and Chaochuan Formations, was deposited in 124—112 Ma, whereas the upper rock suite, corresponding to Chaochuan Formation, was deposited in 104-91 Ma".
- gale2020a, p. 1067: "With a duration of 8.2 Myr from 121.4 ± 0.6 until 113.2 ± 0.3 the GTS2020 Aptian is only the third longest stage in the Cretaceous".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Chaochuan as Ma and colleagues' 104–91 Ma upper suite.* Only the English abstract says so (p. 57). The Chinese abstract (p. 56), the stratigraphic comparison (p. 66) and the conclusions (p. 69) place the Chaochuan in the 124–112 Ma lower assemblage and give the upper one to the Fangyan, so the English wording is a slip that the paper itself contradicts.

**Open.** Whether the Aptian tuff of the Lishui Basin and the Albian–Cenomanian dates relayed for the formation elsewhere date the same rock; an isotopic age from the Chaochuan in the Yongkang Basin itself would settle the dispute.
