# Coyote Point

## What age do Beveridge and colleagues (2022) give the Coyote Point Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian. Beveridge and colleagues (2022) model the member's base at 80.61 +0.39/−0.51 Ma and its top at 79.00 +0.98/−0.99 Ma. Taken to the far ends of both windows, 81.00 to 78.01 Ma, the member still lies inside the Campanian on the ICS chart v2024/12 (83.6 to 72.2 Ma), as it does on the GTS2020 boundaries the paper uses. The window at the top is about two million years wide because a depositional hiatus separates the member from the overlying Pardner Canyon Member. The paper also relays earlier sequence-stratigraphic work that takes a sequence boundary at the member's top to be the global ~80 Ma boundary. Governs the Campanian in `stages` on [Coyote Point](../../../stratigraphy/c/coyote-point.yml).

**Evidence.**
- beveridge2022a, p. 4, 2.2 Chronostratigraphy: "a sequence boundary at the top of the Coyote Point Member (formerly the upper member), inferred to reflect the globally recognized ~80 Ma sequence boundary".
- beveridge2022a, p. 5, 2.2 Chronostratigraphy: "the Campanian stage is constrained to between 83.6 (±0.2) Ma and 72.1 (±0.2) Ma".
- beveridge2022a, p. 13, Table 2: "Reynolds Point – Coyote Point Mbr Boundary", 80.61 +0.39/−0.51 Ma; "Coyote Point – Pardner Canyon Mbr Boundary", 79.00 +0.98/−0.99 Ma.
- beveridge2022a, p. 13, 6.1 Member boundaries and features: "a significant depositional hiatus between the Coyote Point and Pardner Canyon members"; "a member boundary age of 79.0 Ma with a large uncertainty window of approximately two million years".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
