# Cancha de Bochas

## How do Currie and colleagues (2009) define and date the Cancha de Bochas Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Currie and colleagues (2009) designate the member, the second of four in the Ischigualasto Formation, named for the Cancha de Bochas area of Ischigualasto Provincial Park in the lower part of their Section 2. They define it by its calcareous paleosols: the base is the first mudstone with calcareous nodules above the base of the formation, and the top is the uppermost such mudstone in the lower third of the formation. They give all four members a Carnian age of deposition, and their stratigraphic chart (Fig. 2) places the 227.8 ± 0.3 Ma sanidine date of Rogers and colleagues (1993), from a bentonite about 80 m above the base of the formation, in this member. That date is Carnian on the ICS chart v2024/12 (Norian base 227.3 Ma), so the old time scale behind their Carnian (Gradstein and colleagues, 1995) does not change the stage here, unlike in the overlying [Valle de la Luna](../v/valle-de-la-luna.md). Governs `stages: [Carnian]` and `parent: Ischigualasto` on [Cancha de Bochas](../../../stratigraphy/c/cancha-de-bochas.yml).

**Evidence.**
- currie2009a, p. 85, Appendix A: "The newly designated units are, in ascending order, the La Peña Member, the Cancha de Bochas Member, the Valle de la Luna Member, and the Quebrada de la Sal Member. Vertebrate fossils and isotopic ages of volcanic rocks in the Ischigualasto Formation indicate a Carnian age of deposition for all of the lithostratigraphic members described below".
- currie2009a, p. 85, A.2. Cancha de Bochas Member: "The Cancha de Bochas Member consists of ~65–125 m of mudstone and sandstone, with rare interbeds of bentonite and basalt. The member is named for the “Cancha de Bochas” area of the Ischigualasto Provincial Park in the lower part of Section 2"; "The base of the member is placed at the first mudstone containing calcareous nodules above the base of the formation. The top of the member is placed at the top of the upper most mudstone containing calcareous nodules in the lower 1/3 of the formation."
- currie2009a, p. 76: sanidine "from a bentonite sampled ~80 m above the base of the formation near Section 2 yield an 40Ar/39Ar date of 227.8 ± 0.3 Ma (Rogers et al., 1993)"; "Collectively, these ages support a Carnian age of deposition based on the Triassic time-scale of Gradstein et al. (1995)".
- currie2009a, Fig. 2, p. 76: the asterisked date "227.8 ±0.3" stands beside "Cancha de Bochas Mbr." in the Carnian; the caption reads "Time scale adapted from Gradstein et al. (1995) and Alcober (1996)."
