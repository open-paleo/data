# Cooper Canyon

## What do the cited works say about the age and name of the Cooper Canyon Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Martz and colleagues (2013) date the Post Quarry, in the lower part of the type section, to the Adamanian biozone and "possibly late Lacian or earliest Alaunian (late early Norian or earliest middle Norian)", and allow that the lowest beds of the formation may be Otischalkian. They withdraw the Revueltian reading of the quarry, which rested on correlating the Cooper Canyon with the Bull Canyon of New Mexico. The name is Lehman and colleagues' (1992) replacement for Chatterjee's (1986) "Cooper Member", which was preoccupied. Governs `stages` and the name on [Cooper Canyon](../../../stratigraphy/c/cooper-canyon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martz2013a, p. 339 (early-view p. 1), title: "Cooper Canyon Formation (Dockum Group, Upper Triassic) of southern Garza County, western Texas".
- martz2013a, p. 339 (early-view p. 1), Abstract: "The age of the Post Quarry assemblage is possibly late Lacian or earliest Alaunian (late early Norian or earliest middle Norian), between 220 and 215 Ma."
- martz2013a, p. 340 (early-view p. 2): the assemblage was "previously considered representative of the Revueltian land vertebrate “faunachron”", "largely because the Cooper Canyon Formation was accepted as correlative with the Bull Canyon Formation of New Mexico"; "However, recent lithostratigraphic revisions (Martz 2008) have cast doubt on" that correlation.
- martz2013a, p. 357 (early-view p. 19): "The Post Quarry contains Leptosuchus"; "This makes the Post Quarry Adamanian by definition"; the Boren Quarry, near the base of the formation, "probably lies within the Otischalkian biozone, and the base of the Adamanian biozone ... occurs somewhere within the lower unit of the Cooper Canyon Formation".
- martz2013a, p. 358 (early-view p. 20): "the lower unit of the Cooper Canyon Formation correlates lithostratigraphically with the Tecovas Formation (Martz 2008)"; "As with the lower unit of the Cooper Canyon Formation, the lowermost Tecovas Formation may fall within the Otischalkian biozone".
- cather2013a, p. 108: "The Bull Canyon interval is equivalent to the upper part (Martz, 2008) of the earlier-named Cooper Member of Chatterjee (1986) in Texas. As noted by Lucas et al. (1994), however, the term Cooper is preoccupied. Although rectified by Lehman et al. (1992) by revision of the name to Cooper Canyon Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Revueltian age for the Post Quarry assemblage.* Chatterjee (1986) and later workers read the quarry as Revueltian because they took the Cooper Canyon as the equivalent of the Bull Canyon of New Mexico. Martz and colleagues (2013) correlate the lower unit of the formation with the Tecovas instead, and the quarry's *Leptosuchus* places it in the Adamanian.
- *Cooper Canyon as the name of the Dockum above the Santa Rosa Sandstone in southeastern New Mexico.* Cather and colleagues (2013) report that the extension "was later realized to be the result of a miscorrelation in Texas" (cather2013a, p. 108), and assign those beds to the Tecovas.

**Open.** Whether the Otischalkian lower beds reach into the Carnian, which would add a stage to the formation. Martz and colleagues (2013) give no stage for the Otischalkian part.
