# Cerro Lisandro

## Is the formation Cerro Lisandro or Lisandro?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Cerro Lisandro: the unit was first named "Estratos de Cerro Lisandro", later shortened to Lisandro, and the full original name was restored by Leanza and Hugo (1997). Governs `name` on [Cerro Lisandro](../../../stratigraphy/c/cerro-lisandro.yml) and `location.formation` on the records that name it.

**Evidence.**
- garrido2010a, p. 139: "Herrero Ducloux (en Fossa Mancini et al., 1938) definió a esta unidad bajo el nombre de "Estratos de Cerro Lisandro"" ("Herrero Ducloux defined this unit under the name 'Cerro Lisandro Strata'", translated) and "Finalmente, Leanza & Hugo (1997) reintroducen su denominación original completa, quedando reconocida de esta forma bajo el nombre de Fm. Cerro Lisandro" ("Finally, Leanza and Hugo (1997) reintroduce its full original name, so that it is now recognized as the Cerro Lisandro Formation", translated).
- garrido2010a, p. 126: the "Fm. Lisandro sensu Ramos, 1981" is the same unit.

**Ruled out.**
- *Lisandro.* A later shortening of the original name.

**Open.** Herrero Ducloux (1938) and Leanza and Hugo (1997) have not been read; the history is Garrido's.
