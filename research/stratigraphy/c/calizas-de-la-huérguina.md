# Calizas de La Huérguina

## Does the Calizas de La Huérguina reach the Hauterivian?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No. The formation is Barremian. The disagreement that remains, upper against lower Barremian, is finer than `stages` records, so it is not a dispute. Governs `stages: [Barremian]` and the absence of a `dispute` on [Calizas de La Huérguina](../../../stratigraphy/c/calizas-de-la-huérguina.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ortega2010a, p. 203, Horizon and locality: "Las Hoyas site. Calizas de La Huérguina Formation, Upper Barremian (Lower Cretaceous)".
- pérez-moreno1994a, Horizon and locality: "Las Hoyas fossil site, Cuenca Province, Spain. Calizas de La Huérguina Formation, Upper Hauterivian–Lower Barremian (Lower Cretaceous)".
- pittman2020c, p. 66: "the Early Cretaceous (Barremian) Las Hoyas lagerstätte (La Huerguina Formation)"; p. 60, Table 5: "Calizas de la Huérguina Formation … Early Cretaceous … Barremian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper Hauterivian, after Pérez-Moreno and colleagues (1994).* Two of its authors, Ortega and Sanz, date the same Las Hoyas site Upper Barremian in 2010. The 2010 paper does not say it revises the earlier dating; treating it as a revision is our reading. Carrano and colleagues' "late Barremian" does not add a second dating: their occurrence line ends "(Ortega *et al.* 2010)" (carrano2012a, p. 228).
- *Hauterivian, from Ding and colleagues' appendix.* "Calizas de la Huérguina Formation (128.5–125 Ma)" (ding2020a, p. 153) opens in the Hauterivian on the current chart (base Barremian 125.77 Ma). The same table gives the Wessex Formation "(129.4–125 Ma)" (p. 153), so the Las Hoyas range lies inside the interval the table uses for the Barremian, and Pittman and colleagues' chapter names the stage, Barremian, twice (pittman2020c, pp. 60, 66). A stated stage is carried over ([CONTRIBUTING](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#reading-ages-on-the-chart)).
- *"?Hauterivian–Maastrichtian" (Rauhut 2003).* This is the "Temporal range" of Ornithomimosauria, listed above ten formations (rauhut2003b, p. 38), not an age for one of them, and query-marked. The same paper puts *Pelecanimimus* in "the Barremian lithographic limestones of Las Hoyas" (p. 102).

**Open.** Upper or lower Barremian, which `stages` cannot express.
