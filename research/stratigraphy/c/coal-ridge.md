# Coal Ridge

## What age do Warner-Cowgill and colleagues (2025) give their site in the Coal Ridge Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Late Campanian, between about 76.3 and 75.8 Ma. The *Daspletosaurus* site lies in the Coal Ridge Member about 13 m below the base of the overlying Woodhawk Member, and above the Judith River–Belly River discontinuity, which the paper gives as about 76.3 Ma after Rogers and colleagues (2016, 2024). Placing the site on the Bayesian age model of Ramezani and colleagues (2022) gives a maximum model age of 76.12 +0.14/−0.47 Ma, measured against the Woodhawk stratotype section, and 75.81 +0.33/−0.37 Ma against section 91-JRT-7, which lies along strike with the site and which the paper takes as the reliable estimate. Both lie inside the Campanian. Fig. 2's caption puts the site "~15 m below" the Woodhawk contact; the text and Fig. 3 give about 13 m. Governs `stages: [Campanian]` on [Coal Ridge](../../../stratigraphy/c/coal-ridge.yml).

**Evidence.**
- warner-cowgill2025a, p. 163: "the site occurs without question in the Coal Ridge Member, which is positioned above the Judith River-Belly River discontinuity"; the discontinuity "has been dated to ~76.3 Ma (Rogers et al. 2016, 2024)".
- warner-cowgill2025a, p. 163: "Approximately 13 m separate the new specimen from the base of the overlying Woodhawk Member."
- warner-cowgill2025a, p. 163: at the stratotype section the site yields "a model age of 76.12 +0.14/- 0.47 Ma (Ramezani et al. 2022). We view this outcome as a maximum model age"; at section 91-JRT-7, "a model age of 75.81 +0.33/-0.37 Ma", which "is therefore deemed a reliable approximation of the specimen's age".
- warner-cowgill2025a, p. 161, Fig. 2: "The fossil site is located ~15 m below the contact with the marine Woodhawk Member".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.
