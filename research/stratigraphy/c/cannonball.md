# Cannonball Member

## What stages do the Cannonball Member and *Psittacosaurus mongoliensis* take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** Berriasian to Albian, for both. Nothing measured in the Cannonball beds dates them; the readings of these beds are correlations, and they disagree. Andres and Norell (2005), the one work sampling the beds, give two alternatives, Berriasian–Valanginian or Aptian–Albian, and add the *Psittacosaurus* range, Barremian to Aptian or early Albian; Rougier and colleagues (2001) give the member Valanginian–"Neocomian"; Sereno (2010) gives the *P. mongoliensis* type horizon as Aptian–Albian. Each is a reading of these beds, so the union is the member's range and the type horizon's. That it matches the Öösh Formation's range is a consequence, not the basis. Governs `stages` on the [Cannonball Member](../../../stratigraphy/c/cannonball.yml) and `stage` on *Psittacosaurus mongoliensis* in [*Psittacosaurus*](../../../genera/P/Psittacosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- andres2005a: "''Cannonball'' beds of the Öösh Formation (type area) ... AGE: Early Cretaceous (Berriasian–Valanginian or Aptian–Albian)"; the *Psittacosaurus* biochron's "established range is ''Barremian to Aptian or possibly too as young as early Albian'' (Lucas, 2001: 170)".
- rougier2001a: "Cannonball Member of Oshih Formation of Berkey and Morris (1927), possibly equivalent of Tevsh Formation (Undurukhin), Valanginian-Neocomian".
- sereno2010a: "Khukhtek Formation (Aptian-Albian); Oshih".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian–Albian alone.* Sereno's reading is one of several of the same beds; Andres and Norell hold the older alternative open.
- *Berriasian–Hauterivian alone.* It rests on correlation with the Tsagaantsav horizon and on basalt dates whose position above or below the beds the sources dispute.

**Open.** A dating measured in the Cannonball beds.
