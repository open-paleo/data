# Calcaires de Clerval

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Calcaires de Clerval at Damparis?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Oxfordian. Mannion and colleagues (2017) assign the Damparis fossil bed to the Calcaires de Clerval and date the formation by its few ammonites, which range from the Rotoides to the Stenocycloides subzones in the region; they put *Vouivria* in the late middle to early late Oxfordian. Before the bed was assigned to this formation, the Damparis assemblage had been dated three ways: upper Oxfordian by Buffetaut (1988), lower Kimmeridgian by de Broin and colleagues (1991), and middle Oxfordian, at the top of the Transversarium zone, by de Broin and colleagues (1992), at the end of a short exchange in print with Buffetaut. Two of the three fall within the Oxfordian. Governs `stages: [Oxfordian]` on [Calcaires de Clerval](../../../stratigraphy/c/calcaires-de-clerval.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mannion2017a, p. 9, Stratigraphic position and age: "The Damparis fossils thus belong to the *Calcaires de Clerval* Formation. The few ammonites yielded by this formation indicate that its deposition ranges from the *Rotoides* to the *Stenocycloides* subzones in the region".
- mannion2017a, p. 18, Systematic Paleontology: "The age of *Vouivria damparisensis* is constrained to the *Rotoides* or *Stenocycloides* subzones, late middle Oxfordian to early late Oxfordian, Late Jurassic".
- mannion2017a, p. 9, Stratigraphic position and age: "Buffetaut (1988) attributed the dinosaur assemblage to the upper Oxfordian, whereas Broin et al. (1991) placed it in the lower Kimmeridgian"; after "a short debate in the literature", de Broin and colleagues (1992) "proposed a middle Oxfordian age, at the top of the *Transversarium* zone".
- debroin1991a, p. 14: "In a level of lower Kimmeridgian age, the Solvay quarry yielded [Lapparent, 1943] the elements of a sauropod dinosaur skeleton" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Lower Kimmeridgian.* De Broin and colleagues (1991) dated the Damparis level so. The earlier stage names for the bed, *Astartien* and *Séquanien*, denote a facies that in Franche-Comté "extends from the upper Oxfordian to the lower Kimmeridgian" (mannion2017a, p. 9), so they do not fix the stage. The same authors proposed a middle Oxfordian age in 1992, and the ammonite range Mannion and colleagues give for the formation does not reach the Kimmeridgian.

**Open.** Nothing.
