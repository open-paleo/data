# Chuanjie

## How is the epithet of *Chuanjiesaurus* written in the notes?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** *anaensis*, without the apostrophe. Fang and colleagues (2000) print *a'naensis*, but ICZN Article 32.5.2 requires an apostrophe in a name to be deleted, and the genus record and later papers write *anaensis*. The note keeps the apostrophe only inside a quotation. Governs the epithet in `references[fang2000a].notes` on [Chuanjie](../../../stratigraphy/c/chuanjie.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fang2000a, translation, Fig. 3: "Chuanjiesaurus a'naensis gen et sp. nov. during excavation".
- [*Chuanjiesaurus anaensis*](../../../genera/C/Chuanjiesaurus.yml) is the species name on the genus record.

**Ruled out.**
- *a'naensis in our prose.* The describing paper's form, which the Code corrects; it is not a name the dataset may print outside a quotation.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What does Huang (2021) show for the Chuanjie Formation's age?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Huang's (2021) correlation chart draws the Chuanjie from 175.0 Ma (latest Toarcian on the timescale the chart uses) to 166.1 Ma (top of the Bathonian), above the Lower Lufeng; the Callovian is excluded. The chart does not change the record's Aalenian–Bathonian, which rests on magnetostratigraphy. Governs `stages: [Aalenian, Bajocian, Bathonian]` on Chuanjie, and keeps the Toarcian in the [Lufeng Formation](../../../stratigraphy/l/lufeng.yml) below.

**Evidence.**
- huang2021a, p. 368, Fig. 3, "Jurassic lithostratigraphic correlation chart of China: South China, West China and Tibet Stratigraphic Regions": the 滇中 (Central Yunnan) column runs 一平浪组, 下禄丰组 (Lower Lufeng), 川街组 (Chuanjie), 老罗村组, 麻地山组, 安宁组, 马头山组. The page is a single image; the Chuanjie appears nowhere in the text. Measured against the chart's own ticks, the series boundaries fall at 174.1 and 163.5 Ma (GTS 2012), the Chuanjie top at 166.1 Ma and its base on the 175 Ma line, a round-number line shared by several other columns.
- huang2021a, p. 366: "西部区(图3)包括准噶尔盆地、塔里木盆地、柴达木盆地、四川盆地、滇中盆地等大型盆地" ("the Western Region (Fig. 3) includes large basins such as the Junggar, Tarim, Qaidam, Sichuan and Central Yunnan basins", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Callovian.* The chart puts the Chuanjie top at the Bathonian–Callovian boundary.
- *Huang's chart as a stage-level determination.* The base drawn at 175.0 Ma lies about 0.9 Myr below the base of the Aalenian (174.1 Ma), and the same line serves other basins' columns. Reading the Chuanjie's base as a latest-Toarcian determination over-reads a drafting line (our reading). The stages come from earlier magnetostratigraphy ("upper Aalenian through the lower Bathonian"; Huang and colleagues 2005).

**Open.** Nothing for the stages. The chart's 175.0 Ma base is close enough that a relay of it as "the top of Toarcian to Bathonian" is a fair reading of the drawing.
