# Caturrita

## Does the Caturrita Formation reach into the Carnian?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** No. The formation is Norian. The one dating from its own rocks is a U-Pb maximum age from its fossil-rich basal beds, early Norian, and every Carnian traces to a single earlier reading. Governs `stages: [Norian]` on [Caturrita](../../../stratigraphy/c/caturrita.yml), and is consistent with `stage: [Norian]` on [*Unaysaurus tolentinoi*](../../../genera/U/Unaysaurus.yml), [*Guaibasaurus candelariensis*](../../../genera/G/Guaibasaurus.yml) and [*Macrocollum itaquii*](../../../genera/M/Macrocollum.yml).

**Evidence.**
- langer2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 7: "The single youngest analysis from this sample (z24*) with a 206Pb/238U date from 225.42 ± 0.25/0.285/0.37 Ma places a maximum limit on the age of deposition of the fossil-rich beds of the Caturrita Formation"; p. 8: "Our Caturrita Formation maximum age is early Norian (Lacian)". The sample comes from the basal sandstones at Linha São Luiz, which yield *Guaibasaurus*.
- müller2023a, geological setting: the formation "is considered early Norian (Late Triassic) according high-precision U–Pb zircon geochronology, which indicates a maximum age of 225.42 ± 0.37 Ma (Langer et al., 2018)", relaying it.

**Ruled out.**
- *Carnian.* Leal and colleagues (2004) give "Caturrita (Carnian-Norian)" and "Late Triassic (Carnian-Norian) Caturrita Formation (Andreis et al. 1980)" (leal2004a, pp. 2–3), relaying Andreis and colleagues (1980). No source read for this note dates any Caturrita bed to the Carnian on its own evidence, and the maximum age from the lowest dated beds is Norian.
- *Rhaetian.* Peyre de Fabrègues and colleagues (2015) give "Late Triassic (Norian)" for *Guaibasaurus* (peyredefabrègues2015a, Table 2); the Norian–Rhaetian rows of that table belong to other formations.

**Open.** The age of the formation's upper beds. Langer and colleagues (2018, p. 8) report that the overlying beds at Linha São Luiz are "putatively Jurassic", after Rohn and colleagues (2014), and infer a hiatus above the dated level; that reading is relayed and adds nothing here until its source is read.
