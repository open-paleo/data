# Cullín Grande

## What do Martinelli and colleagues (2007) say about the age and beds of the Cullín Grande Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Early Albian, relayed rather than determined. Martinelli and colleagues (2007) take the age from Leanza and Hugo's (1995, 1997, 2001) division of the Lohan Cura Formation into a Late Aptian Puesto Quiroga Member and an Early Albian Cullín Grande Member, and from Leanza's (2003) correlation of the member with the Pichi Neuquén and Cañadón de la Zorra members of the upper Rayoso Formation, which they cite as 2002. They add no evidence of their own for the age. At Cerro de Los Leones they describe a sandy meandering river system whose pedogenic calcrete indicates a semi-arid climate, and log four fossiliferous levels: the sauropods *Ligabuesaurus* and *Agustinia* come from levels 2 and 3, sauropods are most abundant in level 2, and turtle remains are abundant in the lower pelitic level. Governs the Albian in `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` and the `lithology` on [Cullín Grande](../../../stratigraphy/c/cullín-grande.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martinelli2007b, p. 537, Abstract: "a sandy meandering fluvial system"; "Sedimentological and paleopedological features suggest semi-arid conditions during the deposition of the Cullín Grande Member".
- martinelli2007b, p. 539: "Leanza and Hugo (1995, 1997, 2001) divided the Lohan Cura Formation into two sub-units: the Puesto Quiroga Member (Late Aptian) and the Cullín Grande Member (Early Albian)"; "The Cullín Grande Member includes 95 m of interstratified coarse-grained red sandstones, with intercalations of mudstone levels"; the member "was correlated by Leanza (2002) in the central area of the basin with the Pichi Neuquén and Cañadón de la Zorra Members (upper section of the Rayoso Formation), and assigned to the Early Albian".
- martinelli2007b, p. 539: "Well-developed pedogenic calcrete indicates paleosol formation under semi-arid climatic conditions"; "Four fossiliferous levels were located in the outcrops of the Cerro de Los Leones locality"; *Ligabuesaurus leanzai* and *Agustinia ligabuei* "were discovered in fossiliferous levels 2 and 3, respectively".
- martinelli2007b, p. 548: "The available data suggest a relative abundance of sauropods in fossiliferous level 2 of the Cullín Grande Member at Cerro de Los Leones."
- martinelli2007b, p. 549: turtle remains "are extremely abundant in the lower pelitic level of the Cullín Grande Member at Cerro de Los Leones".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** The member's thickness. Martinelli and colleagues (2007) give 95 m without naming a section; Leanza and Hugo (1995) give 92.20 m at the type locality. A measured section at Cerro de Los Leones would show whether the two figures describe different places.
