# Caiuá

## What Late Cretaceous stage does the Caiuá take?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** The Cenomanian, from Menegazzo and colleagues (2016), added under the union rule to the Aptian–Albian of Arai and Dias-Brito (2023). Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian, Cenomanian]` and the Late Cretaceous in `period` on [Caiuá](../../../stratigraphy/c/caiuá.yml).

**Evidence.**
- menegazzo2016a, p. 138: the fauna "is suggestive of Cenomanian age for the Caiua Formation", and, without the hedge, "As demonstrated by the paleontological data, the sedimentation of the Bauru Basin began in the Cenomanian with the deposition of the Caiuá and Pirapozinho formations".
- menegazzo2016a, p. 146: sedimentation "began in the Cenomanian with the Caiuá and Pirapozinho formations".
- fernandes2000a, Abstract: "The Upper Cretaceous sequence is now divided in two groups, partially contemporaneous: Caiuá Group … and Bauru Group", an epoch without a stage.

**Ruled out.**
- *Turonian.* Menegazzo and colleagues' "Cenomanian-Turonian stratigraphic interval" (Fig. 4C) is shared with the Pirapozinho Formation, whose age they give as "Cenomanian–early Turonian?"; nothing in the paper dates the Caiuá to the Turonian.
- *Coniacian–Maastrichtian.* Fernandes and Coimbra (2000) give it for the Bauru Basin as a whole, not for the group.
- *Coniacian–Campanian.* Langer and colleagues (2019) write that "a younger (Late Cretaceous) age can be suggested" from inferred synchronicity with the Adamantina Formation; a suggested correlation is not a reading.

**Open.** Nothing.
