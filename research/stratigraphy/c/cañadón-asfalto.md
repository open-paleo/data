# Cañadón Asfalto

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Cañadón Asfalto?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Toarcian to Bajocian. Cúneo and colleagues (2013) date the formation by U–Pb on zircon from the middle to late Toarcian to the Aalenian, with the Bajocian queried, and give its middle and upper sections as Aalenian to probably Bajocian; the interval is taken whole, as in the [lower Cañadón Asfalto taxa](../../topics/lower-cañadón-asfalto-taxa.md) note. Salani's (2007) K–Ar date from the base, as Cúneo and colleagues report it, falls in the same Aalenian–Bajocian. These dates replace the formation's traditional Callovian–Oxfordian age. Holwerda and colleagues (2021) add a hedged earliest Bathonian, which is not adopted. Governs `stages` and `period` on [Cañadón Asfalto](../../../stratigraphy/c/cañadón-asfalto.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cúneo2013a, p. 1274 (in-press version, p. 8), 5. Concluding remarks: "a mid-late Toarcian to Aalenian (–Bajocian?) age for the Cañadón Asfalto Formation".
- cúneo2013a, p. 1272 (in-press version, p. 6), 4.1. Geological implications: deposition resumed "at ca. 179 Ma (early-mid Toarcian) with the deposition of the Cañadón Asfalto Formation, which most probably continued into the early Middle Jurassic (Aalenian or Bajocian)"; "the exact age of the upper Cañadón Asfalto Formation is poorly constrained".
- cúneo2013a, p. 1269 (in-press version, p. 3), 2. Regional geology and stratigraphy: the formation "has been traditionally considered Middle to Late Jurassic (Callovian–Oxfordian) in age mainly based on its record of freshwater invertebrates … and plants"; "Salani (2007) reported a whole-rock K–Ar date of 170.9 ± 4.4 Ma (Aalenian–Bajocian) from the basal Cañadón Asfalto Formation (no supporting analytical data provided)".
- cúneo2013a, p. 1269 (in-press version, p. 3), 2. Regional geology and stratigraphy: "Some workers have subdivided the formation into a lower lacustrine/tuffaceous (Las Chacritas) member and an upper siliciclastic fluvial (Puesto Almada) member"; "Significantly younger radioisotopic dates of 161 ± 3 Ma, based on LA-ICPMS U–Pb analyses of tuffaceous zircon …, and 147.1 ± 3.3 Ma, based on biotite K–Ar analysis of a dacitic tuff … from what has been described as the upper Puesto Almada Member, greatly expanded the age range of the formation to the Jurassic–Cretaceous boundary. However, a significant portion of the fluvial strata commonly assigned to the latter member arguably belongs to the overlying Cañadón Calcáreo Formation".
- holwerda2021a, p. 580, Geological setting: "The early Middle Jurassic (Toarcian-Bajocian, possibly earliest Bathonian) Cañadón Asfalto Formation conformably overlies the Early Jurassic (Pliensbachian-early Toarcian …) Lonco Trapial Formation"; "the youngest radiometric age for this formation has been given as Bajocian-Bathonian (Cabaleri *et al.* 2010)".
- holwerda2021a, p. 575, Abstract: the *Patagosaurus* holotype locality, "Cerro Cóndor North, Cañadón Asfalto Formation, Patagonia, Argentina, dated to latest Early−Middle Jurassic".

**Ruled out.**
- *Callovian–Oxfordian.* The traditional age, from freshwater invertebrates and plants, which the U–Pb dates of Cúneo and colleagues (2013) replace.
- *A range reaching the Jurassic–Cretaceous boundary, from dates of 161 ± 3 Ma and 147.1 ± 3.3 Ma.* Cúneo and colleagues (2013) report that the 161 ± 3 Ma dates came with no supporting analytical data, and question both, since much of the fluvial strata assigned to the Puesto Almada Member, the source of the 147.1 Ma date, "arguably belongs" to the Cañadón Calcáreo Formation. Whether those beds belong to the formation is the unit's dispute. Cúneo and colleagues tie the 161 ± 3 Ma dates to no member.
- *Earliest Bathonian.* Holwerda and colleagues (2021) give it as "possibly", a hedge rather than a reading, and support it only by relaying Cabaleri and colleagues (2010) for a "Bajocian-Bathonian" youngest radiometric age.

**Open.** Cabaleri and colleagues (2010), the U–Pb abstract on the Las Chacritas and Puesto Almada members, has not been read. Two papers relay it differently: Cúneo and colleagues (2013), who list it as Cabaleri and colleagues 2010b, give its U–Pb dates as 161 ± 3 Ma, while Holwerda and colleagues (2021) give its youngest age as Bajocian–Bathonian. Reading it would show which member each date comes from and which relay is right.
