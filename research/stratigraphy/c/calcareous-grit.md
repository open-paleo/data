# Calcareous Grit

## Does *Priodontognathus* go in the Lower Calcareous Grit Formation?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No; the record keeps the informal Calcareous Grit. Cope and colleagues (1980) set out a Lower Calcareous Grit Formation in the Lower Oxfordian and an Upper Calcareous Grit Formation in the Upper Oxfordian, and Seeley (1893), the source of the matrix attribution, gives only "Calcareous Grit", so the matrix does not choose between them. Weishampel and colleagues' (2004) "?Lower Calcareous Grit" is a compiler's query with no stated ground. Either formation is Oxfordian, so the stage does not depend on the answer. Governs `location.formation: Calcareous Grit` on [*Priodontognathus phillipsii*](../../../genera/P/Priodontognathus.yml) and `informal: true` on [Calcareous Grit](../../../stratigraphy/c/calcareous-grit.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- weishampel2004b, p. 545: "?Lower Calcareous Grit (Seeley 1875; Galton 1980e)".

**Ruled out.** - *The Lower Calcareous Grit Formation, with a hedge.* It would rest on the compiler's query alone.

**Open.** Which of the Calcareous Grits the matrix represents, as before.

## Where does the Calcareous Grit's Oxfordian come from?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** From Cope and colleagues (1980), not Seeley (1893). Seeley, the primary for the matrix attribution, uses only rock-unit names (Coralline Oolite, Corallian, Calcareous Grit, Oxford and Kimeridge Clays) and gives no stage; the "(Oxfordian, Upper Jurassic)" that Galton (1985) attaches when citing him is Galton's. Cope and colleagues' Yorkshire columns set out two formations under the name, a Lower Calcareous Grit Formation in the Lower Oxfordian and an Upper Calcareous Grit Formation in the Upper Oxfordian, with a Middle Calcareous Grit inside the Coralline Oolite Formation. Whichever of them the matrix represents, the stage is Oxfordian. Governs `period: [Late Jurassic]` and `stages: [Oxfordian]` on [Calcareous Grit](../../../stratigraphy/c/calcareous-grit.yml).

**Evidence.**
- seeley1893a, p. 57: "There was no evidence of the stratigraphical position of the fossil other than the matrix in which it was contained which resembled Calcareous Grit, and a fossil within it which is a typical example of the variety of Pecten vagans, named P. fibrosus. On this evidence Professor Judd, F.R.S., and Mr. R. Etheridge, F.R.S., at the time the paper was read, suggested that the fossil was from the Calcareous Grit of Yorkshire."
- seeley1893a, p. 52: the femur's label, "Found A.D. 1838, on the Earl of Carlisle's estate at Slingsby, Yorkshire, imbedded in a thin stratum of earth and calcareous matter between two of the upper strata of limestone in a quarry three quarters of a mile east of the village of Slingsby".
- seeley1893a, p. 57: the maxilla "may be a portion of the head of the Slingsby reptile"; "If this suggestion should be supported by further evidence, the name Priodontognathus may take precedence of Omosaurus".
- galton1985d, p. 251: the maxilla "probably came from the Calcareous Grit (Oxfordian, Upper Jurassic) of Yorkshire (Seeley, 1893)".
- cope1980a, Fig. 11b, columns O16 (Vale of Pickering) and O17 (Hambleton and Tabular Hills): "LOWER CALCAREOUS GRIT FORM." beneath the "CORALLINE OOLITE FORMATION", in the Lower Oxfordian *cordatum* Zone; "UPPER CALCAREOUS GRIT FORMATION" above it, in the Upper Oxfordian; "MIDDLE CALCAREOUS GRIT" within the Coralline Oolite Formation in column O17.
- cope1980a, p. 71, notes to column O11: the Long Stanton fauna "is placed towards the base of the glosense Zone following the discovery of elements of this fauna in the basal Upper Calcareous Grit in Yorkshire".

**Ruled out.**
- *Seeley (1893) as the source of the Oxfordian.* His text names no stage or system; Galton (1985) supplies the parenthesis when citing him.
- *The Slingsby quarry as the maxilla's locality.* Only the femur carries the 1838 label; Seeley raises the identity of the two specimens as a possibility conditional on "further evidence", and gives the maxilla no history before 1861. This adds the femur's label to the 2026-09-30 entry of [*Priodontognathus*](../../genera/P/Priodontognathus.md), which rules out Slingsby.

**Open.** Which of the Calcareous Grits the matrix represents. Seeley gives only "Calcareous Grit"; the stage does not depend on the answer.
