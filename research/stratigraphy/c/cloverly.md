# Cloverly

## Does the Cloverly Formation begin in the Berriasian?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** No. D'Emic and colleagues name the Valanginian as the formation's lowest stage; their "ca. 140 Ma" is an approximate bound inferred from other work, not a dated sample, so the stage they name governs. Governs the Valanginian start of `stages` on [Cloverly](../../../stratigraphy/c/cloverly.yml).

**Evidence.**
- d'emic2019a, abstract: deposition "spanned the Valanginian–Cenomanian stages (ca. 140 Ma–98 Ma)"; the lowest dated sample, CL-7 at the base of the Little Sheep Mudstone Member about 2.5 m above the Pryor Conglomerate, gives 129.4 ± 3.4 Ma (Table 3).

**Ruled out.**
- *Berriasian from "ca. 140 Ma".* On the current chart 140 Ma is late Berriasian, but the figure rests on the absence of younger zircons in Pryor equivalents, a lull in arc magmatism and Berriasian–Hauterivian palynology from beds below the Pryor, not on a date from the Cloverly; CONTRIBUTING's rule on reading ages on the chart follows the stage a paper names when its number is not a measured date.

**Open.** Nothing.

## How should a Cloverly taxon be dated?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** By member, using D'Emic and colleagues' (2019) member ages, not by the formation-wide range. Governs the stages of Cloverly taxa, such as [*Zephyrosaurus schaffi*](../../../genera/Z/Zephyrosaurus.yml) (provisionally Aptian), and the [Himes](../../../stratigraphy/h/himes.yml) Member's [Albian, Cenomanian].

**Evidence.**
- d'emic2019a, p. 3: "the Pryor Conglomerate, was deposited ca. 140–130 Ma"; the "Little Sheep Mudstone Member was deposited ca. 124–109 Ma"; "(ca. 109–98 Ma), sediment sourced from the east was deposited as the Himes Member and Greybull Sandstone".
- d'emic2019a, pp. 7, 9: the Pryor is Ostrom's Unit IV, the Little Sheep Unit V and the Himes Units VI–VII; p. 32: "Units IV–VII of Ostrom (1970) represent facies and cannot be used as timelines".
- zanno2023a, Fig. 21: *Zephyrosaurus* "(middle of the Little Sheep Member, Cloverly Formation [122], mean age ~116.5 Myr [109])", citing Oreska and colleagues (2013) and D'Emic and colleagues (2019).

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian–Albian for the whole formation.* Farke and colleagues (2014) are not an all-Albian source: they give the *Aquilops* horizon as "?middle–late Albian, between 104 and 109 million years old" (farke2014a, p. 1) and the basal Pryor as "pre-Aptian" (p. 4).

**Open.** *Zephyrosaurus*'s member. Oreska and colleagues (2013) report "cf. ZEPHYROSAURUS sp." teeth from localities in Units V and VI (oreska2013a, pp. 266–267, 278) and never mention *Z. schaffi* or its holotype, so Zanno and colleagues' "middle of the Little Sheep" for the holotype rests on a citation that does not concern it. Sues (1980), the describing paper, would give the holotype's horizon.
