# Candeleros

## Does Gallina and Apesteguía's "Cenomanian-Turonian" date the Candeleros?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** No; the formation stays Cenomanian. Every Turonian given for the Candeleros traces to a span across several formations, to a date from the overlying Huincul, or to relays of those. Leanza and colleagues (2004) date the whole Río Limay Subgroup Cenomanian to early Turonian but the Candeleros itself early Cenomanian; Corbella and colleagues' (2004) fission-track date is on a tuff in the Huincul, and they call the Candeleros "Late Cenomanian at least"; Makovicky and colleagues (2012) attribute a "Cenomanian–Turonian" Candeleros to Leanza and colleagues (2004), who do not give it. Gallina and Apesteguía's (2005) unsourced label belongs to the same framework, the same paper citing Corbella and colleagues for the Huincul. Settles the Open line of the entry below. Governs `stages: [Cenomanian]` on [Candeleros](../../../stratigraphy/c/candeleros.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leanza2004a: "On the basis of basin analysis, its age can be regarded as Cenomanian to Early Turonian" for the Río Limay Subgroup; "The age of the Candeleros Formation may be regarded as Early Cenomanian."
- corbella2004a, p. 231: the younger Huincul "does not necessarily imply a much younger age for the underlying Candeleros Formation, which is Late Cenomanian at least".
- makovicky2012a, p. 91: "The Candeleros Formation is regarded as Cenomanian–Turonian in age (Leanza et al., 2004)."
- gallina2005a, p. 153: "the upper member of the Candeleros Formation (Cenomanian-Turonian)", with no source; p. 154 dates the Huincul after Leanza and Hugo (2001) and Corbella and colleagues (2004).

**Ruled out.**
- *The Turonian from Gallina and Apesteguía (2005).* An unsourced label in the framework above, not a dating of the formation.
- *The Turonian from Makovicky and colleagues (2012) and Pittman and colleagues (2020).* Both cite Leanza and colleagues (2004) for an age that paper gives the subgroup, not the Candeleros.
- *The Huincul's fission-track date.* It dates the unit above.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the Candeleros Albian, or does it reach the Turonian?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** Neither. The formation is Cenomanian, on the zircon dating and the unconformity beneath it. Governs `period: [Late Cretaceous]` and `stages: [Cenomanian]` on [Candeleros](../../../stratigraphy/c/candeleros.yml).

**Evidence.**
- garrido2010a, p. 135: "se asigna a la Fm. Candeleros una edad Cenomaniano Inferior" ("the Candeleros Fm. is assigned a Lower Cenomanian age", translated); p. 134 reports that on "la asociación icnológica de vertebrados" Calvo (1991) "ubicó a estos depósitos dentro del Albiano" ("placed these deposits within the Albian", translated); p. 133, figure caption: "Contacto entre la Fm. Candeleros (Cenomaniano) y la Fm. Lohan Cura (Albiano)" ("Contact between the Candeleros Fm. (Cenomanian) and the Lohan Cura Fm. (Albian)", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian.* It comes from one author's faunal and ichnological correlations at member rank, in the scheme that made the unit "the Candeleros Member of the Rio Limay Formation", "dated as Albian based on the vertebrate fauna" (calvo1999a, Abstract). Garrido (2010) reports it and sets it aside for the Lower Cenomanian. The one later instance read is Calvo relaying himself: "Albiano (sensu CALVO, 1999) de la Formación Candeleros" ("Albian (sensu CALVO, 1999) from the Candeleros Formation", translated; calvo2010a, p. 101).
- *Turonian, from three-formation spans.* Leanza and colleagues give "the Candeleros, Huincul and Cerro Lisandro formations. The age of these rocks is probably Cenomanian–Early Turonian" (leanza2004a, p. 77), and Bellardini and colleagues give "(Candeleros, Huincul and Cerro Lisandro formations, Cenomanian – Turonian in age)" (bellardini2023a, p. 2589; bellardini2025a, p. 3, Geological setting). Each is one range for three superposed formations, the uppermost of which carries the Turonian.
- *Coniacian.* Apesteguía (2007) gives "lower section of the Candeleros Formation (presumably), Coniacian (Leanza and Hugo, 1997)" (apesteguía2007a, p. 544). The map notice he cites gives the Coniacian to six formations together: "las Formaciones Candeleros, Huincul, Cerro Lisandro, Portezuelo, Plottier y Bajo de la Carpa se habrían depositado durante el Cenomaniano al Coniaciano" ("the Candeleros, Huincul, Cerro Lisandro, Portezuelo, Plottier and Bajo de la Carpa formations would have been deposited during the Cenomanian to Coniacian", translated; leanza1997a, Grupo Neuquén).

**Open.** Gallina and Apesteguía (2005) describe La Buitrera as the place where "the upper member of the Candeleros Formation (Cenomanian-Turonian) as well as the basal member of the Huincul Formation (Turonian-Coniacian) crop out" (gallina2005a, p. 153), with no source given for either age. Whether that is a reading of the formation's age or a passing locality label has not been decided; if it counts, the formation takes the Turonian.
