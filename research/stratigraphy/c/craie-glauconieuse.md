# Craie glauconieuse

## Is the Northern Ireland "Glauconitic Chalk" the Normandy Craie glauconieuse?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. "Glauconitic Chalk" is Portlock's 1843 name for part of the Ulster chalk, now within the Ulster White Limestone Group; it is not a variant of the Norman Cenomanian formation. Governs keeping "Glauconitic Chalk" out of the variants on Craie glauconieuse.

**Evidence.**
- hopson2005a, p. 49: "Portlock (1843) suggested three divisions … the Lower Chalk (Glauconitic Chalk)"; Fletcher "excluded the 'Glauconitic Chalk' … regarding this as a local, structurally controlled, facies"; p. 55 (Ulster White Limestone Group), previous names: "Upper Chalk (White Limestone) and Lower Chalk (Glauconitic Chalk) by Portlock (1843)".
- buffetaut2024a, p. 179: the Norman unit appears in English as "lower Cenomanian glauconitic Chalk", lower-case.

**Ruled out.**
- *A published English name "Glauconitic Chalk" for the Craie glauconieuse.* Hoyez and colleagues (2020) write in French; no English version of their paper exists.

**Open.** Nothing.
