# Chilson

## Why are Sohn's 118 to 128 m.y. not placed on the chart?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** Because they are a stage bracket given numbers, not a date. Sohn (1979) infers a pre-Aptian age for the Lakota Formation from its ostracodes, and then, reading van Hinte's (1976) time scale for the interval between the Morrison and the base of the Aptian, estimates the Chilson's ostracode-bearing rocks at "somewhere between 118 m.y. and 128 m.y.". A number hung on a biostratigraphic level is that level's stage, so the stage is carried over and the number is not re-placed. Governs the note on sohn1979a on [Chilson](../../../stratigraphy/c/chilson.yml); `stages` unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- sohn1979a, p. 4: "I estimate the timespan of the rocks represented by the ostracodes to be less than 10 m.y., somewhere between 118 m.y. and 128 m.y. before the present."
- sohn1979a, p. 1: "the Lakota Formation is inferred to be pre-Aptian in age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Placing 118 to 128 m.y. on the ICS chart.* They are not measured ages.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Chilson Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Berriasian to Valanginian, as a dating of the lower part of unit 2 only. Cifelli and colleagues (2014) date that part late Berriasian–Valanginian from ostracods and charophytes, which come mostly from the lower part of unit 2; unit 1 has yielded none, as the 2026-11-23 entry on the [Lakota](../l/lakota.md) records with the dating itself. The member's upper limit is therefore not fixed by these data. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian]` on [Chilson](../../../stratigraphy/c/chilson.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cifelli2014a, p. 35: "The lower Lakota Formation (specifically the lower part of Chilson Member unit 2) in the southern Black Hills has been comparatively well sampled micropaleontologically".
- cifelli2014a, p. 46, Discussion: "given that most of the biostratigraphically informative ostracods and charophytes in Chilson unit 2 derive from the lower part of the section …, upper age constraints for the member remain to be firmly established".

**Ruled out.** Nothing among the works cited here.

**Open.** The member's upper limit. Microfossils from the upper part of unit 2 would settle it; the lower limit stays open as the Lakota entry says.
