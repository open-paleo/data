# Charmouth Mudstone

## Where do the base and top of the Charmouth Mudstone lie in Page's (2009) ammonite zonation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Page (2009) puts the base of the formation, above the Blue Lias, in the Semicostatum Chronozone of the Lower Sinemurian over most of south-west England, and on the Devon-Dorset coast in the succeeding Scipionanum Subchronozone above a non-sequence. On the Dorset coast the top 60 cm of the formation yields *Amaltheus bifurcatus*, which marks, at least in part, the base of the Upper Pliensbachian; in East Somerset the age of the top is unclear and the formation passes upward into the Dyrham Silts Formation. The abstract gives the West Dorset ammonite faunas a top in the "topmost Davoei Chronozone" of the Lower Pliensbachian, slightly lower than the text's *bifurcatus* level; both are Pliensbachian. Page credits the name to Cox and colleagues, whom he cites as 1998, as a replacement for the informal "Lower Lias clays". Governs `stages: [Sinemurian, Pliensbachian]` on [Charmouth Mudstone](../../../stratigraphy/c/charmouth-mudstone.yml).

**Evidence.**
- page2009a, p. 19: the faunas of the West Dorset coast "range from mid Lower Sinemurian (Semicostatum Chronozone) to Lower Pliensbachian (topmost Davoei Chronozone)".
- page2009a, p. 20: "proposed by Cox et al. (1998, available at www.bgs.ac.uk) as … a replacement for the informal term 'Lower Lias clays'".
- page2009a, p. 21: "the base of the Charmouth Mudstone Formation lies within the Semicostatum Chronozone throughout most of the region"; "on the Devon-Dorset coast the base of the Formation lies within the succeeding Scipionanum Subchronozone with a non-sequence below", with a Lyra Subchronozone fauna "at the top of the Blue Lias Formation".
- page2009a, p. 27: "The top 60 cm or so of the Seatown Mudstone Member and hence the Formation on the Dorset coast has yielded Amaltheus bifurcatus", "indicating, in part at least, PbZ24 at the base of the Stokesi Subchronozone (Margaritatus Chronozone) and hence the base of the Upper Pliensbachian"; "The age of the top of the Formation in E Somerset is unclear and the sequence passes upwards into the Dyrham Silts Formation".
