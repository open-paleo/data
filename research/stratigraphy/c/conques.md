# Conques

## How did Cuevas (1992) define the Conques Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Cuevas (1992) names the Conques Mudstones, Sandstones and Conglomerates Formation as the second of the six formations into which he divides the Tremp Group, with two limestone members: the Basturs Limestones and, about 50 m above them, the Tossal d'Obà Limestones. The formation is variegated mudstone of mostly light tones with lenticular sandstone, and with conglomerate toward the base on the northeastern margin of the basin. The type section, north of Basturs in the Barranco de Basturs, is about 400 m thick. The base is generally transitional with the gray mudstones of the Posa Formation; the top is a rapid transition to the red mudstones of the Talarn Formation, except north of Tremp, where the contact is erosive. The paper gives the formation no age (see the 2026-11-23 entry on [Tremp](../t/tremp.md)). Governs `lithology` and `parent: Tremp` on [Conques](../../../stratigraphy/c/conques.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cuevas1992a, p. 97: "Conques Mudstones, Sandstones and Conglomerates Formation. It comprises two members: the Tossal d'Obà Limestones and the Basturs Limestones" (translated).
- cuevas1992a, p. 99: "variegated mudstones, of predominantly light tones, which intercalate lenticular sandstone levels and, on the NE margin of the Basin, conglomerates toward the base"; "The base is generally transitional with the gray mudstones of the Posa Formation. The top is marked by a rapid transition to the red mudstones of the Talarn Formation, except in the area north of Tremp, where this boundary takes the form of an erosive contact" (translated).
- cuevas1992a, p. 100: the type section was measured "north of Basturs, in the Barranco de Basturs, where it has a total thickness of about 400 m"; the Tossal d'Obà Member "lies about 50 m above the Basturs Member" (translated).
