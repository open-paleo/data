# Inferior Oolite

## What do the cited works say about the rank and age of the Inferior Oolite?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** A group with no parent unit, Aalenian to Bathonian. Barron and colleagues (2012) treat it as the Inferior Oolite Group, give its parent as "None", and date it "Aalenian to Bathonian, Opalinum Zone to Zigzag Zone", which their text puts as "Early Aalenian to earliest Bathonian"; the Zigzag Zone is the earliest Bathonian zone, so the two statements agree. In southern England the group lies on the Lias Group and under the Great Oolite Group. The Aalenian and early Bajocian part is missing locally: "Aalenian to lower Bajocian strata are absent adjacent to the Mendip High", which narrows the group's age at that place and not its range. Governs `rank: group`, the absent `parent`, and `stages: [Aalenian, Bajocian, Bathonian]` on the [Inferior Oolite](../../../stratigraphy/i/inferior-oolite.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barron2012a, p. 8: "Aalenian to lower Bajocian strata are absent adjacent to the Mendip High but are seen to the south in the Wessex Basin (Somerset and Dorset)".
- barron2012a, p. 17: "the Lower Jurassic Lias Group is overlain in turn by the Inferior Oolite and Great Oolite groups of Aalenian to earliest Callovian age".
- barron2012a, p. 23: the Inferior Oolite Group "ranges from Early Aalenian to earliest Bathonian in age".
- barron2012a, p. 29: "Parent None"; "Age Aalenian to Bathonian, Opalinum Zone to Zigzag Zone".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aalenian to earliest Callovian for this group.* The span on p. 17 covers the Inferior Oolite and Great Oolite groups together; the group's own entry and text end in the earliest Bathonian.

**Open.** Nothing.
