# Isalo III

## What do Rauhut and López-Arbarello (2009) say about the Isalo III fauna and its age?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** They treat the Isalo III as late Middle Jurassic, roughly contemporaneous with the Tiouaren Formation of Niger and the Cañadón Asfalto Formation of Argentina, and use its sauropods as a southern Gondwanan comparison fauna. Following Buffetaut (2005), *Archaeodontosaurus* is a very basal sauropod, "almost surprisingly basal for a late Middle Jurassic taxon". *Lapparentosaurus*, from the same unit, is harder to place: earlier analyses found it in a weakly supported position within titanosauriforms, while its vertebrae point to a much more basal position. The northern African *Jobaria* and *Atlasaurus* group shows no close relationship with the Cañadón Asfalto or, as far as can be said, the Isalo III sauropods. The authors suggest that the southern Gondwanan faunas may be uniformly more basal than the northern African ones, which might be true of the Isalo III with uncertainty from *Lapparentosaurus*, and offer as one possible explanation a barrier of aridity and deserts across central Gondwana, with occasional exchange along the more humid eastern margin that might account for a more derived *Lapparentosaurus*. The paper dates the unit only by this comparison, so it supports the period and adds no stage. Governs `period: [Middle Jurassic]`, leaving `stages: [Bathonian]` unchanged, on [Isalo III](../../../stratigraphy/i/isalo-iii.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2009a, p. 264: the northern African group "does not show any close relationship with roughly contemporaneous sauropod faunas from the Cañadón Asfalto Formation of Argentina, or (as far as can be said) the Isalo III Formation of Madagascar"; "Archaeodontosaurus from the Isalo III Formation seems to be a very basal form (Buffetaut, 2005), almost surprisingly basal for a late Middle Jurassic taxon. Lapparentosaurus from the same unit is a much more problematic taxon"; it was found "in a weakly supported position within titanosauriforms", but "the structure of the vertebrae indicates a much more basal position for this taxon".
- rauhut2009a, p. 264: "The same might be true for the Isalo III Formation of Madagascar, though some uncertainty remains, due to the problematic phylogenetic position of Lapparentosaurus"; a barrier "possibly due to pronounced aridity and associated extensive deserts in central Gondwana"; exchange "possibly along the more humid eastern Gondwanan margin, which might account for the possibly more derived phylogenetic position of Lapparentosaurus".
- rauhut2009a, p. 265: an assemblage that, "in comparison with faunas from southern Gondwana, such as that of the Cañadón Asfalto Formation of Argentina or the Isalo III Formation of Madagascar", indicates "faunal differences between northern and southern Gondwanan dinosaur faunas at that time".

**Ruled out.**
- *Callovian, from "late Middle Jurassic".* The phrase places the Isalo III in a comparison of roughly contemporaneous faunas; the paper does not date the unit to a stage, and the Bathonian rests on the papers that do.

**Open.** Nothing.
