# Itombe

## What does the Coniacian on the Tadi Beds and the Itombe rest on?

*2026-09-27*

**Conclusion.** On Mateus and colleagues (2019), who report the *Angolatitan* locality at Iembe as Coniacian on an ammonite. It does not rest on the 1960 "Senonian" reading of the Tadi Beds, which stays excluded. Governs `stages: [Turonian, Coniacian]` on [Itombe](../../../stratigraphy/i/itombe.yml) and on its bed Tadi.

**Evidence.**
- mateus2019a, p. 62: *Angolatitan adamastor* is from Iembe, "originally considered Turonian in age but following the discovery of the ammonite *Protexanites* sp. at Iembe, it is now considered Coniacian".
- mateus2011a, p. 222: "Tadi Beds, Itombe Formation, Late Turonian (around 90 M.a.)" for the same taxon.

**Ruled out.**
- *Coniacian from the 1960 study.* The term "Tadi beds" was coined in "the 1960 unpublished study of G. Brognon, G. Verrier, and R Thiers", which "considered the 'Tadi Beds' to represent the base of the 'Senonian' (= Coniacian), but the fish fauna led Antunes (1961, 1964: 56) to consider the Tadi Beds to be of late Turonian age" (mateus2011a, p. 222). The reading is unpublished, known only through later papers, and set aside in the sentence that reports it. It would not support the Coniacian if the 2019 dating were withdrawn.

**Open.** Mateus and colleagues (2019) name neither the bed nor the formation; the Iembe locality is placed in the Tadi Beds of the Itombe by Mateus and colleagues (2011). The Coniacian therefore reaches the Tadi through that placement, and the Itombe through the Tadi.
