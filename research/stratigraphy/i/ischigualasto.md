# Ischigualasto

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Ischigualasto Formation and its contacts?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Carnian and Norian on the dated ashes. Rogers and colleagues (1993) dated a bentonite about 80 m above the base, in the Cancha de Bochas Member, at 227.8 ± 0.3 Ma. Ezcurra (2010) relays a recalculation of that date to about 230.3–231.4 Ma, has deposition begin "approximately 231 Ma during the late Carnian", and calls the lower third of the formation, which holds *Herrerasaurus*, *Eoraptor* and *Panphagia*, late Carnian. Shipman (2004) places the lower half "in the middle Carnian Stage" on the same tuff. Near the top, Shipman prints his own ash at 352 m in his section 3 as 218 ± 1.7 Ma in the text, 218.5 Ma in some figures and 222.1 ± 2.2 Ma in others. Ezcurra's recalculated 219.4–220.4 ± 1.7 Ma, which puts the top of the Valle de la Luna Member "in the mid-Norian", starts from a figure the thesis does not print (see [Valle de la Luna](../v/valle-de-la-luna.md)). All of these near-top figures are Norian on the ICS chart v2024/12 (Norian base 227.3 Ma), and Ezcurra puts the Carnian–Norian boundary near the middle of the formation. Cox (1965) had already assigned the fauna provisionally to the Carnian. He relied on Colbert's (1963) view that saurischians arose only at the start of the Late Triassic, and on *Aetosauroides*, which Casamiquela (1961) held close to the Norian *Aëtosaurus*. The base is the Peña Conglomerate, above a regional unconformity on the Los Rastros Formation (Shipman, 2004). The upper contact with the Los Colorados Formation is sharp at Cerro Rajado, but Caselli and colleagues (2001) say its nature varies across the basin, and Shipman has the Los Colorados lying unconformably on the formation in the north and conformably in the south. Following Currie and colleagues (2009), Ezcurra recognizes four members, from the lowest: La Peña, Cancha de Bochas, Valle de la Luna and Quebrada de la Sal. Governs the Carnian and Norian in `stages: [Ladinian, Carnian, Norian]` on [Ischigualasto](../../../stratigraphy/i/ischigualasto.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ezcurra2010a, p. 371: "The lower third of the Ischigualasto Fm. (late Carnian) preserves the remains of the basal saurischian dinosaurs Herrerasaurus, Eoraptor and Panphagia".
- ezcurra2010a, p. 372: four members, "from the lowermost to the uppermost: the La Peña, Cancha de Bochas, Valle de la Luna and Quebrada de la Sal members"; sanidine from a bentonite "approximately 80 m above the base of the formation (Cancha de Bochas Member)" "yielded an 40Ar/39Ar date of 227.8 ± 0.3 Ma (Rogers et al. 1993)", recalculated "to be about 230.3–231.4 ± 0.3 Ma"; "the deposition of the sedimentary unit began approximately 231 Ma during the late Carnian"; the recalculated age "is about 219.4–220.4 ± 1.7 Ma. This age locates the top of the Valle de la Luna Member of the Ischigualasto Fm. in the mid-Norian"; "the Carnian–Norian boundary should be located close to the middle of the formation".
- shipman2004a, p. 14, Abstract: the formation "was deposited during the late Norian Age"; p. 26: "the Carnian (Upper Triassic) Ischigualasto Formation".
- shipman2004a, p. 28: "Above the Los Rastros Formation is a regional unconformity. The unconformity is overlain by the Peña Conglomerate that represents the base of the Ischigualasto Formation"; Rogers and colleagues' tuff "places the lower half of the Ischigualasto Formation in the middle Carnian Stage"; the Los Colorados Formation "lies unconformably in the north and conformably to the south on top of the Ischigualasto Formation".
- shipman2004a, p. 77: "The date we did obtain was from section 3 at 352 m and produced a date of 218 ± 1.7Ma".
- shipman2004a, Fig. A-9, p. 63, Fig. A-11, p. 65, and Fig. B-10, p. 105: "352m 222.1± 2.2 Ma" beside section 3.
- cox1965a, p. 509: "If Colbert's opinion is nevertheless accepted, it would indicate a Carnian age for the Ischigualasto deposits, from which Reig (1963) has described a carnosaurian and two genera of coelurosaurian saurischians. Such an Upper Triassic date for this fauna is also supported by the presence of a pseudosuchian, Aetosauroides, which Casamiquela (1961) considers to be closely related to Aëtosaurus from the Norian of Germany".
- cox1965a, p. 510: the Argentinian fauna "has above provisionally been assigned to the Carnian"; "The datings must therefore be regarded as provisional".
- caselli2001a, p. 175: "a sharp contact with the Ischigualasto Formation was observed. This relationship, which could be clearly seen on the southeastern slope of Cerro Rajado, has been a matter of controversy, apparently varying according to positions in the basin" (translated).
- caselli2001a, p. 176, Fig. 1: the legend groups "Fm. Los Rastros, Chañares e Ischigualasto" under "Triásico medio" ("Middle Triassic", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Norian for the whole formation.* Shipman's abstract (p. 14) is contradicted by the thesis's own "Carnian (Upper Triassic) Ischigualasto Formation" (p. 26) and its middle Carnian lower half (p. 28). A slip that its own document contradicts is not a reading (see [methods](../../methods.md)).
- *Middle Triassic from Caselli and colleagues' map.* "Triásico medio" is a legend label covering three formations, with no argument. A label is not a reading that supports an epoch in `period` (see [methods](../../methods.md)).

**Open.** The Ladinian in `stages`, and with it the Middle Triassic in `period`, come from Martínez and Alcober (2009) and Reig (1963), which this entry does not examine. Martínez and Alcober's "228 My (earliest Carnian)" level near the base lies 0.7 Myr below the current base of the Norian. Shipman's three figures for the 352 m ash (218 ± 1.7, 218.5 and 222.1 ± 2.2 Ma) are not reconciled in the thesis; all three are Norian.
