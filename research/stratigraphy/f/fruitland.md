# Fruitland

## What do the ash-bed dates of Fassett and Steiner (1997) give for the age of the Fruitland Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian. Fassett and Steiner (1997) date two ash beds within the formation in the southwestern San Juan Basin, one in its lowest outcropping coal bed at 75.56 ± 0.41 Ma and one at the top of the formation at 74.11 ± 0.62 Ma. Both ages, with their errors, fall before the base of the Maastrichtian, at 72.2 Ma on the current ICS chart as in GTS2020 (our reading). Governs `stages: [Campanian]` on [Fruitland](../../../stratigraphy/f/fruitland.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fassett1997a, p. 239: "The section traversed the upper part of the continental Upper Cretaceous Fruitland Formation, the Kirtland Shale, and the lower part of the Tertiary Ojo Alamo Sandstone."
- fassett1997a, p. 243: ash DEP "is in the upper part of the stratigraphically lowest outcropping Fruitland Formation coal bed in the San Juan Basin"; ash 4 "is at the top of a coal bed at the top of the Fruitland Formation".
- fassett1997a, p. 243: "ash DEP, 29 m, 75.56 ±0.41 Ma; ash 2, 136m, 74.56 ± 0.13 Ma; ash 4, 181 m, 74.11 ±0.62 Ma".
- gale2020a, p. 1041: the base of the Maastrichtian "is approximately 72.2 Ma".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
