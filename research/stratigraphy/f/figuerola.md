# Figuerola

## Is Fondevilla and colleagues' late Maastrichtian a live dispute?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute is removed and the stages stand, Maastrichtian and Danian. Fondevilla and colleagues (2019) conclude that the formation "only records the late Maastrichtian", but their own Fig. 9 draws the Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary near its top, and the chrons they assign, C31n to C29n, reach across it; a statement its own document contradicts is not a reading. Colombo and Cuevas (1993) place the boundary in the upper third of the formation. The point now sits in the note on fondevilla2019a. Governs the removed `dispute` and that note on [Figuerola](../../../stratigraphy/f/figuerola.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fondevilla2019a, p. 174: "Hence, the latter formation only records the late Maastrichtian (Fig. 8)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Maastrichtian only.* Contradicted by the same paper.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does Fondevilla and colleagues' "only records the late Maastrichtian" take the Figuerola Formation out of the Danian?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** No. Fondevilla and colleagues (2019) re-date the underlying La Maçana Formation from the late Campanian to the early Maastrichtian, and so reassign the magnetozones that Galbrun and colleagues (1993) found in the Figuerola from chrons C32n–C29n to C31n–C29n. They conclude that the formation "only records the late Maastrichtian", but the chrons they assign still end in C29r and C29n, which span the Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary, and their chart of the Àger section draws the 66 Ma line through the formation's upper part. The reassignment moves the formation's base, not its top (our reading of the chron lists), and leaves the 2026-10-08 entry standing. Colombo and Cuevas (1993) place the boundary in the formation on paleomagnetic and paleontological data, while saying that the paleontological data alone leave its position uncertain over 15–20 m, and they put the disappearance of Cretaceous charophytes and dinosaurs about two million years before the boundary. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian, Danian]` and the `dispute` summary on [Figuerola](../../../stratigraphy/f/figuerola.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fondevilla2019a, p. 174, 2.2.4. The Àger Syncline (Fontllonga section): Galbrun and colleagues correlated the Figuerola reversals "with chrons C32n, C31r, C31n, C30r, C30n, C29r and C29n" and "stated that all the late Maastrichtian chrons were represented in the Figuerola Fm and that it contained the K-Pg transition"; "the La Maçana Fm is dated as early Maastrichtian instead of late Campanian. After this age recalibration, the magnetozones found in the Figuerola Fm by Galbrun et al. (1993) represent chrons C31n-C29n instead of chrons C32n-C29n interval. Hence, the latter formation only records the late Maastrichtian (Fig. 8)."
- fondevilla2019a, Fig. 9: the Àger section, with the 66 Ma line drawn within chron C29r in the upper part of the "Lower Red Garumnian" Figuerola Fm, and C29n labeled Danian.
- colombo1993a, p. 15, abstract: "Accordingly with the new paleomagnetic and paleontologic data, the disappearance of the cretaceous charophytes and dinosaurs seems to have occurred in the studied area about two million years before K/T boundary event."
- colombo1993a, p. 20: the Figuerola "corresponds (Puigdefábregas et al., 1989) to the lower detrital interval [tramo detrítico inferior] with a thickness of about 195 m" (translated).
- colombo1993a, p. 28: "current paleontological knowledge does not allow the position of the K/T boundary in the Àger area to be known with sufficient accuracy, since there is an interval of imprecision of the order of 15-20 m" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian only, as the formation's full range.* Fondevilla and colleagues' (2019) sentence read on its own would end the Figuerola at the boundary. Their own chron list for the formation includes C29r and C29n, and their Fig. 9 draws 66 Ma inside it, so the sentence does not support dropping the Danian. It is not a typographical slip either, so it is not set aside under the rule on slips in [methods](../../methods.md); it stays as reported in the dispute summary.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does the Figuerola Formation reach the Danian?

*2026-10-08*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Colombo and Cuevas, who name the Fontllonga Group, place the Cretaceous–Tertiary boundary in the upper third of the Figuerola, so it takes the Danian as well as the Maastrichtian. The group runs from the Maastrichtian into the lower Thanetian. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian, Danian]`, `period` and `parent: Fontllonga` on [Figuerola](../../../stratigraphy/f/figuerola.yml), and the new [Fontllonga](../../../stratigraphy/f/fontllonga.yml) entry. [*Fylax thyrakolasus*](../../../genera/F/Fylax.yml) stays Maastrichtian on its own dated horizon.

**Evidence.**
- colombo1993a, p. 15: "ese evento se produce en el tercio superior de la unidad terrígena de la Fm. Figuerola" ("this event occurs in the upper third of the terrigenous unit of the Figuerola Fm.", translated); the deposits formed "entre el Maastrichtiense y el Paleógeno inferior"; p. 30: the section spans "desde el Maastrichtiense inferior a Taneciense inferior", with chron C29r, "donde se sitúa el límite K/T", identified.
- prieto-márquez2021a, p. 427: the *Fylax* type horizon is "chron C29r within the Figuerola Formation", from "uppermost Maastrichtian strata".

**Ruled out.**
- *The boundary at the Milla limestones.* Colombo and Cuevas report it was earlier tied to the Milla carbonates, above the Figuerola, and move it down into the Figuerola.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which group does the Figuerola Formation belong to?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The Fontllonga Group of the Àger Syncline, not the [Tremp Group](../../../stratigraphy/t/tremp.yml). Governs the `parent` of Figuerola (no Fontllonga unit exists yet) and the formation of [*Fylax thyrakolasus*](../../../genera/F/Fylax.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fondevilla2019a, p. 170: "Colombo and Cuevas (1993) did the same for the Àger Syncline, replacing the Tremp Fm by the Fontllonga Group", in which "the La Maçana Fm replaces the 'Grey Garumnian'; the Figuerola Fm is equivalent to the 'Lower Red Garumnian'; the Millà Fm … the Perauba Complex".
- prieto-márquez2021a, p. 427: the *Fylax* type locality, Fontllonga-R, is "in the southern limb of the Àger syncline"; type horizon "chron C29r within the Figuerola Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Figuerola as the Talarn Formation.* In the Tremp Syncline "the Conques and Talarn formations are equivalent to the 'Lower Red Garumnian'" (fondevilla2019a, p. 170); the Figuerola is the Àger Syncline's equivalent, a separate unit in a separate syncline (our reading of the two statements).
- *A "Fontllonga Formation".* Prieto-Márquez and colleagues print "Fontllonga Formation" in their Fig. 1C caption (p. 426) and conclusions (p. 433); Fontllonga is the group.

**Open.** Pujalte and Schmitz's scheme, which Fondevilla and colleagues cite, has not been read.
