# Figuerola

## Does the Figuerola Formation reach the Danian?

*2026-10-08*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Colombo and Cuevas, who name the Fontllonga Group, place the Cretaceous–Tertiary boundary in the upper third of the Figuerola, so it takes the Danian as well as the Maastrichtian. The group runs from the Maastrichtian into the lower Thanetian. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian, Danian]`, `period` and `parent: Fontllonga` on [Figuerola](../../../stratigraphy/f/figuerola.yml), and the new [Fontllonga](../../../stratigraphy/f/fontllonga.yml) entry. [*Fylax thyrakolasus*](../../../genera/F/Fylax.yml) stays Maastrichtian on its own dated horizon.

**Evidence.**
- colombo1993a, p. 15: "ese evento se produce en el tercio superior de la unidad terrígena de la Fm. Figuerola" ("this event occurs in the upper third of the terrigenous unit of the Figuerola Fm.", translated); the deposits formed "entre el Maastrichtiense y el Paleógeno inferior"; p. 30: the section spans "desde el Maastrichtiense inferior a Taneciense inferior", with chron C29r, "donde se sitúa el límite K/T", identified.
- prieto-márquez2021a, p. 427: the *Fylax* type horizon is "chron C29r within the Figuerola Formation", from "uppermost Maastrichtian strata".

**Ruled out.**
- *The boundary at the Milla limestones.* Colombo and Cuevas report it was earlier tied to the Milla carbonates, above the Figuerola, and move it down into the Figuerola.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which group does the Figuerola Formation belong to?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The Fontllonga Group of the Àger Syncline, not the [Tremp Group](../../../stratigraphy/t/tremp.yml). Governs the `parent` of Figuerola (no Fontllonga unit exists yet) and the formation of [*Fylax thyrakolasus*](../../../genera/F/Fylax.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fondevilla2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 8: "Colombo and Cuevas (1993) did the same for the Àger Syncline, replacing the Tremp Fm by the Fontllonga Group", in which "the La Maçana Fm replaces the 'Grey Garumnian'; the Figuerola Fm is equivalent to the 'Lower Red Garumnian'; the Millà Fm … the Perauba Complex".
- prieto-márquez2021a, p. 427: the *Fylax* type locality, Fontllonga-R, is "in the southern limb of the Àger syncline"; type horizon "chron C29r within the Figuerola Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Figuerola as the Talarn Formation.* In the Tremp Syncline "the Conques and Talarn formations are equivalent to the 'Lower Red Garumnian'" (fondevilla2019a, p. 8); the Figuerola is the Àger Syncline's equivalent, a separate unit in a separate syncline (our reading of the two statements).
- *A "Fontllonga Formation".* Prieto-Márquez and colleagues print "Fontllonga Formation" in their Fig. 1C caption (p. 426) and conclusions (p. 433); Fontllonga is the group.

**Open.** Pujalte and Schmitz's scheme, which Fondevilla and colleagues cite, has not been read.
