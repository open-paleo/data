# Fontllonga

## Which units make up the Fontllonga Group, and what bounds it?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Colombo and Cuevas (1993) name the Fontllonga Group for the predominantly continental beds of the Àger sector between the Cretaceous Bona Formation, the upper part of which they call marine Maastrichtian, and the Ilerdian alveolina limestones of the Cadí Formation. It has four concordant units: the La Massana Limestones Formation (basal limestones), the Figuerola Formation (lower detrital interval), the Millà Formation (intermediate carbonates) and the Perauba Complex (upper complex). The group's age, and the Cretaceous–Tertiary boundary in the upper Figuerola, are in the 2026-10-08 entry in [Figuerola](figuerola.md). Governs `rank: group` on [Fontllonga](../../../stratigraphy/f/fontllonga.yml) and `parent: Fontllonga` on [Figuerola](../../../stratigraphy/f/figuerola.yml).

**Evidence.**
- colombo1993a, pp. 18–20: between "the Cretaceous carbonate and terrigenous materials of the Bona Fm., which form the substrate" and "the Ilerdian marine carbonate materials (limestones with alveolinas) (Cadí Fm.), which form the upper boundary" lie the predominantly continental materials, which "receive the name Fontllonga Group" (translated).
- colombo1993a, p. 20: "four mutually concordant units are distinguished …: basal limestones (Massana Fm.), lower detrital interval (Figuerola Fm.), intermediate carbonates (Millà Fm.) and upper complex (Perauba Complex)" (translated).
- colombo1993a, p. 25: "a regressive sequence from the upper part of the marine Maastrichtian (Bona Fm.) up to the top of the intermediate carbonates (Millà Fm.)" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *The Àger beds as the Tremp Group or Tremp Formation.* The continental fill of the southern Àger basin had been assigned to the Tremp Group. Colombo and Cuevas advise against that name there because of "the variability of the underlying substrate, the lithological differences and the isolation of the Àger outcrops from those of Tremp" (colombo1993a, p. 18, translated). The 2026-09-30 entry in [Figuerola](figuerola.md) reaches the same result for the Figuerola from Fondevilla and colleagues (2019).

**Open.** Nothing.
