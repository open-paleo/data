# Fengjiahe

## Which stages do the Yimen sauropodomorphs take?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** The formation's full range, Hettangian to Toarcian. Neither describing paper names a stage, and the Pliensbachian the records carried comes from reference works that cite no source for it. Governs `stage: [Hettangian, Sinemurian, Pliensbachian, Toarcian]` on [*Irisosaurus yimenensis*](../../../genera/I/Irisosaurus.yml) and [*Yimenosaurus youngi*](../../../genera/Y/Yimenosaurus.yml), replacing `[Pliensbachian]` on both, under the rule that a taxon with no statement about its horizon takes its unit's range.

**Evidence.**
- peyredefabrègues2020a, Horizon and locality: "Upper member of the Fengjiahe Formation, Lower Jurassic", at Jiaojiadian area B near Zhanmatian village, Yimen County; no stage appears in the paper.
- bai1990a, Locality and stratigraphic position: "Early Jurassic Fengjiahe Fm. at Jiaojiadian, Yimen Co., Yunnan Province"; no stage.

**Ruled out.**
- *Pliensbachian.* Jones (2026) gives it for *Yimenosaurus* in a table (jones2026b) and "Hettangian/Sinemurian" for *Irisosaurus* from the same locality; Paul (2024) gives it for *Yimenosaurus* (paul2024a). Neither cites a source.
- *Late Sinemurian–Toarcian from the Yuxisaurus site.* Yao and colleagues (2022) give the upper Fengjiahe at Jiaojiadian as "late Sinemurian–Toarcian (Huang et al., 2005)" (yao2022a), relaying a magnetostratigraphic age for the Lufeng Formation, not a dating of these beds.

**Open.** Nothing.
