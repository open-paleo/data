# Fort

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Fort Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Bathonian. Pandey and colleagues (2012) date the member by its position, above the Late Bajocian coral-bearing horizon of the Joyan Member and below the Late Bathonian ammonite-bearing Bada Bag Member, as Early to Middle or possibly Late Bathonian. They set aside its bivalves and foraminifera, on which earlier workers had dated it, as not age-diagnostic. The member has four facies units, the lowest non-marine fine sandstone, passing up through brackish into fully marine beds. Governs `stages: [Bathonian]` on [Fort](../../../stratigraphy/f/fort.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pandey2012a, p. 66, Table 2: Fort, "Early–Mid. Bathonian", author "Narayanan et al., 1961", type section "fort-hill sections, just north of Jaisalmer city".
- pandey2012a, p. 72: four facies units, the lowest "Facies unit 7, well-sorted, fine-grained sandstone"; "The non-marine sediments of facies unit 7 change to the brackish water facies of unit 8, which in turn is replaced by facies units 9–10 corresponding to fully marine conditions".
- pandey2012a, p. 72: the bivalves and foraminifera on which earlier workers "assigned this member to Bathonian": "this fauna may also occur in Callovian, and it seems that these bivalves and foraminifers are not age-diagnostic".
- pandey2012a, pp. 72–73: from "the stratigraphic position of this member above the Late Bajocian coral bearing horizon of the Joyan Member and below the Late Bathonian ammonite-bearing Bada Bag Member, the age of the Fort Member can be safely assigned to the Early Bathonian to Middle Bathonian/?Late Bathonian".
- kulkarni2008a, p. 737: from bivalves restricted to the Lower Callovian of Kachchh, Kachhara and Jodhawat "have assigned a Lower Callovian age to this zone", at the top of the Fort scarp; Khosla and colleagues "suggested a Bajocian-Bathonian age for the Fort Member".

**Ruled out.**
- *Dating the member by its bivalves and foraminifera.* Pandey and colleagues (2012) hold that this fauna also occurs in the Callovian and does not fix the age, and date the member by its position instead. The same objection applies to the Lower Callovian that Kachhara and Jodhawat (1999) gave a bivalve zone at the top of the Fort scarp, as Kulkarni and colleagues (2008) report it; extending Pandey's objection to that zone is an inference, since Pandey and colleagues discuss a different list of taxa.
- *Bajocian.* Khosla and colleagues (2006) put the Fort Member beds in a single ostracod zone, the "*Trichordis hadibhadangensis* Range Zone (Bajocian-Bathonian)" (khosla2006a, p. 1, Abstract), which Kulkarni and colleagues (2008) report as a Bajocian–Bathonian age for the member. The span is the zone's, not a finding that any of the member is Bajocian, and the member lies above the Late Bajocian coral horizon of the Joyan Member (pandey2012a, pp. 72–73).

**Open.** Nothing.
