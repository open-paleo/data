# Fuson

## Does D'Emic and colleagues' correlation put the Fuson Member in the Aptian and Albian?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** Not yet. D'Emic and colleagues (2019) date the Little Sheep Mudstone of the Cloverly Formation with new zircon ages and correlate it with the Minnewaste Limestone and Fuson Member, which they say "were deposited in both the Aptian and the Albian". Their dates are not from the Fuson; its age rests on the works they cite for the correlation, Finzel (2014, 2017) and Kirkland and colleagues (2016), which have not been read. The ostracod age of Cifelli and colleagues (2014), from a Fuson locality, stands. Governs `stages: [Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on [Fuson](../../../stratigraphy/f/fuson.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- d'emic2019a, p. 30: "Our new age constraints on the Little Sheep Mudstone establish its correlation with lacustrine deposits of the Ruby Ranch Member of the Cedar Mountain Formation and the Minnewaste Limestone and Fuson Member of the Lakota Formation (Figure 6; Finzel 2014, 2017; Kirkland et al. 2016 and references therein). These units ... were deposited in both the Aptian and the Albian."
- cifelli2014a: OMNH V1254, "Fuson Member, Lakota Formation (Lower Cretaceous: upper Valanginian–lower Barremian)", on ostracod assemblages of the eastern sequences.
- sohn1979a, p. 1: the Lakota Formation "is Barremian or older rather than late Aptian as previously considered", on ostracodes from the Chilson Member.

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian and Albian on D'Emic and colleagues' authority alone.* The age applies to a group of correlated units, and the Fuson's part in it is relayed from works not yet read.

**Open.** Whether Finzel (2014, 2017) or Kirkland and colleagues (2016) date the Fuson itself. If they do, the Aptian–Albian reading would stand against the ostracod age, and the entry would need a dispute or new stages.
