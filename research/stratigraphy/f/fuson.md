# Fuson

## What dates the Fuson to the late Valanginian–early Barremian?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Ostracods from one measured section. Cifelli and colleagues (2014) assign the Stage Barn Canyon Road section, which holds the mammal site OMNH V1254, to the Fuson Member because its ostracod assemblage differs from those of the Chilson Member (unit 2) in the southern Black Hills and matches those of the eastern sequences, and they give those eastern assemblages late Valanginian–early Barremian age constraints. The span takes in the whole Hauterivian. Boyd and Pagnac (2015) carry the same age to unit L2, which they correlate with the lower Fuson, at the *Dakotadon* type locality (see [*Dakotadon*](../../genera/D/Dakotadon.md)); they name the Fuson the uppermost of the Lakota's three members. Governs `stages: [Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on [Fuson](../../../stratigraphy/f/fuson.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cifelli2014a, pp. 35–36, Localities sampled: "the ostracod assemblage recovered from the Stage Barn Canyon Road section is strongly different from those of the Chilson Member (unit 2) in the southern sequence"; it "matches those recovered from other localities of the eastern sequences, in the presumed Fuson Member"; "Estimated age constraints for the section including OMNH locality V1254, and other ostracod assemblages of the eastern sequences, are late Valanginian–early Barremian (Fig. 2)."
- cifelli2014a, p. 38, Systematic paleontology: "OMNH 1254, Fuson Member, Lakota Formation (Lower Cretaceous: upper Valanginian–lower Barremian), Meade County, South Dakota, USA".
- boyd2015b, p. 1, Abstract: field work "that relocated the type locality, confirming its provenance from unit L2 (lower Fuson Member equivalent) of the Lakota Formation".
- boyd2015b, p. 6: "Three members of the Lakota Formation are recognized, the basal Chilson Member, the Minnewaste Limestone Member, and the upper-most Fuson Member".

**Ruled out.** Nothing beyond the Aptian–Albian reading that the 2026-10-23 entry rules out, and the Barremian of earlier *Dakotadon* papers that Boyd and Pagnac (2015) supersede (see [*Dakotadon*](../../genera/D/Dakotadon.md)).

**Open.** Cifelli and colleagues (2014) write that assigning the Stage Barn Canyon Road sequence to the Chilson Member (unit 2) or the Fuson Member "remains open to question", and that Sohn (1979) put a sample locality at or near the section in the Chilson, "probably unit 2"; they assign it to the Fuson on the ostracods. A dated section through the Fuson elsewhere would test the age.

## Does D'Emic and colleagues' correlation put the Fuson Member in the Aptian and Albian?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** Not yet. D'Emic and colleagues (2019) date the Little Sheep Mudstone of the Cloverly Formation with new zircon ages and correlate it with the Minnewaste Limestone and Fuson Member, which they say "were deposited in both the Aptian and the Albian". Their dates are not from the Fuson; its age rests on the works they cite for the correlation, Finzel (2014, 2017) and Kirkland and colleagues (2016), which have not been read. The ostracod age of Cifelli and colleagues (2014), from a Fuson locality, stands. Governs `stages: [Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on [Fuson](../../../stratigraphy/f/fuson.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- d'emic2019a, p. 30: "Our new age constraints on the Little Sheep Mudstone establish its correlation with lacustrine deposits of the Ruby Ranch Member of the Cedar Mountain Formation and the Minnewaste Limestone and Fuson Member of the Lakota Formation (Figure 6; Finzel 2014, 2017; Kirkland et al. 2016 and references therein). These units ... were deposited in both the Aptian and the Albian."
- cifelli2014a: OMNH V1254, "Fuson Member, Lakota Formation (Lower Cretaceous: upper Valanginian–lower Barremian)", on ostracod assemblages of the eastern sequences.
- sohn1979a, p. 1: the Lakota Formation "is Barremian or older rather than late Aptian as previously considered", on ostracodes from the Chilson Member.

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian and Albian on D'Emic and colleagues' authority alone.* The age applies to a group of correlated units, and the Fuson's part in it is relayed from works not yet read.

**Open.** Whether Finzel (2014, 2017) or Kirkland and colleagues (2016) date the Fuson itself. If they do, the Aptian–Albian reading would stand against the ostracod age, and the entry would need a dispute or new stages.
