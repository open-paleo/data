# Farta Pão

## Does the Farta Pão Formation reach the Berriasian?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** Disputed. Strontium-isotope ages make it latest Kimmeridgian to latest Tithonian, while older microfossil work and Wilson's (1988) chart carry its top into the Berriasian. Governs `stages: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` and the dispute on Farta Pão.

**Evidence.**
- schneider2009a, p. 1949: "The Farta Pao formation ranges from the latest Kimmeridgian to the latest Tithonian", with the top unit "the late Early to Late Tithonian Freixial Member"; p. 1966: "Three of the remaining four samples … indicate a Late Tithonian age". No sample is Berriasian.
- mateus2017a, p. 86: the formation is "dated as latest Kimmeridgian to latest Tithonian, Schneider et al., 2009".

**Ruled out.**
- *That Wilson (1988) gives no stage.* Wilson's text gives none (wilson1988a, p. 400), but his Fig. 4 (p. 401, "Summary of the Mesozoic lithostratigraphy of the southern part of the Lusitanian Basin") draws the Sintra-area Farta Pão from inside the Tithonian to about the middle Berriasian, on the 1985 timescale. This is the source of the Berriasian top in the "sensu Wilson" usage.

**Open.** The Berriasian. Mateus and colleagues report the uppermost unit as "also considered as Berriasian based on foraminifera, ostracods and charophytes (Ramalho, 1971; Rey, 1972)" (mateus2017a, p. 87); Schneider and colleagues cite the same Ramalho (1971) for an age ending in the Tithonian (pp. 1952–1953), so both sides rest partly on one source.
