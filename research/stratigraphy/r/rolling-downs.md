# Rolling Downs

## Do the Manuka and Griman Creek take the Rolling Downs Group as parent, and how old is it?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Yes; the group is Early to Late Cretaceous, with no stages. Vine and colleagues (1967) divide it into the Wilgunya Sub-group and the Manuka Sub-group, and Exon (1976) and Herne and colleagues (2019) place the Griman Creek Formation in it. No work read dates the group as a whole, and the Wilgunya's formations are dated only by relays (Vine and colleagues credit the Wallumbilla's Aptian–Albian to Day 1967), so the union of its members cannot be completed; its period rests on the dated members, the Manuka and the Griman Creek. Governs the new entry [Rolling Downs](../../../stratigraphy/r/rolling-downs.yml) and `parent` on [Manuka](../../../stratigraphy/m/manuka.yml) and [Griman Creek](../../../stratigraphy/g/griman-creek.yml).

**Evidence.**
- vine1967a, p. 148: "We propose to retain the name Wilgunya, as Wilgunya Sub-group, for this sequence."
- exon1976a, pp. 59 and 61: "Within the Rolling Downs Group the major aquifers are the Coreena Member and the lower part of the Griman Creek Formation."

**Ruled out.** - *Aptian to Turonian.* The Wilgunya's Aptian is a relay.

**Open.** A dating of the Wilgunya Sub-group's formations from their own sources.
