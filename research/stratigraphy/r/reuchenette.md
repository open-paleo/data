# Reuchenette

## Does the Reuchenette Formation have formal members?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes, in the Ajoie: the Vabenau, Banné, Courtedoux and Chevenez members of Comment and colleagues (2015). Governs possible member records under Reuchenette, and the placement of [*Amanzia greppini*](../../../genera/A/Amanzia.yml) in the lower formation.

**Evidence.**
- comment2015a, p. 162: "En Ajoie, la Formation de Reuchenette est subdivisée en quatre membres : le Membre de Vabenau (Comment et al., 2011), le Membre du Banné (Marcou, 1848) … le Membre de Courtedoux (Comment et al., 2011) … et le Membre de Chevenez (nov.)" ("In Ajoie, the Reuchenette Formation is subdivided into four members", translated); the Courtedoux holds "plusieurs niveaux à traces de dinosaures" ("several dinosaur track levels", translated). English abstract, p. 161: "The upper part of the Reuchenette Formation (Upper Kimmeridgian) is the new Chevenez Member."
- schwarz2020a, p. 10: the *Amanzia* quarry is in the "Lower part of the Reuchenette Formation (divisum zone), early Kimmeridgian"; p. 8: "The surfaces at the Combe du Pont are only a couple of meters below the Banné Marls", the track surfaces to which the quarry is correlated.

**Ruled out.**
- *Jank's nine intervals as the member scheme.* Jank and colleagues divide the record "into nine intervals" (jank2006a, p. 175); Comment and colleagues list "Banné marls (Jank et al., 2006a, b, c)" as a synonym of their member (p. 169).
- *"Unter-Virgula-Schichten" for Amanzia.* That horizon is given for the *Allosaurus meriani* tooth (chure2000a, pp. 209–210) and for a Bern locality in a table (mcintosh1990a, Table 16.1), not for the *Amanzia* quarry.

**Open.** Who first gave the Banné member rank. Comment and colleagues credit it to Marcou (1848) on p. 162 and to Thurmann (1832) on p. 169; Jank already wrote "Banné Member sensu Marçou 1848; in Gygi 2000" (jank2006a, p. 178). Whether the members apply outside the Ajoie is open; their Fig. 8 (p. 181) correlates them to Montbéliard.

## Does Jank's thesis carry the Reuchenette's age?

*2026-09-21*

**Conclusion.** No. The age rests on the published version, Jank and colleagues (2006), and in international stages the whole of their span is Kimmeridgian. Governs `stages: [Kimmeridgian]` on [Reuchenette](../../../stratigraphy/r/reuchenette.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jank2006a, p. 175, title: "(?Oxfordian, Kimmeridgian sensu gallico, Ajoie-Region)"; abstract: the record is "assigned to the Late Oxfordian to Late Kimmeridgian *sensu gallico* (middle Eudoxus-Zone)".
- hesselbo2020a, p. 972: the "Idoceras planula Zone of the Sub-Mediterranean province … should be coeval with the lowest Kimmeridgian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Oxfordian to late Kimmeridgian, from the thesis.* Jank's 2004 Basel doctoral thesis states "late Oxfordian to late Kimmeridgian" in its title, where the refereed version queries the Oxfordian. A thesis does not carry a value alone (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-thesis-never-carries-a-value-alone)).
- *Oxfordian, from the published version.* The only Oxfordian in Jank and colleagues' frame is the queried Planula Zone, which falls in the lowest international Kimmeridgian.

**Open.** Nothing.
