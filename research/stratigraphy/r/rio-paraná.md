# Rio Paraná

## Does Batezelli's stacking bear on the Rio Paraná's Aptian–Albian?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Yes, and it now stands in the dispute. Arai and Dias-Brito (2023) extend the age of Goioerê fossils to the Rio Paraná because the units are interfingered, after Fernandes and Coimbra (1994), who with Fernandes and Coimbra (2000) describe a lateral, transitional contact; Batezelli (2015) stacks the Goio Erê, the Rio Paraná and the Santo Anastácio in ascending order. If the Rio Paraná lies above the Goio Erê, the stated reason for the extension does not hold. The stages stay, as the dispute's subject. Governs the `dispute` summary on [Rio Paraná](../../../stratigraphy/r/rio-paraná.yml); `stages` unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- arai2023a, p. 6: "O Grupo Caiuá deve ser considerado como Cretáceo Inferior (Aptiano–Albiano) em função das idades sugeridas por pterossauros" ["The Caiuá Group should be considered as Lower Cretaceous (Aptian–Albian) in view of the ages suggested by pterosaurs", translated]; the fossils come from the Goioerê, and their age "pode ser estendida para as formações Rio Paraná (FERNANDES & COIMBRA 1994)" because "as unidades se encontram interdigitadas".
- fernandes2000a, p. 717: "Tem contato lateral gradual com a Formação Goio Erê." ["It has a gradual lateral contact with the Goio Erê Formation", translated].
- batezelli2015a, pp. 3–4: the Caiuá Group "consists of three formations (in ascending stratigraphic order, with major depositional environments in parentheses): the Goio Erê Formation (margins of Draa), the Rio Paraná Formation (inner Draa) and the Santo Anastácio Formation (Paleosols)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** A date or fossil from the Rio Paraná itself.

## What do the cited works say about the definition, group and age of the Rio Paraná?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Fernandes and Coimbra (1994) define the formation as one of three in the Caiuá Group, after Fernandes (1992) divided the old Caiuá unit into the Goio Erê and Rio Paraná formations and so raised it to a group. They designate the lock cuts of the Porto Primavera hydroelectric plant as its holostratotype and the cut at km 501 of highway BR 376 near Paranavaí as its parastratotype, give it up to about 250 m, and describe transitional contacts with the Goio Erê and Santo Anastácio formations and an erosive base on the Serra Geral Formation. The only age they give is for the whole Bauru Basin. Arai and Dias-Brito (2023) date the Caiuá Group Aptian–Albian on pterosaurs and the noasaurid Berthasaura from the Goioerê Formation, and extend that age to the Rio Paraná because the two are interdigitated. Langer and colleagues (2019) call it "likely early Late Cretaceous" without a stage, treat the group's three formations as coeval "according to some stratigraphic schemes", and assign the Cruzeiro do Oeste site to it (see [*Vespersaurus*](../../genera/V/Vespersaurus.md)). Dias and colleagues (2010) date zircons from four samples in São Paulo State and call the formation "part of the Bauru Group", the usage in which the Bauru Group took in the Caiuá sandstones before Fernandes and Coimbra separated the two groups. Governs `parent: Caiuá` and `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` on [Rio Paraná](../../../stratigraphy/r/rio-paraná.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fernandes1994a, p. 166: Fernandes (1992) "divided the Caiuá unit into the Goio Erê and Rio Paraná formations, which led to its consequent elevation to the rank of group"; "the formal definition of the Caiuá Group is proposed as the grouping of the Goio Erê, Rio Paraná and Santo Anastácio formations" (translated).
- fernandes1994a, p. 166: in an earlier scheme "the Bauru unit - presented as a group - incorporated the Caiuá and Santo Anastácio sandstones" (translated).
- fernandes1994a, p. 167: "the Rio Paraná Formation has maximum thicknesses on the order of 250 meters" (translated).
- fernandes1994a, p. 169: "The cuts of the lock of the Porto Primavera hydroelectric plant (Pontal do Paranapanema, SP) are designated here as the holostratotype of the Rio Paraná Formation"; the exposure "at km 501 of highway BR 376, near Paranavaí (PR)" is "its parastratotype" (translated).
- fernandes1994a, p. 170: "The Rio Paraná Formation has transitional contacts with the Goio Erê and Santo Anastácio formations, an erosive basal contact with the Serra Geral Formation" (translated).
- fernandes1994a, p. 174: "the continental sedimentation in the Bauru Basin is associated with the Turonian–Maastrichtian interval of the Late Cretaceous", from "the Senonian age of the fossils" and an assumed correlation with the Santos Basin (translated).
- arai2023a, p. 1, Abstract: "The Caiuá Group is here sustained as the Aptian–Albian".
- arai2023a, p. 6, 2.1 Caiuá Group: the pterosaurs and Berthasaura "were found in the Goioerê Formation and their age can be extended to the Rio Paraná … and Pirapozinho … formations, since, according to these authors, the units are interdigitated" (translated).
- langer2019a, p. 5, Type-locality and horizon: "Dark red sandstones of the likely early Late Cretaceous Rio Paraná Formation".
- langer2019a, p. 2, Geological Settings: "According to some stratigraphic schemes, the Caiuá Group is composed of three coeval units: the Rio Paraná, Goio Erê, and Santo Anastácio formations"; the Cruzeiro do Oeste site "is located in an area more commonly mapped as covered by sediments of the Rio Paraná Formation. This assignment is confirmed here".
- dias2010a, p. 17: "They are four samples of sedimentary rocks of Rio Parana Formation, which is part of the Bauru Group".

**Ruled out.**
- *Turonian–Maastrichtian.* Fernandes and Coimbra (1994) give it for sedimentation across the Bauru Basin, not for this formation, from fossils and an assumed correlation with a marine basin.
- *Bauru Group as the parent.* Dias and colleagues (2010) use the older scheme in which the Bauru Group included the Caiuá; the defining paper places the formation in the Caiuá Group, beside a separate Bauru Group.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the 2026-10-21 entry's Open.

## Does Dias and colleagues' "90-100 Ma" put the Rio Paraná in the Cenomanian or Turonian?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** No. The figure is asserted twice without a source or an argument, and the paper's own zircon ages are provenance and source-area cooling ages hundreds of millions of years older, so the number is not a measurement of the formation. CONTRIBUTING places a bare number on the chart only when it is the measurement. The statement stays on the Late Cretaceous side of the dispute, beside Langer and colleagues' (2019) "likely early Late Cretaceous", without adding a stage. Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]`, `period: [Early Cretaceous]` and the dispute summary on [Rio Paraná](../../../stratigraphy/r/rio-paraná.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dias2010a, p. 20: "the 90-100 Ma old sediments of the Rio Paraná Formation"; p. 21: "the sediments of the Rio Paraná Formation, formed between 90 to 100 Ma old before present". Neither carries a citation.
- dias2010a, p. 17: the formation "is part of the Bauru Group", whose stratigraphic age "is estimated among 100 to 80 Ma", also uncited.
- dias2010a, p. 20: the U-Th-Pb ages give the source areas, mainly Brasiliano rocks "ranging from 523 ± 4 to 795 ± 14 Ma"; the fission-track ages are "apparent ages" tied to recycling or denudation of the source rocks. No maximum depositional age is computed.

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian–Turonian from 90–100 Ma.* On the ICS chart v2024/12 the range falls in the Cenomanian and Turonian, but nothing in the paper measures it. Its youngest zircon ages are fission-track peaks near 270–290 Ma.

**Open.** The source of the 90–100 Ma figure. Dias and colleagues (2011) give the same figure for the Vale do Rio do Peixe Formation of the Bauru Group, also without a source.
