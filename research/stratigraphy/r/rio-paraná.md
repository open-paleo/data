# Rio Paraná

## Does Dias and colleagues' "90-100 Ma" put the Rio Paraná in the Cenomanian or Turonian?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** No. The figure is asserted twice without a source or an argument, and the paper's own zircon ages are provenance and source-area cooling ages hundreds of millions of years older, so the number is not a measurement of the formation. CONTRIBUTING places a bare number on the chart only when it is the measurement. The statement stays on the Late Cretaceous side of the dispute, beside Langer and colleagues' (2019) "likely early Late Cretaceous", without adding a stage. Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]`, `period: [Early Cretaceous]` and the dispute summary on [Rio Paraná](../../../stratigraphy/r/rio-paraná.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dias2010a, p. 20: "the 90-100 Ma old sediments of the Rio Paraná Formation"; p. 21: "the sediments of the Rio Paraná Formation, formed between 90 to 100 Ma old before present". Neither carries a citation.
- dias2010a, p. 17: the formation "is part of the Bauru Group", whose stratigraphic age "is estimated among 100 to 80 Ma", also uncited.
- dias2010a, p. 20: the U-Th-Pb ages give the source areas, mainly Brasiliano rocks "ranging from 523 ± 4 to 795 ± 14 Ma"; the fission-track ages are "apparent ages" tied to recycling or denudation of the source rocks. No maximum depositional age is computed.

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian–Turonian from 90–100 Ma.* On the ICS chart v2024/12 the range falls in the Cenomanian and Turonian, but nothing in the paper measures it. Its youngest zircon ages are fission-track peaks near 270–290 Ma.

**Open.** The source of the 90–100 Ma figure. Dias and colleagues (2011) give the same figure for the Vale do Rio do Peixe Formation of the Bauru Group, also without a source.
