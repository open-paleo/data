# Rock Point

## Is the Rock Point Rhaetian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Not established. Hunt (1998) calls it Apachean and equates the Apachean with the Rhaetian after Lucas and Hunt (1993); others read the Apachean as late Norian–Rhaetian or late Norian. Governs `stages: [Norian, Rhaetian]` on Rock Point, and the empty `location.member` on [*Coelophysis bauri*](../../../genera/C/Coelophysis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hunt1998a, p. 512, Fig. 1 caption and pp. 521–522: "the younger (Apachean) Rock Point Formation"; every dated mention of the Rock Point in the paper is "Apachean", none a stage. p. 513: "Apachean (Rhaetian) (Lucas & Hunt 1993b)".
- rinehart2009a, p. 1: "Apachean (late Norian-Rhaetian)"; p. 24: "We favor the late Norian age for the Whitaker quarry".

**Ruled out.**
- *Rhaetian as an independent vote.* Hunt's "Apachean (Rhaetian)" is a relay of Lucas and Hunt (1993b), so a Rhaetian reached through it counts once. Novas and colleagues (2021) give "the Rhaetian Rock Point Member" for *Daemonosaurus* (novas2021a, pre-proof, p. 47) and "Rhaetian Owl Rock Member" on p. 22, contradicting themselves.
- *Coelophysis neotype in the Rock Point.* Hunt (1998, p. 521) puts "the neotype of *Coelophysis* (AMNH 7224) from the Ghost Ranch quarry that is the younger (Apachean) Rock Point Formation". The Ghost Ranch correlation with the Rock Point was "called into question … (Zeigler et al., 2008)" by magnetostratigraphy (cather2013a, p. 110), and Nesbitt and colleagues give the same beds in their own nomenclature (nesbitt2020a, p. 152), so no member is assigned.

**Open.** The name's scope. Lucas and Hunt (1992) "renamed these beds the Rock Point Formation for a unit at the top of the Chinle in the Four Corners area" (cather2013a, p. 110), applying an existing name (the type Rock Point is Harshbarger and colleagues 1957, per rinehart2009a, p. 21). The *C. bauri* syntypes are Revueltian, "early-middle Norian" (hunt1998a, p. 511).
