# Potomac

## What rank do the Potomac and the Arundel take?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** The Potomac is a group, Early to Late Cretaceous, and the Arundel stays a formation within it, as does the Patuxent. Carrano (2024) retains "the official USGS nomenclatures", the Arundel Clay of the Potomac Group, against Frederickson and colleagues' (2018) reduction of the group to a formation and of the Arundel to a facies, after Kranz (1998); Stanford and colleagues (2011) place the Patuxent and the Arundel in the group. Frederickson and colleagues give the Potomac as Lower to basal Upper Cretaceous, which sets the epochs; no work read dates it to stages. No Arundel material is filed on the Patuxent, whose one taxon, *Propanoplosaurus*, Stanford and colleagues place there themselves. No "Arundel Clay" variant is added, since it differs only by the lithologic term. Governs the new entry [Potomac](../../../stratigraphy/p/potomac.yml) and `parent` on [Arundel](../../../stratigraphy/a/arundel.yml) and [Patuxent](../../../stratigraphy/p/patuxent.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carrano2024a, p. 2: "Those authors followed Kranz (1998) and others in reducing the Potomac Group to a formation, and the Arundel Clay to an intermittent facies therein. I retain the official USGS nomenclatures".

**Ruled out.** - *Arundel as a facies or member.* Not the USGS scheme, and its proponents differ on the details.

**Open.** Nothing.
