# Presidente Prudente

## What age do Pinheiro and colleagues (2018) give the Presidente Prudente, and on what grounds?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian to early Maastrichtian. Pinheiro and colleagues (2018) list the Presidente Prudente among four formations added to the Bauru Group by later work, credited to Fernandes and Coimbra (1996), and give it "Campanian–Early Maastrichtian" in their synthesis of earlier studies. For the uppermost sequence of the formation at the AM1 site, Alfredo Marcondes, they correlate the subaerial unconformity that bounds it with the upper unconformity of the Adamantina and with the Adamantina–Marília contact, take that unconformity as the Campanian–Maastrichtian boundary on the Maastrichtian age of the Marília, and so date the sequence late Campanian to early Maastrichtian. Their revised chart draws the Presidente Prudente as lenses within the Adamantina in the Campanian. Governs `stages: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` and `parent: Bauru` on [Presidente Prudente](../../../stratigraphy/p/presidente-prudente.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pinheiro2018a, p. 6, Historical background: the Bauru Group's four formations "together with four more units derived from recent stratigraphic refinement", among them "Presidente Prudente [87]" (reference 87 is Fernandes and Coimbra, 1996); the formations' ages "based on the integration of all studies done so far here analyzed" include "Presidente Prudente (Campanian–Early Maastrichtian)".
- pinheiro2018a, p. 2: "the uppermost depositional sequence of the Presidente Prudente Formation (Late Campanian–Early Maastrichtian)".
- pinheiro2018a, p. 10: at the AM1 site the boundary of the upper sequence "is a subaerial unconformity", which "is correlated with the upper unconformity of the Adamantina Formation in Ibirá, and São José do Rio Preto regions, as well as with the subaerial unconformity of the contact between the Adamantina and Marília formations"; "Based on the Maastrichtian age for the Marília Formation, we consider this subaerial unconformity to be the boundary between the Campanian and Maastrichtian"; the specimens "are dated as late Campanian to early Maastrichtian in age".
- pinheiro2018a, p. 9, Fig. 5: lenses labeled "PP Fm." inside the "AD Fm." bodies in the Campanian.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Pinheiro and colleagues credit the formation to Fernandes and Coimbra (1996); Fernandes and Coimbra (2000) credit it to Fernandes (1998). Reading Fernandes and Coimbra (1996) would show whether it already names the Presidente Prudente.
