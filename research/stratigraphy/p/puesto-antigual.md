## Is the Puesto Antigual Member Barremian, or Barremian–early Aptian?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Barremian. The early Aptian belongs to the La Amarga Formation as a whole, not to its basal member. Governs `stages: [Barremian]` on Puesto Antigual, and the stages of species whose type horizon is in it ([*Amargasaurus*](../../../genera/A/Amargasaurus.yml), [*Amargastegos*](../../../genera/A/Amargastegos.yml), [*Amargatitanis*](../../../genera/A/Amargatitanis.yml), [*Ligabueino*](../../../genera/L/Ligabueino.yml)).

**Evidence.**
- leanza1995a, p. 31, unnumbered "[Cuadro] estratigráfico propuesto para la región analizada" ("proposed stratigraphic chart for the region analyzed", translated): the Puesto Antigual, Bañados de Caichigüe and Piedra Parada members all sit in the Barremian row, and a hiatus fills the base of the Aptian below the Puesto Quiroga Member of the Lohan Cura Formation. This is the paper that names the three members.
- leanza2003a, p. 20, Edad y correlaciones: the unconformity at the base of the Puesto Antigual is "Intra-Barremiano", and the Bañados de Caichigüe Member above it "se adjudica al Barremiano" ("is assigned to the Barremian", translated). That brackets the member within the Barremian.
- leanza2004a, p. 63, Fig. 2: all three members drawn in the Barremian row.
- gallina2016a, Locality and horizon (accepted manuscript): "the Puesto Antigual Member of the La Amarga Formation, Barremian, Lower Cretaceous".
- salgado2006b, p. 703: "Amargasaurus cazaui (Barremiano inferior, Puesto Antigual, Formación La Amarga)" ("lower Barremian", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian–early Aptian for the member.* Pereda-Suberbiola and colleagues (2013) give "The basal Puesto Antigual Member (about 30 m in thickness, Barremian–lower Aptian)" (pereda-suberbiola2013a, p. 2 of the online-first file, Geological setting), citing only Leanza and colleagues (2004). Leanza and colleagues give that range for the formation: "this unit may be regarded as Barremian–Early Aptian in age (Leanza and Hugo, 1995, 1997)" (leanza2004a, p. 65). The members are introduced two sentences later, and the Puesto Antigual gets a thickness and lithology but no age. A third party's copy of a parent unit's range is the parent's statement, not a determination for the child.
- *Upper Barremian–lower Aptian by correlation.* Leanza (2003) attributes the formation to "Barremiano superior – Aptiano inferior" by correlating it with the Huitrín Formation (leanza2003a, p. 1, Resumen). That is again formation-level, and the same report brackets the basal member within the Barremian (p. 20).

**Open.** Leanza and colleagues (2004, p. 65) cite Leanza and Hugo (1995) for "Barremian–Early Aptian", but the 1995 text gives the formation as "Hauteriviano tardío / Barremiano" ("late Hauterivian/Barremian", translated; leanza1995a, p. 30) and its chart has no Aptian member. The formation's own range is not settled by this note. The top member, the Piedra Parada (*Zapalasaurus*), does carry an Aptian assignment of its own: "Barremian-lower Aptian" (salgado2006b, p. 695, abstract), given as "Barremiano superior – Aptiano inferior" on p. 699.
