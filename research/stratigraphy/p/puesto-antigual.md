# Puesto Antigual

## Where does the La Amarga's Aptian trace to, and does it reach the Puesto Antigual?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** To Leanza (2003) and Leanza and colleagues (2004), both cited here, and it does not reach this member. Leanza and Hugo (1995, 1997) give the formation the late Hauterivian to Barremian (see [La Amarga](../l/la-amarga.md)); the Aptian enters with Leanza's (2003) late Barremian–early Aptian for the formation, which Leanza and colleagues (2004) repeat as Barremian–Early Aptian. Leanza (2003) draws the Puesto Antigual in the Barremian and gives the Aptian to the Piedra Parada above it. Governs `stages: [Barremian]`, unchanged, and the note on leanza2003a on [Puesto Antigual](../../../stratigraphy/p/puesto-antigual.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leanza2003a, p. 19, Fig. 7: "BARREMIANO" beside "Mb. Puesto Antigual" and "Mb. Bañados de Caichigüe"; "APTIANO" beside "Mb. Piedra Parada".
- leanza2003a, p. 1: the La Amarga Formation "se atribuye al Barremiano superior – Aptiano inferior" ["is assigned to the Late Barremian – Early Aptian", translated].

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.

## How did Leanza and Hugo define the Puesto Antigual Member, and what does the Barremian–Early Aptian of Leanza and colleagues (2004) rest on?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Leanza and Hugo (1995) named the member as the lowest of three new members of the La Amarga Formation, replacing the Pichi Picún Leufú, Ortiz and Limay members, which they assign to the older Fortín Nogueira Group. Its type locality is at Puesto Antigual, on the right bank of the arroyo La Amarga north of cerro China Muerta, where it is 28.90 m thick, lies unconformably on the Agrio Formation and passes gradually into the Bañados de Caichigüe Member. Leanza and Hugo (1997) restate the definition, name the Pichi Picún Leufú Member as the term it replaces, and report *Amargasaurus cazaui* from it. Both works date the La Amarga Formation late Hauterivian and Barremian, and the 1995 chart puts all three members in the Barremian. Leanza and colleagues (2004) cite both works for a formation age of "Barremian–Early Aptian", reached by basin analysis, stratigraphic relationships and tectonosedimentary aspects; neither cited work gives the Aptian. Pereda-Suberbiola and colleagues (2013) copy that formation range onto the member. This extends the 2026-10-01 entry's Open, which checked only the 1995 text, and does not change its conclusion. Governs `stages: [Barremian]` and `parent: La Amarga` on [Puesto Antigual](../../../stratigraphy/p/puesto-antigual.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leanza1995a, p. 30: "Puesto Antigual Member nom. nov.: Covers the Agrio Formation in unconformity and passes gradually to the Bañados de Caichigüe Member. The area of Puesto Antigual is designated as the type locality, situated on the right margin of the arroyo La Amarga and north of the cerro China Muerta, where it shows 28.90 m in thickness" (translated).
- leanza1995a, p. 30: the La Amarga Formation "is usually assigned to the Late Hauterivian / Barremian" from its microfauna; the members formerly distinguished in it "(Pichi Picún Leufú, Ortiz and Limay)" belong "to the Fortín Nogueira Group, lateral equivalent of the Mendoza Group", so new members are proposed (translated).
- leanza1995a, p. 32: "the upper Hauterivian / Barremian age usually assigned to the La Amarga Formation" (translated), given as the lower limit of the Lohan Cura Formation above.
- leanza1997a, p. 53: the Pichi Picún Leufú, Ortíz and Limay of the río Limay region, "evidentemente de carácter Mendociano, no pueden ser aceptados como integrantes de la Formación La Amarga" ["evidently Mendozan in character, cannot be accepted as part of the La Amarga Formation", translated].
- leanza1997a, p. 54: the member "Fue propuesto por Leanza y Hugo (1995: 30) para reemplazar al término de Miembro Pichi Picún Leufú con se designaba anteriormente a estas sedimentitas" ["Proposed by Leanza and Hugo (1995: 30) to replace the term Pichi Picún Leufú Member, by which these sedimentites were previously designated", translated]; it "Cubre en discordancia a la Zona de Transición de la Formación Agrio y pasa gradualmente al Miembro Bañados de Caichigüe" ["unconformably overlies the Transition Zone of the Agrio Formation and passes gradually into the Bañados de Caichigüe Member", translated]; its environment is "una combinación de ríos anastomosados en su base y lagunas someras en la parte superior" ["a combination of anastomosing rivers at its base and shallow lagoons in the upper part", translated]; "De este Miembro procede el dinosuario saurópodo *Amargasaurus cazaui*" ["The sauropod dinosaur *Amargasaurus cazaui* … comes from this Member", translated].
- leanza1997a, p. 57: the La Amarga Formation, on its microfauna and on lying above Agrio beds with lower and middle Hauterivian ammonites, "se asigna al Hauteriviano superior y Barremiano" ["is assigned to the late Hauterivian and Barremian", translated].
- leanza2004a, p. 65: "Based on basin analysis, stratigraphical relationships and tectonosedimentary aspects, this unit may be regarded as Barremian–Early Aptian in age (Leanza and Hugo, 1995, 1997)"; "The Puesto Antigual Member (28.9 m) is composed of fluvial channel sands of braided rivers with well-developed palaeosol tops".
- pereda-suberbiola2013a, p. 3 of the online-first file, Locality, unit and age: "basal Puesto Antigual Member, La Amarga Formation, Lower Cretaceous, Barremian–lower Aptian (Leanza et al. 2004)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Pichi Picún Leufú as another name for the member.* Earlier workers used it for these beds, but Leanza and Hugo drop it because the Pichi Picún Leufú of the río Limay is a Mendocian unit below the La Amarga (leanza1997a, pp. 53–54). The name belongs to that older unit; the *Amargatitanis* note treats it the same way (see [*Amargatitanis*](../../genera/A/Amargatitanis.md)).
- *An Aptian for the member from Leanza and colleagues (2004).* Their range is for the formation, rests on basin analysis and tectonosedimentary aspects rather than fossils or radiometric dates, and the two works they cite for it give late Hauterivian–Barremian (leanza1995a, p. 30; leanza1997a, p. 57).

**Open.** Both Leanza and Hugo works give the La Amarga Formation a late Hauterivian age as well. That bears on the [La Amarga](../../../stratigraphy/l/la-amarga.yml), not on this member, and is not weighed here.

## Is the Puesto Antigual Member Barremian, or Barremian–early Aptian?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Barremian. The early Aptian belongs to the La Amarga Formation as a whole, not to its basal member. Governs `stages: [Barremian]` on Puesto Antigual, and the stages of species whose type horizon is in it ([*Amargasaurus*](../../../genera/A/Amargasaurus.yml), [*Amargastegos*](../../../genera/A/Amargastegos.yml), [*Amargatitanis*](../../../genera/A/Amargatitanis.yml), [*Ligabueino*](../../../genera/L/Ligabueino.yml)).

**Evidence.**
- leanza1995a, p. 31, unnumbered "[Cuadro] estratigráfico propuesto para la región analizada" ("proposed stratigraphic chart for the region analyzed", translated): the Puesto Antigual, Bañados de Caichigüe and Piedra Parada members all sit in the Barremian row, and a hiatus fills the base of the Aptian below the Puesto Quiroga Member of the Lohan Cura Formation. This is the paper that names the three members.
- leanza2003a, p. 20, Edad y correlaciones: the unconformity at the base of the Puesto Antigual is "Intra-Barremiano", and the Bañados de Caichigüe Member above it "se adjudica al Barremiano" ("is assigned to the Barremian", translated). That brackets the member within the Barremian.
- leanza2004a, p. 63, Fig. 2: all three members drawn in the Barremian row.
- gallina2016a, Locality and horizon (accepted manuscript): "the Puesto Antigual Member of the La Amarga Formation, Barremian, Lower Cretaceous".
- salgado2006b, p. 703: "Amargasaurus cazaui (Barremiano inferior, Puesto Antigual, Formación La Amarga)" ("lower Barremian", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian–early Aptian for the member.* Pereda-Suberbiola and colleagues (2013) give "The basal Puesto Antigual Member (about 30 m in thickness, Barremian–lower Aptian)" (pereda-suberbiola2013a, p. 2 of the online-first file, Geological setting), citing only Leanza and colleagues (2004). Leanza and colleagues give that range for the formation: "this unit may be regarded as Barremian–Early Aptian in age (Leanza and Hugo, 1995, 1997)" (leanza2004a, p. 65). The members are introduced two sentences later, and the Puesto Antigual gets a thickness and lithology but no age. A third party's copy of a parent unit's range is the parent's statement, not a determination for the child.
- *Upper Barremian–lower Aptian by correlation.* Leanza (2003) attributes the formation to "Barremiano superior – Aptiano inferior" by correlating it with the Huitrín Formation (leanza2003a, p. 1, Resumen). That is again formation-level, and the same report brackets the basal member within the Barremian (p. 20).

**Open.** Leanza and colleagues (2004, p. 65) cite Leanza and Hugo (1995) for "Barremian–Early Aptian", but the 1995 text gives the formation as "Hauteriviano tardío / Barremiano" ("late Hauterivian/Barremian", translated; leanza1995a, p. 30) and its chart has no Aptian member. The formation's own range is not settled by this note. The top member, the Piedra Parada (*Zapalasaurus*), does carry an Aptian assignment of its own: "Barremian-lower Aptian" (salgado2006b, p. 695, abstract), given as "Barremiano superior – Aptiano inferior" on p. 699.
