# Parecis

## Does the Parecis take the Santonian from the basalts at its base?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Not yet; Bittencourt and Langer (2011) are cited and the Santonian waits on the original date. Bittencourt and Langer give the Paredão Grande basalts, "at the base of the Parecis Group", as 83.9 ± 0.4 Ma, late Santonian, after Gibson and colleagues (1997); Weska (2006), whose column makes the Paredão Grande the group's basal formation, gives the same laser Ar–Ar date after his own 1996 thesis and Gibson and colleagues. Both relay it, a second-hand date adds no stage until its original is read, and a thesis never sole-sources a value. Governs the pointers to bittencourt2011a, gibson1997a and weska1996a and the note on weska2006a on [Parecis](../../../stratigraphy/p/parecis.yml); `stages` unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- bittencourt2011a, p. 33: "The basalts of the Paredão Grande Formation, at the base of the Parecis Group, were dated 83.9 ± 0.4 Ma (Gibson et al. 1997), i.e. Late Santonian".
- weska2006a, p. 75: "A idade obtida por Weska (1996) e Gibson et al. (1997) para um dos corpos dos derrames da Raizinha sugere que o resfriamento magmático ocorreu há 83,9 ± 0,4 Ma. Essa idade foi obtida através do método Ar40/Ar39 a laser." ["The age obtained by Weska (1996) and Gibson et al. (1997) for one of the bodies of the Raizinha lava flows suggests that magmatic cooling occurred 83.9 ± 0.4 Ma ago. This age was obtained through the Ar40/Ar39 laser method", translated].

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Gibson and colleagues (1997) has not been read; it would show the sample and whether the dated flow lies within the group, which would add the Santonian.

## What does Weska (2006) propose for the Parecis, and what age does the paper give it?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Late Cretaceous, with no stage. Weska (2006) writes that the rocks of the Parecis and Bauru groups in Mato Grosso were deposited during the Upper Cretaceous, in erosive unconformity over the intracratonic Parecis and Paraná basins. Treating the two groups as time-equivalent and alike, the paper proposes a single stratigraphic column for both basins, suppresses the Bauru Group in Mato Grosso, and makes up the Parecis Group of the Paredão Grande, Salto das Nuvens, Cachoeira do Bom Jardim and Utiariti formations. The paper names no stage; the Campanian–Maastrichtian on the group comes from Delcourt (2017), who cites Weska (2006) together with Bittencourt and Langer (2011). Governs `period: [Late Cretaceous]` on [Parecis](../../../stratigraphy/p/parecis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- weska2006a, p. 71, Abstract: "The work carried out thus far on the Upper Cretaceous of Mato Grosso suggests the development of a single stratigraphic column for both basins, as well as the suppression of the Bauru Group in Mato Grosso. In this context, the Parecis Group can be characterized by the Paredão Grande, Salto das Nuvens, Cachoeira do Bom Jardim, and Utiariti formations" (translated).
- weska2006a, p. 73: "The rocks of the Parecis and Bauru groups in Mato Grosso were deposited during the Upper Cretaceous, in erosive unconformity over the intracratonic basins of Parecis and Paraná" (translated).
- weska2006a, p. 79: "a new stratigraphic column is proposed composed of the Paredão Grande, Salto das Nuvens, Cachoeira do Bom Jardim and Utiariti formations, encompassed in the Parecis Group, suppressing the Bauru Group" (translated).

**Ruled out.** Nothing was weighed and rejected.

**Open.** Whether the group reaches below the Campanian. Weska (2006, p. 71) puts the Paredão Grande basaltic magmatism, which the paper places at the base of the group, at about 85 Ma, and Bittencourt and Langer (2011) date those basalts to the late Santonian while giving the group's dinosaur fauna a Campanian–Maastrichtian age.
