# Prince Creek

## What stages does the Prince Creek Formation span?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Campanian–Maastrichtian (reaching the Paleocene) since Mull and colleagues (2003) restricted the formation; the Coniacian and Turonian readings belong to the older, broader unit. Governs `stages: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on Prince Creek and the stage of [*Pachyrhinosaurus perotorum*](../../../genera/P/Pachyrhinosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fiorillo2010a, p. 457: Mull and colleagues (2003) restricted "the Prince Creek Formation to units overlying, and interfingering with, the marine Schrader Bluff Formation"; p. 458: Ocean Point tuffs "range from approximately 68 to 71 Ma, with a best age estimate of 69.1 +/– 0.3 Ma", relaying Conrad and colleagues (1990).
- loewen2024a, p. 64, Table 2: *P. perotorum* from the Prince Creek Formation at 73.4–70.0 Ma.

**Ruled out.**
- *Coniacian.* Brett-Surman (1989) lists "Prince Creek Fm, Coniacian ?" (brett-surman1989a, p. 145), under the older scheme, in which the formation included "the Tuluvak Tongue, a lower, coarser-grained tongue generally below and interfingering with the Schrader Bluff Formation" (fiorillo2010a, p. 457).
- *Turonian.* Frederiksen's (1990) Turonian is for another unit: "The Seabee Formation is at least partly of Turonian age" (frederiksen1990a, p. E3).

**Open.** Nothing for the stages. Mull and colleagues (2003) have not been read.
