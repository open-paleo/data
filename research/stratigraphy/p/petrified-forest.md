# Petrified Forest

## Does Martz and Parker's "to the Rhaetian" put the Petrified Forest Member in the Rhaetian?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** No. The Revueltian biochron that the member falls in is "about 215–207 Ma", and the Rhaetian end is a label on the timescales of Muttoni and colleagues (2004) and Hüsing and colleagues (2011), which place the base of the Rhaetian older than the current chart does. The same paper gives the interval as "middle and late Norian" elsewhere, and the dates it prints for the member are Norian on the ICS chart v2024/12 (Rhaetian base 205.7 Ma). Governs `stages: [Norian]` on [Petrified Forest](../../../stratigraphy/p/petrified-forest.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- martz2017a, p. 105: "a duration for the Revueltian biochron of about 215–207 Ma (about 8 Ma), which extends from the earliest Sevatian (early middle Norian) to the Rhaetian (Muttoni et al., 2004; Hüsing et al., 2011)".
- martz2017a, p. 112: "The Revueltian estimated holochron extends from about 215 to 207 Ma (an interval of about 8 million years) during the Alaunian and Sevatian (middle and late Norian)".
- martz2017a, p. 105: "a radioisotopic date of about 207.8 Ma (Ramezani et al., 2011; W.G. Parker, unpublished data) was produced from immediately below the lowermost ledge-forming carbonate beds" of the overlying Owl Rock Member; p. 77, Fig. 10: 209.926 Ma at the Black Forest Bed within the member.

**Ruled out.**
- *Rhaetian.* The figures behind it, about 207 Ma, are Norian on the current chart, and CONTRIBUTING's rule on reading ages against the chart a paper used takes the number over the older stage. The paper's own two statements of the interval disagree, and the one without the cited timescales gives the Norian alone.

**Open.** Nothing.
