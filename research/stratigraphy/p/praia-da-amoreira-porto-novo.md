# Praia da Amoreira-Porto Novo

## What age does Hill (1989) give the Praia da Amoreira member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Mid-to-late Kimmeridgian, for the lower of the two members only, and by position rather than fossils. Hill finds no biostratigraphically useful palynomorphs in the Praia da Amoreira member and brackets it between the Abadia formation below, late Oxfordian to mid-Kimmeridgian after Ellwood (1987), and the Praia Azul member, dated basal Tithonian after Leinfelder (1987). Hill keeps the Praia da Amoreira and Porto Novo as separate members of the Lourinhã formation, the first succeeded on the coast by the second, and gives the Porto Novo no age. The Kimmeridgian in `stages` therefore has support from Hill for the lower part of the combined member; the Tithonian comes from other works. Governs the Kimmeridgian in `stages` on [Praia da Amoreira-Porto Novo](../../../stratigraphy/p/praia-da-amoreira-porto-novo.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hill1989a, p. 539, Abstract: "During a late Jurassic rifting event, a series of alluvial fans distributed coarse, basement-derived clastics, southeastward from the western margin of the basin."
- hill1989a, p. 540, Geological setting: "The sediments described form the basal Praia da Amoreira member of the mudrock-rich, dominantly fluviatile, Lourinhã formation. They indicate that during a late Jurassic rifting phase, a series of low-relief alluvial fans distributed coarse basement-derived clastics"; "On the coast, the Praia da Amoreira member is succeeded vertically by deposits of a fluvial meander belt which characterize the Porto Novo member".
- hill1989a, p. 540, Geological setting: the member "has yielded no biostratigraphically useful palynomorphs, and its age can only be inferred from its stratigraphic position above the Abadia formation which is of late Oxfordian to mid-Kimmeridgian age (Ellwood 1987)"; "The younger deltaic sediments of the Praia Azul member have been dated as basal Tithonian by Leinfelder (1987). Thus it is probable that the Praia da Amoreira member is of mid-to-late Kimmeridgian age."

**Ruled out.** Nothing among the works cited here.

**Open.** Hill's age is an inference from position, resting on Ellwood's (1987) thesis for the Abadia below and Leinfelder (1987) for the Praia Azul above; neither has been read.
