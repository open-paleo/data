# Pingling

## What age do Zhao and colleagues (1991) give the Pingling Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Maastrichtian, with the formation's top at the Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary. Zhao and colleagues (1991) place the boundary at the contact between the Pingling and the overlying Shanghu Formation, in the upper part of chron 29R, a reversed interval that takes in the upper Pingling and the base of the Shanghu. They name a stage only for the dinosaur-egg assemblage the Pingling shares with the underlying Yuanpu Formation, which they date to the Maastrichtian; they give no stage for the Pingling by itself. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]` and `period: [Late Cretaceous]` on [Pingling](../../../stratigraphy/p/pingling.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhao1991a, p. 1 (translated): "the boundary between the Pingling Formation and the Shanghu Formation, located in the upper part of geomagnetic polarity zone 29R, has been determined to be the K/T boundary".
- zhao1991a, p. 3 (translated): chron 29R "includes the upper part of the Pingling Formation and the base of the Shanghu Formation".
- zhao1991a, p. 6 (translated): the egg species of both formations belong to "the same fossil assemblage, dating to the latest Late Cretaceous (Maastrichtian)".
- zhao1991a, p. 14, Summary: the Cretaceous-Tertiary boundary "should be CGD 182.3m by linear interpolation, i.e. 20m above the base of the Shanghu Formation"; p. 15: "the boundary between the Pingling Formation and the Shanghu Fomation is selected as the Cretaceous-Tertiary boundary"; p. 16: the eggshells of both formations "are considered as representing a single assemblage, which indicates clearly Maastrichtian in age".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Danian upper Pingling, because chron 29R includes it.* The paper puts the boundary at the Pingling–Shanghu contact, and its linear interpolation would put it higher still, 20 m into the Shanghu. Neither reading places the boundary inside the Pingling, so the part of 29R within the formation is Cretaceous in the paper's account.

**Open.** Nothing.
