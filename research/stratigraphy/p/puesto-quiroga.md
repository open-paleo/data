# Puesto Quiroga

## How did Leanza and Hugo (1995) define the Puesto Quiroga, and what age do the cited works give it?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Aptian–Albian, as the lower member of the [Lohan Cura](../../../stratigraphy/l/lohan-cura.yml). Leanza and Hugo (1995) erected the member as the lower of the formation's two members, with its type locality on the northern slope of Cerro Lohan Cura, a few meters above the level of Puesto Quiroga, where it is 85 m thick. Their section opens with a 4 m polymict basal conglomerate, continues in brick-red and reddish-brown sandstones and conglomerates, and ends in 57 m of red and purple mudstones with thin gray and brown sandstones; they identify that mudstone package with the "China Muerta Facies" of Roll (1939). The paper's text dates only the formation, by its bounds: its maximum age "can reach the Aptian", given the late Hauterivian–Barremian age of the underlying La Amarga Formation, and its minimum age is that of the unconformity at the base of the Neuquén Group, put in the early Cenomanian (a late Albian estimate for that minimum rests on a personal communication). Leanza (1999) ascribes the formation to the late Aptian and Albian, and Carballido and colleagues (2012) and Salgado and colleagues (2004) give the member itself Aptian–Albian. Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` and `parent: Lohan Cura` on [Puesto Quiroga](../../../stratigraphy/p/puesto-quiroga.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leanza1995a, p. 31: "the Lohan Cura Formation can be subdivided, from bottom to top, into two Members"; "Puesto Quiroga Member nom. nov.: Its type locality is found on the northern slope of Cerro Lohan Cura, a few meters above the elevation of Puesto Quiroga, where it reaches 85 m in thickness" (translated).
- leanza1995a, p. 31: "a polymict basal conglomerate of brick-red tone 4 m in thickness, followed by 26 m composed of an alternation of brick-red and brownish-reddish sandstones and conglomerates. There follow 57 m of red and purple mudstones"; "It is precisely this package of red mudstones that belongs to the 'China Muerta Facies' (see Roll 1939)" (translated).
- leanza1995a, p. 31, chart "Proposed stratigraphic chart for the analyzed region": the Puesto Quiroga Member in the Aptian row, below the Cullín Grande Member.
- leanza1995a, p. 31: the minimum age of the formation "can be estimated at the Lower Cenomanian (97±3 Ma)" (translated).
- leanza1995a, p. 32: "the maximum age can reach the Aptian, given the upper Hauterivian / Barremian age usually assigned to Formation La Amarga"; the "China Muerta Facies" "corresponds exactly to the mudstone section of the Puesto Quiroga Member that constitutes the lower part of the Lohan Cura Formation" (translated).
- leanza1999a, p. 21: "the Lohan Cura Formation can be ascribed to the late Aptian and Albian"; "an alternation of 24 m of red and red brownish conglomeradic sandstones and siltstones".
- carballido2012a, p. 634: "The age of this member is considered as Aptian–Albian".
- salgado2004a, p. 903: "the Lower Cretaceous (Aptian-Albian) Puesto Quiroga Member of the Lohan Cura Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian only.* Leanza and Hugo's (1995) chart draws the member in the Aptian row, but the chart is the paper's only statement on the member's age and gives no evidence for it. Their text dates the formation as a whole between bounds that allow the Albian, and the later works cited give the member, or the formation, Aptian and Albian (leanza1999a, p. 21; carballido2012a, p. 634; salgado2004a, p. 903).

**Open.** Leanza and Hugo's (1995) parts of the type section (4, 26 and 57 m) add to 87 m against the 85 m they state; Leanza (1999) gives the middle interval as 24 m, which matches the total.
