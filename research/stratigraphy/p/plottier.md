# Plottier

## What is the age of the Plottier Formation?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Late Coniacian, possibly reaching the early Santonian. Governs `stages: [Coniacian, Santonian]` on Plottier.

**Evidence.**
- garrido2010a, p. 148: "se considera a esta última edad como la más probable, pudiendo llegar extenderse hasta el Santoniano Temprano" ("this latter age is considered the most probable, potentially extending to the Early Santonian", translated). "This latter age" is Hugo and Leanza's (2001) "dentro del Coniaciano Tardío" ("within the Late Coniacian"), weighed against Musacchio's (2006) and Musacchio and Vallati's (2007) "Coniaciano – Santoniano" from microfossils.
- gonzálezriga2016a, p. 2, Type locality and horizon: "basal-most bed of the Upper Cretaceous (upper Coniacian–lower Santonian, ~86 Ma) Plottier Formation".
- filippi2011a, p. 2: "from the late part of the Coniacian to the early part of the Santonian; Legarreta and Gulisano, 1989; Cruz et al., 1989".

**Ruled out.**
- *Santonian only.* Simón and Salgado (2023) give "Notocolossus gonzalezparejasi (Plottier Formation, Santonian)" (simón2023a, p. 719), and Filippi and colleagues (2011) say just "Santonian" in their locality section (filippi2011a, p. 4). Both repeat the sources above and do not date the formation themselves.
- *Middle to upper Santonian.* Salgado and colleagues (2009) print "Plottier (middle to upper? Santonian)" (salgado2009b, p. 768) with no supporting evidence. An outlier.
- *~86 Ma as a Santonian date.* González Riga and colleagues' (2016) ~86 Ma sits at the Coniacian–Santonian boundary of the 2012 timescale (base Santonian 86.3 ± 0.5 Ma) but in the latest Coniacian of the 2020 timescale (85.7 ± 0.2 Ma; gale2020a, p. 1066, Table 27.4; radioisotopic date 85.66 ± 0.19 Ma in Table 27.3, p. 1064). It does not push the formation into the Santonian.

**Open.** No radiometric date exists for the formation. Garrido's Plottier section mentions none, while his sections on the Portezuelo and Bajo de la Carpa say outright that none exists; that the Plottier has none is an inference from that silence.
