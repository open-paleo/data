# Portland

## Are the Tithonian and the Late Triassic readings for the Portland?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No. The Portland of this entry is the Early Jurassic formation of the Hartford Basin. Governs `period: [Early Jurassic]` and `stages: [Hettangian, Sinemurian, Pliensbachian, Toarcian]` on [Portland](../../../stratigraphy/p/portland.yml).

**Evidence.**
- weishampel2004b, p. 532, under 3.20, Massachusetts: "2. Portland Formation (Olsen 1980a; … = Turner Falls Formation)", "Age: Pliensbachian-Toarcian".
- weishampel2004b, p. 546: the English entries "Portland Stone (Phillips 1871; Delair 1993; Benton and Spencer 1995)", "Age: early Tithonian (= early Portlandian; Cope et al. 1980b)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tithonian.* The Tithonian in *The Dinosauria* distribution chapter belongs to the Portland Stone of southern England, listed under English counties. That is a different unit with a similar name.
- *Late Triassic.* White (1973) gives *Ammosaurus* and *Yaleosaurus* from the "Upper Triassic, Newark Series, Portland Sandstone" (white1973a, catalogue entries for those genera). A catalogue alone does not carry an epoch (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-reference-work-corroborates-but-almost-never-carries-a-value-alone)), and the record's Early Jurassic rests on Olsen and Galton (1984), who give palynoflorules and K/Ar dates.

**Open.** Nothing.
