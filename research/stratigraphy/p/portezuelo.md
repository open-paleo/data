# Portezuelo

## What is the age of the Portezuelo Formation?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Late Turonian–early Coniacian, following Garrido (2010). Governs `stages: [Turonian, Coniacian]` on Portezuelo and on the species whose type horizon is in it.

**Evidence.**
- garrido2010a, p. 144: "entre el Turoniano Tardío – Coniaciano Temprano, criterio también adoptado en este trabajo" ("between Late Turonian – Early Coniacian, a criterion also adopted in this work", translated). Garrido adopts the age of Hugo and Leanza (2001) and Leanza and colleagues (2004).
- garrido2010a, p. 144: "Formación Los Bastos (nom. nov.)", placed "por encima de la Fm. Portezuelo" ("above the Portezuelo Fm.", translated). Sierra Barrosa follows on p. 146. Strata that older work could include in a broadly drawn Portezuelo are now separate formations; that the new formations account for the older Santonian readings is an inference, not a statement in the paper.
- calvo2004b, p. 565, abstract: "Portezuelo Formation (Upper Turonian-Lower Coniacian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Turonian–Santonian.* Calvo and colleagues (2004) report it from sequence stratigraphy, "interpreted as Turonian through Santonian on the basis of sequence stratigraphy" (calvo2004b, p. 566), while their own abstract gives upper Turonian–lower Coniacian (p. 565). Benson and colleagues (2009) repeat "Portezuelo Formation (Turonian–Santonian)" (benson2009e, p. 72) and cite Calvo and colleagues. Both predate Garrido (2010).
- *Upper Turonian–lower Santonian?* Salgado and colleagues (2009) give "Portezuelo (upper Turonian – lower Santonian?)" (salgado2009b, p. 768), with the Santonian queried. It predates Garrido (2010) and gives no new evidence.

**Open.** Nothing. No radiometric date exists for the formation; Garrido (2010, p. 143) says so outright.
