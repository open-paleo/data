# Deadhorse Coulee

## Does the renaming of Deadhorse Coulee rename the member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** No. The coulee is now Black Coulee, and Ryan and colleagues (2012) use the current name for the locality while keeping the Deadhorse Coulee Member for the unit. This is the same pattern as Bayn Dzak against Bayan Zag: the locality takes the current name and the unit keeps the name it was erected under, under the house-spelling rule in [methods](../../methods.md). Governs `name: Deadhorse Coulee` on [Deadhorse Coulee](../../../stratigraphy/d/deadhorse-coulee.yml), and `locality: Black Coulee` with `member: Deadhorse Coulee` on [*Gryphoceratops*](../../../genera/G/Gryphoceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ryan2012b, p. 73: "Black Coulee (formerly Deadhorse Coulee ...), approximately 10 km east of Writing-on-Stone Provincial Park, Alberta, from the Deadhorse Coulee Member of the Milk River Formation (late Santonian)".
- ryan2012b, p. 71, Fig. 2: the holotype "was collected from Black Coulee (formerly Deadhorse Coulee)".
- evans2013b, p. 2, Locality and horizon: specimens from "the Deadhorse Coulee Member of the Milk River Formation" were collected "in exposures adjacent to Black Coulee (formerly Deadhorse Coulee)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Renaming the member to follow the coulee.* Both Ryan and colleagues (2012) and Evans and colleagues (2013) give the coulee's new name and keep the member's, and none of the works cited uses a Black Coulee Member.

**Open.** Nothing.
