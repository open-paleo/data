# Densuș-Ciula

## Does the Densuș-Ciula Formation reach into the Campanian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes: it covers much of the upper Campanian as well as the Maastrichtian. Governs `stages: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on Densuș-Ciula.

**Evidence.**
- albert2025a, p. 1: the formation "covers a good part of the upper Campanian"; the syntectonic reading applies to the "earliest dinosaur-bearing strata".
- magyar2026a, p. 2, relays it.

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian only.* Earlier compilations give the formation as Maastrichtian in tables (norman2004a, Table 19.1; ősi2012a, Table 2; augustin2022a, p. 2). Albert and colleagues (2025) supersede them.

**Open.** Nothing.
