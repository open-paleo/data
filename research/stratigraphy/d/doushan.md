# Doushan

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Doushan?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Aptian. The Doushan's own K-Ar dates of 119 to 121 Ma, from Hu and colleagues (1982) as Gao and Cheng (1999) relay them, lie wholly in the Aptian on the ICS chart v2024/12, whose base of the Aptian is 121.4 Ma. Gao and Cheng read the same dates as Barremian on the chart of Harland and colleagues (1990); Averianov and colleagues (2006) point out that on that chart too the interval is Aptian. Governs `stages: [Aptian]` on [Doushan](../../../stratigraphy/d/doushan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gao1999a, p. 456: the Qingshan Group "is subdivided into the Zhujiazhuang, Shuimutou, and Doushan formations"; "The Doushan Formation has been dated as 119–121 Ma (Hu et al., 1982), while its underlying Shuimutou Formation has been dated as 122.6 Ma (Hu and Hu, 1986); therefore, radiometric dates indicate that the Qingshan Group is Barremian in age (Harland et al., 1990)".
- averianov2006a, p. 391: "This time interval is within the Aptian, according to Harland et al. (1989), not Barremian, as was thought by Gao & Cheng (1999) with reference to the same stratigraphic charts."
- averianov2000a, p. 335: the Shuimutou Formation "has a K/Ar date 122 Ma (early Aptian; Cheng et al. 1995)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian, from Gao and Cheng (1999).* It rests on reading 119–121 Ma and 122.6 Ma against Harland and colleagues (1990), on which, as Averianov and colleagues (2006) note, the Doushan interval is Aptian. On the ICS chart v2024/12 all of 119–121 Ma is Aptian.
- *Late Barremian from the Shuimutou date.* The 122.6 Ma of Gao and Cheng (1999), and the 122 Ma that Averianov and Skutschas (2000) label early Aptian on an older chart, are late Barremian on the ICS chart v2024/12, but they date the Shuimutou Formation beneath the Doushan. They are a floor for the Doushan, not an age for it.

**Open.** None of the works cited gives an uncertainty for the K-Ar dates of Hu and colleagues (1982), and 121 Ma lies only 0.4 Myr above the base of the Aptian, so an error larger than that would leave the lowest Doushan open to the late Barremian. Hu and colleagues (1982), which has not been read, would settle the error.
