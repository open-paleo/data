# Dzharakuduk

## Is the Dzharakuduk Formation the unit exposed at Dzharakuduk?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** No. The name serves both a formation and a locality complex, and the two occurrences are at different places and of different ages. Averianov and Sues (2016) place the *Urbacodon itemirensis* dentary in the lower Cenomanian Dzharakuduk Formation at Itemir, and the troodontid material from the escarpment near the settlement of Dzharakuduk in the upper Turonian Bissekty Formation. Governs `stages: [Cenomanian]` on [Dzharakuduk](../../../stratigraphy/d/dzharakuduk.yml) and the formation of the type locality on [*Urbacodon*](../../../genera/U/Urbacodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2016a, p. 100: the holotype comes "from the Dzharakuduk Formation (Upper Cretaceous: lower Cenomanian) at the Itemir locality (IT-01) in the Itemir-Dzharakuduk Depression of the central Kyzylkum Desert".
- averianov2016a, p. 99: the dentary came "from the lower part of the Cenomanian Dzharakuduk Formation of Itemir"; similar teeth and bones "are known also from the upper Turonian Bissekty Formation of Dzharakuduk".
- averianov2016a, p. 99: Bissekty strata "are exposed along an approximately 8 km long escarpment near the small settlement of Dzharakuduk".
- averianov2016a, p. 99, Fig. 1: the two occurrences are the "Dzharakuduk Formation at Itemir and Bissekty Formation at Dzharakuduk", with the Itemir well and the "Dzharakuduk wells" marked separately.

**Ruled out.**
- *Fossils from the Dzharakuduk escarpment come from the Dzharakuduk Formation.* The shared name invites the reading, but Averianov and Sues (2016) assign the material from Dzharakuduk to the Bissekty Formation and date it to the Turonian; the Dzharakuduk Formation occurrence is at Itemir.

**Open.** Averianov and Sues (2016, p. 109) raise "possibly a slightly younger age of the Dzharakuduk Formation" than the Khodzhakul Formation while still calling both Cenomanian. None of the works cited dates the formation apart from the *Urbacodon* papers.
