# Dockum

## Does the Dockum Group include Middle Triassic beds?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The Dockum is Late Triassic (Carnian–Norian); the Middle Triassic beds once included are now the Anton Chico Formation beneath it. Governs `period: [Late Triassic]` and `stages: [Carnian, Norian]` on Dockum.

**Evidence.**
- lehman2005a, p. 326: "the Anton Chico Formation, underlies the Dockum Group in eastern New Mexico".
- lucas1994a, p. 105: "the lowermost member of the Santa Rosa Formation, as then identified, is of Middle Triassic (early Anisian) age (Lucas and Morales, 1985)" and "Lucas and Hunt (1987) renamed the lowermost member of the Santa Rosa Formation the Anton Chico Formation".
- lucas1995a, p. 5: "the Camp Springs Member is of Late Triassic age and disconformably overlies Upper Permian strata".
- martz2013a, p. 339 (abstract): the *Postosuchus* Post Quarry is "possibly late Lacian or earliest Alaunian (late early Norian or earliest middle Norian), between 220 and 215 Ma".

**Ruled out.**
- *Middle Triassic from Galton (1985).* Galton's captions place *Postosuchus* in the "Dockum Formation (Middle Triassic) of Texas, U.S.A., after CHATTERJEE (1985)" (galton1985c, p. 6, Fig. 3B) and repeat the age for a poposaurid (p. 8, Fig. 4E). The Post Quarry is Norian.
- *Lucas and Morales's "Middle Triassic amphibian" as Dockum evidence.* The beds were the lowermost Santa Rosa, later split off as the Anton Chico (lucas1994a, p. 105).

**Open.** Nothing.
