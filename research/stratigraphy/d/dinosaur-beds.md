# Dinosaur Beds

## Is the Malawi "Dinosaur Beds" a formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No formal rank. Dixey (1928) introduced the unit as "a new formation" in the generic sense of the time, and every later paper read for this note uses "Dinosaur Beds" without a rank, with lower and upper parts. Governs the empty `rank` on Dinosaur Beds and `location.part: upper` on [*Malawisaurus dixeyi*](../../../genera/M/Malawisaurus.yml); bears on [*Karongasaurus gittelmani*](../../../genera/K/Karongasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dixey1928a, p. 55: "a new formation existed there that had not previously been recognised"; p. 61: "The lower Dinosaur Beds can generally be distinguished in the field"; "The upper Dinosaur Beds are characterised by". Dixey is the sole author; Haughton "communicated" the paper.
- jacobs1993a, p. 525: "Upper member of the Early Cretaceous Dinosaur Beds (Lupata Group); Mwakasyunguti area".
- gomani2005a, p. 2: "the same area of the Dinosaur Beds (Dixey 1928; Jacobs et al. 1990, 1992)", "of Early Cretaceous (?Aptian) age"; gorscak2014a, p. 1133: "the Aptian Dinosaur Beds (DB) of Malawi"; gorscak2017a, p. 2 and gorscak2019a, p. 1 likewise.

**Ruled out.**
- *Dinosaur Beds Formation.* Mannion and colleagues (2026) write "the Aptian Dinosaur Beds Formation of Malawi (Jacobs et al. 1993, Gomani 2005)" (mannion2026a, p. 2); neither source they cite uses the rank.
- *Karoo-aged beds, or a label no one erected.* Dixey (1928, p. 55) erected the unit, and the beds are Cretaceous.

**Open.** The rank of Jacobs and colleagues' "Upper member" and their "Lupata Group" assignment, which the registry does not record.
