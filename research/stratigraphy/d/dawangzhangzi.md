# Dawangzhangzi

## Is the Dakangpu Bed the Dawangzhangzi Bed?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Every source that relates the two names equates them, so a record takes the member value Dawangzhangzi, with Dakangpu as a variant. A locality called "Dawangzhangzi Village" is not evidence for the bed. Governs `variants: [Dakangpu]` on Dawangzhangzi and `location.member: Dawangzhangzi` on species from the Dakangpu Bed, such as [*Jinzhousaurus yangi*](../../../genera/J/Jinzhousaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- qiu2019a, p. 2: "The Dakangpu Bed (same horizon to Dawangzhangzi Bed) of Yixian Formation".
- wang2001a, p. 1669: "Dakangpu member, the middle part of the Yixian Formation (comparable to Dawangzhangzi Bed)"; p. 1672: "The Dakangpu Bed is the same as the Dawangzhangzi Bed of the middle Yixian Formation", citing earlier work.
- lefèvre2014a, p. 791: "Dakangpu Member (equivalent to the Dawangzhangzi Beds) of the Yixian Formation"; wu2012a, p. 296, the same.

**Ruled out.**
- *Dakangpu as a separate unit.* McDonald and colleagues (2010, 2021) and Wu and colleagues (2010) use "Dakangpu" alone without equating it (for example "Jinzhousaurus yangi from the Dakangpu Member of the Yixian Formation", mcdonald2010b, p. 33), but none treats it as distinct from the Dawangzhangzi.
- *A Dawangzhangzi Village locality as the Dawangzhangzi Bed.* Ji and colleagues (2016) describe a specimen "collected from the Jianshangou Bed of the Lower Cretaceous Yixian Formation at Dawangzhangzi Village, Lingyuan County" (ji2016a, p. 183). The village name gives no horizon.

**Open.** The localities written Wangjiagou, Baicaigou and Bataigou (Baitaigou), in Toutai Township, Yixian County, may be one locality complex. Arbour and Currie (2015) equate Baicaigou with Wangjiagou ("Baicaigou locality (WangJiagou in Xu et al. 2001)", arbour2015a, online-first p. 16); the other identifications are an inference. Xu and colleagues (2003) place Wangjiagou in the lower Yixian ("义县组下部", xu2003a, p. 195), which would not be the Dawangzhangzi Bed.

## Where does the Dawangzhangzi's member rank come from?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** From consistency with the Yixian's other subdivisions, not from a paper that ranks this unit. Wang and colleagues (2016) raise the Lujiatun and Jianshangou to member rank in the Sihetun Sub-basin and do not mention the Dawangzhangzi or the Dakangpu; the member rank is extended to it so that the Yixian's subdivisions share one rank (see [Yixian](../y/yixian.md)). Governs `rank: member` on [Dawangzhangzi](../../../stratigraphy/d/dawangzhangzi.yml), and `location.member: Dawangzhangzi` on records such as [*Bolong yixianensis*](../../../genera/B/Bolong.yml) and [*Jinzhousaurus yangi*](../../../genera/J/Jinzhousaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wang2016a, p. 110, abstract: "we propose a modified lithostratigraphic nomenclature in the Sihetun Sub-basin in which the Yixian Formation is divided into three members: the Lujiatun Member … the Xiatulaigou Member … and the Jianshangou Member". Neither Dawangzhangzi nor Dakangpu appears in the paper.
- wu2012a, p. 296: "Dakangpu Member (equivalent to the Dawangzhangzi Beds)".
- jin2008a, p. 2821: "the Dawangzhangzi Bed of the Yixian Formation in Lingyuan"; qiu2025a, p. 2: "The Dawangzhangzi bed of Yixian Formation".
- chang2017a, accepted manuscript, p. 1: the formation divided "from bottom to top into the Lujiatun Unit, Lower Lava Unit, Jianshangou Unit, Upper Lava Unit, Dawangzhangzi Unit and Jingangshan Unit".

**Ruled out.**
- *Wang and colleagues (2016) as the source of this unit's rank.* Their three members are those of the Sihetun Sub-basin. The Dawangzhangzi localities cited here, in Lingyuan and at Toutai in Yixian County, lie outside it; that is our inference from the place names, not a statement in the paper.
- *"Dakangpu Member" as a ranking of this unit.* Wu and Godefroit (2012) give the Bolong horizon as "Dakangpu Member", but the naming paper's Chinese text reads 大康堡层, "Dakangpu Bed" (wu2010a, p. 128, translated).

**Open.** Whether the unit keeps member rank. Among the works cited here, it is a bed in most, a unit in Chang and colleagues (2017), and a member only in the Dakangpu lineage, and none of them erects it as a member. Recording it as a bed would move `location.member` to `location.bed` on every record that names it.
