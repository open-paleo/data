# Denver

## Does the Denver reach the Danian?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Raynolds (2002) defines the Denver Formation as the andesitic part of the D1 sequence, and places within D1 both the transition from dinosaur-bearing to mammal-bearing beds on South Table Mountain, which it relays from Brown (1943), and "earliest Paleocene (Puercan)" mammal localities, South Table Mountain among them. That South Table Mountain is Denver rather than another part of D1 is read from the western cross section, which draws the mountain in the dark-gray andesitic shading that the same figure labels "Denver" at Green Mountain, over the dotted base labeled "Arapahoe". Brown (1943) places the transition inside the Denver Formation itself, and Eberle (2003) finds Puercan zones in the formation. Governs `Danian` in `stages` and `Paleocene` in `period` on [Denver](../../../stratigraphy/d/denver.yml).

**Evidence.**
- raynolds2002a, p. 122: "The andesitic debris has been used in the northern part of the basin to define the Denver Formation, here considered a component of the D1 sequence".
- raynolds2002a, p. 113: relaying Soister, "strata below the paleosol belonging to the Dawson Arkose and Denver Formation are Paleocene and Cretaceous".
- raynolds2002a, p. 118, Fig. 8: South Table Mountain (STM) drawn in the shading the caption explains as "Dark gray shading in D1 sequence indicates andesitic facies", the pattern labeled "Denver" over "Arapahoe" at Green Mountain.
- raynolds2002a, p. 128: "Brown (1943) reported the faunal transition from dinosaur-bearing beds into early Tertiary mammal-bearing beds that occurs within the D1 sequence on South Table Mountain. Numerous earliest Paleocene (Puercan) mammal localities have been identified in D1 sequence strata, principally at Corral Bluffs, South Littleton, West Bijou Creek, and South Table Mountain".
- raynolds2002a, p. 128: "Lava flows capping North and South Table Mountains have been dated at between 63 and 64 Ma".
- raynolds2002a, p. 119: "I suggest that future use of the names Arapahoe Formation, Denver Formation, and Dawson Arkose be done only with careful definition of the sense intended".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Danian age from the Table Mountain lava flows.* The flows, dated at 63 to 64 Ma, fall inside the Danian on the ICS chart v2024/12, but they cap the mountains: they give the Denver there a ceiling, not an age. The Danian rests on the Puercan mammals below them.
- *The faunal transition as Raynolds's own observation.* Raynolds (2002) relays it from Brown (1943); it counts once, as Brown's.

**Open.** Nothing.
