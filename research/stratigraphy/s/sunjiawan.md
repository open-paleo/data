# Sunjiawan

## What is the age of the Sunjiawan Formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; Albian–Cenomanian is the range the evidence supports. Governs `stages` on Sunjiawan and on its taxa, [*Shuangmiaosaurus gilmorei*](../../../genera/S/Shuangmiaosaurus.yml), [*Crichtonsaurus bohlini*](../../../genera/C/Crichtonsaurus.yml) and [*Crichtonpelta benxiensis*](../../../genera/C/Crichtonpelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jiang2006a, p. 215: "the Zhanglaogongtun and the overlying Sunjiawan formations are tentatively considered as late Albian in age in this paper"; the conclusions on the same page drop "tentatively".
- you2003b, p. 148: "Sunjiawan Formation, mid-Cretaceous"; p. 154: "we believe the Sunjiawan Formation to be early Late Cretaceous in age", an inference from the phylogenetic position of *Shuangmiaosaurus*, which the paper erects.

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian–Turonian.* Dong (2002) concludes "Cenomanian-Turonian" from "the morphology of *Crichtonsaurus* and its associated vertebrate assemblage" (dong2002a, pp. 283–284, translated from the Chinese and matching the English abstract). No date or other independent evidence is offered.
- *Turonian from the K–Ar dates.* Jiang and colleagues report "K–Ar dating of the Zhanglaogongtun Formation in the Jianguo area of Fuxin indicated ages of 93.3 and 109.7 Ma" (jiang2006a, p. 215). These are on the underlying formation; 93.3 Ma falls in the Turonian, which would place the Sunjiawan no older than Turonian if taken at face value (our inference), but Jiang and colleagues themselves conclude late Albian.

**Open.** The formation's age. Later papers give single stages without new evidence; the record's notes cite Cenomanian (Xing and colleagues 2024) and Albian (Averianov and colleagues 2020), which were not re-examined for this note.
