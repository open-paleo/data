# Shanyang

## Does any record follow Shan (2025) in putting *Qinlingosaurus* in the Shanyang?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No. [*Qinlingosaurus*](../../../genera/Q/Qinlingosaurus.yml) is filed in the Hongtuling, after Xue and colleagues (1996), who describe it. Governs nothing on [Shanyang](../../../stratigraphy/s/shanyang.yml).

**Evidence.** None.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does Zhao and colleagues' Early Cretaceous date the Shanyang Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** No. Zhao and colleagues (2008) print the locality "Niupanggou, Shanyang, Shanxi", the Shanyang Formation and "Early Cretaceous" on three stacked lines of the distribution field for *Shanyangosaurus niupanggouensis*, with no source for the age, and give the province as Shanxi where Xue and colleagues (1996) give Shaanxi. Xue and colleagues, who described the Shanyang dinosaurs, place them in the Upper Cretaceous Shanyang Formation, and the remarks that immediately follow Zhao and colleagues' distribution field cite Holtz and colleagues (2004), whose table gives the formation as Late Cretaceous. The same volume prints Early Cretaceous in the same form for the Hongtuling Formation, the other unit Xue and colleagues report dinosaurs from and also place in the Upper Cretaceous, and that entry's remarks again cite Holtz and colleagues. Governs the absence of the Early Cretaceous from `period` on [Shanyang](../../../stratigraphy/s/shanyang.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhao2008a, p. 307: "Niupanggou, Shanyang, Shanxi / Shanyang Formation / Early Cretaceous", followed by "Holtz et al. (2004) regarded this taxon as Avetheropoda incertae sedis".
- zhao2008a, p. 285: "Hongtuling range, Luonan, Shaanxi / Hongtuling Formation / Early Cretaceous", followed by "Holtz et al. (2004) regarded this taxon as Sauropoda Nomina dubia".
- xue1996a, p. 76: "Niupanggou, Shanyang, Shaanxi; upper part of the Upper Cretaceous Shanyang Formation" (translated).
- xue1996a, p. 79: "Hongtuling, Luonan, Shaanxi; Upper Cretaceous Hongtuling Formation" (translated).
- holtz2004a, p. 77, Table 4.1: *Shanyangosaurus niupanggouensis*, "Shanyang Formation (Shaanxi), People's Republic of China", "Late Cretaceous".

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Cretaceous, from Zhao and colleagues (2008).* The compilation gives no source for the age, and it is contradicted by the work that described the fossils and by the work its own remarks cite. A reference work corroborates a value but does not carry one alone (see [methods](../../methods.md)).

**Open.** Nothing.
