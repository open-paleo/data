# Sao Khua

## Does the Sao Khua Formation take the Barremian?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** No. The 2026-09-30 entry's conclusion governs the unit as well as its taxa: Tucker and colleagues' late Valanginian–early Hauterivian, with the late Barremian ruled out. That a maximum depositional age leaves a younger age open is a possibility, not a reading, and adds no stage. Governs `stages: [Valanginian, Hauterivian]` on [Sao Khua](../../../stratigraphy/s/sao-khua.yml), replacing `[Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]`, and matches the stages of its six taxa.

**Evidence.**
- tucker2022a, p. 15: "a tightly constrained late Valanginian–early Hauterivian age of 133.6–132.1 Ma for Sao Khua Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian from the open upper bound.* A maximum age does not exclude younger stages, but no source read gives the formation a Barremian on evidence other than the bivalve correlation the 2026-09-30 entry rules out.

**Open.** As in the 2026-09-30 entry: how young the formation can be.

## What is the age of the Sao Khua Formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Late Valanginian to early Hauterivian on Tucker and colleagues' (2022) detrital-zircon reading, which assumes the youngest zircons are near-depositional; a younger age is not excluded by maximum ages alone. Governs `stages` on Sao Khua and on its taxa, such as [*Phuwiangosaurus sirindhornae*](../../../genera/P/Phuwiangosaurus.yml) and [*Siamotyrannus isanensis*](../../../genera/S/Siamotyrannus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tucker2022a, p. 15: "a tightly constrained late Valanginian–early Hauterivian age of 133.6–132.1 Ma for Sao Khua Formation"; p. 11: the zircon populations are "near-contemporaneous … near-synchronous with sedimentation". The middle Sao Khua maximum is 133.8 ± 1.8 Ma (p. 1, Table 1) and the overlying Phu Phan's 132.4 ± 2.0 Ma (p. 1; ±2.6 on p. 11).
- gale2020a, p. 1029: "The base of the Hauterivian is taken at 132.6 Ma".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Barremian.* Tumpeesuwan and colleagues (2010) "roughly" assign the formation "to a Late Barremian age based on the geological range of the subgenus" of one non-marine bivalve (tumpeesuwan2010a, p. 104); the same species elsewhere comes from a formation "assigned to Hauterivian-Barremian" (p. 103). Later late-Barremian statements trace to them (for example samathi2024a, p. 2, which also lists Buffetaut and Suteethorn 1999 among them in error).
- *Berriasian–Aptian, Berriasian–Barremian.* These are outer brackets: "the Sao Khua Formation must fall within the age range Berriasian-Aptian" from the neighboring formations (racey1996a, p. 25); "probably younger than Berriasian and older than Aptian" (buffetaut1999a, p. 14); and an earlier palaeontological "Berriasian to Barremian" that Jia and colleagues (2022) quote while giving their own reading, "早白垩世瓦兰今期之后的沉积" ("deposited after the Early Cretaceous Valanginian", translated; jia2022a, p. 3493).

**Open.** How young the formation can be. A maximum depositional age does not by itself rule out the Barremian, and Tucker and colleagues' closed bracket depends on their near-syndepositional assumption. Their abstract's "no later than 133.8" and "no earlier than 132.4" do not follow from maximum ages alone.
