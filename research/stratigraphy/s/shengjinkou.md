# Shengjinkou

## Is the Shengjinkou Aptian?

*2026-09-21*

**Conclusion.** No. The only Aptian for the formation is that of Averianov and colleagues (2025), who credit it to Wang and colleagues (2021), and Wang and colleagues give no stage. The stage comes from the clam shrimp of Zheng and colleagues (2023). Governs `stages: [Valanginian]` on [Shengjinkou](../../../stratigraphy/s/shengjinkou.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2025b, p. 8: *Hamititan* "is based on a series of articulated strongly procoelous caudal vertebrae from the Aptian Shengjinkou Formation of Xinjiang, China (Wang et al., 2021)".
- wang2021a, p. 2: "the Shengjinkou Formation of the Tugulu Group, which is distributed in the Turpan-Hami Basin, south of the Tian Shan Mountains in Xinjiang, China".
- zheng2023a, p. 5: the clam shrimp evidence "places the upper Shengjinkou Formation within the Valanginian Stage".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian.* Wang and colleagues write "Early Cretaceous" throughout and name no stage. The one Aptian in their paper is in the title of a cited *Moabosaurus* paper. See [Averianov and colleagues (2025)](../../references/a/averianov2025b.md) on how that paper cites.
- *A Shengjinkou in another basin, to which the Aptian might belong.* Wang and colleagues place the formation in the Tugulu Group of the Turpan-Hami Basin, the parent the record gives. Averianov and colleagues cite Wang for the same taxon, so both mean this unit.

**Open.** Nothing.
