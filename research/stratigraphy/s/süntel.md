# Süntel

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Süntel and of bed 83 at Langenberg?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian, Late Jurassic. Zuo and colleagues (2018) divide the formation by ostracod zones of the genus *Macrodentina*: the Lower Süntel reaches the top of zone 12 and the Middle Süntel spans zones 13 to 14, and the base, on the Korallenoolith Formation, is diachronous, at the Oxfordian–Kimmeridgian boundary or in the Early Kimmeridgian. In the Langenberg section only zone 9 represents the Lower Süntel; zones 10 to 12 are absent. Evers and Wings (2020) place the Langenberg vertebrates in bed 83 of Fischer's (1991) section; the bed's position and age, and the rejected bed 93, are set out in the [*Europasaurus*](../../genera/E/Europasaurus.md) note. Governs `stages: [Kimmeridgian]` and `period: [Late Jurassic]` on [Süntel](../../../stratigraphy/s/süntel.yml) and on its bed 83.

**Evidence.**
- zuo2017a, p. 2, Geological setting: the study covers "the Lower to Middle Süntel Formation"; the boundary with the Korallenoolith Formation "is diachronous, corresponding to the Oxfordian–Kimmeridgian boundary or Early Kimmeridgian".
- zuo2017a, p. 3, Geological setting: "The Lower Süntel Formation covers the interval up to the top of zone 12, and the Middle and Upper Süntel Formations encompass zones 13 to 14 and zones 15 to 16, respectively".
- zuo2017a, p. 9, Biostratigraphy: "The Süntel Formation is constrained by the ostracod genus *Macrodentina*"; "In the Langenberg section, ostracod zone 9 (21.0–32.7 m) is present in the Lower Süntel Formation, whereas ostracod zones 10 to 12 are lacking".
- evers2020a, p. 2, Locality, geology and stratigraphy: "After the stratigraphic subdivision of Fischer (1991), most of the terrestrial vertebrate remains … were found in bed 83".
- evers2020a, p. 3, Taphonomy: "The bone-bearing sections of bed 83 were usually 30–50 cm thick".

**Ruled out.** Nothing beyond bed 93, which the *Europasaurus* note rejects.

**Open.** Zuo and colleagues' text gives the Upper Süntel zones 15 to 16 and the Kimmeridgian "the interval between ostracod zones 7 and 15" (zuo2017a, p. 3), while their Fig. 2 (p. 4) draws the whole formation in the Kimmeridgian and puts zone 16 beside the Gigas-Schichten, above the base of the Tithonian (our reading of the figure). Whether the top of the formation reaches the Tithonian does not touch the Lower and Middle Süntel at Langenberg. A stratigraphic treatment of the Upper Süntel would settle it.
