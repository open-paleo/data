# Serra da Galga

## What do the cited works say about the definition and age of the Serra da Galga?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** A formation of the Bauru Group, erected by Soares and colleagues (2020) from the former Serra da Galga and Ponte Alta members of the Marília Formation, and Maastrichtian, as the 2026-10-01 entry of [Marília and Serra da Galga](../../topics/marília-and-serra-da-galga.md) concludes. Soares and colleagues give the unit no stage; their only age statement is the basin's span, which names no Campanian. The direct dating is micropaleontological: Arai and Dias-Brito (2023) date the Serra da Galga Formation by charophytes and ostracods. Menegazzo and colleagues (2016) date the Marília in the broad sense Maastrichtian, and Iori and colleagues (2021) note that most of the species behind that dating come from the deposits now in this unit. Before 2020, Fernandes and Coimbra (2000) described the unit as a member and set its type section. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]`, `rank: formation` and `parent: Bauru` on [Serra da Galga](../../../stratigraphy/s/serra-da-galga.yml).

**Evidence.**
- soares2020a, p. 951 (early-view version, p. 1), Abstract: "this work demonstrates how the previously named Serra da Galga and Ponte Alta members reveal a unique set of lithological, architectural, and genetic signatures that permits to separate them from the Marília Formation. Finally, a new lithostratigraphic classification for the unit is proposed: the Serra da Galga Formation".
- soares2020a, p. 953 (early-view version, p. 3): "Barcelos (1984) organized the formation into three members: the Echaporã Member in the southeastern and northwestern parts, and the Serra da Galga and Ponte Alta members restricted to the northeastern part of the Bauru Group".
- soares2020a, p. 952 (early-view version, p. 2): "The basin evolved from the Coniacian to the Maastrichtian, based on biostratigraphic information (Dias-Brito et al., 2001) and absolute dating of igneous intrusions close to the margins of the basin".
- arai2023a, p. 2, English abstract: "micropaleontology of charophytes and ostracods has indicated Maastrichtian (upper Maastrichtian?) strata in the Serra da Galga Formation".
- santucci2001a, p. 307, Abstract: "The presence of the genus *Aeolosaurus* in the Adamantina Formation from Álvares Machado and Monte Alto (São Paulo State) and in the Serra da Galga Member of the Marília Formation in Uberaba (Minas Gerais State) indicates a Late Campanian/Early Maastrichtian age for this portion of the Bauru Group." The Portuguese summary on the same page places the genus "at the base of the Serra da Galga Member" (translated).
- santucci2001a, p. 313: the occurrence "at the base of the Serra da Galga Member of the Marília Formation of Uberaba" is "indicative of Neocampanian/Eomaastrichtian age for the respective portions of these units" (translated), the prefixes meaning late and early.
- menegazzo2016a, p. 133: earlier work "stated that the Adamantina and Araçatuba formations are Campanian–Maastrichtian and the Marília Formation is Maastrichtian, considering the record of ostracods and charophytes".
- menegazzo2016a, p. 136: "The genus *Aeolosaurus* has been used to attribute Campanian–Maastrichtian to the Adamantina and Marília formations (Bertini et al., 1999; Santucci and Bertini, 2001)", but on Martinelli and colleagues (2011) and later work "the evidence of this genus in the Bauru Basin is inconclusive".
- iori2021a, p. 2: the Marília "was traditionally divided in the Echaporã, Serra da Galga, and Ponte Alta members"; "the majority of species used to formerly establish the relative dating of Marília Formation comes from the deposits now recognized as the new unit Serra da Galga Formation. Hence, considering the lateral relationships among the three formerly known members, a similar age is assumed."
- fernandes2000a, p. 723: the Serra da Galga Member "occurs only on the northeastern border of the basin (Triângulo Mineiro). Its current maximum thickness is approximately 110 m (Barbosa et al. 1970)"; "It overlies the Uberaba Formation, with which it has slightly erosive contacts (diastems)"; "As the type section of the Serra da Galga Member, the cut of the old lime kiln of Serra do Veadinho (Peirópolis) is adopted", with "the cut at km 153 of BR 050 highway" as a complementary section (all translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian–Maastrichtian, from Aeolosaurus.* As in the 2026-10-01 entry of [Marília and Serra da Galga](../../topics/marília-and-serra-da-galga.md); Menegazzo and colleagues (2016) reach the same view of the *Aeolosaurus* evidence.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the 2026-10-01 entry's Open line on the pre-Marília hiatus.
