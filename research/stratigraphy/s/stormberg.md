# Stormberg

## Does the Stormberg Group get an entry?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Yes, as an informal group, by user ruling. The South African Committee for Stratigraphy discarded the name, and Lucas and Hancox (2001) call it defunct, but Bordy and colleagues (2020) use it in their own voice for the Molteno, Elliot and Clarens formations, and it remains in wide use. Bordy and colleagues give it as Upper Triassic–Lower Jurassic; by way of its members it runs from the Carnian of the Molteno (Visser 1984) through the Elliot to the Pliensbachian of the Clarens. Governs the new entry [Stormberg](../../../stratigraphy/s/stormberg.yml) and `parent` on [Elliot](../../../stratigraphy/e/elliot.yml) and [Clarens](../../../stratigraphy/c/clarens.yml).

**Evidence.**
- visser1984a, p. 6: "The South African Committee for Stratigrahy (SACS) discarded the name “Stormberg”, in spite of its usage since 1859."
- bordy2020a, p. 4: "the overlying Stormberg Group, which encompasses the Molteno, Elliot and Clarens formations".
- visser1984a, p. 19: "a major sedimentary cycle starting during the Late Triassic (Carnian)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
