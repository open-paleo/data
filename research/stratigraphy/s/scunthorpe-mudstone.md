# Scunthorpe Mudstone

## What do the cited works say about the definition, thickness and age of the Scunthorpe Mudstone?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Cox and colleagues (1999) define the formation in their Lias Group framework, listing the "Scunthorpe Mudstones Formation" of Gaunt and colleagues (1992) under its previous nomenclature. They take its type section in the BGS Blyborough Borehole, the borehole in which Gaunt and colleagues placed its base, and its primary reference section in Borehole F/B5 at Fulbeck, after Brandon and colleagues (1990). The formation rests with a sharp contact on the Cotham Member, or locally the Langport Member, of the Lilstock Formation (Penarth Group), reaches 128 m, and runs from the uppermost Rhaetian to the Upper Sinemurian. Its six members (Frodingham Ironstone, Foston, Beckingham, Granby, Barnby and Barnstone) are those of Brandon and colleagues (1990), with the Frodingham Ironstone after Gaunt and colleagues (1992). Governs `stages: [Rhaetian, Hettangian, Sinemurian]`, `period: [Late Triassic, Early Jurassic]` and `variants: [Scunthorpe Mudstones]` on [Scunthorpe Mudstone](../../../stratigraphy/s/scunthorpe-mudstone.yml).
