# Sebeș

## Is the Șard Formation another name for the Sebeș?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Csiki-Sava and colleagues (2015) equate the two in their own captions, crediting a photograph to Vlad Codrea. Their bibliography lists a 2012 paper on "the Şard Formation" of the same south-western Transylvanian Basin. Governs `variants: [Șard]` on [Sebeș](../../../stratigraphy/s/sebeș.yml).

**Evidence.**
- csiki-sava2015a, p. 61, Fig. 10C: *Allodaposuchus precedens* "(Sebeş = Şard Formation, Oarda de Jos, southwestern Transylvanian Basin; photo by Vlad Codrea/Massimo Delfino)"; Fig. 10H: *Balaur bondoc* "(Sebeş = Şard Formation, Sebeş-Glod, southewestern Transylvanian Basin …)".
- csiki-sava2015a, p. 138: Mariş (2012), "The sedimentology of the Upper Cretaceous continental deposits from the south-western part of the Transylvanian Basin, the Şard Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Şard and Sebeş, with cedillas, as further variants.* The cedilla forms are the usual substitution for the comma-below letters in Romanian typesetting, not another name. The registry keeps the comma-below forms, as in its file name.

**Open.** Nothing.
