# Shawan

## Does Luo and Wu (1994) date the Shawan beds Hettangian?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Yes; the Hettangian stays. Answers the 2026-11-26 entry's Open line. Luo and Wu (1994) date the faunal assemblage of the Dull Purplish Beds of the Lufeng Basin itself, by their own correlation of its tritylodont *Bienotherium* and sphenodontian *Clevosaurus*, as "Early Jurassic in age, probably Hettangian", a reading in their own voice with a hedge of confidence. Sun and Cui (1986) and Sun and colleagues (1985) give only the formation's Early Jurassic. The Hettangian reaches the type area directly, not only by correlation with the Kunming Basin core, so [*Jingshanosaurus xinwaensis*](../../../genera/J/Jingshanosaurus.yml) and [*Xingxiulong chengi*](../../../genera/X/Xingxiulong.yml) keep it. Young (1951) and Luo and Wu (1995) are no longer needed for the stage. Governs the Hettangian in `stages` and the pointers to luo1994a, sun1985a and sun1986a on [Shawan](../../../stratigraphy/s/shawan.yml); `stages` unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- luo1994a, p. 268: "We suggest that the Bienotherium-saurischian faunal assemblage in the Dull Purplish Beds of the Lower Lufeng Formation is also Early Jurassic in age, probably Hettangian."
- sun1986a, p. 278: "Because there are no radiometric records of these sediments, the dating relies upon paleontological evidence."

**Ruled out.**
- *Dropping the Hettangian for want of a type-area reading.* Luo and Wu (1994) give one.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does any work read date the Shawan Member to the Hettangian?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Not yet; the Hettangian is held pending the originals. Barrett and colleagues (2005) give the Dull Purplish Beds as Hettangian "Traditionally", after Young (1951), Sun and colleagues (1985), Sun and Cui (1986) and Luo and Wu (1994, 1995), and the holotype horizon of *Lufengosaurus* as Hettangian after Luo and Wu; Liang and colleagues (2024) give the member's "(Hettangian)" in passing, after other work. Chen and colleagues (2026) date the base of the Lufeng in a Kunming Basin core and never name the Shawan. Huang and colleagues' (2005) own magnetostratigraphy puts part of the member "roughly" in the middle Sinemurian and gives its lowest sites no age. A reading another work reports adds a stage only once its original is read; Luo and Wu (1994) and Sun and colleagues (1985) are held and come next, and Luo and Wu (1995), Young (1951) and Sun and Cui (1986) are on the fetch list. Governs nothing yet; `stages` on [Shawan](../../../stratigraphy/s/shawan.yml) unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- barrett2005b, p. 806: "Traditionally, the Lower Lufeng Formation has been divided into two horizons: the lower Dull Purplish Beds (Hettangian) and upper Dark Red Beds (Sinemurian), which can be distinguished on the basis of lithology and faunal content (Young, 1951; Sun et al., 1985; Sun and Cui, 1986; Luo and Wu, 1994, 1995)."
- barrett2005b, p. 807: "Horizon—Dull Purplish Beds of the Lower Lufeng Formation, Lower Jurassic (Hettangian: Luo and Wu, 1994, 1995)."
- huang2005a, p. 325: "采点21~14的层位大致相对于西涅缪尔阶（Sinemurian）中部" ["the horizons of sites 21–14 roughly correspond to the middle Sinemurian", translated].

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether Luo and Wu (1994, 1995), Sun and colleagues (1985), Sun and Cui (1986) or Young (1951) date the Dull Purplish Beds to the Hettangian themselves. If none does, the Hettangian goes, and [*Jingshanosaurus xinwaensis*](../../../genera/J/Jingshanosaurus.yml) and [*Xingxiulong chengi*](../../../genera/X/Xingxiulong.yml), Hettangian only, need re-dating.

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Shawan Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Hettangian to Sinemurian. The base is Hettangian on the dated base of the Lufeng Formation in the Wande core, as the 2026-10-01 entry of the [Lufeng](../l/lufeng.md) note concludes. The Sinemurian rests on two things. Huang and colleagues (2005) correlate the lowest sampled sites of the member, roughly, with the middle Sinemurian. And Fang and colleagues (2000) give the member 528 m in the Lufeng Basin, which seems too much to have accumulated in the roughly 0.6 Myr of the Hettangian left after the base of the Lufeng; that is our inference from the thickness and the end of the Hettangian at 199.5 Ma, not a statement in any work cited. None of the works cited dates a horizon inside the member. Governs `stages: [Hettangian, Sinemurian]` on [Shawan](../../../stratigraphy/s/shawan.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- fang2000a, translation, p. 1: the authors subdivide the region's red beds "by erecting the following nomenclature"; p. 5: the formations of the earlier scheme "are hereby reduced to member status".
- fang2000a, translation, p. 6: the member is "Represented by units 1-7 with a thickness of 107.7 m, the type section is at Shawan and Dahuangtian, Lufeng, Co."; it "is unconformable with the underlying purple phyllites of the Proterozoic Etou'an (chang?) Fm. of the Kunyang Group. In the Lufeng Basin this unit is 528 m thick". The paper dates its units only as J1, J2 and J3 and names no international stage.
- huang2005a, p. 323: in the Lufeng area "the Jurassic generally overlies the Kunyang Group with angular unconformity, and the Lower Jurassic Yubacun Formation is absent" (translated); the Shawan Member is "dark purplish-red and dark purple mudstone intercalated with thin-bedded siltstone", with conglomerate at the base, 107.7 m thick, over the "pre-Sinian Etouchang Formation" (translated).
- huang2005a, p. 324, Table 2: sampling sites 1–54 are in the Shawan Member and 55–151 in the Zhangjiaao Member.
- huang2005a, p. 325: the sites "from the base of the Zhangjiaao Member through the Shawan Member, have unstable rock magnetism and disordered directions"; "The horizons of sites 21–14 roughly correspond to the middle Sinemurian" (translated); p. 328: the Lufeng Formation "belongs roughly to the upper Sinemurian through the Toarcian" (translated).
- barrett2005b, p. 806: "Traditionally, the Lower Lufeng Formation has been divided into two horizons: the lower Dull Purplish Beds (Hettangian) and upper Dark Red Beds (Sinemurian)"; "Fang et al. (2000) proposed a new nomenclature, re-naming the Lower Lufeng Formation the 'Lufeng Formation,' the Dull Purplish Beds the 'Shawan Member,' and the Dark Red Beds the 'Zhangjiawa Member'".
- hesselbo2020a, p. 1004, Table 26.4: Hettangian 201.4–199.5 Ma.

**Ruled out.**
- *Hettangian alone, after the traditional division.* Barrett and colleagues (2005) report the Dull Purplish Beds as Hettangian under the older two-horizon scheme. That assignment predates the dating of the base of the Lufeng, and Huang and colleagues (2005) correlate sites low in the member with the middle Sinemurian.
- *A Sinemurian base, after Huang and colleagues (2005).* The correlation is "roughly", from sites the paper itself calls magnetically unstable, with no numerical anchor; the 2026-10-01 entry of the Lufeng note rules out a Sinemurian base for the formation on the astrochronology of Chen and colleagues (2026).

**Open.** At the type section the member rests unconformably on Proterozoic rocks with no Yubacun Formation beneath it (Fang and colleagues 2000; Huang and colleagues 2005), while the Hettangian base comes from a core in the Kunming Basin where the Lufeng overlies the Yubacun. The base of the member in Lufeng County may therefore be younger than the base of the formation in the core. A dated horizon within the member in the Lufeng Basin would settle both its base there and where it passes into the Sinemurian.
