# Suining

## What age does the zircon date of Wang and colleagues (2019) give the Suining?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** A maximum depositional age of 114.4 ± 1.1 Ma for the middle of the formation at its classic fossil outcrops in the central Sichuan Basin, which is Aptian on the ICS chart v2024/12 (Aptian 121.4–113.2 Ma). Wang and colleagues (2019) take the youngest zircons to be close to the depositional age and correlate the formation with Lower Cretaceous (Aptian) units, against its traditional Late Jurassic age from bivalve, *Estheria*, ostracod and vertebrate correlations, which their own map still uses. A maximum is a limit, not an age, so the Aptian is not added to the formation; the conflict with the Late Jurassic reading stands as a dispute. Governs `dispute` and `period: [Late Jurassic]` on [Suining](../../../stratigraphy/s/suining.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wang2019c, p. 3, 3. Detrital Zircon U-Pb geochronology: "12 concordant analyses out of 20 total analyses yielded weighted mean ages of 114.4±1.1 Ma (MSWD=2.9)"; "we interpret this age as the crystallization age of the zircon grains, which also represents a maximum depositional age estimate for the Suining Formation".
- wang2019c, p. 2, 1. Introduction: the sample is "a tuffaceous sandstone collected from the middle of the Mamenchisaurus-bearing Suining Formation; the classic fossil outcrops of the central Sichuan Basin".
- wang2019c, p. 4, 4.1 Age of the Suining Formation: "the crystallization age of the youngest zircons should be very close to the depositional age. Our new and more precise zircon U-Pb zircon age data allow novel correlation of the Suining Formation with other Lower Cretaceous (Aptian) units".
- wang2019c, p. 2, 2. Geological background and sampling: "The Suining Formation was proposed to be Late Jurassic based on lateral correlations of the bivalve, Estheria, ostracod and vertebrate assemblages".
- wang2019c, Fig. 2 caption: "J₃s: Upper Jurassic Suining Formation"; "It is noted that we herein follow the traditional classification of these formations."
- wang2019c, p. 6, 4.3 Age of Mamenchisaurus and mamenchisaurids: "Mamenchisaurus anyuensis, is known from the top of the Suining Formation and the bottom of the Penglaizhen Formation in Anyue County of the central Sichuan Basin".
- wang2019c, p. 8, 5. Conclusions: "We interpret this age as the maximum depositional age for the Suining Formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian as the formation's age.* Wang and colleagues correlate the formation with Aptian units, but their figure comes from one sample in the middle of the formation and they themselves call it a maximum depositional age. The beds are no older than about 114 Ma; the figure does not by itself say how much younger they are, or date the rest of the formation.

**Open.** A depositional age for the formation. A date from a level other than the sampled middle bed, or an age that is not a maximum, would settle whether the Late Jurassic reading can stand.
