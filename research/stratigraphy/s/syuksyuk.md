# Syuksyuk

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Syuksyuk?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Turonian to Campanian, the union of the readings. The Stratigraphic Dictionary of the USSR (1979) defines the formation between the Dzhamsugum below and the Darbazin above and dates it "Upper Turonian—Coniacian". Nessov (1995) gives later ages wherever he dates a locality he assigns to it: Santonian at Kyrkkuduk I, the *Jaxartosaurus* locality, at the Syuk-Syuk wells and at Kazgurt in its upper part; Santonian to a queried lower Campanian for its upper part at Mansurata II; and Santonian to lower Campanian for an undescribed theropod from Syuk-Syuk. Nessov's rejection of a late Turonian age concerns the Syuk-Syuk wells bed (see the 2026-11-23 entry on the [Darbazin](../d/darbazin.md)); the dictionary's Turonian is for the formation, and none of the works cited argues against it. Governs `stages: [Turonian, Coniacian, Santonian, Campanian]` on [Syuksyuk](../../../stratigraphy/s/syuksyuk.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- vereshchagin1979a, p. 378: "Named after Syuksyuk settlement, Pritashkent district, Central Asia. G. A. Belenky, 1966"; "Stratotype at Syuksyuk settlement, 10 km east of Dzhilga railway station"; "Lies on the Dzhamsugum Formation and is overlain by the Darbazin Formation. Belongs to the Upper Turonian—Coniacian" (translated).
- nessov1995a, p. 102, locality 107: Kyrkkuduk I, "along the northern foot of the Alymtau Ridge … Syuk-Syuk Formation. Upper Cretaceous, Santonian"; locality 106: Kazgurt, "Upper part of the Syuk-Syuk Formation (Suslov 1987). Upper Cretaceous, Santonian"; p. 104: "*Jaxartosaurus aralensis* Riab. from Kyrkkuduk I" (translated).
- nessov1995a, p. 101, locality 99: the Syuk-Syuk wells, "Lower part of the Darbazin Formation (Rozhdestvensky, 1964a). Syuk-Syuk Formation (Stratigraphic Dictionary, 1979), rather than the Beleutin Formation (Rozhdestvensky, 1971: 30). Upper Cretaceous, Santonian" (translated).
- nessov1995a, p. 103, locality 118: Mansurata II, "Upper part of the Syuk-Syuk Formation (Upper Cretaceous, Santonian -- ? lower Campanian) (Belenky, 1961: 47, 49)"; and "The Beleutin Formation is not present in the Tashkent region" (translated).
- nessov1995a, p. 45: an undescribed theropod "from the Santonian - lower Campanian of Syuk-Syuk, Kazakhstan (Yu. V. Suslov, oral communication)" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *The Beleutin Formation for the Syuk-Syuk wells bed.* An earlier attribution that Nessov (1995) quotes and rejects: he places the bed in the Syuk-Syuk Formation "rather than the Beleutin Formation", and writes that the Beleutin is not present in the Tashkent region.

**Open.** Whether the Campanian stands. Its readings are Nessov's queried "Santonian -- ? lower Campanian" for the upper part at Mansurata II, given with a citation of Belenky (1961), and the theropod line, which rests on an oral communication and is not a published determination (see [methods](../../methods.md), on personal communications). Belenky (1961, 1966), who named the formation and on whose work both the dictionary and Nessov draw, has not been read.
