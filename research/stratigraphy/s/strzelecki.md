# Strzelecki

## Does the Wonthaggi take the Strzelecki Group as parent, and how old is it?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Yes; the group is Early Cretaceous, with no stages. Tosolini and colleagues (1999) define the group, with an undifferentiated upper part that others call the Wonthaggi Formation, and Herne and colleagues (2019) give the Wonthaggi in the Strzelecki Group. Tosolini and colleagues' "Berriasian to Albian" follows published spore–pollen data not read; their own palynology dates the Rintoul Creek Formation Valanginian. Governs the new entry [Strzelecki](../../../stratigraphy/s/strzelecki.yml) and `parent` on [Wonthaggi](../../../stratigraphy/w/wonthaggi.yml).
