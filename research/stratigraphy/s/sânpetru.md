# Sânpetru

## Is the Sânpetru Formation Maastrichtian, or does it start in the Campanian?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** Maastrichtian, on Panaiotu and Panaiotu's preferred magnetostratigraphic correlation read against the current base of the Maastrichtian. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]` on Sânpetru and the stages of its taxa, such as [*Bradycneme draculae*](../../../genera/B/Bradycneme.yml) and [*Ferenceratops shqiperorum*](../../../genera/F/Ferenceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- panaiotu2010a, p. 349: the lower member "started at the end of Chron C32n and continued during the reversed Chron C31r … C32n.1n is preferred".
- gale2020a, p. 1041: the base of the Maastrichtian is at "Chron C32n.2n.88 … approximately 72.2 Ma", which is older than C32n.1n, so a start in C32n.1n is Maastrichtian (our reading).

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Campanian start.* Panaiotu and Panaiotu's own conclusion, "Late Campanian–Maastrichtian", used the 2004 timescale, which placed the Campanian–Maastrichtian boundary at 70.6 Ma, inside C31r. On the current placement the preferred correlation falls entirely in the Maastrichtian.
- *Csiki-Sava and colleagues' 71.6–68.2 Ma as independent support.* They follow Panaiotu and Panaiotu (csiki-sava2016a, in-press version, p. 9).

**Open.** The Maastrichtian depends on the preferred correlation. Panaiotu and Panaiotu's alternative, a start in C32n.2n, could begin before 72.2 Ma, in the latest Campanian.
