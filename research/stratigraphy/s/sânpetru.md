# Sânpetru

## Does the variant Sînpetru stay?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Sînpetru is the Romanian spelling before the 1993 reform and the one most of the paleontological literature prints; [methods](../../methods.md) now counts a spelling an orthographic reform replaced as a variant. Governs `variants` on [Sânpetru](../../../stratigraphy/s/sânpetru.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.** None.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What do the cited works say about the age and the spelling of the Sânpetru Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Maastrichtian, unchanged, as the 2026-10-03 entry concludes, and the headword Sânpetru. Panaiotu and Panaiotu (2010) date the formation from the magnetostratigraphy of ten localities along the Sibișel Valley. Their "Late Campanian–Maastrichtian" follows from a Campanian–Maastrichtian boundary at 70.6 Ma, and they state the alternative themselves: with the boundary at 72.5 Ma the formation lies wholly in the Maastrichtian. Csiki-Sava and colleagues (2016) follow that sampling and put the stratotype at roughly 71.6 to 68.2 Ma, early to early late Maastrichtian, with the eastern-bank succession correlated to it only provisionally. The name is printed both ways: Panaiotu and Panaiotu (2010) use Sânpetru, the current Romanian spelling, while Csiki-Sava and colleagues (2016), Augustin and colleagues (2023) and Díez Díaz and colleagues (2025) use the older Sînpetru in their own text. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]` and `name: Sânpetru` on [Sânpetru](../../../stratigraphy/s/sânpetru.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- panaiotu2010a, p. 346: samples were collected "from ten localities distributed along the Sibişel valley".
- panaiotu2010a, p. 349: "Since the boundary between Campanian and Maastrichtian is at 70.6 ± 0.5 Ma ... inside Chron C31r, the proposed correlation implies the starting of the sedimentation in this part of the Sânpetru Formation during the Late Campanian, earlier than it was usually assumed"; "If it is placed at 72.5 ± 0.5 Ma ... the Sânpetru Formation developed only inside the Maastrichtian"; "the Sânpetru formation is Late Campanian–Maastrichtian in age"; "The age of the upper member of the Sânpetru Formation is still under debate".
- csiki-sava2016a, p. 670: the magnetostratigraphic sampling of Panaiotu and Panaiotu (2010) "definitively established a dominantly early to early late Maastrichtian age for the entire Sibişel Valley succession, well constrained to the time interval between C32N1 and the basalmost part of C30R"; the stratotype "represents roughly the time interval between 71.6 and 68.2 Ma, and currently can be considered the most precisely dated continuous succession of the continental uppermost Cretaceous from Transylvania"; the eastern-bank succession "remains unconstrained" and is provisionally correlated with "the lower-middle part of the left side succession, and thus early to earliest late Maastrichtian in age".
- csiki-sava2016a, p. 689: in the "upper middle to terminal part of the Sibişel Valley stratotype of the Sînpetru Formation", "(palynologically calibrated) magnetostratigraphy supports an early late Maastrichtian age".
- díezdíaz2025a, p. 5, Geological setting: "the Sînpetru Formation (Maastrichtian) in the central part of the basin; the partly volcanoclastic Densus-Ciula Formation (Maastrichtian) in its northwestern marginal areas".
- weishampel2003a, p. 68: "in this post-Ceausescu era, some spellings in the Romanian language have changed, among them Sînpetru to Sanpetru".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sînpetru as the headword.* It is the spelling before the change in Romanian orthography that Weishampel and colleagues (2003) note, and it remains in print; the house spelling follows the current form, as the 2026-08-06 entry of the [*Zalmoxes*](../../genera/Z/Zalmoxes.md) note concludes.

**Open.** The age of the upper member, which Panaiotu and Panaiotu call "still under debate", and of the eastern-bank succession, which Csiki-Sava and colleagues correlate with the stratotype on lithology alone. A magnetostratigraphic or palynological dating of either would settle whether the formation reaches above the early late Maastrichtian.

## Is the Sânpetru Formation Maastrichtian, or does it start in the Campanian?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** Maastrichtian, on Panaiotu and Panaiotu's preferred magnetostratigraphic correlation read against the current base of the Maastrichtian. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]` on Sânpetru and the stages of its taxa, such as [*Bradycneme draculae*](../../../genera/B/Bradycneme.yml) and [*Ferenceratops shqiperorum*](../../../genera/F/Ferenceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- panaiotu2010a, p. 349: the lower member "started at the end of Chron C32n and continued during the reversed Chron C31r … C32n.1n is preferred".
- gale2020a, p. 1041: the base of the Maastrichtian is at "Chron C32n.2n.88 … approximately 72.2 Ma", which is older than C32n.1n, so a start in C32n.1n is Maastrichtian (our reading).

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Campanian start.* Panaiotu and Panaiotu's own conclusion, "Late Campanian–Maastrichtian", used the 2004 timescale, which placed the Campanian–Maastrichtian boundary at 70.6 Ma, inside C31r. On the current placement the preferred correlation falls entirely in the Maastrichtian.
- *Csiki-Sava and colleagues' 71.6–68.2 Ma as independent support.* They follow Panaiotu and Panaiotu (csiki-sava2016a, p. 670).

**Open.** The Maastrichtian depends on the preferred correlation. Panaiotu and Panaiotu's alternative, a start in C32n.2n, could begin before 72.2 Ma, in the latest Campanian.
