# Santa Maria

## Should the Santa Maria Formation keep the Ladinian?

*2026-10-01*

Answers the open point of the entry below.

**Conclusion.** Yes, as an inference: the lower, *Dinodontosaurus*, part has no direct date, but its own-voice correlations give a Ladinian–early Carnian age. Governs the Ladinian in `stages: [Ladinian, Carnian]` on Santa Maria.

**Evidence.**
- martinelli2017a, p. 527: "The age of the Dinodontosaurus AZ is inferred … both suggesting a late Ladinian–early Carnian age", from the Chañares correlation and a dated level.
- pavanatto2018a, pp. 179–180: "(Pinheiros-Chiniquá Sequence, Ladinian–early Carnian)"; the zone "might have been deposited during Ladinian to early Carnian".
- novas2021a, accepted manuscript, p. 12: the zone "may also include two different faunal associations", and "the older Tarjadia AZ is inferred to extend into the Ladinian (Ezcurra et a., 2017, 2021b)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Carnian only for the lower part.* Sues and Schoch's "Carnian-age Dinodontosaurus and Santacruzodon assemblage zones" (sues2025a, p. 469) is weighed against the three own-voice Ladinian–early Carnian readings above.

**Open.** A direct date for the *Dinodontosaurus* zone.

## What is the age of the Santa Maria Formation?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** The upper part (*Hyperodapedon* Assemblage Zone) is late Carnian by U-Pb; the lower part (*Dinodontosaurus* Assemblage Zone) is traditionally Ladinian and not re-examined here. Governs `stages: [Ladinian, Carnian]` on Santa Maria, `stages: [Carnian]` on its [Alemoa Member](../../../stratigraphy/a/alemoa.yml), and `stage: [Carnian]` on [*Saturnalia tupiniquim*](../../../genera/S/Saturnalia.yml), [*Bagualosaurus agudoensis*](../../../genera/B/Bagualosaurus.yml) and [*Pampadromaeus barberenai*](../../../genera/P/Pampadromaeus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- langer2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 6: 233.23 ± 0.73 Ma "serves as the best maximum estimate for the depositional age of the bone bed near the top of the Santa Maria Formation".
- langer2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 7: the bone bed is at "Cerro da Alemoa (type-locality of Saturnalia tupiniquim)", and the date "is consistent with a late Carnian (Tuvalian) age for their associated fauna". The date "supports the correlation of the Hyperodapedon Assemblage-Zone (AZ) of the Santa Maria Formation to the Scaphonyx-Exaeretodon-Herrerasaurus biozone" of the Ischigualasto Formation.
- langer2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 7: 225.42 ± 0.37 Ma is "the maximum age of the Caturrita Formation", which is "early Norian (Lacian)". The Norian lies in the overlying unit, not in the Santa Maria.

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper Anisian or lower Ladinian for the whole formation.* Reig (1963) assigns the Santa Maria fauna "tentativamente, al Anisiano superior o al Ladiniano inferior" ("tentatively, to the upper Anisian or lower Ladinian", translated; reig1963a, p. 19), by faunal correlation. It predates radiometric dating and does not address the *Hyperodapedon* beds dated above.
- *"Probably Middle Triassic."* Cox (1965) gives "the Santa Maria Formation, of probably Middle Triassic age" (cox1965a, p. 477), with no dating of its own.
- *Early Triassic.* This reading does not come from Caster (1952), who puts "the Santa Maria episode … more nearly equivalent to the Middle Triassic hiatus and the Molteno" (caster1952a, p. 143). It comes from a reading of the wide spread his correlation chart gives the Santa Maria (fig. 15; "an enormous spread on the correlation table", p. 142), relayed in Colbert (1970).

**Open.** The age of the lower, *Dinodontosaurus*, part. Sues and Schoch (2025) call it "the Carnian-age Dinodontosaurus and Santacruzodon assemblage zones of the Santa Maria Supersequence" (sues2025a, p. 469), writing of the supersequence rather than the formation, and Langer and colleagues (2018, accepted manuscript, p. 8) say the *Dinodontosaurus* zone "is supposed to be coeval with the Chañares fauna". Whether the Ladinian should go was not examined for this note.
