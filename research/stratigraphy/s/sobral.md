# Sobral

## Are the coastal and inland Sobral the same unit?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The sources treat the deltaic sandstones of the Arruda Subbasin and the coastal outcrops between Santa Cruz and Areia Branca as one unit under different names and ranks: the Sobral Member of the Farta Pão Formation, the Sobral Formation, or Hill's Praia Azul Member of the Lourinhã Formation. Governs the references and `parent: Farta Pão` on [Sobral](../../../stratigraphy/s/sobral.yml), including the coastal papers it cites.

**Evidence.**
- schneider2009a: the member "can be traced into the Turcifal Subbasin of the Torres Vedras region, and along the Atlantic coast from Santa Cruz (where it forms part of Hill's (1989) Praia Azul member) in the south to Praia do Areia Branca west of Lourinhã in the north".
- kullberg2013a: the Sobral Formation "= Praia Azul Member of the Lourinhã Formation, sensus Hill".
- mocho2014a, p. 880: the inland *Lourinhasaurus* lectotype is from the "Sobral Formation (or Sobral Member of the Farta Pão Formation sensu Schneider et al., 2009)", the same unit that yielded the coastal *Lusotitan* and *Lourinhanosaurus* (p. 877).

**Ruled out.**
- *Two bodies of rock sharing a name.* No source read separates an inland Sobral from a coastal one; the coastal papers name the same unit at another rank.

**Open.** The unit's extent on the coast, which authors draw differently: Mateus and colleagues (2017) report a scheme that takes the lower coastal package into the Sobral as well (mateus2017a, p. 82).
