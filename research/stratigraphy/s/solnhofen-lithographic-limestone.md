# Solnhofen Lithographic Limestone

## Does Holtz's (2004) "Kimmeridgian or basal Tithonian" change the unit's stages?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** No. A paper's undecided dating between two readings contributes every stage it allows, so Holtz's reading gives the Kimmeridgian and the Tithonian, and both are already in the unit's stages from Foth and colleagues' (2025) late Kimmeridgian to early Tithonian. The record note once said the hedge was not added because it falls at a stage boundary; that is not the rule. Governs `stages: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` on [Solnhofen Lithographic Limestone](../../../stratigraphy/s/solnhofen-lithographic-limestone.yml).

**Evidence.**
- holtz2004a, p. 105: "the Kimmeridgian or basal Tithonian Solnhofen Lithographic Limestone of Jachenhausen".
- foth2025a, p. 18: "partially laminated, partially silty limestones of late Kimmeridgian to early Tithonian age, commonly referred to as the 'Solnhofen limestone'".
- foth2025a, p. 19: the Altmühltal Formation "begins with the latest Kimmeridgian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Leaving Holtz's reading out because it hedges at a boundary.* CONTRIBUTING ([Age](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#age-period-and-stages)) has an undecided dating contribute every alternative it allows.

**Open.** Nothing.
