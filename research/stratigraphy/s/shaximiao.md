# Shaximiao

## Are the Xiashaximiao and Shangshaximiao two formations, or the lower and upper parts of one?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** One formation, the Shaximiao, with its lower and upper parts recorded in `part`. Recent papers name a single Shaximiao Formation and divide it into a Lower and an Upper Member, and Moore and colleagues (2020) date "the entirety of the Shaximiao Formation" as one unit. Xiashaximiao and Shangshaximiao are the Chinese forms of "lower Shaximiao" and "upper Shaximiao" (*xia*, lower; *shang*, upper), as Xing and colleagues (2015) gloss them. The older scheme that treats them as two formations names the same rock differently. Governs `name: Shaximiao` and `rank: formation` on [Shaximiao](../../../stratigraphy/s/shaximiao.yml); `location.formation: Shaximiao` with `location.part: lower` on [*Abrosaurus dongpoi*](../../../genera/A/Abrosaurus.yml), [*Agilisaurus louderbacki*](../../../genera/A/Agilisaurus.yml), [*Bashanosaurus primitivus*](../../../genera/B/Bashanosaurus.yml), [*Chuandongocoelurus primitivus*](../../../genera/C/Chuandongocoelurus.yml), [*Dashanpusaurus dongi*](../../../genera/D/Dashanpusaurus.yml), [*Datousaurus bashanensis*](../../../genera/D/Datousaurus.yml), [*Gasosaurus constructus*](../../../genera/G/Gasosaurus.yml), [*Hexinlusaurus multidens*](../../../genera/H/Hexinlusaurus.yml), [*Huayangosaurus taibaii*](../../../genera/H/Huayangosaurus.yml), [*Kaijiangosaurus lini*](../../../genera/K/Kaijiangosaurus.yml), [*Omeisaurus tianfuensis*, *O. jiaoi* and *O. puxiani*](../../../genera/O/Omeisaurus.yml), [*Protognathosaurus oxyodon*](../../../genera/P/Protognathosaurus.yml), [*Shunosaurus lii*](../../../genera/S/Shunosaurus.yml), [*Xiaosaurus dashanpensis*](../../../genera/X/Xiaosaurus.yml), [*Xuanhanosaurus qilixiaensis*](../../../genera/X/Xuanhanosaurus.yml) and [*Yuzhoulong qurenensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yuzhoulong.yml); and with `location.part: upper` on [*Chialingosaurus kuani*](../../../genera/C/Chialingosaurus.yml), [*Chungkingosaurus jiangbeiensis*](../../../genera/C/Chungkingosaurus.yml), [*Daanosaurus zhangi*](../../../genera/D/Daanosaurus.yml), [*Gigantspinosaurus sichuanensis*](../../../genera/G/Gigantspinosaurus.yml), [*Gongbusaurus shiyii*](../../../genera/G/Gongbusaurus.yml), [*Leshansaurus qianweiensis*](../../../genera/L/Leshansaurus.yml), [*Mamenchisaurus constructus*, *M. hochuanensis*, *M. youngi* and *M. sanjiangensis*](../../../genera/M/Mamenchisaurus.yml), [*Omeisaurus junghsiensis*](../../../genera/O/Omeisaurus.yml), [*Tuojiangosaurus multispinus*](../../../genera/T/Tuojiangosaurus.yml), [*Yandusaurus hongheensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yandusaurus.yml), [*Yangchuanosaurus shangyouensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yangchuanosaurus.yml), [*Yingshanosaurus jichuanensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yingshanosaurus.yml) and [*Zigongosaurus fuxiensis*](../../../genera/Z/Zigongosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hui2021a, p. 2, Introduction: "The Shaximiao Formation is divided into Lower Member and Upper Members"; Locality and Horizon: "Lower Member of the Shaximiao Formation, Middle Jurassic (Bajocian)".
- moore2020a, Introduction: "radiometric dating of detrital zircons from beds of the lower Shaximiao Formation at Dashanpu now places the entire Shaximiao Formation … within the Late Jurassic"; Implications for sauropod palaeobiogeography: "placing the entirety of the Shaximiao Formation within the Late Jurassic".
- xing2015a, p. e889701-3: the Jurassic of the Sichuan Basin as the "Zhenzhuchong, Ziliujing, Xintiangou, Xiashaximiao (lower Shaximiao), Shangshaximiao (upper Shaximiao), Suining, and Penglaizhen formations (Peng et al., 2005)".
- dai2022a, 2.4 Locality and horizon: the *Yuzhoulong* remains "were found in purplish-red silty mudstones located in the middle portion of the Lower Shaximiao Formation".
- peng1992a, Locality and horizon: "Dashanpu, Zigong, Sichuan; Lower Shaximiao Formation, Middle Jurassic".

**Ruled out.**
- *Xiashaximiao and Shangshaximiao as separate formations.* Jones (2026) follows the scheme that Xing and colleagues (2015) take from Peng and colleagues (2005). It gives "Xiashaximiao Fm." for *Dashanpusaurus* and *Datousaurus* (jones2026b, p. 379), *Gasosaurus* (jones2026c, p. 498), *Yuzhoulong* (jones2026b, Fossil Specimens table) and *Xuanhanosaurus* (jones2026c, Fossil Specimens table). It gives "Shangshaximiao Fm." for *Daanosaurus* and *M. constructus* (jones2026b, p. 376), *Gigantspinosaurus*, *Chialingosaurus* and *Chungkingosaurus* (jones2026a, p. 402), *Tuojiangosaurus* and *Yingshanosaurus* (jones2026a, Fossil Specimens table), and *Yangchuanosaurus* (jones2026c, Fossil Specimens table). Xing's own gloss makes the two names the lower and upper Shaximiao, and the papers above treat those as parts of one formation.
- *"Lower Xiashaximiao" as a unit.* Jones (2026) gives *Agilisaurus*, *Xiaosaurus*, *Yandusaurus* and *Hexinlusaurus* from the "Lower Xiashaximiao Fm." (jones2026a, p. 397 and Ornithischia table). The phrase repeats "lower", and names no unit beneath the lower Shaximiao.
- *"Upper Shaximiao Formation" or "Lower Shaximiao Formation" as formation names.* *The Dinosauria* writes "Upper Shaximiao Formation" for *Chialingosaurus*, *Chungkingosaurus* and *M. constructus* (weishampel2004a, p. 344, Table 16.1; upchurch2004a, p. 262, Table 13.1), and "Lower Shaximiao Formation" for *Datousaurus* and *Gasosaurus* (upchurch2004a, p. 261, Table 13.1; holtz2004a, p. 77, Table 4.1). These are the parts of the one formation written with capitals, as Dai and colleagues (2022) write "The Lower Shaximiao Formation is widely exposed in the Sichuan Basin" (dai2022a, Introduction).

**Open.** Whether "Lower Member" and "Upper Member" are formal names. The papers read here capitalize them, but none gives a type section or a definition. Until one does, they stay as `part: lower` and `part: upper` under the rule that a member takes a formal name and a position goes in `part` (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-member-takes-a-formal-name-a-position-goes-in-part)).
