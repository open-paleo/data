# Obernkirchen

## Is the Oberer Hauptsandstein the Obernkirchen Sandstein, and where does it sit?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Hornung and colleagues (2012) use "Obernkirchen Sandstone" only as a synonym of the Oberer Hauptsandstein, the upper of two sandstone tongues at the distal edge of the eastern sediment wedge, and place it as a thin interval within the Obernkirchen Member, the lower member of the Bückeberg Formation. They date it late Berriasian. Governs `variants: [Obernkirchen Sandstone, Oberer Hauptsandstein]`, `stages: [Berriasian]` and the bed's place in the Obernkirchen Member on the Obernkirchen Sandstein of [Obernkirchen](../../../stratigraphy/o/obernkirchen.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hornung2012a, p. 234: "The Obernkirchen Sandstone forms a thin interval within the Obernkirchen Member, the lower member of the Bückeberg Formation".
- hornung2012a, p. 236: the wedge's "distalmost outreaches consist of two sandstone tongues, a lower one (Unterer Hauptsandstein, UHS …) and an upper one (Oberer Hauptsandstein, OHS …), separated by 10–25 m of fine-grained sediments"; "The OHS is equivalent to the Obernkirchen Sandstone sensu stricto and the latter term is used herein exclusively as a synonym of the OHS."
- hornung2012a, p. 260, Conclusions: "13 dinosaur tracksites have been identified in the Obernkirchen Sandstone (Obernkirchen Member, Bückeberg Formation, Late Berriasian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Unterer Hauptsandstein as part of the Obernkirchen Sandstein.* Hornung and colleagues separate the lower tongue from the upper by 10–25 m of fine-grained sediments and restrict the name to the upper one, so the Unterer Hauptsandstein is neither the unit nor a variant of its name.

**Open.** Nothing.
