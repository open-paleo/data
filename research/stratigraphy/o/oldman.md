# Oldman

## What do the cited works say about the age and contacts of the Oldman?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian. Eberth (2005) gives the Oldman as the middle of the three formations of the Belly River Group at Dinosaur Provincial Park, 40 m thick, the light-colored beds that Dowling (1916, 1917) called the "Pale Beds". It lies conformably on the Herronton Sandstone Zone at the top of the Foremost and is cut at the top by a regional disconformity, and it records the seaway's maximum regression during the Campanian. Governs `stages: [Campanian]` and `period: [Late Cretaceous]` on [Oldman](../../../stratigraphy/o/oldman.yml).

**Evidence.**
- eberth2005a, p. 59: the Belly River Group at the Park "consists of the Foremost (170 m), Oldman (40 m), and Dinosaur Park (70 m) formations"; the Oldman is "made up of light-colored alluvial sandstones and mudstones that were first referred to as the 'Pale Beds' by Dowling (1916, 1917)"; it "conformably overlies the Herronton Sandstone Zone at the top of the Foremost but is truncated at the top by a regional disconformity".
- eberth2005a, p. 60: "The Oldman Formation records the maximum regression of the Western Interior Seaway during the Campanian".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
