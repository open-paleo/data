# Xinlong

## Does the Xinlong keep the Albian?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** No; the Albian is dropped. Sethapanichsakul and colleagues (2026), the only source of it, do not date the formation: they name its age in passing, as "Aptian" on their second page and as "Aptian–Albian" in a table row and in a sentence on the likeness of the Khok Kruat fauna, which cites Manitkoon and colleagues (2022) and Cuny and colleagues (2017) for the likeness. A passing label is not a reading, and the paper contradicts it itself. Mo and colleagues (2016) give the Aptian alone. Governs `stages: [Aptian]` and the note on sethapanichsakul2026a on [Xinlong](../../../stratigraphy/x/xinlong.yml); its four genera, all Aptian, are unaffected.

**Evidence.**
- sethapanichsakul2026a, p. 2: "The vertebrate fauna of the Khok Kruat Formation shows similarities to that of the Aptian Xinlong Formation of China".
- sethapanichsakul2026a, p. 19, Table 5: "Fusuisaurus (Mo et al. 2020; LCL 63) | Xinlong Formation Aptian–Albian".
- sethapanichsakul2026a, p. 20: "The vertebrate assemblage from the Khok Kruat Formation has been suggested to be similar to that of the Aptian–Albian Xinlong Formation that outcrops in Guangxi, southern China".

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian from Sethapanichsakul and colleagues (2026).* A passing label, contradicted on the paper's second page.

**Open.** Unchanged: a direct dating of the Xinlong.

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Xinlong?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Mo and colleagues (2016) propose an Aptian age for the Xinlong Formation as a whole by faunal correlation: its sharks, turtles and ornithopods match those of the Khok Kruat Formation of Thailand, which they take as Aptian on earlier biostratigraphic work rather than dating it themselves. They give the Xinlong no Albian. Ji and Zhang (2022) adopt their Aptian and apply it to the fossil-bearing Member 2. Governs the Aptian in `stages` and `lithology` on [Xinlong](../../../stratigraphy/x/xinlong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mo2016a, p. 143: the assemblage "seems to be particularly close to that from the Khok Kruat Formation, considered as Aptian in age, as shown especially by sharks and turtles and by the presence of iguanodontians. An Aptian age is therefore proposed for the Xinlong Formation."
- mo2016a, p. 155: the Khok Kruat Formation is "referred to the Aptian on the basis of various biostratigraphical markers"; the correlation "leads to the conclusion that the Xinlong Formation is, in all likelihood, Aptian in age".
- mo2016a, p. 145: three shark taxa "were previously restricted to the Khok Kruat Formation (Aptian–?Albian) of Thailand".
- mo2016a, p. 144: the formation "consists of purple-red, yellow-grey siltstones, fine sandstones, some mudstones and sandy conglomerates".
- ji2022a, p. 2: "the geological age of the fossil-bearing Member 2 of the Xinlong Formation should be the Aptian" (translated), after Mo and colleagues (2016).

**Ruled out.**
- *An Albian age from Mo and colleagues (2016).* The paper's only Albian is the Khok Kruat's "Aptian–?Albian" in the shark section, which dates the Thai unit, not the Xinlong; for the Xinlong they give the Aptian alone.
- *A dating of Member 2 by Mo and colleagues (2016).* They date the formation and do not divide it into members; the Member 2 framing is Ji and Zhang's (2022), drawn from regional geological maps.

**Open.** The Xinlong's Aptian is no firmer than the Khok Kruat's, which Mo and colleagues (2016) relay from other work. A direct dating of the Xinlong would settle it.
