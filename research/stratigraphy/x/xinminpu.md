## What do Zanno (2010) and Chen (1983) add on the age and subdivision of the Xinminpu?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Neither changes the group's age. Zanno (2010) relays both of its datings from Tang and colleagues (2001), so its Aptian–Albian for the Yujingzi Basin counts once, as Tang's, and does not remove the late Barremian that Tang and colleagues give the lower part. Chen (1983) gives the group as Lower Cretaceous in the Yumen district and divides it into a lower and an upper formation, over the Upper Jurassic Chijinpu Formation across a disconformity or unconformity, though with a molluscan fauna close to the Chijinpu's. Chen prints the lower formation as "Xiangou"; that it is the [Xiagou](../../../stratigraphy/x/xiagou.yml), the lower unit of the group, is an inference from its position and spelling, not a statement in the paper. Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous]` on [Xinminpu](../../../stratigraphy/x/xinminpu.yml), and `parent: Xinminpu` on [Xiagou](../../../stratigraphy/x/xiagou.yml) and [Zhonggou](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhonggou.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zanno2010a, p. 519, *Suzhousaurus megatherioides*, Occurrence: "Yujingzi Basin, north-western Gansu Province, China. Xinminpu (= Xinminbao) Group, Early Cretaceous (Aptian-Albian, Tang et al. 2001)".
- zanno2010a, p. 524, 'Nanshiungosaurus' bohlini, Occurrence: "Gongpoquan Basin, Mazongshan Area, Gansu Province, uppermost Xinminbao Group (late Early or early Late Cretaceous, Tang et al. 2001)".
- tang2001b, p. 125: "the abundance of angiosperm pollen is far below the level of 10% of the total palynoflora that is typical of the Late Cretaceous angiosperm assemblages ..., thus precluding a Late Cretaceous age for the Xinminbao Group".
- chen1983a, p. 134: "In the Yumen district, the Lower Cretaceous Xinminpu Group is subdivided into the Xiangou Formation (below) and the Zhonggou Formation (above) composed of purple, greyish-green or yellowish-brown conglomerates, sandstones, siltstones, mudstones and shales", with conchostracans, ostracods, pelecypods, gastropods, an insect (*Coptoclava longipoda*) and a fish (*Sunolepis*).
- chen1983a, p. 134: "The relation of the Lower Cretaceous Xinminpu Group to the Upper Jurassic Chijinpu Formation is disconformable or unconformable in this district, but the aspect of their molluscan fossils is very close".

**Ruled out.**
- *An Aptian floor from Zanno (2010).* Its "Aptian-Albian" cites Tang and colleagues (2001), whose range for the lower part reaches the late Barremian; a relayed statement adds nothing to the reading it relays and cannot narrow it.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does the Xinminpu Group reach the Late Cretaceous?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** No. The source of its age excludes the Late Cretaceous; the group is Barremian–Albian. Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous]` and `stages: [Barremian, Aptian, Albian]` on Xinminpu.

**Evidence.**
- tang2001b, p. 125: the evidence is "precluding a Late Cretaceous age for the Xinminbao Group"; p. 115: the lower part is "Late Barremian–Aptian in age, although the middle and upper parts could be as young as Albian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Early or early Late Cretaceous.* Zanno (2010) gives "late Early or early Late Cretaceous, Tang et al. 2001" (zanno2010a, p. 524), citing the paper that rules the Late Cretaceous out.

**Open.** Nothing.
