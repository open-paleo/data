# Xinminpu

## Does the Xinminpu Group reach the Late Cretaceous?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** No. The source of its age excludes the Late Cretaceous; the group is Barremian–Albian. Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous]` and `stages: [Barremian, Aptian, Albian]` on Xinminpu.

**Evidence.**
- tang2001b, p. 125: the evidence is "precluding a Late Cretaceous age for the Xinminbao Group"; p. 115: the lower part is "Late Barremian–Aptian in age, although the middle and upper parts could be as young as Albian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Early or early Late Cretaceous.* Zanno (2010) gives "late Early or early Late Cretaceous, Tang et al. 2001" (zanno2010a, p. 524), citing the paper that rules the Late Cretaceous out.

**Open.** Nothing.
