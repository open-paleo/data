# Xiagou

## Is Chen's "Xiangou" a variant of the Xiagou?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No; it is a misprint. Chen (1983) prints "Xiangou Formation" once, beside the Zhonggou Formation above it, for the lower Xinminpu Group. Governs the absent `variants` on [Xiagou](../../../stratigraphy/x/xiagou.yml).

**Evidence.**
- chen1983a, p. 134: "the Lower Cretaceous Xinminpu Group is subdivided into the Xiangou Formation (below) and the Zhonggou Formation (above)"

**Ruled out.** - *Xiangou.* A misprint.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What do Zheng and colleagues (2021) give for the age of the Xiagou?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** Late early Aptian to early Albian, a determination for the formation itself. Answers the 2026-11-23 entry's Open line. Zheng and colleagues (2021) correlate the Xiagou with the upper Jiufotang and Shahai formations and support the span with its ostracods, its bivalves, OAE1b in the uppermost Xiagou of the Yujinzi Basin, and their own 112.4 Ma basalt at the base of the overlying Zhonggou, which they treat as a maximum age for that bed. The Albian now rests on this paper rather than on relays. Their charophytes' "late Barremian to early Aptian" is given for the Chijinpu and Xiagou together. Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` on [Xiagou](../../../stratigraphy/x/xiagou.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- zheng2021c, p. 16: "the Xiagou Formation correlates with the upper Jiufotang and Shahai formations with a late early Aptian-early Albian age".
- zheng2021c, p. 11: "The above bivalve assemblage from the Xiagou Formation providea middle Aptian-early Albian age."
- zheng2021c, p. 2: "In the Yujinzi Basin, a pronounced positive carbon isotopic excursion in the Xinminpu Group (uppermost Xiagou Formation and lower Zhonggou Formation) is interpreted to represent OAE1b (Suarez et al., 2018)."
- zheng2021c, p. 15: "These data indicate a late Barremian to early Aptian age for the Chijinpu and Xiagou formations."

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian, from the charophytes.* The age is given to two formations together, which dates neither.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Do the early Aptian dates from Changma fix the age of the whole Xiagou Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** No. Suarez and colleagues (2013) date the lacustrine section at the Changma bird quarries, not the formation: their correlation places "the Xiagou lacustrine strata in this locality" in the early Aptian, and their discussion speaks of "the age of the bird quarries" (see the 2026-09-30 entry on the bird quarries). Zhou and colleagues (2026) give the formation itself as "Lower Cretaceous (lower Aptian)" and follow Suarez and colleagues for the quarries. They also report arguments that the formation is "late early Aptian to early Albian", from Zheng and colleagues (2021) and personal communications, but judge that the early Aptian dates cast doubt on the hypothesis that the Changma fauna is younger than the other Jehol bird localities. Neither paper narrows the formation's range to the quarry level. Governs `stages` on [Xiagou](../../../stratigraphy/x/xiagou.yml).

**Evidence.**
- suarez2013a, p. 143, abstract: "This correlation places the Xiagou lacustrine strata in this locality within the early Aptian Stage, specifically, the Selli Equivalent, which is associated with Ocean Anoxic Event 1a."
- suarez2013a, p. 145: "The similarity of taxa found in the Xiagou Formation and the underlying Chijinpu Formation with those found in the Jehol Biota of Liaoning Province suggests a Barremian to Aptian age".
- zhou2026a, p. 89, abstract: "Lacustrine sediments of the Lower Cretaceous (lower Aptian) Xiagou Formation exposed near the village of Changma".
- zhou2026a, p. 105: "chemostratigraphic evidence now suggests that the bird-bearing quarries in the Xiagou Formation of the Changma Basin were deposited during the early Aptian, approximately 124–120 Ma (Suarez et al. 2013)".
- zhou2026a, p. 105: "recent arguments that the Xiagou Formation is actually late early Aptian to early Albian in age (Zheng et al. 2021; M. Suarez, C. Suarez, pers. comms. to M.C.L.), this remains a possibility. Nevertheless, the aforementioned early Aptian chemostratigraphic dates for this latter formation (Suarez et al. 2013) cast doubt on this hypothesis".

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Aptian as the age of the whole formation.* The chemostratigraphy dates a section at one locality, as Suarez and colleagues (2013) word it themselves. Zhou and colleagues' (2026) "lower Aptian" for the formation carries that date to the unit, and Suarez and colleagues (2018) place the uppermost Xiagou of the Yujingzi Basin in the upper Aptian (see the 2026-09-30 entry on the Yujingzi gray beds).
- *Barremian–Aptian, from similarity to the Jehol Biota.* Suarez and colleagues (2013) report this third earlier reading, a faunal comparison drawn from earlier work, alongside the Hauterivian from pollen and the Barremian from ostracodes and charophytes. Their chemostratigraphy supersedes it for the quarry level, as it does the other two (our characterization, as in the 2026-09-30 entry on the bird quarries).

**Open.** Zhou and colleagues (2026) relay the late early Aptian to early Albian reading without giving its basis. Zheng and colleagues (2021), not read, are the published source for it and would show whether it is a determination for the formation.

## How old are the Xiagou bird quarries at Changma?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Early Aptian, for the bird quarries specifically, not for the whole formation. Governs `stage: [Aptian]` on Changma taxa such as [*Jian changmaensis*](../../../genera/J/Jian.yml), and leaves Xiagou's formation range at Aptian–Albian.

**Evidence.**
- suarez2013a, p. 153: "Assignment of the Xiagou Formation to the C3 to C7 segments firmly places the age of the bird quarries in the early Aptian Age", from carbon-isotope chemostratigraphy tied to OAE 1a.

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian, Hauterivian.* Suarez and colleagues report these from earlier work: "ostracode and charophyte biostratigraphy … suggests a Barremian Age, while pollen suggests a Hauterivian Age" (suarez2013a, p. 145, after You and colleagues 2006). The chemostratigraphy supersedes them for the quarry level (our characterization).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Are the Yujingzi Basin gray beds Xiagou or Zhonggou Formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The gray mudstone facies, which holds most of the Yujingzi Basin dinosaurs, is the uppermost Xiagou Formation, upper Aptian; the red beds above are the lower [Zhonggou Formation](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhonggou.yml), lower Albian. Governs `location.formation: Xiagou` and `stage: [Aptian]` on Yujingzi gray-bed taxa, such as [*Xuwulong yueluni*](../../../genera/X/Xuwulong.yml), [*Jintasaurus meniscus*](../../../genera/J/Jintasaurus.yml), [*Xiongguanlong baimoensis*](../../../genera/X/Xiongguanlong.yml), [*Beishanlong grandis*](../../../genera/B/Beishanlong.yml), [*Qiaowanlong kangxii*](../../../genera/Q/Qiaowanlong.yml) and *Archaeoceratops yujingziensis* ([Archaeoceratops](../../../genera/A/Archaeoceratops.yml)).

**Evidence.**
- suarez2018a, p. 12, abstract: "the middle gray mudstone facies, which contains a diverse fauna including theropods, sauropods, and ornithopods … is placed within the upper Aptian. The upper red sandstone facies, which contains therizinosaurs and … *Auroraceratops*, is placed in the lower Albian"; p. 20: the section is "interpreted to represent the uppermost Xiagou Formation and the lower Zhonggou Formation". Pairing gray = uppermost Xiagou joins the abstract and the conclusions (a small inference).
- you2018a, p. 6: "our working hypothesis is that the gray-variegated beds in the Yujingzi Basin belong to the upper portion of the Xiagou Formation and have an Aptian age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Zhonggou Formation for the gray-bed taxa.* You and colleagues (2014) assigned a Yujingzi taxon to the Zhonggou "(Liu, Y.-Q., pers. comm.)" (you2014a, p. 75); earlier descriptions give only "Xinminpu Group, late Early Cretaceous (Aptian-Albian)" (you2011a, p. 52). Suarez and colleagues (2018, p. 18) report that older view before assigning the "lower unit" fauna to the Xiagou.

**Open.** Taxa from the red beds and from the Gongpoquan Basin are excluded: You and colleagues (2018, p. 6) put "the red beds in both the Yujingzi and Gongpoquan basins" at the beginning of the Zhonggou, and "the gray beds in the Gongpoquan Basin" at "probably early–mid-Albian". *Auroraceratops* is mostly from the Yujingzi red beds (suarez2018a, p. 14), so it is lower Zhonggou. *Suzhousaurus* spans both: "the holotype from the gray-variegated beds and a second specimen from the red beds" (you2018a, p. 8).
