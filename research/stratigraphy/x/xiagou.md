# Xiagou

## How old are the Xiagou bird quarries at Changma?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Early Aptian, for the bird quarries specifically, not for the whole formation. Governs `stage: [Aptian]` on Changma taxa such as [*Jian changmaensis*](../../../genera/J/Jian.yml), and leaves Xiagou's formation range at Aptian–Albian.

**Evidence.**
- suarez2013a, p. 153: "Assignment of the Xiagou Formation to the C3 to C7 segments firmly places the age of the bird quarries in the early Aptian Age", from carbon-isotope chemostratigraphy tied to OAE 1a.

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian, Hauterivian.* Suarez and colleagues report these from earlier work: "ostracode and charophyte biostratigraphy … suggests a Barremian Age, while pollen suggests a Hauterivian Age" (suarez2013a, p. 145, after You and colleagues 2006). The chemostratigraphy supersedes them for the quarry level (our characterization).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Are the Yujingzi Basin gray beds Xiagou or Zhonggou Formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The gray mudstone facies, which holds most of the Yujingzi Basin dinosaurs, is the uppermost Xiagou Formation, upper Aptian; the red beds above are the lower [Zhonggou Formation](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhonggou.yml), lower Albian. Governs `location.formation: Xiagou` and `stage: [Aptian]` on Yujingzi gray-bed taxa, such as [*Xuwulong yueluni*](../../../genera/X/Xuwulong.yml), [*Jintasaurus meniscus*](../../../genera/J/Jintasaurus.yml), [*Xiongguanlong baimoensis*](../../../genera/X/Xiongguanlong.yml), [*Beishanlong grandis*](../../../genera/B/Beishanlong.yml), [*Qiaowanlong kangxii*](../../../genera/Q/Qiaowanlong.yml) and *Archaeoceratops yujingziensis* ([Archaeoceratops](../../../genera/A/Archaeoceratops.yml)).

**Evidence.**
- suarez2018a, p. 12, abstract: "the middle gray mudstone facies, which contains a diverse fauna including theropods, sauropods, and ornithopods … is placed within the upper Aptian. The upper red sandstone facies, which contains therizinosaurs and … *Auroraceratops*, is placed in the lower Albian"; p. 20: the section is "interpreted to represent the uppermost Xiagou Formation and the lower Zhonggou Formation". Pairing gray = uppermost Xiagou joins the abstract and the conclusions (a small inference).
- you2018a, p. 6: "our working hypothesis is that the gray-variegated beds in the Yujingzi Basin belong to the upper portion of the Xiagou Formation and have an Aptian age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Zhonggou Formation for the gray-bed taxa.* You and colleagues (2014) assigned a Yujingzi taxon to the Zhonggou "(Liu, Y.-Q., pers. comm.)" (you2014a, p. 75); earlier descriptions give only "Xinminpu Group, late Early Cretaceous (Aptian-Albian)" (you2011a, p. 52). Suarez and colleagues (2018, p. 18) report that older view before assigning the "lower unit" fauna to the Xiagou.

**Open.** Taxa from the red beds and from the Gongpoquan Basin are excluded: You and colleagues (2018, p. 6) put "the red beds in both the Yujingzi and Gongpoquan basins" at the beginning of the Zhonggou, and "the gray beds in the Gongpoquan Basin" at "probably early–mid-Albian". *Auroraceratops* is mostly from the Yujingzi red beds (suarez2018a, p. 14), so it is lower Zhonggou. *Suzhousaurus* spans both: "the holotype from the gray-variegated beds and a second specimen from the red beds" (you2018a, p. 8).
