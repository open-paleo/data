# Xintiangou

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Xintiangou Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Toarcian to Bajocian. Li and colleagues (2021) state the formation's age as Aalenian–Toarcian from preliminary U-Pb dating of 180–170 Ma, and relay an early Middle Jurassic age on sporopollen and *Euestheria* from Wang and colleagues (2010). Li and colleagues (2011) give Aalenian–Bajocian, by placing the *Shunosaurus*–*Omeisaurus* assemblage above in the Bajocian–Bathonian. The union is Toarcian–Bajocian, and the Bajocian rests on Li and colleagues (2011). Governs `stages: [Toarcian, Aalenian, Bajocian]` and `period: [Early Jurassic, Middle Jurassic]` on [Xintiangou](../../../stratigraphy/x/xintiangou.yml).

**Evidence.**
- li2021a, p. 1089 (online-first version, p. 1): "According to Classopollis-Cyathidites-Callialasporites sporopollen assemblage and Euestheria characteristics, the Xintiangou Formation is regarded as the early Middle Jurassic (Wang et al. 2010)"; "our preliminary U-Pb dating suggests the Xintiangou Formation ranges between 180 and 170 Ma, corresponding to the Aalenian-Toarcian age (Middle Jurassic to Early Jurassic)".
- li2021a, p. 1089 (online-first version, p. 1): "conformably contacted with the underling Ziliujing Formation and overlying Lower Shaximiao Formation".
- li2011a, p. 28: "leaving an Aalenian–Bajocian time for the underlying, Middle Jurassic Xintiangou Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Bajocian from the 170 Ma end of Li and colleagues' (2021) dating.* On the ICS chart v2024/12 the base of the Bajocian is 170.9 Ma, so 170 Ma falls in the Bajocian, but the paper states the span as Aalenian–Toarcian, and a stated stage is carried over rather than re-placed from its number ([methods](../../methods.md)).

**Open.** The radiometric range. Li and colleagues (2021) call their U-Pb dating preliminary and say that "More U-Pb dating work is being done"; a published dating with its samples and data would settle the span.
