# Eumeralla

## What does Wagstaff and McEwen Mason's (1989) dating contribute to the age of the Eumeralla Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** An Aptian and Albian age for its vertebrate localities, not a span for the formation. Wagstaff and McEwen Mason (1989) never name the Eumeralla: they date localities by group, placing those in the Otway Group, among them Dinosaur Cove and Eric the Red, in the *Crybelosporites striatus* Subzone, of late Aptian–early Albian age. Agnolín and colleagues (2010) carry that age over to the Dinosaur Cove localities of the Eumeralla Formation. The paper supports neither the Hauterivian nor the Barremian for this unit, and its late Aptian–early Albian is narrower than the formation's span and concerns localities rather than the unit; the Hauterivian and Barremian come from Bell and colleagues (2018). The authors make the age depend on the isochronicity of spore–pollen zones across eastern Australia, since the zones are tied to marine faunas only in basins to the north. Governs the Aptian and Albian in `stages: [Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian, Albian]` on [Eumeralla](../../../stratigraphy/e/eumeralla.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wagstaff1989a, p. 54, Abstract: "the localities in Otway Group sediments are of late Aptian–early Albian age (Crybelosporites striatus Subzone of Dettmann & Playford 1969) and the localities in the Strzelecki Group of Valanginian–Aptian age"; "This age determination is dependent on the isochronicity of spore–pollen zones across eastern Australia."
- wagstaff1989a, p. 60, Age of the Otway Group Localities: "The Otway Group localities at Dinosaur Cove, Eric the Red, Point Franklin, Point Lewis, and Marengo fall within the *Cr. striatus* Subzone"; "late Aptian–early Albian".
- wagstaff1989a, p. 60, Fig. 5: "the Cr. striatus Zone of latest Aptian–early Albian age".
- wagstaff1989a, p. 61, Summary: "The vertebrate localities in the Otway Group sediments that could be precisely dated are of late Aptian–early Albian age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Wagstaff and McEwen Mason (1989) as a source of the Hauterivian or Barremian.* Their older range, Valanginian–Aptian, is for the localities of the Strzelecki Group, not the Otway Group.
- *Their late Aptian–early Albian as the age of the whole formation.* It dates the sampled vertebrate localities, and the paper does not name the formation.

**Open.** The Hauterivian and Barremian rest on Bell and colleagues' (2018) "late Hauterivian–Albian", given in passing without a source; a work on the stratigraphy of the Otway Group would settle the formation's lower limit. Herne and colleagues (2018) put the base of the *Crybelosporites striatus* zone at the Aptian–Albian boundary (see [*Diluvicursor*](../../genera/D/Diluvicursor.md)), against Wagstaff and McEwen Mason's late or latest Aptian; whether the Dinosaur Cove localities reach the Aptian depends on which zonation holds.
