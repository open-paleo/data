# Eumeralla

## Does the Eumeralla keep the Hauterivian?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No; the formation runs from the Barremian to the Albian. Supersedes the 2026-11-25 entries on the stages. Felton (1997) dates the formation's four informal units from well palynology, Eumeralla I "?Late Barremian–Albian" and Eumeralla II from the late Aptian; the query qualifies her own reading. The Hauterivian rests only on Bell and colleagues' (2018) "late Hauterivian–Albian", which cites nothing and which their own Fig. 1C does not draw, the base sitting in the uppermost Barremian. Governs `stages`, Barremian to Albian, and the note on bell2018a on [Eumeralla](../../../stratigraphy/e/eumeralla.yml); its genera, Barremian to Albian, are unaffected.

**Evidence.**
- felton1997a, p. 719, Table 2: Eumeralla I "?Late Barremian–Albian"; Eumeralla II "Late Aptian–early to mid-Albian".
- bell2018a, p. 6: the "Eumeralla Formation (late Hauterivian–Albian) in Victoria".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hauterivian.* Unsourced and contradicted by the same paper's chart.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which zonation governs the Eumeralla's Aptian?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** Neither needs to: the formation keeps the Aptian under both. Answers the zonation question in the 2026-11-23 entry's Open line. Herne and colleagues (2018) put the base of Helby and colleagues' (1987) *Crybelosporites striatus* zone at the Aptian–Albian boundary, yet give the formation itself as "late Aptian–early Albian"; Wagstaff and McEwen Mason (1989) date the *Cr. striatus* Subzone of their Otway Group localities late Aptian–early Albian, after Burger (1980) and Dettmann (1986); and Felton (1997) gives the formation "?Late Barremian–Albian". The two zonations differ only on whether the formation's vertebrate localities reach the Aptian, which concerns the records from those localities, not the formation's span. Governs `stages` on [Eumeralla](../../../stratigraphy/e/eumeralla.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- herne2018a, p. 7: "Fossil localities of the Eumeralla Formation fall within the Crybelosporites striatus spore–pollen zone of Helby, Morgan & Partridge (1987), the base of which is at the Aptian–Albian boundary (113 Ma, following the time-scale of Gradstein, Ogg & Schmitz, 2012)".
- herne2018a, p. 63: "dominant forest tree types in the Eumeralla Formation during the late Aptian–early Albian".
- wagstaff1989a, p. 59: "Correlations with these basins has also been used to date the younger Cr. striatus Subzone as late Aptian–early Albian (Burger 1980, Dettmann 1986)."
- wagstaff1989a, p. 61: "The vertebrate localities in the Otway Group sediments that could be precisely dated are of late Aptian–early Albian age, as all fall within the Cr. striatus Subzone of the D. speciosus Zone."

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether the Dinosaur Cove localities reach the Aptian still depends on which zonation holds; *Leaellynasaura amicagraphica* carries both readings. *Timimus hermani*, from Dinosaur Cove East, takes only the Albian; that record has not been checked against Wagstaff and McEwen Mason's late Aptian–early Albian for Dinosaur Cove.

## What does Felton's (1997) lithostratigraphy give for the age of the Eumeralla?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** Late Barremian, queried, to Albian, queried into the Cenomanian at the top. Answers the 2026-11-23 entry's Open line on the lower limit only in part. Felton (1997) divides the formation into four informal units dated by well palynology from earlier zonal work, from "?Late Barremian–Albian" for Eumeralla I to "Albian (–?Cenomanian)" for Eumeralla IV, and in her discussion has the drainage system persist "to the end of the Albian". The Barremian to Albian now rests on the formation's own lithostratigraphy; the Hauterivian stays on Bell and colleagues (2018) under the union rule. Governs `stages`, Hauterivian to Albian, on [Eumeralla](../../../stratigraphy/e/eumeralla.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- felton1997a, Table 2, p. 719: Eumeralla I, "?Late Barremian–Albian; ?upper F. wonthaggiensis–C. striatus"; Eumeralla IV, "Albian (–?Cenomanian); upper C. paradoxa–P. pannosus".
- felton1997a, p. 727: the drainage system "persisted to the end of the Albian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian.* Felton queries it for the top unit only, and her own text ends the system at the end of the Albian.

**Open.** The Hauterivian still rests on Bell and colleagues' (2018) passing "late Hauterivian–Albian".

## What does Wagstaff and McEwen Mason's (1989) dating contribute to the age of the Eumeralla Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** An Aptian and Albian age for its vertebrate localities, not a span for the formation. Wagstaff and McEwen Mason (1989) never name the Eumeralla: they date localities by group, placing those in the Otway Group, among them Dinosaur Cove and Eric the Red, in the *Crybelosporites striatus* Subzone, of late Aptian–early Albian age. Agnolín and colleagues (2010) carry that age over to the Dinosaur Cove localities of the Eumeralla Formation. The paper supports neither the Hauterivian nor the Barremian for this unit, and its late Aptian–early Albian is narrower than the formation's span and concerns localities rather than the unit; the Hauterivian and Barremian come from Bell and colleagues (2018). The authors make the age depend on the isochronicity of spore–pollen zones across eastern Australia, since the zones are tied to marine faunas only in basins to the north. Governs the Aptian and Albian in `stages: [Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian, Albian]` on [Eumeralla](../../../stratigraphy/e/eumeralla.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wagstaff1989a, p. 54, Abstract: "the localities in Otway Group sediments are of late Aptian–early Albian age (Crybelosporites striatus Subzone of Dettmann & Playford 1969) and the localities in the Strzelecki Group of Valanginian–Aptian age"; "This age determination is dependent on the isochronicity of spore–pollen zones across eastern Australia."
- wagstaff1989a, p. 60, Age of the Otway Group Localities: "The Otway Group localities at Dinosaur Cove, Eric the Red, Point Franklin, Point Lewis, and Marengo fall within the *Cr. striatus* Subzone"; "late Aptian–early Albian".
- wagstaff1989a, p. 60, Fig. 5: "the Cr. striatus Zone of latest Aptian–early Albian age".
- wagstaff1989a, p. 61, Summary: "The vertebrate localities in the Otway Group sediments that could be precisely dated are of late Aptian–early Albian age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Wagstaff and McEwen Mason (1989) as a source of the Hauterivian or Barremian.* Their older range, Valanginian–Aptian, is for the localities of the Strzelecki Group, not the Otway Group.
- *Their late Aptian–early Albian as the age of the whole formation.* It dates the sampled vertebrate localities, and the paper does not name the formation.

**Open.** The Hauterivian and Barremian rest on Bell and colleagues' (2018) "late Hauterivian–Albian", given in passing without a source; a work on the stratigraphy of the Otway Group would settle the formation's lower limit. Herne and colleagues (2018) put the base of the *Crybelosporites striatus* zone at the Aptian–Albian boundary (see [*Diluvicursor*](../../genera/D/Diluvicursor.md)), against Wagstaff and McEwen Mason's late or latest Aptian; whether the Dinosaur Cove localities reach the Aptian depends on which zonation holds.
