# Echkar

## Does the lowest Echkar Formation share the Aptian–Albian GAD 5 level at Gadoufaoua?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** No. Taquet, who described the Gadoufaoua levels, places GAD 5 in the upper part of the Elrhaz Formation, and every later account of a GAD 5 specimen that names a formation follows him. Only Sereno and colleagues (1998, 1999) extend GAD 5 into the lower Echkar, crediting Taquet (1976), whose held text says the opposite. The Echkar gains no Aptian or Albian from Gadoufaoua. Governs `stages: [Cenomanian]` on [Echkar](../../../stratigraphy/e/echkar.yml), unchanged for this question, and `formation: Elrhaz` on the Gadoufaoua genera, as the [*Nigersaurus*](../../genera/N/Nigersaurus.md) and [*Elrhazosaurus*](../../genera/E/Elrhazosaurus.md) entries already hold.

**Evidence.**
- taquet1976a, Chapter III, *Ouranosaurus nigeriensis* sp. nov.: "Deposit of Gadoufaoua, GAD 5 formation (upper part of the formation of Elrhaz)" (translated).
- taquet1999a, p. 86: "Gisement de Gadoufaoua, level GAD 5 (upper part of the Elrhaz Formation), Tégama Series, Aptian".
- galton1982a, p. 153: "GAD 5, upper part of El Rhaz formation, Tégama Series, Aptian".
- wilson2015a, p. 4, Table 1: "*Nigersaurus taqueti* | Elrhaz (GAD 5) | Aptian/Albian".

**Ruled out.**
- *GAD 5 spanning the Elrhaz–Echkar contact.* Sereno and colleagues write that "GAD 5 appears to include the upper part of the Elrhaz Formation and the lower part of the Echkar Formation" (sereno1998b, note 14) and that it "corresponds with the upper part of the Elrhaz Formation and the lower part of the Echkar Formation" (sereno1999a, p. 1346, note 12), each citing Taquet (1976); the part of Taquet's monograph held gives GAD 5 as upper Elrhaz only.

**Open.** Taquet's geology chapters, which define the GAD levels and the Elrhaz–Echkar contact at Gadoufaoua, have not been read; only Chapter III of the monograph is held.
