# Elrhaz

## What does Taquet (1976) say about the position and age of GAD 5?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Taquet (1976) gives GAD 5 as a level in the upper part of the Elrhaz Formation, of the Tégama series, and dates it Aptian in each of the three lines that place it. The *Ouranosaurus* holotype came from the top of the level, partly above a cliff-forming sandstone and conglomerate that Taquet calls the cliff of Elrhaz, and the paratype, from the east border of an airfield 4 km south of the Innocents, is placed in the same level. The monograph's one Albian that touches *Ouranosaurus* brackets three taxa together and is not a date for GAD 5. Governs the Aptian in `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` on GAD 5 and the bed's place in [Elrhaz](../../../stratigraphy/e/elrhaz.yml).

**Evidence.**
- taquet1976a, p. ? (translation, Chapter III, *Ouranosaurus nigeriensis* sp. nov.): "Locality of type: … Deposit of Gadoufaoua, GAD 5 formation (upper part of the formation of Elrhaz), Tégama series, Aptian" (translated).
- taquet1976a, p. ? (translation, same section): "Locality of the paratype: 4 km south of the formation of the Innocents, east border of airfield, … Gadoufaoua deposit, GAD 5 (upper part of the formation of Elrhaz), Tégama series, Aptian" (translated).
- taquet1976a, p. ? (translation, same section): "Distribution: Deposit of Gadoufaoua GAD 5, Elrhaz formation, Tégama series, Aptian, north-east Niger" (translated).
- taquet1976a, p. ? (translation, Chapter III, A. Introduction, Conditions of deposit): "The level at which the skeleton was discovered constituted the superior part of the GAD 5, partly situated above a sandstone and conglomerate layer well readily identifiable, forming a cliff (cliff of Elrhaz, fig. 3)" (translated).
- taquet1976a, p. ? (translation, Chapter III, Discussion): *Ouranosaurus nigeriensis* "is a little more recent (Aptian) than the majority of the representatives of the genus *Iguanodon*"; with the two species of *Probactrosaurus*, "all three have been found in the level of the end of the Lower Cretaceous (Aptian or Albian)" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *GAD 5 as a unit of formation rank.* The held copy is an English translation, and its "GAD 5 formation" is not a rank claim: the same section puts GAD 5 inside "the formation of Elrhaz", the introduction calls it "the level at which the skeleton was discovered", and the translation also writes "formation of the Innocents" for a named place at Gadoufaoua. That "formation" in "GAD 5 formation" is the translator's word is an inference; the French text was not checked.
- *An Albian for GAD 5 from Taquet (1976).* The "(Aptian or Albian)" is a bracket for *Ouranosaurus* and the two *Probactrosaurus* species together, written just after Taquet gives *Ouranosaurus* alone as Aptian. Every line that places GAD 5 gives the Aptian only.

**Open.** The original French wording of the type-locality line, and Taquet's geology chapters, which define the GAD levels, have not been read.
