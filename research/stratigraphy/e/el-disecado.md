# El Disecado

## What do the cited works say about the age of the El Disecado?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Renne and colleagues (1991) date tuffs in the member by 40Ar/39Ar on single sanidine grains: 74.46 ±0.08 Ma for the tuff at its base (EG-35); 74.25 ±0.07 and 74.20 ±0.15 Ma for two samples (EG-16, EG-48) of one tuff about 400 m above the base, within the member; and 73.59 ±0.09 Ma for a tuff (EG-42) about 150 m below its top. Their composite column (Fig. 2) places the whole El Gallo Formation in the Campanian, and their text puts the Campanian–Maastrichtian boundary within the overlying Rosario Formation, more than 400 m above the highest dated tuff. The dates were calculated with Fish Canyon sanidine at 27.84 Ma. Recalibrating them to a later monitor age, which is older than 27.84 Ma, would make every date older by around one percent; this is an inference, not something the paper states. A shift that size moves the dates away from the top of the Campanian at 72.1 Ma and leaves them far from its base, so the stage holds. Governs `stages: [Campanian]` on [El Disecado](../../../stratigraphy/e/el-disecado.yml).

**Evidence.**
- renne1991a, p. 459, Abstract: "the alluvial Late Cretaceous (Campanian) El Gallo Formation".
- renne1991a, p. 460: "Two other bentonites occur at ca. 400 and 800 m above the base, within the El Disecado member. The tuff occurring at ca. 400 m was sampled both north and south of the Arroyo El Rosario (Figure 1, samples EG-48 and EG-16)"; "EG-16 (74.25 ±0.07 Ma) and EG-48 (74.20 ±0.15 Ma)".
- renne1991a, p. 460: "73.59 ±0.09 Ma for the tuff (EG-42) approximately 150 m below the top of the Disecado Member"; "The date (74.46 ±0.08 Ma) obtained for the tuff (EG-35) at the base of the Disecado Member".
- renne1991a, p. 460: "An age of 27.84 Ma for the Fish Canyon sanidine"; "was used to calculate J values".
- renne1991a, p. 460, Fig. 2: the column is labeled Campanian through the El Gallo Formation, with the Maastrichtian beginning in the Rosario Formation.
- renne1991a, p. 462: the boundary "in the El Rosario area occurs within the Rosario Formation, more than 400 m above the stratigraphically highest tuff dated at 73.59 ± 0.09 Ma in this study".

**Ruled out.**
- *A stage other than the Campanian once the dates are recalibrated.* The dates rest on an older monitor age, but any recalibration to a later Fish Canyon age makes them older, away from the Campanian–Maastrichtian boundary, and by much less than the distance to the base of the Campanian.

**Open.** Nothing.
