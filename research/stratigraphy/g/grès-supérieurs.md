# Grès supérieurs

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Grès supérieurs?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Barremian to Cenomanian, with the Aptian and Albian as the core. Sha (2007) dates the two trigonioidid bivalve assemblages of southern Laos as Aptian, probably reaching the Albian, and as mainly Albian, possibly from the Aptian into the Cenomanian. Allain and colleagues (1999) give the formation as Aptian–Albian. Buffetaut (1991) dates the dinosaur-bearing beds of Muong Phalane, which the paper names by locality and never as the formation, as late Early to basal Late Cretaceous, through a satellite-imagery correlation with the Khok Kruat Formation of Thailand and the primitive hadrosaurid material. The late Barremian comes only from Allain and colleagues' (2012) correlation with the Khok Kruat and Phu Pan formations; none of the works cited dates the Laotian beds themselves that low. Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous, Late Cretaceous]` and `stages: [Barremian, Aptian, Albian, Cenomanian]` on [Grès supérieurs](../../../stratigraphy/g/grès-supérieurs.yml).

**Evidence.**
- allain2012a, p. 370, Horizon and locality: the formation "is considered here the lateral equivalent of the Khok Kruat and Phu Pan formations in neighbouring Thailand and is probably late Barremian to early Cenomanian in age", citing Buffetaut (1991), Allain and colleagues (1999), Sha (2007) and Racey and Goodall (2009).
- buffetaut1991b, p. 59, Abstract: the fauna "was considered by him as Senonian in age"; "the hadrosaurid material (especially the ilia) indicates a very primitive form", which "suggests an earlier age than that accepted by HOFFET"; "correlations based on satellite imagery suggest that the dinosaur-bearing beds of Muong Phalane are the equivalent of the Khok Kruat Formation of the Khorat Plateau"; "The dinosaur-bearing beds of southern Laos are thus likely to be late Early Cretaceous to basal Late Cretaceous in age".
- sha2007a, p. 72: the *Trigonioides kobayashi*–*Plicatounio suzukii* assemblage of "Ban Vang Soung, Ban Na Pho and Ban Ta Seng of lower Laos" is "Aptian in age and probably extends up into the Albian"; p. 73: the Ban Na Gnom Bed "were determined as Late Cretaceous (Senonian) in age by Hoffet (1937)", while its assemblage "is mainly Albian" and "probably extends up into the Cenomanian and down into the Aptian".
- racey2009b, p. 69, Abstract: palynology suggests "an Early Cretaceous (Berriasian–Barremian) age for the Phu Kradung, Sao Khua and Phra Wihan Formations, and an Aptian age for the Khok Kruat Formation"; the Phu Phan "must also be Early Cretaceous based on the age of the under- and overlying formations".

**Ruled out.**
- *Senonian.* Hoffet's age for the fauna and for the bivalves of the Ban Na Gnom Bed. Buffetaut (1991) rejects it on the primitive hadrosaurid ilia and the Khok Kruat correlation, and Sha (2007) dates the same bivalve assemblage as mainly Albian.
- *Khok Kruat or Phu Pan as other names for the Grès supérieurs.* Allain and colleagues (2012) correlate units of two countries as lateral equivalents; that is not a synonymy, and neither name is a variant of this unit.

**Open.** The late Barremian is reached only through Thailand. Racey and Goodall (2009), whom Allain and colleagues cite, date the Khok Kruat as Aptian and bracket the Phu Phan between the Berriasian–Barremian Sao Khua below and the Khok Kruat above; reading a Barremian lower limit for the Phu Pan from that bracket is our inference, and neither paper gives an age for the Laotian beds. A date or index fossil from the Grès supérieurs below the Aptian would settle whether the Barremian belongs.

## Is the Grès supérieurs a formally erected formation?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Not shown. No paper read erects it, and Allain and colleagues (2012) still write the name in quotation marks. The unit is the standard name for the dinosaur-bearing beds of southern Laos, so the records from them keep it in `formation` whatever its formal status. Whether it takes `informal: true` waits on the works that would show whether anyone erected it. Governs the absent `informal` flag on [Grès supérieurs](../../../stratigraphy/g/grès-supérieurs.yml), and `location.formation` on [*Ichthyovenator laosensis*](../../../genera/I/Ichthyovenator.yml) and [*Tangvayosaurus hoffeti*](../../../genera/T/Tangvayosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- allain2012a: "Ban Kalum, Tang Vay area, Savannakhet Province, Laos; “Grès supérieurs” Formation; Early Cretaceous (Hoffet 1937; Allain et al. 1997, 1999)".
- allain1999a: titled "Un nouveau genre de dinosaure sauropode de la Formation des Grès supérieurs (Aptien-Albien) du Laos", with the name lowercase as "Grès supérieurs formation" in the English title.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether anyone erected the unit. Hoffet's regional geology (1933, 1937) and later syntheses of Indochinese stratigraphy are where an erection or formal definition would be.
