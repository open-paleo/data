# Grès supérieurs

## Is the Grès supérieurs a formally erected formation?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Not shown. No paper read erects it, and Allain and colleagues (2012) still write the name in quotation marks. The unit is the standard name for the dinosaur-bearing beds of southern Laos, so the records from them keep it in `formation` whatever its formal status. Whether it takes `informal: true` waits on the works that would show whether anyone erected it. Governs the absent `informal` flag on [Grès supérieurs](../../../stratigraphy/g/grès-supérieurs.yml), and `location.formation` on [*Ichthyovenator laosensis*](../../../genera/I/Ichthyovenator.yml) and [*Tangvayosaurus hoffeti*](../../../genera/T/Tangvayosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- allain2012a: "Ban Kalum, Tang Vay area, Savannakhet Province, Laos; “Grès supérieurs” Formation; Early Cretaceous (Hoffet 1937; Allain et al. 1997, 1999)".
- allain1999a: titled "Un nouveau genre de dinosaure sauropode de la Formation des Grès supérieurs (Aptien-Albien) du Laos", with the name lowercase as "Grès supérieurs formation" in the English title.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether anyone erected the unit. Hoffet's regional geology (1933, 1937) and later syntheses of Indochinese stratigraphy are where an erection or formal definition would be.
