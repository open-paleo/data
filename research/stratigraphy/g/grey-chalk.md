# Grey Chalk

## What is the Grey Chalk Subgroup, and how old is it?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** A subgroup of the Chalk Group, Cenomanian. Hopson (2005) divides the Chalk Group into the Grey Chalk and White Chalk subgroups, places the West Melbury Marly Chalk and Zig Zag Chalk formations in the Grey Chalk in the Southern Province, and dates it directly from its ammonite zones. Its clayey, flintless chalk gives the lithology. Governs the new entry [Grey Chalk](../../../stratigraphy/g/grey-chalk.yml) and `parent` on [West Melbury Marly Chalk](../../../stratigraphy/w/west-melbury-marly-chalk.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hopson2005a, p. 15: "The group is formally divided into two subgroups, the Grey Chalk and White Chalk subgroups".
- hopson2005a, p. 16: "Upper Cretaceous, Cenomanian. Mantelliceras mantelli to Calycoceras guerangeri zones."

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
