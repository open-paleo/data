# Gara Sbaa

## Does any record confuse the Gara Sbaa Formation with the Gara Sbaa Member of the Akrabou?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No. [*Rebbachisaurus*](../../../genera/R/Rebbachisaurus.yml) and [*Deltadromeus*](../../../genera/D/Deltadromeus.yml) are filed in the Gara Sbaa Formation, and no record or entry names the Akrabou Formation or its Gara Sbaa Member, a limestone that Ibrahim and colleagues (2020) name. Governs nothing on [Gara Sbaa](../../../stratigraphy/g/gara-sbaa.yml).

**Evidence.** None.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What age do the cited works give the Gara Sbaa, and what else does its name denote?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Cenomanian. Ibrahim and colleagues (2020) erect the formation as the lower of the two formations of their Kem Kem Group, and open their review of its age by noting that the "Kem Kem beds" have "been regarded variously as mid or early Late Cretaceous (Albian-Cenomanian)". That sentence reports earlier estimates, not their own reading. Their own conclusion, from the elasmobranchs that Sereno and colleagues (1996) collected in both formations, is a Cenomanian age for the Gara Sbaa and the Douira alike, since no elasmobranch genus restricted to the Albian or earlier has been found. The same string also names a locality, Gara Sbaa, which yielded *Rebbachisaurus garasbae*, and the paper calls the basal unit of the overlying Cenomanian–Turonian limestone at that locality the "Gara Sbaa Member". Governs `stages: [Cenomanian]` on [Gara Sbaa](../../../stratigraphy/g/gara-sbaa.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ibrahim2020a, p. 1, Abstract: "These strata are recognized here as the Kem Kem Group, which is composed of the lower Gara Sbaa and upper Douira formations".
- ibrahim2020a, p. 5: "The age of the 'Kem Kem beds' has been regarded variously as mid or early Late Cretaceous (Albian-Cenomanian)".
- ibrahim2020a, p. 8: *Rebbachisaurus garasbae* "from the locality Gara Sbaa".
- ibrahim2020a, p. 48, Fig. 34: "the Gara Sbaa Member of the Cenomanian-Turonian limestone".
- ibrahim2020a, p. 52: "No elasmobranch genera have been recovered with an age range restricted to the Albian or earlier. The evidence from elasmobranchs, thus, suggests a Cenomanian age for both the Gara Sbaa and Douira formations".
- sereno1996a, p. 987: "Nine elasmobranch species strongly support a Cenomanian age for the deposit".

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian.* It enters only through the sentence on how the "Kem Kem beds" have been regarded, a spread of earlier readings that the same paper resolves to the Cenomanian on the elasmobranchs (ibrahim2020a, pp. 5, 52); a reported spread adds no stage of its own ([methods](../../methods.md)).
- *The locality or the limestone member as this unit.* The name Gara Sbaa also denotes the locality and a member of the overlying limestone (ibrahim2020a, pp. 8, 48); neither is the formation.

**Open.** Nothing.
