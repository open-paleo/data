# Guifeng

## What is the age of the Guifeng Group?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Coniacian–Maastrichtian, with a dispute over the basal age. Governs `stages` (Coniacian–Maastrichtian) and the dispute on Guifeng, and the stages of its formations, such as [Tangbian](../../../stratigraphy/t/tangbian.yml) and [Lianhe](../../../stratigraphy/l/lianhe.yml).

**Evidence.**
- li2018a, p. 1130 (the authors' English): "the Guifeng Gr might start in early Late Cretaceous (after~100Ma)".
- xi2018a, p. 275: "two isotopic ages of 89–86 Ma from the basalt of the Hekou Formation imply that the age of the Guifeng Group might be early to mid-late Cretaceous (Wu et al., 2002)"; xi2021a, p. 387, gives "晚白垩世中晚期" ("middle to late Late Cretaceous", translated), also citing Wu and colleagues (2002). The two Xi papers are therefore one source, not two.

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian–Danian only, from palaeomagnetism.* Xing and colleagues give "Palaeomagnetic studies have dated the Guifeng Group to 71.4–65.0 Ma (Gu 1991; Zuo et al. 1991)" (xing2020b, p. 1), relaying work from the Jitai Basin, not the Ganzhou Basin.

**Open.** Two points. Li and colleagues treat "Hekou and Tangbian formations" as "contemporaneous heterotopic facies in different environment" and the Hekou and Lianhe as "probably the same sequences but named differently" (li2018a, p. 1130, following Xie 2001), which conflicts with the registry's stacked Campanian Tangbian and Maastrichtian Lianhe. And Xi and colleagues' Fig. 4 (xi2021a, p. 386) draws the Lianhe on into the Danian, which the registry does not record.
