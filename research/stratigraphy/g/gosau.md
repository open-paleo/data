# Gosau

## Does the Grünbach Formation take the Gosau Group as parent, and how old is the group?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Yes; the group is Late Cretaceous to Paleocene, with no stages. Csiki-Sava and colleagues (2015) and Augustin and colleagues (2023) place the Grünbach Formation in the Gosau Group, and Csiki-Sava and colleagues give the group as "Upper Cretaceous to Palaeocene" in their own voice. The subgroup is left out: Csiki-Sava and colleagues give a Lower Gosau Subgroup after Summesberger and colleagues (2000), while Hradecká and colleagues (1999) put the coal-bearing beds in an Upper Gosau Subgroup. Hradecká and colleagues' "Santonian to the Paleocene" covers only the Grünbach–Neue Welt part of the group. Governs the new entry [Gosau](../../../stratigraphy/g/gosau.yml) and `parent` on [Grünbach](../../../stratigraphy/g/grünbach.yml).

**Evidence.**
- csiki-sava2015a, p. 36: "The vertebrate-bearing beds at Muthmannsdorf are part of the Upper Cretaceous to Palaeocene Gosau Group of the eastern Alps."
- augustin2023a, p. 173: "assignable to the lower Campanian Grünbach Formation of the Gosau Group".

**Ruled out.** - *A subgroup.* The two works that name one disagree.

**Open.** Nothing.
