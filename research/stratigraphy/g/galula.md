*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No; the Maastrichtian is dropped, and the formation runs Aptian to Campanian. Supersedes the 2026-11-25 entry. ## Does Widlansky and colleagues' preferred C32 correlation add the Maastrichtian to the Galula?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Supersedes the 2026-11-23 entry's reading that the members run from the Aptian to the Campanian. Widlansky and colleagues (2018) prefer correlating the Namba Member's reversals to Chron C32, which "would place the TZ-07 fossil localities in the upper Campanian or lowermost Maastrichtian". That is the paper's own undecided dating between two stages, and under CONTRIBUTING it contributes both, as their alternative correlation to Chron C34 contributes its older span. Roberts and colleagues (2010), who name the formation, give it a middle Cretaceous age, Aptian to Cenomanian, and "ca. 120–90 Ma" from its fauna; that adds nothing outside the current span. Governs `stages`, Aptian to Maastrichtian, on [Galula](../../../stratigraphy/g/galula.yml), and Cenomanian to Maastrichtian on its [Namba Member](../../../stratigraphy/n/namba.yml); no record in either unit has a stage outside the new spans.

**Evidence.**

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Galula Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Widlansky and colleagues (2018) date the formation through its members by magnetostratigraphy. The Mtuka Member, which lacks clear reversals, is placed less firmly in the middle Cretaceous (Aptian–Cenomanian), and the Namba Member "no older than Late Cretaceous (Cenomanian-Campanian)", so that together the members run from the Aptian to the Campanian. For the three short reversed intervals in the Namba they prefer a correlation to Chron C32, which would put the TZ-07 localities in the upper Campanian or lowermost Maastrichtian, and keep as an alternative the short reversals proposed near the end of the Cretaceous Normal Superchron (Chron C34). They add that geological age constraints are less supportive of a Campanian age and suggest a middle Cretaceous age for at least part of the unit. Their lithology quotes Roberts and colleagues (2010). Governs `stages`, `period` and `lithology` on [Galula](../../../stratigraphy/g/galula.yml).

**Evidence.**
- widlansky2018a, p. ?, Abstract: "one interpretation correlates the Namba Member to Chron C32. An alternative correlation assigns reversals in the Namba Member to recently proposed short reversals near the end of the Cretaceous Normal Superchron (Chron C34)"; "These data suggest that the Namba Member is no older than Late Cretaceous (Cenomanian-Campanian), with the Mtuka Member less well constrained to the middle Cretaceous (Aptian-Cenomanian)".
- widlansky2018a, p. ?, 5.1 Correlation to the Geomagnetic Polarity Timescale (GPTS): the C32 correlation "would place the TZ-07 fossil localities in the upper Campanian or lowermost Maastrichtian"; "Geological age constraints, however, are less supportive of a Campanian age for the Galula Formation, and rather suggest a middle Cretaceous age for at least part of the unit"; "We consider this as an alternative correlation for the Namba Member, with a preferred correlation for the Namba Member being Chron C32".
- widlansky2018a, p. ?, 2.1.1 Stratigraphy and Sedimentology: a "sequence of red, pink, purple and occasionally white colored sandstones, conglomerates and mudstones", quoted from Roberts and colleagues (2010).

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Which correlation of the Namba reversals holds, and so whether the Namba is Campanian or older. Widlansky and colleagues (2018) write that settling it "will require the application of additional chronostratigraphic methods".
