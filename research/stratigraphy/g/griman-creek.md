# Griman Creek

## Does the Cenomanian date from Lightning Ridge apply to the whole Griman Creek Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** No. Bell and colleagues (2019) date only the exposures around Lightning Ridge and their fauna, to a maximum depositional age of 100.2–96.6 Ma, and say the range may not hold for the deeper parts of the formation there or for its exposures elsewhere in the Surat Basin. For those they report, without setting them aside, fission-track ages of about 107 to 99 Ma and palynology giving a middle Albian age from Queensland, and they state that the Lightning Ridge section cannot yet be tied into the formation's regional stratigraphy. The formation therefore spans both stages; the Cenomanian of its fauna at Lightning Ridge is the subject of the 2026-09-30 entry in [Wallangulla Sandstone](../w/wallangulla-sandstone.md). Governs `stages: [Albian, Cenomanian]` and `period: [Early Cretaceous, Late Cretaceous]` on [Griman Creek](../../../stratigraphy/g/griman-creek.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bell2019b, p. 655, Abstract: the new dates "confirm an early to mid-Cenomanian age for the fauna, rather than Albian, as has been reported previously".
- bell2019b, p. 656, Geological setting: "Fission-track analyses on core samples from the GCF in Queensland indicate an age of ~107 Ma, with sedimentation ceasing at ~99 Ma"; "palynological evidence (reference unit APK5; *Coptospora paradoxa* spore-pollen zone) support a middle Albian age".
- bell2019b, p. 660: the zircons "do not—and nor are they intended to—necessarily provide context for significantly deeper parts of the Griman Creek Formation (up to 200 m) or to exposures of the GCF elsewhere in the Surat Basin".
- bell2019b, p. 667, Discussion: "The GCF preferred maximum depositional age range is early to mid-Cenomanian, with a range of 100.2–96.6 Ma. It should be stressed that this age range relates to the available exposures (and its fauna) around Lightning Ridge and may or may not be relevant to significantly deeper parts of the GCF at Lightning Ridge ... or to exposures of the GCF in other parts of the Surat Basin".
- bell2019b, p. 669, Conclusions: "the local stratigraphy around Lightning Ridge cannot at this stage be tied into the regional stratigraphy of the GCF".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Cenomanian formation.* The title of Bell and colleagues (2019) labels the formation "(Cenomanian)", and Bell and colleagues (2018) write "Griman Creek Formation (Cenomanian)"; the 2019 text restricts the Cenomanian date to the Lightning Ridge exposures and leaves the Albian ages from Queensland standing.

**Open.** The age of the formation away from Lightning Ridge and below its exposed section there. Bell and colleagues (2019) treat the two fission-track ages as too few to fix it, and a date from the Queensland part of the formation, or a tie between the Lightning Ridge section and the regional stratigraphy, would settle it.
