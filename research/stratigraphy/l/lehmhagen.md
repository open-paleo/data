# Lehmhagen

## What do the cited works say about the horizon and age of the Lehmhagen?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Lower Toarcian. Haubold (1990) does not name the member. After Ernst (1967), he places the *Emausaurus* holotype in the lowest concretion layer of a 30 to 50 cm "bituminous shale", interpreted as the equivalent of the Posidonia Shale, in a Liassic clay pit east of the village he prints as "Klein-Lehmahagen", near Grimmen. He dates the shale to the zone of *Dactylioceras tenuicostatum* and the basal part of the zone of *Harpoceras falcifer*, the lowest Lias epsilon, earliest Toarcian, and gives the holotype's occurrence as lower Toarcian, *D. tenuicostatum* zone. Ansorge and colleagues (2025) erect the Lehmhagen Member of the Posidonia Shale Formation, named after the village of Klein Lehmhagen, with its type section in the northern part of the Grimmen clay pit, and date it to the lower Toarcian *semicelatum* Subzone. Governs `stages: [Toarcian]` and `parent: Posidonia Shale` on [Lehmhagen](../../../stratigraphy/l/lehmhagen.yml).

**Evidence.**
- haubold1990a, p. 151: the remains were "found in a Liassic clay pit (Pl. I, 1) east of the village of Klein-Lehmahagen near Grimmen" (translated).
- haubold1990a, p. 151: "According to ERNST (1967), the discovery horizon is the lowest concretion layer within the 30 to 50 cm thick 'bituminous shale', interpreted as the equivalent of the Posidonian Shale, in the zone of *Dactylioceras tenuicostatum* and the basal parts of the zone of *Harpoceras falcifer*, lowest Lias epsilon, earliest Toarcian" (translated).
- haubold1990a, p. 153: "Lower Jurassic, lowermost Lias epsilon, lower Toarcian, *Dactylioceras tenuicostatum* zone (equivalent to the Posidonia Shale), clay pit near Grimmen" (translated).
- ansorge2025a, p. 280: "Lehmhagen Member: new member (lower Toarcian)"; "named after the village Klein Lehmhagen"; "The type section, located in the northern part of the Grimmen clay pit".
- ansorge2025a, p. 275, Fig. 7: "Grimmen clay pit, units 2–7, Posidonia Shale Formation, Lehmhagen Member, lower Toarcian, *semicelatum* Subzone".
