# Longjiang Formation

## What stages do the Longjiang and the Pigeon Hill taxa take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** Barremian and Aptian. Wang and colleagues (2019) date a tuff in the Pigeon Hill fossil-bearing bed at 121.23 ± 0.74 Ma by zircon U-Pb, the tuff's formation age rather than a maximum. GTS2020 puts the base of the Aptian at 121.4 ± 0.6 Ma, so the date sits at the boundary within both uncertainties and the horizon takes both sides, as for [*Mussaurus*](../../genera/M/Mussaurus.md). The date is for a locality inside the formation, so it dates the formation too; that Pigeon Hill is Longjiang rather than Guanghua is settled in [Pigeon Hill](../../topics/pigeon-hill.md). The same paper places the fossil strata in the upper part of the formation. Governs `stages` on the [Longjiang Formation](../../../stratigraphy/l/longjiang.yml), and `stage` and `part: upper` on [*Daurlong wangi*](../../../genera/D/Daurlong.yml) and [*Migmanychion laiyang*](../../../genera/M/Migmanychion.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wang2019d: "A total of 99 analyses defined a weighted mean 206Pb/238U age of 121±0.74 Ma (MSWD=1.5) (Fig. 2), which represents the formation age of volcanic tuff"; "the precise geochronological age (121.23±0.74 Ma) confirms that the fossil strata belong to the upper part of the Longjiang Formation".
- wang2023a: the locality is "chronostratigraphically dated at about 121 Ma based on Zircon U-Pb dating of volcanic tuff samples (Wang et al., 2019)".
- gale2020a: "the base of the Aptian is taken at 121.4 Ma".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian alone.* The date and the boundary overlap within their uncertainties.

**Open.** Nothing.
