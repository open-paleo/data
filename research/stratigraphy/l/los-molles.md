# Los Molles

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Los Molles?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Toarcian to Bajocian for the formation, and early Bajocian for the level that yielded *Isaberrysaura*. Salgado and colleagues (2017) give the formation's span in the abstract and again in the type horizon, without a source for it in either place, and put the formation at about 1,042 m thick at Los Molles. They date the fossil-bearing level, laminated pelites rich in ammonite-bearing concretions some 40 m below the contact with the overlying Lajas Formation, by the ammonite *Sonninia altecostata* rather than by the formation's span. Governs `stages: [Toarcian, Aalenian, Bajocian]` on [Los Molles](../../../stratigraphy/l/los-molles.yml) and `stage: [Bajocian]` on [*Isaberrysaura mollensis*](../../../genera/I/Isaberrysaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- salgado2017a, p. 1, Abstract: "the marine-deltaic deposits of the Los Molles Formation (Toarcian-Bajocian)".
- salgado2017a, p. 2, Systematic palaeontology: "Los Molles Formation (Toarcian-Bajocian), which in this sector reaches a thickness of approximately 1,042 m"; "laminated pelites rich in ammonitiferous concretions and vertebrate remains, located some 40m below the contact with the overlying unit (Lajas Formation, Bajocian-Bathonian)"; "The presence of the ammonite *Sonninia altecostata* allows the fossil-bearing level to be situated biochronologically in the early Bajocian".

**Ruled out.**
- *The formation's Toarcian–Bajocian span as the age of the* Isaberrysaura *level.* The same paper fixes the level by ammonite to the early Bajocian, so the species takes the Bajocian only.

**Open.** Nothing.
