# Lower Argiles Rutilantes

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Lower Argiles Rutilantes?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian. Cojan and Moreau (2006) give the Aix-en-Provence Basin succession as the Calcaire de Rognac Formation, "the overlying Upper Argiles Rutilantes Formation" and the Calcaire de Vitrolles Formation, and their Fig. 2 puts the "Lower Argiles Rutilantes Fm" beneath the Calcaire de Rognac, level with the Campanian. The Calcaire de Rognac was itself deposited "during most of Chron 32" in the east of the basin, so the formation below it is older than that. Tortosa and colleagues (2014) give "upper part of the Lower Rognacian, Late Campanian" for the Jas Neuf Sud locality. Governs `stages` on [Lower Argiles Rutilantes](../../../stratigraphy/l/lower-argiles-rutilantes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cojan2006a, pp. 590–591: "(1) the Calcaire de Rognac Formation (C. Rognac Fm), dominated by lacustrine carbonate facies; (2) the overlying Upper Argiles Rutilantes Formation (U. Argiles Fm), ranging from palustrine to fluvial deposits; and (3) the Calcaire de Vitrolles Formation".
- cojan2006a, p. 590, Fig. 2: the lithostratigraphy column reads, from the top, "C. Vitrolles Fm", "Poudingue de la Galante Mbr", "Upper Argiles Rutilantes Fm", "C. Rognac Fm" and "Lower Argiles Rutilantes Fm", the last level with "Campanian" in the marine column and with the lowest part of the Rognacian.
- cojan2006a, p. 591: "The C. Rognac Fm was deposited during most of Chron 32 and the lower part of Chron 31R in the eastern part of the basin".
- cojan2006a, p. 600: at Rousset, above the C. Rognac Fm, "the Argiles Rutilantes Fm up to the P. Galante Mbr is a R–N sequence", which "can be attributed to anomaly 31 or the 31R–30N sequence".
- tortosa2014a, p. 66: "Lower Argiles Rutilantes Formation, upper part of the Lower Rognacian, Late Campanian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Anomaly 31 or the 31R–30N sequence as the age of this formation.* Cojan and Moreau attribute that polarity sequence to the Argiles Rutilantes above the Calcaire de Rognac at Rousset, which is the Upper formation; it says nothing of the formation below the Calcaire de Rognac. Their Fig. 2 puts Chron 31 in the Maastrichtian, which would be the wrong stage here.

**Open.** Nothing.
