# Lourinhã Formation

## Does the Lourinhã reach the Berriasian?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** Yes, at its top: Kimmeridgian to Berriasian, with the disagreement kept in the dispute. The Berriasian does not rest on the Assenta Member alone: Pena dos Reis and colleagues (1996) give it for the formation, and Salminen and colleagues' (2014) own magnetostratigraphy places the Jurassic–Cretaceous boundary 8 m below the formation's top at Porto da Calada, which agrees with Mateus and colleagues' (2017) earliest Berriasian for the last metres of the Assenta. That the 8 m lies within the Lourinhã is our reading of their log: the boundary at "around 52 m" and the base of the overlying Porto da Calada Formation "at our 60 m". Governs `stages: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian, Berriasian]` and the dispute on [Lourinhã](../../../stratigraphy/l/lourinhã.yml).

**Evidence.**
- penadosreis1996a: "The Tithonian and Berriasian are represented in the center and North by the Lourinhã formation".
- salminen2014a: the boundary at "around 52 m of the log", an improvement on Rey (1993), "which tentatively located the J–K boundary at the base of the Porto da Calada Formation (at our 60 m)"; the same paper's background calls the Assenta "Tithonian in age".
- mateus2017a: "the last few meters of the Assenta Mb. can be considered as earliest Berriasian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Salminen and colleagues (2014) as a Tithonian-only reading.* Their background says so; their result does not.

**Open.** Where the boundary falls elsewhere in the formation; the magnetostratigraphy is tentative and from one section.
