# Los Bastos

## How did Garrido (2010) define the Los Bastos Formation, and what age does it give?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Garrido (2010) names the formation as new, in the Río Neuquén Subgroup of the Neuquén Group, for red mudstones with minor sandstone between the Portezuelo Formation below and the Sierra Barrosa Formation, named in the same paper, above. The type locality is at the Los Bastos hydrocarbon field, between Sierra Barrosa and Cerro Senillosa. With no absolute dates, the paper infers an early to middle Coniacian age from the formation's position: the Portezuelo below is late Turonian–early Coniacian, a dating it adopts from earlier work, and the Sierra Barrosa above is middle–late Coniacian. Governs `stages: [Coniacian]` and `lithology` on [Los Bastos](../../../stratigraphy/l/los-bastos.yml).

**Evidence.**
- garrido2010a, p. 144: "This designation refers to a succession of red mudstones with minor intercalations of sandstones, positioned lithostratigraphically above the Portezuelo Formation and below a psammite-dominated succession, designated in this work as the Sierra Barrosa Formation (nom. nov.)" (translated).
- garrido2010a, p. 144: the type locality is "at the foot of the outcrops on which sits the operating facility of the Los Bastos hydrocarbon deposit", "between the localities of Sierra Barrosa and Cerro Senillosa" (translated).
- garrido2010a, p. 144: the Portezuelo Formation is placed "between Late Turonian – Early Coniacian, a criterion also adopted in this work" (translated).
- garrido2010a, pp. 144, 146: "there is a lack of absolute dating that would establish the age of these deposits. Based on stratigraphic relations, an age ranging from Early Coniacian to Middle Coniacian is inferred for this unit" (translated).
- garrido2010a, p. 146: the Sierra Barrosa Formation, on its stratigraphic relations, "is assigned to the Middle-Late Coniacian" (translated).

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** The age is inferred from position alone; a radiometric date or a biostratigraphic study of the formation would test it.
