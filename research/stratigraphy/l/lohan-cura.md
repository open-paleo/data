# Lohan Cura

## How do Leanza and Hugo (1995) define the Lohan Cura and bound its age, and what does Garrido (2010) add?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Leanza and Hugo (1995) erect the formation for continental beds, 177.20 m thick, between the La Amarga Formation below and the Candeleros Formation above, each contact an unconformity, with Cerro Lohan Cura as the type locality and the Puesto Quiroga and Cullín Grande members from bottom to top. They bracket its age rather than measure it. The ceiling is the unconformity at the base of the Neuquén Group, which they date to the Lower Cenomanian (97 ± 3 Ma) after Orchuela and Ploszkiewicz (1984); the floor is the upper Hauterivian–Barremian usually given to the La Amarga, so the maximum "can reach the Aptian". Their stratigraphic chart draws the formation across the upper Aptian and the Albian, with a hiatus above and below, and Leanza (1999) ascribes it to the late Aptian and Albian "based on tectosedimentary aspects and field relationships", correlating it with the Rayoso Formation of the central basin. Garrido (2010) gives the Lohan Cura an estimated Albian age after Leanza and Hugo (1995) and Leanza (1999), narrowing what those works give, and adds that at the El Sauce field near Picún Leufú, a contact Leanza and Hugo (1997) pointed out, the top of the formation is a level of gypsum interlaminated with fine green mudstones. Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` on [Lohan Cura](../../../stratigraphy/l/lohan-cura.yml), unchanged.

The two works differ on the upper contact. Leanza and Hugo (1995) have the Candeleros cover the Lohan Cura "in the same manner", in unconformity; Garrido (2010) writes that outside the Huincul Ridge the angular relation disappears and the Candeleros rests "concordantly" on the Lohan Cura. No value depends on the difference.

**Evidence.**
- leanza1995a, p. 30: "Lohan Cura Formation nom. nov.: This designation is proposed to identify a set of continental sedimentites that overlie Formation La Amarga in unconformity and are covered in the same manner by Formation Candeleros, reaching 177.20 m in thickness"; "The area of Cerro Lohan Cura ... is designated as the type locality" (translated).
- leanza1995a, p. 31: "the Lohan Cura Formation can be subdivided, from bottom to top, into two Members", the Puesto Quiroga and the Cullín Grande (translated).
- leanza1995a, p. 31: "The minimum age of the Lohan Cura Formation is given by the age of the unconformity existing at the base of the Neuquén Group which, according to Orchuela and Ploszkiewicz (1984), can be estimated at the Lower Cenomanian (97±3 Ma)" (translated).
- leanza1995a, p. 32: "or perhaps slightly older, according to reptile records present at the base of Formation Candeleros that would indicate an upper Albian age (J.F. Bonaparte, personal communication, 1995). On the other hand, the maximum age can reach the Aptian, given the upper Hauterivian / Barremian age usually assigned to Formation La Amarga" (translated).
- leanza1995a, p. 31, unnumbered chart, "Cuadro estratigráfico propuesto para la región analizada" ("proposed stratigraphic chart for the region analyzed", translated): the Lohan Cura, with the Puesto Quiroga below the Cullín Grande, spans the upper part of the Aptian row and the lower part of the Albian row, with hatched gaps above and below.
- leanza1999a, p. 21, Lohan Cura Formation: "Based on tectosedimentary aspects and field relationships, the Lohan Cura Formation can be ascribed to the late Aptian and Albian and it can be correlated in the central part of the basin with the Rayoso Formation".
- garrido2010a, p. 132: "in the region located immediately south of the Huincul Ridge, the Candeleros Formation rests concordantly on deposits of the Lohan Cura Formation, the latter with an estimated age in the Albian (Leanza & Hugo, 1995; Leanza, 1999)" (translated).
- garrido2010a, p. 132: at the El Sauce field, "approximately 8 km north of the city of Picún Leufú", "the boundary between the Lohan Cura and Candeleros Formations is given by a level of evaporites (gypsum interlaminated with fine layers of green mudstones), which constitutes the top of the first unit" (translated); p. 133, Fig. 8c: "Contact between the Candeleros Formation (Cenomanian) and the Lohan Cura Formation (Albian)" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian.* The Lower Cenomanian, 97 ± 3 Ma, dates the unconformity at the base of the Neuquén Group above the formation. It is a ceiling, not an age of the Lohan Cura. On the ICS chart v2024/12 (base Cenomanian 100.5 Ma, base Turonian 93.9 Ma) the whole span is Cenomanian, so the stage Leanza and Hugo give it stands on the current chart.
- *A late Albian ceiling from reptiles at the base of the Candeleros.* Leanza and Hugo offer it as "perhaps slightly older" on a personal communication, which is not a published determination.
- *The Rayoso Formation as the name for these beds.* Leanza and Hugo (1995, p. 32) relay an earlier assignment of the beds south of the Huincul Ridge to the Rayoso Formation and reject it: the Rayoso of the central basin has evaporites "of marked marine influence", while the Lohan Cura is plainly continental (translated).

**Open.** None of the works cited for the formation dates it by fossils or radiometric ages from its own beds; the Aptian–Albian rests on the bracket between the La Amarga and the Neuquén Group, on Leanza and Hugo's chart and on Leanza's (1999) tectosedimentary reading. A palynological or radiometric age from either member would settle how far into the Aptian it reaches.
