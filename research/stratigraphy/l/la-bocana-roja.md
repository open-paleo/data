# La Bocana Roja

## Is the La Bocana Roja Formation Turonian or Campanian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Disputed. A detrital-zircon maximum age makes the upper part no older than Cenomanian–Turonian; the dinosaur fauna argues for the middle or late Campanian, and the overlying El Gallo Formation sets a minimum of about 75.8 Ma. Governs `stages` (Cenomanian–Campanian) and the dispute on La Bocana Roja, and bears on `stage` for [*Labocania anomala*](../../../genera/L/Labocania.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kane2022a, p. 9: "date the upper part of the fluvial bedrock (La Bocana Roja Fm.) at 93.6±1.1 Ma (Cenomanian-Turonian)"; p. 8: "we revise the age considerably from Campanian to Turonian (93.6±1.1 Ma)". Its p. 7 gives the stage as "(Turonian)".
- rivera-sylva2024a, p. 263: "because detrital zircons are reworked from older sediments, this is only a maximum age"; "The dinosaur fauna therefore strongly argues for a mid- or late Campanian age"; "suggest a minimum age of 75.84 ± 0.05 Ma for the La Bocana Roja Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Turonian as a settled age.* The zircon date is a maximum, as Rivera-Sylva and colleagues note.
- *Bivalves and ammonites as independent support for the Campanian.* Rivera-Sylva and colleagues' "late middle Campanian" from bivalves and ammonites (p. 263) concerns the overlying Punta Baja Formation and is relayed from Kane and colleagues, who discount the bivalve age (kane2022a, p. 7).

**Open.** The age within that bracket. The 75.84 Ma minimum is, in Rivera-Sylva and colleagues' account, the base of the El Disecado Member of the El Gallo; Kane and colleagues give an El Gallo maximum age of 86.8 ± 1.8 Ma "Immediately above the contact" (p. 7), so 75.84 Ma may not date the very base of the El Gallo. The *Labocania* site LACM 7257 has absorbed LACM 6755, which is listed as "El Gallo Fm. (El Diseado [sic] member)" (rivera-sylva2024a, p. 263); if that listing applies, the horizon would be El Gallo, not La Bocana Roja.
