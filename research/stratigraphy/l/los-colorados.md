# Los Colorados

## What stages does the Los Colorados span?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Norian. The papers that place the fauna give the late Norian, and the magnetostratigraphic ages in the record fall in the Norian on either GTS2020 base of the Rhaetian. Governs `stages: [Norian]` and `period: [Late Triassic]` on [Los Colorados](../../../stratigraphy/l/los-colorados.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martínez2011a, p. 209: "the overlying Los Colorados Formation near the close of the Late Triassic (late Norian)".
- mcphee2017b, p. 457: "The Los Colorados fauna is restricted to the La Esquina Member (upper third of the formation), with an upper age bound of ~213 Ma".
- weishampel2004b, p. 528, under 63.1, Provincia de La Rioja: "3. Los Colorados Formation … Age: Norian (Bonaparte 1973; Stipanicic 1983)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Cretaceous.* Reig (1963) reports, in his review of earlier datings, that Bodenbender (1911) assigned "those we now distinguish as Los Colorados Beds" to the "Andean Upper Cretaceous" (reig1963a, p. 17, translated). Reig gives it as history, not as his own reading, and he goes on to the Triassic schemes that replaced it.
- *Rhaetian, from the same review.* Reig also reports that Frenguelli (1948) restricted the beds "to the Rhaetian" and that Groeber and Stipanicic (1952) kept "the Los Colorados in the Rhaetian with a certain hesitation" (reig1963a, p. 17, translated). These are the older faunal correlations that the record's notes already treat as superseded by the magnetostratigraphic ages.
- *Middle Triassic.* Reig gives the *Herrerasaurus* material, which includes a femur found "en los Estratos de Los Colorados", the provenance "'Estratos de Ischigualasto' y 'Estratos de Los Colorados' … Mesotriásico superior" ("Upper Middle Triassic", reig1963a, p. 9). The age is his dating of the Ischigualasto fauna, which he concludes "represents the highest Middle Triassic" (Conclusions, translated), carried onto a provenance line that names both units. It does not date the Los Colorados.
- *Carnian.* The distribution chapter of *The Dinosauria* gives the La Rioja Los Colorados entry as Norian. The "Age: Carnian (Bonaparte 1973; Stipanicic 1983)" printed beside it, in the next column, belongs to the San Juan entry for the Ischigualasto Formation (weishampel2004b, p. 528).

**Open.** Nothing.
