# Lulworth

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Lulworth?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Tithonian to Berriasian. Galton (1978) reports both bounds: following Casey (1971), the Lulworth Beds are "Upper Volgian or 'Tithonian', Upper Jurassic", because the Dirt Bed lies below the Cinder Bed, which Casey takes to mark the Jurassic–Cretaceous boundary in Dorset; and Dodson and colleagues (1964), who review the problems of dating the beds, "assign them to the Berriasian". Galton's own horizon line for *Echinodon* calls the Lulworth Beds Lower Cretaceous. The Tithonian bound rests on Galton's "Tithonian" label for Casey's Upper Volgian. The regional stage does not convert to one international stage without a correlation: Hesselbo and colleagues (2020) report that in 1996 the Russian Interdepartmental Stratigraphic Committee resolved to equate the Lower and Middle Volgian with the Tithonian and to "assign the Upper Volgian … to the lowermost Cretaceous", which would place Casey's Upper Volgian in the Berriasian if his British Upper Volgian is the Russian one. Galton uses "Lulworth Beds", the name Casey (1963) proposed, where later work uses Formation. Galton places the *Echinodon* Dirt Bed in "the lower part of the Middle Purbeck Beds" on p. 151 and in the "lower part of Lulworth Beds" on p. 155; which bed *Echinodon* came from is settled in [Purbeck: Echinodon and Owenodon](../../topics/purbeck-echinodon-and-owenodon.md). Governs `period` and `stages` on the [Lulworth Formation](../../../stratigraphy/l/lulworth.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1978a, p. 151: "The Dirt Bed is below the Cinder Bed that marks the Jurassic-Cretaceous boundary in Dorset according to CASEY (1963, 1971) so *Echinodon* and the mammals occur in the Lulworth Beds of CASEY (1971, Upper Volgian or 'Tithonian', Upper Jurassic)"; "Problems of determination of the age of the Lulworth Beds are reviewed by DODSON et al (1964) who assign them to the Berriasian"; *Echinodon* came from "the freshwater Dirt Bed (Mammal Bed) in the lower part of the Middle Purbeck Beds".
- galton1978a, p. 155, Horizon and Locality: "freshwater Dirt Bed (Mammal Bed) in lower part of Lulworth Beds (Middle Purbeck Beds, Lower Cretaceous) of Durlston Bay near Swanage, Dorset".
- hesselbo2020a, p. 974: "In 1996 the Russian Interdepartmental Stratigraphic Committee resolved to equate the Lower and Middle Volgian … to the Tithonian Stage, assign the Upper Volgian (*Kachpurites fulgens* through *Craspedites nodiger* ammonite zones) to the lowermost Cretaceous".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hesselbo and colleagues (2020) as the authority that assigns the Upper Volgian to the Cretaceous.* The assignment is the Russian committee's 1996 resolution, which the chapter reports; it is not the chapter's own correlation.

**Open.** Whether the Upper Volgian of Casey (1971) is the Russian Upper Volgian, which decides whether it converts to latest Tithonian or to Berriasian. Casey (1971) and Dodson and colleagues (1964) would settle what each says; neither has been read.
