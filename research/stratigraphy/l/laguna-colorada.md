# Laguna Colorada

## Do Pol and colleagues' dates give the Laguna Colorada a stage?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No; the formation keeps the Early Jurassic only. Supersedes the 2026-11-23 entry. Pol and colleagues (2021) date tuffaceous siltstones in the *Mussaurus* interval, in the middle of the formation, at 192.78 ± 0.14 and 192.74 ± 0.14 Ma, and their methods interpret each as "the maximum age of deposition for the corresponding strata"; their "Sinemurian" labels that maximum on the 2013 chart. A maximum depositional age is a limit, not an age, so the 2026-11-23 entry's "that age or younger" read a limit as a stage. Bandeira and colleagues' (2024) "Sinemurian" is a table entry beside a citation of Baez and Marsicano (2001), whose title gives the Upper Triassic. Pol and colleagues give the formation as deposited "during the Early Jurassic", which keeps the epoch. [*Mussaurus patagonicus*](../../../genera/M/Mussaurus.yml) takes the formation's range. Governs the absent `stages` and the notes on pol2021a and bandeira2024a on [Laguna Colorada](../../../stratigraphy/l/laguna-colorada.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pol2021a, p. 2: "These dates are younger than previously thought and now assign an Early Jurassic (Sinemurian8) maximum age for the Mussaurus bearing sediments."
- pol2021a, p. 6: the dates are "interpreted as the maximum age of deposition for the corresponding strata".
- pol2021a, p. 2: the formation "is a 170 m-thick succession of fluvio-lacustrine sediments deposited in a post-rift thermally induced sag basin during the Early Jurassic".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sinemurian and Pliensbachian.* A maximum depositional age, and a table label.

**Open.** A date from the lower formation or a depositional age for the *Mussaurus* beds.

## What age do the U–Pb dates of Pol and colleagues (2021) give the Laguna Colorada?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Sinemurian–Pliensbachian, Early Jurassic. Pol and colleagues (2021) date tuffaceous siltstones intercalated with the *Mussaurus*-bearing interval, in the middle of the formation at its type locality, at 192.78 ± 0.14 and 192.74 ± 0.14 Ma, and call this an Early Jurassic (Sinemurian) maximum age, labeling it on the 2013 ICS chart. The current chart (v2024/12, following GTS2020) starts the Pliensbachian at 192.9 Ma, against 190.8 Ma in GTS2012, so both dates fall within error of the Sinemurian–Pliensbachian boundary; as maxima, the dated beds are that age or younger. The same paper describes the whole formation as deposited during the Early Jurassic. Governs `stages: [Sinemurian, Pliensbachian]` and `period: [Early Jurassic]` on [Laguna Colorada](../../../stratigraphy/l/laguna-colorada.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pol2021a, p. 2, untitled opening section: "U–Pb geochronology (CA-ID-TIMS method) from tuffaceous siltstones intercalated with the vertebrate-bearing interval that yielded two overlapping dates of 192.78±0.14 Ma and 192.74±0.14 Ma"; "These dates are younger than previously thought and now assign an Early Jurassic (Sinemurian) maximum age for the Mussaurus bearing sediments."
- pol2021a, p. 2: the type locality of the Laguna Colorada Formation "is a 170 m-thick succession of fluvio-lacustrine sediments deposited in a post-rift thermally induced sag basin during the Early Jurassic"; the fossils come from "a restricted 3.0 m-thick interval of pedogenically modified massive reddish-brown siltstone in the middle of the Laguna Colorada Formation".
- pol2021a, p. 7, References: the stage label cites "8. Cohen, K. M., Finney, S. C., Gibbard, P. L. & Fan, J.-X. The ICS international chronostratigraphic chart. Episodes 36, 199–204 (2013)."
- hesselbo2020a, p. 1001: "The Sinemurian/Pliensbachian boundary is assigned an age of 192.9 Myr".
- hesselbo2020a, p. 1004, Table 26.4: base Pliensbachian 190.8 Ma in GTS2012 and 192.9 Ma in GTS2020.

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Triassic (Norian).* The formation "has long been regarded as Late Triassic (Norian) in age", "mainly based on occurrences of the Dicroidium paleoflora found in the vicinity of the vertebrate-bearing fossil horizons" (pol2021a, p. 2); Apaldetti and colleagues (2011) still list *Mussaurus* among the taxa "from the Norian Laguna Colorada Formation" (apaldetti2011a, p. 1, Introduction). The U–Pb dates, which are younger, replace this reading.
- *Sinemurian alone, as Pol and colleagues label it.* The label comes from the 2013 chart, on which the Pliensbachian began at 190.8 Ma; on the current chart the dates sit at the boundary.

**Open.** Only the middle of the formation is dated. Its lower part lies below the dated interval and its upper part above it, and no work cited dates either.
