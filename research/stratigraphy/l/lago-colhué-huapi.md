# Lago Colhué Huapi

## What do Casal and colleagues (2015) base the age of the Lago Colhué Huapi Formation on?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Coniacian to Maastrichtian, from dinosaurs and faunal correlation. Caudal vertebrae preliminarily referred to Rebbachisauridae, from the first red mudstones at estancia Ocho Hermanos, place the lowest levels at most in the Coniacian, because the paper takes the clade to reach no later than the Turonian–Coniacian in Gondwana. The *Aeolosaurus*–hadrosaur association at the headwaters of the Río Chico ties those beds to the Campanian units of northern Patagonia, so they are at least early Campanian, and the fauna there groups statistically with that of the late Campanian to early Maastrichtian Allen Formation. Governs `stages: [Coniacian, Santonian, Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on [Lago Colhué Huapi](../../../stratigraphy/l/lago-colhué-huapi.yml).

**Evidence.**
- casal2015a, p. 77, Abstract: "Although its base and top are transitional, this unit is sometimes limited by an erosional surface in its contact with the Salamanca Formation"; the unit has an age "Coniacian-Maastrichtian, and faunistic affinities with the Allen Fomation of the Neuquén basin".
- casal2015a, p. 89: in Patagonia the record of Rebbachisauridae "is restricted to the interval between the Albian and the Cenomanian", and the clade "could reach at most the Turonian–Coniacian in Gondwana" (translated).
- casal2015a, p. 89: the caudal vertebrae UNPSJB-Pv 1011, "preliminarily assigned to Rebbachisauridae" and "coming from the first appearances of the red mudstones of the new unit proposed here, at estancia Ocho Hermanos, in the sierra San Bernardo, suggest that the age of these levels would correspond at most to the Coniacian" (translated).
- casal2015a, p. 89: the association of *Aeolosaurus colhuehuapensis* and *Secernosaurus koerneri* at the headwaters of the Río Chico "would indicate a correspondence of those levels with the units of northern Patagonia mentioned. Therefore, the age of the sediments at the headwaters of the Río Chico should correspond, at least, to the Early Campanian" (translated).
- casal2015a, p. 90: the same statistical pattern links the fauna of the headwaters of the Río Chico with "the Allen Formation (Upper Campanian–Lower Maastrichtian ...)"; "In summary, the age of the proposed formation is comprised between the Coniacian–Maastrichtian" (translated).
- casal2015a, p. 90: "Suárez et al. (2014) obtained radiometric ages (SHRIMP U-Pb) of 97.6 and 96 Ma (Cenomanian) for the Laguna Palacios Formation. However, the paleontological content of the new unit and its stratigraphic relationship with the Laguna Palacios Formation rule out the Cenomanian age as the minimum age of the Chubut Group" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *A Cenomanian minimum age from the overlying Laguna Palacios.* Suárez and colleagues (2014), as Casal and colleagues (2015) relay them, date the Laguna Palacios Formation, which covers this unit, to 97.6 and 96 Ma. Casal and colleagues reject that as a minimum age for the Chubut Group on the new unit's fossils and its position below the Laguna Palacios.

**Open.** The age rests on vertebrate correlation, with a preliminary identification at the base; none of the works cited gives a radiometric date from the unit itself. Suárez and colleagues (2014) have not been read; their paper would show which beds they dated as Laguna Palacios and how that bears on a unit beneath them.
