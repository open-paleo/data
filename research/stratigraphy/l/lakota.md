# Lakota

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Lakota Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Berriasian to Barremian, unchanged, as the 2026-09-30 entry concludes. Cifelli and colleagues (2014) correlate the formation with the upper Berriasian–lower Barremian from ostracods and charophytes, and date two sampled horizons separately: the lower Chilson Member late Berriasian–Valanginian and a Fuson Member section late Valanginian–early Barremian. Boyd and Pagnac (2015) relay that dating and put the *Dakotadon* type locality in the lower Fuson. Sohn (1979) assigns no stage: "pre-Aptian" excludes the Aptian and licenses nothing, under the methods rule on bounds. Kirkland and colleagues (1993) and Weishampel and Bjork (1989) give a Barremian that both credit to Sohn (1979), so with Sohn they count as one reading, not three. D'Emic and colleagues' (2019) Aptian–Albian for the Fuson Member is a correlation from the Cloverly Formation, outside the basin. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on [Lakota](../../../stratigraphy/l/lakota.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- cifelli2014a, accepted manuscript, p. 1, Abstract: the Lakota is "a unit correlated with the upper Berriasian–lower Barremian and positioned stratigraphically between the underlying Morrison Formation and Aptian–Albian units exposed elsewhere in North America"; p. 5: "the nonmarine Lower Cretaceous Lakota Formation of the Black Hills region"; p. 6: "A pre-Aptian age for the Lakota Formation (Fig. 2, Berriasian to Barremian) is generally supported by charophyte data".
- cifelli2014a, accepted manuscript, p. 9: age constraints for the lower part of Chilson Member unit 2 "are late Berriasian–Valanginian"; p. 10: for the section assigned to the Fuson Member they "are late Valanginian–early Barremian"; p. 41: the mammals "derive from two separate horizons with different estimated ages (late Berriasian–Valanginian and late Valanginian–early Barremian, respectively)".
- boyd2015b, p. 1, Abstract: field work relocated the type locality, "confirming its provenance from unit L2 (lower Fuson Member equivalent) of the Lakota Formation", and with "recently revised ages for the members of the Lakota Formation based on charophyte and ostracod biostratigraphy" constrains the taxon "to the late Valanginian to early Barremian"; p. 7, Geologic Setting: "Early interpretations of the age of the Lakota Formation varied from Valanginian to Aptian".
- kirkland1993a, p. 3: "The Lakota Formation has been dated independently of vertebrate fossils as Barremian (Sohn, 1979)."
- weishampel1989a, p. 57: "Early Cretaceous, possibly Barremian (Sohn, 1979)"; p. 65: *Iguanodon* in the Lakota "is further evidence of a possible Barremian age for the unit (Sohn, 1979)".
- d'emic2019a, p. 29: the disconformity in the Lakota spans "most of the Aptian with the exception of local deposition of the Minnewaste Limestone, which has been attributed to local damming of sediment"; p. 30: the Little Sheep Mudstone correlates with "the Minnewaste Limestone and Fuson Member of the Lakota Formation", among units that "were deposited in both the Aptian and the Albian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian for the Fuson Member, after D'Emic and colleagues (2019).* The Aptian–Albian dates the Cloverly's Little Sheep Mudstone and reaches the Fuson only by correlation from outside the basin. Within the formation, the ostracods and charophytes date the Fuson section late Valanginian–early Barremian (Cifelli and colleagues 2014), and D'Emic and colleagues themselves put most of the Aptian in a disconformity in the Lakota, with only the local Minnewaste Limestone deposited during it.
- *Valanginian to Aptian, the early interpretations Boyd and Pagnac (2015) report.* A reported spread of earlier readings, not a dating. Its Aptian end is the "late Aptian as previously considered" that Sohn (1979) rejects, and the later microfossil work carries the base below its Valanginian.
- *Kirkland and colleagues (1993) and Weishampel and Bjork (1989) as readings independent of Sohn (1979).* Both cite Sohn for their Barremian, and Kirkland and colleagues' "independently of vertebrate fossils" means from ostracods, Sohn's evidence. Sohn's own statement is "Barremian or older", so neither narrows the range.

**Open.** The lower limit. Cifelli and colleagues report no ostracods or charophytes from unit 1 of the Chilson Member, so "the lower age constraint for the unit as a whole remains poorly resolved" (cifelli2014a, accepted manuscript, p. 40). A dated horizon or a microfossil assemblage from unit 1 would settle it. Whether the Minnewaste Limestone reaches the Aptian stays open, as in the 2026-09-30 entry.

## Do the Lakota taxa take the formation's range?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** No, not where a member is known. [*Hoplitosaurus marshi*](../../../genera/H/Hoplitosaurus.yml) and [*Osmakasaurus depressus*](../../../genera/O/Osmakasaurus.yml) come from the [Chilson](../../../stratigraphy/c/chilson.yml) Member and take its Berriasian–Valanginian. Supersedes the 2026-09-30 entry's claim to govern the Lakota stages of those two taxa; its conclusion on the formation's own range stands.

**Evidence.**
- boyd2015b: the Chilson is "the basal Chilson Member" of the Lakota, and the horizon of the two holotypes "is positioned within the Chilson Member".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the Lakota Formation pre-Aptian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes: Berriasian–Barremian, with a disconformity spanning most of the Aptian above. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on Lakota, and the Lakota stages of [*Hoplitosaurus marshi*](../../../genera/H/Hoplitosaurus.yml) and [*Osmakasaurus depressus*](../../../genera/O/Osmakasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sohn1979a, title page and p. 1: "the Lakota Formation is considered to be pre-Aptian in age", "Barremian or older rather than late Aptian", from ostracodes in the Chilson Member (p. 4).
- boyd2015b, p. 7: later work would "extend the lower-most units to the Berriasian and limit the upper-most to the Barremian", relaying Cifelli and colleagues (2014).
- d'emic2019a, p. 29: "a significant disconformity present in the Lakota Formation in South Dakota that spans most of the Aptian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian–Aptian.* McDonald and colleagues (2011) give "Lakota Formation, Barremian–Aptian (Tschudy et al. 1984)" (mcdonald2011a, p. 61); Tschudy and colleagues (1984) never mention the Lakota (zero occurrences in the paper).

**Open.** How the Minnewaste Limestone and Fuson Member relate to the disconformity. D'Emic and colleagues (2019, p. 30) correlate them with units "deposited in both the Aptian and the Albian".
