# Lakota

## Is the Lakota Formation pre-Aptian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes: Berriasian–Barremian, with a disconformity spanning most of the Aptian above. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on Lakota, and the Lakota stages of [*Hoplitosaurus marshi*](../../../genera/H/Hoplitosaurus.yml) and [*Osmakasaurus depressus*](../../../genera/O/Osmakasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sohn1979a, title page and p. 1: "the Lakota Formation is considered to be pre-Aptian in age", "Barremian or older rather than late Aptian", from ostracodes in the Chilson Member (p. 4).
- boyd2015b, p. 7: later work would "extend the lower-most units to the Berriasian and limit the upper-most to the Barremian", relaying Cifelli and colleagues (2014).
- d'emic2019a, p. 29: "a significant disconformity present in the Lakota Formation in South Dakota that spans most of the Aptian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian–Aptian.* McDonald and colleagues (2011) give "Lakota Formation, Barremian–Aptian (Tschudy et al. 1984)" (mcdonald2011a, p. 61); Tschudy and colleagues (1984) never mention the Lakota (zero occurrences in the paper).

**Open.** How the Minnewaste Limestone and Fuson Member relate to the disconformity. D'Emic and colleagues (2019, p. 30) correlate them with units "deposited in both the Aptian and the Albian".
