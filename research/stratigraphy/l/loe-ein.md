# Loe-ein

## Which sources does Weishampel and colleagues' Lura row cite?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Zhao (1983), Dong (1990), Sun and colleagues (1992) and unpublished data from Zhao. Zhao (1983), as held, names neither the Lura nor the Loe-ein Formation, so the unpublished data are the one cited source that could carry the name Lura. The dispute summary now says so. Governs the `dispute` summary on [Loe-ein](../../../stratigraphy/l/loe-ein.yml).

**Evidence.**
- weishampel2004b, p. 565: "1. Lura Formation (?= Loe-ein Formation; Zhao 1983; Dong 1990b; Sun et al. 1992; Zhao unpubl. data)", with an Early Cretaceous age.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Unchanged: whether the Lura Formation is the Loe-ein.

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Loe-ein?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian to Hauterivian. Dong (1990) gives the formation as "Kimmeridgian or Neocomian", a plain either-or between two readings that are not adjacent, so it contributes both and every stage between them, the Tithonian included; the Neocomian is the Berriasian, Valanginian and Hauterivian (Gale and colleagues, 2020). Sun and colleagues (1992) give "Late Jurassic or Early Cretaceous", which allows the same span. Weishampel and colleagues (2004) give only "Early Cretaceous", in a list row that relays Sun and colleagues and a work then in press; it adds no stage and does not remove the Kimmeridgian. Governs `period: [Late Jurassic, Early Cretaceous]` and `stages: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian, Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian]` on [Loe-ein](../../../stratigraphy/l/loe-ein.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1990b, p. 264: *Monkonosaurus* from "Lawulashan, Monko County, Qamdo District, Tibet. Loe-ein Formation, Lower Cretaceous or Upper Jurassic".
- dong1990b, p. 266: "*Monkonosaurus* from the Loe-ein Formation (Kimmeridgian or Neocomian), and *Wuerhosaurus* from the Lower Cretaceous Tugulu Group of Neocomian age".
- gale2020a, p. 1024: the Neocomian "has subsequently been subdivided into three stages—the Berriasian, Valanginian and Hauterivian".
- sun1992a, p. 163: *Monkonosaurus lawulacus*, "Lawulashan, Monko, Tibet", "Loe-ein Formation", "Late Jurassic or Early Cretaceous".
- weishampel2004b, p. 565, Xizang Zizhiqu: "Lura Formation (?= Loe-ein Formation; Zhao 1983; Dong 1990b; Sun et al. 1992; Zhao unpubl. data)", with *Monkonosaurus lawulacus* and "Age: Early Cretaceous (Sun et al. 1992; Yao et al., in press)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Cretaceous alone, from Weishampel and colleagues (2004).* The row is a compilation, gives an epoch rather than a stage, and relays Sun and colleagues (1992), who give "Late Jurassic or Early Cretaceous", with the Late Jurassic dropped; its other source was in press when the book appeared.
- *The Kimmeridgian and the Neocomian without the Tithonian.* Dong's "Kimmeridgian or Neocomian" is a plain either-or, not a boundary hedge, so the stage between the two readings comes with them.

**Open.** Whether the "Lura Formation" of Weishampel and colleagues (2004) is the Loe-ein. The row queries the equation itself and cites unpublished data among its sources, and none of the works it cites that we hold uses the name Lura (see the dispute on [Loe-ein](../../../stratigraphy/l/loe-ein.yml)).
