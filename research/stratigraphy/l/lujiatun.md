# Lujiatun

## What stage are the Lujiatun Beds?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Barremian, by a narrow margin at the base: 125.755 Ma is 0.015 Myr above the base of the Barremian on the ICS chart (125.77 Ma) and 0.75 Myr above it in GTS 2020 (126.5 ± 0.7 Ma). It is clear of the Aptian by more than 4 Myr. Governs `stages: [Barremian]` on Lujiatun and the Barremian on the Lujiatun-horizon species, such as [*Psittacosaurus lujiatunensis*](../../../genera/P/Psittacosaurus.yml) and [*Jeholosaurus shangyuanensis*](../../../genera/J/Jeholosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhong2021a, p. 1: the Lujiatun Unit's "onset at 125.755 ± 0.061 Ma (CY17–9)" by CA-ID-TIMS U-Pb on zircon (±0.27 Ma with systematic uncertainty; sample CY17-18 gives 125.684 Ma), an "extraordinarily short duration of the LJT Unit (<71 ± 86 Kyr)", and the Yixian's "termination at 124.122 ± 0.048 Ma".
- gale2020a, p. 1067: "The Barremian Stage lasted 5.1 Myr from 126.5 ± 0.7 to 121.4 ± 0.6 Ma". GTS 2020 gives no numerical age for the early/late Barremian boundary in its text (p. 1031); its Fig. 27.9 (p. 1047) plots 125.755 Ma in the lower Barremian.
- chang2017a, accepted manuscript, p. 10: "weighted mean age of 125.92 ± 0.62 Ma" on basalts "from the lowest part of the Lower Lava Unit, immediately above its contact with the Lujiatun Unit" (p. 6), a minimum age for the beds.

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian–Aptian boundary, or earliest Aptian.* He and colleagues (2006) give "123.2 ± 1.0 Ma, Aptian"; Chang and colleagues recalculate it to "124.4 ± 1.0 Ma" (chang2017a, accepted manuscript, p. 10), and Zhong and colleagues note that "the existing ages of the Lujiatun Unit … are younger than those of the immediate overlying Lower Lava Unit" (zhong2021a, p. 1). The Aptian readings combine that date with the older Aptian bases of GTS 2004 (125.0 Ma) or GTS 2012 (126.3 Ma; gale2020a, p. 1066, Table 27.4).
- *Late Barremian.* Nothing in GTS 2020 supports it for 125.755 Ma, and its Fig. 27.9 places that age in the lower Barremian.
- *"Late Barremian or earliest Albian."* Sereno (2010) prints this in two type-horizon blocks (sereno2010a, p. 28), while the text puts the beds "no older than 123–125 Ma, straddling the Barremian-Aptian boundary" (p. 22). The Albian appears to be a slip (our reading).
- *Hauterivian.* Sereno and colleagues (2009) give "Lujiatun Beds [Hauterivian-Barremian stages, ~130 million years ago (Ma)]" (sereno2009a, p. 419) and "(Barremian-Aptian, ~125 Ma)" on p. 422; Cuenca-Bescós and colleagues (2003) plot "Lujiatum Beds, Yixian Formation (Hauterivian …)" on a map (cuenca-bescós2003a, p. 580, Fig. 6). Neither has a date that supports it.

**Open.** Whether the Barremian holds on a future chart. The margin against the ICS base is smaller than the date's systematic uncertainty, and the Lower Lava minimum (125.92 ± 0.62 Ma) would fall in the latest Hauterivian on that chart.
