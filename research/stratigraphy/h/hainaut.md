# Hainaut

## What is the Hainaut Group, and how old is it?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** A group of continental clays, sands and conglomerates in the Mons Basin, Late Jurassic to Early Cretaceous, with no stages. Robaszynski and colleagues (2001) place the Sainte-Barbe Clays Formation in it and date it from "Purbeckian" to Aptian on its flora and fauna; "Purbeckian" is an obsolete regional term that converts to stages only through a published correlation, so the range sets the epochs but not a run of stages, and the possible Early Cenomanian is a possibility. Governs the new entry [Hainaut](../../../stratigraphy/h/hainaut.yml) and `parent` on [Sainte-Barbe Clays](../../../stratigraphy/s/sainte-barbe-clays.yml).

**Evidence.**
- robaszynski2001a, p. 123: "Sainte-Barbe Clays Formation (SBA): grey, sandy, lignitic and highly fossiliferous clays, filling a sinkhole."
- robaszynski2001a, p. 124: "Age: Late Jurassic (“Purbeckian”) to Early Cretaceous (Aptian) and possibly even extending into Early Cenomanian".

**Ruled out.** - *Stages from the 'Purbeckian'.* An obsolete regional term without a quoted correlation.
- *Cenomanian.* A possibility.

**Open.** Nothing.
