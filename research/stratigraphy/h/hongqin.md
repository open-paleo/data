# Hongqin Formation

## What stages do the Hongqin sauropods take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** None; both records take `period: [Middle Jurassic]` alone, as the Hongqin takes no `stages`. Every source read dates the formation and both type horizons by epoch, and the Middle Jurassic itself rests on an unpublished survey's invertebrates. Governs the absent `stage` on [*Anhuilong diboensis*](../../../genera/A/Anhuilong.yml) and [*Huangshanlong anhuiensis*](../../../genera/H/Huangshanlong.yml), and the absent `stages` on the [Hongqin Formation](../../../stratigraphy/h/hongqin.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ren2020b: "The Hongqin Formation was originally regarded to be of Middle Jurassic age according to the study of invertebrate (Bureau of Geology and Mineral Resources of Anhui Province 332 Geological team 1971, unpublished)"; the *Anhuilong* quarry is in "the lower part of the Middle Jurassic Hongqin Formation".
- huang2014a: *Huangshanlong* is "from the Middle Jurassic Hongqin Formation", in the upper of two fossil levels.

**Ruled out.**
- *Bajocian.* Li and colleagues (1982) list the Hongqin among several units sharing a bivalve assemblage that Ma Qihong called "of Middle Jurassic age, possibly Bajocian" (li1982a). That dates a widespread assemblage, relayed and hedged, not this formation.
- *Aalenian to Callovian.* The whole epoch written as stages reads as a stage-level dating no source gives.

**Open.** Stage-level dating of the Hongqin, which the regional stratigraphic work on the Tunxi and Huangshan area may give.
