# Hongtuling

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Hongtuling?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Late Cretaceous, as Xue and colleagues (1996) give it, with no stage. The 2026-10-01 entry in [Qinlingosaurus and Shanyangosaurus ages](../../topics/qinlingosaurus-and-shanyangosaurus-ages.md) settles the age of *Qinlingosaurus* and rules out the Early Cretaceous of Zhao and colleagues (2008); this entry adds the grounds that bear on the unit. Xue and colleagues state the age twice on one page, in the genus entry and the species entry. Zhao and colleagues give Early Cretaceous only in a compiled distribution field. Governs `period: [Late Cretaceous]` on [Hongtuling](../../../stratigraphy/h/hongtuling.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xue1996a, p. 79: for the genus, "Hongtuling, Luonan, Shaanxi; Late Cretaceous" (translated); for the species, "Hongtuling, Luonan, Shaanxi; Upper Cretaceous Hongtuling Formation" (translated).
- zhao2008a, p. 285: the distribution field stacks "Hongtuling range, Luonan, Shaanxi", "Hongtuling Formation" and "Early Cretaceous" on three lines; the remarks that close the entry read "Holtz *et al.* (2004) regarded this taxon as Sauropoda *Nomina dubia*".
- weishampel2004b, p. 598: "Sauropoda indet. (= *Qinlingosaurus luonanensis*)" in the Shanyang Formation fauna, with "Age: Late Cretaceous (Xue et al. 1996)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Cretaceous, after Zhao and colleagues (2008).* The age is the third line of a field that stacks locality, unit and age, with no argument or source given for it. Against it stand the naming paper's two statements of a Late or Upper Cretaceous age, and the remarks closing Zhao and colleagues' own entry, which cite Holtz and colleagues (2004). That chapter does not mention *Qinlingosaurus* in the copy read, but the volume it belongs to, the second edition of *The Dinosauria*, gives the occurrence as Late Cretaceous in its distribution chapter (Weishampel and colleagues, 2004), after Xue and colleagues.

**Open.** Whether the bone-bearing beds at Hongtuling belong to the Hongtuling Formation that Zhang (2009) defines at the same type locality as Eocene, a question the unit's dispute carries. A section or map tying the bone level to the lexicon's unit would settle it.
