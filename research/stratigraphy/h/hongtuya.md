# Hongtuya

## Are the Zhucheng bone beds in the Hongtuya or the Xingezhuang Formation?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** The Hongtuya, low in the formation. An and colleagues (2015), whose subject is the dinosaur bone beds of Zhucheng, place them in the Hongtuya and name *Zhuchengtyrannus* among its taxa. Governs `location.formation: Hongtuya` on [*Zhuchengtyrannus magnus*](../../../genera/Z/Zhuchengtyrannus.yml) and [*Zhuchengceratops inexpectus*](../../../genera/Z/Zhuchengceratops.yml), and `location.part: lower` on the latter.

**Evidence.**
- an2015a, p. 81: "Many dinosaur fossils, only found in the uppermost Upper Cretaceous strata, such as Sinoceratops zhuchengensis, Zhuchengtyrannus magnus, Tsintaosaurus spinorhinus, Tanius chinkankouenssi, and Tanius laiyangensis, have been reported in recent years from the Hongtuya Formation".
- an2015a, p. 81: "Dinosaur bones are mostly buried in the lower Hongtuya Formation, so the depositional age of the dinosaur bone beds must be >73.5 Ma".
- an2015a, p. 82: zircons from "conglomerates collected from the dinosaur bone beds of the Hongtuya Formation in Kugou".
- xu2010c, p. 2, Type locality and horizon: "Kugou, Zhucheng County, Shandong Province, China. Upper Cretaceous Wangshi Group".

**Ruled out.**
- *Xingezhuang Formation.* Jones (2026) prints "Xingezhuang Fm., China" for both species (jones2026a, Euceratopsia table; jones2026c, Tyrannosauridae table). The likely origin is Hone and colleagues (2011), who say the Zhucheng material "lies near the transition between the Xingezhuang Formation and the overlying Hongtuya Formation" (hone2011a, p. 496); the Xingezhuang is the formation below the Hongtuya in the same group. An and colleagues, who studied the bone beds directly, assign them to the Hongtuya.

**Open.** *Zhuchengceratops* is not in An and colleagues' list of taxa. Its record follows from Xu and colleagues' Kugou type locality and An and colleagues' statement about the Kugou bone beds; that the holotype lies in those beds is an inference, since neither paper places this specimen in the section.
