# Hekou

## Is every "Hekou" in the literature this unit?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. The registry's Hekou is the Lower Cretaceous Hekou Group of the Lanzhou-Minhe Basin, Gansu and Qinghai, and every record that uses it is a Lanzhou-area taxon. Other Hekou formations exist, including a Late Cretaceous one in Jiangxi and an Early Cretaceous one in Fujian, and rank alone does not separate them. Governs which taxa may take `group: Hekou`, such as [*Lanzhousaurus magnidens*](../../../genera/L/Lanzhousaurus.yml) and [*Daxiatitan binglingi*](../../../genera/D/Daxiatitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- li2014a, p. 1: "Hekou Group (Lower Cretaceous) of the Lanzhou-Minhe Basin, Gansu Province"; you2008a, p. 1: "Lower Cretaceous Hekou Group of Lanzhou Basin".
- xing2024a, p. 115: "the Guifeng Group consists of the Hekou, Tangbian, and Lianhe formations", in the Ganzhou Basin, Jiangxi; also han2024a, p. 5, and yao2026a, p. 2.
- zhang2009b, pp. 387–388: six homonymous Hekou formations, among them one in Fujian (Early Cretaceous) and one typified at "Hekou town in Fuhui Township, Qianshan County, Jiangxi Province … Age: Late Cretaceous"; no Hekou Group entry.

**Ruled out.**
- *Rank as the separator* ("Group = Gansu, Formation = Jiangxi"). The Gansu–Qinghai unit is also printed as a formation: "Hekou Formation in the Minhe Basin, Qinghai" (tang2001b, p. 122) and "Hekou Formation (Lower Cretaceous) of the Xining and Minhe Basin" (vanitterbeeck2001a, p. 60). A formation-rank Hekou must be placed by its basin.

**Open.** Who erected the Jiangxi Hekou Formation. Later papers cite Ling (1996), "Establishment of the Cretaceous Formations of Maodian, Hekou and Tangbian in Jiangxi Province" (by title only, in xing2024a's references), while the *Stratigraphic Lexicon* credits a 1997 Jiangxi provincial report and places the type in Qianshan County, northeastern Jiangxi, not the Ganzhou Basin. Jin and colleagues (2022) instead put a Ganzhou Hekou Formation in the Nanxiong Group (jin2022a, p. 2).
