# Hasandong

## Is the Hasandong Formation Hauterivian–Barremian or Aptian–Albian?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** Aptian–Albian, with a dispute: a detrital-zircon maximum age makes it no older than about 112 Ma (Albian), while relayed sources keep the Aptian. Governs `stages: [Aptian, Albian]` and the dispute on Hasandong, and the stages of [*Pukyongosaurus millenniumi*](../../../genera/P/Pukyongosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lee2010b, p. 651: youngest detrital zircon "109.0 ± 3.4 Ma for the Hasandong Formation"; p. 656: these are "each representing the maximum depositional age", and "the Sindong Group was deposited during the late Aptian to late Albian, which is younger than previously thought". The 109.0 Ma value is a single grain (Table 1, p. 652; the next grains are 111.4 and 112.2 Ma); even at its older limit (112.4 Ma) it falls in the Albian (our arithmetic).

**Ruled out.**
- *Hauterivian–Barremian.* Dong and colleagues report that the formation "generally been assigned to the Hauterivian on the basis of charophyte, spore and pollen study, although an early Aptian age was suggested from paleomagnetism" (dong2001a, p. 42); Barrett and colleagues give "Hasandong Formation (Hauterivian)" (barrett2002a, p. 1207), and Wilson and Upchurch (2009, wilson2009a, p. 234, Table 7) and Carrano and colleagues (2012, online Appendix 3) give Barremian or Hauterivian–Barremian, all relaying Lee and colleagues (1997, 2001). The zircon maximum is younger.

**Open.** The Aptian. A maximum depositional age does not set stages on its own, so the Aptian stands as the other side of the dispute: Sha (2007) correlates the Hasandong with the "mainly Aptian" Guilin Formation through a "Barremian-Albian (mainly Aptian) trigonioidid assemblage" (sha2007a, p. 80), and Yun and colleagues give "Aptian-Albian" (yun2020a, p. 2), relaying papers not read here (one of which may itself be a maximum age).
