# Hupyeongdong

## What bounds the Hupyeongdong Formation's age?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** Albian, from its position below the Sagok Formation (about 100–97 Ma) and its correlation within the Hayang Group; no date from the formation itself is available. Governs `stages: [Albian]` on Hupyeongdong and its [Gugyedong Member](../../../stratigraphy/g/gugyedong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- choi2019a, p. 552: "후평동층은 … 하부의 구미동층원과 상부의 구계동층원으로 구성되는데" ("the Hupyeongdong Formation consists of the lower Gumidong Member and the upper Gugyedong Member", translated); pp. 574, 578: the Sagok Formation is "약 100~97 Ma" ("about 100–97 Ma", translated), resting on the roughly 97 Ma Gusandong Tuff relayed from Jwa (2009).

**Ruled out.**
- *106–100 Ma, late Albian.* An earlier bracket took the maximum depositional ages of the overlying units (104.9, 99.8 and 96 Ma) as ceilings for the Hupyeongdong. A maximum depositional age bounds the unit it was measured in from below; it does not bound the unit beneath it from above.
- *Aptian–Albian from Choi (2019).* The "Aptian-Albian" Choi gives for the Hayang Group (p. 552) is relayed palynology (Choi 1985, 1987; Seo 1985), not the paper's own dating.

**Open.** A direct date for the formation.
