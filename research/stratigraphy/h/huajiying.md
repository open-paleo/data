# Huajiying

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Huajiying?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Jin and colleagues (2008) give the formation as Early Cretaceous and date the assemblage of its lower, Sichakou sedimentary member at about 130.7 Ma, inside the Hauterivian on the ICS chart v2024/12; they leave the age of the upper, Qiaotou member open. Ji and colleagues (2005), who erect *Jinfengopteryx* in the Qiaotou, give no age of their own. The Barremian comes from Pittman and colleagues (2020), who date the formation "Hauterivian-Barremian". Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous]` and `stages: [Hauterivian, Barremian]` on [Huajiying](../../../stratigraphy/h/huajiying.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jin2008a, p. 2822, Fig. 2: "Sections of the Sichakou Sedimentary Member of the Early Cretaceous Huajiying Formation in the Sichakou basin, Fengning, Hebei".
- jin2008a, p. 2823: "the bird fossil-bearing sedimentary beds, together with the underlying set of intermediate-basic volcanics, are assigned to the Huajiying Formation, as the second and the first member of this volcanic-sedimentary formation in the Sichakou and Senjitu area".
- jin2008a, p. 2824: "The age of the lower assemblage is about 130.7 Ma, which is the earliest vertebrate fossil assemblage hitherto known in the Jehol Biota; and the age of the upper assemblage is not very clear yet by now".
- jin2008a, p. 2826: "the Qiaotou sedimentary member of the Huajiying Formation and the Dawangzhangzi Bed of the Yixian Formation ... still remain to be preciously dated for their known ages (122.1―126.9 Ma) are not widely accepted due to lack of accuracy".
- ji2005a, p. 198: the holotype is from "the dark gray thin-bedded tuffaceous silty mudstones of the middle part of the Qiaotou Formation in Waigoumen area, Fengning County"; under Locality and horizon, "Longfengshan Hill, Fengning County".
- ji2005a, pp. 198–199, Geological age: "The age of the Qiaotou Formation is equivocal. It has been suggested to be Late Jurassic by some paleontologists, or Early Cretaceous by others. The work of the isotopic dating is in process".

**Ruled out.**
- *An age from Ji and colleagues (2005).* The paper names no stage, and its one statement of age relays earlier Late Jurassic and Early Cretaceous readings with isotopic dating still to come. It contributes the Qiaotou's rank and the type locality, not an age.
- *The 122.1–126.9 Ma figures for the Qiaotou member.* Jin and colleagues (2008) report them only to say they "are not widely accepted due to lack of accuracy", and they are not counted as a dating of the formation.

**Open.** The Qiaotou member has no accepted date in the works cited; a precise date for it would show whether the formation reaches the Barremian on its own evidence.
