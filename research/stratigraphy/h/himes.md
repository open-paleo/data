# Himes

## What do D'Emic and colleagues (2019) and Ostrom (1970) say about the age of the Himes Member?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Albian to earliest Cenomanian, from D'Emic and colleagues (2019). They date the member's flora to the middle Albian–earliest Cenomanian, and their detrital zircons give maximum depositional ages of 107.0 ± 1.3 Ma at the base of the member south of Cody and 103.6 ± 1.3 Ma for its fluvial sandbody northwest of Lovell. Ostrom (1970), who equates the member with his Units VI and VII after Moberly (1960) named it, dates the whole Cloverly "most probably" late Aptian to early Albian, with Unit VII queried as early Albian. His Aptian is a probable maximum from ostracods and charophytes collected outside the Bighorn Basin, and his Middle Albian a minimum from fossils in the overlying Thermopolis Shale. This adds to the 2026-09-30 entry of the [Cloverly](../c/cloverly.md) note, which dates Cloverly taxa by member, and supersedes nothing. Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous, Late Cretaceous]` and `stages: [Albian, Cenomanian]` on [Himes](../../../stratigraphy/h/himes.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- d'emic2019a, p. 32: "the flora of the Himes Member dates to the middle Albian–earliest Cenomanian and remains of flowering plants are present".
- d'emic2019a, p. 27: lacustrine deposition could have continued "until at least 107.0 ± 1.3 Ma (base of the Himes Member) in the western portion of the Bighorn Basin and just prior to 103.6 ± 1.3 Ma (fluvial sandbody of the Himes Member) in the eastern portion of the basin"; p. 29: "the maximum depositional age at the base of the member south of Cody and age of the top of the fluvial sandbody northwest of Lovell".
- ostrom1970a, p. 38: Moberly's Cloverly includes "the Himes Member based on exposures along the west limb of Little Sheep Anticline between Himes and Lovell, Wyoming (p. 1148)"; "the Himes Member equals Units VI and VII".
- ostrom1970a, p. 142: the ostracod and charophyte samples "were collected well outside of our study area", so "we must consider Aptian as only a probable maximum age for these strata"; fossils in the upper Thermopolis Shale establish "a minimum age limit of Middle Albian for the underlying Sykes Mountain and Cloverly Formations"; p. 143: the Cloverly "most probably is late Aptian (Unit V?) to Early Albian (Unit VII?)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Aptian to early Albian, after Ostrom (1970).* Ostrom's stages are queried unit by unit, and the Aptian maximum rests on microfossils from outside the Bighorn Basin, which he matches to Unit V rather than to the Himes. D'Emic and colleagues treat Ostrom's units as facies rather than timelines (see the Cloverly note's 2026-09-30 entry) and date the Himes flora no older than the middle Albian.

**Open.** Nothing.
