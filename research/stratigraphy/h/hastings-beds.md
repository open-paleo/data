# Hastings Beds

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Hastings Beds?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Berriasian to Valanginian. Barker and colleagues (2023) date the group's formations in turn: the Ashdown late Berriasian to early Valanginian, the Wadhurst Clay Valanginian and the Tunbridge Wells late Valanginian, so the group runs from the late Berriasian to the late Valanginian. That matches the "Berriasian–Valanginian Hastings Beds Group" of Taylor and Naish (2007). Barker and colleagues print the shorter name Hastings Group, a difference in the rank word only, and make the group the basal unit of the Wealden Supergroup in the Weald sub-basin, as the [Wealden units](../../topics/wealden-units.md) note has it. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian]` and `period: [Early Cretaceous]` on [Hastings Beds](../../../stratigraphy/h/hastings-beds.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barker2023a, p. 6, Geological context and provenance of HASMG G369a: "The Hastings Group, itself the basal unit of the Wealden Supergroup within the Weald sub-basin ... is comprised of the older (late Berriasian–early Valanginian) Ashdown Formation, followed by the Wadhurst Clay Formation (Valanginian) and Tunbridge Wells Formation (late Valanginian".
- taylor2007a, p. 1547, abstract: "the Berriasian–Valanginian Hastings Beds Group of Ecclesbourne Glen, East Sussex, England".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
