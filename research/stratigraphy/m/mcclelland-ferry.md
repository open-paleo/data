# McClelland Ferry

## What do the cited works say about the age of the McClelland Ferry?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian. Ramezani and colleagues (2022) build a Bayesian age-stratigraphic model of the Judith River Formation on three U-Pb bentonite ages: 78.594 ± 0.024 Ma from Coal Marker A at Kennedy Coulee, 76.329 ± 0.035 Ma from the Stafford Ferry tuff in the McClelland Ferry Member, and 75.213 ± 0.021 Ma from the Powerplant Ferry tuff in the Bearpaw Formation. The model puts the base of the member at 78.70 +2.0/−0.11 Ma and its top at 76.269 +0.052/−0.34 Ma. They treat the base as a maximum estimate, because it rests on correlating the Kennedy Coulee coals with the Taber coal zone, which "is expected to be time transgressive". Taken to the far ends of both error bars, 80.70 to 75.93 Ma, the member lies wholly within the Campanian (83.6–72.2 Ma on the ICS chart v2024/12). Governs `stages: [Campanian]` on [McClelland Ferry](../../../stratigraphy/m/mcclelland-ferry.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ramezani2022a, p. 8, Results: "an age of 78.594±0.024 Ma from a bentonite (sample KC061517-1) within Coal Marker A bed at Kennedy Coulee"; "76.329±0.035 Ma (Stafford Ferry tuff, McClelland Ferry Member)"; "The three U–Pb ages can be used to construct an age-stratigraphic model"; "the base and top of the McClelland Ferry Member are constrained to 78.70+2.0/−0.11 Ma and 76.269+0.052/−0.34 Ma, respectively"; "there is considerable uncertainty associated with the lower age constraint as the Taber coal zone is expected to be time transgressive".
- ramezani2022a, p. 9, Results: "the ca.78.70 Ma age for the member boundary should be considered a maximum estimate".
- ramezani2022a, p. 15, Methods: for the Judith River Fm. "we employed a Bayesian age-stratigraphic model using the Bchron software package".
