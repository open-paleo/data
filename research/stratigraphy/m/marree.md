# Marree

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Marree?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Aptian to Albian. Alexander and colleagues (2007) define the subgroup as the basin-margin equivalent of the Coorikiana Sandstone and the Oodnadatta Formation, which they date Albian and middle to late Albian, and Barrett and colleagues (2010) and Burger (1980) put the Bulldog Shale in the subgroup or the old Marree Formation; Alexander and colleagues date the Bulldog Shale Early Aptian to mid Albian. Alexander and colleagues do not themselves place the Bulldog Shale in the subgroup: its age reaches the subgroup through the other two works, and that step is an inference from their statements. Burger (1980) relays the question whether the foraminiferal zone of the lower Marree is Aptian or earliest Albian; either answer lies within the two stages. Governs `stages` on [Marree](../../../stratigraphy/m/marree.yml).

**Evidence.**
- alexander2007a, p. 23: "Coorikiana Sandstone passes laterally into Wallumbilla Formation towards the basin centre and into Marree Subgroup towards the basin margin"; "The name Marree Subgroup is applied to the shaly marine sequence where Coorikiana Sandstone is absent".
- alexander2007a, p. 23: "Oodnadatta Formation passes laterally into the upper Wallumbilla Formation, Toolebuc Formation and Allaru Mudstone towards the basin centre, and into Marree Subgroup towards the basin margin."
- alexander2007a, p. 22: "The Bulldog Shale is assigned to the Early Aptian – mid Albian PK2.1–PK3.1 and *Cyclosporites hughesii – Coptospora paradoxa* palynozones."
- alexander2007a, p. 23: "The Coorikiana Sandstone is Early Cretaceous (Albian)"; "The Oodnadatta Formation is Early Cretaceous (middle–late Albian)".
- barrett2010b, p. 294: the Bulldog Shale is "a constituent of the Marree Subgroup, which was formerly called the Marree Formation".
- burger1980a, p. 14: the interval in the Santos Oodnadatta well lies within Ludbrook's (1966) "Aptian *Herrgottella jonesi* Zone, which is associated with the lower 'Marree Formation' (equivalent to the Bulldog Shale …)"; Evans and Hawkins (1967) "suggested that her *Herrgottella jonesi* Zone could very well represent the earliest Albian. My own examination of samples from the 'Marree Formation' in DFS Innamincka 1 well, South Australia, corroborates their interpretation."

**Ruled out.**
Nothing.

**Open.** Whether the *Herrgottella jonesi* Zone is Aptian, as Ludbrook (1966) held, or earliest Albian, as Evans and Hawkins (1967) suggested and Burger (1980) supports; Burger leaves it to later work. The Aptian of the subgroup does not rest on that zone alone: Alexander and colleagues' Bulldog Shale reaches the Early Aptian.
