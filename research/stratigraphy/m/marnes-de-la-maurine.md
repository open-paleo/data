# Marnes de la Maurine

## Does the Marnes de la Maurine keep the Campanian?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No; the member is Maastrichtian, and the dispute is removed. Supersedes the 2026-11-23 entry. Bilotte (1985), whom Le Loeuff (2005) credits, dates the member Maastrichtian and confines the Campanian to the Marnes rouges de Campagne two members below; Le Loeuff's "upper Campanian to lower Maastrichtian (Bilotte 1985)" misstates him, and his Campanian alternative for the Grès des Estous rests on a personal communication, not a published determination. [*Ampelosaurus atacis*](../../../genera/A/Ampelosaurus.yml), from the base of the member at Bellevue, takes the Maastrichtian. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]`, the removed `dispute` and the note on leloeuff2005a on [Marnes de la Maurine](../../../stratigraphy/m/marnes-de-la-maurine.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leloeuff2005a, p. 118: "Horizon. Marnes Rouges Inférieures Formation, Marnes de la Maurine member, upper Campanian to lower Maastrichtian (Bilotte 1985)."
- bilotte1985a, p. 242: the Marnes rouges de Campagne are "datées du Campanien supérieur" ["dated to the upper Campanian", translated].

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian.* A misrelay and a personal communication.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does the Marnes de la Maurine reach the Campanian?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Campanian and Maastrichtian, with a dispute, the Campanian resting on Le Loeuff (2005) alone. Le Loeuff places the *Ampelosaurus* type locality, Bellevue, at the bottom of the member and gives the horizon as upper Campanian to lower Maastrichtian, citing Bilotte (1985). Bilotte dates this member Maastrichtian (see [Marnes Rouges Inférieures](marnes-rouges-inférieures.md)). The Campanian comes from a personal communication from Bilotte that the Grès des Estous, the member below, could be upper Campanian, against the usual Maastrichtian age that Le Loeuff traces to an upper Campanian palynoflora in the Marnes de Campagne beneath it. The normal polarity at Bellevue is, in Le Loeuff's words, consistent with either age. Le Loeuff (1995) gives the same horizon a lower Maastrichtian age after Bessière and colleagues (1989). Governs `stages: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` and the `dispute` on [Marnes de la Maurine](../../../stratigraphy/m/marnes-de-la-maurine.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leloeuff2005a, p. 118: "Horizon. Marnes Rouges Inférieures Formation, Marnes de la Maurine member, upper Campanian to lower Maastrichtian (Bilotte 1985)".
- leloeuff2005a, p. 118: "Usually referred as to the Maastrichtian because of the discovery of an upper Campanian palynoflora in the underlying marnes de Campagne member (Bilotte 1985), the grès des Estous could also be of upper Campanian age (M. Bilotte, pers. comm.)"; Bellevue "shows a normal magnetic polarity, consistent with either hypothesis"; "The locality is situated at the bottom of the Marnes de la Maurine member".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether the Campanian stands. The rule that a personal communication is not a published determination ([methods](../../methods.md)) lets one weigh in a live debate between published readings but never add a stage on its own. Here the communication concerns the Grès des Estous, not the Marnes de la Maurine, and no other work cited dates the Marnes de la Maurine Campanian. If it does not count, the member is Maastrichtian on Bilotte (1985) and Le Loeuff (1995), and the dispute lapses. A published dating of the Grès des Estous or of the Bellevue horizon would settle it.
