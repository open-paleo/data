# Malmros Klint

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Malmros Klint?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Late Norian. Kent and Clemmensen (1996) sampled the whole unit at Tait Bjerg with the lowest 80 m of the Carlsberg Fjord beds above it, and their preferred correlation with the Newark sequence puts that section at about 211 to 207.5 Ma, in the late Norian; the quotations for the correlation are in the 2026-11-23 entry of [Fleming Fjord](../f/fleming-fjord.md). They equate the Malmros Klint and lower Ørsted Dal with the Sevatian, and from its sedimentary cycles estimate that the Malmros Klint accumulated in a little more than 2 m.y. Both ends of the range fall in the Norian on the current chart, whose Rhaetian begins at 205.7 Ma. Governs `stages: [Norian]` on [Malmros Klint](../../../stratigraphy/m/malmros-klint.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kent1996a, p. 125: the sampled section "encompassed the entire Malmros Klint Member (130 m) and the lower part (80 m) of the overlying Carlsberg Fjord beds of the Ørsted Dal Member".
- kent1996a, p. 133: "If this interpretation is correct, the Malmros Klint Member accumulated in a little more than 2 m.y."
- kent1996a, p. 133: the preferred option "would place the Malmros Klint and lower Ørsted Dal Members as time equivalent to the Sevatian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Norian, from the second correlation.* Kent and Clemmensen's alternative match, of magnetozone F2r with Newark magnetochron E14r, would put the section at about 218 to 212.5 Ma. They prefer the first option "Based on the available biostratigraphic age constraints and cyclostratigraphic duration estimates" (kent1996a, p. 133). Either option leaves the stage Norian.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `kent1996a`: Kent, D. V.; Clemmensen, L. B. (1996). Paleomagnetism and cycle stratigraphy of the Triassic Fleming Fjord and Gipsdalen Formations of East Greenland. *Bulletin of the Geological Society of Denmark* 42: 121-136.
