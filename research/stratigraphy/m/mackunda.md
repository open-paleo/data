# Mackunda

## What does Tucker and colleagues (2016) give the Mackunda?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Two detrital-zircon maximum depositional ages, about 102.9 Ma for the uppermost formation and 130.5 Ma for its middle part, the second far older than the formation's late Albian biostratigraphic age. Maximum ages are limits and add no stage. Governs the note on tucker2016a on [Mackunda](../../../stratigraphy/m/mackunda.yml); `stages` unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- tucker2016a, p. 1464: "A single uppermost Mackunda Formation sample from GSQ Longreach 1–1B core"; p. 1468, Table 1: average ages "102.9 Ma" for Longreach and "130.5 Ma" for Maneroo_331, from the middle of the formation.
- tucker2016a, p. 1469: the middle Mackunda sample is "significantly older than its purported depositional age of late Albian (ca. 105 Ma) based on biostratigraphic constraints".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What do Herne and colleagues (2026) say about the age of the Mackunda?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Herne and colleagues (2026) give no date for the formation itself. Their "mid-Cenomanian" for the *Muttaburrasaurus langdoni* holotype rests on a maximum likelihood age of 96.3 ± 8.6 Ma from detrital zircons in a coquina at a nearby locality (QML1817), which they propose as "the maximum depositional age" of the holotype; the upper end of their formal "Cenomanian–lower Turonian" is the pooled maximum age of the overlying Winton Formation. Both figures are limits. Their statement that Tucker and colleagues (2016) dated the formation to the upper Albian does not match that paper, which gives the Mackunda only maximum depositional ages and calls the late Albian a "purported" age from biostratigraphy. The Albian and Cenomanian on the unit rest on the late Albian–early Cenomanian spore-pollen assemblages of Gray and colleagues (2002). Governs `stages` on [Mackunda](../../../stratigraphy/m/mackunda.yml).

**Evidence.**
- herne2026a, p. 10, Systematic palaeontology: "∼seven km SE of the Muttaburra township, central western Queensland, Australia. Mackunda Formation, Cenomanian–lower Turonian (96.3 ± 8.6 Ma to 93.51 ± 1.19 Ma)".
- herne2026a, p. 9, Geochronology: "The pooled detrital zircon age for the Winton Formation returned MLA of 93.5 ± 1.2 Ma"; "The Winton Formation overlies the Mackunda Formation".
- herne2026a, p. 9, Geochronology: "the detrital zircon age of the Mackunda Formation reported here based on the coquina at QML1817, is independent of the mudrock hosting the Muttaburrasaurus langdoni holotype"; "we propose that the age of QML1817 (96.3 ± 8.6 Ma) represents the maximum depositional age of the Muttaburrasaurus langdoni holotype".
- herne2026a, p. 9, Geochronology: "The pooled detrital zircon age data for the Mackunda Formation returned a MLA of 104.7 ± 1.6 Ma".
- herne2026a, p. 163, Geochronological age of Muttaburrasaurus langdoni: "now constrain the oldest depositional age of the Mackunda Formation in the region of the holotype in the Cenomanian"; "mid-Cenomanian of the Mackunda Formation".
- herne2026a, p. 2, Introduction: "From U/Pb isotopes in detrital zircon, Tucker et al. (2016) dated the Mackunda Formation to the upper Albian (∼104–102 Ma)".
- tucker2016a, pp. 1465, 1467: "a sample derived from the middle Mackunda Formation (Maneroo_331) yields the oldest maximum depositional age at or near the Hauterivian-Barremian boundary".
- tucker2016a, p. 1467: "all samples were combined (n = 6) to better evaluate the late-stage provenance history of the Eromanga Basin during the late Albian to Cenomanian (ca. 104–92 Ma)".
- tucker2016a, p. 1469: Maneroo 331's youngest zircon "is significantly older than its purported depositional age of late Albian (ca. 105 Ma) based on biostratigraphic constraints".
- gray2002a, p. 54: "The Mackunda Formation embraces mid-Cretaceous (late Albian–early Cenomanian) spore-pollen assemblages".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Turonian for the formation.* The "lower Turonian" of the formal horizon is reached only through 93.51 ± 1.19 Ma, the maximum age of the Winton Formation above; a maximum for the overlying unit says nothing about the Mackunda's top. The 2026-10-13 entry on [*Muttaburrasaurus*](../../genera/M/Muttaburrasaurus.md) rejects it for the species on the same ground.
- *A Cenomanian date from the 96.3 Ma figure.* It is a maximum depositional age, by the authors' own description, from a sample whose relation to the holotype bed they cannot determine, and its ± 8.6 Myr runs from the Albian to the Coniacian. The Cenomanian stands on the spore-pollen assemblages, not on this number.
- *An upper Albian dating of the formation by Tucker and colleagues (2016), as Herne and colleagues (2026) relay it.* Tucker and colleagues (2016) give their middle Mackunda sample a maximum depositional age near the Hauterivian–Barremian boundary, give 104–92 Ma as the late Albian to Cenomanian interval over which they pooled all Winton and Mackunda samples, and call the late Albian of that sample a "purported" age "based on biostratigraphic constraints". None of these is a determination of the Mackunda's age, so the relay adds no reading; Herne and colleagues' own re-evaluation of the pooled data, 104.7 ± 1.6 Ma, is again a maximum.
- *A Hauterivian or Barremian from Tucker and colleagues (2016).* Their middle Mackunda figure is a maximum depositional age, from a sample dominated by older recycled grains, and licenses no stage.

**Open.** Nothing.
