# Maanshan Member

## What stages do the Maanshan Member and *Sanpasaurus* take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** Pliensbachian and Toarcian, by the member's place between two dated members; no source dates the Maanshan or the *Sanpasaurus* quarry itself. Yao and colleagues (2025) put the Maanshan between the Dongyuemiao below, which they date to the late Sinemurian to early Pliensbachian, and the Da'anzhai above, which they date to the Toarcian. A member between them can date no earlier than the first and no later than the second; the bracket is our inference from their two datings, not a statement of theirs. The older Toarcian reading of the Dongyuemiao that McPhee and colleagues (2016) report would put the Maanshan in the Toarcian alone, inside the same bracket. Governs `stages` on the [Maanshan Member](../../../stratigraphy/m/maanshan.yml) and `stage` on [*Sanpasaurus yaoi*](../../../genera/S/Sanpasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yao2025a: the members are "arranged in ascending order: Dongyuemiao Member, Ma'anshan Member, and Da'anzhai Member"; the Dongyuemiao: "Geochemical and palynological data even suggest a late Sinemurian to early Pliensbachian age for this member"; "The uppermost layer of the Ziliujing Formation, the Da'anzhai Member, has been solidly dated to the Toarcian stage".
- mcphee2016a: "the underlying Gongxianosaurus-bearing Dongyuemiao Member has been regarded as Toarcian in age (Meng, Li & Chen, 2003). If the latter is accurate, then the age of the Maanshan Member is no older than the late Early Jurassic."
- jones2026b: *Sanpasaurus yaoi* in the "Ziliujing Fm., China *Early Jurassic, Toarcian*", with no basis given.

**Ruled out.**
- *Toarcian alone.* It rests on a compilation, or on the older dating of the Dongyuemiao alone.
- *The Ziliujing's Sinemurian to Toarcian.* The member lies above the Dongyuemiao, so it cannot reach the Sinemurian.

**Open.** A dating measured in the Maanshan.
