# Miaogou

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Miaogou Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Barremian to Albian, unchanged. Hao and colleagues (2024) call the formation Lower Cretaceous and give it as Aptian–Albian, and report that the Maortu deposits are "consistently assigned to the upper Lower Cretaceous" and further to the Barremian–Albian of Van Itterbeeck and colleagues (2001). That Barremian–Albian is Van Itterbeeck and colleagues' own reading, from charophytes, for the continental strata of the Dashuiguo area; the paper never names the Miaogou, and reaches it only through Hao and colleagues' statement that the Miaogou incorporated the formerly abandoned Dashuigou Formation, a name the two papers spell differently. Van Itterbeeck and colleagues add two narrower constraints: an Aptian–Albian age for the nearby Moartu dinosaur locality, which they relay from earlier studies of its *Probactrosaurus*, and a correlation with formations of the Khukhetian "Age", which Jerzykiewicz and Russell (1991) end before the Late Albian, at about 100 Ma, capping the young end within the Albian. Governs `stages: [Barremian, Aptian, Albian]` on [Miaogou](../../../stratigraphy/m/miaogou.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hao2024a, p. 1, Abstract: "the Lower Cretaceous Miaogou Formation of the Maortu locality in Chilantai, Inner Mongolia, China".
- hao2024a, p. 2, Geological setting: the Maortu fossils "were mistakenly claimed to belong to 'Wulanhushao (Suhongtu) Formation' (Vickaryous et al., 2001), 'Dashuigou Formation' (Weishampel et al., 2004) and 'Ulansuhai Formation' (Weishampel et al., 2004; Brusatte et al., 2009)"; the unit "actually belongs to the Miaogou Formation, which incorporated the formerly abandoned Dashuigou Formation and Wulanhushao Formation".
- hao2024a, p. 2, Geological setting: the deposits "are consistently assigned to the upper Lower Cretaceous", "and further to the Barremian-Albian age proposed by Van Itterbeek et al. (2001) based on the stratigraphy, sedimentology and charophytan evidence of the area".
- hao2024a, p. 3, Systematic paleontology: "Miaogou Formation (Aptian-Albian)".
- vanitterbeeck2001a, p. 51: dinosaur bones were found "in equivalent strata at the Moartu locality, situated in the vicinity of the studied area"; Probactrosaurus gobiensis and P. alashanicus were described from it, and "Based on the phylogenetic position of the described dinosaurs an Aptian-Albian âge was proposed for the strata".
- vanitterbeeck2001a, p. 62, Conclusions: "Based on the charophytan evidence, the continental strata in the Dashuiguo area are considered to be Barremian-Albian in âge"; the correlated formations "belong to the Khukhetian 'Age' which according to Jerzykiewicz & Russell, (1991) ends before the Late Albian, at about 100 Ma".
- vanitterbeeck2001a, p. 63: "the Dashuiguo formation can be correlated with the Jingchuang Formation of the Zhidan group of the Ordos Basin".

**Ruled out.**
- *Dashuigou, Wulanhushao (Suhongtu) and Ulansuhai as other names for the Miaogou.* Hao and colleagues give them as units the Maortu fossils were "mistakenly claimed to belong to". The Dashuigou and Wulanhushao are formations the Miaogou absorbed, and the Ulansuhai is a separate Upper Cretaceous unit (see [Maortu and Dashuigou](../../topics/maortu-and-dashuigou-miaogou.md)); these are reassignments and absorptions, not alternate names for the Miaogou.

**Open.** Whether the "Dashuiguo formation" that Van Itterbeeck and colleagues correlate (p. 63) is the abandoned Dashuigou Formation that Hao and colleagues say the Miaogou incorporated. The Barremian rests on that equation of the two spellings, which none of the works cited states; Hao and colleagues apply the Barremian–Albian to the Maortu deposits, not to the Dashuigou Formation by name.
