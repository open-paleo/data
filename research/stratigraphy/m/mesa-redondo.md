# Mesa Redondo

## Does Atchley and colleagues' date keep the Mesa Redondo's Carnian?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Atchley and colleagues (2013) date a tuffaceous sandstone in the member, 2 m above the base of the Chinle, to 227.604 ± 0.28 Ma at its widest uncertainty, Carnian on the ICS chart v2024/12, and read their dated beds as depositional ages (see [Chinle](../c/chinle.md)). Governs the Carnian in `stages` and the note on atchley2013a on [Mesa Redondo](../../../stratigraphy/m/mesa-redondo.yml); `stages` unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- atchley2013a, p. 1153: "five form a coherent cluster with a weighted mean 206Pb/238U age of 227.604 ± 0.082/0.13/0.28 Ma (MSWD = 1.5, Fig. 4)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
