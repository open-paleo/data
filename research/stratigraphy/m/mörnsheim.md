# Mörnsheim

## What do the cited works say about the position and age of the Mörnsheim?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Early Tithonian, above the Bavarian plattenkalks. Rauhut and colleagues (2019) place the formation over the Altmühltal and Painten formations, distinguish it from the Altmühltal by its much larger share of silicified limestones, above all in its lower part, and assign it to the moernsheimensis horizon, the uppermost horizon of the Hybonotum zone of the Early Tithonian. Niebuhr and Pürner (2014) give the formation as middle Lower Tithonian, upper hybonotum zone, and put the moernsheimensis horizon in its lower part near Mörnsheim and Daiting; Rauhut and colleagues' specimen comes from that lower part. Both readings fall in the Tithonian. Governs `stages: [Tithonian]` and `period: [Late Jurassic]` on [Mörnsheim](../../../stratigraphy/m/mörnsheim.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2019b, p. 4, Geological and palaeontological context: the Altmühltal and Painten formations "underly the Mörnsheim Formation"; it "differs from the Altmühltal Formation in the considerably higher amount of silicified limestones ('Kieselplattenkalke'), especially in its lower part"; "Biostratigraphically, this unit represents the uppermost horizon of the Hybonotum zone, the moernsheimensis horizon, and is thus slightly younger than the Upper Solnhofen Member of the Altmühltal Formation".
- niebuhr2014a, p. 43 (translated): ammonites give "the middle Lower Tithonian (upper hybonotum zone)"; "In the lower Mörnsheim Formation near Mörnsheim and Daiting Schweigert (2007) has documented the moernsheimensis fauna horizon of the lower moernsheimensis subzone".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether the upper part of the formation also lies in the moernsheimensis horizon. Rauhut and colleagues (2019) give the horizon for the whole unit, while Niebuhr and Pürner (2014) document it only in the lower part. The stage is Tithonian on either reading.
