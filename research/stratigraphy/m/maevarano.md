# Maevarano

## Does the Maevarano reach below the Maastrichtian?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No. Rogers and colleagues (2000) date the fossiliferous Maevarano, through the Anembalemba Member, as Maastrichtian "rather than Campanian", and Krause and colleagues (2007) find no evidence for anything older. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]` on [Maevarano](../../../stratigraphy/m/maevarano.yml) and on [Anembalemba](../../../stratigraphy/a/anembalemba.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rogers2000a, p. 275: "the most fossiliferous portions of the Maevarano Formation are Maastrichtian in age, rather than Campanian as previously reported"; p. 297: the Anembalemba Member at Berivotra correlates "with the Berivotra Formation".
- krause2007a, p. 3: "Though previously regarded as ranging in age from Turonian to Campanian, there is no litho-, bio-, or magnetostratigraphic evidence to suggest anything other than a Maastrichtian age for the Maevarano Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian.* Tykoski and Rowe (2004) give "Maevarano Formation (Majunga), Madagascar | Campanian" in three rows of their Table 3.1 (tykoski2004a, pp. 49–50), and that chapter is the only source for it. A table row in a reference work does not carry a stage (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-reference-work-corroborates-but-almost-never-carries-a-value-alone)), and Rogers and colleagues' revision replaced the Campanian four years earlier.
- *Turonian to lower Senonian.* Huene (1929) gives *Titanosaurus madagascariensis* "del cretáceo superior (Turonense superior hasta Senonense inferior) de Maevarano, en el norte de Madagascar" ("from the Upper Cretaceous (upper Turonian to lower Senonian) of Maevarano, in the north of Madagascar", translated), with a footnote following Lemoine's "Turon hasta Emscher" (huene1929a, p. 88). This is the older range that Krause and colleagues reject.
- *Coniacian.* Krause and colleagues' only Coniacian is for "the presumably older (Coniacian?) Ankazomihaboka Sandstones of the Mahajanga Basin", after Curry (1997) (krause2007a, p. 10), a different unit.

**Open.** Nothing.
