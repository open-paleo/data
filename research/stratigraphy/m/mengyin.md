# Mengyin

## How old is the Mengyin Formation?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** Berriasian–Valanginian, between its youngest detrital zircons and the oldest overlying eruption age. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian]` on Mengyin; the overlying [Qingshan Group](../../../stratigraphy/q/qingshan.yml) supplies the ceiling.

**Evidence.**
- xu2015b, in-press version, p. 3: youngest detrital zircons "weighted average age of 145 ± 4 Ma"; the overlying "maximum U–Pb zircon age … is 135.6 ± 3.9 Ma (Zhu et al., 2010)"; the formation "constrained to 145–136 Ma".

**Ruled out.**
- *144–130.7 Ma.* Moore and colleagues write "the Mengyin Formation is 144–130.7Ma (J. Xu & Li 2015)" (moore2020a, accepted manuscript, p. 79), which differs from Xu and Li's bracket; 130.7 Ma is probably 132.7 − 2.0, a detrital maximum of an overlying member (our inference).
- *Tithonian, Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian.* These rested on readings superseded by the bracket above.

**Open.** Nothing.
