# Marnes Rouges Inférieures

## On what grounds does Bilotte (1985) date the Marnes Rouges Inférieures?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Bilotte (1985) divides the formation, a 200 m fluvial complex, into four members: from the base, the Marnes rouges de Campagne, the Grès des Estous, the Marnes rouges de la Maurine and the Poudingue fleuri. The Campagne member lies directly on the Grès d'Alet and is referred to the Campanian "essentially" on its position, with a palynoflora from the Couleurs stream placed in zone 20b of the Campanian; the formation summary calls it upper Campanian, the member description and the conclusions only Campanian. The upper three members are Maastrichtian on their dinosaurs, eggshell and other fauna and flora. Governs `stages: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on [Marnes Rouges Inférieures](../../../stratigraphy/m/marnes-rouges-inférieures.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bilotte1985a, p. 242: "Fluvial complex (200 m) in which 4 distinct members are recognized"; "the Red Marls of Campagne, floodplain silts, dated to the upper Campanian"; "These three latter units are rich in fragments of dinosaur eggs and bones and dated to the Maastrichtian" (translated).
- bilotte1985a, p. 250: the Campagne member is "directly superimposed on the Alet Sandstones"; a palynological study of samples "from the Couleurs stream" found taxa "which would place the assemblage in the Campanian (zone 20b of the S.N.E.A.)" (translated).
- bilotte1985a, p. 252: "The Alet Sandstone and the first member of the Lower Red Marls are referred to the Campanian, essentially on arguments of position; the 3 other members of this formation … are Maastrichtian (Bégudo-Rognacian) because of their faunal and floral content" (translated).

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
