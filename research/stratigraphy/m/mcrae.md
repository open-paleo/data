# McRae

## Is the McRae a group, and how old is it?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** A group, Campanian to Maastrichtian. Lucas and colleagues (2019) raise the McRae Formation to group rank, with the José Creek, Hall Lake and Double Canyon formations; Dalman and colleagues (2021) follow them. The group is dated by way of its members: Amato and colleagues' (2017) U–Pb dates make the lower Jose Creek and lowest Hall Lake late Campanian, and Lucas and colleagues date the lower Hall Lake late Maastrichtian from its vertebrates. Their Paleocene for the upper beds is "possibly", a possibility. Governs the new entry [McRae](../../../stratigraphy/m/mcrae.yml) and `parent` on [Hall Lake](../../../stratigraphy/h/hall-lake.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lucas2019a, p. 3: "The very thick McRae Formation is raised in rank to the McRae Group, and its constituent members are raised to formations."
- amato2017a, p. 1224: "U-Pb zircon dates of a dacite clast (75.0 ± 1.1 Ma) and four ash-fall tuffs (ranging from 74.9 ± 1.1 Ma to 73.2 ± 0.7 Ma) in the lower part of the McRae Formation indicate a late Campanian age".
- lucas2019a, p. 3: "Vertebrate biostratigraphy indicates that the lower part of the Hall Lake Formation is Lancian (late Maastrichtian) in age."

**Ruled out.** - *Paleocene.* A possibility.

**Open.** Nothing.
