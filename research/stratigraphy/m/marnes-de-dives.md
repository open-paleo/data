# Marnes de Dives

## How much of the Marnes de Dives crops out at the Vaches Noires, and of what age?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Only the top 6–7 m of the formation's 20 m, all in the Lamberti Zone at the end of the late Callovian. Lebrun and Courville (2013) place the bulk of the formation farther west, and put the overlying Marnes de Villers in all or part of the basal Oxfordian (Mariae Zone). Governs `stages: [Callovian]` on [Marnes de Dives](../../../stratigraphy/m/marnes-de-dives.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lebrun2013a, p. 22 (translated): "only the final 6-7 m (out of 20 m) of the Marnes de Dives are 'visible' at the Vaches-Noires, the bulk of the formation being known further to the west, and only various successive faunas of the Lamberti Zone (terminal biochronological units of the Upper Callovian) are represented".
- lebrun2013a, p. 22 (translated): the Marnes de Villers "represent all or part of the basal Oxfordian (Mariae Zone)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Oxfordian on the Marnes de Dives.* Carrano and colleagues (2012) give *Streptospondylus*, from the same cliffs, as "Marnes de Dives; late Callovian–early Oxfordian", and Allain (2001) gives its horizon as "Marls of the Upper Callovian or Lower Oxfordian of the Falaises des Vaches Noires" (translated). The cliff marls take in the Marnes de Villers above the Marnes de Dives, which Lebrun and Courville place in the basal Oxfordian, while the Marnes de Dives exposed in the cliffs are Lamberti Zone throughout. The Oxfordian in those two works most likely reflects uncertainty over which of the cliff's beds yielded the material (an inference: neither work says so), not an Oxfordian Marnes de Dives.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Who named the Marnes de Dives?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Douvillé (1881), who named the Marnes de Dives (zone of *A. lamberti*) and the Marnes de Villers above it (zone of *A. mariae*), subdividing the older Argile de Dives. Governs the naming reference on Marnes de Dives.

**Evidence.**
- douvillé1881a, p. 441: "il peut être désigné sous le nom de : I. MARNES DE DIVES, ou zone à A. Lamberti" ("it may be designated under the name: I. Marnes de Dives, or zone of A. Lamberti", translated); p. 442: "II. MARNES DE VILLERS, ou zone à A. Mariæ"; the table on p. 473 pairs the two.
- lebrun2013a, p. 18: "La stratigraphie d'Hébert est ensuite précisée par Henri Douvillé (1881) … Il subdivise notamment l'Argile de Dives" ("Hébert's stratigraphy is then refined by Henri Douvillé (1881) … In particular he subdivides the Argile de Dives", translated); p. 16 credits "à Alexandre Brongniart (1829) la distinction des Argiles de Dives" ("to Alexandre Brongniart (1829) the distinction of the Argiles de Dives", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Hébert as the author of the Argile de Dives.* Lebrun (2013) credits the Argile de Dives to Brongniart (1829); Douvillé refined Hébert's horizon scheme.

**Open.** The modern lithostratigraphy. Lebrun's synthetic section follows Dugué (1991); it was not read for this note.
