# Marnes de Dives

## Who named the Marnes de Dives?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Douvillé (1881), who named the Marnes de Dives (zone of *A. lamberti*) and the Marnes de Villers above it (zone of *A. mariae*), subdividing the older Argile de Dives. Governs the naming reference on Marnes de Dives.

**Evidence.**
- douvillé1881a, p. 441: "il peut être désigné sous le nom de : I. MARNES DE DIVES, ou zone à A. Lamberti" ("it may be designated under the name: I. Marnes de Dives, or zone of A. Lamberti", translated); p. 442: "II. MARNES DE VILLERS, ou zone à A. Mariæ"; the table on p. 473 pairs the two.
- lebrun2013a, p. 18: "La stratigraphie d'Hébert est ensuite précisée par Henri Douvillé (1881) … Il subdivise notamment l'Argile de Dives" ("Hébert's stratigraphy is then refined by Henri Douvillé (1881) … In particular he subdivides the Argile de Dives", translated); p. 16 credits "à Alexandre Brongniart (1829) la distinction des Argiles de Dives" ("to Alexandre Brongniart (1829) the distinction of the Argiles de Dives", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Hébert as the author of the Argile de Dives.* Lebrun (2013) credits the Argile de Dives to Brongniart (1829); Douvillé refined Hébert's horizon scheme.

**Open.** The modern lithostratigraphy. Lebrun's synthetic section follows Dugué (1991); it was not read for this note.
