# Morrison

## What do the cited works say about the age of the top of the Morrison?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** The youngest dates for the formation, about 150 and 149 Ma, lie close to the Kimmeridgian–Tithonian boundary on the current chart, and none reaches the Berriasian. Kirkland and colleagues (2016) place the top in "the lower third of the Tithonian" on the ICS chart of 2013; the 2026-10-03 entry in [Morrison Tithonian labels](../../topics/morrison-tithonian-labels.md) re-places both dates on the current chart. Cifelli and colleagues (2014) give the formation as mainly Kimmeridgian–Tithonian and allow an early Berriasian only as a possibility, after Kowallis and colleagues (1998) and Miall and colleagues (2008). Governs the Tithonian in `stages` on [Morrison](../../../stratigraphy/m/morrison.yml), and bears on its Berriasian (see Open).

**Evidence.**
- kirkland2016a, p. 118: "The youngest ages for Morrison strata in this area are (1) 148.96 +2.54 to -2.21 Ma based on U-Pb ages determined by laser ablation of zircons extracted from a volcanic ash bed about 9 m below the base of the Naturita Formation"; "(2) 150.00 ± 0.52 Ma from near the top of the Brushy Basin Member ... based on a recalibrated 40Ar/39Ar age"; "Thus, the top of the Morrison Formation dates to the lower third of the Tithonian Stage near but not to the end of the Jurassic".
- cifelli2014a, p. 32, Introduction: "The Late Jurassic Morrison Formation (mainly Kimmeridgian–Tithonian, though time of deposition for the unit as a whole may also include the late Oxfordian and early Berriasian, see for example Kowallis et al. 1998; Miall et al. 2008)".
- cifelli2014a, p. 33, Geologic setting: "The lower boundary of nearly all Lower Cretaceous nonmarine deposits throughout the Western Interior foreland basin is represented by an unconformity", which separates them "from nonmarine Upper Jurassic (to possibly lower Berriasian) strata comprising the Morrison Formation in most of the US part of the basin".

**Ruled out.**
- *The top in the lower third of the Tithonian on the current chart.* Kirkland and colleagues place their dates on the 2013 chart. On the ICS chart v2024/12, with the base of the Tithonian at 149.2 Ma, the 150.00 ± 0.52 Ma age is Kimmeridgian, and the 148.96 Ma age is Tithonian only at its central value: its uncertainty of +2.54 to −2.21 Myr spans both stages. The 2026-10-03 topic entry gives the central-value placements; this entry adds the uncertainty.
- *A Berriasian top from Cifelli and colleagues' account of the basal Cretaceous unconformity.* Its "to possibly lower Berriasian" repeats the hedge of the paper's introduction and adds no date. The 2026-10-03 topic entry rules out a Berriasian Morrison on the introduction's statement, and the dates of Kirkland and colleagues (2016), about 149 to 150 Ma, stop some 6 Myr short of the base of the Berriasian at 143.1 Ma on the current chart.

**Open.** The Morrison record lists the Berriasian in `stages` and the Early Cretaceous in `period`, although the 2026-10-03 topic entry rules out a Berriasian Morrison. The two works Cifelli and colleagues cite for the hedge, Kowallis and colleagues (1998) and Miall and colleagues (2008), have not been weighed here; whether either gives the Berriasian as a plain reading decides whether the stage and the period stay.
