# Magnesian Conglomerate

## Is the Magnesian Conglomerate Carnian?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No source gives the unit a Carnian age in its own right. Governs `stages: [Norian, Rhaetian]` on [Magnesian Conglomerate](../../../stratigraphy/m/magnesian-conglomerate.yml), in that the Carnian is not added.

**Evidence.**
- vickers-rich1999a, p. 195: "the Late Triassic Magnesian Conglomerate fissure site in the Carboniferous Limestone from Durdham Down near Bristol, England … dated as Carnian to Rhaetian (Benton and Spencer 1995)".
- galton2007a, p. 515: the Clifton deposit is "a Triassic (Rheatian) dolomitized cave breccia or fissure fill (not in Magnesian/ Dolomitic Conglomerate …) in Lower Carboniferous (Dinantian) marine limestone".

**Ruled out.**
- *Carnian.* Vickers-Rich and colleagues (1999) report the Carnian–Rhaetian from Benton and Spencer (1995), as the age of the Durdham Down fissure. The date is second-hand, and it is for a fissure fill that Galton (2007) places outside the unit.

**Open.** Whether the Durdham Down ages date the unit at all. The record's Norian–Rhaetian also comes from statements about the Durdham Down occurrence (galton1990a, peyredefabrègues2015a).
