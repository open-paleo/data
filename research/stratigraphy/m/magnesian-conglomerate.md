# Magnesian Conglomerate

## What stages does the Magnesian Conglomerate take?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** None; the unit takes `period: [Late Triassic]` alone. Its former Norian–Rhaetian came from two works that date the *Thecodontosaurus* occurrence, which is the Durdham Down fissure fill and not this unit. What dates the conglomerate itself is an epoch from the survey map, an old correlation with the Muschelkalk, and a relayed Rhaetian whose two relayers read it oppositely. Governs `period` and the absent `stages` on [Magnesian Conglomerate](../../../stratigraphy/m/magnesian-conglomerate.yml).

**Evidence.**
- foffa2014a, p. 318, Fig. 1: "The Dolomitic conglomerate, Mercia Mudstone Group and Penarth Group are Late Triassic."
- galton2007a: the Durdham Down deposit is "a Triassic (Rhaetian) dolomitized cave breccia or fissure fill (not in Magnesian/Dolomitic Conglomerate … )".
- galton1990a and peyredefabrègues2015a: "?Norian-Rhaetian" and Norian to Rhaetian for the *Thecodontosaurus* occurrence.

**Ruled out.**
- *Norian–Rhaetian.* It dates the fissure fill.
- *The Muschelkalk.* Etheridge's (1870) correlation; a correlative's age does not date a unit.
- *Rhaetian from Moore (1867, 1881).* Benton and colleagues (2000) relay it as an age for the conglomerate (benton2000b, p. 80); Galton (2007) reads the same work as dating the fissures and leaving the conglomerate older.

**Open.** A stage-level dating of the conglomerate.

## Is the Magnesian Conglomerate Carnian?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** No source gives the unit a Carnian age in its own right. Governs `stages: [Norian, Rhaetian]` on [Magnesian Conglomerate](../../../stratigraphy/m/magnesian-conglomerate.yml), in that the Carnian is not added.

**Evidence.**
- vickers-rich1999a, p. 195: "the Late Triassic Magnesian Conglomerate fissure site in the Carboniferous Limestone from Durdham Down near Bristol, England … dated as Carnian to Rhaetian (Benton and Spencer 1995)".
- galton2007a, p. 515: the Clifton deposit is "a Triassic (Rheatian) dolomitized cave breccia or fissure fill (not in Magnesian/ Dolomitic Conglomerate …) in Lower Carboniferous (Dinantian) marine limestone".

**Ruled out.**
- *Carnian.* Vickers-Rich and colleagues (1999) report the Carnian–Rhaetian from Benton and Spencer (1995), as the age of the Durdham Down fissure. The date is second-hand, and it is for a fissure fill that Galton (2007) places outside the unit.

**Open.** Whether the Durdham Down ages date the unit at all. The record's Norian–Rhaetian also comes from statements about the Durdham Down occurrence (galton1990a, peyredefabrègues2015a).
