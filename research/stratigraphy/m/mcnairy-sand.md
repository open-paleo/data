# McNairy Sand

## Is the McNairy Sand Member dated at its own level?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes, early Maastrichtian, stated for the member itself rather than relayed from the [Ripley Formation](../../../stratigraphy/r/ripley.yml)'s Campanian–Maastrichtian range. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]` on McNairy Sand and on [*Hypsibema crassicauda*](../../../genera/H/Hypsibema.yml).

**Evidence.**
- baird1979a, pp. 14–15: "the McNairy Sand Member of the Ripley Formation, considered early Maestrichtian in age".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Ripley Formation's Campanian.* That range belongs to the formation as a whole; Baird and Horner give the member its own age.

**Open.** Baird and Horner give no source for the age ("considered"), so it reports a received age rather than their own determination.

## References

- `baird1979a`: Baird, D.; Horner, J. R. (1979). Cretaceous dinosaurs of North Carolina. *Brimleyana* 2: 1-28.
