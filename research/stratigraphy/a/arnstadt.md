# Arnstadt

## Is the Löwenstein another name for the Arnstadt, and what age do the cited works give the Arnstadt?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** The Löwenstein is a correlative, not another name. Sues and Schoch (2025) give type horizons in the Stubensandstein of Baden-Württemberg as "Löwenstein Formation (equivalent of Arnstadt Formation)", once adding "in the basin", and write of the Arnstadt "and its marginal equivalents". That is an equivalence between rock in two areas, as with the Ifezouane and the Gara Sbaa of the [Kem Kem](../../../stratigraphy/k/kem-kem.yml), so neither name is a variant of the other and the two stay separate entries. The same work dates the upper part of the Arnstadt, which yielded *Eudimorphodon* teeth in the district of Bitburg-Prüm, as late Norian–Rhaetian. Governs the absence of each name from the other's `variants` on [Arnstadt](../../../stratigraphy/a/arnstadt.yml) and [Löwenstein](../../../stratigraphy/l/löwenstein.yml), and `stages: [Norian, Rhaetian]` on Arnstadt.

**Evidence.**
- sues2025a, p. 417: for *Proterochersis robusta*, "Type horizon. Lower Stubensandstein (S1), Löwenstein Formation (equivalent of Arnstadt Formation), Middle Keuper Subgroup. Age: Late Triassic (Norian: Lacian)."
- sues2025a, p. 461: "Löwenstein Formation (equivalent of Arnstadt Formation in the basin)"; "the Arnstadt Formation and its marginal equivalents in Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, Saxony-Anhalt, and Thuringia"; for *Eudimorphodon* sp., "Locality. District of Bitburg-Prüm, Rhineland-Palatinate", "Horizon. Upper part of Arnstadt Formation, Middle Keuper Subgroup. Age: Upper Triassic (late Norian–Rhaetian)."

**Ruled out.**
- *The Löwenstein as a variant of the Arnstadt.* "Equivalent" in Sues and Schoch relates the marginal unit to the basin unit; a variant is another name papers print for the same unit ([methods](../../methods.md)).

**Open.** Nothing.
