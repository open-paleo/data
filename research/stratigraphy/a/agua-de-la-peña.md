# Agua de la Peña

## Which formations make up the Agua de la Peña Group, and what range do they give it?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** The group takes in the Chañares (or Ischichuca), Los Rastros, Ischigualasto and Los Colorados formations, the Los Colorados at the top, and runs Ladinian to Norian on the ages of its formations. Caselli and colleagues (2001) and Shipman (2004) give the membership; Caselli and colleagues set the Talampaya and Tarjados formations below the group and the Lower Jurassic Baldecitos Formation above it, both outside. Ezcurra (2010), placing the Ischigualasto in the group after Bossi (1971), gives the formation below it as "Ladinian-early Carnian" and the one above as "Norian", which sets the group's lowest and highest stages; the Carnian and Norian of the Ischigualasto itself are in the [Ischigualasto](../i/ischigualasto.md) entry, and the Los Colorados's Norian in the [Los Colorados](../l/los-colorados.md) entries. No work cited dates the group as a unit more finely than Caselli and colleagues' Middle to Late Triassic for the basin fill and Shipman's "Middle to Late Triassic Agua de la Pena Group". Governs `period: [Middle Triassic, Late Triassic]` and `stages: [Ladinian, Carnian, Norian]` on [Agua de la Peña](../../../stratigraphy/a/agua-de-la-peña.yml).

**Evidence.**
- caselli2001a, p. 173: the basin's continental Triassic sediments "have been grouped into the Talampaya and Tarjados Formations ..., and the Agua de la Peña Group (Bossi 1971), within which the Los Colorados Formation represents the upper terms of the succession" (translated).
- caselli2001a, p. 174, Geological framework: the fault-bounded depressions "were filled in western Argentina with continental sediments during the Middle to Late Triassic (Ischigualasto-Villa Unión, Marayes, Cuyo, and San Luis basins)" (translated).
- caselli2001a, p. 176, Fig. 1: the legend brackets "Fm. Los Colorados" ("Upper Triassic") and "Fm. Los Rastros, Chañares e Ischigualasto" ("Middle Triassic") as "Grupo Agua de la Peña"; "Fms. Talampaya y Tarjados" ("Lower Triassic") and "Fm. Baldecitos" ("Lower Jurassic") stand outside the bracket (translated).
- ezcurra2010a, p. 372: the Ischigualasto Formation "crops out in the San Juan and La Rioja provinces of NW Argentina, as part of the Agua de la Pena Group of the Ischigualasto-Villa Union Basin (Bossi 1971). It lies between the Los Rastros Fm. (Ladinian-early Carnian) below and the Los Colorados Fm. (Norian) above".
- shipman2004a, p. 18: the basin's Triassic strata "comprise two Groups: (1) the Early Triassic Paganzo III Group (containing the Talampaya Formation and the Tarjados Formation) and (2) the Middle to Late Triassic Agua de la Pena Group (containing the Chanares/Ischichuca Formations, the Los Rastros Formation, the Ischigualasto Formation, and the Los Colorados Formation".
- ezcurra2010a, p. 373, Fig. 1: "Map of SE Agua de la Pena Group (San Juan and La Rioja provinces, NW Argentina)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Triassic or Early Jurassic in the group.* The Lower Triassic Talampaya and Tarjados and the Lower Jurassic Baldecitos are on Caselli and colleagues' map, but outside the bracket that marks the group, and their text names the Talampaya and Tarjados separately from the group; Shipman (2004) puts those two in an Early Triassic Paganzo III Group.
- *A Middle Triassic Ischigualasto, from the same map.* Its "Middle Triassic" for the Los Rastros, Chañares and Ischigualasto is older than the late Carnian Ezcurra (2010) gives the Ischigualasto; it is a legend label, ruled out in the [Ischigualasto](../i/ischigualasto.md) entry. It changes nothing for the group, whose Middle Triassic rests on the Ladinian of the Los Rastros.

**Open.** Nothing.
