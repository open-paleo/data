# Altmühltal

## Is the Solnhofen Lithographic Limestone the Altmühltal Formation?

*2026-11-18*

**Conclusion.** Yes; the Solnhofen Lithographic Limestone is the traditional name for the plattenkalks Niebuhr and Pürner (2014) formalized as the Altmühltal Formation, and the registry carries it as a variant. Its separate entry, which listed Altmühltal among its own variants, is retired, since no record or unit names it. Governs `variants` on [Altmühltal](../../../stratigraphy/a/altmühltal.yml), and supersedes the 2026-10-28 entry below as a ruling on a unit of its own.

**Evidence.**
- niebuhr2014a, p. 7: "The Altmühltal Formation contains the typical lithographic plattenkalks of the western Southern Franconian Alb, which formerly has been termed 'Solnhofener Schichten'".
- foth2025a, p. 18: "the 'Solnhofen limestone' *sensu stricto* is part of the Altmühltal Formation and restricted to the area northwest of Ingolstadt, whereas the contemporaneous plattenkalks more to the east belong to the Painten Formation".
- ding2020a, appendix table: Archaeopteryx horizons given as "Solnhofen Limestone (Altmühltal Formation)".

**Ruled out.**
- *A separate entry.* No record has named the Solnhofen Lithographic Limestone since *Wellnhoferia* was retired into *Archaeopteryx*, and no unit gives it as parent, so it fails the registry rule in research/methods.md; it was also a second entry for one unit.
- *The broad sense as the Altmühltal.* The name has also covered the contemporaneous eastern plattenkalks, now the Painten Formation: Holtz and colleagues (2004) use it for *Compsognathus* at Jachenhausen, which the *Compsognathus* note places in the Painten. A record meeting the name in that sense takes the Painten, not this variant.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does Holtz's (2004) "Kimmeridgian or basal Tithonian" change the unit's stages?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** No. A paper's undecided dating between two readings contributes every stage it allows, so Holtz's reading gives the Kimmeridgian and the Tithonian, and both are already in the unit's stages from Foth and colleagues' (2025) late Kimmeridgian to early Tithonian. The record note once said the hedge was not added because it falls at a stage boundary; that is not the rule. Governed `stages: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` on the Solnhofen Lithographic Limestone entry, now a variant of [Altmühltal](../../../stratigraphy/a/altmühltal.yml).

**Evidence.**
- holtz2004a, p. 105: "the Kimmeridgian or basal Tithonian Solnhofen Lithographic Limestone of Jachenhausen".
- foth2025a, p. 18: "partially laminated, partially silty limestones of late Kimmeridgian to early Tithonian age, commonly referred to as the 'Solnhofen limestone'".
- foth2025a, p. 19: the Altmühltal Formation "begins with the latest Kimmeridgian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Leaving Holtz's reading out because it hedges at a boundary.* CONTRIBUTING ([Age](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#age-period-and-stages)) has an undecided dating contribute every alternative it allows.

**Open.** Nothing.
