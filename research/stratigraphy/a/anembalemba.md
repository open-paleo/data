# Anembalemba

## How is the Anembalemba Member dated?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** By correlation with the overlying marine Berivotra Formation, not by fossils of its own. Rogers and colleagues (2000) correlate the member, "at least in part", with the Berivotra Formation, which fossils date as Maastrichtian and planktonic foraminifera as late Maastrichtian in the Berivotra region; none of the works cited dates the member from its own fossils. Krause and colleagues (2007) describe the member's deposits and its fauna but add no age evidence of their own. Governs `stages: [Maastrichtian]` on [Anembalemba](../../../stratigraphy/a/anembalemba.yml), with the 2026-09-20 entry in [Maevarano](../m/maevarano.md).

**Evidence.**
- rogers2000a, p. 294: "Regional thickness trends further suggest that exposures of the Anembalemba Member in the Berivotra field area correlate with exposures of the Berivotra Formation to the north along the coast"; "the underlying Anembalemba Member also correlates, at least in part, with the Berivotra Formation"; "on the basis of fossils, the age of the entire Berivotra Formation can be confidently interpreted as Maastrichtian", and "recent work on planktonic foraminifera indicates that exposures of the formation in the Berivotra region are late Maastrichtian in age".
- rogers2000a, p. 282: facies 2 of the member "consists of fine- to coarse-grained clay-rich sandstone characterized by very poor sorting".
- krause2007a, p. 2: "The Anembalemba Member preserves sediments deposited during flooding events between crystalline highlands to the east and the Mozambique Channel to the west in a lowland floodplain setting reflective of a highly seasonal, semiarid climate".
- krause2007a, p. 3: the vast majority of *Majungasaurus* specimens collected by the Mahajanga Basin Project "were recovered from the 10- to 15-m thick Anembalemba Member, but some specimens (primarily isolated teeth) were also found in the Mesorobe and Miadana members". Rogers and colleagues spell the first of these "Masorobe" (rogers2000a, p. 275).

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian.* The age of earlier work, replaced by Rogers and colleagues' revision; see the 2026-09-20 entry in [Maevarano](../m/maevarano.md).

**Open.** Rogers and colleagues correlate "Most, if not all" of the member with the Berivotra Formation (rogers2000a, p. 297). The works cited do not date any part of the member that lies below the Berivotra correlation.
