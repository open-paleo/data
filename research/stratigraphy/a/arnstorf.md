# Arnstorf

## Is the Arnstorf a member of the Torleite, and which records lie in it?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Yes; it is entered as a member, Kimmeridgian, and *Juravenator* and *Sciurumimus* are filed in it. Niebuhr and Pürner (2014) formally define the Torleite Formation with six subformations, which their English abstract calls members; in the eastern Southern Franconian Alb the Arnstorf is the upper of two, and they give the Schamhaupten site, which yielded *Juravenator*, and the Rygol quarry at Painten, which yielded *Sciurumimus*, as sites of its siliceous-plate facies. The rank list has no subformation, so the unit is a member. The Torleite is dated to the Kimmeridgian alone, so the member takes that stage. Governs the new entry [Arnstorf](../../../stratigraphy/a/arnstorf.yml) and `location.member` on [*Juravenator starki*](../../../genera/J/Juravenator.yml) and [*Sciurumimus albersdoerferi*](../../../genera/S/Sciurumimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- niebuhr2014a, p. 34: "Fossilien: Die Kieselplattenfazies der Arnstorf-Subformation ist außerordentlich fossilreich. Exemplarisch lieferte die berühmte Fossillagerstätte Schamhaupten über 200 verschiedene Taxa (Viohl & Zapp 2006), darunter auch den Theropoden Juravenator starki" ["Fossils: The siliceous platy facies of the Arnstorf Subformation is extraordinarily fossil-rich. Exemplary, the famous fossil locality Schamhaupten yielded over 200 different taxa (Viohl & Zapp 2006), including the theropod Juravenator starki", translated].
- niebuhr2014a, p. 33: "Im Steinbruch Rygol bei Painten … sind die obersten ca. 20 m der Arnstorf-Subformation und die Hangendgrenze zur Painten-Formation aufgeschlossen." ["In the Rygol quarry near Painten … the uppermost approximately 20 m of the Arnstorf Subformation and the upper boundary to the Painten Formation are exposed.", translated].

**Ruled out.** Nothing.
