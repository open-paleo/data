# Argiles et Grès à Reptiles

## Does the notice's "Rognacian (= Danian)" give the unit a Danian age?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** No. Answers the 2026-11-23 entry's Open line. The Aix-en-Provence notice heads the unit "Rognacian (= Danian)" but files that stage under its Mesozoic ("Secondary") series, with "Bégudian (= Maastrichtian)" below it and the Cretaceous series ending at the top of the Rognacian. By the notice's own arrangement its Danian is a Cretaceous stage, so it is not the Paleocene Danian of the current chart, and it adds no Paleocene. The notice gives no basis for either equation. The stages rest on later works. Governs `period` and `stages: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on [Argiles et Grès à Reptiles](../../../stratigraphy/a/argiles-et-grès-à-reptiles.yml), unchanged, and the note on catzigras1969a there.

**Evidence.**
- catzigras1969a, p. 6: under "SECONDARY", "C8. Rognacian (= Danian). The stage generally comprises four main formations" (translated).
- catzigras1969a, p. 7: "C7. Bégudian (= Maastrichtian) (300 m)" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Danian, or Paleocene, from the notice.* The notice places its Danian in the Mesozoic.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What does the Aix-en-Provence map notice say about the age and fossils of the Argiles et Grès à Reptiles?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** The notice places both parts of the unit, below and above the Calcaire de Rognac, in its Rognacian, the top stage of its Mesozoic ("Secondaire") series, and gives the Eocene age of the upper part only as an earlier view. It reports dinosaur bones from the lower part near Rousset and dinosaur eggs from both parts. Governs `period` on [Argiles et Grès à Reptiles](../../../stratigraphy/a/argiles-et-grès-à-reptiles.yml).

**Evidence.**
- catzigras1969a, p. 6: under "Secondaire", "C8. Rognacian (= Danian)", whose top member, the Galante Conglomerates, is where "the Cretaceous series terminates, in the eastern part of the Arc basin" (translated).
- catzigras1969a, p. 6: "C8c. Upper Clays and Sandstones with Reptiles (100 m). This assemblage, formerly considered Eocene, contains dinosaur eggs (north of Rousset and at Roques Hautes)" (translated).
- catzigras1969a, p. 6: "C8a. Lower Clays and Sandstones with Reptiles (250 to 300 m). They form, north of Fuveau, the base of the Rognacian series" (translated).
- catzigras1969a, p. 7: "In the vicinity of Rousset, the sandstones, often pisolithic, yielded bones of *Rhabdodon priseum* and especially of *Hypselosaurus priscus*"; eggs are found "in the clays or the sandstones, even in the breccias, of the lower or upper Rognacian" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *An Eocene age for the upper Argiles et Grès à Reptiles.* The notice reports it as what the unit was "formerly considered" and maps the unit under C8, below the Galante Conglomerates that end its Cretaceous series and below the Montien.

**Open.** The notice equates its Rognacian with the Danian while listing it in the Mesozoic. How that equation, and its "Bégudien (= Maestrichtien)" below (p. 7), bear on the unit's stages is not settled here; the stages rest on later works.
