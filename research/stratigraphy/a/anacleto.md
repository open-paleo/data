# Anacleto

## What do Hugo and Leanza (2001) and Garrido (2010) say about the age of the Anacleto?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Hugo and Leanza (2001) bracket the formation as late Santonian to early Campanian, between the Santonian age Bonaparte (1991) gave the Bajo de la Carpa below it and the Huantráiquica orogenic phase at 74 ± 3 Ma; Garrido (2010) places it in the early to middle Campanian from paleomagnetic studies at Auca Mahuevo. Both fall within the stages. Governs `stages` on [Anacleto](../../../stratigraphy/a/anacleto.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hugo2001a, p. 18: "Following the conception of Ramos (1981), the Río Colorado Formation is raised to the rank of Subgroup and the Bajo de la Carpa and Anacleto Members to that of Formation", the Río Colorado Formation being Cazau and Uliana's (1973), "constituted by the Bajo de la Carpa and Anacleto Members" (translated).
- hugo2001a, p. 20: "this uppermost unit of the Neuquén Group"; "Its type locality is at the Aguada de Anacleto, on the southwest slope of cerro Senillosa, some 50 km west of the city of Neuquén" (translated).
- hugo2001a, p. 21: "Taking into account that the age of the Huantráiquica phase of the Patagonídico orogenic cycle has been fixed at 74 ± 3 Ma, it is estimated that the Anacleto Formation cannot be younger than lower Campanian. On the other hand, Bonaparte (1991) … recorded for this latter unit [the Bajo de la Carpa] a Santonian age. Therefore, the Anacleto Formation is assigned to the upper Santonian - lower Campanian" (translated).
- garrido2010a, p. 157: "Legarreta & Gulisano (1989) attributed to these deposits an age between 83 and 80 Ma, placing it within the Early Campanian (Carta Estratigráfica Internacional, 2009)" (translated).
- garrido2010a, p. 157: "Paleomagnetic studies provided by Dingus et al. (2000) indicate that the lower deposits of the Fm. Anacleto in the Auca Mahuevo area developed during an interval of reversed polarity correlatable with the C33R interval (83.5 to 79.5 Ma)"; "new studies … on its upper levels (Dingus et al., 2009), indicate an interval of normal polarity correlatable with the C33N interval, developed between 79.5 and 74.5 Ma"; "the age of the Fm. Anacleto would be situated in the Lower to Middle Campanian" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *An Early Campanian stated by Legarreta and Gulisano (1989).* Garrido (2010) gives their figure as 83 to 80 Ma and places it in the Early Campanian with a citation of the 2009 International Stratigraphic Chart, twenty years after their paper; the stage is Garrido's reading of the number on that chart.

**Open.** Legarreta and Gulisano (1989) would show whether 83 to 80 Ma was a measured age or a correlation, and what stage, if any, they gave it.
