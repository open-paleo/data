# Ambolafotsy

## What do the cited works say about the age and divisions of the Ambolafotsy?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Turonian throughout. Randrianasolo and Philip (1989) divide the Ambolafotsy into three formations: a lower formation of marls with no members, and middle and upper formations each in members a, b and c. They date the lower Ambolafotsy to the Lower Turonian by planktonic foraminifera and nannoplankton, and the upper Ambolafotsy to the Middle and Upper Turonian by the ammonite *Subprionocyclus neptuni* in member b and the *Marginotruncana sigali* zone in member c. The middle Ambolafotsy has no fossils of stratigraphic value and takes its age, a large part of the Middle Turonian, from the beds that bracket it. Farke and Sertich (2013) treat the same section as one informal formation in lower, middle and upper units, and place the *Dahalokely* site in the middle unit. Governs `stages: [Turonian]` on the [Ambolafotsy](../../../stratigraphy/a/ambolafotsy.yml).

**Evidence.**
- randrianasolo1989a, p. 120 (translated): "THE MARLS OF THE LOWER AMBOLAFOTSY (Lower Turonian)"; "This formation is well-dated by the microfauna because it contains: the *Whiteinella aprica* zone … and the base of the *H. helvetica* zone (planktonic foraminifera); the *Quadrum gartneri* zone (Nannoplankton)".
- randrianasolo1989a, p. 121 (translated): "Containing no fossils of stratigraphic value, this formation is dated by the marls of the lower Ambolafotsy and the sandstones of the upper Ambolafotsy that bracket it. It thus corresponds to a large part of the middle Turonian."
- randrianasolo1989a, p. 122, Fig. 2: the column divides the section into "AMBOL. inf.", "AMBOLAFOTSY moyenne" (members a–c) and "AMBOLAFOTSY supérieure" (members a–c) in its Formations column.
- randrianasolo1989a, p. 124 (translated): "THE UPPER AMBOLAFOTSY (Middle and Upper Turonian)"; "It is dated by the presence, in member b, of *Subprionocyclus neptuni* and, in member c, of the *Marginotruncana sigali* zone."
- farke2013a, p. 2, Geological Context: "The section containing the Dahalokely site is informally termed the 'Ambolafotsy Formation,' and is divided into lower, middle (containing the type locality; Figure 1), and upper units".

**Ruled out.** Nothing among the works cited here.

**Open.** Whether the Ambolafotsy is one unit or three. Randrianasolo and Philip (1989) rank each part as a formation; Farke and Sertich (2013) and Rauhut and Carrano (2016) treat the whole as one informally termed formation. None of the works cited here formally erects either arrangement.
