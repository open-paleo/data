# Alcântara

## Does Bandeira and colleagues' (2024) Albian–Cenomanian row add the Albian?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** No. Bandeira and colleagues (2024) give the Alcântara Formation as "Albian – Cenomanian" in one row of an occurrence table, for cf. *Ouranosaurus* sp., and cite Novas (2009) for it. A compilation row repeats the reading it cites rather than making one, and a relayed statement adds nothing to the reading it relays; Novas (2009) has not been read. The Albian of the Undifferentiated Unit below is already ruled out as the Alcântara's in the 2026-11-23 entry. Governs `stages: [Cenomanian]` on [Alcântara](../../../stratigraphy/a/alcântara.yml), unchanged, and the note on bandeira2024a there.

**Evidence.**
- bandeira2024a, Table 3, p. 29: "cf. Ouranosaurus sp. | Brazil | Alcântara Formation | Albian – Cenomanian | Novas (2009)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian, from Bandeira and colleagues' table.* It relays Novas (2009) and makes no dating of its own.

**Open.** What Novas (2009) bases the Albian on, and whether the specimen comes from the Alcântara or the unit below; reading it would settle both.

## How do Bittencourt and Langer (2011) divide the Itapecuru Group, and which localities do they place in the Alcântara?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Bittencourt and Langer (2011), following Rossetti (2001), divide the Itapecuru Group of the São Luís-Grajaú Basin into three units: an Albian "Undifferentiated Unit" at the base; the Alcântara Formation, of stratified sandstones, mudstones and limestones deposited in an estuary during the early Cenomanian; and the Cujupe Formation, Late Cretaceous to Paleocene and without dinosaurs. They take the informal "Itapecuru Formation" of the Itapecuru-Mirim region to correspond roughly to the Undifferentiated Unit. The basin's main dinosaur localities therefore fall in two units: Coroatá and Mata in the Undifferentiated Unit or "Itapecuru Formation", and Ilha do Cajual and Praia da Baronesa in the Alcântara. Governs `stages: [Cenomanian]` and `lithology` on [Alcântara](../../../stratigraphy/a/alcântara.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bittencourt2011a, p. 29, São Luís-Grajaú Basin: "According to Rossetti (2001)", the Itapecuru Group "is divided in three stratigraphic units: a) the Albian age 'Undifferentiated Unit' […]; b) the Alcântara Formation, composed of stratified sandstones, mudstones, and limestones deposited during the early Cenomanian in an estuarine environment; c) the Cujupe Formation whose age ranges from the Late Cretaceous to Paleocene, and devoid of dinosaur records".
- bittencourt2011a, p. 29, São Luís-Grajaú Basin: "The so-called 'Itapecuru Formation', which crops out in the Itapecuru-Mirim region […], is an informal unit that roughly corresponds to the 'Undifferentiated Unit'".
- bittencourt2011a, p. 31, São Luís-Grajaú Basin: "The most important fossil localities with dinosaur remains of the São Luís-Grajaú Basin are: Coroatá and Mata ('Undifferentiated Unit'/'Itapecuru Formation' […]) and Ilha do Cajual and Praia da Baronesa (Alcântara Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian.* In the division Bittencourt and Langer (2011) follow, the Albian is the age of the Undifferentiated Unit beneath the Alcântara. Dinosaur records from Coroatá and Mata come from that unit or from the informal "Itapecuru Formation" that corresponds to it, and do not date the Alcântara.

**Open.** Nothing.
