# Arundel

## What do Carrano (2024) and Ostrom (1970) say about the age of the Arundel?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Carrano (2024) gives the Dinosaur Park locality near Muirkirk as late Aptian, on the site's palynoflora as described by Brenner (1963) and Robbins (1991); the Aptian–Albian it gives for *Acrocanthosaurus* is the genus's range across North America, not a dating of the formation. Ostrom (1970) dates the formation only against the Cloverly, as somewhat older than a unit it gives as late Aptian to early Albian. Governs `Aptian` in `stages` on [Arundel](../../../stratigraphy/a/arundel.yml); Ostrom (1970) governs no stage.

**Evidence.**
- carrano2024a, p. 2, 2. Geological and geographical context: "The Arundel Clay (or Arundel Formation) of the Potomac Group crops out intermittently in Maryland and the District of Columbia, and may persist in the subsurface in Virginia as well".
- carrano2024a, p. 2, 2. Geological and geographical context: "The palynoflora of the site was described by Brenner (1963) and Robbins (1991) and supports a late Aptian age for the vertebrate-bearing layer".
- carrano2024a, p. 4, 4. Systematic paleontology: "USNM Loc. 41614, the former Cherokee-Sanford Brick Clay pit, now Dinosaur Park, near Muirkirk, Prince George's County, Maryland; Arundel Clay, late Aptian, Lower Cretaceous".
- carrano2024a, p. 17, 7. Conclusions: "This confirms the presence of this taxon in the Arundel Clay ... Acrocanthosaurus ranged across North America during Aptian–Albian time".
- ostrom1970a, p. 2, Abstract: "Comparison with faunas from the Arundel Formation of Maryland, the Glen Rose Formation (Trinity Group) of Texas and Oklahoma, and the Wealden beds of northwestern Europe indicate at least partial contemporaneity of the Cloverly and Glen Rose Formations and a somewhat greater age for the Arundel and Wealden beds. The age of the Cloverly ... is probably Late Aptian and Early Albian".

**Ruled out.**
- *A stage from Ostrom's comparison.* "A somewhat greater age" than a late Aptian to early Albian Cloverly is a bound by comparison, not a determination: it says the Arundel is older without saying how much, and so licenses no stage.

**Open.** Nothing.
