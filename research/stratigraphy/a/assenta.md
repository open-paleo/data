# Assenta

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Assenta?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Tithonian to earliest Berriasian. Mateus and colleagues (2017) find the microfossils of the topmost marine limestone at Porto da Calada like those of the Farta Pão Formation and judge the unit "essentially Tithonian"; adding the ages of the units above and below, they propose "a late early Tithonian to earlier Berriasian age". Applying the tentative magnetostratigraphy of the Porto da Calada section by Salminen and colleagues (2014), they put the Tithonian–Berriasian boundary at the base of magnetozone M18r, around 52 m in the section and about 5 m below the contact with the overlying Porto da Calada Formation, so "the last few meters of the Assenta Mb. can be considered as earliest Berriasian". Their field-trip summary gives the top of the member exposed at Porto da Calada as a meandering fluvial system "with intercalations of shallow-marine lagoonal carbonates", dated "upper Tithonian to lowermost Berriasian". They name it one of the upper members of the Lourinhã Formation, contrasted with the Santa Rita Member, its lateral equivalent north of the Caldas da Rainha structure. **Evidence.**
- mateus2017a, p. 75, Abstract: "the top of the Assenta Member of the Lourinhã Formation (meandering fluvial system with intercalations of shallow-marine lagoonal carbonates; upper Tithonian to lowermost Berriasian)".
- mateus2017a, p. 78: "the clear contrast in sedimentary characteristics between the upper members of the Lourinhã Formation, namely, the Assenta Mb. (to the southeast) and the Santa Rita Mb."
- mateus2017a, p. 85: the member "is laterally equivalent, to the north of the Caldas da Rainha structure (Santa Cruz), to the Santa Rita Mb."
- mateus2017a, p. 86: "the unit should be essentially Tithonian"; "a late early Tithonian to earlier Berriasian age can be proposed for the unit"; "the J/K Tithonian–Berriasian boundary is at the base of magnetozone M18r, around 52 m in the Porto da Calada section (Fig. 11). After this work the last few meters of the Assenta Mb. can be considered as earliest Berriasian".
- mateus2017a, p. 88: the boundary "was tentatively located at 51–53 m (Fig. 11), about 5 m below the limit between the Assenta Mb. and the Porto da Calada Fm."
