# Qingshan

## What stages does the Qingshan Group span?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** Valanginian–Albian, from eruption ages on its volcanics in western Shandong and younger dates in the Jiaolai Basin. Governs `stages` (Valanginian–Albian) on Qingshan, and the floor of the underlying [Mengyin Formation](../../../stratigraphy/m/mengyin.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhu2010a, p. 281: the volcanics "yielded LA-ICPMS 206Pb/238U zircon weighted mean ages ranging from 135.6 ± 3.9 to 115.5 ± 0.9 Ma"; in the Jiaolai Basin "ages of 119 and 120 Ma".
- ling2009a, p. 644: "a mean 206Pb/238U age of 128.0± 1.6 Ma" in the Mengyin Basin.
- xu2015b, in-press version, p. 3: the middle and upper members "yield 40Ar–39Ar ages of 124.3 ± 0.6 Ma" (relayed from Qiu 2002); the lower member's ages range "from 136 to 128 Ma (Ling et al., 2009; Zhu et al., 2010)"; Jiaolai Ar–Ar ages of 120–105 Ma.

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian alone.* Gao and Cheng give the group as "Barremian in age (Harland et al., 1990)" (gao1999a, p. 456); its own dates span the Valanginian to the Aptian at least.

**Open.** The top. Ling and colleagues report 106–98 Ma for the Jiaozhou Basin (ling2009a, p. 640, relayed from Ling 2007), which would reach the Cenomanian; this was not weighed for the group's range. The Albian rests on the Jiaolai Ar–Ar ages.
