# Qingshan

## Does the Qingshan Group reach the Cenomanian?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Ling and colleagues' Jiaozhou eruption ages run to 98 ± 1 Ma, Cenomanian on the ICS chart v2024/12, and under the union rule the group's range takes them. This settles the Open line of the 2026-10-02 entry. Governs `stages` (Valanginian–Cenomanian) and `period: [Early Cretaceous, Late Cretaceous]` on [Qingshan](../../../stratigraphy/q/qingshan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ling2009a, p. 640: the Qingshan volcanic sequences of the Mengyin and Jiaozhou basins "were formed at 128± 2 and 106– 98 Ma", the Jiaozhou figures "106±2,105±4 and 98±1 Ma for the lowest part of the Houkuang Fm and the lower and upper parts of the Shiqianzhuang Fm", relayed from earlier zircon dating.
- These are eruption ages on the unit's own volcanics, not detrital maxima, so they date the unit. The base of the Cenomanian is 100.5 Ma, above 98 ± 1 Ma across its uncertainty.

**Ruled out.**
- *Keeping the Albian top because the Jiaozhou dates are relayed.* Relayed evidence may decide a value, and the Albian top itself rests on regional Ar–Ar ages relayed by Xu and colleagues (2015).
- *Barremian–early Albian from Xi and colleagues (2021).* Under the union rule a narrower reading adds to the others and does not replace them.

**Open.** Ling (2007), the source of the Jiaozhou ages, has not been read.

## What stages does the Qingshan Group span?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** Valanginian–Albian, from eruption ages on its volcanics in western Shandong and younger dates in the Jiaolai Basin. Governs `stages` (Valanginian–Albian) on Qingshan, and the floor of the underlying [Mengyin Formation](../../../stratigraphy/m/mengyin.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhu2010a, p. 281: the volcanics "yielded LA-ICPMS 206Pb/238U zircon weighted mean ages ranging from 135.6 ± 3.9 to 115.5 ± 0.9 Ma"; in the Jiaolai Basin "ages of 119 and 120 Ma".
- ling2009a, p. 644: "a mean 206Pb/238U age of 128.0± 1.6 Ma" in the Mengyin Basin.
- xu2015b, in-press version, p. 3: the middle and upper members "yield 40Ar–39Ar ages of 124.3 ± 0.6 Ma" (relayed from Qiu 2002); the lower member's ages range "from 136 to 128 Ma (Ling et al., 2009; Zhu et al., 2010)"; Jiaolai Ar–Ar ages of 120–105 Ma.

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian alone.* Gao and Cheng give the group as "Barremian in age (Harland et al., 1990)" (gao1999a, p. 456); its own dates span the Valanginian to the Aptian at least.

**Open.** The top. Ling and colleagues report 106–98 Ma for the Jiaozhou Basin (ling2009a, p. 640, relayed from Ling 2007), which would reach the Cenomanian; this was not weighed for the group's range. The Albian rests on the Jiaolai Ar–Ar ages.
