# Qingshan

## Where do the dated Qingshan volcanics fall on the current chart, basin by basin?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** On the ICS chart v2024/12 the dates the cited works give run from the Valanginian to the Albian in western Shandong and the Jiaolai Basin, with the Jiaozhou ages carrying the top into the Cenomanian as the 2026-10-09 entry records. In the Luxi area, the lower member's relayed U–Pb ages of 136 to 128 Ma are Valanginian to Hauterivian, the middle member's 124.3 ± 0.6 Ma Ar–Ar age is Barremian, and the upper member's 117.8 to 114.8 Ma is Aptian. Zhu and colleagues (2010) assign all the intermediate to acid volcanics of the Yishu grabens and the Mengyin and Pingyi basins to the unit; their twelve ages of 135.6 ± 3.9 to 115.5 ± 0.9 Ma are Valanginian to Aptian, and their Jiaolai Basin ages of 119 and 120 Ma are Aptian. Ling and colleagues (2009) take 128.0 ± 1.6 Ma as the timing of the Qingshan volcanics in the Mengyin Basin, Hauterivian across its whole uncertainty; there the group has only two of the four volcanic cycles of the eastern sections, the Bamudi and Fanggezhuang formations. Xu and Li (2015) list the unit in the Early Cretaceous fill of the Jiaolai Basin and relay Ar–Ar ages of 120 to 105 Ma for its volcanics there, Aptian to Albian. Governs `stages: [Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian, Albian, Cenomanian]` on [Qingshan](../../../stratigraphy/q/qingshan.yml), unchanged. It adds to the 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-09 entries and supersedes neither.

**Evidence.**
- xu2015b, in-press version, p. 3, 2.2 Mesozoic stratigraphic frameworks: "the lower member of the Qingshan Formation had U–Pb zircon ages ranging from 136 to 128 Ma", "while the middle and the upper member yield 40Ar–39Ar ages of 124.3 ± 0.6 Ma and 117.8– 114.8 Ma (the main period of volcanic eruption), respectively"; "The sequences of the Jiaolai basin, from bottom to top, comprise the Early Cretaceous Laiyang and Qingshan Formations (Groups) and the Late Cretaceous Wangshi Formation (Group)".
- xu2015b, in-press version, p. 4, 2.2 Mesozoic stratigraphic frameworks: "volcanic rocks from the Qingshan Formation gave whole-rock 40Ar–39Ar ages clustering at 120–105 Ma".
- zhu2010a, p. 281: "The intermediate to acid volcanic rocks are all assigned to the Qingshan Formation in this region. Twelve volcanic samples from the Yishu grabens and the Mengyin Basin (fig. 1a) yielded LA-ICPMS 206Pb/238U zircon weighted mean ages ranging from 135.6 ± 3.9 to 115.5 ± 0.9 Ma"; in the Jiaolai Basin, "volcanic rocks of the Qingshan Formation with LA-ICPMS zircon ages of 119 and 120 Ma".
- ling2009a, p. 641: the eastern samples "are from the Jiaozhou basin comprising all of the four volcanic cycles, whereas the western samples are from the Mengyin basin consisting of the Bamudi and Fanggezhuang Formations".
- ling2009a, p. 644: "eleven zircons comprise a mean 206Pb/238U age of 128.0± 1.6 Ma", and "the age of 128 Ma is interpreted as the timing of the Qingshan volcanic rocks in Mengyin basin".
- On the ICS chart v2024/12 the Valanginian begins at 137.05 Ma, the Hauterivian at 132.6, the Barremian at 125.77, the Aptian at 121.4 and the Albian at 113.2.

**Ruled out.**
- *A Berriasian floor from Zhu and colleagues' oldest age.* 135.6 ± 3.9 Ma reaches back past the base of the Valanginian at 137.05 Ma only at the edge of its uncertainty; its central value is Valanginian.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the 2026-10-09 entry's open line.

## Does the Qingshan Group reach the Cenomanian?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Ling and colleagues' Jiaozhou eruption ages run to 98 ± 1 Ma, Cenomanian on the ICS chart v2024/12, and under the union rule the group's range takes them. This settles the Open line of the 2026-10-02 entry. Governs `stages` (Valanginian–Cenomanian) and `period: [Early Cretaceous, Late Cretaceous]` on [Qingshan](../../../stratigraphy/q/qingshan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ling2009a, p. 640: the Qingshan volcanic sequences of the Mengyin and Jiaozhou basins "were formed at 128± 2 and 106– 98 Ma", the Jiaozhou figures "106±2,105±4 and 98±1 Ma for the lowest part of the Houkuang Fm and the lower and upper parts of the Shiqianzhuang Fm", relayed from earlier zircon dating.
- These are eruption ages on the unit's own volcanics, not detrital maxima, so they date the unit. The base of the Cenomanian is 100.5 Ma, above 98 ± 1 Ma across its uncertainty.

**Ruled out.**
- *Keeping the Albian top because the Jiaozhou dates are relayed.* Relayed evidence may decide a value, and the Albian top itself rests on regional Ar–Ar ages relayed by Xu and colleagues (2015).
- *Barremian–early Albian from Xi and colleagues (2021).* Under the union rule a narrower reading adds to the others and does not replace them.

**Open.** Ling (2007), the source of the Jiaozhou ages, has not been read.

## What stages does the Qingshan Group span?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** Valanginian–Albian, from eruption ages on its volcanics in western Shandong and younger dates in the Jiaolai Basin. Governs `stages` (Valanginian–Albian) on Qingshan, and the floor of the underlying [Mengyin Formation](../../../stratigraphy/m/mengyin.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhu2010a, p. 281: the volcanics "yielded LA-ICPMS 206Pb/238U zircon weighted mean ages ranging from 135.6 ± 3.9 to 115.5 ± 0.9 Ma"; in the Jiaolai Basin "ages of 119 and 120 Ma".
- ling2009a, p. 644: "a mean 206Pb/238U age of 128.0± 1.6 Ma" in the Mengyin Basin.
- xu2015b, in-press version, p. 3: the middle and upper members "yield 40Ar–39Ar ages of 124.3 ± 0.6 Ma" (relayed from Qiu 2002); the lower member's ages range "from 136 to 128 Ma (Ling et al., 2009; Zhu et al., 2010)"; Jiaolai Ar–Ar ages of 120–105 Ma.

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian alone.* Gao and Cheng give the group as "Barremian in age (Harland et al., 1990)" (gao1999a, p. 456); its own dates span the Valanginian to the Aptian at least.

**Open.** The top. Ling and colleagues report 106–98 Ma for the Jiaozhou Basin (ling2009a, p. 640, relayed from Ling 2007), which would reach the Cenomanian; this was not weighed for the group's range. The Albian rests on the Jiaolai Ar–Ar ages.
