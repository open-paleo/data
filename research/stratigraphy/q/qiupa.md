# Qiupa

## Which basin is the Qiupa Formation in?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The Tantou Basin, in Luanchuan County, western Henan. "Luanchuan Basin" is a county-name variant printed in a minority of papers. Governs the basin named in notes on Qiupa and on its taxa, such as [*Qiupalong henanensis*](../../../genera/Q/Qiupalong.yml), [*Luanchuanraptor henanensis*](../../../genera/L/Luanchuanraptor.yml) and [*Yulong mini*](../../../genera/Y/Yulong.yml); the registry has no basin field.

**Evidence.**
- dong1979a, English translation, p. 7: "constructing a small reservoir in the Tantou Basin".
- xu2011b, pp. 213–214: "The Qiupa Formation in Luanchuan County of the Tantou Basin".
- lü2015b, p. 1: "uppermost Cretaceous of the Tantou Basin in Henan Province"; lü2018a, p. 28: "the Qiupa Formation, Tantou Basin, Henan Province".
- wei2022a, pp. 2–3: "the Meso-Cenozoic Tantou Basin at the north of Luanchuan County"; xi2021a, p. 385: "秋扒组主要发育于栾川潭头盆地" ("the Qiupa Formation is developed mainly in the Luanchuan Tantou Basin", translated).
- lü2008c (meeting abstract): "the late Late Cretaceous sediments of the Tantou Basin in Luanchuan County".

**Ruled out.**
- *Luanchuan Basin as the basin's name.* It is printed: "Near Qiupa, Luanchuan Basin, Henan Province, the Late Cretaceous Qiupa Formation" (lü2008d, p. 665), and also in Pittman and Xu (2020, pittman2020a, p. 155) and Xing and colleagues (2024, "the Qiupa Formation in Luanchuan Basin (Dong, 1979 …)", xing2024b, p. 1), who cite Dong (1979), who wrote Tantou. Seven sources read for this note name the Tantou Basin and three the Luanchuan Basin; Luanchuan is the county ("Luanchuan County", xu2011b, p. 214).

**Open.** Jiang and colleagues (2011), whose title names a "Luanchuan-Tantou Basin", has not been read. Qiupa is a town ("Qiupa Town, Luanchuan County", lü2013d, p. 167).
