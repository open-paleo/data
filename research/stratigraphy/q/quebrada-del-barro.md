# Quebrada del Barro

## What age do the cited works give the Quebrada del Barro Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Late Norian to Rhaetian, the age Martínez and Apaldetti (2017) give the formation after the beds that yielded *Leyesaurus* were separated from its top as the Balde de Leyes Formation; the separation and the type's horizon are set out in the 2026-10-03 entry in [*Leyesaurus*](../../genera/L/Leyesaurus.md). Apaldetti and colleagues (2011) wrote before that separation: they doubted the Norian age that Bossi and Bonaparte (1978) based on a referral to *Riojasaurus*, and on *Leyesaurus* suggested an Early Jurassic one. Governs `stages: [Norian, Rhaetian]` and `period: [Late Triassic]` on [Quebrada del Barro](../../../stratigraphy/q/quebrada-del-barro.yml).

**Evidence.**
- apaldetti2011a, p. 1, Abstract: the formation is "an Upper Triassic-Lower Jurassic unit".
- apaldetti2011a, p. 1, Introduction: "Riojasaurus sp. from the Norian? Quebrada del Barro Formation", a specimen "known only by a partial pes without any diagnostic features".
- apaldetti2011a, p. 2, Geological setting: "The age of this unit was originally proposed as Cretaceous [28,29], and later as Upper Triassic [23,30]"; Bossi and Bonaparte "assigned a Norian age for Quebrada del Barro Formation based in the discovery of some vertebrate remains questionably assigned to Riojasaurus".
- apaldetti2011a, p. 4, Age of Quebrada del Barro Formation: "the rationale for the Norian age of the Quebrada del Barro Formation proposed by Bossi and Bonaparte [22] is pulled into question"; "we suggest that the Quebrada del Barro Formation may be of Lower Jurassic age".
- martínez2015a, p. 379: "we tentatively assess a late Norian–Rhaetian age for the QBF".
- martínez2015a, p. 380: with *Leyesaurus* assigned to another unit, "the main argument against the Norian age" of the formation, "–the presence of *Leyesaurus*, a taxon with Early Jurassic affinities–was no longer valid".
- martínez2017a, p. 489: "A late Norian–Rhaetian age is suggested for this unit based on the faunal assemblage known from the upper section of the Quebrada del Barro Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cretaceous.* The age first proposed for the unit, which Apaldetti and colleagues (2011) report was replaced by an Upper Triassic one.
- *Norian on the Riojasaurus referral.* Bossi and Bonaparte (1978) based a Norian age on a partial pes referred to *Riojasaurus*; Apaldetti and colleagues (2011) find the pes undiagnostic and question that basis. The Norian in `stages` comes instead from the faunal assemblage that Martínez and colleagues (2015) compare.
- *Early Jurassic, or Late Triassic to Early Jurassic.* Apaldetti and colleagues (2011) drew the Jurassic age from *Leyesaurus*, found in the uppermost level of the formation as then bounded. Those beds are now the Balde de Leyes Formation, and Martínez and colleagues (2015) treat the argument as no longer valid for the Quebrada del Barro.

**Open.** The late Norian–Rhaetian is a faunal comparison that Martínez and colleagues (2015) call tentative; a numerical date from the formation would settle it.
