# Ulaanoosh

## What do Khand and colleagues (2000) make of the 126 Ma basalt?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** They accept it, and the stages stand on Yu and colleagues (2020). Supersedes the 2026-09-30 entry's statement that the source reporting the date "doubts it". Khand and colleagues take the 126 ± 3 Ma basalt covering the red beds at Uush mountain as Neocomian volcanism and conclude that "we cannot put" those beds into the upper Lower Cretaceous, while the same chapter reports Khand's ostracod dating of the formation as Albian–Cenomanian. Their conclusion about the beds rests on the basalt above them, a limit, and a bound licenses no stage; Yu and colleagues date the formation Albian–Cenomanian, and its lower member Aptian–Albian, from fossils. Governs the note on khand2000a on [Ulaanoosh](../../../stratigraphy/u/ulaanoosh.yml); `stages` unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- khand2000a, p. 60: "At the Uush mountain the red beds of the Baruunbayan Formation is covered by basalts. The K-Ar isotopic dating of this basalt yields an age of 126 ± 3 Ma, and we cannot put this red-colored and coarse-grained sediments into the upper part of the Lower Cretaceous."

**Ruled out.**
- *A pre-Aptian age for the beds.* An inference from a limit.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Where does Khand and colleagues' 126 ± 3 Ma basalt date fall on the current chart?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Before the Aptian on either chart. On ICS chart v2024/12, whose Barremian begins at 125.77 Ma, 126 ± 3 Ma runs from the Hauterivian into the Barremian, with its central value in the latest Hauterivian; in GTS2020, whose Barremian begins at 126.5 Ma, the central value is earliest Barremian. Khand and colleagues (2000) give the date for basalts covering the red beds at Uush mountain, which they assign to the Baruunbayan Formation. Yu and colleagues (2020) date the formation Albian–Cenomanian from vertebrate and invertebrate fossils. The date adds no stage, since the 2026-09-30 entry rules it out as an age for the beds. Governs the absence of any stage older than the Aptian in `stages` on [Ulaanoosh](../../../stratigraphy/u/ulaanoosh.yml).

**Evidence.**
- khand2000a, p. 60: "At the Uush mountain the red beds of the Baruunbayan Formation is covered by basalts. The K-Ar isotopic dating of this basalt yields an age of 126 ± 3 Ma".
- gale2020a, p. 1067: "The Barremian Stage lasted 5.1 Myr from 126.5 ± 0.7 to 121.4 ± 0.6 Ma".
- yu2020a, p. 2, Locality and horizon: the age is "based on vertebrate and invertebrate fossils such as dinosaurs, dinosaur eggs, molluscs, ostracods, and turtles".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Hauterivian or Barremian stage for the formation.* The date was measured on the basalt above the beds, not on the beds, and the 2026-09-30 entry sets it aside against their fossils.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is Khand's "Ulaanuush Formation" this unit, and does its K-Ar date make it Neocomian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Ulaanuush and Ulaanoosh are one name for one unit in the southern Gobi (Ömnögovi). Its age is Aptian–Cenomanian from fossils; the Neocomian K-Ar date is on an overlying basalt, and the source that reports it doubts it. Governs the `variants` and `stages: [Aptian, Albian, Cenomanian]` on Ulaanoosh, and the stages of [*Beg tse*](../../../genera/B/Beg.yml).

**Evidence.**
- khand2000a, p. 60: "Some authors [11] established in this basin a new formation named the Ulaanuush Formation"; yu2020a, p. 2: "These beds are now considered to be part of the Ulaanoosh Formation", formerly mapped as Baruunbayan.
- benton2000c, p. xxvii: "Ulaanuush (= Ulaan Öösh)", "Ulaanöösh Svita".
- yu2020a, p. 2: the formation "ranges from late Early to early Late Cretaceous (Albian-Cenomanian) based on vertebrate and invertebrate fossils", with "The lower member (Aptian-Albian)".
- khand2000a, p. 64: Khand's ostracods show "the Baruunbayan Formation is transitional between the Lower and the Upper Cretaceous, and the Age is Albian-Cenomanian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Neocomian.* Khand (2000) relays "The K-Ar isotopic dating of basalts overlying the Baruunbayan Formation at the Uush mountain [11] … is 126 ± 3 Ma" (p. 64) and concludes the volcanics formed "in the Neocomian Stage", adding that "the isotopic age of the basalt is not consistent with the previous age assignment" (p. 60). The date is on the basalt, a minimum age for the beds beneath, and conflicts with their fossils.
- *Eastern Gobi.* Both sources place the basin in the southern Gobi: "Basins of Umnogobi (Southern Gobi)" (khand2000a, p. 60) and "Baruunbayan, Ömnögovi aimag" (yu2020a, p. 1).

**Open.** Whether the Aptian belongs in the range. Yu and colleagues give the lower member Aptian–Albian but the formation Albian–Cenomanian (p. 2).
