# Ulaanoosh

## Is Khand's "Ulaanuush Formation" this unit, and does its K-Ar date make it Neocomian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Ulaanuush and Ulaanoosh are one name for one unit in the southern Gobi (Ömnögovi). Its age is Aptian–Cenomanian from fossils; the Neocomian K-Ar date is on an overlying basalt, and the source that reports it doubts it. Governs the `variants` and `stages: [Aptian, Albian, Cenomanian]` on Ulaanoosh, and the stages of [*Beg tse*](../../../genera/B/Beg.yml).

**Evidence.**
- khand2000a, p. 60: "Some authors [11] established in this basin a new formation named the Ulaanuush Formation"; yu2020a, p. 2: "These beds are now considered to be part of the Ulaanoosh Formation", formerly mapped as Baruunbayan.
- benton2000c, p. xxvii: "Ulaanuush (= Ulaan Öösh)", "Ulaanöösh Svita".
- yu2020a, p. 2: the formation "ranges from late Early to early Late Cretaceous (Albian-Cenomanian) based on vertebrate and invertebrate fossils", with "The lower member (Aptian-Albian)".
- khand2000a, p. 64: Khand's ostracods show "the Baruunbayan Formation is transitional between the Lower and the Upper Cretaceous, and the Age is Albian-Cenomanian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Neocomian.* Khand (2000) relays "The K-Ar isotopic dating of basalts overlying the Baruunbayan Formation at the Uush mountain [11] … is 126 ± 3 Ma" (p. 64) and concludes the volcanics formed "in the Neocomian Stage", adding that "the isotopic age of the basalt is not consistent with the previous age assignment" (p. 60). The date is on the basalt, a minimum age for the beds beneath, and conflicts with their fossils.
- *Eastern Gobi.* Both sources place the basin in the southern Gobi: "Basins of Umnogobi (Southern Gobi)" (khand2000a, p. 60) and "Baruunbayan, Ömnögovi aimag" (yu2020a, p. 1).

**Open.** Whether the Aptian belongs in the range. Yu and colleagues give the lower member Aptian–Albian but the formation Albian–Cenomanian (p. 2).
