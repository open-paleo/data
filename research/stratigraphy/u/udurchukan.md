# Udurchukan

## What do the cited works say about the age of the Udurchukan Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Every work cited for the formation that gives a stage of its own puts it in the Maastrichtian; the Campanian comes from two older readings that Alifanov and Bolotsky (2010) relay for "the bone-bearing beds of the Amur Region": the Campanian–Maastrichtian boundary of Bolotsky and Kurzanov (1991), and a range from the late Campanian to the middle Maastrichtian, which they attribute to Nessov (1997). They then list later readings, all within the Maastrichtian (early to "middle", "middle", or "middle" to late), and date their own material Maastrichtian. Nessov (1997) gives the range for the lower part of the Tsagayan Formation at Blagoveshchensk, as "terminal Campanian–early Maastrichtian–?middle Maastrichtian"; Bolotsky and Kurzanov (1991) have not been read. Alifanov and Bolotsky (2010) give the Udurchukan as the unit "previously referred to as the Tsagayan Formation or Lower Tsagayan Formation", and treat the Blagoveshchensk beds as "close in genesis and age" to those at Kundur. Governs the Campanian in `stages: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on [Udurchukan](../../../stratigraphy/u/udurchukan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- alifanov2010a, p. 84, Introduction: "Remains come from grayish green clays, with a gravel inclusion in the roof of the Udurchukan Formation (previously referred to as the Tsagayan Formation or Lower Tsagayan Formation)".
- alifanov2010a, p. 84, Introduction: "The bonebearing beds of the Amur Region were dated to the Campanian–Maastrichtian boundary (Bolotsky and Kurzanov, 1991) or a wide range from the Late Campanian to the Middle Maastrichtian (Nessov, 1997). Recent studies suggest the Maastrichtian age, i.e., Early–“Middle” …, “Middle” …, or “Middle”–Late … Maastrichtian".
- alifanov2010a, p. 84, Introduction: "The bone beds of the Blagoveshchensk locality are close in genesis and age to the beds of the Kundur locality."
- alifanov2010a, p. 85, *Arkharavia heterocoelica*: "Kundur locality; Upper Cretaceous, Maastrichtian, Udurchukan Formation".
- nessov1997a, p. ?, locality 81, Blagoveshchensk: "Lower part of the Tsagayan Formation, terminal Campanian–early Maastrichtian–?middle Maastrichtian" (translated).

**Ruled out.** Nothing among the works cited here.

**Open.** Whether the Campanian reaches the Udurchukan at Kundur rests on Bolotsky and Kurzanov (1991), which has not been read, and on Nessov's (1997) range for Blagoveshchensk, which Alifanov and Bolotsky (2010) extend to the Kundur beds by their similarity.
