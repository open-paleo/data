# Ulansuhai

## What is the Ulansuhai Formation, and how old is it?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Ulansuhai and Wulansuhai are one Upper Cretaceous unit, typified north of Wuliji, Alxa Zuo Banner, Inner Mongolia, and younger than about 92 Ma. Governs the name, variants and `period: [Late Cretaceous]` on Ulansuhai.

**Evidence.**
- zhang2009b, entry "Wulansuhai Formation": "First appeared in a 1986 manuscript by No.1 Inner Mongolia Regional Geological Survey Team", published in *Regional Geology of Inner Mongolia* (1991); "Type locality: Wulansuhai, north of Wuliji, Alxa Zuo Banner"; "Age: Late Cretaceous."
- kobayashi2003b, p. 236: "Basalts from the Suhongtu Formation were dated from 146 to 92 Ma … the Ulansuhai Formation is at least younger than 92 Ma".
- hao2024a, p. 2, Fig. 1 caption: "Bayan Mandahu … and Suhongtu … localities where the Upper Cretaceous Ulansuhai Formation emerges".

**Ruled out.**
- *Wulanhushao Formation.* A separate unit, typified "close to Qinggeletu, Alxa Zuo Banner" (zhang2009b, entry "Wulanhushao Formation"), now folded into the Miaogou Formation (hao2024a, p. 2).
- *Lower Cretaceous dinosaurs from Maortu and Dashuigou.* These are Miaogou Formation; see [Maortu and Dashuigou](../../topics/maortu-and-dashuigou-miaogou.md).

**Open.** Whether "Bayan Mandahu Formation" is a formal unit or an informal name for the Ulansuhai's eastern outcrop. Hao and colleagues (2024) treat Bayan Mandahu as a locality of the Ulansuhai; calling the name informal is an inference not checked against the papers that use it as a formation.
