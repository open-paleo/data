# Jiufotang

## Do the Meileyingzi Beds have a stage of their own?

*2026-10-06*

Supersedes the Aptian the Meileyingzi Beds and *Psittacosaurus meileyingensis* carried until 2026-10-06.

**Conclusion.** No. Sereno's (2010) "(Aptian)" follows the formation's name in the type horizon line, so the beds take the Jiufotang's epoch and no stage (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-parent-units-range-repeated-for-a-child-unit-is-the-parents-statement)). No source read dates the horizon of *P. meileyingensis* itself, so the species takes the formation's range, Aptian–Albian (see [methods](../../methods.md#decide-a-species-stage-on-statements-about-its-horizon-not-about-its-formation)). Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous]` and the absence of `stages` on the Meileyingzi Beds of [Jiufotang](../../../stratigraphy/j/jiufotang.yml), and `period.stage: [Aptian, Albian]` on [*Psittacosaurus meileyingensis*](../../../genera/P/Psittacosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno2010a, p. 31, Type Horizon and Locality: "Meileyingzi Beds, Jiufotang Formation (Aptian); Beipiao, Liaoning Province, People's Republic of China".
- sereno1988b, p. 377: the species "is known only from the type locality", in "the same beds (Jiufotang Formation)" as a skull of *P. mongoliensis*; the paper gives no stage.

**Ruled out.**
- *Meileyingzi Beds, Aptian.* The parenthesis follows "Jiufotang Formation", not "Meileyingzi Beds".
- *P. meileyingensis, Aptian alone.* It rested on the same formation-level parenthesis, which is one of the readings that make up the Jiufotang's Aptian–Albian.

**Open.** A dating of the Meileyingzi Beds within the Jiufotang would narrow both the beds and the species.
