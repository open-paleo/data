# Jaisalmer

## Is the Hamira's "Early Jurassic to Bajocian" an age?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** No; it is a bound, and the stages stand, Bajocian to Oxfordian. Confirms the 2026-11-23 entry. Pandey and colleagues (2012) record no index fossils from the Hamira Member and assign it to "the interval from Early Jurassic to Bajocian" from the fossils of the Joyan Member above, a ceiling from position; the same span fills the Hamira's shared cell in their tables. Governs `stages` and `period` on [Jaisalmer](../../../stratigraphy/j/jaisalmer.yml), unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- pandey2012a, p. 69: "Hitherto, no index fossils has been recorded from this member, however, on the basis of fossil records in the overlying Joyan Member, the Hamira Member can be safely assigned to the interval from Early Jurassic to Bajocian (Table 4)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Jurassic and Aalenian.* An outer bound from the member above.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What ages do Pandey and colleagues (2012) give the members of the Jaisalmer Formation?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Bajocian to Oxfordian. In a review of the Jurassic facies and stratigraphy of the Jaisalmer Basin, Pandey and colleagues (2012) date five of the formation's six members to stages: the Joyan Bajocian, the Fort Early to Middle Bathonian, the Bada Bag Late Bathonian, the Kuldhar Callovian and the Jajiya Oxfordian. The lowest member, the Hamira, has no index fossils. The paper assigns it "the interval from Early Jurassic to Bajocian" from the fossils of the overlying Joyan, and its Table 2 gives it one age cell, "Early Jurassic–Bajocian", with the Thaiat and Odania members of the underlying Lathi Formation. That range is a bound set from above, not a dating of the member, so it adds no stage below the Bajocian and no Early Jurassic, under the rule on outer bounds in [methods](../../methods.md). Governs `period: [Middle Jurassic, Late Jurassic]` and `stages: [Bajocian, Bathonian, Callovian, Oxfordian]` on [Jaisalmer](../../../stratigraphy/j/jaisalmer.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pandey2012a, p. 66, Table 2: Joyan "Bajocian", Fort "Early–Mid. Bathonian", Bada Bag "Late Bathonian", Kuldhar "Callovian", Jajiya "Oxfordian"; the Hamira, Thaiat and Odania rows share the cell "Early Jurassic–Bajocian".
- pandey2012a, p. 69: "Hitherto, no index fossils has been recorded from this member, however, on the basis of fossil records in the overlying Joyan Member, the Hamira Member can be safely assigned to the interval from Early Jurassic to Bajocian".

**Ruled out.**
- *An Early Jurassic or Aalenian base for the formation.* It would rest on the Hamira's "Early Jurassic to Bajocian", which the paper derives only from the member above, with no fossil from the Hamira itself.

**Open.** The Hamira's own age. An index fossil from the member would show whether the formation reaches below the Bajocian.
