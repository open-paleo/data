# Jehol

## What do the cited works say about the composition and age of the Jehol Group?

*2026-11-23*

**Conclusion.** Barrett and Wang (2007) give the group as the Jiufotang, Yixian and, queried, Dabeigou formations, cropping out in Liaoning Province and adjacent areas of northeastern China, and date it to the late Early Cretaceous: biostratigraphic correlation first indicated a Late Jurassic age, but radiometric dates and a reinterpretation of the biostratigraphic evidence place the group in the late Early Cretaceous. The same dates show that the *Eosestheria*–*Ephemeropsis*–*Lycoptera* assemblage, which the group shares with the Mengyin Formation of Shandong, is of late Early Cretaceous age. The paper names no stage, so it bears on `period` and not on `stages`. Governs `period: [Early Cretaceous]` on the [Jehol Group](../../../stratigraphy/j/jehol.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barrett2007b, p. 265: "The Jehol Group consists of the Jiufotang, Yixian and Dabeigou (?) Formations and crops out in Liaoning Province and adjacent areas of northeastern China"; "Although biostratigraphical correlations initially indicated a Late Jurassic age for these units, recently obtained radiometric dates and reinterpretation of the biostratigraphical evidence indicate that the group was deposited during the late Early Cretaceous".
- barrett2007b, p. 269: "radiometric dates obtained from the Jehol Group have demonstrated that the *Eosestheria*–*Ephemeropsis*–*Lycoptera* assemblage is of late Early Cretaceous age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Jurassic.* The age the early biostratigraphic correlations gave the group and every unit sharing its fauna; Barrett and Wang (2007) report it overturned by radiometric dating.

**Open.** Nothing.
