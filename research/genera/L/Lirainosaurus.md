# *Lirainosaurus*

## Is Lirainosaurus from the Marnes Rouges Inférieures?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype is from the Laño quarry in the Condado de Treviño, Spain; the Marnes Rouges Inférieures is a French unit, the type formation of *Ampelosaurus*. Governs `location.locality: Laño quarry` and the empty `location.formation` on *Lirainosaurus astibiae*.

**Evidence.**
- sanz1999a, p. 237: "Locus typicus. Laño 1A, 1B and L2 levels, Laño quarry, Condado de Treviño".
- leloeuff1995a, English translation, p. 2: for *Ampelosaurus atacis*, "Stratum typicum: Marnes Rouges Inférieures Formation, member of the red marls of the Maurine".

**Ruled out.**
- *Marnes Rouges Inférieures Formation, Spain.* Jones (2026) gives this in a specimen table (jones2026b, p. 383). The only French link is femora referred to "cf. *Lirainosaurus astibiae*", including some from Bellevue, reported by Díez Díaz and colleagues after Vila and colleagues (2012) (díezdíaz2013a, p. 13); they do not bear on the holotype.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does the Laño quarry have a formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No formal formation. The beds are known by informal level codes; a Sedano Formation equivalence is reported but not adopted. Governs the empty `location.formation` on *Lirainosaurus* and other Laño taxa, and `stage: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]`.

**Evidence.**
- sanz1999a, p. 237: "Upper part of unit B of Baceta et al. (1999); S3U1 unit of Astibia et al. (1987, 1990)", "lower Maastrichtian or, more probably, upper Campanian".
- díezdíaz2013a, p. 4: a "unit equivalent to the Sedano Formation of the North-Castilian Platform (Floquet, 1991; Berreteaga, 2008)".
- fondevilla2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 19: "Laño (late Campanian, chron C32n.2n)"; díezdíaz2015a, p. 294: "three fossiliferous beds dated as late Campanian (Corral et al., 2016)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Vitoria Formation.* Carrano and Sampson (2008) give "S2U1 and S1U3 beds, Vitoria Formation; late Campanian" in a table (carrano2008a, p. 207); no other source read for this note uses the name. Their bed codes differ from the S3U1 printed by Sanz and colleagues (1999), Díez Díaz and colleagues (2013, p. 4) and others.
- *"Unnamed unit" as a formation value.* Csiki-Sava and colleagues label Laño taxa "unnamed unit, Laño, Condado de Treviño" in a figure caption (csiki-sava2015a, p. 48, Fig. 8).

**Open.** Whether to record the Sedano Formation. Isasmendi and colleagues' (2022) "equivalent materials to the Sedano Formation" has not been read, and the late Campanian age's numerical bracket rests on Corral and colleagues (2016), also unread.
