# *Lufengosaurus*

## What is the *Lufengosaurus huenei* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** IVPP V15. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IVPP V15]` on [*Lufengosaurus huenei*](../../../genera/L/Lufengosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barrett2005b, p. 807: "Holotype—IVPP V15, a complete skeleton with skull."
- jones2026b, p. 373: "IVPP V15—skull and skeleton".
- molina-pérez2020a, speed of bipedal sauropodomorphs table: "*Lufengosaurus huenei (L. magnus)* | IVPP V.82" and "*Lufengosaurus huenei* | IVPP V 15".

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP V.82.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give it for the synonym *L. magnus*, in a speed table; they list IVPP V15 for *L. huenei* itself.

**Open.** Nothing.
