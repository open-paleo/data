# *Linheraptor*

## Is the *Linheraptor* holotype from China or Mongolia?

*2026-08-02*

**Conclusion.** China: Xu and colleagues (2010) place it at Bayan Mandahu in Inner Mongolia. Governs `location.country: CN` and `location.region: CN-NM` on [*Linheraptor exquisitus*](../../../genera/L/Linheraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2010b, p. 2: "Bayan Mandahu, Inner Mongolia, China, a lateral equivalent of the Djadokhta Formation"; "Locality and Horizon. Bayan Mandahu, "The Gate" locality, Wulansuhai Formation, Campanian, Upper Cretaceous".
- jones2026c, Eudromaeosauria table: "Djadokhta Fm., Mongolia *Late Cretaceous, Campanian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Djadokhta Formation, Mongolia.* Jones (2026) gives the Mongolian unit that the describing paper names only as a lateral equivalent. A correlation does not move the locality across the border.

**Open.** Nothing on the country. The formation name is a separate question: the record carries Bayan Mandahu, and the describing paper's Wulansuhai is the name of the same rock in its type area (see [Ulansuhai](../../stratigraphy/u/ulansuhai.md)).
