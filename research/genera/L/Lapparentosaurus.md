# *Lapparentosaurus*

## Is the *Lapparentosaurus* holotype from Andranomamy, Tsinjorano or the Manary-Abo site?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** Andranomamy, the only locality the describing paper gives. Tsinjorano is the area where Lavocat excavated a different sauropod in the 1950s, and the Manary-Abo site, where that sauropod was found, lies close to the *Lapparentosaurus* site but is not it. Governs `location.locality: Andranomamy` on [*Lapparentosaurus madagascariensis*](../../../genera/L/Lapparentosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bonaparte1986a, p. 251: "*Holotype*. Two associated neural arches of posterior dorsals … MAA. 91–92"; "*Locality and age*. South of the Kamoro River, near Andranomamy, northern Madagascar; Bathonian (Facies Isalo III)"; the type "is part of a collection of at least five incomplete specimens, made in five small hills very near one another".
- mannion2010a, p. 281: "Lavocat prospected in the south-west of the Majunga Basin (near Tsinjorano) in 1954 … At the Bathonian Manary-Abo site … a relatively complete sauropod individual was excavated", MNHN MAJ 289; Ogier's material (MNHN MAA) was "collected by L. Ginsburg in 1962 … found south of the Kamoro River, near Andranomany, northern Madagascar, close to the Manary-Abo site".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tsinjorano.* It names the area of Lavocat's 1954 work and of MNHN MAJ 289, a specimen that, as Mannion (2010, p. 281) reports, Läng and Goussard (2007) do not refer to *Lapparentosaurus*. The type material was collected by Ginsburg in 1962 near Andranomamy.
- *Manary-Abo.* Mannion's Text-fig. 3 marks with one star "the Manary-Abo locality from where *Lapparentosaurus* Bonaparte, 1986a and the Lavocat … specimen were discovered" (mannion2010a, p. 280), but his text puts Ogier's material "close to the Manary-Abo site" (p. 281). The figure shows two nearby sites with one symbol.

**Open.** The spelling of the village: Bonaparte (1986) prints Andranomamy, Mannion (2010) Andranomany. Ogier (1975), the description of the collection, has not been read and would settle it.

## How are the *Lapparentosaurus* holotype's numbers written?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** MNHN.F.MAA91 and MNHN.F.MAA92: Bonaparte's (1986) "MAA. 91–92" with the prefix of the Paris museum's fossil collection, each number listed on its own. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MNHN.F.MAA91, MNHN.F.MAA92]` on [*Lapparentosaurus madagascariensis*](../../../genera/L/Lapparentosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bonaparte1986a, p. 251: "Holotype. Two associated neural arches of posterior dorsals (Fig. 19.4). Collection: Muséum Nationale d'Histoire Naturelle, Paris, MAA. 91–92."
- jones2026b, p. 375: "MAA 91–92—vertebral fragments; MAA series—skeletal fragments".
- molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropods: "D001-01-78 Adult", a femur.

**Ruled out.**
- *D001-01-78.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give a femur in their column for the largest specimen, not the type.

**Open.** Nothing.
