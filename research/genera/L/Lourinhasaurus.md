# *Lourinhasaurus*

## Does the Lourinhã Formation in the erecting paper apply to the *Lourinhasaurus* lectotype?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** No. Dantas and colleagues (1998) give the Lourinhã Formation only for the Praia de Porto Dinheiro skeleton they referred to the species. That skeleton is now the holotype of *Dinheirosaurus lourinhanensis*. Their entry for the Moinho do Carmo type material names no unit. The record's unit rests on the Moinho do Carmo sources; see [Lusitanian Basin members](../../topics/lusitanian-basin-members.md). Governs `location.formation: Farta Pão` and `location.member: Sobral` on [*Lourinhasaurus alenquerensis*](../../../genera/L/Lourinhasaurus.yml), against the Lourinhã Formation.

**Evidence.**
- dantas1998a, English translation, *Lourinhasaurus alenquerensis*, Holotype: "collection from the bed of Moinho do Carmo, Alenquer, Portugal; Upper Jurassic".
- dantas1998a, English translation, Referred Material: "Material originating from the Praia de Porto Dinheiro beds (Lourinhã, Portugal); Bacia Lusitânica; Lourinhã Formation; Upper Jurassic (Upper Kimmeridgian – Lower Tithonian)".
- mannion2011a, p. 522: the Porto Dinheiro sauropod "remains were subsequently referred to Lourinhasaurus (Dantas et al. 1998a). This material was later described by Bonaparte & Mateus (1999), who recognized it as distinct from Lourinhasaurus and erected the new binomial Dinheirosaurus lourinhanensis".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lourinhã Formation from the erecting paper.* The abstract says the material studied was "recovered from the Lourinhã Formation … Praia de Porto Dinheiro outcrop", and that is the referred skeleton, not the type.
- *"Sobral Member, Lourinhã Form."* Tschopp and colleagues print this for the Moinho do Carmo lectotype (tschopp2015a, p. 7, Table 2). The same table puts the site in "Moinho do Carmo, Alenquer, Lourinhã", joining two municipalities. The inland Sobral of the Arruda sub-basin is placed in the Farta Pão (see the topic note above).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which catalogue numbers make up the *Lourinhasaurus* lectotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** The thirty MG numbers Mocho and colleagues (2014) list for the lectotype. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` (MG 2 through MG 30388 as listed, thirty ids) and `type_specimen.institution: MG` on [*Lourinhasaurus alenquerensis*](../../../genera/L/Lourinhasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mocho2014a, p. 879: "Lectotype: Partial skeleton (Fig. 2D) housed in MG LNEG composed by: cervical vertebrae remains (MG4956, MG30373, MG30377, and MG30379), 12 dorsal vertebrae and several neurapophyses fragments (MG4956: 11 dorsal centra; MG30378 …)".
- jones2026b, p. 380: "MG LNEG—partial skeleton (Mocho et al., 2014)".
- molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropods, Macronaria: "MIGM".

**Ruled out.**
- *MIGM 4956 alone.* The record once held this, one of the thirty numbers, under the older MIGM prefix. Jones (2026) names the collection, citing Mocho and colleagues, and Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give only "MIGM"; neither gives a single number.

**Open.** Nothing.
