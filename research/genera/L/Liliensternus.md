# *Liliensternus*

## Is the *Liliensternus* type material from the Trossingen Formation?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** No. The syntypes come from the nodular marls (Knollenmergel) of the upper Keuper at the Grosser Gleichberg in Thuringia, which Sues and Schoch (2025) place at the top of the Arnstadt Formation. Sues and Schoch use the Trossingen Formation for localities in Baden-Württemberg and Bavaria, and the only Trossingen Formation material they attach to the species is unpublished referred material from Ellingen, Bavaria. Governs `location.formation: Arnstadt`, `location.part: upper` and `location.locality: Grosser Gleichberg` on [*Liliensternus liliensterni*](../../../genera/L/Liliensternus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- huene1934a, p. 145 (translated): the remains were excavated "from the nodular marls of the upper Keuper at the great Gleichberg"; "The locality lies southwest of Hildburghausen"; "In the northern quarry, the bones were found 13 to 14 m below fossil-bearing Rhaetian".
- sues2025a, p. 465: "*Type locality.* Grosser Gleichberg, SW of Hildburghausen, Thuringia. *Type horizon.* Top of Arnstadt Formation, Middle Keuper Subgroup"; "*Referred material.* Still unpublished cranial and postcranial remains of several individuals from the Trossingen Formation of Ellingen in the Weißenburg-Gunzenhausen district of Bavaria belong to *Liliensternus liliensterni* or a closely related taxon (Kirmse et al. 2024)".
- rauhut2003b, p. 15: "*Occurrence.* Knollenmergel, Thüringen and Württemberg, Germany; ?Frick, Switzerland."

**Ruled out.**
- *Trossingen Formation.* The type locality is in Thuringia (huene1934a, p. 145; sues2025a, p. 465). The Ellingen remains are referred material, and Rauhut's (2003) Württemberg occurrence is listed beside the syntypes' Thuringia without being tied to them.

**Open.** Nothing.
