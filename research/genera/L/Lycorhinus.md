# *Lycorhinus*

## Where does the *Lycorhinus angustidens* holotype come from?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** Paballong, near Mount Fletcher, Eastern Cape, in the upper Elliot Formation, at 30°26′S, 28°31′E. Norman and colleagues (2011) and Sereno (2012) both print that position from the same table of Kitching and Raath (1984), so it is one original statement. Governs `location.locality`, `location.region`, `location.part` and `location.coordinates` on [*Lycorhinus angustidens*](../../../genera/L/Lycorhinus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- haughton1924a, pp. 343–344: the type is "Portion of lower jaw with teeth. S.A.M. Cat. No. 3606", from "Paballong, Mount Fletcher, C.P.", horizon "Red Beds".
- norman2011a, p. 234: "Upper Elliot Formation at Paballong (30°26′S, 28°31′E; Kitching & Raath, 1984: table 1), near Mount Fletcher, Eastern Cape Province".
- sereno2012a, p. 133: "Type locality. Paballong, near Mount Fletcher, Transkei (Herschel) District, Cape Province, South Africa; S30°26', E28°31' (Kitching and Raath 1984)".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Buck Camp", Golden Gate Highlands National Park, Free State, 28°30′S, 28°37′E.* This is the locality of BP/1/4244, the holotype of *Lanasaurus scalpridens* (norman2011a, p. 239), which Sereno treats as a junior synonym and refers to *L. angustidens* (sereno2012a, p. 14). The specimen is referred material, not the type, and the site is about 220 km from Paballong.
- *Witskop near Burgersdorp.* Norman and colleagues record that "Broom (1932) mistakenly suggested that the holotype specimen was collected from Witskop near Burgersdorp" (norman2011a, p. 234).

**Open.** Nothing. Kitching and Raath (1984), the source of the coordinate, has not been read.
