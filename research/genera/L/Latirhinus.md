# *Latirhinus*

## Does the position printed in the *Latirhinus* describing paper mark the holotype quarry?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** It cannot be shown to, so no coordinate is recorded. Prieto-Márquez and Serrano Brañas (2012) print 25°28′15″ N, 101°42′00″ W for the holotype's site in the ejido Presa San Antonio; Serrano-Brañas and colleagues (2020) print the same pair for BENC 2/2, the *Paraxenisaurus* quarry, which they place 1 km southeast of the town of Presa San Antonio. One pair printed for two excavations marks at most one of them, and the later paper ties it to the other. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` and `location.locality: Ejido Presa San Antonio` on [*Latirhinus uitstlani*](../../../genera/L/Latirhinus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- prieto-márquez2012a, p. 608: "IGM 6583 was collected from a site located in the ejido (communal land) Presa San Antonio, in the town of Parras de la Fuente, southern Coahuila, northern Mexico (Figure 1(A)) (coordinates 25° 28′ 15″ N and 101° 42′00″ W)".
- serrano-brañas2020a, p. 2, Geographic and stratigraphic setting: "The first collecting site (BENC 2 /2) is located 1 km southeast of the Presa San Antonio town in the Municipality of Parras de la Fuente (25° 28′15″N, 101° 42′ 00″ W)".
- ramírez-velasco2021b, Systematic Paleontology, Locality and horizon: "SPA-88-9 quarry, Ejido Presa San Antonio, Parras de la Fuente Municipality, Coahuila, northern Mexico".
- serrano-brañas2022a, Systematic paleontology: "the SPA-88-9 quarry is also known as Coah 14-1 in Serrano-Brañas, 2006"; "IGM 12716 was collected at Quarry Coah 14-2, also in the Ejido Presa San Antonio"; the sacrum "was collected in a different quarry (Coah 14-2) within the Presa San Antonio area, so it was never considered part of the original skeleton".

**Ruled out.**
- *25°28′15″ N, 101°42′00″ W as the quarry's position.* Printed again for BENC 2/2, a different excavation 1 km from the town (serrano-brañas2020a, p. 2).
- *"Coah 14" as the locality.* The describing paper does not use the code. The holotype quarry is Coah 14-1, and Coah 14-2 is a second quarry in the same ejido whose sacrum is not part of the type (serrano-brañas2022a), so a bare "Coah 14" does not say which.

**Open.** Which site, if either, the printed pair marks. A position for the SPA-88-9 quarry from the 1988 field records would settle it.
