# *Lessemsaurus*

## What is the *Lessemsaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** PVL 4822-1, from Bonaparte (1999), as Pol and Powell (2007) restricted it. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [PVL 4822-1]` on [*Lessemsaurus sauropoides*](../../../genera/L/Lessemsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bonaparte1999d, Specimen PVL 4822, Holotype: "PVL 4822-1, a group of presacral vertebrae and sacrals" (translated).
- pol2007a, Systematic palaeontology: "the holotype is now restricted to the eight presacral neural arches originally described and figured by Bonaparte (1999) and catalogued as PVL 4822-1".
- jones2026b, p. 375: "PVL 4822–1—neural arches".
- molina-pérez2020a, chronology of the largest early sauropodomorphs: "*Lessemsaurus sauropoides* CRILAR-PV 302".
- apaldetti2018a, p. 1228: "CRILAR PV-302: left ilium, articulated ischia", listed as referred material.

**Ruled out.**
- *CRILAR-PV 302.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give the larger specimen that Apaldetti and colleagues (2018) refer to the species, in a table of size records, not as the type.

**Open.** Nothing.
