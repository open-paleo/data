# *Labocania*

## Which stages does *Labocania* take?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** The [La Bocana Roja](../../../stratigraphy/l/la-bocana-roja.yml)'s range, Cenomanian to Campanian. No source read dates the type horizon, LACM locality 7257, so under the rule that a taxon with no statement about its horizon takes its unit's range, the record takes the unit's stages. Governs `stage: [Cenomanian, Turonian, Coniacian, Santonian, Campanian]` on [*Labocania anomala*](../../../genera/L/Labocania.yml), replacing `[Campanian]`.

**Evidence.**
- molnar1974a, p. 1010: "The 'La Bocana Roja' may thus be presumed late Campanian or earlier in age. It is probably not older than Cenomanian", from the dated El Gallo above and the first appearance of hadrosaurs; the area "has not been mapped".
- kane2022a: a detrital-zircon maximum depositional age of 93.6 ± 1.1 Ma for the upper part of the formation, not sampled at the *Labocania* locality.

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian from the fauna.* Rivera-Sylva and Longrich (2024) argue that *Labocania* is "referable to the Teratophoneini, a clade known only from the Campanian" (rivera-sylva2024a, p. 262); that dates the bed by the taxon itself and is the vertebrate side of the unit's dispute, not a dating of the horizon.

**Open.** Whether the type locality lies in the La Bocana Roja or in the El Disecado Member of the El Gallo Formation, which Rivera-Sylva and Longrich (2024, p. 263) raise from the museum's locality records.

## What is the *Labocania anomala* holotype's number?

*2026-08-02*

**Conclusion.** IGM 5307. The material was published as LACM 20877 and is now catalogued in Mexico under the IGM number. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IGM 5307]`, `type_specimen.institution: IGM-MX` and the `former_ids` entry from LACM 20877 on [*Labocania anomala*](../../../genera/L/Labocania.yml).

**Evidence.**
- molnar1974a, p. 1010: "Type specimen.-LACM 20877."
- rivera-sylva2024a, p. 246: "the only tyrannosaur described so far from Mexico, *Labocania anomala* (IGM 5307)"; their restudy is "of the type material of IGM 5307".
- jones2026c, Coelurosauria table: "LACM 20877—cranial and skeletal fragments (Molnar, 1974)".

**Ruled out.**
- *LACM 20877 as the current number.* It is the number in the describing paper, which Jones (2026) repeats. Rivera-Sylva and Longrich (2024) restudied the specimen as IGM 5307.

**Open.** When the transfer happened. Neither paper dates it.
