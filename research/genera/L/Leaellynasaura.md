# *Leaellynasaura*

## What stages does *Leaellynasaura* take?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Aptian and Albian. The describing paper dates the Otway Basin coastal sites, Dinosaur Cove among them, "latest Aptian–early Albian"; the record's Albian alone dropped its Aptian. Herne and colleagues (2019) give two values that contradict each other and add nothing outside that span. Governs `stage: [Aptian, Albian]` on [*Leaellynasaura amicagraphica*](../../../genera/L/Leaellynasaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rich1989a: "Wagstaff & McEwen Mason (this volume, p. 54) have palynologically dated most of the tetrapod-bearing sites in the Otway and Gippsland Basins. Accordingly, the Otway Basin coastal sites are latest Aptian–early Albian".
- herne2019a, p. 575: *Leaellynasaura amicagraphica* is "from the younger, early Albian Eumeralla Formation"; p. 577, Table 5: "Eumeralla Formation, Otway Group, Otway Basin (lower Aptian)", printed for every Dinosaur Cove taxon.

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian alone.* No source read gives it for the horizon.
- *Lower Aptian, from Herne and colleagues' Table 5.* Their own text says early Albian.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which formation is the *Leaellynasaura* holotype from, when its describing paper names only the Otway Group?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** The Eumeralla Formation. Rich and Rich (1989) give Slippery Rock, Dinosaur Cove, as "Otway Group" and name no formation; Herne and colleagues (2019) assign *L. amicagraphica* and Dinosaur Cove to the Eumeralla Formation of the Otway Group. Governs `location.formation: Eumeralla` on [*Leaellynasaura amicagraphica*](../../../genera/L/Leaellynasaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rich1989a, p. 19: "Slippery Rock, Dinosaur Cove, 38°46'54±1″ S, 143°24'15±1″ E, Otway Group, latest Aptian–early Albian"; p. 21: "TYPE LOCALITY AND STRATIGRAPHIC POSITION: Slippery Rock, Dinosaur Cove, Otway Group, latest Aptian–early Albian."
- herne2019a, p. 575: "*Atlascopcosaurus loadsi* … and *Leaellynasaura amicagraphica*, are from the younger, early Albian Eumeralla Formation"; p. 577, Table 5: "*Leaellynasaura amicagraphica* Rich and Rich, 1989 | Dinosaur Cove, Victoria | Eumeralla Formation, Otway Group, Otway Basin (lower Aptian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *`group: Otway` with no formation.* The describing paper stops at the group, but a later paper names the formation for this species (herne2019a, pp. 575, 577).

**Open.** The stage. Herne and colleagues call the Eumeralla "early Albian" in their text and "lower Aptian" in their Table 5, for the same species. They cite Korasidis and colleagues (2016) for the dating of the formation; that paper has not been read.
