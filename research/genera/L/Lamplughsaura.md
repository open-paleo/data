# *Lamplughsaura*

## How does the printed position of the *Lamplughsaura* holotype read?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** Longitude first. Kutty and colleagues (2007) print "(79°32′, 19°15′)" with no hemisphere letters; read as 79°32′ E, 19°15′ N it lies in Adilabad District, the district the same sentence names, and converts to 19.25, 79.533333, to the whole arcminute. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Lamplughsaura dharmaramensis*](../../../genera/L/Lamplughsaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kutty2007a, p. 1220: "Holotype, ISI R257 … Upper Dharmaram Formation, Lower Jurassic (Sinemurian, ~200 Ma). North of Krishnapur village (79°32′, 19°15′), Adilabad District, Andhra Pradesh, southern India (Fig. 1.2)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Latitude first.* Taken in the order printed, the pair falls in the high Arctic, not in India.
- *79°30′, 19°15′.* The same paper prints it for "Northwest of Krishnapur village", the site of ISI R265, the *Pradhania gracilis* holotype (kutty2007a, p. 1232); see [*Pradhania*](../P/Pradhania.md).

**Open.** Nothing.
