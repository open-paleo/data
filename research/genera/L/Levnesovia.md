# *Levnesovia*

## Is CDZh-17a the *Levnesovia* holotype's site?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** No. Sues and Averianov (2009) give the locality only as Dzharakuduk. The site code CDZh-17a is printed by Currie and colleagues (1993) for a caenagnathid dentary collected in 1979. Governs `location.locality: Dzharakuduk` on [*Levnesovia transoxiana*](../../../genera/L/Levnesovia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sues2009a, p. 2549, Locality and horizon: "Dzharakuduk, Navoi Viloyat (district), central Kyzylkum Desert, Uzbekistan. Bissekty Formation; Late Cretaceous (Middle–Late Turonian)."
- currie1993a, p. 2258: "CMGP 401/12457, anterior region of fused dentaries collected in 1979 from Dzhyrakuduk locality (site CDZh-17a) … Lower part of Bissekty Formation (upper Turonian), 26 m above base of formation."
- nessov1995a, p. 95, locality 70 (translated): "Dzharakuduk II* (=Bissekty, =Kulbeke, =Dzharakuduk), bluffs northeast and east of the Dzharakuduk settlement and the abandoned Dzharakuduk well".

**Ruled out.**
- *CDZh-17a.* The site of CMGP 401/12457 (currie1993a, p. 2258), a different specimen of a different clade. Its "26 m above base of formation" belongs to that dentary as well.
- *Dzharakuduk II and Kulbeke as finer sites.* Nessov (1995) gives Dzharakuduk II, Bissekty, Kulbeke and Dzharakuduk as names for one locality, so adding them to the describing paper's Dzharakuduk names no smaller place.

**Open.** Where along the Dzharakuduk escarpment, and at what level, USNM 538191 was found. No paper cited here gives a site code or height for it.
