# *Lophostropheus*

## Is *Lophostropheus* a coelophysoid?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Yes, on the analyses that resolve it. Its describers recover it as the sister of Coelophysidae and Ezcurra (2012) at the base of Coelophysoidea; Baron (2024) leaves it unresolved at the base of Neotheropoda. Governs `parent: Coelophysoidea` and the dispute on [*Lophostropheus airelensis*](../../../genera/L/Lophostropheus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ezcurra2007a, Fig. 6, p. 78: "The present analysis places the Airel theropod into a clade within the Coelophysoidea, composed of the French taxon and Coelophysidae. Dilophosaurus and L. liliensterni were regarded as sucessive more basal members of Coelophysoidea".
- ezcurra2012a: "Liliensternus, Procompsognathus, Lophostropheus, Gojirasaurus and a specimen previously identified as a juvenile of Dracovenator, were recovered within a polytomy at the base of Coelophysoidea".
- baron2024a, Fig. 4, p. 7: *Lophostropheus* with *Liliensternus*, in a polytomy with *Dilophosaurus*, the coelophysids and Averostra.

**Ruled out.**
- *A grouping with Dilophosaurus on the averostran stem.* Jones (2026) attributes it to Baron and colleagues (2017); the analysis on their dataset read here, Baron (2024), leaves *Lophostropheus* in an unresolved polytomy rather than with *Dilophosaurus*.

**Open.** Whether analyses on the dataset of Baron and colleagues resolve it.
