# *Liaoningotitan*

## Which member of the Yixian Formation is the *Liaoningotitan* holotype from?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** The Jianshangou Member. The describing paper's Chinese text names the Jianshangou Bed (尖山沟层), which the data records at member rank as it records the Dawangzhangzi Bed for *Liaoningosaurus*. The stage is unchanged, since the Jianshangou is Barremian. Governs `location.member: Jianshangou` and `location.locality: Xiaobeigou Village` on [*Liaoningotitan sinensis*](../../../genera/L/Liaoningotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhou2018a, p. 328, Locality and horizon: "辽宁西部北票市上园镇小北沟；早白垩世义县组尖山沟层（约 125 Ma）" ("Xiaobeigou, Shangyuan Town, Beipiao City, western Liaoning; Jianshangou Bed of the Lower Cretaceous Yixian Formation (ca. 125 Ma)", translated), checked on the PDF.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
