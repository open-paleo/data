# *Limaysaurus*

## Does the *Limaysaurus* holotype come from the top of the Candeleros?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The describing paper ties the top of the Candeleros to the holotype by number. The "Upper" in Salgado and colleagues (2004) qualifies the age, not the unit, and is not the source of the part. Governs `location.part: upper` on [*Limaysaurus tessonei*](../../../genera/L/Limaysaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- calvo1995a, p. 15, Locality and horizon: "Top of the Candeleros Member (MUCPv-205 and 206) and base of the Huincul Member (MUCPv-153) of the Rio Limay Formation"; the holotype is "MUCPv-205".
- salgado2004a: "Upper Cretaceous (lower Cenomanian) Candeleros Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *No part, from Salgado and colleagues' wording.* Their "Upper" is the age; the part rests on Calvo and Salgado's line.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Limaysaurus* type locality 5 km or 10 km southwest of El Chocón?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** About 5 km, as the describing paper gives it. Calvo (1999), by one of the describing authors, gives 10 km for the same holotype without remarking on the change. Governs `location.locality: 5 km southwest of El Chocón` on [*Limaysaurus tessonei*](../../../genera/L/Limaysaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- calvo1995a, p. 15: "*Locality and horizon* - Approximately 5 km Southwest of El Chocón locality, Neuquén Province, Patagonia, Argentina (Fig. 2). Top of the Candeleros Member (MUCPv-205 and 206)".
- calvo1999a, p. 19: the holotype, MUCPv-205, was excavated in 1988, and "The site lies 10 km SW of El Chocón Locality."

**Ruled out.**
- *10 km southwest.* A later restatement that gives no reason for departing from the describing paper.

**Open.** Which distance is right. Calvo (1999, p. 20) places MUCPv-206 "approximately 1,000 meters away from MUCPv-205" at the same level, which does not account for a 5 km difference. A map or position for the MUCPv-205 quarry would settle it.
