# *Lingwulong*

## Is the Lingwulong holotype from the Yan'an or the Zhiluo Formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Disputed. The describing paper says Yan'an Formation, late Toarcian–Bajocian; a later dedicated field study puts the quarry at the top of Member 2 of the overlying [Zhiluo Formation](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhiluo.yml), which would make *Lingwulong* younger. Governs `location.formation` and `stage` on *Lingwulong shenqi*, and requires a dispute note on both.

**Evidence.**
- xu2018b, p. 2, Horizon and locality: "Yanan Formation, late Early to early Middle Jurassic (late Toarcian–Bajocian)".
- you2019a, p. 31: "灵武恐龙化石赋存于中侏罗统直罗组二段顶部" ("the Lingwu dinosaur fossils occur at the top of Member 2 of the Middle Jurassic Zhiluo Formation", translated); p. 33: "赋存于剖面第5层" ("in Layer 5 of the section", translated). The study uses "露头观测、剖面测制和钻探工程" ("outcrop observation, section measurement and drilling", translated), including its own borehole, and cites Xu and colleagues (2018).
- you2019a, p. 32: the Zhiluo has a "冲刷接触" ("scour contact", translated) with the underlying Yan'an Formation's No. 1 coal.

**Ruled out.**
- *A stage from You and colleagues (2019).* The study gives no stage. "Bathonian–early Oxfordian" for the Zhiluo appears first in Dai and colleagues (2022, dai2022a, p. 26, citing You and colleagues) and is repeated by Ren and colleagues (2023, ren2023a, p. 5), who also give "Bathonian-Callovian" (p. 2). Mannion and colleagues (2025) pair "Bathonian–Callovian … Zhiluo Formation (You et al., 2019)" with an "Aalenian … Yan'an Formation" (mannion2025a, p. 40), which differs from Xu and colleagues' late Toarcian–Bajocian. These stage values are attributions to You and colleagues, not their statements.
- *You Hai-Lu as author.* The study is by You Shuisheng and colleagues of the Sichuan regional survey (you2019a, p. 31).

**Open.** Which formation. You and colleagues (2019, p. 31) record three views, Yan'an, Zhiluo and Anding, with a minority extending the beds into the Cretaceous. A stage for the Zhiluo quarry level has not been published.
