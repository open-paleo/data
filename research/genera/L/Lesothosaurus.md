# *Lesothosaurus*

## Which specimens make up the *Lesothosaurus* type series?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; the record follows Sereno (1991). Galton (1978) erected *Lesothosaurus diagnosticus* on two syntypes, UCL B17 and UCL B23, which Sereno lists as BMNH RUB17 and RUB23. Sereno (1991) lists those two and a third, BMNH R11956, a partial skull, without saying where it came from or why it joins the type series. Governs the three-specimen `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Lesothosaurus diagnosticus*](../../../genera/L/Lesothosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1978a, p. 156: "Syntypes: UCL B17 — assemblage of bones representing at least two individuals …; UCL B23 — an almost complete skull"; "Horizon and Localities: both from Red Beds of Stormberg Series (Lower Jurassic) of Lesotho (see THULBORN 1970, 1972 for details)".
- sereno1991a, p. 169: "Syntypes—BMNH RUB17 …; BMNH RUB23 …; BMNH R11956, partial skull"; "Locality and Horizon—Likhoele, near Mapheteng, Lesotho, southern Africa; 'Red Beds,' upper Elliot Formation".
- sereno1991a, p. 168: the RUB17 and RUB23 material "was discovered in 1963–1964 in Lesotho by an expedition from University College (London)"; p. 169: "Further well-preserved cranial and postcranial remains of *L. diagnosticus* were collected in 1967–1968 in Lesotho by a joint expedition".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether R11956 is part of the material on which Galton named the species. Sereno gives no collector or year for it; he describes later material from the 1967–68 expedition separately, as referred specimens. Thulborn's descriptions (1970, 1972), to which Galton defers for collecting details, have not been read; they or the NHMUK register entry for R11956 would settle it.
