# *Loricatosaurus*

## Is the *Loricatosaurus* holotype Callovian or Oxfordian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Callovian, from the Oxford Clay at Fletton; see [*Lexovisaurus*](Lexovisaurus.md) for the same horizon. Governs `period.stage: [Callovian]` on [*Loricatosaurus priscus*](../../../genera/L/Loricatosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maidment2008a, p. 373: "The holotype is from the Oxford Clay (Callovian: Middle Jurassic) of Fletton, Peterborough (Galton 1985)".
- jones2026a, Stegosauria table: "Oxford Clay Fm., United Kingdom *Middle Jurassic, Oxfordian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Oxfordian.* Jones (2026) gives it in a table without a source; the describing paper gives the Callovian.

**Open.** Nothing.
