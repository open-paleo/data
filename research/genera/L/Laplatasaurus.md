# *Laplatasaurus*

## What is the *Laplatasaurus* lectotype's number?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** MLP-CS 1128, the right tibia, and MLP-CS 1127, the right fibula, as Gallina and Otero (2015) give the lectotype. Bonaparte and Gasparini (1979), who designated it, cataloged the pair as MLP 26-306 with the Cinco Saltos numbers 1128 and 1127. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MLP-CS 1128, MLP-CS 1127]` on [*Laplatasaurus araukanicus*](../../../genera/L/Laplatasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bonaparte1979c, p. 399: "Lectotype: M.L.P. 26-306 right tibia (C.S. 1128) and right fibula (C.S. 1127)".
- gallina2015a: "Lectotype. MLP-CS 1128, right tibia and MLP-CS 1127, right fibula".

**Ruled out.**
- *MLP 26-306 alone.* The number covers both bones, which carry their own Cinco Saltos numbers, and the latest revision uses those.

**Open.** Nothing.
