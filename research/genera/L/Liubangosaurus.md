# *Liubangosaurus*

## Is the *Liubangosaurus* holotype from the Napai or the Xinlong Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Xinlong Formation, the current name for the unit Mo and colleagues (2010) called the Napai Formation. The Liubang quarry also yielded [*Fusuisaurus zhaoi*](../../../genera/F/Fusuisaurus.yml), so both records take the same unit. Governs `location.formation: Xinlong` and the empty `location.member` and `location.part` on [*Liubangosaurus hei*](../../../genera/L/Liubangosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mo2010a, abstract: the holotype "was discovered in the Lower Cretaceous Napai Formation of Guangxi Province"; Introduction: the Liubang quarry yielded "a very large one (described as *Fusuisaurus zhaoi* by Mo et al., 2006) and two smaller ones".
- mo2016a, p. 144: "the Xinlong Formation (then known as the Napai Formation) of south-western Guangxi"; *Liubangosaurus hei* is "Also from the Liubang quarry".
- jones2026b, p. 380: "Napai Fm.".

**Ruled out.**
- *Napai Formation.* The older name, which Jones (2026) repeats. Mo and colleagues (2016) give Napai as the name the Xinlong Formation once had, and Napai survives as the name of the basin.

**Open.** Nothing.
