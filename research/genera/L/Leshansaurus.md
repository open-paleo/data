# *Leshansaurus*

## Is *Leshansaurus* from the upper or the lower Shaximiao Formation?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** The upper. The describing paper places the holotype in the upper part of the Upper Shaximiao Formation. Li and colleagues (2011) list the species in the *Shunosaurus*–*Omeisaurus* assemblage of the lower member without discussing its horizon. Governs `location.part: upper` on [*Leshansaurus qianweiensis*](../../../genera/L/Leshansaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- li2009b, p. 1204 (translated): "Yuanbaoshan (Luanbaoshan), Tongyi Village, Xiaoguzhen, Qianwei County, Leshan City; longitude 104°03′18″E, latitude 29°05′21″N; upper part of the Upper Shaximiao Formation, Late Jurassic in age."
- li2011a, p. 23: "The famous vertebrate fossil assemblage, called the *Shunosaurus*-*Omeisaurus* assemblage (from the lower member of Shaximiao Formation)", whose list of Megalosauridae begins "*Leshansaurus qianweiensis* Li et al., 2009".

**Ruled out.**
- *The lower member.* Li and colleagues (2011) give no horizon for the specimen; the describing paper's locality line, written for this holotype, gives the upper part of the formation.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `li2011a`: Li, K.; Liu, J.; Yang, C.; Hu, F. (2011). Dinosaur assemblages from the Middle Jurassic Shaximiao Formation and Chuanjie Formation in the Sichuan-Yunnan Basin, China.
