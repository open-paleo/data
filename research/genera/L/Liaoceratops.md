# *Liaoceratops*

## Which of the two sites in the *Liaoceratops* locality line is the holotype's?

*2026-09-10*

**Conclusion.** Yanzigou. Xu and colleagues (2002) give "Yanzigou and Lujiatun, Shangyuan" in a locality line that covers both the holotype and a referred juvenile skull, and say the specific name refers to "the village near which the holotype was found". Governs `location.locality: Yanzigou, Shangyuan` on [*Liaoceratops yanzigouensis*](../../../genera/L/Liaoceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2002b, p. 314: "The specific name 'yanzigou' refers to the village near which the holotype was found"; "*Holotype*. IVPP … V12738, an almost complete skull (Fig. 1). *Referred specimen*. IVPP V12633, a juvenile skull (Fig. 2)"; "*Locality and horizon*. Yanzigou and Lujiatun, Shangyuan, western Liaoning, China; the lowest part of the Yixian Formation, probably Early Cretaceous."

**Ruled out.**
- *"Yanzigou and Lujiatun, Shangyuan" for the holotype.* The line is written for both specimens, and the etymology places the holotype at Yanzigou. That the second site, Lujiatun, is the juvenile's is our inference; the paper does not say so.
- *`member: Lujiatun`.* Lujiatun appears only as a place name in the locality line, with no rank word, and the horizon is given only as "the lowest part of the Yixian Formation".

**Open.** Nothing.
