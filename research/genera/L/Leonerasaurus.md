# *Leonerasaurus*

## Where does *Leonerasaurus* sit?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** Sauropodiformes incertae sedis. Every analysis read places it inside Sauropodiformes, from the base of the clade to beside Sauropoda, so the parent holds while the finer position stays in dispute. Governs `parent: Sauropodiformes` and the dispute on [*Leonerasaurus*](../../../genera/L/Leonerasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pol2011b: "All MPTs depict Leonerasaurus as the sister taxon of the clade composed of Melanorosaurus readi and Sauropoda"; placing it "more basally within Anchisauria" costs two extra steps.
- mcphee2015b: "Leonerasaurus is recovered in a highly derived position near the base of Sauropoda", a result "potentially explicable via the large amounts of missing information within this OTU".
- otero2015a: "*Sefapanosaurus* is retrieved within a polytomy along with *Leonerasaurus*, *Aardonyx*, and more derived sauropodomorphs."
- rauhut2020a: "At the base of Sauropodiformes, *Leonerasaurus* and *Sefapanosaurus* take several equally parsimonious positions".

**Ruled out.**
- *Among basal massopodans.* The earlier summary's alternative; no analysis read places it there. The nearest is Pol and colleagues' slightly suboptimal position inside Anchisauria, which is still within Sauropodiformes.

**Open.** Nothing.
