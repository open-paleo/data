# *Liaoningosaurus*

## Is the *Liaoningosaurus* holotype from the Dawangzhangzi Bed or the Jianshangou Bed?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** The Dawangzhangzi Bed, recorded at member rank. Xu and colleagues (2001) give the locality as WangJiagou, Yixian, and the horizon only as the Yixian Formation; Arbour and Currie (2015) call the site the Baicaigou locality and place the holotype in the Dawangzhangzi Bed. Governs `location.member: Dawangzhangzi` and `location.locality: Wangjiagou` on [*Liaoningosaurus paradoxus*](../../../genera/L/Liaoningosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2001a, p. 297: "Type locality and horizon. WangJiagou, Yixian, Liaoning Province, China; Yixian Formation, Lower Cretaceous."
- arbour2015a, p. 16 of the online-first file, *Liaoningosaurus paradoxus*: "Holotype locality and age. Baicaigou locality (WangJiagou in Xu et al. 2001), Dawangzhangzi Bed of Yixian Formation, about 122 Ma (Xu & Norell 2006)."
- ji2016a, p. 183: the referred specimen XHPM-1206 "was collected from the Jianshangou Bed of the Lower Cretaceous Yixian Formation at Dawangzhangzi Village, Lingyuan County … about 160 km west and slightly south of Wangjiagou, Yixian, where the holotype of *L. paradoxus*" was found.

**Ruled out.**
- *Jianshangou Bed.* It is the horizon of XHPM-1206, a referred specimen; in Ji and colleagues (2016) "Dawangzhangzi" is that specimen's village, about 160 km from the holotype site, not the holotype's bed.

**Open.** The source of Arbour and Currie's bed assignment. The describing paper names no bed, and Arbour and Currie cite Xu and Norell (2006) only for the age.
