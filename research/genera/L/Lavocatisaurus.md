# *Lavocatisaurus*

## Do the Agrio del Medio positions given for *Rayososaurus* apply to the *Lavocatisaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** No. Carballido and colleagues (2010) give two bearings from the Agrio del Medio school and a coordinate, all for the site of *Rayososaurus agrioensis*, which they place in the Candeleros Formation. The *Lavocatisaurus* holotype comes from the Pichi Neuquén Member of the Rayoso Formation, and its describing paper names the site only as the "Agrio del Medio site". Governs `location.locality: Agrio del Medio`, `location.member: Pichi Neuquén` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Lavocatisaurus agrioensis*](../../../genera/L/Lavocatisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- canudo2018a, p. 680: "The rebbachisaurid bones studied in this paper were found in the middle part of the Pichi Neuquén Member, approximately 44 metres from its base"; p. 682: "Type locality: Agrio del Medio site, Neuquén Province, Argentina."
- carballido2010a, p. 495: according to Bonaparte, the *Rayososaurus* excavation "was some 3 km to the south of the school in Agrio del Medio"; p. 496: the relocated site is "2.4 km east-southeast of Primary School No. 244 in Agrio del Medio (38˚ 21' 10'' S – 69˚ 54' 57'' W)", and "the bonebearing level corresponds to the upper section of the Candeleros Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *38°21′10″ S, 69°54′57″ W.* The relocated *Rayososaurus* site (carballido2010a, p. 496), another taxon in another formation.
- *"3 km south of Agrio del Medio".* Bonaparte's account of the *Rayososaurus* site; Carballido and colleagues say the published references "lack precise data" and replace it with their 2.4 km bearing (carballido2010a, pp. 495–496).

**Open.** The position of the *Lavocatisaurus* site. Salgado and colleagues (2012), who first placed these bones in the Rayoso Formation (canudo2018a, p. 680), have not been read.
