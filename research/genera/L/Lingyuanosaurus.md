# *Lingyuanosaurus*

## Is *Lingyuanosaurus* a therizinosauroid?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Every analysis that includes it recovers it within Therizinosauroidea, more derived than *Beipiaosaurus*, though with low support. Governs `parent: Therizinosauroidea` on [*Lingyuanosaurus sihedangensis*](../../../genera/L/Lingyuanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yao2019a, Results: "The strict consensus of the most parsimonious trees placed Lingyuanosaurus within Terizinosauroidea, in a position between early-branching therizinosaurs such as Falcarius, Jianchangosaurus and Beipiaosaurus and late-branching the[rizinosaurs]".
- kobayashi2022a, Fig. 6, p. 7: the same order, with *Lingyuanosaurus* above *Beipiaosaurus*.
- liao2026a, Fig. 12, p. 29: *Lingyuanosaurus* within the node labelled Therizinosauroidea, above *Beipiaosaurus*.

**Ruled out.**
- *Therizinosauria as the parent.* No analysis places it outside Therizinosauroidea. Jones (2026) calls it a basal therizinosaur but places it after *Beipiaosaurus*, as the analyses do.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Lingyuanosaurus* holotype from the Yixian or the Jiufotang Formation?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved, so the record stops at the Jehol Group. Yao and colleagues (2019) report that the Sihedang beds have been assigned to both formations and decline to choose. Governs `location.group: Jehol` on [*Lingyuanosaurus sihedangensis*](../../../genera/L/Lingyuanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yao2019a, p. 2, Horizon and Locality: "Jehol Group, Lower Cretaceous. Sihedang Locality, Lingyuan City, Liaoning Province, China. The fossil-bearing strata at Sihedang have been assigned to the Yixian Formation in some studies but to the Jiufotang Formation in others. Resolving this stratigraphic issue is beyond the scope of the present paper."
- xu2017a, p. 130, Locality and horizon (for *Zhongjianosaurus*, from the same locality): "Sihedang, Lingyuan County, Liaoning Province, China. Possibly Yixian Formation, Aptian, Cretaceous (Swisher et al., 2001)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Yixian Formation, Barremian, from Jones (2026).* The Princeton Encyclopedia's specimen table gives "Yixian Fm., China *Early Cretaceous, Barremian*" (jones2026c, Therizinosauria table), while the same book's entry says the age "is uncertain and could lie anywhere in the range of 133–120 million years" (jones2026c, *Lingyuanosaurus sihedangensis* entry). A compilation's table does not settle what the describing paper leaves open.

**Open.** Which formation. Yao and colleagues cite two studies for each assignment (their references 13–16), none of which has been read here. Xu and Qin's "Possibly Yixian" is a hedge, not an assignment.
