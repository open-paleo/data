# *Lurdusaurus*

## What stage and horizon does *Lurdusaurus* take?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Aptian, from level GAD 5 of the Elrhaz Formation, as the describing paper gives the type horizon. [*Ouranosaurus*](../../../genera/O/Ouranosaurus.yml), from the same level 100 m away, reads the same way. Governs `stage: [Aptian]` and `location.bed: GAD 5` on [*Lurdusaurus arenatus*](../../../genera/L/Lurdusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- taquet1999a, Type locality: "“Camp des deux arbres,” 7 km southeast of Elrhaz, 16°42' latitude 9°20' longitude. Gisement de Gadoufaoua, level GAD 5 (upper part of the Elrhaz Formation), Tégama Series, Aptian. 100 m north of the type locality of *Ouranosaurus nigeriensis*."
- taquet1999a, Abstract: "*Lurdusaurus arenatus*, nov.g., nov.sp., from the Aptian of Niger".

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian.* No source read gives it for this horizon.
- *`part: upper` in place of the bed.* The paper names the level, and `part` attaches to the finest named unit.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `taquet1999a`: Taquet, P.; Russell, D. A. (1999). A massively-constructed iguanodont from Gadoufaoua, lower Cretaceous of Niger. *Annales de Paléontologie* 85(1): 85-96. doi:10.1016/s0753-3969(99)80009-3
