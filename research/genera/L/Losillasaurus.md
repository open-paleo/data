# *Losillasaurus*

## Which formation is the *Losillasaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Villar del Arzobispo Formation. Casanovas and colleagues (2001) gave only a lithology; later work places the Losilla sites in the same unit as *Turiasaurus*, and Campos-Soto and colleagues (2019) name it. Governs `location.formation: Villar del Arzobispo` on [*Losillasaurus giganteus*](../../../genera/L/Losillasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- casanovas2001a, p. 110: "Horizon: Levels of micaceous arenite" (translated).
- royo-torres2020a, Introduction: *Losillasaurus* "was found at the La Cañada site (Losilla de Aras, Valencia) and originates from the same geological unit as *Turiasaurus*".
- royo-torres2020a, Introduction: "a complete anterior caudal vertebra (SHN (JJS) 180) from Baleal (Peniche, Portugal), Praia de Amoreira-Porto Novo Formation".
- campos-soto2019a, Results: *Turiasaurus riodevensis* and *Losillasaurus giganteus* "were discovered … in the Riodeva and Losilla-Alpuente areas, respectively …, in the deposits of the Villar del Arzobispo Fm".
- upchurch2004a, Table 13.1: *L. giganteus*, "Unnamed unit (Valencia), Spain".
- jones2026b, p. 376: "Villar del Arzobispo Fm., Spain Praia de Amoreira-Porto Novo Fm., Portugal".

**Ruled out.**
- *Unnamed unit.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) wrote before any paper assigned the site to a formation, and the describing paper gives only "Levels of micaceous arenite".
- *Praia de Amoreira–Porto Novo.* Jones (2026) adds the Portuguese unit of SHN 180, a caudal vertebra from Baleal that Royo-Torres and colleagues (2020) identify, and lists it in the same row. The type locality is La Cañada in Valencia.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What age is the *Losillasaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian to Tithonian, the age Campos-Soto and colleagues (2019) give the Villar del Arzobispo deposits. Governs `period.stage: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` on [*Losillasaurus giganteus*](../../../genera/L/Losillasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- campos-soto2019a, p. 472: the Villar del Arzobispo Formation "has been considered as Tithonian–Berriasian …, although a Kimmeridgian age has been demonstrated recently at least locally".
- campos-soto2019a, p. 471, abstract: the deposits "should be correlated with other Kimmeridgian–Tithonian dinosaur-bearing deposits of Iberia".
- upchurch2004a, Table 13.1: "Late Jurassic—Early Cretaceous".
- jones2026b, p. 376: "*Late Jurassic, Kimmeridgian/Tithonian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Into the Early Cretaceous.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) follow the describing paper's Jurassic–Cretaceous boundary age, which predates the revision; Jones (2026) agrees with the Kimmeridgian–Tithonian.

**Open.** The paper does not date the La Cañada site itself, so the age is the formation's in this basin.
