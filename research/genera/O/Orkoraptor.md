# *Orkoraptor*

## Is the *Orkoraptor* holotype from the Cerro Fortaleza or the Mata Amarilla Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Cerro Fortaleza Formation. The describing paper's "Pari Aike Formation" at Los Hornos Hill is the unit that later work calls the Cerro Fortaleza, as for [*Puertasaurus*](../P/Puertasaurus.md) from the same site. Governs `location.formation: Cerro Fortaleza` on [*Orkoraptor burkei*](../../../genera/O/Orkoraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas2008b, p. 470: "Locality and horizon. Los Hornos Hill, southern coast of Viedma Lake … Pari Aike Formation, Upper Cretaceous".
- egerton2013a, p. 268: "A distinct dinosaurian fauna has been described from the "Pari Aike Formation," which we interpret as the Cerro Fortaleza Formation", listing *Orkoraptor burkei* (Novas and colleagues 2008).
- novas2019a, p. 274: "The Cerro Fortaleza beds, at Cerro de Los Hornos fossil site, shed different dinosaur taxa (i.e., *Talenkauen, Orkoraptor, Puertasaurus, Dreadnoughtus*".
- aranciagarolando2022a, p. 2, Introduction: "*Orkoraptor burkei* from the Cerro Fortaleza Formation (Campanian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Mata Amarilla Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Mata Amarilla Fm., Argentina, Late Cretaceous, Cenomanian" (jones2026c, Carcharodontosauria systematics table). The reading goes back to Novas and colleagues (2013), "the beds that yielded Orkoraptor (i.e., Mata Amarilla Formation, former Pari Aike Formation)" (novas2013a, p. 194). Novas and colleagues (2019) since place *Orkoraptor* in the Cerro Fortaleza beds at Cerro de Los Hornos, and Egerton and colleagues (2013) and Aranciaga Rolando and colleagues (2022) do the same.

**Open.** Nothing.
