# *Opisthocoelicaudia*

## What is the *Opisthocoelicaudia skarzynskii* holotype's number?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** MPC-D 100/404, formerly ZPAL MgD-I/48. Currie and colleagues (2018) record that the skeleton described under the Warsaw number was transferred to Ulaanbaatar and recatalogued; later papers that print the ZPAL number keep the number of the description. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MPC-D 100/404]` and its `former_ids` entry on [*Opisthocoelicaudia skarzynskii*](../../../genera/O/Opisthocoelicaudia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- borsuk-bialynicka1977a: the holotype as ZPAL MgD-I/48.
- currie2018a, p. 6: "The specimen was initially housed at the Institute of Paleobiology (Warsaw), where it was catalogued as ZPAL MgD-I/48. However, it was subsequently transferred to the Institute of Paleontology and Geology (Ulaan Baatar) where it has the catalogue number of MPC-D100/404."
- currie2018a, Table 1: "MPC-D100/404 (originally Zpal MgD-I/48) … Holotype skeleton".
- jones2026b: "MPC-D100/404 [aka ZPAL MgD-I/48]—skeleton".

**Ruled out.**
- *ZPAL MgD-I/48 as the current number.* Averianov and Efimov (2018), Bellardini and colleagues (2022) and Poropat and colleagues (2023) print it after the transfer; Averianov and Efimov's table cites the 1977 description for it.

**Open.** Nothing.
