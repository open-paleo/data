# *Owenodon*

## Where does *Owenodon* sit?

*2026-10-24*

**Conclusion.** Dryomorpha incertae sedis. Herne and colleagues' (2019) implied-weighting analysis recovers it as the sister of *Camptosaurus* + *Iguanodon*, outside Ankylopollexia as Madzia and colleagues (2021) define it; Galton (2009), Norman (2012) and Madzia, Jagt and Mulder (2019) place it inside Ankylopollexia. Dryomorpha is the nearest clade all of them share. The topology is read from Herne and colleagues' tree figure, which their text does not state. Governs `parent: Dryomorpha` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Owenodon hoggii*](../../../genera/O/Owenodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- herne2019a, Fig. 25.2, p. 574 (figure reading): in the implied-weighting strict consensus, Dryomorpha comprises the dryosaurids and *Owenodon* + (*Camptosaurus* + *Iguanodon*).
- madzia2019a, Supplementary Information II (figure reading): both strict consensus trees leave *Owenodon hoggii* in the polytomy containing *Camptosaurus dispar* and *Iguanodon bernissartensis*.
- fonseca2024a: these taxa "fall near the root of, or sometimes outside of, Ankylopollexia, along with Owenodon hoggii", citing Herne and colleagues among others.

**Ruled out.**
- *Ankylopollexia incertae sedis.* Not shared by Herne and colleagues' result. That analysis is incidental to *Owenodon* and samples only two ankylopollexians, but it is a published topology, and the nearest-agreed-parent rule counts it.

**Open.** An analysis aimed at *Owenodon*.

## Is the *Owenodon* holotype NHMUK PV R 2998 or "NHM R2998"?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** NHMUK PV R 2998, the dentary Norman (2012) gives as the holotype. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NHMUK PV R 2998]` on [*Owenodon hoggii*](../../../genera/O/Owenodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- norman2012a, p. 181: "*Holotype.* NHMUK R2998, a nearly complete right dentary (Fig. 15.6) containing 15 alveoli".
- galton2009a, p. 241: "*Iguanodon hoggii* Owen, 1874 is based on an almost complete right dentary (NHM R2998)".

**Ruled out.**
- *"NHM R2998".* Jones (2026) prints "NHM R2998" (jones2026a, *Owenodon hoggii* table row), the abbreviation Galton (2009) uses. It is the same number with an informal code for the museum; the registry writes the London collection as NHMUK PV (see [NHMUK catalogue prefixes](../../topics/nhmuk-catalogue-prefixes.md)).

**Open.** Nothing.
