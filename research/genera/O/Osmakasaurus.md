# *Osmakasaurus*

## Does *Osmakasaurus* share the *Hoplitosaurus* stage?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The *Osmakasaurus* holotype was found with the *Hoplitosaurus* holotype, in the same two pieces of rock, so the two take the same stage, Berriasian–Valanginian, the age of the [Chilson](../../../stratigraphy/c/chilson.yml) member of the Lakota Formation. Governs `stage: [Berriasian, Valanginian]` on [*Osmakasaurus depressus*](../../../genera/O/Osmakasaurus.yml), matching [*Hoplitosaurus marshi*](../../../genera/H/Hoplitosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1909a, pp. 292–293: USNM 4753 "was associated with the fragmentary skeleton, No. 4752, U.S.N.M., described by Dr. F. A. Lucas as *Stegosaurus marshii* and later referred by him to the new genus *Hoplitosaurus*. Most of the elements preserved were inclosed in two large pieces of rock."

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian–Aptian*, the record's earlier value. It comes from McDonald and colleagues (2011), whose source for it does not mention the Lakota; see [Lakota](../../stratigraphy/l/lakota.md).
- *Barremian.* Norman (2004) gives "?C. depressus" from the "Lakota Formation (South Dakota)" as "Barremian" (norman2004a, Table 19.1). This is a formation-wide age, and it predates the dating of the Lakota's members. Jones (2026) gives the species as "Lakota Fm., South Dakota USA, Early Cretaceous, Berriasian/Valanginian" (jones2026a, Styracosterna systematics table), which agrees with this record.

**Open.** Nothing.
