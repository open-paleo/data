# *Orodromeus*

## Is the *Orodromeus* holotype from MOR locality TM-006?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** Not recorded. Horner and Weishampel (1988) give the holotype's site as the Egg Mountain site with no locality number. TM-006 is printed only by Varricchio and colleagues (2025), for the Egg Mountain site of a troodontid juvenile, MOR 430. Governs `location.locality: Egg Mountain site, Willow Creek Anticline` on [*Orodromeus makelai*](../../../genera/O/Orodromeus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- horner1988a, p. 257: "Holotype: MOR294, virtually complete skull"; "Locality: Egg Mountain site, Willow Creek Anticline, Teton County, Montana, USA."
- varricchio2025a, p. 222: "MOR 246 was collected at Egg Island (MOR locality TM-024) and MOR 430 from the nearly stratigraphically equivalent Egg Mountain site (TM-006), approximately 1.4 km away." The paper does not mention *Orodromeus* or MOR 294.

**Ruled out.** Nothing. TM-006 is under Open, because no source contradicts it for MOR 294.

**Open.** Whether TM-006 is the number of the whole Egg Mountain site, and so of MOR 294's site too. Both papers use the name "Egg Mountain site", which makes it likely, but no paper prints the number for this holotype. A paper that ties MOR 294 to a locality number, or a Museum of the Rockies locality list, would settle it.
