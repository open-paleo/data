# *Olorotitan*

## Is the Kundur position in Godefroit and colleagues (2011) the site's or the village's?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** The site's. Godefroit and colleagues head each dinosaur locality with a position, and the two headings other papers can be checked against, Blagoveschensk and Wulaga, give the positions printed for the dinosaur sites, not for the towns. The Kundur heading converts to 49.082639, 130.859472. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Olorotitan arharensis*](../../../genera/O/Olorotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- godefroit2011a, p. 171: "Kundur ( N49°04'57. 5″ / E130°51'34. 1″)", heading the section on the locality that "has yielded the nearly complete skeleton of a new lambeosaurine hadrosaurid, Olorotitan arharensis"; p. 173: "The Kundur site, located 10 km southeast of the village Kundur".
- godefroit2011a, p. 166: "Blagoveschensk ( N50° 16' 11. 6″ / E127° 28' 33. 1″)", the pair bolotsky2004a, p. 352, prints for the *Kerberosaurus* holotype's site, "Upper part of Nagornaia street (50° 16′ 11.6″ N; 127° 28′ 33.1″ E)".
- godefroit2011a, p. 177: "Wulaga ( N48°23'40. 9″ / E130°08'44. 6″)"; xing2022a, p. e2085111-6, prints "48° 23′40.73′′N, 130°08′44.12′′E" for the "outcrop near the grain warehouse in the west of Wulaga Town".

**Ruled out.**
- *A landmark for Kundur village.* The earlier deletion held that Godefroit and colleagues give the pair "as a landmark for the village of Kundur, 10 km from the holotype". The paper does not say so: the pair heads the locality section, and the checkable headings mark sites.
- *The deleted pair as unsourced.* It lies about 16 m from the printed position, a rounded copy of it.

**Open.** No source says in words what the Kundur heading marks; the reading rests on the form of the other headings.
