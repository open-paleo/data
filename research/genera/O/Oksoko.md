# *Oksoko*

## Does *Oksoko* take a region?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype was poached and its site within the Nemegt Formation is unknown; Bügiin Tsav and Guriliin Tsav are the sites of the lawfully collected referred specimens, and the Nemegt crops out in more than one aimag. Governs the empty `location.region` on [*Oksoko avarsan*](../../../genera/O/Oksoko.yml).

**Evidence.**
- funston2020a, Localities and Horizon: "Bugiin Tsav and Guriliin Tsav, Nemegt Basin. Nemegt Formation (lower Maastrichtian)", given for the holotype and referred specimens together.
- funston2020a, Discussion: "Because the specimens were poached and their exact provenance within the Nemegt Formation is unknown, the sedimentology of the site cannot be assessed."
- funston2020a, Ethics: the material "includes both legitimately collected (MPC-D 100/33, MPC-D 102/12) and unlawfully collected specimens (MPC-D 102/11, MPC-D 102/110) that were confiscated before leaving the country by Mongolian authorities in December 2006."

**Ruled out.**
- *Bayankhongor (MN-069), from the named sites.* Those are the referred specimens' sites, not the holotype's, and the [Bügiin Tsav](../../topics/bügiin-tsav.md) note places that site in Ömnögovi on current boundaries.

**Open.** Nothing.
