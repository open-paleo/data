# *Orthogoniosaurus*

## Where does *Orthogoniosaurus* sit, and is its status in dispute?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** Theropoda incertae sedis, and no dispute: the block is removed. The single tooth has been undiagnostic since Walker (1964), and the papers that argue its affinity agree only on Theropoda. Its earlier Ceratosauria placement rested on two reference-work listings that do not discuss it. Governs `parent: Theropoda` and the absence of a dispute on [*Orthogoniosaurus*](../../../genera/O/Orthogoniosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- walker1964a: "*Orthogoniosaurus* Das-Gupta 1931, from the same formation, being founded upon an isolated tooth, is here considered to be indeterminable."
- molnar1990b: "The tooth may derive from one of the other nominate Jabalpur theropods, but it cannot be shown to be carnosaurian."
- olshevsky1995a: the material "cannot be identified below the level of \"large theropod.\""
- chure2000a: "It is here considered a *nomen nudum* and Theropoda *incertae sedis* and is excluded from the Allosauridae", the term nomen nudum being misapplied to a described name.

**Ruled out.**
- *Ceratosauria incertae sedis.* The earlier parent; it rests on tykoski2004a's table of nomina dubia and weishampel2004a's fauna list, neither of which discusses the genus.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which Bara Simla bed is the *Orthogoniosaurus* tooth from?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The Sauropod bed, on Huene and Matley's (1933) reading of the matrix. Das-Gupta (1930) names no bed; he gives only "green marly clay" of the Lameta beds in the Jubbulpore Cantonment, and Huene and Matley match that to their Sauropod bed, a clay in the lowest Mottled Nodular beds, rather than to the Carnosaur bed in the greensand under the Main Limestone. Governs `location.bed: Sauropod Bed` on [*Orthogoniosaurus matleyi*](../../../genera/O/Orthogoniosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- das-gupta1930a, p. 367: "The tooth that is described here was obtained from the green marly clay of the Lameta beds developed in the Jubbulpore Cantonment."
- huene1933a, p. 59, footnote: "It apparently came from the horizon of the Sauropod bed as it is stated to have been found in 'green marly clay'."
- huene1933a, p. 4: the Sauropod bed "is a deposit of red and green, marly and somewhat sandy clay"; p. 3: the Carnosaur bed lies "At the top of the greensand zone, immediately under the Main Limestone".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Carnosaur bed.* It lies in the greensand zone under the Main Limestone (huene1933a, p. 3), not in a clay, and it is the bed of [*Ornithomimoides mobilis*](../../../genera/O/Ornithomimoides.yml), about 50 yards from the Sauropod bed (p. 4).

**Open.** The assignment is a lithological match made after the fact, which Huene and Matley mark with "apparently"; Das-Gupta names neither a bed nor Bara Simla, and the footnote assigns a horizon, not a site. Matley's (1921) account of the section, which Das-Gupta cites, has not been read.
