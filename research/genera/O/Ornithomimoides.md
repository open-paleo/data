# *Ornithomimoides*

## Does Wilson's (2012) synonymy with *Coeluroides* change the record?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** No. Wilson (2012) proposes that *Coeluroides* and *Ornithomimoides* share autapomorphies and be grouped as *Coeluroides*, but only in a conference abstract, with no data, and later works report the proposal without adopting it. Under the policy of adopting taxonomic changes only on broad consensus, the record keeps the genus as a nomen dubium at Abelisauroidea. This answers the Open line of the entry below. Governs `status: nomen dubium` and the absence of a synonymy on [*Ornithomimoides*](../../../genera/O/Ornithomimoides.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wilson2012a: "*Compsosuchus*, *Coeluroides*, and *Ornithomimoides* pertain to individuals slightly larger than *Laevisuchus*/*Jubbulpuria*. The latter two are morphologically distinct from *Laevisuchus*/*Jubbulpuria* and share autapomorphies with one another suggesting that they can be grouped together as *Coeluroides*, the first-named of the two genera."
- jones2026c: "Another study proposed synonymy with *Coeluroides* (Wilson, 2012)."
- molina-pérez2019a marks both *Ornithomimoides* species "*Coeluroides largus?* Wilson 2012".

**Ruled out.**
- *A junior synonym of Coeluroides.* Proposed in an abstract only and not taken up.

**Open.** A full publication of Wilson's revision.

## Is the status or placement of *Ornithomimoides* in dispute?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute block is removed and the parent stays Abelisauroidea. Novas and colleagues (2004) treat the genus as a nomen dubium, an indeterminate abelisauroid; Carrano and Sampson (2008) refer its species to Abelisauridae and Noasauridae, both within Abelisauroidea, without defending the genus. No paper read argues it is valid. Governs `parent: Abelisauroidea` and the absence of a dispute on [*Ornithomimoides*](../../../genera/O/Ornithomimoides.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas2004b: the vertebrae "are not dorsals but caudal vertebrae"; "we follow previous authors (Norman, 1990) in considering *Ornithomimoides* as a *nomen dubium*, the set of vertebrae representing proximal caudals of an Abelisauroidea gen. et sp. indet."
- carrano2008a: "*Ornithomimoides mobilis* is referred to Abelisauridae"; "We refer *O.? barasimlensis* to Noasauridae."

**Ruled out.**
- *A live dispute.* The placement is agreed at Abelisauroidea, and validity is not argued.

**Open.** A proposed synonymy with *Coeluroides*, which Jones (2026) reports (jones2026c), comes from a 2012 abstract that has not been read.
