# *Orthomerus*

## Where does *Orthomerus* sit?

*2026-10-24*

**Conclusion.** Hadrosauroidea incertae sedis. Madzia, Jagt and Mulder (2019), the only analysis that includes it, assign it to Hadrosauroidea and find a finer placement impossible; their majority-rule trees put it just outside Hadrosauridae and a subset of second-run trees inside. The Hadrosauridae placement comes from listings without analysis. Governs `parent: Hadrosauroidea` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Orthomerus dolloi*](../../../genera/O/Orthomerus.yml), and the removal of its dispute block.

**Evidence.**
- madzia2019a, p. 13: "on a subset of trees O. dolloi is nested within the Hadrosauridae. Still, in the vast majority of trees it is placed outside the clade"; p. 17: "more precise placement within the clade seems impossible. As such, its common referral to Hadrosauridae is questionable."

**Ruled out.**
- *Hadrosauridae.* Rests on Horner and colleagues' (2004) listing of the name among hadrosaurid nomina dubia, with no analysis.

**Open.** Nothing.
