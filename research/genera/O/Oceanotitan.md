# *Oceanotitan*

## Is *Oceanotitan* a somphospondylan?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Not established; it sits at Macronaria incertae sedis. Its describers recovered it as the earliest somphospondylan on one matrix only, and their later analysis places it outside Titanosauriformes. Governs `parent: Macronaria`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Oceanotitan dantasi*](../../../genera/O/Oceanotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mocho2019a, Fig. 17, p. 16: "the earliest branching member of Somphospondyli" on the Mannion matrix; a basal titanosauriform polytomy on the Carballido matrix.
- mocho2023a, Fig. 24, p. 37: under implied weights, the sister of *Aragosaurus*, outside Titanosauriformes.
- mannion2026a: "now regarded as a non-titanosauriform macronarian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Somphospondyli as the parent.* Only one of the two describers' matrices recovers it there, and their later analysis does not.

**Open.** Nothing.
