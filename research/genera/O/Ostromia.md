# *Ostromia*

## Where is the *Ostromia* holotype, and what are its numbers?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** Teylers Museum, Haarlem, registry entry `TM-NL`, as TM 6928 and TM 6929, the main slab and counterslab. The record had been filed under the Ditsong museum in Pretoria, whose old fossil prefix is also TM. Both museums now hold TM-prefixed specimens in the data, so the Teylers entry takes the country suffix and the Ditsong museum keeps TM as an alias. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` and `institution: TM-NL` on [*Ostromia crassipes*](../../../genera/O/Ostromia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- foth2017a: "Holotype. Teylers Museum TM 6928, 6929, part and counterpart of a fragmentary skeleton"; Fig. 1: "Counterslab, Teylers Museum TM 6929 (left) and main slab, TM 6928 (right)".
- sabaj2020a: "TM: Teylers Museum, Haarlem … *Current as*: TM. *Also as*: TMH, TSMHN", and DNMNH "*Also as*: TM (fossils)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Pretoria.* Nothing ties the Haarlem specimen to South Africa; the filing followed the shared prefix.

**Open.** Nothing.
