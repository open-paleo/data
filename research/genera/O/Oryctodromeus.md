# *Oryctodromeus*

## Is the Oryctodromeus type horizon Cenomanian or Albian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Cenomanian. No source read for this note puts the type horizon in the Albian. Governs `stage: [Cenomanian]` and `location.part: upper` of the [Blackleaf Formation](../../../stratigraphy/b/blackleaf.yml) on *Oryctodromeus cubicularis*.

**Evidence.**
- varricchio2007a, p. 1361: the holotype is from the "upper portion of the mid-Cretaceous Blackleaf Formation"; p. 1362: "Palynomorphs and radiometric dates indicate a Cenomanian age for this portion of the formation", relaying Dyman and Nichols (1988) and Zartman and colleagues (1995).

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian for the type horizon.* The Albian attaches to other material. Ullmann and colleagues (2011) note "the underlying Flood Member is the latest Albian" and that "Both the Wayan and BL1 assemblages include Oryctodromeus" (ullmann2011a, pp. 311, 324); BL1 lies in the uppermost Flood Member, but its material is identified only as "hypsilophodontid grade" (p. 319).

**Open.** Krumenacker and colleagues' papers with "Albian-Cenomanian" in their titles (2017, 2019) have not been read.

## Is the *Oryctodromeus* holotype from the Vaughn Member?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** No member is recorded. Varricchio and colleagues (2007) place the holotype in the upper, volcaniclastic lithofacies of the Blackleaf Formation near the Lima Peaks and say only that this lithofacies "may correlate" with the Vaughn Member. Governs the empty `location.member` on [*Oryctodromeus cubicularis*](../../../genera/O/Oryctodromeus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- varricchio2007a, p. 1361: the specimens "come from the upper portion of the mid-Cretaceous Blackleaf Formation near the Lima Peaks, Beaverhead Co., Montana", in what Dyman and Nichols (1988) "designate … the 'volcaniclastic lithofacies'".
- varricchio2007a, p. 1362: "The upper, volcaniclastic lithofacies may correlate with the Vaughn Member described from more northern outcrops near Great Falls, Montana (Cobban et al. 1976)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing. The Vaughn Member is a hedged correlation, not a reading against the record.

**Open.** Whether the Lima Peaks beds are the Vaughn Member. Varricchio and colleagues cite Zartman and colleagues (1995) for the dates on this part of the formation, and that paper's title, as their reference list gives it, places "the Vaughn Member of the mid-Cretaceous Blackleaf Formation" in southwestern Montana. Zartman and colleagues (1995) has not been read; it, or a later description of the type locality's section, would settle whether the member applies.
