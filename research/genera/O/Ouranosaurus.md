# *Ouranosaurus*

## Is "Camp des deux arbres" the *Ouranosaurus* type locality, or the *Lurdusaurus* one?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** Both. Taquet (1976) gives the camp and "16°42' lat. 9°20' long." as the type locality of GDF 300, and Taquet and Russell (1999) give the same camp and the same position for the *Lurdusaurus* holotype, 100 m to the north. At arcminute precision the two records share one point. Governs `location.locality: Camp des deux arbres, Gadoufaoua`, `location.coordinates` and `location.bed: GAD 5` on [*Ouranosaurus nigeriensis*](../../../genera/O/Ouranosaurus.yml), and `location.locality` and `location.coordinates` on [*Lurdusaurus arenatus*](../../../genera/L/Lurdusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- taquet1976a, Chapter III, *Ouranosaurus nigeriensis* sp. nov.: "Locality of type: Camp of the two trees, 7 km S.E. of Elrhaz, 16°42' lat. 9°20' long. Deposit of Gadoufaoua, GAD 5 formation (upper part of the formation of Elrhaz)" (translated).
- taquet1976a, Chapter III, A. Introduction: the skeleton lay "7 km to the south-east of the wells of Elrhaz, 400 m from the camp of the Two Trees, geographic coordinates = 16° 42' lat., 9° 20' long." (translated).
- taquet1999a, p. 86: the *Lurdusaurus* type locality is "'Camp des deux arbres,' 7 km southeast of Elrhaz, 16°42' latitude 9°20' longitude … 100 m north of the type locality of *Ouranosaurus nigeriensis*".

**Ruled out.**
- *The locality string copied from Lurdusaurus.* Taquet and Russell's "100 m north of the type locality of *Ouranosaurus nigeriensis*" reads as if the camp were the *Lurdusaurus* site alone, but Taquet's own type-locality line gives the camp for GDF 300.
- *16°26′, 9°8′.* Taquet prints this for the paratype GDF 381, "4 km south of the formation of the Innocents, east border of airfield" (taquet1976a, Locality of the paratype, translated), not for the holotype.

**Open.** Nothing.
