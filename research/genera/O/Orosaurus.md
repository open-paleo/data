# *Orosaurus*

## Does the *Orosaurus* locality rest on van Heerden's "probably found with"?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The locality comes from Alfred Brown's consignment list as Huxley (1867) quotes it, which names No. 1, the bone Huxley called *Orosaurus*; Lydekker (1889) identifies that bone as NHMUK R 1626. Van Heerden's (1979) hedge restates the association and adds the horizon. Governs `location.locality: Stormberg range of mountains, about thirty miles from the town of Aliwal North` on [*Orosaurus capensis*](../../../genera/O/Orosaurus.yml). `location.part: lower` of the Elliot rests on van Heerden alone.

**Evidence.**
- huxley1867b, p. 4: the femora "(and the accompanying fossils numbered 1, 3, 4, 6, 9*a*, 9*b*, 20*a*, 20*b*, 21, and 49 *a, b, c, d, e*) were obtained 'in the Stormberg range of mountains in the division of Aliwal North, and about thirty miles from the town of Aliwal North'", quoting Brown's letter.
- huxley1867b, p. 4: "No. 1 is the broken articular end of a long bone of considerable size"; p. 5: "I shall apply the title of *Orosaurus* to the fossil reptile indicated by the femur".
- lydekker1889c, p. 353: Huxley "applied the name *Orosaurus* to a large bone which he regarded as the distal extremity of a femur", and the bone "has been recently presented by its describer to the British Museum (No. R. 1626)".
- vanheerden1979a, p. 64: "Holotype: BM(NH) R 1626, the proximal end of a tibia, severely distorted. Locality: Probably found with the holotype of *E. browni* (BM(NH) R 1625). Horizon: Base of the Elliot Formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *An unknown locality.* Van Heerden's "Probably" reads as doubt about where R 1626 came from, but Huxley's list puts No. 1 in the same Stormberg consignment as the *Euskelosaurus* femora.

**Open.** The horizon. Brown's letter gives only "the strata containing the fossils about 1200 feet from the base" of the mountains (huxley1867b, p. 4); "Base of the Elliot Formation" is van Heerden's, the horizon he gives the *E. browni* holotype R 1625 (vanheerden1979a, p. 25); that his R 1626 line follows from the association is our inference. A source on the Stormberg beds that places Brown's site would settle it.
