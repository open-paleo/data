# *Tazoudasaurus*

## What is the Tazoudasaurus holotype's number and repository?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** The describing paper gives "To 2000–1" at the "Musée des Sciences de la Terre", Rabat; the redescription numbers the elements CPSGM To1-nnn, in the Service géologique du Maroc collections (SGM in Sabaj). "To" is the Toundoute locality. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` and `institution: SGM` on *Tazoudasaurus naimi*.

**Evidence.**
- allain2004a, p. 203: "Holotype. To 2000–1, 'Musée des Sciences de la Terre', Rabat".
- allain2008a, pp. 350–351: "CPSGM collections paléontologiques du Service géologique du Maroc"; "left mandible (CPSGM To1-275)"; Fig. 3: "localities (To1; To1'; Pt)".
- sabaj2020a, p. 641: "SGM … Also as: CPSGM".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Tazouda museum as the repository.* A museum building at Tazouda exists (benvenuti2024a, p. 4) but no describing paper places the type there.

**Open.** Which form to record: the 2004 number with the SGM code ("SGM To 2000-1", which neither paper prints) or the 2008 element numbers.

## Which Toundoute locality is the *Tazoudasaurus* holotype from?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** Fossil locality B of Allain and colleagues (2004), the upper bone bed, at the Douar of Tazouda. Allain and Aquesbi (2008) gloss locality A as the lower bone bed (To2) and B as the upper (To1/Pt), and every holotype element they list is numbered CPSGM To1. Governs `location.locality: Douar of Tazouda, locality B` on [*Tazoudasaurus naimi*](../../../genera/T/Tazoudasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- allain2008a, p. 351, Horizon and locality: "Douar of Tazouda, near the village of Toundoute in the Province of Ouarzazate, High Atlas of Morocco. Lower and upper bone-beds ('Fossil locality A (or To2) and B (or To1/Pt)' of Allain et al. 2004 …)"; Holotype: "left mandible (CPSGM To1-275) … left metatarsal II (CPSGM To1-265)".
- allain2004a, p. 203: "Douar of Tazouda near Toundoute village in the Province of Ouarzazate, High Atlas of Morocco".
- montenat2005a, p. 261: "The dinosaur deposits of the douar of Tabia, about 3 km NE of Toundoute (province of Ouarzazate)" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Locality A.* The lower bone bed, To2. No holotype element carries a To2 number; the To2 material Allain and Aquesbi describe, such as the manus MHNM To2-112, belongs to other individuals.

**Open.** Whether the douar of Tabia, where Montenat and colleagues (2005) put the dinosaur deposits, is the Douar of Tazouda under another name. Montenat and colleagues, whose authors include Allain, describe the same upper bone bed but never name Tazouda, and Benvenuti and colleagues (2024) name a Tabia River valley and a Tazouda museum as different places in the area (benvenuti2024a, p. 4, Fig. 3). A map that names both would settle it.

## Is "To" in the *Tazoudasaurus* holotype number an institution code?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** No. "To" is the Toundoute locality's prefix, used in the numbers of all the bone-bed material, and the holotype is To 2000-1 in the Rabat collections of the Service géologique du Maroc (SGM). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [SGM To 2000-1]` and `institution: SGM` on [*Tazoudasaurus naimi*](../../../genera/T/Tazoudasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- allain2004a, p. 203: "Holotype. To 2000–1, 'Musée des Sciences de la Terre', Rabat, Morocco"; "Referred specimen. To 2000–2".
- allain2008a, pp. 350–351: "CPSGM collections paléontologiques du Service géologique du Maroc"; Fig. 3: "localities (To1; To1'; Pt)".

**Ruled out.**
- *"To" as an institution code.* It names the site: Allain and Aquesbi (2008) number the localities To1 and To2 and the elements CPSGM To1-nnn.
- *CPSGM To2000-1.* Jones (2026) prints "**CPSGM To2000-1**—partial skeleton" (jones2026b, Sauropoda table). It is the same specimen with the code of SGM's palaeontological collections; the record uses the institution's code.
- *To 2000-3.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give "To 2000-3" (molina-pérez2020a, List of sauropods). The describing paper numbers the holotype To 2000-1 and the other specimen To 2000-2, and prints no To 2000-3.

**Open.** The form the record should take is left open in the 2026-10-04 entry above.
