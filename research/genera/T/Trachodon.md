# *Trachodon*

## Is the status of *Trachodon* in dispute?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute block is removed and the parent stays Hadrosauridae. Every treatment read since Lull and Wright (1942) holds the type tooth undiagnostic and the name unusable. The removed block's "wastebasket" is a fair description of the name's history but not a word any source read uses: the name spread to flat-headed hadrosaurs and to a family, Trachodontidae, while its type teeth came from crested forms. Governs `parent: Hadrosauridae` and the absence of a dispute on [*Trachodon*](../../../genera/T/Trachodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brett-surman1989a: "Lull and Wright (1942) correctly considered the name Trachodon unusable; hence it is a nomen dubium."
- coombs1988b: "any hadrosaur taxon based on isolated teeth or dependent upon tooth morphology for its definition is a nomen dubium. Conspicuous among such taxa is Trachodon mirabilis Leidy (1856)".
- currie1990a: "The name was subsequently applied to a flat-headed duckbilled dinosaur, and even became a family (Trachodontidae-Lydekker 1888)"; "the type specimens of *Trachodon* turned out to be from crested forms (Lambeosaurinae)".
- prieto-márquez2016a: "the first hadrosaurid genus described [Leidy, 1856], long considered a nomen dubium".

**Ruled out.**
- *A lambeosaurine placement for the type tooth.* An opinion relayed in lull1942a and currie1990a that no later paper read adopts.

**Open.** Nothing.
