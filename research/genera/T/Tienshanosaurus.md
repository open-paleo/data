# *Tienshanosaurus*

## Where, and at what level, was the *Tienshanosaurus* holotype found?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** At Paikushan, which Young (1937) puts "about 250 li North of Chitai", at no known level within the Shishugou Formation. The quarry has not been relocated, and the record carries no `part`. Governs `location.locality: Paikushan` and the absence of `location.part` on [*Tienshanosaurus chitaiensis*](../../../genera/T/Tienshanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- young1937a, Introduction: "both were collected in December 1930 at Paikushan (白骨山) (or Luanshantze 乱山子) about 250 li North of Chitai (奇台)".
- dong1990a, IV. Sauropods of the Wucaiwan Region: "its precise locality is vague. C.C. Young's original description indicates that it should have been derived from Baigudui, north of Qitai."
- currie1993c, p. 2038: "The general location of the excavation site is known to be in the foothills of the Tian Shan southeast of Qitai (Chitai), but the quarry could not be relocated in 1987."
- moore2018a, p. 7: "Tienshanosaurus chitaiensis Young, 1937 was excavated from an unknown stratigraphic level in roughly the same area".

**Ruled out.**
- *The upper Shishugou Formation.* Moore and colleagues (2023) give *Tienshanosaurus* as "from the upper portions of the formation", citing Moore and colleagues (2018, 2020) (moore2023a, p. 40). The 2018 paper calls the level unknown, and no statement of the level for *Tienshanosaurus* was found in the 2020 paper.
- *The Upper Shaximiao Formation.* Upchurch (1995) writes that the genus "is known from a partial skeleton from the Upper Shaximiao Formation (Upper Jurassic) of the Junggar Basin" (upchurch1995a, (d) The Euhelopodidae). The Shaximiao is a unit of the Sichuan Basin, and no other source places the holotype in it.

**Open.** Which way from Qitai the site lies. Young's account says north; Currie and Zhao (1993) say southeast, in the Tian Shan foothills, and date the excavation to 1931 against Young's December 1930. Yuan's field records would settle it.
