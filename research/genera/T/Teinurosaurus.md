# *Teinurosaurus*

## Is the *Teinurosaurus* holotype from the Mont-Lambert Formation?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** No source that discusses the vertebra places it in a formation, so the record names none. Jones (2026) gives "Mont-Lambert Fm." in a compilation entry that cites no source. The accounts of the vertebra itself give only Boulogne-sur-Mer and an age: the "upper Malm" or "Kimmeridge" for Huene (1932), the "Upper Jurassic" for Galton and Powell (1980). Governs the absence of `location.formation` on [*Teinurosaurus sauvagei*](../../../genera/T/Teinurosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jones2026c, p. 486, *Teinurosaurus sauvagei*: "Location: France", "Occurrence: Mont-Lambert Fm.", "Age: 145 MYA (Late Jurassic)".
- huene1932a, *Caudocoelus sauvagei*: a distal caudal vertebra "from the upper Malm of the Boulogne-sur-Mer area" (translated); Fig. 6: "from the Kimmeridge of Boulogne-sur-mer" (translated).
- galton1980c, p. 436: "Sauvage (1888, 1897-1898) also referred five vertebrae from the Upper Jurassic of Boulogne-sur-Mer to I. prestwichii. A caudal vertebra … is the holotype of the coelurid taxon Caudocoelus sauvagei von Huene (1932)."
- sauvage1874a, plate explanation, Fig. 1: "Portlandian with Ammonites portlandicus from Mont-Lambert" (translated), for a crocodilian jaw.

**Ruled out.** Nothing. No source read contradicts a Mont-Lambert provenance; it is unsupported, and is left open.

**Open.** Whether the vertebra came from the beds Jones calls the Mont-Lambert Formation. In the older literature Mont-Lambert is a place near Boulogne that yielded Portlandian crocodilians (Sauvage, 1874), more than twenty years before the vertebra was described. Sauvage's 1897–1898 description of the vertebra would settle where it was found; it has not been read. The same question bears on the record's Tithonian stage, which matches Jones's 145 Ma and no other source here; Huene (1932) and White (1973) give the Kimmeridgian (white1973a, *Caudocoelus*: "Upper Jurassic, Kimeridgean—Europe, France, Boulogne").
