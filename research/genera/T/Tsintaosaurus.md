# *Tsintaosaurus*

## Is the *Tsintaosaurus* holotype from the Hsikou gully near Chingkankou?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** The record names the village, Chingkankou, which is where Prieto-Márquez and Wagner (2013) place the species. No source read ties the gully name Hsikou to the holotype, IVPP V725. In the passage Chure (2000) quotes from Young (1958), Hsikou is the find site of IVPP K87, a theropod dorsal vertebra. Young's statement that IVPP V829 came from "the same gully" as the type of *Tsintaosaurus* is relayed without naming the gully. Governs `location.locality: Chingkankou` on [*Tsintaosaurus spinorhinus*](../../../genera/T/Tsintaosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- prieto-márquez2013b, p. 2, Occurrence: "Jingangkou Formation, Wangshi Group, near the village of Chingkankou, 20 km south of the city of Laiyang, Shandong Province".
- prieto-márquez2013b, p. 4, Nasal: "According to Young [17], the specimen was found in the same gully where the type and hypodigm of Tsintaosaurus spinorhinus were excavated", of IVPP V829.
- chure2000a, p. 527, IVPP K87: "from a gully (Hsikou) about 1 kilometer N.W. of the village of Chingkankou about 120 kilometres south of Laiyang", quoting Young (1958, pp. 99–100).

**Ruled out.**
- *Hsikou as the holotype's site.* The only source read that names Hsikou gives it for IVPP K87. It may be the *Tsintaosaurus* gully as well, but that has not been checked.

**Open.** Two things that Young (1958) would settle, since that monograph has not been read: which gully yielded IVPP V725, and the distance from Laiyang. Chure's quotation of Young gives about 120 km, and Prieto-Márquez and Wagner give 20 km for the same village.
