# *Tongtianlong*

## Is *Tongtianlong* from the Hekou or the Nanxiong Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The record follows the describing paper's Nanxiong Formation. Jones (2026) agrees ("Nanxiong Fm., China", jones2026c, Oviraptoridae table). Whether the Ganzhou Basin taxa belong in a Jiangxi formation instead is a single question for all of them (see [Nanxiong Formation and the Ganzhou Basin](../../topics/nanxiong-and-guifeng.md)). Governs `location.formation: Nanxiong` on [*Tongtianlong limosus*](../../../genera/T/Tongtianlong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2016a, p. 2, Type locality and horizon: "The building site of the No. 3 high school of Ganxian …; Nanxiong Formation (Maastrichtian, Upper Cretaceous)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hekou Formation as a settled value.* The record held Hekou with no source. Jin and colleagues (2022) place the Ganxian high-school site "where the holotype specimen of oviraptorid *Tongtianlong limosus* … was discovered" in the Hekou only conditionally: the sites' "exact stratigraphic positions and correlations need more investigation. However, if the result of the geological survey is correct", they belong to it (jin2022a, p. 3). A conditional assignment does not replace the describing paper's unit.

**Open.** The Ganzhou formation question in the topic note; a stratigraphic study of the Ganxian site would settle it.
