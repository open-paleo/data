# *Talarurus*

## Is the "Bayanshiree Svita" a different unit from the Bayanshiree Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** No. *Svita* is the Russian rank term used for a formation in Mongolian stratigraphy, and Park and colleagues (2019) place the holotype and their new skulls, from the formation's type section, in the Bayanshiree Formation. Governs `location.formation: Bayanshiree` on [*Talarurus plicatospineus*](../../../genera/T/Talarurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- park2019a, *Talarurus plicatospineus* Maleev, 1952, Locality and horizon: "Holotype and all other materials are from the Upper Cretaceous (Cenomanian–Santonian) Bayanshiree Formation, Bayan Shiree cliffs, eastern Gobi Desert, Mongolia."
- park2019a, 3. Materials and methods: the new specimens "were collected from the sandstone layer at the type section of the Bayanshiree Formation".
- benton2000c, p. xxiii: "Baruungoyot Formation/ Svita", the form in which the glossary pairs each Mongolian formation with its svita.

**Ruled out.**
- *A separate "Bayanshiree Svita".* Vickaryous and colleagues (2004) give "Bayanshiree Svita (Dornogov'), Bayanshiree Svita (Ömnögov'), Mongolia" (vickaryous2004a, Table 17.1). It is the same unit under the Russian rank word; the spelling of the name is settled in [Bayanshiree](../../stratigraphy/b/bayanshiree.md).

**Open.** Nothing.
