# *Telmatosaurus*

## Who coined *Hecatasaurus*?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** Brown (1910), in a footnote proposing it to replace *Limnosaurus* Nopcsa, which Marsh's crocodile name preoccupied. Brown overlooked that Nopcsa (1903) had already replaced it with *Telmatosaurus*, so *Hecatasaurus* is the younger name and a junior objective synonym. Nopcsa (1903) does not mention *Hecatasaurus*. Governs the *Hecatasaurus* synonym on [*Telmatosaurus*](../../../genera/T/Telmatosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown1910a, p. 273: "between *Trachodon* and the European genus Hecatasaurus", with the footnote "To replace *Limnosaurus* Nopcsa, 1900; preoccupied by *Limnosaurus ziphodon* Marsh, 1871 … Type, *Limnosaurus transsylvanicus* Nopcsa". Read from the page image.
- nopcsa1903a, pp. 94–95: "I therefore propose to name the Dinosaur mentioned … Telmatosaurus." No other replacement name appears.
- nopcsa1915a, p. 4: "Owing to his unfamiliarity with the literature, B. Brown in 1910 proposed the highly superfluous name *Hecatasaurus* for *Limnosaurus* Nopcsa" (translated). Read from the page image.

**Ruled out.**
- *Nopcsa (1903) as the author of Hecatasaurus.* The record credited him; the letter names only *Limnosaurus* and *Telmatosaurus*.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is Nopcsa's *Limnosaurus* paper of 1899 or 1900?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** 1900, as the store has it. The paper was presented to the Vienna Academy in June 1899, and Nopcsa cites it as 1899, but nothing read shows it was printed that year; Brown (1910) and Weishampel and colleagues (1993) give 1900. Governs `year: 1900` on [nopcsa1900a](../../../references/n/nopcsa1900a.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nopcsa1900a, p. 555: "Vorgelegt in der Sitzung am 22. Juni 1899" ("Presented at the session of 22 June 1899", translated). The article pages carry no imprint year.
- nopcsa1903a, p. 94: "the name *Limnosaurus*, which I proposed in 1899".
- brown1910a, p. 273: "Nopcsa, Denk. Ak. Wien, Vol. LXVIII, 1900, pp. 555–591".
- weishampel1993a, p. 380: Nopcsa presented the study to the Academy "on 21 June 1899", "but the publication of this work appears not to have been until 1900".

**Ruled out.**
- *1899.* The date of the presentation, not of publication.

**Open.** Weishampel and colleagues (1993) give 21 June; the paper prints 22 June.
