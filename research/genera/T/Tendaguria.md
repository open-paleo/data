# *Tendaguria*

## Is *Tendaguria tanzaniensis* Tithonian or Kimmeridgian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Tithonian. The redescription places the Nambango site, probably, in the Upper Dinosaur Member and dates it Tithonian. The member's possible reach into the Berriasian is discussed in [Tendaguru](../../stratigraphy/t/tendaguru.md). Governs `period.stage: [Tithonian]` on [*Tendaguria tanzaniensis*](../../../genera/T/Tendaguria.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mannion2019a, p. 812: "Nambango (site NB), about 15 km southeast of Tendaguru Hill, Lindi District, southeastern Tanzania (Fig. 1); probably from the Upper Dinosaur Member (Upper Saurian Bed), Tendaguru Formation; Tithonian, Late Jurassic".

**Ruled out.**
- *Kimmeridgian.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) give "Tendaguru Formation (Mtwara), Tanzania | Kimmeridgian" (upchurch2004a, Table 13.1), with no horizon below the formation. The table row gives no source for the stage, and the redescription's horizon is the Upper Dinosaur Member, which no source read for [Tendaguru](../../stratigraphy/t/tendaguru.md) dates Kimmeridgian. Jones (2026) agrees with the record (jones2026b, Eusauropoda table).

**Open.** The member assignment is Mannion and colleagues' "probably"; a section through Nambango would settle it.
