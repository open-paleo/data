# *Tarascosaurus*

## Is the *Tarascosaurus* holotype from the Var or the Bouches-du-Rhône?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** The Var. The holotype's only provenance is "Lambeau du Beausset", the Triassic klippe on the Beausset syncline, and the paper Le Loeuff and Buffetaut (1991) cite for that klippe places it in the Var. Tortosa and colleagues (2014) and Csiki-Sava and colleagues (2015) both place the taxon at Le Beausset, Var. The describing paper's introduction gives the syncline as a whole as "(Bouches-du-Rhône, France)". Governs `location.region: FR-83` on [*Tarascosaurus salluvicus*](../../../genera/T/Tarascosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leloeuff1991a, p. 586: a new theropod "from the Beausset Syncline (Bouches-du-Rhône, France)" (translated).
- leloeuff1991a, p. 587: the material "bore only the notation 'Upper Cretaceous, Lambeau du Beausset' for provenance" (translated); p. 592, References: "BERTRAND M. 1887 - Ilot triasique du Beausset (Var)".
- tortosa2014a, p. 72, Fig. 8: "Le Beausset, Var (9)".
- csiki-sava2015a, p. 44: "'Fuvelian' (lower Campanian) deposits of Le Beausset (Var), which were described by Le Loeuff and Buffetaut (1991) as the new taxon Tarascosaurus salluvicus".

**Ruled out.**
- *Bouches-du-Rhône (FR-13).* The describing paper gives it for the syncline, not for the klippe that the label names, and its own cited source on the klippe titles it as in the Var. That the syncline extends beyond the Var is our reading of the geography, not a statement in the sources read.

**Open.** Nothing.
