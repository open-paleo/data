# *Tiamat*

## Which numbers make up the *Tiamat valdecii* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** The eight numbers of the designation: UFRJ-DG 527-R, 574-R, 591-R, 606-R, 635-R, 636-R, 638-R and 704-R. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Tiamat valdecii*](../../../genera/T/Tiamat.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pereira2024a, p. 5, Systematic palaeontology: "Holotype: UFRJ-DG 636-R, UFRJ-DG 638-R, UFRJ-DG 606-R (anterior caudal vertebrae), UFRJ-DG 635-R, UFRJ-DG 591-R, UFRJ-DG 527-R, UFRJ-DG 704-R (middle caudal vertebrae), and UFRJ-DG 574-R (partial neural arch)."

**Ruled out.**
- *UFRJ-DG 625-R.* The record once held it; the paper never prints it.
- *UFRJ-DG 368-R.* The record once held it. The paper prints it once, in the introduction's list of the material, where it stands among the three anterior caudals in the place of 636-R (pereira2024a, p. 2: "three anterior caudal vertebra (UFRJ-DG 638-R, UFRJ-DG 368-R, UFRJ-DG 606-R)"); 636-R occurs seven times, including in the designation. Reading 368-R as a transposition of 636-R is an inference.
- *Seven numbers without 574-R.* Jones (2026) gives "FRJ-DG [636, 638, 606, 635, 591, 527, 704]-R—caudal vertebrae" (jones2026b, p. 382), which matches the designation except that it leaves out the neural arch 574-R and drops the U of the prefix.

**Open.** Nothing.
