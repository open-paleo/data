# *Tanius*

## What is *Tanius chingkankouensis*?

*2026-10-29*

**Conclusion.** A nomen dubium, carried on the record as a species with that status. Young (1958) named it on a composite of vertebrae, a sacrum, a scapula, an ilium and an ischium. It was then treated as a junior synonym of *T. sinensis* (Horner and colleagues 2004) or as a nomen dubium (Prieto-Márquez and Wagner 2013). Zhang and colleagues (2017) reinstated it on its scapula; Borinder and colleagues (2021), redescribing *T. sinensis*, answered that the scapula differs from that of *T. sinensis* and that the material combines bones of different hadrosauroids, and ruled it a nomen dubium. No held paper replies to Borinder. Governs the *Tanius chingkankouensis* species block, `status: nomen dubium`, on [*Tanius*](../../../genera/T/Tanius.yml).

**Evidence.**
- horner2004a, p. 439: "*T. sinensis* Wiman, 1929 (including *T. chingkankouensis* Young, 1958a)".
- prieto-márquez2013b, p. 2: "we consider Tanius chingkankouensis Young, 1958 a nomen dubium pending further revision".
- borinder2021a, p. e1914642-15: Zhang and colleagues (2017) "argued that T. chingkankouensis must be valid because its scapular blade is straight", yet the *T. sinensis* scapula "differs markedly"; *T. chingkankouensis* "should be regarded as a nomen dubium (sensu Prieto-Márquez and Wagner, 2013)".

**Ruled out.**
- *A junior synonym of T. sinensis.* Borinder and colleagues (2021) find the material not referable to *T. sinensis*.
- *A valid species.* The reinstatement of Zhang and colleagues (2017) has been answered.

**Open.** Young (1958) and Zhang and colleagues (2017) have not been read; the composite's specimen numbers are not recorded.
