# *Thanatotheristes*

## Is the *Thanatotheristes degrootorum* holotype TMP 2010.5.7 or TMP 2018.16.1?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** TMP 2010.5.7, the partial skull. TMP 2018.16.1 is the referred maxilla from another locality. The number is stored in the padded Tyrrell form. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [TMP 2010.005.0007]` on [*Thanatotheristes degrootorum*](../../../genera/T/Thanatotheristes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- voris2020a, p. 3, Systematic paleontology: "Holotype. TMP 2010.5.7 ..., a partial skull from a large subadult individual ... that includes the right maxilla, right jugal, right postorbital".
- voris2020a, p. 3, Systematic paleontology: "Referred specimen. TMP 2018.16.1, a partial right maxilla from a subadult individual. TMP 2018.16.1 was found along Twelve Mile Coulee".

**Ruled out.**
- *TMP 2018.016.0001.* The record once held this padded form of the referred maxilla's number as the holotype. Jones (2026) has the two the right way round: "RTMP 2010.5.7—cranial fragments; RTMP 2018.16.1—cranial fragment" (jones2026c, p. 502). RTMP and TMP are one collection's prefix.

**Open.** Nothing.
