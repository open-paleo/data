# *Tenontosaurus*

## Is *Tenontosaurus* a rhabdodontomorph?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it sits at Iguanodontia incertae sedis. Analyses on the matrix of Poole (2022) and its descendants recover it within Rhabdodontomorpha. Most analyses on the matrices of Butler and colleagues (2008) and Boyd (2015) recover it outside, as an early iguanodontian near Dryomorpha, and several runs in the papers that recover it inside do not. Governs `parent: Iguanodontia`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Tenontosaurus*](../../../genera/T/Tenontosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- poole2022a, p. 22: "In the parsimony analysis, rhabdodontomorphans also includes Tenontosaurus, while the Bayesian analysis finds Tenontosaurus as the sister to Rhabdodontomorpha".
- zanno2023a, p. 44: "Although we find evidence for a monophyletic Rhabodontomorpha that includes Tenontosaurus and Iani, we note weak and/ or conflicting character support".
- fonseca2024a, p. 35: "This result is consistent across all analyses here, with Tenontosaurus tilletti nesting in Rhabdodontomorpha".
- czepiński2025a, p. 5: the equal-weights analysis "failed to reconstruct a monophyletic Rhabdodontomorpha"; under implied weighting "All runs found Rhabdodontomorpha".
- butler2008b, Fig. 4, p. 16: the two species of *Tenontosaurus* as the sister of Dryosauridae plus Ankylopollexia, with Rhabdodontidae outside.
- dieudonné2020a, p. 1: "Tenontosaurus is found as the basalmost iguanodontian. The monophyly of Rhabdodontomorpha in a position more derived than Tenontosaurus is supported by the present analysis".
- maidment2023a, Text-fig. 22, p. 49: Rhabdodontidae branching before *Tenontosaurus tilletti*, which branches before Dryosauridae.
- dieudonné2026a, Discussion: *Tenontosaurus* and *Iani* "are very distant from Ankylopollexia and Rhabdodontomorpha"; Conclusions: "The Thescelosaurinae now include Iani and Tenontosaurus".

**Ruled out.**
- *Rhabdodontomorpha as the parent.* Analyses on several independent matrices place it outside the clade.
- *Neornithischia as the parent.* The thescelosaurid result rests on one matrix, that of Dieudonné and colleagues (2026); every other analysis places it within Iguanodontia.

**Open.** Whether the matrices converge on the position of Tenontosauridae.
