# *Troodon*

## Is the *Troodon formosus* holotype from the Two Medicine Formation?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype, the tooth ANSP 9259, comes from the Judith River badlands of central Montana and is placed in the Judith River Formation. The Two Medicine Formation is the source of the referred material Varricchio and colleagues (2025) describe, MOR 553 and the other MOR specimens, and of the neotype they propose. Governs `location.formation: Judith River` and `location.locality` on [*Troodon formosus*](../../../genera/T/Troodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- varricchio2025a, p. 219: "In 1855, Ferdinand Hayden collected a single tooth from the Judith River badlands of central Montana."
- varricchio2025a, p. 222: "Holotype. Tooth (ANSP 9259) from the Judith River Formation, Montana, U.S.A. by original designation".
- varricchio2025a, p. 237: "We propose that T. formosus would benefit from the introduction of a neotype, namely MOR 553. This Two Medicine material, described in part here".

**Ruled out.**
- *Two Medicine Formation.* It is the formation of the MOR specimens, which are referred material and a proposed neotype, not the holotype. A position in the Two Medicine outcrop of northwestern Montana belongs with them as well.

**Open.** The neotype petition. Varricchio and colleagues (2025, p. 237) note that "only the International Commission on Zoological Nomenclature has the power to implement a neotype". If the Commission sets MOR 553 as the neotype, the record's type specimen and its whole `location` change to that specimen's Two Medicine site. Until then the record follows ANSP 9259.

## Is CMN 8540 the *Troodon formosus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype is the tooth ANSP 9259; CMN 8540 is the dentary that is the type of *Polyodontosaurus grandis*, a synonym. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ANSP 9259]` on [*Troodon formosus*](../../../genera/T/Troodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- currie1987a, p. 73: "Holotype-Academy of Natural Sciences, Philadelphia ANSP 9259, an isolated anterior tooth from the Judith River Formation of Montana."
- currie1987a, p. 73: "Material Studied-NMC 8540, type of *Polyodontosaurus grandis* Gilmore 1932".
- varricchio2025a, p. 222: "Holotype. Tooth (ANSP 9259) from the Judith River Formation, Montana, U.S.A. by original designation".

**Ruled out.**
- *CMN 8540 (printed NMC 8540 in older papers).* It is the type of *Polyodontosaurus grandis*, which Jones (2026) lists as a synonym of *Troodon formosus* (jones2026c, synonym table); taking it as the *Troodon* type mistakes a synonym's type for the senior name's.

**Open.** Nothing.
