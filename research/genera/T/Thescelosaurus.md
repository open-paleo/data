# *Thescelosaurus*

## Is the *Thescelosaurus neglectus* type from the Lance Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The type, USNM 7757, comes from Doegie Creek in Niobrara County, Wyoming, and Gilmore's osteology of it is titled as a description of a dinosaur "from the Lance Formation of Wyoming". Governs `location.formation: Lance` on [*Thescelosaurus neglectus*](../../../genera/T/Thescelosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1915a, p. 591: "Osteology of Thescelosaurus, an orthopodous dinosaur from the Lance Formation of Wyoming".
- gilmore1915a, p. 591, Summary of Known Material: "Type, No. 7757, U.S.N.M. ... From Doegie Creek, Niobrara County, Wyoming."

**Ruled out.**
- *Horseshoe Canyon, Frenchman or Hell Creek.* Jones (2026) gives "Horseshoe Canyon Fm., Frenchman Fm., Canada; Hell Creek Fm., USA" against "USNM 7757—partial skeleton ... numerous specimens" (jones2026a, Thescelosauridae table). The cell lists the units of referred material across the genus and leaves out the Lance, the type's unit. The Frenchman and Hell Creek material is represented in the record by *T. assiniboiensis* and *T. garbanii*.

**Open.** Nothing.
