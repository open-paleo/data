# *Trigonosaurus*

## Does a source print a position for the *Trigonosaurus* quarry?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Soares and colleagues (2020) print a position for the Price 1 Geosite, which they equate with the "Caieira Site" and "Ponto 1 do Price", and Campos and colleagues (2005) collected the holotype at the Caieira quarry, the site Price called "Ponto 1". It converts to -19.724136, -47.746514. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Trigonosaurus pricei*](../../../genera/T/Trigonosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- soares2020a, article-file p. 4, 3 Methods: "the Price 1 Geosite (19°43′26.89″S - 47°44′47.45″W; also known in the literature as 'Caieira Site' or 'Ponto 1 do Price')", one of three "type-sections for the Serra da Galga Formation deposits".
- campos2005a, p. 565, Abstract: *Trigonosaurus pricei* "is based on two specimens, both collected at the Caieira Quarry near Peirópolis"; p. 567, Type-locality: "the quarry known as "Caieira" (locality 120 of BERTINI, 1993)", "about 2km north of Peirópolis".
- silvajunior2022a, p. 2: Price unearthed titanosaur remains "from the quarry known as "Caieira", a site he called "Ponto 1"", among them Series B, "MCT 1488-R", the *T. pricei* holotype.

**Ruled out.**
- *No coordinate in Soares and colleagues.* The earlier deletion rested on the claim that the paper "prints no coordinate at all". The scan dropped the degree, minute and second marks, so a search for them found nothing; the printed page carries the full position.
- *The deleted pair, -19.601944, -47.615833.* It lies about 19 km from the printed position and no source read prints it.

**Open.** The tie between the printed point and the holotype runs through the site's names, Caieira and Ponto 1, not through a statement about the specimen, and Soares and colleagues give the point for a geological type section, not a quarry. A position printed for the Caieira quarry itself would confirm it.
