# *Tyrannomimus*

## Does the Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry coordinate apply to the *Tyrannomimus* holotype?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** Yes, as the quarry's position. The coordinate is printed by Azuma and colleagues (2016) in the type-locality line of *Fukuivenator*, for the Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry as a whole. Hattori and colleagues (2023) place the *Tyrannomimus* type in Bonebed 1 of that quarry and print no coordinate. The pair locates the quarry, not either specimen within it. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Tyrannomimus fukuiensis*](../../../genera/T/Tyrannomimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- azuma2016a, p. 2, Type locality and horizon: "FPDM-V8461 was found in the Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry, which is on the Sugiyama River in the northern part of the city of Katsuyama, Fukui, Japan (36° 7′ 17.9″ N, 136° 32′ 41.4″ E)".
- hattori2023a, p. 3: "The type and referred specimens were found from the Bonebed 1 of the Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry in the northern part of Katsuyama, Fukui."

**Ruled out.**
- *Deleting the pair as another specimen's site.* It was printed for a *Fukuivenator* specimen, but the position it gives is the quarry, and the quarry is this holotype's site.

**Open.** Which bonebed *Fukuivenator* came from. Azuma and colleagues do not say, so whether the point marks Bonebed 1 or another part of the quarry is unknown.
