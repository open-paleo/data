# *Tengrisaurus*

## Does the Mogoito coordinate printed by Averianov and colleagues (2021) give the holotype's position?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** No. The pair locates the Mogoito locality as a whole, "a series of natural outcrops in shallow ravines", while the holotype and the two other original caudals came from one small outcrop in one ravine, Promoina Klevenskogo. The record carries no coordinate. Governs the absence of `location.coordinates` on [*Tengrisaurus starkovi*](../../../genera/T/Tengrisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2021e, p. 3, Materials and methods: the site "is situated at the foot of the Khambin Ridge and presented by a series of natural outcrops in shallow ravines on the western bank of The Lake Gusinoe between villages of Gusinoe Ozero and Baraty (GPS coordinates: 51°12′10.44″ E, 106°17′18.51″ N)".
- averianov2017a, p. 11, Remarks: the three caudals "were found in a close proximity in a small outcrop 5–6 m in length in a ravine called Promoina Klevenskogo".

**Ruled out.**
- *51.202900, 106.288475.* The printed pair, with its E and N labels exchanged as the latitude and longitude of Buryatia require. It is the locality's position, not the ravine's.
- *51.195557, 106.274719.* The pair the record held before 2026-09-08. It matches no printed value and lies about 1.3 km from the printed pair.

**Open.** Where Promoina Klevenskogo lies within Mogoito. Dmitriev and Rozhdestvensky (1968), whom Averianov and Skutschas (2017) cite for the ravine, may locate it; it has not been read.

## Is *Tengrisaurus starkovi* Valanginian or Barremian–Aptian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Valanginian, the age Averianov and colleagues (2025) give the type horizon. The same group of authors had given Barremian–Aptian in 2017 and 2021, and the 2025 paper replaces it without discussing the change. Governs `period.stage: [Valanginian]` on [*Tengrisaurus starkovi*](../../../genera/T/Tengrisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2025b, p. 4, Locality and horizon: "Mogoito, Buryatia, Russia; Murtoi Formation, Lower Cretaceous (Valanginian)"; p. 2: the formation's geological setting "was described in detail in Averianov et al. (2021), Averianov et al. (2022), and Arzhannikova et al. (2023)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian–Aptian.* The describing paper gives "Murtoi Formation, Lower Cretaceous (Barremian-Aptian)" in the type horizon (averianov2017a, p. 8), and Averianov and colleagues (2021) write that the formation "has traditionally been dated as Berriasian–Hauterivian" but that "the studies based on vertebrates suggest a younger, late Barremian to middle Aptian age" (averianov2021e, p. 3). Jones (2026) follows these, "Murtoi Fm., Russia *Early Cretaceous, Barremian/Aptian*" (jones2026b, Lithostrotia table). The 2025 paper is the latest statement by the same authors about the same horizon.

**Open.** The basis for the Valanginian. Averianov and colleagues (2022) and Arzhannikova and colleagues (2023), which the 2025 paper cites for the geological setting, have not been read. If they date the Mogoito beds by a measured age rather than by fauna, they settle the question.
