# *Tengrisaurus*

## Does the Mogoito coordinate printed by Averianov and colleagues (2021) give the holotype's position?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** No. The pair locates the Mogoito locality as a whole, "a series of natural outcrops in shallow ravines", while the holotype and the two other original caudals came from one small outcrop in one ravine, Promoina Klevenskogo. The record carries no coordinate. Governs the absence of `location.coordinates` on [*Tengrisaurus starkovi*](../../../genera/T/Tengrisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2021e, p. 3, Materials and methods: the site "is situated at the foot of the Khambin Ridge and presented by a series of natural outcrops in shallow ravines on the western bank of The Lake Gusinoe between villages of Gusinoe Ozero and Baraty (GPS coordinates: 51°12′10.44″ E, 106°17′18.51″ N)".
- averianov2017a, p. 11, Remarks: the three caudals "were found in a close proximity in a small outcrop 5–6 m in length in a ravine called Promoina Klevenskogo".

**Ruled out.**
- *51.202900, 106.288475.* The printed pair, with its E and N labels exchanged as the latitude and longitude of Buryatia require. It is the locality's position, not the ravine's.
- *51.195557, 106.274719.* The pair the record held before 2026-09-08. It matches no printed value and lies about 1.3 km from the printed pair.

**Open.** Where Promoina Klevenskogo lies within Mogoito. Dmitriev and Rozhdestvensky (1968), whom Averianov and Skutschas (2017) cite for the ravine, may locate it; it has not been read.
