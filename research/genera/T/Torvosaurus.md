# *Torvosaurus*

## Is *Torvosaurus gurneyi* a junior synonym of *T. tanneri*?

*2026-11-10*

**Conclusion.** The question is live, and the record keeps *T. gurneyi* as a species with `status: disputed`. Warner-Cowgill and colleagues (2026) synonymize it firmly, but no later work has yet taken the synonymy up or answered it, and the consensus policy in CONTRIBUTING adopts a revision only once it has been. Governs `status` on *T. gurneyi* and the `dispute` on [*Torvosaurus*](../../../genera/T/Torvosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- warner-cowgill2026a, p. 39: two of the four differences on which *T. gurneyi* was diagnosed "(tooth count, interdental plate shape) result from the incomplete and poorly preserved state of BYUVP 9122", and the other two "may result from intraspecies variation".
- warner-cowgill2026a, p. 40: "Because there is currently insufficient evidence for the taxonomic validity of T. 'gurneyi', that taxon must be regarded as a junior synonym of T. tanneri."

**Ruled out.**
- *Adopting the synonymy now.* A single paper, accepted in September 2026, with no subsequent analysis taking it up; CONTRIBUTING's Adopting a taxonomic revision section asks for the proposal to be accepted or adopted by later work first.
- *Keeping `status: valid` with only a dispute.* CONTRIBUTING reserves `disputed` for a taxon whose own validity is in question, which is exactly what the 2026 paper contests.

**Open.** A response to Warner-Cowgill and colleagues (2026), or later analyses that adopt or reject the synonymy, would settle it.

## Is the *Torvosaurus tanneri* holotype the left humerus alone?

*2026-11-10*

**Conclusion.** No. Galton and Jensen (1979) designate as holotype the six long bones of both forelimbs of one individual, all under the single number BYU 2002. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` and `type_specimen.material` on *T. tanneri* in [*Torvosaurus*](../../../genera/T/Torvosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1979a, p. 1: "Holotype: BYU 2002, left and right long bones of forelimb."
- galton1979a, p. 4: "the bones of the holotype undoubtedly represent left and right sides of the same animal and have been numbered accordingly."

**Ruled out.**
- *The left humerus alone.* Warner-Cowgill and colleagues (2026) print "Holotype. BYUVP 2002, left humerus." (warner-cowgill2026a, p. 4). They state no restriction or redesignation, and the type is taken from the designating paper (see `methods.md`, "Take a type specimen's number from the designating paper, not a compilation").

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which diagnosis does the record give?

*2026-11-10*

**Conclusion.** The formal craniomandibular diagnoses of Warner-Cowgill and colleagues (2026): three apomorphies for the genus and seven autapomorphies for *T. tanneri*, six numbered and a surangular flange stated after them. They replace Galton and Jensen's (1979) postcranial diagnosis, which its authors called preliminary, and *T. tanneri* takes `described_in: warner-cowgill2026a`. The paper's "unique combination" and differential-diagnosis characters are not autapomorphies and are left out. Governs the genus `diagnostic_features` and the *T. tanneri* `diagnostic_features` and `described_in` on [*Torvosaurus*](../../../genera/T/Torvosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1979a, abstract: the species is given "a preliminary diagnosis".
- warner-cowgill2026a, p. 3: "Megalosaurid distinguished from all other basal or non-coelurosaurian tetanurans by the following apomorphies of the craniomandibular skeleton".
- warner-cowgill2026a, p. 4: *T. tanneri* differs "from all basal or non-coelurosaurian tetanurans by the following autapomorphies of the craniomandibular skeleton".

**Ruled out.**
- *Keeping Galton and Jensen's characters alongside.* Their own authors called them preliminary, and the 2026 paper diagnoses the genus and species anew (user ruling, 2026-11-10).

**Open.** With ten maxillary alveoli now an autapomorphy of *T. tanneri* (warner-cowgill2026a, p. 4), the *T. gurneyi* differentium "fewer than eleven teeth" no longer separates the two species. The record keeps it while the synonymy question above stays open.
