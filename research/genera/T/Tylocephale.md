# *Tylocephale*

## Is the *Tylocephale gilmorei* holotype Maastrichtian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** No source read gives the Baruungoyot Formation, or this horizon, a Maastrichtian age on its own. The describing paper dates the type horizon only by formation and mammal zone, and Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz (1974), who redefined the formation, give it as "probably of Campanian age". The record holds Campanian. Governs `period.stage: [Campanian]` on [*Tylocephale gilmorei*](../../../genera/T/Tylocephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maryańska1974a, p. 51: "Type horizon: Upper Cretaceous, Barun Goyot Formation, zone of *Djadochtatherium catopsaloides* Kielan-Jaworowska, *Nemegtbaatar gobiensis* Kielan-Jaworowska and *Chulsanbaatar vulgaris* Kielan-Jaworowska."
- gradziński1974a, p. 111, Abstract: the formation "is composed of clastic continental sediments of red-beds type; it is probably of Campanian age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian.* Jones (2026) prints "Barun Goyot Fm., Mongolia *Late Cretaceous, Maastrichtian*" (jones2026a, Pachycephalosauria table) and cites no stratigraphic source. Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz (1974) give the Maastrichtian to the overlying Nemegt Formation, not to this one.

**Open.** Whether the record should carry the formation's full range. No statement dates the Khulsan type horizon apart from the formation, and the [Baruungoyot](../../../stratigraphy/b/baruungoyot.yml) registry entry carries Santonian to Maastrichtian, including Shuvalov's (2000) Santonian–Campanian. A dated section at Khulsan would settle it.
