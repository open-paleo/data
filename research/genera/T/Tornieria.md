# *Tornieria*

## Is "Quarry A" the name of the *Tornieria* type site, or only Fraas's label for the skeleton?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** The letter A names both the skeleton and the place it was dug. Fraas's field letter labels the holotype skeleton, and Remes (2007) uses the same letter for the site, listing "localities A ... e, F, and k" and calling A the type locality of *Tornieria africana*. Tschopp and colleagues (2015) write "Tendaguru Quarry A". Governs `location.locality: Tendaguru Quarry A` on [*Tornieria africana*](../../../genera/T/Tornieria.yml).

**Evidence.**
- remes2006a, p. 652: the holotype elements are of "'skeleton A' of Fraas (1908)".
- remes2006a, p. 653: an astragalus "was collected from the surface at the excavation site of skeleton A (Fraas, 1908)".
- remes2007a, p. 654: "localities A (the type locality of Tornieria africana; Fraas 1908), e, F, and k (origin of the referred specimen of T. africana; Remes 2006), all about 400 m south of Tendaguru Hill".
- tschopp2015a, Table 1: "Tendaguru Quarry A, Tanzania".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Quarry A" as a quarry-style name made up from a skeleton letter.* Remes (2006) uses A mostly for the skeleton, but his 2007 paper uses it as a locality name, as he does for the lettered sites e, F and k.

**Open.** Nothing.
