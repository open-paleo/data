# *Taohelong*

## Where does *Taohelong* sit within Ankylosauria?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** Ankylosauria incertae sedis. Readings since the description include Polacanthinae, Nodosaurinae, an unresolved nodosaurid polytomy, a position outside the main clades, and a wildcard; Ankylosauria is the level all of them share. The describing paper's polacanthine placement comes from its majority-rule tree only. Governs `parent: Ankylosauria` and the dispute on [*Taohelong*](../../../genera/T/Taohelong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yang2013a: "Cladistic analysis shows that *Taohelong* is a member of polacanthine nodosaurid ankylosaur"; but "In the strict consensus tree, *Taohelong* belongs to the Nodosauridae, but the relationships among the various members of the Nodosauridae cannot be resolved."
- madzia2021a: "Under the primary reference phylogeny, Polacanthinae comprises Polacanthus foxii and Taohelong jinchengensis"; Nodosaurinae under its reference phylogeny also includes *Taohelong jinchengensis*.
- arbour2015a: "Dongyangopelta, Sauroplites and Taohelong form an unresolved polytomy within Nodosauridae".
- raven2023a: "Silvisaurus and Taohelong are often found as sister-taxa in the analyses presented herein, although they are not usually recovered in any of the four main ankylosaur clades."
- soto-acuña2024a: "Through IterPCR the following taxa were found as wildcard taxa: Ahsihlepelta, Cedarpelta, Dongyangopelta, Hylaeosaurus, Minmi, Sauroplites, Taohelong, Zhejiangosaurus."

**Ruled out.**
- *Polacanthinae as the adopted placement.* It rests on the describing paper's majority-rule tree; its strict consensus and later analyses do not resolve it there.

**Open.** The Nodosaurinae analysis Madzia and colleagues (2021) take as their reference phylogeny has not been read.
