# *Tuebingosaurus*

## Does the "lower dinosaur bed" give the *Tuebingosaurus* holotype a `part` of the Trossingen Formation?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype came from the lower of the two bone beds dug at the Obere Mühle quarry in Trossingen. Fernández and Werneburg (2022) call it the "lower dinosaur bed", and Sues and Schoch (2025) give it as the "Untere Saurierschicht". "Lower" ranks it below the upper bed at the same quarry. It does not place the bed in the lower part of the formation, and no source read gives the bed's position within the Trossingen. Governs the absence of `location.part`, and `location.locality: Obere Mühle`, on [*Tuebingosaurus maierfritzorum*](../../../genera/T/Tuebingosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fernández2022a, p. 774, table of GPIT specimens: GPIT-PV-30787 "Collected from lower dinosaur bed, Obere Mühle"; GPIT-PV-30786 and GPIT-PV-30788 "Collected from upper dinosaur bed, Obere Mühle".
- sues2025a, p. 463, *Tuebingosaurus maierfritzorum*: "Type locality. Obere Mühle, at Deibhalde, on eastern edge of Trossingen, Tuttlingen district, Baden-Württemberg. Type horizon. "Untere Saurierschicht," Trossingen Formation, Middle Keuper Subgroup."

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`.* It would turn a comparison between two beds at one quarry into a position within the formation.
- *A site near Tübingen.* Fernández and Werneburg (2022, p. 771) write that the specimen was found "during an excavation in 1922 near the city of Tübingen". The quarry is on the edge of Trossingen, as Sues and Schoch place it. Tübingen is where the collection is housed.

**Open.** Whether the "Untere Saurierschicht" should be recorded as a `bed`. Sues and Schoch print it in quotation marks as a named horizon. Recording it would be one decision for every record from the Obere Mühle beds.

## Is the *Tuebingosaurus* holotype from the Trossingen or the Löwenstein Formation?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** The Trossingen Formation. The describing paper names it in its title, and Sues and Schoch (2025) give the type horizon as the "Untere Saurierschicht" of the Trossingen Formation. Governs `location.formation: Trossingen` on [*Tuebingosaurus maierfritzorum*](../../../genera/T/Tuebingosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fernández2022a, p. 771: "A new massopodan sauropodomorph from Trossingen Formation (Germany) hidden as 'Plateosaurus' for 100 years in the historical Tübingen collection".
- sues2025a, p. 463: "Type horizon. "Untere Saurierschicht," Trossingen Formation, Middle Keuper Subgroup. Age: Late Triassic (Norian: Sevatian)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Löwenstein Formation.* The record once held it. The Löwenstein underlies the Trossingen, and the describing paper names Löwenstein only among the beds that other GPIT specimens came from (fernández2022a, p. 772). Jones (2026) agrees with the primary sources: "Trossingen Fm., Germany, Late Triassic, Norian" (jones2026b, Sauropoda table).

**Open.** Nothing.
