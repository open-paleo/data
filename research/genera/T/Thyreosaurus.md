## Is the *Thyreosaurus* holotype HIUC-BN00?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** No. The describing paper gives HIIUC BN00, the code for Hassan II University of Casablanca, written with a space and no hyphen. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [HIIUC BN00]` on [*Thyreosaurus atlasicus*](../../../genera/T/Thyreosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zafaty2024a, Institutional abbreviations: "HIIUC, Hassan II University of Casablanca, Casablanca, Morocco; BN, Boulahfa North".
- zafaty2024a, 4.1. Systematic paleontology: "Holotype: HIIUC BN00, a partial postcranial skeleton that includes 9 dorsal vertebrae and 21 dorsal rib remains, a limb bone (fibula?), and six dermal elements."

**Ruled out.**
- *HIUC-BN00.* Jones (2026) prints "**HIUC-BN00**—partial skeleton" (jones2026a, Stegosauria table), dropping an I.
- *HIIUC-BN00.* The record's form before 2026-08-03. The paper writes no hyphen.

**Open.** Nothing.
