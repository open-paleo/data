# *Tastavinsaurus*

## How is the coordinate printed for Arsis 1 read?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** As degrees and decimal minutes, N 40° 41.85′ and E 0° 2.95′, which is 40.6975, 0.0492. Canudo and colleagues (2008) print the figures with second marks, but 85 and 95 cannot be seconds, so the last figure of each is hundredths of a minute. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Tastavinsaurus sanzi*](../../../genera/T/Tastavinsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- canudo2008a, p. 713, Locality and Age: "Arsis 1 (Ars1) locality (N 40° 41′ 85″, E 0° 2′ 95″), Peñarroya de Tastavins, Teruel Province (Aragon), northeastern Spain".

**Ruled out.**
- *40.706944, 0.059722.* The pair the record held before 2026-09-08, about 1.4 km from the decimal-minute reading. It is what the printed figures give when 85″ and 95″ are carried over as seconds (41′ 85″ becoming 42′ 25″, and 2′ 95″ becoming 3′ 35″). That the old pair arose this way is an inference; nothing records where it came from.

**Open.** Royo-Torres (2009), the monograph on the specimen and its site, may print the position in another form and would confirm the reading. It has not been read.
