# *Tambatitanis*

## Is *Tambatitanis* from the Ohyamashimo Formation or the Sasayama Group?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Ohyamashimo Formation. The describing paper's provisional "'Lower' Formation of the Sasayama Group" is the lower of the group's two formations, which later papers name the Ohyamashimo, and they place the *Tambatitanis* find in it. Governs `location.formation: Ohyamashimo` on [*Tambatitanis amicitiae*](../../../genera/T/Tambatitanis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- saegusa2014a, p. 9: "Type horizon and age: 'Lower' Formation of the Sasayama Group, Early Cretaceous (probably early Albian)".
- tanaka2024a, Geological Setting: "The Sasayama Group, comprising the Ohyamashimo and Sawada formations in ascending order"; "The Ohyamashimo Formation has yielded abundant vertebrate fossils including … dinosaurs (Saegusa & Ikeda 2014 …)"; the Miyada site lies "c. 8.5 km east of the Kamitaki locality, where the sauropod *Tambatitanis amicitiae* (Saegusa & Ikeda 2014) was discovered in 2006".
- kubota2024a, p. 3, Geological setting: "The Ohyamashimo Formation, the lower unit of the Sasayama Group".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sasayama Group as the formation.* Jones (2026) prints "Sasayama Grp., Japan" (jones2026b, Titanosauria table). It is the group, one rank above the Ohyamashimo.

**Open.** Nothing.
