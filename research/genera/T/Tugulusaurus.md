# *Tugulusaurus*

## Is the *Tugulusaurus* holotype from Wuerho field locality 64041 or 64041-2?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** 64041-2. Dong (1973) gives that number for the *Tugulusaurus* material, V. 4025, and uses the bare 64041 for other specimens: the cf. *Asiatosaurus* tooth and vertebra, and the tooth of *Phaedrolosaurus*. The numbers are the paper's field locality numbers, not IVPP site numbers. Governs `location.locality: Wuerho, field locality 64041-2` on [*Tugulusaurus faciles*](../../../genera/T/Tugulusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1973a, *Tugulusaurus faciles* gen. et sp. nov., Material (translated): "Four caudal vertebrae, and a fairly incomplete right hindlimb. Field locality number 64041-2; Institute of Vertebrate Paleontology and Paleoanthropology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, catalogue number V. 4025."
- dong1973a, cf. *Asiatosaurus mongoliensis*, Material (translated): "One complete spoon-shaped tooth; field locality number 64041 ... catalogue number V. 4021-1."
- dong1973a, *Phaedrolosaurus ilikensis*, Material (translated): "One complete tooth, field locality number 64041, Institute of Vertebrate Paleontology catalogue number V. 4024-1".
- dong1973a, II. Theropoda (translated): "This material comes from four field locality numbers: 64041, 64041-2, 64041-9, 64041-17."

**Ruled out.**
- *64041, or "IVPP site 64041".* The bare number is a separate field locality in the same paper, the source of other taxa's material. The IVPP prefix belongs to the catalogue numbers, not to the field localities.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the Tugulu Group a rival to the Lianmuqin Formation for *Tugulusaurus*?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** No. The Lianmuqin is the uppermost formation of the Tugulu Group, and the redescription of the holotype names the formation. Governs `location.formation: Lianmuqin` on [*Tugulusaurus faciles*](../../../genera/T/Tugulusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2005c, p. 108, Locality and Horizon: "Wuerho, Lianmugin Formation, Xinjiang, China".
- zheng2023a, p. 2 (online-first version): "the Tugulu Group consists (in ascending order) of the Qingshuihe, Hutubihe, Shengjinkou, and Lianmuqin Formations".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tugulu Group as the formation.* Holtz and colleagues (2004) give "Tugulu Group (Xinjiang Uygur Zizhiqu), People's Republic of China" (holtz2004a, p. 73, Table 4.1). It is the group containing the Lianmuqin. Dong (1973) gives field locality numbers but no formation for the material.

**Open.** Nothing.
