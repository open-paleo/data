# *Turiasaurus*

## In which zone and datum is the *Turiasaurus* grid reference given?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** Royo-Torres and colleagues (2006) print the site as a Universal Transverse Mercator pair without naming the zone or the datum. The record converts it as zone 30 north on WGS84. Zone 30 is the only zone that puts the pair in Teruel Province, but that is inferred from geography, not stated. Read on ED50, the datum of Spanish topographic maps in 2006, the same figures fall about 240 m to the south-southwest. That conversion was computed for this note, not taken from a source. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Turiasaurus riodevensis*](../../../genera/T/Turiasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- royo-torres2006a, p. 1926, Locality and horizon: "Riodeva (fig. S1), Teruel Province, Aragón, Spain (grid coordinates Universal Transverse Mercator 659049 and 4443553), site RD-10 (known as Barrihonda–El Humero)".

**Ruled out.**
- *No printed coordinate.* The pair is printed in the type-locality line, so the value is sourced even though no degrees appear anywhere in the paper.

**Open.** The datum. A later Riodeva paper by the same team that prints the site in degrees, or the supplementary figure S1, would settle whether the figures are ED50 or WGS84.
