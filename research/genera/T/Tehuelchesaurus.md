# *Tehuelchesaurus*

## Is the *Tehuelchesaurus benitezii* type from the Cañadón Asfalto or the Cañadón Calcáreo?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Cañadón Calcáreo. Carballido and colleagues (2011), redescribing the type, place its beds in the upper section that was once included in the Cañadón Asfalto and is now a separate formation. Governs `location.formation: Cañadón Calcáreo` on [*Tehuelchesaurus benitezii*](../../../genera/T/Tehuelchesaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carballido2011a, p. 606: the taxon was described "based on a partially articulated skeleton, allegedly found in the Middle Jurassic Cañadón Asfalto Formation".
- carballido2011a, p. 606, Geological setting: "the distinction of a separate formation, as advocated by Proserpio (1987), seems justified ... Thus, the type of *Tehuelchesaurus* comes from the Cañadón Calcáreo Formation *sensu* Proserpio (1987)."
- cúneo2013a, p. 1273 (in-press version, p. 7): "The middle lacustrine section of the formation is host to the neosauropod dinosaur Tehuelchesaurus benitezii recovered from the Estancia Fernández locality", of the Cañadón Calcáreo Formation.

**Ruled out.**
- *Cañadón Asfalto.* Jones (2026) gives "Cañadón Asfalto Fm., Argentina" (jones2026b, Eusauropoda table), and Upchurch and colleagues (2004) "Cañodon Asfalto Formation (Chubut), Argentina" (upchurch2004a, Table 13.1). Both follow the describing paper's assignment, made when the upper beds were still counted in the Cañadón Asfalto.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What stage is *Tehuelchesaurus benitezii*?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Late Jurassic, Oxfordian to Tithonian, the range of the Cañadón Calcáreo Formation. Governs `stages: [Oxfordian, Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` on [*Tehuelchesaurus benitezii*](../../../genera/T/Tehuelchesaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cúneo2013a, p. 1270 (in-press version, p. 4): a tuff "directly overlying the lower, thin-bedded, lacustrine section of the Cañadón Calcáreo Formation" gives "a weighted mean 206Pb/238U date of 157.449± 0.056/0.090/0.19 Ma".
- cúneo2013a, p. 1267, abstract: "The diverse palynofloral assemblage of the Cañadón Calcáreo Formation that was once considered to be early Cretaceous in age, is now entirely constrained to the Late Jurassic."

**Ruled out.**
- *Toarcian, Early to Middle Jurassic.* Jones (2026) gives "Early–Middle Jurassic, Toarcian/?" (jones2026b, Eusauropoda table), with the Cañadón Asfalto assignment; Upchurch and colleagues (2004) give "Callovian" (upchurch2004a, Table 13.1). Both are ages of the Cañadón Asfalto, the formation the type is no longer assigned to.

**Open.** Where the type's horizon sits relative to the dated tuff. Cúneo and colleagues place *Tehuelchesaurus* in the middle lacustrine section and the tuff above the lower lacustrine section; the paper does not say whether the type lies above or below it, so the record keeps the formation's range.
