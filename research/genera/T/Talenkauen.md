# *Talenkauen*

## Is the *Talenkauen santacrucensis* type from the Cerro Fortaleza or the Mata Amarilla Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Cerro Fortaleza. The describing paper's "Pari Aike Fm." at Los Hornos Hill is the unit now called the Cerro Fortaleza Formation, and later papers place *Talenkauen* there. Governs `location.formation: Cerro Fortaleza` on [*Talenkauen santacrucensis*](../../../genera/T/Talenkauen.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas2004a, p. 76: "Locality and horizon. Los Hornos Hill, southern coast of Viedma Lake, Santa Cruz Province, Argentina ... Pari Aike Fm., Upper Cretaceous, Maastrichtian".
- novas2019a, p. 274: "The Cerro Fortaleza beds, at Cerro de Los Hornos fossil site, shed different dinosaur taxa (i.e., *Talenkauen, Orkoraptor, Puertasaurus, Dreadnoughtus*...)".
- bandeira2024a, Table 3: "Talenkauen santacrucensis | Argentina | Cerro Fortaleza Formation | Campanian – Maastrichtian | Novas et al. (2004)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Mata Amarilla Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Mata Amarilla Fm., Argentina" (jones2026a, Ornithopoda table). Both the Mata Amarilla and the Cerro Fortaleza were once called the Pari Aike Formation; the describing paper's Maastrichtian age for its "Pari Aike" points to the younger unit, not to the Cenomanian Mata Amarilla.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What stage is *Talenkauen santacrucensis*?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Campanian, as recorded; the Cenomanian belongs to the Mata Amarilla Formation. Governs `stages: [Campanian]` on [*Talenkauen santacrucensis*](../../../genera/T/Talenkauen.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sickmann2018a, New Detrital Zircon MDAs: samples from the "terrestrial Cerro Fortaleza Formation in this area show maximum depositional ages throughout the Campanian with the stratigraphically highest sample EP-13 providing a YC1σ MDA of 76.2 ± 1.6 Ma".
- varela2012a, p. 359, abstract: a tuff of the middle section of the Mata Amarilla Formation yields "a U-Pb concordia age of 96.23±0.71 Ma, which corresponds to the middle Cenomanian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian.* Jones (2026) gives "Mata Amarilla Fm., Argentina, Late Cretaceous, Cenomanian" (jones2026a, Ornithopoda table), and Novas and colleagues (2019) call it "the Cenomanian-Turonian *Talenkauen*" (novas2019a, p. 242), in the same paper that places it in the Cerro Fortaleza beds (p. 274). It is the Mata Amarilla's age, carried with the shared old name Pari Aike.

**Open.** Whether the stage should include the Maastrichtian. The Sickmann and colleagues dates are maximum depositional ages, and none is from Los Hornos Hill; the describing paper gives "Maastrichtian" (novas2004a, p. 76), and Bandeira and colleagues give "Campanian – Maastrichtian" (bandeira2024a, Table 3). No source dates the type horizon itself.
