# *Titanosaurus*

## Is the *Titanosaurus indicus* holotype unnumbered?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The surviving holotype caudal vertebra, rediscovered in the Geological Survey of India collections, carries two numbers from the Survey's specimen register. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [GSI 2191/2194]` on [*Titanosaurus indicus*](../../../genera/T/Titanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mohabey2013a, p. 35, Fig. 2: "Rediscovered holotypic caudal vertebra of India's first dinosaur, *Titanosaurus indicus* … the other two numbers (GSI '2191', '2194') represent serial numbers as recorded in the Specimen Register of the Curatorial Division, GSI."

**Ruled out.**
- *Unnumbered.* Jones (2026) gives "**Unnumbered**—caudal vertebra *(Lydekker, 1877)*" (jones2026b, Titanosauria table), citing Lydekker (1877). The register numbers were published with the vertebra's rediscovery by Mohabey and colleagues (2013).

**Open.** Whether 2191 and 2194 are two numbers for this one vertebra or the numbers of Lydekker's two caudals. Mohabey and colleagues describe both as written on the one bone; the record keeps them as one id.
