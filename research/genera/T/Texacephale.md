# *Texacephale*

## Is *Texacephale langstoni* a nomen dubium?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Every work after the original description that assesses the diagnosis rejects it, and none defends the genus; the open question is what the holotype is, an indeterminate pachycephalosaurid or probably *Stegoceras*, both within Pachycephalosauridae. Governs `status: nomen dubium`, `placement: incertae sedis`, the absence of `diagnostic_features` and the absence of a `dispute` block on [*Texacephale langstoni*](../../../genera/T/Texacephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lehman2010a, p. 798: "The three characters purported to be diagnostic of *Texacephale* (parietal articulation with flange-like processes, narrow nasal boss with convex dorsal margin, and low pedicel separating braincase from skull roof) are all expressed to varied degree in specimens referred to other pachycephalosaur taxa."
- jasinski2011a, p. 213: "Thus, we regard this taxon as a *nomen dubium*"; p. 211: "we also regard LSUMNS 20010 as Pachycephalosauridae indeterminate."
- williamson2016a, p. 41: "Jasinski and Sullivan (2011) concluded that it is a nomen dubium, a decision that we agree with and is followed here."
- wick2024a, p. 89: the holotype "is probably an adult semaphoront of *Stegoceras*".
- woodruff2025a, p. 4: "not only do we question its morphologic interpretations, but also its taxonomic validity".

**Ruled out.**
- *Valid, or disputed.* Longrich and colleagues (2010) erected the genus on three autapomorphies; no later work read defends them.

**Open.** Whether the holotype is referable to *Stegoceras* (Wick and Lehman, 2024) or remains indeterminate (Jasinski and Sullivan, 2011).
