# *Transylvanosaurus*

## Which unit is the *Transylvanosaurus* holotype from?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** The Pui Beds, an informal unit never formally erected but in use across the Haţeg literature, recorded under the informal-units rule. Augustin and colleagues (2022) place the holotype in the Bărbat River Valley succession, which Therrien (2005) set apart from the Sânpetru Formation. The registry entry keeps "Beds" in the name because Therrien reports that "Pui Formation is already in use". Governs `location.formation: Pui Beds` on [*Transylvanosaurus platycephalus*](../../../genera/T/Transylvanosaurus.yml) and the [Pui Beds](../../../stratigraphy/p/pui-beds.yml) entry.

**Evidence.**
- augustin2022a, Type Stratum: "the middle part of the uppermost Cretaceous continental succession from the Barbat River Valley section, informally also referred to as the 'Barbat Formation' (Therrien, 2005) or the 'Pui Beds'".
- augustin2023a: the holotype "comes from the 'middle' Maastrichtian of the 'Pui Beds'".
- díezdíaz2025a: "as yet not formally defined sedimentary successions in the west-central ('Râul Mare Beds' …) and eastern-central ('Pui Beds', upper part of lower to lower part of upper Maastrichtian) areas of the depression".
- therrien2005a: the Pui beds "are distinct from the Sânpetru Formation and may warrant their own formational recognition (Bărbat Formation)".
- jones2026a: ""Pui Beds" of Hateg Basin, Romania".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sânpetru Formation.* The earlier assignment; Augustin and colleagues (2022) treat the Pui Beds as Sânpetru-correlative, not part of it.
- *No formation.* The name is widely used, so it stays in `formation` marked informal.

**Open.** Nothing.
