# *Archaeornithomimus*

## Which quarry produced the lectotype, AMNH 6565?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** The Kaisen Quarry, American Museum field no. 140, in the upper 10 m of the Iren Dabasu Formation, as Smith and Galton (1990) give it. Gilmore (1933) gives his two cotypes jointly as from field nos. 140 and 141, and one of his plate captions puts them in no. 141. Governs `location.locality: Kaisen Quarry` and `location.part: upper` on [*Archaeornithomimus asiaticus*](../../../genera/A/Archaeornithomimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1933a, p. 27: "COTYPES.—Nos. 6565, 6569. A. M. N. H. Consist of a nearly complete fore foot and much of an associated hind foot. Collected by Mr. Peter Kaisen, 1923. Field Nos. 140, 141"; "Most of the vertebrae are from the "Kaisen Quarry" (Field No. 140)".
- gilmore1933a, p. 31: the manus, AMNH 6569, was "found by Mr. Peter Kaisen in a quarry at Iren Dabasu in 1923 (Field No. 140)".
- smith1990a, p. 256: "Specimen numbers AMNH 6558, 6565, 6569, and 6576 from Quarry 140; AMNH 6570 from Quarry 141"; "Type Occurrence - Upper 10m of the Iren Dabasu Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Johnson Quarry, field no. 141.* Gilmore's caption to Plate I, fig. 1 reads "'Johnson Quarry,' field No. 141 … The type of *Bactrosaurus johnsoni* and cotypes of *Ornithomimus asiaticus* were found in this deposit" (gilmore1933a, Pl. I), against his own text, which ties the cotype manus to no. 140. Smith and Galton assign only AMNH 6570, a referred specimen, to Quarry 141.

**Open.** Where Quarry 140 lies today. Currie and Eberth (1993) match their Iren Dabasu sites to the American Museum numbers, list no site for 140, and equate 141 with their site K only with a query (currie1993d, Table 3).
