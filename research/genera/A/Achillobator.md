# *Achillobator*

## Does Hicks and colleagues' Burkhant GPS position locate the *Achillobator giganticus* holotype?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** No. The only printed position for Burkhant is the GPS point of a 16 m paleomagnetic section that Hicks and colleagues (1999) sampled at the locality, and they tie no specimen to it. The record carries no coordinate. Governs `location.coordinates: (none)` on [*Achillobator giganticus*](../../../genera/A/Achillobator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hicks1999a, p. 838, Table 1: "Burkhant Section", "44°20′23.70″N, 109°51′32.23″E, altitude 809 m, standard error 12.7 m"; at Burkhant "a short 16-m section comprising three sites was collected from a relatively small exposure of the Bayn Shireh Formation".
- hicks1999a, p. 837: collections from Burkhant, "located approximately 8 km NW of the stratotype locality", are "far less diverse, but include an indeterminate sauropod and a large indeterminate dromaeosaurid".

**Ruled out.**
- *The pair formerly stored, 44.34, 109.858887.* It is Hicks and colleagues' section point with the latitude rounded to two decimals (the converted value is 44.339917, 109.858953, about 11 m away; our computation). A section sampled for paleomagnetism is not the holotype's site, and the paper does not say where on the locality the dromaeosaurid was found or whether it is this specimen.

**Open.** The holotype's position at Burkhant. Perle and colleagues' (1999) describing monograph has not been read and may give it.
