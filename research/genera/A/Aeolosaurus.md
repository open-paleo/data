# *Aeolosaurus*

## Is the *Aeolosaurus rionegrinus* holotype MJG-R 1 or MPCA 27174?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** MJG-R 1, on Santucci and Filippi's (2022) holotype line. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MJG-R 1]` on [*Aeolosaurus rionegrinus*](../../../genera/A/Aeolosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- santucci2022a, p. 377: "Holotype MJG-R 1, seven anterior caudal vertebrae, right and left incomplete scapulae, right and left humeri".
- jones2026b, p. 384: "MJG-R 1—partial skeleton (Powell, 1987)".
- molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropods: "MPCA 27174 Indeterminate age", "Skeleton - Powell 1987".

**Ruled out.**
- *MPCA 27174.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give it with Powell (1987) as the source. No paper read gives that number for the holotype, and Jones (2026), citing the same paper, gives MJG-R 1.

**Open.** Powell (1987), the describing paper, has not been read. It would show whether MPCA 27174 appears there at all.
