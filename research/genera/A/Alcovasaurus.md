# *Alcovasaurus*

## Where does *Alcovasaurus* sit within Stegosauridae?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** Stegosauridae incertae sedis. Sánchez-Fenollosa and Cobos (2025) recover it in Dacentrurinae and Costa and Mateus (2019) refer it there; Maidment and colleagues (2020) and Raven and colleagues (2023) recover it elsewhere within Stegosauridae. Its position outside Eurypoda in Raven and Maidment (2017) has no current proponent, so the record carries no dispute block. Governs `parent: Stegosauridae` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Alcovasaurus longispinus*](../../../genera/A/Alcovasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sánchez-fenollosa2025a, p. 182: "These phylogenetic analyses (Fig. 5; File S1 [figs S2–S8]) are the first to include *Al. longispinus* and *K. aethiopicus* within Dacentrurinae."
- maidment2020a, p. 92: "the MPTs differ only in their placement of Alcovasaurus in relation to Paranthodon and Tuojiangosaurus"; Fig. 12 (figure reading): *Alcovasaurus* lies outside the clade holding *Dacentrurus*.
- raven2023a, Results, p. 9: sister-taxon relationships between "Loricatosaurus and Alcovasaurus".
- raven2017a, Abstract: "The recently described genus Alcovasaurus is resolved as a basal thyreophoran, but this is most likely a consequence of a very high degree of missing data and the questionable ontogenetic stage of the specimen."

**Ruled out.**
- *Outside Eurypoda.* Raven and Maidment (2017) doubt their own result, and Sánchez-Fenollosa and Cobos (2025) trace it to miscoded characters.
- *Dacentrurinae.* Supported by one analysis and one referral, against two analyses that place it elsewhere.

**Open.** Its subfamily.

## How are the *Alcovasaurus* locality and level recorded?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** Reed's *Stegosaurus* Quarry, about 1.5 miles east of Alcova, in the upper Morrison Formation. The quarry name is Galton and Carpenter's (2016) and the bearing Gilmore's (1914), whose page prints 1½ miles; "Alcova Quarry" is a name no source uses. The level is Galton and Carpenter's own placement, "high in equivalent strata to the Brushy Basin Member", although it rests on photographs of the site. Governs `location.locality` and `location.part: upper` on [*Alcovasaurus longispinus*](../../../genera/A/Alcovasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1914b, p. 111: "Type-locality—About 1½ miles east of Alcova, Natrona County, Wyoming" (read on the PDF).
- galton2016a, p. 189: an "unknown locality high in equivalent strata to the Brushy Basin Member of the Morrison Formation"; Fig. 1: "reed's *Stegosaurus* Quarry, Alcova, Wyoming".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Alcova Quarry".* No source read uses it.

**Open.** Nothing.
