# *Aratasaurus*

## What does the describing paper establish about the *Aratasaurus* site?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** The locality, Mina Pedra Branca near Santana do Cariri, rests on the account of the local resident who donated the slab, and the paper's comparison of the slab's shale with the mine's section supports it. It is not a matrix identification. The coordinate pair the paper prints cannot be the site and is not recorded. Governs `location.locality: Mina Pedra Branca` and the absence of `location.coordinates` on [*Aratasaurus museunacionali*](../../../genera/A/Aratasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sayão2020a, p. 2, Geological setting: "The material was donated to the Museu de Paleontologia Plácido Cidade Nuvens by a local resident of Santana do Cariri who informed that it came from the Mina Pedra Branca"; "Comparisons with the shale encompassing the fossil, and the layers of this mine are consistent with this assignment (Fig. 2)".
- sayão2020a, p. 5, Horizon and locality: "Coordinates: S 39° 42′ 37″; W 92° 08′ 05″."

**Ruled out.**
- *The printed pair, as printed or repaired.* A latitude of 39° S and a longitude of 92° W put the site in the Pacific Ocean, while the paper's own Fig. 1 places the mine beside Santana do Cariri in the Araripe Basin of Ceará. Repairing it would mean deciding which printed figure is which and supplying a latitude the paper does not print.

**Open.** A position for the mine. None of the papers read for this note prints one.
