# *Acrocanthosaurus*

## Does Stovall and Langston's Section 26 description locate the *Acrocanthosaurus atokensis* holotype?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** No. The only legal description in the describing paper is for the Arnold farm, where the paratype was found. The holotype came from the Cochran farm, which the paper places only by a bearing from the first site. Governs `location.locality: Cochran farm` and `location.coordinates: (none)` on [*Acrocanthosaurus atokensis*](../../../genera/A/Acrocanthosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- stovall1950a, p. 697: the first skeleton was "on the farm of Mr. Herman Arnold in the southeast part of the southwest quarter of Section 26, Township 4 South, Range 14 East"; "a second skeleton was found on the section line three quarters of a mile east on the land of Mr. W. P. Cochran".
- stovall1950a, pp. 698–699: "The more complete individual came from the Cochran farm and is designated as the type"; the type is M.U.O. 8-0-S9 and the paratype M.U.O. 8-0-S8.
- currie2000a, p. 208: "The holotype is OMNH 10146 (MUO 8-0-S9 of Stovall & Langston 1950) and the paratype is OMNH 10147 (MUO 8-0-S8)", from "Atoka County, Oklahoma".
- currie2000a, p. 210: NCSM 14345 "was recovered from the Antlers Formation (Trinity Group, Lower Cretaceous) of McCurtain County, Oklahoma".

**Ruled out.**
- *Section 26, T4S, R14E as the holotype's site.* It is the Arnold farm, the paratype's. The Cochran farm lies three-quarters of a mile east along a section line, and the paper gives no section number for it.
- *A McCurtain County position.* That county is the provenance of NCSM 14345, a referred specimen, not of the holotype from Atoka County.

**Open.** Whether a legal description or position exists for the Cochran farm site. The Oklahoma Museum catalogue of type specimens (Czaplewski and colleagues, 1994), which has not been read, may give one.

## Is the *Acrocanthosaurus atokensis* holotype OMNH 10146 or OMNH 10168?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** OMNH 10146, Stovall and Langston's type, MUO 8-0-S9. OMNH 10168 is the number of the parts of a referred specimen that the Oklahoma museum collected. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [OMNH 10146]` on [*Acrocanthosaurus atokensis*](../../../genera/A/Acrocanthosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- currie2000a, p. 208: "The holotype is OMNH 10146 (MUO 8-0-S9 of Stovall & Langston 1950) and the paratype is OMNH 10147 (MUO 8-0-S8)"; "Parts of another specimen … were collected by the Oklahoma Museum of Natural History and were catalogued as OMNH 10168", the rest of that specimen being NCSM 14345.
- carrano2012a, p. 222: "Holotype. OMNH 10146 (= MUO 8-0-S9), an incomplete skull and partial postcranial skeleton".
- jones2026c, p. 499: "OMNH 10146—partial skull and skeleton; OMNH 10147—partial skeleton"; molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods, Carcharodontosauridae: "OMNH 10168 Undetermined age".

**Ruled out.**
- *OMNH 10168.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give it in a column that lists the largest specimen. It is the OMNH share of NCSM 14345, which Currie and Carpenter (2000) describe as a new specimen.

**Open.** Nothing.
