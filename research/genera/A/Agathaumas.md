# *Agathaumas*

## Which formation is the *Agathaumas* holotype from?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** The Lance Formation. The value rests on White's (1973) catalogue. The papers that describe the specimen and its site use the names of their time: Cope (1872) a coal series and Hatcher (1907) the lower Laramie. Governs `location.formation: Lance` on [*Agathaumas sylvestris*](../../../genera/A/Agathaumas.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cope1872a, p. 1: the bones lay "between the thinner or lower strata of the Bitter Creek series of coal, which at this point, occupy a position of elevation and crop out high on the bluffs".
- hatcher1907a, Revision of genera, *Agathaumas*: "It comes from the lower Laramie, near Black Buttes Station, Wyoming."
- white1973a, pp. 119–120: "Upper Cretaceous, Lance Formation—North America, Wyoming, Sweetwater County, Black Buttes."

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** A stratigraphic source for the Lance at Black Buttes. No primary paper read for this note restates the Black Buttes horizon under a modern unit name. Stanton and Knowlton (1897), as quoted by Brown (1943), held that in the Bitter Creek valley the beds equivalent to the Denver and Arapahoe "cannot be separated from the Laramie" (brown1943b, Laramie and Lance). A later stratigraphic paper on the Black Buttes section that names the unit would settle whether White's Lance is a revision or a catalogue's shorthand.
