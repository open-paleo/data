# *Arkansaurus*

## Why does the *Arkansaurus* holotype stop at group rank?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** The Trinity Group, with no formation. The describing paper says the group has not been mapped to formation level where the specimen was found, and the paper describing the Holly Creek Formation's fauna names the genus only as a Trinity Group fossil. Governs `location.group: Trinity` and the empty `location.formation` on [*Arkansaurus fridayi*](../../../genera/A/Arkansaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hunt2018a, p. e1421209-2, Fig. 1: "*Arkansaurus* is known from the Trinity Group in southwestern Arkansas, which has not been mapped in this area to the formation level."
- suarez2021a, p. 2, Introduction: "In Arkansas, however, few body fossils have been recovered from the Trinity Group; a notable exception being the theropod dinosaur *Arkansaurus fridayi*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Holly Creek Formation.* Hunt and Quinn name it for other Arkansas material, "fragmentary theropod, sauropod, and ankylosaur remains noted from the Lower Cretaceous Holly Creek Formation" (hunt2018a, p. e1421209-1), not for this specimen, and Suarez and colleagues place *Arkansaurus* only in the Trinity Group.
- *Glen Rose Formation.* Hunt and Quinn write that the Trinity Group near the site "is generally correlative to the Glen Rose Formation of central Texas" (hunt2018a, p. e1421209-2), a correlation with a unit in another state rather than an assignment of the site.

**Open.** A formation-level map of the Trinity Group around the site would settle the formation.

## How is the town near the site spelled?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** Lockesburg, the town in Sevier County. Hunt and Quinn print "Locksburg". Governs `location.locality: Lockesburg` on [*Arkansaurus fridayi*](../../../genera/A/Arkansaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hunt2018a, p. e1421209-2, Type Horizon and Locality: the type was discovered in the Trinity Group "near Locksburg, Arkansas"; Fig. 1C shows the "location of Sevier County in Arkansas".

**Ruled out.**
- *Locksburg.* The describing paper's spelling of Lockesburg. That the two are one town is our reading of where the paper puts the site, not a statement of any source.

**Open.** Nothing.
