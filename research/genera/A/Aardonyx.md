# *Aardonyx*

## Do the printed Spion Kop 932 coordinates locate the *Aardonyx celestae* holotype?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** No. Both positions printed for Spion Kop 932 lie at or within about 80 m of the Sauropod Quarry, which yielded *Arcusaurus* and *Pulanesaura*, and the *Aardonyx* holotype comes from Marc's Quarry, a separate quarry just over a kilometer away on the same farm. No source read prints a position for Marc's Quarry, so the record carries none. Governs `location.coordinates: (none)` and `location.locality: Marc's Quarry, farm Spion Kop 932` on [*Aardonyx celestae*](../../../genera/A/Aardonyx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yates2010b, p. 788: the material comes from the "Marc's Quarry (MQ) bone bed on the farm Spion Kop 932, Senekal District, Free State, South Africa".
- yates2011a, p. 611, Horizon and Locality, for the *Arcusaurus* holotype: "Sauropod Quarry, Spion Kop 932, (S28°28.002′ E027°49.523′)".
- mcphee2015a, p. 2, Type locality and horizon: the *Pulanesaura* quarry "is located just over a kilometer East-North East of the holotype locality of *Aardonyx celestae*"; the Fig. 1 caption distinguishes "SQ, 'Sauropod [*Pulanesaura*] Quarry'" from "MQ, 'Marc's [*Aardonyx*] Quarry'".
- mcphee2017b, p. 445, Table 1: one row, "Heelbo (Spion Kop 932) Farm", "28°28'1.27"S 27°49'34.07"E", lists *Arcusaurus* (BP/1/6235), *Aardonyx celestae* (BP/1/6254, holotype) and *Pulanesaura* together, with fossil elevations of 1720 m for *Arcusaurus* and 1745 m for *Aardonyx*.

**Ruled out.**
- *McPhee and colleagues' (2017) table coordinate.* It is one position given for the whole farm row, which holds taxa from at least two quarries. It lies about 80 m from the Sauropod Quarry position that Yates and colleagues (2011) print, and so about a kilometer from Marc's Quarry (our computation, from the two printed values and McPhee and colleagues' 2015 distance).
- *Yates and colleagues' (2011) Sauropod Quarry coordinate.* It is printed for the *Arcusaurus* holotype's quarry, which McPhee and colleagues (2015) place over a kilometer east-northeast of Marc's Quarry.
- *The pair formerly stored, -28.466389, 27.824444.* No source read prints it. It lies about 100 m from the Sauropod Quarry position (our computation), so it is not Marc's Quarry either.

**Open.** Where Marc's Quarry lies. The describing paper's Fig. 2a is a map of South Africa only; a printed position for Marc's Quarry, or a site map giving its distance and bearing from the Sauropod Quarry precisely, would settle it.
