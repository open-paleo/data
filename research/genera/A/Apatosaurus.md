# *Apatosaurus*

## Where was the *Apatosaurus ajax* holotype found?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** At Lakes' Quarry 10 near Morrison, Colorado, in the upper Brushy Basin Member of the Morrison Formation, as Tschopp and colleagues (2015) give it. Marsh (1877) names no site. Governs `location.locality: Lakes' Quarry 10, Morrison`, `location.member: Brushy Basin` and `location.part: upper` on [*Apatosaurus ajax*](../../../genera/A/Apatosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- marsh1877c, p. 514: the skeleton "is from the Jurassic beds in the Eastern foot hills of the Rocky Mountains, but from a somewhat lower horizon than the type of *Atlantosaurus*".
- tschopp2015a, p. 6, Table 2: YPM 1860, "Lakes Quarry 10, Morrison, Gunnison County, CO, USA", "Kimmeridgian/Tithonian, Upper Brushy Basin Member, Morrison Form."
- tschopp2015a, p. 254: "Lakes' Quarry 10, Morrison, Gunnison County, Colorado (YPM 1860). Upper-most Morrison Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Como Bluff, Wyoming.* Tschopp and colleagues give Como Bluff for two other Marsh types: *Apatosaurus grandis*, named in the same 1877 paper and now *Camarasaurus grandis* ("Reed's Quarry 1, Como Bluff, Albany County, WY", p. 6, Table 2), and *Brontosaurus excelsus* ("Reed's Quarries 10 and 11, Como Bluff, Albany County, Wyoming", p. 256). No source read places YPM 1860 there.

**Open.** Nothing.
