# *Amurosaurus*

## Which unit holds the *Amurosaurus riabinini* holotype?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Udurchukan Formation at Blagoveschensk. The Chinese Yuliangzi Formation is the unit of referred material that came to this species through the *Sahaliyania* synonymy. Governs `location.formation: Udurchukan` on [*Amurosaurus riabinini*](../../../genera/A/Amurosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xing2022a, p. e2085111-1, Abstract: "*Amurosaurus riabinini* from the upper Udurchukan Formation (middle Maastrichtian) at the Blagoveschensk locality"; the Wulaga specimens "previously ascribed to *S. elunchunorum*" come from "the upper Yuliangzi Formation".
- jones2026a, p. 409: "Udurchurkan Fm., Russia; Yuliangze Fm., China".
- weishampel2004a, p. 441: "Tsagaan Svita (Yevreyskaya), Russia", "late Maastrichtian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Yuliangzi Formation.* Jones (2026) lists it beside the Udurchukan for the species. It holds the referred Wulaga material, not the holotype.
- *Tsagaan Svita.* Weishampel and colleagues (2004) give this unit. Xing and colleagues (2022), the primary read for this note, do not use it. Its relation to the Udurchukan was not checked.

**Open.** What rank the Tsagaan (Tsagayan) holds relative to the Udurchukan. A Russian stratigraphic source for the Zeya–Bureya Basin would settle it.
