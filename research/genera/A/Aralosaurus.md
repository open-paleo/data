# *Aralosaurus*

## Is the *Aralosaurus* holotype from the Beleutinsk or the Bostobe Formation?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** The Bostobe. Rozhdestvensky (1968) placed the holotype in the Beleutinsk Formation; Nessov (1997) assigns the Shakh-Shakh beds to the Bostobe and names the Beleutinsk only to exclude it, and Averianov and Sues (2007) treat Shakh-Shakh as a group of Bostobe localities. Governs `location.formation: Bostobe` on [*Aralosaurus tuberiferus*](../../../genera/A/Aralosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rozhdestvensky1968a, Genus *Aralosaurus*, Holotype: "lower half of the Upper Cretaceous (Beleutinsk Formation); Central Kazakhstan, Shakh-Shakh plateau, approx. 80 km to the north of Karmachka (from Dzhusala)" (translated; the translation does not carry the original folios).
- nessov1997a, p. 109, locality 39: "Shakh-Shakh, a mountain near the edge of a scarp, to the east of the Dzhusaly-Karsakpay route, 90 km east of Dzhusaly station", "Bostobinskian Formation, but not Beleutinskian, Santonian-Lower Campanian" (translated).
- averianov2007a, p. 93: Shakh-Shakh "denotes a series of localities in the upper Santonian to lower Campanian Bostobe Formation in the northeast Aral Sea area of Kazakhstan".

**Ruled out.**
- *Beleutinsk Formation.* The describing paper's unit, which Nessov's entry for Shakh-Shakh rejects by name. That he treats the Beleutinsk as a different unit rather than an older name for the Bostobe is our reading of "but not Beleutinskian", and on that reading it is not a variant of the Bostobe. That Nessov's Shakh-Shakh mountain and Rozhdestvensky's Shakh-Shakh plateau are one site is also our inference, since the two papers measure from different places.

**Open.** Nothing.
