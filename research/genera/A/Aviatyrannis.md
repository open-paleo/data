# *Aviatyrannis*

## Is the *Aviatyrannis jurassica* holotype IPFUB Gui Th 1 alone, or Gui Th 1 through 3?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** IPFUB Gui Th 1 alone, an almost complete right ilium. Rauhut (2003) lists Gui Th 2 and Gui Th 3 as referred material. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IPFUB Gui Th 1]` on [*Aviatyrannis jurassica*](../../../genera/A/Aviatyrannis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2003a, p. 904: "Holotype. IPFUB Gui Th 1, almost complete right ilium"; "Referred material. A fragmentary ilium IPFUB Gui Th 2 …, partial right ischium IPFUB Gui Th 3 …, several premaxillary teeth".
- jones2026c, p. 501: "IPFUB Gui Th 1 through 3—partial pelvis".

**Ruled out.**
- *IPFUB Gui Th 1 through 3.* Jones (2026) gives the three numbers as the type. That counts the two referred elements as part of it.

**Open.** Nothing.
