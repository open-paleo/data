# *Antarctosaurus*

## Is the Antarctosaurus wichmannianus holotype MACN 6904 or 6804?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** MACN 6904. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: MACN 6904` on *Antarctosaurus wichmannianus*.

**Evidence.**
- powell2003a, p. 45: "Holotype. MACN 6904.", the earliest statement of the number read for this note; wilson2003b, p. 131, prints "(MACN 6904)" the same year.
- curryrogers2014a, p. 614: "(MACN 6904; Huene, 1929…; Powell, 2003)"; mannion2019b, p. 12: "type specimen MACN 6904".
- huene1929a, p. 66: the describing work gives no catalogue number.

**Ruled out.**
- *MACN 6804.* Printed by Martinelli and Forasiepi (2004, martinelli2004a, p. 276), Curry Rogers and Forster (2004, curryrogers2004a), Mannion and Otero (2011, mannion2011b, pp. 359, 361) and later (gallina2015a, p. 493; poropat2015a, p. 97; molina-pérez2020a, p. 169), a recurring transposition. González Riga (2003) prints "MACN 6809" (gonzálezriga2003a, p. 164).

**Open.** Powell's 1986 thesis, the presumed first source of the number, has not been read.
