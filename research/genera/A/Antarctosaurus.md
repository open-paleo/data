# *Antarctosaurus*

## Is the Antarctosaurus wichmannianus holotype MACN 6904 or 6804?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** MACN 6904. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: MACN 6904` on *Antarctosaurus wichmannianus*.

**Evidence.**
- powell2003a, p. 45: "Holotype. MACN 6904.", the earliest statement of the number read for this note; wilson2003b, p. 131, prints "(MACN 6904)" the same year.
- curryrogers2014a, p. 614: "(MACN 6904; Huene, 1929…; Powell, 2003)"; mannion2019b, p. 12: "type specimen MACN 6904".
- huene1929a, p. 66: the describing work gives no catalogue number.

**Ruled out.**
- *MACN 6804.* Printed by Martinelli and Forasiepi (2004, martinelli2004a, p. 276), Curry Rogers and Forster (2004, curryrogers2004a), Mannion and Otero (2011, mannion2011b, pp. 359, 361) and later (gallina2015a, p. 493; poropat2015a, p. 97; molina-pérez2020a, p. 169), a recurring transposition. González Riga (2003) prints "MACN 6809" (gonzálezriga2003a, p. 164).

**Open.** Powell's 1986 thesis, the presumed first source of the number, has not been read.

## Which unit is Wichmann's site opposite General Roca in?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** The Anacleto Formation, on Leanza and colleagues' (2004) placement of the site from the geological mapping of Hugo and Leanza (2001). Governs `location.formation: Anacleto` on [*Antarctosaurus wichmannianus*](../../../genera/A/Antarctosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leanza2004a, p. 71: "From the 'Huayquería occidental', immediately to the west of Paso Córdova (Río Negro Province), in strata belonging to the Anacleto Formation according to the field observations of Hugo and Leanza (2001a), Wichmann (1916) reported the finding of a gracile titanosaurid dinosaur later studied by von Huene (1929) who named it Antarctosaurus wichmannianus."
- powell2003a, p. 45: "Stratigraphic position. Anacleto Member? Rio Colorado Formation. Neuquen Group (Bonaparte & Gasparini, 1979)"; p. 11 gives the same line for the General Roca locality, which Powell relocated from Wichmann's photographs.

**Ruled out.**
- *Bajo de la Carpa.* Bonaparte and Gasparini (1979) give the holotype's horizon as "Neuquén Group, Río Colorado Formation, probably Bajo de la Carpa Member" (bonaparte1979c, p. 400, translated). The mapping Leanza and colleagues rely on places the site in the Anacleto.
- *Bonaparte and Gasparini as the source of Powell's Anacleto.* Powell cites them for "Anacleto Member?", but their paper, read in English translation, does not use the name Anacleto; its only member for this species is the Bajo de la Carpa. The query and the member name are Powell's own reading or come from a source he does not cite.

**Open.** Hugo and Leanza's (2001) General Roca map sheet, the observation behind the assignment, has not been read, and Bonaparte and Gasparini's paper has been read only in translation.
