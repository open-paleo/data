# *Animantarx*

## What stage does *Animantarx* take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** Cenomanian. The describing paper places the taxon in the Mussentuchit Member and cites a radiometric date of 98.39 ± 0.07 Ma, which is Cenomanian on the current chart; its "Late Albian or Early Cenomanian" is the older chart's label for that age, as methods.md sets out for the Mussentuchit. Governs `stage: [Cenomanian]` on [*Animantarx ramaljonesi*](../../../genera/A/Animantarx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carpenter1999a: the fauna suggests "a Late Albian or Early Cenomanian age for the Mussentuchit Member. Such an age conclusion is supported by other lines of evidence, including a radiometric date of 98.39 ± 0.07 million years ago".

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian.* It is the old-chart label for a date that is Cenomanian now.
- *An Albian from Chure and colleagues' 104.46 Ma.* That date is for sediments at Dinosaur National Monument (chure2010a), a different section, not the *Animantarx* site.

**Open.** Nothing.
