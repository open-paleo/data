# *Aepisaurus*

## Is the *Aepisaurus* humerus Albian or Aptian?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** Albian, the age Le Loeuff (1993, 2013) gives. The Aptian of the older literature is Huene's, relayed from Kilian's regional synthesis. Neither age comes from a stratigraphic study of the find site read for this note. Governs `period.stage: [Albian]` on [*Aepisaurus elephantinus*](../../../genera/A/Aepisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leloeuff1993a, p. 105: "*Aepisaurus elephantinus* from the Albian of the Mont Ventoux (undetermined Sauropoda)"; section 2, Albian: described "from the Grès Verts of Mont Ventoux, near Bédoin (Vaucluse, France)".
- leloeuff2013a, p. 23: the humerus was "found at Bédoin in the surroundings of the Mont Ventoux in southern France (Vaucluse) in Albian deposits".
- huene1929a, p. 88: according to the Lower Cretaceous part of Kilian's *Lethaea geognostica*, the find comes from "Aptian sandy-glauconitic beds" (translated).
- huene1927b, Family Titanosauridae: "*Aepisaurus* (Gervais). Aptian, Southern France."

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** The Aptian. Gervais (1852) gives no age, and nothing read for this note argues against Kilian's Aptian; Le Loeuff states the Albian without discussing it. Kilian's *Lethaea geognostica*, or a modern account of the Grès verts at Bédoin, would settle which stage the beds are.
