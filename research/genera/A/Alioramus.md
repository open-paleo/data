# *Alioramus*

## Where was the *Alioramus remotus* holotype collected?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** Nogon Tsav, the locality named in the title and introduction of Kurzanov's (1976) description and by Brusatte and colleagues (2012), who put it in Bayankhongor Aimag. Kurzanov's holotype line and figure captions name a different place, Ingenii-Khobur, and the conflict is unresolved. Governs `location.locality: Nogon Tsav` and `location.region: MN-069` on [*Alioramus remotus*](../../../genera/A/Alioramus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kurzanov1976b, introduction (translated): "The Upper Cretaceous locality of Nogon-Tsav, located in the Zaltaika Gobi, 20 km NW of Mt. Ongon-Ulan-Ula".
- kurzanov1976b, holotype line (translated): "No. 3141/1, PIN AN SSSR; incomplete skull, fragments of the postcranial skeleton; Ingenii-Khoburskaya hollow, southern Mongolia"; the figure captions give "southern Mongolia, Ingenii-Khobur".
- brusatte2012b, Introduction: "The specimen was collected at the Nogon Tsav locality in Bayankhnongor Aimag".
- benton2000c, p. xxv: "Ingenii Höövör [locality]", "Ingeni Khöövör (= Ingenii Höövör)", "Khobur (= Höövör)"; p. xxvi: "Nogoon Tsav gorge [locality]". Benton lists the two as separate localities.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether Kurzanov's "Ingenii-Khoburskaya hollow" is a second site, a basin that contains Nogon Tsav, or a rendering introduced in the English translation. The Russian text (pp. 93–104), or the PIN catalogue entry for 3141/1, would settle it.

## On what grounds is *Alioramus remotus* in the lower Nemegt Formation?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** On an assumed equivalence. Kurzanov (1976) names no formation; he places the holotype in the lower, gray part of a two-part local sequence at Nogon Tsav. Brusatte and colleagues (2009), citing Kurochkin and Barsbold (2000), write that Nogon Tsav is often treated as a Nemegt equivalent but has not been correlated with it. `part: lower` therefore records Kurzanov's lower gray beds, not a level in a measured Nemegt section. Governs `location.formation: Nemegt` and `location.part: lower` on [*Alioramus remotus*](../../../genera/A/Alioramus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kurzanov1976b, introduction (translated): the deposits are "clearly subdivided into two deposits"; "The upper red-colored beds are characterized only by scattered fragments of skulls"; *Alioramus remotus* "occurs in the lower gray beds". Holotype line: "upper half of the Upper Cretaceous".
- brusatte2009b, p. 17261: "The type locality for *A. remotus*, Nogon-Tsav, is often referred to as a Nemegt equivalent, but detailed correlations have yet to be undertaken, and faunal differences suggest that Tsaagan Khushuu and Nogon-Tsav may be different in age".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** A correlation of the Nogon Tsav section with the Nemegt Formation would decide both the formation and whether the lower gray beds are lower Nemegt.
