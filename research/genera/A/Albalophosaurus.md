# *Albalophosaurus*

## Is the *Albalophosaurus yamaguchiorum* horizon Valanginian–Hauterivian or Barremian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Barremian. Sano (2015) and Sano and Yabe (2017) date the vertebrate-bearing beds of the upper Kuwajima Formation at Kaseki-kabe between a late Hauterivian brackish horizon below and the Barremian–Aptian Akaiwa Formation above. Governs `stage: [Barremian]` on [*Albalophosaurus yamaguchiorum*](../../../genera/A/Albalophosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sano2015a, p. 38: "At Kaseki-kabe in the Shiramine area, a vertebrate fossil-bearing stratum is in the upper part of the Kuwajima Formation, and is assigned to Barremian, because it is younger than the age of brackish horizon, and earlier than Barremian–Aptian Akaiwa Formation."
- sano2017a, 3.1.2 Kaseki-kabe (Fossil-bluff) in the Shiramine area: "A Barremian age is inferred for this fossil-bearing horizon, because it is younger than the age of the last brackish horizon (lower part of the Kuwajima Formation, late Hauterivian)".
- ohashi2009a, *Albalophosaurus yamaguchiorum*, Type Locality and Horizon: "uppermost Kuwajima Formation, Lower Cretaceous (Valanginian–Hauterivian)".
- jones2026a, p. 410: "Kuwajima Fm., Japan Early Cretaceous, Valanginian/ Hauterivian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Valanginian–Hauterivian.* The describing paper gives this age, and Jones (2026) follows it. Sano's later reading dates the horizon itself, and it places the late Hauterivian brackish beds below it.

**Open.** The Barremian is a bracket between two dated units, not a date on the bed.
