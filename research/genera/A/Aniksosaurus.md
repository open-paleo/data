# *Aniksosaurus*

## Is the *Aniksosaurus darwini* holotype MDT-PV 1/48 or the whole MDT-PV 1 assemblage?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** MDT-PV 1/48, an articulated right hind limb. Martínez and Novas (2006) put the rest of the MDT-PV 1 material from the site in the hypodigm. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MDT-PV 1/48]` on [*Aniksosaurus darwini*](../../../genera/A/Aniksosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martínez2006a, p. 245: "Holotype. MDT-PV 1/48. Articulated right hindlimb including femur, fibula, tibia and foot"; "Hypodigm. Associated material collected at the site where the holotype was discovered", which includes "left ulna (1/17)".
- jones2026c, p. 500: "MTD-PV 1—partial skeleton".
- molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods, Ornithomimosauria: "MTD-PV 1/17 Undetermined age", "Incomplete ulna".

**Ruled out.**
- *MTD-PV 1.* Jones (2026) cites the whole assemblage number, with the letters of the collection code transposed.
- *MTD-PV 1/17.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give the left ulna, which is part of the hypodigm. Their column lists the largest specimen, not the type.

**Open.** Nothing.
