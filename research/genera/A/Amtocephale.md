# *Amtocephale*

## Is *Amtocephale* a pachycephalosaurine?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it stays at Pachycephalosauridae without a subfamily. Schott and Evans (2016) recover it as the basal pachycephalosaurine, and two later analyses keep it in an unresolved pachycephalosaurine polytomy, but the two most recent analyses group it with *Stegoceras* and *Foraminacephale* or leave it at the base of the family. Governs `parent: Pachycephalosauridae` and the dispute on [*Amtocephale gobiensis*](../../../genera/A/Amtocephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- watabe2011a, p. 494: *Amtocephale* "nested within the Prenocephale clade (including Tylocephale)"; the authors add "we do not put much faith in this phylogenetic analysis".
- schott2016a, Fig. 27, p. 35: *Amtocephale* as the basal branch within the node labelled Pachycephalosaurinae. With *Foraminacephale* coded from the dome only, the consensus collapses "Homalocephale, Tylocephale, Foraminacephale, Amtocephale, Prenocephale, and Acrotholus into a polytomy with the Sphaerotholus–Pachycephalosaurus clade" (Fig. 28, p. 36).
- dyer2023a, Fig. 20A, p. 96: in the pachycephalosaurine polytomy of the 50% consensus; pruned from the agreement subtree (Fig. 20C).
- chinzorig2025a, Fig. 4, p. 1143: *Amtocephale* as the sister of *Stegoceras validum* plus *Foraminacephale brevis*.
- woodruff2025a, Figs 14 and 15, pp. 18–19: *Amtocephale* in the basal polytomy of Pachycephalosauridae.

**Ruled out.**
- *Pachycephalosaurinae as the parent.* Chinzorig and colleagues (2025) and Woodruff and colleagues (2025) do not recover it there, and the analyses that do leave it in a polytomy or drop it under resampling.

**Open.** Whether more complete material settles its subfamily.
