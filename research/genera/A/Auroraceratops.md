# *Auroraceratops*

## Why does the *Auroraceratops* holotype stop at the Xinminpu Group?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** Both papers that state the holotype's own occurrence stop at the group. You and colleagues (2005) give the Xinminpu Group for the Gongpoquan Basin, and Morschhauser and colleagues (2018) give the Xinminpu Group for the Gongpoquan material and the Zhonggou Formation only for the Yujingzi Basin material, about 100 km away. A Zhonggou placement for the Gongpoquan red beds is You and colleagues' (2018) working hypothesis. Governs `location.group: Xinminpu` and the empty `location.formation` on [*Auroraceratops rugosus*](../../../genera/A/Auroraceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- you2005b, p. 593: "Locality and Horizon: Gongpoquan Basin, Gansu Province, P. R. China; Xinminpu Group, late Early Cretaceous (Tang et al., 2001)."
- morschhauser2018a, p. 118: "Occurrence and Age—Xinminpu Group, Gonpoquan Basin, Mazongshan area, Gansu Province. Zhonggou Formation, Yujingzi Basin, Gansu Province. Albian."
- you2018a, p. 6: in the Gongpoquan Basin "the *Auroraceratops rugosus* holotype-bearing red beds underlie the gray beds that yielded most of the dinosaur taxa"; "we can thus tentatively correlate the two red beds based on the presence of these taxa"; "our working hypothesis is that … the red beds in both the Yujingzi and Gongpoquan basins represent the beginning of the Zhonggou Formation with an early Albian age".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether the Gongpoquan red beds are the base of the Zhonggou. You and colleagues correlate them with the Yujingzi red beds through the taxa the two share, *Auroraceratops* among them, and in the same paragraph write that "the Cretaceous beds in the Mazongshan and Jiuquan areas cannot be easily correlated" (you2018a, p. 6). A measured section or a chemostratigraphic profile in the Gongpoquan Basin would settle it. The other Gongpoquan records, [*Archaeoceratops oshimai*](../../../genera/A/Archaeoceratops.yml), [*Equijubus normani*](../../../genera/E/Equijubus.yml) and [*Gongpoquansaurus mazongshanensis*](../../../genera/G/Gongpoquansaurus.yml), carry `formation: Zhonggou` on that hypothesis.

## Does the *Auroraceratops rugosus* holotype number carry an institution prefix?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** Yes. You and colleagues (2005) give IG-2004-VD-001 as a specimen of the Institute of Geology, Chinese Academy of Geological Sciences, so the record carries the institution's prefix in front of the paper's number. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CAGS IG-2004-VD-001]` on [*Auroraceratops rugosus*](../../../genera/A/Auroraceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- you2005b, 2 Systematic Paleontology: "Holotype: A nearly complete skull and jaws. IG-2004- VD-001: Institute of Geology, Chinese Academy of Geological Sciences, Beijing, P. R. China."
- jones2026a, p. 410: "IG-2004-VD-001—skull".

**Ruled out.**
- *IG-2004-VD-001 as a full number.* Jones (2026) prints the number without the institution the paper names beside it. It is the same specimen.

**Open.** Nothing.
