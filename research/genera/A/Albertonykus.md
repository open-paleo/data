# *Albertonykus*

## Is the Albertosaurus bonebed in the Tolman Member or the Carbon Member?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** The Tolman Member. The bonebed sits in an interval less than 10 m thick at the Tolman–Carbon contact whose deposits fit either member, and Eberth and Braman (2012), who erected both members, follow Eberth and Currie (2010) in assigning it to unit 4, the Tolman. Governs `location.member: Tolman` on [*Albertonykus borealis*](../../../genera/A/Albertonykus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- eberth2012a, p. 1068: a transitional zone that "consists of a mixture of deposits that can be interpreted as characteristic of either the Tolman or Carbon members. Eberth and Currie (2010) noted this transitional interval, and placed the unique Albertosaurus bonebed within it and assigned it to "Unit 4" (Tolman Member)."
- eberth2012a, p. 1081: the Carbon Member "correlates with the part of "Unit 5" of Eberth (2004, 2010) that does not include the "Whitemud subunit"".
- larson2010a, p. 1159: "The Albertosaurus bonebed locality at the top of unit 4 of the Horseshoe Canyon Formation was deposited during the initial stages of a trend to a warmer and wetter climate that is represented in unit 5."

**Ruled out.**
- *Carbon Member.* The lithology of the interval allows it, but Eberth and Braman place the bonebed in unit 4, which they equate with the Tolman, and Larson and colleagues put it at the top of unit 4, below unit 5 (the Carbon).

**Open.** Eberth and Currie (2010), the stratigraphic study of the bonebed, would give the grounds for the unit 4 assignment; it has not been read.
