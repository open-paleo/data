# *Abrosaurus*

## Is *Abrosaurus* a macronarian?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Not established; it sits at Eusauropoda incertae sedis. No phylogenetic analysis has included it, and the published opinions range from basal macronarian to mamenchisaurid. Governs `parent: Eusauropoda`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Abrosaurus dongpoi*](../../../genera/A/Abrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- upchurch2004a, pp. 305–306: three characters "are strongly indicative of macronarian affinities. More information is needed before a definitive referral can be made", and "for the time being we regard Abrosaurus as a basal member of Macronaria".
- mannion2019a, p. 864: *Abrosaurus* has not been included in a phylogenetic analysis.

**Ruled out.**
- *Macronaria as the parent.* The referral is provisional and untested, and Wilson (2005) and Peng and colleagues (2005) place it elsewhere among eusauropods.

**Open.** Its position once its cranial material is described and analysed.

## What does Ouyang's "Lower Jurassic Xiaximiao Fm." give for *Abrosaurus dongpoi*?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** The lower Shaximiao Formation, of Middle Jurassic age. The English translation's horizon line reads "Lower Jurassic Xiaximiao Fm.", but the same paper calls the Dashanpu sauropods Middle Jurassic and dates *Abrosaurus* to "the early Middle Jurassic", so "Lower Jurassic" is not the paper's age. "Xiaximiao" matches no unit name; the later literature places the Dashanpu assemblage, *Abrosaurus* included, in the lower Shaximiao (Xiashaximiao) Formation. Reading "Xiaximiao" as a shortened Xiashaximiao is our inference; the Chinese original has not been read. Governs `location.formation: Shaximiao`, `location.part: lower` and `Middle Jurassic` in `period.name` on [*Abrosaurus dongpoi*](../../../genera/A/Abrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ouyang1989a (read in English translation, without the original folios), Locality and stratigraphic position: "Lower Jurassic Xiaximiao Fm., Dashanpu, Zigong Co., Sichuan Province"; Introduction: "three genera and species of Middle Jurassic sauropods documented from the locality of Dashanpu"; Comparison and discussion: "its age represents the early evolution of the sauropods, or the early Middle Jurassic".
- lucas2001b, p. 138: the Dashanpu assemblage "is from the lower Shaximiao (= Xiashaximiao) Formation", and the taxa listed include "*Abrosaurus dongpoensis* Ouyang".
- upchurch2004a, Table 13.1: *A. dongpoi* from the "Lower Shaximiao Formation (Sichuan), People's Republic of China", "Bathonian–Callovian".

**Ruled out.**
- *An Early Jurassic age.* "Lower Jurassic" appears only in the translated horizon line; the paper's own introduction and discussion give the Middle Jurassic.
- *"Xiaximiao" as a separate formation.* No source read uses the name for any unit, and every later source places the Dashanpu taxa in the Shaximiao.

**Open.** Whether "Lower" in the horizon line belongs to the formation name, as "Lower Shaximiao", rather than to "Jurassic". Ouyang's Chinese text would settle it.
