# *Adamantisaurus*

## Is the *Adamantisaurus mezzalirai* holotype from Flórida Paulista or from Cut 45 at Pacaembu?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** The record follows the describing paper: near Flórida Paulista, on the railway between Adamantina and Irapurú. Powell (2003) gives a titanosaur site in a railway cut, Cut Number 45 in the municipality of Pacaembu Paulista, which he also traces to Mezzalira's (1959) report of the railway finds, but he lists only "Titanosauridae indet." from it and names no specimen, so it is not tied to this holotype. Governs `location.locality: near Flórida Paulista, on the railway between Adamantina and Irapurú` on [*Adamantisaurus mezzalirai*](../../../genera/A/Adamantisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- santucci2006a, p. 60: the remains "were collected in 1958 from deposits of the Adamantina Formation near Flórida Paulista … during the construction of the railway between Adamantina and Irapurú", and "were first mentioned in the literature by Mezzalira (1959)"; p. 61, Type horizon and locality: "near the city of Flórida Paulista (railway between Adamantina and Irapurú), São Paulo, Brazil".
- powell2003a, p. 19, in the entry for Pacaembu Paulista: the railway cuts were "prospected by Dr S Mezzalira who published a summary of the Findings (Mezzalira 1959)"; Geographic Location: "Cut Number 45, km 15 (the cuts are numbered from Adamantina to Paulicea) Municipality of Pacaembu Paulista"; faunal list: "Titanosauridae indet.".
- santucci2001a, p. 311: Mezzalira stated "that the femur was collected by workers who constructed the railway branch between Adamantina and Flórida Paulista" (translated); p. 313: "in the western and central regions there are remains at Mirandópolis, Pacaembú Paulista, Guararapes", with no specimens named (translated).

**Ruled out.** Nothing; Cut 45 remains a candidate (see Open).

**Open.** Whether Powell's Cut 45 is the *Adamantisaurus* site. Both accounts trace to Mezzalira's 1959 report of the railway finds, and Santucci and Bertini's 2001 list of titanosaur localities names Pacaembú Paulista while their account of this specimen names only Flórida Paulista. Mezzalira (1959), not read, would settle which cut yielded the caudal series.
