# *Amargatitanis*

## Is the *Amargatitanis* holotype from the La Amarga Formation or the Pichi Picún Leufú Formation?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** The Puesto Antigual Member of the La Amarga Formation, at Bonaparte's 1983 site 1. The Pichi Picún Leufú is the unit written in the 1983 field notebook, which Bonaparte himself later replaced. Governs `location.formation: La Amarga`, `location.member: Puesto Antigual` and `location.locality: La Amarga (site 1)` on [*Amargatitanis macni*](../../../genera/A/Amargatitanis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- apesteguía2007a, p. 540, Horizont and locality: "As described by Cazau and written by Bonaparte in his notebook, the levels correspond to the Kimmeridgian Pichi Picún Leufú Formation (Fossa Mancini et al., 1938), the basal unit of the Fortín Nogueira Group. If true, these fossils are much older than expected"; pp. 540–541: "However, the preservation is identical to that of bones coming form the La Amarga Formation and is here considered in that context."
- apesteguía2007a, p. 534, Fig. 1 caption, on the 1983 field-book sketch of four sites: "Note that the formational names involved are the Pichi Picún Leufú and Ortíz Formations. Later, Bonaparte considered these localities to all be in the La Amarga Formation."
- gallina2016a, accepted manuscript, Fig. 1 caption: "note that Bonaparte referred to "Pichi Picún Leufú Formation", but later was considered to be La Amarga Formation"; Locality and horizon: "the Puesto Antigual Member of the La Amarga Formation, Barremian, Lower Cretaceous".
- gallina2016a, Comments on the site of provenance of MACN PV N53: the four sites lay "in an area of no more than 400 meters", and Bonaparte's list of the site 1 bones ends "All this material is labeled with the number 1".

**Ruled out.**
- *Pichi Picún Leufú Formation (Kimmeridgian), or the Ortíz Formation.* These are the names in the 1983 field notes. Apesteguía (2007) rejects them on preservation, and both he and Gallina (2016) report that Bonaparte later placed the sites in the La Amarga Formation.
- *The scapula MACN PV N34 and caudals MACN PV N51 as site 1 material.* Apesteguía's Fig. 1 caption lists "one scapula" among the site 1 bones (p. 534). Gallina excludes both because "they are not even mentioned in the field notes from 1983 and consequently they were not labeled with the number "1"", and suggests the scapula may come from Bonaparte's 1986 fieldwork.

**Open.** Nothing.
