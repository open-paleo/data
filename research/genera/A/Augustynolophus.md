# *Augustynolophus*

## Which site and which coordinate belong to the *Augustynolophus* holotype?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** LACM locality CIT 357 in the Tumey Hills, for which the paper prints no coordinates. The Panoche Hills, and the coordinate printed for LACM locality CIT 336, belong to the referred specimen LACM/CIT 2760. Governs `location.locality: LACM locality CIT 357, Tumey Hills` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Augustynolophus morrisi*](../../../genera/A/Augustynolophus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- prieto-márquez2015a, p. 229: "*Holotype.* LACM/CIT 2852"; "LACM/CIT 2852 came from LACM locality CIT 357 (no coordinates available) of the Tumey Hills, whereas LACM/CIT 2760 was collected in LACM locality CIT 336 (36º 40 21 N, 120º 42 42 E) of the Panoche Hills."

**Ruled out.**
- *The Panoche Hills, or a combined "Panoche-Tumey Hills".* The introduction places both skeletons in "the upper Maastrichtian Moreno Formation of the Panoche Hills of Fresno County" (p. 225), and the Fig. 1 caption calls LACM/CIT 2852 "a referred specimen" while placing it in the Tumey Hills (p. 226). The Occurrence paragraph ties each specimen to its own LACM locality and agrees with the holotype line (p. 229).
- *The CIT 336 coordinate.* Printed for the referred specimen's site, and with an east longitude for a California locality.

**Open.** Nothing.
