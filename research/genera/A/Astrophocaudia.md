# *Astrophocaudia*

## Where was the *Astrophocaudia* holotype found, and whose coordinate does the record carry?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** Walnut Creek, southeast of Decatur in Wise County, Texas. D'Emic (2012) takes the coordinate from Thurmond's (1974) appendix entry for the "Walnut Creek B local fauna", an assemblage that includes a sauropod, and checks it against Langston's field notes for the sauropod site. Governs `location.locality: Walnut Creek` and `location.coordinates: [33.15, -97.566667]` on [*Astrophocaudia slaughteri*](../../../genera/A/Astrophocaudia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- d'emic2012c, Locality, horizon and age: "The holotype comes from Walnut Creek, southeast of Decatur, Wise County, Texas, 33° 09′ N, 97° 34′ W (Thurmond 1974; Fig. 2)"; "Thurmond (1974, appendix) gave latitude and longitude coordinates for a "Walnut Creek B local fauna" that includes a sauropod and is in accordance with the locality given in Langston's notes."

**Ruled out.**
- *Wall, Texas.* Bilelo's (1969) report, which D'Emic rejects: "There is a city called 'Wall' in Texas, but it is several hundred kilometres from Wise County"; Langston recorded the site as "5.6 miles south and 0.9 miles west of Decatur" (d'emic2012c, Locality, horizon and age).
- *Walnut Creek B local fauna.* Thurmond's name for a faunal assemblage, under which the coordinate is listed; D'Emic names the site itself Walnut Creek.

**Open.** The coordinate is printed to the arcminute, and Thurmond (1974) has not been read.

## Is the *Astrophocaudia slaughteri* holotype one number or two?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** Two. D'Emic (2012) gives the holotype as SMU 61732 and the tooth SMU 203/73655. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [SMU 61732, SMU 203/73655]` on [*Astrophocaudia slaughteri*](../../../genera/A/Astrophocaudia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- d'emic2012c, Systematic palaeontology: "Holotype. SMU 61732 and 203/73655; a tooth (SMU 203/73655), two cervical vertebrae, fragments of dorsal vertebrae, 24 caudal vertebrae".
- jones2026b, p. 380: "SMU 61732—partial skeleton; 203/73655—tooth"; molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropods, Somphospondyli: "SMU 61732 & 203/73655".

**Ruled out.**
- *SMU 203/73655 alone.* The record once held only the tooth's number. Both reference works agree with the designating paper.

**Open.** Nothing.
