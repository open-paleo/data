# *Anabisetia*

## Is the *Anabisetia* holotype from the Huincul or the Cerro Lisandro?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** The Cerro Lisandro Formation. The first report put the Cerro Bayo Mesa specimens in the Huincul, and the describing paper, by the same two authors, moved them on a later stratigraphic study. Governs `location.formation: Cerro Lisandro` on [*Anabisetia saldiviai*](../../../genera/A/Anabisetia.yml); the form of the name is settled in [Cerro Lisandro](../../stratigraphy/c/cerro-lisandro.md).

**Evidence.**
- coria2002b, p. 503, Introduction: the bones were found "at Cerro Bayo Mesa, a locality 30 km South of Plaza Huincul"; "In a short communication (Coria and Calvo, 1996), the specimens were stratigraphically assigned to the Huincul Member (lower section of Rio Limay Formation). Current stratigraphic analysis indicates that the fossil-bearing strata belong to the Lisandro Formation (upper section of Rio Limay Group) (Garrido, 2000)."
- coria2002b, p. 503, Locality and Horizon: "Cerro Bayo Mesa, 30 km south of Plaza Huincul, Neuquen Province, Argentina. Lisandro Formation (Cenomanian) (Garrido, 2000)".
- leanza2004a, p. 69, Cerro Lisandro Formation: "At Cerro Bayo Mesa, 30 km south of Plaza Huincul, the four specimens found of the ornithopod Anabisetia saldiviai Coria and Calvo, 2002 were not associated with sauropods".

**Ruled out.**
- *Huincul (as the Huincul Member of the Río Limay Formation).* Coria and Calvo's (1996) assignment, withdrawn by the same authors in 2002 on Garrido's (2000) stratigraphic analysis.

**Open.** Garrido's (2000) analysis is an unpublished undergraduate thesis and has not been read; the 2002 paper and Leanza and colleagues (2004) are the published statements of it.
