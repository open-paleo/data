# *Atrociraptor*

## Which member holds the *Atrociraptor* holotype, and is it from the *Albertosaurus* bonebed?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** No member is recorded, and the holotype is not from the bonebed. Currie and Varricchio (2004) place it "about 5 m above the Daly Coal Seam #7" at a UTM position about 5 km west of the Royal Tyrrell Museum, and name no member. The referred teeth come from the *Albertosaurus* bonebed, a different site. Governs the empty `location.member` and `location.coordinates: [51.509137, -112.842681]` on [*Atrociraptor marshalli*](../../../genera/A/Atrociraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- currie2004a, p. 114: "The holotype was recovered from strata about 5 m above the Daly Coal Seam #7 (Gibson 1977) in the Horseshoe Canyon Formation (upper Campanian or lower Maastrichtian, Upper Cretaceous) at UTM 12U 372,125 E, 5,708,055 N, which is about 5 km west of the Royal Tyrrell Museum of Palaeontology in Drumheller, Alberta."
- larson2010a, p. 1175: "All shed teeth attributed to *A. marshalli* (Figs. 13E–13F) were collected from the *Albertosaurus* bonebed".
- eberth2012a, p. 1063: the Drumheller Member "is capped by and includes the #6–7 coal zone"; p. 1064: the Horsethief Member "is bounded below by the #6–7 coal zone of the Drumheller Member and, at its base, includes all or the upper part of the Dbz".

**Ruled out.**
- *The* Albertosaurus *bonebed.* The site of Larson and colleagues' referred teeth (larson2010a, p. 1175); Currie and Varricchio give the holotype its own position near Drumheller.

**Open.** The member. A level 5 m above the #7 coal falls, on Eberth and Braman's definitions, near the base of the Horsethief Member; that placement is our inference, and it assumes that Gibson's Daly seam #7 is the top of Eberth and Braman's #6–7 coal zone. Eberth and Braman also note that the Drumheller bentonite zone at the base of the Horsethief "sometimes straddles the contact between the Drumheller and Horsethief members" (eberth2012a, p. 1063). A paper placing TMP 1995.166.0001 in a member would settle it. The UTM position names no datum; read on NAD27 instead of NAD83, it moves about 230 m.
