# *Ahshislepelta*

## Is *Ahshislepelta* an ankylosaurid or a nodosaurid?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it stays at Ankylosauria incertae sedis. Analyses on the matrix of Arbour and Currie (2015) and on Kuzmin and colleagues' (2020) dataset recover it as a basal ankylosaurid; Rivera-Sylva and colleagues (2018) and Riguetti and colleagues (2022) recover it among nodosaurids. Others prune it for missing data. Governs `parent: Ankylosauria`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Ahshislepelta minor*](../../../genera/A/Ahshislepelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- burns2011a, p. 170: placed in Ankylosauridae, Ankylosaurinae, in the systematic header.
- arbour2015a, p. 48: "Within Ankylosauridae, Gastonia and Ahshislepelta were recovered as the most basal ankylosaurids".
- kuzmin2020a, Fig. 16, p. 118: *Ahshislepelta* as the first branch of Ankylosauridae.
- rivera-sylva2018a, Fig. 6, p. 90: *Ahshislepelta* with *Niobrarasaurus* within Nodosauridae.
- riguetti2022b, p. 20: *Patagopelta* "recovered as a Nodosaurinae (sensu Madzia et al. 2021) in a polytomy with Ashislepelta, Borealopelta, Pawpawsaurus, the Paw Paw scuteling and Texasetes"; "The overall branch support is very low".
- wiersma2018a, Results: "The high proportion of missing data for Ahshislepelta minor caused it to act as a wildcard taxon".

**Ruled out.**
- *Ankylosauridae as the parent.* The nodosaurid results of Rivera-Sylva and colleagues (2018) and Riguetti and colleagues (2022) contradict it.
- *Nodosauridae or Nodosaurinae as the parent.* The ankylosaurid results of Arbour and Currie (2015) and Kuzmin and colleagues (2020) contradict it.

**Open.** Whether more complete material settles its family.
