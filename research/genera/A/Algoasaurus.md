# *Algoasaurus*

## What number does the *Algoasaurus bauri* type carry?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** The record holds AMNH FR 5631. Broom (1904) gives no catalogue number. McPhee and colleagues (2016) report that the material Broom figured is lost and that AMNH 5631, an ungual, possibly belongs to the original assemblage. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [AMNH FR 5631]` on [*Algoasaurus bauri*](../../../genera/A/Algoasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- broom1904a, p. 445: bones found at the Port Elizabeth Brick and Tile Company's quarry at Despatch, of which "A few fragments of vertebrae and ribs have been collected by the Port Elizabeth Museum"; no number is given.
- mcphee2016b, 1.2 Previous work on Sauropoda in the Kirkwood Formation: "the material figured by Broom (1904) was lost at some point during the 20th century"; "the recent rediscovery of elements possibly pertaining to the original assemblage (SAM-PK-K1500, a caudal vertebra … and AMNH 5631, an ungual phalanx inexplicably housed at the American Natural History Museum, New York)".
- jones2026b, p. 380: "Unnumbered and AMNH 5631—fragments; SAM-PK-K1500—tail vertebra (McPhee et al., 2016)".

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 5631 as a different number.* Jones (2026) gives the same number without the collection letters FR.

**Open.** Whether AMNH 5631 is part of Broom's type material: McPhee and colleagues say only "possibly". No source read prints the FR form for this number, and none says whether SAM-PK-K1500 has an equal claim.
