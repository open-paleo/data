# *Ajnabia*

## Which museum holds the *Ajnabia* holotype?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** The Marrakech Museum of Natural History, registry entry `MHNM-AC`. The record had been filed under the bare `MHNM` key, the Musée d'histoire naturelle de Miguasha in Quebec, which Sabaj also codes MHNM; that key now carries the country suffix `MHNM-CA`, since no bare key may remain where codes collide. Governs `type_specimen.institution: MHNM-AC` on [*Ajnabia odysseus*](../../../genera/A/Ajnabia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- longrich2021a: "The holotype, MHNM.KHG.222, is accessioned in the Marrakech Museum of Natural History under the auspices of the Université Cadi Ayyad, Marrakech, Morocco."
- sabaj2020a: "MHNM: Musée d'histoire naturelle, Parc national de Miguasha" and "MHNM: Muséum d'Histoire Naturelle de Marrakech, Marrakesh".

**Ruled out.**
- *Miguasha.* Nothing ties the specimen to Quebec; the filing followed the shared code.

**Open.** Nothing.
