# *Aerosteon*

## Which formation is the *Aerosteon* holotype from, the Anacleto or the Plottier?

*2026-08-11*

**Conclusion.** The Anacleto Formation, the describing paper's assignment, kept while the Plottier reading is unconfirmed. Porfiri and colleagues (2018) report the alternative, and Ibiricu and colleagues (2025) call the horizon uncertain. The first of the two papers Porfiri and colleagues cite for the Plottier does not name it. Governs `location.formation: Anacleto` on [*Aerosteon riocoloradensis*](../../../genera/A/Aerosteon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno2008b, p. 4: "Horizon. Anacleto Formation, Neuquén Group; Santonian (ca. 84 Mya)"; the type locality is "Cañadon Amarillo … 1 km north of the Río Colorado near the southern border of Mendoza Province".
- carrano2012a, p. 222: "Cañadon Amarillo, Mendoza, Argentina; Anacleto Formation, Río Colorado Subgroup, Neuquén Group; early Campanian".
- porfiri2018a, p. 316: *Aerosteon* is "from either the upper Coniacian–lowermost Santonian Plottier Formation (Novas et al., 2013; Ezcurra and Novas, 2016) or the lower Campanian Anacleto Formation (Sereno et al., 2008)".
- ibiricu2025a, Fig. 6 caption: "Stratigraphic position of Aerosteon riocoloradensis within the upper Coniacian–lower Campanian is uncertain", citing Porfiri and colleagues (2018).

**Ruled out.**
- *Plottier Formation, on Novas and colleagues (2013).* The passage Porfiri and colleagues cite describes *Aerosteon* as "from the Santonian Cañadón Amarillo Formation of western Argentina" (novas2013a, section 2.3.2, Megaraptora) and does not mention the Plottier. "Cañadón Amarillo" is the type locality's name in the describing paper.
- *Plottier Formation, on Jones (2026).* The systematics table gives "Plottier Fm., Argentina" for *A. riocoloradensis* (jones2026c, Carcharodontosauria table) without citing a stratigraphic source; a compilation does not move the record by itself.

**Open.** Whether the holotype horizon is Plottier. Ezcurra and Novas (2016), the other work Porfiri and colleagues cite for that reading, has not been read and would settle what it argues. The type-locality line prints the position only to half a degree ("S 37.5°, W 70.5°", sereno2008b, p. 4), and no source read for this note gives a finer one.
