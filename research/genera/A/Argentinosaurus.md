# *Argentinosaurus*

## Is Weishampel's "Río Limay Formation" a rival to the Huincul Formation for *Argentinosaurus huinculensis*?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** No. Bonaparte and Coria (1993) put the holotype in the Huincul Member of the Río Limay Formation. Current Neuquén Basin usage raises the Río Limay to a subgroup and the Huincul to a formation. Governs `location.formation: Huincul` on [*Argentinosaurus huinculensis*](../../../genera/A/Argentinosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bonaparte1993a (read in English translation, without the original folios), Introduction: the discovery "comes from the Huincul Member of the Río Limay Formation (Province of Neuquén)".
- garrido2010a, Summary: "The Río Limay Subgroup comprises the Candeleros and Huincul Formations".
- weishampel2004a, p. 267: "Río Limay Formation (Neuquén), Argentina"; weishampel2004b, pp. 602–603, 63.6 Provincia de Neuquén: "Río Limay Formation (… includes Candeleros, Huincul, and Cerro Lisandro formations)".
- jones2026b, p. 383: "Huincul Fm., Argentina".

**Ruled out.**
- *Río Limay Formation.* The rank of the time. The distribution chapter of the same work lists the Huincul inside it (see [Neuquén Group subgroups](../../topics/neuquén-group-subgroups.md)).

**Open.** Nothing.
