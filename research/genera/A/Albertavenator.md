# *Albertavenator*

## Is RTMP 93.105.1 a different specimen from the *Albertavenator curriei* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. It is TMP 1993.105.0001 with the museum's older prefix and without the padding. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [TMP 1993.105.0001]` on [*Albertavenator curriei*](../../../genera/A/Albertavenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- evans2017a, Systematic paleontology, *Albertavenator curriei* gen. et sp. nov.: "HOLOTYPE: TMP 1993.105.0001, an incomplete left frontal, lacking the postorbital process."
- jones2026c, p. 508: "RTMP 93.105.1—partial frontal; RTMP 96.5.8—partial frontal".

**Ruled out.**
- *RTMP 93.105.1 as a separate number.* Jones (2026) prints the same specimen in the superseded RTMP prefix and the unpadded form (see [methods](../../methods.md)). RTMP 96.5.8 is the second frontal, which is not the type.

**Open.** Nothing.
