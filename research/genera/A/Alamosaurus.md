# *Alamosaurus*

## Is the *Alamosaurus sanjuanensis* holotype USNM 10486?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** Yes, the left scapula that Gilmore (1922) designated. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [USNM 10486]` on [*Alamosaurus sanjuanensis*](../../../genera/A/Alamosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1922a, p. 2: "Type.—Cat. No. 10,486, U. S. N. M., consists of the nearly complete left scapula."
- jones2026b, p. 385: "USNM 10486—scapula; USNM 10487—ischium (Gilmore, 1922)".
- molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropods, Saltasauridae: "SMP VP-1850 Indeterminate age", "Cervical vertebra - Fowler & Sullivan 2011"; its table of tails gives "SMP-2104".

**Ruled out.**
- *SMP VP-1850 and SMP-2104.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) cite these as large specimens from later papers. They are referred material, not the type.

**Open.** Nothing.
