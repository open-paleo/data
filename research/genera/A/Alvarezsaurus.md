# *Alvarezsaurus*

## Where was the *Alvarezsaurus* holotype collected?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** Boca del Sapo, at Neuquén city. Of the sources read, only a compilation table pairs that name with *Alvarezsaurus*; the one primary-literature statement places the find near the Universidad Nacional del Comahue building in the same city. Governs `location.locality: Boca del Sapo` on [*Alvarezsaurus calvoi*](../../../genera/A/Alvarezsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pittman2020a, p. 151, Appendix: *Alvarezsaurus calvoi*, "Boca del Sapo, Universidad Nacional del Comahue, Neuquén, Argentina"; p. 155 gives the same string for *Neuquenornis volans* and *Patagopteryx deferrariisi*, both also named by Bonaparte (1991).
- martinelli2007a, p. 12: *Alvarezsaurus* "was found near the building of the Universidad Nacional del Comahue, Neuquén City, Neuquén Province", while *Achillesaurus* came from Paso Córdova, "about 55 kilometers East of the Neuquén City".
- huene1929a, p. 12: "Near Neuquén, on the elevated south bank of the Neuquén River … At Boca del Sapo, which is a striking prominent rocky outcrop" (translated).
- powell2003a, p. 8: "'Boca del Sapo' is included in the area named 'Neuquen'".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Boca del Sapo, Universidad Nacional del Comahue" as one locality.* The string is a copy of the compilation row, which joins a site and a university. Martinelli and Vera (2007) use the university as a landmark for the find-spot, and it is also where MUCPv specimens are kept.

**Open.** Whether Boca del Sapo and the site near the university building are the same place. Both lie at Neuquén city, and Powell (2003) puts Boca del Sapo within the "Neuquén" area, but no source read gives the distance between them; treating them as one site is an inference. Bonaparte (1991), the describing paper, has not been read and would settle which name it gives.
