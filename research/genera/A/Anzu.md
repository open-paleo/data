# *Anzu*

## Which of the specimens Lamanna and colleagues describe is the *Anzu wyliei* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** CM 78000. CM 78001, MRF 319 and FMNH PR 2296 are referred specimens. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CM 78000]` on [*Anzu wyliei*](../../../genera/A/Anzu.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lamanna2014a, Systematic paleontology: "Holotype. CM 78000, a disarticulated but closely associated partial skeleton"; "Each of the three referred specimens (CM 78001, MRF 319, and FMNH PR 2296)".
- jones2026c, p. 506: "CM 78000—partial skull and skeleton; CM 78001—partial skull and skeleton; MRF 318—partial skeleton".
- molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods, Oviraptosauria: "MRF 319 Undetermined age", "Frontal bone"; its speed table for oviraptorosaurs gives "CM 78001".

**Ruled out.**
- *CM 78001 and MRF 319.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) use them as their largest or measured specimen. Both are referred specimens in the describing paper.

**Open.** Nothing.
