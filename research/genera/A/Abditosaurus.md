# *Abditosaurus*

## Which catalogue numbers make up the *Abditosaurus kuehnei* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** The 40 numbers that Vila and colleagues (2022) list element by element for the holotype: 35 MCD numbers and five MNCN numbers. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Abditosaurus kuehnei*](../../../genera/A/Abditosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- vila2022a, Supplementary Information, 1.2 Systematic palaeontology, "List of material (holotype)", entry "Present study": "Fragments of isolated teeth (MCD-6749, MCD-6751); 12 (3rd to 14th) articulated and partially preserved cervical and anterior dorsal vertebrae (MCD-9882)", through "right tibia (MNCN79837-79838-79848); right fibula (MCD-6723); distal half of left fibula with adhered calcaneum (MNCN-79847)".
- jones2026b, p. 385: "Various MNCN and MCD specimens—partial skeleton".

**Ruled out.**
- *The range MCD 6718–6751.* The record once held it. The range takes in seven numbers the list does not give the type (6726, 6727, 6737, 6739, 6740, 6741, 6743) and leaves out eight MCD numbers and all five MNCN numbers. Jones (2026) names both collections and agrees in substance with the list.

**Open.** Nothing.
