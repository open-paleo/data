# *Acantholipan*

## What stage does *Acantholipan* take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** Santonian. The paper that names the taxon dates the Pen Formation at the Los Primos locality Santonian, and it is led by the author of the 2011 report that gave the same beds as Campanian; it redescribes the same specimen and refers back to that report for the site, so the later dating is the author's revision, not a second reading to join to the first. Governs `stage: [Santonian]` on [*Acantholipan gonzalezi*](../../../genera/A/Acantholipan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rivera-sylva2018a, Horizon and locality: "The material was recovered from the Pen Formation (Upper Cretaceous, Santonian), at the Los Primos locality, south of the town of San Miguel (Rivera-Sylva et al. 2011a: Fig. 1)".
- rivera-sylva2011a, p. 373: "the marine member of the Pen Formation (Campanian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian, or Santonian–Campanian.* The Campanian is the lead author's earlier dating, which his own later paper replaces.

**Open.** Nothing.
