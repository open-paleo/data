# *Arrhinoceratops*

## Is ROM 796 the specimen Parks (1925) numbered 5135? (revisited)

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** Yes. No paper read states this specimen's renumbering, but the Royal Ontario Museum's own online collection record catalogs the *Arrhinoceratops* skull as ROM 796 (relayed by the project maintainer; the page could not be fetched here), and papers read state the same renumbering for another of Parks's "Ct." specimens: his No. 4670 Ct., the *Corythosaurus intermedius* holotype, is ROM 776. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ROM 796]` on [*Arrhinoceratops brachyops*](../../../genera/A/Arrhinoceratops.yml), confirming the 2026-08-12 entry.

**Evidence.**
- brett-surman1989a: "ROM 776 Corythosaurus casuarius, C. intermedius type, GSC 4670".
- lull1942a: "Holotype: No. 4670 Ct. R.O.M.P. Almost complete skull", the same *C. intermedius* holotype under Parks's number.
- dalman2021a: "ROM 796 (holotype)".

**Ruled out.**
- *ROM 5135.* The museum catalogs the skull as ROM 796.

**Open.** Mallon and colleagues (2014), who may state the equation for this skull, have not been read.

## Is ROM 796 the specimen Parks (1925) numbered 5135?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** Yes, on the evidence of later usage. Parks gives the type as "No. 5135 Ct." of the Royal Ontario Museum; later papers that examine the skull call the holotype ROM 796, and Jones (2026) gives the two numbers as one specimen. No paper read states when or why the number changed. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: ROM 796` on [*Arrhinoceratops brachyops*](../../../genera/A/Arrhinoceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- parks1925a, p. 7: "Type, No. 5135 Ct. Royal Ontario Museum of Palæontology."
- dalman2021a, p. 320: "adults such as ROM 796 (holotype) and ROM 1439".
- jones2026a, *Arrhinoceratops* entry: "ROM 796 (aka ROM 5135)—skull".

**Ruled out.**
- *ROM 5135.* The designating paper's number. The papers read that cite the skull use ROM 796, and only Jones's compilation equates the two.

**Open.** The renumbering itself. Mallon and colleagues' (2014) redescription of the species, which has not been read, is the likeliest paper to state it.
