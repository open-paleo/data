# *Aegyptosaurus*

## What is the *Aegyptosaurus baharijensis* type's Munich number?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** 1912 VIII 61, the number Stromer (1932) gives for the type individual. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [BSP 1912 VIII 61]` on [*Aegyptosaurus baharijensis*](../../../genera/A/Aegyptosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- stromer1932a, p. 16 (translated): "The type is the skeletal remains described above of an individual (Munich No. 1912 VIII 61)".
- jones2026b, p. 381: "1912VIII161—fragments (destroyed)"; molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropods, Titanosauria: "BSP 1912 VIII 61".

**Ruled out.**
- *1912VIII161.* Jones (2026) has one digit more than the designating paper. Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give the paper's number.

**Open.** Nothing.
