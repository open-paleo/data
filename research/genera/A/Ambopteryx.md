# *Ambopteryx*

## Is the *Ambopteryx* bed in the Haifanggou or the Tiaojishan Formation?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** The Haifanggou, as the describing paper's "stratigraphic equivalent of the Haifanggou Formation". Governs `location.formation: Haifanggou` on [*Ambopteryx longibrachium*](../../../genera/A/Ambopteryx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wang2019a, p. 256, Locality and horizon: "Near Wubaiding Village, Reshuitang Town, Lingyuan City, Liaoning Province, China; early Upper Jurassic, stratigraphic equivalent of the Haifanggou Formation, Yanliao Biota".
- wang2019a, Methods, Provenance of IVPP V24192: "Strata cropping out near Wubaiding Village were previously considered to belong to the Tiaojishan Formation. However, a few recent lithostratigraphic studies and biostratigraphic studies (mainly based on insects) have argued that the strata that are exposed in Wubaiding Village belong to the underlying Haifanggou Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tiaojishan Formation.* The earlier assignment of the Wubaiding Village strata, which Wang and colleagues (2019) report as revised by later work. Their Tiaojishan localities are elsewhere: "The Tiaojishan Formation is mainly exposed in localities near Linglongta (Jianchang County, Liaoning Province), Nanshimen, Bawanggou and Fanzhangzi (Qinglong County, Hebei Province)" (wang2019a, Methods).

**Open.** The assignment is a correlation, and the same paragraph calls the correlation between the Yanliao Biota strata "still debated". The studies Wang and colleagues cite for the reassignment, and Liu and colleagues' (2006) date behind the Tiaojishan reading, have not been read.
