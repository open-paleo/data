# *Amanzia*

## Is *Amanzia* a turiasaur?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it sits at Eusauropoda incertae sedis. Its describers recover it within Turiasauria only under implied weights; under equal weights it is the sister of Neosauropoda. Governs `parent: Eusauropoda`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Amanzia greppini*](../../../genera/A/Amanzia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- schwarz2020a, Fig. 19, p. 43: under equal weights "Amanzia is placed as the sister taxon to Neosauropoda"; under extended implied weights it is "placed within Turiasauria, as the sister taxon of Zby". Both with Bremer support of 1.

**Ruled out.**
- *Turiasauria as the parent.* The equal-weights analysis places it outside the clade.

**Open.** Its position in further analyses.
