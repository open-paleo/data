# *Adeopapposaurus*

## Which part of the Cañón del Colorado Formation is the *Adeopapposaurus* holotype from?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** The middle member. Martínez and Colombi (2011) divide the formation into lower, middle and upper members and name the middle member's pelitic facies as the source of *Adeopapposaurus*; the describing paper's "Upper Pelitic Member" belongs to an earlier scheme in which Martínez also took part. That the two names cover the same beds is our reading; the placement of the taxon is theirs. Governs `location.part: middle` on [*Adeopapposaurus mognai*](../../../genera/A/Adeopapposaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martínez2009a: PVSJ610 is from the "Upper Pelitic Member (Martínez, 2002b)", "148 m from the base".
- martínez2011b, p. 96: "Se definieron tres miembros denominados de la base al techo: miembros inferior, medio y superior" ("Three members were defined, named from base to top: lower, middle and upper members", translated); p. 101, under "Miembro medio": "Las facies pelíticas de este miembro albergan una importante paleofauna monoespecífica del dinosaurio sauropodomorfo *Adeopapposaurus mognai*" ("The pelitic facies of this member hold an important monospecific paleofauna of the sauropodomorph dinosaur *Adeopapposaurus mognai*", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: upper`.* The earlier scheme's name, which its author's later revision replaces.

**Open.** Nothing.
