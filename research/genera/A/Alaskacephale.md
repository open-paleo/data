# *Alaskacephale*

## Is the *Alaskacephale gangloffi* holotype Campanian or Maastrichtian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Maastrichtian, on the current chart. Gangloff and colleagues (2005) labelled it late Campanian from tephra dates of 71–72 Ma and 69 Ma at the nearby bone beds, read on Obradovich's 1993 time scale. On the ICS chart v2024/12 the Maastrichtian begins at 72.2 Ma. Governs `stage: [Maastrichtian]` on [*Alaskacephale gangloffi*](../../../genera/A/Alaskacephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gangloff2005a, p. 998: the bone beds 4 river km north "are interbedded with several rhyolitic tephra beds"; glass shards gave "dates ranging from 68 to 71 My with a weighted mean of 69 My"; a sanidine reanalysis "would place the lowermost bone bed between 71 and 72 My. Therefore, the pachycephalosaur squamosal is assigned a late Campanian age based on the revised time scale of Obradovich (1993)."
- sullivan2006a, *Alaskacephale gangloffi*, n. sp.: "Formation and Age—Prince Creek Formation, Colville Group; late Campanian (?early Edmontonian)".
- jones2026a, p. 413: "Prince Creek Fm., Alaska USA Late Cretaceous, Campanian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Campanian.* Gangloff and colleagues looked the stage up on the chart of their day, and Sullivan (2006) and Jones (2026) repeat it. The numbers the label rests on fall in the Maastrichtian on the current chart, so the number governs ([CONTRIBUTING](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#reading-ages-on-the-chart)).

**Open.** The holotype was found as talus, and the dates come from the bone beds 4 river km away. Giving the squamosal the bone beds' age is the describing paper's inference.
