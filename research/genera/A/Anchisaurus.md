# *Anchisaurus*

## What stages does *Anchisaurus* take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** Pliensbachian and Toarcian. Olsen and Galton (1984) date the beds that yield the Portland's anchisaurids by their palynoflorules, a statement about the horizon; the Hettangian–Sinemurian the record held had no horizon source, and the one Hettangian–Sinemurian source on the Portland entry dates a trace fossil. Governs `stage: [Pliensbachian, Toarcian]` on [*Anchisaurus polyzelus*](../../../genera/A/Anchisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- olsen1984a: the protosuchian crocodiles and anchisaurid prosauropods "are associated with palynoflorules ranging in age from Pliensbachian to Toarcian".
- galton2004a: *Anchisaurus polyzelus*, "Pliensbachian or Toarcian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hettangian–Sinemurian.* No source read dates the anchisaurid horizon so.

**Open.** A later primary dating of the upper Portland at Manchester.

## Is the *Anchisaurus polyzelus* type ACM 41/109 or YPM 1883?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** YPM 1883, the neotype the Commission designated in 2015 after setting aside all earlier type fixations for *Megadactylus polyzelus*. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [YPM 1883]` and `specimen_type: neotype` on [*Anchisaurus polyzelus*](../../../genera/A/Anchisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- iczn2015a, p. 176: the Commission "has set aside all previous type fixations for the name *Megadactylus polyzelus* Hitchcock, 1865 and designated specimen YPM1883 in the Peabody Museum of Natural History of Yale University as the neotype"; abstract: "The fragmentary and non-diagnostic holotype of *M. polyzelus* has been replaced with a diagnostic neotype".
- jones2026b, p. 373: "ACM 41/109—partial skeleton; YPM 1883—partial skull and skeleton (Galton, 2012)", both marked as types.

**Ruled out.**
- *ACM 41/109.* It is Hitchcock's original holotype, which the 2015 ruling set aside.

**Open.** Nothing.
