# *Atlasaurus*

## Is the *Atlasaurus* holotype from the Tilougguit Formation?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** No. It comes from the base of the Grès des Guettioua. The type-locality line of Monbaron and colleagues (1999) names the Tilougguit Formation, but the same paper's geological section puts the site at the base of the Guettioua Formation, and Jenny and colleagues (1981) define the Tilougguit Formation as the unit beneath the Couches rouges, whose top limestones are reworked into the base of the Grès des Guettioua at Wawmda itself. Governs `location.formation: Grès des Guettioua` on [*Atlasaurus imelakei*](../../../genera/A/Atlasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- monbaron1999a, p. 520: Wawmda "is located at the northern border of the vast synclinal basin of Tilougguit"; "The locality is at the base of a rubefied, detritic sequence in the 'Couches rouges' termed the Guettioua Formation"; and, in the systematic section, "Type locality: Wawmda,Tilougguit Formation, Bathonian-Callovian".
- jenny1981a, p. 632 (translated): "The Red Beds, which rest above the 'Calcaires corniches' or the Tilougguit Formation, are divided into three lithostratigraphic units: — the Grès des Guettioua Formation".
- jenny1981a, p. 636 (translated): "At Wawmda (416.3/162.1), blocks of summital limestones of the Tilougguit Formation reworked at the base of the Guettioua Sandstones are found".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tilougguit Formation.* The unit of the type-locality line (monbaron1999a, p. 520), which in Jenny and colleagues' scheme lies below the Couches rouges and so below the bone-bearing sandstones. Tilougguit is also the name of the syncline at whose border the site lies; that the type-locality line uses the basin's name is our reading.

**Open.** Jenny and colleagues print a Lambert position for Wawmda, "(416,3/162,1)" (jenny1981a, p. 636), with "Lambert coordinates ... taken from the same regular topographic maps" (p. 627, translated). They give it for the outcrop of reworked blocks; whether it also marks the skeleton's quarry is not stated.
