# *Alxasaurus*

## What is the Alxasaurus holotype's number?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** The larger individual of field number 88402, catalogued as IVPP RV 93001. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on *Alxasaurus elesitaiensis*: IVPP RV 93001, with field number 88402 in the notes.

**Evidence.**
- russell1993b, p. 2108: "Type IVPP 88402 (larger individual)." and "Referred specimens IVPP 88402 (smaller individual), 88501."
- zanno2010a, p. 516: "Holotype. IVPP 88402 (larger individual)"; p. 505: the elements are "not marked with the holotype accession number".
- zhao2008a, p. 321: "Holotype IVPP RV 93001 (field No 88402)", with the smaller individual and 88501 under RV 93002 and RV 93003.

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP V88402a and V88402b.* Jones (2026) gives "IVPP V88402a—jaw and skeleton; IVPP V88402b—partial skeleton" in a table (jones2026c, p. 505); Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "IVPP 88402a" (molina-pérez2019a, p. 270). No describing paper uses the suffixes or a V prefix.

**Open.** Whether the RV numbers are in general use; the describing paper and Zanno give only the field number.
