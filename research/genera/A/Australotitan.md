# *Australotitan*

## Does the printed Plevna Downs coordinate belong to the *Australotitan* site?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** No. Beeston and colleagues (2024) print one pair for Plevna Downs Station and give it for four site codes at once, EML011(a), the type site, among them, while Hocknull and colleagues (2021) spread the station's sites over a northern and a southern area. The pair is a position for the station, not for EML011(a), and the record carries no coordinates. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` and `location.locality: EML011(a), Plevna Downs Station` on [*Australotitan cooperensis*](../../../genera/A/Australotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- beeston2024a, p. 97: "EML01, EML010, EML011(a), EML013, Plevna Downs Station (26°40′52.51″S, 142°35′39.65″E), 85 km west of Eromanga, Queensland, Australia"; p. 96: the same pair for "EML010 and EML012, Plevna Downs Station".
- hocknull2021a, p. 19: "At least fourteen dinosaur bone-bearing fossil sites have so far been discovered in the southern-central Winton Formation. These sites are divided into two areas of northern and southern Plevna Downs Station"; "The type locality for type specimen of *Australotitan cooperensis* comes from the southern Plevna Downs Station, EML011(a)".

**Ruled out.**
- *[-26.681253, 142.594347].* The conversion of Beeston and colleagues' pair. The paper gives one pair per station elsewhere too: Belmont Station's pair stands for QM L1333 (beeston2024a, p. 97) and for AODL 0001 and AODL 0080 (p. 96).

**Open.** Nothing.
