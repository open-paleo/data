# *Andesaurus*

## Which bearing and distance from El Chocón does the holotype site lie at?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** Five kilometers southeast of El Chocón, as the describing paper gives it. Governs `location.locality: El Chocón, 5 km southeast` on [*Andesaurus delgadoi*](../../../genera/A/Andesaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- calvo1991a, Horizon and locality (read in English translation): "Medium sands of the lower section of the Candeleros Member of the Río Limay Formation, Neuquén Group, 5 km southeast of the El Chocón locality".
- calvo1999a, p. 22: "*Andesaurus delgadoi* (MUCPv–132) was the first dinosaur collected from the area surrounding Lake Ezquiel Ramos Mexía … The holotype was found 7 km southwest of El Chocón."

**Ruled out.** Nothing. Calvo's 1999 account, the later of the two by the same first author, gives a different distance and the opposite side of south, and nothing read for this note decides between them.

**Open.** Which account is right. The Spanish original of Calvo and Bonaparte (1991) has not been read, so a slip in the translation cannot be excluded; its Fig. 1, a sketch of the site, would also show the bearing. Mannion and Calvo's (2011) redescription of the holotype has not been read either.
