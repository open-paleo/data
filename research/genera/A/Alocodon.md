# *Alocodon*

## Which number does the *Alocodon kuehnei* holotype carry?

*2026-07-29*

**Conclusion.** P X 2. Thulborn (1973), who designated the types, numbers the holotype cheek crown P × 2 and the paratype premaxillary crown P × 1. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: IPFUB P X 2` on [*Alocodon kuehnei*](../../../genera/A/Alocodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- thulborn1973a, p. 93: "The holotype is a single cheek tooth crown (numbered P × 2). A single premaxillary tooth crown (numbered P × 1) is designated paratype."

**Ruled out.**
- *P X 1.* Mateus and Milàn's table of the Portuguese Late Jurassic dinosaurs gives the type material as "MG (ex-IPFUB P X 1), Cabaços Fm., Oxfordian, Pedrógão" (mateus2009b, p. 254, Table 1). That is the paratype's number.

**Open.** Nothing.
