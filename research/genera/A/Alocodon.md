# *Alocodon*

## Which unit holds the *Alocodon kuehnei* horizon?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Unsettled; the record keeps the Cabaços and its Callovian–Oxfordian until Barrón and Azerêdo (2003) is read. Thulborn (1973), who names the species, dates the Pedrógão marls Upper Callovian on ostracods, which at Pedrógão matches the beds below the disconformity under the Cabaços; only Mateus and Milàn's (2009) table puts the horizon in the Cabaços (see [Cabaços](../../stratigraphy/c/cabaços.md)). Governs `formation` and `stage` on [*Alocodon kuehnei*](../../../genera/A/Alocodon.yml), unchanged for now.

**Evidence.**
- thulborn1973a, p. 94: "All specimens were collected from marls of Upper Callovian age near the village of Pedrógão".
- mateus2009b, p. 254, Table 1: "Cabaços Fm., Oxfordian, Pedrógão".

**Ruled out.** Nothing yet.

**Open.** Whether the horizon lies below the disconformity; if it does, the record leaves the Cabaços.

## Which number does the *Alocodon kuehnei* holotype carry?

*2026-07-29*

**Conclusion.** P X 2. Thulborn (1973), who designated the types, numbers the holotype cheek crown P × 2 and the paratype premaxillary crown P × 1. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: IPFUB P X 2` on [*Alocodon kuehnei*](../../../genera/A/Alocodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- thulborn1973a, p. 93: "The holotype is a single cheek tooth crown (numbered P × 2). A single premaxillary tooth crown (numbered P × 1) is designated paratype."

**Ruled out.**
- *P X 1.* Mateus and Milàn's table of the Portuguese Late Jurassic dinosaurs gives the type material as "MG (ex-IPFUB P X 1), Cabaços Fm., Oxfordian, Pedrógão" (mateus2009b, p. 254, Table 1). That is the paratype's number.

**Open.** Nothing.
