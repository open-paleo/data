# *Antarctopelta*

## Has a coordinate for the holotype site been published?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** No. The site was fixed by GPS in 2009, and both papers that report the fix leave the position out. Governs `location.coordinates`, which is empty, on [*Antarctopelta oliveroi*](../../../genera/A/Antarctopelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- coria2011a, p. R10 (translated): "During the 2009 Antarctic Summer Campaign, it was possible to relocate the site of origin of these remains, which allowed, for the first time, the determination of their geographic coordinates through the use of GPS." The abstract prints no coordinate.
- soto-acuña2024a, p. 82, Locality and horizon: "The exact site was relocated in 2009, which made it possible to obtain its geolocation in geographic coordinates (Coria et al., 2011)." The paper gives no coordinate; its Fig. 1 (p. 81) marks the locality with a star on a map.

**Ruled out.**
- *A position from the Trinisaura paper.* Coria and colleagues (2013) found *Trinisaura* at Santa Marta Cove and state "GPS coordinates are kept at the Museo de La Plata and provided under request" (coria2013b, Stratigraphic horizon), so they supply no position for either specimen.

**Open.** The 2009 fix itself, which the Museo de La Plata or the authors of the 2011 abstract would hold.
