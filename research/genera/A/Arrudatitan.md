# *Arrudatitan*

## Where is the *Arrudatitan* type locality?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** The Santa Irene farm, in Cândido Rodrigues municipality near its boundary with Monte Alto, at the GPS position that Silva Junior and colleagues (2022) print from Iori (2019). The same paper corrects the site's position on the map in Santucci and Arruda-Campos (2011), which is about 9 km off. Governs `location.locality: Santa Irene farm` and `location.coordinates: [-21.328972, -48.581833]` on [*Arrudatitan maximus*](../../../genera/A/Arrudatitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- silvajunior2022b, Geological settings: the 'Santa Irene' farm, "a property located near the limits between Monte Alto and Cândido Rodrigues municipalities (Iori 2019; GPS = S21°19′44.3″/W 48°34′ 54.6″)"; "its position in their map (Santucci and Arruda-Campos 2011: Figure 1) is not correct. In fact, the outcrop is located further 9 km to northwest of the point marked at that map, within Cândido Rodrigues municipality."

**Ruled out.**
- *The point marked on Santucci and Arruda-Campos's (2011) map.* Silva Junior and colleagues, Iori among them, put the outcrop 9 km northwest of it.

**Open.** Neither Santucci and Arruda-Campos (2011), the paper naming the species, nor Iori (2019) has been read.
