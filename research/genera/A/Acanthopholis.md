# *Acanthopholis*

## Where does *Acanthopholis* sit?

*2026-10-24*

**Conclusion.** Nodosauridae incertae sedis. Pereda-Suberbiola and Barrett (1999), the only work read that places the material at family level, refer it to Nodosauridae while treating the name as a nomen dubium; the compilations that list it as a nomen dubium assign no family, which is silence rather than dissent. Governs `parent: Nodosauridae` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Acanthopholis horrida*](../../../genera/A/Acanthopholis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pereda-suberbiola1999a, p. 179, Table 1: "' Acanthopholis horridus' Huxley, 1867 | Nomen dubium, Nodosauridae indet."
- pereda-suberbiola1999a, p. 184: "The assignment of the material originally identified as 'A. horridus' to the Nodosauridae is based on tooth structure (see above), and on the occurrence of long conical spines and solid keeled scutes (Coombs and Maryanska 1990). In addition, the posteroventral orientation of the occipital condyle also suggests nodosaurid affinities".
- vickaryous2004a, Table 17.1: *A. horridus* among the nomina dubia of Ankylosauria, with no family.

**Ruled out.**
- *Ankylosauria incertae sedis.* No work read places the material outside Nodosauridae.

**Open.** Pereda-Suberbiola and Barrett also note that the teeth lack unequivocal ankylosaurian synapomorphies and that the condyle angle is atypical for nodosaurids (pp. 180, 184); a restudy could weaken the family referral.
