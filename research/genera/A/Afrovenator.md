# *Afrovenator*

## Is the Afrovenator holotype UC OBA 1 or MNN TIG1?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** The same specimen: catalogued temporarily as UC OBA 1 when described, later given as MNN TIG1, whose current form is MNBH TIG1. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id`, `institution` and the former numbers on *Afrovenator abakensis*.

**Evidence.**
- sereno1994a, p. 270, note 8: "The holotype is temporarily catalogued as UC OBA 1 at the University of Chicago."
- carrano2012a, p. 222: "Holotype. MNN TIG1 (= UC OBA 1), an incomplete skull and skeleton", the only source read for this note that states the equivalence; it gives no date or source for it.
- sabaj2020a, p. 642: "Obsolete as: MNN (fossils). Current as: MNBH (fossils)."; the form MNBH TIG1 is in print (isasmendi2024a, p. 27).

**Ruled out.**
- *UC OBA 1 as an error.* It is the number in the describing paper and most later literature (57 occurrences in 9 papers read for this note), so it stays as a former number, not a mistake.

**Open.** When the transfer to Niger took place; no source read for this note dates it.
