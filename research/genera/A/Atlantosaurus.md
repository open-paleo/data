# *Atlantosaurus*

## What stages does *Atlantosaurus montanus* take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** The Morrison Formation's range, Oxfordian to Berriasian. No source read dates Lakes' Quarry 1 more finely than the formation. Tschopp and colleagues (2015) date Lakes' Quarry 10, the site of *Apatosaurus ajax* and *Atlantosaurus immanis*, but McIntosh and Berman (1975) keep the two quarries apart, so that dating does not carry to the *A. montanus* type. Governs `stage: [Oxfordian, Kimmeridgian, Tithonian, Berriasian]` and `resolution: unit` on [*Atlantosaurus montanus*](../../../genera/A/Atlantosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mcintosh1975a: "Quarry 1, on the other hand, produced in addition to the type sacrum of *Atlantosaurus montanus*, other sauropod material, including *Camarasaurus*."
- tschopp2015a: Lakes' Quarry 10 is "Late Kimmeridgian to Early Tithonian"; *A. montanus* is not in their table.
- jones2026b: "Morrison Fm., United States *Late Jurassic, Kimmeridgian/Tithonian*", with no basis given.

**Ruled out.**
- *Kimmeridgian–Tithonian.* It rests on a compilation alone.
- *Quarry 10's Late Kimmeridgian to Early Tithonian.* It dates another site.

**Open.** A dating of Lakes' Quarry 1.

## Which Morrison quarry produced the *Atlantosaurus montanus* type?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** Quarry 1 at Morrison, Colorado. McIntosh and Berman (1975) separate it from Quarry 10, which yielded the types of *Apatosaurus ajax* and *Atlantosaurus immanis*; material from the two quarries reached Marsh in one accession. Governs `location.locality: Lakes' Quarry 1, Morrison` on [*Atlantosaurus montanus*](../../../genera/A/Atlantosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mcintosh1975a, p. 197: "accession 1002 actually came from two different quarries, nos. 1 and 10".
- mcintosh1975a, p. 198: "The total yield from quarry 10 was two very large, partial skeletons, the type of *A. ajax,* YPM 1860, and the type of *Atlantosaurus immanis,* YPM 1840"; "Quarry 1, on the other hand, produced in addition to the type sacrum of *Atlantosaurus montanus,* other sauropod material, including *Camarasaurus.*"

**Ruled out.**
- *Quarry 10.* The source of the *A. ajax* and *A. immanis* types (mcintosh1975a, p. 198). The cranial fragment that Marsh (1896) figured as *A. montanus* came in the mixed accession, and "we will probably never know whether it was properly assigned to YPM 1860, or if Marsh later learned ... that it came from quarry 1" (p. 198). That doubt concerns the fragment; the shipment boxes "identified its contents as well as the quarry from which each specimen came", and the fragment was the item they did not cover.

**Open.** Nothing.
