# *Amygdalodon*

## Is Cañadón Puelman a different type locality from the Pampa de Agnía slope?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** No. Cabrera (1947) puts the site on the eastern slope of the Pampa de Agnía range, at the headwaters of a tributary of Cañadón Puelman, so the "Cañadón Puelman" of Rauhut (2003) and Pol and colleagues (2022) names the drainage of the same site. Governs `location.locality: Eastern slope of the Pampa de Agnía range` on [*Amygdalodon patagonicus*](../../../genera/A/Amygdalodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cabrera1947a, pp. 1–2: the fossil "was on the eastern slope of the Pampa de Agnía mountain range, to the southwest of Cerro Camerón and about 8 kilometers to the west-southwest of the estancia of García Prieto (formerly estancia Gallina), at the headwaters of a right-bank tributary of Cañadón Puelman" (translated); p. 4, Locality: "Eastern slope of the Pampa de Agnía mountain range" (translated).
- rauhut2003c, p. 179, Type locality and horizon: "Cañadón Puelman, Pampa de Agnia, Chubut, Argentina".
- pol2022a, p. 135, Locality and Age: "Cañadón Puelman, southwest of Cerro Carnerero, Sierra del Cerro Negro, Chubut Province, Argentina".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cañadón Puelman as a unit.* The name is also stratigraphic: Lesta and Ferello's Cañadón Puelman Formation was one of the units of their Lonco Trapial Group (nullo1975a, p. 142), and Rauhut writes "Lonco Trapial (= Cañadón Puelman) Formation" for the volcanic unit above the Cerro Carnerero (rauhut2003c, p. 174). In the type-locality lines it is a place.

**Open.** Two names in the sources do not match. Cabrera's site is southwest of "cerro Camerón" (so printed, p. 1) and Pol and colleagues' is southwest of Cerro Carnerero; and Pol and colleagues name the range Sierra del Cerro Negro where Cabrera and Rauhut name Pampa de Agnía. Whether these are the same hill and the same range was not checked against a map or the regional geology.
