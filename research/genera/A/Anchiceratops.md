# *Anchiceratops*

## Where was the *Anchiceratops ornatus* holotype collected?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** Seven miles below Tolman Ferry on the Red Deer River, as Brown (1914) gives it for AMNH 5251. The site 12 miles northwest of Morrin is the type locality of *Anchiceratops longirostris*. Governs `location.locality: 7 miles below Tolman Ferry, Red Deer River` on [*Anchiceratops ornatus*](../../../genera/A/Anchiceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown1914a, p. 539: "Type of genus and species No. 5251 … Horizon and locality. Edmonton formation, 50 ft. above river, 7 miles below Tolman Ferry, Red Deer River, Alberta, Canada."
- sternberg1929b, p. 34: the 1924 skull, Cat. No. 8535, came from "sec. 8, tp. 32, range 21, W. 4th prin. mer., on the west side of and 70 feet above Red Deer river. This locality is about 12 miles northwest of Morrin, Alberta, and approximately the same as that from which Brown collected his types."

**Ruled out.**
- *"12 miles north of Morrin (70 ft level)".* That is Sternberg's site and height for No. 8535, the type of *A. longirostris*, not Brown's for AMNH 5251. Sternberg calls the two sites only "approximately the same", and the heights differ: 70 feet against Brown's 50 feet.

**Open.** The member. Eberth and Braman (2012) divide the Horseshoe Canyon Formation into seven members, among them a Morrin and a Tolman (eberth2012a, p. 1053, abstract), both named for places that also appear in the two locality descriptions above. A town in a locality is not a member assignment. Fowler and Freedman Fowler (2020) give the species a range, "Drumheller to Morrin Mbr" (fowler2020a, p. 34, Fig. 10) and "co-occurring in the Horsethief and Morrin members" with *Arrhinoceratops* (p. 39), which does not place AMNH 5251. Mallon and colleagues' (2011) study of *Anchiceratops* skull variation has not been read and may place the holotype.
