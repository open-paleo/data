# *Aristosuchus*

## Was the *Aristosuchus* holotype found at Brook?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** No site is known, and the record names only the Isle of Wight. BMNH R178 was long confused with *Calamospondylus oweni*, which Fox found at Brook, but Naish (2002) shows that they are different specimens and that the find spot of BMNH R178 is unknown. Governs the empty `location.locality` and `location.region: GB-IOW` on [*Aristosuchus pusillus*](../../../genera/A/Aristosuchus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- naish2002a, p. 154: "*C. oweni* was discovered by the Reverend William Fox at Brook, Isle of Wight"; it "has been universally confused with *Aristosuchus pusillus*".
- naish2002a, p. 161: Fox "discovered a second specimen on the Isle of Wight (the exact location is unknown)"; "This specimen is BMNH R178 and Fox made it clear in his correspondence to Owen that it was different from the holotype of *Calamospondylus oweni*."

**Ruled out.**
- *Brook.* The locality of *Calamospondylus oweni*. It reaches BMNH R178 only through the identification of the two specimens that Naish rejects: "several authors assumed that Fox's *Calamospondylus oweni* was a reference to the same specimen as BMNH R 178" (naish2002a, p. 161).

**Open.** Naish's map caption says both specimens "were both probably collected from the southwest side of the island" (naish2002a, p. 153, Fig. 1), which names no site.
