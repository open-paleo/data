# *Alectrosaurus*

## Which of Gilmore's two cotypes is the *Alectrosaurus olseni* type?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** AMNH 6554, the hind limb, which Mader and Bradley (1989) chose as lectotype. Gilmore (1933) founded the species on two cotypes. Mader and Bradley show that the other one, the AMNH 6368 forelimb material, is not tyrannosaurid. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [AMNH 6554]` and `specimen_type: lectotype` on [*Alectrosaurus olseni*](../../../genera/A/Alectrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1933a, p. 34: "COTYPES.—No. 6368, A.M.N.H., consists of right humerus, first phalanx of digit II and ungual of digit I of manus; No. 6554, A.M.N.H., consists of a right femur (lacking proximal end), tibia, fibula, astragalus, calcaneum and pes".
- mader1989a, p. 41: "One syntype, which includes a virtually complete hind limb, was correctly diagnosed by Gilmore as a tyrannosaur and is recognized here as the lectotype of the species"; the second syntype's forelimb elements "are all tentatively referred to the Segnosauridae".
- jones2026c, p. 501: "AMNH 6554—partial skeleton".

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 6368 as holotype.* The record once held it. It is Gilmore's other cotype, which Mader and Bradley exclude from the genus. Jones (2026) agrees with the lectotype.

**Open.** Nothing.
