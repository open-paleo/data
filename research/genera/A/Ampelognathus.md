# *Ampelognathus*

## Where does *Ampelognathus* sit?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it sits at Ornithopoda incertae sedis. Its describers recover it outside Iguanodontia, as the sister of *Thescelosaurus* plus Iguanodontia, with low support; Czepiński and Madzia (2025) recover it within Rhabdodontomorpha under implied weighting only. Governs `parent: Ornithopoda`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Ampelognathus coheni*](../../../genera/A/Ampelognathus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tykoski2023a, p. e2257238-4: "Ampelognathus coheni was recovered within Ornithopoda, as the sister taxon to the clade consisting of Thescelosaurus and Iguanodontia"; "Support for the position of A. coheni is low, and it takes only one additional step for Ampelognathus to become a 'wildcard' taxon".
- czepiński2025a, p. 5: "All runs found Rhabdodontomorpha composed of a basal polytomy comprising Transylvanosaurus platycephalus, the 'Quarante rhabdodontomorph', Ampelognathus coheni, and monophyletic tenontosaurids and rhabdodontoids"; the equal-weights analysis "failed to reconstruct a monophyletic Rhabdodontomorpha".
- fonseca2024a: *Ampelognathus* "not included in this study as they were described after the data collection was completed".

**Ruled out.**
- *Rhabdodontomorpha as the parent.* Only the weighted analyses of Czepiński and Madzia (2025) recover it there; its describers place it outside Iguanodontia.
- *Iguanodontia as the parent.* Its describers place it outside the clade.

**Open.** Whether further analyses converge.

## Which member of the Lewisville Formation holds the *Ampelognathus* holotype?

*2026-08-12*

**Conclusion.** Unknown: the describing paper narrows it to the Arlington or the Tarrant Member and does not choose. Governs the absence of `location.member` on [*Ampelognathus coheni*](../../../genera/A/Ampelognathus.yml), with `location.formation: Lewisville`.

**Evidence.**
- tykoski2023a, p. e2257238-2: "The exact stratigraphic level of the spillway exposures within the Lewisville Formation is difficult to determine because the small, isolated rock exposures and increasing urban cover in the region complicate reconstruction of local Lewisville Formation stratigraphy."
- tykoski2023a, p. e2257238-2: "The mix of marine muds, intervals of sandier lenses and layers, and occasional fossil wood, suggests the spillway site is in either the Arlington Member or Tarrant Member of the Lewisville Formation (Denne et al., 2016)."
- tykoski2023a, p. e2257238-3, Fig. 1 caption: "Bracket and question mark is estimated stratigraphic level of DMNH 2021-05-02."

**Ruled out.** Nothing; neither member is excluded.

**Open.** The member. The holotype "came from almost the same stratigraphic level and within 100 meters of the holotype of the enantiornithine bird *Flexomornis howei*" (tykoski2023a, p. e2257238-2), so Tykoski and Fiorillo's (2010) description of that bird, not read for this note, may fix the level. A local section placing the spillway sandy zone within Denne and colleagues' (2016) members would settle it.
