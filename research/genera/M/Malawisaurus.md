# *Malawisaurus*

## Who named *Malawisaurus dixeyi*, and who erected *Malawisaurus*?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Haughton (1928) named the species, as *Gigantosaurus dixeyi*; Jacobs and colleagues (1993) erected *Malawisaurus* for it and moved the species there. Governs the species `erected_in: haughton1928a` and the genus-level `erected_in: jacobs1993a` on [*Malawisaurus dixeyi*](../../../genera/M/Malawisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- haughton1928a, p. 70: "*Gigantosaurus dixeyi*, sp. n."; the bones "are numbered 7405 in the South African Museum Collection".
- haughton1928a, p. 72: "it is proposed therefore to make them the type of a new species, which can be called *G. dixeyi*, sp. nov."
- jacobs1993a, p. 524: "a new genus is erected to accommodate the titanosaurid species from Malawi"; "Genus MALAWISAURUS gen. nov."
- jacobs1993a, p. 525: "*Malawisaurus dixeyi* (Haughton, 1928) comb. nov.", with the synonymy entry "*Gigantosaurus dixeyi sp. nov.*, Haughton, p. 70".

**Ruled out.**
- *Jacobs and colleagues (1993) as the species' authority.* Their p. 525 heading is a new combination of Haughton's species, not a new species, and keeps Haughton's name and date in parentheses.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which specimen is the *Malawisaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** SAM 7405, the anterior caudal vertebra Haughton (1928) illustrated, as Jacobs and colleagues (1993) fix it. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [SAM 7405]` on [*Malawisaurus dixeyi*](../../../genera/M/Malawisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jacobs1993a, p. 525: "The type is taken to be the anterior caudal vertebra illustrated by Haughton (1928) and catalogued as South African Museum no. 7405. The remainder of the material included by Haughton with the type ... is considered topotypic."

**Ruled out.**
- *MAI-201.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "MAI-201" in a table of running speeds (molina-pérez2020a, p. 169), a different number from the type's, used for the estimate.

**Open.** Nothing.
