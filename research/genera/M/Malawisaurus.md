# *Malawisaurus*

## Which specimen is the *Malawisaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** SAM 7405, the anterior caudal vertebra Haughton (1928) illustrated, as Jacobs and colleagues (1993) fix it. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [SAM 7405]` on [*Malawisaurus dixeyi*](../../../genera/M/Malawisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jacobs1993a, p. 525: "The type is taken to be the anterior caudal vertebra illustrated by Haughton (1928) and catalogued as South African Museum no. 7405. The remainder of the material included by Haughton with the type ... is considered topotypic."

**Ruled out.**
- *MAI-201.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "MAI-201" in a table of running speeds (molina-pérez2020a, p. 169), a different number from the type's, used for the estimate.

**Open.** Nothing.

- `molina-pérez2020a`: Molina-Pérez, R.; Larramendi, A. (2020). Dinosaur Facts and Figures: The Sauropods and Other Sauropodomorphs. Princeton University Press.
