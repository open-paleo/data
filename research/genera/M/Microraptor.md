# *Microraptor*

## Which specimen is the *Microraptor zhaoianus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** IVPP V12330, from the describing paper. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IVPP V12330]` on [*Microraptor zhaoianus*](../../../genera/M/Microraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2000c, p. 705: "Holotype. IVPP (The Institute of Vertebrate Paleontology and Paleonanthropology) V 12330, a partial skeleton preserved in main (Fig. 1) and counter slabs."

**Ruled out.**
- *NGMC 00-12-A.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "Microraptor zhaoianus (juvenile) NGMC 00-12-A" in a table of running speeds (molina-pérez2019a, p. 178), a different specimen.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `xu2000c`: Xu, X.; Zhou, Z.; Wang, X. (2000). The smallest known non-avian theropod dinosaur. *Nature* 408(6813): 705-708. doi:10.1038/35047056
- `molina-pérez2019a`: Molina-Pérez, R.; Larramendi, A. (2019). Dinosaur Facts and Figures: The Theropods and Other Dinosauriformes. Princeton University Press.
