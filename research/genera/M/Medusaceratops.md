# *Medusaceratops*

## Is the Mansfield bonebed in the upper or the lower part of the Judith River Formation?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** It is in the McClelland Ferry Member, and the record gives no `part`. Ryan and colleagues (2010) put the bonebed in "the upper part of the Judith River Formation"; Chiba and colleagues (2018), with Ryan among the authors, place it after the formation's revision by Rogers and colleagues (2016) in the McClelland Ferry Member, in beds "commonly referred to the lower Judith River Formation". Governs `location.member: McClelland Ferry` and the empty `location.part` on [*Medusaceratops lokii*](../../../genera/M/Medusaceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- chiba2018a, Systematic paleontology, Locality and horizon: "The bonebed layer is located ~10 m above the top of the Marker A coal (Goodwin and Deino, 1989) in the McClelland Ferry Member of the Judith River Formation (Rogers et al., 2016)."
- chiba2018a, Geological context: "the middle Campanian alluvial deposits, commonly referred to the lower Judith River Formation (Goodwin and Deino, 1989), crop out extensively"; "the beds exposed at Kennedy Coulee correlate to the McClelland Ferry Member to the south".
- ryan2010a, p. 182: the bonebed "occurs in the upper part of the Judith River Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: upper`.* Ryan and colleagues' placement predates the member scheme and is replaced by the 2018 paper that shares an author.
- *`part: lower`.* "Lower" describes the bed's place in the formation, while the record's finest unit is the member, so a `part` would assert a position inside the McClelland Ferry that no source gives.

**Open.** Nothing.
