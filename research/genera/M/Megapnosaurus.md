# *Megapnosaurus*

## Which stages does the *Megapnosaurus rhodesiensis* holotype take?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Its unit's epoch, with no stage. Raath (1969) places the holotype near the top of the formation and dates the deposits Upper Triassic by Bond's correlation with the Red Beds; later works give the Early Jurassic after Raath. Governs the absent `stage` on [*Megapnosaurus rhodesiensis*](../../../genera/M/Megapnosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.** None beyond the unit entry.

**Ruled out.** Nothing beyond the [Forest Sandstone](../../stratigraphy/f/forest-sandstone.md) entry.

**Open.** Nothing.
