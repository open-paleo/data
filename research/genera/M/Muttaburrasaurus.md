# *Muttaburrasaurus*

## What stages does *Muttaburrasaurus* take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** Albian and Cenomanian. The describing paper gives the Mackunda Formation as Albian, and Herne and colleagues (2026) place the holotype in the "mid-Cenomanian of the Mackunda Formation"; the two readings are joined. Their formal entry's "Cenomanian–lower Turonian" runs from the holotype's maximum depositional age to the overlying Winton Formation's maximum age, so its Turonian end does not date this horizon, as the [Mackunda](../../../stratigraphy/m/mackunda.yml) entry records. Governs `stage: [Albian, Cenomanian]` on [*Muttaburrasaurus langdoni*](../../../genera/M/Muttaburrasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bartholomai1981a: "HORIZON: Mackunda Fm. AGE: Albian (Vine and Day, 1965)."
- herne2026a: the holotype in the "mid-Cenomanian of the Mackunda Formation"; "the age of QML1817 (96.3 ± 8.6 Ma) represents the maximum depositional age of the *Muttaburrasaurus langdoni* holotype"; "The pooled detrital zircon age for the Winton Formation returned MLA of 93.5 ± 1.2 Ma".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Turonian.* It is the upper end of a bracket set by the overlying formation's maximum age.

**Open.** Tucker and colleagues (2016), whose upper Albian dating Herne and colleagues report, have not been read.
