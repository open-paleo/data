# *Marshosaurus*

## Does Madsen's printed longitude for the *Marshosaurus* type locality read 11° or 110°?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** 110° 41′ 15″ W. The printed page reads "long. 11° 41' 15" W", with a digit dropped in typesetting: 11° W is in the Atlantic, and the same line places the quarry in east central Utah with a Salt Lake meridian survey description. The record converts lat. 39° 19′ 00″ N, long. 110° 41′ 15″ W. Governs `location.coordinates: [39.316667, -110.6875]` on [*Marshosaurus bicentesimus*](../../../genera/M/Marshosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- madsen1976a, p. 52, Type locality: "Cleveland-Lloyd Dinosaur Quarry, east central Utah: NE¼NE¼ sec. 28, T. 17 S., R. 11 E., S.L.B.M., lat. 39° 19' 00" N., long. 11° 41' 15" W." (page image).

**Ruled out.**
- *Deleting the pair as unprintable, or as a damaged copy of an unknown value.* Only the hundreds digit is missing, and the minutes and seconds are printed in full, so the correction does not require choosing among printed digits.

**Open.** Nothing.
