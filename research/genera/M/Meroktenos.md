# *Meroktenos*

## Is the Meroktenos holotype from the lower or the upper Elliot Formation?

*2026-08-30*

**Conclusion.** The lower Elliot Formation. Gauffre (1993) placed the material in the upper Elliot; Peyre de Fabrègues and Allain (2016) trace it to a 1959 collection that Costedoat's (1962) thesis places at the center of the Lower Red Beds, below the base of the upper Elliot. Governs `location.part: lower` and `stage: [Norian, Rhaetian]` on [*Meroktenos thabanensis*](../../../genera/M/Meroktenos.yml).

**Evidence.**
- peyredefabrègues2016a, p. 4: "Both lower and upper Elliot fossil-bearing rocks crop out in the Thabana Morena area"; the remains were collected "at the center of the Lower Red Beds, well below the *Tritylodon* Acme Zone" (quoting Costedoat 1962), a zone "at the base of the upper Elliot Formation", and "the Lower Red Beds are now considered to be the equivalent of the lower Elliot Formation".
- peyredefabrègues2016a, p. 6: "Type horizon. Lower Elliot Formation, Upper Triassic."

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper Elliot Formation, Early Jurassic.* "In the original publication, Gauffre (1993a) affirmed that the material is from the upper Elliot Formation" (peyredefabrègues2016a, p. 4), and later compilations followed it. Gauffre himself moved it to the Late Triassic in 1996, on Costedoat's evidence (same page).
- *A site 4–5 km south of Thabana Morena village.* Gauffre gives this, but Peyre de Fabrègues and Allain still treat the site as unknown: "although Gauffre (1993a:648) mentions a location ''4–5 km south of Thabana Morena village,'' remains unknown" (p. 6). The locality stays at Thabana Morena.
- *"Thabana Morena (zone A/6)" as the locality.* The parenthesis is a zone of the scheme Paul Ellenberger set up in 1970 "for the Molteno, Elliot, and Clarens formations in Lesotho", in which he placed the Maphutseng bone bed "in Zone A/4" (peyredefabrègues2019a, p. e1732996-4). It is a stratigraphic position, not part of the place name.

**Open.** Whether this material was ever assigned to zone A/6. No paper read assigns it a zone; Ellenberger (1970) or Gauffre (1993) would show it.
