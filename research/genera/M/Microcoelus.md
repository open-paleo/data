# *Microcoelus*

## What stage does *Microcoelus* take?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Santonian, the Bajo de la Carpa Formation's range. Powell's Campanian sits in the holotype's entry but dates the unit as a whole, from geological maps, and the [Bajo de la Carpa](../../stratigraphy/b/bajo-de-la-carpa.md) note has since dated the formation Santonian only. Governs `stage: [Santonian]` on [*Microcoelus patagonicus*](../../../genera/M/Microcoelus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- powell2003a, *Microcoelus patagonicus*: "Holotype. MLP Ly 23. An incomplete anterior dorsal vertebra"; "Stratigraphic position. Rio Colorado Formation, Bajo de La Carpa Member? (Neuquen Group). Age. Late Cretaceous. Campanian (Uliana & Dellapa 1981)."
- powell2003a, for a Río Colorado locality south of Catriel: "Probably Rio Colorado Formation, according to the geological maps of Uliana and Dellape (1981)"; "Campanian (Uliana & Dellape 1981)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian.* It is the unit's age from 1981 mapping, not evidence from the holotype's horizon, and it is the dating the Bajo de la Carpa note rules out.

**Open.** Nothing.
