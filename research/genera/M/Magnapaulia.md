# *Magnapaulia*

## Is IGM 5845 the *Magnapaulia* holotype?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** No. The designating paper gives LACM 17715 as the holotype. Ramírez-Velasco and colleagues (2021) list "IGM 5845 (holotype)", but Ramírez-Velasco (2022) labels the skeletal composition "17715 (partially IGM 5845)", so IGM 5845 appears to be part of the same specimen under a Mexican number rather than a rival type. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: LACM 17715` on [*Magnapaulia laticaudus*](../../../genera/M/Magnapaulia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- prieto-márquez2012b, p. 3: "Holotype. LACM 17715, consisting of left premaxilla, left maxilla, left jugal, partial atlas"; p. 30: the description rests on "first hand examination of the holotype and referred materials of this species housed at the LACM in Los Angeles (California, USA) and the Instituto de Geología".
- ramírez-velasco2022a, p. 3, Fig. 2 caption: "Magnapaulia laticaudus skeletal composition: LACM 17702, 17705, 17707, 17712 (partially IGM 5844), 17715 (partially IGM 5845)".

**Ruled out.**
- *IGM 5845 as the holotype.* Ramírez-Velasco and colleagues write "IGM 5845 (holotype), 5843, 5844 y 5846" (ramírez-velasco2021b, p. 2), against the designating paper; the 2022 caption treats IGM 5845 as part of LACM 17715.

**Open.** Which elements of LACM 17715 carry the IGM number, and whether `specimen_id` should list IGM 5845 beside it. The 2022 caption is the only source read that links the two numbers.
