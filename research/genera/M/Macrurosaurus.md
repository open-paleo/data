# *Macrurosaurus*

## Which of the two syntype localities does the *Macrurosaurus* type take?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** Coldham Common, Barnwell. Seeley's syntypes are two series from two phosphate workings a few miles apart. CAMSM B55630, the specimen the record holds, is the first vertebra of the Barnwell series, which Le Loeuff (1993) proposes as the lectotype. Governs `location.locality: Coldham Common, Barnwell` on [*Macrurosaurus semnus*](../../../genera/M/Macrurosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- seeley1876a, p. 440: the Woodwardian Museum acquired "a series of some 25 associated and successive caudal vertebræ, found at one of the deeper phosphatite washings on Coldham Common, Barnwell"; "the Rev. W. Stokes Shaw … obtained from a similar working at Barton, a locality a few miles westward, another associated series of 15 smaller vertebrae".
- leloeuff1993a, p. 107: the first series is "from the Cambridge Greensand of Coldham Common, Barnwell … (B55630 to B55652)", and the second, "B55653 to B55654", was obtained "from Barton"; p. 108: "the first procoelous caudal vertebra of the first series should be regarded as the lectotype. Therefore, the name *M. semnus* should apply to this vertebra (B55630)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barton.* It is the locality of the second, Shaw, series (seeley1876a, p. 440), not of B55630.
- *Both series as one individual, and so one site.* Seeley thought the two series "not improbably part of the same individual"; Le Loeuff finds this "highly unlikely" and the first series "itself is a composite one" (leloeuff1993a, p. 107).

**Open.** Whether Le Loeuff's "should be regarded as the lectotype" is a designation; the record calls B55630 the holotype.
