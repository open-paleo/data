# *Miragaia*

## Where does *Miragaia* sit?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** Stegosauridae. Its describers and Costa and Mateus (2019) place it in Dacentrurinae as sister to *Dacentrurus*, and the proposed synonymy with *Dacentrurus armatus* would put it there too; Raven and Maidment (2017) and Raven and colleagues (2023) recover it closer to *Stegosaurus* than to *Dacentrurus*, which excludes it from the stem-defined Dacentrurinae. Every hypothesis places it in Stegosauridae. Governs `parent: Stegosauridae` on [*Miragaia longicollum*](../../../genera/M/Miragaia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mateus2009a: "Miragaia is resolved as the sister taxon to D. armatus and they are united in the new stem-based clade Dacentrurinae, defined as all stegosaurs more closely related to D. armatus (Owen 1875) than to S. armatus Marsh 1877."
- raven2017a, Conclusions: "'Dacentrurus' longicollum is more closely related to 'Stegosaurus' mjosi than to any other stegosaur, warranting the re-erection of the generic names Miragaia longicollum and Hesperosaurus mjosi for these taxa."
- raven2023a, Results, p. 9: sister-taxon relationships between "Huayangosaurus and Isaberrysaura, Loricatosaurus and Alcovasaurus, and Adratiklit and Miragaia"; Fig. 1, p. 10 (figure reading): *Dacentrurus* branches outside the node joining *Miragaia* + *Adratiklit* with the stegosaurines.
- sánchez-fenollosa2024a, Abstract: "'Mi. longicollum' is proposed as a subjective junior synonym of D. armatus."

**Ruled out.**
- *Dacentrurinae.* It holds under the describers' and Costa and Mateus's results and under the synonymy, but not under the two Raven analyses.

**Open.** Whether *Miragaia* is valid, which the record keeps as disputed.
