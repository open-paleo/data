# *Mochlodon*

## Who named *Mochlodon suessi*, and what is its lectotype?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** The species dates from Bunzel's 1871 memoir, as *Iguanodon suessii*; Seeley (1881) erected *Mochlodon* for it, so the genus keeps Seeley's date. The lectotype is the right dentary, PIUW 2349/2, alone: Seeley refers a parietal and a scapula only as probably *Mochlodon*. Governs `erected_in: seeley1881a` on [*Mochlodon*](../../../genera/M/Mochlodon.yml), and `erected_in: bunzel1871a` and the lectotype's `material` on *Mochlodon suessi*.

**Evidence.**
- ősi2012a: "*Mochlodon suessi* (Bunzel 1871)"; "Type species. *Iguanodon suessii* Bunzel, later recombined as *Mochlodon suessii* by Seeley"; "Lectotype. Right dentary (PIUW 2349/2)."
- seeley1881a: "*Parietal bone of a small Dinosaur (probably* Mochlodon*)*"; "*Scapula (probably of* Mochlodon*)*"; Plate XXVIII, fig. 1, "probably referable to *Mochlodon Suessii*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Seeley (1881) for the species.* It recombines Bunzel's species.
- *The parietal and scapula in the lectotype.* Seeley's referrals are tentative, and the lectotype designation names the dentary.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which mine yielded the Mochlodon suessi lectotype, and in which unit?

*2026-08-30*

**Conclusion.** The Konstantin mining tunnel of the Felbering Mine at Muthmannsdorf, in the Grünbach Formation of the Gosau Group, as Ősi and colleagues (2012) give the type locality and horizon. Governs `location.locality` and `location.formation: Grünbach` on [*Mochlodon suessi*](../../../genera/M/Mochlodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ősi2012a, p. 6, Systematic Paleontology: "Type locality. Konstantin mining tunnel, Felbering Mine, Muthmannsdorf, Wiener Neustadt-Land district, Niederösterreich (Lower Austria), Austria. Type horizon. Grünbach Formation, Gosau Group, lower Campanian."

**Ruled out.**
- *The Gute Hoffnung mine.* The species' etymology in the record names it, but no paper read does, and Ősi and colleagues name the Felbering Mine.
- *"Neue Welt, west of Wiener Neustadt" as the locality.* Suess (1881) gives "the valley of the 'Neue Welt,' the spot where the bones were gathered", where "several shafts pass twice through one and the same seam of coal" (suess1881a, p. 702). That names a mining valley, not a site; Ősi and colleagues name the mine and the tunnel.

**Open.** Whether "Grünbach Formation" is a formally erected unit or the name of the Grünbach syncline applied to its Gosau fill. Plöchinger's (1961) account of the Grünbach and Neue Welt Gosau basin, or Wagreich and Marschalko (1995), would settle it.
