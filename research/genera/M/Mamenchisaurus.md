# *Mamenchisaurus*

## What is the Mamenchisaurus hochuanensis holotype's number?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** CCG V 20401 (Chengdu College of Geology, now Chengdu University of Technology). The describing paper gives no number. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` and `material` on *Mamenchisaurus hochuanensis*.

**Evidence.**
- ksepka2006a, p. 6, Table 1: *M. hochuanensis* with CCG V 20401, the earliest attestation read for this note; p. 2: "CCG, Chengdu College of Geology, Sichuan, China".
- moore2023a, p. 42: "In the articulated holotype skeleton of M. hochuanensis (CCG V 20401)"; p. 4: "CCG, Chengdu University of Technology, Chengdu, China".
- young1972a, English translation, p. 3: the describing paper gives no catalogue number; it notes the "femur is represented only by a right distal end".

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP 3.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi print "IVPP 3" for the holotype in two tables (molina-pérez2020a, pp. 160, 252).
- *GCC V 20401.* Zhao (2008) writes "GCC V 20401", the same letters reordered.

**Open.** The Chinese original of the describing paper has not been read, so whether it gives a number is unconfirmed.
