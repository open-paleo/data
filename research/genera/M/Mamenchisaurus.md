# *Mamenchisaurus*

## What is the Mamenchisaurus hochuanensis holotype's number?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** CCG V 20401 (Chengdu College of Geology, now Chengdu University of Technology). The describing paper gives no number. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` and `material` on *Mamenchisaurus hochuanensis*.

**Evidence.**
- ksepka2006a, p. 6, Table 1: *M. hochuanensis* with CCG V 20401, the earliest attestation read for this note; p. 2: "CCG, Chengdu College of Geology, Sichuan, China".
- moore2023a, p. 42: "In the articulated holotype skeleton of M. hochuanensis (CCG V 20401)"; p. 4: "CCG, Chengdu University of Technology, Chengdu, China".
- young1972a, English translation, p. 3: the describing paper gives no catalogue number; it notes the "femur is represented only by a right distal end".

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP 3.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi print "IVPP 3" for the holotype in two tables (molina-pérez2020a, pp. 160, 252).
- *GCC V 20401.* Zhao (2008) writes "GCC V 20401", the same letters reordered.

**Open.** The Chinese original of the describing paper has not been read, so whether it gives a number is unconfirmed.

## Where in the Shaximiao Formation is the *Mamenchisaurus constructus* holotype?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** In the upper Shaximiao, from Dong and colleagues (1983), who documented the type locality in the field in 1979. Young (1954) declined to place the horizon because no one had visited the site. Governs `location.part: upper` on [*Mamenchisaurus constructus*](../../../genera/M/Mamenchisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 55: "In 1979 the authors conducted a documentation of the *M. constructus* type locality which lay approximately 300 m from Mamenchi Ferry, on the right bank of the Jinshajiang (upper Yangzi) River in southeast Yibin Co. which lies stratigraphically in the middle of the Upper Shaximiao Fm."
- young1954a, p. 365: "馬門溪龍的地層位置，因未作實際觀察，不易確定" ("the stratigraphic position of *Mamenchisaurus* is difficult to determine, as no actual field observation was made", translated); p. 366: the age is "上侏羅紀或下白堊紀" ("Upper Jurassic or Lower Cretaceous", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *No part, because the describing paper gives none.* Young's silence rests on the absence of fieldwork, which Dong and colleagues supplied.

**Open.** Dong and colleagues are not in the record's `references`. The site lies about 300 m from the ferry the locality is named for.

## What is the *Mamenchisaurus youngi* holotype's number?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** ZDM 0083, from the describing paper. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ZDM 0083]` on [*Mamenchisaurus youngi*](../../../genera/M/Mamenchisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pi1996a, English translation, under Specimen: "Specimen No ZDM0083 is housed at the Zigong Dinosaur Museum."

**Ruled out.**
- *ZDM 003.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "ZDM 003" in a table of estimated running speeds (molina-pérez2020a, p. 168), one digit short.

**Open.** The Chinese original has not been read; the number is from the translation.
