# *Monolophosaurus*

## Is the coordinate in the Monolophosaurus type-locality line the holotype's position?

*2026-08-30*

**Conclusion.** No. Zhao and Currie (1993) print 44°30′N, 90°0′E after "34 km northeast of Jiangjunmiao", and Russell and Zheng (1993), in the same volume, print the same pair for the *Mamenchisaurus sinocanadorum* quarry 22 km north of Jiangjunmiao. Two sites at different distances and bearings from one reference point cannot share a position, so the pair is a regional figure, not this specimen's site. The record carries no coordinate. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Monolophosaurus jiangi*](../../../genera/M/Monolophosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhao1993a, p. 2028: "Collected from the Middle Jurassic Wucaiwan Formation 34 km northeast of Jiangjunmiao (44°30′N, 90°0′E) in the Jiangjunmiao Depression".
- russell1993c, p. 2082: "Latitude 44°30′N, longitude 90°00′E, in heavily dissected exposures in the upper part of the Shishugou Formation along the southern edge of a large mesa, 22 km north of Jiangjunmiao".
- moore2023a, p. 6: "the coordinate data provided by these authors (44°30′ N, 90°00′ E) place the type locality about 32 km south of its actual location".

**Ruled out.**
- *44.5, 90.0 as the Monolophosaurus site.* The same pair stands for a second site at another bearing and distance, and Moore and colleagues (2023) find it about 32 km from where that second site actually is.

**Open.** The holotype's position. The 2010 redescriptions of the skull (Brusatte and colleagues) and the postcranium (Zhao and colleagues) have not been read, and either may print one.
