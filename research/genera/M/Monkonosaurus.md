# *Monkonosaurus*

## Which formation is the *Monkonosaurus* holotype from?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** The Loe-ein Formation. Dong (1990), whose description made the name available, places the holotype in it, and Maidment and Wei (2006) repeat it. Governs `location.formation: Loe-ein` on [*Monkonosaurus lawulacus*](../../../genera/M/Monkonosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1990b, p. 264: "Locality and horizon. Lawulashan, Monko County, Qamdo District, Tibet. Loe-ein Formation, Lower Cretaceous or Upper Jurassic."
- maidment2006a, p. 631: "Upper Jurassic to Lower Cretaceous Loe-ein Formation, Lawulashan, Monko, Qamdo District, eastern Tibet (Dong, 1990)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Lura Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Lura Fm., China" (jones2026a, p. 402). *The Dinosauria* gives "Lura Formation (Xizang Zizhiqu)" in its stegosaur table (weishampel2004a, p. 345, Table 16.1), and its distribution chapter gives "Lura Formation (?= Loe-ein Formation; Zhao 1983; Dong 1990b; Sun et al. 1992)" (weishampel2004b, p. 565), with the equation queried. Dong (1990), whom the chapter cites, names only the Loe-ein.

**Open.** Whether the Lura and the Loe-ein are one unit. No source read here relates the two.

## What stage is the *Monkonosaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian to Hauterivian. Dong (1990) leaves the age undecided between the Kimmeridgian and the Neocomian, and that either-or contributes both readings and every stage between them. Governs `stage: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian, Berriasian, Valanginian, Hauterivian]` on [*Monkonosaurus lawulacus*](../../../genera/M/Monkonosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1990b, p. 266: *Monkonosaurus* is "from the Loe-ein Formation (Kimmeridgian or Neocomian)"; p. 264: "Lower Cretaceous or Upper Jurassic".

**Ruled out.**
- *Oxfordian.* Jones (2026) gives "Late Jurassic, Oxfordian (?)" (jones2026a, p. 402), a single stage that no source read here gives for the Loe-ein, and older than Dong's earliest stage.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does the Monkonosaurus holotype have a catalogue number?

*2026-07-20*

**Conclusion.** No. Dong (1990), whose description made the name available, gives the holotype's elements without a number, and Maidment and Wei (2006), who relocated the ilio-sacral block at the IVPP, list it as unnumbered. Governs the empty `type_specimen.specimen_id` and the `type_specimen.notes` on [*Monkonosaurus lawulacus*](../../../genera/M/Monkonosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1990b, p. 264: "Material. Holotype, two incomplete vertebrae, three plates, and a complete sacrum."
- maidment2006a, p. 631: "Holotype. IVPP unnumbered, comprising two partial vertebrae, three dermal plates and a complete ilio-sacral block (Dong, 1990)."

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP V 6975.* Jones (2026) gives "IVPP V 6975—fragments (Zhao, 1986)" in a specimen table (jones2026a, p. 402). Neither primary paper read prints this number.

**Open.** Whether the number comes from Zhao (1986), which Jones cites for it. That work names the species without a description and has not been read.
