# *Magyarosaurus*

## How was *M. dacus* fixed as the type species?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** By subsequent designation, not by Huene (1932). Huene proposed *Magyarosaurus* for three species and named no type species; White (1973) is the earliest designation read, "Genotype: *dacus*". Olshevsky (1991) tags *M. hungaricus* as the type instead, but a later designation does not displace an earlier one (ICZN Art. 69.1.1). Governs `type_species: true` on *Magyarosaurus dacus* in [*Magyarosaurus*](../../../genera/M/Magyarosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- huene1932a, p. 275: "so I propose the new generic name *Magyarosaurus* for these species, namely *Magyarosaurus dacus* Nopcsa sp., *Magyarosaurus transsylvanicus* n. sp., *Magyarosaurus* (?) *hungaricus* n. sp. The genus *Magyarosaurus* is characterized on the basis of the two small species (*dacus* and *transsylvanicus*) as follows" (translated). The "Typus" Huene fixes on p. 263 is the type specimen of *dacus*, not the type species of the genus.
- white1973a, p. 139: "*Magyarosaurus* Huene, 1932 … Genotype: *dacus*." Read from the page image.
- olshevsky1991a, p. 145: "*M. hungaricus* von Huene, 1932 (Type)".

**Ruled out.**
- *M. hungaricus as type species.* Olshevsky's (1991) tag comes after White's (1973) designation.

**Open.** Kuhn's *Fossilium Catalogus* (1939) may designate earlier than White (1973); it has not been read.

## Which formation is the *Magyarosaurus dacus* lectotype from?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Densuș-Ciula Formation, low in its middle part, near Vălioara. Huene's Sânpetru is a mistake that Díez Díaz and colleagues (2025) correct. Governs `location.formation: Densuș-Ciula` and `location.part: middle` on [*Magyarosaurus dacus*](../../../genera/M/Magyarosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- díezdíaz2025a, p. 28: the lectotype locality "occurs in the lowermost part of the unnamed middle member of the Densuș-Ciula Formation".
- díezdíaz2025a, p. 12: "Huene (1932) mistakenly mentioned that SZTFH Ob.3091 came from Sânpetru, but the type locality for this taxon must be located somewhere in the vicinity of Vălioara, the locus of Kadić's collecting efforts."

**Ruled out.**
- *Sânpetru Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Sânpetru Fm., Romania" (jones2026b, p. 383), and *The Dinosauria* "Sânpetru Formation, ?including Densus Ciula Formation" (upchurch2004a, p. 269, Table 13.1). Both follow Huene's locality, which the revision shows to be wrong for this specimen.

**Open.** The middle member has no formal name, so the record gives it as `part`.

## Which specimen is the *Magyarosaurus dacus* lectotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** SZTFH Ob.3091, an anterior caudal vertebra in the Budapest collection. Díez Díaz and colleagues (2025) follow Huene (1932) in treating it as the type. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [SZTFH Ob.3091]` and `specimen_type: lectotype` on [*Magyarosaurus dacus*](../../../genera/M/Magyarosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- díezdíaz2025a, p. 28: "Lectotype. Anterior caudal vertebra (SZTFH Ob.3091, Assemblage A)."
- díezdíaz2025a, p. 12: "we follow Huene (1932) in regarding SZTFH Ob.3091 as the lectotype for the species".

**Ruled out.**
- *London specimens.* Jones (2026) gives "NHM-R.3861—dorsal vertebrae" (jones2026b, p. 383); Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "BMNH R.3861a" (molina-pérez2020a, p. 69) and, in a speed table, "BMNH R.3853" (p. 169). The lectotype is in Budapest, and the revision gives NHMUK R.3853 as the type of Huene's ?*Magyarosaurus hungaricus* (díezdíaz2025a, p. 16).

**Open.** Nothing.
