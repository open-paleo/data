# *Mongolosaurus*

## Which stage does the *Mongolosaurus* holotype take?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** None; the record keeps the Early Cretaceous only. Mannion (2011) gives the horizon as "Aptian-Albian (?)" by correlation of the On Gong with the Ondai Sair, and the On Gong has no stage of its own (see [On Gong](../../stratigraphy/o/on-gong.md)). Governs the absent `stage` on [*Mongolosaurus haplodon*](../../../genera/M/Mongolosaurus.yml), replacing `[Aptian, Albian]`.

**Evidence.**
- mannion2011b, p. 358: "Aptian-Albian (?), Early Cretaceous, On Gong Formation (Hu Khung Ulan locality)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian–Albian.* The age of the correlated Ondai Sair.

**Open.** Nothing.
