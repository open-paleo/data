# *Magnamanus*

## Does the Magnamanus holotype have a single number, and where is it held?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** No single number: the holotype is one individual spread over five lots at the Museo Numantino de Soria (MNS), each element carrying its own sub-number. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` (element numbers) and `institution` (Museo Numantino de Soria, not Stuttgart) on *Magnamanus soriaensis*.

**Evidence.**
- fuentesvidarte2016a, p. 410: the material bears "las siguientes siglas: 2000/132, 2001/122, 2002/95, 2003/69 y 2004/54" ("the following codes", translated), and the abbreviations give "MNS, Museo Numantino de Soria, España"; p. 411: "Holotipo. Esqueleto parcial que comprende…" ("Holotype. Partial skeleton comprising…", translated), with no number; p. 443: "El material estudiado pertenece a un solo individuo adulto" ("The material studied belongs to a single adult individual", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *A type series.* The paper describes one individual, so the five lots make one holotype.
- *MNS 2000/132.1064 as the holotype's number.* Sánchez-Fenollosa and colleagues caption it "Magnamanus soriaensis holotype MNS 2000/132.1064" (sánchez-fenollosa2023a, p. 1026, Fig. 10); it is the number of one holotype element, the right metatarsal IV (fuentesvidarte2016a, p. 434).
- *Lots 2000/122, 2001/95 and 202/95.* These appear in the describing paper (pp. 415, 419, 421) but not in its list of lots; they are probably slips for listed lots (our reading).

**Open.** Lot 2001/122 also holds the *Soriatitan* holotype (royo-torres2017a, accepted manuscript, p. 7), with element sub-numbers that do not overlap those of *Magnamanus* (our count).

## Do the five MNS lot numbers identify the *Magnamanus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The lots are accession batches, and the holotype elements carry sub-numbers within them, so the record lists the element numbers the describing paper ties to the holotype. The first lot is 2000/132. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` (the MNS element numbers) on [*Magnamanus soriaensis*](../../../genera/M/Magnamanus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fuentesvidarte2016a, p. 410: the material bears "las siguientes siglas: 2000/132, 2001/122, 2002/95, 2003/69 y 2004/54" ("the following codes", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *The five lot numbers as the holotype's ids.* Jones (2026) gives "MNS 2000/132, 2001/122, 2002/95, 2003/69, and 2004/54—partial jaw and skeleton" (jones2026a, p. 405). Lot 2001/122 also holds the *Soriatitan* holotype (see the entry above), so a lot number does not pick out *Magnamanus* material.
- *MNS 200/132.* The record's earlier value, a slip for lot 2000/132.

**Open.** Nothing.
