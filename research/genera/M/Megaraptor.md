# *Megaraptor*

## Which formation is the *Megaraptor* holotype from?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Portezuelo Formation. Novas (1998) gives the Río Neuquén Formation, which later work ranks as a subgroup containing the Portezuelo, and Calvo and colleagues (2004) place Novas's holotype in the Portezuelo. Governs `location.formation: Portezuelo` on [*Megaraptor namunhuaiquii*](../../../genera/M/Megaraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas1998a, p. 4: "Locality and Horizon—Upper Cretaceous (Turonian-Coniacian ...), Rio Neuquen Formation, Sierra del Portezuelo, Neuquen Province, Argentina."
- calvo2004b, p. 565: Novas's specimen is "from the Portezuelo Formation of the Río Neuquén Group".

**Ruled out.**
- *Río Neuquén Formation.* *The Dinosauria* gives "Río Neuquén Formation (Neuquén), Argentina" (weishampel2004a, p. 199, Table 10.1), the describing paper's name under the older ranks. It is now the subgroup above the Portezuelo (see [Neuquén Group subgroups](../../topics/neuquén-group-subgroups.md)). Jones (2026) gives "Portezuelo Fm." (jones2026c, p. 499).

**Open.** Nothing.
