# *Maraapunisaurus*

## Does the Maraapunisaurus holotype have a sourced member of the Morrison Formation?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. The only member assignment comes from a paper that treats *M. fragillimus* as a species of *Amphicoelias* and gives one horizon for both species; the papers that separate the genus give formation only. Governs the empty `location.member` on *Maraapunisaurus fragillimus*.

**Evidence.**
- carpenter2018a, p. 227: "Upper Jurassic Morrison Formation in Garden Park", with no member; p. 235: "AMNH FR 5777 cannot be referred to the genus *Amphicoelias*".
- mannion2021a, p. 23: "we agree with Carpenter [17] that it differs in a number of anatomical features from A. altus", and calls Cope Quarry III "a nearby locality" to the *A. altus* quarry.
- tschopp2015a, p. 6, Table 2: a row for Cope Quarry 3 with no member; Cope Quarry 12 (*A. altus*) is "Upper-most Brushy Basin Member, Morrison Formation (probably Tithonian)" (p. 252).

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper Brushy Basin Member.* Woodruff and Foster (2014) write that "the A. fragillimus (and A. altus) quarry sits within the Tithonian portion of the upper Brushy Basin Member of the Morrison Formation (Turner, Peterson, 1999)" (woodruff2014a, p. 216), on the view that "fragillimus should be synonomized into altus" (p. 217). They name no quarry numbers, so the sentence treats Quarry III and Quarry XII as one (our reading); the member is the *A. altus* quarry's, relayed from Turner and Peterson (1999).

**Open.** A member for Cope Quarry III. Turner and Peterson (1999), which would show what was measured at Garden Park, has not been read.
