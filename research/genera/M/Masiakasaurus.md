# *Masiakasaurus*

## Which specimen is the *Masiakasaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** UA 8680, a right dentary, from the describing paper. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [UA 8680]` on [*Masiakasaurus knopfleri*](../../../genera/M/Masiakasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sampson2001a, p. 504: "Holotype. Université d'Antananarivo (UA) 8680, well-preserved right dentary with several teeth"; the referred specimens are "FMNH PR 2108–2182" and "UA 8681–8696".

**Ruled out.**
- *FMNH PR2481.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "FMNH PR2481" in a table of running speeds (molina-pérez2019a, p. 175). It is a partial skeleton described by Carrano and colleagues: "two dorsal vertebrae associated with a partial skeleton (FMNH PR 2481)" (carrano2011a, p. 18).

**Open.** Nothing.
