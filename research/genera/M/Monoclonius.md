# *Monoclonius*

## Is the Monoclonius crassus type from the Coal Ridge Member?

*2026-08-30*

**Conclusion.** Not on the sources read. The record keeps the Judith River Formation with `part: lower`, from Hatcher's (1907) statement that Cope took the type from sandstones near the base of the Judith River beds near the mouth of Birch Creek, and records no member. Governs `location.locality: mouth of Birch Creek`, `location.part: lower` and the empty `location.member` on [*Monoclonius crassus*](../../../genera/M/Monoclonius.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hatcher1907a, p. 176: "It was from the sandstones near the base of the Judith River beds near the mouth of Birch Creek that Professor Cope, in 1876, secured the type of Monoclonius crassus"; Birch Creek empties "into the Missouri River nearly opposite the mouth of Dog Creek"; "The type of Monoclonius sphenocerus Cope was found by Charles H. Sternberg on the Missouri River near Cow Island, Montana; level not recorded."

**Ruled out.**
- *Coal Ridge Member.* Mallon and colleagues (2016) write that "near the area of Cow Island, Cope and Sternberg later found partial cranial remains of the indeterminate centrosaurines 'Monoclonius crassus', 'M. sphenocerus', 'M. recurvicornis', and 'M. fissus'", and that "it seems likely that these problematic taxa are restricted to the latter member", the lower Coal Ridge, citing "R. Rogers, pers. comm., 2016" (mallon2016a, p. 34). The statement is a hedge about four species together, and it puts *M. crassus* near Cow Island, where Hatcher separates the Birch Creek site of *M. crassus* from the Cow Island site of *M. sphenocerus*.

**Open.** Which member the basal sandstones at the mouth of Birch Creek belong to. Cope's (1876) account of the species names no site (cope1876a, pp. 255–256), and his paper's title gives only the "Fort Union Beds of Montana", so Hatcher's account is the earliest read that places the type. Sahni's (1972) restudy of Cope's type localities, not read, may place the site in the current member scheme.
