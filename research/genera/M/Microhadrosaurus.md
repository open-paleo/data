# *Microhadrosaurus*

## Is the status of *Microhadrosaurus* in dispute?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute block is removed and the parent stays Hadrosauridae. Every treatment read from Brett-Surman (1989) to Xing and colleagues (2016) calls it a nomen dubium, and none argues for a placement below Hadrosauridae. The juvenile reading is Brett-Surman's and Xing and colleagues', not the describing paper's: Dong (1979) calls the jaw that of a small individual. Governs `parent: Hadrosauridae` and the absence of a dispute on [*Microhadrosaurus*](../../../genera/M/Microhadrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brett-surman1989a: "This genus is based on a partial left mandible of a juvenile"; "In the absence of any morphologic features of taxonomic utility, I regard Microhadrosaurus as a nomen dubium."
- xing2016a: "the specimen is only an incomplete juvenile".
- dong1979a: "This is a relatively small individual … representing the smallest species of hadrosaur known" (translated).
