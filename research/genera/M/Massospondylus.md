# *Massospondylus*

## Which site is the *Massospondylus carinatus* type locality, and what does its coordinate mark?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** Bormansdrift 133 Farm, Clocolan District, the site of the neotype BP/1/4934. The coordinate is the position Barrett and colleagues (2019) give for the one hill on the farm with fossiliferous outcrop, which they consider the most likely source of the neotype; the 1980 excavation itself was not recorded to a point in any source read. Governs `location.locality: Bormansdrift 133` and `location.coordinates: [-28.962739, 27.430377]` on [*Massospondylus carinatus*](../../../genera/M/Massospondylus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barrett2019a, p. 118: "BP/1/4934 was collected in March 1980 from the upper Elliot Formation of Bormansdrift 133 Farm, in the Clocolan District"; "only one small hill on this farm preserves suitable outcrop for fossil prospecting … significant vertebrate remains were collected from this hill at approximately 28.962739°S, 27.430377°E, at an elevation of 1712 m, and we consider this area to be the most likely source of the neotype".
- barrett2019a, p. 119, Locality and horizon: "Bormansdrift 133 Farm, Clocholan District, Free State Province, South Africa".

**Ruled out.**
- *Beauchef Abbey 215, Harrismith District.* This is the farm of Owen's five syntype vertebrae, destroyed in 1941: "These specimens were from the farm Beauchef Abbey 215 in the Harrismith District" (barrett2019a, p. 116). The neotype replaced them, so the type locality is the neotype's.
- *"Bornmansdrift" as the farm's spelling.* Barrett and colleagues print it once ("also on Bornmansdrift 133", p. 118) against five uses of Bormansdrift, including the Locality and horizon line.

**Open.** Kitching and Raath (1984), cited for "locality details" (barrett2019a, p. 118), have not been read and may record where on the farm the neotype was dug.
