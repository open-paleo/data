# *Macrocollum*

## Which formation is the *Macrocollum* holotype from, when its describing paper names only a sequence?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** The Caturrita Formation. Müller and colleagues (2018) place the Wachholz site only in the "upper portion of the Candelária Sequence", a sequence-stratigraphic unit, and never name a formation. Müller and colleagues (2023) equate the Caturrita Formation with that upper portion and list *M. itaquii* among its sauropodomorphs. Governs `location.formation: Caturrita` on [*Macrocollum itaquii*](../../../genera/M/Macrocollum.yml).

**Evidence.**
- müller2018a, p. 2: "The specimens were collected at the Wachholz site (29°36′46.42″ S; 53°15′54.06″ W), Agudo, Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil; upper portion of the Candelária Sequence, Paraná Basin"; p. 4: "M. itaquii was recovered from rocks belonging to the upper part of the Candelária Sequence".
- müller2023a, p. 1072: "the overlapping Caturrita Formation (upper portion of the Candelaria Sequence)", and "the third species of sauropodomorph from the Caturrita Formation was M. itaquii (Müller et al., 2018)".

**Ruled out.**
- *No formation, because the describing paper gives only a sequence.* A sequence does not fill `formation`, but the same research group names the lithostratigraphic unit for this taxon (müller2023a, p. 1072), so the field takes the formation they give.

**Open.** Nothing.
