# *Macrogryphosaurus*

## Is the *Macrogryphosaurus* holotype from the Portezuelo or the Sierra Barrosa Formation?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** The Sierra Barrosa Formation, as a presumption. Calvo and colleagues (2007) wrote before Garrido (2010) split the old Portezuelo strata; Garrido made the "Upper Portezuelo" of the older schemes the Sierra Barrosa Formation and presumed that the holotype came from it, and Rozadilla and colleagues (2020) follow him while calling the site's stratigraphy under revision. Governs `location.formation: Sierra Barrosa` and `stage: [Coniacian]` on [*Macrogryphosaurus gondwanicus*](../../../genera/M/Macrogryphosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- calvo2007c, p. 472: "It comes from the Portezuelo Formation (Coniacian), Neuquén Group."
- garrido2010a, p. 146: the Sierra Barrosa Formation "fue reconocida en los esquemas originales de Herrero Ducloux (1938, 1939) bajo la denominación de 'Portezuelo Superior'" ("was recognized in the original schemes of Herrero Ducloux (1938, 1939) under the name 'Upper Portezuelo'", translated); for *Macrogryphosaurus gondwanicus*, "se presume que provendría de niveles de esta unidad" ("it is presumed that it would come from levels of this unit", translated).
- rozadilla2020a, p. 5, Locality and horizon: "Garrido (2010) … reinterpreted the rocks where Macrogryphosaurus holotype as a unit distinguished from the Portezuelo Formation, and consequently it was named as Sierra Barrosa Formation"; "the stratigraphy of the site where Macrogryphosaurus was unearthed is under revision".

**Ruled out.**
- *Portezuelo Formation.* The describing paper's assignment uses the formation as it was drawn before Garrido (2010) separated the Los Bastos and Sierra Barrosa formations from it (garrido2010a, p. 146).

**Open.** Garrido's assignment is a presumption from the site's position, not a section measured at it, and Rozadilla and colleagues expect a revision. A study of the Mari Menuco site's stratigraphy would settle it.
