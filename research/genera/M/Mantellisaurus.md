# *Mantellisaurus*

## Where on the Isle of Wight was the *Mantellisaurus* holotype found, and in which member?

*2026-08-29*

**Conclusion.** On the foreshore of Brighstone Bay, between Shepherd's Chine and Atherfield Point, in the Vectis Formation, with no member recorded. Hooley's two accounts give Brighstone Bay and a cliff fall near Atherfield; Bonsor and colleagues (2023) reconcile them, since Atherfield is only a group of coastguard cottages bounded by Atherfield Point at the end of Brighstone Bay. Governs `location.locality`, `location.formation: Vectis` and the empty `location.member` on [*Mantellisaurus atherfieldensis*](../../../genera/M/Mantellisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hooley1925a, p. 1: the skeleton "was obtained from the débris of Wealden Shales, after a fall of the cliff near Atherfield (Isle of Wight)".
- bonsor2023a, p. 4: "Hooley (1917) first described the discovery of the holotype … as coming from the Wealden Shales at Brighstone Bay and later in his monograph (Hooley 1925) as from a cliff fall, in the Wealden Shales at Atherfield"; "This makes it almost certain that NHMUK PV R5764 was found on the foreshore of Brighstone Bay, between Shepherd's Chine and Atherfield Point"; the cliff there contains the "Vectis Formation (formerly known as the Wealden Shales), which, apart from a short section of Barnes High Sandstone on the foreshore at the chine, is represented entirely by the Shepherd's Chine Member. This would place NHMUK PV R5764 close to the Barremian–Aptian boundary."

**Ruled out.**
- *Atherfield as a separate site.* "Since the eighteenth century, Atherfield's coastal presence has only consisted of a group of coast guard cottages, and is delineated to the east by Atherfield Point, which marks the end of Brighstone Bay" (bonsor2023a, p. 4).

**Open.** The member. Bonsor and colleagues head their plate legends (Plate 1) "Shepherd's Chine Member, Vectis Formation", but the placement is inferred from what the cliff exposes along the stretch where they place the find, not from a record of the bed the skeleton fell from, and the Barnes High Sandstone also crops out there. A record of the debris's source bed would settle it.
