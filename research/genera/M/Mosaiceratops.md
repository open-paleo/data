# *Mosaiceratops*

## What is the *Mosaiceratops* holotype's museum code?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** ZMNH, the Zhejiang Museum of Natural History, as the describing paper prints it. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ZMNH M8856]` on [*Mosaiceratops azumai*](../../../genera/M/Mosaiceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zheng2015a, p. 2, Systematic palaeontology: "Holotype. ZMNH M8856 (housed in the collections of the Zhejiang Museum of Natural History, Hangzhou, China)".

**Ruled out.**
- *ZHMN M8856.* Jones (2026) prints "ZHMN M8856" (jones2026a, p. 410), with the letters of the code transposed.

**Open.** Nothing.
