# *Malarguesaurus*

## Is the *Malarguesaurus* holotype from the Portezuelo or the Los Bastos Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** Los Bastos. González Riga, who described the taxon from the Portezuelo in 2009, later assigns it to the Los Bastos, the formation Garrido (2010) erected above the Portezuelo. Governs `location.formation: Los Bastos` on [*Malarguesaurus florenciae*](../../../genera/M/Malarguesaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gonzálezriga2013a, p. 4: "Malarguesaurus was found in strata assigned to Los Bastos Formation (early-middle Coniacian)".
- garrido2010a, p. 144: "Formación Los Bastos (nom. nov.)", placed "por encima de la Fm. Portezuelo" ("above the Portezuelo Fm.", translated).
- bellardini2022a, p. 1382: *Malarguesaurus* "was formalized on the basis of several postcranial elements from the Portezuelo Formation (upper Turonian/lower Coniacian), but more recently González Riga & Ortiz David (2014) stated that the holotype was collected from Los Bastos Formation (Coniacian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Portezuelo Formation, Turonian–Coniacian.* González Riga and colleagues (2009) give "Horizon and locality. Portezuelo Formation, Río Neuquén Subgroup ... upper Turonian-lower Coniacian" (gonzálezriga2009a, p. 138), and Jones (2026) repeats it (jones2026b, p. 380). The describing paper predates the Los Bastos, and its senior author revised the assignment.

**Open.** The record's `stage: [Coniacian]` follows the Los Bastos assignment; this entry decides only the formation.
