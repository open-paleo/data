# *Mesetasaurus*

## Which stages does the *Mesetasaurus* holotype take?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** Santonian and Campanian. The describing paper's Coniacian–Maastrichtian is the range of Aeolosaurini, the clade the taxon nests in, offered as a suggestion; for the formation itself the paper gives "Santonian or early Campanian" (see [Guichón](../../stratigraphy/g/guichón.md)). Governs `stage: [Santonian, Campanian]` on [*Mesetasaurus*](../../../genera/M/Mesetasaurus.yml), replacing `[Coniacian, Santonian, Campanian, Maastrichtian]`.

**Evidence.**
- sotonúñez2026a, p. 298: "Evidence favors a younger age for the unit (i.e., Santonian or early Campanian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Coniacian and Maastrichtian from the range of Aeolosaurini.* A clade's range, not the horizon's age.

**Open.** Nothing.
