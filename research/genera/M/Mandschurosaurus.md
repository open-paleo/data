# *Mandschurosaurus*

## What is the Mandschurosaurus holotype's number, and how much of the mount is original?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Riabinin catalogued it as No. 1644 of the Museum of the Geological Committee (now the Chernyshev museum, St. Petersburg); the mounted skeleton is heavily restored and includes bone from more than one individual. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id`, `institution` and the `material` note on *Mandschurosaurus amurensis*.

**Evidence.**
- riabinin1930a, p. 27: the comparative-measurements table heads its *Mandschurosaurus amurensis* column with No. 1644, Museum of the Geological Committee (a table header).
- riabinin1930a, p. 6: "количество гипса достигло в скелете 35%" ("the amount of plaster in the skeleton reached 35%", translated), quoting his 1925 account; the skeleton "был монтирован в Музее Геологического Комитета" ("was mounted in the Museum of the Geological Committee", translated) in 1925.
- bolotsky2014a, p. 318: the mount is "on display in the F. N. Chernishev Scientific Research Geological Institute, St. Petersburg".

**Ruled out.**
- *A single individual throughout.* Riabinin calls the mount a restoration of "единого образца" ("a single specimen", translated; p. 6) but assigns the right femur to "другому, более мощному индивидууму" ("another, more robust individual", translated; p. 25).

**Open.** How many individuals. Godefroit and colleagues read Riabinin as admitting that "the holotype skeleton is clearly reconstructed from several individuals" (godefroit2000a, p. 877); Riabinin himself names a second individual for one bone only.
