# *Mononykus*

## What stage is the *Mononykus* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Maastrichtian, from the describing paper's horizon line for the holotype. Governs `stage: [Maastrichtian]` on [*Mononykus olecranus*](../../../genera/M/Mononykus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- perle1993a, p. 623: "Locality and horizon of holotype. Upper Cretaceous (Maestrichtian) Nemegt Formation, Bugin Tsav, Mongolia." The paper names the genus *Mononychus*, a preoccupied name later replaced by *Mononykus*.

**Ruled out.**
- *?middle Campanian.* *The Dinosauria* gives "Djadokhta Formation, Nemegt Formation (Ömnögov'), Mongolia; Iren Dabasu Formation" with "?middle Campanian" (weishampel2004a, p. 211, Table 11.1), one row for the species across three units. The describing paper dates the holotype's own horizon.

**Open.** Nothing.

- `weishampel2004a`: Weishampel, D. B.; Dodson, P.; Osmólska, H. (2004). The Dinosauria, Second Edition. University of California Press, Berkeley.
