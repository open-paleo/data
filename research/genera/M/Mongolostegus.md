# *Mongolostegus*

## What is the *Mongolostegus* holotype's number?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** PIN 3779-15. The describing paper writes "PIN, no. 3779-15"; the record drops the "no.". Element numbers such as 3779-15/3 identify single bones of the holotype. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [PIN 3779-15]` on [*Mongolostegus exspectabilis*](../../../genera/M/Mongolostegus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tumanova2018a, p. 1773: "Holotype. PIN, no. 3779-15, left pubis and anterior process of right pubis, one sacrocaudal and five anterior caudal vertebrae in articulation".
- tumanova2018a, Fig. 1 caption: the pubis as "specimen PIN, no. 3779-15/3".

**Ruled out.**
- *"PIN no. 3779–15" as a different number.* Jones (2026) prints it with the "no." (jones2026a, p. 402). It is the same number.
- *PIN 3779-15/3 as the holotype's number.* It numbers one element.

**Open.** Nothing.
