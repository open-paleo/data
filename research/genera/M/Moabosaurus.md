# *Moabosaurus*

## Is the Dalton Wells Quarry in the lower or upper Yellow Cat Member?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** Neither part is recorded. The sources agree the quarry lies in the lowest Yellow Cat beds at Dalton Wells, but differ on which unit those beds are: Britt and colleagues and others call it the base of the member, and Kirkland and colleagues place the fauna in the upper Yellow Cat, which at Dalton Wells rests almost directly on the Morrison. Governs the empty `location.part` and the location notes on [*Moabosaurus utahensis*](../../../genera/M/Moabosaurus.yml), replacing `part: lower`.

**Evidence.**
- kirkland2016a, p. 118: "The Dalton Wells Quarry preserves a dinosaur fauna consistent with that of the upper part of the Yellow Cat Member"; at the south end of the outcrops, at Dalton Wells, "there is no carbonate unit in the unusually thin Yellow Cat Member, and the upper Yellow Cat is separated from the underlying Morrison Formation by a 'single' mudstone conglomerate bed".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lower or upper as settled.* "Base of the member" and "upper Yellow Cat" can both hold if the lower Yellow Cat is absent at Dalton Wells; that reconciliation is our reading of Kirkland and colleagues, not a statement in either source.

**Open.** Whether the lower Yellow Cat is present at Dalton Wells.

## Is BYU 7510 the *Moabosaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype is BYU 14387, three dorsal vertebrae; 7510 is the BYU locality number of the Dalton Wells Quarry. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [BYU 14387]` on [*Moabosaurus utahensis*](../../../genera/M/Moabosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- britt2017a, p. 193: "Holotype.— BYU 14387, three closely associated dorsal vertebrae, two of which were found in articulation, with the other separated by 20 cm."
- britt2017a, p. 190, Fig. 1 caption: "all specimens described in this paper are from the Dalton Wells Quarry (BYU locality 7510)".

**Ruled out.**
- *BYU 7510.* Jones (2026) gives "BYU 7510—three dorsal vertebrae" (jones2026b, p. 375): the holotype's material under the quarry's locality number.

**Open.** Nothing.
