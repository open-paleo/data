# *Melanorosaurus*

## Which specimen is the *Melanorosaurus* type?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** SAM-PK-3449, which Barrett and Choiniere (2024) designate lectotype from Haughton's two syntype numbers. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [SAM-PK-3449]` and `specimen_type: lectotype` on [*Melanorosaurus readi*](../../../genera/M/Melanorosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barrett2024b, p. 5: "we hereby designate SAM-PK-3449 as the lectotype of this species"; p. 4: "the other component of the syntype series (SAM-PK-3450) was found 18 m away from the lectotype, implying that it belongs to a different individual(s)."

**Ruled out.**
- *SAM 3449 and SAM 3450 together.* Jones (2026) sets both in bold as type material (jones2026b, p. 373). The lectotype designation leaves SAM-PK-3450 out of the type.
- *SAM 3450 or NM QR1551.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "Melanorosaurus readi SAM 3450" in a table of historical size estimates (molina-pérez2020a, p. 69) and NM QR1551 in their speed tables (pp. 167–168). NM QR1551 is referred material.

**Open.** Nothing.
