# *Melanorosaurus*

## Is *Melanorosaurus* inside Sauropoda?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** No, under both definitions now in use, so the dispute block is removed. It is the external specifier of Yates's (2007) stem definition, and it lies several nodes outside the node-based *Vulcanodon* definition, which Rauhut and colleagues (2020) and Barrett and Choiniere (2024) follow. What remains open is how Sauropoda should be defined, a question about the clade rather than this genus. Barrett and Choiniere (2024) also answer the doubt over its validity. Governs `parent: Sauropodiformes` and the absence of a dispute on [*Melanorosaurus*](../../../genera/M/Melanorosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barrett2024b: Yates's definition, "all taxa more closely related to Saltasaurus than to Melanorosaurus", is "the most widely adopted"; "we advocate abandonment of any stem-based definition of Sauropoda using Melanorosaurus, and support instead the 'traditional' node-based definition of Sauropoda, rooted on Vulcanodon"; the lectotype OTU "is the earliest-branching lineage of a clade that is sister to Lessemsauridae"; "Although we show that this taxon is likely valid".
- rauhut2020a: "we follow the emerging consensus to use the node-based definition originally proposed by Salgado et al."
- mcphee2017a: "the validity of *Melanorosaurs* is currently in serious doubt", answered by Barrett and Choiniere (2024).

**Ruled out.**
- *"Just inside" Sauropoda.* The earlier summary's alternative. No paper read places it there; it would follow only from older stem definitions with *Plateosaurus* as external specifier, which none of the papers read uses.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which specimen is the *Melanorosaurus* type?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** SAM-PK-3449, which Barrett and Choiniere (2024) designate lectotype from Haughton's two syntype numbers. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [SAM-PK-3449]` and `specimen_type: lectotype` on [*Melanorosaurus readi*](../../../genera/M/Melanorosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barrett2024b, p. 5: "we hereby designate SAM-PK-3449 as the lectotype of this species"; p. 4: "the other component of the syntype series (SAM-PK-3450) was found 18 m away from the lectotype, implying that it belongs to a different individual(s)."

**Ruled out.**
- *SAM 3449 and SAM 3450 together.* Jones (2026) sets both in bold as type material (jones2026b, p. 373). The lectotype designation leaves SAM-PK-3450 out of the type.
- *SAM 3450 or NM QR1551.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "Melanorosaurus readi SAM 3450" in a table of historical size estimates (molina-pérez2020a, p. 69) and NM QR1551 in their speed tables (pp. 167–168). NM QR1551 is referred material.

**Open.** Nothing.
