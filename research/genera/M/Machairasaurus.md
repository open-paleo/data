# *Machairasaurus*

## Are Bayan Mandahu and Wulansuhai one unit?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** Not established. The record keeps `location.formation: Bayan Mandahu`, the describing paper's name, but the 2026-08 entry's statement that Wulansuhai is "the other name … for the same red beds" and not a different unit goes further than the sources: "Bayan Mandahu Formation" has no published definition, and the identification of these beds with the Ulansuhai (Wulansuhai) Formation of Alxa rests on Hao and colleagues (2024), citing a thesis, and on a hypothesis in Evans and colleagues (2021). Supersedes that entry's conclusion and its **Open** line; see [Bayan Mandahu](../../stratigraphy/b/bayan-mandahu.md).

**Evidence.**
- lü2013a, p. 13: "Bayan Mandahu Formation ( = Wulansuhai Formation [40])", citing Xu and colleagues (2013), who give "the Bayan Mandahu beds (the Wulansuhai Formation)" (xu2013a, p. 97) without the Wulansuhai's definition.

**Ruled out.**
- *Wulansuhai as the same unit, stated as fact.* See [Bayan Mandahu](../../stratigraphy/b/bayan-mandahu.md).

**Open.** Whether the Bayan Mandahu beds belong to the Ulansuhai Formation; see [Bayan Mandahu](../../stratigraphy/b/bayan-mandahu.md).

## Is the *Machairasaurus* holotype from the Bayan Mandahu or the Wulansuhai Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The record takes the describing paper's name, Bayan Mandahu Formation. Wulansuhai is the other name papers print for the same red beds at Bayan Mandahu, not a different rock unit. Governs `location.formation: Bayan Mandahu` on [*Machairasaurus leptonychus*](../../../genera/M/Machairasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- longrich2010a, p. 947: "Horizon and type locality. Bayan Mandahu Formation, Late Cretaceous (Campanian), Bayan Mandahu, Inner Mongolia."
- lü2013a, p. 13: *Machairasaurus leptonychus* and others "are from the Upper Cretaceous Bayan Mandahu Formation ( = Wulansuhai Formation [40])".
- xu2013a, p. 85: "the Upper Cretaceous Wulansuhai Formation of Bayan Mandahu".

**Ruled out.**
- *Wulansuhai as a rival unit.* Jones (2026) gives "Wulansuhai Fm., China" (jones2026c, p. 507). The equivalence above makes it the same rock under the other name.

**Open.** Whether "Bayan Mandahu Formation" is a formal unit or a local name for the eastern outcrop of the Ulansuhai; see [Ulansuhai](../../stratigraphy/u/ulansuhai.md).
