# *Machairasaurus*

## Is the *Machairasaurus* holotype from the Bayan Mandahu or the Wulansuhai Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The record takes the describing paper's name, Bayan Mandahu Formation. Wulansuhai is the other name papers print for the same red beds at Bayan Mandahu, not a different rock unit. Governs `location.formation: Bayan Mandahu` on [*Machairasaurus leptonychus*](../../../genera/M/Machairasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- longrich2010a, p. 947: "Horizon and type locality. Bayan Mandahu Formation, Late Cretaceous (Campanian), Bayan Mandahu, Inner Mongolia."
- lü2013a, p. 13: *Machairasaurus leptonychus* and others "are from the Upper Cretaceous Bayan Mandahu Formation ( = Wulansuhai Formation [40])".
- xu2013a, p. 85: "the Upper Cretaceous Wulansuhai Formation of Bayan Mandahu".

**Ruled out.**
- *Wulansuhai as a rival unit.* Jones (2026) gives "Wulansuhai Fm., China" (jones2026c, p. 507). The equivalence above makes it the same rock under the other name.

**Open.** Whether "Bayan Mandahu Formation" is a formal unit or a local name for the eastern outcrop of the Ulansuhai; see [Ulansuhai](../../stratigraphy/u/ulansuhai.md).
