# *Judiceratops*

## Is the *Judiceratops* holotype from the *Brachylophosaurus* site, and which member is it from?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** Not from the *Brachylophosaurus* site, and the member is not known. Longrich (2013) gives the locality only as Canadian Creek in the Judith River Formation. Loewen and colleagues (2024) give the *Judiceratops* site and the *Brachylophosaurus goodwini* holotype locality different distances and bearings from the Loki Quarry. Governs `location.locality: Canadian Creek` and the empty `location.member` on [*Judiceratops tigris*](../../../genera/J/Judiceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- longrich2013b, p. 53, Horizon and locality: "Upper Campanian Judith River Formation, Canadian Creek, 50 km north of Rudyard, Hill County, Montana, USA."
- loewen2024a, p. 4: "The Loki Quarry is 4.9 km northwest of the site where the holotype of the putative chasmosaurine ceratopsid Judiceratops tigris (YPM VPPU 022404) was collected", and "lies 2.8 km west of the Brachylophosaurus goodwini (Horner, 1988) holotype locality (UCMP Locality No. V83125)".
- mallon2016a, p. 33: fossil occurrences in the Kennedy Coulee area "are generally attributable to the lower McClelland Ferry Member"; "The ceratopsid Judiceratops [6] likewise occurs in Kennedy Coulee, but its stratigraphic position is not precisely known."

**Ruled out.**
- *The* Brachylophosaurus *site.* On Loewen and colleagues' figures the two sites are roughly 3.5 km apart (our calculation from the stated distances and bearings). Mallon and colleagues (2016, p. 33) also place *B. goodwini* in "the upper muddy unit of the Kennedy Coulee area, which corresponds to Unit 1 of the Oldman Formation".

**Open.** The member. Loewen and colleagues write that *Lokiceratops*, from "the lower part of the McClelland Ferry Member" (loewen2024a, p. 3), "closely overlaps the holotype of Wendiceratops and the chasmosaurine Judiceratops tigris (Longrich, 2013)" at the stratigraphic resolution available (p. 70), against Mallon and colleagues' "not precisely known". Whether that overlap is enough for `member: McClelland Ferry` is undecided. Campbell's (2015) reassessment of *Judiceratops* has not been read.
