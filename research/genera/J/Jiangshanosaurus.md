# *Jiangshanosaurus*

## Is Jiangshanosaurus Albian?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. The Albian rests on one Rb-Sr date that later work supersedes; the [Jinhua Formation](../../../stratigraphy/j/jinhua.yml) is early Late Cretaceous. Governs `period: [Late Cretaceous]` and the stages on *Jiangshanosaurus lixianensis*.

**Evidence.**
- mannion2019b, p. 2, Introduction: "then dated as Albian (late Early Cretaceous), but now regarded as early Late Cretaceous in age [63]", where reference 63 is Yu and colleagues (2010).
- yu2010a: the lower basalt dated "(101.8±3.2) Ma".

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian.* Tang and colleagues (2001) give "同位素年龄值由江西省区域地质调查队测定为105Ma(Rb-Sr等时线法)，应为早白垩世晚期的Albian期" ("the isotopic age, determined by the Jiangxi Regional Geological Survey Team, is 105 Ma (Rb-Sr isochron method), corresponding to the Albian of the late Early Cretaceous", translated; tang2001a, p. 272), and in their English abstract "allegedly from the lower part of Jinhua Formation in Albian (105Ma) horizon" (p. 277). About a dozen later papers give Albian or Aptian–Albian; each traces to Tang and colleagues directly or through compilations (our count), so they add no independent evidence.

**Open.** Nothing.
