# *Jinfengopteryx*

## Which unit is the Jinfengopteryx holotype from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The [Qiaotou Member](../../../stratigraphy/q/qiaotou.yml) of the [Huajiying Formation](../../../stratigraphy/h/huajiying.yml), Senjitu basin, Hebei. Governs `location.formation: Huajiying` and `location.member: Qiaotou` on *Jinfengopteryx elegans*.

**Evidence.**
- jin2008a, p. 2824: "the sedimentary member along Qiaotou-Daluozigou (Qiaotou sedimentary member, Huajiying Formation in this paper)", whose horizon "also yielded a feathered dinosaur—Jinfengopteryx elegans".
- jin2008a, p. 2821, abstract: the Senjitu basin succession "is corresponding to the Dawangzhangzi Bed of the Yixian Formation in Lingyuan, western Liaoning, and the latter [the Sichakou basin] is approximately equivalent to the Dadianzi Formation in Luanping".
- xing2020a, pre-proof, Introduction: "the lower part of the formation (Sichakou and Qiaotou members)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Qiaotou Formation.* Ji and Ji (2007) give "the Early Cretaceous Qiaotou Formation of Fengning County" (ji2007b, p. 338), a formation rank that goes back to Ji and colleagues (2005). Jin and colleagues (2008) use member rank "in this paper" without arguing against the formation rank; they do not cite Ji and Ji (2007).
- *Hauterivian Dabeigou/Dadianzi Formation.* Xu and colleagues (2017) place *Jinfengopteryx* "from the Hauterivian Dabeigou/Dadianzi Formation" (xu2017b, p. 2), citing Jin and colleagues (2008), who correlate the Dadianzi with the separate Sichakou basin and the *Jinfengopteryx* beds with the Dawangzhangzi Bed (jin2008a, pp. 2821, 2825). This is a misreading of their source, not a dissenting assignment.

**Open.** Nothing.
