# *Jian*

## Does *Jian* take the lower part of the Xiagou Formation?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** No. The describing paper gives the horizon as lower Aptian on its own account and places the holotype near Changma village, without naming a quarry or a level in the formation. The lower Xiagou is where You and colleagues (2018) put the Changma bird-bearing layers in general, and no source places this specimen in them. The Aptian does not depend on that placement. Governs the empty `location.part` on [*Jian changmaensis*](../../../genera/J/Jian.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhou2026a, Locality and horizon: "Near the village of Changma (39°51.322'N, 96°46.773'E), Changma Basin, northwestern Gansu Province, People's Republic of China (Fig. 1); Lower Cretaceous (lower Aptian; Li and Yang 2004; You et al. 2006; X. Li et al. 2013; Suarez et al. 2013, 2017; …)".
- you2018a, p. 5: "the early Aptian age (124–120 Ma) for the bird-bearing layers in the lower section of the Xiagou Formation in the Changma Basin based on chemostratigraphy (Suarez et al., 2013)".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`, inferred from the age's citations.* Citing the bird-quarry datings for the age does not place the holotype in the quarries.

**Open.** Whether GSGM-D050 comes from the Changma bird quarries. A statement tying the specimen to a quarry would settle it.
