# *Jeholosaurus*

## Does *Jeholosaurus* sit in Jeholosauridae?

*2026-11-20*

**Conclusion.** No. *Jeholosaurus* sits at Neornithischia, `incertae sedis`, and Jeholosauridae is not a node in the tree. The family held only *Jeholosaurus* in our tree, and a single-genus node is collapsed into the level above. The nearest parent agreed across the live placements is Neornithischia: several analyses put *Jeholosaurus*, with *Yueosaurus*, near the base of Neornithischia outside Thescelosauridae and Cerapoda, while Fonseca and colleagues (2024) put it within Thescelosaurinae. Governs `parent: Neornithischia` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Jeholosaurus*](../../../genera/J/Jeholosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- han2012a: "JEHOLOSAURIDAE, fam. nov."; the reduced consensus "places *Jeholosaurus*, *Changchunsaurus*, and *Haya* together within a clade (Jeholosauridae)".
- boyd2015a: "The clade consisting of *J. shangyuanensis* + *Y. tiantaiensis* is unambiguously united with all other neornithischians".
- manitkoon2023a: "*Minimorcursor* gen. nov. is the sister group of a clade formed of Jeholosauridae, Thescelosauridae, *Nanosaurus*, *Hypsilophodon*, and Cerapoda".
- fonseca2024a: "Jeholosauridae is not recognized as a clade by our analyses, as *Jeholosaurus shangyuanensis* and *Changchunsaurus parvus* are united within Thescelosaurinae, rendering Jeholosauridae as an inapplicable clade".

**Ruled out.**
- *Keeping Jeholosauridae with Yueosaurus as a second member.* Fonseca and colleagues (2024) place *Jeholosaurus* within Thescelosaurinae and *Yueosaurus* outside it, as a successive sister to Orodrominae and Thescelosaurinae, so a family uniting the two asserts one side of the dispute.
- *Thescelosaurinae as the parent.* The earlier analyses place *Jeholosaurus* outside Thescelosauridae.
