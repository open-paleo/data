# *Jianchangosaurus*

## Is *Jianchangosaurus* an immature *Beipiaosaurus*?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Not adopted; *Jianchangosaurus* stays a valid genus. Cau (2024) coded its juvenile holotype in the *Beipiaosaurus* unit and called it a junior synonym, but later analyses read here keep the two apart, with other taxa between them. Governs `status: valid`, `parent: Therizinosauria` and the dispute on [*Jianchangosaurus yixianensis*](../../../genera/J/Jianchangosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pu2013a, Phylogenetic analysis: "Jianchangosaurus yixianensis is more derived than Falcarius utahensis and a sister taxon to the Therizinosauroidea (the clade of Beipiaosaurus inexpectus and higher".
- cau2024a, pp. vi–vii: "The genus Jixiangosaurus (Pu et al., 2013) is considered a junior synonym of Beipiaosaurus (Xu et al., 1999)"; "The holotype of Jianchangosaurus is provisionally coded in the Beipiaosaurus OTU (i.e., used to code the immature features)".
- liao2026a, Phylogenetic results: "K. changliensis as an early branching therizinosaurian positioned between J. yixianensis and B. inexpectus".

**Ruled out.**
- *The synonymy as adopted.* It rests on one analysis, where it is a coding decision, and the later works read here treat the two genera as distinct without citing it.

**Open.** Whether a test of the growth series between the two takes it up.
