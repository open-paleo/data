# *Janenschia*

## Which stages does the *Janenschia* holotype take?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** The [Upper Dinosaur Member](../../../stratigraphy/u/upper-dinosaur.yml)'s range, Kimmeridgian to Berriasian. Every age statement for the holotype dates the member, not Quarry B, so under the rule that a taxon with no statement about its horizon takes its unit's range, the record takes the member's stages. Governs `stage: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian, Berriasian]` and `period: [Late Jurassic, Early Cretaceous]` on [*Janenschia robusta*](../../../genera/J/Janenschia.yml), replacing `[Tithonian]`. Supersedes the 2026-08-05 entry, whose ruling-out of the Kimmeridgian answered Upchurch and colleagues' formation-wide figure; Wild gives the upper Kimmeridgian for the member itself.

**Evidence.**
- wild1991a: "Stratum typicum: Upper Saurian marl, upper Kimmeridgian (Janensch 1961: 188)".
- mannion2019a: "Upper Dinosaur Member (Upper Saurian Bed), Tendaguru Formation; Tithonian, Late Jurassic".
- bussert2009a, p. 165: "More work is needed to decide unambiguously whether the Upper Dinosaur Member is wholly Late Jurassic or, in part, Early Cretaceous."

**Ruled out.**
- *Tithonian alone.* Mannion and colleagues' Tithonian is the member's age, relayed, and the member's own sources range from Wild's upper Kimmeridgian to a possible Berriasian.

**Open.** Whether Wild's upper Kimmeridgian survives Bussert and colleagues' member-by-member dating, which puts the Middle Dinosaur Member below at late Kimmeridgian (bussert2009a, p. 161); that bears on the member's stages, not on this record's rule.

## What age is the *Janenschia* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Tithonian, from the Upper Dinosaur Member at Quarry B, as Mannion and colleagues (2019) give it. Governs `period.stage: [Tithonian]` on [*Janenschia robusta*](../../../genera/J/Janenschia.yml). The member's wider range is discussed in [Tendaguru](../../stratigraphy/t/tendaguru.md).

**Evidence.**
- mannion2019a, Systematic palaeontology, *Janenschia*: "Type locality and horizon: Quarry B, approximately 900 m southeast of Tendaguru Hill …; Upper Dinosaur Member (Upper Saurian Bed), Tendaguru Formation; Tithonian, Late Jurassic".
- jones2026b, p. 375: "Tendaguru Fm., Tanzania *Late Jurassic, Tithonian*".
- upchurch2004a, Table 13.1: *J. robusta*, "Tendaguru Formation (Mtwara), Tanzania", "Kimmeridgian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Kimmeridgian.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) give it for the formation as a whole, with no member.

**Open.** Whether the Upper Dinosaur Member reaches the Berriasian; see the Tendaguru note.
