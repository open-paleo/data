# *Juravenator*

## Which formation is the *Juravenator* holotype from?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** The Torleite Formation, in Niebuhr and Pürner's (2014) scheme for the Franconian Alb, which places the Schamhaupten locality, and *Juravenator* with it, in the Arnstorf Subformation at the top of the Torleite. Göhlich and Chiappe (2006) give only a biostratigraphic horizon. Governs `location.formation: Torleite` on [*Juravenator starki*](../../../genera/J/Juravenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- göhlich2006a, p. 329, Horizon and locality: "Silicified, laminated limestone, Late Jurassic, Upper Malm, Late Kimmeridgian, Malm Epsilon 2 (setatum subzone), Quarry Stark, west of Schamhaupten".
- niebuhr2014a, p. 32: "Arnstorf Subformation of the eastern Southern Frankenalb (… upper section of the Torleite Formation)" (translated); p. 34: "the famous fossil locality Schamhaupten yielded over 200 different taxa (Viohl & Zapp 2006), including the theropod *Juravenator starki*" (translated), under the Arnstorf Subformation.
- foth2020a, Taxon sampling: the holotypes of *Sciurumimus albersdoerferi* and *Juravenator starki* (JME Sch 200) are "from the Torleite Formation (upper Kimmeridgian) of the Jurassic limestones of Bavaria (see Niebuhr & Pürner 2014)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Painten Formation.* Chiappe and Göhlich (2010) follow Viohl and Zapp (2006, 2007) in placing the plattenkalk in "the Late Kimmeridgian Schamhaupten Subformation, which is considered to be part of the Painten Formation" (chiappe2010a, p. 260). That is the older scheme. In Niebuhr and Pürner's, the Painten Formation overlies the Torleite: the Torleite's upper boundary is with the "Geisental and Painten Formations of the Southern Frankenalb" (niebuhr2014a, p. 32, translated). The same change applies at Painten itself (see [*Sciurumimus*](../S/Sciurumimus.md)).

**Open.** Nothing.
