# *Jiangxisaurus*

## Is *Jiangxisaurus* from Tankou Town?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** No. Wei and colleagues (2013) give the type locality as Longling, Nankang. The row of Han and colleagues' (2024) Table 1 headed "Jiangxisaurus ganzhouensis", which gives Tankou Town, describes the sauropod *Jiangxititan ganzhouensis* and credits that taxon's describing paper. Governs `location.locality: Longling, Nankang` on [*Jiangxisaurus ganzhouensis*](../../../genera/J/Jiangxisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wei2013a, p. 900: "Type Locality and horizon: Longling, Nankang of Ganzhou City, Jiangxi Province; Red sandstones of Upper Cretaceous Nanxiong Formation".
- han2024a, p. 3, Table 1 (continued): "Jiangxisaurus ganzhouensis", "Three posterior-most cervicals and four anteriormost dorsals", "Tankou Town, Nankang County, Ganzhou City, Jiangxi Province; Nanxiong Formation", "Lognkosauria", "Mo, Fu, et al. (2023)".
- mo2023a, p. 2443: the *Jiangxititan* holotype comes from Tankou Town, "about five kilometres away from Longling Town, the locality of Gannansaurus".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tankou Town.* The table row's material, classification and source are those of *Jiangxititan* (mo2023a). *Jiangxisaurus* is an oviraptorid known from a partial skull and skeleton (wei2013a, p. 900).

**Open.** Nothing.

## How is the *Jiangxisaurus* holotype's number written?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** HGM 41HIII-0421, the house form of the number Wei and colleagues (2013) print as HGM41HIII0421. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [HGM 41HIII-0421]` on [*Jiangxisaurus ganzhouensis*](../../../genera/J/Jiangxisaurus.yml), written in the same form as [*Huanansaurus*](../../../genera/H/Huanansaurus.yml)'s.

**Evidence.**
- wei2013a, p. 900: "Holotype: An incomplete skeleton (HGM41HIII0421) with a partial skull".
- jones2026c, Oviraptoridae table: "HGM 41HIII-0421—partial skull and skeleton".
- molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods, Oviraptorosauria: "HGM41HIII0421".

**Ruled out.**
- *HGM41-HIII0421.* The record once held this, which splits the number in a place no source does. Jones (2026) has the house form.

**Open.** Nothing.
