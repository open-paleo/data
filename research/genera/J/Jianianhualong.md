# *Jianianhualong*

## Is *Jianianhualong* a sinovenatorine?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it sits at Troodontidae incertae sedis. Analyses split about evenly on its membership in Sinovenatorinae. Governs `parent: Troodontidae`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Jianianhualong tengi*](../../../genera/J/Jianianhualong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hartman2019a, p. 35: a member of Sinovenatorinae; one step moves it to the troodontines.
- cau2021a, p. 14: outside Sinovenatorinae, in a more derived clade.

**Ruled out.**
- *Sinovenatorinae as the parent.* Cau and Madzia (2021), Pei and colleagues (2021) and Wang and colleagues (2023) place it outside.

**Open.** Whether the matrices converge.

## How is the *Jianianhualong* locality written?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** "Baicaigou", the closed-up form used for the same place on [*Jinzhousaurus*](../../../genera/J/Jinzhousaurus.yml) and in the notes on the site; the dataset writes one form for a place. Governs `location.locality: Baicaigou` on [*Jianianhualong tengi*](../../../genera/J/Jianianhualong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2017b, p. 2: the holotype is from "Baicai Gou".
- wang2001a, p. 1669: the *Jinzhousaurus* holotype is from Baicaigou.

**Ruled out.**
- *"Baicai Gou".* It is one paper's spacing of the same name.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Does *Jianianhualong* take the member of the *Jinzhousaurus* site at Baicaigou?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** No member is recorded. Xu and colleagues (2017) place the holotype at Baicai Gou in the Yixian Formation and name no member. Baicaigou is also the *Jinzhousaurus* type site, which Wang and Xu (2001) place in the Dakangpu, recorded as the Dawangzhangzi Member (see [Dawangzhangzi](../../stratigraphy/d/dawangzhangzi.md)); giving DLXH 1218 that member would rest on the shared place name alone. Governs the empty `location.member` and `location.locality: Baicai Gou` on [*Jianianhualong tengi*](../../../genera/J/Jianianhualong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2017b, p. 2, Locality and horizon: "Baicai Gou … Yixian County, western Liaoning, China; Lower Cretaceous Yixian Formation", with a dating paper (Swisher and colleagues 2001) as the only reference for the horizon.
- wang2001a, p. 1669, for IVPP V12691: "Baicaigou, Toutai, Yixian County, Liaoning; Dakangpu member, the middle part of the Yixian Formation (comparable to Dawangzhangzi Bed)".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Baicaigou, Toutai" as this holotype's locality line.* It is Wang and Xu's line for the *Jinzhousaurus* holotype. Xu and colleagues (2017) do not write Toutai, a township with several fossil sites.

**Open.** Whether DLXH 1218 came from the *Jinzhousaurus* beds. The Toutai sites may form one locality complex whose levels differ (see the Open part of the Dawangzhangzi note), so the site name does not fix the horizon. A paper that places this specimen in a member would settle it.
