# *Jaculinykus*

## Do the Red Walls and Fanti and colleagues' coordinate belong to the *Jaculinykus* holotype?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** No source ties either to it. Kubo and colleagues (2023) name the type locality only as the Nemegt locality and keep the site's coordinates with the specimen at the Institute of Paleontology in Ulaanbaatar. The Red Walls, and a pair within about 15 m of the coordinate once stored for *Jaculinykus*, are where Fanti and colleagues (2012) place MPC-D 107/16, a referred specimen of *Nemegtomaia* (see [*Nemegtomaia*](../N/Nemegtomaia.md)). Governs `location.locality: Nemegt` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Jaculinykus yaruui*](../../../genera/J/Jaculinykus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kubo2023a, p. 6, Type locality and horizon: "Nemegt locality [35,36,42], Ömnögovi Province, Mongolia"; p. 2: "Precise GPS coordinates of the site are accessioned with MPC-D 100/209 at the IP-MAS, where they are available upon request."
- fanti2012a, p. 4, Methods: MPC-D 107/16 "was collected from the uppermost layers of the Baruungoyot Formation in the eastern portion of the Nemegt locality from the Red Walls (N 43°30.327′, E 101°04.706′)".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Red Walls pair as the holotype's position.* Fanti and colleagues print it for MPC-D 107/16, and Kubo and colleagues hold the *Jaculinykus* position back.

**Open.** Whether the holotype came from the Red Walls. Both specimens are from the upper Baruungoyot at the Nemegt locality, and nothing read for this note places *Jaculinykus* inside or outside the Red Walls area. The coordinates accessioned with MPC-D 100/209 would settle it.
