# *Jinyunpelta*

## Is the *Jinyunpelta* holotype from a quarry at Shuhong?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** No. Zheng and colleagues (2018) give the locality as Lijin Industrial Park, Huzhen Town. Shuhong appears in the paper only on the locality map, as a town southwest of the fossil site, and in the map caption, which names the Shuhong basin beside the Huzhen basin. Governs `location.locality: Lijin Industrial Park, Huzhen Town` on [*Jinyunpelta sinensis*](../../../genera/J/Jinyunpelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zheng2018a, p. 3, Systematic palaeontology: "Locality: Lijin Industrial Park, Huzhen Town, Jinyun County, Zhejiang Province, China (Fig. 1)."
- zheng2018a, p. 1: a farmer "found a new ankylosaurian fossil in a construction site in Huzhen, Jinyun", and excavations followed "at this site, and others, in Huzhen Town between 2008 and 2014".
- zheng2018a, p. 2, Fig. 1: "(d) Geological map of Huzhen and Shuhong basins in Jinyun County showing type locality of Jinyunpelta sinensis (marked by a star)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Shuhong Quarry.* The paper names no quarry at Shuhong. On Fig. 1c and 1d the star for the fossil site sits beside Huzhen, and Shuhong is a separate town to the southwest (read from the maps).

**Open.** Nothing.
