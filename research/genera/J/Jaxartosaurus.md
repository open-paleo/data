# *Jaxartosaurus*

## Which unit, and which of the two Kyrkkuduk sites, is the *Jaxartosaurus* type material from?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** The Syuksyuk Formation at Kyrkkuduk I, on Nessov (1995), the only source read for this note that assigns the site a unit. Riabinin (1939) gives the horizon only as Upper Cretaceous conglomerates, and Rozhdestvensky (1968) names no unit but places the type material in the "dinosaur horizon", below the *Procheneosaurus convincens* skeleton; the Stratigraphic Dictionary of the USSR (1979) says most of the Syuksyuk corresponds to the Dinosaur Horizon. That Rozhdestvensky's horizon and the dictionary's are the same is our reading. Governs `location.formation: Syuksyuk`, `location.locality: Kyrkkuduk I` and `stage: [Coniacian, Santonian]` on [*Jaxartosaurus aralensis*](../../../genera/J/Jaxartosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- riabinin1939a, p. 8: "Locality. The Kyrk-Kuduk gorge, in the region of the Alym-Tau ridge, west of the Sary-Agach station in Kazakhstan. Geological age. Upper Cretaceous conglomerates (Cenomanian-Turonian)" (translated).
- rozhdestvensky1968a, Genus *Jaxartosaurus*: the lectotype, No. 1/5009, is from the "Upper Cretaceous (Coniacian–Santonian) of southern Kazakhstan (Kyrk-Kuduk region near the Alym-Tau range)"; Geologic Age of the Bone-bearing Layers: the age "can be placed not in the Cenomanian–Turonian interval as Rjabinin (1939) thought, but as Coniacian if not Santonian", the upper limit set by *P. convincens*, found "immediately above the 'dinosaur horizon' with *Jaxartosaurus*" (translated).
- nessov1995a, p. 102, locality 107: "Kyrkkuduk I, area about 2 km in length along the northern foot of the Alymtau Ridge, 6-7 km south of the Kyrkkuduk well (Prinada, 1927), but, in our opinion, also 3-5 km southwest of the Kyrkkuduk well … Syuk-Syuk Formation. Upper Cretaceous, Santonian"; p. 104: "*Jaxartosaurus aralensis* Riab. from Kyrkkuduk I" (translated).
- vereshchagin1979a, p. 378, Syuksyuk Formation: "most of the S. Formation corresponds to the Dinosaur Horizon, and its top to the base of the Lower Darbazin subformation" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Kyrkkuduk II, and the Darbazin there.* Nessov's locality 142 is "Kyrkkuduk II, northern foot of Alym Tau Ridge south of the Kyrkkuduk well, site 'Gray Meza' … Lower Darbazin subformation … lower Campanian? - middle Campanian" (nessov1995a, p. 109, translated), a second site with small theropods, *Brachychampsa* and young hadrosaurids, and no *Jaxartosaurus*.
- *Dabrazinskaya Svita.* Weishampel and colleagues (2004) list "Jaxartosaurus aralensis (including Procheneosaurus convincens)" under the "Dabrazinskaya Svita" (weishampel2004b, p. 594). The unit comes with the merger of the two taxa: *P. convincens*, now *Kazaklambia*, is from the Syuk-Syuk wells (see [*Kazaklambia*](../K/Kazaklambia.md)), above the *Jaxartosaurus* horizon on Rozhdestvensky's account. Bell and Brink (2013) give "the Kyrk–Kuduk locality (Dabrazinskaya Svita, lower Santonian)" (bell2013a, pp. 271–272), citing Godefroit and colleagues (2004), while their introduction puts *Jaxartosaurus* "from a slightly lower (Santonian) stratigraphic level" than *P. convincens* (bell2013a, p. 266).
- *Cenomanian–Turonian.* Riabinin's (1939) age; Rozhdestvensky (1968) rejects it by name.

**Open.** What Godefroit and colleagues (2004), the redescription Bell and Brink cite, say about the unit; it has not been read. Nessov also writes that "Some of the localities Nos. 107-117 may be synonymous" (nessov1995a, p. 103, translated) and gives two positions for Kyrkkuduk I, so the site itself is not fixed on the ground.
