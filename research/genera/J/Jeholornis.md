# *Jeholornis*

## Does *Jeholornis curvipes* take the Yizhou Fossil and Geology Park as its locality?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** No. The describing paper gives the park as the type locality, but the same paper lists it as the repository, and does not say the specimen was dug there. An institution's name on its own is where a specimen is kept, not where it was found. Governs the empty `location.locality` and the location note on *Jeholornis curvipes* in [*Jeholornis*](../../../genera/J/Jeholornis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lefèvre2014a, p. 791: "*Type locality and horizon:* Yizhou Fossil and Geology Park, Liaoning Province, (north-eastern China) Dakangpu Member (equivalent to the Dawangzhangzi Beds) of the Yixian Formation".
- lefèvre2014a, Institutional abbreviations: "YFGP, Yizhou Fossil and Geology Park, Yizhou, China"; the holotype is "YFGP-yb2".

**Ruled out.**
- *The park as the locality.* It is the repository's name, and the paper does not say the park is the dig site.

**Open.** Where the holotype was dug. A paper placing the specimen at a named quarry would settle it.
