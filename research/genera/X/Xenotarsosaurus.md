# *Xenotarsosaurus*

## What stages does *Xenotarsosaurus* take?

*2026-10-10*

**Conclusion.** Cenomanian and Turonian, from Martínez and colleagues' (2004) dating of the same horizon at Ocho Hermanos; it replaces the "pre-Maastrichtian Senonian" the describing paper gave, by largely the same authors. Governs `stage: [Cenomanian, Turonian]` on [*Xenotarsosaurus bonapartei*](../../../genera/X/Xenotarsosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martínez1986a: "Upper part of the lower member of the Bajo Barreal Formation, pre-Maastrichtian Senonian of Chubut, Argentina" (translated).
- martínez2004a: "Upper portion of the Lower Member of the Bajo Barreal Formation (Upper Cretaceous: late Cenomanian-early Turonian [Archangelsky et al., 1994; Bridge et al., 2000; Lamanna et al., 2002])"; the "Ocho Hermanos" tetrapod assemblage "includes … the ceratosauroid theropod *Xenotarsosaurus* (Martínez et al., 1986)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Coniacian to Campanian.* The Senonian is the authors' own earlier reading, revised by them for the same horizon; counting it again would count one origin twice.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which institution holds the second *Xenotarsosaurus bonapartei* type number?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** The Universidad Nacional de la Patagonia San Juan Bosco, under both numbers. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [UNPSJB-PV 184, UNPSJB-PV 612]` on [*Xenotarsosaurus bonapartei*](../../../genera/X/Xenotarsosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martínez1986a, Holotype: "Two incomplete cervical-dorsal vertebrae, femur, tibia, fibula, complete astragalocalcaneum of the right side, belonging to the same individual; collection of paleovertebrates of the Universidad Nacional de la Patagonia San Juan Bosco (Comodoro Rivadavia - U. N. P. S. J. B. - Pv. 184 and U. N. P. S. J. B. - Pv. 612)" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *PVL 612.* Jones (2026) gives "PVL 612—partial leg; UNPSJB PV 184—two dorsal vertebrae" (jones2026c, Abelisauridae and Brachyrostra table), and Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give "PVL 612" (molina-pérez2019a, Abelisauridae). PVL is the Fundación-Instituto Miguel Lillo's prefix; the describing paper puts both numbers in the UNPSJB collection, as one individual.

**Open.** Nothing.
