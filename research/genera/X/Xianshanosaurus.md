# *Xianshanosaurus*

## What places the Shijiagou Quarry in the Haoling Formation?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** Haoling is kept as the current name for the part of the old "Mangchuan Formation" that yields the Ruyang sauropods, but no source read places the Shijiagou Quarry in Xu and colleagues' (2012) three-formation scheme. Governs `location.formation: Haoling`, and the Aptian–Albian stage that follows it, on [*Xianshanosaurus shijiagouensis*](../../../genera/X/Xianshanosaurus.yml) and [*Luoyanggia liudianensis*](../../../genera/L/Luoyanggia.yml), whose holotype comes from the same quarry.

**Evidence.**
- lü2009b, p. 52: "Shijiagou of Haoling Village, Liudian Town, Ruyang County of Henan Province. Mangchuan Formation", and "The upper unit of the deposits ( ? Early Late Cretaceous) of this study"; p. 50 gives the *Luoyanggia* holotype "A quarry at Shijiagou village" and the same upper unit.
- lü2013b, p. 203: the "'Mangchuan Formation', can be subdivided into three units (formations), in ascending stratigraphical order: the Xiahedong, Haoling and Shangdonggou formations"; p. 211: "Xianshanosaurus has been recorded from same formation as the new genus".
- han2024a, p. 18: *Xianshanosaurus* is one of "Four titanosauriforms ... known from the Lower Cretaceous Haoling Formation of Henan Province", citing the 2009 paper.

**Ruled out.**
- *Lü and colleagues (2013) as an independent placement in the Haoling.* Their "same formation" sentence cites the 2009 paper, which names only the "Mangchuan Formation", and the 2013 paper's own horizon line for *Yunmenglong* still reads "'Mangchuan Formation', late Early to early Late Cretaceous" (p. 204). The sentence may mean the old Mangchuan; reading it as a placement in the Haoling is an inference.

**Open.** Whether the quarry is Haoling or Shangdonggou. Lü and colleagues (2009) put both holotypes in their "upper unit", which "is characterized by several layers of light-colored thick-layered conglomerates", while "The sauropod dinosaurs and small theropod dinosaurs are rich in the lower unit" (p. 56). Their quarry section shows "Brownish red silty mudstone, dinosaurs were found from lower part of the Layer 1" between "Light-colored thick-layered conglomerates" (p. 48, Fig. 4). The 2013 paper describes the Haoling as "massive, brownish red muddy siltstones" (p. 203), which fits the fossil layer, and maps the upper part of the old Mangchuan to "the early Late Cretaceous Shangdonggou Formation" (p. 204), which fits the 2009 label. Xu and colleagues (2012), who erect the three formations, would settle it; that paper has not been read.
