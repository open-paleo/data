# *Xixiposaurus*

## Where does *Xixiposaurus* sit?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** Plateosauria incertae sedis. The describing paper's analysis places it in a Plateosauridae that includes *Plateosaurus*, which falls outside Massopoda as Massopoda is defined; later papers call it a possible massospondylid without analyzing it; the two recent analyses that include it find it unstable. Plateosauria is the level shared by the plateosaurid and massospondylid readings. Governs `parent: Plateosauria` and the dispute on [*Xixiposaurus*](../../../genera/X/Xixiposaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sekiya2010a: "Although Mussaurus is the sister taxon of Xixiposaurus"; "Gyposaurus moved into Plateosauridae, which includes Coloradisaurus, Mussaurus, Xixiposaurus gen. sp., Gyposaurus, Plateosaurus and Riojasaurus."
- wang2025a: "the medium-sized massospondylid Lufengosaurus (and possibly Xixiposaurus)".
- hu2026a: "the massospondylid members including Lufengosaurus and putative Xixiposaurus".
- barrett2026a: "Application of iterPCR identified six taxa (Agnosphitys, Guaibasaurus, Xixiposaurus, Sefapanosaurus, Irisosaurus and Blikanasaurus) as having multiple most-parsimonious positions"; barrett2024a likewise excludes it from the reduced consensus.

**Ruled out.**
- *Massopoda.* The earlier parent; no paper read places it there, and the describing paper's plateosaurid result falls outside it.
- *Never included in a phylogenetic analysis.* Jones (2026) says so (jones2026b), but sekiya2010a, barrett2024a and barrett2026a all include it.

**Open.** Where Barrett and colleagues' equally parsimonious positions fall; their supplementary trees have not been read.

## Is the Xixiposaurus holotype ZLJ0108 or ZLJ01018?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** ZLJ0108; "ZLJ01018" is a misprint in the same paper. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: ZLJ0108` on *Xixiposaurus suni*.

**Evidence.**
- sekiya2010a, p. 7: "正型标本：一件保存有头骨和完整的头后骨。标本号：ZLJ0108" ("Holotype: one specimen preserving the skull and complete postcranium. Specimen number: ZLJ0108", translated); the English summary (p. 14) gives "The holotype (ZLJ0108)".
- peyredefabrègues2020a, p. 4, Fig. 4: "Xixiposaurus suni (holotype ZLJ0108, modified from Sekiya".

**Ruled out.**
- *ZLJ01018.* The describing paper prints it in its Table 1 and 2 captions (p. 10) and once in the English summary (p. 14: "ZLJ01018 bears four premaxilla teeth"), against ZLJ0108 in the designation; Jones (2026) copies it (jones2026b).

**Open.** Nothing. (Shorter ZLJ numbers do occur in the series, so the length alone does not mark an error; the designation line does.)
