# *Xinjiangtitan*

## What stages does *Xinjiangtitan* take?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** Callovian and Oxfordian, on Maisch and Matzke's (2019) hedged reading of this horizon, the only primary statement that gives it stages. Every other stage on offer dates the Junggar Basin Qigu, whose correlation with the Turpan Basin beds the sources doubt, or comes from a compilation. Supersedes the Open lines of the two entries below. Governs `period` and `stage: [Callovian, Oxfordian]` on [*Xinjiangtitan shanshanesis*](../../../genera/X/Xinjiangtitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maisch2019a, p. 115: "As far as stratigraphic comparisons can be made, bearing the insecurities of dating and correlation, particularly in the Turpan Basin, in mind, *Xinjiangtitan* appears to be the oldest form (Callovian or Oxfordian)."
- upchurch2021a: "there is currently no explicit evidence that the putative outcrops of the Qigu Formation in the Turpan Basin correlate with those in the Junggar Basin".

**Ruled out.**
- *Callovian alone.* It rests on compilations (jones2026b).
- *A Junggar date.* Wu and colleagues' (2013) 164.6 Ma tuff and the 151 Ma that Zhang and colleagues (2022) relay are Junggar Basin values; the second is also a maximum depositional age.

**Open.** The Qigu Formation's own stages, which would have to cover the Junggar and Turpan beds under one name.

## Do Upchurch and colleagues (2021) give the Qiketai for *Xinjiangtitan*?

*2026-10-10*

**Conclusion.** Yes, as a relay of Maisch and Matzke (2019). This corrects the earlier 2026-10-10 entry, which ruled them out as a Qiketai source; they add no independent evidence, so its conclusion, the Qigu, stands. Governs the `upchurch2021a` reference on [*Xinjiangtitan shanshanesis*](../../../genera/X/Xinjiangtitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- upchurch2021a: "the fact that Xinjiangtitan was recovered from the Qiketai Formation (Maisch et al., 2019) while Hudiesaurus was found in the overlying Kalazha Formation, lends additional support to the retention of their taxonomic separation."

**Ruled out.**
- *Upchurch and colleagues as an independent Qiketai reading.* They cite Maisch and Matzke for it.

**Open.** As in the earlier entry: the stage.

## Which formation holds the *Xinjiangtitan* holotype?

*2026-10-10*

**Conclusion.** The Qigu Formation, the describing paper's assignment, which both redescriptions repeat. The Qiketai reading goes back to Maisch and Matzke (2019), who give no source for it; later compilations repeat them. Qiketai is also the town near the quarry. Governs `location.formation: Qigu` on [*Xinjiangtitan shanshanesis*](../../../genera/X/Xinjiangtitan.yml) and the new [Qigu](../../../stratigraphy/q/qigu.yml) entry.

**Evidence.**
- wu2013a: the holotype comes from "the Middle Jurassic Qigu Formation"; the Shanshan strata run "from bottom to top" through the "Xishancun Formation, Sanjianfang Formation, Qiketai Formation, Qigu Formation, and the Upper Jurassic Kalazha Formation".
- zhang2018b: "Wu et al. (2013) considered that the fossil bed was from the Qigu Formation".
- zhang2022a: "The horizon was from the brown – red, grey – green argillaceous siltstone at the middle beds of the Qigu Formation".
- maisch2019a: the holotype comes "from strata attributed to the Qiketai Formation", and "Xing et al. (2015) erroneously stated that X. shanshanensis was recovered from the Qigu Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Qiketai Formation.* It rests on Maisch and Matzke's attribution, which names no source and does not address the describing paper's own Qigu.
- *Upchurch and colleagues (2021) as a Qiketai source.* Their "Kalazha Formation … of Qiketai" is the *Hudiesaurus* horizon, with Qiketai as the village.

**Open.** The stage. The record's Callovian has no source among those cited, and the Qigu ages on offer are Junggar Basin dates whose correlation with the Turpan Basin is doubted (upchurch2021a).
