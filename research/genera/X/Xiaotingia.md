# *Xiaotingia*

## Is the *Xiaotingia* holotype from the Daxishan site or the Yaolugou site?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** From neither, as far as any source can say: the quarry is unknown, and the locality stays at the Linglongta area the dealer named. Governs `location.locality: Linglongta` on [*Xiaotingia zhengi*](../../../genera/X/Xiaotingia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2011d, Supplementary Information, section 2: the specimen "was acquired by the Shandong Tianyu Museum of Nature from a fossil dealer, according to whom the specimen was collected in the Linglongta area", and "he could not provide accurate information as to the quarry in which the specimen was collected."
- xu2011d, Supplementary Information, section 2: Daxishan appears only as the comparison site, chosen because its Tiaojishan beds "have been investigated more extensively than those of other localities".

**Ruled out.**
- *Daxishan.* The biogeography appendix by Ding and colleagues in Pittman and Xu (2020) lists "Daxishan, Linglongta, Jianchang Liaoning, China" for *Xiaotingia zhengi* (pittman2020a, p. 157, Appendix), with no source for the row. The describing paper's own account of the specimen says the quarry was never identified.
- *Yaolugou.* Peng and colleagues (2012) report *Tianyulong* from "the Yaolugou site" and *Anchiornis* "from the same location", and add that Xu and colleagues (2011) "reported an *Archaeopteryx*-like theropod (*Xiaotingia zhengi*) from that locality as well" (peng2012a, 1 Introduction). The 2011 paper and its supplement name no quarry, and Peng and colleagues' own results date the Daxishan and Yaolugou horizons separately (peng2012a, 4.2).

**Open.** The quarry, and with it the formation, which rests on slab lithology (see the record's location note). The supplement names what would settle both: "additional specimens with definite locality information, or micro-sedimentary analysis of the slab".
