# *Xinjiangovenator*

## Which Wuerho field locality is the *Xinjiangovenator* holotype from?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** Field locality 64041-9, from Dong's (1973) catalogue of the hind limb, not 64041, the site of the tooth that is now the *Phaedrolosaurus* lectotype. Governs `location.locality: Wuerho, field locality 64041-9` on [*Xinjiangovenator parvus*](../../../genera/X/Xinjiangovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1973a, p. 46: "One complete tooth, field locality number 64041, Institute of Vertebrate Paleontology catalogue number V. 4024-1; a pair of tibia-fibula with associated astragalus and calcaneum, field locality number 64041-9, catalogue number V. 4024-2" (translated).
- rauhut2005c, p. 112: "Holotype-IVPP 4024-2, articulated partial right hind limb. Locality and Horizon-Wuerho, Lianmugin Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Field locality 64041.* It is the tooth's number in the same sentence of Dong (1973), and the tooth, "IVPP V 4024-1, isolated tooth", is the lectotype of [*Phaedrolosaurus ilikensis*](../../../genera/P/Phaedrolosaurus.yml) (rauhut2005c, p. 111). Dong listed both elements as the material of *Phaedrolosaurus*, so a reading that takes the first number for the whole entry gives the hind limb the tooth's site.

**Open.** Nothing.
