# *Xiyunykus*

## Is the *Xiyunykus* holotype from the Shengjinkou Formation?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** No source places the holotype in any formation. Xu and colleagues (2018) give only the upper part of the Tugulu Group at Wucaiwan, so the record stays at group rank. Governs `location.group: Tugulu`, `location.part: upper` and the absence of `location.formation` on [*Xiyunykus pengi*](../../../genera/X/Xiyunykus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2018c, p. 2853, Locality and Horizon: "Wucaiwan area, Junggar Basin, Xinjiang, China; Upper Cretaceous (Barremian-Aptian?) upper part of the Tugulu Group", citing Eberth and colleagues (2001).
- eberth2001a, p. 1642: "the productive Tugulu Formation localities in the Huoxiaoshan area (Wucaiwan, Pingfengshan, and Huangnitan localities)". The work Xu and colleagues cite for the horizon names no subdivision of the Tugulu at Wucaiwan.

**Ruled out.**
- *That the Shengjinkou lies only south of the Tian Shan, and so cannot hold a Wucaiwan specimen.* Wang and colleagues (2021) write of "the Shengjinkou Formation of the Tugulu Group, which is distributed in the Turpan-Hami Basin, south of the Tian Shan Mountains" (wang2021a, p. 2), and list *Xiyunykus* among the Tugulu Group taxa "of the Junggar Basin, north of the Tian Shan Mountains" (p. 1). The sentence places the Hami sites; it does not confine the formation. Zheng and colleagues (2023) give the Tugulu Group "within the southern Junggar Basin proper" as "the Qingshuihe, Hutubihe, Shengjinkou, and Lianmuqin Formations", and use a tuff to separate "the Shengjinkou and Lianmuqin Formations in the NW Junggar Basin" (zheng2023a, p. 2). The Shengjinkou is a Junggar unit too, so geography does not exclude it.

**Open.** Which formation of the upper Tugulu holds the holotype. The Shengjinkou and the Lianmuqin are the upper two formations of the Junggar scheme, but no source read assigns the specimen to either, or says whether the group is divided at Wucaiwan. A measured section at the quarry, tied to the Junggar formations, would settle it.
