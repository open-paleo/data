# *Xingxiulong*

## Which stages does the *Xingxiulong yueorum* holotype take?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** The [Zhangjiawa Member](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhangjiawa.yml)'s, Hettangian to Toarcian. Chen and colleagues (2025), who name the species, give its horizon only as the Early Jurassic Lufeng Formation, and the record names the member, so it takes the member's range (see [Zhangjiawa](../../stratigraphy/z/zhangjiawa.md), whose Toarcian is disputed). Governs `stage: [Hettangian, Sinemurian, Pliensbachian, Toarcian]` and `resolution: unit` on [*Xingxiulong yueorum*](../../../genera/X/Xingxiulong.yml), replacing `[Hettangian, Sinemurian]`.

**Evidence.**
- chen2025b, Abstract: "a new species of the early-diverging sauropodomorph *Xingxiulong* from the Lower Jurassic Lufeng Formation is erected on the basis of a postcranial skeleton".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hettangian–Sinemurian as the holotype's own reading.* No work the record cites gives the horizon a stage.

**Open.** Nothing.
