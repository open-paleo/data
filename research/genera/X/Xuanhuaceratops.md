# *Xuanhuaceratops*

## Is *Xuanhuaceratops* from the Houcheng or the Tuchengzi Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Houcheng. The Houcheng and Tuchengzi are laterally equivalent strata under two regional names, and the type locality, Yanjiagou, is in Hebei, where the describing paper and later work use Houcheng. Jones (2026) agrees ("Houcheng Fm., China", jones2026a, Ceratopsia table). Governs `location.formation: Houcheng` on [*Xuanhuaceratops niei*](../../../genera/X/Xuanhuaceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhao2006a, Systematic Paleontology, Horizon and Locality: "Houcheng Formation, probably Late Jurassic, Yanjiagou, Xuanhua Area, Hebei Province, China. The Houcheng Formation is generally considered to be Late Jurassic in age and laterally equivalent to the Tuchengzi Formation (Wang, 1998)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tuchengzi Formation.* The record held it with no source. Zhao and colleagues (2006) call the Tuchengzi the lateral equivalent, not the unit at Yanjiagou. The two names are not spellings of one name, so the Tuchengzi is not a variant of the Houcheng.

**Open.** Nothing.
