# *Kulceratops*

## Which site, and which part of Uzbekistan, is the *Kulceratops* holotype from?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** Site SH-21 at Khodzhakul, in Karakalpakstan. Nessov (1995) gives SH-21 in the holotype paragraph and SH-20 in two plate explanations. His locality list treats SH-20 and SH-21 as sites of one locality, so the two numbers do not place the specimen in different places; the record follows the paragraph that designates the type. Governs `location.locality` and `location.region` on [*Kulceratops kulensis*](../../../genera/K/Kulceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nessov1995a, p. 53 (translated): "Holotype. - CCMGE No. 495/12457, left maxilla, with broken anterior process; Karakalpakistan, ravines northeast of the Khodzhakul settlement, Lower Cretaceous, upper Albian, lower or middle part of the Khodzhakul Formation, site SH-21."
- nessov1995a, p. 88 (translated), locality 29, Khodzhakul I, Karakalpakistan: "At the same locality, lower or middle part of the Khodzhakul Formation ..., sites SH-20 and SH-21."
- nessov1995a, p. 147, Plate IV (translated): "left maxilla of the early protoceratopsid *Kulceratops kulensis* gen. n., sp. n, site SH-20, holotype, No. 495".
- nessov1995a, p. 151, Plate XIV (translated): "Found here (at site SH-20, marked by crosses) were a maxilla of the protoceratopsid *Kulceratops kulensis*".

**Ruled out.**
- *SH-20.* It is the number in both plate explanations, but the systematic paragraph that designates the type gives SH-21.
- *Navoiy (UZ-NW).* No source cited for the taxon places it there. Nessov puts the holotype, the type species and the locality in Karakalpakistan each time he names a region (pp. 52–53, 88).

**Open.** Which of the two sites yielded the maxilla. The internal conflict is Nessov's own, and a field catalogue or a later study of the CCMGE specimen would settle it.
