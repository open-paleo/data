# *Kotasaurus*

## Is the coordinate printed for the *Kotasaurus* site in degrees and minutes?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** Yes, on the evidence of the same paper's map. Kareem and colleagues (2024) give the site, after Yadagiri (1988), "at the coordinates 19.07N, 79.51E". Read as 19°07′N, 79°51′E, the figure converts to 19.116667, 79.85 (our conversion), and that is where their own map plots the site. Read as decimal degrees it lies about 36 km further west, beyond the west edge of the same map. The record carries no coordinate. Governs `location.coordinates`, and supports `location.region: IN-TS`, on [*Kotasaurus yamanpalliensis*](../../../genera/K/Kotasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kareem2024a, p. e2396814-2, Type Locality and Horizon: "The exact locality of the Kotasaurus site is reported (Yadagiri, 1988) to be 5 km west of Yamanpalli village, at the coordinates 19.07N, 79.51E"; "a village by the name of Vemanpally exists at these coordinates".
- kareem2024a, p. e2396814-2, Fig. 1: the map is graduated at 19°00′ and 80°00′ and has a 10 km scale bar. It plots the *Kotasaurus* locality about 12 km north of 19°00′ and about 17 km west of 80°00′, near 19°07′N, 79°50′E (our measurement). The *Barapasaurus* locality on the same map plots near 18°44′N, 80°06′E, close to the position stored for [*Barapasaurus tagorei*](../../../genera/B/Barapasaurus.yml), which tests the graduation.
- kareem2024a, p. e2396814-2, Fossil Materials: the fossils "were collected by the GSI SR in the 1970s near the village of Yamanpalli (present day Vemanpally) in Telangana State".

**Ruled out.**
- *19.07, 79.51 as decimal degrees.* That point is about 52 km west of the 80°00′ meridian, while Fig. 1 plots the site within 20 km of it, and Kareem and colleagues state that Vemanpally lies at the printed coordinates. Read this way, the site would not be near the village the paper names.
- *Maharashtra (IN-MH).* Yadagiri (2001) places the site "40 km north of the type locality of Barapasaurus", a Maharashtra locality, (yadagiri2001a, Discussion), which can suggest the same state. Kareem and colleagues place the collection in Telangana State.

**Open.** Yadagiri (1988), the source Kareem and colleagues cite for the figure, has not been read. It would show whether the original prints degree and minute marks. Until it is read, the degree-and-minute reading rests on Kareem and colleagues' map, not on a printed value in that notation.
