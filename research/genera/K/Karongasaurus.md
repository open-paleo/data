# *Karongasaurus*

## Which unit and part is the *Karongasaurus* holotype from?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** The upper part of the Dinosaur Beds. The describing paper places all its specimens in the Dinosaur Beds and the holotype in quarry CD-9, which Winkler and colleagues put in the upper member. Governs `location.formation: Dinosaur Beds`, `location.part: upper` and the location notes on [*Karongasaurus gittelmani*](../../../genera/K/Karongasaurus.yml), matching [*Malawisaurus dixeyi*](../../../genera/M/Malawisaurus.yml) from the same quarry. The unit's empty rank is governed by the [Dinosaur Beds](../../stratigraphy/d/dinosaur-beds.md) note.

**Evidence.**
- gomani2005a, Introduction: "All the specimens described here were collected from the same area of the Dinosaur Beds (Dixey 1928; Jacobs et al. 1990, 1992) near Mwakasyunguti"; Table 1 lists the holotype dentary, Mal-175, from CD-9.
- winkler2000a: quarries CD-1 and CD-6 lie "near the base of the upper member, well below the level of CD-9".
- dixey1928a, p. 55: the beds were "a new formation … that had not previously been recognised", to be separated "from the Karroo on the one hand and from the so-called Recent on the other".

**Ruled out.**
- *No unit, as "a working label for the fossiliferous Karoo-aged beds … not a unit anyone erected".* The record's earlier note; no source read uses these words, Dixey erected the beds and set them apart from the Karroo, and every later paper read dates them Early Cretaceous.

**Open.** Nothing.
