# *Kinnareemimus*

## What age is the *Kinnareemimus* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Valanginian to Hauterivian, the Sao Khua age of Tucker and colleagues (2022); see [Sao Khua](../../stratigraphy/s/sao-khua.md) for the reasoning and its limits. Governs `period.stage: [Valanginian, Hauterivian]` on [*Kinnareemimus khonkaenensis*](../../../genera/K/Kinnareemimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tucker2022a, p. 15: "a tightly constrained late Valanginian–early Hauterivian age of 133.6–132.1 Ma for Sao Khua Formation".
- jones2026c, p. 503: "Sao Khua Fm., Thailand *Early Cretaceous, Valanginian/Hauterivian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian.* The record once held it. It traces to Tumpeesuwan and colleagues' (2010) bivalve correlation, which the Sao Khua note rejects. Jones (2026) gives the Valanginian–Hauterivian reading.

**Open.** Tucker and colleagues sampled the middle and upper formation, not the Phu Wiang sites, so no date is specific to this horizon; the Sao Khua note's Open applies.

## What is the *Kinnareemimus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** PW5A-100, one left third metatarsal, from Buffetaut and colleagues (2009). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [PW5A-100]` on [*Kinnareemimus khonkaenensis*](../../../genera/K/Kinnareemimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- buffetaut2009a, p. 230: "Holotype. An incomplete left third metatarsal, with the distal end and part of the shaft (PW5A-100)", followed by "Referred material", which runs from PW5A-101 to PW5A-131.
- jones2026c, p. 503: "SM-PW5A-100 through 131—skeletal fragments".
- molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods, Ornithomimosauria: "PWSA-110".

**Ruled out.**
- *PW5A-100 through 131.* Jones (2026) spans the whole referred assemblage.
- *PWSA-110.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) misprint both the prefix and the number.

**Open.** Nothing.
