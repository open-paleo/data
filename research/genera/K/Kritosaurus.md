# *Kritosaurus*

## Is the *Kritosaurus* holotype from west of Chaco Canyon?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** No. Brown (1910) places Ojo Alamo in Chaco Cañon, "twenty miles west of the old Indian pueblo, Bonito", and describes AMNH 5799 as the most important fossil from "this locality" near Ojo Alamo. No source cited for the taxon puts the site west of the canyon. Governs `location.locality` on [*Kritosaurus navajovius*](../../../genera/K/Kritosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown1910a, p. 267: "A new locality for dinosaurs near Ojo Alamo, in northern New Mexico"; "Ojo Alamo is an Indian trading post in Chaco Cañon, about ninety-five miles northwest of Gallup, New Mexico, and twenty miles west of the old Indian pueblo, Bonito, now known as Putnam".
- brown1910a, p. 269: "The most important fossil from this locality is a skull, lower jaws, and atlas, of a hitherto undescribed genus"; "Type of species No. 5799, American Museum collection".
- prieto-márquez2013c, Introduction: AMNH 5799 "was collected in 1904 from late Campanian strata of the upper Kirtland Formation near Ojo Alamo, San Juan County, New Mexico".

**Ruled out.**
- *"West of Chaco Canyon".* Brown puts Ojo Alamo in Chaco Cañon itself; what lies twenty miles to its east is Pueblo Bonito, a site within the canyon, not the canyon as a whole. The phrase reads as a garbled form of that sentence, which is our inference.

**Open.** Brown gives no closer position for the skull than the locality near the trading post, and the Kirtland exposures there are extensive. Williamson (2000), whom Prieto-Márquez (2013) cites for the member assignment, has not been read and may give one.
