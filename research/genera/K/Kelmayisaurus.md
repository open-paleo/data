# *Kelmayisaurus*

## Is the *Kelmayisaurus* holotype from the Lianmuqin Formation or the Tugulu Group?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Lianmuqin Formation, the uppermost formation of the Tugulu Group, from Brusatte and colleagues' (2012) reassessment. The describing paper gives only a field locality number. Governs `location.formation: Lianmuqin` on [*Kelmayisaurus petrolicus*](../../../genera/K/Kelmayisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brusatte2012c, p. 66: "Type locality: Near Wuerho (Urdo), Junggar Basin, Xinjiang, China"; "Type horizon: Lianmugin Formation, Tugulu Group (Early Cretaceous: ?Valanginian–Albian)".
- holtz2004a, Table 4.1: *Kelmayisaurus petrolicus*, "Tugulu Group".
- jones2026c, Carcharodontosauria table: "Lianmuqin Fm., China".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tugulu Group as a rival reading.* Holtz (2004) names the group that contains the Lianmuqin. "Lianmugin" is a romanization of the same name.

**Open.** Nothing.
