# *Kiyacursor*

## Where does the *Kiyacursor* coordinate come from?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** From the figure caption of Averianov and colleagues (2006), a monograph on *Psittacosaurus sibiricus*, which prints a position to the whole arcminute for each of the three Shestakovo sites. Shestakovo 1, the site the describing paper gives for the *Kiyacursor* holotype, converts to 55.9, 87.933333. The describing paper prints no coordinate and refers its locality map to a supplementary figure. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Kiyacursor longipes*](../../../genera/K/Kiyacursor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2006a, p. 361, Fig. 1: "the localities Shestakovo 1 (N 55° 54', E 87° 56'), Shestakovo 2 (N 55° 56', E 87° 59') and Shestakovo 3 (N 55° 53', E 87° 59')".
- averianov2024a, Locality and horizon: "Shestakovo 1, Kemerovo oblast—Kuzbass, Russia (electronic supplementary material, figure S1). Ilek Formation, Lower Cretaceous (Aptian)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Shestakovo 3 (55°53′N, 87°59′E).* It is the type locality of *P. sibiricus* ("TYPE LOCALITY AND HORIZON. Shestakovo 3", averianov2006a, p. 362), not of *Kiyacursor*.
- *55.903332, 87.957779.* This five-decimal pair matches none of the three positions the caption prints, and lies about 1.6 km east of the Shestakovo 1 value.

**Open.** The supplementary figure S1 of Averianov and colleagues (2024) has not been read; it may give a finer position for Shestakovo 1.
