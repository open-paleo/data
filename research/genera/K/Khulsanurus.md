# *Khulsanurus*

## What age is the *Khulsanurus* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Campanian, as Averianov and Lopatin (2021) give for the Khulsan beds that yielded it. Governs `period.stage: [Campanian]` on [*Khulsanurus magnificus*](../../../genera/K/Khulsanurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2021c, p. 2127: the holotype is from "Khulsan, Gobi Desert, Mongolia; Barungoyot Formation, Upper Cretaceous (Campanian)".
- napoli2021a, p. 3, describing *Kuru* from the same locality: "All that can be said with certainty is that the Barun Goyot strata at Khulsan are Campanian to Maastrichtian in age."
- jones2026c, p. 504: "Barun Goyot Fm., Mongolia *Late Cretaceous, Maastrichtian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian alone.* Jones (2026) gives it in a table without a source.

**Open.** Whether the Khulsan beds reach the Maastrichtian. Napoli and colleagues' sentence concerns the Khulsan strata in general; read as a statement about this horizon, it would add the Maastrichtian. See [Baruungoyot](../../stratigraphy/b/baruungoyot.md) for the formation.
