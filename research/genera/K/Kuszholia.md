# *Kuszholia*

## Which site is the *Kuszholia mengi* holotype from?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** Site TsBI-52 at Dzharakuduk, written CBI-52 in the transliteration of Nessov's 1995 translation. Nessov (1997) gives the site in a plate explanation that also corrects the specimen number. Governs `location.locality` and `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Kuszholia mengi*](../../../genera/K/Kuszholia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nessov1997a, Table 18 (translated): "posterior part of sacrum *Kuszholia mengi* Nessov, ZIN PO 4602 (previously erroneously cited as ZIN PO 3486), holotype, coll. TsBI-52".
- The prefixes are one series: Nessov and colleagues (1989) give the *Turanoceratops* holotype's site as TsBI-27 and Nessov (1995) gives it as CBI-27 (see [Bissekty](../../stratigraphy/b/bissekty.md)).

**Ruled out.**
- *CBI-16.* Nessov (1995) gives this site for a "centrum with long, slit-like pleurocoel, possibly from bird ?*Kuszholia* sp. or from a theropod or a segnosaur" (nessov1995a, p. 146, Plate II, translated), a referred specimen and not the holotype.
- *CBI-4 (TsBI-4).* The site of ZIN PO 4623, "first two sacral vertebrae, presumably from *Kuszholia mengi*" (nessov1997a, Table 18, translated), another specimen.
- *ZIN PO 3486 as the holotype's number.* Nessov (1997) calls it an erroneous citation of ZIN PO 4602.

**Open.** The erecting paper, Nessov (1992), has not been read. It would show where the number 3486 was first printed and whether it gives the site.
