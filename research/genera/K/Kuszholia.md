# *Kuszholia*

## What is the status of *Kuszholia*, and is it disputed?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** Valid, of uncertain placement within Avialae, with no live dispute; the dispute block is removed. No paper read calls it a nomen dubium. Zelenkov (2011), the one later study of the holotype, redescribes and figures it as a taxon of uncertain position, and Weishampel and colleagues (2004) list it in their table of Avialae rather than among nomina dubia. The removed block's other claims had no source in the papers read. Governs `status: valid`, `parent: Avialae` and the absence of a dispute on [*Kuszholia*](../../../genera/K/Kuszholia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zelenkov2011a: "*Kuszholia mengi* is represented by a caudal fragment of the synsacrum"; "This taxon of uncertain taxonomic position (Kurochkin, 2000)".
- nessov1997a: "Patagopterygiformes(?): Kuszholiidae: Kuszholia mengi".
- weishampel2004a lists *Kuszholia mengi* under Ornithothoraces in its Avialae table.

**Ruled out.**
- *Nomen dubium.* The earlier status; no paper read states it.
- *"Isolated pneumatic vertebrae".* The holotype is a fragment of a synsacrum, and no paper read calls it pneumatic.
- *Enantiornithine.* Zelenkov's "either an enantiornithine or primitive ornithuromorph" concerns a different synsacrum, ZIN PH 9/109, which he distinguishes from *K. mengi* (zelenkov2011a).
- *A non-avialan coelurosaur.* The nearest statement concerns a referred centrum, "possibly from bird ?Kuszholia sp. or from a theropod or a segnosaur" (nessov1995a), not the holotype.

**Open.** The diagnosis. The record carries no diagnostic features, and the naming paper has not been read.

## Which site is the *Kuszholia mengi* holotype from?

*2026-08-28*

**Conclusion.** Site TsBI-52 at Dzharakuduk, written CBI-52 in the transliteration of Nessov's 1995 translation. Nessov (1997) gives the site in a plate explanation that also corrects the specimen number. Governs `location.locality` and `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Kuszholia mengi*](../../../genera/K/Kuszholia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nessov1997a, Table 18 (translated): "posterior part of sacrum *Kuszholia mengi* Nessov, ZIN PO 4602 (previously erroneously cited as ZIN PO 3486), holotype, coll. TsBI-52".
- The prefixes are one series: Nessov and colleagues (1989) give the *Turanoceratops* holotype's site as TsBI-27 and Nessov (1995) gives it as CBI-27 (see [Bissekty](../../stratigraphy/b/bissekty.md)).

**Ruled out.**
- *CBI-16.* Nessov (1995) gives this site for a "centrum with long, slit-like pleurocoel, possibly from bird ?*Kuszholia* sp. or from a theropod or a segnosaur" (nessov1995a, p. 146, Plate II, translated), a referred specimen and not the holotype.
- *CBI-4 (TsBI-4).* The site of ZIN PO 4623, "first two sacral vertebrae, presumably from *Kuszholia mengi*" (nessov1997a, Table 18, translated), another specimen.
- *ZIN PO 3486 as the holotype's number.* Nessov (1997) calls it an erroneous citation of ZIN PO 4602.

**Open.** The erecting paper, Nessov (1992), has not been read. It would show where the number 3486 was first printed and whether it gives the site.
