# *Kol*

## Is *Kol* an alvarezsaurid?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Not established; it sits at Maniraptora incertae sedis. Its describers named it an alvarezsaurid without an analysis; later work finds it closer to oviraptorosaurs or excludes it from the family. Governs `parent: Maniraptora`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Kol ghuva*](../../../genera/K/Kol.yml).

**Evidence.**
- turner2009a: "Kol ghuva is clearly a very large alvarezsaurid".
- agnolín2012a, p. 47: "Kol is more reminiscent of oviraptorosaurians than alvarezsaurids".
- hartman2019a, p. 31: "Kol resolves as a relative of Avimimus as suggested by Agnolin et al. (2012), a single step places it in Alvarezsauridae".
- averianov2021d, p. 1101: "Here, we exclude Kol from Alvarezsauridae".

**Ruled out.**
- *Alvarezsauridae as the parent.* Hartman and colleagues (2019) recover it with *Avimimus*, and Averianov and Lopatin (2021) exclude it.

**Open.** Its affinity once more than the foot is known.
