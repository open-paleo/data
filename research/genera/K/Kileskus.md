# *Kileskus*

## What is the *Kileskus aristotocus* holotype?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** ZIN PH 5/117, the right maxilla alone, from Averianov and colleagues (2010). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ZIN PH 5/117]` on [*Kileskus aristotocus*](../../../genera/K/Kileskus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2010a, *Kileskus aristotocus* sp. nov.: "Holotype. ZIN PH 5/117, incomplete right maxilla." The next paragraph, "Material", lists ZIN PH 6/117 to 13/117 element by element.
- jones2026c, Tyrannosauroidea table: "ZIN PH 5/117 through 12/117—cranial and skeletal fragments".
- molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods, Tyrannosauroidea: "ZIN PH 5/117".

**Ruled out.**
- *ZIN PH 5/117 through 12/117.* Jones (2026) runs the holotype into the referred material that Averianov and colleagues list separately.

**Open.** Nothing.
