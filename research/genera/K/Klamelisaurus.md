# *Klamelisaurus*

## Is the *Klamelisaurus* holotype from the Wucaiwan or the Shishugou Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The lower Shishugou Formation. Wucaiwan is the name its beds were described under before they were included in the Shishugou. Governs `location.formation: Shishugou`, `location.part: lower` and the empty `location.member` on [*Klamelisaurus gobiensis*](../../../genera/K/Klamelisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- moore2020a, Systematic palaeontology: the specimen came from "the top of what was formerly called the 'Wucaiwan Formation' and is now considered to constitute the lower beds of the Shishugou Formation (Clark et al. 2006)".
- upchurch2004a, Table 13.1: *K. gobiensis*, "Wucaiwan Formation (Xinjiang Uygur Zizhiqu), People's Republic of China", "Middle Jurassic".
- jones2026b, p. 376: "Shishugou Fm., China *Middle Jurassic, Callovian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Wucaiwan Formation.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) follow the describing paper's name, which Moore and colleagues (2020) call superseded.
- *A Wucaiwan member.* Moore and colleagues say only "lower beds", which is a position, not a member name.

**Open.** Nothing.
