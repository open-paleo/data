# *Kholumolumo*

## Is the *Kholumolumo* locality Maphutseng or Thotobolo ea 'Ma-Beata?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Maphutseng, the describing paper's type locality. Thotobolo ea 'Ma-Beata is the spot where the first pile of bones was found in 1955; the holotype is one tibia from a bone bed excavated until 1970, and the paper does not tie it to that spot. Governs `location.locality: Maphutseng` on [*Kholumolumo ellenbergerorum*](../../../genera/K/Kholumolumo.yml).

**Evidence.**
- peyredefabrègues2019a, p. e1732996-5: "Type Locality—Maphutseng, Mohale's Hoek District, Lesotho".
- peyredefabrègues2019a, p. e1732996-3: "In September 1955, they uncovered a pile of well-preserved bones at the place called 'Thotobolo ea 'Ma-Beata'"; the name is that "of the place where the first pile of bones was found, not far from the huts of the village of Maphutseng".

**Ruled out.**
- *Thotobolo ea 'Ma-Beata.* It names the first find spot, not shown to be the holotype's.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What stage does *Kholumolumo* take?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Norian, the describing paper's own reading of the Maphutseng bone bed. It rejects a latest Triassic placement on the Ellenbergers' field observations and on magnetostratigraphy; the magnetostratigraphic paper it cites does not cover Maphutseng, so the Norian rests on the field placement the describing paper adopts. Governs `stage: [Norian]` on [*Kholumolumo ellenbergerorum*](../../../genera/K/Kholumolumo.yml).

**Evidence.**
- peyredefabrègues2019a, p. e1732996-4: "Based on lithostratigraphic assessments (Van Gend et al., 2015), it has been recently suggested that the Maphutseng bone bed is in the uppermost part of the LEF and thus is of latest Triassic age (ca. 205 Ma). This has been contradicted by other lithostratigraphic observations (Ellenberger and Ellenberger, 1956a; B. Battail, pers. comm., 2018) and magnetostratigraphy (Scissio et al., 2017), which place the site in the lower part of the LEF, suggesting a Norian age (ca. 210 Ma) for the bone bed."
- sciscio2017a: its sections are Barkly Pass, Quthing, Likhoele, Likhoele east, Maseru, Damplaats, Oldenburg, Seeiso and Golden Gate; Maphutseng is not among them.

**Ruled out.**
- *Norian and Rhaetian, counting the latest Triassic reading.* The describing paper weighs it and rejects it on field evidence.

**Open.** Van Gend and colleagues (2015), the source of the latest Triassic reading, has not been read.

## What is the *Kholumolumo* holotype's number?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** MNHN.F.LES381m, a right tibia, from Peyre de Fabrègues and Allain (2019). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MNHN.F.LES381m]` on [*Kholumolumo ellenbergerorum*](../../../genera/K/Kholumolumo.yml).

**Evidence.**
- peyredefabrègues2019a, p. e1732996-5: "Holotype—MNHN.F.LES381m, a complete right tibia".
- jones2026b, p. 373: "MNHN.ELES381m—tibia; MNHN.ELES series—various fragments".

**Ruled out.**
- *MNHN.ELES381m.* Jones (2026) prints E for F, here and in the series it lists after the holotype.

**Open.** Nothing.
