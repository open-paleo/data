# *Khaan*

## What is the *Khaan* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** IGM 100/1127, from Clark and colleagues (2001). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IGM 100/1127]` on [*Khaan mckennai*](../../../genera/K/Khaan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- clark2001a, p. 211: "Holotype—IGM 100/1127, a complete skeleton, slightly disarticulated in the thorax."
- clark2001a, Fig. 2: "skull of the holotype specimen, IGM 100/1127 … and referred specimen, IGM 100/1002".
- molina-pérez2019a, Oviraptorosaurs speed table: "MPC D-100/1002"; List of Theropods, Oviraptorosauria: "IGM 100/973".
- jones2026c, Oviraptoridae table: "IGM 100/1127—skull and skeleton; IGM 100/973—skull and partial skeleton; IGM 100/1002—partial skull and skeleton".

**Ruled out.**
- *IGM 100/1002 and IGM 100/973.* These are referred specimens. Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) print them in a speed table and in their column for the largest specimen, not as the type. Jones (2026) lists all three and puts the holotype first.

**Open.** Nothing.
