# *Fukuivenator*

## At which level of the Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry was the *Fukuivenator* holotype found?

*2026-08-23*

**Conclusion.** At the highest of the levels that Hattori and colleagues (2021) mark in their section of the quarry, above the *Fukuititan* and *Koshisaurus* horizons and well above the Bonebed I level of *Fukuiraptor* and *Fukuisaurus*. The level appears only in their Fig. 1C. No text names its bonebed, so the record carries no `bed`. Governs the empty `location.bed` on [*Fukuivenator paradoxus*](../../../genera/F/Fukuivenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hattori2021a, p. 2, Fig. 1C: "stratigraphic section of the part of the Kitadani Formation in the Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry, representing the stratigraphic position of FPDM-V-8461". The section places FPDM-V-8461 at its top, above the labels for *Fukuititan nipponensis* and *Koshisaurus katsuyama*, with *Fukuiraptor kitadaniensis* and *Fukuisaurus tetoriensis* at its base.
- hattori2021a, p. 2, Fig. 1B: "photograph of the Kitadani Dinosaur Quarry in 2007 with an arrowhead indicating the locality for FPDM-V-8461".
- shibata2015b, p. 421: "Other horizons (BB II and III), which are approximately 10 m above BB I, yielded *Fukuititan* elements and a well-preserved small theropod skeleton (Shibata & Goto 2008), respectively."

**Ruled out.**
- *Bonebed I.* Hattori and colleagues' section puts the holotype well above the level of the Bonebed I taxa.

**Open.** Whether the level is Shibata and Azuma's Bonebed III. Their "small theropod skeleton" from Bonebed III fits the *Fukuivenator* holotype, whose site Hattori and colleagues mark on a 2007 photograph of the quarry, but Shibata and Azuma do not name it. That identification is an inference. Shibata and Goto (2008), the excavation report both papers cite, has not been read and would settle it.
