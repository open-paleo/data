# *Ferenceratops*

## Where does *Ferenceratops* sit?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** Cerapoda incertae sedis. Maidment and colleagues (2026), who erected the genus, recover it within Ceratopsia; Dieudonné and colleagues (2026b), in a matrix that also samples ceratopsians, recover it among the rhabdodontid ornithopods. Cerapoda is the nearest clade both results accept. Governs `parent: Cerapoda` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Ferenceratops shqiperorum*](../../../genera/F/Ferenceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maidment2026a, p. 401: "*Ferenceratops* and *Ajkaceratops* were recovered within a monophyletic Ceratopsia in an unresolved clade containing the ceratopsids, *Turanoceratops*, *Zuniceratops* and *Mosaiceratops*".
- dieudonné2026b, p. 5: "Zalmoxes shqiperorum was renamed Ferenceratops shqiperorum according to Maidment et al. (2026)"; Fig. 13, p. 31 (figure reading): *Ferenceratops shqiperorum* is the sister taxon of *Zalmoxes robustus*, with *Rhabdodon* and *Mochlodon*, within Ornithopoda.
- dieudonné2026a, p. 10, describing the matrix the later analysis modifies: "We also added the ceratopsians *Auroraceratops rugosus* […], *Mosaiceratops azumai* […], *Protoceratops andrewsi*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Ceratopsia.* Holds under Maidment and colleagues' analyses only. Dieudonné and colleagues do not discuss that result, but their tree, which could have placed the taxon among the ceratopsians it samples, does not.

**Open.** Whether a single analysis sampling both hypotheses' key taxa, including *Ajkaceratops*, separates them.

## Which formation and locality is the Ferenceratops holotype NHMUK R.4900 from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The [Sânpetru Formation](../../../stratigraphy/s/sânpetru.yml), Sibișel Valley, after Augustin and colleagues' correction of the individual's provenance. Governs `location.formation: Sânpetru` and `location.locality: Sibișel Valley` on *Ferenceratops shqiperorum*.

**Evidence.**
- augustin2023a, p. 176: correcting the attribution of the individual, after Nopcsa (1925, p. 286), and placing it in the Sibișel Valley.
- maidment2026a, p. 400, repeats Augustin and colleagues' account.

**Ruled out.**
- *Vurpăr.* Weishampel and colleagues (2003) give Vurpăr and "individual T (Nopcsa 1928)" (weishampel2003a, p. 95); Augustin and colleagues call a Vurpăr co-occurrence "questionable".

**Open.** The Sibișel Valley detail is Augustin and colleagues' addition rather than Nopcsa's wording. Referred material that would extend the taxon into the Densuș-Ciula Formation is not endorsed by Maidment and colleagues (2026).
