# *Falcarius*

## Is the *Falcarius* type horizon Barremian or Berriasian–Valanginian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Barremian, as the describing paper gives it for the basal Yellow Cat Member at the Crystal Geyser Quarry. Jones (2026) agrees. Governs `stage: [Barremian]` on [*Falcarius utahensis*](../../../genera/F/Falcarius.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kirkland2005a, p. 84, Horizon and locality: "The basal Yellow Cat Member is estimated to be Barremian in age on the basis of the preserved dinosaur fauna … charophytes and palynomorphs".
- joeckel2019a, accepted manuscript, p. 11, U-Pb geochronology: the dated sections "range through the Berriasian, Valanginian, and Hauterivian stages", while "earlier studies documented the presence of Barremian–Aptian strata in other parts of the Yellow Cat Member", so "the stratigraphic architecture of the Yellow Cat Member is probably more complex than earlier conceptions based on lithostratigraphic perspectives".
- jones2026c, Therizinosauria table: "Cedar Mt. Fm., Utah, USA *Early Cretaceous, Barremian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Berriasian–Valanginian.* The record once held this, the age of the lower Yellow Cat at Utahraptor Ridge. Joeckel and colleagues (2019) find the member diachronous, so that age does not carry to Crystal Geyser (see [Yellow Cat](../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.md)).

**Open.** Nothing in the paper itself dates the Crystal Geyser beds. The describing paper's Barremian rests on fauna, charophytes and a personal communication on the palynomorphs. The maximum ages reported for the site are limits, not ages (see [Yellow Cat](../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.md)).
