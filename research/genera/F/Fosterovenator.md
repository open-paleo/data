# *Fosterovenator*

## Where does *Fosterovenator* sit?

*2026-10-15*

**Conclusion.** Averostra incertae sedis. The describing paper reads the tibia as tetanuran but labels the genus a ceratosaurid and concludes it is a basal abelisauroid, and Skawiński and colleagues (2017) find the fibula unlike those of ceratosaurians. Under the nearest-agreed-parent rule a taxon read as either a ceratosaur or a tetanuran sits at Averostra. No phylogenetic analysis read has included it. Governs `parent: Averostra` and the dispute on [*Fosterovenator*](../../../genera/F/Fosterovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dalman2014a: "the YPM VP 058267A tibia clearly belongs to a basal tetanuran theropod"; "*Fosterovenator churei* (YPM VP 058267) is identified as a ceratosaurid theropod, which based on its overall morphology is more closely related to *Elaphrosaurus* than to *Ceratosaurus*"; it and the Garden Park tibia "most likely represent the first occurrence of basal Late Jurassic abelisauroids in the Northern Hemisphere".
- skawiński2017a: "The tubercle is small, if present, in paratype of *Fosterovenator* (Dalman [2014]), which suggest that it is not ceratosaurian."

**Ruled out.**
- *Ceratosauria incertae sedis.* The earlier parent; it takes one side of the describing paper's own split.
- *"Its validity is uncertain."* The earlier summary's claim. Only Jones (2026), a reference work, questions validity: it "may or may not represent a valid taxon" (jones2026c).

**Open.** A redescription or a phylogenetic analysis including the specimens.
