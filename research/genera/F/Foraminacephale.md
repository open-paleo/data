# *Foraminacephale*

## Is the *Foraminacephale* holotype from the Dinosaur Park Formation or the Oldman Formation?

*2026-08-23*

**Conclusion.** The Dinosaur Park Formation, as Schott and Evans (2016) give the type horizon. Sullivan (2003) gives the holotype the Oldman Formation. His paper postdates the 1993 division of the Belly River beds, so his "Oldman" is not the older name for beds now in the Dinosaur Park (see [Oldman and Dinosaur Park](../../topics/oldman-and-dinosaur-park.md)). It is a different reading of the same specimen. Neither paper gives the evidence for its horizon line. Governs `location.formation: Dinosaur Park` on [*Foraminacephale brevis*](../../../genera/F/Foraminacephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown1943a, p. 125: CMN 1423 (then G.S.C. No. 1423) is from the "Belly River formation, east side of Red Deer River below Berry Creek, Alberta, Canada. Collected by L. M. Lambe, 1901".
- sullivan2003b, p. 197: "Holotype-CMN 1423, complete frontoparietal. Horizon-Oldman Formation."
- schott2016a, p. 3, Systematic Palaeontology: "Holotype: CMN 1423"; "Type locality and horizon: Upper Belly River Group (Dinosaur Park Formation)".
- schott2016a, p. 3: "Sullivan (2003, 2006) reported that at least some specimens of this taxon were derived from the Oldman Formation (sensu Eberth & Hamblin, 1993); however, no specimen numbers were cited in support of this claim."

**Ruled out.**
- *Sullivan's Oldman as a superseded name for the Dinosaur Park.* Sullivan uses the post-1993 units throughout. For the *Stegoceras validum* lectotype, whose type locality he gives in the same words as Brown and Schlaikjer's site for CMN 1423, "East side of the Red Deer River below the mouth of Berry Creek", he writes "Dinosaur Park Formation (= 'Belly River beds/Formation,' of earlier workers, David Eberth, pers. comm., 2000)" (sullivan2003b, p. 186), adding "a remote possibility that the specimen came from the Oldman Formation" (p. 190). His Oldman for CMN 1423 is therefore a choice between the two formations, not the older terminology.

**Open.** What places CMN 1423 in either formation. Schott and Evans's objection to Sullivan's Oldman specimens does not address his holotype line, and Sullivan reads the same locality description as most probably Dinosaur Park for *Stegoceras*. That the two descriptions mark one site is an inference from their wording. A source tying Lambe's 1901 site to a measured level, or to the Oldman–Dinosaur Park contact, would settle it.
