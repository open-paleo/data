# *Fulgurotherium*

## Where does *Fulgurotherium* sit? (revisited)

*2026-10-24*

**Conclusion.** Ornithopoda incertae sedis. Supersedes the 2026-10-16 entry, which took Elasmaria to lie within Iguanodontia. In both analyses that include the taxon, Elasmaria is the sister group of Iguanodontia, as our tree and the 2026-10-20 Elasmaria note also place it, and Bell and colleagues (2019) identify the holotype as a non-iguanodontian ornithopod. The readings are Iguanodontia indet., a non-iguanodontian ornithopod, and Elasmaria beside Iguanodontia; Ornithopoda is the nearest clade all of them share. This revives the 2026-10-16 entry's ruled-out Ornithopoda on new evidence, with the user's approval. Governs `parent: Ornithopoda` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Fulgurotherium australe*](../../../genera/F/Fulgurotherium.yml), and the removal of its dispute block.

**Evidence.**
- rozadilla2019a, p. 39: "The strict consensus tree found most Gondwanan ornithopods (i.e. Gasparinisaura, Trinisaura, Qantassaurus, 'Fulgurotherium', Kangnasaurus, Morrosaurus, 'Atlascopcosaurus', Anabisetia, Talenkauen, Macrogryphosaurus) in a monophyletic group, but ingroup relationships are unresolved"; Fig. 31 (figure reading): that Elasmaria polytomy is the sister of the clade holding *Dryosaurus*, *Iguanodon*, *Rhabdodon* and *Tenontosaurus*.
- bandeira2024a, Fig. 19A, p. 25 (figure reading): *Fulgurotherium australe* lies in the node labeled Elasmaria, the sister of the node labeled Iguanodontia.
- bell2019b, p. 25: the holotype "is only identifiable as a non-iguanodontian ornithopod (sensu Madzia et al., 2018)"; its Table 1 lists it as Ornithopoda indet.
- agnolín2010a: the referred specimens "are placed in Iguanodontia indet.", in a sense that includes *Gasparinisaura* and *Notohypsilophodon*.

**Ruled out.**
- *Iguanodontia incertae sedis.* Rests on placing Elasmaria inside Iguanodontia, which neither tree that scores the taxon does.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Where does *Fulgurotherium* sit?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** Iguanodontia incertae sedis. Its status as a nomen dubium is not contested, but its placement is: Agnolin and colleagues (2010) leave the type femur Iguanodontia indet., while the reference phylogeny for Elasmaria and Bandeira and colleagues' (2024) analysis keep it among elasmarians. Elasmaria lies within Iguanodontia, so Iguanodontia is the level both share. Governs `parent: Iguanodontia` and the dispute on [*Fulgurotherium*](../../../genera/F/Fulgurotherium.yml).

**Evidence.**
- agnolín2010a: "the type material of *Fulgurotherium australe* is undiagnostic. As such, the species should be considered a *nomen dubium*"; its table gives "Iguanodontia indet. (nomen dubium)".
- bell2019b: "Iguanodontia indet. (NHMUK R3719; holotype of '*Fulgurotherium australe*')"; the species "is now regarded as a *nomen dubium*".
- madzia2021a: "Under the primary reference phylogeny, Elasmaria comprises … Fulgurotherium austral[e]".
- bandeira2024a: "These two taxa fell in a polytomy with *Gasparinisaura cincosaltensis* and *Fulgurotherium australe*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Elasmaria.* The earlier parent; the work that judged the type material places it no finer than Iguanodontia.
- *Ornithopoda.* Norman and colleagues' (2004) non-iguanodontian reading, which Agnolin and colleagues (2010) dispute and no later paper repeats.

**Open.** The elasmarian reference phylogeny has not been read.
