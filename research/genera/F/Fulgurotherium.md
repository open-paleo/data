# *Fulgurotherium*

## Where does *Fulgurotherium* sit?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** Iguanodontia incertae sedis. Its status as a nomen dubium is not contested, but its placement is: Agnolin and colleagues (2010) leave the type femur Iguanodontia indet., while the reference phylogeny for Elasmaria and Bandeira and colleagues' (2024) analysis keep it among elasmarians. Elasmaria lies within Iguanodontia, so Iguanodontia is the level both share. Governs `parent: Iguanodontia` and the dispute on [*Fulgurotherium*](../../../genera/F/Fulgurotherium.yml).

**Evidence.**
- agnolín2010a: "the type material of *Fulgurotherium australe* is undiagnostic. As such, the species should be considered a *nomen dubium*"; its table gives "Iguanodontia indet. (nomen dubium)".
- bell2019b: "Iguanodontia indet. (NHMUK R3719; holotype of '*Fulgurotherium australe*')"; the species "is now regarded as a *nomen dubium*".
- madzia2021a: "Under the primary reference phylogeny, Elasmaria comprises … Fulgurotherium austral[e]".
- bandeira2024a: "These two taxa fell in a polytomy with *Gasparinisaura cincosaltensis* and *Fulgurotherium australe*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Elasmaria.* The earlier parent; the work that judged the type material places it no finer than Iguanodontia.
- *Ornithopoda.* Norman and colleagues' (2004) non-iguanodontian reading, which Agnolin and colleagues (2010) dispute and no later paper repeats.

**Open.** The elasmarian reference phylogeny has not been read.
