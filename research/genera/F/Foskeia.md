# *Foskeia*

## Is the placement of *Foskeia* in dispute?

*2026-10-15*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute block is removed. Every analysis and review since 2019 places the material, long known as the Vegagete ornithopod, in Rhabdodontomorpha, as a rhabdodontid or as the sister of Rhabdodontidae, and the describing paper's analysis agrees. The one dissent is Bell and colleagues' (2019) polytomy, which no later work repeats. The contested content of Rhabdodontomorpha, which the describing paper discusses, concerns other genera and leaves the record's parent unchanged. Governs `parent: Rhabdodontomorpha` and the absence of a dispute on [*Foskeia*](../../../genera/F/Foskeia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dieudonné2026a: "*Foskeia pelendonum* shares an exclusive relationship with the Rhabdodontidae"; "we include *Foskeia pelendonum* in a new clade of rhabdodontomorphs: Rhabdodontia".
- dieudonné2020a: "The Vegagete ornithopod becomes the closest outgroup of this family".
- madzia2021a: "Rhabdodontomorpha comprises Muttaburrasaurus langdoni, Fostoria dhimbangunmal, the 'Vegagete ornithopod', and members of the clade Rhabdodontidae".
- fonseca2024a: "the 'Vegagete ornithopod': an early rhabdodontid".
- bell2019a: the Vegagete ornithopod "is placed in a polytomy with Gasparinisaura and a variety of 'parksosaurid' and traditionally more basally branching neornithischians".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Its placement is provisional."* The earlier dispute summary. Being recently named is not a dispute, and no current work places it outside Rhabdodontomorpha.
- *A Rhabdodontia node.* Only the describing paper uses the clade so far.

**Open.** Nothing.
