# *Fusuisaurus*

## Is the *Fusuisaurus* holotype from the Napai or the Xinlong Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Xinlong Formation. "Napai Formation" is the earlier name for the same red beds of the Napai Basin. Mo and colleagues (2016), with the describing paper's first author, replace it. Napai remains the name of the basin. Governs `location.formation: Xinlong` on [*Fusuisaurus zhaoi*](../../../genera/F/Fusuisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mo2006a, p. 486: "Locality and horizon: Liubang village, Fusui County, Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region; Napai Formation, Early Cretaceous."
- mo2016a, p. 144: "the name 'Xinlong Formation' is more precise and has precedence over 'Napai Formation' (Guangxi Bureau of Geology and Mineral Resources, 1985); we therefore follow Mo, Xu & Buffetaut (2010) and Mo et al. (2014a) in using it here"; and a partial skeleton "from Liubang … was described by Mo et al. (2006) as *Fusuisaurus zhaoi*".
- ji2022a, p. 2: "Prior to 2010 … the Lower Cretaceous of this basin was often called the Napai Formation (Hou Lianhai et al., 1975; Mo et al., 2006, 2010; Buffetaut et al., 2008), while subsequent dinosaur research papers mostly used the term Xinlong Formation" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Napai Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Napai Fm., China" (jones2026b, p. 379), citing the 2006 describing paper. That is the superseded name, not a separate reading.

**Open.** Nothing.
