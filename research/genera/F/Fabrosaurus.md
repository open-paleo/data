# *Fabrosaurus*

## Which number does the *Fabrosaurus australis* holotype carry?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** MNHN LES 9. Ginsburg (1964) makes the single jaw fragment the type and deposits it in Paris without giving a number. Galton (1978) is the earliest paper read that prints one. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MNHN LES 9]` on [*Fabrosaurus australis*](../../../genera/F/Fabrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ginsburg1964a, p. 2367: "The single specimen serves as type and has been deposited in the Muséum National d'Histoire naturelle de Paris" (translated).
- galton1978a, p. 156: "Holotype: MNHN No. LES 9, right dentary fragment (Fig. 6)"; p. 150, Fig. 6 caption: "Fabrosaurus australis holotype, MNHN LES 9".

**Ruled out.**
- *MNHN No. LES 9.* Jones (2026) gives "MNHN No. LES 9—partial jaw (Ginsburg, 1964)" (jones2026a, Ornithischia table), which follows Galton's Holotype line. "No." is not part of the number, and Galton's own figure caption leaves it out.

**Open.** Nothing.
