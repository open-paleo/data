# *Fujianvenator*

## Is the *Fujianvenator* holotype from the lower or the middle Nanyuan Formation?

*2026-08-23*

**Conclusion.** The lower part, as Xu and colleagues (2023), who excavated the section, give it. Governs `location.part: lower` on [*Fujianvenator prodigiosus*](../../../genera/F/Fujianvenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2023a, Methods, Geological and preservational setting of the IVPP V31985-bearing horizon: "The section that we excavated is 3.6 m in depth and belongs to the lower part of the Nanyuan Formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *Middle Nanyuan.* Paul (2024) gives "Southeastern China; middle Nanyuan" (paul2024b, p. 190) without a source. It is a field guide entry against the excavators' own statement.

**Open.** Nothing.
