# *Fulengia*

## Is *Fulengia youngi* a juvenile *Lufengosaurus*?

*2026-10-08*

**Conclusion.** No source read supports the synonymy. Sereno (1991) refers the skull to Prosauropoda indet. and rejects the characters offered for *Lufengosaurus*; the name is a nomen dubium. Governs `status: nomen dubium`, the absence of `diagnostic_features` and the dispute summary on [*Fulengia youngi*](../../../genera/F/Fulengia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno1991a, Tawasaurus and Fulengia: "F. youngi is here considered a nomen dubium, and CUP 2037 and CUP 2038 are referred to Prosauropoda indet."
- galton2004a lists *Fulengia youngi* among the prosauropod nomina dubia.

**Ruled out.**
- *Junior synonym of* Lufengosaurus huenei. Galton (1990) listed it as one, following Evans and Milner (1989), which has not been read. Sereno (1991) finds the shared characters to be prosauropod synapomorphies or plesiomorphies that do not tie it to *L. huenei*, and Galton and Upchurch (2004) no longer list it under *Lufengosaurus*.

**Open.** Nothing.
