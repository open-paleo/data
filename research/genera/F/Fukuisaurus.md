# *Fukuisaurus*

## Is *Fukuisaurus* a hadrosauriform?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** Not securely. Several analyses place it within Hadrosauriformes, often as the sister of *Proa*. Others, on the McDonald and Verdú matrices, leave it in a polytomy with Hadrosauriformes at the base of Styracosterna. Styracosterna is the clade every analysis accepts. Governs `parent: Styracosterna` and the dispute on [*Fukuisaurus tetoriensis*](../../../genera/F/Fukuisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kobayashi2003a, p. 172: "The strict consensus tree shows that Fukuisaurus is more derived than the monophyletic group of Iguanodon + Ouranosaurus".
- shibata2015b, Abstract: *Koshisaurus* "is more derived than the contemporaneous Fukuisaurus, which is a non-hadrosauroid hadrosauriform".
- párraga2019a, Fig. 3, p. 252: *Fukuisaurus* + *Proa* within the node labelled Hadrosauriformes.
- poole2022a, Iguanodontidae: "another small clade of styracosternans that includes Iguanodon, along with Equijubus, Proa, and Fukuisaurus".
- mcdonald2012a, Fig. 10, p. 73: *Fukuisaurus* in a polytomy with *Barilium* and the Hadrosauriformes node.
- verdú2017a, Fig. 20, p. 31: *Fukuisaurus* in the basal polytomy of Styracosterna, outside the Hadrosauriformes node.
- santos-cubedo2021a, p. 23: "Barilium dawsoni and Fukuisaurus tetoriensis are the most basal members of Hadrosauriformes", in a three-way polytomy with the clade of Iguanodontidae and Hadrosauroidea (Fig. 11, p. 24), outside the common ancestor of *Iguanodon* and *Hadrosaurus*.

**Ruled out.**
- *Hadrosauriformes as the clade the analyses agree on.* The McDonald, Verdú and Santos-Cubedo trees do not place it inside that node.
- *Madzia and colleagues (2021) as an analysis.* They list *Fukuisaurus* in Styracosterna, outside Hadrosauriformes, under a reference phylogeny from earlier work; the placement is not theirs.

**Open.** Whether it pairs with *Proa*, as several matrices recover, in an analysis that also resolves the base of Hadrosauriformes.
