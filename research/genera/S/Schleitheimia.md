# *Schleitheimia*

## Is the *Schleitheimia schutzi* holotype PIMUZ A/III 550 or A/III 350?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** PIMUZ A/III 550, a partial right ilium. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [PIMUZ A/III 550]` on [*Schleitheimia schutzi*](../../../genera/S/Schleitheimia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2020a, p. 9: "*Holotype.* PIMUZ A/III 550, partial right ilium."
- rauhut2020a, p. 6, Table 1: "PIMUZ A/III 550 | Right ilium | Schleitheim; excavation Schutz | Schleitheimia schutzi; holotype".

**Ruled out.**
- *PIMUZ A/III 350.* Jones (2026) gives "PIMUZ A/III 350—partial right ilium" (jones2026b, p. 375). The paper prints no A/III 350.

**Open.** Nothing.
