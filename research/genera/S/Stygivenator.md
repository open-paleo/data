# *Stygivenator*

## What is the number of the Jordan theropod (Aublysodon molnaris), and how did Paul spell the name?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** LACM 28471; Paul (1988) printed the name *Aublysodon molnaris*, corrected to *molnari* by Olshevsky. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: LACM 28471` and the synonym spelling on *Stygivenator molnari*.

**Evidence.**
- molnar1978a, p. 73: "catalogued as LACM 28471", repeated in the text-figure captions.
- paul1988a, p. 325: "AUBLYSODON MOLNARIS new species / TYPE—LACM 28741".
- olshevsky1995a, manuscript p. 42: "he produced his paper on LACM 28471"; "Aublysodon molnari (incorrectly spelled molnaris in the earlier editions of his book)".

**Ruled out.**
- *LACM 28741.* Paul's number transposes Molnar's.
- *"Aublysodon molnari" as Paul's spelling.* Paul printed *molnaris*.
- *LACM 23845.* A different specimen, the holotype of *Dinotyrannus megagracilis* (olshevsky1995a, Fig. 37).

**Open.** Nothing.
