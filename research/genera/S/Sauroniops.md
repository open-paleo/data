# *Sauroniops*

## Which unit of the Kem Kem Group is the *Sauroniops* holotype from?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** No source places it in a formation of the group, so the record stops at the Kem Kem Group. The holotype was collected commercially, and both attributions in print rest on the rock adhering to it. Governs `location.group: Kem Kem` and the empty `location.formation` on [*Sauroniops pachytholus*](../../../genera/S/Sauroniops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cau2013a, in-press version, p. 3: "Type horizon and locality. Ifezouane Formation (Cenomanian, Upper Cretaceous; Cavin et al., 2010)"; the specimen "was collected by a local commercial collector", and its "taphonomic comparison … and the lithology of the matrix" support the referral.
- cau2025a, p. 181: the matrix of OPH2211 (cf. *Sauroniops*) is a conglomerate "typical of the Gara Sbaa Formation"; "This differs from the sediment encrusting the holotype of *Sauroniops pachytholus* which is a reddish fine-grained sandstone (AC, pers. obs.), more characteristic of the higher parts of the Kem Kem successions".

**Ruled out.**
- *Ifezouane Formation.* Ibrahim and colleagues (2020) reject the Tinghir-region names for Kem Kem strata: "We do not agree with applying formational names from the Tinghir region (e.g., Ifezouane and Aoufous formations) to the distinctive, mapable strata of the Kem Kem embayment" (ibrahim2020a, Stratigraphic resolution).
- *Gara Sbaa Formation by correlation.* Ibrahim and colleagues' Table 7 sets the Ifezouane beside the Gara Sbaa (ibrahim2020a, Hamadian Supergroup), so Cau and colleagues' "Ifezouane" can be read as Gara Sbaa. Cau and Paterna (2025) describe the holotype's matrix as unlike the Gara Sbaa conglomerate (cau2025a, p. 181).

**Open.** The Douira Formation is not ruled out, but no source assigns the holotype to it: "the higher parts of the Kem Kem successions" is a lithological comparison, and Cau and Paterna add that "lithology alone is not a sufficient criterion for assessing a proper zonation".
