# *Sinusonasus*

## Is *Sinusonasus* a sinovenatorine?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it sits at Troodontidae incertae sedis. Two analyses recover it in Sinovenatorinae and five outside, several pairing it with *Xixiasaurus*. Governs `parent: Troodontidae`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Sinusonasus magnodens*](../../../genera/S/Sinusonasus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- shen2017b, p. 775: sister of *Daliansaurus* within Sinovenatorinae.
- hartman2019a, Fig. 17: sister of *Xixiasaurus*, outside Sinovenatorinae; constraining it into the subfamily costs six steps.

**Ruled out.**
- *Sinovenatorinae as the parent.* Analyses on several matrices place it outside the subfamily.

**Open.** Its relationship to *Xixiasaurus*.
