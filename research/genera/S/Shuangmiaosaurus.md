# *Shuangmiaosaurus*

## Does the *Shuangmiaosaurus* record carry Dong's GPS fix?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** No. Dong (2002) printed the fix for the fossil site of the material he identified as *Crichtonsaurus*, and no source ties the *Shuangmiaosaurus* holotype, LPM 0165, to that point; the record had carried it on the inference that the two came from one site. Governs the absence of `location.coordinates` on [*Shuangmiaosaurus gilmorei*](../../../genera/S/Shuangmiaosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong2002a, p. 277: "用GPS测得化石点位置是东经120°42′49″，北纬41°42′40″" ("by GPS the position of the fossil site was determined as 120°42′49″E, 41°42′40″N", translated), for a batch from the Shuangmiao area that held "sauropods, ornithopods, and ankylosaurs" (translated).
- you2003b, p. 148: the material was excavated "from fossiliferous horizons of the Sunjiawan Formation near Shuangmiao Village".

**Ruled out.**
- *Dong's fix as the holotype's position.* It is printed for another taxon's material; reading "the fossil site" as covering LPM 0165 is an inference.

**Open.** A source that places LPM 0165.

## References

- `dong2002a`: Dong, Z. (2002). A new armored dinosaur (Ankylosauria) from Beipiao Basin, Liaoning Province, northeastern China. *Vertebrata PalAsiatica* 40(4): 276-285.
- `you2003b`: You, H.; Ji, Q.; Li, J.; Li, Y. (2003). A new hadrosauroid dinosaur from the mid-Cretaceous of Liaoning, China. *Acta Geologica Sinica (English Edition)* 77(2): 148-154. doi:10.1111/j.1755-6724.2003.tb00557.x
