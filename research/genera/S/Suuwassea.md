# *Suuwassea*

## Where does the name Rattlesnake Ridge Quarry come from, and which part of the Morrison is the Suuwassea holotype from?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** The site name is printed by Tschopp and colleagues (2015), not by the describing paper, which withholds the site. No `member` or `part` is recorded, because the two placements disagree and the describing paper's is queried. Governs `location.locality: Rattlesnake Ridge Quarry` and the absent `location.member` and `location.part` on [*Suuwassea emilieae*](../../../genera/S/Suuwassea.yml).

**Evidence.**
- harris2004a, p. 199: "Type locality: Southern Carbon County, Montana, U.S.A. … more specific locality information is not provided here, but is on file at the ANS"; "Type horizon: Morrison Formation (?Brushy Basin Member equivalent), ?Tithonian."
- tschopp2015a, p. 7, Table 2: ANS 21122, "Rattlesnake Ridge Quarry, Carbon County, MT, USA", "Late Kimmeridgian; Lower Morrison Form."
- whitlock2025a, p. 9: "the older Kimmeridgian Rattlesnake Ridge site that yielded *Suuwassea* [Myers and Storrs, 2007]".

**Ruled out.**
- *Myers and Storrs (2007) as the source of the name or the age.* Whitlock and colleagues credit them, but their paper describes the Mother's Day Quarry and does not mention *Suuwassea*, Rattlesnake Ridge or Carbon County; its Salt Wash placement rests on "F. Peterson, personal communication, 2004".
- *Brushy Basin Member.* Harris and Dodson's "?Brushy Basin Member equivalent" is queried, and Tschopp and colleagues give "Lower Morrison Form." instead.

**Open.** Which part of the Morrison holds the quarry. The site record "on file at the ANS" would settle the locality.
