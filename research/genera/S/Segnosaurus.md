# *Segnosaurus*

## Which specimen is the *Segnosaurus galbinensis* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. 100/80 of Perle (1979), now IGM 100/80. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IGM 100/80]` on [*Segnosaurus galbinensis*](../../../genera/S/Segnosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- perle1979a, p. 46: "Holotype – No. 100/80, PST GIN AN MNR; lower jaws, incomplete humerus, complete radius and ulna"; "In addition to the holotype, fragments of postcranial skeletons of two specimens. Paratype No. 100/82 PST" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *IGM 100/82.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) tabulate the species under "IGM 100/82" (molina-pérez2019a, p. 177). Perle names it a paratype.
- *The hypodigm as the type.* Jones (2026) lists "IGM 100/80 … IGM 100/82 … IGM 100/83 … IGM 100/81" together without marking the type (jones2026c, p. 505).

**Open.** Nothing.
