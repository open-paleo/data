# *Sinosaurus*

## Is the *Sinosaurus triassicus* holotype from Qinglongshan, and which Lufeng member is it from?

*2026-09-05*

**Conclusion.** The holotype, IVPP V34, is from Huangchiatien in Lufeng County, not Qinglongshan, and its member is unresolved, so none is recorded. Young (1948) gives its horizon only as "the Red beds of Huangchiatien". Governs `location.locality: Huangchiatien` and the absence of `location.member` on [*Sinosaurus triassicus*](../../../genera/S/Sinosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- young1948a, p. 79: for V34, "Horizon and locality. Upper Triassic from the Red beds of Huangchiatien, Lufeng, Yunnan."
- young1948a, p. 75: for *Lukousaurus yini*, "Horizon and locality: Upper Triassic Dark Red Beds of the Lower Lufeng Series from Huangchiatien, Lufeng, Yunnan."
- young1948a, p. 83: isolated teeth "collected from the surface"; "Most of them were found from the upper level (the Dark Red Beds near Ehrchuanshan, Liuchiaching and Huangchiatien (V35, V36, V37 V48) but a few of them also from the lower level (Dull Purplish Beds Near Tachun (V38)".
- carrano2012a, p. 229: for "*Dilophosaurus*" *sinensis*, "Occurrence. Qinglongshan near Muchulang Village, Xiyangyi Rural Tribal District, Jinning County, Yunnan, China".
- wang2017b, p. 178, Fig. 1: "the holotype and LFGT LDM-L10 of *Sinosaurus triassicus* are recovered from Lufeng County, while *Sinosaurus sinensis* (= "*Dilophosaurus*" *sinensis*) KMV 8701 is from Jinning County".
- xing2012b, pp. 17–18: "The dinosaur fauna in the Shawan Member includes: ... *Lukousaurus yini, Sinosaurus triassicus, Yunnanosaurus huangi*"; p. 17: "The Zhangjia'ao Member was originally recognized as the "Deep Red Beds" of the Upper Member of the Lower Lufeng Formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *Qinglongshan, Muchulang Village.* It is the site of KMV 8701, the skeleton described as "*Dilophosaurus*" *sinensis* and later referred to *Sinosaurus*, in Jinning County (carrano2012a, p. 229; wang2017b, p. 178; xing2012b, p. 10, Fig. 1.1: "5), Qinglongshan (KMV 8701)").
- *Shawan Member.* Xing (2012) lists the species in the Shawan Member fauna, but the same list includes *Lukousaurus yini*, which Young places in the Dark Red Beds, and Xing describes the Zhangjia'ao Member as the former "Deep Red Beds" of the upper member. Reading Xing's "Deep Red Beds" as Young's "Dark Red Beds" is our inference. The same thesis's map puts V30 and V31 at Dahuangtian (xing2012b, p. 10, Fig. 1.1), where Young reports them from Mahuangching, "about 60 km west of Lufeng" (young1948a, p. 87).

**Open.** Whether V34 came from the dark red (Zhangjiawa) beds or the dull purplish (Shawan) beds. Young's upper-level teeth include Huangchiatien specimens, which favors the Zhangjiawa, but they were picked up from the surface and do not date the holotype. A record of the level at which V34 was collected would settle it.
