# *Styracosaurus*

## Which quarry and level is the Styracosaurus albertensis holotype, CMN 344, from?

*2026-09-07*

**Conclusion.** Quarry 016 in Dinosaur Provincial Park, in the upper Dinosaur Park Formation. The coordinate is converted from the UTM reference in Brown and colleagues' specimen table, in Zone 12U. Governs `location.locality`, `location.part: upper` and `location.coordinates` on [*Styracosaurus albertensis*](../../../genera/S/Styracosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown2020a, p. 92, Appendix 1: the CMN 344 row gives "Quarry 016, DPP", stratigraphy "43.5 - 47.5" m, and UTM "472167.854" E, "5622232.802" N.
- brown2020a, p. 70: CMN 344 is among specimens that "represent outcrops of upper levels of the Dinosaur Park Formation"; the Fig. 1 caption gives "UTM Coordinates (Zone 12U)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sand Creek.* Ryan and colleagues give it for ROM 1436, a parietal "collected by Levi Sternberg in 1935 on the west branch of Sand Creek" (ryan2007b, p. 960), not for the holotype.
- *Quarry 105.* Brown and colleagues give CMN 344's quarry as 016, and no source read gives a Quarry 105 for any *Styracosaurus* specimen.

**Open.** Nothing.
