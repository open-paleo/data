# *Siamotyrannus*

## Where does *Siamotyrannus* sit?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** In Metriacanthosaurinae. The analyses that retain it place it among the allosauroids, as a metriacanthosaurine with *Metriacanthosaurus* and *Sinraptor*. The tyrannosaurid assignment of its describers is not followed. Samathi and colleagues (2019) dissent, regarding it as a possible basal coelurosaur. Governs `parent: Metriacanthosaurinae` and the dispute on [*Siamotyrannus isanensis*](../../../genera/S/Siamotyrannus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- buffetaut1996a, abstract: "an early and primitive representative of the Tyrannosauridae".
- rauhut2003b, p. 127: deleted from the final analysis, "the taxon is only known from poor material that has not been described in detail (Buffetaut et al. 1996)".
- carrano2012a, p. 242: "The pubic obturator foramen is open, but not broadly so as in tyrannosaurids, instead resembling the unusual condition exhibited by Sinraptor and Yangchuanosaurus"; p. 247: "Metriacanthosaurus + Sinraptor dongi + Siamotyrannus".
- rauhut2024a, p. 42: "Alpkarakush was confirmed as a metriacanthosaurid and represented the sister taxon to a clade uniting the remaining metriacanthosaurids Sinraptor, Siamotyrannus, and Metriacanthosaurus".
- samathi2019a, p. 257: "Siamotyrannus is an avetheropod, possibly a basal coelurosaur (Samathi 2013; Samathi and Chanthasit 2017; present work) or basal allosauroid (e.g., Rauhut 2003; Carrano et al. 2012)".

**Ruled out.**
- *A megaraptoran affinity.* Jones (2026) attributes one to Samathi and colleagues (2019), but they refer *Phuwiangvenator* to Megaraptora and recover *Siamotyrannus* outside the megaraptoran clade, in a polytomy with *Chilantaisaurus* and *Gualicho* (Fig. 18, p. 255).
- *Avetheropoda as the parent.* The basal coelurosaur reading comes from one group and is offered as a possibility; the analyses of Carrano and colleagues (2012), Chokchaloemwong and colleagues (2019) and Rauhut and colleagues (2024) recover it as a metriacanthosaurid.
- *Tyrannosauridae.* Carrano and colleagues (2012, p. 242) find the pelvic characters behind it shared with *Sinraptor* and *Yangchuanosaurus*.

**Open.** Whether a full description of the holotype settles the coelurosaur reading.
