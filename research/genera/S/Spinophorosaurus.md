# *Spinophorosaurus*

## Which unit of the Irhazer Group yielded *Spinophorosaurus*?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** The Argiles de l'Irhazer, the unranked lower unit below the Tiourarén Formation, as Remes and colleagues (2009) place the site; the record stays filed in the group, with the horizon in its location note (see [Irhazer](../../stratigraphy/i/irhazer.md)). Governs the location note on [*Spinophorosaurus nigerensis*](../../../genera/S/Spinophorosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.** None.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Nothing.
