# *Szechuanosaurus*

## Where does *Szechuanosaurus* sit, and is its status in dispute?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** Theropoda incertae sedis, and no dispute: the block is removed. The type teeth are not diagnostic below Theropoda, and the skeleton once referred to the species, the only evidence of tetanuran or allosauroid affinity, has been assigned to *Yangchuanosaurus shangyouensis*. Governs `parent: Theropoda` and the absence of a dispute on [*Szechuanosaurus*](../../../genera/S/Szechuanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carrano2012a: Young's four teeth "are not diagnostic below the level of Theropoda. As this is the type species of the genus *Szechuanosaurus*, that taxon cannot be diagnosed and should not be used to receive additional species"; the holotype "is non-diagnostic and therefore *S. campi* is a *nomen dubium*"; of CV 00214, "we assign CV 00214 to *Y. shangyouensis* given its close similarity and derivation from the same stratum and geographic region."

**Ruled out.**
- *Tetanurae incertae sedis.* The earlier parent; it rested on CV 00214, which is no longer part of the genus.

**Open.** Nothing.
