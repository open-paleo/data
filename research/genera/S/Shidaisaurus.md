# *Shidaisaurus*

## Is the *Shidaisaurus* holotype from the Chuanjie Formation or the Zhanghe Formation?

*2026-09-04*

**Conclusion.** The Chuanjie Formation. Wu and colleagues (2009) place the holotype near the base of the Upper Lufeng Formation, and Zou and colleagues (2025) place *Shidaisaurus* in the Chuanjie Formation, the lower of the two units the Upper Lufeng was later divided into. The Zhanghe is a separate formation in the Chuxiong subregion, which Zou and colleagues describe as comparable to the former Upper Lufeng, not as the same unit. Governs `location.formation: Chuanjie` on [*Shidaisaurus jinae*](../../../genera/S/Shidaisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wu2009a, p. 10: "Near the base of the Upper Lufeng Formation (early Middle Jurassic); A'na, Laochangjing village, Chuanjie township, Lufeng County, Yunnan, China."
- zou2025a, p. 42, Discussion: "*Shidaisaurus* ... was found in Chuanjie Formation (Middle Jurassic) in Lufeng City, Yunnan, which is 122 km from the type locality of *Yuanmouraptor*."
- zou2025a, p. 2, Geological setting: "Zhanghe Formation is representative Middle Jurassic deposit of Chuxiong subregion"; "its lithologic and biostratigraphic characteristics are comparable to those of once-called Upper Lufeng Formation, which was reassigned as Middle Jurassic Chuanjie Formation and Laoluocun Formation from bottom to top".

**Ruled out.**
- *Zhanghe Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Zhanghe Fm., China *Middle Jurassic, Aalenian/Bajocian*" in the theropod systematics table (jones2026c). Zou and colleagues, who describe the Zhanghe theropod *Yuanmouraptor*, put *Shidaisaurus* in the Chuanjie, 122 km away. The table's "Aalenian/Bajocian" is the age Zou and colleagues give for the Zhanghe at the *Yuanmouraptor* type locality (zou2025a, p. 6), which suggests the row took the correlative unit for the formation (our inference).

**Open.** Nothing.
