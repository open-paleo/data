# *Sinopeltosaurus*

## Is *Sinopeltosaurus* a thyreophoran?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Not demonstrably; it sits at Ornithischia incertae sedis. The tarsus was described as an indeterminate ornithischian with no character supporting Thyreophora, and the later revision leaves a thyreophoran identity only possible. Governs `parent: Ornithischia`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Sinopeltosaurus minimus*](../../../genera/S/Sinopeltosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- irmis2008a, p. 122: "FMNH CUP 2338 should be considered an indeterminate basal ornithischian that is more derived than Pisanosaurus. No discrete character states support placement within Thyreophora".
- galton2016a, p. 203: the specimen was "correctly described by Irmis & Knoll (2008) as Ornithischia indet.", and Ulansky's names "are not valid for purposes of zoological nomenclature".

**Ruled out.**
- *Thyreophora as the parent.* Its only support is Ulansky's (2014) referral to Scelidosauridae, under names Galton and Carpenter (2016) rule unavailable.

**Open.** Nothing.
