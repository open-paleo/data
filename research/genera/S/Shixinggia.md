# *Shixinggia*

## What is the *Shixinggia oblita* holotype's number?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** BPV-112, Beijing Natural History Museum. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [BPV-112]` and `institution: BMNHC` on [*Shixinggia oblita*](../../../genera/S/Shixinggia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2005a, p. 414: "Holotype: A nearly complete sacrum with three posterior dorsal and three anterior caudal vertebrae naturally articulated … (The specimen is stored in the Beijing Natural History Museum; BPV-112)."

**Ruled out.**
- *BVP-112.* Jones (2026) gives "BVP-112" (jones2026c, p. 507), which swaps two letters of the code. Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give BPV-112 (molina-pérez2019a, p. 272).

**Open.** Nothing.
