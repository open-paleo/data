# *Stephanosaurus*

## Does Gilmore's Berry Creek locality belong to the Stephanosaurus type?

*2026-09-07*

**Conclusion.** No. Gilmore (1924) gives "1½ miles below the mouth of Berry creek" for the maxilla and dentary that Lambe later used to characterize the genus, which carry their own catalogue numbers. The type individual, NMC 419, has no recorded site finer than the Red Deer River district. Governs `location.locality: Red Deer River district` on [*Stephanosaurus marginatus*](../../../genera/S/Stephanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lambe1902a, p. 71: remains "met with in abundance in the Red Deer river district", of which "a number were discovered associated with each other, the remains of one individual".
- gilmore1924b, p. 30: the maxilla and dentary "Both were labelled as coming from the west side of Red Deer river, 1½ miles below the mouth of Berry creek, but the maxilla was collected August 1, 1898, whereas the dentary was not discovered until August 19", and Lambe gave them "separate catalogue numbers".

**Ruled out.**
- *West side of the Red Deer River, 1½ miles below the mouth of Berry Creek.* The label of the two jaw elements, not of NMC 419 (gilmore1924b, p. 30).

**Open.** Nothing.
