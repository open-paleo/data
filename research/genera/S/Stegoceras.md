# *Stegoceras*

## Which locality and formation is the Stegoceras validum lectotype, CMN 515, from?

*2026-09-06*

**Conclusion.** The east side of the Red Deer River below the mouth of Berry Creek, in the Dinosaur Park Formation, as Sullivan (2003) gives them. The Steveville locality and the coordinate printed by Wall and Galton (1979) belong to the holotype of *Stegoceras browni*. Governs `location.locality` and `location.formation: Dinosaur Park` on [*Stegoceras validum*](../../../genera/S/Stegoceras.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sullivan2003b, p. 185: "Type Locality-East side of the Red Deer River below the mouth of Berry Creek, Alberta, Canada."
- sullivan2003b, p. 189: the lectotype "comes from a place along the Red Deer River where most of the fossiliferous exposures are in the Dinosaur Park Formation", with "a remote possibility that the specimen came from the Oldman Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *1 mile below Steveville, 75 ft above the river, at 50.4N, 111.1W.* Wall and Galton give this for AMNH 5450, the *S. browni* holotype: "Oldman Formation, left bank 75 ft (23 m) above the Red Deer River, 1 mile (1.6 km) below Steveville, Alberta. Coordinates 50.4N, 111.1 W." (wall1979a, p. 1178).
- *Oldman Formation.* Jasinski and colleagues caption CMN 515 as "from the Oldman Formation, Alberta, Canada" (jasinski2011a, p. 205, Fig. 3), without discussing its horizon. Sullivan places it in the Dinosaur Park; see also [Oldman and Dinosaur Park](../../topics/oldman-and-dinosaur-park.md).

**Open.** The Oldman origin that Sullivan reports as Eberth's "remote possibility" (a personal communication). A measured level for the quarry would settle it; no source read gives one.
