# *Spinops*

## Is "NHMUK R16307" a different number from the stored *Spinops* holotype?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** No. NHMUK R16307 is the number the describing paper prints, and the record writes it with the PV collection prefix. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NHMUK PV R 16307]` on [*Spinops sternbergorum*](../../../genera/S/Spinops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- farke2011a, p. 694: "*Holotype*: NHMUK R16307, a partial parietal bone".

**Ruled out.**
- *A different specimen.* Jones (2026) prints "NHMUK R16307—partial parietal" (jones2026a, p. 412), the paper's own form. Within the palaeontological collection the R is part of the number and PV is the collection prefix (see [NHMUK catalogue prefixes](../../topics/nhmuk-catalogue-prefixes.md)).

**Open.** Nothing.
