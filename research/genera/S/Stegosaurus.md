# *Stegosaurus*

## What are the type specimens of *Stegosaurus ungulatus*?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Two syntypes, YPM 1853 and YPM 1858; no work read designates a lectotype. Galton's (2010) calling YPM 1853 "The paralectotype" implies a lectotype but does not designate one, and his own figure caption still calls YPM 1853 the holotype. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [YPM 1853, YPM 1858]`, `specimen_type: syntype` and `location.locality` on *Stegosaurus ungulatus* in [*Stegosaurus*](../../../genera/S/Stegosaurus.yml), and settles the earlier entry's Open question on YPM 1853's status.

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1914b: "based upon the cotypes of S. ungulatus (Nos. 1853 and 1858, Yale Museum)"; "the type-specimen of that species [S. duplex] is one of the cotypes upon which Marsh founded the species S. ungulatus".
- galton2010a: "*Stegosaurus ungulatus* is based upon two syntypes (YPM 1853 from Reed's YPM Quarry 12; YPM 1858 from Reed's YPM Quarry 11 …)"; "The paralectotype (YPM 1853) includes the posterior part of the skull".
- maidment2008a: "The species was based upon two specimens (YPM 1853 and YPM 1858)".

**Ruled out.**
- *YPM 1853 as holotype.* The species was founded on two specimens.
- *YPM 1858 as lectotype.* It rests on Galton's indirect wording, not a designation; it would also make *S. duplex* an objective synonym.

**Open.** A lectotype designation, if one exists outside the works read.

## Which Como Bluff quarry did YPM 1853, the Stegosaurus ungulatus type, come from?

*2026-09-06*

**Conclusion.** Reed's Quarry 12. Marsh (1879) names no quarry or place for the species; the attribution comes from later work on the Yale collections. Governs `location.locality: Quarry 12, Como Bluff` on *Stegosaurus ungulatus* ([*Stegosaurus*](../../../genera/S/Stegosaurus.yml)).

**Evidence.**
- galton2010a, p. 191: "YPM 1853 from Reed's YPM Quarry 12; YPM 1858 from Reed's YPM Quarry 11; both from Como Bluff".
- maidment2008a, p. 381: the species "was based upon two specimens (YPM 1853 and YPM 1858) from the Morrison Formation of Reed's Quarry 12 and Reed's Quarry 11, respectively".
- dalman2014a, p. 182: Quarry 12 "consists of a gray silty claystone bed in which the skeleton of *Stegosaurus ungulatus* (Marsh, 1879) was found".

**Ruled out.**
- *Quarry 13.* Reed's Quarry 13 yielded other types: the *Stegosaurus sulcatus* holotype, USNM 4937, "excavated from Reed's YPM Quarry 13 Middle" (galton2010a, p. 194), and the *Diracodon laticeps* holotype, YPM 1885, "discovered in Reed's YPM Quarry 13W" (p. 193).

**Open.** Galton calls YPM 1853 "The paralectotype" (galton2010a, p. 191), which implies that a lectotype was chosen between the two syntypes; no source read names it or its author. If it is YPM 1858, the type locality is Quarry 11. Carpenter and Galton (2001, pp. 84–90), which Galton cites for the details, has not been read. No source read names Robber's Roost for either specimen.
