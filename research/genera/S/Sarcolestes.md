# *Sarcolestes*

## Is the placement of *Sarcolestes* in dispute?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute block is removed. Since Vickaryous and colleagues (2004), every treatment read places it at Ankylosauria incertae sedis or as an early-diverging ankylosaur outside the main clades. The nodosaurid readings of Galton (1983) and Sereno (1998) are historical. Governs `parent: Ankylosauria` and the absence of a dispute on [*Sarcolestes*](../../../genera/S/Sarcolestes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- vickaryous2004a: "Although previous efforts had suggested nodosaurid affinity for a variety of other taxa, these are herein assigned to Ankylosauria incertae sedis. Among these taxa are three of the oldest known ankylosaurs, Sarcolestes leedsi".
- naish2008a: "In its rostrally continuous tooth row and proportionally small predentary S. leedsi resembles nodosaurids (Galton 1983a) and it was regarded as the sister-taxon to Nodosaurinae by Sereno (1998)."
- raven2023a: "it is recovered as the earliest-diverging ankylosaur, although it is also possible that a lack of strong phylogenetic signal is causing the taxon to be recovered in a basal position".

**Ruled out.**
- *Nodosauridae.* Historical; not followed since Vickaryous and colleagues (2004).

**Open.** Nothing.
