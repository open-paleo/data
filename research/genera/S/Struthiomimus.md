# *Struthiomimus*

## Is the Struthiomimus altus holotype CMN 930 or "930f"?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** CMN 930 (formerly NMC 930); the "f" in Osborn's "930f" is not part of the number. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: CMN 930` on *Struthiomimus altus*.

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1920a, p. 131: "Ornithomimus altus Lambe, 1902, Cat. No. 930, Ottawa Museum, Belly River formation".
- lambe1902a, p. 50: the type comprises "a complete right hind limb (including the foot), the phalanges of the left foot in place, a pubic bone, and an ischium, of one individual", with no catalogue number.

**Ruled out.**
- *930f.* Osborn prints "Catalogue number 930f Ottawa Museum" (osborn1917a, p. 742) and repeats it in a table header (p. 743); Ostrom (1970) copies "NMC 930f". No other source uses the suffix, and the museum number is 930 (our reading of the "f" as a printing slip).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is AMNH 5339 part of the *Struthiomimus altus* type?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The type is Lambe's (1902) specimen, CMN 930 (see the 2026-10-04 entry). AMNH 5339 is the nearly complete skeleton that Osborn's (1917) description rests on. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CMN 930]` on [*Struthiomimus altus*](../../../genera/S/Struthiomimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- osborn1917a, p. 743: "the nearly complete skeleton, American Museum No. 5339, which forms the basis of the present description"; on the same page a table sets "930f Ottawa Museum" beside "Amer. Mus. 5339".
- gilmore1920a, p. 131: "Ornithomimus altus Lambe, 1902, Cat. No. 930, Ottawa Museum".

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 5339.* Jones (2026) lists "CMN 930—partial skeleton; AMNH 5339—partial skull and skeleton (Osborn, 1917)" (jones2026c, p. 503), and Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) tabulate the species under AMNH 5339 (molina-pérez2019a, p. 177). Osborn's captions read "*Struthiomimus altus*, genotype, Amer. Mus. Nos. 5339" (osborn1917a, p. 741). There "genotype" names the type species of the genus, as in his "*Ornithomimus velox* Marsh, genotype" on the same page, not a type specimen (our reading).

**Open.** Nothing.
