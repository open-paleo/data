# *Struthiomimus*

## Is the Struthiomimus altus holotype CMN 930 or "930f"?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** CMN 930 (formerly NMC 930); the "f" in Osborn's "930f" is not part of the number. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: CMN 930` on *Struthiomimus altus*.

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1920a, p. 131: "Ornithomimus altus Lambe, 1902, Cat. No. 930, Ottawa Museum, Belly River formation".
- lambe1902a, p. 50: the type comprises "a complete right hind limb (including the foot), the phalanges of the left foot in place, a pubic bone, and an ischium, of one individual", with no catalogue number.

**Ruled out.**
- *930f.* Osborn prints "Catalogue number 930f Ottawa Museum" (osborn1917a, p. 742) and repeats it in a table header (p. 743); Ostrom (1970) copies "NMC 930f". No other source uses the suffix, and the museum number is 930 (our reading of the "f" as a printing slip).

**Open.** Nothing.
