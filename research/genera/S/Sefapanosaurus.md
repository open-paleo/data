# *Sefapanosaurus*

## Is the *Sefapanosaurus* holotype from the lower or the upper Elliot Formation?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** Neither can be told. The describing paper has no record of the level, and both parts of the formation crop out around Zastron, so the record carries the Elliot Formation with no `part`, and stages spanning both parts. Governs the empty `location.part`, and `stage: [Norian, Rhaetian, Hettangian]`, on [*Sefapanosaurus zastronensis*](../../../genera/S/Sefapanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- otero2015a, p. 592, Type locality and horizon: "There is no precise information about the exact stratigraphical level where the remains were found. However, around Zastron both lower and upper Elliot Formations crop out (Bordy, Hancox & Rubidge, 2004); hence, we assign *S. zastronensis* to the Upper Triassic−Lower Jurassic Elliot Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lower Elliot Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Lower Elliot Fm., South Africa, Late Triassic, Norian/Rhaetian" in a specimen table (jones2026b, p. 374), citing Otero and colleagues (2015), whose paper says the level is unknown.

**Open.** The level of the remains, collected by A. W. Keyser between 1936 and 1946. A field record of the collecting site would settle it.
