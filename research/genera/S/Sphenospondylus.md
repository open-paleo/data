# *Sphenospondylus*

## Is Seeley's "Wealden of Brook" the Wessex or the Vectis?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** The Wessex Formation: Brook on the Isle of Wight exposes the Wessex, as Galton (2009) and Norman (2012) both read Seeley's locality. It does not set a formation on *Sphenospondylus*, whose holotype, R142 in the Fox Collection, has no recorded provenance; the location note's conditional Wessex stands. Governs the absent `location.formation` on [*Sphenospondylus gracilis*](../../../genera/S/Sphenospondylus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- seeley1883b, p. 55, title: the genus founded on specimens "from the Wealden of Brook in the Isle of Wight".
- galton2009a, p. 262: "the locality, not previously given, was Brook, Isle of Wight, so the pubis came from the Wessex Formation".
- norman2012a, p. 196: "Brook Chine, Isle of Wight (U.K.). Wealden Group, Wessex Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Vectis Formation.* Neither reading of Brook gives it.
- *Wessex on the record.* The holotype is not from Seeley's Brook material.

**Open.** The provenance of R142.

## Which Lydekker work names *Sphenospondylus gracilis*?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** The Quarterly Journal note, which proposes the epithet; the Catalogue, which followed it, cites the note for the name. Governs `species[].erected_in: lydekker1888b` on [*Sphenospondylus*](../../../genera/S/Sphenospondylus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lydekker1888b, p. 47: "since Prof. Seeley has not applied a specific name to the type of *Sphenospondylus*, I propose that it should be known as *S. gracilis*"; the note was "Read November 23, 1887" and refers to "the forthcoming 'Catalogue of Fossil Reptilia'".
- lydekker1888a: "*Sphenospondylus gracilis*, Lydekker", with the footnote "*Ibid.* vol. xliv. p. 47 (1888)", citing the note.

**Ruled out.**
- *The Catalogue as the naming work.* It cites the note for the name.

**Open.** Nothing.
