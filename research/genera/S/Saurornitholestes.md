# *Saurornitholestes*

## What is the *Saurornitholestes langstoni* holotype's current number?

*2026-08-02*

**Conclusion.** TMP 1974.010.0005, the Royal Tyrrell Museum number of the specimen Sues (1978) designated as PMAA P74.10.5. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [TMP 1974.010.0005]` and the `former_ids` entry on [*Saurornitholestes langstoni*](../../../genera/S/Saurornitholestes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sues1978a, p. 382: "Holotype. PMAA P74.10.5, associated remains of an incomplete skeleton".
- jasinski2015a, p. 82, Fig. 1 caption: "*Saurornitholestes langstoni* (TMP 1974.010.0005, holotype)".

**Ruled out.**
- *RTMP 74.10.5 as a different number.* Jones (2026) gives "RTMP 74.10.5" (jones2026c, p. 511). This is the same Tyrrell number in its unpadded form with the older RTMP prefix (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-specimen_id-takes-the-registrys-prefix-and-the-institutions-established-form)).
- *RTMP 95.92.16.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) list this tooth as the species' specimen (molina-pérez2019a, p. 274). It is a referred tooth, not the type.

**Open.** When the material moved from the Provincial Museum to the Tyrrell. None of the works read dates it.
