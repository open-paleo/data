# *Supersaurus*

## Is the Supersaurus holotype BYU 9025 or Jensen's BYU 5500?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** BYU 9025, the current number of the scapulocoracoid Jensen published as BYU 5500. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: BYU 9025` on *Supersaurus vivianae*; its side is disputed.

**Evidence.**
- jensen1985a, p. 701: "HOLOTYPE.—BYU 5500, scapulocoracoid 2.44 m (8') long", a "right scapulocoracoid"; referred "BYU 5501, scapulocoracoid 2.70 m".
- lovelace2008a, p. 529, Table 1: BYU 9025 as the holotype left scapulocoracoid, and BYU 12962 as the right, "mate to BYU 9025".

**Ruled out.**
- *BYU 12962 as the holotype.* Lovelace and colleagues' text gives "Holotype – BYU 12962 JENSEN (1985), a large diplodocid left scapulocoracoid" (lovelace2008a, p. 528), against their own Table 1.

**Open.** Two points. No source read for this note prints the concordance BYU 5500 = BYU 9025; it is inferred from the lengths (Jensen's 2.44 m; Li and colleagues give BYU 9025 as 2350 mm, li2014a, p. 19, Table 5). And the side: Jensen says right, Lovelace and colleagues' table says left, and their Fig. 10 caption says "right scapulacoracoid (BYU 9025)" (p. 536).
