# *Sigilmassasaurus*

## Does the Sigilmassasaurus holotype CMN 41857 have a formation or bed?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. It was bought, not collected in a measured section, and the only formation statement is hedged between two units. The record keeps the [Kem Kem Group](../../../stratigraphy/k/kem-kem.yml) with no formation, member or bed. Governs those empty fields on *Sigilmassasaurus brevicollis*.

**Evidence.**
- russell1996b, p. 361: "TYPE. — Cervical vertebra NMC 41857 (Fig. 10)", with no locality or bed; abstract, p. 349: "The 'Grès rouges infracénomaniens' of southern Morocco, possibly of Albian age"; p. 397: fossils "from the Tafilalt displayed at the Tucson Gem and Mineral Show between 1992 and 1994".
- evers2015a, p. 8: "Type locality and horizon: Kem Kem area, probably close to the town of Taouz (K Martyn, pers. comm. to OR, 2013), Ifezouane or Aoufous Formation, Cenomanian".
- ibrahim2020a, p. 8: "commercially acquired fossils, probably collected from the Kem Kem beds".

**Ruled out.**
- *Gara Sbaa or Douira Formation.* Ibrahim and colleagues (2020) do not assign the specimen to either.
- *Kem Kem Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Kem Kem Fm., Morocco" in a table (jones2026c, p. 498); Kem Kem is a group.
- *The base of the sequence.* Russell's "suggests that the bone was derived from the base of the sequence" (russell1996b, p. 369) is about another specimen, NMC 41629.

**Open.** Whether "Ifezouane or Aoufous" can be narrowed. The locality comes from a personal communication (Evers and colleagues describe their source only by initials); McFeeters and colleagues (2013), on the specimen's provenance, have not been read.
