# *Staurikosaurus*

## Which level of the Alemoa Member is the Staurikosaurus holotype from?

*2026-09-06*

**Conclusion.** No `part` is recorded. Langer and colleagues (2018) correlate the exposures at the type locality with the lower Alemoa at Cerro da Alemoa, but identify those exposures with the type site only as "most probably". Governs the absent `location.part`, with `location.member: Alemoa`, on [*Staurikosaurus pricei*](../../../genera/S/Staurikosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bittencourt2009a, p. 3: "Type locality. An outcrop of the Alemoa Member, Santa Maria Formation (Carnian), locality Sanga Grande".
- langer2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 4: about 3 m of the Alemoa Member exposed along small ravines "that most probably correspond to what remains of the type-locality (Sanga Grande) of *Staurikosaurus pricei* (Colbert, 1970), correlating with the lower portion of the unit at Cerro da Alemoa".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: upper`.* Novas and colleagues write that the outcrop "belongs to the upper portion of Santa Maria Formation" (novas2021a, journal pre-proof, p. 34). That is a position in the formation, and the record's finest unit is the member.

**Open.** Whether the Sanga Grande ravines are the type site. If that identification is accepted, `part: lower` follows from Langer and colleagues.
