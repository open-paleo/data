# *Sonidosaurus*

## What is the *Sonidosaurus saihangaobiensis* holotype's number?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** LH V 0010, Long Hao Geologic and Paleontological Research Center. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [LH V 0010]` on [*Sonidosaurus saihangaobiensis*](../../../genera/S/Sonidosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2006c, p. 21: "Holotype: LH V 0010 (Long Hao Geologic and Paleontological Research Center), a partial skeleton preserving five dorsal vertebrae".

**Ruled out.**
- *LH V0010 as a different number.* Jones (2026) gives "LH V0010" (jones2026b, p. 385). It is the same number without the space. Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) print "LH V 0010" (molina-pérez2020a, p. 268).

**Open.** Nothing.
