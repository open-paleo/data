# *Sonidosaurus*

## Where does *Sonidosaurus* sit within Titanosauria?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved within Titanosauria. Its describers ran no numerical analysis; Ksepka and Norell (2010) recover it at the base of the clade, outside Lithostrotia, and Averianov and Sues (2016) regard it as a lithostrotian. Governs `parent: Titanosauria` and the dispute on [*Sonidosaurus saihangaobiensis*](../../../genera/S/Sonidosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2006c, p. 23: "A numerical cladistic analysis is beyond the scope of the present paper"; p. 25: "the titanosaurian affinities of Sonidosaurus are strongly supported (Characters 1-13), but its systematic position within the Titanosauria is ambiguous".
- ksepka2010a, Fig. 4, p. 12: *Sonidosaurus* in a polytomy with IGM 100/3005 and the node uniting *Malawisaurus* and Lithostrotia; p. 13: "Whether the Late Cretaceous titanosaur Sonidosaurus saihangaobiensis from Inner Mongolia and IGM 100/3005 form a clade remains unresolved".
- averianov2020a, p. 20: "the phylogenetic position of Sonidosaurus is unclear".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lithostrotia as the parent.* The analysis of Ksepka and Norell (2010) places it outside the clade.

**Open.** Its position in further analyses.

## What is the *Sonidosaurus saihangaobiensis* holotype's number?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** LH V 0010, Long Hao Geologic and Paleontological Research Center. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [LH V 0010]` on [*Sonidosaurus saihangaobiensis*](../../../genera/S/Sonidosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2006c, p. 21: "Holotype: LH V 0010 (Long Hao Geologic and Paleontological Research Center), a partial skeleton preserving five dorsal vertebrae".

**Ruled out.**
- *LH V0010 as a different number.* Jones (2026) gives "LH V0010" (jones2026b, p. 385). It is the same number without the space. Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) print "LH V 0010" (molina-pérez2020a, p. 268).

**Open.** Nothing.
