# *Sanjuansaurus*

## Which member of the Ischigualasto Formation is the *Sanjuansaurus* holotype from?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** The Cancha de Bochas Member, as the describing paper gives it. The holotype lies 40 m above the base of the formation, which is the height the same paper gives for the base of the Cancha de Bochas, so the specimen sits at the boundary with the La Peña Member below. Governs `location.member` on [*Sanjuansaurus gordilloi*](../../../genera/S/Sanjuansaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- alcober2010a, p. 56: the members are "the La Peña (from the base to 40 m), the Cancha de Bochas (40 to 180 m)".
- alcober2010a, p. 57: "excavated at the La Gallinita locality, which is located in the lowest levels of the Cancha de Bochas Member. It was found 40 m above the base of the formation."
- alcober2010a, p. 60, Type locality: "found in the Cancha de Bochas Member (Currie et al. 2009), 40 m above the base of Ischigualasto Formation. The type locality, informally called "Herrera de la base", is located 3 km northwestern of "Cancha de Bochas" locality".

**Ruled out.**
- *La Peña Member.* Novas and colleagues (2021) write that the holotype was "collected from 40 metres above the base of the Ischigualasto Formation (La Peña Member)" (novas2021a, accepted manuscript, p. 43). They give the same height as the describing paper and no new section, and that height is the base of the Cancha de Bochas in the describing paper's own scheme.

**Open.** Whether the level is at or just below the member boundary. A measured section through the type locality that places the specimen against the La Peña–Cancha de Bochas contact would settle it. The describing paper names the site both "La Gallinita" (p. 57) and "Herrera de la base" (p. 60) without saying whether they are one site; the record takes the type-locality name.
