# *Sarcosaurus*

## Which unit and age belong to the *Sarcosaurus woodi* holotype, and which to the Wilmcote material?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** The holotype is from the *bucklandi* zone of the Scunthorpe Mudstone Formation at Barrow upon Soar, Leicestershire, lowermost Sinemurian. The Blue Lias Formation, its Rugby Limestone Member, Wilmcote and the Hettangian belong to the referred specimens from Warwickshire. Governs `location.formation`, `location.locality` and `stage: [Sinemurian]` on [*Sarcosaurus woodi*](../../../genera/S/Sarcosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ezcurra2020a, advance-access version, p. 6, Systematic palaeontology: "Type locality and horizon: Barrow upon Soar, Leicestershire, England, UK. Bucklandi zone of the Scunthorpe Mudstone Formation, Lias Group, Early Jurassic (lowermost Sinemurian)".
- ezcurra2020a, p. 6: "Locality and horizons of referred specimens: Wilmcote, Warwickshire, England, UK. NHMUK PV R3542 is from the angulata zone of the Blue Lias Formation … (upper Hettangian); and WARMS G667–690 comes from the bucklandi Zone of the upper part of the Rugby Limestone Member of the Blue Lias Formation".
- andrews1921a, p. 570: "from the Lower Lias of Barrow-on-Soar, Leicestershire".

**Ruled out.**
- *Blue Lias Formation, Wilmcote, Hettangian–Sinemurian.* Each belongs to the referred material, NHMUK PV R3542 (the type of *Sarcosaurus andrewsi*) and WARMS G667–690, not to the holotype (ezcurra2020a, p. 6).

**Open.** Nothing.
