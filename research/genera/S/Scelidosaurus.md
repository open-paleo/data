# *Scelidosaurus*

## Is the *Scelidosaurus harrisonii* lectotype from the "Lower Lias" or the Charmouth Mudstone Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Black Ven Marl Member of the Charmouth Mudstone Formation, Upper Sinemurian. No bed is recorded. Governs `location.formation: Charmouth Mudstone`, `location.member: Black Ven Marl` and the empty `location.bed` on [*Scelidosaurus harrisonii*](../../../genera/S/Scelidosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- norman2020a, p. 9: "*Stratigraphic range:* Charmouth Mudstone Formation (Upper Sinemurian), Black Ven Marl Member, *Asteroceras obtusum* Zone, *Obtusum* Subzone".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Lower Lias".* Weishampel and colleagues (2004) give "Lower Lias (Dorset), England | late Sinemurian" (weishampel2004a, p. 336, Table 15.1). "Lower Lias" is an older informal name for the interval, not a unit of the current scheme (see [Lias Group](../../topics/lias-group.md)). Jones (2026) gives "Charmouth Mudstone Fm., United Kingdom" (jones2026a, p. 399).
- *The Topstones or Stonebarrow Flatstones Bed.* Norman names the nodule beds "locally referred to as the Topstones Bed (David Sole pers. comm., 2018) and (perhaps) the Stonebarrow Flatstones Bed" (norman2020a, p. 9). One rests on a personal communication and the other on a "perhaps", so neither is recorded (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-personal-communication-is-not-a-published-determination)).

**Open.** Nothing.
