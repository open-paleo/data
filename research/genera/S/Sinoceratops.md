# *Sinoceratops*

## Where in the Hongtuya Formation does the *Sinoceratops* holotype lie?

*2026-09-05*

**Conclusion.** No part is recorded. The describing paper gives only the Wangshi Group. Later statements that place the Zangjiazhuang site within the Hongtuya disagree: two put it in the lower part, one of them citing Liu and colleagues (2010), and one puts both of its bone beds in the middle part, citing Liu and colleagues (2011) and Kuang and colleagues (2013). No source ties ZCDM V0010 to either of the two bone beds. *Sinankylosaurus* and *Zhuchengtitan* from the same site carry `part: lower` from their describing papers' statements about their own specimens. Governs the absence of `location.part` on [*Sinoceratops zhuchengensis*](../../../genera/S/Sinoceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2010e, p. 1632, Type locality and horizon: "Zangjiazhuang, Zhucheng, Shandong Province, China. Upper Cretaceous Wangshi Group".
- mo2017a, p. 1502: "上白垩统王氏群中上部（红土崖组底部和辛格庄组上部）" ("the middle and upper Upper Cretaceous Wangshi Group (the base of the Hongtuya Formation and the upper part of the Xingezhuang Formation)", translated), for the *Zhuchengtitan* site at Zangjiazhuang, citing Liu and colleagues (2010).
- wang2020a, p. 959: *Sinankylosaurus* is from "上白垩统王氏群红土崖组下部" ("the lower part of the Hongtuya Formation, Upper Cretaceous Wangshi Group", translated), at Zangjiazhuang.
- xing2014b, Systematic Paleontology, *Shantungosaurus giganteus*, Locality and horizon: "Two stratigraphically separate bonebeds with a five-meter-thick interval were recognized at the Zangjiazhuang locality"; "the two bonebeds at the Zangjiazhuang locality are stratigraphically higher and restricted to the middle portion (Liu et al., 2011; Kuang et al., 2013)".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`.* It has two site statements behind it, against Xing and colleagues' middle portion. The two readings rest on different studies by the same group of authors, and neither concerns this holotype.
- *`part: middle`.* Xing and colleagues' statement is opposed by the two lower-part statements.
- *An and colleagues' "Dinosaur bones are mostly buried in the lower Hongtuya Formation" as a further vote for lower* (an2015a, p. 81). It covers the Zhucheng bone beds together, including Kugou and Longgujian, which Xing and colleagues place below Zangjiazhuang.

**Open.** Which Zangjiazhuang bone bed held ZCDM V0010, and where the two beds sit in the formation. Liu and colleagues (2010, 2011) and Kuang and colleagues (2013) have not been read.

## Is the *Sinoceratops zhuchengensis* holotype from the Hongtuya or the Xingezhuang Formation?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** The Hongtuya Formation. An and colleagues (2015), who dated the Zhucheng bone beds, name the species among the Hongtuya taxa. Governs `location.formation: Hongtuya` on [*Sinoceratops zhuchengensis*](../../../genera/S/Sinoceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- an2015a, p. 81: "Many dinosaur fossils, only found in the uppermost Upper Cretaceous strata, such as Sinoceratops zhuchengensis, Zhuchengtyrannus magnus, Tsintaosaurus spinorhinus … have been reported in recent years from the Hongtuya Formation".
- xu2010e, p. 1632, Type locality and horizon: "Zangjiazhuang, Zhucheng, Shandong Province, China. Upper Cretaceous Wangshi Group".

**Ruled out.**
- *Xingezhuang Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Xingezhuang Fm., China" (jones2026a, p. 412). The one source read that names the Xingezhuang at Zangjiazhuang puts the site at the contact, "the base of the Hongtuya Formation and the upper part of the Xingezhuang Formation" (mo2017a, p. 1502, translated). That statement concerns *Zhuchengtitan*, not this holotype.

**Open.** Nothing on the formation. Where in the Hongtuya the holotype lies is the subject of the 2026-09-05 entry.
