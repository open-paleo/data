## Where are the Stegouros holotype's position and horizon printed?

*2026-09-07*

**Conclusion.** The coordinate is printed only in the paper's Reporting Summary. The lower Dorotea follows the main text's horizon line rather than the Reporting Summary's "lower to mid section". Governs `location.coordinates` [-50.711867, -72.541411], `location.locality: SP17` and `location.part: lower` on [*Stegouros elengassen*](../../../genera/S/Stegouros.yml).

**Evidence.**
- soto-acuña2021a, p. 259, Locality and horizon: "Río de las Chinas Valley, Estancia Cerro Guido … Lower section of the Dorotea Formation (upper Campanian—lower Maastrichtian)".
- soto-acuña2021a, Reporting Summary, p. 2, Field work: "Location 50° 42' 42.72'' S /72° 32' 29, 08'' W".
- soto-acuña2021a, Reporting Summary, p. 3, Specimen provenance: "The site of provenance (SP17)"; "The fossil-bearing level is located within the lower to mid section of Dorotea Formation."

**Ruled out.**
