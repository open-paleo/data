# *Siamraptor*

## Is the *Siamraptor suwati* holotype one element or the whole NRRU-F01020001–022 series?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** One element: NRRU-F01020008, the posterior half of a right mandible. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NRRU-F01020008]` on [*Siamraptor suwati*](../../../genera/S/Siamraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- chokchaloemwong2019a, p. 6, Systematic paleontology: "**Holotype.** An articulated posterior half of the right mandible comprising the surangular, prearticular, and articular (NRRU-F01020008)."; "**Referred materials.** Disarticulated cranial and postcranial elements from at least three individuals".

**Ruled out.**
- *NRRU-F01020001 through 22.* Jones (2026) gives "NRRU-F01020001 through 22—cranial and skeletal fragments" (jones2026c, p. 499). This is the whole recovered assemblage, the holotype together with referred material from at least three individuals.

**Open.** Nothing.
