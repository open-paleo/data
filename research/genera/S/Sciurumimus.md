# *Sciurumimus*

## Which formation is the *Sciurumimus* holotype from?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** The Torleite Formation, in Niebuhr and Pürner's (2014) scheme for the Franconian Alb. They place the find, from the Rygol quarry near Painten, in the siliceous platy facies described under the Torleite, and Foth and colleagues (2020) give the Torleite for the holotype. Governs `location.formation: Torleite` on [*Sciurumimus albersdoerferi*](../../../genera/S/Sciurumimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2012a, p. 11746: "Rygol quarry, near Painten, Bavaria, Germany. Thin-bedded to laminated micritic limestones that are equivalent to the upper part of the Rögling Formation".
- niebuhr2014a, p. 34, under the Torleite Formation: *Sciurumimus albersdoerferi* "wurde im Steinbruch Rygol bei Painten in der Kieselplattenfazies gefunden" ("was found in the Rygol quarry near Painten in the siliceous platy facies", translated).
- foth2020a, Taxon sampling: "The holotypes of *Sciurumimus albersdoerferi* (BMMS BK 11) and *Juravenator starki* (JME Sch 200) represent both early juvenile theropod dinosaurs from the Torleite Formation (upper Kimmeridgian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Painten Formation.* Rauhut and colleagues (2016) list *Sciurumimus* under "Painten Fm." in a table of theropod occurrences (rauhut2016a, p. 46, Table 3). The Painten overlies the Torleite: "the upper part of the Torleite Formation, which underlies the Painten Formation" (foth2025a, p. 19). The quarry's name, Painten, is the only link to it.
- *Rögling as the formation.* In Niebuhr and Pürner's scheme the Rögling is a subformation of the Geisental Formation, above the Torleite: the Torleite type section runs "mit der Hangendgrenze zur Rögling-Subformation der Geisental-Formation" ("with its upper boundary at the Rögling Subformation of the Geisental Formation", translated; niebuhr2014a, p. 32). Rauhut and colleagues' "Rögling Formation" is the name of the older scheme.

**Open.** Nothing.
