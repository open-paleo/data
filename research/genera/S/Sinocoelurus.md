# *Sinocoelurus*

## How many teeth and numbers make up the Sinocoelurus type material?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Four teeth catalogued under three numbers, V232–234; no holotype was designated, so they are syntypes (our reading). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IVPP V232, IVPP V233, IVPP V234]` and `specimen_type: syntype` on *Sinocoelurus fragilis*.

**Evidence.**
- young1942a, p. 297: "Four isolated teeth, V232-234 (Pl. I, Figs. 6-8)"; p. 298 gives four sets of dimensions and identifies the worn tooth as "(V232) which is worn".
- young1942a, pp. 300–301: Young uses the same convention for *Szechuanosaurus*: "One broken tooth, V235; another broken tooth, V236; three fragmentary teeth, V238 and one nearly complete tooth, V239", "the six teeth".

**Ruled out.**
- *Four numbers, V232–V235.* Dong and colleagues list "Four isolated teeth (V232, V233, V234, V235)" (dong1983a, English translation, p. 57) and call the damaged tooth V233 (p. 58); V235 is a *Szechuanosaurus* number, and Young names the worn tooth V232.

**Open.** Nothing.
