# *Scansoriopteryx*

## Which unit is the Scansoriopteryx holotype from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Probably the Daohugou beds of the [Haifanggou Formation](../../../stratigraphy/h/haifanggou.yml), Bathonian–Callovian, at Daohugou, Ningcheng County, Inner Mongolia, if *Scansoriopteryx* and *Epidendrosaurus* are the same species. Governs `stage: [Bathonian, Callovian]` on *Scansoriopteryx heilmanni*, and bears on its empty `location.formation` and its region (CN-LN in the record; Daohugou is CN-NM).

**Evidence.**
- zhang2002a, p. 395: *Epidendrosaurus* from "Daohugou, Ningcheng County, East of Nei Mongol. Daohugou Formation, (?) Late Jurassic (Zhang 2002)."
- jones2026c, p. 393: "concluded that the two specimens are in fact the same species".
- xu2016b, p. 2231: "The strata exposed at the Daohugou Locality belong mainly to the Bathonian-Callovian Haifanggou Formation." See [the Daohugou and Linglongta phases](../../topics/daohugou-and-linglongta-phases.md).

**Ruled out.**
- *Basal Yixian Formation, Dawangzhangzi.* Czerkas and Yuan (2002) give "collected from the basal part of the Yixian Formation in the Dawangzhanzi fossil site of Lingyaun City, western Liaoning" (czerkas2002a, p. 70), with an age of "Late Jurassic to Early Cretaceous". No other source read for this note places it in the Yixian. If the two names are one species, the specimen's stated provenance conflicts with the *Epidendrosaurus* locality.
- *Tiaojishan Formation, Oxfordian.* Jones (2026) gives "Tiaojishan Fm., China, Late Jurassic, Oxfordian" in its specimen table (jones2026c, p. 508), assigning Oxfordian to every Yanliao paravian; the Daohugou beds belong to the older Haifanggou phase.

**Open.** Whether the *Scansoriopteryx* holotype itself came from Daohugou. The placement depends on the synonymy with *Epidendrosaurus*, and the Daohugou assignment is not stated for *Scansoriopteryx* by name in Xu and colleagues (2016). Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) date the two names differently, "the Middle Jurassic and early Lower Cretaceous, respectively" (molina-pérez2019a, p. 57).
