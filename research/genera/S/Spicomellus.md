# *Spicomellus*

## Which formation is the Boulahfa site in, and what is the Spicomellus holotype's provenance?

*2026-09-06*

**Conclusion.** The El Mers III Formation. Maidment and colleagues (2021) moved the site up from the El Mers II on later fieldwork. The *Spicomellus* holotype's locality comes from the dealers who sold it, checked by the authors' visits to the site. Governs `location.formation: El Mers III` and `location.locality: Boulahfa` on [*Spicomellus afer*](../../../genera/S/Spicomellus.yml) and [*Adratiklit boulahfa*](../../../genera/A/Adratiklit.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maidment2021a, p. 1576: "Horizon and locality. El Mers III Formation (Upper Bathonian/ Callovian, Middle Jurassic …), Boulahfa, south of Boulemane"; "Maidment et al. reported the horizon as lying in the El Mers II Formation, but subsequent field observations at the site suggest that it may lie slightly higher in the stratigraphy."
- maidment2021a, p. 1579: "The locality information was provided by Moussa Direct and confirmed through discussion with the Moroccan fossil dealer who sold it to Moussa Direct", and two of the authors "visited the locality in 2019 and 2020"; the same paper places "*Spicomellus* and *Adratiklit* in the Middle Jurassic El Mers III Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *El Mers II Formation.* Maidment and colleagues (2020) gave "El Mers Group, probably El Mers II Formation (Bathonian)" (maidment2020a, p. 85), which the 2021 paper revises.
- *The Boulahfa North coordinate.* Zafaty and colleagues print "33.320520°N; 4.712273°W" for HIIUC BN, a site "located north of the Boulahfa quarry" (zafaty2024a, HAL manuscript, Introduction and Materials and methods), which yielded a different stegosaur.

**Open.** The *Spicomellus* findspot rests on dealer testimony; no excavation of the holotype was observed.
