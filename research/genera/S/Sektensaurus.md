# *Sektensaurus*

## What is the *Sektensaurus sanjuanboscoi* holotype's collection code?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** UNPSJB-PV 1054, with its parts numbered 1054/2 to 1054/16 and higher. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [UNPSJB-PV 1054]` on [*Sektensaurus sanjuanboscoi*](../../../genera/S/Sektensaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ibiricu2019a, p. 277, Holotype: "UNPSJB-PV 1054, an associated partial skeleton consisting of an incomplete dorsal arch (UNPSJB-PV 1054/2), three dorsal centra (UNPSJB-PV 1054/3-5)".

**Ruled out.**
- *UNPSJB-PB 1054.* Jones (2026) gives "UNPSJB-PB 1054—partial skeleton" (jones2026a, p. 403). The paper writes UNPSJB-PV throughout, so PB replaces the V with a B.

**Open.** Nothing.
