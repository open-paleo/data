# *Struthiosaurus*

## Which formation is the Struthiosaurus transylvanicus holotype from?

*2026-09-07*

**Conclusion.** The Sânpetru Formation, on the word of a compilation: Nopcsa (1915) names only the village, and the review by Csiki-Sava and colleagues (2015) only the Haţeg Basin. Governs `location.formation: Sânpetru` and `location.locality: Sânpetru` on *Struthiosaurus transylvanicus* ([*Struthiosaurus*](../../../genera/S/Struthiosaurus.yml)).

**Evidence.**
- nopcsa1915a, Deskriptiver Teil: "Einige zusammengehörende, bei Szentpéterfalva gefundene Reste bilden den Typus der neuen Species" ("Some associated remains found at Szentpéterfalva form the type of the new species", translated).
- vickaryous2004a, p. 366, Table 17.1: "*S. transylvanicus* Nopcsa, 1915 | Sânpetru Formation (Hunedoara), unnamed unit (Alba), Romania | late Maastrichtian".
- csiki-sava2015a, p. 64: Nopcsa "named associated fossils (braincase, vertebrae, and elements of the shoulder girdle) of a small-sized nodosaurid from the Haţeg Basin as *Struthiosaurus transylvanicus*".

**Open.** A primary source placing the type in the Sânpetru Formation. Pereda Suberbiola and Galton (1997), the revision of this material, is the likeliest and has not been read. That Szentpéterfalva is Sânpetru is our reading; no source read states it.
