# *Saurolophus*

## Are PIN 551/8 and PIN 551/356 the same *Saurolophus angustirostris* holotype?

*2026-10-10*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Supersedes the 2026-09-03 entry, which kept PIN 551/8 but ruled out PIN 551/356 as a different specimen. Bell and colleagues (2017) equate the two numbers, Bell (2011) labels each the holotype, and the reason given for separating them, Dewaele and colleagues (2015) listing PIN 551/356 among the subadults, matches Bell's own description of the holotype as a subadult. `specimen_id` stays PIN 551/8, the form most sources give, and the other number is recorded in the block's notes rather than as a `former_ids` hop, because no source read says which number came first or which the collection uses now. Governs `type_specimen.notes` on *Saurolophus angustirostris* in [*Saurolophus*](../../../genera/S/Saurolophus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bell2017b, p. 81: "the complete holotype skeleton (PIN 551–8, =PIN 551/ 356) was used as a comparative standard".
- bell2017b, p. 77, Fig. 3: "B, PIN 551-356 (holotype)"; the text adds that "Rozhdestvensky (1952) chose the best of those specimens (PIN 551/ 356)".
- bell2011a, p. 705: "PIN 551/8 (holotype; sub− adult skull and skeleton)"; p. 704, Table 1: "PIN 551/356 (holotype)".
- dewaele2015a, Results: the frontal is "progressively larger in subadults (PIN 551/356) and adults".

**Ruled out.**
- *PIN 551/356 as a different specimen.* The 2026-09-03 entry read Dewaele's "subadults (PIN 551/356)" as excluding the holotype, but the holotype is itself a subadult.
- *A `former_ids` entry.* It needs a direction and a reason; Bell and colleagues (2017) tie PIN 551/356 to Rozhdestvensky's choice, which suggests it is the older form, but they do not state a renumbering.

**Open.** Which number is current at the Paleontological Institute, and when the second came into use. Rozhdestvensky (1952), who designated the holotype, and the later monographic descriptions of its skull and postcranium are the likely sources; none is held.

## Do later sources or figures settle which specimen is MPC-D 100/764?

*2026-10-10*

**Conclusion.** No. Three papers added since the 2026-10-04 entry, and the figures of all three papers that print the number, leave the conflict where it was, and one of the original papers weakens the adult reading. Bell (2011) lists "MPC 100/764 (adult skull)" among specimens "of unknown provenance", which fits Hone's adult in age but not his account of a skeleton excavated at Bugin Tsav in 1995, and fits the poached block of Dewaele and colleagues (2015) in provenance but not in age. Governs nothing yet; the two `sensu` labels on *Saurolophus angustirostris* stay. Adds to the 2026-10-04 entry, which it does not supersede.

**Evidence.**
- bell2011a, p. 705: "All specimens come from the late ?Campanian–Maastrichtian Nemegt Formation from the areas of Nemegt and Altan Uul, Mongolia except UALVP49067 and MPC 100/764, which are of unknown provenance."
- hone2010a, p. 628: the skeleton was excavated "in 1995 by the HMNS−MPC joint paleontological expedition party" at Bugin Tsav, and "is being described in full by other authors".
- Figures checked at high resolution, with no catalog label legible on any specimen: hone2010a, Figs 1–3 (one hand-inked mark on the humerus, two glyphs, not a number); dewaele2015a, Figs 1, 3–5, 7–9 and 14; bell2011a, Figs 2–3, the photographed adult skull.
- suzuki2000a, p. 84: of the 1995 season, only "Preparation of the skull of Gallimimus (ornithomimid) collected from Bugin Tsav in 1995"; its specimen table uses expedition field numbers ("980804 BgT UB-1,2,3", *Saurolophus* footprint casts), not MPC numbers.
- watabe2010a, p. 55: Bugin Tsav was surveyed "in 1993, 1994, 1995, 1998, and 2006", and its *Saurolophus* skeletons "are discovered solely from the lower part"; the report lists no specimens.
- bell2017b, p. 86: at the Dragon's Tomb, "small juvenile and nestling-sized individuals were not observed in the deposit"; p. 88: poaching there "did not start until sometime after September 2001". Its specimen table uses 2009 field numbers (DT-001 to DT-052), not MPC numbers.

**Ruled out.**
- *Bell (2011) as independent support for the adult reading.* His provenance statement conflicts with Hone's, so he may be describing a different adult skull under the number.

**Open.** The report on the 1995 expedition season and the preparation report of the Hayashibara Museum of Natural Sciences for 1999–2008, both in its *Research Bulletin*, are the likeliest printed sources for the field and catalog numbers of the Bugin Tsav skeleton; neither has been read. Failing those, the authors of the two papers.

## Is MPC-D 100/764 the adult Saurolophus or the perinatal nest?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Both carry the number in print: an adult *Saurolophus* described by Hone (2010) and Bell (2011), and a perinatal block reported under the same number by Dewaele and colleagues (2015). The records keep both, each qualified by its paper. Governs `notable_specimens` on *Saurolophus angustirostris*.

**Evidence.**
- hone2010a, p. 628: "(MPC−D100/764, currently on loan at HMNS) … The specimen was an adult animal"; Fig. 1: "MPC−D100/764, a left humerus of hadrosaurid *Saurolophus*".
- bell2011a, p. 705: "MPC 100/764 (adult skull)".
- dewaele2015a, p. 2: "In 2013, the specimen was transferred to the Royal Belgian Institute of Natural Sciences… led to the official return of the specimen to Mongolia, where it is now catalogued as specimen MPC-D100/764."

**Ruled out.**
- *One specimen.* The adult and the perinatal block are different specimens.

**Open.** Which catalogue entry is correct. Dewaele and colleagues do not mention the earlier specimen; that the repatriated block was given an existing number is an inference.

## Is the *Saurolophus angustirostris* holotype PIN 551/8 or PIN 551/356?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** PIN 551/8. Bell's (2011) text gives that number, and so do Słowiak and colleagues (2020). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on *Saurolophus angustirostris* in [*Saurolophus*](../../../genera/S/Saurolophus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bell2011a, p. 705: "Holotype: PIN 551/8"; "PIN 551/8 (holotype; subadult skull and skeleton)".
- słowiak2020a, p. 584: "the most complete skeleton of Saurolophus angustirostris (PIN 551/8, holotype)".

**Ruled out.**
- *PIN 551/356.* Bell's Table 1 heads one column "PIN 551/356 (holotype)" (bell2011a, p. 704). The same paper's text gives PIN 551/8, and Dewaele and colleagues (2015, Table 2) list PIN 551/356 among the subadults (dewaele2015a, p. 30).

**Open.** The designating paper, Rozhdestvensky (1952), has not been read; it would confirm the number.
