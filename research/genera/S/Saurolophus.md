# *Saurolophus*

## Is MPC-D 100/764 the adult Saurolophus or the perinatal nest?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Both carry the number in print: an adult *Saurolophus* described by Hone (2010) and Bell (2011), and a perinatal block reported under the same number by Dewaele and colleagues (2015). The records keep both, each qualified by its paper. Governs `notable_specimens` on *Saurolophus angustirostris*.

**Evidence.**
- hone2010a, p. 628: "(MPC−D100/764, currently on loan at HMNS) … The specimen was an adult animal"; Fig. 1: "MPC−D100/764, a left humerus of hadrosaurid *Saurolophus*".
- bell2011a, p. 705: "MPC 100/764 (adult skull)".
- dewaele2015a, p. 2: "In 2013, the specimen was transferred to the Royal Belgian Institute of Natural Sciences… led to the official return of the specimen to Mongolia, where it is now catalogued as specimen MPC-D100/764."

**Ruled out.**
- *One specimen.* The adult and the perinatal block are different specimens.

**Open.** Which catalogue entry is correct. Dewaele and colleagues do not mention the earlier specimen; that the repatriated block was given an existing number is an inference.
