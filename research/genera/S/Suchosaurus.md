# *Suchosaurus*

## Which unit is the Suchosaurus cultridens holotype tooth from?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** The Grinstead Clay. Governs `location.formation: Grinstead Clay` on [*Suchosaurus cultridens*](../../../genera/S/Suchosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barker2021a, p. 2: teeth referred to the nomen dubium "*Suchosaurus cultridens*", "from the Valanginian Grinstead Clay Formation of West Sussex".
- barker2023a, p. 4, Fig. 1 caption: "the Grinstead Clay Formation, which subdivides the Tunbridge Wells Sands Formation in [Batten & Austen (2011)] and from which the ''*Suchosaurus cultridens*'' type specimen was discovered".

**Ruled out.**
- *Wadhurst Clay.* Tucker and colleagues list "the Valanginian–Hauterivian Wahurst Clay Formation of England (e.g., the nomen dubium "*Suchosaurus cultridens*" (Mateus et al., 2011)" (tucker2022a, p. 16). Mateus and colleagues (2011), the work they cite, name no unit for the tooth (mateus2011b, searched for unit and place names), and Barker and colleagues give the Grinstead Clay.
- *Tunbridge Wells Sand Formation.* Carrano and colleagues give it for the *Valdoraptor* metatarsals from Cuckfield (carrano2012a, p. 256), not for this tooth. That the Grinstead Clay is a member of the Tunbridge Wells Sand in other schemes is a question of rank, settled for the registry in [Wealden units](../../topics/wealden-units.md).

**Open.** Nothing.
