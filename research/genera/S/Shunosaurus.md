# *Shunosaurus*

## How was IVPP V9065 established as the Shunosaurus lii holotype?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** It is the sole specimen listed when the species was named (so the holotype by monotypy, our reading), and Dong and colleagues (1984) call it the type in passing. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: IVPP V9065` and `specimen_type: holotype` on *Shunosaurus lii*.

**Evidence.**
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 20: "Specimens: An incomplete skeleton … (V9065.1-23)", under a "Specimens" heading where other taxa have "Type specimen"; the heading difference may be the translator's.
- dong1984b, p. 69: "原型标本为一具不完整的骨架，缺失头骨，尾椎。化石编号 V 9065。" ("the type specimen is an incomplete skeleton lacking the skull and caudals; specimen number V 9065", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *The 1983 pelvis as part of V9065.* Dong and colleagues later write that it was "曾误认为是 V 9065 号标本的" ("previously mistaken as belonging to specimen V 9065", translated) and refer it to *Datousaurus* (dong1984b, p. 72).

**Open.** Whether the 1983 diagnosis's "high and long ilium" rests on that pelvis, since the holotype's ilium is incomplete (dong1983a, English translation, pp. 20–21; our reading). Zhang (1988), on the referred skeleton ZDM T5401, has not been read.

## Is *Shunosaurus lii* from the Shaximiao Formation or a separate Xiashaximiao Formation?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** The lower part of the Shaximiao Formation. Current usage treats the Shaximiao as one formation with a lower and an upper part. Governs `location.formation: Shaximiao` and `location.part: lower` on [*Shunosaurus lii*](../../../genera/S/Shunosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- moore2020a, p. 1301: "radiometric dating of detrital zircons from beds of the lower Shaximiao Formation at Dashanpu now places the entire Shaximiao Formation" in the Late Jurassic.
- hui2021a, title: "New stegosaurs from the Middle Jurassic Lower Member of the Shaximiao Formation of Chongqing, China".

**Ruled out.**
- *Xiashaximiao Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Xiashaximiao Fm., China" (jones2026b, p. 376). *Xia* is Chinese for lower, and the name treats the lower part as a formation of its own, which is the older scheme.
- *"Lower Shaximiao Formation" as a formal name.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) give "Lower Shaximiao Formation (Sichuan)" (upchurch2004a, p. 262, Table 13.1). A positional word is never part of a formal name (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-member-takes-a-formal-name-a-position-goes-in-part)).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is ZG65430 the *Shunosaurus lii* type?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype is IVPP V9065, a skeleton without its skull (see the 2026-10-04 entry). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IVPP V9065]` on [*Shunosaurus lii*](../../../genera/S/Shunosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1984b, p. 69: "原型标本为一具不完整的骨架，缺失头骨，尾椎。化石编号 V 9065。" ("the type specimen is an incomplete skeleton lacking the skull and caudals; specimen number V 9065", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *ZG65430.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give "ZG65430 Subadult | Skull" for the species (molina-pérez2020a, p. 250). It is a skull, and the type has none. Jones (2026) gives "IVPP V9065" (jones2026b, p. 376).

**Open.** Nothing.
