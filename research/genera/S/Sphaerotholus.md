# *Sphaerotholus*

## Which part of the Hell Creek is the Sphaerotholus triregnum holotype from?

*2026-09-06*

**Conclusion.** No `part` is recorded. Woodruff and colleagues (2023) place ROM 53583 in the lower half of the formation and, in the same paper, in the middle third. Governs the absent `location.part` on *Sphaerotholus triregnum* ([*Sphaerotholus*](../../../genera/S/Sphaerotholus.yml)).

**Evidence.**
- woodruff2023a, p. 4, Horizon & locality: "from the lower half of the Hell Creek Formation", "no more than 500 m from the holotype locality of the dromaeosaurid *Acheroraptor*"; "its precise stratigraphic position in the Hell Creek Formation is poorly constrained. However, palynological analysis of the *Acheroraptor* type locality indicates that it appears to be in approximately the upper lower to middle third of the Hell Creek Formation (Wilkinson et al. 2020)".
- woodruff2023a, p. 18, Fig. 9 caption: "the occurrence of *S. triregnum* sp. nov. (ROM 53583) from within the middle third of the Hell Creek Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`.* The paper qualifies its "lower half" as poorly constrained, and its palynological placement of the neighboring *Acheroraptor* site reaches the middle third.

**Open.** Wilkinson and colleagues (2020), the palynological source, has not been read.
