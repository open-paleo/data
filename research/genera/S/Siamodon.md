# *Siamodon*

## Is *Siamodon* a valid hadrosauroid?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** Its validity is disputed, and it is placed no closer than Hadrosauriformes. Its describers distinguish it from other advanced iguanodontians; Norman (2015) finds no autapomorphies and treats it as a nomen dubium until more material is found. The describers place it among hadrosauroids by comparison, but the one analysis that resolves it puts it outside Hadrosauroidea. Governs `status: disputed`, `parent: Hadrosauriformes` and the dispute on [*Siamodon nimngami*](../../../genera/S/Siamodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- buffetaut2011a, p. 57: the maxilla "indicates that it belongs to a group of advanced iguanodontians, more derived than Iguanodon and closely related forms but less derived than hadrosaurids"; p. 58: "The closest similarities seem to be with Probactrosaurus".
- norman2015a, p. 61: "The diagnosis of this taxon does not include any autapomorphic states, which suggests that until more material is collected and described, this taxonomic name should be considered a nomen dubium. It is possible that this material is referable to Ratchasimasaurus".
- poole2022a, p. 61: *Siamodon* and *Ratchasimasaurus* "were named based on non-overlapping material", and "the fragmentary specimens reveal only characters" widespread in Styracosterna.
- madzia2019a, Figs 12–13, pp. 14–15: *Siamodon* in a polytomy at the Hadrosauriformes node, beside the *Iguanodon* clade and Hadrosauroidea.

**Ruled out.**
- *Hadrosauroidea as the placement.* It rests on comparison in the describing paper; the only analysis that resolves the genus places it outside Hadrosauroidea.
- *A synonym of Ratchasimasaurus.* Norman (2015) raises the possibility, but the two are based on different bones, a maxilla and a dentary, and no one has argued the case on shared characters.

**Open.** Whether further material from the Khok Kruat Formation supports a diagnosis.
