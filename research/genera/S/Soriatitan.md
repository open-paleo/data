# *Soriatitan*

## What coordinate does the Zorralbo I site take?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** The center of the grid square both describing papers print, 41.765506, -2.525391. The pair the records held lay about 0.6 km west of that square and no source prints it, so it is corrected as a degraded copy. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Soriatitan golmayensis*](../../../genera/S/Soriatitan.yml) and [*Magnamanus soriaensis*](../../../genera/M/Magnamanus.yml), both from Zorralbo I.

**Evidence.**
- royo-torres2017a, Geological setting: "The Zorralbo I site is located near the village of Zorralbo, in the municipality of Golmayo … (UTM coordinates: 30TWM394238".
- fuentesvidarte2016a: "The Zorralbo I site (UTM coordinates 30 TWM394238)" (translated).
- 30TWM394238 names the 100 m square whose center converts to 41.765506, -2.525391 and whose south-west corner to 41.765058, -2.525996 (our conversion).

**Ruled out.**
- *41.766701, -2.5333.* No source read prints it, and it falls outside the printed square.

**Open.** Nothing.
