# *Sinocalliopteryx*

## Is the *Sinocalliopteryx* holotype from the Hengdaozi Bed?

*2026-09-05*

**Conclusion.** No bed is recorded. Ji and colleagues (2007) use Hengdaozi as a place name, "Hengdaozi at the Sihetun area of Beipiao", with no rank word. Wang and colleagues (2016) designate a type section for the Hengdaozi Bed, one of the four beds of their Jianshangou Member, and describe a village of the same name 3.2 km east of the Sihetun Fossil Museum. Neither paper places the holotype in the bed. Governs `location.locality: Hengdaozi, Sihetun`, `location.member: Jianshangou` and the absence of `location.bed` on [*Sinocalliopteryx gigas*](../../../genera/S/Sinocalliopteryx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ji2007a, p. 9: "Type locality and horizon: Hengdaozi at the Sihetun area of Beipiao, western Liaoning Province, northeastern China; Yixian Formation, Lower Cretaceous".
- xing2012a, p. 1, Abstract: "Both specimens come from the Jianshangou Beds of the lower Yixian Formation".
- wang2016a, p. 118: the Hengdaozi Bed lies "above their Jiulongsong bed and our Anjiagou Bed and below our new Huangbanjigou Bed"; "Hengdaozi (横道子), Beipiao City, Chaoyang Prefecture, Liaoning Province ... is a village about 2.4 km south of site of the Huangbanjigou no. 1 core and 3.2 km east of the Sihetun Fossil Museum."

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether the holotype lies in the Hengdaozi Bed. Taking the bed from the village name is a name match, not a stratigraphic statement. Wang and colleagues do not mention *Sinocalliopteryx*. A paper giving the holotype's bed within the Jianshangou Member would settle it.
