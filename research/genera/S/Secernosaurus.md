# *Secernosaurus*

## Is the *Secernosaurus* holotype from the Lago Colhué Huapi Formation?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** The record follows Casal and colleagues (2015), who erected the Lago Colhué Huapi Formation for the strata at the headwaters of the Río Chico and count *Secernosaurus*, found about 4 km from the river's mouth, in its fauna. Ibiricu and colleagues (2019) write that the specimen has no clear stratigraphic provenance. Governs `location.formation: Lago Colhué Huapi` and `location.locality: Río Chico` on [*Secernosaurus koerneri*](../../../genera/S/Secernosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- casal2015a, p. 86: hadrosaurs at the headwaters of the Río Chico are "documentada por *Secernosaurus koerneri* (Brett-Surman 1979), hallado aproximadamente a 4 km de la embocadura del río Chico" ("documented by *Secernosaurus koerneri*, found about 4 km from the mouth of the Río Chico", translated).
- ibiricu2019a, p. 276, Introduction: *Secernosaurus koerneri* is "controversial in some aspects … because there is not a clear stratigraphic provenance".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lago Colhué Huapi as the locality.* The lake gives the formation its name; no source read places the holotype at a site of that name.

**Open.** Whether the holotype's horizon is known. Casal and colleagues place it by its find area, and Ibiricu and colleagues, among whom Casal is an author, call the provenance unclear. A field record or measured section that places the holotype in the formation would settle it.
