# *Sinosauropteryx*

## Is "Shangyuanxiang" a different site from the "Jianshangou-Sihetun area"?

*2026-09-05*

**Conclusion.** Probably not. The two papers on the holotype name its site differently, and the record follows Chen and colleagues (1998), who describe the part and counterpart together. Ji and Ji (1996), who name the species, give "the village of Shangyuanxiang". *Xiang* is the pinyin word for a township, so the name probably refers to Shangyuan township around Sihetun rather than to a separate site (our reading). Governs `location.locality: Jianshangou-Sihetun area` on [*Sinosauropteryx prima*](../../../genera/S/Sinosauropteryx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ji1996a, English translation, Description: "Type: A relatively complete skeleton housed at the Chinese Geological Museum (specimen #GMV2123)"; "Locality and stratigraphic position: Lower section of the Upper Jurassic Yixian Fm. from the village of Shangyuanxiang, Beipiao Municipality, Liaoning Province."
- chen1998a, p. 147: "Holotype. Part (National Geological Museum of China, GMV 2123) and counterpart (Nanjing Institute of Geology and Palaeontology NIGP 127586)"; "Locality and horizon. Jianshangou-Sihetun area of Beipiao, Liaoning".
- wang2016a, p. 118: the Anjiagou Bed yielded "the type specimen of *Sinosauropteryx prima* (Chen et al., 1998)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** No source read states that Shangyuanxiang and the Jianshangou-Sihetun area are the same place. Only an English translation of Ji and Ji (1996) has been read, so the Chinese place name is unchecked.

## Is NIGP 127587 part of the *Sinosauropteryx prima* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype is GMV 2123 with its counterpart slab NIGP 127586. NIGP 127587 is a second, referred skeleton. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [GMV 2123, NIGP 127586]` on [*Sinosauropteryx prima*](../../../genera/S/Sinosauropteryx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ji1996a, English translation, Description: "Type: A relatively complete skeleton housed at the Chinese Geological Museum (specimen #GMV2123)".
- chen1998a, p. 147: "Holotype. Part (National Geological Museum of China, GMV 2123) and counterpart (Nanjing Institute of Geology and Palaeontology NIGP 127586)"; "Referred specimen. Nanjing Institute of Geology and Palaeontology NIGP 127587."

**Ruled out.**
- *NIGP 127587.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) list it as the species' specimen (molina-pérez2019a, p. 263). Chen and colleagues refer it. Jones (2026) gives "GMV 2123 and NIGP 127586" as the type and NIGP 127587 after them (jones2026c, p. 501).

**Open.** Nothing.
