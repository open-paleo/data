# *Shamosaurus*

## Is the *Shamosaurus* holotype PIN 3779/2, and what is PIN 3779/1?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** The holotype is PIN 3779/2, the skull with part of the postcranial skeleton. PIN 3779/1 is a second, partial skull from the same locality, which Tumanova lists under "? *Shamosaurus*". Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Shamosaurus scutatus*](../../../genera/S/Shamosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tumanova1987a, p. 7, Material: "3779/2 — череп с неполным посткраниальным скелетом и частью панциря. Хамрын-Ус" ("3779/2 — skull with incomplete postcranial skeleton and part of the armor. Khamryn-Us", translated), and under "? Shamosaurus", "3779/1 — часть черепа" ("part of skull", translated).
- arbour2015a, advance-access version, p. 20: "Holotype. PIN 3779/2, skull and partial postcranial skeleton"; "Referred specimens. PIN 3779/1, portion of skull, (Khamryn-Us; Dzun Bayn Formation)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Two specimens numbered 3779/2.* The species account reads "Кроме голотипа, экз. ПИН N 3779/2 — часть черепа" ("Besides the holotype, spec. PIN no. 3779/2 — part of the skull", translated; tumanova1987a, p. 23). The same paper's list of material gives the partial skull as 3779/1 (p. 7), so the repeated number is an error in the species account.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which aimag is the *Shamosaurus* type locality in?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** Dornogovi (MN-063), from where Khamryn-Us lies. No paper read names the aimag. Governs `location.region` on [*Shamosaurus scutatus*](../../../genera/S/Shamosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- arbour2015a, advance-access version, p. 20: "Khamryn-Us (D Khamareen Us), southeastern Gobi, Mongolia".
- tumanova1987a, p. 7: Tumanova places localities by part of the Gobi, not by aimag: "Баин-Ширэ, Восточная Гоби" ("Bain-Shire, East Gobi") and "Байшин-Цав, Юго-Восточная Гоби" ("Baishin-Tsav, Southeast Gobi", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Ömnögovi (MN-053) from "South Gobi".* Tumanova's distribution line reads "МНР, Южная Гоби" ("Mongolian People's Republic, South Gobi", translated; tumanova1987a, p. 24). Her other Gobi terms on p. 7 are parts of the desert, so "South Gobi" is not the name of the aimag Ömnögovi.

**Open.** A published statement of Khamryn-Us's aimag, which would confirm the derived code.
