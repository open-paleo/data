# *Shuvuuia*

## Which specimen is the *Shuvuuia deserti* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** MGI 100/975, stored as IGM 100/975 (MGI and IGM are aliases of the Mongolian collection code MPC). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IGM 100/975]` on [*Shuvuuia deserti*](../../../genera/S/Shuvuuia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- chiappe1998a, p. 276: "Holotype. MGI 100/975, Mongolian Geological Institute, Ulaanbaatar."; "Referred material. MGI N 100/99, MGI 100/1001."

**Ruled out.**
- *MPD 100/120.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) tabulate the species under this number (molina-pérez2019a, p. 177). Chiappe and colleagues do not give it.
- *IGM 100/977 and 100/1001 as part of the type.* Jones (2026) lists them with IGM 100/975 (jones2026c, p. 504). Chiappe and colleagues refer 100/1001 and name 100/977 only in their locality line.

**Open.** Nothing.
