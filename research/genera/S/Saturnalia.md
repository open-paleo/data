# *Saturnalia*

## Which coordinate and locality name belong to the *Saturnalia* type site?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** The record keeps the coordinate the describing paper prints for the type locality, 29°40'S, 53°45'W, and the site name Wald Sanga. Marsola and colleagues (2018) print a precise coordinate for the Waldsanga site, about 4 km from the rounded pair (our calculation), and state that the site yielded the *Saturnalia* types. Governs `location.coordinates` and `location.locality` on [*Saturnalia tupiniquim*](../../../genera/S/Saturnalia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- langer1999a, p. 515: holotype and paratypes "proviennent de la même localité, à proximité de la ville de Santa Maria … (53°45' W ; 29°40' S)" ("come from the same locality, near the city of Santa Maria", translated).
- langer1999a, p. 512: "a private piece of land no. 194.5, on BR-509 road, on the outskirts of the city of Santa Maria", "presumably the locality known as 'Wald Sanga', where the type material of the cynodont Gomphodontosuchus brasiliensis Huene and the archosaur Hoplitosuchus raui Huene were excavated".
- marsola2018a, p. e1531878-2: "Site known as Waldsanga (Huene, 1942; Langer et al., 2007) or Cerro da Alemoa (Da Rosa, 2004, 2015), at coordinates 29°41′51.86″S, 53°46′26.56″W"; "The same site has also yielded the type specimens of the early sauropodomorph Saturnalia tupiniquim (Langer et al., 1999)".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Huene Location" as part of the site name.* Langer and colleagues name Huene only as the author of two other taxa from the presumed same site (langer1999a, p. 512); no source read calls the site after him.

**Open.** Whether Marsola and colleagues' coordinate should replace the rounded pair. Their statement that the site yielded *Saturnalia* removes the describing paper's "presumably", but their coordinate is printed for the *Nhandumirim* type locality, and whether the two excavations were at one point of the outcrop is not stated.
