# *Serendipaceratops*

## Is *Serendipaceratops* valid?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** Disputed. Agnolin and colleagues (2010) call it a nomen dubium diagnosable only to Genasauria; Rich and colleagues (2014) rebut them point by point, argue for a ceratopsian synapomorphy, and give an emended diagnosis; later work still treats the referral as challenged. Neither status has consensus, so the record is `disputed`, carries the diagnostic features of the emended diagnosis, and stays at Genasauria. This answers the Open line of the entry below. Governs `status: disputed`, `diagnostic_features` and the dispute on [*Serendipaceratops*](../../../genera/S/Serendipaceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rich2014a: the midshaft width ratio is "a unique synapomorphic character state … that occurs in ceratopsians to the exclusion of all other genasaurians"; "we conclude that S. arthurcclarkei can not be a nomen dubium but represents a valid taxon"; the emended diagnosis cites "the extreme degree of mediolateral compression of the midshaft of the ulna (Variable 5/Variable 10, midshaft width ratio = 2.06)".
- agnolín2010a: "in a strict sense *Serendipaceratops* cannot be diagnosed beyond Genasauria, and the taxon should be considered a *nomen dubium*."
- ryan2023a: the ulnae's leptoceratopsid-like affinities "have been challenged (Agnolin et al. 2010)"; bandeira2024a lists the genus under "CERATOPSIA?".

**Ruled out.**
- *Status nomen dubium.* It takes one side of a live disagreement.
- *Ceratopsia as the parent.* Later work has not taken up the 2014 placement.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the dispute itself.

## Where does *Serendipaceratops* sit?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** Genasauria incertae sedis, as a nomen dubium for now. The ulna has been read as neoceratopsian, as ankylosaur-like, and as diagnosable only to Genasauria; all three fall within Genasauria. The ceratopsian reading was defended again after Agnolin and colleagues' (2010) rejection, so the placement is live. Governs `parent: Genasauria` and the dispute on [*Serendipaceratops*](../../../genera/S/Serendipaceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rich2003a: "There are now two ulnae from the Early Cretaceous of Australia that can be tentatively referred to the Neoceratopsia."
- agnolín2010a: "the proportions of the ulna of *Serendipaceratops* are closer to *Minmi* sp. and other ankylosaurians … than to any neoceratopsian"; "in a strict sense *Serendipaceratops* cannot be diagnosed beyond Genasauria, and the taxon should be considered a *nomen dubium*."
- poropat2014a: a 2014 study "lends renewed support to the identification of Serendipaceratops as a genuine Australian ceratopsian".
- ryan2023a: "These ulnae have been reported to have leptoceratopsid-like affinities (Rich and Vickers-Rich 2003), although this has been challenged (Agnolin et al. 2010)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Ornithischia incertae sedis.* The earlier parent; broader than any reading requires.

**Open.** The 2014 defense of the ceratopsian identity has not been read; it may bear on the nomen dubium. Jones (2026) credits an ankylosaur reading to a 2021 paper that could not be matched to a held work (jones2026a).
