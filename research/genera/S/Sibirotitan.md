# *Sibirotitan*

## What stage is the *Sibirotitan astrosacralis* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Barremian–Aptian. The describing paper gives a queried Barremian for the type locality, and a later paper by the same group gives the Aptian for the same site. Governs `stage: [Barremian, Aptian]` on [*Sibirotitan astrosacralis*](../../../genera/S/Sibirotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2018a, p. 4: "Type locality and horizon: Shestakovo 1 vertebrate locality, Kemerovo Province, Russia; Ilek Fm., Lower Cretaceous (Barremian?)"; p. 2, Geographic and geological settings: "The age of the Ilek Fm. at the Shestakovo 1 locality is poorly constrained"; "a Barremian age for the Shestakovo 1 locality is not definitive".
- averianov2024a, p. 2, Fig. 1 caption: "Shestakovo 1, Kemerovo oblast—Kuzbass, Russia; Ilek Formation, Lower Cretaceous (Aptian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian alone.* Jones (2026) gives "Ilek Fm., Russia *Early Cretaceous, Barremian*" (jones2026b, p. 381), which drops the query the describing paper attaches.
- *Aptian alone.* The record's earlier value. The describing paper's own reading is the queried Barremian.
- *Aptian–Albian by correlation with Khoboor.* Averianov and colleagues report an earlier correlation of the locality with the Aptian–Albian Khoboor locality in Mongolia, and reject it because the Shestakovo gobiconodontids differ from the Khoboor species (averianov2018a, p. 2).

**Open.** The locality's age. A date from the Shestakovo 1 beds would settle it.
