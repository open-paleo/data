# *Sinovenator*

## Which site is the *Sinovenator* holotype from?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Lujiatun or Yanzigou, which the record gives together as the describing paper does. The single-site "Lujiatun" came from Yin and colleagues' (2018) locality line, which belongs to a referred specimen, not the holotype. Governs `location.locality: Lujiatun and Yanzigou, Shangyuan` on [*Sinovenator changii*](../../../genera/S/Sinovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2002a, p. 780, Locality and horizon: "Lujiatun and Yanzigou, Shanyuan, western Liaoning, China", for the holotype IVPP V12615 and the referred IVPP V12583 together.
- yin2018a, p. 3, Locality, horizon and age: "Lujiatun, Shangyuan, Beipiao City, western Liaoning, China … This specimen was collected from villagers at Lujiatun. The exact location where this specimen was discovered is unknown", for the referred PMOL-AD00102.

**Ruled out.**
- *Lujiatun alone.* Its only source is another specimen's locality line.

**Open.** Which of the two sites yielded the holotype.
