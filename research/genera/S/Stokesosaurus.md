# *Stokesosaurus*

## What is the *Stokesosaurus clevelandi* holotype's current number?

*2026-08-02*

**Conclusion.** UMNH VP 7473, the Natural History Museum of Utah number for the left ilium Madsen (1974) designated as UUVP 2938. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [UMNH VP 7473]` and the `former_ids` entry on [*Stokesosaurus clevelandi*](../../../genera/S/Stokesosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- madsen1974a, p. 27: "Type.-Left ilium, UUVP 2938".
- brusatte2013b, p. 49, figure caption: "Left ilium of *Stokesosaurus clevelandi*, Madsen 1974 (UMNH VP 7473), Morrison Formation, Utah, USA".

**Ruled out.**
- *UUVP 2938 as the current number.* Jones (2026) gives "UUVP 2938—ilium" (jones2026c, p. 502). This is the number before the University of Utah collection moved to the museum.
- *UUVP 2320.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give it for the species (molina-pérez2019a, p. 265). Madsen calls it "the larger referred specimen" (madsen1974a, p. 29).

**Open.** No source read states the renumbering. That UMNH VP 7473 is the specimen Madsen numbered UUVP 2938 is our inference from Brusatte and Benson's caption, which attributes a left ilium to "Madsen 1974".
