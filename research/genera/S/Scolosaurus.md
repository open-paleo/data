# *Scolosaurus*

## Where is the *Scolosaurus cutleri* holotype quarry, and in which formation?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** The coordinate is Arbour and colleagues' (2013) position for Quarry Q080, converted from their UTM figure (12U 471,365 E, 5,622,322 N gives 50.751968, −111.405922; our conversion). The quarry's identity is doubted in print, and the formation goes with it: the lower Dinosaur Park Formation if Q080 is right, possibly the upper Oldman if it is not. Governs `location.coordinates`, `location.formation: Dinosaur Park` and `part: lower` on *Scolosaurus cutleri* in [*Scolosaurus*](../../../genera/S/Scolosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- arbour2013a, p. 31: "Dinosaur Provincial Park, Quarry Q080, 12U, 5,622,321.978 N, 471,365.051 E; there is uncertainty over whether this is the correct quarry or whether it is from several hundred meters farther north"; "Lower part of the Dinosaur Park Formation (or possibly Oldman Formation if mapped quarry position is wrong)".
- penkalski2013b, accepted manuscript, p. 5: "An exact location including latitude, longitude, and elevation were given in Currie and Russell (2005) but Darren Tanke (pers. comm., 2010) believes this is not the *Scolosaurus* quarry which may actually be a different quarry lying near the base of the Dinosaur Park Formation."
- penkalski2018a, p. 265: "Type locality: Sternberg (1950) quarry № 105, just southeast of Happy Jack's Ferry"; "Horizon: Base of DPF or Upper Oldman Fm."
- nopcsa1928a, p. 52, relaying Levi Sternberg: "about one half mile below «Happy Jack» ferry on the Red Deer River. This would make the location in Dead Lodge Canyon … the skeleton was found about halfway up".

**Ruled out.**
- *50.748589, −111.406532.* This pair was also stored for *Saurornitholestes langstoni* (Quarry 140), and none of the papers read prints it for either specimen.
- *"Quarry 80 of the Deadlodge Canyon locality" as one site name.* No paper read prints the two together; Arbour and colleagues give Q080, the older papers Dead Lodge Canyon.

**Open.** Whether Q080 is the *Scolosaurus* quarry, and whether Sternberg's quarry 105 and Q080 are the same site; no paper read correlates the two numbering schemes. Currie and Russell (2005), whose position Penkalski and Blows report, has not been read.
