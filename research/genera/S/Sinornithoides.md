# *Sinornithoides*

## Is the *Sinornithoides* village Muhuaxiao or Huamuxiao?

*2026-09-05*

**Conclusion.** Huamuxiao. The describing paper, Russell and Dong (1993), prints "Muhuaxiao Village" on its locality page. Russell and Zhao (1996) cite that page for the same beds and write "Huamuxiao", placing the village "4.1 km west of Duguijiahan", the distance the 1993 paper gives. Later papers on the taxon also write Huamuxiao. Governs `location.locality: 0.7 km west of Huamuxiao Village` on [*Sinornithoides youngi*](../../../genera/S/Sinornithoides.yml).

**Evidence.**
- russell1993a, p. 2170, Locality: "Exposures of cross-bedded red sandstone 0.7 km west of Muhuaxiao Village (39°36′N, 108°41′E). The village is in turn 4.1 km west of the town of Duguijiahan, Ordos, Inner Mongolia."
- russell1996a, p. 645: red sandstones "(T. Jerzykiewicz in Russell and Dong 1993*b*, p. 2170) and greyish mudstones of the Ejinhoro Formation exposed near the villages of Huamuxiao and Amulonggui"; "Huamuxiao village is located 4.1 km west of Duguijiahan".
- averianov2007a, p. 87: "*Sinornithoides* from Huamuxiao (China; Early Cretaceous)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Muhuaxiao as a different village.* Russell and Zhao give the same distance from the same town and cite the 1993 locality page for the beds there.

**Open.** Which form is correct. Neither Russell paper gives the name in Chinese characters; the record follows the later form used by the same first author.
