# *Siamosaurus*

## What does the *Siamosaurus* type locality, Phu Wiang, rest on?

*2026-09-04*

**Conclusion.** On Carrano and colleagues (2012), who give the type teeth, TF 2043a–i, as from the Sao Khua Formation of Phu Wiang. Buffetaut and Ingavat (1986), who name the species, have not been read. Governs `location.locality: Phu Wiang` and `location.region: TH-40` on [*Siamosaurus suteethorni*](../../../genera/S/Siamosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carrano2012a, p. 255: "*Siamosaurus* is based on isolated teeth (TF 2043a-i) from the Sao Khua Formation (Barremian–Aptian) of Phu Wiang, Thailand".
- carrano2012a, Appendix 3: "TF 2043a-i", with localities "Phu Wiang, Khon Kaen province; Wat Sakawan and Ban Non Mek, Kalasin province, Thailand".

**Ruled out.**
- *Wat Sakawan or Ban Non Mek, Kalasin Province.* The appendix row lists them alongside Phu Wiang against the type-series number. The main text of the same paper gives Phu Wiang alone for TF 2043a–i, so the Kalasin sites are read as the localities of other teeth in the row (our reading).

**Open.** Which Phu Wiang site the teeth came from. Other Phu Wiang taxa have numbered sites, such as Phu Wiang 9 for *Siamotyrannus*. Buffetaut and Ingavat (1986), whose title names Phu Wiang, would settle it.
