# *Sauroplites*

## Which unit and age is the Sauroplites holotype from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The Tebch beds, which have no formal lithostratigraphic unit; Barremian–early Aptian. Governs the empty `location.formation` and `location.group`, and `stage` on *Sauroplites scutiger*: Barremian and Aptian, not Aptian alone.

**Evidence.**
- eberth1993a, p. 2102: "the Tebch sediments and basalt are not recognized as formal lithostratigraphic units"; the "entire section (46–50 m thick)" is "divisible into at least three lithostratigraphic units"; p. 2103: "incorrectly mapped as Tertiary".
- eberth1993a, p. 2104: palynology "indicates a Barremian or possibly early Aptian age"; the capping basalt gives a "mean age of the two plateaus of 110.59 ± 0.52 Ma", "a minimum age of mid-Aptian for the Tebch sediments".

**Ruled out.**
- *Zhidan Group.* Oyabu and colleagues place *Sauroplites* "from the Zhidan Group (Barremian–Aptian; Xi et al., 2018)" (oyabu2026a, pre-proof, pp. 6–7). The Zhidan Group is an Ordos Basin unit typified in Zhidan County, Shaanxi (zhang2009b, p. 1400), about 540 km from Tebch (our calculation).
- *Tebch Formation.* Godefroit and colleagues (1999) write "the Tebch Formation (Barremian - early Aptian: Eberth et al., 1993)" (godefroit1999a, p. 30). Eberth and colleagues, whom they cite, say the beds are not formal units; no formation of that name has been defined (the *Stratigraphic Lexicon of China* has no entry).
- *Xinminpu Group.* Dong (2002) puts three ankylosaurs, including the Tebch material, in "河西走廊地区早白垩世新民堡群" ("the Early Cretaceous Xinminpu Group of the Hexi Corridor", translated; dong2002a, p. 283). Tebch is in Urad Rear Banner, Inner Mongolia, outside the Hexi Corridor.
- *Aptian alone.* The palynology gives Barremian or possibly early Aptian, and the basalt only a minimum. Weishampel and colleagues (2004) give "Early Cretaceous (not younger than mid-Aptian; Eberth et al. 1993)" under an "Unnamed unit" (weishampel2004b, p. 568).

**Open.** Nothing.
