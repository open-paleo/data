# *Seitaad*

## Is the *Seitaad ruessi* holotype in the Navajo Sandstone or the underlying Kayenta Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The base of the Navajo Sandstone, one meter above Kayenta facies. Governs `location.formation: Navajo Sandstone` and `location.part: lower` on [*Seitaad ruessi*](../../../genera/S/Seitaad.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sertich2010a, p. 2, Systematic Paleontology: "UMNH VP 18040 was preserved in a bed of massive sandstone at the base of the Navajo Sandstone, one meter above an interbedded mudstone and sandstone facies of the Kayenta Formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *Kayenta Formation.* The holotype lies above the Kayenta facies, not within them.
- *"Navajo Ss." as another unit.* Jones (2026) gives "Navajo Ss., United States" (jones2026b, p. 374). It is the same unit, with "Ss." standing for Sandstone.

**Open.** Nothing.
