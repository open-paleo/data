# *Sanpasaurus*

## How is the *Sanpasaurus* type locality written, and where is it from Weiyuan?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** Young's own form, "Changshanling in Potzewan", about 4 km northwest of Weiyuan. Young prints the characters 長山嶺 and 卜子灣 for the two names, and McPhee and colleagues (2016) keep Changshanling. Governs `location.locality: Changshanling, Potzewan` on [*Sanpasaurus yaoi*](../../../genera/S/Sanpasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- young1944a, p. 187: "Locality 1. (Changshanling² in Potzewan³, about 4 km NW of the Weiyuan city)"; the footnote gives "1. 威遠; 2. 長山嶺; 3. 卜子灣".
- mcphee2016a, p. 3, Introduction: "the Maanshan (= Ma'anshan) Member of the Ziliujing Formation close to the town of Changshanling, near Weiyuan City".

**Ruled out.**
- *"The Changling region north of the village of Puziwan … approximately 4 km northeast of Weiyuan."* This is the English translation of Dong and colleagues (1983) (dong1983a, translation by W. Downs, ch. III, History of dinosaur research in the Sichuan Basin). "Puziwan" is a romanization in the translation, which chooses its own spellings elsewhere (a translator's note keeps Wade-Giles "Chungking"), and "Changling" lacks the middle syllable of 長山嶺. The same sentence credits the collection to "Professor Xixin Yue", where Young credits H. H. Yao, after whom the species is named. Against Young's "NW", the translation's "northeast" is one of three departures in one sentence and is not adopted.

**Open.** Whether the bearing in the Chinese original of Dong and colleagues (1983) reads northwest or northeast; the original text has not been read. Whether 卜子灣 is the same place as the modern Puziwan of Weiyuan County, which no paper read here states.
