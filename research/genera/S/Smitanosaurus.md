# *Smitanosaurus*

## Is the Smitanosaurus holotype from the lower Morrison?

*2026-09-06*

**Conclusion.** No `part` is recorded. Whitlock and Wilson Mantilla (2020) relay a lower-Morrison placement for Felch Quarry 1 and, in the same sentence, cite a challenge to the method behind it. Governs the absent `location.part` on [*Smitanosaurus agilis*](../../../genera/S/Smitanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- whitlock2020a, p. 4: "The quarry itself lies below the 'clay change,' suggesting it belongs to the lower part of the Morrison Formation (Evanoff and Carpenter, 1998), although the validity of this clay change as correlative tool with beds on the Colorado Plateau has been called into question (Trujillo, 2006)."

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`.* The placement is Evanoff and Carpenter's, and the paper that reports it doubts the correlation it rests on.

**Open.** Evanoff and Carpenter (1998) and Trujillo (2006), neither read, would show whether the clay change places the quarry.
