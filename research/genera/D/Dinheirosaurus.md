# *Dinheirosaurus*

## Is *Dinheirosaurus lourinhanensis* Kimmeridgian, Tithonian, or both?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Late Kimmeridgian to early Tithonian, as the redescription gives it for the Praia da Amoreira–Porto Novo Member. Governs `stage: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` on [*Dinheirosaurus lourinhanensis*](../../../genera/D/Dinheirosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mannion2011a, p. 523, Locality and horizon: "Amoreira-Porto Novo Member, Lourinhã Formation; late Kimmeridgian–early Tithonian, Late Jurassic".
- bonaparte1999a, p. 15: "Horizon. Upper section of the Camadas de Alcobaca Formation, (= "Formação da Lourinhã"), (Fig. 1), Late Kimmeridgian."

**Ruled out.**
- *Tithonian alone.* The record once held it, and no source read gives it. The only Tithonian dating of the type locality is Mohr's palynology, which Bonaparte and Mateus report as "late Tithonian to early Berriasian on palynological data by Mohr (1989)" and set against Helmdach's late Kimmeridgian, "confirmed by Manupella (1996)" (bonaparte1999a, p. 17).
- *Kimmeridgian alone.* Jones (2026) gives "Late Jurassic, Kimmeridgian" (jones2026b, p. 377), and Upchurch and colleagues give "Kimmeridgian" (upchurch2004a, p. 265, Table 13.1). Both match the erecting paper and omit the early Tithonian that the redescription adds for the member.

**Open.** Nothing.
