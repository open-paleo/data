# *Diplodocus*

## Which Sheep Creek quarry is the *Diplodocus carnegii* holotype from?

*2026-08-19*

**Conclusion.** Quarry D(3). Hatcher (1901) treats CM 84 and its cotype CM 94 as one bone deposit on Sheep Creek, calls it Quarry D in a footnote and quarry C in his plate caption; Tschopp and colleagues (2015) separate the two skeletons, giving D(3) for the holotype and D for the cotype. Governs `location.locality: Sheep Creek, Camp Carnegie quarry, Quarry D(3)` on [*Diplodocus carnegii*](../../../genera/D/Diplodocus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hatcher1901a, p. 3: CM 94 came from "further excavations in the same quarry on Sheep Creek, in Albany County, Wyoming" that had yielded CM 84; the footnote on "this quarry, or bone deposit" reads "Known as Quarry D on field labels and notebooks".
- hatcher1901a, Plate I: "Diagram of quarry C, near Camp Carnegie on Sheep Creek, in Albany County, Wyoming. The bones lying within the double full line belong to *Diplodocus* skeleton No. 84."
- tschopp2015a, p. 6, Table 2: *Diplodocus carnegii*, CM 84, "Sheep Creek Quarry D(3), Albany County, WY, USA"; p. 260: "Sheep Creek Quarries D (CM 94) and D(3) (CM 84), Albany County, Wyoming".

**Ruled out.**
- *Quarry C.* It appears only in Hatcher's plate caption; his text footnote gives Quarry D from the field labels and notebooks, and Tschopp and colleagues use D and D(3).
- *Quarry D for the holotype.* Tschopp and colleagues give D for the cotype CM 94 and D(3) for CM 84.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Diplodocus carnegii* holotype CM 84 or CM 94?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** CM 84. Hatcher (1901) founded the species on both Carnegie skeletons, Nos. 84 and 94, and Tschopp and colleagues (2015) give CM 84 as the holotype and CM 94 as the cotype. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CM 84]` on [*Diplodocus carnegii*](../../../genera/D/Diplodocus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hatcher1901a, p. 57: "I consider our skeletons (Nos. 84 and 94) as belonging to a distinct species for which I propose the name of *Diplodocus carnegii*".
- tschopp2015a, p. 6, Table 2: "Diplodocus carnegii Hatcher, 1901 | CM 84 | … | CM 94 (cotype)"; p. 8: "The holotype of *D. carnegii* is one of a few specimens of *Diplodocus* that includes cervical vertebrae."

**Ruled out.**
- *CM 94.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) list "*Diplodocus carnegii* (estimated) | CM 94" in a sauropod speed table (molina-pérez2020a, Speed of quadrupedal sauropodomorphs). It is the cotype, the second skeleton Hatcher described; the same book's tail-length table gives CM 84 for the species.

**Open.** Nothing.
