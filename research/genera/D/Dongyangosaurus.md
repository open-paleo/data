# *Dongyangosaurus*

## Is the *Dongyangosaurus* holotype from the Fangyan or the Jinhua Formation?

*2026-08-20*

**Conclusion.** The Jinhua. Lü and colleagues (2008) name the Fangyan Formation but place the site near the eastern margin of the Jinqu Basin, and Yu and colleagues (2010) list *Dongyangosaurus* in the Jinhua Formation of that basin, where their table gives no Fangyan. Mannion and colleagues (2019) state the correction, citing Yu and colleagues. Governs `location.formation: Jinhua` on [*Dongyangosaurus sinensis*](../../../genera/D/Dongyangosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2008b, p. 225: the sauropod "was found in the Cretaceous Fangyan Formation, Dongyang of Zhejiang Province near the eastern margin of the Jinqu Basin"; p. 226, Type locality and horizon: "Baidian Village, Dongyang City, Zhejiang Province (Fig. 1); Fangyan Formation".
- yu2010a, p. 97, Table 2: *Dongyangosaurus sinensis* under the Jinhua Formation (金华组) in the Jinqu Basin (金衢盆地) column; the Fangyan Formation (方岩组) appears only in the Yongkang and Lishui basin columns; the same page gives *Dictyoolithus hongpoensis* from "the Fangyan Formation of the Yongkang Group in the Lishui Basin" (translated).
- mannion2019b, p. 3: "These authors stated that it came from the early Late Cretaceous Fangyan Formation, but subsequent studies have shown that this unit is actually the Jinhua Formation ([63,67] and references therein)."

**Ruled out.**
- *The Fangyan Formation.* It is a unit of the Yongkang Group in other basins, still in use under its own name, so it is neither the holotype's unit nor an older name for the Jinhua.

**Open.** Nothing.
