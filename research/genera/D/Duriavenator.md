# *Duriavenator*

## Does "Upper Inferior Oolite" give *Duriavenator* a part?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** No. "Upper Inferior Oolite" is a chronostratigraphic division of the Wessex Inferior Oolite, not the upper part of the rock unit, as the [*Magnosaurus*](../M/Magnosaurus.md) entry rules for "Middle Inferior Oolite" from the same sequence. The record keeps the group, with the ammonite zone and subzone in the location note. Governs the empty `location.part` on [*Duriavenator hesperis*](../../../genera/D/Duriavenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- benson2008a, Locality and horizon: "Greenhill, Sherborne, Dorset. The material was collected from the Upper Inferior Oolite, *Parkinsonia parkinsoni* ammonite Zone, *Garantiana garantiana* Subzone (Waldman 1974), late Bajocian".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: upper`.* The positional word belongs to a chronostratigraphic term.

**Open.** Nothing.
