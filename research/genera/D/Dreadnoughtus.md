# *Dreadnoughtus*

## Is the *Dreadnoughtus* holotype from Cerro Fortaleza or from the Cerro Los Hornos site?

*2026-08-20*

**Conclusion.** Cerro Fortaleza, on the east bank of the Río La Leona, as the describing paper gives it. Governs `location.locality: Cerro Fortaleza, east bank of the Río La Leona` and `location.formation: Cerro Fortaleza` on [*Dreadnoughtus schrani*](../../../genera/D/Dreadnoughtus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lacovara2014a, p. 3, Systematic palaeontology: "Type locality. Cerro Fortaleza, east bank of the Río La Leona, Santa Cruz Province, south-western Patagonia, Argentina (coordinates on file at MPM). Horizon. Cerro Fortaleza Formation, approximately 350 m below the top of the formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *Cerro de Los Hornos.* Novas and colleagues (2019) write that "The Cerro Fortaleza beds, at Cerro de Los Hornos fossil site, shed different dinosaur taxa (i.e., *Talenkauen, Orkoraptor, Puertasaurus, Dreadnoughtus*...)" (novas2019a, p. 274), citing Lacovara and colleagues (2014) among others. Cerro Los Hornos is the *Talenkauen* site "on the southern coast of Lago Viedma" (egerton2013a, p. 269), and Lacovara and colleagues name Cerro Fortaleza on the Río La Leona. The sentence gathers the formation's fauna under one site.

**Open.** Nothing.
