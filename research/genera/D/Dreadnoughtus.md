# *Dreadnoughtus*

## Is the *Dreadnoughtus* holotype from Cerro Fortaleza or from the Cerro Los Hornos site?

*2026-08-20*

**Conclusion.** Cerro Fortaleza, on the east bank of the Río La Leona, as the describing paper gives it. Governs `location.locality: Cerro Fortaleza, east bank of the Río La Leona` and `location.formation: Cerro Fortaleza` on [*Dreadnoughtus schrani*](../../../genera/D/Dreadnoughtus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lacovara2014a, p. 3, Systematic palaeontology: "Type locality. Cerro Fortaleza, east bank of the Río La Leona, Santa Cruz Province, south-western Patagonia, Argentina (coordinates on file at MPM). Horizon. Cerro Fortaleza Formation, approximately 350 m below the top of the formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *Cerro de Los Hornos.* Novas and colleagues (2019) write that "The Cerro Fortaleza beds, at Cerro de Los Hornos fossil site, shed different dinosaur taxa (i.e., *Talenkauen, Orkoraptor, Puertasaurus, Dreadnoughtus*...)" (novas2019a, p. 274), citing Lacovara and colleagues (2014) among others. Cerro Los Hornos is the *Talenkauen* site "on the southern coast of Lago Viedma" (egerton2013a, p. 269), and Lacovara and colleagues name Cerro Fortaleza on the Río La Leona. The sentence gathers the formation's fauna under one site.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Dreadnoughtus* holotype from the Mata Amarilla Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** No. The Cerro Fortaleza Formation, as the describing paper gives it. The Mata Amarilla is the Cenomanian unit, and Sickmann and colleagues (2018) separate the Campanian Cerro Fortaleza from it. Governs `location.formation: Cerro Fortaleza` on [*Dreadnoughtus schrani*](../../../genera/D/Dreadnoughtus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lacovara2014a, p. 3, Systematic palaeontology: "Horizon. Cerro Fortaleza Formation".
- sickmann2018a, Nomenclature: their "Cerro Fortaleza Formation" "includes rocks that have sometimes been named "Mata Amarilla Formation" … and have been assigned a Cenomanian age".
- novas2019a, p. 274: "Mata Amarilla, in proximities of the Sheuen River, afforded carcharodontosaurid remains (Novas et al., 1999) which are congruent with a Cenomanian age", against "Cerro Fortaleza Formation is Campanian (around 76.2Ma, based on studies by Sickman et al., 2018)"; Novas and colleagues list *Dreadnoughtus* among the Cerro Fortaleza taxa.

**Ruled out.**
- *Mata Amarilla Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Mata Amarilla Fm., Argentina" (jones2026b, p. 383). Varela and colleagues dated beds near Tres Lagos as Cenomanian and called them Mata Amarilla (varela2012a, abstract). Sickmann and colleagues' detrital-zircon ages put the terrestrial beds they map as Cerro Fortaleza in the Campanian or younger, and Novas and colleagues (2019) keep the Mata Amarilla name for the Cenomanian beds near the Sheuen River. None of the papers on this specimen uses the Mata Amarilla name.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Dreadnoughtus* holotype Cenomanian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** No. Its horizon in the Cerro Fortaleza Formation is no older than Campanian. Governs the absence of a Cenomanian in `stage` on [*Dreadnoughtus schrani*](../../../genera/D/Dreadnoughtus.yml), which holds `[Campanian]`.

**Evidence.**
- lacovara2014a, p. 3, Systematic palaeontology: "Horizon. Cerro Fortaleza Formation, approximately 350 m below the top of the formation. Age. Late Cretaceous, Campanian–Maastrichtian".
- sickmann2018a, New Detrital Zircon MDAs: "Samples throughout the terrestrial Cerro Fortaleza Formation in this area show maximum depositional ages throughout the Campanian with the stratigraphically highest sample EP-13 providing a YC1σ MDA of 76.2 ± 1.6 Ma".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cenomanian.* Jones (2026) gives "Mata Amarilla Fm., Argentina, Late Cretaceous, Cenomanian" (jones2026b, p. 383). The Cenomanian is the Mata Amarilla's, dated by a tuff at "96.23±0.71 Ma, which corresponds to the middle Cenomanian" (varela2012a, abstract), and Sickmann and colleagues put the Cerro Fortaleza's maxima throughout the Campanian.

**Open.** The Maastrichtian. The describing paper gives Campanian–Maastrichtian, and Sickmann and colleagues' ages are maxima, which exclude older stages but not younger ones. No source read places the holotype's level within the Campanian.
