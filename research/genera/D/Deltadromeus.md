# *Deltadromeus*

## Where in the Kem Kem was the *Deltadromeus* holotype found, and does a printed coordinate give its position?

*2026-08-19*

**Conclusion.** At Aferdou N'Chaft, in the upper part of the Gara Sbaa Formation. No source prints a position for the 1995 find spot, so no coordinate is recorded. Governs `location.locality: Aferdou N'Chaft`, `location.formation: Gara Sbaa`, `location.part: upper` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Deltadromeus agilis*](../../../genera/D/Deltadromeus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ibrahim2020a, p. 54: "Also in the Gara Sbaa Formation at the locality Aferdou N'Chaft, a partial skeleton of *Deltadromeus agilis* was discovered weathering from a coarse-grained sandstone".
- ibrahim2020a, p. 10: the 1995 expedition found "a partial postcranial skeleton of the theropod *Deltadromeus agilis* in the upper part of the lower unit".
- sereno1996a, p. 987, Fig. 1C: "Section of the Kem Kem beds at Gara es Sbaa", with silhouettes marking "the stratigraphic level of the skull of *Carcharodontosaurus* and skeleton of *Deltadromeus*".
- ibrahim2020a, p. 11, Table 2: "Six fossil vertebrate localities with geographic coordinates prospected during field work in 2008", among them "Aferdou N'Chaft 30°53'51.23"N 3°52'13.42"E" and "Gara Sbaa 30°30'40.64"N 4°50'42.87"E".

**Ruled out.**
- *Er Remlia.* Ibrahim and colleagues gloss Sereno and colleagues' "Er Remlia" as the village nearest Iferda N'Ahouar (ibrahim2020a, p. 15, Table 4), the site of the *Carcharodontosaurus* cranium, not of this skeleton.
- *Gara es Sbaa as the find spot.* In Sereno and colleagues (1996) it names the measured section onto which the skeleton's level is plotted, not the place it was found.
- *Table 2's Aferdou N'Chaft point.* It is a 2008 fieldwork position for a locality that exposes both formations of the group: the same paper reports a turtle cranium "discovered in 2008 by a local in the Douira Formation at Aferdou N'Chaft east of Taouz" (ibrahim2020a, p. 90) and a neosuchian jugal "found in situ in the Douira Formation at the locality Aferdou N'Chaft" (p. 107). The point is not tied to the 1995 find spot. Its longitude is also printed east, though the site lies west of Greenwich.
- *The pair formerly stored, [30.505667, −4.836939].* It lies about a kilometer from Table 2's Gara Sbaa row read as west, the *Rebbachisaurus garasbae* locality, and the same pair was stored for that taxon.

**Open.** Where the 1995 skeleton lay within Aferdou N'Chaft. The field records of the University of Chicago expedition would settle it.

## Is "Kem Kem" the *Deltadromeus agilis* holotype's formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** No. Ibrahim and colleagues (2020) define the Kem Kem as a group made of the Gara Sbaa and Douira formations, and place the *Deltadromeus* skeleton in the upper third of the Gara Sbaa. Governs `location.formation: Gara Sbaa` on [*Deltadromeus agilis*](../../../genera/D/Deltadromeus.yml), alongside the 2026-08-19 entry.

**Evidence.**
- ibrahim2020a, p. 1, Abstract: "These strata are recognized here as the Kem Kem Group, which is composed of the lower Gara Sbaa and upper Douira formations."
- ibrahim2020a, Theropoda, *Deltadromeus*: the skeleton "was discovered weathering from a coarsegrained sandstone in the upper third of the Gara Sbaa Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Kem Kem Beds as the formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Kem Kem Beds, Morocco" (jones2026c, p. 494); Holtz and colleagues (2004) give "Upper Kem Kem Beds (Ksar-es-Souk), Morocco" (holtz2004a, p. 76, Table 4.1), as does the distribution chapter (weishampel2004b, p. 604). All three predate Ibrahim and colleagues' formal scheme, which makes Kem Kem the name of the group that contains the Gara Sbaa, not a rival formation.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Deltadromeus agilis* holotype SGM-Din 2 or UCRC PV11?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** SGM-Din 2, the number the describing paper gives for the holotype in the collections of the Moroccan Ministère de l'Energie et des Mines. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [SGM-Din 2]` on [*Deltadromeus agilis*](../../../genera/D/Deltadromeus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno1996a, note 33: "The holotypic skeleton (SGM-Din 2) is in the collections of the Ministère de l'Energie et des Mines, Rabat, Morocco."
- sereno1996a, Table 1 and Fig. 3: "*D. agilis* (SGM-Din 2)".
- ibrahim2020a, Theropoda, *Deltadromeus*: "The partial and only known postcranial skeleton of *Deltadromeus agilis* (Sereno et al. 1996, UCRC PV11)"; Fig. 115: "Mounted skeletal reconstruction using casts of the holotype UCRC PV11".

**Ruled out.**
- *UCRC PV11 in place of SGM-Din 2.* Jones (2026) prints "UCRC PV11—partial skeleton (Sereno et al., 1996)" as the holotype (jones2026c, p. 494). The paper it cites gives SGM-Din 2. Ibrahim and colleagues (2020), with Sereno among the authors, use UCRC PV11 for the holotype but do not say the specimen was renumbered or moved, so it does not replace the designating paper's number.

**Open.** Whether UCRC PV11 is a later University of Chicago number for the same skeleton. A collection record, or a paper stating the renumbering, would settle it, and the record would then carry both numbers.
