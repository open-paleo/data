# *Dyoplosaurus*

## Where was the *Dyoplosaurus* holotype found, and is the site Happy Jack Ferry?

*2026-08-21*

**Conclusion.** Sternberg's (1950) quarry 93, which the Royal Tyrrell Museum numbers Q002, at the base of the Dinosaur Park Formation. Happy Jack Ferry is the landmark for two *Scolosaurus* quarries, not for this one. The coordinate is the UTM position Arbour and colleagues (2009) print for the quarry, converted. Governs `location.locality: Royal Tyrrell Museum Quarry Q002`, `location.part: lower` and `location.coordinates: [50.752628, -111.470833]` on [*Dyoplosaurus acutosquameus*](../../../genera/D/Dyoplosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- arbour2009a, p. 1119: "Royal Tyrrell Museum Quarry no. Q002, Sternberg Map no. 93, UTM East 466,786.58 West 562,2422.48 (NAD 83), Elevation 669.745 m ASL"; "Base of the Dinosaur Park Formation".
- penkalski2018a, p. 265: "Type locality: Sternberg (1950) quarry № 93, south side of Red Deer River, about 8 miles southeast of Steveville, Alberta"; "Horizon: Lower DPF, around 8 m above the DPF/OF contact."

**Ruled out.**
- *Happy Jack Ferry.* Penkalski (2018) gives quarry 105, the *Scolosaurus cutleri* type locality, as "just southeast of Happy Jack's Ferry" (p. 265), and quarry 112, that of *Scolosaurus thronus*, as "2.4 mi ENE of Happy Jack's Ferry" (p. 266); Nopcsa (1928) has the *S. cutleri* type found "about one half mile below «Happy Jack» ferry" (nopcsa1928a, p. 54). Neither paper on ROM 784 mentions the ferry.
- *The second UTM figure as a westing.* Arbour and colleagues label it "West" and misplace a comma. Read as a northing of 5,622,422.48 m with the easting 466,786.58 m in UTM zone 12, which the paper does not print but in which Dinosaur Provincial Park lies, it gives 50.752628, -111.470833 (our conversion).

**Open.** Nothing.
