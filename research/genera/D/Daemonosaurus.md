# *Daemonosaurus*

## Which member of the Chinle Formation is the *Daemonosaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** None is recorded. The describing paper and the later osteology both place the Coelophysis Quarry in the informal "siltstone member" of the Chinle. Nesbitt and Sues (2020) report that matching that unit to a named member is contested, between the Petrified Forest Member and the Rock Point. Governs the empty `location.member` on [*Daemonosaurus chauliodus*](../../../genera/D/Daemonosaurus.yml); the same question for *Coelophysis* is in [Rock Point](../../stratigraphy/r/rock-point.md).

**Evidence.**
- sues2011a, p. 3460, Locality and horizon: "Coelophysis Quarry [20], Ghost Ranch, 20 km northwest of Abiquiú, Rio Arriba County, New Mexico, USA … 'Siltstone member' of the Chinle Formation".
- nesbitt2020a, p. 152: "The correlation of this informal unit with other units of the Chinle Formation and Dockum Group remains contentious. Some authors (e.g. Schwartz & Gillette, 1994) considered the 'siltstone member' equivalent to the Petrified Forest Member … whereas Lucas & Hunt (1993) and Rinehart et al. (2009) proposed correlation with the Rock Point Member"; p. 153, Locality and horizon: "'Siltstone member' of the Chinle Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Rock Point Member.* Novas and colleagues (2021) say *Daemonosaurus* "was discovered from the Rhaetian Rock Point Member of the Chinle Formation" (novas2021a, pre-proof, p. 47), citing Sues and colleagues (2011), who give only the "siltstone member". It is one side of the correlation that Nesbitt and Sues call contentious, not a horizon reported for this specimen.
- *Petrified Forest Member.* The other side of the same correlation.

**Open.** The correlation itself. Schwartz and Gillette (1994) on the quarry's geology have not been read.
