# *Diplotomodon*

## Which units and stages do the later readings give *Diplotomodon*?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** Two either-or readings, both without a stated basis: Gallagher (1993) gives the Navesink or the Hornerstown, Molnar (1990) the Navesink or the Mount Laurel. No formation is recorded, and the stage takes every alternative they allow: Maastrichtian for the Navesink and Hornerstown, as Gallagher dates them, and the Mount Laurel's late Campanian or early Maastrichtian. Governs the location note and `stage: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on [*Diplotomodon horrificus*](../../../genera/D/Diplotomodon.yml). Adds to the 2026-08-19 entry's Ruled-out line on Prieto-Márquez and colleagues: Gallagher writes "Navesink (= New Egypt) Fm.", so their New Egypt at Mullica Hill is the Navesink under another name.

**Evidence.**
- gallagher1993a: "*Diplotomodon horrificus* Leidy, 1865 … Type: ANSP 9680, large partial tooth from Mullica Hill, Gloucester Co., N.J., either from Navesink or Hornerstown Fm., Maastrichtian."
- molnar1990b, p. 156: "a single tooth, now lost, from the "Green-sand formations" (probably either the Navesink or Mount Laurel Formation) of Gloucester County".
- gallagher1993a, Mount Laurel Formation: "AGE—Late Campanian (Owens et al., 1970; Olsson, 1989); early Maastrichtian (Olsson, 1975; Petters, 1976; Jordan and Smith, 1983)".
- gallagher1993a: "Navesink (= New Egypt) Fm." for specimens from the region.

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian alone.* It drops the Mount Laurel alternative of Molnar's reading.

**Open.** Which unit yielded the tooth; neither reading gives its basis.

## Which formation is the *Diplotomodon* tooth from?

*2026-08-19*

**Conclusion.** No formation is recorded. Leidy (1865) found the tooth among shark teeth from Mullica Hill in a private collection and places it no more finely than the New Jersey greensands. Governs the absence of `location.formation` and `location.locality: Mullica Hill` on [*Diplotomodon horrificus*](../../../genera/D/Diplotomodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leidy1865a, p. 102: "Among some teeth of Sharks, from Mullica Hill, Gloucester County, New Jersey, in the collection of Dr. William B. Atkinson, I observed a tooth different from any I had previously seen, from the Green-sand formations."
- prieto-márquez2006a, p. 91: "YPM 1593: Mullica Hill, Gloucester County, New Jersey; New Egypt Formation, Upper Cretaceous".
- jones2026c, p. 484: *Diplotomodon horrificus*, "Occurrence: Navesink Fm. Age: 70 MYA (Late Cretaceous)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Navesink Formation.* Jones (2026) gives it with no source, and a compilation does not set a value alone. Prieto-Márquez and colleagues (2006) give the New Egypt Formation at Mullica Hill for YPM 1593, a hadrosaur specimen, and nothing ties the tooth to that bed.

**Open.** Which greensand unit Atkinson's shark-tooth lot came from. A collection record for the Atkinson material, or a source on which units the Mullica Hill pits worked, would settle it.
