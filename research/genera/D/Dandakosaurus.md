# *Dandakosaurus*

## How far west of Yamanpalli is the *Dandakosaurus* type locality?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** Five kilometers, as Yadagiri (1982) gives it in the describing paper. Chure (2000) gives "5 miles". That his figure restates Yadagiri's line in another unit, rather than reporting a second measurement, is our reading: Chure gives the same district and horizon and no source for the distance. Governs `location.locality: 5 km west of Yamanpalli` on [*Dandakosaurus indicus*](../../../genera/D/Dandakosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yadagiri1982a, p. 3: "Locality: 5 kms west of Yamanpalli, Adilabad district, Andhra Pradesh, India".
- chure2000a, p. 327: "LOCALITY: 5 miles west of Yamanpalli, Adilabad District, Andhra Prasedh, India"; on the same page the type material is "apparently deposited in the collections of the Geological Survey of India", which is "not explicitly stated".

**Ruled out.**
- *5 miles (about 8 km) west of Yamanpalli.* Chure's entry repeats Yadagiri's district and horizon, changes only the unit of distance, and cites no field record; the describing paper's figure is the primary one.

**Open.** Nothing.
