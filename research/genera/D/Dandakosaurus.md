# *Dandakosaurus*

## Is the placement of *Dandakosaurus* in dispute?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute block is removed and the parent stays Averostra. Its status as a nomen dubium is not contested, and every reading of its affinity falls within Averostra: a ceratosaur in Olshevsky's (1991) list, megalosaur- or carnosaur-like in Yadagiri (1982) and Chure (2000), and averostran in Ezcurra and colleagues (2020), the most recent. The ceratosaur-versus-tetanuran framing of the earlier block rested on a 1991 list without reasoning. Governs `parent: Averostra` and the absence of a dispute on [*Dandakosaurus*](../../../genera/D/Dandakosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- chure2000a: "The type of *Dandakosaurus indicus,* the proximal end of the pubis, is not diagnostic. The taxon is here considered a *nomen dubium* and Theropoda incertae sedis"; "The dorsal vertebra is opisthocoelous, which strongly suggests camosaurian affinities."
- yadagiri1982a: "The Dandakosaurus has certain similarities with Antrodemus … the family is treated as incerte sedis for the present."
- ezcurra2020a: "the presence of opisthocoelous dorsal vertebrae … suggests that it is a more derived, averostran theropod".

**Ruled out.**
- *Theropoda incertae sedis.* Chure's own placement; it leaves the referred vertebrae aside, whereas every reading that uses them falls within Averostra.

**Open.** Whether the referred vertebrae belong with the type pubis; Chure doubts it.

## How far west of Yamanpalli is the *Dandakosaurus* type locality?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** Five kilometers, as Yadagiri (1982) gives it in the describing paper. Chure (2000) gives "5 miles". That his figure restates Yadagiri's line in another unit, rather than reporting a second measurement, is our reading: Chure gives the same district and horizon and no source for the distance. Governs `location.locality: 5 km west of Yamanpalli` on [*Dandakosaurus indicus*](../../../genera/D/Dandakosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yadagiri1982a, p. 3: "Locality: 5 kms west of Yamanpalli, Adilabad district, Andhra Pradesh, India".
- chure2000a, p. 327: "LOCALITY: 5 miles west of Yamanpalli, Adilabad District, Andhra Prasedh, India"; on the same page the type material is "apparently deposited in the collections of the Geological Survey of India", which is "not explicitly stated".

**Ruled out.**
- *5 miles (about 8 km) west of Yamanpalli.* Chure's entry repeats Yadagiri's district and horizon, changes only the unit of distance, and cites no field record; the describing paper's figure is the primary one.

**Open.** Nothing.
