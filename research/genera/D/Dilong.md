# *Dilong*

## Is the *Dilong* holotype from a Lujiatun Member, or from Zhangjiagou?

*2026-09-10*

**Conclusion.** Neither. Xu and colleagues (2004) give the holotype's locality as Lujiatun, Beipiao, in the lower Yixian Formation, with no rank word for Lujiatun. Wang and colleagues (2016), who raise the Lujiatun to a member, do not mention *Dilong*, so a member taken from them rests only on the place name matching the unit name. Zhangjiagou is the site of a referred specimen. Governs `location.locality: Lujiatun, Beipiao` and the absence of `location.member` on [*Dilong paradoxus*](../../../genera/D/Dilong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2004d, p. 680, Horizon and locality: "Lujiatun, Beipiao, western Liaoning; older than 128 and younger than 139 million years fine sand beds of the lower part of the Yixian Formation".
- xu2004d, p. 682: "A fragmentary specimen (IVPP V11579), which was collected from approximately 125-million-year-old grey shale of the Yixian Formation of Zhangjiagou locality, Beipiao, western Liaoning, can be referred to Dilong".

**Ruled out.**
- *`member: Lujiatun`.* Its only basis is Wang and colleagues' (2016) member rank, and their paper does not name *Dilong* or its holotype.
- *Zhangjiagou.* It is the locality of IVPP V11579, the referred specimen with filamentous integument, in grey shale, not of the holotype IVPP V14243 from fine sand beds.

**Open.** Whether the fine sand beds at Lujiatun are the Lujiatun Beds. A paper placing IVPP V14243 in a named Yixian unit would settle the member.
