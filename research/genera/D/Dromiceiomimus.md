# *Dromiceiomimus*

## Is the *Dromiceiomimus brevitertius* holotype ROM 797?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The number comes from Russell (1972), who erected *Dromiceiomimus*. Parks (1926) founded the species on the University of Toronto specimen of 1924 and describes it by its elements. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ROM 797]` on [*Dromiceiomimus brevitertius*](../../../genera/D/Dromiceiomimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- russell1972a, *Dromiceiomimus brevitertius*, Type: "Type ROM 797, sacrum and adjacent caudal vertebrae, several distal caudal vertebrae, pelvis, both hind limbs".
- parks1926a, Introduction: "The specimen on which the new species is founded consists of the sacrum, pelvic girdle, two complete hind limbs, four proximal caudal vertebrae attached, seven scattered distal caudal vertebrae, and two imperfect ribs."

**Ruled out.**
- *CMN 12228 or CMN 12068.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) list both in their theropod speed table (molina-pérez2019a, Biology: Biomechanics: Locomotion). Russell (1972) lists them, as NMC 12228 and NMC 12068, among the referred specimens after the type.

**Open.** Nothing.
