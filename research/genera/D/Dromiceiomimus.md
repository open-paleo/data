# *Dromiceiomimus*

## How is the type species's epithet spelled, and who is its authority?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** *brevitertius* (Parks, 1926). Parks named *Struthiomimus brevetertius*; Russell (1972), erecting *Dromiceiomimus* for it, spelled the epithet *brevitertius* and attributed it to Parks (1926), and later work follows that spelling with the same attribution. Under ICZN Article 33.3.1 an incorrect subsequent spelling in prevailing usage and attributed to the original publication is deemed the correct original spelling; if Russell's change counts as a demonstrably intentional emendation instead (Article 33.2.1), Article 33.2.3.1 preserves it on the same two conditions. Either way the species keeps Parks (1926) as its authority and Russell (1972) erected the genus. Governs `species[].name: Dromiceiomimus brevitertius`, the species `erected_in: parks1926a` and the genus-level `erected_in: russell1972a` on [*Dromiceiomimus*](../../../genera/D/Dromiceiomimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- parks1926a, p. 65, title: "Struthiomimus brevetertius — A new species of dinosaur from the Edmonton Formation of Alberta".
- russell1972a, p. 388: "given the binomial name of Dromiceiomimus brevitertius (Parks 1926) and regarded as the type species of the genus". The original spelling appears in the paper only in the title of Parks's paper in the reference list (p. 402).
- kobayashi2003b, p. 240: "Dromiceiomimus brevitertius Parks, 1926".
- serrano-brañas2016a, p. 60: "Dromiceiomimus brevitertius (Parks, 1926)".

**Ruled out.**
- *brevetertius as the valid spelling.* It is the original spelling, but Russell's spelling is the one in use and is attributed to Parks (1926), which is the case Articles 33.3.1 and 33.2.3.1 preserve.
- *Russell (1972) as the species authority.* The record gave it as the species' `erected_in`. Russell erected the genus; the epithet and its type are Parks's, so the species authority is Parks (1926), and Russell's paper becomes the genus authority.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Dromiceiomimus brevitertius* holotype ROM 797?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The number comes from Russell (1972), who erected *Dromiceiomimus*. Parks (1926) founded the species on the University of Toronto specimen of 1924 and describes it by its elements. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ROM 797]` on [*Dromiceiomimus brevitertius*](../../../genera/D/Dromiceiomimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- russell1972a, *Dromiceiomimus brevitertius*, Type: "Type ROM 797, sacrum and adjacent caudal vertebrae, several distal caudal vertebrae, pelvis, both hind limbs".
- parks1926a, Introduction: "The specimen on which the new species is founded consists of the sacrum, pelvic girdle, two complete hind limbs, four proximal caudal vertebrae attached, seven scattered distal caudal vertebrae, and two imperfect ribs."

**Ruled out.**
- *CMN 12228 or CMN 12068.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) list both in their theropod speed table (molina-pérez2019a, Biology: Biomechanics: Locomotion). Russell (1972) lists them, as NMC 12228 and NMC 12068, among the referred specimens after the type.

**Open.** Nothing.
