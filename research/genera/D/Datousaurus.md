# *Datousaurus*

## Which IVPP numbers belong to the *Datousaurus* type?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** IVPP V7262, the skeleton, and V7263, the skull fragments. Dong and Tang (1984) describe a skull-less skeleton with field number D区 003 as the basis of the new genus, then list V7262 and V7263 as the specimen numbers. That D区 003 is V7262 is our reading; the paper does not equate the two numbers. V7261, from the same quarry, is a *Shunosaurus* skeleton in the same paper. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IVPP V7262, IVPP V7263]` on [*Datousaurus bashanensis*](../../../genera/D/Datousaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1984b, p. 70: a skeleton lacking the skull, "field number D区 003", is named *Datousaurus bashanensis*; "Fossil numbers IVPP V7262 (skeleton), V7263 (skull fragments)" (translated).
- dong1984b, p. 69, under *Shunosaurus lii*: "In 1980, some skull fragments and a relatively complete skeleton were collected from the Dashanpu fossil quarry, fossil number V7261" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP V7261.* Dong and Tang give it as new *Shunosaurus lii* material, used to supplement the description of that species.

**Open.** The holotype designation. The paper names a genus on D区 003 and lists V7262 and V7263 without the word holotype; the monograph it promises would settle which number is the type.

## References
