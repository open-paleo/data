# *Dzharacursor*

## Which site and catalogue number belong to the *Dzharacursor* holotype?

*2026-08-21*

**Conclusion.** The record follows the holotype entry of Nessov (1995): CCMGE 479/12457, site CBI-14, in the middle part of the Bissekty Formation. The same work's plate caption gives the figured holotype femur as "site CBI-4, holotype, No. 726", and its account of the Dzharakuduk section lists the femur at the CBI-4 level, about 52 m above the base of the formation, not at the CBI-14 level, about 57 m. Averianov and Sues (2024) repeat the split, with 479/12457 in their text and 726/12457 in their figure caption. Governs `location.locality: CBI-14` and `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CCMGE 479/12457]` on [*Dzharacursor bissektensis*](../../../genera/D/Dzharacursor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nessov1995a, p. 40 (translated): the species account, headed "Pl. III, fig. 7.", gives "Holotype - CCMGE No. 479/12457, right femur; Uzbekistan, Dzharakuduk; Upper Cretaceous, Coniacian, middle part of the Bissekty Formation, site CBI-14".
- nessov1995a, Plate III caption (translated): "7 - femur of ornithomimid *Archaeornithomimus* (?) *bissektensis* sp. n., site CBI-4, holotype, No. 726"; the same caption gives "5 proximal part of femur of a theropod, site CDZH-16, No. 479" and "6 - caudal vertebra ... site CBI-14, No. 725".
- nessov1995a, p. 95 (translated): "Level approximately 52 m above the base of the formation (site CBI-4)", at which "the femur of *Archaeornithomimus*(?) *bissektensis* sp. n." was found; "Level approximately 57 m above the base of the formation (site CBI-14, lower part of the site CBI-17)".
- averianov2024b, p. 2: "Holotype—CCMGE 479/12457, right femur of a juvenile individual"; p. 3, Fig. 1 caption: "Holotype of *Archaeornithomimus* (?) *bissektensis* Nesov, 1995, CCMGE 726/12457".

**Ruled out.** Nothing. Neither reading has a source against it.

**Open.** Which number and site are the holotype's. Two of Nessov's three statements give CBI-4, and both values in the holotype entry occur on the neighboring figures of the same plate caption (No. 479 on figure 5, CBI-14 on figure 6), which suggests the entry took them from there; that is our inference. The CCMGE register or the specimen's label would settle it.
