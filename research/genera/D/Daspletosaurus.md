# *Daspletosaurus*

## Which quarry and formation is the *Daspletosaurus torosus* holotype from?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** Sternberg's quarry 88 in the Oldman Formation near Steveville. Russell's (1970) type line prints "Sternberg 1905" for the quarry number. The 1905 is a misprint for Sternberg's 1950 Steveville locality map, which is the source Russell cites for every other quarry number in the paper. Carr and colleagues (2017) say *D. torosus* is restricted to the lower two-thirds of the Dinosaur Park Formation. Warner-Cowgill and colleagues (2025) answer that the holotype came from the Oldman and that Carr's range rests on other specimens. Governs `location.locality: Quarry 88` and `location.formation: Oldman` on [*Daspletosaurus torosus*](../../../genera/D/Daspletosaurus.yml). The Oldman and Dinosaur Park boundary in general is in [Oldman and Dinosaur Park](../../topics/oldman-and-dinosaur-park.md).

**Evidence.**
- russell1970a, p. 14, Type: "NMC 8506 skull and skeleton, lacking hind limbs (quarry 88 of Sternberg 1905)"; the species' material is from the "Oldman Formation, near Steveville, Alberta, except where noted" (p. 13). Elsewhere: "AMNH 5664 … (quarry 54 of Sternberg 1950)". The bibliography lists Sternberg works of 1945, 1946, 1949, 1950 and 1966, and none of 1905; the 1950 entry is "Map 969A Steveville west of the fourth meridian, notes on fossil localities".
- carr2017a, p. 7, Discussion: "D. torosus specimens are restricted to the lower two-thirds of the DPF (~76.7–75.2Ma)".
- warner-cowgill2025a, p. 170: "Carr et al. (2017) did not indicate which specimens of D. torosus represent the upper limit of this stratigraphic range. CMN 8506 was recovered from the Oldman Formation, not the overlying Dinosaur Park Formation (Paulina-Carabajal et al. 2021)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Dinosaur Park Formation.* Carr and colleagues' range is for referred specimens, and Warner-Cowgill and colleagues place the holotype itself in the Oldman.
- *Quarry 72, Steveville.* The locality stored before. Russell prints quarry 88 for NMC 8506, and no source read gives a quarry 72 for any *Daspletosaurus*.

**Open.** Paulina-Carabajal and colleagues (2021), on whom Warner-Cowgill and colleagues rest the Oldman placement, have not been read; nor has Sternberg's 1950 map, which would give quarry 88's position.

## Is the *Daspletosaurus torosus* holotype NMC 8506 or AMNH 5438?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** NMC 8506, the skull and skeleton Russell (1970) designates as the type; AMNH 5438 is his paratype. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NMC 8506]` on [*Daspletosaurus torosus*](../../../genera/D/Daspletosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- russell1970a, p. 14: "Type NMC 8506 skull and skeleton, lacking hind limbs (quarry 88 of Sternberg 1905)"; "Paratype AMNH 5438 sacrum and adjacent thoracic and caudal vertebrae, pelvis, right femur, left tibia and second metatarsal (Little Sandhill Creek basin)."

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 5438.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) list "*Daspletosaurus torosus* | AMNH 5438" in their theropod speed table (molina-pérez2019a, Biology: Biomechanics: Locomotion). It is the paratype, and the same book's record table gives "CMN 8506" for the species.

**Open.** Nothing.
