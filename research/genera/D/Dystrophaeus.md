# *Dystrophaeus*

## Where and in which unit was the *Dystrophaeus* holotype found, and does Cope's position stand?

*2026-08-21*

**Conclusion.** East Canyon Quarry, San Juan County, in the lower half of the Tidwell Member of the Morrison Formation, as the relocated and re-excavated type locality gives it. No coordinate is recorded. Cope (1877) prints "lat. 38° 15′; lon. 110°", a longitude to the whole degree, which at this latitude spans about 87 km of ground. Governs `location.locality: East Canyon Quarry`, `location.formation: Morrison`, `location.member: Tidwell`, `location.part: lower` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Dystrophaeus viaemalae*](../../../genera/D/Dystrophaeus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cope1877a, p. 583: "The locality is in the Painted Canyon not far from the Sierra Abajo in South-eastern Utah, near the Colorado boundary; lat. 38° 15′; lon. 110°."
- tschopp2015a, p. 6, Table 2: USNM 2364, "East Canyon Quarry, San Juan County, UT, USA", "Oxfordian; low in Morrison Form."
- foster2016a, p. 138: "Reinvestigation and further excavation of the type locality during the 2014–2016 field seasons has confirmed that the specimen is from the lower half of the Tidwell Member".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cope's coordinate, 38.25, -110.* A whole degree of longitude is no position for a quarry. White (1973) repeats the pair and puts the site in Wayne County ("Utah, Wayne County, Painted Canyon, Lat. 38° 15′ N. Long. 110° W.", white1973a, p. 131), while the relocated quarry is in San Juan County; that White's county follows from the coordinate is our inference.
- *The Summerville Formation.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) keep the genus "because the material comes from the Summerville Formation" (upchurch2004a, p. 299), and the distribution chapter of the same volume lists it under the Summerville as "Callovian-Oxfordian (Anderson and Lucas 1996)" (weishampel2004b, p. 538). The later fieldwork at the relocated site places it low in the Morrison, in the Tidwell Member.

**Open.** Whether East Canyon Quarry lies in Cope's Painted Canyon. No source read relates the two names. Gillette's (1996) papers on the relocation, and the full paper behind Foster and colleagues' 2016 abstract, would settle it and may print the quarry's position.
