# *Dracopelta*

## Which number identifies the Dracopelta holotype?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** The holotype is one individual catalogued under MG 5787 (rib cage and armor), MG 3 (autopodium) and unnumbered material; Russo and Mateus (2021) recommend MG 3 for the whole specimen. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on *Dracopelta zbyszewskii*: either MG 3 alone, following the recommendation, or both numbers with a note.

**Evidence.**
- galton1980b, p. 452: "Type specimen : SGP, a partial rib cage with armor", with no number.
- russo2021a, p. 28, Fig. 1 caption: "(a) IGM 5787, ribcage and dermal armour; (b) IGM 3, autopodium; (c) … (stored at LNEG, no inventory number)"; p. 32: "MG 5787 (former IGM 5787) … and MG 3 (IGM 3), an incomplete autopodium"; p. 33: "we recommend the lowest number (MG 3) for the entire holotype specimen".
- russo2021a, p. 33: "All SGP specimens were automatically converted to MG in 1993 without changing the number itself", so SGP, IGM and MG numbers coincide.

**Ruled out.**
- *MG 5787 alone.* Jones (2026) gives "MG 5787—partial skeleton (Russo and Mateus, 2021)" in a table (jones2026a, p. 398), citing a paper that recommends MG 3.

**Open.** The recommendation is not a nomenclatural act. Galton counts "thirteen dorsal vertebrae" (galton1980b, p. 451), Russo and Mateus 12 (p. 32).

## Is the *Dracopelta* holotype from Ribamar, and from the Kimmeridgian?

*2026-08-20*

**Conclusion.** No. It came from a roadcut between Barril and Praia da Assenta Sul, in Mafra, in the Assenta Member at the top of the Lourinhã Formation, uppermost lower to upper Tithonian, as Russo and Mateus (2021) establish from field notes, photographs and a visit to the site. Galton's (1980) "Ribomar" and Kimmeridgian rest on a handwritten "Ribamar" label, and two places of that name lie 25 km apart. Governs `location.locality: Praia da Assenta Sul`, `location.member: Assenta` and `location.coordinates: [39.052167, -9.412]` on [*Dracopelta zbyszewskii*](../../../genera/D/Dracopelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1980b, p. 452: "Type locality and horizon : Ribomar on west coast of Portugal from the Kimmeridgian (Upper Jurassic)."
- russo2021a, p. 29: the only official information was "a short, handwritten "Ribamar'' label associated with the specimen"; p. 30: "there are two localities with the same name, Ribamar, 25 km apart".
- russo2021a, p. 31: "the exact type locality at 39°03'07.8" N 9°24'43.2" W, a roadcut between Barril and Praia da Assenta Sul, in the municipality of Mafra", "5 km North of Ribamar, Mafra"; p. 32: the beds match "the uppermost part of the Lourinhã Formation, the Assenta Member".

**Ruled out.**
- *Ribamar in Mafra.* Antunes and Mateus (2003) note "outcrops of Lower Cretaceous, Valanginian to Albian layers and no Jurassic beds at all" there (antunes2003a, p. 84).
- *Ribamar near Lourinhã.* Antunes and Mateus (2003, p. 85) offer it as "another possibility"; the site Russo and Mateus identify is 5 km north of Ribamar in Mafra.
- *"400 m East of Praia do Sul, near Assenta, Torres Vedras".* This is Pereda-Suberbiola and colleagues' (2005) locality as Russo and Mateus report it, given "without figuring the location or providing coordinates" (russo2021a, p. 30); Russo and Mateus place the roadcut in Mafra.
- *Kimmeridgian.* Russo and Mateus date the site to the uppermost lower to upper Tithonian (russo2021a, p. 32).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Dracopelta* holotype Tithonian or Kimmeridgian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Tithonian. Russo and Mateus (2021) relocated the type section in the Assenta Member and adopt an uppermost lower to upper Tithonian age for it, well above the Kimmeridgian–Tithonian boundary. Jones (2026) gives the same stage. Governs `period.stage: [Tithonian]` on [*Dracopelta zbyszewskii*](../../../genera/D/Dracopelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- russo2021a, p. 32: "we agree with the uppermost lower Tithonian-upper Tithonian age of D. zbyszewskii. This specimen is much higher than the Kimmeridgian/Tithonian boundary seen in the outcrops to the North".
- jones2026a, Thyreophora table: "Lourinhã Fm., Portugal *Late Jurassic, Tithonian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Kimmeridgian.* Vickaryous and colleagues (2004) give "Kimmeridgian" (vickaryous2004a, p. 366, Table 17.1), Galton's (1980) age for a locality that rested on a "Ribamar" label (see the 2026-08-20 entry).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Dracopelta* holotype from the Lourinhã Formation or the Freixial?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** The Lourinhã Formation, in its Assenta Member, where Russo and Mateus (2021) place the relocated type section. Jones (2026) gives the same formation. Governs `location.formation: Lourinhã` and `location.member: Assenta` on [*Dracopelta zbyszewskii*](../../../genera/D/Dracopelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- russo2021a, p. 32: the type section matches "the uppermost part of the Lourinhã Formation, the Assenta Member (Mateus et al., 2017)".
- jones2026a, Thyreophora table: "Lourinhã Fm., Portugal".

**Ruled out.**
- *Freixial Formation.* Mateus and Milàn give "MG5787, Freixial Fm., Tithonian" in a table of the Portuguese dinosaur fauna (mateus2009b, p. 254, Table 1). Russo and Mateus (2021), with Mateus an author of both, place the site in the Assenta Member after visiting it. Schneider and colleagues (2009) treat the Freixial as a member of the Farta Pão Formation (see [Farta Pão](../../stratigraphy/f/farta-pão.md)).
- *An unnamed unit.* Vickaryous and colleagues (2004) list "Unnamed unit (Lisboa), Portugal" (vickaryous2004a, p. 366, Table 17.1). Galton (1980) names no unit, and the site was not relocated until 2021.

**Open.** Nothing.
