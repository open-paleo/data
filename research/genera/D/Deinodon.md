# *Deinodon*

## What unit and site are the *Deinodon horridus* type teeth from?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** The Judith River Formation, on the statements of Osborn (1905) and Matthew and Brown (1922); the site was never recorded. Leidy (1856) names no formation and gives only Hayden's collecting area, "the Bad Lands of the Judith River, Nebraska Territory". The badlands of a drainage are not a site. Governs `location.formation: Judith River` and the empty `location.locality` on [*Deinodon horridus*](../../../genera/D/Deinodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leidy1856b, p. 72: "Notices of remains of extinct Reptiles and Fishes, discovered by Dr. F. V. Hayden in the Bad Lands of the Judith River, Nebraska Territory"; p. 73: the remains, "I suspect, indicate the existence of a formation like that of the Wealden of Europe".
- osborn1905a, p. 259: "In 1856, Deinodon horridus, from the Judith River Beds of Montana, was securely founded by Leidy".
- matthew1922a, p. 375, chronological list, under "Formation and locality": "Deinodon horridus Leidy, 1856 | Judith R., Mont."; p. 379: "D. horridus of the Judith River, Montana".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Wealden-age formation.* Leidy's comparison with the Wealden is a guess about age and names no unit.
- *"Judith River badlands" as the locality.* The area Hayden collected over, which is all any source read gives.

**Open.** Nothing.
