# *Dornraptor*

## Is the *Dornraptor* holotype from the Blue Lias or the Charmouth Mudstone Formation?

*2026-08-20*

**Conclusion.** The Blue Lias, as Baron (2024) gives it; the Charmouth Mudstone is an open alternative. Norman (2020) places the *Scelidosaurus* material from the cliffs between Charmouth and Lyme Regis in the Charmouth Mudstone Formation, Black Ven Marl Member, and records that the knee joint now NHMUK OR39496 was among the fossils sold from Harrison's collection after his death, but gives no horizon for the knee itself. Governs `location.formation: Blue Lias` and `location.locality: Charmouth` on [*Dornraptor normani*](../../../genera/D/Dornraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- baron2024a, p. 5: "Age. Hettangian-Sinemurian, Early Jurassic (Cope et al., 1980)"; p. 6: "Occurrence. Blue Lias Formation, Lower Lias Group, Charmouth, UK."
- norman2020a, p. 9: for *Scelidosaurus harrisonii*, "Stratigraphic range: Charmouth Mudstone Formation (Upper Sinemurian), Black Ven Marl Member, *Asteroceras obtusum* Zone, *Obtusum* Subzone (Hesselbo & Jenkyns, 1995)"; "Locality: Cliff exposures on The Spittles–Black Ven between Charmouth and Lyme Regis, Dorset".
- norman2020a, p. 4: after Harrison's death in 1864, Mrs Harrison sold several fossils, and "one notable specimen was the scelidosaur 'type' knee joint (NHMUK OR39496...)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing. Neither formation has a source against it for this specimen.

**Open.** Which formation. Norman's horizon is given for *Scelidosaurus*, and it reaches this specimen only through Harrison's collection, which is our inference. If the knee is from the Black Ven Marl, its age narrows from Baron's Hettangian–Sinemurian to the late Sinemurian. Choiniere and colleagues (2020), on a small theropod from the Lias Group of Charmouth, have not been read and may place this specimen.
