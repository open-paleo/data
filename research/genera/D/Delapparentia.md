# *Delapparentia*

## Which unit and province is the *Delapparentia* holotype from, and on which side of Galve is La Maca 3?

*2026-08-19*

**Conclusion.** The middle part of the Camarillas Formation at La Maca 3, Galve, in Teruel Province. Two readings in widely used compilations are errors that Ruiz-Omeñaca (2011) names as such. The two papers on the holotype disagree on the direction from Galve; the map in Gasca and colleagues (2015) agrees with their own text. Governs `location.formation: Camarillas`, `location.part: middle`, `location.locality: La Maca 3, Galve` and `location.region: ES-TE` on [*Delapparentia turolensis*](../../../genera/D/Delapparentia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ruiz-omeñaca2011a, p. 85: La Maca hill is "about 2 km to the W of Galve" (translated), and its four sites lie "in the middle part of the Camarillas Formation (lower Barremian)" (translated).
- ruiz-omeñaca2011a, p. 101: Norman and Weishampel (1990) and Weishampel (1990) "erroneously cite the Camarillas Formation as 'Las Zabacheras Beds'", and Norman (2004) and Weishampel and colleagues (2004) "erroneously cite the taxa from the Camarillas Formation in Galve as from the province of La Rioja" (translated).
- gasca2015a, p. 58: La Maca-3 "is located on a small hill two kilometres east of the village of Galve"; the fossiliferous lutites lie "in the middle part of the Camarillas Formation".
- gasca2015a, p. 59, Fig. 1C: the geological map plots the La Maca-3 star east of Galve.

**Ruled out.**
- *"Las Zabacheras Beds" as the unit.* Ruiz-Omeñaca (2011) lists it among the errors of the secondary literature. Las Zabacheras is the *Aragosaurus* site, excavated at the same time as La Maca 3, and the two sites' bones were mixed in the Museo de Teruel until Lapparent separated them (ruiz-omeñaca2011a, p. 87, translated).
- *La Rioja.* Ruiz-Omeñaca (2011) names it an error; Galve is in Teruel.
- *West of Galve.* Ruiz-Omeñaca's "W" is contradicted by the text and map of Gasca and colleagues (2015), whose author list includes Ruiz-Omeñaca. That the "W" is a slip is our reading; the record carries no bearing or coordinate that depends on it.

**Open.** Nothing.
