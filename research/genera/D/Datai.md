# *Datai*

## What stages does *Datai* take?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** Cenomanian and Turonian, the range of the Zhoutian Formation. The describing paper's only statement about the horizon is the Turonian–early Coniacian label already ruled out for the formation, and its 96–90 Ma figures fall in the Cenomanian and Turonian. Governs `stage` on [*Datai yingliangis*](../../../genera/D/Datai.yml), in the [Zhoutian Formation](../../../stratigraphy/z/zhoutian.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xing2024a, p. 114, Type locality and horizon: "Zhoutian Formation, Ganzhou Group (Turonian–early Coniacian; Upper Cretaceous; 96–90 Ma)".
- xing2024a, Introduction: "a new ankylosaurine from the Coniacian–Turonian (early Late Cretaceous) of southeastern China".
- The [Ganzhou](../../stratigraphy/g/ganzhou.md) entry of 2026-10-03: the 96–90 Ma restates the conditional floor of Li and colleagues (2018), and falls in the Cenomanian and Turonian on the ICS chart v2024/12.

**Ruled out.**
- *Turonian alone.* It keeps one end of a label whose numbers do not match it and drops the Cenomanian those numbers give; no source dates the horizon more narrowly than the formation.
- *Turonian–early Coniacian.* Ruled out for the formation in the Ganzhou entry; the paper offers nothing about the Mazhou horizon itself beyond that label.

**Open.** Nothing.
