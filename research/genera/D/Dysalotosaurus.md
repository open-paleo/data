# *Dysalotosaurus*

## Are Quarry Ig and the WJ site different localities, and does the name-bearing material come from them?

*2026-08-21*

**Conclusion.** They are one site. "Ig" was the field name for the quarry northwest of Tendaguru Hill near Kindope, and "WJ" the name that replaced it during the 1912 excavations; the papers on *Dysalotosaurus* write it as Ig/WJ. The species has no holotype, and Galton (1983) states that every specimen of it came from Quarry Ig, which places Virchow's cervical vertebrae there as well; no paper read gives a field number tying those two vertebrae to the site. Governs `location.locality: Tendaguru Quarry Ig, Kindope` on [*Dysalotosaurus lettowvorbecki*](../../../genera/D/Dysalotosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1983a, p. 209: "All the specimens of *D. lettowvorbecki* were collected from Quarry Ig northwest of the Tendaguru Hill near the small settlement of Kindope".
- rocchi2026a, p. 2, Introduction: catalogued elements "from the excavation site Ig/WJ ('iguanodontid'/'Werner Janensch') in Tendaguru are attributed to Dy. lettowvorbecki".

**Ruled out.**
- *WJ as a second quarry.* Rocchi and colleagues (2026) gloss the two letters as two names for one excavation site. Hübner and colleagues (2021), on the history of the excavation, give the date of the change from "Ig" to "WJ" as early June 1912.

**Open.** Nothing.
