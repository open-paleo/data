# *Deinonychus*

## Is the *Deinonychus antirrhopus* holotype YPM 5205 alone?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Ostrom (1969) designates the pes YPM 5205 as the type and lists the other Yale and AMNH specimens as hypodigm. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [YPM 5205]` on [*Deinonychus antirrhopus*](../../../genera/D/Deinonychus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ostrom1969a, p. 3: "TYPE: YPM 5205, a complete left pes and an incomplete right pes. HYPODIGM: YPM 5201, 5202, and 5203, three series of articulated caudal vertebrae", followed by YPM 5204, "YPM 5206, nearly complete left and right manus" and YPM 5210, the skull.

**Ruled out.**
- *YPM 5205, 5203, 5206, 5210.* Jones (2026) gives "YPM 5205, 5203, 5206, 5210—skull and skeleton (Ostrom, 1969)" (jones2026c, Eudromaeosauria table). The three added numbers are hypodigm specimens in the paper Jones cites, so the row folds the hypodigm into the type.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `ostrom1969a`: Ostrom, J. H. (1969). A new theropod dinosaur from the Lower Cretaceous of Montana. *Postilla* 128: 1-17.
- `jones2026c`: Jones, B. (2026). The Princeton Encyclopedia of Dinosaurs: Theropods. Princeton University Press.
