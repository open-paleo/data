# *Dakotadon*

## Which member of the Lakota Formation is the Dakotadon holotype from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The lower [Fuson Member](../../../stratigraphy/f/fuson.yml), late Valanginian to early Barremian, reached by correlating the specimen's bed. Governs `location.member: Fuson`, `location.part: lower` and `stage: [Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian]` on *Dakotadon lakotaensis*.

**Evidence.**
- boyd2015b, p. 5, Fig. 3 caption: "Position of SDSM 8656 is interpreted to be within unit L2, which correlates with the lower Fuson Member"; p. 8: "Distribution: Lower Fuson Member, Lakota Formation".
- boyd2015b, p. 8: the "lower Fuson Member, suggests a late Valanginian to early Barremian age (Cifelli, Davis & Sames, 2014)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian from the describing paper.* Weishampel and Bjork (1989) give "Age-Early Cretaceous, possibly Barremian (Sohn, 1979)" (weishampel1989a, p. 57) and, as Boyd and Pagnac note, "did not provide a precise stratigraphic position or even identify a specific member" (boyd2015b, p. 6). Sohn (1979) himself concludes only that the Lakota is "pre-Aptian", "Barremian or older" (sohn1979a, title page and p. 1), from ostracodes in the Chilson Member; "possibly Barremian" is Weishampel and Bjork's narrowing.

**Open.** The member assignment is an interpretation by correlation, not a measured section through the quarry.
