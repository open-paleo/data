# *Dolichosuchus*

## Is the *Dolichosuchus* tibia from the lower Stubensandstein of the Kaltental *Aetosaurus* quarry?

*2026-08-19*

**Conclusion.** Not established. The tibia comes from Stuttgart-Kaltental, but which of the area's quarries yielded it was never recorded, so its level within the Löwenstein Formation is either the lower or the middle Stubensandstein. No `part` is recorded. Governs `location.locality: Stuttgart-Kaltental`, `location.formation: Löwenstein` and the absence of `location.part` on [*Dolichosuchus cristatus*](../../../genera/D/Dolichosuchus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2000a, p. 84: "there are numerous abandoned Stubensandstein quarries around Stuttgart and the exact provenance of the specimens was never recorded"; "The quarry near Kaltental which produced the famous association of 22 specimens of the stagonolepidid *Aetosaurus* FRAAS was placed in the lower Stubensandstein (WILD, 1989), but it is by no means sure whether *Dolichosuchus* comes from the same site"; "both specimens must have been derived from lower or middle Stubensandstein".
- sues2025a, p. 465: "Type locality. Stuttgart-Kaltental, Baden-Württemberg. Type horizon. Lower or Middle Stubensandstein (S1 or S2), Löwenstein Formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`, from the* Aetosaurus *quarry.* Rauhut and Hungerbühler (2000) state that the tibia is not known to come from that quarry.

**Open.** Nothing that a field could hold: the either-or between S1 and S2 is the finest the sources give.

## Is the *Dolichosuchus* holotype numbered 38058 or 38056?

*2026-08-19*

**Conclusion.** 38058, the number Huene (1932) gives in naming the species and Sues and colleagues (2025) repeat. Rauhut and Hungerbühler (2000) print 38056 once. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NHMUK PV OR 38058]` on [*Dolichosuchus cristatus*](../../../genera/D/Dolichosuchus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- huene1932a, p. 31: "The left tibia No. 38 058 in the British Museum (Nat. Hist.), London, from the Stubensandstein of Kaltenthal near Stuttgart" (translated).
- sues2025a, p. 465: "Holotype. NHMUK PV OR 38058, nearly complete left tibia."
- rauhut2000a, p. 76: "The specimen (BMNH 38056) shows a large cnemial crest".

**Ruled out.**
- *38056.* It occurs only in this one sentence of Rauhut and Hungerbühler (2000), and we read it as a misprint of the same specimen; the paper describes no second tibia.

**Open.** Nothing.
