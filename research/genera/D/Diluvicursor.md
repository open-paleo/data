# *Diluvicursor*

## Is the *Diluvicursor* holotype early Albian or early Aptian?

*2026-08-19*

**Conclusion.** Early Albian. Herne and colleagues (2018) place the Eumeralla fossil localities in the *Crybelosporites striatus* spore–pollen zone, whose base is at the Aptian–Albian boundary. Their 2019 paper repeats the lower Albian in its text, and the "lower Aptian" in its distribution table contradicts that text. Governs `period.stage: [Albian]` on [*Diluvicursor pickeringi*](../../../genera/D/Diluvicursor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- herne2018a, p. 1, Abstract: "named from the lower Albian of the Eumeralla Formation".
- herne2018a, p. 7: "Fossil localities of the Eumeralla Formation fall within the Crybelosporites striatus spore–pollen zone of Helby, Morgan & Partridge (1987), the base of which is at the Aptian–Albian boundary".
- herne2019a, p. 543: "*Diluvicursor pickeringi* Herne et al., 2018, all of which are from the lower Albian of the Eumeralla Formation in the Otway Basin"; p. 575: "the younger, early Albian Eumeralla Formation".
- herne2019a, p. 577, Table 5: "*Diluvicursor pickeringi* Herne et al., 2018 | Eric the Red West, Victoria | Eumeralla Formation, Otway Group, Otway Basin (lower Aptian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Early Aptian.* Table 5 of Herne and colleagues (2019) gives "lower Aptian" to every Eumeralla row, from Dinosaur Cove to Eric the Red West, while the same paper's text twice calls the Eumeralla taxa lower or early Albian, and nothing in the paper argues for an older age. We read the table entry as a slip.

**Open.** Nothing for the species. The [Eumeralla](../../../stratigraphy/e/eumeralla.yml) entry quotes the same Table 5 wording for the unit; whether it supports the unit's Aptian is a question for that entry.
