# *Daliansaurus*

## Is *Daliansaurus* a sinovenatorine?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Only on its describers' analysis; it sits at Troodontidae incertae sedis. Every later analysis places it more derived, outside Sinovenatorinae. Governs `parent: Troodontidae`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Daliansaurus liaoningensis*](../../../genera/D/Daliansaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- shen2017b, p. 775: "Daliansaurus and Sinusonasus are placed as sister taxa" within Sinovenatorinae.
- hartman2019a, Fig. 17: *Daliansaurus* outside Sinovenatorinae, below *Hesperornithoides*, *Xixiasaurus* and *Sinusonasus*; constraining it into Sinovenatorinae costs four steps.

**Ruled out.**
- *Sinovenatorinae as the parent.* Hartman and colleagues (2019), Cau and Madzia (2021), Sellés and colleagues (2021), Wang and colleagues (2023) and Kubota and colleagues (2024) all place it outside.

**Open.** Its position among the more derived troodontids.
