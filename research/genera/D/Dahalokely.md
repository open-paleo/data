# *Dahalokely*

## What is the number of the *Dahalokely* holotype?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** UA 9855. Farke and Sertich (2013) give that number in the holotype line and every figure caption. Their Measurements section and the captions of Tables 1 and 2 call the holotype "UA 8678", which is the number of the *Majungasaurus* skeleton the same paper uses for its body-length comparison. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [UA 9855]` on [*Dahalokely tokana*](../../../genera/D/Dahalokely.yml).

**Evidence.**
- farke2013a, p. 3, Holotype: "UA 9855, a partial axial column including cervical vertebra C?5, dorsal vertebrae D?1, D?2, and D?6–D?9"; p. 4, Fig. 2 caption: "Dahalokely tokana, holotype (UA 9855)".
- farke2013a, p. 2, Measurements: "Dimensions of the Dahalokely tokana holotype, UA 8678"; p. 3, Table 1 caption: "Selected measurements in millimeters of the vertebrae from the Dahalokely tokana holotype, UA 8678".
- farke2013a, p. 16, Body size estimation: "Majungasaurus crenatissimus (UA 8678; University of Antananarivo …)"; "UA 8678 was chosen for the analysis here because it is the most complete axial column known".

**Ruled out.**
- *UA 8678.* The paper's own *Majungasaurus* specimen. It appears for *Dahalokely* only in the measurement text and two table captions, against the holotype line and all figure captions.

**Open.** Nothing.
