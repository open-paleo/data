# *Dicraeosaurus*

## Do the two *Dicraeosaurus* types come from one site?

*2026-08-19*

**Conclusion.** No. Janensch (1914) founds *D. hansemanni* on skeleton m from Kindope, in the middle Saurian zone, and *D. sattleri* on find M, in the uppermost saurian horizon about half a kilometer south of Tendaguru hill. The labels differ only in case, and the two sites lie on opposite sides of the hill. Governs `location.locality: Kindope` and `location.member: Middle Dinosaur` on [*D. hansemanni*](../../../genera/D/Dicraeosaurus.yml), and `location.locality: Locality M` and `location.member: Upper Dinosaur` on [*D. sattleri*](../../../genera/D/Dicraeosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- janensch1914a, *Dicraeosaurus Hansemanni* nov. gen. nov. sp.: "Dieser neuen Art ist das Skelett m von Kindope aus der mittleren Saurierzone zugrunde gelegt" ("This new species is based on skeleton m from Kindope, from the middle Saurian zone", translated).
- janensch1914a, *Dicraeosaurus Sattleri* n. sp.: "der ... Fund M aus der obersten Saurierstufe, etwa ½ km südlich von der Tendaguru-kuppe gefunden" ("find M, ... found in the uppermost saurian horizon about ½ km south of the Tendaguru hill", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *One shared position for both.* The pair formerly stored on both species, [−9.696665, 39.228008], cannot fit a site at Kindope, which Janensch places northwest of Tendaguru (janensch1914a, *Brachiosaurus Fraasi* n. sp.), and one south of the hill.
- *Site m or M as the locality of the other species.* Each letter belongs to one type; Kindope also yielded skeleton Y, the type of *Brachiosaurus fraasi* (janensch1914a, *Brachiosaurus Fraasi* n. sp.), so "Kindope" names an area with several finds rather than one quarry.

**Open.** Positions for both sites. Neither is printed in the sources read.

## References

- `janensch1914a`: Janensch, W. (1914). Übersicht über die Wirbeltierfauna der Tendaguru-Schichten [Overview of the vertebrate fauna of the Tendaguru beds]. *Archiv für Biontologie* 3: 81-110.
