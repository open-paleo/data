# *Dyslocosaurus*

## Is the *Dyslocosaurus* holotype from the Morrison or the Lance Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Morrison, as the describing paper's stated preference. It is not a determination. The only documentary evidence, the museum's own record, says Lance. McIntosh and colleagues (1992) favor the Morrison because of how the bones are preserved and because diplodocids are unknown from the Late Cretaceous of North America, and they say they cannot rule the Lance out. Governs `location.formation: Morrison` and `location.notes` on [*Dyslocosaurus polyonychius*](../../../genera/D/Dyslocosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mcintosh1992a, p. 158: "Distribution—Formation uncertain, probably from the Morrison Formation (see discussion); vicinity of Lance Creek, eastern Wyoming."
- mcintosh1992a, Provenance: "The information recorded in the museum file is simply: 'Lance Formation, vicinity of Lance Creek, eastern Wyoming.'"
- mcintosh1992a, p. 166: "The preservation, however, strongly suggests derivation from one of the small exposures of the Morrison Formation within Niobrara County. Although we favor the latter conclusion, in absence of more precise data on the collection site we cannot dismiss the possibility that Loomis's stratigraphic determination is correct."
- tschopp2015a, Table 1: AC 663, "not sure if same individual, or even same locality"; "Morrison, or Lance Form."

**Ruled out.**
- *Lance Formation as the recorded unit.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) give "?Lance Formation (?Morrison Formation)" with "?Maastrichtian (?Kimmeridgian–Tithonian)" (upchurch2004a, p. 265, Table 13.1). The distribution chapter lists the species in the Lance fauna, "(may be from the Morrison Formation)" (weishampel2004b, p. 585). Both put the museum label first. The describing paper, which examined the preservation, favors the Morrison, and they give no new evidence of their own.

**Open.** Which unit the specimen came from. Loomis's field notes give no locality. A site, or a matrix or preservation study against both units, would settle it.
