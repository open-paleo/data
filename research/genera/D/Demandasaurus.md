# *Demandasaurus*

## Is the *Demandasaurus darwini* holotype one catalogue number or a series?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** A series. Torcida Fernández-Baldor and colleagues (2011) give a separate MDS-RVII number for each bone of one partial skeleton, 59 in all, from the premaxillae (MDS-RVII,275 and MDS-RVII,332) to the left femur (MDS-RVII,16). Jones (2026) agrees in substance. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id`, the 59 MDS-RVII numbers, on [*Demandasaurus darwini*](../../../genera/D/Demandasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- torcidafernández-baldor2011a, p. 537: "Holotype: A partial skeleton represented by the right (MDS-RVII,275) and left (MDS-RVII,332) premaxillae, left dentary (MDS-RVII,443), six isolated teeth"; p. 538: "and left femur (MDS-RVII,16)".
- jones2026b, Rebbachisauridae table: "MDS–RVII series—partial skeleton (Fernández-Baldor et al., 2011)".

**Ruled out.**
- *MDS-RVII,16 alone.* The record once held only this number, which is the left femur, one element of the holotype.

**Open.** Nothing.
