# *Zhengheornis*

## What stage is the *Zhengheornis* holotype?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian to Tithonian. The holotype lies in layer six, above the *Fujianvenator* horizon and above the highest dated tuffite in the section (149.9 ± 1.1 Ma, Kimmeridgian at its central value); the next age up is the ignimbrite over the fossil-bearing sediments, 148 ± 1.9 Ma, which is Tithonian. The bracket spans the stage boundary (base Tithonian 149.2 Ma, ICS chart v2024/12), so both stages stand. That the ignimbrite lies above layer six is our reading of "overlie the fossil-bearing sediments": the sample is not drawn on the log. Governs `stage: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` and `location.part: lower` on [*Zhengheornis buyu*](../../../genera/Z/Zhengheornis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wang2026a, Materials and Methods: "The holotype of *Zhengheornis* and IVPP V33260 were preserved in 'layer six,' above the horizon ('layer two') that preserves the holotypes of *Fujianvenator* and *Baminornis* [see the composite stratigraphic log in figure 4 in (10)]"; the fauna is given as "148 to 150 million years ago (Ma)".
- xu2023a, Fig. 4a: the composite log of the 3.6 m section labels layers 1 to 13, with the 149.9 ± 1.1 Ma tuffite between layers 3 and 5, below layer six.
- xu2023a, Methods: "concordant zircon U-Pb ages of 150.2 ± 1.1 Ma (PM702-1) and 149.9 ± 1.1 Ma (PM701-4) for the lower and upper layers of the IVPP V31985-bearing horizons, respectively … Concordant zircon U-Pb ages of 150 ± 1.8 Ma (Bdx03) and 148 ± 1.9 Ma (Bdx04) were obtained from the ignimbrite samples", which "underlie (Bdx03) and overlie (Bdx04) the fossil-bearing sediments"; "The section that we excavated is 3.6 m in depth and belongs to the lower part of the Nanyuan Formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *Tithonian alone.* The describing paper gives "Upper Jurassic, Nanyuan Formation [Tithonian stage; (10)]" (wang2026a), relaying the 2023 paper's label, which belongs to an older chart (see [Morrison Tithonian labels](../../topics/morrison-tithonian-labels.md)).
- *Kimmeridgian alone, as for Fujianvenator.* The two Kimmeridgian tuffites bracket layer two, not layer six; no Kimmeridgian age caps layer six.

**Open.** Nothing.
