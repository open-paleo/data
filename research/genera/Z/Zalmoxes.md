# *Zalmoxes*

## Which paper named *Zalmoxes robustus*?

*2026-11-12*

**Conclusion.** Nopcsa's paper in volume 68 of the Vienna *Denkschriften* (keyed 1900), as *Mochlodon (?) robustum*; Weishampel and colleagues (2003) erected *Zalmoxes* for it. Governs `erected_in: nopcsa1900a` and `erected_as: Mochlodon robustum` on *Z. robustus*, and the genus-level `erected_in: weishampel2003a`, on [*Zalmoxes*](../../../genera/Z/Zalmoxes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nopcsa1900a, p. 579: "An einem ebenfalls in meiner Sammlung befindlichen grossen *Mochlodon*-Unterkiefer, der wohl ebenfalls eine neue Species repräsentirt (*Mochlodon (?) robustum* nov. spec.)" ("On a large *Mochlodon* lower jaw, also in my collection, which probably also represents a new species", translated).
- nopcsa1902b, p. 149: "und 1899 stellte ich für einen besonders starken *Mochlodon*-Unterkiefer die Species *Mochlodon (?) robustum* auf" ("and in 1899 I established the species *Mochlodon (?) robustum* for a particularly strong *Mochlodon* lower jaw", translated), with a footnote to "Denkschr. d. k. Akad. d. Wiss. Wien 1899, p. 579".

**Ruled out.**
- *Nopcsa (1902).* Weishampel and colleagues give "*Mochlodon robustum* (corrected to *robustus*) Nopcsa 1902a *partim*" (weishampel2003a, Synonymy). In that paper Nopcsa cites the name as already established and sinks it into *Mochlodon suessi*; it does not name the species.
- *Weishampel and colleagues (2003) as the species' authority.* They erected the genus for an existing species.

**Open.** Whether the 1900 name is available. Before 1931 a name needs a description, definition or indication (ICZN Art. 12), and the p. 579 entry gives only "a large *Mochlodon* lower jaw" in Nopcsa's collection, where the *Camptosaurus inkeyi* named on the same page has a footnoted description. If the name was not made available in 1900, the next work to describe the jaw under that name would be its author.

## Is the *Zalmoxes robustus* holotype from the Sânpetru or the Densuș-Ciula Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Sânpetru Formation, at Sânpetru. The Densuș-Ciula has yielded referred material only. The house spelling Sânpetru follows the current Romanian orthography; the describing paper prints "Sanpetru". Governs `location.formation: Sânpetru` on [*Zalmoxes robustus*](../../../genera/Z/Zalmoxes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- weishampel2003a, p. 70: "HOLOTYPE. BMNH R.3392; a right dentary"; "LOCALITY AND HORIZON. Sanpetru, Hunedoara County, Romania. Sanpetru Formation (upper Maastrichtian ...)".
- weishampel2003a, p. 70: "REFERRED SPECIMENS. See Appendix 1 (Sanpetru and Densuş Ciula formations (upper Maastrichtian), Sanpetru, Valioara, Tus˘ţea, Pui, Hunedoara County".
- weishampel2003a, p. 68: "termed the Sanpetru Formation (in this post-Ceausescu era, some spellings in the Romanian language have changed, among them Sînpetru to Sanpetru)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sânpetru and Densuș-Ciula together.* Jones (2026) gives "Sânpetru Fm Densus-Ciula Fm., Romania" against "NHMUK R. 3392—partial jaw; numerous specimens" (jones2026a, Iguanodontia table). The cell covers the species' referred material; the describing paper separates the holotype's horizon from the referred specimens'.

**Open.** Nothing.
