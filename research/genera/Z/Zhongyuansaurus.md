# *Zhongyuansaurus*

## Which formation holds the *Zhongyuansaurus* holotype?

*2026-10-10*

**Conclusion.** The record keeps the Haoling Formation, under the methods rule that keeps the current modern name where no primary source gives one, with the basis set out in a note. The describing paper gives the Mangchuan Formation, which Lü and colleagues (2013) split into three; that the Haoling is the unit at Liudian rests on Lü and colleagues placing a different sauropod in it near Santun and Liudian (our inference). Governs `location.formation: Haoling` and its note on [*Zhongyuansaurus luoyangensis*](../../../genera/Z/Zhongyuansaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2007a: "Liudian Township, Ruyang County, Henan Province; Mangchuan Formation (Lü et al., 2006)".
- arbour2015a: HGM 41HIII-0002 from "Henan Province, Ruyang County, Liu Dianxiang; Mangchuan Formation".
- lü2013b: the "'Mangchuan Formation', can be subdivided into three units (formations), in ascending stratigraphical order: the Xiahedong, Haoling and Shangdonggou formations"; their sauropod comes from the Haoling, "close to the Santun-Liudian area".

**Ruled out.**
- *Recording the Mangchuan.* It is the superseded name of a unit now divided in three.

**Open.** A section or paper placing the *Zhongyuansaurus* quarry within the Xiahedong, Haoling or Shangdonggou.
