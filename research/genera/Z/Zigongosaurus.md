# *Zigongosaurus*

## Is the type locality "Wujiaba Dam"?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** Wujiaba. "Dam" is not part of the name. Governs `location.locality` on [*Zigongosaurus fuxiensis*](../../../genera/Z/Zigongosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1977a, p. 307: "自贡伍家坝恐龙动物群" ("the Wujiaba dinosaur fauna of Zigong", translated). The name is written 伍家坝, with 坝 (ba) as its last syllable.
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 14: "a systematic excavation in the region of Wujiaba Dam, Zigong Co."

**Ruled out.**
- *Wujiaba Dam.* The form appears in the English translation of Dong and colleagues (1983), on pp. 14 and 29. In Sichuan place names 坝 is a stretch of flat ground, as in Shapingba, so "Dam" renders the last syllable of Wujiaba a second time. That reading of 坝 is ours, and the Chinese text of the 1983 paper was not checked; that its translator added the word is an inference.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is *Zigongosaurus* from the Upper or the Lower Shaximiao?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** The Upper Shaximiao, at the base. Governs `location.formation: Shaximiao` and `location.part: upper` on [*Zigongosaurus fuxiensis*](../../../genera/Z/Zigongosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 14: the Wujiaba quarry "is confirmed to lie at the base of the Upper Shaximiao Fm. approximately 15 m above a conchostracan-bearing shale".
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 29: material from the Wujiaba excavation "subsequently erected as the new genus and species Zigongosaurus fuxiensis".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lower Shaximiao, Bathonian–Callovian.* McIntosh (1990) gives "Xiashaximiao Formation" in the row for *Omeisaurus junghsiensis*, with *Zigongosaurus fuxiensis* listed as its synonym (mcintosh1990a, Table 16.1). Upchurch and colleagues (2004) give "Lower Shaximiao Formation" for "M. fuxiensis (Hou, Chao, et Chu, 1976)" (upchurch2004a, Table 13.1). Both are table rows that do not discuss the horizon, and McIntosh's row dates another species under which *fuxiensis* is subsumed. Dong and colleagues place the quarry itself at the base of the Upper Shaximiao.

**Open.** Nothing.
