# *Zuoyunlong*

## Is the *Zuoyunlong* holotype from "2011 Locality 7"?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** No. That designation is the site of the *Yunganglong* holotype. The *Zuoyunlong* paper locates its holotype only "in the vicinity of Zuoyun County". Governs `location.locality`, which is unset, on [*Zuoyunlong huangi*](../../../genera/Z/Zuoyunlong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wang2013a, Introduction: "a partial associated hadrosauroid skeleton from 2011 Locality 7 (SXMG V 00001, field number ZY007) is described".
- wang2017c, Type locality and horizon: "In the vicinity of Zuoyun County, Datong City, Shanxi Province, People's Republic of China".
- wang2017c, Holotype: the ilium "(field number ZY004-001)" and the ischium "(field number ZY004-002)".

**Ruled out.**
- *2011 Locality 7.* Wang and colleagues (2017) report *Zuoyunlong* "from the same locality (Zuoyun County, Shanxi Province, northern China) and horizon (Zhumapu Formation) as *Yunganglong*" (Introduction), but the parenthesis defines that locality as the county. The holotype's field numbers begin ZY004, where *Yunganglong*'s begin ZY007, and Wang and colleagues (2013) report that in 2011 and 2012 "a number of dinosaur localities were discovered and excavated in Zuoyun County". That ZY004 marks a different numbered site is an inference from the numbering; neither paper says so.

**Open.** Which numbered site yielded the holotype. A field record, or a later paper naming the quarry, would settle it.
