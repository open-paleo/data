# *Zephyrosaurus*

## Is *Zephyrosaurus schaffi* Albian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** No; it is recorded as Aptian, provisionally. D'Emic and colleagues (2019) date the Little Sheep Mudstone Member to about 124–109 Ma, and Zanno and colleagues (2023) put *Zephyrosaurus* in the middle of that member at a mean age near 116.5 Ma, which is Aptian. The reasoning on Cloverly member ages, and the doubt over the source of that horizon, are in [Cloverly](../../stratigraphy/c/cloverly.md). Governs `stages: [Aptian]` on [*Zephyrosaurus schaffi*](../../../genera/Z/Zephyrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- d'emic2019a, p. 3: the "Little Sheep Mudstone Member was deposited ca. 124–109 Ma".
- zanno2023a, Fig. 21: *Zephyrosaurus* "(middle of the Little Sheep Member, Cloverly Formation [122], mean age ~116.5 Myr [109])".

**Ruled out.**
- *Albian.* The record once held it. It follows the older member ages in which the Little Sheep was wholly Albian, as at the *Aquilops* horizon, "between 104 and 109 million years old" (farke2014a, p. 1). Weishampel and colleagues (2004) already gave "late Aptian" (weishampel2004a, Table 18.1).
- *Aptian or Albian.* Jones (2026) gives "Cloverly Fm., Montana USA, Early Cretaceous, Aptian/Albian" (jones2026a, Thescelosauridae table), a formation-wide range.

**Open.** The holotype's member. Zanno and colleagues cite Oreska and colleagues (2013) for the horizon, and that paper does not discuss the holotype (see [Cloverly](../../stratigraphy/c/cloverly.md)). Sues (1980) would settle it.
