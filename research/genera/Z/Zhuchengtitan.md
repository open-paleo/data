# *Zhuchengtitan*

## Is the *Zhuchengtitan* holotype from the Hongtuya or the Xingezhuang Formation?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** Recorded as the lower Hongtuya Formation. The describing paper places the horizon across the Hongtuya–Xingezhuang contact, which the record cannot hold. Governs `location.formation: Hongtuya` and `location.part: lower` on [*Zhuchengtitan zangjiazhuangensis*](../../../genera/Z/Zhuchengtitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mo2017a, p. 1502: "上白垩统王氏群中上部（红土崖组底部和辛格庄组上部）" ("the middle and upper part of the Upper Cretaceous Wangshi Group (the base of the Hongtuya Formation and the upper part of the Xingezhuang Formation)", translated).
- an2015a, p. 81: "Dinosaur bones are mostly buried in the lower Hongtuya Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper Xingezhuang Formation.* The describing paper does not exclude it. It is not adopted because An and colleagues (2015) place the Zhucheng dinosaur bones mostly in the lower Hongtuya; that statement concerns the bone beds generally, not this humerus, so applying it here is an inference.

**Open.** Whether the humerus lies above or below the contact. A measured section through the 2008 Zangjiazhuang excavation pit that places ZJZ-57 would settle it.
