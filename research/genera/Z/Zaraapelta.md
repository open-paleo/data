# *Zaraapelta*

## Is the *Zaraapelta* holotype from a numbered Hermiin Tsav locality, or from the Lower White Beds?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** Hermiin Tsav, with no numeral, and not the Lower White Beds. The describing paper gives the holotype as from Hermiin Tsav, with no numeral and no named bed, in the Baruungoyot Formation. Governs `location.locality: Hermiin Tsav` and `location.formation: Baruungoyot` on [*Zaraapelta nomadis*](../../../genera/Z/Zaraapelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- arbour2014b, p. 642, Holotype locality and horizon: "43°28.345′N, 99°51.032′E (WGS 84), Hermiin Tsav, Gobi Desert, Mongolia; Baruungoyot Formation".
- gradziński1972a, p. 27: the Khermeen Tsav valley "built of three lithologic complexes: the Lower White Beds, which form the base of the exposed sequence", then "the Red Beds, forming the middle part of the slopes".
- gradziński1977a, p. 297: the deposition of the Barun Goyot Formation "was probably time-equivalent with sediments forming the red beds of the Khermeen Tsav I and II localities".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lower White Beds.* No source read ties the holotype to this complex. Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz (1972) make the Lower White Beds the base of the Khermeen Tsav sequence, below the Red Beds, and Gradziński and colleagues (1977) correlate the red beds, not the white beds, with the Barun Goyot Formation. A Lower White Beds horizon would therefore contradict the formation the describing paper gives.

**Open.** Whether the holotype is from Hermiin Tsav I or II. Gradziński and colleagues (1977) recognize those two localities "only within the area of occurrence of the red beds in Khermeen Tsav region, while the dinosaur bearing white beds of the same region are referred to as upper white beds of Khermeen Tsav" (gradziński1977a, p. 297, footnote), so a numbered locality would fit the holotype's formation. The describing paper gives no numeral, and Arbour and colleagues (2014) use the numbered forms only for referred specimens of other taxa (arbour2014b, p. 638).
