# *Zizhongosaurus*

## Which formation is the Zizhongosaurus holotype from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Probably the [Xintiangou Formation](../../../stratigraphy/x/xintiangou.yml), queried as the describing paper queries it; not the [Ziliujing](../../../stratigraphy/z/ziliujing.yml). Governs `location.formation` (Xintiangou, with a query note) and `location.locality: Luochuanjing` on *Zizhongosaurus chuanchengensis*.

**Evidence.**
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 18: "From a purple-red mudstone (Xintiangou Fm.?) approximately 15-20 m above the Daanzhai Limestone Mem. at Luochuanjing, Zizhong Co."
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 4: "The Ziliujing Fm. currently encompasses the Dongyuemiao, Maanshan, and Daanzhai members"; p. 6: the Xintiangou rests on "the underlying Daanzhai Mem. of the Ziliujing Fm." and has "purple-red mudstones".

**Ruled out.**
- *Ziliujing Formation, Aalenian.* Jones (2026) gives "Ziliujing Fm., China, Middle Jurassic, Aalenian" in a specimen table (jones2026b, p. 375); Hunt and colleagues (1994) give "Ziliujing Fm." with "?Sin/Toa" in a table (hunt1995a, p. 277, Table I). Neither discusses the horizon. The describing paper places the bones 15–20 m above the top member of the Ziliujing.

**Open.** The age. Dong and colleagues assign the Xintiangou to the Middle Jurassic (p. 6) but read the fossil's "primitive characters" as implying "an Early Jurassic chronology" for "the purple sediments overlying the Daanzhai Limestone" (p. 19). The fauna does not decide between the units: "Cetiosaurinae indet." is listed from both the Xintiangou (p. 6) and the Maanshan Member of the Ziliujing (p. 5). McPhee and colleagues (2016) place the specimen "from the Daanzhai Member of the Ziliujing Formation", "potentially" early Middle Jurassic, which was not re-examined for this note.

## Is the *Zizhongosaurus* type one number, IVPP V9067, or three?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** Three: IVPP V9067.1, V9067.2 and V9067.3, one for each of the dorsal spine, the humerus and the pubis. They are the same material that the reference works list as V9067. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IVPP V9067.1, IVPP V9067.2, IVPP V9067.3]` on [*Zizhongosaurus chuanchengensis*](../../../genera/Z/Zizhongosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 18: "Specimens: A complete dorsal neural spine, a right humerus, a pubis, and several other fragments (V9067.1-3)".
- dong1983a, English translation, *Zizhongosaurus chuanchengensis*, Description: "V9067.1 is a relatively well preserved long piece of dorsal spine"; "V9067.2 is a partial shaft of a right humerus with its proximal end from the same individual"; "V9067.3 is an incomplete piece of pubis".

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP V9067 alone.* Jones (2026) gives "IVPP V9067—fragments" (jones2026b, Fossil Specimens table), and Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "IVPP V9067" (molina-pérez2020a, specimen table). Both use the umbrella number for the same bones. It is not a rival specimen.

**Open.** Nothing.
