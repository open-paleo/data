# *Zby*

## Does Mateus and colleagues' (2017) "Porto Novo Member" change the *Zby* member?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** No; the record keeps the combined Praia da Amoreira–Porto Novo Member with `part: upper`. Mateus and colleagues (2017) describe Hill's two members and place the *Zby* site at Vale das Pombas in the upper one, the Porto Novo, but in the same section they map the two together, so their "top of the Porto Novo Mb." is the upper part of the combined unit the record already gives. Supersedes the ruling-out of Hill's Porto Novo Member in the entry below as far as it says the first author never uses that member: he does, in this paper. And "Porto Novo" alone is not a name for the combined unit: every source read uses it for Hill's upper member or for the place, so it is not a variant of the combined member. Governs `location.member` and `location.part: upper` on [*Zby atlanticus*](../../../genera/Z/Zby.yml), and the dropped "Porto Novo" variant on [Praia da Amoreira-Porto Novo](../../../stratigraphy/p/praia-da-amoreira-porto-novo.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mateus2017a, Fig. 6 caption: "Bay of Vale das Pombas (north of Paimogo) – upper Kimmeridgian outcrop of the Porto Novo Member of the Lourinhã Fm. (Fieldtrip Stop 2), with the dinosaur fossil locations: A, *Lourinhanosaurus* eggs and nest; B, Turiasaur sauropod *Zby atlanticus*."
- mateus2017a: "Two members are well exposed: the older Praia da Amoreira and Porto Novo members"; "The dinosaur sites are located at the top of the Porto Novo Mb."
- mateus2017a, Praia da Amoreira and Porto Novo members: "The Porto Novo and the Praia da Amoreira Members (Hill, 1989) were mapped together by Manuppella et al. (1999) and we follow this approach in this work."

**Ruled out.**
- *Hill's Porto Novo Member as a separate entry for the Zby site.* The paper placing *Zby* there maps it with the Praia da Amoreira as one unit.
- *"Porto Novo" as a variant of the combined member.* No source read uses it so.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Zby* holotype from the Porto Novo Member or the combined Praia da Amoreira–Porto Novo Member?

*2026-09-20*

**Conclusion.** The combined member. Mateus and colleagues (2014) name the Amoreira–Porto Novo Member twice and the Porto Novo alone never, so a bare "Porto Novo" for this specimen shortens the combined name rather than naming Hill's (1989) separate upper member (see [Lusitanian Basin members](../../topics/lusitanian-basin-members.md) and the rule on different spellings in [methods](../../methods.md)). Their "top of" is the record's `part`. Governs `location.member: Praia da Amoreira-Porto Novo` and `location.part: upper` on [*Zby atlanticus*](../../../genera/Z/Zby.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mateus2014a, p. 618: "the late Kimmeridgian Amoreira–Porto Novo Member of the Lourinhã Formation at Vale Pombas, Lourinhã"; p. 619, Locality and Horizon: "top of the Amoreira–Porto Novo Member of the Lourinhã Formation, late Kimmeridgian".
- mateus2017a, p. 82: "The Porto Novo and the Praia da Amoreira Members (Hill, 1989) were mapped together by Manuppella et al. (1999) and we follow this approach in this work".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hill's Porto Novo Member.* The describing paper does not use it, and its first author maps Hill's two members as one.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the Amoreira–Porto Novo the *Zby* holotype's formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** No. It is a member of the Lourinhã Formation, and the describing paper gives both ranks in one horizon line. Governs `location.formation: Lourinhã` and `location.member: Praia da Amoreira-Porto Novo` on [*Zby atlanticus*](../../../genera/Z/Zby.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mateus2014a, p. 619, Locality and Horizon: "top of the Amoreira–Porto Novo Member of the Lourinhã Formation, late Kimmeridgian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Praia da Amoreira-Porto Novo as the formation.* The record once held the member in the formation field. Jones (2026) gives "Lourinhã Fm., Portugal, Late Jurassic, Kimmeridgian" (jones2026b, Eusauropoda table), which agrees with the describing paper. The member's name and scope are set out in [Lusitanian Basin members](../../topics/lusitanian-basin-members.md).

**Open.** Nothing.
