# *Zapsalis*

## Is the *Zapsalis* holotype AMNH 3953 or AMNH 3853?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** AMNH FARB 3953, the number in the caption that Currie and Evans (2019) give for the holotype tooth. Their running text cites the same specimen twice as 3853, which is not followed. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Zapsalis abradens*](../../../genera/Z/Zapsalis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- currie2019a, p. 708, Fig. 21: "Holotype of Z. abradens (AMNH FARB 3953), second left premaxillary tooth".
- currie2019a, p. 711: "the holotype of Zapsalis (AMNH 3853, Fig. 21)"; "the holotype of Zapsalis (AMNH FARB 3853)".

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 3853.* Both text citations refer to the tooth figured in Fig. 21, so the two numbers name one specimen and one of them is a misprint. The paper does not say which. The caption is followed because it is the only place the paper labels the specimen itself; the text's number is a cross-reference to that figure.

**Open.** Which printing is the misprint. The AMNH catalogue entry for the specimen would settle it.
