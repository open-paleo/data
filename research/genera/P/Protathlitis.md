# *Protathlitis*

## What are the Protathlitis holotype numbers, and where is it held?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Six numbers in the ANA series, which are field numbers, not an institution code; the describing paper names no repository. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` and `institution: UNKNOWN` on *Protathlitis cinctorrensis*.

**Evidence.**
- santos-cubedo2023a, p. 3: "Holotype: fragment of a right maxilla (8ANA-109) … five caudal vertebrae (3ANA83, 4ANA43, 4ANA69, 4ANA76, 5ANA78)"; the Ana site "remained unexcavated until 2002".

**Ruled out.**
- *ANA as an institution.* The prefix names the Ana site; the leading digit is, in our reading, the excavation campaign. The paper does not explain the numbers.

**Open.** The repository, and the campaign years.
