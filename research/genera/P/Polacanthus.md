# *Polacanthus*

## Is the Polacanthus holotype from the Barnes High Sandstone Member?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** No. Barnes High is the cliff the holotype came from, in the Wessex Formation; the Barnes High Sandstone Member that shares the name is part of the overlying Vectis Formation. Governs `location.locality: Barnes High`, `location.formation: Wessex` and the absence of `location.member` on [*Polacanthus foxii*](../../../genera/P/Polacanthus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- raven2020a, p. 12: "Type Locality—Barnes High, Isle of Wight, U.K. Wessex Formation (Barremian; Austen and Batten, 2018)."
- galton2009a, p. 248, Fig. 16 caption: "P. foxii, holotype NHM R175 from Wessex Formation of Barnes High, Isle of Wight".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barnes High Sandstone Member.* Bonsor and colleagues (2023) describe "the Wessex Formation … and the overlying Vectis Formation" (bonsor2023a, p. 3) and put the member in the Vectis: "The Vectis Formation comprises the Cowleaze Chine Member and the younger Shepherd's Chine Member … intercalated by a 2–6 m thick arenaceous unit, the Barnes High Sandstone Member" (bonsor2023a, pp. 3–4). Every source read places the holotype in the Wessex Formation below it.

**Open.** The holotype's level within the Wessex Formation. Austen and Batten (2018), whom Raven and colleagues cite for the age of the exposure, have not been read for it.
