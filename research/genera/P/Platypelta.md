# *Platypelta*

## What do "125 ft" and Sternberg's locality 84 say about where the Platypelta holotype was found?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** Sand Creek, in the lowermost Dinosaur Park Formation about 5–6 m above the contact with the Oldman. The "125 ft" in the 1914 collection list is a height above the Red Deer River, not a level in the section, and the quarry itself is not identified. Governs `location.locality: Sand Creek`, `location.formation: Dinosaur Park` and `location.part: lower` on [*Platypelta coombsi*](../../../genera/P/Platypelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- penkalski2018a, p. 267: "Type locality: Sand Creek, Red Deer River, Alberta, about 8 mi SSE of Steveville, Alberta"; "Horizon: Lowermost Dinosaur Park Formation".
- penkalski2018a, pp. 262–263: "Coombs (1971: 55) wrote that AMNH 5337, collected in 1914 by Barnum Brown and Peter Kaisen, is from Sternberg (1950) locality 84, but that quarry is the source of AMNH 5338, a Corythosaurus specimen"; the altitude is "667 m … or 5-6 m above the DPF/OF contact based on Eberth (2005)"; "Judging by the 1914 AMNH collections list, AMNH 5337 and 5338 were found in proximity (possibly in the same quarry?), with both listed as being "125 ft. above the river"".

**Ruled out.**
- *"125 ft" as a stratigraphic height.* Penkalski gives it as the collectors' height "above the river" and compares it with the 667 m altitude.

**Open.** Whether the holotype shares Sternberg's locality 84 with the *Corythosaurus* AMNH 5338. Penkalski leaves it as "possibly in the same quarry?", and Coombs (1971) has not been read.
