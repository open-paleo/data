# *Pachycephalosaurus*

## Is the USNM 12031 type from the Lance of Wyoming or the Hell Creek near Ekalaka?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Lance Formation at Buck Creek, Niobrara County, Wyoming. Brown and Schlaikjer (1943) give that horizon and place for USNM 12031, the type of the species. The Ekalaka, Montana, data belong to AMNH 1696, the type of *P. grangeri*, which the same paper erects and which is now a junior synonym. Governs `location.formation: Lance`, `location.locality`, `location.region: US-WY` and the absence of `location.coordinates` on [*Pachycephalosaurus wyomingensis*](../../../genera/P/Pachycephalosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown1943a, p. 123: "U.S.N.M. No. 12031, "Troodon" wyomingensis, referred to the new genus herein described, type of this species. … Lance formation, 9 miles southwest of Warren P.O., Buck Creek, Niobrara County, Wyoming."
- brown1943a, p. 142: "TYPE: U.S.N.M. No. 12031"; "HORIZON AND LOCALITY: Lance formation. Nine miles southwest of Warren P.O., Buck Creek, Niobrara County, Wyoming."
- brown1943a, p. 123: "A.M.N.H. No. 1696 … Hell Creek Beds of the Lance formation, sec. 17, T. 1 S., R. 55 E., William Winkley Ranch, Powder Hill, north of Ekalaka, Carter County, Montana."

**Ruled out.**
- *Hell Creek Formation, Ekalaka, Montana.* The record's earlier values, with coordinates near Ekalaka. They are the provenance of AMNH 1696, not of the type.
- *The species' range as the type's formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Lance Fm., Hell Creek Fm., USA" for a row covering "USNM 12031—cranial dome; numerous specimens" (jones2026a, Pachycephalosauria systematics table), and The Dinosauria lists Lance, Hell Creek and Judith River occurrences for the species with its synonyms (weishampel2004a, p. 465, Table 21.1). Both describe the hypodigm, not the type.

**Open.** A coordinate. Brown and Schlaikjer give a distance and direction from Warren post office, not a point.
