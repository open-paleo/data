# *Patagonykus*

## Was Patagonykus puertai named in 1996 or 1997?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** 1996, in Novas's Gondwanan Dinosaur Symposium paper in the Memoirs of the Queensland Museum (dated 20 December 1996). Novas's 1997 paper in the Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology is the full description and credits the name to 1996. Governs `erected_in: novas1996a` and `described_in: novas1997b` on *Patagonykus puertai*.

**Evidence.**
- novas1996a, p. 675: "Novas, F.E. 1996 12 20: Alvarezsauridae… Memoirs of the Queensland Museum 39(3): 675-702"; p. 676: "Patagonykus puertai gen. et sp. nov.", "HOLOTYPE PVPH 37, two incomplete dorsal vertabrae"; p. 677: "DIAGNOSIS. Patagonykus puertai is an alvarezsaurid avialian theropod diagnosed by the following"; p. 676 refers the anatomy to a paper in press: "A detailed anatomical description… are given elsewhere (Novas, in press a)".
- novas1997b, p. 141: "PATAGONYKUS Novas, 1996" and "PATAGONYKUS PUERTAI Novas, 1996"; p. 164: "1996a. Alvarezsauridae… Memoirs of the Queensland Museum, 39."

**Ruled out.**
- *Novas (1996), "Dinosaur monophyly".* A different paper of the same year (*Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology* 16: 723–741; novas1997b, p. 164, "1996b").

**Open.** Nothing.
