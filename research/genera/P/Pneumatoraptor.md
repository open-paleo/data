# *Pneumatoraptor*

## Does the published Iharkút coordinate give the position of the Pneumatoraptor holotype?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** No. The one coordinate printed for Iharkút is for the mine as a whole, an open pit with several bone-bearing sites, and the describing paper does not say which site yielded the scapulocoracoid. The record carries no coordinate. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` and `location.locality: Iharkút, Bakony Mountains` on [*Pneumatoraptor fodori*](../../../genera/P/Pneumatoraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ősi2010b, p. 308: "Holotype: MTM V.2008.38.1., nearly complete, left scapulacoracoid"; "Locality: Iharkút, Bakony Mountains, western Hungary."
- csiki-sava2015a, p. 29: Iharkút "is an abandoned and recultivated bauxite open-pit mine … (47°13'52"N, 17°39'01"E; Figs 1, 3, 4). The first fossils were found in the Sz-1 site, at the northen part of the open-pit, and additional bone-yielding horizons were later discovered … among which the Sz-6 site at the southeastern end of the quarry is the most extensive (ca. 5000 m2) and productive."

**Ruled out.**
- *47°13′52″ N, 17°39′01″ E.* Printed for the mine, not for this specimen's site.

**Open.** Which site the holotype came from. Ősi and colleagues (2010) say only that "All the bones and teeth … were recovered from a sedimentary breccia layer that represents the richest bone-yielding horizon within the Santonian" (p. 305); that this is the Sz-6 site, which Csiki-Sava and colleagues (2015) call the most productive, is our inference.
