# *Protoceratops*

## Is *Protoceratops hellenikorhinus* from the Bayan Mandahu or the Djadokhta Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Bayan Mandahu Formation. The describing paper gives it as the type stratum and offers the Djadokhta only as a queried correlation. Governs `location.formation: Bayan Mandahu` on [*Protoceratops hellenikorhinus*](../../../genera/P/Protoceratops.yml). *P. andrewsi* stays in the Djadokhta; the two species come from different units.

**Evidence.**
- lambert2001a, p. 8: "*Stratum typicum:* Bayan Mandahu Formation, ? correlated with the Djadokhta Formation in People's Republic of Mongolia, Campanian, Upper Cretaceous."
- longrich2010a, p. 955: "none of the material found at Bayan Mandahu can be definitively referred to species known from Bayn Dzak or any of the other Djadokhta Formation localities"; *P. hellenikorhinus* is one of the Bayan Mandahu taxa he lists.

**Ruled out.**
- *Djadokhta Formation.* The Dinosauria gives "Djadokhta Formation (Nei Mongol Zizhiqu) People's Republic of China" (weishampel2004a, p. 480, Table 22.1) and "the Upper Cretaceous Djadokhta Formation of Inner Mongolia" (p. 490). It turns the describing paper's queried correlation into an assignment.

**Open.** Whether "Bayan Mandahu Formation" is a formal unit or an informal name for an outcrop of the Ulansuhai; see [Ulansuhai](../../stratigraphy/u/ulansuhai.md).
