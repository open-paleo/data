# *Paranthodon*

## Where exactly was the Paranthodon holotype found?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** At Bushmans River near Woodbury Farm, the site Atherstone called Iguanodonhoek. Its modern position is a reconstruction: McLachlan and McMillan (1976) put it "probably" near the boundary of the farms Bushmans River and Hillsneck. Governs `location.locality` on [*Paranthodon africanus*](../../../genera/P/Paranthodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1981a, p. 301: "The locality of Bushmans River near Woodbury Farm was named Iguanodonhoek"; McLachlan and McMillan "conclude that the site was probably in one of the eroded gullies near the common boundary of the modern farms at Bushmans River and Hillsneck, about 1.6 km south of Dassies Klip".

**Ruled out.**
- *Styl-Krantz.* Owen gave this locality for BMNH 47337a, not for the holotype BMNH 47338, which he gave as "from a marine (liassic ?) formation at Bushmans River" (galton1981a, p. 300).

**Open.** A position for the site. McLachlan and McMillan (1976), who map it on the 1:50 000 sheet 3326 CA Springmount, have not been read.

## Which specimen is the Paranthodon africanus holotype, BMNH 47337 or 47338?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** BMNH 47338, the Bushmans River snout. BMNH 47337 is the holotype of the Permian pareiasaur *Anthodon serrarius*, the taxon the jaw was first filed under. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [BMNH 47338]` on [*Paranthodon africanus*](../../../genera/P/Paranthodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1981a, p. 300: Broom "transferred the Bushmans River jaw fragment (BMNH 47338) from the Permian pareiasaur taxon *Anthodon serrarius* (holotype, BMNH 47337) to the armored dinosaur *Palaeoscincus* as the type of a new species".
- galton1981a, p. 303, Fig. 1 caption: "*Paranthodon africanus* (BROOM), holotype BMNH 47338".
- jones2026a, p. 402: "**BMNH 47338**—partial skull", which agrees.

**Ruled out.**
- *BMNH 47337.* The record held this number until 2026-08-01. Galton (1981) gives it as the holotype of *Anthodon serrarius*, a different animal; the record's material field already described the 47338 snout.

**Open.** Nothing.
