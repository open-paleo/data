# *Puertasaurus*

## Is the holotype from the Pari Aike, the Cerro Fortaleza or the Mata Amarilla Formation?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** The Cerro Fortaleza Formation. The describing paper's "Pari Aike Formation" at Cerro Los Hornos is the unit later work calls the Cerro Fortaleza. Governs `location.formation: Cerro Fortaleza` on [*Puertasaurus reuili*](../../../genera/P/Puertasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas2005b, p. 37: "Locality and Horizon. Cerro Los Hornos, La Leona, Santa Cruz Province, Argentina. Pari Aike Formation, early Maastrichtian".
- egerton2013a, p. 268: "A distinct dinosaurian fauna has been described from the "Pari Aike Formation," which we interpret as the Cerro Fortaleza Formation: *Talenkauen santacrucensis* (Novas et al., 2004), *Puertasaurus reuili* (Novas et al., 2005)".
- novas2019a, p. 274: "The Cerro Fortaleza beds, at Cerro de Los Hornos fossil site, shed different dinosaur taxa (i.e., *Talenkauen, Orkoraptor, Puertasaurus, Dreadnoughtus*".
- sickmann2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 5, Nomenclature: "terrestrial deposits north of Lago Viedma are referred to with the informal delineation "undifferentiated Pari Aike Formation;" and (5) terrestrial deposits between Lago Viedma and Lago Argentino and east past Tres Lagos are referred to as "Cerro Fortaleza Formation.""

**Ruled out.**
- *Pari Aike Formation.* In Sickmann and colleagues' usage the name covers terrestrial beds north of Lago Viedma. Cerro Los Hornos is on the southern coast of the lake (egerton2013a, p. 269), inside their Cerro Fortaleza.
- *Mata Amarilla Formation, Cenomanian.* Jones (2026) gives "Mata Amarilla Fm., Argentina, Late Cretaceous, Cenomanian" (jones2026b, Lithostrotia systematics table). Sickmann and colleagues say their Cerro Fortaleza "includes rocks that have sometimes been named "Mata Amarilla Formation"" and assigned a Cenomanian age (sickmann2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 5), and none of the papers on this specimen uses the name.
- *The Talenkauen position as this holotype's.* Egerton and colleagues print "Lat. 49° 51′ 16.2″ S, Long. 72° 06′ 26.3″ W" for the *Talenkauen* holotype on Cerro Los Hornos (egerton2013a, p. 269). It is another specimen's site.

**Open.** Nothing.
