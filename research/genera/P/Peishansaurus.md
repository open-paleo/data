# *Peishansaurus*

## Where does the *Peishansaurus* jaw belong?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** No closer than Genasauria. The describer placed it tentatively among nodosaurids, but later reviewers doubt that it is ankylosaurian, the latest assessment lists it only as a questionable thyreophoran, and it has also been listed as a possible pachycephalosaur. Genasauria covers both readings. Governs `parent: Genasauria` with `placement: incertae sedis`, and the dispute on [*Peishansaurus philemys*](../../../genera/P/Peishansaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bohlin1953a, p. 67: "FAMILY NODOSAURIDAE GENUS PEISHANSAURUS NOV. GEN."; the type is "A fragment comprising the anterior end of the right lower jaw", and a tooth "shows some resemblance to Ankylosaurus".
- maryańska1977a, p. 90: "nomen dubium ?ankylosaur", "Inst. Vert. Paleont., Peking, no catalogue number".
- vickaryous2004a, p. 368, Table 17.1, Nomina dubia: "Peishansaurus philemys Bohlin, 1953 (doubtfully ankylosaurian)".
- arbour2015a, p. 43: "It is impossible to determine if this material is ankylosaurian from the figures. Status. Nomen dubium; ?Thyreophora indet."
- carpenter2001d, p. 458, Table 21.2, taxa "not accepted as valid because the material does not allow the taxon to be diagnosed": "Peishansaurus philemys Bohlin, 1953 | Jaw fragment (pachycephalosaur)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Thyreophora as the placement every reading accepts.* The latest assessment marks the assignment as questionable, and the pachycephalosaur reading falls outside Thyreophora.
- *The holotype as established to be lost.* None of the sources read says so; Maryańska (1977) gives it as uncatalogued in Beijing, and Coombs (1978) examined a cast, AMNH 2071.

**Open.** Where the holotype is now held.

## Which unit and age is the Peishansaurus holotype from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The [Xinminpu (Xinminbao) Group](../../../stratigraphy/x/xinminpu.yml), with no formation assigned; Barremian–Albian. Governs `location.group`, the empty `location.formation` and `stage: [Barremian, Aptian, Albian]` on *Peishansaurus philemys*.

**Evidence.**
- bohlin1953a, p. 67: "Locality: Ehr-chia-wu-tung in the Peishan north of Yü-men-hsien, Kansu".
- dong2002a, p. 283: "上述3种甲龙化石均产自河西走廊地区早白垩世新民堡群" ("all three of the above ankylosaurs come from the Early Cretaceous Xinminpu Group of the Hexi Corridor", translated). No formation or stage.
- tang2001b, p. 115: the "lower part of the Xinminbao Group are considered to be Late Barremian–Aptian in age, although the middle and upper parts could be as young as Albian". This is about the Mazongshan area and does not name *Peishansaurus*; applying it here is an inference.

**Ruled out.**
- *Minhe Formation.* Weishampel and colleagues (2004) list it under "Minhe Formation" (weishampel2004b, p. 598), a unit typified in Minhe County, Qinghai (zhang2009b), far from the Beishan. No source read for this note places the Ehr-chia-wu-tung beds in it.
- *Aptian–Albian only.* Tang and colleagues' range for the lower part of the group reaches the late Barremian, so an Aptian floor is narrower than the only dated range applied here.

**Open.** A formation within the Xinminpu Group, and a stage narrower than the group's range.
