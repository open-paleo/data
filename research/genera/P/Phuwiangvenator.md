# *Phuwiangvenator*

## Is the *Phuwiangvenator* holotype Barremian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** No. It takes the Valanginian–Hauterivian of the Sao Khua Formation that Tucker and colleagues (2022) give (see [Sao Khua](../../stratigraphy/s/sao-khua.md)), since no source dates Phu Wiang Site 9B on its own. Governs `stage: [Valanginian, Hauterivian]` on [*Phuwiangvenator yaemniyomi*](../../../genera/P/Phuwiangvenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tucker2022a, p. 15: "a tightly constrained late Valanginian–early Hauterivian age of 133.6–132.1 Ma for Sao Khua Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Barremian*, the record's earlier value. The describing paper gives "Type horizon: Sao Khua Formation, probably upper Barremian, Lower Cretaceous" (samathi2019a, p. 242), which follows the bivalve correlation of Tumpeesuwan and colleagues (2010) that the Sao Khua note sets aside. Jones (2026) gives "Sao Khua Fm., Thailand, Early Cretaceous, Valanginian/Hauterivian" (jones2026c, Carcharodontosauria systematics table), which agrees with this record.

**Open.** As for the formation: the Valanginian–Hauterivian rests on maximum depositional ages read as near-depositional, and the samples are not from Phu Wiang.
