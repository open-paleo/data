# *Probrachylophosaurus*

## Is the holotype in the Judith River Formation, or in the Oldman or Foremost?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** The Judith River Formation. The holotype comes from Montana exposures that its describing paper calls Judith River Formation, and the Alberta units appear only as correlations. Governs `location.formation: Judith River` on [*Probrachylophosaurus bergei*](../../../genera/P/Probrachylophosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- freedmanfowler2015a, p. 11, Systematic Paleontology: MOR 2919 comes from "just east of the mouth of Kennedy Coulee along the Milk River near the USA-Canada border, in exposures of the Judith River Formation. The site, MOR locality JR-518 ("Superduck"), is within a grey mudstone stratigraphically equivalent to Unit 1 of the Oldman Formation of Alberta. The bone horizon is 17.5 m above the top of the Marker A Coal of the Taber Coal Zone of the Foremost Formation".
- freedmanfowler2015a, p. 3: "the Montanan Judith River Formation is not subdivided into the formal units of the Belly River Group, the depositional sequences that created the Foremost, Oldman, and Dinosaur Park Formations can be identified in Montana outcrop".

**Ruled out.**
- *Oldman Formation.* Freedman Fowler and Horner place the mudstone at the level of the Oldman's Unit 1 by correlation; they do not extend the Alberta formation into Montana.
- *Foremost Formation.* The Marker A Coal of the Foremost's Taber Coal Zone is the datum the 17.5 m height is measured from, so the holotype lies above it.

**Open.** Nothing.
