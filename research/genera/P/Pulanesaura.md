# *Pulanesaura*

## Where is the holotype's coordinate printed?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** In the paper that names *Arcusaurus*, which prints a position for Sauropod Quarry; the *Pulanesaura* papers print none. The two holotypes come from one quarry. Governs `location.coordinates` (-28.4667, 27.825383) on [*Pulanesaura eocollum*](../../../genera/P/Pulanesaura.yml), the same pair as [*Arcusaurus*](../../../genera/A/Arcusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yates2011a, p. 611: "Horizon and Locality—Sauropod Quarry, Spion Kop 932, (S28°28.002′ E027°49.523′), Senekal District, Free State, South Africa"; the *Arcusaurus* bones "are much smaller than the basal sauropod that is found in the same quarry".
- mcphee2015a, p. 2, Type locality and horizon: "The much smaller *Arcusaurus pereirabalorum* was recovered from the edge of the same quarry"; Fig. 1 labels "SQ, 'Sauropod [*Pulanesaura*] Quarry'".

**Ruled out.** None.
