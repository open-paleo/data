# *Pararhabdodon*

## Is the Pararhabdodon type material from Sant Romà d'Abella or from Tossal de la Doba?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** Sant Romà d'Abella, the 1985 quarry that yielded the type and paratype. Tossal de la Doba is a separate site in the same area. The quarry lies well away from the village whose name it carries, so the village's position is not the site's. Governs `location.locality: Sant Romà d'Abella` on [*Pararhabdodon isonensis*](../../../genera/P/Pararhabdodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- prieto-márquez2006b, p. 930: "Sant Romà d'Abella (SRA) is the original 1985 quarry where the type, paratype, and the 1994 axial and appendicular specimens of *P*. *isonensis* were collected".
- dallavecchia2014a, p. 305: "The name of this site can be misleading because it is relatively far from the village of Sant Romà d'Abella"; p. 306, Fig. 16.8 caption: "Pararhabdodon isonensis, type specimens from Sant Romà d'Abella site"; p. 306: "Tossal de la Doba This site yielded only the proximal part of a left femur".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tossal de la Doba.* Dalla Vecchia (2020) writes that the type material "come[s] from the Tossal de la Doba locality (reported as Sant Romà d'Abella in the literature)" (dallavecchia2020a, p. 599), citing Dalla Vecchia and colleagues (2014). That chapter lists the two as different sites and assigns the type specimens to Sant Romà d'Abella.

**Open.** What the 2020 reassignment rests on; the paper gives no reason beyond the citation.
