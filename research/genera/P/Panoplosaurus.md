# *Panoplosaurus*

## Is the Panoplosaurus holotype from the lower or the upper Dinosaur Park Formation?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The lower part: Quarry 008 lies about 22 m above the contact with the Oldman Formation. The coordinate is the conversion of the UTM position the 2026 redescription prints for the quarry. Governs `location.part: lower` and `location.coordinates: [50.737276, -111.511052]` on [*Panoplosaurus mirus*](../../../genera/P/Panoplosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- livius2026a, p. 22, Holotype Locality and Horizon: "Quarry 008, Dinosaur Provincial Park, Alberta, Canada (located at 12U 463937.645E, 5620734.144N, NAD 83). The quarry occurs in the lower Dinosaur Park Formation (Megaherbivore Assemblage Zone 1 …), approximately 22 m above the contact with the Oldman Formation." The position converts to 50.737276, −111.511052 (our calculation).

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper Dinosaur Park Formation.* Arbour and colleagues (2009) write "Edmontonia rugosidens … is found in the lower part of the Dinosaur Park Formation, Panoplosaurus mirus in the upper part … (Ryan and Evans, 2005)" (arbour2009a, p. 1132), in a paper on *Dyoplosaurus*. The source they cite places both confirmed *Panoplosaurus* specimens "within 25-30 m of the Oldman-Dinosaur Park disconformity" and both nodosaurids "in the lower 30 m of the formation". Livius and colleagues' "Pan. mirus higher up" (livius2026a, p. 22) compares the species' ranges with that of *E. rugosidens*, with "broad stratigraphic overlap … between approximately 20 m and 35 m above the contact"; it is not a statement about the holotype's level.
- *50.733894, −111.51165.* The stored pair before 2026-09-01, about 380 m from the printed position.

**Open.** Nothing.
