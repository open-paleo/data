# *Pukyongosaurus*

## What are the numbers of the Pukyongosaurus holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** PKNU-G.102 to PKNU-G.109, all eight listed. The type is several elements from one quarry, each with its own number. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` (PKNU-G.102 through PKNU-G.109) and `institution: PKNU` on [*Pukyongosaurus millenniumi*](../../../genera/P/Pukyongosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong2001a, p. 44: "Type A fragmentary skeleton, including 7 cervical vertebrae, one dorsal vertebra, one partial clavicle (?), one complete chevron, and isolated bones (Specimen numbers: PKNU-G.102" to 109 "from the same quarry".

**Ruled out.**
- *PKNUG-G.* Jones (2026) prints "PKNUG-G.102–109" (jones2026b, p. 381). The doubled G is not in Dong and colleagues (2001), who write PKNU-G throughout.
- *PKNU-G 102 alone.* The record held one number until 2026-07-30; the type line covers all eight.

**Open.** Nothing.
