# *Pycnonemosaurus*

## Is Fazenda Roncador in the Parecis Group or the Bauru Group?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** The Parecis Group, at group rank and with no formation, following Delcourt's (2017) redescription of this holotype. The reassignment rests on a regional scheme rather than on work at the site, and a later paper keeps the Bauru. Governs `location.group: Parecis` and the empty `location.formation` on [*Pycnonemosaurus nevesi*](../../../genera/P/Pycnonemosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kellner2002a, p. 164: the material was recovered "from the Fazenda Roncador, close to Paulo Creek, about 1km S/SW of the main building of this farm", in "a red conglomeratic sandstone, which is referred to the Bauru Group".
- delcourt2017a, p. 2: "according to Kellner & Campos (2002), this locality is in the Bauru Group. However, recent work has shown that this locality is actually part of the Parecis Group and that it is of Campanian-Masstrichitian age (Weska, 2006; Bittencourt & Langer, 2011)".
- weska2006a, p. 79: for the Cretaceous of Mato Grosso, "a new stratigraphic column is proposed composed of the Paredão Grande, Salto das Nuvens, Cachoeira do Bom Jardim and Utiariti formations, encompassed in the Parecis Group, suppressing the Bauru Group and the units Quilombinho, Cambambe, Marília, Vale do Rio do Peixe and the Tapirapé Sequence" (translated). Weska names neither the taxon nor Fazenda Roncador.
- bittencourt2011a, p. 33: the unit "has been traditionally referred to the Bauru Group", "but recent work suggests that all Cretaceous rocks deposited south of the Paraguai fold belt (Almeida 1984) in Mato Grosso should be included in the Parecis Group (Weska 2006)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Adamantina Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Adamantina Fm., Brazil" (jones2026c, Abelisauridae systematics table). Kellner and Campos name the Adamantina and Marília only for "the outcrops of the upper portion of this stratigraphic unit … in São Paulo", in a sentence about age (kellner2002a, p. 164).
- *Cambambe Formation.* No paper read places this holotype in it, and Weska, the source of the Parecis reassignment, suppresses the name (weska2006a, p. 79).

**Open.** Which group. Iori and colleagues (2021) cite both Kellner and Campos and Bittencourt and Langer and still write that *Pycnonemosaurus* is "probably correlated to the Bauru Group (Kellner and Campos, 2002; Sales et al., 2018)" (iori2021a, p. 2). Sales and colleagues (2018), not read, would show what that correlation rests on.
