# *Proa*

## Is *Proa* a hadrosauriform?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** Not securely. Its describers and most later analyses place it within Hadrosauriformes, but several analyses on other matrices place it on the stem below that clade. Styracosterna is the clade every analysis accepts. Governs `parent: Styracosterna` and the dispute on [*Proa valdearinnoensis*](../../../genera/P/Proa.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mcdonald2012a, Abstract: "A preliminary phylogenetic analysis places Proa in a polytomy with Iguanodon bernissartensis and more derived iguanodontians (Hadrosauroidea)".
- norman2015a, 'Iguanodontoids': "Proa, Jinzhousaurus, Bolong, Barilium, Mantellisaurus, and Iguanodon form a comparatively poorly supported clade".
- madzia2019a, p. 13: a polytomy of "Batyrosaurus, Proa and Ratchasimasaurus" among the Eurasian hadrosauroids.
- poole2022a, p. 11: "the small clade Iguanodontidae includes Iguanodon, Equijubus, Proa, and Fukuisaurus".
- boyd2015b, Results: "The taxa Proa and Koshisaurus are recovered as non-hadrosauriform ankylopollexians by this analysis".
- bonsor2023a, Text-fig. 41, p. 52: *Proa* on the stem, several nodes below *Iguanodon* and Hadrosauroidea.
- bertozzo2025a, Fig. 10, p. 27: *Proa* paired with *Lurdusaurus*, outside the node containing *Iguanodon*.

**Ruled out.**
- *Hadrosauriformes or Hadrosauroidea as agreed.* Boyd and Pagnac (2015), Bonsor and colleagues (2023) and Bertozzo and colleagues (2025) each place it below Hadrosauriformes, on different matrices.

**Open.** Its sister taxon, which varies with the matrix: *Fukuisaurus*, *Lurdusaurus*, *Ratchasimasaurus* or *Batyrosaurus*, or the pair *Jinzhousaurus* + *Bolong*.
