# *Pendraig*

## Is *Pendraig milnerae* Carnian, or Norian to Rhaetian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Norian to Rhaetian, the range the describing paper gives for the Pant-y-ffynnon fissure fills. Governs `stage: [Norian, Rhaetian]` on [*Pendraig milnerae*](../../../genera/P/Pendraig.yml).

**Evidence.**
- spiekman2021a, p. 4, Locality and horizon: "Fissure fills of Pant-y-ffynnon Quarry, southern Wales; earliest occurrence possibly late Norian, latest occurrence possibly late Rhaetian; 214.7–201.3 Ma".
- jones2026c, Coelophysoidea systematics table: "Wales, United Kingdom *Late Triassic, Rhaetian*"; the species account gives "anywhere from 214 to 201 million years old". The Rhaetian is inside the describing paper's range.

**Ruled out.**
- *Carnian.* The record's earlier value. No source cited for this record gives it, and the describing paper's earliest bound is late Norian.

**Open.** The range is Spiekman and colleagues' report of Whiteside and colleagues (2016), which has not been read for this record. [*Pantydraco caducus*](../../../genera/P/Pantydraco.yml), from the same quarry, carries Rhaetian alone.
