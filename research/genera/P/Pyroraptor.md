# *Pyroraptor*

## Which formation, when the describing paper names none?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** The Argiles et Grès à Reptiles of the Aix-en-Provence (Arc) Basin. Allain and Taquet (2000) give only a lithology and the local Begudian stage, so the formation comes from the site's position in the basin. Governs `location.formation: Argiles et Grès à Reptiles` on [*Pyroraptor olympius*](../../../genera/P/Pyroraptor.yml); the basin rule is in [Grès à Reptiles](../../topics/grès-à-reptiles.md).

**Evidence.**
- allain2000a, p. 404: "Locality—The material was collected in La Boucharde, two kilometres to the south-east of Trets, Bouches-du-Rhône, France. Horizon—Fluvio-lacustrine sandstones, Begudian (Upper Campanian to Lower Maastrichtian)."
- catzigras1969a, pp. 6–7: the Aix sheet maps "C8a. Argiles et grès inférieurs à Reptiles" at the base of the Rognacian, and "in the eastern part of the Arc basin from Trets … the Bégudien–Rognacian boundary becomes imprecise" (translated).
- díezdíaz2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 4: "the Argiles et Grès à Reptiles Formation (Aix-en-Provence Basin), which is upper Campanian".

**Ruled out.**
- *The bare "Grès à Reptiles".* Jones (2026) tabulates "Grès à Reptiles Fm., France" (jones2026c, Dromaeosauridae systematics table). That name belongs to the Var (Fox-Amphoux) succession and the Hérault beds, not to the Arc Basin.
- *Jones (2026) as a source for the Arc Basin name.* The *Pyroraptor* entry says the remains "emerged from the Argiles et Grès à Reptiles geologic formation in southern France" in one sentence covering both *Pyroraptor* and *Variraptor* (jones2026c, *P. olympius* entry), and *Variraptor* comes from the Var; the same book's table gives the bare name. It decides nothing either way.
- *Allain and Pereda-Suberbiola (2003) as the basin step.* They place "Fox Amphoux … and La Boucharde … in the Aix-en-Provence Basin" (allain2003a, p. 30), joining the Var site to the Arc Basin, so their basin label does not separate the two units. Trets is placed in the Arc Basin by the map notice instead.

**Open.** Whether the Begudian sandstones at La Boucharde lie within the Argiles et Grès à Reptiles or in the Begudian beds below it. The Aix map notice maps the Bégudien (C7) beneath the Rognacian and says the boundary between them is imprecise east of Trets (catzigras1969a, pp. 6–7). A section through La Boucharde would settle it.
