# *Parksosaurus*

## What is the Parksosaurus holotype's position in the Horseshoe Canyon Formation?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The Tolman Member, through two papers: Larson and colleagues (2010) place the holotype in unit 4 of the formation, and Eberth and Braman (2012) define the Tolman Member as Eberth's units 3 and 4 combined. The "100 feet" of the original description is a height above the Red Deer River, not a level in the section. Governs `location.member: Tolman` and the empty `location.part` on [*Parksosaurus warreni*](../../../genera/P/Parksosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- larson2010a, p. 1171: "The type and only other known specimen of *Parksosaurus* was also recovered from unit 4 in the upper Horseshoe Canyon Formation"; p. 1160: the units are the "Five informal subdivisions … described within the Horseshoe Canyon Formation" by Eberth and O'Connell (1995) and Eberth (2004).
- eberth2012a, p. 1080: the Tolman Member "is correlative with the combined 'Units 3 and 4' of Eberth (2004, 2010)".
- parks1926b, p. 3: "The specimen was found about half a mile from the Red Deer river, on the east side, immediately south of the road to Rumsey ferry, 100 feet above the level of the water."

**Ruled out.**
- *A height in the section.* Parks measures the 100 feet from the river's surface at the site, so it gives no position within the formation and no `part`.

**Open.** Nothing.
