# *Pandoravenator*

## Is the Pandoravenator holotype from the Puesto Almada Member of the Cañadón Asfalto Formation?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The record follows the describing paper: the basal part of the Cañadón Calcáreo Formation, with no member. "Puesto Almada Member" is other workers' name for the section at Puesto Almada, about 1 km away, and the Caja de Pandora section cannot be traced into it. Governs `location.formation: Cañadón Calcáreo`, `location.part: lower` and the empty `location.member` on [*Pandoravenator fernandezorum*](../../../genera/P/Pandoravenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2017a, p. 540: "The section at this locality has been referred to as Puesto Almada Member of the Cañadón Asfalto Formation by other workers (e.g., Cabaleri et al., 2010; Gallego et al., 2011; Volkheimer et al., 2015), but, despite the differences in nomenclature, there is agreement in the Late Jurassic age of these rocks"; "Due to faulting, the section in which the Caja de Pandora locality is placed cannot directly be correlated with the Puesto Almada locality"; the sediments only "very likely" represent the lacustrine–fluvial transition there.
- rauhut2017a, p. 542, Type locality and horizon: "Caja de Pandora locality, ca. 1 km west of the fish locality of Puesto Almada"; "can be referred to the basal part of the Cañadón Calcáreo Formation, latest Oxfordian to Kimmeridgian".
- rauhut2017a, p. 541: "no theropod remains could be discovered in situ, and all remains were found some 5 m below this layer", the fish-bearing bed whose preservation they match.

**Ruled out.**
- *member: Puesto Almada.* The name belongs to the rival scheme that places these beds in the Cañadón Asfalto Formation, and it is given for the Puesto Almada section, not for Caja de Pandora.

**Open.** Whether the Cañadón Calcáreo or the Cañadón Asfalto with a Puesto Almada Member is the better-supported scheme; Cabaleri and colleagues (2010) and the other papers using the member name have not been read.
