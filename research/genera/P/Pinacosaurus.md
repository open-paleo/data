# *Pinacosaurus*

## Which spelling does the record use for *Syrmosaurus viminicaudus*?

*2026-10-29*

**Conclusion.** Supersedes the 2026-10-28 entry, which left the spelling unsettled until Maleev (1952) could be read: the record uses *viminicaudus*, whichever spelling Maleev printed in 1952. If he printed *viminicaudus*, it is the original spelling. If he printed *viminocaudus*, then *viminicaudus* is an incorrect subsequent spelling, since Maleev's own 1954 monograph uses it with no statement of a change; such a spelling, "in prevailing usage and … attributed to the publication of the original spelling", is preserved as correct (ICZN Art. 33.3.1). The held literature uses *viminicaudus* in 18 papers and *viminocaudus* in 5, two of which also use *viminicaudus*, and most credit *viminicaudus* to Maleev (1952). This rests on a reading of the Code, not on the 1952 paper. Governs the spelling in `synonyms[0].reason` and `notable_specimens[0].significance` on [*Pinacosaurus*](../../../genera/P/Pinacosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maleev1954b, English translation by Welch and Carpenter, under "Genus Syrmosaurus Maleev, 1952": "Genus type species - Syrmosaurus viminicaudus Maleev, 1952". The translation spells the name *viminicaudus* throughout and prints no page numbers.
- penkalski2026a, p. 26: "PIN 614–Holotype of 'Syrmosaurus viminocaudus' Maleev, 1952 consists of an almost complete postcranial skeleton".
- arbour2015a, p. 26, lists "*Syrmosaurus viminocaudus* Maleev, 1952" and "*Syrmosaurus viminicaudus* Maleev, 1954" separately, but calls PIN 614 the "holotype of *Syrmosaurus viminicaudus*".
- hill2003a, p. 4, spells the name only *viminicaudus* and lists PIN 614 among the referred specimens of *P. grangeri*.

**Ruled out.**
- *viminocaudus, from the synonymies of Olshevsky (1991), Arbour (2015) and Penkalski (2026).* The 2026-10-28 entry rules out settling the 1952 spelling from the secondary literature, and under Art. 33.3.1 the 1952 spelling does not decide the record's.
- *A length of 3.66 m for PIN 614.* The record gave it; Wikipedia attributes it to Maryańska (1977), but no source read prints it: maryańska1977a lists PIN 614 (p. 94) and measures its limbs (p. 96) without a body length, and maleev1954b gives 4–5 m for the whole animal. The measurement is dropped.

**Open.** What Maleev printed in 1952 remains unread; the paper is held nowhere and has no known translation. The translation of Maleev (1954) may have normalized the spelling.

## How did Maleev (1952) spell the epithet of *Syrmosaurus*?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** Unsettled. The record spells the synonym two ways, *viminicaudus* in `synonyms[0].reason` and *viminocaudus* in `notable_specimens[0].significance`, and the two systematic works that cite Maleev's pages disagree on which he printed in 1952. Neither spelling is adopted until Maleev (1952) is read; the record is unchanged. Bears on `synonyms[0].reason` and `notable_specimens[0].significance` on [*Pinacosaurus*](../../../genera/P/Pinacosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- penkalski2026a, p. 6, Systematic palaeontology: "*=Syrmosaurus viminocaudus* Maleev, 1952 *=Syrmosaurus viminicaudus* Maleev, 1954", separating the two spellings by year.
- tumanova1987a, p. 16: "Syrmosaurus viminicaudus: Малеев, 1952, с. 131; 1954, с. 147" ("Syrmosaurus viminicaudus: Maleev, 1952, p. 131; 1954, p. 147", translated), giving the later spelling for the 1952 paper.
- olshevsky1991a, p. 154, lists the 1952 name as *viminocaudus* and the later one as "*Syrmosaurus viminicaudus* Maleev, 1956 [*sic*]".

**Ruled out.**
- *Settling it from the secondary literature.* Penkalski (2026) and Tumanova (1987) both cite Maleev's pages and contradict each other, and a count of later papers measures which spelling spread, not which Maleev printed.

**Open.** Maleev (1952), the paper naming *Syrmosaurus* (pp. 131–134), would settle the original spelling. If it prints *viminocaudus*, *viminicaudus* is Maleev's own later spelling (1954), and the synonym reason should read *viminocaudus*.
