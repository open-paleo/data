# *Protoavis*

## Where in the Cooper Canyon Formation does the Post Quarry lie?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** Unsettled, so `part` is left empty. Three sources place the quarry in three different parts of the unit. Governs the empty `location.part` with `location.formation: Cooper Canyon` on [*Protoavis texensis*](../../../genera/P/Protoavis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- chatterjee1991a, p. 280: the type skeletons "were collected from the upper Cooper Member, of Early Norian age"; "Both bird skeletons were collected from the Post Quarry, lying side by side slightly above the bone bed".
- lehman2005a, p. 332, Fig. 6 caption: "the 'Post Quarry' in the middle Cooper Canyon Formation".
- martz2013a, p. 342: "The Post Quarry lies near the top of the lower unit of the Cooper Canyon Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: upper`, from Chatterjee (1991).* His "upper Cooper Member" belongs to a scheme in which the Cooper was one of three members of a single Dockum Formation, so "upper" places the quarry in his member, not in the Cooper Canyon Formation as later defined.
- *`part: middle` or `part: lower`.* Lehman and Chatterjee (2005) and Martz and colleagues (2013) disagree, and Martz and colleagues cite Lehman and Chatterjee's own figure for the position, so neither reading can be adopted over the other.

**Open.** Which reading is right. A measured section of the Post Quarry tied to the boundaries of the Cooper Canyon would settle it.
