# *Psittacosaurus*

## Are *P. major* and *P. houi* species of the record?

*2026-10-29*

**Conclusion.** Neither, for now. Hedrick and Dodson (2013) sank *P. major* and "*Hongshanosaurus*" *houi* into *P. lujiatunensis*. Napoli and colleagues (2019) rejected the synonymy of *P. major* in a specialist analysis, and no held paper answers them: later papers that follow Hedrick and Dodson acknowledge the dissent without rebutting it. Ishikawa and colleagues (2025), the latest revision of the Lujiatun psittacosaurids, keep *P. major* apart in their tree but rule only on *P. houi*, which they treat as a valid species; that is a revision a year old, so the record keeps *Hongshanosaurus houi* as its genus-level synonym. Governs the absence of *P. major* and *P. houi* species blocks on [*Psittacosaurus*](../../../genera/P/Psittacosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hedrick2013a, p. 2: an analysis of "P. lujiatunensis, P. major, and Hongshanosaurus houi", by which "each species was shown to be synonymous".
- napoli2019a, p. 3: the 2013 synonymy is "inconclusive"; p. 30: "we regard *Psittacosaurus major* as a valid taxon".
- fonseca2024a, p. 11: the synonymy is "not universally accepted (e.g. … Napoli et al., 2019)".
- ishikawa2025a, p. 3: Napoli and colleagues "argued that P. lujiatunensis and P. major were separate species"; p. 28: the validity of *P. houi* "is supported by nine characters … absent in P. major and P. lujiatunensis"; the conclusion: "Psittacosaurus houi is a valid species".

**Ruled out.**
- *P. major as a settled junior synonym of P. lujiatunensis.* The specialist rebuttal stands unanswered.
- *Adding P. houi now.* It rests on one recent revision.

**Open.** Whether later work adopts *P. houi* and settles *P. major*.

## Which of the three Shestakovo coordinates, and which province, belong to the *P. sibiricus* holotype?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** Shestakovo 3, at 55°53′ N, 87°59′ E, in Kemerovo Province. Governs `location.locality`, `location.coordinates` and `location.region: RU-KEM` on *Psittacosaurus sibiricus* in [*Psittacosaurus*](../../../genera/P/Psittacosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2006a, p. 362: "TYPE LOCALITY AND HORIZON. Shestakovo 3, approximately 40 km south of Mariinsk city, Chebula District, Kemerovo Province, Russia".
- averianov2006a, p. 361, Fig. 1 caption: "Shestakovo 1 (N 55° 54′, E 87° 56′), Shestakovo 2 (N 55° 56′, E 87° 59′) and Shestakovo 3 (N 55° 53′, E 87° 59′)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Shestakovo 1 or 2.* Both are other sites of the same complex, printed in the same caption. Shestakovo 1 is where the first psittacosaur remains, PIN 2860/1, were found in 1953 (averianov2006a, p. 360), and that specimen is not the holotype.
- *Gorno-Altai.* Averianov and colleagues report that "the Shestakovo locality was often erroneously placed in 'Gorno-Altayaskaya [=Altaiskaya] Avtonomnaya Oblast' (Weishampel 1990: 104, fig. 3.21 and some subsequent authors), instead of Kemerovo Province" (averianov2006a, p. 361).

**Open.** Nothing.
