# *Psittacosaurus*

## Which of the three Shestakovo coordinates, and which province, belong to the *P. sibiricus* holotype?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** Shestakovo 3, at 55°53′ N, 87°59′ E, in Kemerovo Province. Governs `location.locality`, `location.coordinates` and `location.region: RU-KEM` on *Psittacosaurus sibiricus* in [*Psittacosaurus*](../../../genera/P/Psittacosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2006a, p. 362: "TYPE LOCALITY AND HORIZON. Shestakovo 3, approximately 40 km south of Mariinsk city, Chebula District, Kemerovo Province, Russia".
- averianov2006a, p. 361, Fig. 1 caption: "Shestakovo 1 (N 55° 54′, E 87° 56′), Shestakovo 2 (N 55° 56′, E 87° 59′) and Shestakovo 3 (N 55° 53′, E 87° 59′)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Shestakovo 1 or 2.* Both are other sites of the same complex, printed in the same caption. Shestakovo 1 is where the first psittacosaur remains, PIN 2860/1, were found in 1953 (averianov2006a, p. 360), and that specimen is not the holotype.
- *Gorno-Altai.* Averianov and colleagues report that "the Shestakovo locality was often erroneously placed in 'Gorno-Altayaskaya [=Altaiskaya] Avtonomnaya Oblast' (Weishampel 1990: 104, fig. 3.21 and some subsequent authors), instead of Kemerovo Province" (averianov2006a, p. 361).

**Open.** Nothing.
