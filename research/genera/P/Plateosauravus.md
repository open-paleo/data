# *Plateosauravus*

## What is the type material of Plateosauravus cullingworthi?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Haughton (1924) named co-types (syntypes): the bones of two animals from one pocket. No lectotype has been designated. The broader "type series" in recent work adds bones Haughton assigned elsewhere. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_type: syntype` and `specimen_id` on *Plateosauravus cullingworthi*.

**Evidence.**
- haughton1924a, p. 407: "a 'pocket' of isolated bones belonging to two individuals — a larger and a smaller"; p. 416: "*Co-types.* Portions of two animals in South African Museum. Catalogue numbers as in description."
- haughton1924a, p. 427: for *Euskelosaurus africanus*, "*Type.* Sacral vertebrae, ischia. (S. Af. Mus. Cat. No. 3608.)".
- mcphee2015b, p. 20: "the 'type' series (SAM-PK-3341–3356, 3602–3603, 3607–3609) is composed of three or more moderately sized individuals", with "type" in quotation marks; "(SAM-PK-3607-3609) was originally selected as the type material for Euskelosaurus africanus by Haughton (1924)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Syntypes by default.* Haughton did not leave the type unfixed; he named co-types.
- *SAM-PK-3607–3609 as Haughton's E. africanus type.* Haughton gives No. 3608 only (p. 427); the range is McPhee and colleagues'.
- *A holotype or lectotype.* Huene erected the genus without fixing a type: "Diese Form stammt in mehreren Individuen von Kromme Spruit" ("this form comes from several individuals from Kromme Spruit", translated; huene1932a, p. 208).

**Open.** Which numbers are Haughton's co-types. His "Catalogue numbers as in description" leaves the list to be assembled from his text; other papers give different lists (jones2026b, p. 374; fernández2022a, pp. 779, 783).
