# *Papiliovenator*

## Where does the Papiliovenator coordinate come from?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** From the caption of the locality map in the describing paper, the only place it prints the position. Governs `location.coordinates: [41.792586, 106.729933]` on [*Papiliovenator neimengguensis*](../../../genera/P/Papiliovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pei2021a, p. 2, Fig. 1 caption: "Locality (E106° 43′ 47.76″, N41° 47′ 33.31″) of the holotype (BNMNH-PV030) of Papiliovenator neimengguensis", which converts to 41.792586, 106.729933. The Locality and horizon paragraph on the same page gives no coordinate.

**Ruled out.**
- *41.743919, 106.744362.* The record stored this pair until 2026-09-01. It lies about 5.5 km from the printed position and within 4 m of the coordinate stored for [*Wulatelong*](../../../genera/W/Wulatelong.yml), another Bayan Mandahu specimen (see [*Wulatelong*](../W/Wulatelong.md)): a neighbor's site, not this holotype's.

**Open.** Nothing.
