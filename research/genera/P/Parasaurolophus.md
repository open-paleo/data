# *Parasaurolophus*

## Which member of the Kirtland Formation is the Parasaurolophus tubicen holotype from?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The De-na-zin Member, by correlation rather than by a recorded horizon. Wiman (1931) gave only the upper Kirtland near its contact with the Ojo Alamo Sandstone; Sullivan and Williamson (1999) assign the holotype to the De-na-zin from its black preservation, which matches the referred skull NMMNH P-25100 from a documented De-na-zin locality. Governs `location.member: De-na-zin` on [*Parasaurolophus tubicen*](../../../genera/P/Parasaurolophus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sullivan1999b, p. 36: "Wiman (1931) described *P. tubicen* as coming from the upper part of the Kirtland Formation near the Kirtland-Ojo Alamo Sandstone transition"; "The holotype of *P. tubicen* bears the same distinctive black preservation as the new specimen (NMMNH P-25100) from the De-na-zin Member of the Kirtland Formation, and we are therefore confident that the holotype came from the same horizon".

**Ruled out.**
- *Naashoibito Member.* Lehman (1981, 1985) placed the holotype there; Sullivan and Williamson reply that "There is no evidence that the holotype of *Parasaurolophus tubicen* is from the Naashoibito Member (contra Lehman 1981; 1985)" (sullivan1999b, p. 37).
- *Ojo Alamo Formation.* Lull and Wright (1942) "cited the specimen as coming from the 'Ojo Alamo formation,' which contradicts the statement by Wiman (1931)" (sullivan1999b, p. 36).

**Open.** Wiman (1931) has not been read for this note; his horizon is known here only through Sullivan and Williamson's summary.

## References

- `sullivan1999b`: Sullivan, R. M.; Williamson, T. E. (1999). A new skull of Parasaurolophus (Dinosauria: Hadrosauridae) from the Kirtland Formation of New Mexico and a revision of the genus. *New Mexico Museum of Natural History and Science Bulletin* 15: 1-52.
