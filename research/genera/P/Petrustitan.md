# *Petrustitan*

## Which site is the Petrustitan lectotype from, and what is its number?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The Sibişel Valley south of Sânpetru, with no finer site recorded, and NHMUK R.3853. The "Szentpeterfalva" label in the London collection belongs to a different individual. Governs `location.locality: Sibişel Valley, south of Sânpetru` and `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Petrustitan hungaricus*](../../../genera/P/Petrustitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- díaz2025a, p. 49, Type locality: "Sibişel River Valley, south of Sânpetru locality, central Haţeg Basin, Hunedoara County, Romania (Fig. 1E). More precise locality information is unavailable."
- díaz2025a, p. 21: a set of bones "inventoried under NHMUK R.4891" carries a handwritten label reading "associated bones of Titanosaurus, Szentpeterfalva (all bones of this individual marked 'green 4')"; the Fig. 17 caption calls it "Individual H, Lithostrotia incertae sedis, Green Four locality, Sânpetru".
- díaz2025a, p. 49: Huene described the fibula "albeit incorrectly citing it as Brit. Mus. N. H. No. 3833 in the text as well as in the caption to his plate 47".

**Ruled out.**
- *Szentpéterfalva, or the Green Four locality, as the lectotype's site.* The label is tied to NHMUK R.4891, which Díez Díaz and colleagues (2025) refer to Lithostrotia incertae sedis, not to *Petrustitan*.
- *NHMUK R.3833.* Huene's (1932) number, which Díez Díaz and colleagues call a miscitation. Their own Table 3 (p. 93), which lists "the specimens described and figured by Huene (1932)", also prints R.3833 against the fibula, so the wrong number appears in the paper that corrects it.

**Open.** Nothing.
