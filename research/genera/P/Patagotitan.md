# *Patagotitan*

## Which member is the Patagotitan holotype from, and is a coordinate published?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The upper Cerro Castaño Member of the Cerro Barcino Formation, from a dated sample in the La Flecha quarry; Carmona and colleagues' (2016) stratigraphic study of the site gives the same placement, "en la sección superior del Miembro Cerro Castaño" ("in the upper section of the Cerro Castaño Member", translated; p. 131). No coordinate for the quarry is printed. Governs `location.member: Cerro Castaño`, `location.part: upper` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Patagotitan mayorum*](../../../genera/P/Patagotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carballido2017a, Supplementary Information, Table S1: sample Cenizo-P, "30 cm above the bone layer FLV1, upper Cerro Castaño Member, Cerro Barcino Formation". The quarry's three bone levels are FLV1, FLV2 and FLV3, and the holotype is from FLV3: "The MNI from FLV3 is two … This specimen (MPEF-PV 3400) was selected as the holotype" (Supplementary Information, Description of the "La Flecha" quarry).

**Ruled out.**
- *43°59′24″S and 68°02′00″W as the quarry's position.* These are corner labels on the frame of Carmona and colleagues' Fig. 1c (p. 129), which spans 43°59′24″–44°03′00″S and 68°02′00″–68°12′00″W; the quarry is a symbol inside the frame.
- *−43.842499, −68.583611.* The record stored this pair until 2026-09-01. It lies outside that map frame, more than 30 km from its nearest corner (our calculation).
- *FLV3 as a bed.* It is the quarry's own label for one of its bone levels.

**Open.** A printed position for the quarry.
