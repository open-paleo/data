# *Protarchaeopteryx*

## Which unit of the Sihetun succession yielded the holotype?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** The Anjiagou Bed of the Jianshangou Member of the Yixian Formation. No paper places NGMC 2125 in the Anjiagou Bed by name: the value combines Qiang and colleagues' (1998) horizon for this specimen with Wang and colleagues' (2016) renaming of those strata (our inference from the two statements). Governs `location.formation: Yixian`, `location.member: Jianshangou` and `location.bed: Anjiagou` on [*Protarchaeopteryx robusta*](../../../genera/P/Protarchaeopteryx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ji1997a, p. 39: "The first marl in the Upper Jurassic lower Yixian Fm. west of the village of Sihetun".
- qiang1998a, p. 753: for NGMC 2125, "Sihetun area near Beipiao City, Liaoning, China. Jiulongsong Member of Chaomidianzi Formation, Jehol Group. This underlies the Yixian Formation".
- wang2016a, p. 117: "The Chaomidianzi (Chiappe et al., 1999) and Sihetun (Li et al., 1998) formations are clear junior synonyms of the Jianshangou bed of the Yixian Formation"; p. 118: "We propose not to use the name Jiulongsong bed (Chiappe et al., 1999; Ji et al., 2004) that had been applied to these strata", in favor of the Anjiagou Bed.

**Ruled out.**
- *Chaomidianzi Formation, below the Yixian.* Qiang and colleagues' formation is a junior synonym of the Jianshangou of the Yixian, not an older unit beneath it (wang2016a, p. 117).
- *Jiulongsong Member.* Wang and colleagues set the name aside because it "is derived from a location in Hebei Province that has an unknown relationship to strata in the Yixian Basin" (wang2016a, p. 118).
- *Anjiagou village as the site.* The position Wang and colleagues print, "1.1 km southwest (41.581665°, 120.802154°) of the Sihetun Museum" (wang2016a, p. 118), is the village the bed is named for, not the holotype's site.

**Open.** Whether the "first marl" of Ji and Ji (1997) lies within the Anjiagou Bed, which Wang and colleagues describe as microlaminated mudstones. A section tying the holotype's marl to Wang and colleagues' beds would settle the bed.
