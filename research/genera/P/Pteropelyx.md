# *Pteropelyx*

## What is the *Pteropelyx* holotype's number?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** AMNH 3971, from Lull and Wright (1942), the earliest paper that gives a number; the describing paper gives none. Brett-Surman's (1989) "AMNH 3791" is a transposition that his own specimen table contradicts. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [AMNH 3971]` on [*Pteropelyx grallipes*](../../../genera/P/Pteropelyx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lull1942a, p. 219: "Holotype: No. 3971 A.M.N.H. Greater part of skeleton, without skull or teeth"; p. 21: "*Pteropelyx grallipes*, No. 3971 A.M.N.H., the greater part of the skeleton, collected by J. C. Isaac in 1876, near Cow Island".
- brett-surman1989a, p. 152, specimen list: "AMNH 3971 Pteropelyx grallipes"; p. 93: "The type species is based on pelvic and limb bones (AMNH 3791)".
- cope1889a: the type is "the greater part of the skeleton of an individual found by Mr. J. C. Isaac near Cow Island, Montana", with no number.

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 3791.* It appears only in one line of Brett-Surman's prose, against his own table and Lull and Wright.

**Open.** Nothing.
