# *Pegomastax*

## Is the Voyizane longitude 27°25′ or 27°26′?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** 27°26′E, as Kitching and Raath (1984) print it in the table both Sereno (2012) and Norman and colleagues (2011) cite; Sereno's 27°25′ is a misprint of that table. This settles the choice the 2026-09-01 entry made without a reason. Governs `location.coordinates: [-30.566668, 27.433332]` on [*Pegomastax africana*](../../../genera/P/Pegomastax.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kitching1984a, p. 118, table: ""Voisana" (Voyizane, Foisani) | 3027CB STERKSPRUIT | 30°34'S 27°26'E | Elliot Fm. (U)", checked on the page image.
- sereno2012a, p. 149, Type locality: "Voyizane (= Voisana) … S30°34', E27°25' (Crompton and Charig 1962; Kitching and Raath 1984)".

**Ruled out.**
- *27°25′.* It is Sereno's copy of the table, which prints 27°26′.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What is the Pegomastax type locality, and which longitude does it take?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** Voyizane, at 30°34′S, 27°26′E. Sereno (2012) gives the holotype's locality as Voyizane but prints the longitude as 27°25′; Norman and colleagues (2011) print 27°26′ for the same locality, and both cite Kitching and Raath (1984), table 1. The record keeps 27°26′. Governs `location.locality: Voyizane` and `location.coordinates: [-30.566668, 27.433332]` on [*Pegomastax africana*](../../../genera/P/Pegomastax.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno2012a, p. 149, Type locality: "Voyizane (= Voisana), Transkei (Herschel) District, Cape Province, South Africa; S30°34′, E27°25′ (Crompton and Charig 1962; Kitching and Raath 1984)".
- norman2011a, p. 189: SAM-PK-K1332 was found "on the northern slopes of Kromspruit (alternative spelling Krommespruit) Mountain, Voyizane (= 'Voisana'; 30°34′S, 27°26′E; Kitching & Raath, 1984: table 1)"; p. 191: SAM-PK-K1334 from "Site 18a, Voisana, Kromspruit 9 Farm … (30°34′S, 27°26′E; Kitching & Raath, 1984)".
- barrett2009b, p. 1033: the *Massospondylus kaalae* type locality is "Near Voyizane village (30°34'S; 27°26'E)", citing Barrett (2004).

**Ruled out.**
- *Krommespruit as the holotype's locality.* Norman and colleagues tie Krommespruit to SAM-PK-K1332 and SAM-PK-K1334, two *Heterodontosaurus* specimens. Sereno names only Voyizane for SAM-PK-K10488.

**Open.** The longitude. The arcminute that separates the readings is about 1.6 km (our calculation). Sereno prints 27°25′ for this holotype; the 27°26′ kept here is printed for other specimens at the same locality. Kitching and Raath (1984), table 1, which both papers cite, has not been read and would settle which figure is the copying error.
