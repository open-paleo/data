# *Phyllodon*

## From which meridian does Thulborn's map reference for Guimarota run?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The meridian of Lisbon. Converted from it, Thulborn's "0° 20' E., 39° 44' N." gives 39.733333, −8.798573, just south of Leiria, where he puts the pit. The constant used for the Lisbon meridian (about 9°07′55″ W of Greenwich) is ours; Thulborn does not print it, and his reference is to the whole arcminute, so the position is good only to within about 2 km. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Phyllodon henkeli*](../../../genera/P/Phyllodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- thulborn1973a, p. 108: "Map reference: Carta Militar de Portugal, Sheet 297 (Leiria), 0° 20' E., 39° 44' N. The locality is a small coal pit south of the town of Leiria."
- thulborn1973a, p. 90: Pedrógão, "Sheet 272 (Vieira de Leiria), 0° 11' E., 39° 55' N … a small pit south of the coastal village of Pedrógão"; p. 118: Porto Pinheiro, "Sheet 349 (Lourinhã), 0° 13′ W., 39° 13′ N."

**Ruled out.**
- *Greenwich.* Read from Greenwich, all three of Thulborn's references fall in eastern Spain or the sea off it; read from Lisbon, they fall near Leiria, on the coast at Pedrógão and near Lourinhã, the places his text names (our calculation, comparing the converted points with the named places).

**Open.** The meridian constant of the Carta Militar sheets of the period, which would confirm the conversion to better than the arcminute. The Pedrógão and Porto Pinheiro references convert the same way; [*Alocodon*](../../../genera/A/Alocodon.yml) and [*Trimucrodon*](../../../genera/T/Trimucrodon.yml) carry no coordinate.

## Who lists *Phyllodon henkeli* as a nomen dubium?

*2026-10-08*

**Conclusion.** Norman and colleagues (2004), in the *Basal Ornithopoda* chapter of *The Dinosauria*. The record previously cited norman2004a, Norman's *Basal Iguanodontia* chapter in the same volume, which does not mention *Phyllodon*. Governs the `norman2004b` reference and `status: nomen dubium` on [*Phyllodon henkeli*](../../../genera/P/Phyllodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- weishampel2004a, *Basal Ornithopoda* (pp. 393–412), nomina dubia table: "Phyllodon henkeli Thulborn, 1973a | Teeth".
- sánchez-fenollosa2023a: "Phyllodon henkeli Thulborn, 1973 (nomen dubium sensu Norman et al. 2004)".
- butler2008b lists *Phyllodon henkeli* among tooth taxa whose "taxonomic validity ... is additionally questionable".

**Ruled out.**
- *norman2004a as the source.* The *Basal Iguanodontia* chapter never names the genus.

**Open.** Nothing.
