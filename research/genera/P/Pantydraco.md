# *Pantydraco*

## How old is the *Pantydraco* fissure fill?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Rhaetian or Early Jurassic, as the describing paper gives it: the record takes the Rhaetian stage and both periods, since the Jurassic side names no stage. *Pendraig*, from another fissure in the quarry, keeps its own Norian–Rhaetian. Governs `period: [Late Triassic, Early Jurassic]` and `stage: [Rhaetian]` on [*Pantydraco caducus*](../../../genera/P/Pantydraco.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton2007b, p. 123, Type horizon: "A Rhaetian (Upper Triassic) or Lower Jurassic fissure fill at the old Pant-y-ffynnon Quarry"; Conclusions: "Uncertainity regarding the age of the two fissure fills, with the Welsh one possibly being Early Jurassic rather than Late Triassic".
- spiekman2021a, for the *Pendraig* holotype: "earliest occurrence possibly late Norian, latest occurrence possibly late Rhaetian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Late Triassic alone.* The Lower Jurassic is one side of the describing paper's either-or, which counts as a reading.

**Open.** Nothing.
