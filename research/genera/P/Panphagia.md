# *Panphagia*

## Is the Panphagia holotype from the La Peña or the Cancha de Bochas Member?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The lowest Cancha de Bochas Member. The describing paper gives the horizon only as 40 m above the base of the Ischigualasto Formation, which is the boundary between the two lowest members in the four-member scheme; Alcober and Martínez (2010) put that level in the base of the Cancha de Bochas. Governs `location.member: Cancha de Bochas` and `location.part: lower` on [*Panphagia protos*](../../../genera/P/Panphagia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martínez2009b, p. 2, Horizon and Age: "40 m above the base of the Ischigualasto Formation".
- alcober2010a, p. 56: the members are "the La Peña (from the base to 40 m), the Cancha de Bochas (40 to 180 m)"; p. 57: the *Sanjuansaurus* site "is located in the lowest levels of the Cancha de Bochas Member. It was found 40 m above the base of the formation", and "the holotype of *Panphagia protos*" was "recovered from the same level".
- novas2021a, accepted manuscript, p. 77: "The only known specimen comes from Valle Pintado, lower levels of Cancha de Bochas Member, Ischigualasto Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *La Peña Member.* A 40 m level can be read into the La Peña's range "from the base to 40 m", but the two papers that name a member for this horizon both give the Cancha de Bochas.

**Open.** Nothing.
