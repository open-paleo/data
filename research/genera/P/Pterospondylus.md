# *Pterospondylus*

## What stage does *Pterospondylus* take?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Norian. Sues and Schoch (2025) date the type horizon Norian (Sevatian), and Rauhut and Hungerbühler (2000) give evidence that the holotype came from the Norian claystone, not the Rhaetian beds above; Huene's (1932) Rhaetic is the reading both answer. Governs `stage: [Norian]` on [*Pterospondylus trielbae*](../../../genera/P/Pterospondylus.yml), and bears on the earlier entry's Open question about the source bed.

**Evidence.**
- sues2025a, p. 464: "Type horizon. Top of Arnstadt Formation, Middle Keuper Subgroup. Age: Late Triassic (Norian: Sevatian)."
- rauhut2000a, pp. 84–85: the pit has "a lower claystone layer, which is considered to represent the equivalent of the Knollenmergel, overlain by sand- and siltstones of probably Rhaetian age"; "at least a part of the turtle remains were derived from the lower part of the section"; "Since the holotype of *P. trielbae* was found within a turtle carapace, it might therefore be of Late Norian age, rather than Rhaetian."
- huene1932a: "From the uppermost (Rhaetic) layers near Halberstadt."

**Ruled out.**
- *Rhaetian.* It rests on Huene's attribution, which the two later horizon statements answer.

**Open.** Rauhut and Hungerbühler's argument is probabilistic ("might therefore be"); a record of the turtle carapace's bed would settle it.

## Which formation at the Baerecke-Limpricht pit yielded the holotype?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** The top of the Arnstadt Formation. Governs `location.formation: Arnstadt` and `location.part: upper` on [*Pterospondylus trielbae*](../../../genera/P/Pterospondylus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sues2025a, p. 464: "Type locality. Baerecke-Limpricht brick-clay pit along the present-day highway B79 between Halberstadt and Quedlinburg, on the southeastern edge of Halberstadt, Saxony-Anhalt. Type horizon. Top of Arnstadt Formation, Middle Keuper Subgroup."
- rauhut2000a, p. 80: "Occurrence: Knollenmergel, Baerecke quarry, Halberstadt, Sachsen-Anhalt, Germany."

**Ruled out.**
- *Trossingen Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Occurrence: Trossingen Fm." (jones2026c, *Pterospondylus trielbae* entry). Sues and Schoch, writing on the same pit, use the Trossingen only for the Trossingen beds themselves and say that the Halberstadt *Plateosaurus* material "is stratigraphically younger than that from Trossingen" (sues2025a, p. 462). The "Knollenmergel" of the older papers names the claystone by its likeness to the southern German beds; that is a correlation, not a placement in the Trossingen (our reading).

**Open.** Whether the holotype came from the Arnstadt claystone or from the beds above it. Rauhut and Hungerbühler describe the pit as "a lower claystone layer, which is considered to represent the equivalent of the Knollenmergel, overlain by sand- and siltstones of probably Rhaetian age" (rauhut2000a, pp. 84–85), and Sues and Schoch refer other material from the pit, with a query, to the Rhaetian Exter Formation (sues2025a, p. 452). Rauhut and Hungerbühler infer the lower layer because, "According to JAEKEL (1913), at least a part of the turtle remains were derived from the lower part of the section", and the vertebra was found in a turtle shell (p. 85). Jaekel's own account of where that shell lay would settle it.
