# *Pawpawsaurus*

## Where does the Pawpawsaurus coordinate come from?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** From Carpenter and Kirkland (1998), who print an approximate position for this taxon's type locality; the describing paper keeps the locality data on file at SMU. Governs `location.coordinates: [32.866669, -97.300003]` and `location.locality: SMU locality 263` on [*Pawpawsaurus campbelli*](../../../genera/P/Pawpawsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lee1996a, p. 233, Type Locality and Horizon: "The Paw Paw Formation, late Albian, Tarrant County, Texas (SMU locality 263, all locality information on file at SMU)".
- carpenter1998c, p. 253: "**Type Horizon and Locality**—Paw Paw Formation (late Albian: Lee, 1996), ~32°52'N, ~97°18'W, Tarrant County, Texas", which converts to 32.866667, −97.3. The same paper gives *Texasetes* a different approximate position, "~97°20'W, 32°56'N" (p. 265).

**Ruled out.**
- *A geocode of Fort Worth.* The stored pair is Carpenter and Kirkland's printed position for this taxon, not a city center.
- *Fort Worth as the locality.* Lee (1996) uses it only for the region: "All Texas Early Cretaceous nodosaurid specimens are from the Albian Paw Paw Formation in the Fort Worth area" (lee1996a, p. 232).

**Open.** Whether an approximate position, printed by a later paper while the describing paper withholds the site, should stand. The locality data on file at SMU would settle it.
