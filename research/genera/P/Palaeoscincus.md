# *Palaeoscincus*

## Which formation is the Palaeoscincus costatus holotype from?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The Judith River Formation, kept as the conventional assignment. Leidy (1856) names no rock unit, only the badlands of the Judith River, and no source read for this note ties the tooth ANSP 9263 to the formation as now bounded. Governs `location.formation: Judith River` on [*Palaeoscincus costatus*](../../../genera/P/Palaeoscincus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- leidy1856b, p. 72: "Notices of remains of extinct Reptiles and Fishes, discovered by Dr. F. V. Hayden in the Bad Lands of the Judith River, Nebraska Territory"; "The genus and species are founded on a single specimen of a tooth".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** A source that places ANSP 9263 in the Judith River Formation as now bounded, rather than in the badlands Leidy names.

## References

- `leidy1856b`: Leidy, J. (1856). Notices of remains of extinct reptiles and fishes, discovered by Dr. F. V. Hayden in the bad lands of the Judith River, Nebraska Territory. *Proceedings of the Academy of Natural Sciences of Philadelphia* 8: 72-73.
