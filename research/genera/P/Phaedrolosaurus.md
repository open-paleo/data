# *Phaedrolosaurus*

## Where does *Phaedrolosaurus* sit, and is its status in dispute?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** Theropoda incertae sedis, and no dispute: the block is removed. Every treatment read since 1990 calls it a nomen dubium, and the dromaeosaurid reading of 1973–1977 is historical. Rauhut and Xu (2005) find the lectotype tooth indistinguishable from any other theropod tooth, so no placement finer than Theropoda is supported; no paper read places it in Tetanurae. Governs `parent: Theropoda` and the absence of a dispute on [*Phaedrolosaurus*](../../../genera/P/Phaedrolosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2005c: the diagnosis "does not list any characters that distinguish this tooth from any other theropod teeth. Thus, Phaedrolosaurus ilikensis should be regarded as a nomen dubium"; its synonymy gives "Coelurosauria indet: Norman, 1990:282 Theropoda indet: Weishampel, 1990:106".
- zhao2008a: "the holotype of this taxon (IVPP V 4024-1) has no diagnostic features and its association with the diagnostic IVPP V 4024-2 could not be demonstrated. *Phaedrolosaurus ilikensis* thus should be regarded as a *nomen dubium*."
- barsbold1999a: it "was originally assigned to the Dromaeosauridae but has been considered a *nomen dubium* by Norman (1990)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Tetanurae incertae sedis.* The earlier parent; no paper read places it there.
- *Dromaeosauridae.* Dong (1973) and Sues (1977); not followed since 1990.

**Open.** Nothing.
