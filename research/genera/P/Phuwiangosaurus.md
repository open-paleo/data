# *Phuwiangosaurus*

## Which catalogue numbers make up the *Phuwiangosaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** SM PW1-0001 through SM PW1-0022, each listed on its own, in the form Suteethorn and colleagues (2009) give. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [SM PW1-0001, …, SM PW1-0022]` on [*Phuwiangosaurus sirindhornae*](../../../genera/P/Phuwiangosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martin1994a, Type (English abridged version): "A partly articulated skeleton n° P.W.1-1 to P.W.1-21".
- suteethorn2009a, p. 195: "Holotype. SM PW1-0001 to SM PW1-0022; partly articulated skeleton including three cervical vertebrae, three dorsal vertebrae, scapulae, left humerus, left ulna, ilia, pubes, ischia, femora, left fibula, coracoid, chevron and rib."

**Ruled out.**
- *SM PW 1*, the record's earlier value. It names the series, not a catalogue number. Jones (2026) gives the series, "SM PW1–1 through 22—partial skeleton (Martin et al., 1994)" (jones2026b, Euhelopodidae systematics table), which agrees with this record in substance, though Martin and colleagues (1994) print 21 numbers, not 22.

**Open.** The two papers count the elements differently, 21 against 22, and neither says what the added number is. The record's material gives four dorsal vertebrae where Suteethorn and colleagues give three.
