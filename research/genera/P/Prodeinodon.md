# *Prodeinodon*

## Is the status or placement of *Prodeinodon* in dispute?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute block is removed and the parent stays Tetanurae. Every treatment read calls it a nomen dubium. Readings that name a group for the teeth are all tetanuran: megalosaurid, tyrannosaurid, allosauroid, carcharodontosaurid or dromaeosaurid; the remainder call the teeth Theropoda indet., which leaves them unplaced rather than placing them elsewhere. Governs `parent: Tetanurae` and the absence of a dispute on [*Prodeinodon*](../../../genera/P/Prodeinodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- molnar1990b: "The teeth of Prodeinodon are obviously theropod but have no characters by which they can be assigned at the infraordinal level."
- lucas2001b: "Prodeinodon is a form genus for relatively large (carnosaur-size) theropod teeth".
- yun2020a: "The hypothesis of \"*Prodeinodon*\" teeth represent dromaeosaurid theropods … is not supported"; it reads them as carcharodontosaurid.
- mo2014a: "This implies that Prodeinodon might be a more derived theropod, likely an allosauroid".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Wastebasket".* The removed block's word is Jones's (2026); the primary sources call it a form genus.

**Open.** Which group the similar East Asian teeth belong to; the proposals are all tetanuran.
