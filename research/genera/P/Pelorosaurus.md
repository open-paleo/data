# *Pelorosaurus*

## Which unit are the Pelorosaurus brevis syntypes from, and is Cuckfield Stone a member?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The Grinstead Clay Formation, with no member. The syntypes come from Tilgate Forest near Cuckfield, which the literature places in the Grinstead Clay (see [Wealden units](../../topics/wealden-units.md)); applying that to these specimens is an inference from the locality. "Cuckfield Stone" is a quarrying name for a rock within the unit. Governs `location.formation: Grinstead Clay` and the empty `location.member` and `location.group` on [*Pelorosaurus brevis*](../../../genera/P/Pelorosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- raven2020a, p. 3, Type Locality: "An imprecisely located quarry in Tilgate Forest, near Cuckfield, West Sussex, U.K.. Grinstead Clay Formation", for *Hylaeosaurus*.
- norman2013a, p. 389: specimens in the Mantell Collection "retain the calcareous, gritty sandstone (Cuckfield Stone) that was quarried for road repairs and general building work".

**Ruled out.**
- *member: Cuckfield Stone.* Norman (2013) describes a rock quarried for road and building work, not a named subdivision of the formation.
- *group: Hastings Beds.* The group is right but redundant once a formation is recorded.

**Open.** Upchurch, Mannion and Barrett (2011) are reported to give the Tunbridge Wells Sand Formation for these specimens; their chapter has not been read, and it is the source that would show whether the Grinstead Clay assignment holds for this series.
