# *Prenocephale*

## Where in the Nemegt locality is the Prenocephale holotype from, and at what level?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** Site 19 on Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz's (1972) map of the northern part of the Nemegt locality, in the Upper Nemegt Beds. The site number is the describing paper's; the level follows from the map paper's statement that every excavation in that area was in the Upper Nemegt Beds. Governs `location.locality: Nemegt (site 19)` and `location.part: upper` on [*Prenocephale prenes*](../../../genera/P/Prenocephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maryańska1974a, p. 53: "Type locality: Nemegt, Nemegt Basin, Gobi Desert, Mongolian People's Republic (see Gradziński & Jerzykiewicz, 1972, Text-fig. 1, No. 19)".
- gradziński1972a, Text-fig. 1 legend: "19 — skull and fragments of the postcranial skeleton of pachycephalosaurid dinosaur"; p. 21: "The excavations in the northern part of the locality Nemegt were carried out only in the Upper Nemegt Beds."

**Ruled out.**
- *The range printed for the mapped area as the holotype's position.* Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz give the area of Text-fig. 1 as lying "between 101°02′—101°03′30″ longitude E and 43°30′30″—43°32′30″ latitude N" (p. 19), a bounding range rather than the site.

**Open.** The site's own position within the mapped area, which neither paper prints.
