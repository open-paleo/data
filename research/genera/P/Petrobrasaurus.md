# *Petrobrasaurus*

## Which numbers make up the *Petrobrasaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** The 31 element numbers MAU-Pv-PH-449/1 through /37 that Filippi and colleagues (2011) list in the holotype statement, each one listed separately, with /21, /23, /24, /28, /33 and /34 absent because the paper does not list them. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Petrobrasaurus puestohernandezi*](../../../genera/P/Petrobrasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- filippi2011a, p. 4: "Holotype. Two isolated teeth (MAU-Pv-PH-449/1 and 22), posterior portion of possibly middle cervical vertebra (MAU-Pv-PH-449/37)", continuing through "fragments of haemal arches (MAU-Pv-PH-449/29, 30, 31 and 32)".

**Ruled out.**
- *"HAU-Pv-PH-449".* Jones (2026) gives "HAU-Pv-PH-449—partial skeleton" (jones2026b, *Petrobrasaurus puestohernandezi* table row). The code is MAU in every number of the holotype statement, and the paper numbers each element rather than the skeleton as a whole.

**Open.** Nothing.
