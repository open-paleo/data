# *Piveteausaurus*

## Is the Piveteausaurus skull Callovian or Oxfordian?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** Callovian (upper Callovian), as Taquet and Welles (1977) give its horizon. The "Oxfordian" of the earlier literature used a stage that then took in the upper Callovian. Governs `period.stage: [Callovian]` on [*Piveteausaurus divesensis*](../../../genera/P/Piveteausaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- taquet1977a, Systematic paleontology, *Piveteausaurus divesensis*: "Horizon: upper Callovian" (translated); the abstract places the skull in the "upper Callovian of Dives, Normandy" (translated).
- walker1964a, p. 125: "The precise horizon of the specimen is not stated in zonal terms and there are difficulties in interpreting the designation 'Oxfordian', the Upper Callovian of Arkell (1956) being included by Dangeard (1951) in this stage. It is only possible to say that the specimen … is approximately contemporaneous with, or more likely slightly younger than, *E. oxoniensis*", which he gives from the "Middle Oxford Clay (Upper Callovian)" (p. 121).

**Ruled out.**
- *Oxfordian.* Walker (1964) names the species from "the Oxfordian of Vaches Noires, near Dives" (p. 124), following Piveteau (1923), and then explains that the label may include the upper Callovian. Taquet and Welles give the horizon as upper Callovian.

**Open.** The ammonite zone of the skull. Neither paper gives one; the Marnes de Dives are the zone of *A. lamberti* (see [Marnes de Dives](../../stratigraphy/m/marnes-de-dives.md)), but tying the skull to that unit's zone is an inference.
