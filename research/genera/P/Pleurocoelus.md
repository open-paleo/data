# *Pleurocoelus*

## Why does Pleurocoelus nanus carry a county, not Muirkirk?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** Marsh (1888) gives the syntypes no site, only "several localities" in Prince George's County, and the later sources read do not tie these four vertebrae to one. The county is the finest place a source gives for them. Governs `location.locality: Prince George's County` on [*Pleurocoelus nanus*](../../../genera/P/Pleurocoelus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- marsh1888a, p. 92: the remains, "representing several individuals … were found at several localities of the Potomac formation in Prince George Co., Maryland."
- d'emic2012c, p. 3 (online-first version): "The type series of Pleurocoelus nanus is the appropriate size to belong to one individual, but the exact provenance of each bone is uncertain. These bones could represent a chimera of individuals or taxa (Hatcher 1903)."

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether the syntypes came from Muirkirk. Gilmore (1920) gives Muirkirk for Arundel theropod material, such as the *Ornithomimus affinis* cotypes "collected near Muirkirk, Prince Georges County, Maryland, by J. B. Hatcher in 1888" (gilmore1920a, p. 137), and Carpenter and Kirkland (1998) give it for [*Priconodon crassus*](../../../genera/P/Priconodon.yml), named in the same paper as *P. nanus*; neither names the *P. nanus* syntypes, so carrying Muirkirk over would be an inference. Hatcher (1903), whom D'Emic cites on these bones, has not been read and may say where they were collected.
