# *Pitekunsaurus*

## Is the Pitekunsaurus holotype from the Anacleto Formation?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The record keeps the Anacleto Formation, the describing paper's assignment, but a later paper by Filippi is reported to have questioned it, and that paper has not been read. Governs `location.formation: Anacleto` on [*Pitekunsaurus macayai*](../../../genera/P/Pitekunsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- filippi2008a, p. 575, Abstract: "from mudstone levels assigned to the Anacleto Formation (Lower–Middle Campanian), corresponding to the uppermost beds of the Neuquén Group".
- agnolín2025a, p. 218: the Anacleto titanosaurs "also include Pitekunsaurus macayai (Filippi & Garrido, 2008; see below), Rinconsaurus caudamirus … and Overosaurus paradasorum … but their stratigraphic position was questioned (Filippi, 2015)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** What Filippi (2015) proposes for this holotype. Pérez-Moreno and colleagues (2023) cite the same paper when placing [*Rinconsaurus*](../../../genera/R/Rinconsaurus.yml) at the base of the Bajo de la Carpa Formation (see [Bajo de la Carpa](../../stratigraphy/b/bajo-de-la-carpa.md)); that it does the same for *Pitekunsaurus* is our guess. Filippi (2015), "Los dinosaurios Sauropoda del Cretácico Superior del norte de la Cuenca Neuquina, Patagonia Argentina", *Boletín del Instituto de Fisiografía y Geología* 85: 19–28, as Agnolín and colleagues list it, would settle it.
