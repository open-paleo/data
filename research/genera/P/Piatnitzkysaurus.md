# *Piatnitzkysaurus*

## Which Cerro Cóndor site is the Piatnitzkysaurus holotype from?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** Cerro Cóndor South. Of the works read for this note, only the caption of Rauhut's (2005) locality map ties the name to this holotype. Governs `location.locality: Cerro Cóndor South` on [*Piatnitzkysaurus floresi*](../../../genera/P/Piatnitzkysaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2005b, p. 88, Text-fig. 1 caption: "Localities: 1, Cerro Cóndor south, type locality of Piatnitzkysaurus floresi Bonaparte, 1979".
- holwerda2021a, p. 578: Cerro Cóndor North "lies approximately 2 km north-east of the first discovery site of Patagosaurus remains: Cerro Cóndor South, close to the village of Cerro Cóndor".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cerro Cóndor North.* It is the type locality of *Patagosaurus fariasi*: "The holotype and hypodigm were found in Cerro Cóndor North, located 5 kilometers to the north of Cerro Cóndor" (bonaparte1986b, *Patagosaurus*, Geographic and stratigraphic location; translated), and Rauhut gives the southern site for *Piatnitzkysaurus*.

**Open.** A statement in a description of the holotype itself. The theropod part of Bonaparte and Lange-Badré's (1986) Cerro Cóndor monograph would be the first source; the text of it read for this note covers only the sauropods. Bonaparte and Lange-Badré's "5 kilometers to the north" of the village and Holwerda and colleagues' "approximately 2 km north-east" of Cerro Cóndor South measure from different points and are not checked against each other.
