# *Patagosaurus*

## Which Cerro Cóndor locality is the Patagosaurus holotype from, and where is it?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** Cerro Cóndor North. The sources agree on the site and disagree on its distance from the village of Cerro Cóndor, so no bearing is recorded. Governs `location.locality: Cerro Cóndor North` on [*Patagosaurus fariasi*](../../../genera/P/Patagosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bonaparte1986b (read in English translation, without the original folios), Geographic and stratigraphic location: "The holotype and hypodigm were found in Cerro Cóndor North, located 5 kilometers to the north of Cerro Cóndor and 100 meters west of the route that connects Cerro Cóndor to Paso del Sapo" (translated).
- holwerda2021a, p. 578: "The type locality of the holotype of *Patagosaurus fariasi* is Cerro Cóndor North, which lies approximately 2 km north-east of the first discovery site of *Patagosaurus* remains: Cerro Cóndor South, close to the village of Cerro Cóndor".
- pol2022a, p. 140: remains of *Patagosaurus* "have been found in three different localities near the Cerro Cóndor village"; the type locality "lies approximately 2 km north from Cerro Cóndor village".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cerro Cóndor South.* It is where *Patagosaurus* remains were first found, but it yielded referred material, not the holotype.

**Open.** The distance: 5 km north of Cerro Cóndor in Bonaparte (1986), about 2 km in Holwerda and colleagues (2021) and Pol and colleagues (2022). A bearing in `locality` waits on which is right.
