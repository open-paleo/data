# *Protohadros*

## Is the *Protohadros* type from the Woodbine Formation or the Lewisville Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Lewisville Formation of the Woodbine Group, Arlington Member. Head (1998) placed SMU 303 in the Arlington Member of what was then the Woodbine Formation; the revised scheme that Tykoski and colleagues (2023) follow raises the Woodbine to a group and makes the Arlington a member of the Lewisville inside it. Governs `location.formation: Lewisville` and `location.member: Arlington` on [*Protohadros byrdi*](../../../genera/P/Protohadros.yml). The rank question for the units is in [the rank that moves with the parent](../../topics/rank-that-moves-with-the-parent.md).

**Evidence.**
- head1998a, p. 718: SMU 303 "is located within a six meter thick section of the Arlington Member of the Woodbine Formation (Dodge, 1969)".
- tykoski2023a, p. e2257238-2: "The Lewisville Formation is the upper formation of the Woodbine Group following recent revision of Woodbine stratigraphy and nomenclature by Denne et al. (2016)"; a site "in either the Arlington Member or Tarrant Member of the Lewisville Formation".
- tykoski2023a, p. e2257238-7: "Protohadros byrdi is the most common and widespread herbivorous dinosaur taxon known in the Lewisville Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Woodbine Formation.* Head's rank, repeated in Norman's table as "Woodbine Formation (Texas), United States" (norman2004a, p. 417, Table 19.1). It names the same rock under the scheme that preceded the revision, not a different unit.

**Open.** Head puts the Tarrant Member in the Eagleford Group (head1998a, p. 718), while Tykoski and colleagues place it in the Lewisville, so the two schemes differ on more than rank. Denne and colleagues (2016), the revision itself, have not been read.
