# *Paralitherizinosaurus*

## Is the Paralitherizinosaurus holotype from the Osoushinai or the Nishichirashinai Formation?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** The Osoushinai Formation, by inference. The concretion was found loose near the confluence of the Rubeshibe River and 36 Ten-zawa Creek, where the Nishichirashinai Formation is exposed, and was assigned to the Osoushinai upstream from its bivalve and lithology. Governs `location.formation: Osoushinai` and `location.locality` on [*Paralitherizinosaurus japonicus*](../../../genera/P/Paralitherizinosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kobayashi2022a, p. 3, Geological setting: "This specimen was contained in an isolated concretion as float, sitting near the confluence of the Rubeshibe River and 36 Ten-zawa Creek"; "The concretion was discovered in an area where the Nishichirashinai Formation (Coniacian to lower Santonian) is exposed"; Murakami and colleagues "concluded that it was originally from an upstream area, where the Oshoushinai Formation (early Campanian) crops out because of the presence of the inoceramid bivalve *Sphenoceramus* in the concretion and the features of lithofaces".
- kobayashi2022a, p. 3, Horizon and locality: "The Osoushinai Formation (early Campanian) of the Yezo Group in Nakagawa Town".

**Ruled out.**
- *Nishichirashinai Formation.* It is the unit at the find site, but the concretion was float, and its fossil and matrix match the Osoushinai.
- *"Oshoushinai" as a different unit.* The paper uses both spellings for the same formation: Osoushinai in its title and horizon line, Oshoushinai in the geological setting and the Fig. 1 caption.

**Open.** The upstream exposure the concretion came from. Murakami and colleagues (2008), who give the stratigraphic column and horizon, have not been read.
