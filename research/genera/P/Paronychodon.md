# *Paronychodon*

## Is *Euronychodon* a synonym of *Paronychodon*?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** Yes, a junior subjective synonym. Sues and Averianov (2013) find the teeth of *Euronychodon portucalensis* indistinguishable from the restricted *Paronychodon* morphotype, following earlier proposals, and later works repeat the synonymy. No work after 1998 argues that *Euronychodon* is diagnosable. Governs the `synonyms` entry for *Euronychodon* on [*Paronychodon*](../../../genera/P/Paronychodon.yml), and the removal of the separate *Euronychodon* record.

**Evidence.**
- antunes1991a, p. 117: "Genus Euronychodon nov. Etymology : European form related to the American Paronychodon"; p. 114: "a new genus of dromaeosaur related to Paronychodon" (translated).
- sues2013a, p. 309: "The teeth of Euronychodon portucalensis are indistinguishable from this morphotype, and Euronychodon should be considered a subjective junior synonym of Paronychodon".
- csiki-sava2015a, p. 56: *Euronychodon* "is now regarded as a subjective junior synonym of Paronychodon".
- makovicky2004b, p. 185, Table 9.1: *Euronychodon portucalensis* and *E. asiaticus* among the nomina dubia.

**Ruled out.**
- *A genus distinct from Paronychodon.* Its describers named it for the absence of longitudinal ridges in some teeth, and Rauhut and Zinke (1995), as Csiki and Grigorescu (1998) report, treated it as valid. Sues and Averianov restrict *Paronychodon* to the unserrated, ridged morphotype, which takes in the *Euronychodon* type.

**Open.** Whether to record *Paronychodon asiaticus*, the combination Sues and Averianov (2013) make for Nessov's *Euronychodon asiaticus* while retaining the species only provisionally.

## Is *Paronychodon lacustris* a valid taxon, and where do its teeth belong?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** It is a nomen dubium, and its teeth cannot be placed below Paraves. The specialists who have examined such teeth treat them as undiagnostic: most can be referred to known theropods, and the rest may be abnormal or developing teeth of various paravians. No later work argues that the name denotes a diagnosable species. Governs `status: nomen dubium`, `parent: Paraves` with `placement: incertae sedis`, and the dispute on [*Paronychodon lacustris*](../../../genera/P/Paronychodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- currie1990a, p. 117: "Because most 'Paronychodon' teeth can be referred to known genera, the name Paronychodon lacustris should be restricted to non-serrate forms"; "It appears more likely that these teeth represent growth abnormalities".
- sues2013a, p. 308: teeth identified as *Paronychodon* "possibly represent dental developmental stages of various paravian theropod dinosaurs", and these "'organ taxa' should not be considered equivalent to diagnosable dinosaurian taxa"; p. 310: "The affinities of Paronychodon remain uncertain".
- makovicky2004b, p. 186, Table 9.1: *Paronychodon lacustris* Cope, 1876a among the nomina dubia of Troodontidae, on teeth.
- csiki-sava2016a, Table: a "Paronychodon" morphotype tooth listed under Paraves.

**Ruled out.**
- *A valid species.* Currie and colleagues (1990) allow that the unserrated teeth "conceivably may represent a distinct taxon", and Larson and Currie (2013) mention in passing that it "may be a valid taxon", but neither argues for a diagnosis, and both exclude it from analysis or refer most such teeth elsewhere.
- *A single family.* Attributions to Troodontidae and to Dromaeosauridae each rest on some of the teeth; the teeth as a whole have been referred to several paravian genera.

**Open.** Nessov (1995) and Sullivan (2006) attribute individual teeth of this form only to Theropoda, without a narrower group.
