# *Padillasaurus*

## Where was the Padillasaurus holotype found, and how firm is its horizon?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** In the La Tordolla area of Vereda Monquira, Villa de Leiva, on the word of the museum that houses it; the exact site was never recorded. The middle Paja Formation is the level that crops out in that area, not a level seen with the bones. Governs `location.locality`, `location.formation: Paja` and `location.part: middle` on [*Padillasaurus leivaensis*](../../../genera/P/Padillasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carballido2015a, p. 2, Geologic Setting: "The holotype material of Padillasaurus was collected by local farmers"; "The exact geographic provenance of the material could not be determined. Nevertheless, the information provided by the staff of the JACVM indicates that the material comes from the La Tordolla area, in the Vereda Monquira, Villa de Leiva"; "At this place, the middle member of the Paja Formation crops out."

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** A collector's account or collection record of the find site. Carballido and colleagues (2015) cite none.
