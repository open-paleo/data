# *Priconodon*

## Why does the Priconodon record leave out the coordinate Carpenter and Kirkland print?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** Their longitude does not fit their own locality. Carpenter and Kirkland (1998) give the type locality as Muirkirk with "~39°1'20"N, 76°8'16"W"; the latitude suits Muirkirk, but 76°8′16″ W lies east of Chesapeake Bay, outside Prince George's County (our check of the converted point, 39.0222, −76.1378). Correcting it would mean choosing which digit is wrong, so the record keeps Muirkirk and no coordinate. Governs `location.locality: Muirkirk` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Priconodon crassus*](../../../genera/P/Priconodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carpenter1998c, p. 254: "Holotype—USNM 2135, worn tooth. Type Locality—Arundel Formation (Aptian–Albian boundary; Doyle and Hickey, 1976), ~39°1'20"N, 76°8'16"W, Muirkirk, Maryland (for a historical discussion see Kranz, 1996)."
- marsh1888a, p. 93: "All the remains supposed to pertain to this animal are from the Potomac formation, Prince George Co., Maryland", with no closer site.

**Ruled out.**
- *39.0222, −76.1378.* The printed value converted as it stands; see the Conclusion.
- *Coffee Mine.* Carpenter and Kirkland name it for "USNM 9154, left tibia, Coffee Mine, Muirkirk, Maryland" (p. 254), a referred specimen, not the holotype tooth.

**Open.** The correct longitude. Kranz (1996), whom Carpenter and Kirkland cite for the history of the site, has not been read.
