# *Pachyrhinosaurus*

## Does the Pachyrhinosaurus perotorum holotype take the Kogosukruk Tongue as its member?

*2026-09-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The Kogosukruk Tongue is a unit of the older subdivision of the Prince Creek Formation, and the paper describing the quarry places it there only in that older scheme's terms. Governs the empty `location.member` on [*Pachyrhinosaurus perotorum*](../../../genera/P/Pachyrhinosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- fiorillo2010a, p. 457: "Previous stratigraphic nomenclature (Chapman et al. 1964) defined two tongues of the Prince Creek Fm., the Tuluvak Tongue … and the Kogosukruk Tongue"; "In terms of this older nomenclature, the Kikak-Tegoseak Quarry is situated within the Kogosukruk Tongue."
- fiorillo2010a, p. 457: Mull and colleagues (2003) "revised the Cretaceous and Tertiary stratigraphic nomenclature in the Colville Basin", restricting the Prince Creek Formation to the units above and interfingering with the Schrader Bluff Formation.

**Ruled out.**
- *member: Kogosukruk.* Fiorillo and colleagues (2010) give it as a translation into Chapman and colleagues' (1964) scheme, which Mull and colleagues (2003) replaced. That the revised scheme names no tongue at the quarry is an inference from this passage.

**Open.** Whether Mull and colleagues (2003) name any subdivision at the Kikak-Tegoseak Quarry; their paper has not been read.
