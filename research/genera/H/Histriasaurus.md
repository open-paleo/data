# *Histriasaurus*

## Does the Histriasaurus holotype WN-V6 survive, and is "WN" a collection code?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** It survives, held by the Museum of Bale and still awaiting accession; "WN" means "without number", not an institution. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: WN-V6` and `institution` on *Histriasaurus boscarollii*.

**Evidence.**
- dallavecchia1998a, p. 120: "The specimen will be stored at the Museum of the Municipality of Bale and is at present without number"; p. 122: "The name Histriasaurus boscarollii is proposed for this new taxon", on a single specimen (so the holotype is fixed by monotypy; our reading, since the paper does not use the word).
- dallavecchia1999a, p. 33, Fig. 47: "WN-V6, posterior dorsal vertebra, holotype of Histriasaurus boscarollii".
- vanderlinden2025a, p. 3: "WN – 'without number,' an informal designation for specimens awaiting accession/Museum of Bale, Croatia"; p. 9, Table 1 footnote: "Bellardini et al. (2023) incorrectly state that the holotype of H. boscarollii is 'lost'; the vertebra still exist and is available for study".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lost.* Bellardini and colleagues list the holotype as lost in a table (bellardini2023a, p. 2597, Table 1); Van der Linden and colleagues report it extant.
- *WN as a collection acronym.* Apesteguía (2007) defines "WN-V, Vertebrate Paleontology Collection of the Municipality of Bale, Croatia" (apesteguía2007a, p. 535); the describing paper says "without number".

**Open.** A permanent catalogue number. Two further bones are at the Institute of Geology, Zagreb, "Nos IG-1 and Nos IG-2" (dallavecchia1998a, p. 106).
