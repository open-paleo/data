# *Hesperosaurus*

## What site and level in the Morrison is the *Hesperosaurus* holotype from?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** The S. B. Smith Ranch in Johnson County, Wyoming, 5 m above the base of the Morrison, recorded as `part: lower` with no member. Carpenter and colleagues (2001) name no quarry and no member. Governs `location.locality: S. B. Smith Ranch`, `location.part: lower` and the empty `location.member` on [*Hesperosaurus mjosi*](../../../genera/H/Hesperosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carpenter2001b, p. 56: "Holotype Locality. Five meters above base (Weege, personal communication), Morrison Formation, S. B. Smith Ranch, Johnson County, Wyoming (exact locality information on file at Hayashibara Museum of Natural History …)"; and "The specimen was found in a fine-grained sandstone 5 m above the contact between the Morrison Formation and the underlying Windy Hill Member of the Sundance Formation (Weege, personal communication). The Morrison Formation is about 53 m in the area, just south of Buffalo, Wyoming."
- galton2010a, p. 195: the holotype "was excavated from 5 m above the base of the Morrison Formation (so in the Salt Wash Member) in Johnson County, Wyoming".

**Ruled out.**
- *Buffalo Quarry.* The paper's only "Buffalo" is the town near which it gives the formation's thickness; it names no quarry.

**Open.** The Salt Wash Member. Galton's "so in" infers the member from the height above the base and cites no study of the section. Hartman and colleagues state that "The Morrison Formation in central Wyoming is considered undivided" (hartman2019a, p. 4); whether that holds for Johnson County, in north-central Wyoming, is our inference. A stratigraphic study of the Morrison near Buffalo that names its members would settle it.

## What is the *Hesperosaurus* holotype's current number?

*2026-08-02*

**Conclusion.** FPDM-V9674. Carpenter and colleagues (2001) published the holotype as HMNH 001; it later carried HMNS 14 at the Hayashibara Museum, and Sonoda and colleagues (2016) record it among the Hayashibara collections transferred to the Fukui Prefectural Dinosaur Museum. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [FPDM-V9674]` and `type_specimen.institution: FPDM` on [*Hesperosaurus mjosi*](../../../genera/H/Hesperosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carpenter2001b, p. 56: "Holotype. HMNH 001, consisting of a nearly complete, disarticulated skull".
- galton2016a, p. 190: "*Hesperosaurus mjosi* Carpenter et al., 2001, part of holotype HMNS 14".
- sonoda2016a, p. 93: the Hayashibara collections went to the Fukui Prefectural Dinosaur Museum "following the closure of HMNS in 2015"; "the holotype of *Hesperosaurus mjosi* Carpenter, Miles and Cloward 2001 (FPDM-V9674) is included in the specimens".
- jones2026a, Stegosauria table: "HMNH 001—partial skull and skeleton (Carpenter et al., 2001)".

**Ruled out.**
- *HMNH 001 as the current number.* It is the number in the describing paper, which Jones (2026) repeats; Sonoda and colleagues (2016) give the renumbering.

**Open.** Nothing.
