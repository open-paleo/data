# *Helioceratops*

## Is the *Helioceratops* holotype from Shanqian, and from the upper Quantou?

*2026-08-26*

**Conclusion.** The record names Liufangzi, the finest site the describing paper gives, and no part of the formation. Shanqian and the upper Quantou are the site and horizon of *Changchunsaurus*, whose quarry Butler and colleagues (2011) say also yielded the *Helioceratops* holotype; no paper read applies either to *Helioceratops* directly. Governs `location.locality: Liufangzi` and the empty `location.part` on [*Helioceratops brachygnathus*](../../../genera/H/Helioceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jin2009a, p. 201: "Locality and horizon: Liufangzi locality, close to Gongzhuling City (Jilin province, China); Quantou Formation (late Early Cretaceous or early Late Cretaceous)".
- zan2005a, p. 183: the *Changchunsaurus* type locality and horizon is "吉林省公主岭市刘房子镇山前，泉头组上部" ("Shanqian, Liufangzi Town, Gongzhuling City, Jilin Province; upper part of the Quantou Formation", translated).
- butler2011a, p. 668: the quarry that yielded the *Changchunsaurus* holotype "has also yielded the holotype specimen (cranial material) of *Helioceratops brachygnathus*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Gongzhuling Quarry.* No source read names a quarry for Gongzhuling; Jin and colleagues write "close to Gongzhuling City" and Butler and colleagues "near Gongzhuling City" (butler2011a, p. 668).

**Open.** Whether to add Shanqian to the locality and `part: upper`. Both follow from Butler and colleagues' sentence joined to Zan and colleagues' type-locality line, which is an inference; Zan and colleagues (2006) and Jin and colleagues (2010), the papers Butler and colleagues cite for "further locality details", have not been read. The *Changchunsaurus* referred specimens carry numbers in the same JLUM L0204-Y series as the *Helioceratops* holotype (zan2005a, p. 183), which fits one collecting site but does not show it.
