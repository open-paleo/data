# *Hamititan*

## Where is the *Hamititan* holotype HM V22 held?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** The Hami Museum, Hami, Xinjiang, under its own registry entry `HM`. The record had been filed under the Museum für Naturkunde, Berlin, through an alias left over from when the registry keyed that museum "HM". Sabaj gives HM to the Great North Museum: Hancock, Newcastle, but no specimen in the data is held there, so no collision exists within the registry and the bare code stands. Governs `type_specimen.institution: HM` on [*Hamititan xinjiangensis*](../../../genera/H/Hamititan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wang2021a, Holotype: "An articulated series of seven anterior to middle caudal (HM V22), including the proximal portions of three chevrons, housed at Hami Museum"; the affiliations list "Hami Museum, Hami 839000, China".
- sabaj2020a: "HM: Great North Museum: Hancock, Newcastle upon Tyne. *Formerly*: Hancock Museum (1884–2006). *Current as*: HM (fossils)."

**Ruled out.**
- *The Berlin museum.* Nothing ties HM V22 to Berlin; the alias was a remnant of an old key.
- *`HM-CN`.* The ISO suffix disambiguates two institutions the registry holds; the Hancock is not among them.

**Open.** If a Hancock specimen is added, both entries take country suffixes in the same commit.
