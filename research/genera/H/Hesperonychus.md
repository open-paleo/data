# *Hesperonychus*

## How high in the Dinosaur Park Formation is the *Hesperonychus* holotype?

*2026-08-26*

**Conclusion.** Unknown from the papers read. Cullen and colleagues (2021) place the one *Hesperonychus* specimen of known stratigraphic position 37 m above the Oldman–Dinosaur Park contact, but do not say which specimen it is. Longrich and Currie (2009) refer pedal phalanges from Dinosaur Provincial Park and other sites as well as the holotype from 20 km east of the park, so the specimen may be a referred one. Governs the absence of a measured level in `location.notes` on [*Hesperonychus elizabethae*](../../../genera/H/Hesperonychus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cullen2021a, accepted manuscript, p. 9, Biostratigraphy of DPF small theropods: *Saurornitholestes* "does not overlap with *Hesperonychus* (at 37 m above the ODC). However, the latter has only a single specimen of known biostratigraphic position (despite other referred specimens being known; Longrich and Currie 2009)".
- longrich2009a, p. 5003: "The holotype was collected from exposures of the Dinosaur Park Formation located on the south side of the Red Deer River, ≈20 km east of Dinosaur Provincial Park"; "Referred material was collected from Dinosaur Provincial Park and surrounding badlands and other locations in southern Alberta", with stratigraphic data for some specimens given in the paper's *Specimen Data* appendix.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Which specimen sits at 37 m; recording the figure as the holotype's level would rest on no source read. Cullen and colleagues' Supplementary Table S3, or the *Specimen Data* section of Longrich and Currie's SI Appendix, would settle it; neither has been read. The appendix may also give the position of the holotype's site, which the main text does not print.
