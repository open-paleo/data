# *Harpymimus*

## Which site is the *Harpymimus* holotype from?

*2026-09-15*

**Conclusion.** Khuren Dukh. Kobayashi and Barsbold (2005) give only a province, but Gates and colleagues (2018) name *Harpymimus* among the finds from the Khuren Dukh beds and mark its site on their locality map. Governs `location.locality: Khuren Dukh` on [*Harpymimus okladnikovi*](../../../genera/H/Harpymimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kobayashi2005b, p. 98: "Type Locality and Horizon. Dundgov (Eastern Gobi Province), the Shinekhudug Formation"; the introduction on the same page refers to "the single specimen of *Harpymimus okladnikovi*".
- gates2018a, p. 2: *Choyrodon* specimens "were discovered at the Khuren Dukh locality of the Lower Member of the Khuren Dukh Formation (Ito et al., 2006) … the same Lower Cretaceous beds that yielded the iguanodontian *Altirhinus kurzanovi* (Norman, 1998), the basal ornithomimosaur *Harpymimus okladnikovi* (Barsbold & Perle, 1984)".
- gates2018a, p. 3, Fig. 1 caption: "Contour lines demarcate topography around fossil sites of Khuren Dukh … 4-site for *Harpimimus okladnikovi*".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** The site's position. Gates and colleagues' map marks site 4 without a coordinate, and Barsbold and Perle's (1984) description has not been checked for one. That site 4 is the holotype is our reading: Kobayashi and Barsbold describe *Harpymimus* from one specimen.

## Which formation is the *Harpymimus* holotype from?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** The Khuren Dukh Formation, in the beds that Gates and colleagues (2018) assign to its Lower Member. Kobayashi and Barsbold (2005) give the Shinekhudug Formation, a name Ito and colleagues (2006) replaced for these beds. Governs `location.formation: Khuren Dukh` on [*Harpymimus okladnikovi*](../../../genera/H/Harpymimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kobayashi2005b, p. 98: "Type Locality and Horizon. Dundgov (Eastern Gobi Province), the Shinekhudug Formation".
- gates2018a, p. 2: the Khuren Dukh locality "of the Lower Member of the Khuren Dukh Formation (Ito et al., 2006)" is in "the same Lower Cretaceous beds that yielded … the basal ornithomimosaur *Harpymimus okladnikovi*".
- jones2026c, p. 503: "Khuren Dukh Fm., Mongolia *Early Cretaceous, Albian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Shinekhudug Formation.* The record once held it. The beds were reassigned to the newly defined Khuren Dukh Formation by Ito and colleagues (2006), and Gates and colleagues place *Harpymimus* in them.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What age is the *Harpymimus* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Albian, the age of the Lower Member beds at Khuren Dukh. Governs `period.stage: [Albian]` on [*Harpymimus okladnikovi*](../../../genera/H/Harpymimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gates2018a, p. 4: for the same beds, "Approximate age is middle to late Albian (Nichols, Matsukawa & Ito, 2006)".
- jones2026c, p. 503: "*Early Cretaceous, Albian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hauterivian–Barremian.* The record once held it, with the Shinekhudug assignment. No source read for this note gives that age for the Khuren Dukh beds.

**Open.** The age is reached through Gates and colleagues' statement that *Harpymimus* comes from the same beds as *Choyrodon*; no paper dates the *Harpymimus* site itself.
