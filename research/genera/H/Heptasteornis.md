# *Heptasteornis*

## Is the Heptasteornis holotype BMNH A 4359, and is A 1528 part of it?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** The holotype is BMNH A 4359; A 1528 is a paratype, not a co-type. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [BMNH A4359]` and `specimen_type: holotype` on *Heptasteornis andrewsi*, with A 1528 in the notes.

**Evidence.**
- harrison1975a, p. 566: "Holotype: distal end of a left tibiotarsus A 4359 … Paratype: another distal end of a left tibiotarsus, A 1528."
- naish2004b, p. 388: "BMNH A4359 and BMNH A 1528 the holotype and paratype"; p. 393: "It is important taxonomically that BMNH A4359 is the holotype of Heptasteornis andrewsi".

**Ruled out.**
- *BMNH A 4358.* Csiki and Grigorescu print "BMNH A.4358" twice (csiki1998a, p. 89), but "BMNH A.1528, 1588, 4359" on p. 96; the 4358 is the paper's own slip, confirmed on the page image.
- *A syntype pair.* The describing paper names one holotype and one paratype.

**Open.** Nothing.
