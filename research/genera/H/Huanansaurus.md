# *Huanansaurus*

## Which formation is the *Huanansaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Nanxiong Formation, as Lü and colleagues (2015) give it. Governs `location.formation: Nanxiong` on [*Huanansaurus ganzhouensis*](../../../genera/H/Huanansaurus.yml). Whether "Nanxiong" is the right name for Ganzhou Basin beds is the question of [Nanxiong Formation and the Ganzhou Basin](../../topics/nanxiong-and-guifeng.md).

**Evidence.**
- lü2015b, p. 3: "Type locality and horizon. In the vicinity of the Ganzhou Railway Station …, Ganzhou City; Campanian-Maastrichtian; Nanxiong Formation (Upper Cretaceous)".
- jones2026c, Oviraptoridae table: "Nanxiong Fm., China *Late Cretaceous, Campanian/ Maastrichtian*".
- jin2022a, p. 3: the railway-station site is "the same site as the holotype of the oviraptorid *Huanansaurus ganzhouensis*"; "if the result of the geological survey is correct …, the strata of all the localities mentioned above should belong to the Hekou Formation, Guifeng Group".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hekou Formation as settled.* The record once held it. Jin and colleagues (2022) place the site in the Hekou only on the condition that a regional survey is right, and say the sites' "exact stratigraphic positions and correlations need more investigation".

**Open.** Which Guifeng Group formation the railway-station beds belong to. A stratigraphic study of the site would settle it, as the topic note says.
