# *Hylaeosaurus*

## Which NHMUK register holds the *Hylaeosaurus* holotype?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** The old register, OR. The redescription of the holotype prints NHMUK PV OR3775 at every mention, as does a later paper; earlier papers write the number in the R register. R and OR are separate registers, and the record follows the paper that redescribes the specimen in the collection, as [*Polacanthoides*](../../../genera/P/Polacanthoides.yml) holds NHMUK PV OR 2584 against an earlier R2584. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NHMUK PV OR 3775]` on [*Hylaeosaurus armatus*](../../../genera/H/Hylaeosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- raven2020a: "Holotype—NHMUK PV OR3775. Partial articulated skeleton", and the same number in every figure caption.
- pond2023a: "the holotype of Hylaeosaurus (NHMUK PV OR 3775; Raven et al., 2020)".
- blows1996a: "The holotype of *Hylaeosaurus armatus* (BMNH R3775, Fig. 4d)"; coombs1978a: "BM (NH) R3775".

**Ruled out.**
- *NHMUK PV R 3775.* The register of the older citations, which the redescription does not use.

**Open.** Nothing.
