# *Hoplitosaurus*

## Which member and stages does *Hoplitosaurus* take?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** The Chilson Member of the Lakota Formation, Berriasian–Valanginian. The holotype was found with the [*Osmakasaurus*](../O/Osmakasaurus.md) holotype in the same two pieces of rock, at a horizon Boyd and colleagues (2015) place within the Chilson, and the taxon takes the member's range rather than the formation's. Governs `location.member: Chilson` and `stage: [Berriasian, Valanginian]` on [*Hoplitosaurus marshi*](../../../genera/H/Hoplitosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1909a, pp. 292–293: USNM 4753 "was associated with the fragmentary skeleton, No. 4752, U.S.N.M., described by Dr. F. A. Lucas as *Stegosaurus marshii* and later referred by him to the new genus *Hoplitosaurus*. Most of the elements preserved were inclosed in two large pieces of rock."
- boyd2015b: the horizon at "the contact between the Unkpapa Sandstone and the Lakota Formation, in or near the same horizon that yields abundant specimens of *Cycadeoides* … is positioned within the Chilson Member of the Lakota Formation (Gott, Wolcott & Bowles, 1974)".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Lakota's Berriasian–Barremian.* It is the formation's range; the holotype's member is dated more narrowly.

**Open.** Nothing.
