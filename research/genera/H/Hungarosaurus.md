# *Hungarosaurus*

## How is the *Hungarosaurus* holotype's number written?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** HNHM Gyn/404. Ősi (2005) writes MTM Gyn/404, and MTM is an alias of HNHM, the Hungarian Natural History Museum's current code in the registry. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [HNHM Gyn/404]` and `type_specimen.institution: HNHM` on [*Hungarosaurus tormai*](../../../genera/H/Hungarosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ősi2005a, p. 371: "Holotype—MTM Gyn/404., skull fragments (premaxilla with one tooth, left lacrimal …".
- jones2026a, Nodosaurinae table: "TM Gyn/404—partial skull and skeleton; MTM Gyn/405, 406, 407—fragments".

**Ruled out.**
- *TM Gyn/404.* Jones (2026) drops the M from the holotype, though not from the referred numbers in the same cell. TM is the Transvaal Museum's code.

**Open.** Nothing.
