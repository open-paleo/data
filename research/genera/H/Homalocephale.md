# *Homalocephale*

## Is the *Homalocephale* holotype from the Western Sayr at Nemegt? (revisited)

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Yes. On Gradziński and colleagues' Text-fig. 2, site 8 lies on the slope beside the dashed sayr line that runs north, unbroken, from the "Western Sayr" label; no other sayr passes near it. Maryańska and Osmólska put the skeleton in a sayr, so the map's only sayr at the site is the one it lay in. Governs `location.locality: Nemegt, Western Sayr, site 8` on [*Homalocephale calathocercos*](../../../genera/H/Homalocephale.yml). Supersedes the **Open** line of the 2026-08-26 entry.

**Evidence.**
- gradziński1969a, p. 39, Text-fig. 2: the symbol for site 8 on the slope along the dashed sayr line running north from the "Western Sayr" label; the Northern and Central Sayrs lie well to the east (checked on the page image).
- maryańska1974a, p. 46: the skeleton "was articulated laying on a shelf above the bottom of the sayr".

**Ruled out.**
- *No sayr named.* The text places the skeleton in a sayr and the map shows one sayr at the site.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Homalocephale* holotype from the Western Sayr at Nemegt?

*2026-08-26*

**Conclusion.** The record names site 8 at Nemegt and no sayr, but the map that numbers the site places it beside the northern arm of the Western Sayr. Maryańska and Osmólska (1974) give the type locality as Gradziński and colleagues' (1969) site 8 and say the skeleton lay "on a shelf above the bottom of the sayr" without naming the sayr. On Gradziński and colleagues' Text-fig. 2, the symbol for site 8 sits on the slope along the dashed sayr line that runs north from the "Western Sayr" label (our reading of the figure). Governs `location.locality: Nemegt, site 8` on [*Homalocephale calathocercos*](../../../genera/H/Homalocephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maryańska1974a, p. 56: "*Type locality:* Nemegt, Nemegt Basin, Gobi Desert, Mongolian People's Republic", citing Gradziński and colleagues' "Text-fig. 2, No. 8"; p. 46: the skeleton "was articulated laying on a shelf above the bottom of the sayr".
- gradziński1969a, p. 38: "The great Western Sayr bounds the area in the W and SW"; p. 39, Text-fig. 2 (map; the legend gives a dashed line for "SAYR"), whose caption on p. 38 explains site 8 as a "skull and partial post-cranial skeleton" of a new ornithischian; p. 41, Text-fig. 3 puts sites 8, 7 and 5 on one bed in the Upper Nemegt Beds.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether to restore "Western Sayr". The map supports it, but no text names the sayr for site 8, and the reading rests on following one dashed line across the figure. The expedition's field records, or a later paper that names the sayr, would settle it.
