# *Heterodontosaurus*

## Is "zone B/1" a horizon for the *Heterodontosaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-26*

**Conclusion.** No source read gives it for the holotype, so the record omits it. "Zone B/1" belongs to Ellenberger's footprint zonation of the Stormberg, in which zone B covers the upper Elliot and Clarens and B/1 is the "niveau de Moyeni" (Moyeni level). Crompton and Charig (1962) and Norman and colleagues (2011) place the holotype on the mountain behind the Tyindini trading store with no zone. Governs `location.locality: Tyindini trading store` and the absence of a zone in `location` on [*Heterodontosaurus tucki*](../../../genera/H/Heterodontosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- crompton1962a, p. 1075: "*Locality.* At an altitude of about 6,200 ft. on the mountain behind Tyindini trading store, in the district of Herschel, Cape Province, South Africa".
- norman2011a, p. 189: "1890 m above sea-level on the mountain behind Tyindini trading store, Herschel District, Eastern Cape Province, Republic of South Africa (30°32′S, 27°32′E; Kitching & Raath, 1984: table 1)".
- olsen1984a, p. 94: "the upper Stormberg assemblage (Zone B of Ellenberger, 1970)", and of the footprint genera "founded on upper Elliot and Clarens material (Zone B of Ellenberger, 1970)"; p. 106, references: Ellenberger (1974), "Le Stormberg Superieur - I. Le biome de la zone B/1 ou niveau de Moyeni: ses biocenoses".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether Ellenberger placed the Tyindini site in B/1. Ellenberger (1970, 1974) and Kitching and Raath (1984), whose table 1 is the source of the coordinate and may carry a zone, have not been read.

## Is the *Heterodontosaurus* holotype from the Clarens or the upper Elliot Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Clarens. The referred skull SAM-PK-K1332 is from the upper Elliot, below the holotype's horizon, and the record's formation follows the holotype. Governs `location.formation: Clarens` on [*Heterodontosaurus tucki*](../../../genera/H/Heterodontosaurus.yml); the stages follow from it (see [Clarens](../../stratigraphy/c/clarens.md)).

**Evidence.**
- norman2011a, p. 189, for the holotype SAM-PK-K337: "Stratigraphical occurrence: Clarens Formation (formerly 'Cave Sandstone', Stormberg Series)".
- norman2011a, p. 191, for SAM-PK-K1332: "From the upper part of the Elliot Formation (formerly 'upper Red Beds'), Stormberg Series; probably of Early Jurassic age (stratigraphically lower than the horizon at which the holotype was found)".
- jones2026a, Ornithischia table: "SAM-PK-K337—skull and skeleton; SAM-PK-K1332—skull and skeleton; …", "Upper Elliot Fm., South Africa *Early Jurassic, Hettangian/Sinemurian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper Elliot Formation.* Jones (2026) gives the unit of the referred skull in a row that lists the holotype with the referred specimens.

**Open.** Nothing.
