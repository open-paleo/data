# *Huayangosaurus*

## Should *Huayangosaurus* sit in a Huayangosauridae node?

*2026-10-15*

**Conclusion.** No; it stays at Stegosauria incertae sedis. Huayangosauridae is a current, defined clade, not a deprecated one, but its content changes from one analysis to the next, so a node would rest on no agreed membership. The earlier summary's "recent works deprecate the node" rested only on a reference work. Governs `parent: Stegosauria` and the dispute on [*Huayangosaurus*](../../../genera/H/Huayangosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maidment2008a: "This study is also the first phylogenetic analysis to include Gigantspinosaurus, which is recovered as the most basal stegosaur."
- madzia2021a: "Composition. Under the primary reference phylogeny, Huayangosauridae comprises Chungkingosaurus jiangbeiensis and Huayangosaurus taibaii."
- sánchez-fenollosa2025a: "Phylogenetic analyses suggest that Stegosauria is divided into two major clades: Huayangosauridae and Stegosauridae"; its Huayangosauridae "included *I. mollensis*, *H. taibaii*, *B. primitivus*, *Ba. baojiensis*, and *G. sichuanensis*".
- raven2023a: Dacentrurinae "could be a similar clade to that of Huayangosauridae of Madzia et al. (2021)", but the labile non-stegosaurine stegosaurids leave no such clade named.

**Ruled out.**
- *"Recent works deprecate the node."* Only Jones (2026) calls the group "of dubious usefulness" (jones2026a); the analyses read use or define it.
- *A Huayangosauridae node.* Its membership is unstable across analyses.

**Open.** Whether a later analysis settles the clade's content.
