# *Haestasaurus*

## Which NHMUK numbers make up the *Haestasaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** NHMUK R1870, the humerus, ulna and radius, and NHMUK R1868, the skin impression, from Upchurch and colleagues (2015). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NHMUK PV R 1868, NHMUK PV R 1870]` on [*Haestasaurus becklesii*](../../../genera/H/Haestasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- upchurch2015a, p. 9, Systematic Palaeontology: "Holotype— NHMUK R1870, an associated left humerus, ulna and radius, and NHMUK R1868, a portion of skin impression from near the elbow region". The same paragraph says that the metacarpal NHMUK R1869 "cannot belong to the same individual as NHMUK R1870".
- jones2026b, p. 375: "BMNH R1870—forelimb; BMNH R 1868—skin impression".
- molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropods, Turiasauria: "NHMUK R1868-1870".

**Ruled out.**
- *R1870 alone.* The record once held only the forelimb.
- *R1868 to R1870.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give a range that takes in R1869, which Upchurch and colleagues exclude.

**Open.** Nothing.
