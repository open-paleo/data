# *Hesperonyx*

## Where does *Hesperonyx* sit?

*2026-10-24*

**Conclusion.** Dryomorpha, with the dispute block kept. Three trees place it inside Dryosauridae and three outside, and its describers call it "a dryomorphan of uncertain affinities"; no author argues for either position, so the block describes a split between analyses. Governs `parent: Dryomorpha` and the dispute block on [*Hesperonyx martinhotomasorum*](../../../genera/H/Hesperonyx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rotatori2023a, p. e2310066-16: "the recovery of H. martinhotomasorum within Dryosauridae is probably due to the long branch attraction effect and different optimization criteria. ... we consider H. martinhotomasorum as dryomorphan of uncertain affinities."
- bertozzo2025a, Fig. 10A (figure reading): inside Dryosauridae under implied weighting.
- bertozzo2025b, Fig. 15 (figure reading): outside Dryosauridae, as the sister of the ankylopollexian clade, under both weightings.
- huang2026a, Extended Data Fig. 1 (figure reading): the sister of *Draconyx* at a node labeled Dryosauridae.

**Ruled out.**
- *Dryosauridae.* Not shared by the parsimony trees of Rotatori and colleagues or by Bertozzo and colleagues (2025b); only Jones (2026), a reference work, states it alone.

**Open.** Nothing.
