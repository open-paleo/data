# *Haplocanthosaurus*

## Is *Haplocanthosaurus* a diplodocoid?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it sits at Neosauropoda incertae sedis. Some analyses recover it as the earliest diplodocoid, and at least as many independent matrices recover it as a basal macronarian or an unplaced neosauropod. Governs `parent: Neosauropoda`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Haplocanthosaurus*](../../../genera/H/Haplocanthosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- whitlock2011a, p. 884: "Haplocanthosaurus is recovered here as the basal-most diplodocoid".
- gomez2024a, Fig. 21, p. 33: *Haplocanthosaurus* as the first branch of Macronaria.
- carballido2011a, Fig. 21B, p. 633: a basal macronarian.
- mannion2011a, p. 540: "In some MPTs, Haplocanthosaurus is the most basal diplodocoid", in others a macronarian or a basal eusauropod.

**Ruled out.**
- *Diplodocoidea as the parent.* Analyses on several independent matrices place it in Macronaria.
- *Eusauropoda as the parent.* Only some trees of Mannion and colleagues (2011) place it outside Neosauropoda.

**Open.** Whether the matrices converge.
