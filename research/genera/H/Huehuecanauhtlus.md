# *Huehuecanauhtlus*

## Which stage does the *Huehuecanauhtlus* holotype take?

*2026-11-25*

**Conclusion.** The Maastrichtian. Its type locality, Barranca Los Bonetes, is the hadrosaur site of Benammi and colleagues (2005), which both papers place at 19°03′N, 100°42′W. The describing paper's Santonian is a second-hand date, and Benammi and colleagues' pollen, from below the hadrosaur levels in the same section, is no older than the Maastrichtian (see [Cutzamala](../../stratigraphy/c/cutzamala.md)). Governs `stage: [Maastrichtian]` on [*Huehuecanauhtlus tiquichensis*](../../../genera/H/Huehuecanauhtlus.yml), replacing `[Santonian]`, and its pointers to benammi2005a and martini2009a.

**Evidence.**
- ramírez-velasco2012a, p. 382: "Barranca Los Bonetes (Fig. 1) is an area within the Tuzantla municipality, located near the limits of this and Tiquicheo municipality, in the State of Michoacán, Mexico (coordinates 19°03′N and 100°42′W)."
- benammi2005a, p. 431: the remains were found "in Barranca Los Bonetes, coordinates Lat 19º03'N and Long 100º42'W" (translated).
- benammi2005a, p. 433: "the mere presence of Nudopollis indicates a maximum age of Maastrichtian" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Santonian from the describing paper.* A date the paper cites from an abstract and a master's thesis rather than reports.

**Open.** Neither Benammi and colleagues (2005) nor Martini and colleagues (2009) name the stage of the hadrosaur beds themselves.
