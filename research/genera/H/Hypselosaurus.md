# *Hypselosaurus*

## Which unit is the *Hypselosaurus priscus* holotype from?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** No formation is recorded. Matheron (1869) places the type in the detrital layers at the base of his Rognac stage, and Le Loeuff (1993) gives only "Rognac (Aix Basin)". The Aix-en-Provence map notice puts the lower Argiles et grès à Reptiles at the base of the Rognacian (see [Grès à Reptiles](../../topics/grès-à-reptiles.md)), but no source read names the unit at Rognac. Governs the empty `location.formation` on [*Hypselosaurus priscus*](../../../genera/H/Hypselosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- matheron1869b, p. 783: "Dans les couches détritiques de la base de l'étage de Rognac : chélonien, grand saurien" ("In the detrital layers at the base of the Rognac stage: chelonian, large saurian", translated).
- leloeuff1993a, p. 110: "In 1869, MATHERON erected the species *Hypselosaurus priscus* for a few scattered bones collected at Rognac (Aix Basin)".
- catzigras1969a, p. 6: "C8a. Argiles et grès inférieurs à Reptiles (250 à 300 m). Ils forment au Nord de Fuveau, la base de la série rognacienne" ("Lower clays and sandstones with reptiles … They form, north of Fuveau, the base of the Rognacian series", translated), below "C8b. Calcaires de Rognac".

**Ruled out.**
- *The lower Argiles et grès à Reptiles on the strength of the Rousset bones.* The map notice reports that "Dans les environs de Rousset, les grès, souvent pisolithiques, ont livré des ossements de *Rhabdodon priseum* et surtout de *Hypselosaurus priscus*" ("Around Rousset the sandstones, often pisolithic, have yielded bones of *Rhabdodon priseum* and above all of *Hypselosaurus priscus*", translated; catzigras1969a, p. 7). These are bones referred to the species from Rousset, not Matheron's type from Rognac, so they place referred material in the unit and say nothing about the holotype.

**Open.** The unit at Rognac. Matheron's basal detrital layers beneath the Rognac limestones fit the notice's sequence, the lower Argiles et grès à Reptiles beneath the Calcaires de Rognac, but the notice does not name Rognac as a locality, and the assignment would be our correlation. No source read applies the bare name "Grès à Reptiles" to this type either. A paper that names the unit at Rognac would settle both. The stage turns on the same question: Le Loeuff (1993) gives "Early Maastrichtian of the Aix-en-Provence Basin" (leloeuff1993a, p. 105), while Tortosa and colleagues (2014) date "the upper part of the Lower Rognacian" to the late Campanian (tortosa2014a, p. 66), so a type at the base of the Rognacian would be older than the record's Maastrichtian (our inference).
