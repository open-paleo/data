# *Heishansaurus*

## Which unit is the *Heishansaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-26*

**Conclusion.** None is recorded. Bohlin (1953) describes the Chia-yü-kuan sediments by lithology and fossils and names no unit. The Minhe Formation comes from Weishampel and colleagues' (2004) compilation, which cites a personal communication for it; Dong (2002) puts the same locality in the Early Cretaceous Xinminpu Group. Dong's Dacaotan (大草滩, "large grass plain") is the pinyin of Bohlin's camp Ta-ts'ao-t'an, which Bohlin glosses "the large grass plain" (our reading of the two names). Governs the empty `location.formation` and `location.group` on [*Heishansaurus pachycephalus*](../../../genera/H/Heishansaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bohlin1953a, p. 43: "North-West of Chia-yü-kuan … a small sediment area was investigated during September and the first days of October 1930. Our camp was at Ta-ts'ao-t'an"; the sediments are "to a great extent developed as paper shales in which *Estheria* and *Lycoptera*-like fishes were found". p. 47: the type is "Field nr 60", "the species from Chia-yü-kuan".
- maryańska1975a, p. 135: lists the unit at Chia Yü Kuan as the informal "Chia Yü Kuan formation", with *Heishansaurus pachycephalus* and *Chiayüsaurus lacustris* in its fauna, and states that "The exact age of these deposits was not determined".
- weishampel2004b, p. 598, Late Cretaceous, under 48.8 Gansu: "Minhe Formation (Bohlin 1953; Maryanska 1977; Mateer, pers. comm.)", with "?Pachycephalosauria indet. (= *Heishansaurus pachycephalus*)" and "Age: Campanian-Maastrichtian (Mateer, pers. comm.)".
- dong2002a, p. 283: "产于嘉峪关北大草滩的 *Heishansaurus pachicephalus*" ("*Heishansaurus pachicephalus*, from Dacaotan north of Jiayuguan", translated); Dacaotan is now a reservoir of the Jiuquan Iron and Steel Company. The three ankylosaurs of the passage "均产自河西走廊地区早白垩世新民堡群" ("all come from the Early Cretaceous Xinminpu Group of the Hexi Corridor", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Minhe Formation.* Its only source is the compilation entry, and both its formation and its Campanian–Maastrichtian age rest on Mateer's personal communication. Bohlin names no unit, and Dong places the locality in a different, Early Cretaceous unit.

**Open.** Whether to record the Xinminpu Group, as [*Chiayusaurus*](../../../genera/C/Chiayusaurus.yml), also from Chia-yü-kuan, and [*Peishansaurus*](../../../genera/P/Peishansaurus.yml), from the same sentence of Dong's, do (see [*Peishansaurus*](../P/Peishansaurus.md)). Dong's sentence is loose: it also puts *Sauroplites*, from Tebch in Inner Mongolia, in the Hexi Corridor (see [*Sauroplites*](../S/Sauroplites.md)). The stage, Campanian–Maastrichtian, is the Minhe compilation's and has no other source.
