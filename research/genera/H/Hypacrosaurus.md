# *Hypacrosaurus*

## How far above Tolman Ferry, and how high, was the *Hypacrosaurus altispinus* holotype found?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** Four miles above Tolman Ferry, fifty feet above the Red Deer River, as Brown (1913) gives it and Lull and Wright (1942) repeat. Governs `location.locality: Four miles above Tolman Ferry` and the height in `location.notes` on [*Hypacrosaurus altispinus*](../../../genera/H/Hypacrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown1913a, p. 395: "*Locality.* Red Deer River, Alberta, Canada. Four miles above Tolman Ferry. Fifty feet above river, five-hundred? feet below top of formation."
- lull1942a, p. 208: "Holotype: No. 5204 A.M.N.H."; "Locality: Red Deer River, 4 miles above Tolman Ferry, 50 feet above the river, Alberta."

**Ruled out.**
- *Five miles above Tolman Ferry, at a 150 ft level.* No source read gives either figure for AMNH 5204. Lull and Wright give five miles for two other specimens: the *Cheneosaurus tolmanensis* holotype, GSC 2246, "about 5 miles above Tolman Ferry" (lull1942a, p. 185), and the referred skull AMNH 5278, "5 miles north of Tolman Ferry, east side of Red Deer River and 115 feet above the river" (lull1942a, p. 208).

**Open.** The member. No source read places AMNH 5204 in a member of the Horseshoe Canyon Formation, and Brown's distance below the top of the formation is queried in his own text. A measured level for the site would settle it.
