# *Hippodraco*

## What stage does *Hippodraco* take?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Barremian. The Yellow Cat Member is diachronous, so a taxon from it takes the stage of its own dated site, and the describing paper dates Andrew's Site from a detrital-zircon age of 124 Ma above the quarry's caprock, which is Barremian on the current chart. The Valanginian set on 2026-08-05 rested on a tephra in the upper Yellow Cat at Utahraptor Ridge, which the [Yellow Cat](../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.md) entry of 2026-09-30 no longer applies to other sites. Governs `stage: [Barremian]` on [*Hippodraco scutodens*](../../../genera/H/Hippodraco.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mcdonald2010b, p. 5: "The age of the Andrew's Site quarry is readily determined to be late Barremian to earliest Aptian, as the aforementioned date of 124 Ma was obtained from detrital zircons sampled from mudstone and sandstone layers stratigraphically above the caprock".
- gale2020a, p. 1031: base of the Aptian 121.4 Ma, so 124 Ma is Barremian (our arithmetic).

**Ruled out.**
- *Valanginian.* It dates the upper Yellow Cat at Utahraptor Ridge, not Andrew's Site.
- *Aptian.* "Earliest Aptian" places 124 Ma on an older chart.

**Open.** A maximum age for the beds above the quarry does not by itself fix the quarry's age; a date from the site would settle it.
