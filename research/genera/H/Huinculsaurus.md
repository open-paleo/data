# *Huinculsaurus*

## Where does *Huinculsaurus* sit within Abelisauroidea?

*2026-10-15*

**Conclusion.** Abelisauroidea incertae sedis. The describing paper recovers it as the sister of *Elaphrosaurus* within a paraphyletic noasaurid grade, Cerroni and colleagues (2022) recover it as a noasaurine, Hendrickx and colleagues (2024) leave it unresolved within Abelisauroidea, and three later analyses prune it as unstable. Abelisauroidea is the level all of them share. Governs `parent: Abelisauroidea` and the dispute on [*Huinculsaurus*](../../../genera/H/Huinculsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- baiano2020a: "places Huinculsaurus as the sister taxon of Elaphrosaurus, and this clade is part of a series of noasaurid-grade lineages which form a paraphyletic series leading to Abelisauridae"; the elaphrosaurine reading is "considered as tentative due to the relatively poor completeness of this specimen".
- cerroni2022a: "the phylogenetic analysis recovered Huinculsaurus as a Noasaurinae (the basal member of this group) … unlike the original description that retrieved it as an elaphrosaurine".
- hendrickx2024a: "Abelisauroidea corresponding to a polytomy comprised of the taxa *Noasaurus*, *Huinculsaurus*, and *Ligabueino*".
- pol2024a, jiménezvelandia2025a and canale2026a list *Huinculsaurus* among the unstable taxa they prune.

**Ruled out.**
- *Elaphrosaurinae.* The earlier parent, followed from Jones (2026), who relays the describing paper (jones2026c); a reference work cannot carry the placement, and later analyses disagree.
- *Noasauridae.* The describing paper's noasaurids are a paraphyletic grade, and Hendrickx and colleagues resolve nothing below Abelisauroidea.

**Open.** Whether later analyses converge.
