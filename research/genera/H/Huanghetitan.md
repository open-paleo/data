# *Huanghetitan*

## What unit is the *Huanghetitan liujiaxiaensis* holotype from?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Hekou Group of the Lanzhou Basin, which You and colleagues (2006) give without a formation; `part: upper` follows Li and colleagues (2014), who place the neighboring *Yongjinglong* quarry in the upper Hekou Group. Governs `location.group: Hekou` and `location.part: upper` on [*Huanghetitan liujiaxiaensis*](../../../genera/H/Huanghetitan.yml); see [Hekou](../../stratigraphy/h/hekou.md) for which Hekou this is.

**Evidence.**
- you2006a, abstract: "sauropod dinosaur fossils from a Lower Cretaceous Hekou Group locality discovered in the Lanzhou Basin, Gansu".
- li2014a, p. 3: "The location of Yongjinglong beside the G75 Highway is less than a kilometer from the quarries of Daxiatitan and Huanghetitan liujiaxiaensis, which are all located in the southeastern part of the Lanzhou-Minhe Basin …; upper Hekou Group, Lower Cretaceous".
- jones2026b, p. 380: "Hekou Grp., China".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Upper" as a formation.* The record once held "Upper" as the formation, a qualifier from "upper Hekou Group".

**Open.** Whether "upper Hekou Group" in Li and colleagues' sentence applies to all three quarries or only to *Yongjinglong*'s. Reading it for *Huanghetitan* is an inference; You and colleagues give no position within the group.
