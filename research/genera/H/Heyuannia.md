# *Heyuannia*

## Which formation is the *Heyuannia huangi* holotype from?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** The Dalangshan Formation, which Lü (2002) names for the quarry at Huangsha village, citing the Guangdong geological bureau's 1988 regional geology. Two other names are printed for the Heyuan Basin beds, Zhutian and Dongyuan; no paper read ties either to the type site through a stratigraphic study. Governs `location.formation: Dalangshan` on [*Heyuannia huangi*](../../../genera/H/Heyuannia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2002a, p. 871: "Type Locality-A quarry at Huangsha village, Heyuan City, Guangdong Province. Dalangshan Formation, Late Cretaceous, ? Maastrichtian (Bureau of Geology and Mineral Resources of Guangdong Province, 1988)".
- zhang2009b, p. 194: the Dalangshan Formation's type locality is "Dalangshan in the estuary of Sanshui River, Sanshui County, Guangdong Province"; p. 1419: the Zhutian Formation's is "Zhutian close to Dafeng, Nanxiong County, Guangdong Province". Neither entry mentions Heyuan.

**Ruled out.**
- *Zhutian Formation, from Fanti and colleagues (2012).* They write that the type specimen was "recovered from the? Maastrichtian Zhutian Formation in southern China" (fanti2012a, p. 13), citing their references 68 and 71: Lü (2002), which names the Dalangshan, and Lü and Zhang (2005), which describes *Shixinggia* from the Pingling Formation of the Nanxiong Basin (lü2005a, p. 414: "Luyuan of Shixing County, Guangdong Province. Pingling Formation"). The Zhutian Formation is typified in Nanxiong County. Jones (2026) gives "Zhutian Fm., China" in the oviraptorid systematics table (jones2026c, Oviraptoridae) with no stratigraphic source.

**Open.** Which unit the Heyuan Basin beds belong to. Xing and colleagues (2024), citing one paper by Lü and colleagues (2009) on a tyrannosaurid tooth from the Heyuan Basin, give both "the Zhutian Formation in Heyuan Basin" (xing2024b, p. 1) and "the Dongyuan Formation of Heyuan, Guangdong" (p. 6). That the Dalangshan, typified in the Sanshui Basin, extends to Heyuan rests on Lü's citation of the 1988 regional geology, which has not been read. That work, or Lü and colleagues (2009), would settle it.
