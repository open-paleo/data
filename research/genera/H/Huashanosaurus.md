# *Huashanosaurus*

## What stages does *Huashanosaurus* take?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** None; the record takes `period: [Early Jurassic, Middle Jurassic]` alone, because every source read dates the horizon by epoch. Governs `period` and the absent `stage` on [*Huashanosaurus qini*](../../../genera/H/Huashanosaurus.yml), in the [Wangmen Formation](../../../stratigraphy/w/wangmen.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mo2025a, Locality and horizon: "Wangmen Formation, Early to Middle Jurassic (Meng, 2000)".
- mo2025a, Geological Setting: the specimens were "collected from exposures of the Wangmen Formation in the Shiwandashan Basin", and "Meng (2000) considered the Wangmen Fm. as Early to Middle Jurassic in age based on the discovery of charophytes".
- meng2000a: "The Wangmen Formation in the Shiwandashan Basin is of early Early Jurassic age", while the beds of the small and medium-sized basins on its northern margin "should provisionally be assigned to the Early–Middle Jurassic" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Pliensbachian to Callovian.* No source read names a stage for the formation or the horizon.
- *Early Jurassic alone.* Mo and colleagues place the quarry "in the Shiwandashan Basin", where Meng gives an early Early Jurassic age, but they assign the horizon Early to Middle Jurassic themselves, writing in the abstract that the formation is "probably somewhat younger". Their "Shiwandashan Basin" does not take up Meng's distinction between the basin and its margin basins.

**Open.** Whether the Ningming quarry lies in the Shiwandashan Basin proper or in one of Meng's margin basins.
