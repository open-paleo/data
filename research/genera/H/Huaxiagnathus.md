# *Huaxiagnathus*

## Is *Huaxiagnathus* a compsognathid, and is *Sinotyrannus* its adult?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** It stays in Compsognathidae, and the synonymy with *Sinotyrannus* is not adopted. Its describers and later analyses place it in or beside the compsognathid grade; none places it in Tyrannosauroidea. Cau and Paterna (2025) propose that *Sinotyrannus* is its mature form, a single proposal that later work read here has not taken up. Governs `parent: Compsognathidae` and the dispute on [*Huaxiagnathus orientalis*](../../../genera/H/Huaxiagnathus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hwang2004a, p. 27: "Huaxiagnathus orientalis falls out as the sister taxon to a Sinosauropteryx-Compsognathus clade and the three taxa together form a monophyletic Compsognathidae".
- dalsasso2011a, p. 126: "Node 180 Included taxa: Huaxiagnathus + Juravenator", within Compsognathidae.
- hartman2019a, p. 29: "several taxa usually considered members of that family (e.g., Huaxiagnathus, Juravenator, Mirischia, Sinocalliopteryx) branch off more basally".
- cau2025a, p. 165: "We consider Sinotyrannus (Ji et al., 2009) a junior synonym of Huaxiagnathus (Hwang et al., 2004)".
- powers2026a, p. e2728669-25: "Kileskus is recovered sister to Sinotyrannus + Yutyrannus".

**Ruled out.**
- *The synonymy with Sinotyrannus as adopted.* It rests on one paper, and the later analyses read here treat *Sinotyrannus* as a separate tyrannosauroid.
- *A tyrannosauroid placement for Huaxiagnathus.* No analysis that includes it recovers one; the link exists only through the proposed synonymy.

**Open.** Whether later work tests the synonymy on the *Sinotyrannus* material.
