# *Haplocheirus*

## Is the *Haplocheirus* holotype from the middle or the upper Shishugou?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** The middle beds, on the authors' own later statement. Choiniere and colleagues (2010) put the holotype in "the upper part of the Shishugou Formation"; the same team's 2014 redescription gives the "Middle beds", and the record follows the later paper. Governs `location.part: middle` on [*Haplocheirus sollers*](../../../genera/H/Haplocheirus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- choiniere2010a, p. 573, note 10: "Locality and horizon: Junggar Basin, Xinjiang, China. Lower orange mudstone beds in the upper part of the Shishugou Formation (Oxfordian)".
- choiniere2014a, p. 3: "Stratigraphic and Geographic Distribution: 'Middle beds' of the Shishugou Formation, Xinjiang, China (fig. 1)", the section being "under- and overlain by radiometrically dated volcanic tuffs" that "bracket the age of the fossils to between 159.7+/-0.3 and 162.2+/-0.2 Ma".
- qin2019a, p. 2, Locality and Horizon: earlier studies put "*Haplocheirus sollers* (about 40 m above the lower tuff)", in a 90 m section between the two tuffs.
- moore2018a, p. 6: the two Wucaiwan tuffs are "one in the middle beds (Tuff T-1) and one in the upper beds (Tuff T-BW)", dated "162.2 ± 0.2 and 159.7 ± 0.3 Ma" respectively; the *Bellusaurus* quarry lies "at the level of the lowest horizons of the upper beds" between them.

**Ruled out.**
- *Upper part (part: upper).* The 2010 reading, superseded by the same authors' 2014 statement.

**Open.** Whether the 40 m level is below the middle-upper contact. The tuffs bracket both the top of the middle beds and the base of the upper beds, and Moore and colleagues put the *Bellusaurus* quarry in the upper beds within the same bracket, so the 40 m figure does not by itself place the holotype in either. The "Middle beds" assignment rests on Choiniere and colleagues' 2014 line. A measured height of the middle-upper contact in the Wucaiwan section, such as Eberth and colleagues' (2010) section, which has not been read, would settle it.
