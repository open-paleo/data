# *Huaxiazhoulong*

## Which stage does the *Huaxiazhoulong* holotype take?

*2026-11-26*

**Conclusion.** None; the record keeps the Late Cretaceous only. Zhu and colleagues (2025) give the Tangbian as "deposited in Campanian" after the correlation charts of Xi and colleagues (2018, 2021), which give the position no basis (see [Tangbian](../../stratigraphy/t/tangbian.md)). Governs the absent `stage` on [*Huaxiazhoulong shouwen*](../../../genera/H/Huaxiazhoulong.yml), replacing `[Campanian]`.

**Evidence.**
- zhu2025a, p. 1: "Tangbian Formation was deposited in Campanian", citing Xi and colleagues (2018, 2021).

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian.* A relay of chart positions.

**Open.** Nothing.
