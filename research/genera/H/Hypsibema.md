# *Hypsibema*

## How should the *Hypsibema* species and placement stand?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** *H. crassicauda* stays a nomen dubium, *H. missouriensis* is disputed, and the genus sits at Hadrosauroidea incertae sedis. Prieto-Márquez and colleagues (2016) treat both species as nondiagnostic hadrosaurids; Brownstein (2018) treats both as valid hadrosauroids, the Missouri species in its own genus *Parrosaurus*, placed within or outside Hadrosauridae. For *H. crassicauda* Brownstein's is a lone reassessment against the nomen dubium of every other treatment read, so its status holds; for *H. missouriensis* the record's `valid` had a 2016 nomen dubium against it. Hadrosauroidea is the level both placements share. Governs `parent: Hadrosauroidea`, `status: disputed` on *H. missouriensis* and the dispute on [*Hypsibema*](../../../genera/H/Hypsibema.yml).

**Evidence.**
- baird1979a: "The enigmatic Hypsibema crassicauda, originally based on a mixture of three suborders, is restricted to tail vertebrae and synonymized with Parrosaurus"; "the sauropod dinosaurs are the only group to which this genus can be plausibly assigned".
- brett-surman1989a: "None of the elements expresses diagnostic features, so this genus is a nomen dubium."
- prieto-márquez2016b: the nondiagnostic Appalachian hadrosaurids "include Hadrosaurus tripos …, H. minor, H. cavatus, Ornithotarsus immanis, Hypsibema crassicauda, and Parrosaurus missouriensis".
- brownstein2018a: "Therefore, the name Parrosaurus missouriensis is preferred herein"; the giant Appalachian forms "may turn out to form a distinct family within or outside Hadrosauridae, but are simply referred to herein as hadrosauroids of uncertain phylogenetic placement".

**Ruled out.**
- *Sauropoda.* Baird and Horner's (1979) placement; rejected by Brett-Surman (1989) and by every later work read.
- *H. crassicauda as disputed.* A single reassessment, Brownstein (2018), not taken up by later work read.

**Open.** Whether the Missouri species belongs in *Hypsibema* or in *Parrosaurus*; the record keeps Baird and Horner's combination.

## What is the Parrosaurus (Neosaurus) missouriensis holotype's number?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** USNM 16735. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: USNM 16735` on *Hypsibema missouriensis*.

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1945a, p. 25: "Type.—U.S.N.M. 16735, consisting of 13 caudal centra and two unidentified fragments", repeated in the description and three figure captions.
- baird1979a, p. 14: "A sequence of 13 caudal centra (USNM 16735)"; white1973a, pp. 141, 144: "Type: Thirteen midcaudal centra. USNM 16735."

**Ruled out.**
- *USNM 16733.* Horner (1979) prints "USNM 16733 Type" in a table (horner1979b, p. 575).

**Open.** Nothing.
