# *Hypsibema*

## What is the Parrosaurus (Neosaurus) missouriensis holotype's number?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** USNM 16735. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: USNM 16735` on *Hypsibema missouriensis*.

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1945a, p. 25: "Type.—U.S.N.M. 16735, consisting of 13 caudal centra and two unidentified fragments", repeated in the description and three figure captions.
- baird1979a, p. 14: "A sequence of 13 caudal centra (USNM 16735)"; white1973a, pp. 141, 144: "Type: Thirteen midcaudal centra. USNM 16735."

**Ruled out.**
- *USNM 16733.* Horner (1979) prints "USNM 16733 Type" in a table (horner1979b, p. 575).

**Open.** Nothing.
