# *Hierosaurus*

## Where does *Hierosaurus* sit?

*2026-10-24*

**Conclusion.** Nodosauridae incertae sedis. Carpenter and colleagues (1995) place it in Nodosauridae and deny it diagnosability because its scutes match those of other nodosaurids, which supports the family referral; Coombs and Maryańska (1990) list it among the nomina dubia of Ankylosauria without a family. Governs `parent: Nodosauridae` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Hierosaurus sternbergii*](../../../genera/H/Hierosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carpenter1995a, p. 276: "THYREOPHORA ANKYLOSAURIA NODOSAURIDAE HIEROSAURUS Wieland, 1909".
- carpenter1995a, p. 295: "Hierosaurus sternbergii, consists of questionably diagnosable material, and hence constitutes a nodosaurid nomen dubium."

**Ruled out.**
- *Ankylosauria incertae sedis.* No work read places it outside Nodosauridae.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What stage is the *Hierosaurus* holotype?

*2026-10-24*

**Conclusion.** Coniacian to Santonian. The only age statement read for the type area is Carpenter and colleagues' for the Smoky Hill Chalk near Hackberry Creek. Governs `stage: [Coniacian, Santonian]` on [*Hierosaurus sternbergii*](../../../genera/H/Hierosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carpenter1995a, p. 276: the type locality is "five miles south of Castle Rock, and three miles south of Hackberry Creek" (quoting Wieland 1911), Smoky Hill Chalk Member.
- carpenter1995a, p. 295: "In the vicinity of Hackberry Creek, the Smoky Hill Chalk is Coniacian to Santonian in age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Coniacian alone.* No source read gives it.

**Open.** Nothing.
