# *Hulsanpes*

## Is the *Hulsanpes* holotype from a numbered site at Khulsan, and does a source print its position?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** Neither. Osmólska (1982) gives the type locality as Khulsan with no site within it, and the theropods marked on Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz's (1972) map of Khulsan are other specimens. The coordinates her type-locality line refers to are a range for the exposures of the whole locality. Governs `location.locality: Khulsan` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Hulsanpes perlei*](../../../genera/H/Hulsanpes.yml). For the holotypes the map does place, see [Khulsan site numbers](../../topics/khulsan-site-numbers.md).

**Evidence.**
- osmólska1982a, p. 441: "Type horizon and locality: Baron Goyot formation (?Lower Campanian), Upper Cretaceous, Khulsan, Nemegt Basin, Gobi Desert, Mongolian People's Republic (for coordinates see: GRADZINSKI & JERZYKIEWICZ 1972: 23)."
- osmólska1982a, p. 440: the holotype is "a small metatarsus, measuring about 4 cm in length"; "In addition to the specimen described below, the theropods thus far found in the Barun Goyot Formation at the Khulsan locality include several incomplete skeletons of Velociraptor sp. and of an undetermined dinosaur, as well as a number of carnosaur teeth (GRADZINSKI & JERZYKIEWICZ 1972: figs. 4 and 5".
- gradziński1972a, p. 23: "The major part of the exposures is concentrated in the area with coordinates 101°07'—101°09' longitude E and 43°28'—43°30' latitude N".
- gradziński1972a, p. 24, Fig. 4: "2 — fragmentary skeleton of a small theropod dinosaur"; "8 — partial skeleton of a small theropod dinosaur".

**Ruled out.**
- *Khulsan, site 2.* Site 2 is a "fragmentary skeleton of a small theropod dinosaur". Osmólska cites the map for the theropods other than the holotype and calls their metatarsi "quite different from that of Hulsanpes perlei" (osmólska1982a, p. 440). That sites 2 and 8 are among those specimens is our reading of her citation; the map marks no other theropods.
- *A coordinate from the type-locality line.* The pages Osmólska points to give a two-minute range in each direction, about 3.7 km north to south and 2.7 km east to west (our calculation), for the locality's exposures. It is an area range, not a position.

**Open.** The site within Khulsan. Cau and Madzia's (2018) redescription of the holotype has not been read, and may say more.
