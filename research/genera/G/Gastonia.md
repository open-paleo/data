# *Gastonia*

## Is the *Gastonia burgei* type horizon Valanginian or Aptian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Valanginian: the holotype is from the upper Yellow Cat Member at Gaston Quarry, and the upper Yellow Cat is dated Valanginian at Utahraptor Ridge. Governs `stage: [Valanginian]` on [*Gastonia burgei*](../../../genera/G/Gastonia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kirkland2016a, p. 129, Fig. 17 caption: "Basicranium of type of *Gastonia burgei* from the upper Yellow Cat, Gaston Quarry".
- joeckel2023a, p. 1, Abstract: at Utahraptor Ridge, "a new cryptotephra zircon eruption age of 135.10 ± 0.30/0.31/0.34 Ma", with carbon-isotope features "tentatively interpreted as the Valanginian double-peak carbon isotope excursion"; p. 3: the top of the prominent "calcrete" lies at about 32 m, below the dated sample SDR-36.35 (p. 4, Fig. 2).
- vickaryous2004a, p. 364, Table 17.1: "Lower Cedar Mountain Formation (Utah), United States", "Berriasian–Hauterivian", a range that contains the Valanginian.

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian.* Jones (2026) gives "Cedar Mt. Fm., Utah USA *Early Cretaceous, Aptian*" (jones2026a, Thyreophora table) with no stratigraphic source. The same row lists the "DMNH series" of Kinneer and colleagues (2016), now [*Gastonia lorriemcwhinneyae*](../../../genera/G/Gastonia.yml) from the Aptian lower Ruby Ranch Member. That the Aptian comes from that material is an inference.

**Open.** No source dates Gaston Quarry itself. The Valanginian is the Utahraptor Ridge age, carried to Gaston Quarry because both lie above Aubrey's marker calcrete, and the Yellow Cat is diachronous (see [Yellow Cat](../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.md)). A date from the Gaston Quarry section would settle it.
