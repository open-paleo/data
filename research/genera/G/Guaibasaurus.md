# *Guaibasaurus*

## Where was the *Guaibasaurus candelariensis* holotype found, and is the printed Linha São Luiz position its site?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** Sesmaria do Pinhal 2, near Candelária. Bonaparte and colleagues (1999) give it by distance from highway RS 287 and Candelária, and Novas and colleagues (2021) name it. The position Langer and colleagues (2018) print is for Linha São Luiz near Faxinal do Soturno, where the referred skeletons were found. Governs `location.locality: Sesmaria do Pinhal 2` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Guaibasaurus candelariensis*](../../../genera/G/Guaibasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bonaparte1999b, p. 90: "From Caturrita Formation, of early Late Triassic age, 100 meters south of RS 287 (RS State Highway), 7.5 km west to the city of Candelária, RS, Brazil".
- novas2021a, p. 85, Probable early sauropodomorphs: the holotype and hindlimb "were discovered in the Sesmaria do Pinhal 2 locality near Candelária"; a later skeleton "was referred to Guaibasaurus (Bonaparte et al., 2007), collected from the Linha São Luiz locality near the town of Faxinal do Soturno".
- langer2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 4: "The Linha São Luiz site (Bonaparte et al., 2010) is located to the north of Faxinal do Soturno (29°33′45″S; 53°26′48″W)", its beds rich in fossils "including *Guaibasaurus candelariensis*".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Linha São Luiz position, 29°33′45″S, 53°26′48″W.* It is the site of referred material, not of MCN-PV 2355 (novas2021a, p. 85). Langer and colleagues name *Guaibasaurus* among the site's fauna, which is how the pair reads as the taxon's.
- *"BR-287 road".* Bonaparte and colleagues give the highway as "RS 287 (RS State Highway)", a state road, not a federal one.

**Open.** Whether "Botucaraí Hill" is another name for Sesmaria do Pinhal 2. Bittencourt and colleagues (2013) describe dinosaur remains from a Caturrita site of that name and have not been read; nor has the redescription by Langer and colleagues (2011), which may be the source of the Sesmaria do Pinhal 2 name and may print a position.

## Is the *Guaibasaurus candelariensis* holotype Norian or Carnian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Norian. The holotype is from the Caturrita Formation (bonaparte1999b, p. 90), whose dated maximum age is early Norian, and the dated Santa Maria Formation below it is Carnian. Governs `stage: [Norian]` on [*Guaibasaurus candelariensis*](../../../genera/G/Guaibasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- langer2018a, accepted manuscript, abstract: "233.23 ± 0.73 Ma … from the Santa Maria Formation and a single zircon 206Pb/238U date of 225.42 ± 0.37 Ma representing the maximum age of the Caturrita Formation".
- langer2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 7: "The younger than 225.5 Ma age for the Caturrita Formation at Linha São Luiz (containing *Guaibasaurus candelariensis*)"; "Our Caturrita Formation maximum age is early Norian (Lacian)".
- langer2004a, p. 26, Table 2.1: *G. candelariensis*, "Caturrita Formation (Rio Grande do Sul), Brazil", "Norian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Carnian.* Jones (2026) gives "Caturrita Fm. and Santa Maria Fm., Brazil, Late Triassic, Carnian" (jones2026b, p. 372) in a row that also lists referred specimens. The Carnian is the Santa Maria's age, and the maximum age excludes it for the Caturrita beds that were dated.

**Open.** The dated sample is from Linha São Luiz, where referred skeletons were found, not from the holotype site near Candelária. Applying its maximum age to the holotype relies on both sites being in the Caturrita, which is an inference. A date from the holotype's own beds would settle it.
