# *Geranosaurus*

## Which stages does the *Geranosaurus atavus* holotype take?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Its unit's Pliensbachian. Broom (1911) gives the Cave Sandstone a Lower Jurassic age from its fossils, an epoch; Norman and colleagues' (2011) "more probably Pliensbachian" points to their relay of the formation's age. Governs `stage: [Pliensbachian]` on [*Geranosaurus atavus*](../../../genera/G/Geranosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.** 
- broom1911a, p. 307: "The specimen is from the Cave sandstone of the Stormberg Series, which, in my opinion, is of Lower Jurassic Age."

**Ruled out.** Nothing beyond the [Clarens](../../stratigraphy/c/clarens.md) entry.

**Open.** Nothing.
