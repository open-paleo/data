# *Garrigatitan*

## Which La Bastide Neuve is the *Garrigatitan* type locality?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** La Bastide Neuve at Velaux, in the Aix-en-Provence Basin, Bouches-du-Rhône. The describing paper names Fox-Amphoux only for the Fox-Amphoux-Métisson site in the Var, as a source of comparable teeth and skull material. Governs `location.locality: Velaux-La Bastide Neuve` and `location.region: FR-13` on [*Garrigatitan meridionalis*](../../../genera/G/Garrigatitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- díezdíaz2021a, online version p. 4, Type locality: "La Bastide Neuve, Velaux; Aix-en-Provence Basin, Bouches-du-Rhône, France".
- díezdíaz2021a, online version p. 4, Type horizon: "'Begudian' (local stage) sandstones, upper Campanian, Upper Cretaceous"; "The holotype and paratype were recovered from level B3 of sequence 2".
- díezdíaz2021a, online version p. 2: comparable teeth come from "the late Campanian – early Maastrichtian Fox-Amphoux-Métisson site (Var department)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Fox-Amphoux-La Bastide Neuve.* This is the *Variraptor* site in the Var, about 70 km from Velaux (see [Grès à Reptiles](../../topics/grès-à-reptiles.md)). The *Garrigatitan* record once carried the [*Variraptor*](../../../genera/V/Variraptor.yml) record's locality string and position under this name. Nothing in the describing paper places *Garrigatitan* there.

**Open.** The formation. The describing paper gives only the Begudian local stage. The Argiles et Grès à Reptiles comes from Díez Díaz and colleagues' (2018) assignment of the Velaux site, which the Grès à Reptiles note leaves open for the same site's *Matheronodon*.
