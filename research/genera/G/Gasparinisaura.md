# *Gasparinisaura*

## What do Dieudonné and colleagues (2026b) change for *Gasparinisaura*?

*2026-10-10*

**Conclusion.** The diagnosis, not the placement. Their redescription of the holotype skull emends the diagnosis, removing characters they attribute to crushing or to variation, and becomes the species' `described_in`. Their analysis puts *Gasparinisaura* in an unresolved polytomy with both specifiers of the node-based Elasmaria, so by that clade's definition it stays inside Elasmaria, the current parent. Their new subfamily Dysalotosaurinae, and their nesting of Elasmaria within Dryosauridae, are a single research group's results and are recorded in the [Elasmaria](../../../clades/Elasmaria.yml) dispute, not adopted. Governs `diagnostic_features`, `described_in` and `parent` on [*Gasparinisaura cincosaltensis*](../../../genera/G/Gasparinisaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dieudonné2026b, p. 7, Emended diagnosis: "Gasparinisaura cincosaltensis is recognized from the following autapomorphies", seventeen cranial characters, followed by seven apomorphies "found simultaneously" retained from the earlier diagnosis.
- dieudonné2026b, p. 8, Comments on diagnosis emendation: the jugal–postorbital contact is removed as a postmortem crushing artifact, the four-sacral count because sacral number varies within a species, the long pubic peduncle because it is not long compared with other ornithopods, and the compressed metatarsal II because it is about 28% of the metatarsal breadth.
- dieudonné2026b, p. 29: the strict consensus has a polytomy "that includes Anabisetia saldiviai, Gasparinisaura cincosaltensis, Chakisaurus nekul, Mahuidacursor lipanglef, Morrosaurus antarcticus, Kangnasaurus coetzeei, and the inner specifiers of Elasmaria (i.e. Talenkauen santacrucensis and Macrogryphosaurus gondwanicus)"; the position of *Gasparinisaura* at its base "is still conflictual".
- dieudonné2026b, p. 7: "Dysalotosaurinae is a node-based subfamily, defined as Dysalotosaurus lettowvorbecki, Gasparinisaura cincosaltensis, their common ancestor and all descendants."
- dieudonné2026a, Results: "Dryosauridae includes Elasmaria", and this analysis "is the second to find" that arrangement.

**Ruled out.**
- *Gasparinisaura outside Elasmaria.* The paper calls the position "conflictual" and describes it as close to the base of Elasmaria, but the polytomy holds both specifiers, so the clade's node is the polytomy's node.
- *Parent Dryosauridae, or a Dysalotosaurinae node.* Both rest on the matrix shared by the two 2026 papers of one research group, and neither has yet been taken up by later work; within the tree, Elasmaria's own parent, Iguanodontia, already contains the dryosaurid reading.
- *Keeping the earlier diagnosis.* The redescription removes several of its characters and rewords the jugal characters it keeps.

**Open.** The jugal–quadratojugal suture of Coria and Salgado (1996) is reinterpreted as a fracture within the jugal; the paper calls this tentative.
