# *Genyodectes*

## Where was the *Genyodectes* holotype found, and does Huene's 44°30′ S give its position?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** At a Cañadón Grande in the Departamento Paso de Indios, central Chubut, on Rauhut's (2004) reconstruction, with no recorded coordinate. Woodward (1901) names only "the Cañadon Grande, Chubut". Huene (1929) places the find at Laguna Pelada at 44°30′ S and "a little east of" 70°. Rauhut uses that figure to put the collecting area between Laguna del Mate and Manantial Pelado, and takes the site to be a valley called Cañadón Grande about 20 km east of Laguna del Mate. Governs `location.locality: Cañadón Grande`, `location.region: AR-U` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Genyodectes serus*](../../../genera/G/Genyodectes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- woodward1901a, p. 179: the jaws were "obtained by Mr. Roth from red sandstone in the Cañadon Grande, Chubut".
- huene1929a, p. 17: "traído por S. Roth de Laguna Pelada, gobernación del Chubut. El lugar está situado a 44° 30' latitud sur y un poco al este del 70° de longitud. El nuevo mapa de ferrocarriles argentinos indica en ese lugar a Manantial Pelado; quizá esté cerca de éste" ("brought by S. Roth from Laguna Pelada, Chubut governorate. The place lies at 44° 30′ south latitude and a little east of 70° longitude. The new map of Argentine railways shows Manantial Pelado at that place; perhaps it is near it", translated).
- rauhut2004a, p. 894: Huene "even gave approximate coordinates for the locality (44°30' S, slightly east of 70° W)"; "It thus seems most likely that *Genyodectes* was found in the area between Laguna del Mate and Manantial Pelado, some 70 km southwest of the town of Paso de Indios. In fact, a valley with the name Cañadón Grande is situated only some 20 km to the east of the Laguna del Mate, and it seems very likely that the material is derived from this location".
- rauhut2004a, p. 895, Locality and Horizon: "Cañadón Grande, Departamento Paso de Indios, Chubut Province, Argentina".

**Ruled out.**
- *Huene's 44°30′ S, a little east of 70°, as the holotype's coordinates.* It gives a latitude and the side of a meridian, not a longitude, and it is Huene's position for Laguna Pelada. Rauhut reads it as fixing the collecting area and puts the probable site in a different valley about 20 km from Laguna del Mate. A point at 44.5° S, 70° W stands for neither place.
- *Cañadón Grande of the Río Chubut, near Gaiman.* Huene's other statement (1929, p. 5, as Rauhut quotes it). Rauhut rejects it because "no Mesozoic sediments crop out within some 50 km of the town of Gaiman" (rauhut2004a, p. 894).

**Open.** Whether the valley east of Laguna del Mate is Woodward's Cañadón Grande. Rauhut calls it "very likely", and no collection record ties the specimen to a site. Roth (1908), whose photograph of the site Huene cites, has not been read.
