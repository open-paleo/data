# *Gongbusaurus*

## Is *Gongbusaurus shiyii* a valid taxon?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** No; it is a nomen dubium. It was named for two isolated teeth. Galton (2007) calls it a nomen dubium and finds the teeth closer to ankylosaurs than to ornithopods, the reviews in both editions of *The Dinosauria* list it among nomina dubia, and the analyses of Butler and colleagues (2008) and Boyd (2015) exclude it. Every reading places the teeth within Ornithischia. Governs `status: nomen dubium`, `parent: Ornithischia` with `placement: incertae sedis`, and the dispute on [*Gongbusaurus shiyii*](../../../genera/G/Gongbusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1983a, p. 94 (translated): Fabrosauridae; "A small primitive ornithopod with a rounded premaxillary tooth crown and asymmetrical serrations on the anterior and posterior carinae".
- weishampel1990a, p. 424, Table 19.1, Nomina dubia: "Gongbusaurus shiyii Dong, Zhou, et Zhang, 1983 | 2 isolated teeth".
- norman2004c, p. 326, Table 14.1, Nomina dubia: "Gongbusaurus shiyii Dong, Zhou, et Zhang, 1983 | Teeth".
- galton2007c, p. 24: "This taxon is a nomen dubium that is probably incertae sedis as Ankylosauria rather than Ornithopoda".
- butler2008b, p. 11: among taxa "erected solely or largely on the basis of dental remains", excluded from the analysis.
- boyd2015a, Taxon sampling: "This taxon is based solely on two isolated teeth … this taxon is considered of dubious validity and is excluded from this study".

**Ruled out.**
- *Fabrosauridae or Ornithopoda.* The original placement rests on the teeth, which later work treats as undiagnostic; Galton reads them as ankylosaurian.
- *A valid species.* Only the describing paper and Peng (1992), working from the published description, treat it as valid.

**Open.** Nothing on the type species. The status of "*G.*" *wucaiwanensis*, from Xinjiang, awaits a formal new genus or the relocation of its type material.
