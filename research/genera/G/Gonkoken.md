# *Gonkoken*

## Is the *Gonkoken nanoi* holotype CPAP 3054 or CPAP 2054?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** CPAP 3054, a right ilium, as the describing paper designates it. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CPAP 3054]` on [*Gonkoken nanoi*](../../../genera/G/Gonkoken.yml).

**Evidence.**
- alarcón-muñoz2023a, p. 3, Systematic paleontology: "Holotype—CPAP 3054, right ilium"; "We therefore designated CPAP 3054 as the holotype because of its evident diagnostic features."

**Ruled out.**
- *CPAP 2054.* Jones (2026) gives "CPAP 2054—right ilium" (jones2026a, Hadrosauroidea table), citing the describing paper, which prints 3054 in its holotype line, its discussion and its Fig. 2 caption. The row differs by one digit.

**Open.** Nothing.
