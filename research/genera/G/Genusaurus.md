# *Genusaurus*

## Does *Genusaurus* take a formation?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** No. The describing paper gives the type stratum as greenish clays and glauconitic sands at Bevons within an undifferentiated middle Albian series and names no unit. Governs the empty `location.formation` on [*Genusaurus sisteronis*](../../../genera/G/Genusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- accarie1995a: the bones were found "in the territory around the commune of Bevons, 4.25 km SW of the village of Sisteron"; it never uses the name Marnes Bleues.

**Ruled out.**
- *"Bevons Beds" and "'Bevon' beds".* Jones (2026) gives "Bevons Beds" (jones2026c) and Tykoski and Rowe (2004) "'Bevon' beds (Alpes-de-Haute-Provence), France" (tykoski2004a, Table 3.1); Bevons is the commune, and neither names a unit any source erects.
- *Marnes Bleues.* The describing paper does not use the name.

**Open.** Nothing.
