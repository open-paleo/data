# *Giganotosaurus*

## What is the *Giganotosaurus carolinii* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** MUCPv-CH-1, the partial skeleton Coria and Salgado (1995) designate. MUCPv-95 is a larger dentary that Calvo and Coria (1998) refer to the species. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MUCPv-CH-1]` on [*Giganotosaurus carolinii*](../../../genera/G/Giganotosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- coria1995a, p. 225: "Holotype. MUCPv-CH-1 (Museo de la Universidad Nacional del Comahue, El Chocon collection), a disarticulated skeleton represented by a partial skull".
- calvo1998a, p. 117: "an isolated dentary (MUCPv-95)"; "The new specimen of G. Carolinii is 8% bigger than the holotype."

**Ruled out.**
- *MUCPv-95.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) list MUCPv-95 as the largest specimen and in their chronology of the largest theropods (molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods; Chronology of theropods considered largest historically). Their own entry calls it "6.5% larger than the holotype MUCPv-Ch1".
- *MUCPv-Ch1 as a different specimen.* Jones (2026) and Molina-Pérez and Larramendi print "MUCPv-Ch1" (jones2026c, p. 499); it is the same number as Coria and Salgado's MUCPv-CH-1.

**Open.** Nothing.
