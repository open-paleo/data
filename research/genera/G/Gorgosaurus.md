# *Gorgosaurus*

## Which quarry and which formation is the *Gorgosaurus libratus* holotype from?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** The Dinosaur Park Formation, 3½ miles below the mouth of Berry Creek on the south side of the Red Deer River. Lambe (1914) gives the site and only the Belly River formation of his time; Brochu (2003) places NMC 2120 itself in the Dinosaur Park Formation. Russell (1970) gives the specimen as quarry 36 of Sternberg's 1950 catalogue, and no other quarry number is published for it. Governs `location.formation: Dinosaur Park`, `location.locality` and the quarry number in `location.notes` on [*Gorgosaurus libratus*](../../../genera/G/Gorgosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lambe1914a, p. 13: the skeleton "was discovered by his son, Charles M. Sternberg, 3½ miles below the mouth of Berry creek (Steveville), on the south side of Red Deer river, near the prairie level", in "the Belly River formation".
- brochu2003a, p. 4: "Albertosaurus libratus is based on a nearly complete skeleton (NMC 2120) described by Lambe (1914a, b, 1917) as Gorgosaurus libratus. … It hails from the Dinosaur Park Formation".
- russell1970a, p. 7: "NMC 2120 (type of *G. libratus*) nearly complete skeleton (quarry 36 of Sternberg 1950)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Quarry 6, Sand Creek.* No source read gives either for NMC 2120. Russell's number for it is 36, and Lambe places the find by its distance below Berry Creek without naming another stream.
- *Oldman Formation.* Russell (1970) gives the species' distribution as "Oldman Formation, near Steveville, Alberta" (russell1970a, p. 5). He wrote before the Dinosaur Park Formation was separated from the Oldman, so this is the name of its time and not a rival reading (see [Oldman and Dinosaur Park](../../topics/oldman-and-dinosaur-park.md)); Brochu places the specimen by number.

**Open.** Where quarry 36 lies. Sternberg's 1950 map of the Steveville quarries, which Russell cites, has not been read; it would place the quarry and may show its height in the section.

## What is the *Gorgosaurus libratus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** NMC 2120, the nearly complete skeleton Lambe (1914) described. Lambe gives no catalogue number in the copy read; Russell (1970) and Carpenter (1992) give it as the type. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NMC 2120]` on [*Gorgosaurus libratus*](../../../genera/G/Gorgosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- russell1970a, p. 7: "NMC 2120 (type of *G. libratus*) nearly complete skeleton (quarry 36 of Sternberg 1950)"; in the same list, "AMNH 5458 skull and nearly complete skeleton (Little Sandhill Creek basin)" and "ROM 1247 palatal elements".
- carpenter1992a, p. 258: "Holotype: NMC 2120 nearly complete skeleton from the Judith River Formation, Alberta, Canada."

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 5458 and ROM 1247.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) list AMNH 5458 as the largest specimen and give both numbers in a speed table, ROM 1247 as a juvenile (molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods; Theropod speed). Russell lists both as referred specimens.

**Open.** Nothing.
