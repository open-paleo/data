# *Gobititan*

## Which unit of the Xinminpu Group is the *Gobititan* holotype from?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** The middle part of the Zhonggou Formation, held as a working hypothesis. You and colleagues (2003) place the holotype in the "Middle Gray Unit of the Xinminbao Group" in the Gongpoquan Basin. The unit is the middle of the three that Tang and colleagues (2001) recognize in the group in the Mazongshan area. You and colleagues (2014) assign the Mazongshan Xinminpu Group to the Zhonggou on field work and a personal communication. You and colleagues (2018) treat that as a working hypothesis, because the Mazongshan and Jiuquan beds "cannot be easily correlated". Governs `location.formation: Zhonggou` and `location.part: middle` on [*Gobititan shenzhouensis*](../../../genera/G/Gobititan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- you2003a, p. 424: "Gongpoquan Basin, Mazongshm area, Gansu Province, China. Middle Gray Unit of the Xinminbao Group. Upper Lower Cretaceous (Albian) (Tang et al., 2001b)".
- tang2001b, p. 118, Fig. 3 caption: "the approximate divisions of the Lower Red, Middle Grey and Upper Red units" of the Xinminbao Group.
- you2014a, p. 75: "Our 2010 and 2011 field work confirms that the dinosaur-bearing Xinminpu Group in the Mazongshan area belongs to Zhonggou Formation (Liu, Y.-Q., pers. comm.)".
- you2018a, p. 8: "*Gobititan shenzhouensis* from the gray beds in the Gongpoquan Basin"; p. 6: "the Cretaceous beds in the Mazongshan and Jiuquan areas cannot be easily correlated. As of this publication, our working hypothesis is that … the red beds in both the Yujingzi and Gongpoquan basins represent the beginning of the Zhonggou Formation with an early Albian age, whereas the gray beds in the Gongpoquan Basin are probably early–mid-Albian in age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Xiagou Formation.* Jones (2026) writes that the fossils "were recovered in 1999 from the middle unit of the Xiagou geological formation" (jones2026b, p. 251), while the same book's systematics table gives "Zhonggou Fm." for the taxon. You and colleagues (2018) assign only the Yujingzi Basin's gray-variegated beds to the Xiagou and put the Gongpoquan gray beds above the red beds that begin the Zhonggou.

**Open.** A measured section in the Gongpoquan Basin tied to the dated Xiagou–Zhonggou boundary of the Jiuquan area. You and colleagues (2018) describe red beds under gray beds there but no Upper Red Unit, so how Tang's three units map onto the Zhonggou at this basin is not stated in the sources read.
