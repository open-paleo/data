# *Gallimimus*

## What is the *Gallimimus bullatus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** The skeleton Osmólska and colleagues (1972) designate as the type specimen, which they number G.I. No. DPS 100/11; the record writes it IGM 100/11 under the Mongolian Paleontological Center. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IGM 100/11]` on [*Gallimimus bullatus*](../../../genera/G/Gallimimus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- osmólska1972a, p. 105: "Type specimen: Nearly complete specimen with skull (G.I.No.DPS 100/11)".
- osmólska1972a, p. 110: "Z.Pal.No.Mg.D-I/94 — nearly complete skeleton, lacking: the skull, atlas, distal portion of the tail", in the list of referred material.

**Ruled out.**
- *ZPAL MgD-I/94.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give "*Gallimimus bullatus* (juvenile) | MgD-1/94" in a speed table (molina-pérez2019a, Theropod speed, Ornithomimosaurs). It is a referred skeleton without a skull, not the type.

**Open.** Nothing.
