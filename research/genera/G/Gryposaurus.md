# *Gryposaurus*

## Which formation and site is the *Gryposaurus notabilis* holotype from?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** The Dinosaur Park Formation, at no published site. Lambe (1914) gives only the Belly River formation on the Red Deer River, and Prieto-Márquez (2010) places CMN 2278 by number in the Dinosaur Park Formation. Governs `location.formation: Dinosaur Park` and the empty `location.locality` on [*Gryposaurus notabilis*](../../../genera/G/Gryposaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lambe1914b, p. 145: the skull is part of "the 1913 collection, made by the Geological Survey's vertebrate palæontological party in the Belly River formation on Red Deer river, Alberta".
- prieto-márquez2010d, p. 838: "*G. notabilis*, was described by Lambe in 1914 on the basis of a complete articulated skull and partial postcranium (CMN 2278) recovered from the Late Campanian Dinosaur Park Formation of southern Alberta, Canada."

**Ruled out.**
- *Sandhill Creek, 116 ft above the river.* Lull and Wright (1942) give a Sand Hill Creek site for the holotype of *Kritosaurus incurvimanus*, ROM 4514: "2½ miles above Happy Jack ferry and a mile south of the Red Deer River on Sand Hill Creek. 100 feet above the Red Deer River level" (lull1942a, p. 169), elsewhere "on Sand Creek, near its confluence with Red Deer River" (p. 18). That specimen is now referred to *G. notabilis* but is not its type, and neither passage gives 116 ft.

**Open.** The site of CMN 2278. No source read gives one; the Geological Survey's 1913 field records would settle it.
