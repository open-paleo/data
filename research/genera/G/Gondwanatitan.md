# *Gondwanatitan*

## Is the *Gondwanatitan* holotype from the Adamantina Formation, or from one of the units that replaced it?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** The Adamantina, the unit the taxon literature uses. Fernandes and Coimbra (2000) split the former Adamantina Formation into new units, among them the Vale do Rio do Peixe and the Presidente Prudente, but no source read places the Álvares Machado site in one of them. Governs `location.formation: Adamantina` on [*Gondwanatitan faustoi*](../../../genera/G/Gondwanatitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- santucci2022a, p. 380: "Álvares Machado, southeastern São Paulo State, Brazil (Cunha et al. 1987; Kellner and Azevedo 1999) … Adamantina Formation, Campanian–early Maastrichtian".
- fernandes2000a, p. 720: the Vale do Rio do Peixe Formation "Corresponde a grande parte da antiga Formação Adamantina definida por Soares et al. (1980)" ("corresponds to much of the former Adamantina Formation defined by Soares et al. (1980)", translated); p. 726: the Presidente Prudente Formation crops out in the Presidente Prudente region and corresponds to the restricted part of the Taciba lithofacies of Soares and colleagues.
- santucci2022a, p. 355: for *Austroposeidon* at Presidente Prudente, "Adamantina Formation (=Presidente Prudente Formation)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Which of Fernandes and Coimbra's units holds the site. Álvares Machado lies next to Presidente Prudente, where [*Austroposeidon*](../../../genera/A/Austroposeidon.yml) and [*Brasilotitan*](../../../genera/B/Brasilotitan.yml) are recorded in the Presidente Prudente Formation, but that is geography, not a placement of this holotype. Kellner and Azevedo (1999), the describing paper, and Cunha and colleagues (1987), the first report of the site, have not been read.

## How is the site name spelled?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** Sítio Myzobuchi. The two bibliographies that give the title of Cunha and colleagues' (1987) first report of the site disagree on its spelling, and neither is the report itself. Governs `location.locality: Sítio Myzobuchi` on [*Gondwanatitan faustoi*](../../../genera/G/Gondwanatitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- santucci2001a, p. 314, References: "Nova ocorrência de répteis cretácicos no 'Sítio Myzobuchi', da Formação Bauru, em Álvares Machado, SP", in the Anais of the 10th Congresso Brasileiro de Paleontologia, pp. 143–154.
- santucci2022a, p. 387, References: the same title with "'Sítio Myzobushi'", pp. 155–171.
- jones2026b, *Gondwanatitan* entry: the specimen "was discovered on the farm belonging to Yoshitoshi Myzobuchi in 1983".

**Ruled out.**
- *Myzobushi.* Among the sources read it appears only in Santucci and Filippi's (2022) bibliography, whose page range for the report also differs from Santucci and Bertini's (2001).

**Open.** Cunha and colleagues (1987), which would settle the spelling and the page range, has not been read.

## Does the Presidente Prudente Formation assignment reach the *Gondwanatitan* holotype?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** Not on any source read. The record stays in the Adamantina. The two works that give the Presidente Prudente for this taxon give no reason tied to the Álvares Machado site. Governs `location.formation: Adamantina` on [*Gondwanatitan faustoi*](../../../genera/G/Gondwanatitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- santucci2022a, p. 380: "Álvares Machado, southeastern São Paulo State, Brazil … Adamantina Formation, Campanian–early Maastrichtian".
- santucci2022a, p. 355: "Adamantina Formation (=Presidente Prudente Formation)", written for the *Austroposeidon* site in the city of Presidente Prudente.

**Ruled out.**
- *Presidente Prudente Formation, on Jones (2026).* The systematics table gives "Presidente Prudente Fm., Brazil" (jones2026b, p. 384) with no stratigraphic source.
- *Presidente Prudente Formation, on Bandeira and colleagues (2016).* They write that the unit "has also revealed the presence of two other titanosaurs: *Brasilotitan nemophagus* [7] and *Gondwanatitan faustoi* [32]" (bandeira2016a, p. 17). Their reference 32 is the describing paper, cited for the taxon. The sentence names no locality, section or reason.

**Open.** The same as the 2026-08-24 entry: which of Fernandes and Coimbra's units holds the site. [*Brasilotitan nemophagus*](../../../genera/B/Brasilotitan.yml) takes the Presidente Prudente on the same sentence of Bandeira and colleagues. Applying that sentence to one taxon and not the other is a choice a reader should be able to see.
