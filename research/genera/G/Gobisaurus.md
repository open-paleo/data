# *Gobisaurus*

## Is the *Gobisaurus* holotype from Maortu?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** Maortu, the locality the describing paper's Type locality heading gives, but on belief rather than a record. Vickaryous and colleagues (2001) write that the skeleton is "believed" to come from the same locality as *Chilantaisaurus maortuensis* (now *Shaochilong*), collected by the Sino-Soviet expeditions of 1959–1960, and that "no official documentation exists". Governs `location.locality: Maortu` on [*Gobisaurus domoculus*](../../../genera/G/Gobisaurus.yml); its unit is covered in [Maortu and Dashuigou](../../topics/maortu-and-dashuigou-miaogou.md).

**Evidence.**
- vickaryous2001a, p. 1767: "The latter (IVPP V12563), believed to have been collected from the same locality as *Chilantaisaurus* (although no official documentation exists)".
- vickaryous2001a, p. 1768, Type locality: "IVPP V12563 is believed to have been collected from the same general locality as the large theropod *Chilantaisaurus maortuensis*, approximately 60 km north of Chilantai (Jilantai; 39 45 N, 105 45E), on the east side of Chilantai Salt Lake (Chilantaiyen Chih), Maortu".

**Ruled out.**
- *39°45′ N, 105°45′ E as the site's position.* The pair in the Type locality line is Chilantai's (Jilantai's), the town from which the site is said to lie about 60 km north. It is a reference point, not the find spot.

**Open.** The expedition's field records, if they survive at the Institute of Vertebrate Paleontology and Paleoanthropology, would settle whether this skeleton came from the *Shaochilong* locality.
