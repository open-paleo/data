# *Gryphoceratops*

## Is the *Gryphoceratops* holotype from bonebed 55 in Dinosaur Provincial Park?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** No. ROM 56635 is from Black Coulee, formerly called Deadhorse Coulee, in the Deadhorse Coulee Member of the Milk River Formation. Bonebed 55 is the site of the *Unescoceratops* holotype, which Ryan and colleagues (2012) describe in the same paper and map in the same figure. Governs `location.locality: Black Coulee`, `location.formation: Milk River` and `location.member: Deadhorse Coulee` on [*Gryphoceratops morrisoni*](../../../genera/G/Gryphoceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ryan2012b, p. 74: "Black Coulee (formerly Deadhorse Coulee; Fig. 2), approximately 10 km east of Writing-on-Stone Provincial Park, Alberta, from the Deadhorse Coulee Member of the Milk River Formation (late Santonian)."
- ryan2012b, p. 72, Fig. 2: "TMP 95.12.6 was collected from bonebed 55 (BB55) located in the northwest corner of Dinosaur Provincial Park … ROM 56635 was collected from Black Coulee (formerly Deadhorse Coulee)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Bonebed 55, Dinosaur Provincial Park.* An online encyclopedia article places ROM 56635 "in the northwest corner of Dinosaur Provincial Park, from bonebed 55 of the Milk River Formation", joining the *Unescoceratops* site to the *Gryphoceratops* formation. Ryan and colleagues give bonebed 55 for TMP 95.12.6, "in middle of the Dinosaur Park Formation" (ryan2012b, p. 71).

**Open.** Nothing.
