# *Glyptodontopelta*

## Where does *Glyptodontopelta* sit?

*2026-10-23*

**Conclusion.** Nodosauridae. Burns (2008) assigns it to the family on osteoderm histology and morphology, and Arbour and Currie (2015) recover it inside Nodosauridae in both their strict consensus and majority-rule trees. Ford's (2000) placement in a Stegopeltinae within Ankylosauridae was tested by Arbour and Currie and not recovered. Governs `parent: Nodosauridae` on [*Glyptodontopelta mimus*](../../../genera/G/Glyptodontopelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- burns2008a, p. 1105: "Histological analysis of the armor of G. mimus confirms that it is nodosaurid based on: the presence of at least some rectangular, articulating cervical/pectoral osteoderms and internal (histologic) structure."
- arbour2015a, p. 49: "'Antarctopelta', Glyptodontopelta and Stegopelta were all recovered as derived nodosaurids, but not as sister taxa"; Fig. 11 (figure reading): *Glyptodontopelta mimus* lies inside the node labeled Nodosauridae in both trees.

**Ruled out.**
- *Ankylosauria incertae sedis.* No cited paper says the genus cannot be placed below Ankylosauria.
- *Stegopeltinae, within Ankylosauridae.* Ford (2000) erected the subfamily on osteoderm characters; Arbour and Currie recover its members apart, with *Glyptodontopelta* among the nodosaurids.
- *Nomen dubium.* Vickaryous and colleagues (2004) list *G. mimus* among nomina dubia (vickaryous2004a, Table 17.1); Burns (2008) shows it diagnosable on its osteoderm texture, and Arbour and Currie treat it as valid.

**Open.** Its position within Nodosauridae.
