# *Graciliceratops*

## Is the Shireegiin Gashuun a formation of its own, and does the Bayanshiree assignment reach this holotype?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** The Shireegiin Gashuun is a locality and a basin, and the holotype takes the Bayanshiree through its locality. No source read places ZPAL MgD-I/156 itself in a formation. Maryańska (1977) assigns the Sheeregeen Gashoon sediments, "or at least a part of them", to the Bayn Shireh svita, and Jerzykiewicz and Russell (1991) list Shiregin Gashun among the formation's localities; both write of the beds, and Maryańska's own Sheeregeen Gashoon specimens are ankylosaurs. Governs `location.formation: Bayanshiree` and `location.locality: Shireegiin Gashuun` on [*Graciliceratops mongoliensis*](../../../genera/G/Graciliceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maryańska1977a, p. 134: "The same pertains to Sheeregeen Gashoon sediments … these sediments or at least a part of them, represent the Bayn Shireh svita and are Upper Cenomanian to Lower Santonian in age".
- jerzykiewicz1991a, p. 367: "Shiregin Gashun (Martinson, 1982, loc. 14; Gradzinski et al., 1977)", listed under the Bayn Shiren Formation.
- weishampel2004b, p. 597: "9. Shireegiin Gashuun Formation (= Bayanshiree Svita; Maryańska and Osmólska 1975; Mikhailov et al. 1994; Sereno 2000)", with *Graciliceratops mongoliensis* in its fauna.
- benton2000c, Mongolian place names: "Shireegiin Gashuun [locality]"; "Shireegiin Gashuun Basin".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Shireegiin Gashuun Formation separate from the Bayanshiree.* The distribution chapter of *The Dinosauria* uses the name, but equates it with the Bayanshiree Svita in the same line. Benton and colleagues (2000) list the name only as a locality and a basin.

**Open.** Whether the holotype comes from the part of the Shiregin Gashun beds that Maryańska assigns to the Bayn Shireh svita. Currie and Eberth (1993) keep the Shiregin Gashun sites in a separate column because "there is little overlap between the faunas of the two areas, indicating that the Shiregin Gashun sites were temporally and/or environmentally distinct" (currie1993d, Table 1). A measured section at the holotype's site would settle it.
