# *Gannansaurus*

## Is *Gannansaurus* a titanosaur?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Not on the evidence held; it stays at Somphospondyli. Its describers place it there, closest to *Euhelopus*, without an analysis, and the one analysis that includes it leaves it unresolved near the base of Titanosauriformes, outside the derived titanosaurs. Governs `parent: Somphospondyli` on [*Gannansaurus sinensis*](../../../genera/G/Gannansaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2013c, section 4: "Gannansaurus is assigned to Somphospondyli, based on the following characters: presacral vertebrae composed of spongy bone and mid-dorsal neural spines posterodorsally inclined"; abstract: "more closely related to Euhelopus rather than to other titanosauriform sauropods".
- mo2023a, Results: "Gannansaurus as a titanosauriform outside the late-diverging clade comprising the Diamantinasauria and Lithostrotia", in "a large basal polytomy near the base of the Titanosauriformes".

**Ruled out.**
- *Titanosauria.* Jones (2026) suggests it from anatomy shared with later-described titanosaur material; no analysis read here recovers it.

**Open.** Its position in an analysis that resolves it.
