# *Galvesaurus*

## Where does *Galvesaurus* sit?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it sits at Eusauropoda incertae sedis. Analyses place it in Turiasauria, among macronarians outside Titanosauriformes, or within Titanosauriformes; Eusauropoda is the nearest clade every result shares. Governs `parent: Eusauropoda`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Galvesaurus herreroi*](../../../genera/G/Galvesaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- royo-torres2006a, Fig. 3, p. 1926: Turiasauria with *Losillasaurus* and *Turiasaurus*, outside Neosauropoda.
- mannion2017a, p. 60: "Our topology also provides further support for the macronarian affinities of Galveosaurus … differing from a position within Turiasauria".
- gomez2024a, Fig. 21, p. 33: within Macronaria, outside Titanosauriformes.
- pérez-pueyo2019a, p. 375: "the inclusion of Galvesaurus in the clade Titanosauriformes, as a sister taxon to Lusotitan".

**Ruled out.**
- *Titanosauriformes as the parent.* Most analyses place it outside the clade.
- *Macronaria as the parent.* Royo-Torres and colleagues (2006) and Mocho and colleagues (2014) recover it in Turiasauria, outside Neosauropoda.

**Open.** Whether the turiasaurian result recurs on later matrices.
