# *Goyocephale*

## Which aimag and which unit is the *Goyocephale lattimorei* holotype from?

*2026-08-24*

**Conclusion.** Ömnögovi, in no named unit. Perle and colleagues (1982) place Boro Khovil "10 km west of Dzamyn Khond", 60 km west of Bulgan Somon and south of the Arts Bogd Ull, in the South Gobi Desert, and give the horizon only as Upper Cretaceous red sandstones. Governs `location.region: MN-053` and the empty `location.formation` on [*Goyocephale lattimorei*](../../../genera/G/Goyocephale.yml).

**Evidence.**
- perle1982a, p. 115: "an incomplete skeleton was collected by the senior author at the locality Boro Khovil 10 km west of Dzamyn Khond (60 km west of Bulgan Somon and south of the Arts Bogd Ull Mountain) in the southern Gobi Desert, Mongolia".
- perle1982a, p. 117: "Type horizon: Upper Cretaceous, red sandstones; no precise age determination"; "Type locality: Boro Khovil, 10 km west of Dzamyn Khond, South Gobi Desert, Mongolia".
- sullivan2006a, p. 353: "Upper Cretaceous (Boro Khovil), South Gobi Desert, Mongolia".

**Ruled out.**
- *Övörkhangai.* The distribution chapter of *The Dinosauria* lists the species under "47.5. ÖVÖRKHANGAI, MONGOLIA 1. Unnamed unit (Perle et al. 1982)" (weishampel2004b, p. 597). The paper it cites gives the South Gobi, and its bearings put the site south of the Arts Bogd and west of Bulgan Somon. That the Arts Bogd runs along the boundary between the two aimags, and that this Bulgan Somon is the Ömnögovi district of that name, is our reading of the bearings, not a statement in any source read.
- *A "Boro Khovil Sandstone" formation.* Jones (2026) gives the horizon as "Boro Khovil Sandstone, Mongolia" in a systematics table (jones2026a, Pachycephalosauria table). No paper read erects a unit of that name, and the distribution chapter lists the horizon as an unnamed unit.

**Open.** No source read names the aimag outright; "South Gobi" may be the aimag's English name or a description of the region. A map or gazetteer entry for Boro Khovil would settle it.
