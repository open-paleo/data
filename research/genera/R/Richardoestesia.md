# *Richardoestesia*

## Is *Asiamericana asiatica* a species of *Richardoestesia*?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Sues and Averianov (2013) found denticles on the holotype tooth, which removed the basis for Nessov's alternative that it came from a saurodontid fish, and transferred the species to *Richardoestesia*. Averianov and Sues (2019) revised its diagnosis on 224 Bissekty teeth, and later work uses the name. *Asiamericana* becomes a junior subjective synonym of *Richardoestesia*, and the species takes the Turonian of the Bissekty Formation rather than Nessov's Coniacian. Governs the *Richardoestesia asiatica* species entry and the *Asiamericana* synonym on [*Richardoestesia*](../../../genera/R/Richardoestesia.yml), and the removal of the *Asiamericana* genus record.

**Evidence.**
- nessov1995a, p. 45 (translated): the genus is placed as "SAURISCHIA incertae sedis (THEROPODA?: SPINOSAURIDAE?) or PISCES: OSTEICHTHYES: TELEOSTEI: SAURODONTIDAE", on three teeth from the "Coniacian of Uzbekistan".
- sues2013a, p. 311: "Nessov's comparison of '*Asiamericana*' *asiatica* to teeth of saurodontid fishes was based on overall form and his assumption that '*Asiamericana*' *asiatica* lacks denticles. However, the carinae of CCMGE 460/12457 do have tiny denticles (Fig. 3B), and the tooth closely resembles teeth identified as *Richardoestesia* sp. ... and *Richardoestesia isosceles* (Sankey 2001)."
- sues2013a, p. 310: the species is headed "*Richardoestesia asiatica* (Nessov 1995)", with the holotype from "Bissekty Formation; age: Late Cretaceous (middle–late Turonian)".
- averianov2019b, p. e1614941-3: "Holotype—CCMGE 460/12457, an isolated tooth"; "Bissekty Formation, Upper Cretaceous (middle–upper Turonian)"; a revised diagnosis separating it from *R. gilmorei* by "a fully serrated mesial carina on most specimens and the absence of interdenticular sulci".
- tanaka2021a, Introduction: lists "*Richardoestesia asiatica*" among the predatory theropods described from the Bissekty Formation.

**Ruled out.**
- *A saurodontid fish.* Nessov offered it as an alternative in 1995 and listed the species among the fishes of the middle–upper Bissekty Suite in 1997 (nessov1997a, Dzharakuduk II section, translated: "Saurodontidae; *Asiamericana asiatica*"), and Buffetaut (2008, p. 747) calls the fish reading "certainly correct". All of this predates the denticles reported by Sues and Averianov (2013), and no later work returns to it.
- *Keeping Asiamericana as a separate nomen dubium.* No source read treats the genus as dubious after 2013; the reference works record the transfer (molina-pérez2019a; jones2026c).
- *Coniacian.* Nessov dates the species and its Dzharakuduk level as Coniacian; the two papers that redescribe the holotype both give middle–late Turonian for the Bissekty Formation.

**Open.** The two primary works that hold the transfer share their authors; Tanaka and colleagues (2021) use the name without discussing it.

## Is *Richardoestesia isosceles* valid, disputed or a nomen dubium?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** A nomen dubium. No work read defends the species after Sankey (2001) named it on a single tooth, and the one dedicated revision treats it as dubious. Governs `status` and the removal of `diagnostic_features` on *Richardoestesia isosceles* in [*Richardoestesia*](../../../genera/R/Richardoestesia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2019b, p. e1614941-11: "Based on current evidence, *R. isosceles* cannot be differentiated from *R. asiatica* and should be considered a subjective junior synonym of the latter. However, taking into account the incomplete and somewhat unusual nature of the holotype of *R. isosceles*, this taxon should rather be treated as a nomen dubium."
- averianov2019b, p. e1614941-11: of Sankey's diagnosis, "only two characters really distinguish this species from *R. gilmorei*", and "both features are also present in *R. asiatica* (Nesov, 1995), which has taxonomic priority".
- larson2013a, Discussion: "Rauhut [32] argued that the species was not sufficiently diagnosed and regarded *R. isosceles* as a nomen dubium. Longrich [55] ... regarded *R. isosceles* as a subjective junior synonym of *R. gilmorei*."

**Ruled out.**
- *Valid.* Sankey (2001) is the only source read that treats it as valid; holtz2004a lists it in a compilation table.
- *Disputed.* The later views split only between nomen dubium and junior synonym, both against validity; Larson and Currie (2013) use "cf. *R. isosceles*" as a morphotype without arguing for the species.
- *A junior synonym of R. asiatica.* Averianov and Sues (2019) raise it and decline it for the condition of the holotype.

**Open.** The works by Rauhut (2002) and Longrich (2008) are known here only as Larson and Currie (2013) report them.
