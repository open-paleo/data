# *Rinconsaurus*

## Does a source print a position for the *Rinconsaurus* quarry?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Pérez-Moreno and colleagues (2023) print the Cañadón Río Seco site at 68°55′34.20″W, 37°25′7.80″S, and state that the holotype and paratypes come from a single quarry there. It converts to -37.418833, -68.926167. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Rinconsaurus caudamirus*](../../../genera/R/Rinconsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pérez-moreno2023a, p. 2, 2 Material and methods: "The specimens here described come from the Cañadon Rio Seco site (68°55′34.20″W; 37°25′7.80″S; Fig. 1), located at 2 km south of Rincón de los Sauces".
- pérez-moreno2023a, p. 5, Locality and Horizon: "Both the holotype and paratypes come from a single quarry and not from multiple localities over an area."

**Ruled out.**
- *A corrupted string that cannot be read.* The earlier deletion held that no confident reading of the paper's degrees, minutes and seconds was possible. The corruption is in the converted text only, where the degree and prime marks became zeros; the printed page reads cleanly.
- *The deleted pair, -37.383331, -68.900002.* It lies about 4.6 km from the printed position and no source read prints it.

**Open.** The site's bearing from Rincón de los Sauces, which this paper gives as south and Calvo and González Riga (2003) as north.
