# *Rayososaurus*

## Where was the Rayososaurus holotype dug?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** At the excavation site Carballido and colleagues (2010) relocated in the field, on the western face of a canyon 2.4 km east-southeast of Primary School No. 244 in Agrio del Medio, in the upper Candeleros Formation. The coordinate is the one they print for that site. Governs `location.locality`, `location.coordinates` and `location.part: upper` on [*Rayososaurus agrioensis*](../../../genera/R/Rayososaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carballido2010a, p. 496: "it has been possible to locate the sector where the extraction work was carried out during the field campaign directed by Dr Bonaparte in 1991. This includes the outcroppings of the western face of the canyon situated 2.4 km east-southeast of Primary School No. 244 in Agrio del Medio (38˚ 21' 10'' S – 69˚ 54' 57'' W"; Locality and Horizon: "the bonebearing level corresponds to the upper section of the Candeleros Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Three kilometers south of the school in Agrio del Medio.* Bonaparte's (1996) placement, known here as Carballido and colleagues quote it: "some 3 km to the south of the school in Agrio del Medio" (carballido2010a, p. 495). They introduce it by noting that "the available references regarding the location of the discovery site lack precise data with respect to the exact place where the excavation was made" (p. 495), and replace it with the relocated site.
- *The Rincón Member of the Rayoso Formation.* Leanza (2003) proposed it, as Carballido and colleagues report, while referring to "the considerable margin of doubt in the matter" (carballido2010a, p. 495). The relocated site exposes only the uppermost Candeleros and the base of the Huincul, which "allows us to rule out for a start the Rayoso Formation" (p. 496).

**Open.** Nothing.
