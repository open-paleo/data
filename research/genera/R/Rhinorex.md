# *Rhinorex*

## Is the Rhinorex holotype BYU 13258 or BYU 13528?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** BYU 13258, the number in the holotype designation. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Rhinorex condrupus*](../../../genera/R/Rhinorex.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gates2014a, Systematic palaeontology: "Holotype. BYU 13258, partial, but mostly articulated skeleton and skull."

**Ruled out.**
- *BYU 13528.* The describing paper prints the holotype both ways, and once as "BYU 13285": 13258 in the introduction, the designation and parts of the description, 13528 in the excavation and stratigraphic account and in the captions of Figs 2–7 ("Rhinorex condrupus gen. et sp. nov., BYU 13528", Fig. 4). All three refer to the one skeleton the paper describes, and only the designation states the number as such (our reading). Longrich and colleagues (2024) and Yao and colleagues (2026) cite "BYU 13528" (longrich2024b, Fig. 18; yao2026a, p. 10), repeating the caption form.

**Open.** Which number the Brigham Young University catalogue carries. A collection record would settle it; none of the papers read for this note prints one.
