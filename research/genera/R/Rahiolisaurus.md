# *Rahiolisaurus*

## Whose site does the coordinate printed for the Rahiolisaurus type locality mark?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** Temple Hill, the *Rajasaurus* site. Novas and colleagues (2010) print, to the tenth of a second, the pair Wilson and colleagues (2003) print for Temple Hill, while placing Temple Hill about 400 m northwest of their own quarry. Each paper prints the pair in its own type-locality line, so both records keep it, converted from the printed degrees, minutes and seconds. For *Rahiolisaurus* it is good to about 400 m rather than to the precision printed (our reading of the two statements). Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Rahiolisaurus gujaratensis*](../../../genera/R/Rahiolisaurus.yml) and [*Rajasaurus narmadensis*](../../../genera/R/Rajasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas2010b, p. 47: "*Type locality* Near Rahioli village (23°3′ 26.2″ N, 73°20′ 30.8″ E), Kheda District, Gujarat, western India"; p. 60: *Rajasaurus* is "from the Temple Hill locality, about 400 m northwest of our Rahioli site".
- wilson2003a, p. 5: "*Type locality*.— Temple Hill, near Rahioli (23° 3' 26.2" N, 73° 20' 30.8" E)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Deleting the Rahiolisaurus pair as another specimen's site.* The pair belongs to Temple Hill, but Novas and colleagues print it as the type locality of this holotype, and no source read gives the Rahioli quarry a position of its own. Keeping it records what the describing paper states; the location note says whose figure it is.

**Open.** The quarry's own position. A source that surveys the Rahioli quarry separately from Temple Hill would settle it; Chatterjee and Rudra (1996), who reported the bone bed, have not been read.
