# *Rocasaurus*

## What is the *Rocasaurus muniozi* holotype's museum code?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** MPCA, the Museo Provincial de Cipolletti "Carlos Ameghino". Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MPCA-PV 46]` and `institution: MPCA` on [*Rocasaurus muniozi*](../../../genera/R/Rocasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- salgado2000a, Systematic paleontology: "Holotype. MPCA-Pv 46 (figures 2-6): a juvenile individual represented by a cervical centrum, a cervical neural arch, two dorsal centra" (translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *MCPA-Pv 46.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) print "MCPA-Pv 46" for the species, and "MCPA-Pv 57" for the cf. *Rocasaurus* caudals in the same table (molina-pérez2020a, p. 269). The describing paper writes MPCA, so MCPA swaps two letters of the code.

**Open.** The page in the Spanish original. Only Lamanna's English translation has been read.
