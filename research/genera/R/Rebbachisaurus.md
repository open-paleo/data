# *Rebbachisaurus*

## Where was the Rebbachisaurus holotype found, and does any source give its position?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** On a sandstone butte at the southwest foot of Gara Sbaa, in the Kem Kem beds that Ibrahim and colleagues (2020) name the Gara Sbaa Formation at that locality. No source read prints a position for the butte. Governs `location.locality`, `location.formation` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Rebbachisaurus garasbae*](../../../genera/R/Rebbachisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wilson2015a, p. 5, Locality and Horizon: Lavocat (1954) "further specified that the materials of R. garasbae were found at a locality that 'has the form of a sandstone butte situated at the southwest foot of Gara Sbaa ... the principal locality is a small ledge, 3 m from the summit of the butte'" (translated by Allain).
- ibrahim2020a, section Gara Sbaa Formation: "The Gara Sbaa Formation, with stratotype at the locality Gara Sbaa"; Table 2: "Six fossil vertebrate localities with geographic coordinates prospected during field work in 2008", listing "Gara Sbaa 30°30'40.64"N 4°50'42.87"E".

**Ruled out.**
- *Ibrahim and colleagues' Gara Sbaa point.* It is a 2008 fieldwork position for the locality as a whole, an escarpment that "serves as a geographic landmark in the Kem Kem region" (ibrahim2020a, Fig. 11), not Lavocat's ledge. Its longitude is also printed east, though all of Morocco lies west of Greenwich; read as west it converts to 30.511289, −4.845242, which repairs the hemisphere but still marks the landmark rather than the holotype's site.
- *Tegama Formation.* Compilations list it for this species; Wilson and Allain (2015) show the error: "The provenance of Rebbachisaurus garasbae has been listed erroneously as 'Tegama Formation' (Upchurch et al., 2004a; Weishampel et al., 2004). The Tegama Formation of Niger is actually the provenance of most of the remains attributed to 'R.' tamesnensis" (wilson2015a, p. 5).

**Open.** The butte's position. A later relocation of Lavocat's site that prints a coordinate would settle it.
