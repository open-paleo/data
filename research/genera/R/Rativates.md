# *Rativates*

## Is the *Rativates evadens* holotype from the Oldman or the Dinosaur Park Formation?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** The lower Dinosaur Park Formation. The describing paper places ROM 1790 there. Governs `location.formation: Dinosaur Park` and `location.part: lower` on [*Rativates evadens*](../../../genera/R/Rativates.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mcfeeters2016a, p. 1: "ROM 1790, from the lower Dinosaur Park Formation (upper Campanian) of Alberta".

**Ruled out.**
- *Oldman Formation.* The record's earlier value. No source read places ROM 1790 in the Oldman. Jones (2026) gives "Dinosaur Park Fm., Canada" (jones2026c, p. 503). See [Oldman and Dinosaur Park](../../topics/oldman-and-dinosaur-park.md) for why a Park locality alone does not settle the formation.

**Open.** Nothing.
