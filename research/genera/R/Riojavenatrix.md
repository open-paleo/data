# *Riojavenatrix*

## Which catalogue numbers make up the *Riojavenatrix lacustris* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** The fifteen numbers of the holotype list. Three of them are split into A and B parts, which gives 18 ids. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` (CPI 1637, 1638, 1639A, 1639B, 1640, 1641A, 1641B, 1642–1648, 1675A, 1675B, 1676, 1677, each written out) on [*Riojavenatrix lacustris*](../../../genera/R/Riojavenatrix.yml).

**Evidence.**
- isasmendi2024a, p. 5, Systematic palaeontology: "*Holotype:* A partial skeleton of a single individual, including: *CPI 1637*, left femur; *CPI 1638*, left tibia and astragalus; *CPI 1639A– B*, left fibula; … *CPI 1675A–B*, left pubis; *CPI 1676*, left calcaneum; and *CPI 1677*, fragment of a dorsal neural arch."

**Ruled out.**
- *CPI 1637 alone.* The record's earlier value, the left femur only.
- *"CPI 1640-CPI 1648, CPI 1675–1677".* Jones (2026) gives these ranges (jones2026c, p. 498). They leave out CPI 1637–1639 and compress the rest into ranges, which a `specimen_id` never holds (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-specimen_id-takes-the-registrys-prefix-and-the-institutions-established-form)).

**Open.** Nothing.
