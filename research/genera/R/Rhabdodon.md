# *Rhabdodon*

## What is the Rhabdodon priscus lectotype, and where is it?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** MPLM 30, a partial left dentary, in the Palais Longchamp collection, Marseille, printed earlier as "MNPL 30"; it could not be relocated in 2023. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: MPLM 30`, `specimen_type: lectotype` and `institution` on *Rhabdodon priscus*.

**Evidence.**
- czepiński2025a, p. 12: "Lectotype MPLM 30, a partial left dentary missing the anterior portion", with paralectotypes including "MPLM 51, left radius (paralectotype)"; p. 5: "MPLM, Palais Longchamp Museum, Marseille, France"; p. 4: MPLM 30 and 31 "were not re-located in the MPLM collection during the visit of one of us (Ł.C.) to Marseille in November 2023".
- garcia1999a, p. 416: "Lectotype. Part of left dentary (MNPL 30). Matheron 1869, Lapparent 1947 and Brinkmann 1988: Natural History Museum of Marseilles", relaying the designation.

**Ruled out.**
- *A number from Matheron.* Matheron (1869) prints no catalogue numbers.

**Open.** Whether MNPL and MPLM are the same collection (our reading; Czepiński and colleagues do not use MNPL), and whether MPLM 51 is a radius (Czepiński and colleagues) or an ulna ("left ulna (MNPL 51)", garcia1999a, p. 416).

## Which unit holds the Rhabdodon priscus lectotype?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** No named unit. The paper that states the lectotype's horizon gives a regional stage and a lithology at the Tunnel de la Nerthe, not a formation. Governs the empty `location.formation` on [*Rhabdodon priscus*](../../../genera/R/Rhabdodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- czepiński2025a, p. 12: the Locality line places the "Tunnel de la Nerthe" in "Bouches-du-Rhône, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur"; "Horizon Early Rognacian lacustrian marl (lower Maastrichtian, Upper Cretaceous"; p. 13, Fig. 9: "lectotype and paralectotype dentaries from the Lower Rognacian lacustrine marls of 'Tunnel de la Nerthe'".

**Ruled out.**
- *Marnes Rouges Inférieures Formation.* The Fig. 2 caption of the same paper has the lectotype "from the Marnes Rouges Inférieures Formation, la Nerthe" (czepiński2025a, p. 3), against its own Horizon entry and the Fig. 9 caption for the same specimen. The unit is the Aude's, and la Nerthe is in Bouches-du-Rhône: Weishampel and colleagues list "3. Marnes Rouges Inférieures Formation" under "14.37. Departement de l'Aude, France" (weishampel2004b, p. 590).
- *Grès à Reptiles.* Norman lists "Gres a Reptiles Formation (Bouches-du-Rhône)" among the species' units (norman2004a, p. 414, Table 19.1), in a distribution list for the species, not a statement about the type locality; for why the Provence "Grès à Reptiles" are assigned by basin, see [Grès à Reptiles](../../topics/grès-à-reptiles.md).

**Open.** Whether the la Nerthe marls belong to the Lower Argiles et Grès à Reptiles of the Aix-en-Provence Basin, the Lower Rognacian unit there. Placing them in it would be a correlation of our own from the stage; a source that names the unit at la Nerthe would settle it.
