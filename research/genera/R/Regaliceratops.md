# *Regaliceratops*

## Is the Regaliceratops holotype from the St. Mary River or the Willow Creek Formation?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** The upper St. Mary River Formation, as the describing paper places it, below the contact with the Willow Creek, which crops out in the same area. Governs `location.formation: St. Mary River` and `location.part: upper` on [*Regaliceratops peterhewsi*](../../../genera/R/Regaliceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown2015a, Type Locality, Horizon, and Age: "The uppermost St. Mary River Formation and lowermost Willow Creek Formation are both exposed along the river in this area"; "existing geologic maps of the area suggest that the quarry occurs within the upper 30 m of the St. Mary River Formation"; "This stratigraphic position is supported by the presence of the angiosperm palynomorph *Scollardia trapaformis*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Willow Creek Formation.* Dalman and colleagues' table of chasmosaurines gives "Willow Creek Formation" for *Regaliceratops*, citing Brown and Henderson (2015) (dalman2021b, p. 2, Table 1), the paper that places the quarry in the St. Mary River.

**Open.** The quarry's height below the contact. Brown and Henderson write that "it has been difficult to determine the precise stratigraphic position of the specimen in relation to the contact" because the strata are faulted and the marker beds are not exposed at the quarry; their placement rests on the geologic map they cite and the palynomorph. A measured section tying the quarry to the Battle Formation or Whitemud Member would settle it.
