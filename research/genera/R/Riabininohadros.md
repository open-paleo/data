# *Riabininohadros*

## What age is the Riabininohadros holotype?

*2026-09-03*

**Conclusion.** Late Maastrichtian. Riabinin's (1945) Danian dating of the Besh-Kosh beds was later revised to the upper Maastrichtian. Governs `period: Late Cretaceous` and `stage: Maastrichtian` on [*Riabininohadros weberae*](../../../genera/R/Riabininohadros.yml).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2019a, p. 398: "The age of the fossil was initially considered to be Danian, but was later revised to late Maastrichtian (Jeletzky, 1962)"; p. 399, holotype: "Besh-Kosh Mountain east of Bakhchisaray; unnamed upper Maastrichtian beds (Upper Cretaceous)".
- lopatin2020a, p. 320: "The bone-bearing deposits were originally dated as Danian (Riabinin, 1945, 1946; Yanshin, 1960), but were later assigned to the upper Maastrichtian (see Naidin, 1960; Jeletzky, 1962; *Verkhnii mel*, 1986)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Danian.* Riabinin gives "Lower part of the Danian stage (layer of limestone with *Aequipecten meridionalis* Eichw.) on the summit of Mount Besh-kosh in the vicinity of Bakhchisaray in Crimea" (riabinin1945a, p. 5, translated). Averianov and Lopatin, and Lopatin and Averianov, both report it as revised.

**Open.** The revising papers, Naidin (1960) and Jeletzky, have not been read, so the Maastrichtian is known here as Averianov and Lopatin report it. Averianov and Lopatin's bibliography dates Jeletzky's paper to 1962 (*Journal of Paleontology* 36), and Ulansky (2015) to 1963 (vol. 37); the paper itself would settle the year.
