# *Rapetosaurus*

## What stage is the *Rapetosaurus krausei* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Maastrichtian, the age of the Maevarano Formation (see [Maevarano](../../stratigraphy/m/maevarano.md)). Governs `stage: [Maastrichtian]` on [*Rapetosaurus krausei*](../../../genera/R/Rapetosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rogers2000a, p. 275: "the most fossiliferous portions of the Maevarano Formation are Maastrichtian in age, rather than Campanian as previously reported".
- curryrogers2001a, p. 530, Fig. 1 caption: *Rapetosaurus krausei* "from the Upper Cretaceous Maevarano Formation of Madagascar".

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) give "Maevarano Formation (Majunga), Madagascar | Campanian" (upchurch2004a, p. 270, Table 13.1). It repeats the reading that Rogers and colleagues replaced four years before. Jones (2026) gives the Maastrichtian (jones2026b, p. 386).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is FMNH PR 2209 part of the *Rapetosaurus krausei* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype is the adult skull UA 8698, at the Université d'Antananarivo; FMNH PR 2209 is a referred juvenile skeleton. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [UA 8698]` on [*Rapetosaurus krausei*](../../../genera/R/Rapetosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- curryrogers2001a, p. 530: "Holotype. Université d'Antananarivo UA 8698, adult skull including right maxilla with eight teeth"; "Referred specimens. Field Museum of Natural History FMNH PR 2184-2192, 2194, 2196, 2197, 2209, 2210".
- curryrogers2001a, Fig. 3 caption: postcranial elements "all from referred juvenile skeleton (FMNH PR 2209)".

**Ruled out.**
- *FMNH PR 2209.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give "*Rapetosaurus krausei* (juvenile) | FMNH PR 2209" (molina-pérez2020a, p. 169), a referred specimen. Jones (2026) lists "UA 8698" first and "FMNH PR series—partial juvenile skeleton" after it (jones2026b, p. 386).

**Open.** Nothing.
