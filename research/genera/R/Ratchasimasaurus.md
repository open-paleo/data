# *Ratchasimasaurus*

## Is *Ratchasimasaurus suranareae* a nomen dubium?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** No. Its describers diagnosed it on the dentary, and every published work read treats it as valid. Governs `status: valid` and the dispute summary on [*Ratchasimasaurus suranareae*](../../../genera/R/Ratchasimasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- shibata2011a, Abstract: "A unique character, which is an elongated and flat shape of the dentary ramus, makes it possible to assign the specimen to the new genus of nonhadrosaurid iguanodontian, Ratchasimasaurus suranareae gen. et sp. nov."
- poole2022a lists *Ratchasimasaurus suranareae* with its holotype, NRRU-A2064, among the taxa it treats.
- madzia2021a places it within Hadrosauroidea in its reference phylogeny.

**Ruled out.**
- *A nomen dubium.* The record once mentioned a minority view to this effect. Its source is Poole's 2015 doctoral thesis, which is not a published determination; the published version of that work (Poole, 2022) treats the taxon as valid. Norman (2015) suggests only that *Siamodon* might be referable to it.

**Open.** Nothing.
