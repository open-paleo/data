# *Ruehleia*

## What is the Ruehleia holotype's number, and which institution code applies?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** MB.R. 4718 ("Individual 1"), Museum für Naturkunde, Berlin, whose fossil collections use the code MB. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` and `institution` (MB, not ZMB) on *Ruehleia bedheimensis*.

**Evidence.**
- sues2025a, p. 463: "Holotype. MB.R. 4718 ('Individual 1'), partial postcranial skeleton"; p. 414: "MB, Museum für Naturkunde, Berlin".

**Ruled out.**
- *MB.R.4718–42.* Jones (2026) writes that "a later paper would refer to the remains as MB.R.4718–42 (Otero, 2018)" (jones2026b, p. 41); no such range is given for the holotype in Sues and Schoch.

**Open.** The designating paper. Galton (2001), where the genus was erected, has not been read; Moser dates a related Galton paper "(pro 2001)" (moser2003a, p. 186).
