# *Rajasaurus*

## How is the *Rajasaurus narmadensis* holotype numbered?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** As 33 separate numbers, GSI 21141/1 to GSI 21141/33, one for each element Wilson and colleagues (2003) tabulate. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` (the 33 ids GSI 21141/1 … GSI 21141/33) on [*Rajasaurus narmadensis*](../../../genera/R/Rajasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wilson2003a, p. 4: "*Holotype*.— GSI Type No. 21141/1-33, a partial skeleton consisting of a braincase, cervical centrum, partial dorsal vertebrae, sacrum".
- wilson2003a, p. 5, Table 1: "Field numbers for elements of the holotypic specimen of *Rajasaurus narmadensis* (GSI Type No. 21141)", which lists the 33 elements with no gaps in the numbering.

**Ruled out.**
- *"GSI Type No. 21141/1-33" as one id.* The record's earlier value. A range is written out as its separate numbers (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-specimen_id-takes-the-registrys-prefix-and-the-institutions-established-form)), and "Type No." is not part of the number. Jones (2026) prints the same range, "GSI No. 21141/1-33" (jones2026c, p. 496).

**Open.** Nothing.
