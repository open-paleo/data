# *Vayuraptor*

## Is *Vayuraptor nongbualamphuensis* Barremian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** No. The record follows the detrital-zircon age of the Sao Khua Formation, late Valanginian to early Hauterivian (see [Sao Khua](../../stratigraphy/s/sao-khua.md)). No age has been measured at Phu Wat or anywhere near Nong Bua Lamphu, so the holotype takes the formation's reading. Jones (2026) agrees: "Sao Khua Fm., Thailand *Early Cretaceous, Valanginian/ Hauterivian*" (jones2026c, Coelurosauria table). Governs `period.stage: [Valanginian, Hauterivian]` on [*Vayuraptor nongbualamphuensis*](../../../genera/V/Vayuraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tucker2022a, p. 15: "a tightly constrained late Valanginian–early Hauterivian age of 133.6–132.1 Ma for Sao Khua Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian.* The record's value before 2026-08-05. The describing paper gives the type horizon as "Sao Khua Formation, probably upper Barremian?, Lower Cretaceous" (samathi2019a, p. 248), which rests on the bivalve correlation of Tumpeesuwan and colleagues (2010), rejected in [Sao Khua](../../stratigraphy/s/sao-khua.md).

**Open.** The Barremian is not excluded: Tucker and colleagues' figure is a maximum depositional age, and their samples come from the middle and upper formation, not from Phu Wat.
