# *Valdoraptor*

## Where is the Valdoraptor holotype from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The [Grinstead Clay](../../../stratigraphy/g/grinstead-clay.yml) at Cuckfield, West Sussex, upper Valanginian, by later attribution; Owen, who described the specimen, published no locality. Governs `location.formation: Grinstead Clay`, `location.locality: Cuckfield` and `stage: [Valanginian]` on *Valdoraptor oweni*.

**Evidence.**
- owen1858a, p. 18: "The specimen, No. 2556, in the British Museum", from "the Wealden matrix", with no locality.
- olshevsky1991a, p. 23: "BMNH R2559 … (with the incorrect number R2556)"; p. 113: "a partial left metatarsus from the Upper Wealden of Cuckfield, West Sussex".
- tucker2022a, p. 16: "'Valdoraptor' from the upper Valanginian Grinstead Clay Formation, England"; allain2014a, p. 132: "the Valanginian Grinstead Clay Formation (Naish, 2011)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tunbridge Wells Sand Formation.* Carrano and colleagues give "the Tunbridge Wells Sand Formation (Valanginian) of Cuckfield, Sussex" (carrano2012a, p. 256); the Grinstead Clay lies within or above it depending on rank (see the Grinstead Clay record).
- *Kent.* Weishampel and colleagues list it under "KENT, ENGLAND 1. Hastings Beds" (weishampel2004b, p. 569) and also under West Sussex; Holtz and colleagues give "Hasting Beds (Kent), England", "late Berriasian–Valanginian" (holtz2004a, p. 77, Table 4.1). Neither gives a Kent locality.

**Open.** Where Cuckfield comes from. Lydekker (1889) gives only "the metatarsus from the Wealden figured by Owen" (lydekker1889a, p. 325); Cuckfield appears in Olshevsky (1991) and later. That the register numbers place the specimen with Mantell's Cuckfield material is an inference (Taylor and colleagues give Cuckfield for BMNH R2544–2547, taylor2007a, p. 1559).
