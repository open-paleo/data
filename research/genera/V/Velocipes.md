# *Velocipes*

## Does the *Velocipes* record carry a coordinate?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** Yes: 50° 46′ N, 18° 26′ E, which Skawiński and colleagues (2017) print for the holotype's site. Their sentence names both the forester's lodge, the locality on the holotype's label, and an old clay pit about 200 m north-east of it, where they trace the conglomerate on the bone, and the parenthesis can attach to either. At whole-minute precision the two are the same position, so the reading does not change the value. Governs `location.coordinates: [50.766667, 18.433333]` on [*Velocipes guerichi*](../../../genera/V/Velocipes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- skawiński2017a: "The label on holotype indicates forester's lodge as a precise location. Our preliminary field investigation found the building, which is partly constructed with conglomerate fragments. They are similar to rock fragment, which remain on the bone. They came from old clay-pit approximately 200 metres on the north-east from the lodge (50° 46′ N, 18° 26′ E)."

**Ruled out.**
- *No coordinate.* The pair is printed for this specimen's site, under either reading of the sentence.

**Open.** That the lodge and the pit fall in the same arcminute cell is our inference from the 200 m between them; a point near a minute boundary could round to the next minute.

## Is the *Velocipes* site near Guttenberg?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** No. Huene's "Guttenberg" is an error for Guttentag, now Dobrodzień. The site is the forester's lodge at Kotzuren, now Kocury, in Upper Silesia. Governs `location.locality: Kocury` on [*Velocipes guerichi*](../../../genera/V/Velocipes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- huene1932a, p. 33: the fibula comes "from the Lissauer Breccia (= middle Keuper) of the Försterei Kotzuren near Guttenberg in Upper Silesia" (translated).
- skawiński2017a, Specimen from Kocury: "*Velocipes* was found in the clay-pit or quarry located at Kocury (Kotzuren) village near Dobrodzień (Guttentag – on holotype it is incorretly written as Guttenberg)"; the label "indicates forester's lodge as a precise location".

**Ruled out.**
- *Guttenberg.* Huene prints it, and Skawiński and colleagues (2017) report the same name on the holotype label. They identify the town near Kotzuren as Guttentag (Dobrodzień).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Velocipes guerichi* holotype GPIM UH 252 or BMNH R38058?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** GPIM UH 252, the proximal part of a left fibula in the Hamburg collection. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [GPIM UH 252]` on [*Velocipes guerichi*](../../../genera/V/Velocipes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- skawiński2017a, Systematic palaeontology: "Holotype. GPIM UH No. 252 (Geological-Palaeontological Institute and Museum of the University of Hamburg, specimen No. 252), proximal part of left fibula."

**Ruled out.**
- *BMNH R38058.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give "BMNH R38058" for *Velocipes guerichi* and the same number in the next row for *Dolichosuchus cristatus* (molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods). It is that taxon's number repeated, and no paper read gives a London number for the *Velocipes* fibula.

**Open.** Nothing.
