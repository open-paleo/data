# *Venenosaurus*

## What stage is the *Venenosaurus dicrocei* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Aptian. The holotype comes from the Poison Strip Member of the Cedar Mountain Formation, which Kirkland and colleagues (2016) place in the early to middle Aptian. Governs `stages: [Aptian]` and `location.member: Poison Strip Sandstone` on [*Venenosaurus dicrocei*](../../../genera/V/Venenosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tidwell2001a, p. 142: "Horizon and Locality: Poison Strip Member, Cedar Mountain Formation (Lower Cretaceous); Grand County Utah, United States."
- kirkland2016a, p. 142: for the Poison Strip Member, "Overall, an early to middle Aptian age assignment seems most likely for these strata".

**Ruled out.**
- *Berriasian–Hauterivian.* Upchurch and colleagues (2004) give "Lower Cedar Mountain Formation (Utah), United States" and "Berriasian–Hauterivian" (upchurch2004a, Table 13.1). The row dates the lower Cedar Mountain as a whole, not the Poison Strip Member. Jones (2026) gives Aptian (jones2026b, Macronaria table).

**Open.** Kirkland and colleagues rest the Poison Strip age on biostratigraphy and carbon-isotope chemostratigraphy, with a maximum age of 119.4 ± 2.6 Ma near its base at the Ruby Ranch Road section (kirkland2016a, p. 142); no date comes from Tony's Bone Bed itself.
