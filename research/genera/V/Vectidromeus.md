# *Vectidromeus*

## Which of the two printed positions is the *Vectidromeus* site?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** The latitude and longitude, as the record holds it. Longrich and colleagues (2024) print it twice, once as the position of "the locality of the holotype", while the grid reference in the same sentence lies about 2.4 km east-northeast of it. Choosing which printed value is wrong is not a repair the record makes (see [methods](../../methods.md#a-degraded-copy-of-a-printed-coordinate-is-corrected-not-deleted)). Governs `location.coordinates: [50.632056, -1.477028]` on [*Vectidromeus insularis*](../../../genera/V/Vectidromeus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- longrich2024c, author manuscript p. 8, Locality and Horizon: "Sudmoor Point; approximate Ordnance Survey grid reference SZ 391 827: Lat/long 50° 37' 55.4"N 01° 28' 37.3"W".
- longrich2024c, author manuscript p. 22, Fig. 1 caption: "Sudmoor Point, the locality of the holotype of Vectidromeus insularis, approximately Lat/long 50° 37' 55.4" N 01° 28' 37.3"W".
- longrich2024c, author manuscript p. 7, Geological setting: "Sudmoor Point (SZ 392 828) is on the southeast limb of the Brighstone anticline".
- hendry2010a, p. 2, Aims and Itinerary: "Sudmoor Point (SZ 395826)".

**Ruled out.**
- *The grid reference in place of the latitude and longitude.* The three grid references agree with each other to about 400 m, but two are given for Sudmoor Point in general, and the paper qualifies both of its own values as approximate. Their agreement suggests the latitude and longitude is the outlier; it does not show which value the authors meant.

**Open.** Which position is the site. A large-scale map of Sudmoor Point, or a statement from the authors, would settle it.

## Is the *Vectidromeus insularis* holotype Barremian or Hauterivian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Barremian, the stage the describing paper gives in its horizon line. The same paper allows a latest Hauterivian age, so the stage is not secure (see Open). Governs `stages: [Barremian]` on [*Vectidromeus insularis*](../../../genera/V/Vectidromeus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- longrich2024c, p. 8, Locality and Horizon: "Sudmoor Point ... Wessex Formation, Barremian (Fig. 1). The exposures at Sudmoor Point lie low in the section, near the base of the exposed Wessex Formation on the island".
- longrich2024c, p. 6: "Thus, the specimen is probably either earliest Barremian or latest Hauterivian in age. With the base of the Barremian dated to 125.77 Ma (Cohen et al., 2013), the Sudmoor Point exposures probably date to around 125-126 Ma."
- martill2015a, p. 378: "the exposed Wessex Formation is entirely Barremian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hauterivian.* Jones (2026) gives "Wessex Fm., United Kingdom, Early Cretaceous, Hauterivian" (jones2026a, Ornithopoda table) without a source for the stage. No paper read gives Hauterivian alone for the site.

**Open.** Whether the Sudmoor Point Sandstone lies below the Hauterivian–Barremian boundary. Jacobs and colleagues (2023) report that the boundary falls "a short distance above the Sudmoor Point Sandstone" (jacobs2023a), on palaeomagnetic results given as a personal communication. A published magnetostratigraphy of the Sudmoor Point section would settle it, and with it whether the stage should be Hauterivian–Barremian.
