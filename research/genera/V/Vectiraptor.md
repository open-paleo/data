# *Vectiraptor*

## Is the *Vectiraptor greeni* holotype one number or three?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** Three: IWCMS 2021.31.1, 2021.31.2 and 2021.31.3, two dorsal vertebrae and a partial sacrum. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` and `type_specimen.material` on [*Vectiraptor greeni*](../../../genera/V/Vectiraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- longrich2022a, p. 4, Systematic paleontology: "Holotype. IWCMS 2021.31.1-3 (Figs. 3-10); IWCMS 2021.31.2, anterior dorsal vertebra (Figs. 3,4), IWCMS 2021.31.1, posterior dorsal vertebra (Figs. 5-7), and IWCMS 2021.31.3 partial sacrum (Figs. 8,9)."

**Ruled out.**
- *IWCMS 2021.31.1-3 as one id, with two dorsal vertebrae as the material.* The record once held the range as a single string and left out the sacrum. Jones (2026) agrees with the describing paper: "IWCMS 2021.31.1, .2, and .3—partial vertebrae" (jones2026c, Eudromaeosauria table).

**Open.** Nothing.
