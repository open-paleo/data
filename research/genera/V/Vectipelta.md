# *Vectipelta*

## Is the *Vectipelta* holotype from the foreshore at Chilton Chine?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** No. The skeleton was excavated from a landslipped block in the cliffs about 50 m west of Chilton Chine. Only the vertebrae accessioned as IWCMS 1996.153 were picked up on the beach, the year before. Governs `location.locality: 50 m west of Chilton Chine` and `location.bed: L5` on [*Vectipelta barretti*](../../../genera/V/Vectipelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pond2023a, p. 6, Locality and horizon: "The specimen was discovered in a plant debris bed in a landslipped block that retained original stratigraphy, 50 m west of Chilton Chine"; "Plant debris bed L5 (Gale, 2019), Wessex Formation".
- pond2023a, p. 5: "Several vertebrae belonging to the specimen were collected from the beach in November 1993", accessioned as "IWCMS 1996.153. The remainder of the skeleton was found by Lin Spearpoint in March or April of 1994 in cliffs belonging to Richard Fisk".

**Ruled out.**
- *Foreshore, Chilton Chine.* The record read this until 2026-09-09. The only foreshore in the paper is where a photograph was taken from: "A, the site as seen from the foreshore at Chilton Chine" (pond2023a, p. 5, Fig. 2), a view of the dig site in the landslip. The beach-collected vertebrae were loose finds, and the paper ties the horizon to the landslipped block.

**Open.** Nothing.
