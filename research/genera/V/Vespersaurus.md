# *Vespersaurus*

## Is the *Vespersaurus* holotype from the Rio Paraná or the Goio Erê Formation?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** The Rio Paraná Formation, as Langer and colleagues (2019) assign it after re-examining the site. Later papers that put the same quarry in the Goio Erê do not discuss that reassignment. The quarry name in the record comes from de Souza and colleagues (2021); the describing paper gives only a road cut north of Cruzeiro do Oeste. Governs `location.formation: Rio Paraná` and `location.locality: Cemitério dos Pterossauros Quarry` on [*Vespersaurus paranaensis*](../../../genera/V/Vespersaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- langer2019a, p. 2, Geological Settings: "Previous accounts assign the fossil-bearing deposits of Cruzeiro do Oeste to the Goio Erê Formation, but the site is located in an area more commonly mapped as covered by sediments of the Rio Paraná Formation. This assignment is confirmed here, based on a recent *in loco* revaluation of the stratigraphic relations and sedimentary structures present in the area."
- langer2019a, p. 5, Type-locality and horizon: "Dark red sandstones of the likely early Late Cretaceous Rio Paraná Formation", "exposed at the overbanks of a secondary road", "about 2 km north of Cruzeiro do Oeste".
- desouza2021a, p. 11, Discussion: of the Cemitério dos Pterossauros Quarry, "the first dinosaur formally described was *Vespersaurus paranaensis*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Goio Erê Formation.* De Souza and colleagues (2021) give "Cemitério dos Pterossauros Quarry, Goio Erê Formation" for *Berthasaura*, from the same quarry (desouza2021a, p. 2, Horizon and locality), citing a basin-wide framework rather than the site. Arai and Dias-Brito (2023) list the *Vespersaurus* description among finds "relating to the Goioerê Formation (Caiuá Group)" (arai2023a, p. 6; translated), and treat the Rio Paraná and Goioerê as interdigitated. Neither paper engages the in-place reassignment, so neither is a case against it.

**Open.** Nothing.
