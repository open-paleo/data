# *Velocisaurus*

## Where was the Velocisaurus holotype collected?

*2026-10-03*

**Conclusion.** Neuquén city, on or near the campus of the Universidad Nacional del Comahue. Governs `location.locality: Neuquén city`.

**Evidence.**
- carrano2008a, p. 198: "OCCURRENCE. Campus of the Universidad Nacional del Comahue, Neuquén, Neuquén Province, Argentina; Bajo de la Carpa Formation".
- bonaparte1991a, title: "Los vertebrados fósiles de la Formación Río Colorado, de la Ciudad de Neuquén y Cercanías" ("from the city of Neuquén and surrounding areas", translated). The title only; the paper has not been read.
- powell2003a, p. 8: *Velocisaurus unicus* is listed in the faunal list for the "Neuquén" locality, where "'Boca del Sapo' is included in the area named 'Neuquen'".

**Ruled out.**
- *Boca del Sapo as the type locality.* No source read for this note pairs *Velocisaurus* with Boca del Sapo. Pittman and Xu (2020) give "Boca del Sapo, Universidad Nacional del Comahue" for *Alvarezsaurus*, *Neuquenornis* and *Patagopteryx* from the same describing paper (pittman2020a, Appendix), not for *Velocisaurus*. Powell (2003, p. 8) includes Boca del Sapo in a broader "Neuquén" area. Carrying the Boca del Sapo locality over from those taxa would be an inference.
- *Paso Córdoba (Río Negro).* That is the locality of a referred specimen, MPCN-PV 370, and does not set the type locality.

**Open.** Bonaparte (1991) would give the type locality as described; it has not been read. Other sources read give formation and province only: rauhut2003b, p. 33 ("Río Colorado Formation, Neuquén, Argentina"); weishampel2004a, p. 49, Table 3.1; paul2024a ("Central Argentina; Bajo de la Carpa").

## What stage is the Velocisaurus holotype?

*2026-10-01*

**Conclusion.** Santonian, the age of the Bajo de la Carpa Formation adopted by Garrido (2010). Governs `stage: [Santonian]` on *Velocisaurus unicus*.

**Evidence.**
- garrido2010a, p. 152: the Bajo de la Carpa is "entre los 86 y 83 Ma, situándola dentro del Santoniano … una edad santoniana también es atribuida por Bonaparte (1991)" ("between 86 and 83 Ma, placing it within the Santonian … a Santonian age is also attributed by Bonaparte (1991)", translated).
- leanza2004a, p. 70: "the abelisauroid Velocisaurus unicus" listed under the Bajo de la Carpa Formation.

**Ruled out.**
- *Coniacian.* Carrano and Sampson (2008) give "Bajo de la Carpa Formation … Coniacian, Late Cretaceous (Bonaparte 1991b)" (carrano2008a, p. 198). Garrido (2010, p. 152) reads Bonaparte (1991) as Santonian. A Coniacian Bajo de la Carpa traces to Cruz and colleagues' (1989) range for the whole Río Colorado Subgroup (garrido2010a, pp. 151–152), not to a date for this formation.

**Open.** Bonaparte (1991), the describing paper, would show which stage it gives; it has not been read.
