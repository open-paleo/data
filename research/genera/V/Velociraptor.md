# *Velociraptor*

## Is the *Velociraptor mongoliensis* holotype Maastrichtian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype AMNH 6515 comes from the Djadokhta beds at Shabarakh Usu (Bayn Dzak), and the Djadokhta Formation and its Bayn Dzak Member are Campanian (see [Djadokhta](../../stratigraphy/d/djadokhta.md)). Governs `period.stage: [Campanian]` on [*Velociraptor mongoliensis*](../../../genera/V/Velociraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- osborn1924b, p. 1: "TYPE.-Amer. Mus. 6515, skull and jaws, one front claw and adjoining phalanges. Djadochta beds at Shabarakh Usu, August 11, 1923".
- dashzeveg2005a, p. 18: the Djadokhta falls in the "late part of the Campanian incorporating the end of Chron 33 and Chron 32 between about 75 and 71 Ma".

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian.* Jones (2026) gives "Barun Goyot Fm., Mongolia Djadokhta Fm., Mongolia *Late Cretaceous, Maastrichtian*" (jones2026c, Eudromaeosauria table). The cell covers the genus's referred material from two formations, and no source read for the Djadokhta places it in the Maastrichtian; the Cenomanian to early Maastrichtian span of older work is a list of other authors' views (dashzeveg2005a, p. 17).

**Open.** Nothing.
