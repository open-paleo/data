# *Veterupristisaurus*

## Is the *Veterupristisaurus* locality St (EH) the *Kentrosaurus* quarry St?

*2026-09-14*

**Conclusion.** Yes. St is the stegosaur locality of the German Tendaguru expeditions, which Hans Reck later called EH, and the *Kentrosaurus* lectotype comes from excavation St at Kindope. The two records name one site in different words. Governs `location.locality` on [*Veterupristisaurus milneri*](../../../genera/V/Veterupristisaurus.yml) and on [*Kentrosaurus aethiopicus*](../../../genera/K/Kentrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2011a, p. 223: "Type locality and horizon. Locality St (EH) of the German Tendaguru Expeditions. Middle Dinosaur Member of the Tendaguru Formation".
- rauhut2011a, p. 198: "the stegosaur locality was designated St"; "Hans Reck renamed several localities when he took over the lead of the expeditions from Janensch in 1912", which "is relevant for the locality St (EH of Reck)"; St is one of two localities "situated several kilometres north of Tendaguru (Janensch 1925*b*)".
- mallison2011a, p. 197, Abstract: the *Kentrosaurus* lectotype "is a partial individual from excavation 'St' at Kindope, Tendaguru, Tanzania".

**Ruled out.**
- *EH as a second site.* Rauhut gives EH as Reck's later name for St, so material labelled with either comes from one locality.

**Open.** Two things. First, Rauhut cites Maier (2003), Janensch (1925b) and Heinrich (1999) for the expedition locality names, and none of them has been read, so the St–EH equivalence may be relayed; Rauhut's own type-locality line uses it. Second, which wording the two records should share, which is a house decision rather than a question of fact.
