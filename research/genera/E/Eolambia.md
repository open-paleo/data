# *Eolambia*

## Is the *Eolambia* holotype from the lower or the upper Mussentuchit Member?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** Unsettled, so the record names the member without a part. The describing paper and the *Animantarx* paper from the same site put the Carol Site at the base of the member, while Mori (2009) puts it near the top; the disagreement is over where the member's base lies at this site, not over the bone bed. Governs the absent `location.part` on [*Eolambia caroljonesa*](../../../genera/E/Eolambia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kirkland1998a, Holotype: "from Carol Site, CEUM Loc. # 42em366v, basal Mussentuchit Member of the Cedar Mountain Formation east of Castle Dale, Utah".
- carpenter1999a: *Animantarx* "was collected from just above the base of the Mussentuchit Member near the San Rafael Swell".
- mori2009a, Carol site: "The quarry occurs near the top of the Mussentuchit Member, only about 10 meters below the lowest Dakota Sandstone channel complex", which "contradicts the conclusions of Kirkland and Madsen (2007) who placed the site low in the member"; the difference "ties to differences of interpretation of a a maroon colored smectitic layer below the quarry", included in the Mussentuchit by Mori and in the Ruby Ranch by Kirkland and Madsen, and "the differences of opinion are of little concern until the two members are better diagnosed".
- kirkland2007a: the Mussentuchit "is separated from the underlying Ruby Ranch Member by a black chert-pebble lag, except in the thick outcrops of Cedar Mountain Formation along the north end of the San Rafael Swell, where the contact appears gradational".
- mcdonald2012c, Distribution and horizon: "All specimens of Eolambia caroljonesa have been found in Emery County, Utah, in the Mussentuchit Member, Cedar Mountain Formation (lower Cenomanian)", with no position in the member and no reply to Mori, though Kirkland is a coauthor.

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`.* It takes the describing paper's side of a disagreement over the member boundary that Mori's measured section reopened and no later paper read has settled.
- *`part: upper`.* It takes Mori's side on the same unsettled question.

**Open.** Whether the maroon layer below the quarry is smectitic, and so Mussentuchit. A later section through the Carol Site, or a geologic map of the area east of Castle Dale, would settle it. That the Carol Site lies where Kirkland and Madsen call the contact gradational is our inference from its location.
