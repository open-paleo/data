# *Eucamerotus*

## Where on the Isle of Wight coast was the *Eucamerotus* holotype found?

*2026-08-23*

**Conclusion.** On the shore near Brook (Hulke's "Brooke"), as loose float in a block of stone. Its bed was assigned afterward: William Fox matched the block to a bed near the top of the cliff between Brook and Chilton, which is an identification from the rock, not an observed position. Governs `location.locality: Brook` and `location.formation: Wessex` on [*Eucamerotus foxi*](../../../genera/E/Eucamerotus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hulke1870a, p. 318: "THIS vertebra was found on the shore near Brooke, Isle of Wight, almost completely hidden in a large block of stone"; Fox "without hesitation referred it to a bed which occurs near the top of the high cliff between Brooke and Chilton".
- blows1995a, p. 190: "*Holotype.* BMNH R2522, vertebral neural arch"; "*Type horizon and locality.* Wealden Marls (Wessex Formation), Lower Cretaceous (Barremian) of Brook, Isle of Wight, England."

**Ruled out.**
- *Chilton.* Hulke's third note on the genus describes a trunk centrum "which I obtained from the sea-cliff, near Chilton, Isle of Wight, in 1875" (hulke1879a, p. 752). That is a different specimen, found six years after the holotype neural arch.

**Open.** Nothing.
