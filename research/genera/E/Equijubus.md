# *Equijubus*

## Is the *Equijubus* holotype placed in the Xinminpu Group alone, or in its Zhonggou Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Zhonggou Formation, middle gray unit. The describing paper gives the group only. You and colleagues (2014) place the Gongpoquan dinosaur beds, *Equijubus* among them, in the Zhonggou. Their formation sentence rests on their own field work together with a personal communication. Governs `location.formation: Zhonggou` and `location.part: middle` on [*Equijubus normani*](../../../genera/E/Equijubus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- you2003c, p. 348: "Gongpoquan Basin, Mazongshan area, Gansu Province, China. Middle Grey Unit of Xinminbao Group".
- you2014a, Discussion: "Both *Gongpoquansaurus* and *Equijubus* are from the middle gray unit of the Xinminpu (= Xinminbao) Group in the Gongpoquan Basin"; "The Xinminpu Group includes two formations: the lower Xiagou and the upper Zhonggou formations. Our 2010 and 2011 field work confirms that the dinosaur-bearing Xinminpu Group in the Mazongshan area belongs to Zhonggou Formation (Liu, Y.-Q., pers. comm.)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Xinminbao Group, at group rank.* Norman (2004) gives "Xinminbao Group (Gansu)" (norman2004a, p. 415, Table 19.1), and the distribution chapter lists the species under "1. Xinminbao Group" (weishampel2004b, p. 567). Both predate the formation assignment and repeat the describing paper's horizon. Xinminbao and Xinminpu are two spellings of one group.

**Open.** Whether the Gongpoquan gray beds are Zhonggou. The 2014 sentence leans on a personal communication, and the later case for the correlation is a working hypothesis (see [*Auroraceratops*](../A/Auroraceratops.md)).
