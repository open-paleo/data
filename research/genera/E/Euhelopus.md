# *Euhelopus*

## Where was the *Euhelopus* holotype collected, and is it from Yantai County?

*2026-08-23*

**Conclusion.** In Mengyin County, about 1 km west of the village of Ningchiakou. Zdansky's label, as Wiman (1929) gives it, reads "Meng-Yin-Hsien, NW 40 li, Ning Chia Kou, W 2 li": 40 li northwest of Mengyin and 2 li west of Ningchiakou, which Wilson and Upchurch (2009) convert to about 22 km and 1 km. The record's locality names the village, the nearer of the label's two reference points. Governs `location.locality: Ningchiakou` and `location.region: CN-SD` on [*Euhelopus zdanskyi*](../../../genera/E/Euhelopus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wiman1929a, p. 7 (translated; the page as Wilson and Upchurch cite it): "The specimen a is labeled: Zdansky, April 1923, Shantung, Meng-Yin-Hsien, NW 40 li, Ning Chia Kou, W 2 li."
- wilson2009a, p. 201: Zdansky collected exemplar a "40 *li* northwest of Mengyin and 2 *li* west of 'Ning Chia Kou' (now Ningchiakou) in Yantai County of Shandong Province"; "exemplar a was collected approximately 22 km northeast of Mengyin and approximately 1 km west of Ningchiakou".

**Ruled out.**
- *Yantai County.* Wilson and Upchurch place Ningchiakou "in Yantai County of Shandong Province, which juts into the Bohai and Yellow Seas", but the label puts the site in Meng-Yin-Hsien, Mengyin County, and their own conversion puts it about 22 km from Mengyin.
- *Northeast of Mengyin.* Wilson and Upchurch's "approximately 22 km northeast" contradicts the label's "NW 40 li", which their preceding sentence repeats as northwest.
- *Exemplar b's site.* Specimen b is labeled "Meng-Yin-Hsien, NW 39 li, Ning-Chia-Tung-Kou SE 1 li", "~2-3 km from the location of specimen a" (wiman1929a, p. 7, translated). It is the referred skeleton, not the holotype.

**Open.** Nothing.
