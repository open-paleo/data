# *Echinodon*

## Is the *Echinodon* type material from the Upper Building Stones?

*2026-08-22*

**Conclusion.** No. Thulborn (1973) thought the Upper Building Stones "more probable" than the Mammal Bed, but the same footnote settles on the Middle Purbeck and says "greater stratigraphic precision is not possible". Sereno (2012) names the site, the Mammal Pit, and gives two candidate beds, both in the Lulworth Formation. Governs `location.formation: Lulworth` on [*Echinodon becklesii*](../../../genera/E/Echinodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- thulborn1973a, p. 120, footnote: "It is possible that the material was collected from the «Mammal Bed» at the base of the Middle Purbeck … But it seems more probable that E. becklesii was taken from somewhere within the Upper Building Stones,"; continued on p. 121: "which overlie the «Cinder Bed». It would seem most reasonable to follow Appleby et al (1967) in referring E. becklesii to the Middle Purbeck; greater stratigraphic precision is not possible."
- sereno2012a, p. 31: "'Mammal Pit' located high on the coastal cliff section near the Zig Zag Path at Durlston Bay"; the material may be from the Mammal Bed ("Durlston Bay 83") or "the Feather Bed (Durlston Bay 108) slightly higher in the section (Cherty Freshwater Member)".
- clements1993a, p. 182, Table 1: the Cinder Member is the lowest member of the Durlston Formation, directly above the Lulworth Formation's Cherty Freshwater Member; p. 204: "the Cinder Member (equivalent to DB 111 in the above description)".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Upper Building Stones, and with them the Durlston Formation.* Thulborn's preferred beds lie above the Cinder Bed, which Clements (1993) makes the base of the Durlston, so his reading would put the specimen in the Durlston (our inference from the two papers). The footnote gives no evidence for the preference and ends on the Middle Purbeck, a division that straddles the Lulworth–Durlston boundary (see [Purbeck: Echinodon and Owenodon](../../topics/purbeck-echinodon-and-owenodon.md)). Sereno's two candidate beds, 83 and 108, lie below bed 111. Sereno does not discuss Thulborn's reading.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the member, which the Purbeck topic note leaves open.
