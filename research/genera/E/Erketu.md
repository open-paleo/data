# *Erketu*

## On what does the Bayanshiree assignment of Bor Guvé rest?

*2026-08-22*

**Conclusion.** On a provisional referral. Ksepka and Norell (2010) place the Bor Guvé beds in the Baynshiree Formation on their lithology and on freshwater mollusks reported by a colleague as a personal communication; no section or date ties the holotype site to the unit. Governs `location.formation: Bayanshiree` on [*Erketu ellisoni*](../../../genera/E/Erketu.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ksepka2010a, p. 2: the deposits "are referable to the Baynshiree Formation (Khaand, personal commun.)"; "It appears that the Bor Guvé locality represents an extension of the known Baynshiree beds west of Shine Us Khudug"; the beds "are extraordinarily similar in lithology to classic Baynshiree strata", with "two types of freshwater mollusks typical of Baynshiree strata (Khaand, personal commun.)".
- ksepka2010a, p. 3: "Provisionally we consider the sediments at the Bor Guvé locality to belong to the Baynshiree Formation"; "only this rough stratigraphic framework exists for the Bor Guvé locality".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Javkhlant Formation.* The red beds at nearby Shine Us Khudug belong to the Upper Cretaceous Javkhlant, which overlies the Baynshiree in the area; Ksepka and Norell take Bor Guvé as an extension of the Baynshiree beds instead (ksepka2010a, p. 2).

**Open.** A measured section or mapping of Bor Guvé, or the published mollusk evidence, would settle the assignment.
