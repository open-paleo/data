# *Euronychodon*

## Which unit and site is the *Euronychodon* type tooth from?

*2026-08-23*

**Conclusion.** The Taveiro Formation at Taveiro, southwest of Coimbra. Antunes and Sigogneau-Russell (1991) give the horizon as the clays and sands of Taveiro, and name the Cerâmica do Mondego clay pit as the quarry that "furnished the samples" from Taveiro; that the type tooth TV 20 came from that pit is our reading of that sentence, which they write in the etymology of *Taveirosaurus*. Governs `location.formation: Taveiro` and `location.locality: Taveiro` on [*Euronychodon portucalensis*](../../../genera/E/Euronychodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- antunes1991a, p. 116: Taveiro, "village du District de Coimbra, Portugal, où se situe la carrière « Cerâmica do Mondego, Lda » qui a fourni les échantillons" ("where the Cerâmica do Mondego, Lda quarry, which furnished the samples, is located", translated).
- antunes1991a, p. 117: "*Type :* dent TV 20"; "*Formation et âge :* Argiles et sables de Taveiro, Campanien supérieur ? et Maestrichtien" ("Clays and sands of Taveiro, upper Campanian ? and Maastrichtian", translated).
- kullberg2013a, p. 312: the base of "the Taveiro Formation filling (Reis, 1983)", whose mudstones yield "remains of small vertebrates (such as the dinosaurs *Taveirosaurus* and *Euronychodon*...)" (translated).
- antunes2003a, p. 90, Fig. 15: the type specimens of *Euronychodon portucalensis* and *Taveirosaurus costai*, "both from Taveiro, southwest of Coimbra".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Argilas de Aveiro Formation.* Jones (2026) gives "Occurrence: Argilas de Aveiro Fm." (jones2026c, p. 485). Antunes and Mateus (2003) collected at Aveiro and at Taveiro separately, and treat the Taveiro beds as a lateral equivalent of the Argilas de Aveiro, not as that unit: "in the outskirts of Aveiro and, still later, at Taveiro near Coimbra in beds of approximately the same Maastrichtian age: the latest Cretaceous unit 'Argilas de Aveiro' and its lateral equivalents" (antunes2003a, p. 89).

**Open.** A coordinate. Antunes and Pais (1978) print Portuguese military grid coordinates for their measured section in the Cerâmica do Mondego clay pit, on sheet 240 (Montemor-o-Velho) of the 1:25,000 map. Converting that grid reference, and confirming that the 1991 teeth came from that section, would give the position.
