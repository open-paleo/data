# *Embasaurus*

## Which Kazakh region is Mount Koi-Kara in?

*2026-08-22*

**Conclusion.** Atyrau (`KZ-23`), on a derivation not yet checked against the modern boundary. Riabinin (1931) places Mount Koi-Kara in the Gurievsk District of the Uralsk Government, in the basin of the lower Emba; the code follows from Guryev being the former name of Atyrau (our identification), not from a position for the hill. Governs `location.region: KZ-23` on [*Embasaurus minax*](../../../genera/E/Embasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- riabinin1931a, Summary: "Mount Koi-Kara, district of Gurievsk, government of Uralsk, in the basin of the lower course of Emba River."
- nessov1995a, p. 84 (translated): "Koi-Kara, elevation in the lower basin of the Emba River, Kazakhstan."
- weishampel2004b, p. 563: the "Neocomian Sands (Riabinin 1931b) Theropoda indet. (= Embasaurus" occurrence is listed under "39.2. AQTOBE OBLYSY, KAZAKHSTAN".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether Mount Koi-Kara lies in Atyrau or in Aqtobe (`KZ-15`) today. Weishampel and colleagues give no source for their heading, and the boundary of a 1920s district need not match the modern region's. A modern position for Mount Koi-Kara would settle it.
