# *Eomamenchisaurus*

## Is the *Eomamenchisaurus* holotype CPM 165?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Lü and colleagues (2008) designate CXMVZA 165, kept in the Chuxiong Museum. Sabaj (2020) gives CPM as the Chuxiong Prefectural Museum's current code and lists CXMVZA as another form of it, so the specimen is the same and the code changes. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CPM 165]` and `type_specimen.institution: CPM` on [*Eomamenchisaurus yuanmouensis*](../../../genera/E/Eomamenchisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2008a, p. 17: "Holotype: CXMVZA 165 consisting of eight dorsal vertebrae, a partial sacrum, a right ilium"; "The specimen is stored in the Chuxiong Museum."
- sabaj2020a, p. 616: "CPM: Chuxiong Prefectural Museum, Chuxiong. … Current as: CPM (fossils). Also as: CXMVZA (fossils)."

**Ruled out.**
- *CZMVZA 165.* Jones (2026) gives "CZMVZA 165—partial skeleton (Lü et al., 2008)" (jones2026b, Mamenchisauridae table), which transposes two letters of the paper's code.
- *IVPP V9067.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give "IVPP V9067" (molina-pérez2020a, Mamenchisaurids similar to *Omeisaurus* table) and cite Lü and colleagues (2008), who print no such number.

**Open.** Nothing.
