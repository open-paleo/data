# *Epichirostenotes*

## Is the *Epichirostenotes curriei* holotype Campanian or Maastrichtian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Late Campanian. The erecting paper places the holotype in the lower Horseshoe Canyon Formation at about 72 Ma. Governs `stage: [Campanian]` on [*Epichirostenotes curriei*](../../../genera/E/Epichirostenotes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sullivan2011a, p. 428, Appendix: "Age: late Campanian (early "Edmontonian"), approximately 72 Ma."
- sullivan2011a, p. 426: ROM 43250 is "from the lower part of the Horseshoe Canyon Formation (approximately 72 Ma)", and "Epichirostenotes curriei is known only from the lower Horseshoe Canyon Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian.* Jones (2026) gives "Horseshoe Canyon Fm., Canada, Late Cretaceous, Maastrichtian" (jones2026c, Oviraptorosauria table). The formation spans the upper Campanian to lower Maastrichtian (sues1997a, Distribution: "Horseshoe Canyon Formation (upper Campanian-lower Maastrichtian)"), and the erecting paper puts this specimen in its lower, Campanian part.

**Open.** The paper's "approximately 72 Ma" sits at the Campanian–Maastrichtian boundary (72.1 Ma on the current chart). The stage the paper names is kept, as CONTRIBUTING directs. A measured position for the John Neills Ranch site would settle which side of the boundary it lies on.
