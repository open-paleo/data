# *Eshanosaurus*

## Is the *Eshanosaurus* holotype from the Dull Purplish Beds, the Shawan Member of the Lufeng?

*2026-08-23*

**Conclusion.** No member is recorded. Xu and colleagues (2001) place the jaw in marlite beds at the base of the Lower Lufeng Formation in Eshan County, and put the Dull Purplish Beds, which yielded prosauropods at the same site, about 200 m higher. The [Shawan Member](../../../stratigraphy/s/shawan.yml) carries "Dull Purplish Beds" among its variants, so a member taken from that name would give the jaw the prosauropods' horizon. Governs `location.formation: Lufeng` and `location.part: lower`, with no `location.member`, on [*Eshanosaurus deguchiianus*](../../../genera/E/Eshanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2001b, p. 477: "The marlite beds containing the therizinosaur material are at the base of the Lower Lufeng Formation. Numerous prosauropod specimens (probably *Lufengosaurus*) were collected from the Dull Purplish Beds of the Lower Lufeng Formation at this site, about 200 m above the therizinosaur-bearing bed."
- xu2001b, p. 477, Horizon and Locality: "Lower part of Lower Lufeng Formation, Eshan County, Yunnan".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Dull Purplish Beds, or the Shawan Member.* A horizon for the holotype in the Dull Purplish Beds is the prosauropods' bed; Xu and colleagues put the therizinosaur-bearing bed about 200 m below it.

**Open.** Where the marlite beds fall in the Lufeng as its members are now defined, and the age. Barrett (2009) notes that the basin's basal Jurassic is called the Lower Lufeng or the Fengjiahe Formation "by different authors", that the basin also holds Middle Jurassic and Early Cretaceous deposits, and that "additional collecting and stratigraphical work is needed at the type locality in order to confirm or refute its Early Jurassic age" (barrett2009a, p. 686). A measured section at the Eshan site tied to the Lufeng members would settle both.
