# *Elaltitan*

## Is the *Elaltitan* holotype from the Bajo Barreal or the Lago Colhué Huapi Formation?

*2026-08-22*

**Conclusion.** The Lago Colhué Huapi Formation. Casal and colleagues (2015) erected the unit for the beds above the Bajo Barreal's Upper Member, took the south margin of the Senguerr river bend as its parastratotype, and list PVL 4628 from that margin among the unit's fossils. They do not say where in the 160 m parastratotype section the specimen lies. Governs `location.formation: Lago Colhué Huapi` on [*Elaltitan lilloi*](../../../genera/E/Elaltitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- casal2015a, p. 90, Stratotype (translated): "The parastratotype is the interval exposed on the south margin of the Codo of the Senguerr River (160 m thick)."
- casal2015a, p. 86, Paleontology and biostratigraphy (translated): "From the southern margin of the Codo del Río Senguerr, materials PVL-4628 were recovered … Mannion and Otero (2012) reassigned them to a new genus named Elaltitan lilloi."
- mannion2012a, p. 623: "South (right) bank of the Río Senguerr, in the area between the bend of this river and the Pampa de María Santísima … lower member of the Bajo Barreal Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *The lower member of the Bajo Barreal Formation.* The describing paper's horizon (mannion2012a, p. 623), cited to works on the Bajo Barreal in the region rather than to a section at the site. Casal and colleagues' unit, erected three years later, takes in the beds above the Upper Member on this margin. In their introduction they attribute an Upper Member assignment of the Senguerr dinosaurs to "Sciutto 1981, Mannion and Otero 2012" (casal2015a, p. 78); Mannion and Otero give the lower member.
- *The Laguna Palacios Formation.* Bonaparte and Gasparini's (1979) attribution, as Mannion and Otero report it: "the Laguna Palacios Formation does not crop out in this region" (mannion2012a, p. 623).

**Open.** The stage. Casal and colleagues date the formation as a whole, "between Coniacian–Maastrichtian" (casal2015a, p. 90). Their sentence "The materials come from the first appearances of red mudstones" (p. 86) follows the Ocho Hermanos vertebrae UNPSJB-Pv 1011, and on p. 89 they tie "the first appearances of the red mudstones" and an age "at most to the Coniacian" to those vertebrae alone. That the sentence does not place PVL 4628 at the base of the unit is our reading. A level for PVL 4628 in the parastratotype section would settle the stage.
