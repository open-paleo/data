# *Eoraptor*

## What is the *Eoraptor lunensis* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** PVSJ 512, the articulated skeleton in the Museo de Ciencias Naturales, Universidad Nacional de San Juan, as Sereno and colleagues designate it in 1993 and list it again in the 2013 osteology. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [PVSJ 512]` on [*Eoraptor lunensis*](../../../genera/E/Eoraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno1993a, p. 64: "Holotype. PVSJ 512 (Museo de Ciencias Naturales, Universidad Nacional de San Juan, San Juan, Argentina), an articulated skeleton lacking only the distal caudal vertebrae".
- sereno2013a, p. 84, Table 1: "PVSJ 512 (holotype)"; Table 2: "Blocks composing the holotypic skeleton of *Eoraptor lunensis* (PVSJ 512)".

**Ruled out.**
- *LACM 33213.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give "*Eoraptor lunensis* LACM 33213" against Sereno and colleagues (1993) in a chronology table (molina-pérez2020a, Chronology of early sauropodomorphs considered the smallest historically). Neither Sereno paper prints the number.
- *PVSJ 559.* The same book lists PVSJ 559 as the largest specimen (molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropodomorphs), which is not a type claim; Sereno and colleagues list it as a referred specimen found "in the wall of the trench around the holotypic skeleton" (sereno2013a, p. 84).

**Open.** Nothing.
