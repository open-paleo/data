# *Elrhazosaurus*

## Where at Gadoufaoua is the *Elrhazosaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-22*

**Conclusion.** From GAD 5, the upper part of the Elrhaz Formation, in the region of the niveau des Innocents, as Galton and Taquet (1982) give it when naming *Valdosaurus nigeriensis* on MNHN GDF 332. Galton (2009), who made the species the type of *Elrhazosaurus*, gives the same "upper Elrhaz Formation" in a figure caption. Neither paper prints a coordinate. Governs `location.part: upper` and `location.locality: Gadoufaoua` on [*Elrhazosaurus nigeriensis*](../../../genera/E/Elrhazosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1982a, p. 153: "Left femur GDF 332, Muséum National d'Histoire Naturelle, Paris, collected by P. Taquet in 1973"; "Gadoufaoua, the region of niveau des Innocents (see Taquet, 1976, fig. 7), Republic of Niger"; "GAD 5, upper part of El Rhaz formation, Tégama Series, Aptian (Lower Cretaceous)".
- galton2009a, p. 226, Fig. 5 caption: "from late Aptian upper Elrhaz Formation of Niger, West Africa : M-O: MNHN GDF 332, holotype left femur".
- sereno1999a, p. 1346, note 12: "GAD 5 corresponds with the upper part of the Elrhaz Formation and the lower part of the Echkar Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *The lower Echkar Formation.* GAD 5 spans the Elrhaz–Echkar contact (sereno1999a, p. 1346), but Galton and Taquet place GDF 332 in its Elrhaz part.
- *The niveau des Innocents as another specimen's site only.* The name is printed for the *Cristatusaurus* type, "GAD 5, niveau des Innocents" (taquet1998a, p. 350), but Galton and Taquet print it for GDF 332 as well.

**Open.** Whether "niveau des Innocents" names a site or a level. Galton and Taquet write "the region of niveau des Innocents", while Taquet and Russell (1998) pair it with the level GAD 5, and *niveau* means level. Taquet (1976, fig. 7), which Galton and Taquet cite, has not been read and would settle whether it belongs in `locality`.
