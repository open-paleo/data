# *Epachthosaurus*

## Which part of which formation is the *Epachthosaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-22*

**Conclusion.** The upper portion of the lower member of the Bajo Barreal Formation, the horizon Martínez and colleagues (2004) give for the species at Estancia Ocho Hermanos. Governs `location.formation: Bajo Barreal` and `location.part: lower` on [*Epachthosaurus sciuttoi*](../../../genera/E/Epachthosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martínez2004a, p. 108, in the species entry headed by the holotype MACN-CH 1317: "Horizon and Age Upper portion of the Lower Member of the Bajo Barreal Formation (Upper Cretaceous: late Cenomanian-early Turonian".
- casal2015a, p. 88 (translated): in the Bajo Barreal, "the paleontological record is fundamentally restricted to the upper part of the Lower Member", with these levels "well exposed in sierra San Bernardo".

**Ruled out.**
- *The upper member of the Bajo Barreal.* Powell (1990) wrote "The material here described comes from the upper member of the Bajo Barreal Formation, Chubut Group (Sciuttoi, 1981)" (powell1990a, Stratigraphic provenance and age (translated)). Martínez and colleagues, restudying the type series with a new skeleton from the same ranch, give the lower member.
- *The Lago Colhué Huapi Formation.* The unit crops out at Ocho Hermanos: Casal and colleagues report rebbachisaurid caudals, UNPSJB-Pv 1011, from its first red mudstones there (casal2015a, p. 89). The unit overlies the Bajo Barreal's Upper Member, and the species' horizon is in the lower member, below both.

**Open.** Nothing.
