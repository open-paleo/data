# *Eustreptospondylus*

## Is the *Eustreptospondylus* holotype from Summertown or Wolvercote?

*2026-08-23*

**Conclusion.** Summertown. Sadleir and colleagues (2008) name the brick quarry as Webb's Pit, Summertown, 1.9 km west-northwest of the Oxford University Museum, and explain the "Wolvercote" of James Parker's note as the quarry on the road to Wolvercote. Walker (1964) writes "Summertown brick pit, Wolvercot, near Oxford", which names the same pit. Governs `location.locality: Webb's Pit, Summertown` on [*Eustreptospondylus oxoniensis*](../../../genera/E/Eustreptospondylus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- walker1964a, p. 124: the holotype, "Number J. 13558, from the Athleta Zone, Middle Oxford Clay (Upper Callovian) of Summertown brick pit, Wolvercot, near Oxford".
- sadleir2008a, p. 2, Discovery of the Holotype Specimen: it "was discovered in the brick quarry of Webb's Pit, Summertown, 1·9 km WNW of the Oxford University Museum of Natural History".
- sadleir2008a, p. 4: Parker's note "I did not get the much finer Wolvercote specimen till 1870"; "Parker's use of 'Wolvercote' refers to 'the quarry that is on the road to Wolvercote' and corresponds with the presumed location of Webb's Pit."

**Ruled out.**
- *Wolvercote as the site.* Parker's and Walker's "Wolvercote" is a direction from the pit, as Sadleir and colleagues read Parker's note, not a separate locality.

**Open.** Nothing.
