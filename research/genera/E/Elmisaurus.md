# *Elmisaurus*

## Which specimen is the *Elmisaurus rarus* holotype?

*2026-08-22*

**Conclusion.** ZPAL MgD-I/172, the left tarsometatarsus Osmólska (1981) designated. Funston and colleagues (2018) head their entry "Holotype ZPAL MgD-I/98", a skeleton Osmólska listed as referred; they do not discuss the designation. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ZPAL MgD-I/172]` on [*Elmisaurus rarus*](../../../genera/E/Elmisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- osmólska1981a, p. 83: "Holotype ZPAL MgD-I/172 - complete left tarsometatarsus (lacking Mt I); referred: ZPAL MgD-I/98 … right pes … right manus"; "All from the northern part of Nemegt locality, Nemegt Formation."
- funston2018a, p. 107: "Holotype ZPAL MgD-I/98; partial skeleton including rib fragments, two sacral vertebrae, proximal right scapula, right manus"; "the precise location of the holotype material within the Western Sayr is not known."

**Ruled out.**
- *ZPAL MgD-I/98 as the holotype.* It is Osmólska's referred skeleton, and the number is taken from the designating paper (see [methods](../../methods.md)).

**Open.** Which specimen Funston and colleagues' Western Sayr sentence describes. Under their own heading, "the holotype material" is ZPAL MgD-I/98; Osmólska places all three of her specimens only in "the northern part of Nemegt locality". Whether ZPAL MgD-I/172 itself came from the Western Sayr needs its collection record.
