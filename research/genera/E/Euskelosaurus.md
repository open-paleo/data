# *Euskelosaurus*

## Is BMNH R1625 the holotype or a lectotype of Euskelosaurus browni, and who designated it?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** A lectotype, attributed to Cooper (1980). Governs `type_specimen.specimen_type: lectotype` and `specimen_id: BMNH R1625` on *Euskelosaurus browni*.

**Evidence.**
- galton1985c, p. 14: material "included as part of the paralectotype of Euskelosaurus brownii HUXLEY 1866 by COOPER (1980: 13, fig. 8)"; p. 16: "the lectotype femur of Euskelosaurus browni".
- galton1985a, p. 673, Fig. 1 caption: "Euskelosaurus brownii HUXLEY, 1866 … lectotype BMNH R1625", without naming who designated it.
- yates2003c, p. 63: "Euskelosaurus browni is a nomen dubium based on material that cannot be identified further than Sauropodomorpha".

**Ruled out.**
- *Holotype.* Van Heerden (1979) lists "First specimen (Holotype) BM(NH) R 1625: the shaft of a femur" (vanheerden1979a, p. 25) and never uses "lectotype"; Cooper's later designation of a lectotype, with paralectotypes, implies the original material was treated as several specimens (Huxley "sent four separate collection 'of the same animal'", galton1985c, p. 15, relaying Seeley 1894). That Huxley's material was a syntype series is our inference.

**Open.** Cooper (1980) has not been read, so the designation's page is known only through Galton's citation.
