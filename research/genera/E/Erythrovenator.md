# *Erythrovenator*

## Does *Erythrovenator* take a formation?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** No. The describing paper gives the type horizon only as the Candelária Sequence of the Santa Maria Supersequence, a sequence-stratigraphic unit, and Pavanatto and colleagues (2018) find no index fossils at the Niemeyer Site to tie it to a biozone. Governs the empty `location.formation` on [*Erythrovenator jacuiensis*](../../../genera/E/Erythrovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- müller2021a, Type horizon: "Santa Maria Supersequence, Candelária Sequence, Paraná Basin."
- pavanatto2018a: "There is so far no index fossils collected from this site, which prevents a robust biostratigraphical assessment."

**Ruled out.**
- *The Candelária as a formation.* It is a sequence, not a lithostratigraphic unit.
- *"Santa Maria or Caturrita Fm."* Jones (2026) gives it in a table (jones2026c) without a source; it restates the same uncertainty as an either-or of formations.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is *Erythrovenator jacuiensis* Carnian, Norian, or both?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Carnian to early Norian, the range the describing paper gives without choosing between its ends. Governs `stage: [Carnian, Norian]` on [*Erythrovenator jacuiensis*](../../../genera/E/Erythrovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- müller2021a, Systematic paleontology: "Type horizon. Santa Maria Supersequence, Candelária Sequence, Paraná Basin. The age of these strata ranges from Carnian (233.23 ± 0.73 Ma) to early Norian (225.42 ± 0.37 Ma; Langer et al. 2018)."
- müller2021a, Discussion: "it is uncertain if the fauna is part of the *Riograndia* AZ or if it comprises an unrecognized AZ. Consequently, the age of *Erythrovenator jacuiensis* is estimated between c. 233 Ma to c. 225 Ma (Carnian to early Norian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Carnian alone.* The record once held only the older end of the paper's range.
- *Early Norian alone.* Jones (2026) gives "Late Triassic, Norian (early)" in a specimen table (jones2026c, p. 493). Müller leans younger, writing that the fauna "seems younger than the *Hyperodapedon* AZ" (müller2021a, Discussion), but then declines to assign it to the *Riograndia* zone, so the lean is not a dating.

**Open.** Whether the Niemeyer Site fauna belongs to the *Riograndia* zone. An index fossil from the site would settle it.
