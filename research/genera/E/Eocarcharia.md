# *Eocarcharia*

## What position do Sereno and Brusatte (2008) print for the *Eocarcharia* type locality?

*2026-08-22*

**Conclusion.** None that can be used. The type-locality line prints "N 16°88’ and E 9°88’", and a minute value cannot exceed 59. Which digits are wrong cannot be told from the paper, so the value is not repaired and the record carries no coordinate. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Eocarcharia dinops*](../../../genera/E/Eocarcharia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno2008a, p. 26: "type locality has coordinates N 16°88’ and E 9°88’; referred specimens come from a 10 km stretch of richly fossiliferous outcrop".
- sereno2008a, p. 16, Fig. 1 caption: "the sites (white dots) where the holotypic specimens of Kryptops palaios gen. et sp. nov. (site G88) and Eocarcharia dinops gen. et sp. nov. (site G138) were discovered."
- sereno2008a, p. 17, for *Kryptops*: "coordinates N 16°26’, E 9°7’".

**Ruled out.**
- *N 16°26′, E 9°7′.* Printed in the same paper for the *Kryptops palaios* type locality, site G88, which Fig. 1 separates from G138. It is the position on [*Kryptops palaios*](../../../genera/K/Kryptops.yml).
- *A repaired reading of the 88s.* Treating "88" as a mistyped minute means choosing a digit, and the paper gives no second value to check it against (see the rule on degraded copies in [methods](../../methods.md)).

**Open.** Where site G138 lies. Fig. 1A shows it only as a dot on a regional map; a source printing a position for G138 would settle it.
