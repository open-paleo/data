# *Erectopus*

## On what did Huene found *Erectopus sauvagei*?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** On the bones Sauvage (1882) described as *Megalosaurus superbus*, not on referred material. Huene (1932) held the bones too small for the teeth, left the name *superbus* with the teeth and named the bones *E. sauvagei*. Allain (2005) found the teeth and maxilla match, synonymized *E. sauvagei* with *E. superbus* and designated the maxilla MNHN 2001-4 as the lectotype. *Megalosaurus superbus* is the species' original combination, typed `reassigned` like the dataset's other original combinations. Governs both synonyms on *Erectopus superbus* in [*Erectopus*](../../../genera/E/Erectopus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- huene1932a, p. 238: "Sauvage's species name attaches to the teeth and my generic name to the skeletal bones. The skeletal bones must therefore be newly named at species level here … namely *Erectopus sauvagei* n. sp." (translated).
- allain2005b, p. 75: "*Erectopus sauvagei* is thus considered a junior synonym of *Erectopus superbus*, the maxilla of which is here designated as the lectotype of the species"; "Lectotype. MNHN 2001-4, anterior part of the left maxilla."
- huene1923a, p. 453: "described by Sauvage as Megalosaurus superbus, which I call Erectopus (n. gen.) superbus".

**Ruled out.**
- *E. sauvagei on referred material.* The record said so; Huene's species takes Sauvage's own 1882 bones.

**Open.** Allain (2005) calls *E. sauvagei* a junior synonym without saying objective or subjective; the record keeps `junior subjective`.

## Does *Erectopus* take a unit?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** No. Allain (2005) gives the horizon as the phosphate-bearing beds of La Penthiève, an informal local name he never treats as a unit, so the record names none and the location note quotes his line. Governs the empty `location.formation` on [*Erectopus superbus*](../../../genera/E/Erectopus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- allain2005b: "Horizon and Age. Phosphate-bearing beds of La Penthiève, Mammilatum Zone, Lower Cretaceous, lower Albian."

**Ruled out.**
- *La Penthiève as a unit.* No source erects it.
- *"La Penthièvre Beds Fm."* Jones (2026) gives it (jones2026c), with a misspelling and a rank no source gives.

**Open.** Nothing.
