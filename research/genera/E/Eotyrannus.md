# *Eotyrannus*

## Is the Eotyrannus "Black Band" the Black Band of the English Chalk?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No. The *Eotyrannus* bed is the "Black Band", bed L11 of the [Wessex Formation](../../../stratigraphy/w/wessex.yml) on the Isle of Wight, Barremian; the Black Band of eastern England is a Cenomanian–Turonian member at the base of the Welton Chalk. Governs `location.bed: L11` and the variants on that bed.

**Evidence.**
- lockwood2025a, p. 5: "from the 'Black Band' (bed L11 in Stewart 1978), a plant debris bed cropping out c. 100 m east of Grange Chine".
- hopson2005a, p. 38: "The Black Band Member is formally proposed herein … equivalent to beds C to H of Dodsworth (1996)"; p. 34: "at the Cenomanian-Turonian stage boundary (Hart and Bigg, 1981)".
- dodsworth1996a, p. 45: "The Black Band (beds C to E) has yielded palynological assemblages", in the lowermost Welton Chalk Formation.

**Ruled out.**
- *Literature on the Chalk Black Band as Eotyrannus evidence.* Papers on the Welton Chalk Black Band concern a different unit and age.

**Open.** Nothing.
