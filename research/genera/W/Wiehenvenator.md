# *Wiehenvenator*

## Is WMNM P27698 a number of the *Wiehenvenator* holotype?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** No. The holotype is the set of elements that Rauhut and colleagues (2016) number one by one under WMN P, and P27698 is not one of them. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Wiehenvenator albati*](../../../genera/W/Wiehenvenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2016a, p. 8, Holotype: "Fragmentary skull and skeleton of a single individual; right premaxilla (WMN P27475), right maxilla (WMN P27504) …", through "a manual phalanx (WMN P27482), both fibulae (WMN P27479, 27502), right astragalus (WMN P27458), and a partial right calcaneum (WMN P27484)".
- rauhut2016a, p. 8, Holotype: "Two further partial caudal vertebrae (WMN P29131, 47417) were found at different times in the float at the type locality and probably belong to the same individual."

**Ruled out.**
- *WMNM P27698.* The record held this number until 2026-09-09. The Wikipedia article on the genus describes "A megalosauroid pedal phalanx ((WMNM P27698, P27693)" found "in two parts during October 1999 and April 2000" that "may have belonged to the holotype". The holotype paragraph lists no pedal phalanx, its only phalanx is the manual phalanx P27482, and the digits 27698 do not appear anywhere in the paper. Even on the article's own account the phalanx would be a possible referral, not type material.

**Open.** Where the phalanx and its two numbers were published, and whether it belongs to the holotype individual. No paper read for this note mentions it; the museum's catalogue entry would settle both questions.
