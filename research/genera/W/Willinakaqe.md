# *Willinakaqe*

## Does the *Willinakaqe* holotype keep `part: lower` when its bone-bed is uncertain?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Juárez Valieri and colleagues (2010) place the whole Salitral Moreno locality in the lower section of the Allen Formation, so `part: lower` does not depend on which bone-bed at the site produced the holotype premaxilla. Which bone-bed that was is unknown, and no `bed` is recorded. Governs `location.part: lower` and the absence of `location.bed` on [*Willinakaqe salitralensis*](../../../genera/W/Willinakaqe.yml).

**Evidence.**
- juárezvalieri2010a, p. 220: "Both localities, Salitral Moreno and Islas Malvinas, correspond to the lower section of the Allen Formation (Malargüe Group)".
- juárezvalieri2010a, p. 220: the holotype, paratypes and most referred specimens "were recovered in a single bone-bed together with sauropod and ankylosaurian remains".
- cruzado-caballero2016a, p. 232: "there is no certainty about the stratigraphical provenance of the remains", and fieldworkers "reported that they were collected from at least two different bone-beds at Salitral Moreno", citing García and Salgado (2013).

**Ruled out.**
- *Removing `part: lower` because the provenance is uncertain.* The uncertainty Cruzado-Caballero and Coria (2016) report is about the bed within Salitral Moreno. The lower-section statement is made for the locality as a whole, so it holds for either bed.

**Open.** Which bone-bed the holotype came from. The describing paper says one bed and Cruzado-Caballero and Coria report at least two, relaying García and Salgado (2013), which has not been read. That paper would show whether the beds differ in level.
