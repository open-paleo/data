# *Wannanosaurus*

## Is *Wannanosaurus* Campanian, or does it reach the Maastrichtian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Campanian. No source dates this horizon to the Maastrichtian: the only Maastrichtian in Butler and Zhao's (2009) age is queried and comes from Sullivan (2006), whose statement concerns the flat-headed pachycephalosaurs as a group. Governs `stage: [Campanian]` on [*Wannanosaurus yansiensis*](../../../genera/W/Wannanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- butler2009b, p. 69, Occurrence: "Red sandstones of the upper member of the Xiaoyan Formation (Late Cretaceous: Campanian, Chen and Chang, 1994; ?early Maastrichtian, Sullivan, 2006)".
- sullivan2006a, p. 350: "many of the so-called flat-headed types (*Wannanosaurus*, *Goyocephale*, *Tylocephale*) are also from late Campanian-late Maastrichtian strata".
- chinzorig2025a, Discussion: "this taxon stems from the terminal Cretaceous (Campanian)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Campanian–Maastrichtian.* Jones (2026) prints "Xiaoyan Fm., China *Late Cretaceous, Campanian/ Maastrichtian*" (jones2026a, Pachycephalosauria table). The Maastrichtian half rests on the queried tail of Butler and Zhao's range, which traces to Sullivan's group-level range rather than to a dating of the Xiaoyan.
- *Early Late Cretaceous.* Hou (1977) concludes "based upon comparisons to the English and Mongolian specimens, the age of the Xiaoyan Fm. should be early Late Cretaceous" (hou1977a, English translation, Comparison and discussion). No later source read follows it.

**Open.** Chen and Chang (1994), the source of the Campanian, has not been read.
