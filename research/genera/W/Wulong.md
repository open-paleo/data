# *Wulong*

## Does the *Wulong* holotype number carry an institution code?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** Yes: the describing paper prints the specimen as DNHM D2933, with DNHM the Dalian Natural History Museum; its Holotype line gives the bare number with the museum spelled out. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [DNHM D2933]` on [*Wulong bohaiensis*](../../../genera/W/Wulong.yml). The institution is settled separately (see [XHPM and the Dalian museum codes](../../topics/xhpm-dalian.md)).

**Evidence.**
- poust2020a, Systematic Paleontology, Holotype: "D2933 (Dalian Natural History Museum, Dalian, China), a complete, articulated specimen."
- poust2020a, Fig. 1: "*Wulong bohaiensis*, DNHM D2933"; Abbreviations: "DNHM = Dalian Natural History Museum, Dalian, China".

**Ruled out.**
- *D2933 with no code.* Jones (2026) prints "D2933—skull and skeleton" (jones2026c, Dromaeosauridae table), copying the Holotype line. The same number is not a different specimen; the code comes from the paper's own figure caption and abbreviations.

**Open.** Nothing.
