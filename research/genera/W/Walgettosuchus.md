# *Walgettosuchus*

## Which stage does the *Walgettosuchus woodwardi* holotype take?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Its unit's Albian, Early Cretaceous, replacing the Cenomanian. No work read dates the holotype's level; the Cenomanian came from Bell and colleagues' (2019) maximum depositional age for a bed above the Lightning Ridge fossil beds, which adds no stage (see [Wallangulla Sandstone](../../stratigraphy/w/wallangulla-sandstone.md)). Governs `period` and `stage: [Albian]` on [*Walgettosuchus woodwardi*](../../../genera/W/Walgettosuchus.yml).

**Evidence.** None beyond the unit entry.

**Ruled out.** Nothing beyond the unit entry.
