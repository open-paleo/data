# *Wintonotitan*

## Is *Wintonotitan* a diamantinasaur?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Not on most analyses; it sits at Somphospondyli incertae sedis. Only the matrix of Beeston and colleagues (2024) recovers it in Diamantinasauria; the others place it outside both Diamantinasauria and Titanosauria. Governs `parent: Somphospondyli`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Wintonotitan wattsi*](../../../genera/W/Wintonotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- poropat2015a: a non-titanosaurian somphospondylan, its anatomy "strongly suggests against a close relationship" with *Diamantinasaurus*.
- poropat2023a, Figs 29 and 30, pp. 51–52: outside Diamantinasauria under both weightings.
- beeston2024a, Fig. 21, p. 73: within Diamantinasauria under both weightings.

**Ruled out.**
- *Diamantinasauria as the parent.* Analyses on other matrices place it outside the clade.

**Open.** Whether the result of Beeston and colleagues recurs on other matrices.
