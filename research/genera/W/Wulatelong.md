# *Wulatelong*

## Where does the *Wulatelong* coordinate come from?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** From the holotype's locality line in Xu and colleagues (2013), which prints the position in decimal degrees. Governs `location.coordinates: [41.7439, 106.7444]` on [*Wulatelong gobiensis*](../../../genera/W/Wulatelong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2013a, p. 87, Locality and horizon: "Coordinates in decimal degrees: 41.7439°N, 106.7444°E. Bayan Mandahu area, "The Gate" Locality".
- lambert2001a, p. 7: "*Locus typicus:* Quarry 95BM1/1 (N 41° 44' 485'' - E 106° 44' 703'' - Altitude 1307 m) Bayan Mandahu", the type quarry of *Protoceratops hellenikorhinus*.

**Ruled out.**
- *41.741943, 106.745003.* The record held this pair until 2026-09-09. It lies about 220 m from the position Xu and colleagues print for the holotype, and about 60 m from the *Protoceratops hellenikorhinus* quarry of Lambert and colleagues (2001). Their figures carry second marks, but "485''" cannot be seconds, so they are read as decimal minutes (44.485′ and 44.703′), which convert to 41.741417, 106.745050. Reading the marks this way is an inference. The pair is a neighboring taxon's site, not this specimen's.

**Open.** Nothing.
