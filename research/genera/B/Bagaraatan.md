# *Bagaraatan*

## Does the *Bagaraatan* holotype take a numbered site?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** No. Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz (1972) map two Northern Sayr sites with coeluroid postcranial fragments, 13 and 26, and no source ties ZPAL MgD-I/108 to either. Osmólska's "lower portion of the Nemegt Formation" fits site 26, which their section plots near the base, better than site 13, which it plots high; that is our reading of the figure, not a source's assignment. Governs `location.locality: Northern Sayr, Nemegt` and the location note on [*Bagaraatan ostromi*](../../../genera/B/Bagaraatan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- osmólska1996a, p. 2: the holotype was found "in the sand layer of the lower portion of the Nemegt Formation, in the eastern part of the Northern Sayr".
- gradziński1972a, Fig. 1 caption: "13 — fragment of the postcranial skeleton of the coeluroid dinosaur"; "26 — fragments of the postcranial skeleton of coeluroid dinosaur".

**Ruled out.**
- *Site 13.* Nothing ties the holotype to it, and its plotted level does not fit Osmólska's lower portion.
- *Site 26.* It fits, but only by our reading of the section.

**Open.** A source naming the holotype's site number.

## References

- `gradziński1972a`: Gradziński, R.; Jerzykiewicz, T. (1972). Additional geographical and geological data from the Polish-Mongolian Palaeontological Expeditions. *Palaeontologia Polonica* 27: 17-30.
