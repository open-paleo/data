# *Borealopelta*

## Is the *Borealopelta* holotype Aptian or Albian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Albian. Brown and colleagues (2020) give an Early Albian age for the host beds from combined biostratigraphy. The describing paper gives Aptian in its locality line but calls the member Albian in its geological setting. Governs `stage: [Albian]` on [*Borealopelta markmitchelli*](../../../genera/B/Borealopelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown2020b, p. 4, Specimen and geological context: "Combined data from ammonite, palynological and foraminiferal biostratigraphy indicate an Early Albian age".
- brown2017a, p. 2: "the Albian-aged Wabiskaw Member of the Clearwater Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian.* Brown and colleagues (2017) print "Wabiskaw Member, Clearwater Formation, Aptian stage" in the locality line (brown2017a, p. 1), which their own p. 2 contradicts. The record carried the Aptian until 2026-08-05. Jones (2026) gives "Early Cretaceous, Albian" (jones2026a, p. 399), agreeing with the later paper.

**Open.** Nothing.
