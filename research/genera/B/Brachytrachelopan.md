# *Brachytrachelopan*

## Is the *Brachytrachelopan* holotype Kimmeridgian–Tithonian or Oxfordian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian–Tithonian. The describing paper gives the horizon as Tithonian. Windholz and colleagues (2019) give the taxon "Kimmeridgian–early Tithonian", citing the describing paper and Cúneo and colleagues' (2013) dating of the formation. Governs `stage: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` on [*Brachytrachelopan mesai*](../../../genera/B/Brachytrachelopan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2005a, p. 670, Horizon and locality: "The specimen comes from a fluvial sandstone within the Cañadón Cálcareo Formation, Upper Jurassic (Tithonian)."
- windholz2019a, p. 369: "*Brachytrachelopan mesai* (Cañadón Calcáreo Formation, Kimmeridgian–early Tithonian, Upper Jurassic; Rauhut et al. 2005; Cúneo et al. 2013)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Oxfordian.* Jones (2026) gives "Late Jurassic, Oxfordian/?" (jones2026b, p. 377). The formation as a whole reaches into the Oxfordian (see [Cañadón Calcáreo](../../../stratigraphy/c/cañadón-calcáreo.yml)), but no statement read about this specimen places it there.

**Open.** The Kimmeridgian rests on a dating of the formation rather than of this sandstone. A dated level at the site would settle whether it applies.
