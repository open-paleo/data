# *Buriolestes*

## Which printed coordinate locates the *Buriolestes* holotype?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** The pair Cabreira and colleagues (2016) print for the Buriol ravine, where the holotype was collected. Governs `location.coordinates: [-29.65855, -53.435825]` and `location.locality: Buriol ravine` on [*Buriolestes schultzi*](../../../genera/B/Buriolestes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cabreira2016a, in-press version, p. 1: "The specimens were collected side by side at the Buriol ravine (29°39′30.78″S; 53°26′08.97″W), São João do Polêsine-RS, Brazil; Alemoa Member, Santa Maria Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *29°39′34.2″S, 53°25′47.4″W.* Müller and colleagues (2018) print it for the excavation of the referred specimen CAPPA/UFSM 0035 from "the Buriol outcrop", which they identify as the type locality and the holotype's layer (müller2018b, advance-access version, p. 4). It lies about 590 m east of the 2016 point (our calculation), a second point on the outcrop rather than a correction of the first.

**Open.** Nothing.
