# *Bonatitan*

## Does the published Bajo de Santa Rosa coordinate locate the *Bonatitan* holotype?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** No. The only coordinate printed for Bajo de Santa Rosa among the sources read is the one Novas and colleagues (2009) give for the *Austroraptor* holotype; the papers on *Bonatitan* name the locality and print no position. Governs the empty `location.coordinates`, with `location.locality: Bajo de Santa Rosa`, on [*Bonatitan reigi*](../../../genera/B/Bonatitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martinelli2004a, p. 274, Locality and horizon: "Both specimens were collected in the Bajo de Santa Rosa locality, Río Negro Province, Argentina (Fig. 1); Allen Formation"; p. 258: "The Bajo de Santa Rosa locality is located approximately 150 km south west from the city of Lamarque".
- salgado2014a, online-first version, p. 1: the material was "collected by J.F. Bonaparte between 1990 and 1994 in the surroundings of Salitral de Santa Rosa".
- novas2009a, p. 1103, for *Austroraptor cabazai*: "Bajo de Santa Rosa (40°03′28″ S, 66°48′03″ W), approximately 90 km southwest of Lamarque town".

**Ruled out.**
- *40°03′28″ S, 66°48′03″ W* (−40.057778, −66.800833). Novas and colleagues print it for the *Austroraptor* site, and nothing read ties the *Bonatitan* quarry to that point. The two papers put Bajo de Santa Rosa 150 km and 90 km from Lamarque, so the name covers more ground than one quarry (our reading).

**Open.** Nothing.
