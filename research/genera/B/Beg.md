# *Beg*

## Is Alguu Ulaan Tsav the *Beg* type locality?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** No. Yu and colleagues (2020) name no site for the holotype. They place the type locality "around 14 km from the town of Tsogt-Ovoo", with no bearing, and the abstract gives the area as Baruunbayan. Alguu Ulaan Tsav appears in the same paragraph only as one of four areas where the Ulaanoosh Formation crops out. Governs `location.locality` (no value) on [*Beg tse*](../../../genera/B/Beg.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yu2020a, p. 2, Locality and horizon: "The Beg type locality is around 14 km from the town of Tsogt-Ovoo"; "The Ulaanoosh Formation is distributed in the areas of Ulaanoosh, Alguu Ulaan Tsav, Baruunbayan, and Zuunbayan".
- yu2020a, p. 2, Material: "collected near the town of Tsogt-Ovoo, Ömnögovi aimag (Province)"; "GPS coordinates are available upon reasonable request".
- yu2020a, p. 1, abstract: "a partial skull from Baruunbayan, Ömnögovi aimag, Mongolia".

**Ruled out.**
- *Alguu Ulaan Tsav (once stored as "Algui Ulan Tsav").* The paper lists it among the formation's outcrop areas, in a sentence about the unit, not the specimen.

**Open.** Nothing.
