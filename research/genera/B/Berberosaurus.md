# *Berberosaurus*

## How is the *Berberosaurus* locality written?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** "Douar of Tazouda, locality B", as [*Tazoudasaurus*](../T/Tazoudasaurus.md) records the same upper bone bed. The describing paper gives the Douar of Tazouda as the type locality and the upper bone bed, Allain and colleagues' fossil locality B, as the horizon, about 100 m from the *Tazoudasaurus* holotype site. The *Tazoudasaurus* note's question whether the douar of Tabia is the same place applies here too. Governs `location.locality` on [*Berberosaurus liassicus*](../../../genera/B/Berberosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- allain2007a: "Type Locality—Douar of Tazouda, near the village of Toundoute in the Province of Ouarzazate, High Atlas of Morocco. Horizon and Age—Upper bone-bed ("Fossil locality B" of Allain et al., 2004) of the Toundoute continental series"; the remains "come from a single area of about 4 m2, and is 100 m apart from the site that yielded the holotypic specimen of *Tazoudasaurus*".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Tazouda" alone.* It drops the douar and the locality the paper names.

**Open.** As in the *Tazoudasaurus* note, whether the douar of Tabia is the Douar of Tazouda.
