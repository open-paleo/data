## Is the *Brachypodosaurus* holotype from Chota Simla Hill?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** Unconfirmed. The record keeps "Chota Simla Hill, near Jabalpur", which no source read for this note gives for the humerus; the catalogues give only Jabalpur. Governs `location.locality: Chota Simla Hill, near Jabalpur` on [*Brachypodosaurus gravis*](../../../genera/B/Brachypodosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maryańska1977a, p. 92: "Jubbulpore (22)"; white1973a, *Brachypodosaurus* entry: "India, Jubbulpure".
- wilson2003b, p. 129: "In 1932–33, Matley made collections from Chota Simla, a small hill 0.5 km from Bara Simla", the source of titanosaur material Swinton described in 1947.

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether the humerus came from Chota Simla. The papers read that name the hill concern the sauropod material from it, not this specimen. Chakravarti (1934) would settle it.
