# *Bannykus*

## Is the *Bannykus wulatensis* holotype from Bayan Mandahu?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** No. The describing paper gives the type locality as Chaoge, in the Lower Cretaceous (Aptian) Bayingobi Formation; Bayan Mandahu is a locality of the Upper Cretaceous Ulansuhai Formation. Governs `location.locality: Chaoge` and `location.formation: Bayin-Gobi` on [*Bannykus wulatensis*](../../../genera/B/Bannykus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2018c, p. 2855, Locality and Horizon: "Chaoge, Wulatehouqi, Inner Mongolia, China; Lower Cretaceous (Aptian) Bayingobi Formation"; the epithet is from "Wulatehouqi (Wulate Rear Banner), the county-level administrative division in which the type locality is situated".
- hao2024a, p. 2, Fig. 1 caption: "Bayan Mandahu … and Suhongtu … localities where the Upper Cretaceous Ulansuhai Formation emerges".

**Ruled out.**
- *Bayan Mandahu.* The record carried "Chaoge, Wulatehouqi, Bayan Mandahu" until 2026-08-13. The describing paper does not mention Bayan Mandahu, and the place is a Late Cretaceous locality in another formation (see [Ulansuhai](../../stratigraphy/u/ulansuhai.md)). Wulatehouqi is the county, which belongs in `region`, not the locality.

**Open.** Nothing.
