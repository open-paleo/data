# *Bambiraptor*

## What is the Bambiraptor holotype's number?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** AMNH FARB 30556, formerly FIP 001. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` and former numbers on *Bambiraptor feinbergi*.

**Evidence.**
- burnham2000a, p. 10: "Holotype.—FIP 001 … Graves Museum"; "Additional material.—FIP 002–036".
- turner2012a, p. 55: "HOLOTYPE: AMNH FARB 30556."; p. 56: "The American Museum of Natural History was later gifted with the material", and the "AMNH 001" numbers do not exist: "no such numbers exist for Bambiraptor".

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 001.* Burnham (2004) gives "Holotype: … AMNH 001" (burnham2004a, pp. 70, 74); there is no such number.
- *AMNH 30554.* Norell and colleagues (2006), Turner and colleagues (2007) and Turner (2021) print 30554 (norell2006a, p. 21; turner2021a, pp. 27, 29), a different number from Turner and colleagues' (2012) 30556.

**Open.** The epithet: Turner and colleagues argue "the correct spelling should be 'feinbergorum'" (turner2012a, p. 56).
