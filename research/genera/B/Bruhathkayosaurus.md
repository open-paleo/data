# *Bruhathkayosaurus*

## On which side of Kallamedu was the *Bruhathkayosaurus* holotype found?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** North East of Kallamedu Village, the locality the describing paper gives in the holotype's systematic entry. The same paper gives two other bearings for its excavations and does not say which one yielded the ilium. Governs `location.locality: North East of Kallamedu Village` on [*Bruhathkayosaurus matleyi*](../../../genera/B/Bruhathkayosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- yadagiri1987a, p. 527: "Holotype : Ilium (GSI type PAL/SR/20)"; "Locality : North East of Kallamedu Village".
- yadagiri1987a, p. 523, abstract: "the systematic excavation east of Kallamedu village has resulted in recovering a number of well preserved skeletal parts representing ilium, femur, ischium, tibia and vertebra".
- yadagiri1987a, p. 524: "A very promising site was located north-west of Kallamedu village. A number of fossil bones, belonging to one individual were exposed on the surface"; the trench there "retrieved well preserved skeletal parts. They include ilium, tibia, vertebrae, femur, pubis and radius".
- galton2017a, Discussion: "The bones started to disintegrate in the field jackets even before reaching the GSIH and no longer exist."

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether the ilium came from the north-west trench. That trench produced an ilium with the other large elements the paper describes (our reading), but the paper's locality line says north-east and its abstract says east. The bones themselves are lost (Galton and Ayyasami, 2017), so only the excavation records could settle it.
