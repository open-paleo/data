# *Baiyinosaurus*

## Which stage does the *Baiyinosaurus* holotype take?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** None; the record keeps the Middle Jurassic. Li and colleagues (2024), who name the species, give its horizon as the upper Straw-yellow Sandstone Member, "considered to be Middle Jurassic, late Bathonian", after three works that give only the Middle Jurassic (see [Straw-yellow Sandstone](../../stratigraphy/s/straw-yellow-sandstone.md)). Governs the absent `stage` on [*Baiyinosaurus baojiensis*](../../../genera/B/Baiyinosaurus.yml), replacing `[Bathonian]`.

**Evidence.**
- li2024a, p. 2: "the Straw-yellow Sandstone Member of the Wangjiashan Formation is considered to be late Bathonian in age26–28".

**Ruled out.**
- *Bathonian.* A misrelay.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `li2024a`: Li, N.; Maidment, S. C. R.; Li, D.; You, H.; Peng, G. (2024). A new stegosaur (Dinosauria: Ornithischia) from the Middle Jurassic of Gansu Province, China. *Scientific Reports* 14(1). doi:10.1038/s41598-024-66280-x
