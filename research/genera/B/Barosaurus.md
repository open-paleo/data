# *Barosaurus*

## Is the *Barosaurus lentus* holotype YPM 429 or AMNH 6341?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** YPM 429. AMNH 6341 is a more complete referred skeleton, on which most descriptions of the genus rest. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [YPM 429]` on [*Barosaurus lentus*](../../../genera/B/Barosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tschopp2015a, p. 13: "*Barosaurus lentus*, YPM 429*. Although this specimen is the genoholotype of *Barosaurus* … most characterization of *Barosaurus* is based on another, more complete, and articulated specimen (AMNH 6341, see below)."

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 6341.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give it for the species in their table of the most extreme tails (molina-pérez2020a). It is the referred skeleton, and Jones (2026) lists it after the holotype as "AMNH 6341—partial skeleton" (jones2026b, p. 377).

**Open.** Nothing.
