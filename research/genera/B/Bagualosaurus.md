# *Bagualosaurus*

## On what does the Alemoa Member rest for *Bagualosaurus agudoensis*?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** On two later papers about the same site. Pretto and colleagues (2018) place the Janner site only in the Candelária Sequence and the *Hyperodapedon* Assemblage Zone, which are a sequence and a biozone, not a member. Langer and colleagues (2019) put the Janner site, for *Pampadromaeus*, in "the uppermost part of the Alemoa Member", and Novas and colleagues (2021) give the site as Alemoa Member and say the same levels "also yielded *Bagualosaurus*". Governs `location.member: Alemoa` and `location.locality: Janner site` on [*Bagualosaurus agudoensis*](../../../genera/B/Bagualosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- pretto2018a, p. 3: the holotype "was found at the Janner outcrop, about 2 km from the town of Agudo"; the site correlates "with the *Hyperodapedon* Assemblage Zone, part of the Candelária Sequence of the Santa Maria Formation"; the type horizon is the "Candelária Sequence, Santa Maria Supersequence".
- langer2019b, p. 5, Locality and horizon: "Janner site (53˚17′34.20″ W, 29˚39′10.89″ S) … uppermost part of the Alemoa Member of the Santa Maria Formation", the same printed position as Pretto and colleagues give.
- novas2021a, accepted manuscript, p. 80: *Pampadromaeus* "comes from Janner site or Várzea do Agudo, Alemoa Member, Santa María Formation"; "these levels also yielded Bagualosaurus".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Candelária Sequence as the member.* It is the describing paper's horizon, but it is a sequence-stratigraphic unit that Langer and colleagues equate with "the 'Santa Maria 2' sequence", not a lithostratigraphic member.
- *Várzea do Agudo as a different site.* Novas and colleagues give it as another name for the Janner site.

**Open.** Whether `part: upper` applies. Langer and colleagues' "uppermost part" is stated for the *Pampadromaeus* holotype, and Pretto and colleagues put the *Bagualosaurus* holotype in "the mid-portion of the strata exposed at the Janner site"; neither places the *Bagualosaurus* level within the member.
