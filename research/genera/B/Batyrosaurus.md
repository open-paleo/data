# *Batyrosaurus*

## Is *Batyrosaurus* a hadrosauroid?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The describing paper and most later analyses recover it as a basal hadrosauroid, inside Hadrosauriformes. Only analyses on Norman's matrix place it as the earliest-diverging styracosternan. Governs `parent: Hadrosauroidea` and the dispute on [*Batyrosaurus rozhdestvenskyi*](../../../genera/B/Batyrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- godefroit2012a, p. 334, Abstract: "Batyrosaurus is a basal hadrosauroid more derived than Bolong, Equijubus, Jinzhousaurus, and Altirhinus, but more primitive than Probactrosaurus, Eolambia, and Protohadros".
- verdú2017a, p. 31, Fig. 20: *Batyrosaurus* the sister of *Proa*; p. 32: the authors place it among hadrosauroids against Norman's grouping of it with the "iguanodontoids".
- poole2022a, p. 20: Hadrosauroidea "includes Probactrosaurus, Batyrosaurus, Altirhinus, Jeyawati, Eolambia, Protohadros".
- lockwood2021a, Fig. 23A, p. 879: *Batyrosaurus* + *Penelopognathus*, Bremer support 1, within Hadrosauriformes.
- norman2015a, p. 84: "Three additional taxa basal to this subclade are Batyrosaurus, Hypselospinus, and Ouranosaurus", with *Batyrosaurus* the most basal styracosternan in Fig. 48.
- gasulla2015a, Fig. 16, p. 24: on Norman's matrix, *Batyrosaurus* is the first branch of Styracosterna.

**Ruled out.**
- *Styracosterna, the clade that also covers Norman's arrangement.* That arrangement comes from one matrix, in Norman (2015) and in the run of it by Gasulla and colleagues (2015). Several independent matrices recover a hadrosauroid.
- *The surangular foramen as evidence against a hadrosauroid placement.* Godefroit and colleagues (2012, p. 345) note the same foramen in basal forms including *Probactrosaurus* and *Protohadros*, and Magyar and colleagues (2026, p. 26) report it as usual in basal hadrosauriforms.

**Open.** Its sister taxon: *Penelopognathus* on the Xu matrix, *Proa* on the McDonald matrix, unresolved elsewhere.
