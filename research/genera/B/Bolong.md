# *Bolong*

## Which horizon and site is the *Bolong* holotype from?

*2026-10-02*

**Conclusion.** The Dakangpu Bed of the middle Yixian Formation, recorded under its equivalent the Dawangzhangzi (see [Dawangzhangzi](../../stratigraphy/d/dawangzhangzi.md)), at the village Wu and Godefroit (2012) write Bataigou, in Toutai Township, Yixian County. Governs `location.member: Dawangzhangzi` and `location.locality: Bataigou` on [*Bolong yixianensis*](../../../genera/B/Bolong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- wu2012a, p. 296, Locality and horizon: "Bataigou, Toutai, Yixian County, western Liaoning province, P.R. China. GPS coordinates: N41°36'6.79", E121°7'43.1". Dakangpu Member (equivalent to the Dawangzhangzi Beds) of the middle part of Yixian Formation".
- wu2010a, p. 128: the naming paper's locality line reads 辽宁省义县头台乡白台沟村 and 义县组大康堡层 ("Baitaigou Village, Toutai Township, Yi County, Liaoning Province"; "Dakangpu Bed of the Yixian Formation", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *The lower Yixian Formation.* Zheng and colleagues (2013) assign the juvenile they refer to the species, ZMNH M8812 from Xitaizhi Village, Ningcheng County, Nei Mongol, to "the lower part of Yixian Formation", and say that "detailed information of the fossil locality remains unclear" (zheng2013b, online-first version, p. 1). That is a different specimen from another province.
- *The Jiufotang Formation.* Ji and colleagues (2003) place the *Shenzhouraptor* holotype, from "Baitaigou village of Toutai Township in Yixian County", in the Jiufotang Formation (ji2003b, p. 22). That is a different specimen; Wu and Godefroit place YHZ-001 in the Yixian directly.

**Open.** The spelling of the site. The Chinese text of the naming paper writes 白台沟, which is Baitaigou in pinyin, and Ji and colleagues write Baitaigou; Wu and Godefroit write Bataigou in English. Which form the record carries is undecided.
