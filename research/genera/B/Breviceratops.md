# *Breviceratops*

## Does the *Breviceratops* holotype take site 5 at Khulsan?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** No. Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz (1972) number two Khulsan sites for small protoceratopsid skulls, 4 and 5, and no source says which yielded the holotype; the record names Khulsan alone. Governs `location.locality: Khulsan` on [*Breviceratops kozlowskii*](../../../genera/B/Breviceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gradziński1972a, p. 24: "*4* and *5* — two skulls of small individuals of *Protoceratops* sp.".

**Ruled out.**
- *Site 5.* It picks one of two candidates without a basis.

**Open.** A source naming the holotype's site.

## References

- `gradziński1972a`: Gradziński, R.; Jerzykiewicz, T. (1972). Additional geographical and geological data from the Polish-Mongolian Palaeontological Expeditions. *Palaeontologia Polonica* 27: 17-30.
