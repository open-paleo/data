# *Baminornis*

## What stage is the *Baminornis* holotype?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** Kimmeridgian. The describing paper places the holotype in layer 2 of the Zhenghe excavation site, the *Fujianvenator* layer, which the two tuffites bracketing it date to the Kimmeridgian on the current chart (base Tithonian 149.2 Ma, ICS chart v2024/12). Governs `stage: [Kimmeridgian]` and `location.part: lower` on [*Baminornis zhenghensis*](../../../genera/B/Baminornis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- chen2025a: "the holotypes of *Baminornis* and *Fujianvenator* from 'layer 2'; see fig. 4 in ref. 15", ref. 15 being xu2023a.
- xu2023a: "concordant zircon U-Pb ages of 150.2 ± 1.1 Ma (PM702-1) and 149.9 ± 1.1 Ma (PM701-4) for the lower and upper layers of the IVPP V31985-bearing horizons"; "The section that we excavated is 3.6 m in depth and belongs to the lower part of the Nanyuan Formation."

**Ruled out.**
- *Tithonian.* The describing paper gives "Upper Jurassic, Nanyuan Formation (Tithonian stage; 149.9–150.2 Ma)" (chen2025a). The ages it cites are Kimmeridgian at their central values; the label belongs to an older chart (see [Morrison Tithonian labels](../../topics/morrison-tithonian-labels.md) and [*Fujianvenator*](../F/Fujianvenator.md)).
- *Kimmeridgian to Tithonian, as for Zhengheornis.* That range comes from layer six, above the 149.9 Ma tuffite (see [*Zhengheornis*](../Z/Zhengheornis.md)); layer 2 lies below it.

**Open.** Nothing.
