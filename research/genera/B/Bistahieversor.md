# *Bistahieversor*

## Is the *Bistahieversor* holotype NMMNH P-27469 or OMNH 10131?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** NMMNH P-27469. OMNH 10131 is a referred partial skull and skeleton that had earlier been referred to *Aublysodon*. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NMMNH P-27469]` on [*Bistahieversor sealeyi*](../../../genera/B/Bistahieversor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carr2010a, p. 1: "Holotype—NMMNH P-27469, an articulated skull and skeleton (Figs. 1–3) of an adult"; under Referred Specimens, "OMNH 10131, a partial skull and skeleton of an adult, upper Campanian, upper Fruitland or lower Kirtland formations".
- carr2010a, p. 1, synonymy: "*Aublysodon* cf. *A. mirandus*: Lehman and Carpenter, 1990:1026, figs. 1–6; OMNH 10131."

**Ruled out.**
- *OMNH 10131.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give it in their theropod speed table (molina-pérez2019a). Jones (2026) lists NMMNH P-27469 first and OMNH 10131 among the other specimens (jones2026c, p. 501).

**Open.** Nothing.
