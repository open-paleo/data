# *Beishanlong*

## Is FRDC-GS GJ (06) 01–18 one specimen or eighteen element numbers?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** One accession number for one associated skeleton; "01–18" is not a range of element numbers. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: FRDC-GS GJ (06) 01-18` (one id) on *Beishanlong grandis*.

**Evidence.**
- makovicky2009a, p. 191: "Holotype FRDC-GS GJ (06) 01–18, associated post-cranial skeleton"; the abstract and figures repeat the full number as one specimen, "demonstrating that FRDC-GS GJ (06) 01–18 was still growing".
- makovicky2009a, p. 192, Fig. 1: the holotype's bones are labelled with 17 letter callouts, not numbered 01 to 18.

**Ruled out.**
- *Eighteen element numbers.* The skeleton has more bones than 18 (our count, about 36), and the paper never cites a sub-number.

**Open.** The electronic supplementary Table 1 has not been read. The related *Qiaowanlong* holotype is "FRDC …: GJ 07-14" (you2009b, p. 4078), a different year and format.
