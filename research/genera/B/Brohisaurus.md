# *Brohisaurus*

## Does the printed Sun Chaku coordinate locate the *Brohisaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** No coordinate is recorded. Malkani (2003) prints one for the Sun Chaku locality, but its longitude reads 67° 09′ 66″, and sixty-six seconds is not a value; the paper prints those digits. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Brohisaurus kirthari*](../../../genera/B/Brohisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- malkani2003a, p. 74: "Sun Chaku locality (coordinates; 27° 50' 40'' N; 67° 09' 66'' E) of Karkh area, and Lakha Pir Charo locality (coordinates; 27° 37' 35'' N; 67° 02' 10'' E), of Zidi area"; "Sun Chaku locality is 15 kilometers from Karkh village and 85 kilometres east of Khuzdar".
- malkani2003a, p. 77: "The type specimens are part of collection of fragmentary pieces, made in Sun Chaku locality"; "Referred specimens: MSM-95-K, One broken piece of neural arch laminae was found from the clay rich horizon of Lakha Pir Charo area."

**Ruled out.**
- *27.8444, 67.1683.* This reads 66″ as 1′ 06″. Reading the seconds as 06″, or the minutes as 06′, gives other points, and choosing among them is a repair, not a reading.
- *27.84, 67.16.* The pair once stored is the printed value cut to two decimals, with the same fault in the longitude.
- *27° 37′ 35″ N, 67° 02′ 10″ E.* Printed in the same sentence for Lakha Pir Charo, the site of the referred specimen MSM-95-K, not of the holotype.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `malkani2003a`: Malkani, M. S. (2003). First Jurassic dinosaur fossils found from Kirthar Range, Khuzdar District, Balochistan, Pakistan. *Geological Bulletin of the University of Peshawar* 36: 73-83.
