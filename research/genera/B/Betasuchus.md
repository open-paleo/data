# *Betasuchus*

## Is the *Betasuchus* holotype NHMUK R 42997 or 32997?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** 42997, the number Seeley (1883) gives when he describes the femur as *Megalosaurus bredai*. Huene (1932), who erects *Betasuchus* for it, prints 32997 while citing Seeley, and Carrano and Sampson (2008) repeat Huene's figure. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: NHMUK PV R 42997` on [*Betasuchus bredai*](../../../genera/B/Betasuchus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- seeley1883a, p. 246: "No. 42997. This right femur is of Megalosaurian type."
- madzia2019a, p. 4: "Megalosaurus bredai, now Betasuchus bredai (NHMUK R 42997), a fragmentary right femur", in the same collection as "the syntypes of Orthomerus dolloi (NHMUK R 42954–42957)".

**Ruled out.**
- *32997.* Huene writes that Seeley described "a femur (No. 32997 in the British Museum Nat. Hist. London)" (translated; huene1932a, p. 70), so the number is his citation of Seeley, and it disagrees with Seeley's own. Carrano and Sampson give the same "BMNH 32997" in their Table 2 (carrano2008a, p. 204) and data matrix (p. 235).

**Open.** Nothing.
