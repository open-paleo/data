# *Boreonykus*

## Is the *Boreonykus* holotype TMP 1989.055.0047 or RTMP 89.55.47?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** One specimen, written TMP 1989.055.0047 as the describing paper writes it. RTMP 89.55.47 is the same number under the museum's superseded prefix, without the padding. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [TMP 1989.055.0047]` on [*Boreonykus certekorum*](../../../genera/B/Boreonykus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bell2015a, p. e1034359-2: "Holotype—TMP 1989.055.0047, right frontal."

**Ruled out.**
- *RTMP 89.55.47.* Jones (2026) gives this form (jones2026c, p. 509). The registry keeps RTMP as an alias of TMP, and the data writes Tyrrell numbers padded (see [methods](../../methods.md)).

**Open.** Nothing.
