# *Baryonyx*

## Where does the *Baryonyx walkeri* coordinate come from?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** From the National Grid reference that Charig and Milner (1986) print for the Smokejacks Brickworks claypit, "TQ13: 113 373". "TQ13" names the 10 km square, and "113 373" is the six-figure reference inside it, which resolves to 100 m. Converted from OSGB36 to WGS84, the southwest corner of that 100 m square is 51.1242, −0.4110. No source read prints a latitude and longitude. Governs `location.coordinates: [51.1242, -0.411]` and `location.locality: Smokejacks Brickworks` on [*Baryonyx walkeri*](../../../genera/B/Baryonyx.yml).

**Evidence.**
- charig1986a, p. 359, Locality: "Claypit at Smokejacks Brickworks (Ockley Brick Company Limited), Wallis Wood, Ockley, near Dorking, Surrey, England. Map reference TQ13: 113 373."

**Ruled out.**
- *"TQ13" as the whole reference.* Read alone it is a 10 km square, too coarse to record. The six figures after the colon fall inside that square (easting 11.3 km, northing 37.3 km within TQ), so they are the site's reference, not a second locality.
- *51.133331, −0.35.* The pair the record held until 2026-08-13 lies about 4.4 km northeast of the converted reference, and no source read prints it.

**Open.** Nothing.
