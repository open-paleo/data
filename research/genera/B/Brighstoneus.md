# *Brighstoneus*

## Is *Brighstoneus* a hadrosauriform?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it sits at Styracosterna incertae sedis. Its describers named it a hadrosauriform but recovered it outside Hadrosauriformes in their majority-rule tree; the later analyses place it just outside, or at the base of, the clade. Governs `parent: Styracosterna`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Brighstoneus simmondsi*](../../../genera/B/Brighstoneus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lockwood2021a, p. 880: the majority-rule analysis "recovered the same topology as originally described in Xu et al. (2018) but with Brighstoneus as the sister taxon of Ouranosaurus, outside of Hadrosauriformes"; without characters 124 and 125 "Brighstoneus fell outside of Iguanodontidae as the most basally branching taxon in Hadrosauroidea".
- lockwood2024a, p. 65: "recovered outside Hadrosauriformes and Iguanodontidae, as the earliest diverging hadrosauroid".
- rotatori2026a, Results: "a 'high-sailed' styracosternans clade sister to Hadrosauriformes, which includes Brighstoneus simmondsi, Ouranosaurus nigeriensis and Morelladon beltrani".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hadrosauriformes as the parent.* The majority-rule tree of its describers and the analysis of Rotatori and colleagues (2026) place it outside the clade.

**Open.** The papers apply the names Hadrosauriformes and Hadrosauroidea inconsistently, so whether "earliest diverging hadrosauroid" lies inside Hadrosauriformes depends on the definition used.
