# *Balaur*

## Which county is the *Balaur bondoc* holotype from?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** Alba (RO-AB). The holotype comes from Sebeș Glod in the Sebeș Formation, near Sebeș in Alba County; Hunedoara County, in the Hațeg Basin, is where the referred specimens were found, at Tuștea in a different formation. Governs `location.region: RO-AB`, `location.locality: Sebeș Glod` and `location.formation: Sebeș` on [*Balaur bondoc*](../../../genera/B/Balaur.yml).

**Evidence.**
- csiki2010b, p. 15357, Horizon and Locality: "The type specimen was collected from red floodplain mudstones of the lower-middle part of the Maastrichtian Sebeş Formation (13), exposed at the Sebeş Glod locality near Sebeş, Alba County, Romania"; "The referred specimens are from … the Tuştea dinosaur nesting site (14), in the middle part of the Maastrichtian Densuş-Ciula Formation, Haţeg Basin, Hunedoara County, Romania".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hunedoara (RO-HD).* The record carried it until 2026-08-13, with a coordinate pair that lay in the Hațeg Basin rather than near Sebeș (our reading of the deleted pair). Csiki and colleagues give Hunedoara only for the referred specimens' site.

**Open.** Nothing.
