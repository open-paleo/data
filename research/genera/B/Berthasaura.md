# *Berthasaura*

## Why does *Berthasaura* carry the Rio Paraná Formation and the *Vespersaurus* coordinate?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** Both come from Langer and colleagues (2019), describing *Vespersaurus* from the same quarry; the reasoning for the formation is in the [*Vespersaurus*](../V/Vespersaurus.md) note. De Souza and colleagues (2021) place *Berthasaura* in the Cemitério dos Pterossauros Quarry and name *Vespersaurus* as the first dinosaur described from it, so Langer and colleagues' road-cut and the quarry are one site (our inference). Governs `location.formation: Rio Paraná` and `location.coordinates: [-26.759444, -53.064722]` on [*Berthasaura leopoldinae*](../../../genera/B/Berthasaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- desouza2021a, p. 2, Horizon and locality: "Cemitério dos Pterossauros Quarry, Goio Erê Formation, Early Cretaceous (Aptian-Albian), Caiuá Group, Bauru Basin"; "layers that crop out in a rural road at Cruzeiro do Oeste Municipality".
- desouza2021a, p. 11, Discussion: "the first dinosaur formally described was *Vespersaurus paranaensis*".
- langer2019a, p. 5, Type-locality and horizon: "Rio Paraná Formation", "exposed at the overbanks of a secondary road (53° 03′ 53″W, 26° 45′ 34″S) about 2 km north of Cruzeiro do Oeste".

**Ruled out.**
- *Goio Erê Formation.* It is the describing paper's own reading, published two years after the reassignment. De Souza and colleagues cite a basin-wide stratigraphic framework for it and do not discuss Langer and colleagues' re-examination of the site.
- *−26.776112, −53.06472.* The pair the record held until 2026-08-13 matches Langer and colleagues' longitude to the arcsecond but sits one arcminute further south (26°46′34″), about 1.9 km off, and no source read prints it.

**Open.** No source read prints a position for the *Berthasaura* specimen itself. Manzig and colleagues (2014), who first described the quarry's bone bed, may show whether the quarry and Langer and colleagues' road-cut point coincide; that paper has not been read.
