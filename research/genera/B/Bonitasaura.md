# *Bonitasaura*

## Is the *Bonitasaura* holotype MPCA 300 or MPCA 460?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** MPCA 300, the number Apesteguía (2004) gives for the holotype, written in the form the data uses for this museum. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MPCA-PV 300]` on [*Bonitasaura salgadoi*](../../../genera/B/Bonitasaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- apesteguía2004a, p. 494, Holotype: "MPCA 300 (Museo Provincial “Carlos Ameghino”, Cipolletti, Río Negro, Argentina), consists of a partially articulated, subadult skeleton"; Fig. 2 caption: "*Bonitasaura*, MPCA 300, holotype".

**Ruled out.**
- *MPCA 460.* The record carried "MPCA-460" until 2026-08-01. Jones (2026) gives "MPCA 460—partial skull and skeleton; MPCA 467-partial skeleton; 468—tibia" (jones2026b, p. 383), citing a 2015 paper for the numbers. Gallina and Otero (2015) also figure *Bonitasaura* bones as "MPCA 460" and "MPCA-460" (gallina2015a, figure captions), but neither calls it the holotype or states a renumbering.
- *HOS 9.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give it in their list of sauropods (molina-pérez2020a). The describing paper does not use it.

**Open.** Whether MPCA 460 is a later number for the holotype or another specimen. The 2015 paper Jones cites, which describes the postcranium, has not been read and would settle it.
