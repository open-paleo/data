# *Bellusaurus*

## Does Dong's "Wucaiwan Fm." put the *Bellusaurus* quarry in the lower Shishugou?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** No. The quarry is at the base of the upper beds of the Shishugou Formation. Dong (1990) gives its horizon as the "Middle to Late Jurassic Wucaiwan Fm.", the regional name of his time. Moore and colleagues (2018) use "Wucaiwan Formation" only as the former name of the beds at Jiangjunmiao that hold *Klamelisaurus*, now the lower beds, and place the *Bellusaurus* quarry by correlating the Dinosaur Valley section with Wucaiwan. Governs `location.formation: Shishugou` and `location.part: upper` on [*Bellusaurus sui*](../../../genera/B/Bellusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1990a, Locality and age (translated): "Dinosaur Valley, Kelameili, Junggar Basin. Middle to Late Jurassic Wucaiwan Fm."
- moore2018a, p. 5: "The Bellusaurus quarry is situated at the base of the upper beds of the Shishugou Formation"; p. 6: correlation "places the Bellusaurus quarry at the level of the lowest horizons of the upper beds of the Shishugou Formation at Wucaiwan (D. Eberth, 2014, personal communication)".
- moore2018a, p. 7: *Klamelisaurus* lies "at the top of what was formerly called the 'Wucaiwan Formation' … and is now considered to constitute the lower beds of the Shishugou Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lower beds of the Shishugou, by translating Dong's unit.* If Dong's "Wucaiwan Fm." is read as the unit later made the lower beds, the quarry would sit in them. Moore and colleagues place it in the upper beds, above the T-1 tuff of the middle beds.

**Open.** The placement rests on Eberth's unpublished correlation. Moore and colleagues (2023) give "the ?upper portion of the Shishugou Formation at Konglonggou", citing Xu and colleagues (2022), which has not been read (moore2023a, p. 6).

## Does Dong's coordinate locate the *Bellusaurus* quarry?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** No. The one position Dong (1990) prints is for the area the 1983 expedition worked, at the foot of the Kelameili Mountains, not for quarry 83003. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Bellusaurus sui*](../../../genera/B/Bellusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1990a, Introduction (translated): the 1983 expedition "conducted research at the southern foot of the Kelameili Mountains in the northeastern border of the Junggar Basin at north latitude 45°6' and east longitude 89°5', where a collection of dinosaurs were recovered".

**Ruled out.**
- *45.1, 89.083.* The survey area's position, given to the arcminute and for the whole collecting region; the quarry is one of its localities.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Bellusaurus* type IVPP V8299 or IVPP V17768?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** IVPP V8299. Dong (1990) designates it as the type, with V8300 as a referred composite skeleton. Moore and colleagues (2018) number individual elements from the same quarry in a series IVPP V17768. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IVPP V8299]` on [*Bellusaurus sui*](../../../genera/B/Bellusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1990a, *Bellusaurus sui* sp. nov. (translated): "Type (IVPP V8299) includes fragmentary cranial elements and teeth. Referred specimen (V8300) consists of a composite skeleton".
- moore2018a, p. 3: "two maxillae (IVPP V17768.1 and IVPP V17768.3) and the parabasisphenoid (IVPP 8299.2)".

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP V17768.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give it, as a juvenile, in their speed table for quadrupedal sauropodomorphs (molina-pérez2020a). Jones (2026) lists "IVPP V 8299" first and "IVPP V17768 series—multiple juvenile skeletons (Moore et al., 2018)" among the other material (jones2026b, p. 376).

**Open.** Nothing.
