# *Brachyceratops*

## Where does *Brachyceratops* sit?

*2026-10-22*

**Conclusion.** Centrosaurinae incertae sedis. Sampson and colleagues (1997) and McDonald (2011) both identify the type material as juvenile centrosaurines; Gilmore's characters are shared by all centrosaurine juveniles, which is why the genus cannot be diagnosed within the subfamily. Governs `parent: Centrosaurinae` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Brachyceratops montanensis*](../../../genera/B/Brachyceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sampson1997a, Nasal horncore section: "specimens assigned to *Brachyceratops* are Centrosaurinae".
- sampson1997a, *Brachyceratops* section: "many of his generic and specific characters are common to all centrosaurine juveniles".
- mcdonald2011b, Note on *Brachyceratops*: "the holotype (USNM 7951) and other specimens from the type quarry of Brachyceratops are juvenile centrosaurines."

**Ruled out.**
- *Ceratopsidae incertae sedis.* Jones (2026) calls the remains "juvenile ceratopsids" (jones2026a, p. 384), crediting McDonald (2011), who calls them centrosaurines. No primary read stops at the family.

**Open.** Which centrosaurine the juveniles belong to. Sampson and colleagues write that "*Brachyceratops* may represent a juvenile *Einiosaurus*, *Achelousaurus*, or some other taxon but more fossils are needed to resolve this problem."
