# *Brontosaurus*

## Which quarry is the *Brontosaurus excelsus* holotype from?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** Reed's Quarry 10 at Como Bluff, Wyoming. Governs `location.locality: Reed's Quarry 10, Como Bluff` on [*Brontosaurus excelsus*](../../../genera/B/Brontosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mcintosh1975a, p. 196: "YPM 1980 (type of *B. excelsis* from YPM quarry 10) and YPM 1981 (type of *B. amplus* from YPM quarry 11)".
- tschopp2015a, p. 6, Table 2: "Brontosaurus excelsus Marsh, 1879 | YPM 1980 | Reed's Quarry 10, Albany County, WY, USA".

**Ruled out.**
- *Reed's Quarries 10 and 11.* Tschopp and colleagues' species account gives "Reed's Quarries 10 and 11, Como Bluff, Albany County, Wyoming" (tschopp2015a, p. 256). The account covers the holotype and the referred YPM 1981, the *B. amplus* type that McIntosh and Berman (1975) place in quarry 11.
- *Lakes' Quarry 10.* A different quarry 10, near Morrison, Colorado, which yielded the *Apatosaurus ajax* holotype YPM 1860: "Lakes Quarry 10, Morrison, Gunnison County, CO, USA" (tschopp2015a, p. 6, Table 2).

**Open.** Nothing for this specimen. Tschopp and colleagues' Table 2 gives Reed's Quarry 10 for YPM 1981 as well, against McIntosh and Berman's quarry 11; that bears on the referred specimen.
