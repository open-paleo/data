# *Barilium*

## Is *Barilium* a hadrosauriform?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** Not securely. Most analyses recover *Barilium* close to *Iguanodon* and *Mantellisaurus*, inside Hadrosauriformes. Three analyses on independent matrices place it below Hadrosauriformes, and several on the McDonald matrix leave it unresolved at the base of the clade. Styracosterna is the clade every reading accepts. Governs `parent: Styracosterna` and the dispute on [*Barilium dawsoni*](../../../genera/B/Barilium.yml).

**Evidence.**
- norman2015a, p. 79: "Proa, Jinzhousaurus, Bolong, Barilium, Mantellisaurus, and Iguanodon form a comparatively poorly supported clade".
- lockwood2024a, p. 66: "Comptonatus chasei was recovered as a hadrosauriform nested within Iguanodontidae, with Iguanodon bernissartensis, Barilium dawsoni and Mantellisaurus atherfieldensis".
- lockwood2021a, p. 880: "The Wealden Group taxa are usually included in Hadrosauriformes … although Barilium was found outside of this group by McDonald".
- boyd2015b, p. 43: "A clade containing the taxa Lanzhousaurus, Bolong, and Barilium is recovered positioned just above D. lakotaensis", below Hadrosauriformes.
- párraga2019a, Fig. 3, p. 252: *Barilium* as the sister of the clade of *Hypselospinus* + *Hippodraco* and Hadrosauriformes.
- bertozzo2025a, p. 28: "including Cariocecus in our analysis shifted Barilium to a more basal position and resulted in the dissolution of Iguanodontidae".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hadrosauriformes as the least-inclusive uncontested node.* Boyd and Pagnac (2015), Párraga and Prieto-Márquez (2019) and Bertozzo and colleagues (2025) each place it below that node, on different matrices.
- *Iguanodontidae.* The node is not part of the dataset's tree. The grouping of *Barilium* with *Iguanodon*, *Mantellisaurus* and *Comptonatus* is one analysis's result, and others recover different content or none.

**Open.** Whether the full osteology of Norman (2011) bears on the placement or emends the diagnosis; it has not been read.
