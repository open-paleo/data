# *Bagaceratops*

## Is the *Bagaceratops rozhdestvenskyi* holotype from Hermiin Tsav I or Hermiin Tsav II?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** Hermiin Tsav, with no numeral. The describing paper gives "Khermeen Tsav I" in its type-locality line, while both plates that figure the holotype are captioned "Khermeen Tsav II". The two are separate exposures about 10 km apart, so the numeral cannot be taken from the type-locality line alone. Governs `location.locality: Hermiin Tsav` on [*Bagaceratops rozhdestvenskyi*](../../../genera/B/Bagaceratops.yml); the spelling follows [Mongolian place names](../../topics/mongolian-place-names.md).

**Evidence.**
- maryańska1975a, p. 148: "*Type locality:* Khermeen Tsav I, south/west of Nemegt Basin"; the referred material is "all from the Khermeen Tsav formation of locality Khermeen Tsav".
- maryańska1975a, p. 178, captions to Pls XLII and XLIII, the two plates of the holotype ZPAL MgD-I/126: "Upper Cretaceous, Khermeen Tsav formation, Khermeen Tsav II, Gobi Desert, Mongolia".
- maryańska1975a, p. 134, Table 1: the species is listed from "Khermeen Tsav I and II".
- gradziński1972a, p. 27: outcrops "some 10 km North-East of the Central Sayr of Khermeen Tsav I" were "designated … Khermeen Tsav II", and in 1971 fossils were collected "in both Khermeen Tsav I and Khermeen Tsav II"; p. 29: "The majority of fossils was collected from the orange sandstone in Khermeen Tsav II".
- czepiński2019a, Table 1: ZPAL MgD-I/126 from "Hermiin Tsav", with no numeral.

**Ruled out.**
- *Khermeen Tsav I as settled.* It is the type-locality line's reading, but the same paper captions the holotype's plates with Khermeen Tsav II, and its table lists the species from both. Gradziński and Jerzykiewicz (1972) say both exposures were collected in 1971 and that most fossils came from Khermeen Tsav II; that tells which exposure was richer, not which one yielded this specimen.

**Open.** Which exposure yielded ZPAL MgD-I/126. A field catalogue or collection record of the 1971 expedition would settle it.
