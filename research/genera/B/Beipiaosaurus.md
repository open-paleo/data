# *Beipiaosaurus*

## Which member of the Yixian Formation is the *Beipiaosaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** The Jianshangou Member. Xu and colleagues (1999) give only "the lower part of the Yixian Formation" at the Sihetun locality, where a farmer collected the specimen in 1996. Wang and colleagues (2016), who raise the Jianshangou to member rank, describe the Sihetun exposures as the type section of its Anjiagou Bed and the excavations beside them as the work of "local farmers and then teams" from the IVPP in the late 1990s. They do not name *Beipiaosaurus*, so the member follows from the site and the circumstances of collection (our inference). Governs `location.member: Jianshangou` on [*Beipiaosaurus inexpectus*](../../../genera/B/Beipiaosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu1999b, p. 351, Locality and horizon: "Sihetun locality near Beipiao, Liaoning, China. The lower part of the Yixian Formation".
- xu1999b, p. 352, Fig. 1 caption: "The holotype was collected in 1996 by a farmer, Li Yinxian, from the famous Sihetun locality. It was later (1997) determined to be from the lower part of the Yixian Formation."
- wang2016a, p. 112: "Exposures enclosed in the Sihetun Landscape Fossil Bird National Geopark museum … comprise the type section of the new Anjiagou Bed", and the exposures south of the museum "are parts of a larger excavation"; "These excavations were initially the product of local farmers and then teams from the Institute of Vertebrate Paleontology and Paleoanthropology, Beijing (IVPP) during the late 1990s."
- wang2016a, p. 110, abstract: the Jianshangou Member "is divided into 4 units", among them "the Anjiagou Bed, the main tetrapod-bearing unit".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** The bed. The Anjiagou Bed is the likeliest, as the main tetrapod-bearing bed and the one exposed at the Sihetun excavations, but no source read places this specimen in it, and the member holds three other beds. Xu and colleagues (2003), on further holotype material, reportedly give the "Jianshangou Beds"; that paper has not been read.
