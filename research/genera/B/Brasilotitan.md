# *Brasilotitan*

## Is the *Brasilotitan* holotype from the Presidente Prudente or the Adamantina Formation?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** The Presidente Prudente Formation, on Bandeira and colleagues' (2016) assignment of the taxon to it. Machado and colleagues (2013) used the Adamantina Formation, the unit Fernandes and Coimbra (2000) divided, whose Presidente Prudente Formation has its type sections in the same city. Governs `location.formation: Presidente Prudente` on [*Brasilotitan nemophagus*](../../../genera/B/Brasilotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- machado2013a, p. 303, Locality and horizon: "kilometer 571 (22° 07'04S, 51° 26'33W) of the Raposo Tavares state road (SP-270), near Presidente Prudente city … part of the Adamantina Formation".
- bandeira2016a, p. 17: *Austroposeidon* "was collected in deposits referred to the Presidente Prudente Formation (e.g., [29]). This unit has also revealed the presence of two other titanosaurs: *Brasilotitan nemophagus* [7] and *Gondwanatitan faustoi* [32]".
- fernandes2000a, p. 726: the Presidente Prudente Formation has "boas exposições nas imediações de Presidente Prudente" ("good exposures in the vicinity of Presidente Prudente", translated), and "ocorre nas cotas mais altas dos interflúvios regionais, abaixo das quais aflora a Formação Vale do Rio do Peixe" ("occurs at the highest elevations of the regional interfluves, below which the Vale do Rio do Peixe Formation crops out", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Adamantina Formation as a rival reading.* Santucci and Filippi (2022) and Poropat and colleagues (2023) still give the "Adamantina Formation" (santucci2022a, p. 361) and "Adamantina" (poropat2023a, p. 60, Table 12), citing Machado and colleagues for the locality. They carry the describing paper's name forward and give no placement of their own.

**Open.** Which of Fernandes and Coimbra's units the km 571 site lies in. Bandeira and colleagues give no section or reason for the assignment, and near Presidente Prudente the Vale do Rio do Peixe Formation crops out below the Presidente Prudente. The same sentence places *Gondwanatitan*, whose record stays in the Adamantina (see [*Gondwanatitan*](../G/Gondwanatitan.md)).

## Is the *Brasilotitan* holotype MPM 125R or MN 7371-V?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** MPM 125R, at the Museu de Paleontologia de Marília. MN 7371-V is the number of a cast of the dentary at the Museu Nacional. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MPM 125R]` on [*Brasilotitan nemophagus*](../../../genera/B/Brasilotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- machado2013a, p. 303: "Holotype: Right dentary, two cervical vertebrae … all housed at the Museu de Paleontologia de Marília (MPM 125R). Casts of the dentary are housed at the Museu Nacional/UFRJ (MN 7371-V)."

**Ruled out.**
- *UFRG MN 7371-V.* Jones (2026) gives it as the type (jones2026b, p. 384). It is the number of the cast. Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give "MPM 125R" in their list of sauropods (molina-pérez2020a).

**Open.** Nothing.
