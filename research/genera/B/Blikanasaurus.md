# *Blikanasaurus*

## Where was the *Blikanasaurus* holotype found?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** 1.6 km northeast of Blikana Trading Store, Herschel district, in the lowermost part of the lower Elliot Formation, as Galton and Van Heerden (1985) give it. Governs `location.locality: 1.6 km northeast of Blikana Trading Store, Herschel district` and `location.part: lower` on [*Blikanasaurus cromptoni*](../../../genera/B/Blikanasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1985b, p. 510, Type locality and horizon: "1.6 km northeast of Blikana Trading Store, Herschel district, Transkei, South Africa, at an altitude of 1.370 m, from the lowermost part of the lower Elliot Formation".
- mcphee2017b, p. 452: the probable type locality "was retraced with respect to the locality information given in Galton and Van Heerden (1985: 510)", and the holotype's provenance "is considered here as very low down in the LEF".

**Ruled out.**
- *Blikana Mountain, and a position near 30° 36′ S, 27° 37′ E.* Blikana Mountain is the site Sues and colleagues (2004), relaying Gow and colleagues (1990), give for a juvenile *Massospondylus* skull, BP/1/4376: "middle Elliot Formation, Blikana Mountain, Herschel District, latitude 30° 36'S and longitude 27° 37'E" (sues2004a, pp. 241–242). That is another specimen from a higher part of the formation. The pair once stored for *Blikanasaurus*, 30° 34′ S, 27° 37′ E, shares its longitude and lies 2′ north of it.

**Open.** Two questions. First, the coordinate: McPhee and colleagues (2017) list Blikana, with *Blikanasaurus cromptoni* and SAM-PK 403, at 30°33'29.81"S, 27°37'20.38"E (mcphee2017b, p. 445, Table 1), a site they retraced from the 1985 description and call the "probable type locality" (p. 452). They also find the site at 1550 m, not the 1370 m Galton and Van Heerden print, and place the nearest ground at 1370 m more than 40 km to the west (p. 452). Whether a retraced probable site is a position printed for this holotype is undecided. Second, the "zone A/4" once attached to the locality appears in no source read for this note; Yates (2008), on the biostratigraphy of the lower Elliot Formation, has not been read.
