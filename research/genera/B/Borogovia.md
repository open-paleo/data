# *Borogovia*

## Is the *Borogovia* holotype from Altan Uul IV or Bügiin Tsav?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** Altan Uul IV (Altan Ula IV in the describing paper), in the lower Nemegt Formation. Governs `location.locality: Altan Uul IV` and `location.part: lower` on [*Borogovia gracilicrus*](../../../genera/B/Borogovia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- osmólska1987a, p. 138: "Type locality: Altan Ula IV, Nemegt Basin, Gobi Desert, Mongolia"; p. 136: the specimen was found in 1971 "in the deposit of the Nemegt Formation at Altan Ula IV locality".
- cau2021a, p. 9, Systematic Paleontology: "Type locality. Altan Uul IV, lower Nemegt Formation; Maastrichtian, Upper Cretaceous; Mongolia."

**Ruled out.**
- *Bügiin Tsav.* Makovicky and Norell's Troodontidae chapter in *The Dinosauria* says the species "is based on a partial hindlimb from the Bügiin Tsav locality of Mongolia (Osmólska 1987)" (weishampel2004a, p. 192). The paper it cites gives Altan Ula IV for the type and names Bugeen Tsav as the site of a different troodontid: "B. gracilicrus (Altan Ula IV) and S. junior (Bugeen Tsav)" (osmólska1987a, p. 137).

**Open.** Nothing.
