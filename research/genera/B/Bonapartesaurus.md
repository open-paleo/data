# *Bonapartesaurus*

## What part and stages does *Bonapartesaurus* take?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** The lower Allen Formation, late Campanian to early Maastrichtian. The describing paper's type-horizon line names the formation and its age; its correlation of the fossil level with the Lower Member is hedged but is the paper's own reading, so the part stands with the hedge in the note. Governs `location.part: lower` and `stage: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on [*Bonapartesaurus rionegrensis*](../../../genera/B/Bonapartesaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cruzado-caballero2017a, Type Locality and Horizon: "The Salitral Moreno site in General Roca city, Río Negro, Argentina; Allen Formation, Late Cretaceous, late Campanian–early Maastrichtian."
- cruzado-caballero2017a, Geological setting: the fossiliferous level "might be correlative to the Lower Member of the Allen Formation (Hugo et al., 2001)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian alone.* The record's earlier value; the type-horizon line gives late Campanian to early Maastrichtian.

**Open.** Nothing.
