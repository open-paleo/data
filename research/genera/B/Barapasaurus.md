# *Barapasaurus*

## Which printed position is the *Barapasaurus tagorei* site, and which state is it in?

*2026-08-13*

**Conclusion.** Pochampalli, at 18°44′N, 80°05′E, in Maharashtra. Both primary papers print a second pair in the same passage, for Sironcha, the town the site is measured from, and the redescription names a second excavation in Andhra Pradesh that yielded referred bones only. Governs `location.coordinates: [18.733333, 80.083333]`, `location.locality: Pochampalli` and `location.region: IN-MH` on [*Barapasaurus tagorei*](../../../genera/B/Barapasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- jain1975a, p. 223: the bones "were first found at Pochampalli, about 18 km southeast of Sironcha (longitude 79° 58′ E, latitude 18° 51′ N)"; "*Locality*: village Pochampalli (longitude 80° 05′ E, latitude 18° 44′ N) Chanda district, Maharastra, India".
- bandyopadhyay2010a, p. 539: Material A, which includes the sacrum ISIR 50, "had been collected from the area near the village Pochampalli, about 18 km southeast of Sironcha (18°51′N, 79°58′E), Gadchiroli district, Maharashtra".
- bandyopadhyay2010a, p. 540: "another small excavation was carried out in 1961–62 in north of the village Krishnapur (19°13′20″N, 79°31′18″E), Adilabad district, Andhra Pradesh, India (Material B)", listing limb and foot bones only.
- bandyopadhyay2010a, p. 541: "*Type specimen.* Sacrum (ISIR 50)"; "*Locality and horizon.* Pochampalli (18°44′N, 80°05′E), Gadchiroli district, Maharashtra".

**Ruled out.**
- *18.85, 79.966667.* The record carried this pair until 2026-08-13. It is Sironcha's printed position, which both papers give as a reference point 18 km from the site.
- *19°13′20″N, 79°31′18″E, and Andhra Pradesh.* Krishnapur, the site of Material B, which does not include the holotype.

**Open.** Nothing.
