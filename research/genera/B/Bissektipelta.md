# *Bissektipelta*

## Is the *Bissektipelta archibaldi* holotype ZIN PH 1/16 or ZIN PH 1/6?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** ZIN PH 1/16. Parish and Barrett (2004), who erect *Bissektipelta* for the species, and Kuzmin and colleagues (2020), who redescribe the holotype, both give 1/16. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ZIN PH 1/16]` on [*Bissektipelta archibaldi*](../../../genera/B/Bissektipelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- parish2004a, p. 302: "“A.” archibaldi (ZIN PH 1/16"; p. 303, Fig. 3: "Holotype of Bissektipelta (= “Amtosaurus”) archibaldi (ZIN PH 1/16)".
- kuzmin2020a, p. 86: "we redescribe in detail the holotype of *Bissektipelta archibaldi* (ZIN PH 1/16)"; Table 1 measures "ZIN PH 1/16", "ZIN PH 281/16" and "ZIN PH 2329/16".

**Ruled out.**
- *ZIN PH 1/6.* The record carried it until 2026-08-01, and no source read prints it. Jones (2026) gives "ZIN PH 1/16—partial braincase" (jones2026a, p. 400), agreeing with both papers.

**Open.** Nothing.
