# *Qiaowanlong*

## Does the "Middle Unit of the Xinminpu Group" place the holotype in the middle of a section, or in the Zhonggou Formation?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** Neither. The holotype came from the gray-variegated beds of the Yujingzi Basin, which are the uppermost Xiagou Formation (see [Xiagou](../../stratigraphy/x/xiagou.md)). Governs `location.formation: Xiagou` and `location.part: upper` on [*Qiaowanlong kangxii*](../../../genera/Q/Qiaowanlong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- you2009b, p. 4078: "Yujingzi Basin, Jiuquan area, Gansu Province, People's Republic of China. Middle Unit of the Xinminpu Group in this basin, late Early Cretaceous (Aptian–Albian; Li 2008)".
- you2018a, p. 8: "Qiaowanlong kangxii from the gray-variegated beds in the Yujingzi Basin".
- suarez2018a, p. 18: Morschhauser (2012), after Li (2008), identifies "two faunas, one in the 'lower unit'" with "basal hadrosauroids, titanosaurs, ornithomimid", while "The 'middle unit' consists primarily of *Auroraceratops* and the therizinosaur *Suzhousaurus*"; "the more diverse fauna of the 'lower unit' is assigned to the Xiagou Formation and the fauna of the 'middle unit' is assigned to the Zhonggou Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: middle`.* "Middle Unit" is the name of a unit in Li's (2008) faunal scheme, not a position within the Xiagou. Suarez and colleagues' abstract also speaks of a "middle gray mudstone facies" (suarez2018a, p. 12), the middle of three lithofacies, which is their 'lower unit' fauna; the two uses of "middle" do not refer to the same beds.
- *Zhonggou Formation, through Li's 'middle unit'.* Read as Li's unit, You and Li's "Middle Unit" would be the *Auroraceratops*–*Suzhousaurus* fauna that Suarez and colleagues assign to the Zhonggou. You and colleagues (2018), with the same first author, place this holotype in the gray-variegated beds instead, and Suarez and colleagues do not list *Qiaowanlong* in either fauna. That You and Li's "Middle Unit" is Li's faunal unit is an inference: their citation of Li (2008) is attached to the age.
- *Qiaowan as the locality.* Qiaowan is "a cultural relic near the fossil locality" that the genus is named for (you2009b, p. 4078), not the site.

**Open.** Li (2008), an unpublished thesis, defines the units and has not been read; it would show which beds its "Middle Unit" covers.
