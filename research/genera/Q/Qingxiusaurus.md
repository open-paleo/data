# *Qingxiusaurus*

## What unit holds the Shihuoling pit?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** No formation is recorded. The describing paper gives only "Upper Cretaceous red beds", and the two formation names printed for the same pit come from papers on its hadrosaur, *Nanningosaurus*, one of them relayed from unread sources. Governs the empty `location.formation` and `location.locality: Shihuoling, Dashi Village, Nalong Township` on [*Qingxiusaurus youjiangensis*](../../../genera/Q/Qingxiusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mo2008a, p. 147: the 1991 excavation was at "南宁市西北 48 km 的那龙乡大石村石火岭地点", recovering "部分鸭嘴龙类和蜥脚类恐龙材料" ("the Shihuoling locality, Dashi Village, Nalong Township, 48 km northwest of Nanning City", "partial hadrosaurian and sauropod dinosaur material", translated).
- mo2008a, p. 148: "产地与层位 广西壮族自治区南宁市郊那龙乡大石村石火岭；上白垩统红层" ("Locality and horizon: Shihuoling, Dashi Village, Nalong Township, suburb of Nanning City, Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region; Upper Cretaceous red beds", translated), and the age follows from the hadrosaur "从同一发掘坑" ("from the same excavation pit", translated).
- mo2007a, p. 554: the deposit "in the Nalong Basin was once considered to be Paleogene (Yongning Group) or Eocene (Nadu Formation)", and "the portion of the Nadu Formation from which it came is probably the same age as" the *Tsintaosaurus* locality.
- dai2024a, p. 16: "*Nanningosaurus dashiensis* from the late Campanian lower half of the Luowen Formation in Guangxi", citing a 1997 Guangxi geological bureau work, Zhang and Lin (2011) and a personal communication.

**Ruled out.**
- *A placeholder "Unnamed (Nanning Basin)".* The primary papers name the basin after Nalong Township: "the Late Cretaceous red beds of the Nalong Basin" (mo2007a, p. 550). Nanning is the city 48 km to the southeast.
- *"Dashi village, suburb of Nanning City" as the whole locality.* That is the paper's English locality line; its Chinese text names the Shihuoling site within Dashi Village.

**Open.** Two formations are printed for this pit, both in papers on *Nanningosaurus*. Mo and colleagues (2007) use the Nadu Formation of the 1985 regional survey while rejecting that survey's Eocene age; Dai and colleagues (2024) give the lower Luowen Formation, from sources not read here. Either would need a paper that places the Shihuoling beds in the unit directly. The 1997 Guangxi geological bureau work and Mo and colleagues (1998), "Dinosaur fauna from Nalong Basin, Guangxi and new comments on related stratigraphy", would settle it.
