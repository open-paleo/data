# *Qantassaurus*

## Is *Qantassaurus* an elasmarian?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Not shown; it sits at Ornithopoda incertae sedis. No analysis recovers it within Elasmaria: one leaves it in a polytomy of Gondwanan ornithopods and another prunes it as a wildcard, suggesting only that its teeth resemble an elasmarian's. Governs `parent: Ornithopoda`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Qantassaurus intrepidus*](../../../genera/Q/Qantassaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rich1999b, p. 167: named among "hypsilophodontid dinosaurs".
- madzia2017a, p. 6: *Burianosaurus* "situated in a polytomy along with eight Gondwanan ornithopods", *Qantassaurus* among them, outside Iguanodontia.
- herne2019a, p. 573: "Iterative PCR of the IW MPTs also identified Q. intrepidus and Diluvicursor pickeringi as wildcard taxa"; pp. 578–579: comparisons of the dentary teeth "suggest that Q. intrepidus might be an elasmarian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Elasmaria as the parent.* No analysis recovers it there.

**Open.** Its position in an analysis that retains it.

## Which printed coordinate is the holotype's?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** The type-locality position Rich and Vickers-Rich (1999) print for the holotype, 38°39′40″ ± 2″ S, 145°40′52″ ± 3″ E (WGS84), which converts to the stored pair. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Qantassaurus intrepidus*](../../../genera/Q/Qantassaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rich1999b, p. 174: "Type Locality: Flat Rocks fossil vertebrate site, 38° 39′ 40 ± 02″ S., 145° 40′ 52 ± 03″ E. (World Geodetic Standard, 1984)".
- herne2019a, p. 545: the Flat Rocks site lies at "38.660792°S, 145.681009°E, GDA94".

**Ruled out.**
- *Herne and colleagues' GDA94 position.* It is printed for the Flat Rocks site as a whole, which "consists of a series of ∼1 m deep excavations" (herne2019a, p. 545), and lies about 35 m from Rich and Vickers-Rich's value. It is the position stored for [*Galleonosaurus*](../../../genera/G/Galleonosaurus.yml), whose describing paper it comes from. The two records carry two printed values from two papers, not one value copied twice, and neither should be replaced by the other.

**Open.** Nothing.
