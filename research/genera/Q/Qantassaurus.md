# *Qantassaurus*

## Which printed coordinate is the holotype's?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** The type-locality position Rich and Vickers-Rich (1999) print for the holotype, 38°39′40″ ± 2″ S, 145°40′52″ ± 3″ E (WGS84), which converts to the stored pair. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Qantassaurus intrepidus*](../../../genera/Q/Qantassaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rich1999b, p. 174: "Type Locality: Flat Rocks fossil vertebrate site, 38° 39′ 40 ± 02″ S., 145° 40′ 52 ± 03″ E. (World Geodetic Standard, 1984)".
- herne2019a, p. 545: the Flat Rocks site lies at "38.660792°S, 145.681009°E, GDA94".

**Ruled out.**
- *Herne and colleagues' GDA94 position.* It is printed for the Flat Rocks site as a whole, which "consists of a series of ∼1 m deep excavations" (herne2019a, p. 545), and lies about 35 m from Rich and Vickers-Rich's value. It is the position stored for [*Galleonosaurus*](../../../genera/G/Galleonosaurus.yml), whose describing paper it comes from. The two records carry two printed values from two papers, not one value copied twice, and neither should be replaced by the other.

**Open.** Nothing.
