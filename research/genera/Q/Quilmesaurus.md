# *Quilmesaurus*

## Is the type locality Salitral Ojo de Agua or Salitral Moreno?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** Salitral Ojo de Agua, as the describing paper and the redescription both state. Governs `location.locality: Salitral Ojo de Agua` on [*Quilmesaurus curriei*](../../../genera/Q/Quilmesaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- coria2001a, p. 4: "Locality: Salitral Ojo de Agua, 40 km south of Roca City, Rio Negro Province, Argentina".
- juárezvalieri2007a, p. 59: "la localidad Salitral Ojo de Agua, en el centro-norte de la provincia de Río Negro" ("the locality Salitral Ojo de Agua, in the north-center of Río Negro province", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Salitral Moreno.* Aranciaga Rolando and colleagues (2020) describe *Quilmesaurus* as "coming from beds of the Allen Formation in Salitral Moreno (a locality very close to the Arriagada Farm that yielded *Niebla*; Coria 2001)" (aranciagarolando2020a, accepted manuscript, section "Comparisons between *Niebla*, *Abelisaurus* and *Quilmesaurus*"), citing the paper that gives Salitral Ojo de Agua. Rolando and colleagues (2021), from the same group, place the Arriagada Farm in the area "known as 'Salitral Ojo de Agua'" (rolando2021a, p. 2) and list the two as separate sites: "Bajo de Santa Rosa, Salitral Moreno and Salitral Ojo de Agua sites" (p. 1). Salitral Moreno is the site of [*Willinakaqe*](../../../genera/W/Willinakaqe.yml).
- *The Cerro Matadero coordinate.* Rolando and colleagues (2021, p. 2) print a position for the Cerro Matadero quarry on the Arriagada Farm, 70 km south of General Roca, for sauropod specimens; it is not this holotype's site.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is *Quilmesaurus curriei* Maastrichtian, or Campanian to Maastrichtian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Campanian to Maastrichtian. The describing paper puts the holotype in the lower part of the Allen Formation and dates it only at formation level, so the species takes the formation's range. Governs `stage: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` and `location.part: lower` on [*Quilmesaurus curriei*](../../../genera/Q/Quilmesaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- coria2001a, p. 4: "The specimen was collected in the fluvial sandstones of the lower part of the Allen Formation"; "Horizon: Allen Formation, Campanian-Maastrichtian."
- juárezvalieri2007a, p. 59: "levels of the Allen Formation (Campanian-Maastrichtian)" (translated).
- jones2026c, Abelisauridae and Brachyrostra systematics table: "Allen Fm., Argentina *Late Cretaceous, Campanian/ Maastrichtian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian alone.* The record's earlier value. No source cited for this record dates the type horizon to the Maastrichtian alone.
- *Middle Campanian.* Holtz and colleagues' table gives "Allen Formation (Río Negro), Argentina | middle Campanian" (holtz2004a, p. 77, Table 4.1), while the same chapter's text describes the type as from "the Campanian–Maastrichtian Allen Formation" (holtz2004a, p. 109). The table cites no source for the narrower age.

**Open.** Whether the lower Allen is Campanian. Powell (2003) places the lower member at other sites, Cinco Saltos and Lago Pellegrini, in the "Early Maastrichtian", following an ostracod fauna that Ballent (1980) reports from "the uppermost part of the Allen Formation". A dating of the lower Allen at Salitral Ojo de Agua would settle it.
