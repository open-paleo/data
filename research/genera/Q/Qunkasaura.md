# *Qunkasaura*

## Is the holotype from bed G1 at Lo Hueco?

*2026-09-02*

**Conclusion.** No bed is recorded. G1 is the bed of the *Lohuecotitan* holotype, HUE-EC-01, and the paper describing *Qunkasaura* (HUE-EC-04) names no bed. `part: upper` rests on a statement about the whole Lo Hueco site, so it holds whichever bed produced the holotype. Governs the empty `location.bed`, `location.locality: Lo Hueco` and `location.part: upper` on [*Qunkasaura pintiquiniestra*](../../../genera/Q/Qunkasaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- díezdíaz2016a, accepted manuscript, p. 6, Introduction: "The bed in which the described specimen was collected was identified as G1", the specimen being *Lohuecotitan pandafilandi* HUE-EC-01; the Fig. 1 caption calls G1 "one of the excavation areas … at Lo Hueco".
- díezdíaz2016a, accepted manuscript, p. 6: "The Lo Hueco fossil site is placed in sedimentary deposits belonging to the upper part of the *Margas, Arcillas y Yesos de* Villalba de la Sierra Formation".
- mocho2024a, p. 2, Type locality and horizon: "Lo Hueco fossil-site (Fuentes, Cuenca, Castilla-La Mancha, central Spain) …, Margas, arcillas y yesos de Villalba de la Sierra Formation, late Campanian-early Maastrichtian in age".

**Ruled out.**
- *Bed G1, as in "Lo Hueco, Fuentes (G1)".* G1 is tied to another taxon's holotype, and Lo Hueco is "a Campanian-Maastrichtian multi-taxic bonebed" (mocho2024a, p. 1) with more than one excavation area, so a shared site does not give a shared bed.

**Open.** Ortega and colleagues (2015), which Mocho and colleagues cite for the site's age and section, may say which excavation area produced HUE-EC-04; it has not been read.
