# *Cristatusaurus*

## Is "niveau des Innocents" the *Cristatusaurus* holotype's locality or its bed?

*2026-09-16*

**Conclusion.** The locality. "Niveau des Innocents" names a site at Gadoufaoua, and GAD 5, the upper Elrhaz, is the level. Taquet and Russell's type-locality line gives both together. Galton and Taquet separate them under their own headings, and Taquet (1976) measures distances from the Innocents as from a place. Governs `location.locality: Gadoufaoua, niveau des Innocents` and `location.bed: GAD 5` on [*Cristatusaurus lapparenti*](../../../genera/C/Cristatusaurus.yml), matching [*Nigersaurus taqueti*](../../../genera/N/Nigersaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- taquet1998a, p. 350: "Type locality: Gadoufaoua; GAD 5, niveau des Innocents, Tegama Formation, Aptian. Niger."
- galton1982a, p. 153: under LOCALITY, "Gadoufaoua, the region of niveau des Innocents (see Taquet, 1976, fig. 7)"; under HORIZON, "GAD 5, upper part of El Rhaz formation, Tégama Series, Aptian".
- sereno1999a, p. 1346, note: the *Nigersaurus* holotype is "from the locality 'niveau des Innocents' in the Gadoufaoua region".
- taquet1976a, *Ouranosaurus nigeriensis*, translated pages: a second skeleton was found "4 km south of the 'level of the innocents'"; the paratype locality is "4 km south of the formation of the Innocents … GAD 5 (upper part of the formation of Elrhaz)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Innocents as the bed and GAD 5 as the locality.* That inverts the sources: GAD 5 is printed as the horizon and the Innocents as the place, and *niveau* in the name does not make it a level in the stratigraphic sense.

**Open.** Taquet's fig. 7, the map Galton and Taquet cite, is not in the translated pages held here. This entry answers the Open question in [*Elrhazosaurus*](../E/Elrhazosaurus.md) on whether the name is a site: it is.
