# *Chiayusaurus*

## Is the *Chiayusaurus* holotype from Chia-yü-kuan or Hui-hui-p'u?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Chia-yü-kuan. Bohlin (1953) describes the type tooth in his Chia-yü-kuan section and calls it the tooth from there; the Hui-hui-p'u tooth he likens to it is larger, and he keeps it as a separate species. Governs `location.locality: Chia-yü-kuan badlands` on [*Chiayusaurus lacustris*](../../../genera/C/Chiayusaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bohlin1953a, p. 45, under the section heading "Chia-yü-kuan" (p. 43): the genus and species are erected on "a tooth rather like those of *Helopus zdanskyi*", "the only specimen of importance in a small group of finds".
- bohlin1953a, p. 46: "It is with this latter type that the tooth from Chia-yü-kuan should be compared"; "The tooth from the Chia-yü-kuan badlands would have had its place in the left maxillary."

**Ruled out.**
- *Hui-hui-p'u.* Bohlin's Hui-hui-p'u section (from p. 62) describes "Species A. (aff. Chiayūsaurus?)", a large tooth "more than twice as large as the tooth of *Chiayūsaurus lacustris* (p. 45)" (bohlin1953a, p. 64). Bohlin concludes that "At the present stage of our knowledge they must be considered as belonging to distinct species" (p. 65). The Hui-hui-p'u tooth is therefore not the type and not referred to the species.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `bohlin1953a`: Bohlin, B. (1953). VI. Vertebrate Palaeontology 6. Fossil reptiles from Mongolia and Kansu. *Reports from the Scientific Expedition to the North-western Provinces of China under Leadership of Dr. Sven Hedin. The Sino-Swedish Expedition Publication* 37: 1-113.
