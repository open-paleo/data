# *Caenagnathasia*

## Is the *Caenagnathasia* holotype CMGP 401/12457 or CCMGE 401/12457?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** One specimen under two codes. Currie and colleagues (1993) use CMGP, their abbreviation for the Central Museum of Geological Prospecting, St. Petersburg. Sabaj (2020) gives CCMGE as the current code for the Chernyshev Central Museum of Geological Exploration in St. Petersburg, and the record follows Sabaj. Governs `type_specimen.institution: CCMGE` and `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CCMGE 401/12457]` on [*Caenagnathasia martinsoni*](../../../genera/C/Caenagnathasia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- currie1993a, p. 2258: "CMGP 401/12457, anterior region of fused dentaries collected in 1979 from Dzhyrakuduk locality (site CDZh-17a)"; p. 2256: "CMGP, Central Museum of Geological Prospecting, St. Petersburg, Russia".
- sabaj2020a, p. 646, entry 1350: "CCMGE: Chernyshev Central Museum of Geological Exploration, St. Petersburg [formerly Leningrad]"; "*Current as*: CCMGE (fossil vertebrates)".

**Ruled out.**
- *CMGP as the code.* Jones (2026) gives "CMGP 401/12457" (jones2026c, p. 506), the describing paper's code. Sabaj's current code governs (see [methods](../../methods.md)).

**Open.** That CMGP and CCMGE name one museum is our reading: same city, and "prospecting" and "exploration" as two renderings of one Russian name. Sabaj does not list CMGP among the museum's other codes, and a source that does would settle it.
