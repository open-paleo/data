# *Camposaurus*

## Which member of the Chinle is the Placerias Quarry, the *Camposaurus* type locality, in?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** The Mesa Redondo Member of the Chinle Formation, which Nesbitt and colleagues (2007) equate with the Bluewater Creek unit that the describing paper names. Governs `location.formation: Chinle` and `location.member: Mesa Redondo` on [*Camposaurus arizonensis*](../../../genera/C/Camposaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hunt1998a, p. 530: "Type horizon - Bluewater Creek Formation of Chinle Group (Upper Triassic, late Carnian)."
- nesbitt2007a, p. 223, for *'Camposaurus arizonensis'*: "OCCURRENCE. Mesa Redondo Member, Chinle Formation (Lucas *et al.* 1997; Heckert & Lucas 2003), Apache County, Arizona, USA."
- nesbitt2007a, p. 235: "the *Placerias* Quarry near the base of the Mesa Redondo Member (= Bluewater Creek Member) … located above the Shinarump Member at the base of the Chinle Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Bluewater Creek Formation in a Chinle Group.* The describing paper's rank scheme. Nesbitt and colleagues treat the Chinle as a formation and give the same beds as the Mesa Redondo Member, "= Bluewater Creek Member".
- *The Lower Petrified Forest Member.* Long and Murry (1995) list Placerias Quarry material as "Lower Petrified Forest Member, Chinle Formation - Placerias Quarry ( UCMP A269)" (long1995a, p. 174), an older subdivision; Tykoski and Rowe repeat it (tykoski2004a, p. 69) while citing Hunt and colleagues (1998), who name the Bluewater Creek.
- *The Sonsela Member.* Novas and colleagues give "Placerias Quarry (ca. 219 Ma, Sonsela Member of the Chinle Formation)" (novas2021a, p. 21), citing Hunt and colleagues (1998), who do not use the name.

**Open.** Nesbitt and colleagues rest the member on Lucas and colleagues (1997) and Heckert and Lucas (2003), which have not been read here.
