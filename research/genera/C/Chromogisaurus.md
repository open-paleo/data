# *Chromogisaurus*

## Is the *Chromogisaurus* type locality Valle Pintado or Cancha de Bochas?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Valle Pintado, in the lower Cancha de Bochas Member. Ezcurra (2010), describing the holotype, names Valle Pintado as the site and Cancha de Bochas as the member; Martínez and colleagues (2012) give "Cancha de Bochas" as the type locality. The record follows the describing paper, which also ties the site to a numbered section. Governs `location.locality: Valle Pintado` and `location.member: Cancha de Bochas` on [*Chromogisaurus novasi*](../../../genera/C/Chromogisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ezcurra2010a, p. 372: "The type specimen of *Chromogisaurus novasi* (PVSJ 845) was excavated from the north of the Valle Pintado locality, in lower levels of the Cancha de Bochas Member (*sensu* Currie *et al*. 2009)".
- ezcurra2010a, p. 374, Locality and horizon: "northern margin of Valle Pintado site (Section 3 of Currie *et al*. 2009)". The etymology on the same page names "the Valle Pintado (Painted Valley) locality of the Ischigualasto Fm. in which the new taxon was found".
- martínez2012a, p. 52: "Type Locality—Cancha de Bochas, Hollada de Ischigualasto, Ischigualasto Provincial Park"; in the next paragraph, "40 m above the base of the unit in the Cancha de Bochas Member (sensu Currie et al., 2009)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cancha de Bochas as the locality.* Martínez and colleagues give no reason or source for naming Cancha de Bochas as the site, and Ezcurra's locality statements, in the geological setting and in the systematic section, both place PVSJ 845 at the northern margin of Valle Pintado. The two papers agree on the member.

**Open.** Nothing.
