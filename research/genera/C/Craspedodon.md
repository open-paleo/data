# *Craspedodon*

## Where does *Craspedodon* sit?

*2026-10-22*

**Conclusion.** Cerapoda incertae sedis. The teeth have been read as ornithopod and as neoceratopsian, and neither reading rests on a phylogenetic analysis. Ceratopsia and Ornithopoda both sit under Cerapoda, the nearest clade both readings accept. Governs `parent: Cerapoda` and `placement: incertae sedis` on [*Craspedodon lonzeensis*](../../../genera/C/Craspedodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dollo1883a, p. 218 (translated): the teeth "show clearly that this form would necessarily take its place within the order of the *Ornithopoda*."
- godefroit2007a, p. 91: "*Craspedodon lonzeensis* is probably a basal representative of the ceratopsoid clade".
- ősi2010a, p. 467: the teeth were "recently reinterpreted as possibly ceratopsian, but may instead represent a hadrosauroid ornithopod".
- maidment2026a, p. 401: "Dental morphology is highly homoplastic between Ornithopoda and Ceratopsia"; such teeth are "very difficult to distinguish".

**Ruled out.**
- *Ceratopsia.* Holds only under Godefroit and Lambert's (2007) tooth comparison, which Ősi and colleagues (2010) and Maidment and colleagues (2026) question.

**Open.** Its position in a phylogenetic analysis, which isolated teeth are unlikely to settle.

## Is *Craspedodon lonzeensis* a nomen dubium?

*2026-10-22*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The species is founded on isolated teeth, which cannot diagnose a genus, and these teeth cannot reliably be told apart between ceratopsians and ornithopods (Maidment and colleagues, 2026). Governs `status: nomen dubium` and the absence of `diagnostic_features` on [*Craspedodon lonzeensis*](../../../genera/C/Craspedodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- godefroit2007a, p. 91: "The question remains to know whether Craspedodon lonzeensis is a valid taxon or must best be regarded as a nomen dubium"; the authors "prefer to shelve this question".
- maidment2026a, p. 401, as above.

**Ruled out.**
- *Valid.* Godefroit and Lambert (2007) proposed candidate autapomorphies but declined to decide validity. Norman (2004) lists the genus among Iguanodontoidea in a table (norman2004a, Table 19.1), without argument.

**Open.** No primary read calls the name a nomen dubium; the status rests on the treatment of tooth-based names as dubious, which most earlier work did not apply.
