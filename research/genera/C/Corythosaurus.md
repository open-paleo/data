# *Corythosaurus*

## Which formation are the *Corythosaurus* holotypes from?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** Neither can be placed in the Oldman or the Dinosaur Park Formation, so both records take the Belly River Group. Brown (1914) and Parks (1923) give positions in the undivided Belly River beds, before the Dinosaur Park Formation was split from the Oldman, and no later source read places either holotype by its own position; the genus's range in the Dinosaur Park does not, as the [Oldman and Dinosaur Park](../../topics/oldman-and-dinosaur-park.md) note sets out. The stage stays Campanian: the Park exposes only the two formations, both Campanian. Governs `location.group: Belly River`, the empty `location.formation` and the location notes on *Corythosaurus casuarius* and *C. intermedius* in [*Corythosaurus*](../../../genera/C/Corythosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brown1914b, p. 560: AMNH 5240, "*Horizon and locality.* Belly River Beds, 300 feet below top of formation, near Steveville, Red Deer River, Alberta, Canada."
- parks1923a: No. 4670 Ct. (ROM 776) was found "in the badlands of the Belly River formation … about one and a half miles south of the river and two miles above Happy Jack ferry at an elevation of 175 feet (aneroid) above the river".

**Ruled out.**
- *Dinosaur Park, on the genus's range.* A range places the taxon, not the holotype.
- *Oldman, from Brown's 300 feet below the top.* It would take the top as the Belly River's at that section and the Dinosaur Park's thickness there, which is our inference.

**Open.** A source placing either holotype relative to the Oldman–Dinosaur Park contact.
