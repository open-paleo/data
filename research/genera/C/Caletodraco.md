# *Caletodraco*

## Which unit is the Caletodraco holotype from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** The [Craie glauconieuse](../../../stratigraphy/c/craie-glauconieuse.yml), lower Cenomanian, *Mantelliceras dixoni* zone. "The Chalk of the Pays de Caux" is a regional description, not a unit. Governs `location.formation: Craie glauconieuse` and `stage: [Cenomanian]` on *Caletodraco cottardi*.

**Evidence.**
- buffetaut2024a, p. 179: "C3 sequence of Hoyez et al. [17]), corresponding to the Mantelliceras dixoni zone of the lower Cenomanian glauconitic Chalk"; p. 181: "Stratum typicum: Lower Cenomanian glauconitic Chalk, Mantelliceras dixoni zone".
- hoyez2020a, p. 10: in Normandy "trois formations sont distinguées … 1. la Craie glauconieuse … 2. la Craie de Rouen … 3. la Craie d'Antifer" ("three formations are distinguished", translated), after Juignet; the Craie glauconieuse is capped by the Rouen 1 hardground. p. 55: "La séquence Ce3 … englobe la zone à Mantelliceras dixoni" ("sequence Ce3 … covers the Mantelliceras dixoni zone", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *"Chalk of the Pays de Caux" as a formation.* Buffetaut and colleagues write "the Late Cretaceous Chalk of the Pays de Caux" (p. 179) descriptively; the Pays de Caux is "the part of Normandy" inhabited by the Caleti (p. 181), the source of the name.

**Open.** Buffetaut and colleagues write "glauconitic Chalk" in English; equating it with the formation name Craie glauconieuse rests on their citing Hoyez and colleagues' sequence Ce3 (C3), which lies below the Rouen 1 hardground (a small inference).
