# *Coelophysis*

## Is the name-bearing type of *Coelophysis bauri* AMNH 7224?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The articulated skeleton AMNH 7224 is the neotype set under the plenary powers by Opinion 1842. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [AMNH 7224]` and `type_specimen.specimen_type: neotype` on [*Coelophysis bauri*](../../../genera/C/Coelophysis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- iczn1996a, p. 142: "all previous fixations of type specimens for the nominal species *Coelurus bauri* Cope, 1887 are hereby set aside and the articulated skeleton no. AMNH 7224 in the American Museum of Natural History, New York, is designated as the neotype."

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 7246, UCMP 129618.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give these in their theropod speed table, the first as a juvenile (molina-pérez2019a). They are other specimens. Jones (2026) gives "AMNH 7224—skull and skeleton" (jones2026c, p. 494).

**Open.** Nothing.
