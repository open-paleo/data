# *Chaoyangsaurus*

## What is the number of the *Chaoyangsaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** IGCAGS V371, the number the designating paper gives, at the Institute of Geology, Chinese Academy of Geological Sciences. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IGCAGS V371]` and `type_specimen.institution: IGCAGS` on [*Chaoyangsaurus youngi*](../../../genera/C/Chaoyangsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xijin1999a, p. 681: "Type Specimen—IGCAGS V371 (Figs. 2–7), the dorsal part of a skull, a nearly complete mandible, complete axis, an isolated cervical vertebra and five articulated cervicals, a fragmentary humerus and scapula"; "All of the material pertaining to this taxon is housed in the collections of the IGCAGS in Beijing."

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP V11527.* Sereno (2000) writes that *Chaoyangsaurus* "is known only from the holotype specimen (IVPP V11527)" (sereno2000a, p. 487), citing Zhao and colleagues (1999) for the description. That paper numbers the holotype IGCAGS V371 and places all the material at the IGCAGS.

**Open.** What IVPP V11527 is. No source read for this note says whether it is a cast, an IVPP number for the same specimen, or an error.
