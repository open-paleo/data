# *Coahuilaceratops*

## What stage does *Coahuilaceratops* take?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** Campanian and Maastrichtian. The describing paper calls the formation Campanian and estimates the holotype at about 72.0 Ma, the midpoint of the formation's 72.5 to 71.4 Ma. On the current chart the Maastrichtian begins at 72.1 Ma, so the estimate and its range span the boundary, and the stage takes both. Governs `stage: [Campanian, Maastrichtian]` on [*Coahuilaceratops magnacuerna*](../../../genera/C/Coahuilaceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- loewen2010a, Horizon: "the middle part (approximately 230 m level) of the Campanian Cerro del Pueblo Formation"; "Given that the Cerro del Pueblo Formation dates to between approximately 72.5 and 71.4 Ma, we estimate the age of these remains at about 72.0 Ma."

**Ruled out.**
- *Maastrichtian alone.* It converts a midpoint estimate 0.1 Myr from the boundary and drops the paper's own stage.
- *Campanian alone.* The paper's numbers reach into the Maastrichtian on the current chart.

**Open.** Barrera Guevara and colleagues (2024), who are reported to move the holotype to the Cerro Huerta Formation, have not been read.

## Is the *Coahuilaceratops* holotype from the Cerro del Pueblo or the Cerro Huerta?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The middle part of the Cerro del Pueblo Formation. Loewen and colleagues (2010) fixed the site's position by measuring down from the overlying Cerro Huerta beds. Governs `location.formation: Cerro del Pueblo` and `location.part: middle` on [*Coahuilaceratops magnacuerna*](../../../genera/C/Coahuilaceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- loewen2010a, Systematic Paleontology, *Coahuilaceratops magnacuerna*, Horizon: "The locality occurs in the middle facies (approximately 230 m level) of the Campanian Cerro del Pueblo Formation, the basal formation of the Difunta Group."
- loewen2010a, Introduction: "the stratigraphic positions of the sites were estimated by measuring down from the overlying Cerro Huerta beds, placing the ceratopsian material in the middle (approximately 230 m level) of the Cerro del Pueblo Formation".
- rivera-sylva2017a, Conclusions: "*Coahuilaceratops magnacuerna*, a chasmosaurine from the Cerro del Pueblo Formation in southern Coahuila".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cerro Huerta.* The record carried it until 2026-08-06. The paper names the Cerro Huerta only as the overlying unit the sections were measured from. Jones (2026) gives "Cerro del Pueblo Fm., Mexico" (jones2026a, p. 412).

**Open.** Nothing.
