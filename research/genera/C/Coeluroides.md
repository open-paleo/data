# *Coeluroides*

## Is K 27/595 a *Coeluroides* syntype or an allosaurid caudal?

*2026-05-10*

**Conclusion.** A *Coeluroides* syntype. Huene and Matley (1933) print the number twice in their plate captions: on Plate XXI for a *Coeluroides* dorsal arch, and on Plate XV for an allosaurid caudal vertebra. Their text gives the Plate XV vertebra as K27/596. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [GSI K27/562, GSI K27/574, GSI K27/595]` on [*Coeluroides largus*](../../../genera/C/Coeluroides.yml).

**Evidence.**
- huene1933a, p. 61: "A third group is formed by the three dorsal vertebrae, K 27/562 (Pl. XXI, fig. 6), K 27/574 (Pl. XXI, fig. 4), K 27/595 (Pl. XXI, fig. 5)"; Pl. XXI caption: "Fig. 5. Same as fig. 4. Dorsal arch, K 27/595".
- huene1933a, p. 53: among the allosaurid caudals, "K27/594 (Pl. XV, fig. 6), K27/596, (Pl. XV, fig. 1)"; Pl. XV caption: "Fig. 1. Allosaurid ; caudal vertebra, K 27/595".
- novas2004b, p. 82: "Specimen GSI K27/595 (Huene & Matley, 1933, pl.XXI fig.5) is a neural arch that closely resembles proximal caudals of *Majungatholus*".

**Ruled out.**
- *K 27/595 as the allosaurid caudal of Plate XV.* The paper's text gives that figure as K27/596 (p. 53), while the *Coeluroides* paragraph and the Plate XXI caption agree on K 27/595 (p. 61). Reading the Plate XV caption as the misprint is our inference from two printings against one.

**Open.** Whether the numbers marked on the specimens at the Geological Survey of India agree; a check of the collection would settle it.
