# *Cryptarcus*

## Where in the Dinosaur Park Formation is the *Cryptarcus* holotype?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** The middle, from Holmes and colleagues' (2026) section measured at the quarry in 2025, which puts the host sandstone 1.5 to 3 m below the base of the Lethbridge Coal Zone in a formation about 32 m thick at Onefour. The same paper's abstract repeats an earlier "upper", and its introduction a "lower" from Godfrey and Holmes (1995); the measured section is the only one of the three made at the quarry. The placement is conditional on the authors' reading that the top of the Oldman lies just below the section. Governs `location.part: middle`, `location.formation: Dinosaur Park` and `location.locality: near the Onefour Research Station, about 33 km southeast of Manyberries` on [*Cryptarcus russelli*](../../../genera/C/Cryptarcus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- holmes2026a, p. 6: the quarry marked "GSC 126-1"; "the host stratum of CMNFV 8800 is located between 1.5 and 3 m below the base of the Lethbridge Coal Zone, and within the middle of the DPFm in the general Onefour area", preceded by "If this interpretation is correct"; Eberth (2024) "measured the DPFm to be 32 m thick in the Onefour area".
- holmes2026a, p. 1: "from the upper Dinosaur Park Formation of southern Alberta"; p. 2: "from deposits later deemed to be equivalent in age to the lower Dinosaur Park Formation (Godfrey and Holmes 1995)".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: upper`.* The abstract's wording, not the result of the paper's own section.
- *Near the base, or the lower part.* Holmes and colleagues (2001) put NMC 8800 "near the base of the Dinosaur Park Formation" at 658 m above sea level (holmes2001a, p. 1433), and Godfrey and Holmes (1995) gave a lower equivalence. Both predate the measured section, which finds 13.5 m of the formation below the coal zone and the specimen 1.5 to 3 m below the zone's base.
- *The Oldman Formation.* Sternberg's (1940) "Oldman formation (upper part)" is the usage before the 1993 subdivision (see [Oldman and Dinosaur Park](../../topics/oldman-and-dinosaur-park.md)).

**Open.** Whether the Oldman–Dinosaur Park contact lies where Holmes and colleagues place it, covered below the section. Campbell and colleagues (2016), who relocated the quarry and gave "upper", have not been read.
