# *Chasmosaurus*

## What is the *Chasmosaurus belli* holotype, and where is it from?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** NMC 491, part of the coalesced parietals on which Lambe named the species in 1902, from the Belly River beds of the Red Deer River between Berry Creek and Dead Lodge Cañon. The skeleton with skin impressions the record described is the specimen Sternberg's party found in 1913, a referred specimen. No source read places the holotype in the Oldman or the Dinosaur Park Formation, so the record takes the Belly River Group, as [*Corythosaurus*](Corythosaurus.md) and [*Centrosaurus*](Centrosaurus.md) do; the stage stays Campanian. Governs `type_specimen.material`, `completeness`, `location.locality`, `location.group: Belly River` and the empty `location.formation` on *Chasmosaurus belli* in [*Chasmosaurus*](../../../genera/C/Chasmosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lambe1902a: the type is "the coalesced parietals of the posterior crest" of *Monoclonius belli*, "from Red Deer river"; the collections were made "from the Belly River series only, in an extensive area of "bad lands" on either side of Red Deer river between Berry creek and Dead Lodge cañon".
- hatcher1907a: "*M. belli* Lambe … Type No. 491, Geol. Surv. Canada."
- lambe1914b: Sternberg's party found "a fairly complete skeleton with the skull, including skin impressions, of *Chasmosaurus belli*, a species founded by the writer in 1902, on part of the coalesced parietals".

**Ruled out.**
- *The 1913 skeleton as the type.* It was found eleven years after the species was named.
- *Berry Creek as the site.* It is one end of the stretch Lambe collected.
- *Dinosaur Park.* No source places NMC 491 by its own position.

**Open.** A source placing NMC 491 relative to the Oldman–Dinosaur Park contact.
