# *Cathartesaura*

## Is the *Cathartesaura* holotype from an upper or a lower level at La Buitrera?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** A lower level: the lower section of the Huincul Formation. The upper member that Gallina and Apesteguía (2005) also name at La Buitrera is the Candeleros Formation's, a different unit exposed at the same locality. Governs `location.formation: Huincul`, `location.part: lower` and `location.locality: La Buitrera` on [*Cathartesaura anaerobica*](../../../genera/C/Cathartesaura.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gallina2005a, p. 153: at La Buitrera "the upper member of the Candeleros Formation (Cenomanian-Turonian) as well as the basal member of the Huincul Formation (Turonian-Coniacian) crop out."
- gallina2005a, p. 154, Horizon and locality: "Sandy to pelitic levels of the lower section of the Huincul Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *An upper position ("La Buitrera (upper)").* The paper uses "upper" only of the Candeleros Formation's member; the holotype is from the lower Huincul.

**Open.** Nothing.
