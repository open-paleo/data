# *Chirostenotes*

## Which locality and coordinates belong to the *Chirostenotes pergracilis* holotype?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Gilmore's (1924) type locality for CMN 2367, two miles northeast of the mouth of Little Sandhill Creek, with no coordinate. The two other positions printed for *Chirostenotes* material belong to other specimens. Governs `location.locality: 2 miles northeast of the mouth of Little Sandhill Creek, Red Deer River` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Chirostenotes pergracilis*](../../../genera/C/Chirostenotes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1924a, p. 3: "Type: No. 2367, Geol. Surv., Can., consists of the nearly complete articulated digits of both fore feet. Collected by G. F. Sternberg, 1914. Locality: Sec. 17, tp. 21, range 11, W. 4th mer., 2 miles due northeast of the mouth of Little Sandhill creek, Red Deer river, Alberta."

**Ruled out.**
- *The dentary locality, "2½ miles south of the mouth of Little Sandhill creek".* Gilmore gives it for No. 343, a pair of dentaries "provisionally associated" with the type (gilmore1924a, p. 6), and calls the association "purely arbitrary, as No. 343 was discovered three years after the type and a few miles distant from the type locality" (p. 7).
- *UTM 12U 468540, 5621530.* Funston and Currie (2020) print this GPS position for UALVP 59400, a partial skeleton they refer to the species (funston2020c, p. 3, Newly Referred Material). It is that specimen's site, not the holotype's.

**Open.** Nothing.
