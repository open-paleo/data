# *Crichtonsaurus*

## Is the *Crichtonsaurus* coordinate a seeded value?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** No. Dong (2002) measured it himself by GPS at the Shuangmiao fossil site, where the holotype mandible was collected, so it is kept. Governs `location.coordinates: [41.711111, 120.713611]` and `location.locality: Shuangmiao village, Xiafu Township, Beipiao City` on [*Crichtonsaurus bohlini*](../../../genera/C/Crichtonsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong2002a, p. 277: during the May 2001 visit to "Shuangmiao Village, Xiafu Township, Beipiao City", "several specimens were collected, among which a small piece of mandible bearing teeth"; in June, "using GPS determined the position of the fossil locality to be at 120°42′49″ E, 41°42′40″ N" (translated). The conversion is 41.711111, 120.713611.
- arbour2015a, p. 40: "Beipiao Basin, Liaoning, China, ?holotype locality is" the same figures.

**Ruled out.**
- *A seed because other records share it.* The pair has appeared on records in two formations, among them a Yixian taxon. Dong prints it for this site; Arbour and Currie reuse it for *Crichtonpelta*, which is a different specimen (see [*Crichtonpelta*](Crichtonpelta.md)), and the copy on the Yixian taxon traces to no source.
- *Withdrawn because of Arbour and Currie's query.* Their "?" stands before the whole locality line and gives no reason; it does not dispute Dong's reading.

**Open.** Nothing.
