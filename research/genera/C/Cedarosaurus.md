# *Cedarosaurus*

## What places the *Cedarosaurus* holotype in the upper Yellow Cat Member?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Kirkland and colleagues (2016), who list *Cedarosaurus weiskopfae* in the upper Yellow Cat fauna and figure its holotype among the characteristic fossils of the upper member. The describing paper gives the member with no position in it. Governs `location.member: Yellow Cat` and `location.part: upper` on [*Cedarosaurus weiskopfae*](../../../genera/C/Cedarosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tidwell1999a, p. 23, Type Horizon and Locality: "Yellow Cat Member, Cedar Mountain Formation (Lower Cretaceous: Barremian); Grand County Utah, United States."
- kirkland2016a, p. 137, Fig. 22: "Characteristic faunal elements from the upper Yellow Cat Member", among them "(M) Holotype skeleton of brachiosaurid sauropod *Cedarosaurus*"; pp. 137–138, the list headed "Upper Yellow Cat Fauna" includes "*Cedarosaurus weiskopfae* Tidwell and others (1999)".

**Ruled out.**
- *The lower Yellow Cat, read from Tidwell and colleagues (2001).* They write that *Cedarosaurus* "was found by a DMNH field crew at a significantly lower stratigraphic level than *Venenosaurus*" (tidwell2001a, p. 157), and *Venenosaurus* is from the overlying Poison Strip Sandstone Member (p. 140). The comparison is with another member and gives no position within the Yellow Cat; that it is compatible with the upper Yellow Cat is our reading.

**Open.** Nothing.
