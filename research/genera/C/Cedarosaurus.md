# *Cedarosaurus*

## What places the *Cedarosaurus* holotype in the upper Yellow Cat Member?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Kirkland and colleagues (2016), who list *Cedarosaurus weiskopfae* in the upper Yellow Cat fauna and figure its holotype among the characteristic fossils of the upper member. The describing paper gives the member with no position in it. Governs `location.member: Yellow Cat` and `location.part: upper` on [*Cedarosaurus weiskopfae*](../../../genera/C/Cedarosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- tidwell1999a, p. 23, Type Horizon and Locality: "Yellow Cat Member, Cedar Mountain Formation (Lower Cretaceous: Barremian); Grand County Utah, United States."
- kirkland2016a, p. 137, Fig. 22: "Characteristic faunal elements from the upper Yellow Cat Member", among them "(M) Holotype skeleton of brachiosaurid sauropod *Cedarosaurus*"; pp. 137–138, the list headed "Upper Yellow Cat Fauna" includes "*Cedarosaurus weiskopfae* Tidwell and others (1999)".

**Ruled out.**
- *The lower Yellow Cat, read from Tidwell and colleagues (2001).* They write that *Cedarosaurus* "was found by a DMNH field crew at a significantly lower stratigraphic level than *Venenosaurus*" (tidwell2001a, p. 157), and *Venenosaurus* is from the overlying Poison Strip Sandstone Member (p. 140). The comparison is with another member and gives no position within the Yellow Cat; that it is compatible with the upper Yellow Cat is our reading.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which stage is the *Cedarosaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Valanginian. The record takes it from the holotype's place in the upper Yellow Cat Member (see the 2026-08-15 entry) and the dating of the beds above the thick calcrete at Utahraptor Ridge. No date has been published for the Cedarosaurus Quarry itself. Carrying the Utahraptor Ridge section over to the quarry is our inference. Governs `stage: [Valanginian]` on [*Cedarosaurus weiskopfae*](../../../genera/C/Cedarosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kirkland2016a, p. 137, Fig. 22: "Characteristic faunal elements from the upper Yellow Cat Member", among them "Holotype skeleton of brachiosaurid sauropod *Cedarosaurus*".
- joeckel2023a, p. 3: at Utahraptor Ridge, "Another mudstone-dominated interval with intercalated sandstones (32 m and above in Figure 2) overlies the thick 'calcrete.'"; p. 12: "the eruption age of 135.1 ± 0.3 Ma of a cryptotephra from 36.35 m above the contact", and the C-isotope record "from 32.5 to 42.5 m as Valanginian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Barremian–Aptian.* The record carried it until 2026-08-05. The describing paper gives "Yellow Cat Member, Cedar Mountain Formation (Lower Cretaceous: Barremian)" (tidwell1999a, p. 23), and Weishampel and colleagues (2004) give "Lower Cedar Mountain Formation … Barremian" (weishampel2004a, p. 267, Table 13.1). Both are member-wide ages from before the Utahraptor Ridge dates.
- *Albian.* Jones (2026) gives "Cedar Mt. Fm., United States, Early Cretaceous, Albian" (jones2026b, p. 379). No source read places the Yellow Cat Member in the Albian.

**Open.** The member is diachronous (see [Yellow Cat](../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.md)). [*Martharaptor*](../../../genera/M/Martharaptor.yml), also in the upper Yellow Cat, is Barremian on its own site's maximum age. A date from the Cedarosaurus Quarry, or a section tying it to Utahraptor Ridge, would settle the stage.
