# *Calamospondylus*

## Is the *Calamospondylus oweni* holotype from the Wessex or the Vectis Formation, and is BMNH R178 that holotype?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** The Wessex Formation, at Brook, with the horizon unknown. Naish (2002) gives Brook as the site from Fox's own account and puts the specimen "probably" in the Wessex. The holotype is lost; BMNH R178 is a different specimen, the type of *Aristosuchus pusillus*. Governs `location.formation: Wessex` and `location.locality: Brook`, and the absence of a `type_specimen.specimen_id`, on [*Calamospondylus oweni*](../../../genera/C/Calamospondylus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- naish2002a, p. 154: *C. oweni* "was discovered by the Reverend William Fox at Brook, Isle of Wight (Fox in Anon., 1866a; Blows, 1983)".
- naish2002a, p. 153, Fig. 2 caption: "Both *Calamospondylus* and *Aristosuchus* were probably collected from the Wessex Formation, though from which horizon is unknown."
- naish2002a, p. 160: "the fossil described by Fox (in Anon., 1866a, b), and named C. *oweni* by him, is lost"; p. 161: "the name C. *oweni* was not created for BMNH R 178 and *Aristosuchus pusillus* is the correct name for this specimen".
- holtz2004a, p. 78, Table 4.1: "*Calamospondylus oweni* Fox, 1866 Vertebrae, pubes, phalanx (type of *Aristosuchus* Seeley, 1887a)".
- weishampel2004b, p. 559: Isle of Wight, "2. Vectis Formation (= Wealden Shale …) … Theropoda indet. (= *Calamospondylus oweni*)".

**Ruled out.**
- *The Vectis Formation.* The *Dinosauria* distribution chapter lists the name under the Vectis with no site or specimen. The same volume treats *C. oweni* as the type of *Aristosuchus* (holtz2004a, p. 78), which is BMNH R178, a specimen whose site Naish gives as "the exact location is unknown" (naish2002a, p. 161). That the Vectis entry follows from this identification is an inference; the entry gives no reason of its own, and it does not concern Fox's specimen from Brook.
- *BMNH R178 as the holotype.* Ostrom (1970), Norman (1990) and Blows (1998) took it for the type, Naish (2002) reports (naish2002a, p. 154); Fox's letter to Owen calls R178 "quite different" from the specimen he named (naish2002a, p. 160), and the *Dinosauria* table repeats the older identification.

**Open.** The bed. Naish's "probably" rests on where Fox collected, not on a record of the bed; Blows's (1983) edition of Fox's correspondence is the likeliest source of anything finer.
