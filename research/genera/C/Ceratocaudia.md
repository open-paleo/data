# *Ceratocaudia*

## Which member of the Kaladongar Formation is the *Ceratocaudia* holotype from?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Neither member alone. Natarajan and colleagues (2026) give the type horizon as "Mouwana Variegated/Lower Flagstone Member" and explain the slash: the Amrapar succession is a transition from the upper Mouwana Variegated Member to the Lower Flagstone Member, and shows characters of both. The two are separate members of the formation's four. The record carries the formation and no member. Governs `location.formation: Kaladongar` and the absence of `location.member` on [*Ceratocaudia antiqua*](../../../genera/C/Ceratocaudia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- natarajan2026a, p. 2, Geological Setting: the formation "is divided into four members, viz. Cheriya Bet Conglomerate, Mouwana Variegated, Lower Flagstone, and Middle Sandstone at the Khadir Uplift".
- natarajan2026a, p. 2, Lithology of the Amrapar Succession: "The Amrapar succession shows characters of both the Mouwana Variegated Member … and the Lower Flagstone Member"; "It is considered here as a subtle lithostratigraphic transition from the upper Mouwana Variegated Member to the Lower Flagstone Member."
- natarajan2026a, p. 13, Type Locality and Horizon: "Near Amrapar village, Khadir Uplift; Mouwana Variegated/Lower Flagstone Member, Kaladongar Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Mouwana Variegated, as one member printed two ways.* The paper's slash marks a transition between two of its four named members, not two names for one.

**Open.** Fürsich and colleagues (2023), whose scheme the paper follows, has not been read; it might assign the Amrapar section to one member.
