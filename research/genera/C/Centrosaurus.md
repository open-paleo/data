# *Centrosaurus*

## Where on the Red Deer River was the *Centrosaurus* holotype found, and in which formation?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** No site is recorded below the Red Deer River. The holotype parietal crest is one of the two specimens Lambe (1902) described together as *Monoclonius dawsoni*, and he says only that they "were collected on Red Deer river in 1901". The formation and part are inferred from the species' range, not read from a placement of this specimen: Ryan and Russell (2005) restrict *C. apertus* to the lower 30 m of the Dinosaur Park Formation, apart from a time-equivalent Oldman occurrence in the Manyberries region. Governs `location.locality: Red Deer River`, `location.formation: Dinosaur Park` and `location.part: lower` on [*Centrosaurus apertus*](../../../genera/C/Centrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lambe1902a, p. 57: "consisting of the skull of one individual and the posterior crest of another"; p. 60: "The skull and the posterior crest were collected on Red Deer river in 1901."
- lambe1902a, p. 25: the 1898 and 1901 collections came from "bad lands" on either side of Red Deer river "between Berry creek and Dead Lodge cañon".
- ryan2005a, p. 1371: type specimen "NMC 971: a complete parietal"; distribution "Lower 30 m of the Dinosaur Provincial Park Formation, Alberta and Saskatchewan, Canada, and the timeequivalent middle "muddy unit" of the Oldman Formation, Manyberries region".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** *North fork of Sand Creek, at a 50 ft level.* Given for the holotype without a source. The Sand Creek sites printed in the works read belong to other specimens: Brown (1916) gives the *Prosaurolophus maximus* type, AMNH 5386, from the "north fork of Sand Creek, 12 miles below Steveville" (brown1916a, p. 701), and Sternberg (1940) gives the *Centrosaurus longirostris* type, CMN 8795, from "one mile south of mouth of Sand Creek … 102 feet above Red Deer River" (sternberg1940b, p. 473). Neither is CMN 971. Lambe's collecting area does not exclude Sand Creek, so the reading is unsupported rather than contradicted. A field record of the 1901 season would settle the site and, with it, a direct placement in the Dinosaur Park Formation.

## Is the *Centrosaurus apertus* holotype from the Dinosaur Park or the Oldman Formation?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** The Dinosaur Park Formation. Ryan and Russell (2005) restrict *C. apertus* to the lower part of the Dinosaur Park, and the Oldman taxon of their paper is *C. brinkmani*. No source read places this holotype directly (see the 2026-08-15 entry). Governs `location.formation: Dinosaur Park` on [*Centrosaurus apertus*](../../../genera/C/Centrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ryan2005a, p. 1369: "the centrosaurines *Centrosaurus apertus* and *Styracosaurus albertensis* are commonly recovered from the Dinosaur Park Formation, where they also appear to be stratigraphically restricted to the lower and upper part of the formation, respectively".
- ryan2005a, p. 1371: distribution "Lower 30 m of the Dinosaur Provincial Park Formation … and the timeequivalent middle "muddy unit" of the Oldman Formation, Manyberries region".

**Ruled out.**
- *Oldman Formation.* The record carried it until 2026-08-03. The only Oldman occurrence Ryan and Russell give for *C. apertus* is in the Manyberries region, while this holotype was collected on the Red Deer River. Jones (2026) gives "Dinosaur Park Fm., Canada" (jones2026a, p. 411). See [Oldman and Dinosaur Park](../../topics/oldman-and-dinosaur-park.md) on reading an older "Oldman" for Park specimens.

**Open.** Nothing beyond the 2026-08-15 entry.

## Is the *Centrosaurus apertus* holotype CMN 971 or AMNH 3999?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** CMN 971, the parietal that Ryan and Russell (2005) give as the type specimen under the museum's former code NMC. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CMN 971]` on [*Centrosaurus apertus*](../../../genera/C/Centrosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ryan2005a, p. 1371: "TYPE SPECIMEN: NMC 971: a complete parietal."

**Ruled out.**
- *AMNH 3999.* Jones (2026) gives "AMNH 3999—partial skull" as the type (jones2026a, p. 411). No source read connects that number with this parietal.

**Open.** Nothing.
