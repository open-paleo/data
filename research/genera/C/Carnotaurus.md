# *Carnotaurus*

## Which unit, and which part of it, is the *Carnotaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** The lower section of the La Colonia Formation, Maastrichtian, as Carrano and Sampson (2008) list the occurrence, citing Ardolino and Delpino (1987), Bonaparte and colleagues (1990) and Bonaparte (1996). The describing paper's Gorro Frigio Formation of Albian age is the assignment of 1985, made when "the geology of the area is not well known". Governs `location.formation: La Colonia` and `location.part: lower` on [*Carnotaurus sastrei*](../../../genera/C/Carnotaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- bonaparte1985a, p. 149: "The geology of the area is not well known. Here the carnosaur-bearing beds are referred to as the continental Gorro Frigio Formation of Albian age"; p. 150, Locality and Age: "Near the top of the Gorro Frigio Formation, Middle Cretaceous (Albian)."
- carrano2008a, p. 191: "Lower section, La Colonia Formation; Maastrichtian, Late Cretaceous (Ardolino & Delpino 1987; Bonaparte *et al.* 1990; Bonaparte 1996)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Gorro Frigio Formation, Albian.* The describing paper's assignment, which the works Carrano and Sampson (2008) cite replace with the La Colonia. None of the three has been read, so which of them made the change is not established here.
- *An upper position, from "near the top".* Bonaparte (1985) places the skeleton near the top of the Gorro Frigio as he then drew it. That the phrase does not carry over to the La Colonia is our reading; the only position given within the La Colonia is Carrano and Sampson's lower section.

**Open.** Whether the holotype is from the lower or the middle part of the formation. An encyclopedia summary places most La Colonia vertebrates, *Carnotaurus* among them, in the formation's "middle facies association", citing Gasparini and colleagues (2015) and Sterli and colleagues (2021). Neither paper has been read; either would settle `part`.
