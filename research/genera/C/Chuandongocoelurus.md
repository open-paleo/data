# *Chuandongocoelurus*

## Where was the *Chuandongocoelurus* holotype found?

*2026-08-16*

**Conclusion.** At Chuandong, Sichuan, in the lower part of the Shaximiao Formation, which is the only locality a source read gives for CCG 20010. Governs `location.locality: Chuandong` and `location.part: lower` on [*Chuandongocoelurus primitivus*](../../../genera/C/Chuandongocoelurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carrano2012a, p. 226: "Holotype. CCG 20010, a partial postcranial skeleton"; "Occurrence. Chuandong, Sichuan, China; Xiashaximiao Formation; Middle Jurassic (He 1984)".
- li2011a, p. 23: lists "*Chuandongocoelurus primitivus* He, 1984" in the assemblage that "mostly comes from the middle, southern and eastern part of Sichuan Province" in "the lower member of Shaximiao Formation".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Which site the holotype came from. Li and colleagues (2011) write that "Relatively common *Chuandongocoelurus* fossils come from Kaijiang" (li2011a, p. 26), but the same paper says the species "was distinguished based on a tooth" (p. 23, footnote), where Carrano and colleagues (2012) and every other source read describe a postcranial holotype, so its Kaijiang statement is not adopted on its own. Kaijiang lies in eastern Sichuan, and the genus name's Chuandong reads as "eastern Sichuan", so the two may not disagree; that reading of the name is our inference and has not been checked in He (1984). The Dashanpu quarry at Zigong, which the record once carried with a coordinate shared by the Dashanpu taxa, is given for this specimen by no source read. He (1984), the describing work, has not been read and would settle the site.
