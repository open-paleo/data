# *Convolosaurus*

## Is *Convolosaurus* a rhabdodontomorph?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Unresolved; it sits at Ornithopoda incertae sedis. Its describers and analyses on the matrix of Dieudonné and colleagues recover it outside Iguanodontia, near *Hypsilophodon*; analyses on the matrix of Fonseca and colleagues (2024) recover it as the earliest tenontosaurid, within Rhabdodontomorpha. Governs `parent: Ornithopoda`, `placement: incertae sedis` and the dispute on [*Convolosaurus marri*](../../../genera/C/Convolosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- andrzejewski2019a, abstract: "Convolosaurus marri gen. et sp. nov., is recovered outside of Iguanodontia, but forms a clade with Iguanodontia exclusive of Hypsilophodon foxii".
- dieudonné2020a, Fig. 2, p. 4: *Convolosaurus* with *Yueosaurus*, outside Iguanodontia.
- tykoski2023a, Results: *Convolosaurus* among "'hypsilophodont-grade' ornithopods such as Jeholosaurus, Haya, Hypsilophodon".
- fonseca2024a, Tenontosauridae: "Convolosaurus marri has been found previously to be a basal ornithopod between Hypsilophodon foxii and Iguanodontia (Andrzejewski et al., 2019; Dieudonne et al., 2021; Tykoski et al., 2023)"; here *Iani* and *Convolosaurus* are "successive sister taxa to Tenontosaurus".
- dieudonné2026a, p. 36: *Convolosaurus* grouped with Hypsilophodontidae and *Yueosaurus*, outside Iguanodontia.

**Ruled out.**
- *Rhabdodontomorpha as the parent.* Only analyses on the matrix of Fonseca and colleagues recover it there.
- *Iguanodontia as the parent.* Its describers and Dieudonné and colleagues place it outside the clade.

**Open.** Whether further analyses converge.
