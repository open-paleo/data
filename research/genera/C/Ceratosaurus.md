# *Ceratosaurus*

## Does the *Ceratosaurus* holotype take the Brushy Basin Member?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** Yes. The holotype is from Felch Quarry 1, and Chure (2000) gives that quarry's level, 47.9 m above the J5 unconformity in the Brushy Basin Member, for two other types excavated there. A quarry's level is the level of what came out of it, so the statement is about the holotype's site, unlike a level given for a neighbouring excavation. Governs `location.member: Brushy Basin` on [*Ceratosaurus nasicornis*](../../../genera/C/Ceratosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- chure2000a, *Labrosaurus ferox*: "LOCALITY: Felch Quarry 1, Garden Park, near Cañon City, Fremont County, CO. … HORIZON: Brushy Basin Mbr., 47.9 m above the J5 unconformity"; the same horizon is given for the *Allosaurus fragilis* type from the quarry, after Evanoff and Carpenter (1998).

**Ruled out.**
- *No member until USNM 4735 is placed by number.* The quarry's level already places it.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `chure2000a`: Chure, D. J. (2000). A new species of Allosaurus from the Morrison Formation of Dinosaur National Monument (UT-CO) and a revision of the theropod family Allosauridae. Doctoral thesis, Columbia University.
