# *Cryptosaurus*

## Is the placement of *Cryptosaurus* in dispute?

*2026-10-17*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute block is removed and the parent stays Ankylosauria. Naish and Martill (2008) regard the femur as Ankylosauria indet. for lack of autapomorphies, rejecting Galton's nodosaurid referral, and later work treats it as an ankylosaur without a family. Governs `parent: Ankylosauria` and the absence of a dispute on [*Cryptosaurus*](../../../genera/C/Cryptosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- naish2008a: "Cryptosaurus lacks autapomorphies and should be regarded as Ankylosauria indet."
- galton1980a: the femur "is very similar to that of the ankylosaur (armored dinosaur) *Hoplitosaurus*".
- maidment2020a counts it among "The oldest definitive ankylosaurs".

**Ruled out.**
- *Nodosauridae.* Galton's referral, rejected by Naish and Martill (2008).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Cryptosaurus* femur from the Oxford Clay or the Ampthill Clay?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** The Ampthill Clay. Seeley (1875) names the Oxford Clay only in his title. Galton (1980) reports that C. L. Forbes identified the bed as the Ampthill Clay, as Roberts (1892) had listed it, and the later compilations follow him. Governs `location.formation: Ampthill Clay` on [*Cryptosaurus eumerus*](../../../genera/C/Cryptosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- seeley1875b, p. 149: the title, "On the Femur of Cryptosaurus eumerus, Seeley, a Dinosaur from the Oxford Clay of Great Gransden"; the text gives no other horizon.
- galton1980a, p. 79: the femur was "described by SEELEY (1875) from the Oxford Clay of Great Gransden, Cambridgeshire", but "Dr. C. L. FORBES (personal communication) informs me that this femur is from the Ampthill Clay (listed as such by ROBERTS 1892: 49) that is Upper Oxfordian".
- weishampel2004b, p. 546: Cambridgeshire, "Ampthill Clay (Seeley 1869; Galton ...)", "Ankylosauria indet. (= Cryptosaurus eumerus)", "late Oxfordian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Oxford Clay.* Seeley's title is the only source for it, and Galton reports the correction against it, with Roberts's earlier listing.

**Open.** The correction rests on a personal communication and on Roberts (1892), which has not been read; Roberts's list would confirm it.
