# *Chilantaisaurus*

## Is the *Chilantaisaurus* type IVPP V2884, V2884.1 or V2884.2?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** IVPP V2884, the number Hu (1964) gives the whole associated skeleton. V2884.1 and V2884.2 number single elements within it. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [IVPP V2884]` on [*Chilantaisaurus tashuikouensis*](../../../genera/C/Chilantaisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hu1964a, *Chilantaisaurus tashuikouensis*, new species (translated): "Material: A right humerus, the claw of the first digit, a fragmentary left ilium …"; "All the material belongs to the same individual. Specimen registration number V.2884."
- hu1964a (translated): the humerus is "Right humerus V.2884.1 (Fig. 1)"; "The ungual of digit I, V.2884.2 (Fig. 2), is well preserved."

**Ruled out.**
- *IVPP V2884.1.* The record carried the humerus's number until 2026-08-01.
- *IVPP V2884.2.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give the ungual's number in their theropod speed table (molina-pérez2019a). Jones (2026) gives "IVPP V2884—partial limbs (Hu, 1964)" (jones2026c, p. 499), agreeing with Hu.

**Open.** Nothing.
