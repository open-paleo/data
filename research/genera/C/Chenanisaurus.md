# *Chenanisaurus*

## Which part of Couche III is the *Chenanisaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Upper Couche III. Longrich and colleagues (2026) divide Couche III into an upper and a lower part and place *Chenanisaurus* in the upper. The describing paper's only statement of a level is a hedge in a figure caption. Governs `location.part: upper` (with `location.member: Couche III`) on [*Chenanisaurus barbaricus*](../../../genera/C/Chenanisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- longrich2026a, p. 3: in their table of Moroccan phosphate dinosaurs, "*Chenanisaurus barbaricus* | Abelisauridae | Sidi Chennane | Upper Couche III | Latest Maastrichtian", while their new titanosaur is "Lower Couche III". The text on the same page: "previous dinosaur fossils all came from Upper Couche III".
- longrich2017a, p. 43, Fig. 2 caption: "The type specimen comes from the late Maastrichtian of Couche III, most likely the fossil-rich bonebed layer in the middle of Couche III."

**Ruled out.**
- *A middle part.* The 2017 caption's "middle of Couche III" is a probable position ("most likely"). The main text says only that "The type specimen is reported as coming from the upper Maastrichtian (Couche III) of Sidi Chennane" (longrich2017a, p. 43), where "upper" qualifies the age, not the bed. The 2026 paper, by an overlapping group of authors, states the level without a hedge.

**Open.** Whether the bonebed layer of the 2017 caption lies in what the 2026 paper calls Upper Couche III. The 2017 section is redrawn from Kocsis and colleagues (2014); a section marking the division between Upper and Lower Couche III against that bonebed would settle it.
