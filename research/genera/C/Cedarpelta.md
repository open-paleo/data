# *Cedarpelta*

## Which member is the *Cedarpelta* holotype from?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** The uppermost Ruby Ranch Member. The text of the describing paper says so in its abstract, introduction and type-locality line, and Kirkland and colleagues (2016) keep the holotype skull in the upper Ruby Ranch; only the paper's own Table 11.1 gives the Mussentuchit Member. Governs `location.member: Ruby Ranch` and `location.part: upper` on [*Cedarpelta bilbeyhallorum*](../../../genera/C/Cedarpelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carpenter2001a, p. 211: "The specimen was taken from the top of the Ruby Ranch Member of the Lower Cretaceous Cedar Mountain Formation"; p. 216, Type Locality: "Price River 1 (PR-l) locality (CEM site), CEUM 42EM352U, CEU90-2, uppermost Ruby Ranch Member".
- carpenter2001a, p. 212: the overlying Mussentuchit Member is a conglomeratic sandstone "that caps the ridge above the CEM, PR-l, and PR-2 localities".
- carpenter2001a, p. 213, Table 11.1: *Cedarpelta bilbeyhallorum*, "Mussentuchit Member, Cedar Mountain Formation, Utah", "Albian-Cenomanian".
- kirkland2007a, p. 15, Fig. 13: "Type juvenile skull of CEDARPELTA from the CEU Prehistoric Museum's KEM Site in the upper Ruby Ranch Member".
- kirkland2016a, p. 152, Fig. 30: "Type partial juvenile skull of *Cedarpelta* in dorsal view from the KEM site (Utah Em419v) in the upper Ruby Ranch Member at same horizon but across the valley from main Price River Quarries".

**Ruled out.**
- *Mussentuchit Member.* Table 11.1 of the describing paper gives it, against the same paper's text, which puts the Mussentuchit in the sandstone capping the ridge above the sites, and against Kirkland and Madsen (2007) and Kirkland and colleagues (2016).

**Open.** Two questions. An encyclopedia summary says the site "was later interpreted as being from the bottom of the Mussentuchit Member", which may rest on Carpenter and colleagues (2008), who referred material from the Price River II Quarry. That paper has not been read; it would show whether the reinterpretation concerns the holotype's site or only the referred material. The second question is which site the holotype is from. The type-locality line equates PR-1 with the CEM site and gives the number 42EM352U, but the same paper lists "the CEM, PR-l, and PR-2 localities" as three sites (p. 212), and Kirkland and colleagues (2016) number the KEM site Utah Em419v and PR-1 Utah Em352v (p. 152, Fig. 30). If the CEM site is not PR-1, `location.locality: Price River 1` names a different site. A museum locality register, or Carpenter and colleagues (2008), would settle it.
