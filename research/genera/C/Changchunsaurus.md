# *Changchunsaurus*

## What places the *Changchunsaurus* holotype in the upper part of the Quantou Formation?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** The Chinese text of the describing paper, which gives the horizon as the upper part of the Quantou Formation in its abstract and in its locality-and-horizon line. The paper's English summary, and the redescription by Butler and colleagues (2011), give the Quantou Formation with no position in it. Governs `location.formation: Quantou` and `location.part: upper` on [*Changchunsaurus parvus*](../../../genera/C/Changchunsaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zan2005a, p. 182, abstract: the fossil comes from "purplish-red pebbly muddy sandstone of the upper part of the Quantou Formation at Shanqian, Liufangzi Town, Gongzhuling City" (translated).
- zan2005a, p. 183, locality and horizon: "Shanqian, Liufangzi Town, Gongzhuling City, Jilin Province; upper part of the Quantou Formation; Early Cretaceous" (translated).
- zan2005a, p. 192, English summary: "Locality and horizon Shanqian, Liufangzi, Gongzhuling City, Jilin Province, China; Early Cretaceous, Quantou Formation."
- butler2011a, p. 668: "Type locality and horizon. Liufangzi, near Gongzhuling City, Jilin Province, People's Republic of China … Quantou Formation, Songliao Basin, Aptian–Cenomanian".

**Ruled out.**
- *No position in the formation.* The English summary and Butler and colleagues (2011) omit the part; neither contradicts the Chinese text, which is the paper's formal entry.

**Open.** Nothing.
