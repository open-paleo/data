# *Clasmodosaurus*

## Where was Clasmodosaurus spatula named, and was it described?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** In Ameghino's 1899 *Suplemento* to the *Sinopsis geológico-paleontológica* (p. 9), with a short diagnosis but no figure. The 1899 pamphlet has not been read; the text is read in Torcelli's reprint (*Obras Completas* XII, 1921), where the Suplemento's additions are printed as footnotes. Governs `erected_in: ameghino1899b` on *Clasmodosaurus spatula*.

**Evidence.**
- ameghino1899b, Obras Completas XII, p. 703, footnote: "En los *Sauropoda* agréguese: *Clasmodosaurus spatula* n. gen., n. sp., afín del género *Ornithopsis* Seeley" ("In the Sauropoda add: Clasmodosaurus spatula n. gen., n. sp., related to the genus Ornithopsis Seeley", translated), followed by "Dientes de 5 a 6 centímetros de largo y de 9 a 12 milímetros de diámetro… La corona es más larga que la raíz" ("Teeth 5 to 6 cm long and 9 to 12 mm in diameter… The crown is longer than the root", translated), and attributed "(Del «Suplemento: Adiciones y Correcciones», página 9.)" ("from the Supplement: Additions and Corrections, page 9", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *The 1898 Segundo Censo.* The Sauropoda and Theropoda paragraphs of Ameghino (1898, pp. 288–289) do not mention *Clasmodosaurus*.
- *A name without description.* Huene writes that Ameghino "menciona el nombre tan sólo dos veces (Segundo Censo Suppl.), pero sin descripción ni reproducción" ("mentions the name only twice (Segundo Censo Suppl.), but without description or illustration", translated; huene1929a, p. 140); the reprinted footnote has a description, though no illustration.

**Open.** The month of publication of the Suplemento; neither the reprint nor any source read for this note gives it.
