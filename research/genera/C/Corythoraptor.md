# *Corythoraptor*

## Is *Corythoraptor* the adult of *Banji*?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Not adopted; *Corythoraptor* stays a valid genus. Cau (2024) coded it with *Banji* as one unit and called it a junior synonym, but no later work read here takes this up. Governs `status: valid`, `parent: Citipatiinae` and the dispute on [*Corythoraptor jacobsi*](../../../genera/C/Corythoraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2017a, Phylogenetic analysis: "Corythoraptor jacobsi and Huanansaurus ganzhouensis form one clade, and they share the following seven synapomorphies".
- cau2024a, pp. vi–vii: "Some taxonomic units included in the OSP analysis, and originally considered as distinct taxa, have been merged together on the hypothesis that they represent distinct semaphoronts of the same taxon"; "The genus Corythoraptor (Lü et al., 2017) is considered a junior synonym of Banji (Xu & Han, 2010)".
- hao2024a, p. 3: *Banji* and *Corythoraptor* listed as separate taxa.

**Ruled out.**
- *The synonymy as adopted.* It rests on one analysis, where it is a coding decision, and the later works read here treat the two genera as distinct without citing it.

**Open.** Whether a test of the growth series between the two takes it up.
