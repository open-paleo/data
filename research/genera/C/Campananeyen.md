# *Campananeyen*

## Is "Mupaleo" the name of the *Campananeyen* holotype's site?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** Not on what the describing paper says. It names the locality as Barda Atravesada de Las Campanas; "Mupaleo" appears in it only in the title of an abstract cited for another specimen. Governs `location.locality: Barda Atravesada de Las Campanas` on [*Campananeyen fragilissimus*](../../../genera/C/Campananeyen.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lerzo2025b, p. 1796: "The holotype was recovered from the Candeleros Formation of the 'Barda Atravesada de Las Campanas' locality from Villa El Chocón"; "MMCh-PV 71 was recovered in association with a small-sized rebbachisaurid specimen (MMCh-PV 41; Haluza and Canale 2011)".
- lerzo2025b, p. 1807, References: Haluza and Canale (2011), "New evidence on the anatomy, function and evolutionary constraints in sauropod pedes based on new material from Mupaleo site (Candeleros Formation. Neuquén, Patagonia, Argentina".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Mupaleo, Barda Atravesada de Las Campanas".* An earlier version of the record joined the two names as if one contained the other; no source read says so.

**Open.** Since MMCh-PV 41 was found "in association with" the holotype, the Mupaleo site of the 2011 abstract may be the holotype's site or a name within the same barda. That is an inference from the association, not a statement. Haluza and Canale (2011) or Paulina Carabajal and colleagues (2016), the paper cited for the locality, would settle it.
