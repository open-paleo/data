# *Ceratops*

## Where in the Judith River beds was the *Ceratops* holotype found?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Near the top of the Judith River beds, on a northwestern slope about 300 yards from the point of the first hogback that projects into the valley of Cow Creek from the west, about 10 miles above the creek's mouth on the Missouri River. The position is Hatcher's own account of his find, published in Hatcher and colleagues (1907); the naming paper says only "Laramie deposits". Governs `location.formation: Judith River`, `location.part: upper` and `location.locality: Cow Creek` on [*Ceratops montanus*](../../../genera/C/Ceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- marsh1888b, p. 477: "found in place, in the Laramie deposits of the Cretaceous, in Montana, by Mr. J. B. Hatcher".
- hatcher1907a, p. 100: "These remains were found by me in 1888. The horizon was near the top of the Judith River beds, and the exact locality was on the northwestern slope near the summit"; the site is near "the first hogback that projects into the valley of Cow Creek from the west", "about 10 miles above the confluence of that stream with the Missouri River".

**Ruled out.**
- *Laramie.* Marsh's term in 1888 for the beds Hatcher later calls the Judith River beds, writing about the same find; it is the name of its time, not a rival placement.

**Open.** Nothing.
