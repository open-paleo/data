# *Ceratonykus*

## Is the *Ceratonykus* holotype MPC 100/124 or MPC 100/24?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** MPC 100/124, as Alifanov and Barsbold (2009) give it. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MPC 100/124]` on [*Ceratonykus oculatus*](../../../genera/C/Ceratonykus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- alifanov2009a, p. 97: "Holotype. MPC, no. 100/124, incomplete skull including incomplete lower jaw".

**Ruled out.**
- *MPC 100/24.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give it in their theropod speed table and their list of theropods (molina-pérez2019a). It drops a digit of the designating paper's number. Jones (2026) gives "MPC 100/124" (jones2026c, p. 504).

**Open.** Nothing.
