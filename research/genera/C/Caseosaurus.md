# *Caseosaurus*

## Where in Crosby County was the *Caseosaurus* holotype found?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** The site is not known. The paper naming the species gives the type locality as Crosby County, Texas, and says the exact location is unknown, and the redescription by Baron and Williams (2018) adds no site. The record carries Texas and no locality. Governs `location.region: US-TX` and the absence of `location.locality` on [*Caseosaurus crosbyensis*](../../../genera/C/Caseosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hunt1998a, p. 530: "Type locality - Crosby County, Texas - exact location unknown."
- baron2018c, p. 130, Fig. 1: UMMP 8870 "from Tecovas Formation, Crosby County, Texas, USA".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** *The west bank of the Blanco River, in the northern breaks.* A site description given for the specimen without a source. No work read here prints it, and Hunt and colleagues' statement that the site is unknown tells against it without disproving it. Case (1927), whose figures of the ilium Hunt and colleagues reproduce, has not been read and is the likeliest place for a site description.
