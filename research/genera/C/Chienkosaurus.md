# *Chienkosaurus*

## Where does *Chienkosaurus* sit, and is its status in dispute?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** Theropoda incertae sedis, and no dispute: the block is removed. Three of the four type teeth are crocodyliform, and every treatment read since 2000 calls the genus dubious. No paper read places the remaining tooth in Tetanurae on characters; readings run from ceratosaurid to carnosaur to Theropoda indeterminate, so Theropoda is the level they share. Governs `parent: Theropoda` and the absence of a dispute on [*Chienkosaurus*](../../../genera/C/Chienkosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1983a: "three of the four dental specimens actually belong to *Hsisosuchus* while the fourth tooth is a premaxillary tooth of a carnosaur"; "The genus Chienkosaurus is now excluded and the specimen is synonymized with Szechuanosaurus."
- chure2000a: "*Chienkosaurus* is a nomen dubium and is here considered Theropoda indeterminate."
- carrano2012a, on *Szechuanosaurus campi*: "these are not diagnostic below the level of Theropoda".
- young1942a: the teeth's "possible association with the ulna and vertebræ described below which display undoubtedly a member of Ceratosauridæ".

**Ruled out.**
- *Tetanurae incertae sedis.* The earlier parent; it rests on Dong's "carnosaur" label for one tooth and on the genus being listed in a chapter on basal tetanurans, not on characters.

**Open.** Nothing.
