# *Cetiosaurus*

## Which unit is the *Cetiosaurus oxoniensis* lectotype from, and where does its coordinate come from?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** The Forest Marble, immediately above the Great Oolite, at a quarry near Bletchingdon (formerly Kirtlington) Station. Upchurch and Martin (2003) note that the Bletchingdon material is often said to come from the Great Oolite and follow Phillips (1871), who recorded the bones lying above the oolite rather than in it. The coordinate is Upchurch and Martin's National Grid reference SP 483 182, converted from OSGB36 to WGS84; it falls on the south-west corner of the 100 m square the reference names (our conversion). Governs `location.formation: Forest Marble`, `location.locality: Bletchingdon Station` and `location.coordinates: [51.860302, -1.30004]` on [*Cetiosaurus oxoniensis*](../../../genera/C/Cetiosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- upchurch2003a, p. 215, Type Locality: "A quarry near Bletchingdon (old Kirtlington) Station, near Enslow Bridge, Oxon. (Fig. 1), National Grid Reference SP 483 182 (P Powell, pers. comm., 1997)."
- upchurch2003a, p. 215, Type Horizon: the specimens "are often stated as being found in the Great Oolite"; "Phillips (1871:247-248) noted, however, that the *Cetiosaurus* bones were actually found in the Forest Marble (Bathonian, Cope et al., 1980) sediments immediately above the Great Oolite."
- phillips1871a, p. 251: "The bones thus placed did not for the most part actually touch the oolite, still less were imbedded in it".

**Ruled out.**
- *Great Oolite.* The older attribution that Upchurch and Martin (2003) list, among them Owen's and Lydekker's; Phillips's record of the excavation places the bones in the beds above the oolite.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Cetiosaurus oxoniensis* lectotype from Rutland?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** No. The Rutland material is a referred skeleton, LCM G468.1968 from Great Casterton. The lectotype is from the Forest Marble near Bletchingdon Station (see the 2026-08-15 entry). Governs `location.formation: Forest Marble` on [*Cetiosaurus oxoniensis*](../../../genera/C/Cetiosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- upchurch2003a, p. 216: "Referred Material—A partial skeleton (LCM G468.1968) from Great Casterton, Rutland, U.K."

**Ruled out.**
- *Rutland Formation.* The record carried it until 2026-08-06. The only Rutland material in the sources read is the referred skeleton, so the record had paired that specimen's unit with the lectotype's locality (our reading). Weishampel and colleagues (2004) list the species from "Forest Marble Formation (Oxfordshire), "Rutland Clay" (Rutland)" (weishampel2004a, p. 263, Table 13.1), a line that covers both specimens. Jones (2026) gives "Forest Marble, United Kingdom" (jones2026b, p. 375).

**Open.** Nothing.
