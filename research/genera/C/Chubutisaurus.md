# *Chubutisaurus*

## Which member holds the *Chubutisaurus* holotype, and where is its position printed?

*2026-08-16*

**Conclusion.** The Bayo Overo Member of the Cerro Barcino Formation, at the position Salgado (1993) prints, 43°30′S, 68°20′W. Governs `location.member: Bayo Overo` and `location.coordinates: [-43.5, -68.333333]` on [*Chubutisaurus insignis*](../../../genera/C/Chubutisaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2003d, p. 488: "The Bayo Overo Member has yielded the sauropod *Chubutisaurus* (Del Corro, 1975)".
- carrano2012a, p. 257: the *Megalosaurus inexpectatus* teeth were "associated with the holotype skeleton of the sauropod *Chubutisaurus insignis* (del Corro 1975)" and "derived from the Albian–Cenomanian Bayo Overo Member of the Cerro Barcino Formation at Paso de Indios".
- salgado1993a, pp. 265–266: "Cerro Barcino Member, Cerro Barcino, al 50 km north to Los Altares, Paso de Indios (43°30'SL" on p. 265, continued on p. 266 "and 68°20'WL), Chubut Province, Argentina".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cerro Barcino as the member.* Salgado (1993, p. 265) gives "Gorro Frigio Formation (Aptian); Cerro Barcino Member", the older ranking under which the Cerro Barcino was a member. Under the current ranking the Cerro Barcino is the formation and the Bayo Overo one of its members (carrano2012a, p. 257), so Salgado's member is the formation, not a rival horizon.

**Open.** Whether a finer position is printed. Salgado's pair is to the arc-minute. The pair the record held before, [-43.403889, -68.291664], about 11 km from Salgado's, is printed by no source read; the osteology of Carballido and colleagues (2011) in the *Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology* has not been read and may be where it comes from.
