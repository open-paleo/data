# *Ceratosuchops*

## Can the *Ceratosuchops* holotype be placed more finely than the Wessex Formation?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** No. Barker and colleagues (2021) recovered the holotype premaxillae and braincase from isolated sandstone blocks on the foreshore, so the level they came from is unknown. The level the same paper gives for *Riparovenator*, between the Chilton Chine and Brighstone Sandstones, belongs to a specimen recovered in place. Governs `location.formation: Wessex` and the absence of `location.bed` on [*Ceratosuchops inferodios*](../../../genera/C/Ceratosuchops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barker2021a, p. 4, Geological context: "The *Ceratosuchops* premaxillae, braincase and referred postorbital were recovered from isolated sandstone blocks found on the foreshore, and as such their precise original stratigraphic location remains uncertain."
- barker2021a, p. 3, Geological context: the *Riparovenator* braincase and caudal series "were recovered in situ", "likely from an unnamed layer between the Brighstone and Chilton Chine Sandstones"; p. 5, its type horizon: "Between the Chilton Chine and Brighstone Sandstones, Wessex Fm."

**Ruled out.**
- *The* Riparovenator *level.* The two taxa share a locality, but only *Riparovenator* was found in place; the *Ceratosuchops* type horizon is the Wessex Formation (barker2021a, p. 4).

**Open.** Nothing.
