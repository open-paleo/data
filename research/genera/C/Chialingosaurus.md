# *Chialingosaurus*

## Where was the *Chialingosaurus* holotype found?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Taipingtsai, in Quxian (Chuhsien) County, Sichuan, about 200 km north of Chongqing. Young (1959) gives the site and Dong and colleagues (1983), who re-excavated it, give the same place. Governs `location.locality: Taipingtsai` on [*Chialingosaurus kuani*](../../../genera/C/Chialingosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- young1959a, p. 1: "Horizon and locality: Middle or lowest Upper Jurassic from Taipingtsai, Pinganhsiang, Yunghsing, Chuhsien, some 200 kilometers N. of Chungking, Szechuan."
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 95: the specimens were collected in 1957 "from Taipingzhai in the village of Pinganxiang, Yongxing District, Quxian Co."; "Locality and stratigraphic position: Pingan Commune, Quxian Co.; early Late Jurassic Upper Shaximiao Fm., Chungking Group."

**Ruled out.**
- *Zigong.* Maidment and Wei (2006) give "Lower Upper Jurassic Upper Shaximiao Formation, Chungking Group, Zigong, Sichuan Province (Dong, Zhou & Zhang, 1983)" (maidment2006a, p. 629). The source they cite gives Quxian, not Zigong. Their *Tuojiangosaurus* entry gives the same line, "Lower Upper Jurassic Upper Shaximiao Formation, Chungking group, Zigong, Sichuan Province" (p. 622), so the *Chialingosaurus* line looks carried over from it (our reading).

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is *Chialingosaurus kuani* a nomen dubium?

*2026-10-08*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Maidment and Wei (2006) found the holotype to be a juvenile with no autapomorphies, and later work maintains the verdict. Governs `status: nomen dubium` and the absence of `diagnostic_features` on [*Chialingosaurus kuani*](../../../genera/C/Chialingosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maidment2006a, Systematic palaeontology, *Chialingosaurus*, Remarks: "None of the diagnostic characters listed by Young (1959) or Dong, Zhou & Zhang (1983) are autapomorphic ... Therefore 'Chialingosaurus kuani' should be considered a nomen dubium unless the missing holotype material is relocated."
- maidment2008a: Maidment and Wei "were unable to identify any autapomorphies or unique character combinations for Chialingosaurus ... This conclusion is maintained herein."
- raven2023a, Introduction, lists *Chialingosaurus* among taxa "now considered taxonomically invalid".

**Ruled out.**
- *Valid.* Young (1959) and Dong and colleagues (1983) diagnosed it, and Galton and Upchurch (2004) list it in their table of stegosaur genera without arguing its validity; all predate the reassessment.

**Open.** Part of the holotype (IVPP V2300) could not be located in 2006, and Maidment and Wei make their verdict conditional on it staying missing. If the material is found, the question reopens.
