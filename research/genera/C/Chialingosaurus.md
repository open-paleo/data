# *Chialingosaurus*

## Where was the *Chialingosaurus* holotype found?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** Taipingtsai, in Quxian (Chuhsien) County, Sichuan, about 200 km north of Chongqing. Young (1959) gives the site and Dong and colleagues (1983), who re-excavated it, give the same place. Governs `location.locality: Taipingtsai` on [*Chialingosaurus kuani*](../../../genera/C/Chialingosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- young1959a, p. 1: "Horizon and locality: Middle or lowest Upper Jurassic from Taipingtsai, Pinganhsiang, Yunghsing, Chuhsien, some 200 kilometers N. of Chungking, Szechuan."
- dong1983a, English translation, p. 95: the specimens were collected in 1957 "from Taipingzhai in the village of Pinganxiang, Yongxing District, Quxian Co."; "Locality and stratigraphic position: Pingan Commune, Quxian Co.; early Late Jurassic Upper Shaximiao Fm., Chungking Group."

**Ruled out.**
- *Zigong.* Maidment and Wei (2006) give "Lower Upper Jurassic Upper Shaximiao Formation, Chungking Group, Zigong, Sichuan Province (Dong, Zhou & Zhang, 1983)" (maidment2006a, p. 629). The source they cite gives Quxian, not Zigong. Their *Tuojiangosaurus* entry gives the same line, "Lower Upper Jurassic Upper Shaximiao Formation, Chungking group, Zigong, Sichuan Province" (p. 622), so the *Chialingosaurus* line looks carried over from it (our reading).

**Open.** Nothing.
