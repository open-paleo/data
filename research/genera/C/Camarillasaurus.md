# *Camarillasaurus*

## Which catalogue numbers make up the *Camarillasaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** The 42 numbers that Sánchez-Hernández and Benton (2014) list element by element: MPG-KPC1 to 23, 30 to 39 and 43 to 51, each recorded on its own. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` (MPG-KPC1 … MPG-KPC51, 42 entries) on [*Camarillasaurus cirugedae*](../../../genera/C/Camarillasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sánchez-hernández2014a, p. 583: "In detail, the holotype consists of the apical part of a lateral tooth (MPG-KPC43), two almost complete sternal plates (MPG-KPC1, 2) and isolated remains of sternal plates (MPG-KPC34–38) … some rib fragments (MPG-KPC45 and 47–50) … a broken portion of a lateral side of a centrum with prezygapophysis (MPG-KPC51)".

**Ruled out.**
- *MPG-KPC1-46.* The same paper's opening holotype line gives "Specimen MPG-KPC1-46" (sánchez-hernández2014a, p. 583). Its own list contradicts that line: KPC47 to 51 are listed, while KPC24 to 29 and 40 to 42 are not (see [methods](../../methods.md) on a slip that its own document contradicts). The record carried the range until 2026-08-01.
- *MPG-KPC1 through 51.* Jones (2026) gives this range (jones2026c, p. 497). It takes in the nine numbers that the list does not name.

**Open.** The list ends with "several isolated broken fragments of bones", which are given no numbers. Whether KPC24 to 29 and 40 to 42 exist as other material is not stated.
