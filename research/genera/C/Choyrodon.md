# *Choyrodon*

## Which Khuren Dukh site is the *Choyrodon* holotype from?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** The Khuren Dukh locality, with no numbered site. The describing paper names only "the Khuren Dukh locality" and keeps the exact position on file at the museum. In its locality figure the holotype's site is number 2. Governs `location.locality: Khuren Dukh` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Choyrodon barsboldi*](../../../genera/C/Choyrodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gates2018a, p. 4, Locality, horizon, and geologic age: "The quarry from which the holotype specimen derives is located … at the Khuren Dukh locality of the Lower Member of the Khuren Dukh Formation"; "Exact locality information is on file at the MPC."
- gates2018a, p. 3, Fig. 1 caption: "1-site for Choyrodon barsboldi MPC-D100/800; 2-site for Choyrodon barsboldi MPC-D100/801; 3-site for Choyrodon barsboldi MPC-D100/803; 4-site for Harpimimus okladnikovi". The holotype is MPC-D 100/801 (p. 2, Holotype).

**Ruled out.**
- *"Khuren Dukh I".* Gates and colleagues (2018) never use the name. Read as site 1 of their Fig. 1, it is where the referred specimen MPC-D 100/800 was found, not the holotype.

**Open.** Nothing.
