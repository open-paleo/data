# *Claosaurus*

## What member and stages does *Claosaurus* take?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** The Smoky Hill Chalk Member of the Niobrara Chalk, Coniacian to Campanian. Both datings read are about the holotype's site: Carpenter and colleagues (1995) date the chalk along the Smoky Hill River in Logan County near the Santonian–Campanian boundary, and Prieto-Márquez (2011) gives the holotype's beds as late Coniacian. The stage joins them. Governs `location.member: Smoky Hill Chalk` and `stage: [Coniacian, Santonian, Campanian]` on [*Claosaurus agilis*](../../../genera/C/Claosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- carpenter1995a: YPM 1190 "was collected by Marsh on July 30, 1871, on the north side of Smoky Hill River, 26 miles east of Fort Wallace (now Wallace), Kansas"; "in the area of Smoky Hill River in Logan County, it is near the Santonian–Campanian boundary (Stewart, 1990)".
- prieto-márquez2011b: "The holotype and only known exemplar of *Claosaurus agilis* was collected from the Smoky Hill Chalk Member of the Niobrara Chalk Formation (near Smoky Hill River, Logan County, Kansas, USA) (Carpenter *et al*. 1995); these beds are Late Coniacian in age according to Everhart and Ewell (2006)."

**Ruled out.**
- *Coniacian–Santonian.* The record's earlier value, which drops the Campanian side of the boundary dating.

**Open.** The two datings differ by most of the Santonian; Stewart (1990) and Everhart and Ewell (2006) have not been read.
