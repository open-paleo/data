# *Carcharodontosaurus*

## Is the *Carcharodontosaurus saharicus* neotype SGM-Din 1 or UCRC PV12?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** SGM-Din 1, the number Brusatte and Sereno (2007) give when they designate the cranium as the neotype. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: SGM-Din 1` and `location.locality: east of the oasis of Er Remlia` on [*Carcharodontosaurus saharicus*](../../../genera/C/Carcharodontosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brusatte2007a, p. 904, Neotype: "SGM-Din 1, partial cranium lacking the premaxillae, squamosals, quadratojugals, quadrates, and most of the palate"; Locality and Horizon: "The neotype was found east of the oasis of Er Remlia, eastern Morocco". SGM is listed as the "Ministére de l'Energie et des Mines, Rabat".
- ibrahim2020a, Assemblage character: "we review and support the evidence used to designate a well-preserved skull (UCRC PV12) as the neotype of *Carcharodontosaurus saharicus* … (Stromer 1915, Brusatte and Sereno 2007)".

**Ruled out.**
- *UCRC PV12.* Ibrahim and colleagues (2020) give this University of Chicago Research Collection number while citing the designation by Brusatte and Sereno, and state no renumbering or transfer. The designating paper's number stands.

**Open.** What UCRC PV12 refers to (a cast, a field or collection number for the same cranium) is not stated in either paper. Ibrahim and colleagues also map "locality of the neotype of *Carcharodontosaurus saharicus* (Sereno et al. 1996)" as a point of its own (ibrahim2020a, Fig. 9, locality 11), separate from Iferda N'Ahouar, while glossing Sereno and colleagues' "Er Remlia" as the village nearest Iferda N'Ahouar (Table 4); a site name for the neotype would come from that point, which the text does not name.

## Is *Carcharodontosaurus saharicus* known from the Echkar Formation of Niger?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. The Echkar material is the type of *C. iguidensis*. Brusatte and Sereno (2007) erect that species in the same paper that designates the *C. saharicus* neotype, which comes from the Kem Kem beds of Morocco. Governs `location.country: MA` on [*Carcharodontosaurus saharicus*](../../../genera/C/Carcharodontosaurus.yml), and `location.country: NE` and `location.formation: Echkar` on *C. iguidensis* in the same record.

**Evidence.**
- brusatte2007a, p. 904, *C. saharicus*, Locality and Horizon: "The neotype was found east of the oasis of Er Remlia, eastern Morocco; the neotype and referred material are from the upper unit of the Kem Kem beds (Cenomanian)".
- brusatte2007a, p. 904, *C. iguidensis*, Locality and Horizon: "Echkar Formation (Cenomanian), west of the oasis In Abangharit in an area known as Iguidi, Republic of Niger".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Echkar Fm., Niger" for* C. saharicus. Jones (2026) gives this occurrence for the type species, beside "UCRC PV12—partial skull" (jones2026c, p. 499). The occurrence belongs to *C. iguidensis*. The number is ruled out in the 2026-08-14 entry.

**Open.** Nothing.
