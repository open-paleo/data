# *Crichtonpelta*

## Is the coordinate Arbour and Currie print for *Crichtonpelta* the holotype's?

*2026-08-17*

**Conclusion.** No. Arbour and Currie (2015) give the *Crichtonpelta benxiensis* holotype locality as 120°42'49" E, 41°42'40" N, which is to the arcsecond the point Dong (2002) measured by GPS at the Shuangmiao fossil site of *Crichtonsaurus bohlini*. Lü and colleagues (2007), who describe the specimen, give only Beipiao and refer the reader to Dong for the locality map, and no source places BXGMV0012 at Dong's site. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` and `location.locality` on [*Crichtonpelta benxiensis*](../../../genera/C/Crichtonpelta.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2007b, p. 883: "Type locality and horizon: Beipiao, Liaoning Province; Sunjiawan Formation (Cenomanian - Turonian) (The locality map and other information on the age of the formation see Dong (2002))".
- dong2002a, p. 277: with Chen Pi-ji, "using GPS determined the position of the fossil locality to be at 120°42′49″ E, 41°42′40″ N" (translated), the locality from which the *Crichtonsaurus* mandible was collected.
- arbour2015a, p. 21: "Beipiao Basin, Liaoning, China, holotype locality is 120°42'49'' longitude, 41°42'40'' latitude"; p. 40, for *C. bohlini*, the same figures introduced as "?holotype locality".

**Ruled out.**
- *Arbour and Currie's pair as the benxiensis site.* It reproduces another specimen's measurement, and the paper that describes the benxiensis holotype names no site.
- *Locality "Beipiao ankylosaur site", or Shuangmiao village.* No source prints the first, and the second is Dong's locality for the *Crichtonsaurus* holotype.

**Open.** The benxiensis quarry. Arbour and Currie place the referred BXGMV0012-1 "in the same quarry as the holotype" (p. 21) without naming it.
