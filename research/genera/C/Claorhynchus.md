# *Claorhynchus*

## Where is the Claorhynchus trihedrus holotype (AMNH 3978) from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** No locality is established. Cope gave only "Laramie" in the broad sense of his day; later localities are guesses. Governs the empty `location` fields beyond country on *Claorhynchus trihedrus*.

**Evidence.**
- cope1892a, p. 757: the account, in a "Fourth Note on the Dinosauria of the Laramie", names no locality.
- hatcher1902a, p. 382: "(locality and horizon not given)"; hatcher1907a, p. 114: "No locality or definite horizon other than Laramie was given by Cope", and "It was presumably from South Dakota."

**Ruled out.**
- *Judith River, Montana.* Lull (1942) gives "Horizon: ?Judith River, Cretaceous", "Locality: Montana" (lull1942a, p. 214); White (1973) drops the query and adds "Fergus County, Judith River" (white1973a, p. 127). Neither cites a source.
- *Laramie Formation, Colorado.* Jones (2026) gives "Location: Colorado, USA … Occurrence: Laramie Fm. (?)" (jones2026a, p. 385), without a source.

**Open.** Whether the specimen came from the same area as *Manospondylus*. Cope found it "with the fragments of a massive supratemporal bone" and another supratemporal "In the same neighborhood, but several hundred yards distant" from the *Manospondylus* vertebrae (p. 757); linking these, and placing that area in South Dakota, is an inference. Brett-Surman (1989) identifies the material as "most likely the lateral edge of the squamosal of Triceratops" (brett-surman1989a, p. 81).
