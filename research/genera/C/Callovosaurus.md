# *Callovosaurus*

## Is the *Callovosaurus leedsi* holotype BMNH R1993 or R1933?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** R1993, the number Galton (1980) gives the holotype femur each time he cites it when he erects *Callovosaurus*. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [BMNH R1993]` on [*Callovosaurus leedsi*](../../../genera/C/Callovosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1980a, p. 76: "described a left femur (Figs. 2, 3A–F, BMNH R1993) from Fletton"; Fig. 2 caption: "*Callovosaurus leedsi* (Lydekker) n. gen., holotype left femur BMNH R1993".

**Ruled out.**
- *R1933.* Jones (2026) gives "NHMUK R1933—femur (Galton, 1980)" (jones2026a, p. 404), citing the paper that prints 1993. The two middle digits are transposed (our reading).

**Open.** Nothing.
