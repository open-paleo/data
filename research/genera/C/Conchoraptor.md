# *Conchoraptor*

## Is the *Conchoraptor* holotype from the Baruungoyot Formation or the "Red Beds of Hermiin Tsav"?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Baruungoyot Formation, at Hermiin Tsav, as Funston and colleagues (2018) give it. "Red Beds of Hermiin Tsav" names the beds at the type locality, which the literature treats as part of, or equivalent to, the Baruungoyot rather than as a formation of their own (see [Baruungoyot](../../stratigraphy/b/baruungoyot.md)). Governs `location.formation: Baruungoyot` on [*Conchoraptor gracilis*](../../../genera/C/Conchoraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- funston2018a, p. 107, *Conchoraptor gracilis*, Holotype: "MPC-D 100/20; nearly complete skull, partial mandible, and hand; Baruungoyot Formation, Hermiin Tsav, Mongolia."

**Ruled out.**
- *Red Beds of Hermiin Tsav.* Weishampel and colleagues (2004) list the species from "Red Beds of Hermiin Tsav (Ömnögov')" (weishampel2004a, p. 167, Table 8.1). This is the informal "Khermeen Tsav formation" that the Baruungoyot note rules out as a separate unit.
- *Barun Goyot as another formation.* Jones (2026) writes "Barun Goyot Fm." (jones2026c, p. 507), the same formation in the older transcription.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Conchoraptor* holotype MPC-D 100/20 or IGM 100/20?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** MPC-D 100/20, as the redescription gives it. IGM is the institution's former code, which the registry keeps as an alias of MPC. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MPC-D 100/20]` on [*Conchoraptor gracilis*](../../../genera/C/Conchoraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- funston2018a, p. 107, Holotype: "MPC-D 100/20; nearly complete skull, partial mandible, and hand".

**Ruled out.**
- *IGM 100/20.* Jones (2026) gives "IGM 100/20—skull and partial jaw (Barsbold, 1986)" (jones2026c, p. 507). It is the same number under the superseded code, which the redescription does not use.
- *MPC-D102/03.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give it in their theropod speed table (molina-pérez2019a). It is another specimen.

**Open.** Nothing.
