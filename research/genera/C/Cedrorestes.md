# *Cedrorestes*

## Is the *Cedrorestes* holotype from the Yellow Cat Member or the Poison Strip Sandstone?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** The Poison Strip Sandstone Member, following Kirkland and Madsen (2007), McDonald and colleagues (2010) and Kirkland and colleagues (2016). The describing paper places Dave's Camp Site near the top of the Yellow Cat Member, immediately below the Poison Strip. None of the later passages read gives a reason for the change. Governs `location.member: Poison Strip Sandstone` on [*Cedrorestes crichtoni*](../../../genera/C/Cedrorestes.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilpin2007a, p. 81: the specimen "was embedded in a light green, sandy mudstone near the top of the Yellow Cat Member and immediately below the Poison Strip Sandstone Member"; p. 82, Type Locality: "Dave's Camp Site, Yellow Cat Member of the Cedar Mountain Formation, Utah".
- kirkland2007a, p. 12: "Gilpen and others (2006) described the iguanodont *Cedroestes crichtoni* from the Poison Strip Sandstone".
- mcdonald2010b, p. 14, Reassessment of Cedrorestes crichtoni: "DMNH 47994 coming from the Poison Strip Member [20] (originally reported as coming from the upper Yellow Cat [14])".
- kirkland2016a, p. 142: "Iguanodontids are relatively common in the Poison Strip Member"; *Cedrorestes* came "from nearly the same stratigraphic level about 1 km to the northwest" as *Planicoxa venenica*.

**Ruled out.**
- *Yellow Cat Member.* The describing paper's placement, which McDonald and colleagues (2010) call the original report and replace with the Poison Strip. The two readings are separated only by a contact: Gilpin and colleagues put the bone just below the sandstone.

**Open.** What moved the specimen across the contact. Kirkland and Madsen (2007) state the Poison Strip placement without discussing it; a measured section through Dave's Camp Site would settle it.
