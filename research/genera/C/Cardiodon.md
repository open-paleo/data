# *Cardiodon*

## Is BMNH R1527 the *Cardiodon rugulosus* type?

*2026-08-14*

**Conclusion.** No. The type tooth from the Forest Marble of Bradford-on-Avon is lost and has no catalogue number; BMNH R1527 is a second tooth from the Great Oolite near Cirencester that Lydekker (1890) referred to the genus. Governs the absence of `type_specimen.specimen_id`, and `location.formation: Forest Marble` and `location.locality: Bradford-on-Avon`, on [*Cardiodon rugulosus*](../../../genera/C/Cardiodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- upchurch2003a, p. 214: *Cardiodon* "was named, without species, on the basis of a tooth from the Middle Jurassic Forest Marble of Bradford-On-Avon, Wiltshire"; "The whereabouts of this type specimen is not known. A second and apparently similar tooth crown (BMNH R1527), came from the Great Oolite near Cirencester, Gloucestershire, and was referred to *Cardiodon* by Lydekker (1890:236)."

**Ruled out.**
- *BMNH R1527 as the type.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi list "*Cardiodon rugolosus* BMNH R1527" in their sauropod tables, with body-size estimates (molina-pérez2020a, *Cardiodon rugolosus* table rows). A compilation's specimen column, and the number belongs to Lydekker's referred tooth from another locality and unit.
- *An Early Jurassic age.* Jones (2026) gives "197 MYA (Middle Jurassic)" with "Occurrence: Forest Marble" (jones2026b, *Cardiodon rugulosus* entry). The number is Early Jurassic and contradicts the entry's own label and unit; the record dates the Forest Marble Bathonian.

**Open.** Nothing.
