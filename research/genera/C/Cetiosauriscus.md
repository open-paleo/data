# *Cetiosauriscus*

## Which Peterborough brick pit did the *Cetiosauriscus* holotype come from?

*2026-08-15*

**Conclusion.** No pit is named in the works read. Woodward (1905), describing the skeleton, gives the Oxford Clay "near Peterborough"; Charig (1980), naming the species on it, gives "the Oxford Clay of Peterborough, Cambridgeshire"; Tschopp and colleagues (2015) give "Peterborough brick-pit", which the record takes as its locality. Governs `location.locality: Peterborough brick-pit` on [*Cetiosauriscus stewarti*](../../../genera/C/Cetiosauriscus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- woodward1905a, p. 232: a skeleton "disinterred with great skill by Mr. Alfred N. Leeds from the Oxford Clay near Peterborough".
- charig1980a, p. 232, Fig. 13.1: "Original partial skeleton R.3078 in British Museum (Natural History); from the Oxford Clay of Peterborough, Cambridgeshire."
- tschopp2015a, p. 7, Table 2: *Cetiosauriscus stewarti*, NHMUK R.3078, "Peterborough brick-pit, England".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** *The New Peterborough Brick Company's No. 1 pit.* An encyclopedia summary says the skeleton "possibly stems from NPBCL pit No. 1", attributing the discovery account to Noè, Liston and Chapman (2010). That work has not been read; it would show whether the pit is recorded for the find or inferred from Leeds's collecting.
