# *Coahuilasaurus*

## Where is the *Coahuilasaurus* holotype from?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** Presa San Antonio, at the position the describing paper prints for IGM 6685, 25°27′33″ N, 101°42′18″ W, converted to 25.459167, −101.705. Governs `location.locality: Presa San Antonio` and `location.coordinates` on [*Coahuilasaurus lipani*](../../../genera/C/Coahuilasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- longrich2024b, Locality and Horizon: "IGM 6685 comes from Presa San Antonio (Figure 2) in the Parras Basin, near the SPA-88-9 quarry (*Latirhinus* type locality); 25◦27′33′′ N and 101◦42′18′′ W (Serrano-Brañas, 2006; pers. com. Hernández-Rivera)."

**Ruled out.**
- *"1 km north of Presa de San Antonio".* The bearing is Kirkland and colleagues' (2006), which the record does not cite.

**Open.** Whether the pair marks the IGM 6685 site or the nearby SPA-88-9 quarry the same sentence names; it differs from the pair the [*Latirhinus*](../L/Latirhinus.md) describing paper prints for that quarry. By our arithmetic it also lies about 2 km south of the town position that note uses. A source tying the pair to one excavation would settle it.
