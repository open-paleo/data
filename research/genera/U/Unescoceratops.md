# *Unescoceratops*

## Is the *Unescoceratops* holotype from the lower or the middle Dinosaur Park Formation?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** The middle. Ryan and colleagues (2012) place Bonebed 55 about 40 m above the base of the Dinosaur Park Formation and call that the middle of the formation, and Ryan and colleagues (2023) repeat it. Arbour and Evans (2019) write "lower Dinosaur Park Formation" in a summary sentence that cites Ryan and colleagues (2012) as its source, so it relays that paper rather than dating the bed again. Governs `location.part: middle` on [*Unescoceratops koppelhusae*](../../../genera/U/Unescoceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- ryan2012b, p. 70, Horizon and locality: "Bonebed 55 ... in middle of the Dinosaur Park Formation ..., approximately 40 m above the contact with the Oldman Formation."
- ryan2012b, p. 79: "can be placed stratigraphically in the middle of the Dinosaur Park Formation in Dinosaur Provincial Park (DPP), and occurs in the lower portion of the Dinosaur Park faunal zone 2".
- ryan2023a, Introduction: "Unescoceratops is known from the Campanian middle Dinosaur Park Formation (DP Fm) from the holotype dentary (TMP 1995.012.0006) collected from Bonebed 55".
- arbour2019a, p. 16, Stratigraphic and palaeobiogeographic implications: "Unescoceratops from the lower Dinosaur Park Formation (Chinnery, 2004; Chinnery & Horner, 2007; Ryan et al., 2012)".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`.* Arbour and Evans (2019) give it in a list of Campanian leptoceratopsids and cite the describing paper, which says middle. The describing paper's "lower portion" refers to faunal zone 2, not to the formation; that this is where "lower" came from is an inference.

**Open.** Nothing.
