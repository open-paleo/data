# *Unenlagia*

## Is the *Unenlagia comahuensis* type from the Río Neuquén or the Portezuelo Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Portezuelo. The describing paper's "Río Neuquén Formation" is a unit that has since been raised to a subgroup, with its former Portezuelo member now a formation; the type locality, Sierra del Portezuelo, is in it. Governs `location.formation: Portezuelo` on [*Unenlagia comahuensis*](../../../genera/U/Unenlagia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas1997a, Locality and horizon: "Upper Cretaceous (Turonian-Coniacian), Río Neuquén Formation, Sierra del Portezuelo, Neuquén Province, Argentina."
- garrido2010a, p. 126: Cazau and Uliana (1973) "only include within it the members Portezuelo and Plottier", and Ramos (1981) takes that scheme, "elevating the hierarchy of these units from formation to subgroup and from members to formations" (translated).
- porfiri2024a, Background: "*Unenlagia comahuensis*, *Unenlagia paynemili*, *Neuquenraptor argentinus*, and *Pamparaptor micros* ... from the Turonian–Coniacian Portezuelo Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *Río Neuquén Formation.* Norell and Makovicky give "Río Neuquén Formation (Neuquén), Argentina" in *The Dinosauria* (weishampel2004a, Dromaeosauridae table). It is the older rank of the same beds, now the Río Neuquén Subgroup, which contains the Portezuelo and the Plottier; it is not a rival unit. That the specimen lies in the Portezuelo rather than the Plottier rests on Porfiri and colleagues (2024) and on the locality, not on a statement in the describing paper. Jones (2026) gives "Portezuelo Fm." (jones2026c, Dromaeosauridae table).

**Open.** Nothing.
