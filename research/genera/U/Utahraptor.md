# *Utahraptor*

## What stage is the *Utahraptor ostrommaysi* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Valanginian. The Gaston Quarry lies in the upper Yellow Cat Member, above the marker calcrete, and the dated upper Yellow Cat at Utahraptor Ridge is Valanginian. Governs `stages: [Valanginian]`, `location.member: Yellow Cat` and `location.part: upper` on [*Utahraptor ostrommaysi*](../../../genera/U/Utahraptor.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kirkland2016a, p. 125: "Another important site in the medial upper Yellow Cat Member is the Gaston Quarry from which the type specimens of the polacanthid ankylosaur *Gastonia burgei* ... and *Utahraptor ostromaysorum* ... were collected."
- joeckel2023a, p. 3: at Utahraptor Ridge the top of the prominent "calcrete" is at about 32 m; joeckel2023a, p. 12: the eruption age of 135.1 ± 0.3 Ma comes from a cryptotephra "36.35 m above the contact", and "we interpret the C-isotope record from 32.5 to 42.5 m as Valanginian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian.* Jones (2026) gives "Cedar Mt. Fm., Utah, USA, Early Cretaceous, Aptian" (jones2026c, Eudromaeosauria table), an age for the formation that does not place the type within it. The dated upper Yellow Cat is Valanginian.

**Open.** The Valanginian age is measured at Utahraptor Ridge, not at the Gaston Quarry; carrying it to the quarry assumes the marker calcrete is the same horizon at both sites, which is an inference (see [Yellow Cat](../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.md) on the member's diachrony).
