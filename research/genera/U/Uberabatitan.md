# *Uberabatitan*

## What is the catalogue number of the *Uberabatitan ribeiroi* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** There is no single number. The holotype is specimen A, whose bones each keep their own catalogue number with the suffix UrHo; the record lists all 39. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` on [*Uberabatitan ribeiroi*](../../../genera/U/Uberabatitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- salgado2008a, p. 886: "each bone was at first registered with its own catalogue number. In order to identify the different specimens, we have added an acronym following the original number; in the case of the holotype, the acronym is UrHo (Uberabatitan ribeiroi Holotype); for specimens B and C, UrB and UrC, respectively."
- salgado2008a, p. 886: "Holotype. Specimen A (CPP-UrHo): CPP-1058-UrHo, 1057- UrHo, 914-UrHo, 919-UrHo (anterior cervical vertebrae) ...".

**Ruled out.**
- *CPPLIP-912.* The record once held this single number; 912-UrHo is one bone of the 39.
- *"CPP-UrHo".* Jones (2026) gives "CPP-UrHo—partial skeleton" (jones2026b, Rinconsauria table) and Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) "CPP-UrHo Specimen A" (molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropods). It is the label for the individual, not a catalogue number.

**Open.** Nothing.
