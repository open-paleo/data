# *Udelartitan*

## Is the coordinate in the *Udelartitan* locality figure the holotype site?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** No. The coordinate Soto and colleagues (2024) print for the site, in the caption of their locality map, gives a longitude of 55°57'W, which lies east of Paysandú Department, while the same paper places the site in Paysandú Department, near Araújo and Quebracho, in its text and its type-locality line. The record carries the site name and no coordinate. Governs `location.locality: Araújo` and the absence of `location.coordinates` on [*Udelartitan celeste*](../../../genera/U/Udelartitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- soto2024a, Systematic Paleontology, Type locality and horizon: "Araújo, near Quebracho, Paysandú Department, Uruguay".
- soto2024a, Geological and Paleofaunal Setting: "The Guichón Formation crops out in northwestern Uruguay, Paysandú Department"; "The locality bearing the fossils described here is close to Araújo, Paysandú Department."
- soto2024a, Fig. 2: "Black star shows the location of the type-locality of Udelartitan celeste. Coordinates: 31° 58' 34''S, 55°57'43''W."

**Ruled out.**
- *31°58'34"S, 55°57'43"W (-31.976111, -55.961944).* Printed in the Fig. 2 caption. That the longitude falls outside Paysandú, in Tacuarembó Department, comes from the department boundaries, not from a source cited here, and was not checked against a large-scale map.
- *The same pair with the longitude read as 57°57'43"W.* This puts the point in western Paysandú, but it assumes which printed digit is wrong, and nothing in the paper says so.

**Open.** Whether the version of record corrects the caption. The paper was read as the accepted manuscript; the published article (Cretaceous Research 160, 105894) has not been compared. A corrected caption there would settle the coordinate.
