# *Yunyangosaurus*

## Where does the *Yunyangosaurus* coordinate come from?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** From the map in the describing paper's Fig. 1, where a label reading 30°52′46″ N, 108°55′27″ E is joined by a line to the fossil-site symbol. The text and the caption print no coordinate. Governs `location.coordinates: [30.879444, 108.924167]` on [*Yunyangosaurus puanensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yunyangosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dai2020a, p. 2, Fig. 1: the label "30° 52′ 46″ N 108° 55′ 27″ E" beside Puan, with a leader line to the fossil site; the caption reads "Geographical map indicating the location of the fossil site within the Yunyang County".
- dai2020a, p. 2, Locality and horizon: "Laojun Village, Puan Township, Yunyang City, Chongqing, China".

**Ruled out.**
- *30.861668, 108.917221.* The pair the record held until 2026-09-09 appears in no source read for this note, and it lies about 2 km south of the position the figure prints for the same site.

**Open.** Nothing.
