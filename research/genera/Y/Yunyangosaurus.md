# *Yunyangosaurus*

## Does Dai and colleagues' reuse of the label unseat the *Yunyangosaurus* coordinate?

*2026-10-12*

**Conclusion.** No. In the describing paper's Fig. 1 the label is joined by a line to the fossil-site symbol, which ties it to the site; Dai and colleagues (2022) print the same label in their Fig. 1C for the *Bashanosaurus* locality, whose text gives its own position about 3.7 km south. Their reuse is noted but does not override the describing paper's tie of label to site. Governs `location.coordinates: [30.879444, 108.924167]` on [*Yunyangosaurus puanensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yunyangosaurus.yml), confirming the 2026-09-09 entry.

**Evidence.**
- dai2020a, Fig. 1: the label "30°52′46″ N, 108°55′27″ E" is joined by a line to the fossil-site symbol.
- dai2022b, Fig. 1C: the same label for the *Bashanosaurus* locality; the text prints that holotype's position about 3.7 km south (our measurement).

**Ruled out.**
- *Removing the coordinate on Dai and colleagues' reuse.* It is another paper's use of the label for a different site.

**Open.** Whether the label marks the *Yunyangosaurus* quarry or Laojun Village generally; a position printed in a text for the quarry would settle it.

## Where does the *Yunyangosaurus* coordinate come from?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** From the map in the describing paper's Fig. 1, where a label reading 30°52′46″ N, 108°55′27″ E is joined by a line to the fossil-site symbol. The text and the caption print no coordinate. Governs `location.coordinates: [30.879444, 108.924167]` on [*Yunyangosaurus puanensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yunyangosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dai2020a, p. 2, Fig. 1: the label "30° 52′ 46″ N 108° 55′ 27″ E" beside Puan, with a leader line to the fossil site; the caption reads "Geographical map indicating the location of the fossil site within the Yunyang County".
- dai2020a, p. 2, Locality and horizon: "Laojun Village, Puan Township, Yunyang City, Chongqing, China".

**Ruled out.**
- *30.861668, 108.917221.* The pair the record held until 2026-09-09 appears in no source read for this note, and it lies about 2 km south of the position the figure prints for the same site.

**Open.** Nothing.
