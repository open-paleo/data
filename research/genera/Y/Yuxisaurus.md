## Which stages does the *Yuxisaurus kopchicki* holotype take?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Its unit's epoch, with no stage. Yao and colleagues' (2022) "late Sinemurian–Toarcian" for the holotype horizon is the Lufeng Formation's magnetostratigraphy, and Fonseca and colleagues' (2024) Sinemurian–Pliensbachian is an unsourced table entry. Governs the absent `stage` on [*Yuxisaurus kopchicki*](../../../genera/Y/Yuxisaurus.yml).
