# *Yulong*

## Is the *Yulong* holotype HGM 41HIII-0107 or 41HII-0107?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** HGM 41HIII-0107. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [HGM 41HIII-0107]` on [*Yulong mini*](../../../genera/Y/Yulong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2013d, p. 166, Holotype: "Henan Geological Museum HGM 41HIII-0107, an exceptionally well-preserved skeleton with a skull and lower jaws."

**Ruled out.**
- *HGM 41HII-0107.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) print it in a theropod size table (molina-pérez2019a, Theropod speed, Oviraptorosaurs table). The same book prints "HGM 41HIII-0107" in its catalogue row for the species, so the dropped I is a slip within the compilation.

**Open.** Nothing.
