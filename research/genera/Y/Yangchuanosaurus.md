# *Yangchuanosaurus*

## What is the *Yangchuanosaurus shangyouensis* holotype's number?

*2026-10-13*

**Conclusion.** CV00215. The naming paper gives no number; Dong and colleagues (1983) give CV00215 for the type of *Y. shangyouensis* and CV00216 for the separate type of *Y. magnus*, from another site. The earlier adjudication's CV00216 took the other species' number. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CV00215]` on [*Yangchuanosaurus shangyouensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yangchuanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1983a, *Yangchuanosaurus shangyuensis*: "Specimen is on display at the Chungking Museum of Natural History (specimen #CV00215)".
- dong1983a, *Y. magnus* sp. nov.: "(Chungking Museum of Natural History #CV00216)".

**Ruled out.**
- *CV00216.* It is the *Y. magnus* type.

**Open.** Nothing.

## References

- `dong1983a`: Dong, Z.; Zhou, S.; Zhang, H. (1983). [Dinosaurs from the Jurassic of Sichuan]. *Palaeontologica Sinica, New Series C, Whole Number* 162(23): 1-136.
