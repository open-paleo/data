# *Yuzhoulong*

## Does the *Yuzhoulong* record carry a coordinate?

*2026-10-10*

**Conclusion.** No. No source read prints one for the holotype locality, and the describing paper gives none. Governs the absence of `location.coordinates` on [*Yuzhoulong qurenensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yuzhoulong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dai2022a: the locality is given as "Laojun Village, Pu'an Town, Yunyang Country", with no coordinate.

**Ruled out.**
- *A coordinate without a source.* A position must trace to a source that prints it.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Yuzhoulong* holotype CLGPR V00013, or CLGRP V00013 as the describing paper prints it?

*2026-08-03*

**Conclusion.** CLGPR V00013. The describing paper prints the prefix as "CLGRP" once and gives no institutional abbreviation for it. The number belongs to the Chongqing Laboratory of Geoheritage Protection and Research, whose own papers abbreviate it CLGPR. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CLGPR V00013]` on [*Yuzhoulong qurenensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yuzhoulong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dai2022a, 2.1 Holotype: "CLGRP V00013. Partly preserved skull, 12 dorsal vertebrae, 1 sacral vertebra, 10 caudal vertebrae".
- dai2022b, p. 2, Institutional Abbreviations: "CLGPR, Chongqing Laboratory of Geoheritage Protection and Research, Chongqing Municipality, China". That paper numbers the *Bashanosaurus* specimens from the same village CLGPR V00006-1 to V00006-3.

**Ruled out.**
- *CLGRP V0013.* Jones (2026) prints "CLGRP V0013" (jones2026b, Fossil Specimens table). This keeps the describing paper's transposed letters and also drops a zero from the number.

**Open.** Nothing.
