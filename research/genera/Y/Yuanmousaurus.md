# *Yuanmousaurus*

## Where does "Banqing Houshanliangzi" come from for the *Yuanmousaurus* holotype?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** From the paper that names *Eomamenchisaurus*. The describing paper gives the locality only as Jiangyi; Lü and colleagues (2008) give Banqing Houshanliangzi of Jiangyi as the *Eomamenchisaurus* type locality and state that *Yuanmousaurus* came from the same locality. Governs `location.locality: Banqing Houshanliangzi, Jiangyi` on [*Yuanmousaurus jiangyiensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yuanmousaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2006a, p. 2: "Locality and Horizon: Jiangyi, Yuanmou County of Yunnan Province, Zhanghe Formation".
- lü2008a, p. 17: "Another sauropod dinosaur Yuanmousaurus (Lu et al., 2006) was also found at the same locality"; p. 18: "Type locality and horizon: Banqing Houshanliangzi of Jiangyi (Fig. 1), Yuanmou County of Yunnan Province".
- lü2007d, p. 1: *Yuanmousaurus* and "a primitive mamenchisaurid" were "also discovered from the same area"; p. 2, for *Yunnanosaurus youngi*: "Banqing Houshanliangzi of Jiangyi (Fig. 1)".

**Ruled out.** Nothing.

**Open.** Whether "the same locality" means the same quarry. Lü and colleagues (2007) say only "the same area", and the 2006 paper marks the site only on a map (Fig. 1). A field record of the 2000 excavation, or a paper giving the *Yuanmousaurus* quarry by name, would settle it.
