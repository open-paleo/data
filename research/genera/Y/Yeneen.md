# *Yeneen*

## Is the *Yeneen* holotype from "Cerro Overo – La Invernada", and from the same site as *Inawentu*?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** La Invernada, at a site Filippi and colleagues (2026) present as new. Cerro Overo – La Invernada is the wider area. Governs `location.locality: La Invernada` on [*Yeneen houssayi*](../../../genera/Y/Yeneen.yml).

**Evidence.**
- filippi2026a, p. 2652: "The Cerro Overo – La Invernada is an extensive area in the north of the Neuquén Province"; the two sauropods known from it are *Overosaurus* "from Cerro Overo, and *Inawentu oslatus* ... from La Invernada. In this contribution we present a new fossil site from La Invernada (Figure 1), from which three individuals of titanosaurian sauropods come from: *Yeneen houssayi*".
- filippi2026a, p. 2652, Abstract: *Overosaurus* and *Inawentu* are "coming from the Cerro Overo-La Invernada (CO-LI) area".

**Ruled out.**
- *Cerro Overo – La Invernada as the locality.* The paper's title and introduction use it for the area, though its discussion also writes "Cerro Overo – La Invernada locality yielded *Overosaurus*, *Inawentu* and *Yeneen*" (p. 2684). Recording it would merge the site of [*Overosaurus paradasorum*](../../../genera/O/Overosaurus.yml), at Cerro Overo, with the La Invernada sites.

**Open.** Whether the site has a name of its own; the paper marks it only with a star on its Fig. 1 map (p. 2653). The records of [*Inawentu*](../../../genera/I/Inawentu.yml) and [*Viavenator*](../../../genera/V/Viavenator.yml) also read La Invernada, and the shared string does not make the three one quarry. That the *Yeneen* site is distinct from theirs is an inference from "a new fossil site"; the paper does not compare the positions. A site name or a coordinate for the quarry would settle it.
