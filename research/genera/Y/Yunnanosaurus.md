# *Yunnanosaurus*

## Is Huangchiatien a real locality for the Yunnanosaurus huangi holotype, and which beds is it from?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Huangchiatien (黄家田; also Huangjiatian, Huang Jia Tian), near Lufeng, Yunnan, is attested in the papers that describe its fossils and is later called Dahuangtian. The *Y. huangi* holotype is from the dark red beds of the Lower Lufeng Series. Governs `location.locality: Huangchiatien` on *Yunnanosaurus huangi*, and supports the same locality on [*Sinosaurus triassicus*](../../../genera/S/Sinosaurus.yml) and [*Pachysuchus imperfectus*](../../../genera/P/Pachysuchus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- young1942b, p. 65: "Upper Trias from the dark red beds of the Lufeng series in the vicinity of the village Huangchiatien", with the footnote "1. 黃家田; 2. 沙灣".
- young1942b, p. 99: the specific name honors Dr. T. K. Huang, and "In addition, the specific name shows that the remains were unearthed in the vicinity of a small village called Huangchiatien, the farm of the family Huang".
- simmons1965a, p. 49: "Dark Red Beds, Lower Lufeng Series, Huangchiatien, Lufeng, Yunnan"; young1948a, p. 75: "Upper Triassic Dark Red Beds of the Lower Lufeng Series from Huangchiatien, Lufeng, Yunnan".
- luo1994a, p. 265: "the Dahuangtian locality ('Huangjiatian' of Young, 1944, and Simmons, 1965)"; barrett2012a, p. 152: "Huangchiatien (Dahuangtian in more recent publications; Luo and Wu, 1994)".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Dull purplish beds."* Young's first notice gives "Level: Dull purplish beds of the Lower Lufeng Series" (young1940a, p. 237). His full description corrects it to the dark red beds (young1942b, p. 65), and Galton and Upchurch (2004) give "the upper Dark Red Beds of the Lower Lufeng Series" (galton2004a, p. 252).
- *Huangchiatien in Guangdong.* Peyre de Fabrègues and colleagues (2015) give "Huangchiatien, near Lufeng, Guangdong Province" in a table (peyredefabrègues2015a, p. 575, Table 2); Lufeng is in Yunnan.
- *The epithet as a locality name only.* Jones (2026) glosses *Yunnanosaurus huangi* as "lizard from Huangchiatien, Yunnan" (jones2026b, p. 57). Young gives both meanings, the dedication to T. K. Huang first.

**Open.** Whether Dahuangtian is a renaming or a re-romanization of 黄家田: no source read for this note gives its characters. Zhao (2008) prints "Dahuangtian (Huangchiatien)" as one locality. The direction from Lufeng also differs: "about 4 km. NE. of the Lufeng city" (young1942b, p. 65) against "to the northwest of Lufeng" (barrett2012a, p. 152).
