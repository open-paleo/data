# *Yurgovuchia*

## What stage is the *Yurgovuchia doellingi* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Berriasian to Valanginian. The holotype comes from the Doelling's Bowl bone bed in the lower Yellow Cat Member, below the marker calcrete, and at the dated Utahraptor Ridge section the beds below the calcrete run from Berriasian into the earliest Valanginian. Governs `stages: [Berriasian, Valanginian]`, `location.member: Yellow Cat` and `location.part: lower` on [*Yurgovuchia doellingi*](../../../genera/Y/Yurgovuchia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- senter2012b, Geological Setting: "The holotype of Yurgovuchia doellingi ... are from a portion of the Doelling's Bowl bone bed (Gr 300v) that is designated as Don's Place"; "The Doelling's Bowl Dinosaur bone bed is centimeters to decimeters above an interval of distinctive chert layers" in the lower Yellow Cat Member.
- kirkland2016a, p. 125: "a multitaxic Early Cretaceous dinosaur fauna is preserved below the marker calcrete and immediately above the chert interval in the Doelling's Bowl bonebed"; "At Doelling's Bowl, the dromaeosaur *Yurgovuchia* has proven close to *Utahraptor*".
- joeckel2023a, p. 12: "we interpret the C-isotope record from 32.5 to 42.5 m as Valanginian s and the C-isotope record from 0 to 19.25 m as Berriasian", with the top of the calcrete at about 32 m (p. 3).

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian.* Jones (2026) gives "Cedar Mt. Fm., Utah, USA, Early Cretaceous, Aptian" (jones2026c, Eudromaeosauria table). The describing paper's own "about 124 Ma" for the upper member (senter2012b, Geological Setting) is a detrital-zircon reading that predates the Utahraptor Ridge dates.
- *Valanginian alone.* The record once held it. It is the age of the upper member; Doelling's Bowl is below the calcrete.

**Open.** The age is measured at Utahraptor Ridge, not at Doelling's Bowl; carrying it across assumes the calcrete is one horizon at both sites, which is an inference (see [Yellow Cat](../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.md)).
