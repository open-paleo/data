# *Yizhousaurus*

## Is *Yizhousaurus sunae* Sinemurian or Hettangian?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Sinemurian. The holotype comes from the uppermost bed of the upper member of the Lufeng Formation, and the one age argument for that member's fauna favors the Sinemurian. Governs `stage: [Sinemurian]` on [*Yizhousaurus sunae*](../../../genera/Y/Yizhousaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhang2018a, Methods and Materials: LFGT-ZLJ0033 was excavated "from the uppermost bed of the Zhangjiaao Member (Dark Red Beds) Lufeng Formation near the Duwafang Village".
- luo1994a, p. 268, Faunal correlations: "we argue that the upper faunal assemblage is Early Jurassic in age, most probably Sinemurian, and definitely no older than Hettangian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Hettangian.* Jones (2026) gives "Lufeng Fm., China, Early Jurassic, Hettangian" (jones2026b, Fossil Specimens table). That row dates the formation and does not discuss the horizon. The Hettangian belongs to the base of the Lufeng (see [Lufeng](../../stratigraphy/l/lufeng.md)), and this specimen comes from the top of its upper member.

**Open.** No numerical date applies to the bed. Luo and Wu's "most probably Sinemurian" is a faunal correlation for the whole upper member, which the registry dates Hettangian–Sinemurian. Under the rule on deciding a stage from statements about the horizon, a member-wide statement does not narrow the type to the Sinemurian. A dated horizon in the uppermost Zhangjia'ao Member would settle it.
