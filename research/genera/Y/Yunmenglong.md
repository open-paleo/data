# *Yunmenglong*

## Is the *Yunmenglong* holotype 41HIII-0006 or KLR-07-50?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** 41HIII-0006, the Henan Geological Museum registration number. KLR-07-50 is the field number the describing paper gives for the same bones. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [41HIII-0006]` on [*Yunmenglong ruyangensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yunmenglong.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2013b, Systematic paleontology, Holotype: "one complete right femur (field no. KLR-07-50) … The material is housed in the Henan Geological Museum under registration number 41HIII-0006."

**Ruled out.**
- *KLR-07-50.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2020) give it as the specimen number (molina-pérez2020a, List of Sauropods, row for "*Yunmenglong nuyangensis*"), noting it is "Also identified with the catalog number 41HIII-0006". It is the field number, not a catalogue number.

**Open.** Nothing.
