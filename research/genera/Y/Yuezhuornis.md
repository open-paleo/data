# *Yuezhuornis*

## Does the *Yuezhuornis* correlation reach the Tithonian?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** Yes: Kimmeridgian to Tithonian. This supersedes the entry below, which read the correlation as the *Fujianvenator* layers alone. The describing paper names the layers of all three Zhenghe holotypes, and *Zhengheornis* lies in layer six, above the highest dated tuffite (149.9 ± 1.1 Ma); the only age above it is the 148 ± 1.9 Ma ignimbrite over the fossil-bearing sediments, which is Tithonian. The correlated interval therefore spans the stage boundary (base Tithonian 149.2 Ma, ICS chart v2024/12). The paper gives no layer for *Yuezhuornis* itself, so the stage cannot be narrowed within it. Governs `stage: [Kimmeridgian, Tithonian]` on [*Yuezhuornis yangyuanensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yuezhuornis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2026b, Methods, Provenance of IVPP V34427: the horizon "corresponds to the layers that yielded the holotype specimens of *Fujianvenator*, *Baminornis* and *Zhengheornis*".
- wang2026a, Materials and Methods: *Zhengheornis* was "preserved in 'layer six,' above the horizon ('layer two') that preserves the holotypes of *Fujianvenator* and *Baminornis*".
- xu2023a, Fig. 4a and Methods: the 149.9 ± 1.1 Ma tuffite lies below layer six, and "Concordant zircon U-Pb ages of 150 ± 1.8 Ma (Bdx03) and 148 ± 1.9 Ma (Bdx04) were obtained from the ignimbrite samples" that "underlie (Bdx03) and overlie (Bdx04) the fossil-bearing sediments". See [*Zhengheornis*](../Z/Zhengheornis.md).

**Ruled out.**
- *Kimmeridgian alone.* It holds only if the correlation means the *Fujianvenator* layers, which the paper does not say.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Is the *Yuezhuornis* holotype Tithonian?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** No, Kimmeridgian. The describing paper correlates the horizon with the lower Nanyuan layers that yielded *Fujianvenator*, and the dates for those layers are Kimmeridgian on the current chart. Governs `stage: [Kimmeridgian]` and `location.part: lower` on [*Yuezhuornis yangyuanensis*](../../../genera/Y/Yuezhuornis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2026b, Methods, Provenance of IVPP V34427: "Our stratigraphic survey indicates that the *Yuezhuornis*-bearing horizon belongs to the lower part of the Nanyuan Formation and it corresponds to the layers that yielded the holotype specimens of *Fujianvenator*, *Baminornis* and *Zhengheornis* (see ref. 9 for detailed geological information)." Ref. 9 is xu2023a.
- xu2023a gives the fossil-bearing layers "concordant zircon U-Pb ages of 150.2 ± 1.1 Ma (PM702-1) and 149.9 ± 1.1 Ma (PM701-4)", both Kimmeridgian at their central values against the Tithonian base of 149.2 Ma (see [Morrison Tithonian labels](../../topics/morrison-tithonian-labels.md) and [*Fujianvenator*](../F/Fujianvenator.md)).

**Ruled out.**
- *Tithonian.* The describing paper gives "Upper Jurassic, Nanyuan Formation (Tithonian stage)" (xu2026b, p. 690), citing xu2023a for it. It relays the 2023 label and adds no date of its own, and that label was already ruled out for these layers.

**Open.** Nothing.
