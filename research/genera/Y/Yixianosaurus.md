# *Yixianosaurus*

## Is the *Yixianosaurus* holotype from the Dawangzhangzi Bed?

*2026-09-09*

**Conclusion.** The lower part of the Yixian Formation, with no member, as the describing paper gives it. Governs `location.part: lower` and the absence of `location.member` on [*Yixianosaurus longimanus*](../../../genera/Y/Yixianosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- xu2003a, p. 196: "Horizon and locality Lower part of Yixian Formation, Wangjiagou, Yixian County, western Liaoning"; the introduction calls Wangjiagou "a locality that is geographically close to the famous feathered dinosaur site of Sihetun in Beipiao".
- qiu2019a, p. 2, Locality and horizon: *Xingtianosaurus* is from "Wangjiagou, Yixian County, Liaoning Province. The Dakangpu Bed (same horizon to Dawangzhangzi Bed) of Yixian Formation"; p. 3, Fig. 2, puts the Dawangzhangzi Bed above the Lujiatun and Jianshangou beds.
- wang2001a, p. 1672: "The Dakangpu Bed is the same as the Dawangzhangzi Bed of the middle Yixian Formation", a statement relayed from Wang and colleagues (2000); the paper's own horizon line calls the two "comparable" (see [Dawangzhangzi](../../stratigraphy/d/dawangzhangzi.md)).

**Ruled out.** Nothing. The one reading weighed, the Dawangzhangzi Bed, is unresolved (see Open).

**Open.** Whether the holotype is from the Dakangpu (Dawangzhangzi) Bed. No source read gives that bed for this specimen, and Xu and Wang (2003) give the lower part of the formation. But Qiu and colleagues (2019) place another theropod from Wangjiagou in the Dakangpu Bed, which Wang and Xu (2001) put in the middle Yixian. None of the works cited here says whether Wangjiagou exposes more than one bed. Dececchi and colleagues (2012), whose paper on the holotype has not been read, or a measured section at Wangjiagou placing IVPP V12638, would settle it.
