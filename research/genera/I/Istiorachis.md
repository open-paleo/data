# *Istiorachis*

## What position do the describers print for the *Istiorachis* excavation?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** 50.63354, −1.40654. Lockwood and colleagues (2025) print it only in the caption of their locality map, for the excavation of MIWG 6643; their Location & horizon paragraph gives no coordinate. Governs `location.coordinates` on [*Istiorachis macarthurae*](../../../genera/I/Istiorachis.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lockwood2025a, p. 4, Fig. 1: "C, enlarged area showing the site of the excavation of MIWG 6643 in the Black Band (arrowed) (50.63354 N, 1.40654 W)".
- lockwood2025a, p. 5, Location & horizon: the Black Band crops out "c. 100 m east of Grange Chine"; "The site is a c. 1.5-m-thick bed that has occasionally yielded dinosaur remains, including IWCMS 1997.550, the holotype specimen of the tyrannosauroid theropod Eotyrannus lengi".

**Ruled out.**
- *50.630554, −1.404167.* No paper read prints this pair. It lies about 370 m from the caption's position, converts to whole seconds (50°37′50″ N, 1°24′15″ W) (our calculations), and was also stored for [*Eotyrannus lengi*](../../../genera/E/Eotyrannus.yml), whose holotype came from the same bed.

**Open.** Whether the caption's position also marks where the *Eotyrannus* holotype was dug. Lockwood and colleagues tie *Eotyrannus* to the bed, not to the MIWG 6643 excavation, and Naish and Cau (2022) place it "approximately 12 m above beach level near Grange Chine" (naish2022a, p. 4). A position printed for the *Eotyrannus* excavation would settle it.
