# *Ilokelesia*

## Does *Ilokelesia* take the top of the Huincul Formation?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** No. The level at the top of the formation is the one Baiano and colleagues (2020) give for the *Huinculsaurus* holotype, about 10 m north of this one, and a value given for another taxon at the same site does not go in the record. The 10 m is a horizontal distance, and the paper does not say the two holotypes share a level. Governs the empty `location.part` on [*Ilokelesia aguadagrandensis*](../../../genera/I/Ilokelesia.yml). Supersedes the record's 2026-08-27 note, which took the part from that sentence.

**Evidence.**
- baiano2020a, p. 3: MCF-PVPH-36 "was collected at the locality of Aguada Grande … about 10 m north of the holotype specimen of the abelisaurid *Ilokelesia aguadagrandensis* … The fossil-bearing level consists of a medium-sized yellowish sandstone, attributed to the top of the Huincul Formation".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: upper`.* It is [*Huinculsaurus*](../../../genera/H/Huinculsaurus.yml)'s level.

**Open.** Where in the Huincul the *Ilokelesia* holotype lies. A section placing it would settle it.

## References
