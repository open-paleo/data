# *Inosaurus*

## Is the *Inosaurus* type material from the Echkar Formation?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** No. The type material is from In Tedreft, which Lapparent (1960) places lower in the Continental Intercalaire than In Abangarit, and which Sereno and colleagues (2004) put in the Tiourarén Formation. The Echkar is the unit of In Abangarit, the locality of vertebrae Lapparent referred to the species. Governs `location.formation: Tiourarén` on [*Inosaurus tedreftensis*](../../../genera/I/Inosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lapparent1960a, In Tedreft (no. 22): "The In Tedreft locality is situated lower in the Continental Intercalaire"; In Abangarit (no. 18): "the In Abangarit series is situated in the upper part of the Continental Intercalaire, a little below the Cenomanian-Turonian" (translated). Plate VII, fig. 1 figures an *Inosaurus* caudal from In Abangarit and Plate XI, fig. 1 one from In Tedreft.
- sereno2004a, p. 1325: the *Spinostropheus* holotype was found "at In Tedreft"; "Tiourarén Formation". p. 1326, for *Rugops*: "Near In Abangharit, Niger Republic; Echkar Formation".
- carrano2008a, p. 197: In Abangarit, "Echkar Formation, Tegama Group; Cenomanian"; In Tedreft, "Tiouraren Formation, Tegama Group".
- jones2026c, p. 485: *Inosaurus tedreftensis*, "Occurrence: Echkar Fm."

**Ruled out.**
- *Echkar Formation.* Jones (2026) gives it without a source. It is the unit that Sereno and colleagues (2004) and Carrano and Sampson (2008) give for In Abangarit, the locality of the referred vertebrae, and Lapparent places the type locality lower in the series.

**Open.** Nothing.
