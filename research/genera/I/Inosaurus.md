# *Inosaurus*

## Is the status or placement of *Inosaurus* in dispute?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** No; the dispute block is removed and the parent stays Theropoda. Every treatment read since 1973 calls it a nomen dubium or an indeterminate theropod, and the only narrower placement is Lapparent's (1960) own Megalosauridae. The removed block also called the vertebrae "now-lost", which no paper read says, and still gave Tetanurae incertae sedis after the parent had moved to Theropoda. Governs `parent: Theropoda` and the absence of a dispute on [*Inosaurus*](../../../genera/I/Inosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lapparent1960a: "Small theropod, incompletely known by twenty-five vertebrae and a portion of the tibia"; "It seems to be related to the family Megalosauridae" (translated).
- holtz2004a lists *Inosaurus tedreftensis* among the nomina dubia; weishampel2004a gives "Theropoda indet. (= *Inosaurus tedreftensis*, *Elaphrosaurus iguidiensis*, *E. gautieri*)".
- jones2026c: "These fossils have since been largely ignored; it is unknown what kind of theropod they belonged to."

**Ruled out.**
- *"Now-lost" vertebrae.* Not stated in any paper read; carrano2008a's remark that "not all of Lapparent's specimens can be located today" concerns other In Tedreft material.
- *Tetanurae incertae sedis.* Rests only on the genus being listed in a chapter on tetanurans.

**Open.** The nomen dubium rests on reference works and review chapters; no primary paper read re-examines the material.

## Is the *Inosaurus* type material from the Echkar Formation?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** No. The type material is from In Tedreft, which Lapparent (1960) places lower in the Continental Intercalaire than In Abangarit, and which Sereno and colleagues (2004) put in the Tiourarén Formation. The Echkar is the unit of In Abangarit, the locality of vertebrae Lapparent referred to the species. Governs `location.formation: Tiourarén` on [*Inosaurus tedreftensis*](../../../genera/I/Inosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lapparent1960a, In Tedreft (no. 22): "The In Tedreft locality is situated lower in the Continental Intercalaire"; In Abangarit (no. 18): "the In Abangarit series is situated in the upper part of the Continental Intercalaire, a little below the Cenomanian-Turonian" (translated). Plate VII, fig. 1 figures an *Inosaurus* caudal from In Abangarit and Plate XI, fig. 1 one from In Tedreft.
- sereno2004a, p. 1325: the *Spinostropheus* holotype was found "at In Tedreft"; "Tiourarén Formation". p. 1326, for *Rugops*: "Near In Abangharit, Niger Republic; Echkar Formation".
- carrano2008a, p. 197: In Abangarit, "Echkar Formation, Tegama Group; Cenomanian"; In Tedreft, "Tiouraren Formation, Tegama Group".
- jones2026c, p. 485: *Inosaurus tedreftensis*, "Occurrence: Echkar Fm."

**Ruled out.**
- *Echkar Formation.* Jones (2026) gives it without a source. It is the unit that Sereno and colleagues (2004) and Carrano and Sampson (2008) give for In Abangarit, the locality of the referred vertebrae, and Lapparent places the type locality lower in the series.

**Open.** Nothing.
