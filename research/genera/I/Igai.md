# *Igai*

## What is the Igai holotype's number?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Bare Vb-621–640, the collection numbers of the former Technische Universität Berlin series, now held at the Museum für Naturkunde. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id` (Vb-621 to Vb-640) on *Igai semkhu*.

**Evidence.**
- gorscak2022a, p. 3: "Holotype—Vb-621–640, a closely associated partial postcranial skeleton"; p. 2: "Vb; vertebrate fossil collections of the Sonderforschungsbereiches 69 of the Technische Universität Berlin", transferred to the Freie Universität Berlin about 1999; p. 3: "On September 11, 2008, the Kharga Oasis titanosaur skeleton … were relocated to the MfN."

**Ruled out.**
- *MB.R.Vb-621-640.* Jones (2026) gives this form in a table (jones2026b); the describing paper never uses an MB prefix.

**Open.** Whether the specimen has been re-accessioned at the Museum für Naturkunde; the paper uses only the Vb numbers (that it was never re-accessioned is our inference).
