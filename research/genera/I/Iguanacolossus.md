# *Iguanacolossus*

## How is the *Iguanacolossus* locality written?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** "Don's Ridge, UMNH VP locality 1206", the site and the museum locality number the describing paper prints for the holotype. Kirkland and colleagues' "Don's Ridge north" is a direction in a figure caption describing a view, not a site name the describing paper uses. Governs `location.locality` on [*Iguanacolossus fortis*](../../../genera/I/Iguanacolossus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mcdonald2010b, p. 5, Locality and Horizon: "Don's Ridge (discovered by DDD in 2005), UMNH VP locality 1206, near Green River, Grand County, Utah".
- kirkland2016a, p. 197, Fig. 50D: "Cedar Mountain section as viewed from Don's Ridge north, *Iguanocolossus* type locality (UMNH 1206)".

**Ruled out.**
- *"Don's Ridge north".* "North" appears only in the caption, lowercase.

**Open.** Nothing.
