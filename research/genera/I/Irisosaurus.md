# *Irisosaurus*

## Which stages does the *Irisosaurus yimenensis* holotype take?

*2026-11-27*

**Conclusion.** Its unit's epoch, with no stage. Peyre de Fabrègues and colleagues (2020) place the holotype in the upper member of the formation, "Lower Jurassic". Governs the absent `stage` on [*Irisosaurus yimenensis*](../../../genera/I/Irisosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.** None beyond the unit entry.

**Ruled out.** Nothing beyond the [Fengjiahe](../../stratigraphy/f/fengjiahe.md) entry.

**Open.** Nothing.
