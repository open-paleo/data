# *Isanosaurus*

## Is the *Isanosaurus* site in Chaiyaphum or Khon Kaen Province?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** Chaiyaphum. Buffetaut and colleagues (2000) place Phu Nok Khian hill in Chaiyaphum Province. *The Dinosauria* gives Khon Kaen as a label for the Nam Phong Formation, not for the site. Governs `location.region: TH-36` on [*Isanosaurus attavipachi*](../../../genera/I/Isanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- buffetaut2000a, p. 72: "dark red sandstones of the Nam Phong Formation at Phu Nok Khian hill near Ban Non Thaworn village, in Chaiyaphum Province".
- upchurch2004a, p. 261, Table 13.1: *I. attavipachi*, "Nam Phong Formation (Khonkaen), Thailand".
- weishampel2004b, p. 527: the Late Triassic Nam Phong occurrences are listed under "Changwat Khon Kaen, Thailand".

**Ruled out.**
- *Khon Kaen (TH-40).* The table attaches the province to the formation, the same province the distribution chapter of that book uses as the formation's heading, and gives no site; the describing paper names the site and its province.

**Open.** Nothing.
