# *Iani*

## Is *Iani* a rhabdodontomorph?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Yes, as its describers place it, though not by every analysis. Zanno and colleagues (2023) recover it within Rhabdodontomorpha on two independent matrices, and Fonseca and colleagues (2024) and Czepiński and Madzia (2025) recover it there in Tenontosauridae. Dieudonné and colleagues (2026) place it outside Iguanodontia, and Rotatori and colleagues (2026) find it inside or outside the rhabdodontids depending on the method. Governs `parent: Rhabdodontomorpha` and the dispute on [*Iani smithi*](../../../genera/I/Iani.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zanno2023a, p. 41: "Our primary findings—that Iani is closely related to the genus Tenontosaurus and rhabdodontids—are stable regardless of matrix chosen or analytical approach employed".
- fonseca2024a, Tenontosauridae: "Under all analyses, this clade is fully resolved with Iani smithi and Convolosaurus marri as successive sister taxa to Tenontosaurus".
- dieudonné2026a, pp. 35–36: *Tenontosaurus* and *Iani* "are very distant from Ankylopollexia and Rhabdodontomorpha".
- rotatori2026a, Results: under parsimony "The earliest diverging clade within Iguanodontia is Rhabdodontidae, which includes Iani smithi"; in the non-clock Bayesian analysis *Iani* "is not recovered within Rhabdodontidae".

**Ruled out.**
- *Iguanodontia incertae sedis, as for Tenontosaurus.* The analyses that place *Tenontosaurus* outside Rhabdodontomorpha mostly predate *Iani*; the analyses that include *Iani* recover it inside, except on the matrix of Dieudonné and colleagues (2026).

**Open.** Whether *Iani* and *Tenontosaurus* settle together.
