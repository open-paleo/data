# *Iberospinus*

## Which district is the *Iberospinus* type locality in, and what position do the describers print for it?

*2026-08-27*

**Conclusion.** Setúbal district, at the one-decimal position the describing paper prints. Mateus and Estraviz-López (2022) place Praia de Aguncheiras in the municipality of Sesimbra, Setúbal district, at 38.4˚ N, 9.2˚ W; the Lisbon district they also name is where the Museum of Lourinhã, which houses ML1190, stands. Governs `location.region: PT-15` and `location.coordinates: [38.4, -9.2]` on [*Iberospinus natarioi*](../../../genera/I/Iberospinus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mateus2022a, p. 8, Type locality and horizon: "Praia de Aguncheiras (Also known as Praia do Guincho), in the municipality of Sesimbra, Setúbal district, Portugal (38.4˚ N, 9.2˚ W; paleocoordinates: 31.8˚ N, 1.3˚ E)".
- mateus2022a, p. 5: "ML1190 is housed at the Museum of Lourinhã (ML), situated in the village of Lourinhã, Lisbon district, Portugal"; the Data Availability Statement on p. 1 repeats it.
- mateus2022a, p. 4: "The Papo Seco Formation outcrops are located in Cabo Espichel (Espichel Cape) in the Setúbal Peninsula, district of the same name, Municipality of Sesimbra, central west Portugal (38.4˚ N, 9.2˚ W".

**Ruled out.**
- *Lisbon district (PT-11).* The paper gives it only for the museum in Lourinhã, and gives Setúbal for the find site.
- *38.443901, −9.2073.* No paper read prints this four-decimal pair; it lies about 4.9 km from the printed value (our calculation).

**Open.** The printed pair is the same one the paper gives on p. 4 for the Papo Seco outcrops at Cabo Espichel as a whole, so at one decimal it places the cape rather than the beach. A paper that prints the excavation's own position would settle it.
