# *Ischioceratops*

## Which formation is the *Ischioceratops* holotype from?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** The Hongtuya, by way of its locality. He and colleagues (2015) give Kugou in the Wangshi Group and name no formation; An and colleagues (2015) sample "the dinosaur bone beds in the Hongtuya Formation" in the Kugou–Longgujian section and do not name *Ischioceratops*. That the holotype's bed is one of those bone beds is an inference from the locality. Governs `location.formation: Hongtuya` on [*Ischioceratops zhuchengensis*](../../../genera/I/Ischioceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- he2015a, p. 3, Type locality and horizon: "Kugou, Zhucheng, Shandong Province, China; Upper Cretaceous Wangshi Group"; p. 21: the holotype "was found at approximately the same stratigraphic level within the Kugou quarry as the holotype specimen of Zhuchengceratops".
- an2015a, p. 76: "Detrital zircons were separated from six samples of sandstones from the dinosaur bone beds in the Hongtuya Formation (Fig. 2). Four samples … were collected from a section in Kugou–Longgujian".
- jones2026a, p. 411: *Ischioceratops zhuchengensis*, "Xingezhuang Fm., China", and the same for *Zhuchengceratops inexpectus*.

**Ruled out.**
- *Xingezhuang Formation.* Jones (2026) gives it in a table without a source. Neither He and colleagues nor An and colleagues use the name, and An and colleagues put the Kugou bone beds in the Hongtuya.
- *An and colleagues' section coordinates as the holotype's position.* They locate the sampled sections (see [Zhucheng sample numbers](../../topics/zhucheng-sample-numbers.md)); He and colleagues print no position.

**Open.** A paper that places this specimen, or the Kugou quarry, in a formation by name would replace the inference.
