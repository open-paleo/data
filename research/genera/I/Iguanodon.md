# *Iguanodon*

## Which work and pages carry the naming of *Iguanodon bernissartensis*?

*2026-11-13*

**Conclusion.** Van Beneden's report on Boulenger's notice, in the Academy's *Bulletins*, pp. 600–608, where Boulenger's proposal of the name is reported on p. 606. A separately paginated copy of the same report had been filed as a second work under Boulenger's name; it is the same text, and the record now cites the report once. Governs `erected_in: vanbeneden1881a` on *I. bernissartensis* in [*Iguanodon*](../../../genera/I/Iguanodon.yml), and supersedes the key in the 2026-08-06 entry's first Evidence line.

**Evidence.**
- vanbeneden1881a, p. 600: the heading "Sur l'arc pelvien chez les Dinosauriens de Bernissart; par M. G.-A. Boulenger. Rapport de M. P.-J. Van Beneden."
- vanbeneden1881a, p. 606: "M. Boulenger considère les Iguanodons de Bernissart comme devant former une espèce inconnue jusqu'à ce jour et propose, si les recherches ultérieures le confirment, ajoute-t-il, le nom de *Iguanodon bernissartensis*" ("M. Boulenger considers the Bernissart Iguanodons to form a species unknown until now and proposes, if later research confirms it, he adds, the name *Iguanodon bernissartensis*", translated).

**Ruled out.**
- *Boulenger as the author of the work.* The text is Van Beneden's report; Boulenger is the author of the name it reports, which the record's reference note says.
- *The 1–11 pagination.* It belongs to the separately paginated copy, whose second page is numbered 4 where the *Bulletins* printing has 601.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which formation are the *Iguanodon bernissartensis* type specimens from?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Sainte-Barbe Clays Formation of the Bernissart colliery. Governs `location.formation: Sainte-Barbe Clays` on *Iguanodon bernissartensis* in [*Iguanodon*](../../../genera/I/Iguanodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- vanbeneden1881a, p. 600 (translated): "It was at the Sainte-Barbe shaft of the Bernissart coalmine, in the clays filling a gallery driven across the seams, that M. Latinis … ascertained the existence of numerous bones".
- norman2012a, p. 196: "Locality and horizon of the holotypes. *I. bernissartensis*: Bernissart, Hainaut, Belgium. Sainte-Barbe Clays Formation (Late Barremian–earliest Aptian; Yans et al., 2006)".
- jones2026a, Styracosterna table: "Numerous localities across Europe *Early Cretaceous, Barremian/Aptian*".
- norman2004a, systematics table: *I. bernissartensis* from the "Wessex Formation (Isle of Wight), Hastings Beds, Weald Clay (West Sussex), Weald Clay (Surrey), Potton Sands (Bedfordshire), England; Wealden (Hainaut), Belgium" and units in France, Spain, Germany and Mongolia.

**Ruled out.**
- *A different unit from the reference works.* Jones's (2026) cell describes the species' distribution and names no unit. Norman (2004) lists every unit that has yielded referred material; neither bears on the type.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What age are the *Iguanodon bernissartensis* type specimens?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Barremian to Aptian, after Norman (2012), who gives the Sainte-Barbe Clays as late Barremian to earliest Aptian. Governs `period.stage: [Barremian, Aptian]` on *Iguanodon bernissartensis* in [*Iguanodon*](../../../genera/I/Iguanodon.yml).

**Evidence.**
- norman2012a, p. 175: "The Belgian locality at Bernissart, assigned to the Sainte-Barbe Clays Formation (late Barremian–Lower Aptian)".
- jones2026a, Styracosterna table: "*Barremian/Aptian*".
- norman2004a, systematics table: *I. bernissartensis*, "Valanginian Albian", for the units listed in the entry above.

**Ruled out.**
- *Valanginian–Albian.* Norman's (2004) table gives this span for the species' referred material across all its units, not for the Bernissart type.

**Open.** Nothing.
