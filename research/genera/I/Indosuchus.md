# *Indosuchus*

## Which specimen is the *Indosuchus raptorius* type?

*2026-08-04*

**Conclusion.** GSI K27/685, the lectotype Chatterjee (1978) designated from Huene and Matley's (1933) three syntypes. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [GSI K27/685]` and `type_specimen.specimen_type: lectotype` on [*Indosuchus raptorius*](../../../genera/I/Indosuchus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- huene1933a, p. 68: "*Indosuchus raptorius*, gen. et sp. nov. Frontals—K 20/350 and K 27/690, and roof of skull K 27/685", with no type designated.
- chatterjee1978a, p. 573: "This specimen, K27/685, the posterior part of the skull … is designated herein as the lectotype for *Indosuchus raptorius* from Huene's original syntypes"; and "Lectotype.—Posterior part of the skull roof, specimen K27/685".
- novas2004b, p. 69: "GSI K27/685 was designated as lectotype of *Indosuchus raptorius* by Chatterjee (1978)."
- jones2026c, Abelisauridae and Brachyrostra table: "GSI K27/685, GSI K20/350, GSI K20/690—skull fragments".
- molina-pérez2019a, List of Theropods, Abelisauridae: "GSI K20/350".
- white1973a, Alphabetical Listing of Genera, *Indosuchus*: "Type: … IM K20/350."
- chure2000a, p. 371: "White (1973) specifically designated K27/350 as the type".

**Ruled out.**
- *All three syntypes as the type.* Jones (2026) lists the original series, which Chatterjee's designation replaced. The third number is also misprinted: Huene and Matley write K 27/690, not K20/690.
- *K20/350 as the type.* White's (1973) catalogue prints a "Type:" line in each of its 383 entries and never says whether one is a holotype, lectotype or syntype, so its *Indosuchus* line is not read as a lectotype designation. Chure (2000) misquotes it as K27/350. Molina-Pérez and Larramendi (2019) give the same specimen.
- *The AMNH material as lectotype.* Dalman and Gishlick (2011) write that the AMNH specimen was "referred to *Indosuchus raptorius* and designated as a lectotype" in 1978. Chatterjee refers that material to the species but designates K27/685.

**Open.** Nothing.
