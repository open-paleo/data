# *Irritator*

## Is the *Irritator* holotype from the Romualdo Formation or the Santana Formation?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Romualdo Formation. Martill and colleagues (1996) wrote "Romualdo Member of the Santana Formation"; the Santana has since been raised to a group with the Romualdo as one of its formations, so both name the same beds. Governs `location.formation: Romualdo` on [*Irritator challengeri*](../../../genera/I/Irritator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- martill1996a, Locality and stratigraphy: the specimen "occurs within an early diagenetic concretion typical of those from the Romualdo Member of the Santana Formation (Martill 1993)".
- sayão2020a, p. 2: "Nowadays, there is a consensus to consider the former Santana Formation as the Santana Group, that is further divided from base to top, into the Barbalha, Crato, Ipubi and Romualdo formations".
- delcourt2025a, p. 2: the Romualdo theropods include "the well-preserved skull of Irritator challengeri".
- holtz2004a, Table 4.1: *Irritator challengeri*, "Santana Formation", "?Albian".
- jones2026c, Spinosauridae table: "Romualdo Fm., Brazil *Early Cretaceous, Aptian/Albian*".

**Ruled out.**
- *Santana Formation as a separate reading.* Holtz (2004) gives the unit at its older rank; it contains the Romualdo, so it does not disagree with the record.

**Open.** Nothing on the unit. Martill and colleagues report the locality as unknown; see the record's location note.
