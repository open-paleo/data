# *Ibirania*

## Is *Ibirania* from the São José do Rio Preto or the Adamantina Formation?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** The São José do Rio Preto Formation, where the describing paper puts the type horizon. The Adamantina comes from an earlier paper on a referred specimen, and one of its authors is among those who assign the type to the São José do Rio Preto. Governs `location.formation: São José do Rio Preto` on [*Ibirania parva*](../../../genera/I/Ibirania.yml).

**Evidence.**
- navarro2022a, Systematic Paleontology, Type species: "from the late Santonian to early Campanian (Upper Cretaceous) of São José do Rio Preto Formation, Bauru Basin at Ibirá Municipality, São Paulo State, Brazil".
- aureliano2021a, Locality and horizon, for the fibula LPP-PV-0043: "Ibirá municipality, Adamantina Formation (ca. 85.2 ± 2.7 Ma, Santonian; Castro et al., 2018), Bauru Group".
- navarro2022a, Thin Section Sampling: LPP-PV-0043 is "a referred specimen" of *Ibirania parva*. Aureliano is a coauthor of navarro2022a.

**Ruled out.**
- *Adamantina Formation.* It is stated for a referred specimen, not the type horizon, and the later paper describing the type, with the same author, uses the São José do Rio Preto.

**Open.** Nothing.
