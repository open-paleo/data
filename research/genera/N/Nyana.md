# *Nyana*

## Where does *Nyana* sit in the tree?

*2026-11-11*

**Conclusion.** `parent: Massopoda` with `placement: incertae sedis`. Its describers recover it in three positions in their reduced consensus trees, two inside Massospondylidae and one outside it, all within Massopoda, and do not rank them; Massopoda is the nearest clade every position agrees on. Governs `parent`, `placement` and `dispute` on [*Nyana*](../../../genera/N/Nyana.yml).

**Evidence.**
- moopen2026a, 7. Phylogenetic analysis: "N. rooiwalensis is variably recovered as either a sister to Sarahsaurus and nested within Massospondylidae (figures 62 and 63a), a sister to Ngwevu and nested within Massospondylidae (figure 63b) or a sister to Sarahsaurus as an earlybranching massopodan outside of Massospondylidae (figure 63c)."
- moopen2026a, 7. Phylogenetic analysis: "Bremer and bootstrap support values are low for most nodes, with Bremer support values ranging from 1 to 3".

**Ruled out.**
- *`parent: Massospondylidae`.* Two of the three positions, but the third places *Nyana* outside the family, and the paper does not prefer one.

**Open.** A further analysis that resolves *Nyana*'s position relative to Massospondylidae.

## Does the record carry the paper's whole diagnosis and its mass estimate?

*2026-11-11*

**Conclusion.** No. `diagnostic_features` holds the three characters the paper marks as autapomorphies; the other thirteen are a unique combination. `size` is left out: the paper's mass is for the juvenile holotype at its stage of growth, from other taxa's growth curves, and adults "may have reached multi tonne body masses". Governs `diagnostic_features` and `size` on [*Nyana*](../../../genera/N/Nyana.yml).

**Evidence.**
- moopen2026a, Diagnosis: "a unique character combination and three autapomorphies (the latter indicated by '\*')".
- moopen2026a, 8.3: "Nyana probably had a body mass of approximately 400 kg at this ontogenetic stage based on growth curves derived for M. carinatus and Mussaurus".

**Ruled out.**
- *A 400 kg `size`.* It would present one juvenile's estimated mass as the size of the taxon.

**Open.** Nothing.
