# *Nemegtosaurus*

## Which part of the Nemegt locality is the *Nemegtosaurus* skull from?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** No source read names one. Nowinski (1971) gives only "the locality of Nemegt" and sends the reader to Gradziński and colleagues' locality plan for the exact position. Governs `location.locality: Nemegt` on [*Nemegtosaurus mongoliensis*](../../../genera/N/Nemegtosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- nowinski1971a, p. 57: the skull "was found at the locality of Nemegt in the Nemegt Basin"; "The exact location of both specimens is given by GRADZINSKI *et al.* (l. c., Text-figs. 2, 4)."
- gradziński1969a, p. 38, Fig. 2 caption, "Map of locality Nemegt": find 6 is an "almost complete sauropod skull (65 cm long)", the only sauropod skull among the twelve numbered finds.

**Ruled out.** None. Both candidates weighed are under Open, because no source contradicts either.

**Open.** Two readings fit and neither is confirmed.
- *Central Sayr.* No source read gives it for this skull. Gradziński and colleagues describe "the short but broad Central Sayr" as one of the gorges of the Nemegt locality (gradziński1969a, p. 38), but their caption does not say which sayr each find lies in.
- *Find 6 on Gradziński and colleagues' plan.* It is the only sauropod skull on the plan Nowinski cites, but the caption gives no specimen number, and its 65 cm differs from the 560 mm skull length Nowinski measures (nowinski1971a, p. 62, Table).

Currie, Wilson, Fanti, Mainbayar and Tsogtbaatar (2018), "Rediscovery of the type localities of the Late Cretaceous Mongolian sauropods *Nemegtosaurus mongoliensis* and *Opisthocoelicaudia skarzynskii*", would settle both: Carballido and colleagues credit it with "The recent relocation of the quarry from which Nemegtosaurus was excavated" (carballido2022a, p. 279).
