# *Nanosaurus*

## Is the *Nanosaurus agilis* holotype from Garden Park, Colorado, or Quarry 7 at Como Bluff, Wyoming?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** Garden Park. YPM VP 001913 came from Cope's Nipple, near Cañon City, Colorado. Quarry 7 at Como Bluff is the site of the *Laosaurus consors* holotype, YPM VP 001882, which Carpenter and Galton (2018) refer to *N. agilis*. Governs `location.locality: Cope's Nipple, Garden Park Paleontological Recreation Area` and `location.region: US-CO` on [*Nanosaurus agilis*](../../../genera/N/Nanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- barrett2025a, p. 31, Locality and horizon of YPM VP 001913: "Upper Jurassic Morrison Formation, close to the top of a geographic feature known as 'Cope's Nipple,' Garden Park Paleontological Recreation Area, near Cañon City, Colorado".
- carpenter2018b, p. 170: Mudge's 1877 letter to Marsh puts the specimen "from the sandstone about ten feet above the big saurian [i.e., *Camarasaurus supremus*]"; "This places the locality at a small hill now called 'Cope's Nipple.'"

**Ruled out.**
- *Quarry 7, Como Bluff, Wyoming.* This is the site of YPM VP 001882: "Quarry 7, also known as the Three Trees Quarry, Como Bluff, Wyoming" (barrett2025a, p. 39). Carpenter and Galton treat *N. agilis* as "represented by several nearly complete skeletons …, including the holotype of *Laosaurus consors* … That specimen was collected from Como Bluff, Wyoming" (carpenter2018b, p. 175). That is a referred specimen's site under their synonymy, not the site of the name-bearing type, and Barrett and Maidment (2025) do not accept the referral.

**Open.** Nothing.
