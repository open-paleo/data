# *Nigersaurus*

## Does level GAD 5 put the *Nigersaurus* holotype in the Elrhaz or the Echkar Formation?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** The Elrhaz. The describing paper says GAD 5 spans the top of the Elrhaz and the base of the Echkar, but Taquet and Russell (1999) place GAD 5 in the upper Elrhaz, and Wilson and Allain (2015) list *Nigersaurus* as "Elrhaz (GAD 5)". Governs `location.formation: Elrhaz` and `location.bed: GAD 5` on [*Nigersaurus taqueti*](../../../genera/N/Nigersaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno1999a, p. 1346, note 11: the holotype is from "the locality 'niveau des Innocents' in the Gadoufaoua region".
- taquet1999a, p. 86, for the *Lurdusaurus* type at Gadoufaoua: "level GAD 5 (upper part of the Elrhaz Formation)".
- wilson2015a, p. 4, Table 1: "*Nigersaurus taqueti* | Elrhaz (GAD 5) | Aptian/Albian".

**Ruled out.**
- *Echkar Formation.* Sereno and colleagues write that "GAD 5 corresponds with the upper part of the Elrhaz Formation and the lower part of the Echkar Formation, which yielded all of the sauropod remains described here from the Tegama Group" (sereno1999a, p. 1346, note 12). That describes the level, not this skeleton, and the two later sources put the level in the Elrhaz.

**Open.** Nothing.
