# *Nemegtomaia*

## Is the *Nemegtomaia* holotype from the Red Walls, and do Fanti and colleagues' coordinates apply to it?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** Neither. The holotype papers name only the Nemegt locality, in the Nemegt Formation. Fanti and colleagues (2012) give the Red Walls and two coordinate pairs for referred specimens from the Baruungoyot Formation. Governs `location.locality: Nemegt Locality`, `location.formation: Nemegt` and the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Nemegtomaia barsboldi*](../../../genera/N/Nemegtomaia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- lü2004a, p. 96, Type locality and horizon: "The Nemegt locality of Gradzinski *et al.* (1968), Nemegt Basin, southern Gobi Desert, Mongolia; Nemegt Formation, mid-Maastrichtian".
- fanti2012a, p. 5: "the holotype of Nemegtomaia came from the Nemegt Formation less than 500 m from where MPC-D 107/15 was collected."

**Ruled out.**
- *Red Walls.* The site of the referred MPC-D 107/16: "collected from the uppermost layers of the Baruungoyot Formation in the eastern portion of the Nemegt locality from the Red Walls (N 43°30.327′, E 101°04.706′)" (fanti2012a, p. 4, Methods).
- *The Northern Sayr coordinate.* MPC-D 107/15 was collected "from the red sandstones of the Baruungoyot Formation in the Northern Sayr of the Nemegt locality (N 43°30.353′, E 101°03.383′)" (fanti2012a, p. 4, Methods). The holotype lies within 500 m of that point, but in a different formation, and no source gives its own position.

**Open.** Nothing.
