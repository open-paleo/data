# *Nanshiungosaurus*

## Which formation is the *Nanshiungosaurus brevispinus* holotype from?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The Nanxiong Formation, at Dapingcun in Guangdong, from the describing paper. Governs `location.formation: Nanxiong` on [*Nanshiungosaurus brevispinus*](../../../genera/N/Nanshiungosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dong1979a, read in English translation, under Locality and stratigraphic position: "Upper Cretaceous Nanxiong Fm. at the village of Dapingcun, Shuikou Commune (Loc. 74015)".

**Ruled out.**
- *Zhonggou Formation, Albian.* Jones (2026) gives "Zhonggou Fm., China, Early Cretaceous, Albian" for IVPP V4731 (jones2026c, p. 505). This is the horizon of "*Nanshiungosaurus*" *bohlini*, another species from the Mazongshan area of Gansu: Li and colleagues (2007) give it as "a late Early Cretaceous (?Albian ...) horizon of the Xinminpu (= 'Xinminbao') Group in the Mazongshan area of Gansu Province" (li2007a, p. 540), and You and colleagues (2014) put "the dinosaur-bearing Xinminpu Group in the Mazongshan area" in the Zhonggou Formation (you2014a, p. 75). Jones's row gives *N. brevispinus* the other species' horizon.
- *Yuanpu Formation.* *The Dinosauria* gives "Yuanpu Formation (Guandong)" (weishampel2004a, p. 152, Table 7.1), the older broad sense of the Nanxiong Basin red beds (see [Nanxiong Formation and the Ganzhou Basin](../../topics/nanxiong-and-guifeng.md)).

**Open.** Nothing.
