# *Neovenator*

## Where does *Neovenator* sit within Allosauroidea?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** Allosauroidea incertae sedis. Most analyses recover it in Carcharodontosauria, but Kellermann and colleagues (2025) find it outside that clade, lower in Allosauroidea, under some weightings; as one of the clade's specifiers, *Neovenator* then leaves Carcharodontosauria without content. Allosauroidea is the level all current readings share. Apesteguía and colleagues' (2016) placement outside Allosauroidea is treated as superseded: the authors call it in need of further testing, and no later analysis read repeats it. Governs `parent: Allosauroidea` and the dispute on [*Neovenator*](../../../genera/N/Neovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brusatte2008a: "*Neovenator salerii* is recovered as the basal most member of Carcharodontosauridae", in the broad sense used before Carcharodontosauria was named.
- carrano2012a: "the sister lineage to Carcharodontosauria (Carcharodontosauridae + Neovenatoridae)".
- rauhut2024a: Allosauria includes "*Allosaurus*, the Neovenatoridae (including *Neovenator* and *Chilantaisaurus*), and the Carcharodontosauridae".
- aranciagarolando2024a, supplementary information: "*Neovenator* forms the sister taxa of Carcharodontosauridae".
- kellermann2025a: "A Carcharodontosauria, as defined by Benson et al., is recovered in some … but not in all of our analyses. In the other analyses … *Neovenator* is recovered in a more basal position within Allosauroidea, together with its then sister taxon *Chilantaisaurus*."
- apesteguía2016a: "our reanalysis following changes to the matrix found Neovenator as sister taxon to a clade of coelurosaurs plus Gualicho and megaraptorans rather than as a member of Allosauroidea."

**Ruled out.**
- *Carcharodontosauria incertae sedis.* The earlier parent; a current analysis recovers it outside the clade.
- *"Consistently recovered as a basal carcharodontosaurian".* The earlier summary; not all analyses recover it there.
- *"A now-deprecated Neovenatoridae".* The name is in current use (rauhut2024a, tanaka2021a); what was dropped is the megaraptorans, now mostly placed in Coelurosauria (aranciagarolando2022a, kellermann2025a).

**Open.** Whether later analyses settle Carcharodontosauria.

## Does Brusatte and colleagues' grid reference SZ 420817 give the *Neovenator* holotype's position?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** No. The reference marks where Plant Debris Bed 9 first appears in the cliff, 400 m west of Grange Chine. The same paragraph places the holotype excavation about 300 m west of the chine, without a grid reference of its own, and Hutt and colleagues (1996) give the grid reference only on request. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Neovenator salerii*](../../../genera/N/Neovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brusatte2008a, p. 5: Plant Debris Bed 9 "is first seen in the cliff face 400 m west of Grange Chine (SZ 420817), and dips 5 degrees eastward, reaching the beach about 150 m west of Grange Chine."
- brusatte2008a, p. 6: "The holotype was excavated c. 300 m west of Grange Chine at a level 4 m above the modern beach."
- hutt1996a, p. 638: "Type locality: Grange Chine, Brighstone Bay, Isle of Wight, United Kingdom (Grid Reference available on request)."

**Ruled out.**
- *SZ 420817 converted to a point.* A six-figure grid reference is normally a printed coordinate, but this one is printed for a point on the bed's outcrop, not for the excavation. The two lie about 100 m apart along the outcrop, a distance comparable to the reference's own resolution, so the converted point would be near the site but is not its stated position.

**Open.** The grid reference Hutt and colleagues held on file has not been published in any source read.
