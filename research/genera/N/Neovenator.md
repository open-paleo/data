# *Neovenator*

## Does Brusatte and colleagues' grid reference SZ 420817 give the *Neovenator* holotype's position?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** No. The reference marks where Plant Debris Bed 9 first appears in the cliff, 400 m west of Grange Chine. The same paragraph places the holotype excavation about 300 m west of the chine, without a grid reference of its own, and Hutt and colleagues (1996) give the grid reference only on request. Governs the empty `location.coordinates` on [*Neovenator salerii*](../../../genera/N/Neovenator.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brusatte2008a, p. 5: Plant Debris Bed 9 "is first seen in the cliff face 400 m west of Grange Chine (SZ 420817), and dips 5 degrees eastward, reaching the beach about 150 m west of Grange Chine."
- brusatte2008a, p. 6: "The holotype was excavated c. 300 m west of Grange Chine at a level 4 m above the modern beach."
- hutt1996a, p. 638: "Type locality: Grange Chine, Brighstone Bay, Isle of Wight, United Kingdom (Grid Reference available on request)."

**Ruled out.**
- *SZ 420817 converted to a point.* A six-figure grid reference is normally a printed coordinate, but this one is printed for a point on the bed's outcrop, not for the excavation. The two lie about 100 m apart along the outcrop, a distance comparable to the reference's own resolution, so the converted point would be near the site but is not its stated position.

**Open.** The grid reference Hutt and colleagues held on file has not been published in any source read.
