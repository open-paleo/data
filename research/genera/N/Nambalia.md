# *Nambalia*

## Is the whole ISI R273 lot the *Nambalia* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** No. Novas and colleagues (2010) restrict the holotype to three element numbers within ISI R273, and give the rest of the lot as paratypes. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [ISIR 273/1, ISIR 273/2, ISIR 273/3]` on [*Nambalia roychowdhurii*](../../../genera/N/Nambalia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas2010a, p. 335: "Holotype. Part of ISI R273, comprising the following elements found almost in articulation: a right ilium (ISI R273/1), left femur (ISI R273/2), and distal half of the left tibia and fibula with articulated astragalus and calcaneum (ISI R273/3)"; "Paratypes. Part of ISI R273, including partial postcrania of at least two individuals".

**Ruled out.**
- *The ISI R273 series.* Jones (2026) gives "ISI R273 series—various skeletal elements" (jones2026b, p. 372), which takes in the paratypes.

**Open.** Nothing.
