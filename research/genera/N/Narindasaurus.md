# *Narindasaurus*

## Which MNHN numbers make up the *Narindasaurus* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** The eight numbers MNHN MAJ 423 to 430, one individual. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id`, the eight ids MNHN MAJ 423 through MNHN MAJ 430, on [*Narindasaurus thevenini*](../../../genera/N/Narindasaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- royo-torres2020a, Systematic palaeontology: "Holotype: A partial skeleton composed by a right maxillary or premaxillary tooth (MNHN MAJ 423), an anterior caudal vertebra (MNHN MAJ 424)", through "left pubis (MNHN MAJ 430). All elements belong to the same individual".

**Ruled out.**
- *The MNHN MAJ 400 series.* Jones (2026) gives "MNHN MAJ 400-series—fragments" (jones2026b, p. 375), a range far wider than the eight numbers.

**Open.** Nothing.
