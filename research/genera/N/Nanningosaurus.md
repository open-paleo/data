# *Nanningosaurus*

## Does *Nanningosaurus* take a formation?

*2026-10-11*

**Conclusion.** No. The describing paper gives only "Upper Cretaceous red beds"; the Nadu Formation it mentions is the name of a regional survey whose age it rejects, and the lower Luowen Formation is relayed by Dai and colleagues (2024) from sources not read. The same reasoning leaves the formation empty for [*Qingxiusaurus*](../Q/Qingxiusaurus.md), from the same excavation pit. Governs the empty `location.formation` on [*Nanningosaurus dashiensis*](../../../genera/N/Nanningosaurus.yml). Supersedes the reason given when the formation was emptied on 2026-08-31, that no formation was ever named for these beds.

**Evidence.**
- mo2007a, Locality and horizon: "Dashi village, suburb of Nanning City, Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region; Upper Cretaceous red beds."
- mo2007a, Discussion: the deposit "in the Nalong Basin was once considered to be Paleogene (Yongning Group) or Eocene (Nadu Formation)", and "the portion of the Nadu Formation from which it came is probably the same age as the fossil bearing locality that preserves *Tsintaosaurus*".
- dai2024a, p. 16: "*Nanningosaurus dashiensis* from the late Campanian lower half of the Luowen Formation in Guangxi Province", citing a 1997 Guangxi geological bureau work, Zhang and Lin (2011) and a personal communication.

**Ruled out.**
- *Lower Luowen Formation.* It is relayed from works not read, and the *Qingxiusaurus* record from the same pit would disagree.
- *Nadu Formation.* It is the regional survey's name, used by a paper that rejects the survey's dating.

**Open.** Mo, Wang and Huang (1998), "Dinosaur fauna from the Nalong Basin, Guangxi and new comments on related stratigraphy" (*Journal of Guilin Institute of Technology* 18, supplement), reports the 1991 excavation and would likely settle the unit.

## What is the *Nanningosaurus* holotype's number?

*2026-07-30*

**Conclusion.** NHMG 8142, from the describing paper. Mo and colleagues (2007) print it closed up as NHMG8142; the record adds the space used for the institution's other numbers. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [NHMG 8142]` on [*Nanningosaurus dashiensis*](../../../genera/N/Nanningosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- mo2007a, p. 550: "Holotype: NHMG8142, disarticulated partial skeleton including relatively complete right and left maxillae".

**Ruled out.**
- *NMG8142.* Jones (2026) prints "NMG8142—partial skull and skeleton" (jones2026a, p. 406), dropping the H.

**Open.** Nothing.
