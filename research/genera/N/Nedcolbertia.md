# *Nedcolbertia*

## Is the *Nedcolbertia* holotype from the lower or the upper Yellow Cat Member?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** The upper Yellow Cat, at its base. The describing paper's "base of the Yellow Cat Member" was written when a thick nodular calcrete was taken as the base of the Cedar Mountain Formation. Kirkland and Madsen (2007) later place the formation's base lower, at a thin silcrete, and Kirkland and colleagues (2016) put the *Nedcolbertia* sites at the base of the upper Yellow Cat. Governs `location.member: Yellow Cat` and `location.part: upper` on [*Nedcolbertia justinhofmanni*](../../../genera/N/Nedcolbertia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kirkland1998c, p. 240: the remains were "resting directly on a thick nodular calcrete at the very base of the Cedar Mountain Formation"; p. 241: "The type material is from CEUM loc. # 42GR 190v at the base of the Yellow Cat Member of Cedar Mountain Formation".
- kirkland2007a, p. 46, Fig. 27 caption: at the *Nedcolbertia* type locality, "base of Cedar Mountain as now understood to be associated with a thin silcrete below the bottom of photograph".
- kirkland2016a, p. 129: "The *Nedcolbertia justinhoffmani* sites at the base of the upper Yellow Cat Member preserve mostly pedal and caudal elements".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`.* It follows from reading "base of the Yellow Cat Member" (kirkland1998c, p. 241) against the later two-part division of the member. The 1998 paper predates that division, and the base it means is the calcrete. In Kirkland and colleagues' (2016, p. 129) account the upper Yellow Cat's lower lacustrine system "immediately overlies the marker calcrete".

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which specimen is the *Nedcolbertia* holotype?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** CEUM 5071, the juvenile. The two subadults found with it are referred material. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [CEUM 5071]` on [*Nedcolbertia justinhofmanni*](../../../genera/N/Nedcolbertia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kirkland1998c, p. 241: under Holotype, "CEUM 5071, partial associated skeleton of a juvenile specimen"; p. 240: the specimens "consist of a juvenile (CEUM 5071) and two sub-adults (CEUM 5072, 5073)".

**Ruled out.**
- *TMP 96.90.2.* Molina-Pérez and Larramendi give "Nedcolbertia justinhofmanni (juvenile) TMP 96.90.2" in a table of running speeds (molina-pérez2019a, p. 176). That number does not appear in the describing paper.

**Open.** What TMP 96.90.2 is.

## What stage is the *Nedcolbertia* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Valanginian. The holotype sits at the base of the upper Yellow Cat Member, and at Utahraptor Ridge the upper Yellow Cat above the marker calcrete is Valanginian by an eruption age and carbon-isotope correlation. Applying that to the *Nedcolbertia* site, about 1 km from the Gaston Quarry, is an inference: the Yellow Cat is diachronous (see [Yellow Cat](../../stratigraphy/y/yellow-cat.md)). Governs `stage: [Valanginian]` on [*Nedcolbertia justinhofmanni*](../../../genera/N/Nedcolbertia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- joeckel2023a, p. 3: at Utahraptor Ridge a prominent "calcrete" lies at about 26.5–32 m, with the mudstone interval "32 m and above" over it; p. 12: from the "eruption age of 135.1 ± 0.3 Ma of a cryptotephra from 36.35 m above the contact … we interpret the C-isotope record from 32.5 to 42.5 m as Valanginian".
- kirkland2016a, p. 129: "The Nedcolbertia justinhoffmani sites at the base of the upper Yellow Cat Member".
- kirkland1998c, p. 241: the type locality is "one km east-northeast of Gaston Quarry".

**Ruled out.**
- *Aptian.* Jones (2026) gives "Cedar Mt. Fm., Utah, USA, Early Cretaceous, Aptian" (jones2026c, p. 503), a formation-wide reading. The date read here for the upper Yellow Cat is older.

**Open.** A date from the *Nedcolbertia* site itself, or from a section tied to the Gaston Quarry. Joeckel and colleagues call their correlation tentative.
