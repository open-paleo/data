# *Nedcolbertia*

## Is the *Nedcolbertia* holotype from the lower or the upper Yellow Cat Member?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** The upper Yellow Cat, at its base. The describing paper's "base of the Yellow Cat Member" was written when a thick nodular calcrete was taken as the base of the Cedar Mountain Formation. Kirkland and Madsen (2007) later place the formation's base lower, at a thin silcrete, and Kirkland and colleagues (2016) put the *Nedcolbertia* sites at the base of the upper Yellow Cat. Governs `location.member: Yellow Cat` and `location.part: upper` on [*Nedcolbertia justinhofmanni*](../../../genera/N/Nedcolbertia.yml).

**Evidence.**
- kirkland1998c, p. 240: the remains were "resting directly on a thick nodular calcrete at the very base of the Cedar Mountain Formation"; p. 241: "The type material is from CEUM loc. # 42GR 190v at the base of the Yellow Cat Member of Cedar Mountain Formation".
- kirkland2007a, p. 46, Fig. 27 caption: at the *Nedcolbertia* type locality, "base of Cedar Mountain as now understood to be associated with a thin silcrete below the bottom of photograph".
- kirkland2016a, p. 129: "The *Nedcolbertia justinhoffmani* sites at the base of the upper Yellow Cat Member preserve mostly pedal and caudal elements".

**Ruled out.**
- *`part: lower`.* It follows from reading "base of the Yellow Cat Member" (kirkland1998c, p. 241) against the later two-part division of the member. The 1998 paper predates that division, and the base it means is the calcrete. In Kirkland and colleagues' (2016, p. 129) account the upper Yellow Cat's lower lacustrine system "immediately overlies the marker calcrete".

**Open.** Nothing.
