# *Nanotyrannus*

## Is the *Nanotyrannus lancensis* holotype from Sand Creek in Carter County or from near Jordan?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** Sand Creek, Carter County. Gilmore (1946) gives a survey description and the creek in the paper that describes CMNH 7541, and Bakker and colleagues (1988) repeat it. Zanno and Napoli (2025) write that CMNH 7541 was "collected from the Hell Creek Formation near Jordan" and cite no source for it; Jordan is in Garfield County, not Carter County. Governs `location.locality: Sand Creek` and `location.region: US-MT` on [*Nanotyrannus lancensis*](../../../genera/N/Nanotyrannus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- gilmore1946a, p. 2: "sec. 11, R. 61 E., T. 4 S., Sand Creek, Carter County, Mont."
- bakker1988b, Type specimen: CMNH 7541 was "Found by David Dunkle in 1942, in the Hell Creek Formation, SE 1/4, sec. 11, R61E, T4S, Sand Creek, Carter County, Montana".

**Ruled out.**
- *Near Jordan, Garfield County.* Zanno and Napoli, Locality and Horizon of NCSM 40000: "NCSM 40000 was recovered near Jordan, Garfield County, Montana, USA … CMNH 7541 was also collected from the Hell Creek Formation near Jordan. Its stratigraphic position and that of KUVP 156375 are currently unresolved." (zanno2025a). The sentence is attached to another specimen's locality and contradicts the survey description of the 1946 paper.

**Open.** Whether Zanno and Napoli had new provenance information. The sentence cites no source. The "Jordan theropod" is a different specimen from near Jordan (longrich2024d, 1.4. Nanotyrannus), which may explain how the name reached this holotype; that is our inference.
