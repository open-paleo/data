# *Nullotitan*

## Does the *Nullotitan* holotype carry one number or two?

*2026-08-01*

**Conclusion.** Two. The holotype is one individual cataloged under MACN-PV 18644, the cervical collected in 1981, and MPM 21542. Governs `type_specimen.specimen_id: [MACN-PV 18644, MPM 21542]` on [*Nullotitan glaciaris*](../../../genera/N/Nullotitan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas2019a, p. 243: "Holotype. MACN-PV 18644 and MPM 21542, the specimen consists of isolated cervical centrum (presumably Cv3; MACN-PV 18644), fragmentary cervical rib shaft, dorsal rib shaft fragments, several caudal vertebrae".

**Ruled out.**
- *MACN-PV 18644 alone.* The record's earlier value; it leaves out the second number the holotype line gives.

**Open.** The same paper also gives MPM 21542 to three teeth of an indeterminate titanosaur from another locality: "Referred material: MPM 21542, three isolated teeth (locality 4)" (novas2019a, p. 242). One of the two uses is wrong, and the paper does not say which.
