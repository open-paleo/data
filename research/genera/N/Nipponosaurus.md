# *Nipponosaurus*

## What stage is the Nipponosaurus holotype horizon?

*2026-09-30*

**Conclusion.** Late Santonian or early Campanian, Matsumoto's reassessment as reported by Obata (1979). Governs `stage: [Santonian, Campanian]` and the upper [Yezo Group](../../../stratigraphy/y/yezo.yml) placement on *Nipponosaurus sachalinensis*.

**Evidence.**
- matsumoto1979a, p. 58, in Obata's appendix on the ages of reptile fossils (pp. 53–58): "日本竜の産地の層準は，Inoceramus schmidti 帯（K6a2）より下位すなわちInoc. orientalis 帯（K6a1）かそれ以下，つまりカンパニアン下部ないしはサントニアン上部と考えられる" ("the horizon of the Nipponosaurus locality is considered to be below the I. schmidti Zone (K6a2), i.e. the Inoc. orientalis Zone (K6a1) or lower, that is, lower Campanian or upper Santonian", translated). Obata reports it as Matsumoto's judgement after "調査資料を検討する機会を得た" ("had the opportunity to examine the survey data", translated).
- suzuki2004a, Locality and Age: "Upper Ammonites Bed; now Upper Yezo Group. Matsumoto and Obata (1979) advocated a late Santonian-early Campanian age". Matsumoto and Obata do not use the name Upper Yezo Group; the equivalence is Suzuki and colleagues'.

**Ruled out.**
- *The Inoceramus schmidti Zone.* Nagao (1936) reports from the matrix only "An external cast of a small ammonite, supposed to be Puzosia japonica" (nagao1936a, p. 218), and infers from the bed's position that "the complex from which the animal has been taken belongs to this zone of Inoceramus schmidti" (p. 220). Matsumoto puts the horizon below that zone. Suzuki and colleagues' statement that the locality produced *I. schmidti* overstates Nagao.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which group is the *Nipponosaurus* holotype from?

*2026-08-06*

**Conclusion.** The upper Yezo Group. Nagao's (1936) "Upper Ammonites Bed" is now the Upper Yezo Group, as Suzuki and colleagues (2004) and Takasaki and colleagues (2017) give it. The record stops at group rank because no finer horizon can be recovered. Governs `location.group: Yezo` and `location.part: upper` on [*Nipponosaurus sachalinensis*](../../../genera/N/Nipponosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- suzuki2004a, p. 146, Locality and Age: "Upper Ammonites Bed; now Upper Yezo Group"; under Comments, "it is presently impossible for additional geological research to be conducted at the Kawakami colliery by Japanese researchers", and "field notes made at the time of the excavation are missing".
- takasaki2017a, p. 5: "Upper Ammonite Bed; now Upper Yezo Group."

**Ruled out.**
- *Ryugase Group.* Jones (2026) gives "Ryugase Grp., Russia" (jones2026a, p. 409), and *The Dinosauria* gives "Ryugase Group" (horner2004a, p. 442, Table 20.1). Its distribution chapter cites Nagao (1936) and Suzuki and colleagues (2004) for the name (weishampel2004b, p. 593), but Nagao gives only the "Upper Ammonites Bed" and Suzuki and colleagues give the Upper Yezo Group.

**Open.** Nothing.
