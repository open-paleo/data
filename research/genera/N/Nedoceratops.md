# *Nedoceratops*

## Does the *Nedoceratops* skull lie southwest or southeast of the mouth of Lightning Creek?

*2026-08-31*

**Conclusion.** Southwest, as Hatcher's own locality line gives it. The 1905 description and its reprint in the 1907 monograph both say "southwest"; the monograph's localities chapter says "southeast". Governs `location.locality: about three miles southwest of the mouth of Lightning Creek` on [*Nedoceratops hatcheri*](../../../genera/N/Nedoceratops.yml).

**Evidence.**
- hatcher1905a, p. 417, quoting Hatcher's description: "The specimen was found in a hard sandstone concretion about three miles southwest of the mouth of Lightning Creek, Converse County, Wyoming."
- hatcher1907a, p. 149, the *Diceratops hatcheri* account: "about 3 miles southwest of the mouth of Lightning Creek, Converse Co., Wyo. [At the place marked +25 on the map, Pl. LI.]"

**Ruled out.**
- *Southeast of the mouth of Lightning Creek.* The localities chapter of the same monograph has "about 3 miles southeast of the mouth of Lightning Creek, at the point marked +25, Pl. LI" (hatcher1907a, p. 181). It cites the same map point as the species account and adds no other description of the site, so it reads as a restatement in which the bearing changed.

**Open.** Plate LI, the monograph's locality map, would settle which bearing +25 fits.
