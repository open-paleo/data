# *Nhandumirim*

## Which paper recovers *Nhandumirim* as a sauropodomorph close to *Saturnalia*?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Müller and Garcia (2020). Their strict consensus tree places *Nhandumirim waldsangae* within Sauropodomorpha, in a cluster with *Saturnalia tupiniquim*, *Chromogisaurus novasi* and *Bagualosaurus agudoensis*, and their reduced tree labels that clade Saturnaliidae. The placement is shown only in the figures; the text names *Nhandumirim* once, among the taxa added to the matrix. Governs the dispute summary and `parent: Saturnaliidae` on [*Nhandumirim*](../../../genera/N/Nhandumirim.yml).

**Evidence.**
- müller2020b, p. 3, Fig. 1: "Time-calibrated strict consensus tree"; *Nhandumirim waldsangae* sits inside the Sauropodomorpha box beside *Bagualosaurus*, *Saturnalia* and *Chromogisaurus* (read from the figure).
- müller2020b, p. 4, Fig. 2: the "Reduced strict consensus tree" collapses that clade into a terminal labeled Saturnaliidae (read from the figure).
- müller2020b, p. 2, Material and methods: *Nhandumirim waldsangae* is among "The added OTUs".

**Ruled out.**
- *Müller (2021) as the source.* The paper scores *Erythrovenator* into the matrix of Müller and Garcia (2020), but its only tree collapses Sauropodomorpha to one branch, and on *Nhandumirim* it relays others: "the taxon was regarded as a sauropodomorph by some authors (e.g. Pacheco et al. 2019; Müller and Garcia 2020)" (müller2021a, 6. Discussion and conclusions). It was cited here with a note saying its analysis recovered the placement, which overstated it.

**Open.** Nothing.

## Which formation is the *Nhandumirim* holotype from?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** The Santa Maria Formation, Alemoa Member, at Waldsanga (Cerro da Alemoa). Governs `location.formation: Santa Maria`, `location.member: Alemoa` and `location.part: upper` on [*Nhandumirim waldsangae*](../../../genera/N/Nhandumirim.yml).

**Evidence.**
- marsola2018a, p. e1531878-2: "The new specimen comes from deposits of the Alemoa Member of the Santa Maria Formation, at the site known as Waldsanga".
- marsola2018a, p. e1531878-1, abstract: "The Late Triassic (Carnian) upper Santa Maria Formation of south Brazil".

**Ruled out.**
- *Caturrita Formation.* The record's earlier value. No source read here places the holotype in the Caturrita, the unit above the Santa Maria.

**Open.** Nothing.

## What stage is the *Nhandumirim* holotype?

*2026-08-05*

**Conclusion.** Carnian. The describing paper says so, and the Waldsanga site's bone bed is dated. Governs `stage: [Carnian]` on [*Nhandumirim waldsangae*](../../../genera/N/Nhandumirim.yml).

**Evidence.**
- marsola2018a, p. e1531878-1, abstract: "The Late Triassic (Carnian) upper Santa Maria Formation".
- langer2018a, accepted manuscript, p. 7: the dated bone bed is at "Cerro da Alemoa (type-locality of Saturnalia tupiniquim)", and the date "is consistent with a late Carnian (Tuvalian) age for their associated fauna"; 225.42 ± 0.37 Ma is "the maximum age of the Caturrita Formation". Marsola and colleagues give Waldsanga and Cerro da Alemoa as one site.

**Ruled out.**
- *Norian.* The record's earlier value. It came with the Caturrita assignment, and Langer and colleagues place the Norian in the overlying unit (see [Santa Maria](../../stratigraphy/s/santa-maria.md)).

**Open.** Nothing.
