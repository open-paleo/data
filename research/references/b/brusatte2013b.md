# Brusatte and Benson (2013)

## Why is the paper keyed 2013 when it was available online in 2012?

*2026-10-09*

**Conclusion.** 2013, the year of the printed issue. The paper names *Juratyrant*, and an online release counts toward a name's availability only from 2012 and only where the work states its ZooBank registration; this one states none, so the name dates from *Acta Palaeontologica Polonica* 58(1), 2013. Governs the key and `year` of this entry, and the authority year of [*Juratyrant*](../../../genera/J/Juratyrant.yml).

**Evidence.**
- brusatte2013b, p. 47: "Received 27 November 2011, accepted 14 February 2012, available online 17 February 2012."
- brusatte2013b, p. 47, citation line: "Brusatte, S.L. and Benson, R.B.J. 2013. … Acta Palaeontologica Polonica 58 (1): 47–54."
- brusatte2013b, p. 52: the new genus is headed "Genus *Juratyrant* nov.", and the paper carries no ZooBank registration or LSID.

**Ruled out.**
- *2012, the online date.* The first-availability rule would give 2012 for a paper that names nothing, but a name-bearing paper is keyed by the year its names became available, and the 2012 release lacks the ZooBank statement Article 8.5 requires.

**Open.** Nothing.
