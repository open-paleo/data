# Belenky (1961)

## Where was Belenky's 1961 monograph on the Pritashkent chuls published?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** In the Trudy of Tashkent State University, new series, no. 181 (geological sciences, book 16), pp. 1–183, not as a Samarkand University Press book. Belenky's monograph has not been read; the venue rests on the citations below. Governs `journal`, `volume`, `issue` and `pages` on this entry.

**Evidence.**
- vereshchagin1979a, p. 507, ref. 122: «Тр. Ташкент. ун-та. Нов. сер.», 1961, вып. 181, геол. науки, кн. 16, с. 1—183 ("Trudy of Tashkent Univ., new ser., 1961, no. 181, geol. sciences, book 16, pp. 1–183", translated).
- nessov1997a, first page of the reference list: «Труды Ташкент. ун-та. Нов. сер. 1961. Вып. 181. Геол. науки. Кн. 16. С. 1-183»; nessov1995a, English translation, reference list, gives the same as "Belenky, G. A. 1961".
- The series numbers run in order of date: no. 63 in 1955 under the Central Asian University (vereshchagin1979a, p. 507, ref. 121), no. 181 in 1961, no. 234 in 1964 (rozhdestvensky1964a, translation, title block) and no. 273 in 1966 (vereshchagin1979a, p. 507, ref. 123).

**Ruled out.**
- *Samarkand University Press.* Rozhdestvensky (1964) gives "Izdatel'stvo Samarkandskogo Gosudarstvennogo Universiteta, 1961" (rozhdestvensky1964a, translation, p. 11), but the copy read for this note is a 1969 Canadian government translation, and Rozhdestvensky's 1968 paper gives Tashkent, no. 181 (rozhdestvensky1968a, translation, Literature Cited). Its "pp. 1-181" may be the translator's slip.

**Open.** The year the Central Asian University became Tashkent State University; no source read for this note gives it.
