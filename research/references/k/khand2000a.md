# Khand and colleagues (2000)

## Does Khand and colleagues' reference 19 (Vasil'ev and colleagues, Sovetskaya Geologiya 1959) exist?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** No article matching it has been found; the work cited for the Tsagaantsav and Zuunbayan Formations is the 1959 book by Vasil'ev and colleagues, *Geologicheskoe stroenie Mongol'skoy Narodnoy Respubliki* (Gostoptekhizdat, Leningrad, 492 pp.). Cite vasil'ev1959a, not the article. Governs which reference records cite for the original definitions of those formations, such as [Zuunbayan](../../../stratigraphy/z/zuunbayan.yml).

**Evidence.**
- khand2000a, p. 77: "19. V.G. Vasilev, G.L. Grishin and K.B. Mokshantsev, Sovetskaya Geology, 2 (1959) 68. (in Russian)"; p. 62: the Tsagaantsav "Formatiom was established in the basin of Dornogobi at the Tsagaan Tsav well by Vasilev et al. [19]", and "According to the Vasilev et al. [19], it was the lower Zuunbayan Formation".
- shuvalov2000a, p. 277; jerzykiewicz2000a, p. 296; eberth2009a, p. 302: each cites Vasil'ev and colleagues (1959) as "Leningrad: Gostoptekhizdat, 492 pp.".

**Ruled out.**
- *A Sovetskaya Geologiya article.* No article by these authors in that journal, 1959, no. 2, is indexed in elibrary.ru or the Russian State Library catalogue (searched 2026-09-30), and no other source read for this note cites one.

**Open.** The book has not been read; Shuvalov (2000) is the accessible account of it.
