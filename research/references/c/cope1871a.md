# Cope (1871)

## Is Cope's paper on the homologies of reptile cranial bones the same work as his 1869 Synopsis?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** No. Cope (1871), "On the homologies of some of the cranial bones of the Reptilia…", is in the Proceedings of the American Association for the Advancement of Science 19: 194–247; the "Synopsis of the extinct Batrachia, Reptilia and Aves of North America" is Cope (1869) in the Transactions of the American Philosophical Society 14. Hadrosauridae is from the Synopsis; Compsognathidae is listed in the 1871 paper. Governs `journal` on this entry, and `erected_in` for Hadrosauridae (cope1869b) and Compsognathidae (cope1871a).

**Evidence.**
- cope1871a, p. 194: running head "B. NATURAL HISTORY", then "II. ZOÖLOGY. 1. ON THE HOMOLOGIES OF SOME OF THE CRANIAL BONES OF THE REPTILIA…", the section layout of the AAAS Proceedings; the paper ends on p. 247.
- cope1871a, p. 239: "In the 'Transactions of the American Philosophical Society,' 1869, part I. (August), I proposed a system…", and on p. 234 he cites Hadrosauridae as "Cope, Trans. Am. Philos. Soc., 1869, p. 91".
- cope1871a, p. 234, in a catalogue of reptile families: "1. SYMPHYPODA. Cope; Compsognatha, Huxley. Compsognathidæ; Compsognathus, Wagner.", with no earlier authority given (that this erects the family is our reading).
- cope1869b, p. 91: "HADROSAURIDAE. Embracing the genus Hadrosaurus, Leidy."; p. 89 signature line "AMERI. PHILOSO. SOC.—VOL. XIV."

**Ruled out.**
- *Both papers in the Transactions.* Cope himself cites the Transactions as a different venue (p. 239).

**Open.** The copy of the 1871 paper read for this note has no volume title page, so the venue name itself is not printed in it.

- `cope1871a`: Cope, E. D. (1871). On the homologies of some of the cranial bones of the Reptilia, and on the systematic arrangement of the class. *Proceedings of the American Association for the Advancement of Science* 19: 194-247.
