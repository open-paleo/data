# Colombi and colleagues (2015)

## What are the pages of Colombi and colleagues' Balde de Leyes paper?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** Pages 445–455 of *Revista de la Asociación Geológica Argentina* 72(4), as the journal's own table of contents for the issue gives them. Governs `pages`.

**Evidence.**
- The journal's table of contents for volume 72, issue 4, lists the paper, with all ten authors, at 445–455.

**Ruled out.**
- *Pages 456–469.* Martínez and Apaldetti (2017) cite "*Revista de la Asociación Geológica Argentina* 72: 456–469" (martínez2017a, reference list). The issue's table of contents contradicts it.

**Open.** Nothing.
