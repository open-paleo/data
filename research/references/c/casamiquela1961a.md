**Conclusion.** Yes: "Dos nuevos estagonolepoideos argentinos (de Ischigualasto, San Juan)", Revista de la Asociación Geológica Argentina XVI(3–4): 143–203, which cites the 1960 preliminary note as an earlier work. Governs `title` on this entry.

**Ruled out.**
