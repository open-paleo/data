# Thulborn (1973)

## Was Thulborn's paper naming Alocodon, Phyllodon and Trimucrodon published in 1973 or 1975?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** 1973: the volume's imprint reads "LISBOA 1973". Governs `year` on this entry and the authority year of *Alocodon kuehnei*, *Phyllodon henkeli* and *Trimucrodon cuneatus*.

**Evidence.**
- thulborn1973a, volume title page: "MEMÓRIA N.º 22 — (NOVA SÉRIE)… LISBOA 1973"; the paper runs pp. 89–134.
- thulborn1973a, pp. 93, 110, 119: "Alocodon kuehnei gen. et sp. nov.", "Phyllodon henkeli gen. et sp. nov.", "Trimucrodon cuneatus gen. et sp. nov.".
- thulborn1973a, pp. 133–134: an addendum "Since this paper went to press (1972)…" cites Thulborn (1974) on a heterodontosaurid as in press, consistent with a 1973 issue.

**Ruled out.**
- *1975.* Mateus and colleagues date all three species "Thulborn, 1975" and give the paper as "Thulborn, R. A., 1975. Teeth of ornithischian dinosaurs… Fauna do Kimerridgiano… 22: 89-134" (mateus2022b, pp. 60, 64), against the imprint. A bare "Thulborn 1975" elsewhere is usually Thulborn's "Dinosaur polyphyly" paper (*Australian Journal of Zoology* 23: 249–270), a different work.

**Open.** Nothing.
