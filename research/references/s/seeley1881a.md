# Seeley (1881)

## Is Suess's note on the Gosau beds part of Seeley's paper?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** No. Suess's appendix (pp. 702–703) is a separately authored note printed inside Seeley's paper (pp. 620–707) and registered with its own DOI; the two entries stay separate, with overlapping pages. Governs `pages` on seeley1881a and suess1881a, and which of them a record cites for the Gosau horizon.

**Evidence.**
- seeley1881a, p. 620: "42. The REPTILE FAUNA of the GOSAU FORMATION…"; p. 706: "Synopsis of the Bones figured in these Plates"; p. 707: "Discussion. Mr. Hulke considered…", ending "…teeth were not sufficient alone for generic determinations."
- suess1881a, p. 702: "APPENDIX. Note on the Gosau Beds of the Neue Welt, West of Wiener Neustadt. By Prof. Edward Suess"; p. 703 ends "…older than the zone of Hippurites cornu-vaccinum."
- maryańska1975a, p. 175: "Quart. J. Geol. Soc., 37, 620-707".

**Ruled out.**
- *Crossref's ranges.* Crossref gives Seeley 620–706 and Suess 702–707 (DOIs 10.1144/GSL.JGS.1881.037.01-04.49 and .50), as does the Lyell Collection cover sheet ("p620-706"); the printed pages show Suess ends on p. 703 and Seeley's paper, with its discussion, on p. 707.

**Open.** Nothing.
