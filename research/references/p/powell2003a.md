# Powell (2003)

## Is Powell's English revision of South American titanosaurs a separate work from his 1986 thesis?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Yes. Powell (2003) is the published English revision in the Records of the Queen Victoria Museum; the 1986 Tucumán thesis, in Spanish, is a different work that the revision cites as unpublished. Content read in the 2003 revision is cited to powell2003a, not to the thesis. Governs this entry's identity and the choice between powell2003a and powell1986a wherever a record cites what Powell wrote.

**Evidence.**
- powell2003a, p. 1: the work opens directly with its title and "Jaime Eduardo Powell", with no imprint page; the running head on p. 2 gives "Records of the Queen Victoria Museum No. 111", and the cover sheet added by the Biodiversity Heritage Library gives "Records of the Queen Victoria Museum Launceston 111, 1–173". The year 2003 appears only on that cover sheet.
- powell2003a, p. 91: "Powell JE 1986, 'Revision de los titanosauridos de America del Sur. Tesis Doctoral. Univ. Nac. de Tucumán', Argentina, 493 pp. (Unpublished)".
- powell2003a, p. 90: the latest work in the reference list is Lucas and Sullivan (2000), so the text was revised long after 1986.

**Ruled out.**
- *The thesis as the source of the revision's statements.* The thesis has not been read; what the revision says cannot be assumed to be in it.

**Open.** Which of the revision's conclusions were already in the thesis.

## References

- `powell2003a`: Powell, J. E. (2003). Revision of South American Titanosaurid dinosaurs: palaeobiological, palaeobiogeographical and phylogenetic aspects. *Records of the Queen Victoria Museum* 111: 1-173.
- `powell1986a`: Powell, J. E. (1986). Revisión de los titanosáuridos de América del Sur. Doctoral thesis, Universidad Nacional de Tucumán, Tucumán, Argentina.
