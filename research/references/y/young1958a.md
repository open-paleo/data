# Young (1958)

## What is the whole number of Young's Laiyang dinosaur monograph?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** 142: Palaeontologia Sinica, New Series C, no. 16, whole number 142. Young's own later citation gives it, as do thirteen citing reference lists against six. The monograph has not been read. Governs `volume` on this entry.

**Evidence.**
- young1959a, reference list: "1957. The Dinosaurian Remains from Laiyang, Shantung. Pal. Sin; New Ser. C. No. 16, Whole Ser. 142."
- Thirteen citing reference lists give whole number 142, among them sereno1986a, napoli2019a and takasaki2017a.

**Ruled out.**
- *Whole number 42.* Given by six citing reference lists and stored until 2026-10-06.

**Open.** The year. Young's own citation dates the work 1957, against the 1958 the store keys; and the page range, which the citing lists give variously as 1–138, 53–138 and 53–159. The monograph would settle both.
