## Which geological sheet is Hugo and Leanza's General Roca map?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** Sheet 3969-IV, Boletín 308, Buenos Aires, 2001. Governs `title` on this entry.

**Evidence.**

**Ruled out.**
