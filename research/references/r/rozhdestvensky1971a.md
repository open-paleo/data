# Rozhdestvensky (1971)

## Which "Trudy volume 3" is Rozhdestvensky (1971) in?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** Volume 3 of the Trudy of the Joint Soviet-Mongolian *Geological* Expedition, the book *Фауна мезозоя и кайнозоя Западной Монголии* (Nauka, Moscow, 1971), pp. 21–32. The Joint Soviet-Mongolian *Paleontological* Expedition also has a Trudy volume 3, *Палеонтология и биостратиграфия Монголии* (1976), which holds Kurzanov (1976) and Barsbold (1976); the series word is what tells them apart. Governs the title, `book`, `volume` and series string of this entry, and the series strings of kurzanov1976b and barsbold1976a.

**Evidence.**
- barsbold1976a, p. 91: "Изучение динозавров Монголии и их роль в расчленении континентального мезозоя. В сб.: Фауна мезозоя и кайнозоя Западной Монголии. М., «Наука», стр. 21—32." ("A study of the dinosaurs of Mongolia and their role in the subdivision of the continental Mesozoic. In: Fauna of the Mesozoic and Cenozoic of Western Mongolia. Moscow, Nauka, pp. 21–32", translated); maryańska1974a, p. 102, gives the same title.
- tumanova1987a, p. 73: "Тр. Совмест. Сов.-Монг. науч.-исслед. геол. экспедиции. 1971. Вып. 3. С. 21—32" ("Trudy of the Joint Soviet-Mongolian Scientific-Research Geological Expedition, 1971, no. 3, pp. 21–32", translated); maryańska1975a, p. 175, likewise.
- kurzanov1976b, English translation, title page: "PALEONTOLOGY AND BIOSTRATIGRAPHY OF MONGOLIA THE JOINT SOVIET-MONGOLIAN PALEONTOLOGICAL EXPEDITION (Transactions, vol. 3)… 1976".
- tumanova1987a, title page: "СОВМЕСТНАЯ СОВЕТСКО-МОНГОЛЬСКАЯ ПАЛЕОНТОЛОГИЧЕСКАЯ ЭКСПЕДИЦИЯ (Труды, вып. 32)" ("Joint Soviet-Mongolian Paleontological Expedition (Trudy, no. 32)", translated), the same Paleontological series.

**Ruled out.**
- *One series.* A source citing "Trudy 3: 21–32" and one citing the 1971 book by title name the same book; a "Trudy 3" with pages 76–92 or 93–104 is the 1976 Paleontological volume.

**Open.** The 1971 book has not been read; its series statement is known from the citations above.
