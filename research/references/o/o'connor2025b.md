# O'Connor and colleagues (2025)

## Why is the paper keyed 2025 when it asks to be cited as 2026?

*2026-10-07*

**Conclusion.** It was published online on 27 August 2025, and the key and `year` take the year a work first became available. The paper's own citation line gives 2026, the year of the issue. Governs the key and `year` on this entry.

**Evidence.**
- o'connor2025b, p. 1: "Received: February 18, 2025; Accepted: August 22, 2025; Published Online: August 27, 2025".
- o'connor2025b, p. 1: "Citation: O'Connor J.K., Clark A.D., Kuo P.-C., et al., (2026). Avian features of Archaeopteryx feeding apparatus reflect elevated demands of flight. The Innovation 7(2), 101086."

**Ruled out.**
- *o'connor2026a.* The print year, and a key already held by a different paper, O'Connor and Marugán-Lobón (2026) in *Biology Letters*.

**Open.** Nothing.
