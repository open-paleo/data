# Matsumoto and Obata (1979)

## Is "Obata 1979, The ages of the fossil reptiles in Japan, Kaseki 29: 53–58" a separate paper?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** No. It is Obata's appendix (pp. 53–58) inside Matsumoto and Obata's joint paper in *Kaseki* 29: 43–58; cite matsumoto1979a with the page. Governs this entry's `pages` (43–58), and which key a record cites for Obata's age of *Nipponosaurus*.

**Evidence.**
- matsumoto1979a, p. 43: 本邦海成白亜系大型化石についての国際対比上の評価 / 松本達郎・小畠郁生, with the English title in a footnote: "Evaluation of ammonites and other fossils from the Cretaceous of Japan for inter-regional correlation."; the paper ends on p. 58.
- matsumoto1979a, p. 47: 爬虫類化石については本論文の付録として，小畠が要約を示す ("for reptile fossils, Obata gives a summary as an appendix to this paper", translated); p. 53: 付録：爬虫類化石の時代 / 小畠郁生 ("Appendix: the ages of reptile fossils / Obata Ikuwo", translated).
- weishampel2004a, p. 740: "Kaseki 29: 43–58. (In Japanese.)"

**Ruled out.**
- *A separate Obata paper.* Suzuki and colleagues cite "Matumoto, T., and I. Obata. 1979 in Obata, I. 1979 [The ages of the fossil reptiles in Japan. Kaseki. 29:5358" (suzuki2004a, p. 162), which names the appendix inside the joint paper.
- *Pages 43–53.* Takasaki and colleagues give "Fossils. 29:43–53" (takasaki2017a, online-first version, p. 14), which stops before the appendix.

**Open.** Nothing.
