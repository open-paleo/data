# Matheron (1869)

## Which of Matheron's two 1869 papers is "Matheron 1869a" and which "1869b"?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** The store's letters are the reverse of those used in the ICZN case on *Rhabdodon*. Here matheron1869b is the *Note* in the Bulletin de la Société géologique de France (2nd ser., 26: 781–795), where *Rhabdodon* is named, and matheron1869a is the longer *Notice* in the Mémoires of the Marseille Academy (pp. 345–379 and 5 plates), which spells the genus *Rabdodon*. Identify the two by venue, not by letter. Governs `erected_in: matheron1869b` on [*Rhabdodon*](../../../genera/R/Rhabdodon.yml) and [*Hypselosaurus*](../../../genera/H/Hypselosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- matheron1869b, p. 792: "je propose de donner le nom générique de Rhabdodon" ("I propose to give the generic name Rhabdodon", translated); the footnote on the same page refers to "une notice que j'ai lue à l'Académie de Marseille le 1er avril, et qui est sous presse" ("a notice that I read to the Marseille Academy on 1 April, and which is in press", translated).
- matheron1869a, p. 372: "je propose de donner le nom générique de Rabdodon".
- iczn1988a, p. 85: Opinion 1483 refers to "the paper cited in the application as 'Matheron 1869b' (see BZN 43: 271), which used both the spellings Rhabdodon (on the plates) and Rabdodon (in the text)", which fits the Marseille *Notice*, with its plates and *Rabdodon* in the text, not the *Note* (our reading); p. 86 places the name at "Bulletin de la Société Géologique de France, 2ème série, 26: 792".

**Ruled out.**
- *The letters as the ICZN's own.* The Opinion quotes the application's letters in quotation marks; it fixes the name to the Bulletin page, not to a letter.

**Open.** Nothing.
