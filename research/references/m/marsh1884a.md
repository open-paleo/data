# Marsh (1884)

## Is marsh1884a a duplicate of marsh1884b?

*2026-07-08*

**Conclusion.** No. They are two parts of Marsh's series "Principal characters of American Jurassic dinosaurs" in volume 27 of the American Journal of Science, with titles that differ only after the part number. marsh1884a is Part VIII, "The order Theropoda", in issue 160, pp. 329–340; [marsh1884b](marsh1884b.md) is Part VII, on the Diplodocidae, in issue 158, pp. 161–167. The key letters do not follow publication order: Part VII, the earlier, is marsh1884b. Governs `title`, `issue`, `pages` and `doi` on marsh1884a.

**Evidence.**
- marsh1884a, p. 329: "Art. XXXVIII. — Principal Characters of American Jurassic Dinosaurs; by Professor O. C. Marsh. Part VIII. The Order Theropoda."

**Ruled out.**
- *Both entries as Part VIII.* The two articles have different part numbers, article numbers, issues, pages and DOIs.

**Open.** Nothing.
