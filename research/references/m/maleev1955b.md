# Maleev (1955)

## Which of Maleev's two 1955 papers is "Maleev 1955a" and which "1955b"?

*2026-09-08*

**Conclusion.** The store's letters are the reverse of those in the citing literature. Here maleev1955b is "Giant carnivorous dinosaurs of Mongolia" (Doklady 104(4): 634–637), which names *Tyrannosaurus bataar*, and maleev1955a is "New carnivorous dinosaurs from the Upper Cretaceous of Mongolia" (104(5): 779–783), which erects *Tarbosaurus efremovi*. Rozhdestvensky (1965) and Tsuihiji and colleagues (2011) call the first 1955a and the second 1955b. Store letters follow the order entries were added, so identify the two by issue and title, not by letter. Governs the citations of maleev1955a and maleev1955b on [*Tarbosaurus bataar*](../../../genera/T/Tarbosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maleev1955b, first page: "Doklady, Academy of Sciences USSR 1955, 104(4): 634-637. GIANT CARNIVOROUS DINOSAURS OF MONGOLIA"; "Tyrannosaurus bataar sp. nov.".
- maleev1955a, Comparative Notes: *Tarbosaurus efremovi* "is smaller than Tyrannosaurus rex Osborn 1905, and T. bataar Maleev 1955".
- rozhdestvensky1965a, References: "Maleev, Ye. A. 1955a. Gigantskye khishchnyye dinosavry Mongolii. (Giant carnivorous dinosaurs of Mongolia). Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSR, 104, No. 4: 634 - 637."
- tsuihiji2011a, p. 517: "Maleev, E. A. 1955a. [A gigantic carnivorous dinosaur of Mongolia]. Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR 104:634–637."

**Ruled out.**
- *Reading a citing paper's "Maleev (1955a)" as maleev1955a.* In Rozhdestvensky (1965) and Tsuihiji and colleagues (2011), "Maleev, 1955a" is the *Tyrannosaurus bataar* paper, maleev1955b here.

**Open.** Nothing.
