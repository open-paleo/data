# Averianov and colleagues (2025)

## Does the References column of Table 1 give the source of each age?

*2026-09-21*

**Conclusion.** No. The column names the work that reported each taxon, and the body text puts the same citation at the end of sentences that carry a stage. A stage taken from this paper needs its cited work checked before it adds anything. Of three rows checked, two cite works that give no stage and one cites a work that supports its stage. Governs readings taken from this paper on [Shengjinkou](../../../stratigraphy/s/shengjinkou.yml) (`stages: [Valanginian]`), [Hekou](../../../stratigraphy/h/hekou.yml) (`stages: [Valanginian, Hauterivian, Barremian, Aptian, Albian]`) and [Poudingue Ferrugineux](../../../stratigraphy/p/poudingue-ferrugineux.yml) (`stages: [Aptian, Albian]`).

**Evidence.**
- averianov2025b, p. 8, Table 1: "Hamititan xinjiangensis | … | Aptian | Shengjinkou | Wang et al. (2021)"; "Daxiatitan binglingi | … | Aptian | Hekou group | You et al. (2008)"; "Normanniasaurus genceyi | … | Albian | Poudingue Ferrugineux | Le Loeuff et al. (2013)". Body, p. 8: "the Aptian part of the Hekou Group in Gansu, China (You et al., 2008)".
- you2008a, p. 1: "discovered from the Lower Cretaceous Hekou Group of Lanzhou Basin, Gansu Province".
- leloeuff2013a, p. 24: "the fossils came from lower to middle Albian levels of the Poudingue Ferrugineux".

**Ruled out.**
- *The cited work as the source of the stage.* Wang and colleagues (2021) write "Early Cretaceous" and name no stage. You and colleagues (2008) give the Hekou Group as Lower Cretaceous and name no stage. Only Le Loeuff and colleagues (2013) support the stage in their row.

**Open.** The other rows of Table 1 have not been checked against the works they cite.
