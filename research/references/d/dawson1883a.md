# Dawson (1883)

## Do the two printings of Dawson's Bow and Belly River report share their title and pages?

*2026-10-06*

**Conclusion.** No. The separately issued pamphlet (Montreal, Dawson Brothers, 1882) is titled "Preliminary Note on the Geology of the Bow and Belly River Districts, N. W. Territory, with special reference to the coal deposits" and runs pp. 1–19; the Report of Progress printing for 1880–82, part B, is cited as "Preliminary report ... Bow and Belly rivers region", pp. 1–23. The entry describes the Report of Progress printing, the form the Belly River literature cites. Governs the store note on this entry.

- dawson1883a, pamphlet title page: "PRELIMINARY NOTE ON THE GEOLOGY OF THE BOW AND BELLY RIVER DISTRICTS, N. W. TERRITORY, WITH SPECIAL REFERENCE TO THE COAL DEPOSITS. BY GEORGE M. DAWSON ... Montreal: DAWSON BROTHERS. 1882."; text pp. 1–19.

**Ruled out.**
