# Dai and colleagues (2022)

## Why is the paper keyed 2022 when it sits in the 2021 volume?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** 2022, the year the paper was first released. It went online on 3 March 2022, and its issue, *Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology* 41(5), carries the 2021 volume label, which is not a publication date: the paper was accepted only on 30 August 2021. The paper names *Bashanosaurus* and states no ZooBank registration, so its names date from the printed issue, which cannot have been issued before the paper first appeared; both readings give 2022. Governs the key and `year` of this entry, and the authority year of [*Bashanosaurus primitivus*](../../../genera/B/Bashanosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dai2022b, publisher's cover page: "Published online: 03 Mar 2022."
- dai2022b, p. 1: "Citation for this article: Dai, H., Li, N., S. C. R. Maidment, Wei, G., Zhou, Y., Hu, X., Ma, Q., Wang, X., Hu, H., and Peng, G.. 2022."
- dai2022b, p. 14: "Submitted October 6, 2020; revisions received August 27, 2021; accepted August 30, 2021."
- dai2022b, p. 2: the new taxon is headed "BASHANOSAURUS PRIMITIVUS, gen. et sp. nov.", and the paper carries no ZooBank registration or LSID.

**Ruled out.**
- *2021, the volume year.* Volume 41 is the 2021 volume, but an article accepted at the end of August 2021 and first released in March 2022 did not appear in 2021. An issue label is not a publication date.

**Open.** Nothing.
