# Galton (1977)

## Which of Galton's two 1977 Dryosaurus papers is this?

*2026-10-04*

**Conclusion.** The *Nature* paper, 268: 230–232, doi:10.1038/268230a0, which names *Valdosaurus*. Galton published a similarly titled paper the same year in the Milwaukee Public Museum's Special Publications in Biology and Geology 2: 41–54, which has not been read. Governs this entry's title, journal and DOI, and `erected_in: galton1977a` on [*Valdosaurus*](../../../genera/V/Valdosaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- galton1977a, pp. 230–232: "The ornithopod dinosaur Dryosaurus and a Laurasia-Gondwanaland connection in the Upper Jurassic", "PETER M. GALTON", "Nature Vol. 268 21 July 1977"; p. 231, Fig. 2: "Dryosaurus ? canaliculatus Galton (type species of Valdosaurus n. g.)".
- sanz1987a, p. 107: "Galton, P. M. (1977b): Upper Jurassic Ornithopod Dinosaur Dryosaurus and a Laurasia-Gondwanaland connection. Mil-waukee Public Museum. Special Publicat. in Biol. and Geol., 2, 41-54."

**Ruled out.**
- *The Nature title for the Milwaukee paper.* Galton (2009) gives "(1977b) The ornithopod dinosaur Dryosaurus and a Laurasia-Gondwanaland connection in the Upper Jurassic. Milwaukee Public Museum Special Publications in Biology and Geology, 2: 41-54" (galton2009a, p. 266), joining the *Nature* title to the Milwaukee venue.
- *A DOI for the Milwaukee paper from a review.* 10.1086/410998 is Keast's 1979 review, in the Quarterly Review of Biology, of R. M. West (ed.), *Paleontology and Plate Tectonics* (Crossref), which we take to be the Milwaukee volume (our reading); a review's DOI never identifies the work reviewed.

**Open.** The reference lists that letter Galton's 1977 papers call the Milwaukee paper "1977b" (galton2009a; sanz1987a); in the store, galton1977b is Galton's *Staurikosaurus* paper, so the letters cannot be matched across sources.
