# Chaoyangsauridae

## Does Chaoyangsauridae date from Zhao (1983)?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** No; the family dates from Zhao and colleagues (2006). Zhao (1983) proposed only a superfamily, Chaoyoungosauroidea, for *Chaoyoungosaurus*, a genus he did not describe and gave no type species. A family-group name published after 1930 must be formed from an available genus name then used as valid (ICZN Arts. 11.7.1.1, 13.2), so the 1983 name is unavailable, and its stem is not that of *Chaoyangsaurus*, named in 1999. Chaoyangsauridae is therefore not a corrected spelling of the 1983 name, and the rule that a corrected spelling keeps its original authority does not apply. Governs `erected_in: zhao2006a` on [Chaoyangsauridae](../../clades/Chaoyangsauridae.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhao1983a, p. 300: "Chaoyoungosauroidea new superfamily. The chaoyoungosauroids (J3), known thus far only from North China (Chao, in press) … *Chaoyoungosaurus* Chao is here referred."
- zhao2006a, p. 472: "we propose the name Chaoyangsauridae (modified from Chaoyoungsauridae Zhao, 1983) for this probably Late Jurassic lineage of ceratopsians … we define Chaoyangsauridae as a stem-based taxon for all taxa closer to *Chaoyangsaurus youngi* than to either *Triceratops horridus* or *Psittacosaurus mongoliensis*." Read from the page image.
- olshevsky1991a, p. 158: "CHAOYOUNGOSAURIDAE [nomen nudum; emendum ex Chaoyoungosauroidea Zhao, 1983]" and "Genus: Chaoyoungosaurus Zhao, 1983 [nomen nudum]".

**Ruled out.**
- *Zhao (1983) as the authority.* Raised when the rule for corrected spellings changed; the 1983 name is unavailable, and Zhao (1983) prints no "Chaoyoungsauridae", the form Zhao and colleagues (2006) cite.

**Open.** Nothing.
