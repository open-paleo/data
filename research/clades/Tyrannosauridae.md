# Tyrannosauridae

## Is the family Osborn's of 1905 or of 1906?

*2026-10-15*

**Conclusion.** 1906. Osborn's 1905 paper naming *Tyrannosaurus* uses no family name; the family is first proposed in his 1906 paper on the same animal. By coordination the same paper is the authority for Tyrannosaurinae and Tyrannosauroidea. Governs `erected_in` on [Tyrannosauridae](../../clades/Tyrannosauridae.yml), [Tyrannosaurinae](../../clades/Tyrannosaurinae.yml) and [Tyrannosauroidea](../../clades/Tyrannosauroidea.yml).

**Evidence.**
- osborn1905a, pp. 259–265, read in full from the scan: it names *Tyrannosaurus rex*, *Dynamosaurus imperiosus* and *Albertosaurus sarcophagus* as new genera and species and closes, "This review relates only to the large carnivorous dinosaurs and omits reference to the Ornithomimidæ." No family name is given for the carnivores it treats.
- osborn1906a: some of the characters of *Tyrannosaurus* show it to be an *Allosaurus*-like form on a much larger scale, while "others indicate that it belonged to a separate family of the Theropoda which if it shall prove to be distinct may be called Tyrannosauridæ". The proposal is conditional, which does not bar availability for a name published before 1961.
- brusatte2016a writes "Tyrannosauroidea Osborn, 1905".

**Ruled out.**
- *Osborn (1905).* The paper contains no family-group name for the tyrannosaurs.
