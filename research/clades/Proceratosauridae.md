# Proceratosauridae

## Is the family Rauhut and colleagues' or Averianov and colleagues'?

*2026-10-16*

**Conclusion.** Rauhut, Milner and Moore-Fay (2010). They coined the name with a phylogenetic definition after stating in words the characters that unite *Proceratosaurus* with *Guanlong*, which meets the ICZN requirement for a name published after 1930. Averianov and colleagues (2010) diagnosed the family the same year and argued that Rauhut and colleagues' name was a nomen nudum; that reading is not adopted. Governs `erected_in` on [Proceratosauridae](../../clades/Proceratosauridae.yml).

**Evidence.**
- rauhut2010a, p. 184: "Characters uniting *Proceratosaurus* with *Guanlong* are the strongly enlarged nares (character 1; ...), a slight anterior offset of the maxillary ascending ramus from the anterior margin of the maxillary body (character 13; ...), and the presence of a midline cranial crest or horn on the nasals (character 23; ...)."
- rauhut2010a, p. 187: "This clade may be termed the Proceratosauridae, and can be defined as all theropods that are more closely related to *Proceratosaurus* than to *Tyrannosaurus*, *Allosaurus*, *Compsognathus*, *Coelurus*, *Ornithomimus*, or *Deinonychus*."
- averianov2010a, Comments under Proceratosauridae: "According to this Code, every new name published after 1930 must be accompanied by a description or definition that states in words characters that are purported to differentiate the taxon (Article 13.1.1). Thus the name Proceratosauridae as it appeared in Rauhut et al. (2010) is a nomen nudum. Here we provide a diagnosis for the Proceratosauridae and validate usage of this name as a family group taxon."

**Ruled out.**
- *Averianov and colleagues (2010) as the authority.* Their nomen nudum argument treats Rauhut and colleagues' name as lacking characters, but the same paper states the characters uniting the clade's two genera three pages before naming it.
