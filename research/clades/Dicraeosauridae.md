# Dicraeosauridae

## Which work is the authority for the name Dicraeosauridae?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** Janensch (1929), "Material und Formengehalt der Sauropoden in der Ausbeute der Tendaguru-Expedition". Its classification of the sauropods names the subfamily Dicraeosaurinae for *Dicraeosaurus*, the first printed name in the family group, and under coordination the family name takes the same authorship. Governs `erected_in` on [Dicraeosauridae](../../clades/Dicraeosauridae.yml).

**Evidence.**
- janensch1929b, p. 31: the classification lists "Fam. Homalosauropodidae … Subfam. Dicraeosaurinae: *Dicraeosaurus*" (in German, "Unter-Fam. Dicraeosaurinae").
- janensch1929b, p. 30: "Huene's (1927) newest system shows the same number [of families], distinguishing the Cetiosauridae, with the subfamilies Cardiodontidae and Brachiosauridae, the Morosauridae, Dicraeosauridae, Diplodocidae and the Titanosauridae". Its reference list gives that Huene work as "Sichtung der Grundlagen der jetzigen Kenntnis der Sauropoden", *Eclogae geologicae Helvetiae* 20: 444–470.
- huene1927c, p. 463: "A further family appears to be formed by the genus *Dicraeosaurus* (Janensch)". The paper names the Morosauridae and Diplodocidae on the same pages but gives this family no name.

**Ruled out.**
- *Huene (1927).* He recognizes the family but does not name it; Janensch (1929) supplied the name "Dicraeosauridae" in describing Huene's system.
- *Janensch's (1929) monograph on the vertebral column of Dicraeosaurus.* Several works credit the family to "Janensch, 1929" and cite that monograph, which uses no family or subfamily name; the name is in his survey of the Tendaguru sauropods in the same volume.

- `huene1927c`: Huene, F. V. (1927). Sichtung der Grundlagen der jetzigen Kenntnis der Sauropoden [Sorting through the basis of the current knowledge of sauropods]. *Eclogae Geologicae Helvetiae* 20: 444-470.
