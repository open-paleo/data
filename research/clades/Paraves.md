# Paraves

## Does the footnote on Paraves in Sereno's (1997) Table 1 move the authority?

*2026-10-14*

**Conclusion.** No. The name stays credited to Sereno (1997). The table's two footnote marks on its continuation page appear to be transposed: the "new taxon" mark sits on the sauropod clade Macronaria and the citation of a sauropod phylogeny abstract sits on Paraves. Governs `erected_in` on [Paraves](../../clades/Paraves.yml).

**Evidence.**
- sereno1997a, p. 444, Table 1 (continued): "Macronaria" carries footnote c, "New taxon"; "Paraves" carries footnote d, "Wilson & Sereno 1997". The reference list gives that work as "Higher-level phylogeny of sauropod dinosaurs", *Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology* supplement, in press.
- sereno1997a, text on coelurosaurs: "the monophyly of Deinonychosauria plus Aves (Paraves; see Table 1)".
- agnolín2013a, cau2018a, godefroit2013b and pittman2020b each give the authority as Sereno, 1997.

**Ruled out.**
- *Wilson and Sereno (1997) as the authority.* It is an abstract on sauropod phylogeny, a source for Macronaria rather than for a theropod clade, and none of the works above credits Paraves to it.
