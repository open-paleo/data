# Elasmaria

## Where does Elasmaria sit?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Within Ornithopoda, unresolved against Iguanodontia. Analyses on the matrix of Dieudonné and colleagues, and Fonseca and colleagues (2024), recover it within Iguanodontia; analyses on three other matrices, and one early analysis on Dieudonné's, recover it outside. Governs the position of Elasmaria under Ornithopoda in `tree.yml` and the dispute on [Elasmaria](../../clades/Elasmaria.yml).

**Evidence.**
- dieudonné2020a, p. 16: "Elasmaria are therefore members of Iguanodontia".
- madzia2017a, p. 6: *Burianosaurus* "is placed more closely to hadrosaurids than Elasmaria but in a less-derived position than the last common ancestor of Iguanodontia".
- herne2019a, p. 573: "an internally-unresolved Elasmaria within Ornithopoda as the sister clade to Clypeodonta (Fig. 26.2)".
- bertozzo2025a, p. 26: "rhabdodontids, dryosaurids and elasmarians remain poorly resolved in both trees".
- coria2024a, p. 14: the analysis "suggests that it is not within the Elasmaria".
- cruzado-caballero2019a, p. 211: "Boyd (2015) reported Notohypsilophodon within the clade Elasmaria but relocated this clade within Thescelosaurinae and outside Ornithopoda".

**Ruled out.**
- *Iguanodontia as the parent.* Madzia and colleagues (2017), Cruzado-Caballero and colleagues (2019), Herne and colleagues (2019) and Bertozzo and colleagues (2025) do not recover it there.
- *Neornithischia as the parent.* Only Boyd (2015) places it outside Ornithopoda, and Madzia and colleagues (2017), on a modified version of Boyd's matrix, recover it within.

**Open.** Whether Elasmaria is a clade at all: Coria and colleagues (2024) do not recover it, and several analyses exclude *Gasparinisaura*.
