# Caudipteridae

## Caudipteridae or Caudipterygidae, and whose name is it?

*2026-10-28*

**Conclusion.** Caudipteridae Zhou and Wang, 2000. Zhou and Wang named the family "Caudipteridae fam. nov." in April 2000. Its stem does not follow the Greek genitive of *Caudipteryx* (*-pterygis*), but for a family-group name published after 1999 the Code keeps such a spelling as the correct original spelling (ICZN Art. 29.4). Osmólska and colleagues' (2004) "Caudipterygidae (emend.)" is therefore an unjustified emendation, not the correction of a misspelling, and Art. 32.5.3, under which a corrected spelling keeps the original authority, does not list an incorrectly formed stem. The held literature is split between the spellings (Caudipterygidae in eight papers, Caudipteridae in six, most of the recent ones), so prevailing usage (Art. 33.2.3.1) does not establish the emended form. Governs `clade: Caudipteridae`, `erected_in: zhou2000b` and the Caudipterygidae synonym on [Caudipteridae](../../clades/Caudipteridae.yml).

**Evidence.**
- zhou2000b, p. 112: "this paper proposes erecting a new family, the Caudipteridae (Caudipteridae fam. nov.)" (translated); p. 111 gives the heading "Caudipteridae fam. nov.". Read from the page images.
- osmólska2004a, p. 165: "This latter taxon, for which Caudipterygidae (emend.) was established by Zhou and Wang (2000)"; its p. 166 table gives "Caudipterygidae Zhou et Wang, 2000".
- he2008a, p. 179: "Caudipteridae Zhou & Wang, 2000"; qiu2019a, p. 1: "Caudipteridae Zhou et Wang, 2000".

**Ruled out.**
- *Osmólska and colleagues (2004) as the authority.* The record credited them for the emended spelling; under Art. 29.4 their spelling is not a correction.
- *Keeping Caudipterygidae as prevailing usage.* The held papers do not show a substantial majority for it.

**Open.** Art. 29.4 needs the stem to count as the generic name "with the ending elided" (Art. 29.3.3); the Code does not define the ending, and Caudipter- is read here as *Caudipteryx* with *-yx* elided.
