# Herrerasauria

## Where does Herrerasauria sit?

*2026-10-20*

**Conclusion.** Within Saurischia, no closer. Analyses recover herrerasaurs as a branch of Saurischia outside the clade of theropods and sauropodomorphs, as early theropods, or as the sister of Sauropodomorpha; every one of these lies within Saurischia as traditionally used. A single analysis places them outside Dinosauria. Governs the position of Herrerasauria under Saurischia in `tree.yml` and the dispute on [Herrerasauria](../../clades/Herrerasauria.yml).

**Evidence.**
- novas2021a, p. 33: besides the theropod and redefined-Saurischia proposals, "A third proposal indicates that herrerasaurids may constitute a non-Eusaurischia branch of Saurischia, as originally proposed by Langer (2004) and followed by several authors".
- nesbitt2009a, p. 1532: "Herrerasaurus forms a monophyletic Herrerasauridae with Staurikosaurus and Chindesaurus at the base of Theropoda, although clade support is weak".
- sues2011a, p. 3463: the evidence "further supports placement of Eoraptor and Herrerasauridae as basal theropods … rather than as basal saurischians".
- baron2017a, p. 501: "Sauropodomorpha and Herrerasauridae (as the redefined Saurischia) forming its monophyletic outgroup".
- baron2018c, p. 142: "our revised analysis has recovered herrerasaurs outside of Dinosauria".

**Ruled out.**
- *Theropoda as the parent.* The early-theropod result comes mainly from the matrix of Nesbitt and colleagues (2009) and its derivatives, and later analyses on that lineage place herrerasaurs outside the theropod–sauropodomorph clade (Novas and colleagues, 2021; Ezcurra and colleagues, 2025).
- *A position outside Dinosauria.* It rests on one analysis of the dataset of Baron and colleagues (2017).

**Open.** Which position later analyses converge on.
