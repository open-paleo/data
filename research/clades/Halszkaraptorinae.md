# Halszkaraptorinae

## Where does Halszkaraptorinae sit?

*2026-10-21*

**Conclusion.** Within Eumaniraptora, unresolved against Dromaeosauridae. Analyses on the matrix of Cau and colleagues recover it at the base of Dromaeosauridae; Hartman and colleagues (2019) and Wang and colleagues (2026) place it outside, with Unenlagiinae. Governs the position of Halszkaraptorinae under Eumaniraptora in `tree.yml` and the dispute on [Halszkaraptorinae](../../clades/Halszkaraptorinae.yml).

**Evidence.**
- cau2017a, Fig. 3g, p. 398: *Halszkaraptor* (*Hulsanpes* + *Mahakala*) as the basalmost dromaeosaurid clade.
- lee2022a, Fig. 3i, p. 4: at the base of Dromaeosauridae, with *Natovenator*.
- hartman2019a, p. 33: "our trees pair unenlagiines and halszkaraptorines, but uniquely places this Unenlagiidae sister to the dromaeosaurid plus troodontid clade".
- wang2026b, Fig. 4, p. 7: Halszkaraptorinae and Unenlagiinae "closer to birds than dromaeosaurids and troodontids".

**Ruled out.**
- *Dromaeosauridae as the parent.* Two analyses on independent matrices place it outside the family.

**Open.** Unenlagiinae is subject to the same disagreement and still sits under Dromaeosauridae.
