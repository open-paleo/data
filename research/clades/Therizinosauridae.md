# Therizinosauridae

## Which definition of Therizinosauridae sets its content, and which paper is its `described_in`?

*2026-10-19*

**Conclusion.** The record follows the node-based definition of Zanno and colleagues (2009): the least inclusive clade containing *Nothronychus*, *Segnosaurus*, *Erlikosaurus* and *Therizinosaurus*. That paper is the `described_in`. Under this definition *Erliansaurus*, *Neimongosaurus* and *Enigmosaurus* fall outside the family, and Kobayashi and colleagues' (2022) stem-based definition would bring them in. Those three are excluded or included by definition alone: the recent analyses agree on their position. *Suzhousaurus* is a matter of topology. It falls just outside in some analyses and inside the derived polytomy in others, so it stays in Therizinosauroidea with the other three. Governs `erected_in`, `described_in` and the dispute on [Therizinosauridae](../../clades/Therizinosauridae.yml), and the dispute blocks on [*Erliansaurus*](../../genera/E/Erliansaurus.yml), [*Neimongosaurus*](../../genera/N/Neimongosaurus.yml), [*Enigmosaurus*](../../genera/E/Enigmosaurus.yml) and [*Suzhousaurus*](../../genera/S/Suzhousaurus.yml).

**Evidence.**
- maleev1954a, p. 108 (translated): "a new family of turtlelike reptiles from Central Asia, Therizinosauridae, for the present with a single genus, Therizinosaurus".
- zanno2009a, electronic supplementary material, B. Expanded Systematic Paleontology: "Definition. The least inclusive clade containing Nothronychus (UMNH VP 16420), Segnosaurus galbinensis (IGM 100/80), Erlikosaurus andrewsi (IGM 100/111), and Therizinosaurus cheloniformis (PIN 551-483)"; "Here, Therizinosauridae is emended to include Nothronychus (UMNH VP 16420) as an internal specifier and Nanshiungosaurus is removed". The same section lists derived characters the clade shares.
- zanno2010a, p. 520: the same definition followed by "(Zanno et al. 2009)", and "Zanno et al. (2009) provide an extensive review of the definitional history of the taxon and provide a slightly modified definition for the clade".
- kobayashi2022a, p. 6: "A new definition of Terizinosauridae is proposed here for the stability of this clade: the most inclusive clade containing Terizinosaurus cheloniformis but not Alxasaurus elesitaiensis", because under Zanno's definition "members of this clade are not stable because of poor resolution for derived therizinosaurs". Fig. 6a, p. 7: *Erliansaurus* + *Neimongosaurus*, then *Enigmosaurus*, below a polytomy that holds *Suzhousaurus* with *Therizinosaurus*, *Erlikosaurus*, *Segnosaurus* and *Nothronychus*.
- kobayashi2025a, STAR Methods: "Therizinosauridae is the most inclusive clade containing Therizinosaurus cheloniformis but not Alxasaurus elesitaiensis", the definition of Kobayashi and colleagues (2022). Fig. 5, p. 8: *Suzhousaurus* in the derived polytomy with *Therizinosaurus*.
- liao2026a, p. 2, Nomenclature: "We follow the definitions of Zanno (2010b) for Therizinosauria, Therizinosauroidea and Therizinosauridae". Fig. 12: *Neimongosaurus*, *Enigmosaurus* and *Suzhousaurus* as successive outgroups to Therizinosauridae.
- hartman2019a, p. 31: "Suzhousaurus, Erliansaurus, and Neimongosaurus are outside a clade including Nanshiungosaurus, Nothronychus spp., Erlikosaurus, and Segnosaurus".
- pu2013a, Fig. 12A, p. 17: one polytomy labelled Therizinosauridae holding all four genera with the derived therizinosaurs, on Zanno's matrix.
- sues2016a, p. 176: "a monophyletic Therizinosauridae, as defined by Zanno (2010b), is not supported"; Fig. 26, p. 175, shows all four genera in the polytomy labelled Therizinosauridae.

**Ruled out.**
- *`described_in: zanno2010a`.* Zanno (2010) restates the 2009 definition and credits it. The synapomorphy list it points to is in an appendix outside the text read. A paper that applies a definition published elsewhere does not qualify.
- *`described_in: kobayashi2022a`.* It qualifies on its own terms: a new definition and nine synapomorphies. But its definition gives the family a content the record does not use.
- *The stem-based definition of Kobayashi and colleagues (2022).* Only the same group has used it since (Kobayashi and colleagues, 2025). Liao and colleagues (2026) still follow Zanno.
- *Suzhousaurus as excluded or included by the definition alone.* Kobayashi and colleagues (2022) list it among the taxa Zanno's definition excludes, but their own Fig. 6a places it within the polytomy that contains all four of Zanno's specifiers. Its position is a disagreement between trees.

**Open.** Whether the stem-based definition is taken up beyond the group that proposed it.
